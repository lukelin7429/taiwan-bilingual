委託製作雙語網站 — 學校準備指南Request a Bilingual Website: what your school needs to prepare

學校想合作嗎？Interested in working with us?

若學校希望製作雙語網站，請先確認校長、主任與承辦人員有共識，並成立該校雙語網站 LINE 群組。 接著依合作說明整理校徽、照片、校長資料、學校特色、新聞與正式英文校名。

If your school would like a bilingual website, please first make sure the principal, directors, and contact person share the same understanding. Then create a LINE group for the project and prepare the required school materials.

合作方向 Ways We Can Work Together

  • 協助學校建立雙語網站，呈現校園特色、活動紀錄與學生學習成果。We help schools build bilingual websites that showcase school strengths, activities, and student learning.
  • 推動英語、雙語與國際教育，讓學生有更多真實溝通與接待外賓的機會。We support English, bilingual, and international education so students can practice real communication and host international guests.
  • 提供免費英文學習資源，並協助老師運用 AI 製作教材、網站與課堂活動。We provide free English learning resources and help teachers use AI to create materials, websites, and classroom activities.
  • 串連國際志工、實習老師、海外中文學校與臺灣學校，創造可持續的學習連結。We connect international volunteers, student teachers, overseas Chinese schools, and schools in Taiwan for sustainable learning partnerships.

人師教育協會提供學校雙語網站製作服務，秉持義務與免費原則。學校端需要提供資料、確認授權與建立明確窗口，合作才會順利。
My Culture Connect offers bilingual school website support as a free volunteer service. Clear school materials, permission, and a designated contact person help the collaboration move smoothly.
