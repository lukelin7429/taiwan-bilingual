A Camp That Never Stayed in One Country 一個從不待在同一個國家的營隊

For three weeks this July, KangLang's summer break looked nothing like summer break usually looks. Every weekday morning, a group of our students walked through the same front gate they use during the school year — and stepped into a classroom that spent its mornings in California, its afternoons debating how to feed a hungry planet, and its evenings (metaphorically) rehearsing a stage adaptation of The Lion King.

今年七月的這三週，槺榔國小的「暑假」看起來一點都不像暑假。每個平日的早晨，一群孩子走進和上學期間一樣的校門——然後走進一間教室，這間教室的早上人在加州、下午在辯論怎麼餵飽一個飢餓的地球、到了（象徵意義上的）傍晚則在排演一齣舞台版的《獅子王》。

The Immersive English Summer Camp, led by Teacher Edward and Teacher Kylie, was built around a simple bet: that children learn a language fastest when the language is simply how the room happens to be running that day. So English was not a subject with a start and end time. It was the medium for reading five classic picture books, for arguing about Yosemite National Park and Disneyland, for building paper towers, and — three weeks later — for standing under stage lights.

這場「沉浸式英語夏令營」由 Edward 老師與 Kylie 老師帶領，建立在一個簡單的信念上：當一種語言就是教室當天運作的方式本身時，孩子學得最快。於是英語不是一堂有上下課時間的科目，而是閱讀五本經典繪本、爭論優勝美地國家公園和迪士尼樂園、疊紙塔——以及三週後站上舞台燈光下——所使用的媒介。

“Not an English class with a summer camp attached — a summer camp that happened to run entirely in English.”

不是「附帶英語課的夏令營」，而是一整個「剛好完全用英語運作」的夏令營。

Seventeen Goals, Told by Ten-Year-Olds 十七項目標，由十歲的孩子來說

Rather than lecture our campers about the United Nations' seventeen Sustainable Development Goals, Teacher Edward and Teacher Kylie handed the SDGs to the children themselves — two or three goals a day, taught through vocabulary, drawing, and group discussion, and finished off with a full role-play of a Local Conference of Youth (LCOY) Taiwan, the real youth forum recognised by the UNFCCC's official youth constituency, YOUNGO.

Edward 老師與 Kylie 老師沒有用講課的方式教聯合國的十七項永續發展目標（SDGs），而是把 SDGs 直接交到孩子手上——每天兩到三項目標，透過單字、繪畫與小組討論來學習，最後以一場完整的角色扮演作結：模擬「台灣在地青年氣候論壇（LCOY Taiwan）」——這是聯合國氣候變化綱要公約（UNFCCC）官方青年組織 YOUNGO 所認可的真實青年論壇。

Campers built posters, held plush mascots representing the goals — including Taiwan's own black bear, dressed for the occasion — and sat for a group photo in front of the ROC and US flags, holding signs that could have come straight out of an actual climate conference. It is, as far as a summer English camp goes, an unusually ambitious way to teach the word “sustainable.”

孩子們做了海報、抱著代表各項目標的絨毛玩偶——包括特地盛裝出席的台灣黑熊——並在青天白日滿地紅旗與美國國旗前拍了團體照，手上的告示牌看起來就像真的氣候論壇現場一樣。就一個英語夏令營而言，這是一種教「永續」這個詞相當有野心的方式。

How the Three Weeks Were Built

One Theme a Week

一週一個主題
Week 1 · Jul 1–3

America, Up Close 貼近美國

Orientation, classroom rules, and an icebreaker gave way to American Culture class — Yosemite National Park, Disneyland's California Adventure Park, and a day in the life of an American school. Reading began with Five Little Ducks, alongside the first five SDGs: No Poverty, Zero Hunger, Good Health, Quality Education, and Gender Equality.

Week 2 · Jul 6–10

Building the Show 打造這場公演

Daily PE, daily dance rehearsal, and the first read-throughs of a bilingual Lion King stage adaptation and English Drama. Reading moved on to Brown Bear, Brown Bear and The Wheels on the Bus, while SDGs 6–10 — clean water, clean energy, decent work, industry & infrastructure, and reduced inequalities — framed the classroom discussions.

Week 3 · Jul 13–17

Presenting to the World 向世界報告

Our “Youth Climate Action” and “Urban Architect” teams tackled SDGs 11–17 — from sustainable cities and climate action to peace, justice, and global partnership — then staged their own mock youth climate conference. Reading closed with Dear Zoo and Dear Moon, drama moved into full recording, and the camp ended on Closing Ceremony, July 17.

2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組，A 和 B 輪流唸
🎬 Scene · At the mock youth climate conference, a visiting parent stops by a camper's SDG poster.
情境：在模擬青年氣候論壇的攤位前，一位來訪的家長停下來看孩子的 SDG 海報。
B
Wow, what is this presentation about?
哇，這個報告是關於什麼的？
A
It's about the Sustainable Development Goals, the SDGs.
是關於永續發展目標，簡稱 SDGs。
B
That sounds important. Which goal is your favorite?
聽起來很重要。你最喜歡哪一項目標？
A
Mine is climate action. We need to take care of our planet.
我最喜歡氣候行動。我們需要照顧我們的星球。
B
I'm proud of you. Did you enjoy summer camp?
我為你感到驕傲。你喜歡這次夏令營嗎？
A
Yes! We read stories, practiced English, and rehearsed a dance for today.
喜歡！我們讀了故事、練習英文，還為今天排練了一支舞。
