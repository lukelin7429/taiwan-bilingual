Fire Safety: Stop, Drop & Roll消防安全宣導

Children learned CPR and the “stop, drop and roll” rule for when clothes catch fire — important lessons FengChou even shares as a bilingual safety video.

孩子學會 CPR，也學會「身上著火，停、躺、滾」的保命口訣——這些重要的一課，豐洲甚至做成了雙語安全影片。
