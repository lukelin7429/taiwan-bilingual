Southern Taiwan · 南部

Tainan

臺南市

Taiwan's oldest city and former capital, where 400 years of history live on in ancient forts, hundreds of temples and legendary street food.

全台最古老的城市與舊都,四百年歷史藏在古堡、廟宇與小吃裡。

What makes it special

在地特色

Anping Fort

安平古堡

Built by the Dutch in 1624, Taiwan's oldest fortress, wrapped in old banyan trees.

Chihkan Tower

赤崁樓

A 1653 Dutch fort topped with elegant Chinese-style towers.

City of Temples

廟宇之都

Tainan has more historic temples than any other city in Taiwan.

Street-food Capital

小吃之都

Known as Taiwan's street-food capital, with famous bites on every corner.
