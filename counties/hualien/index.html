Hualien

花蓮縣

Taiwan's largest county, where towering marble gorges meet the deep blue Pacific and Indigenous Amis culture thrives.

全台最大的縣,大理石峽谷與太平洋交會,阿美族文化生生不息。

What makes it special

在地特色

Taroko Gorge

太魯閣峽谷

A breathtaking canyon of sheer marble cliffs and rushing rivers.

Pacific Coast

太平洋海岸

The dramatic Qingshui Cliffs plunge straight into the ocean.

Amis Culture

阿美族文化

Home to Taiwan's largest Indigenous group, known for song and harvest festivals.

East Rift Valley

花東縱谷

A lush valley of rice paddies between two mountain ranges.
