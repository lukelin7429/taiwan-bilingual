Outlying Islands · 離島

Matsu (Lienchiang)

連江縣・馬祖

A misty cluster of islands famous for glowing 'blue tears' seas and stone villages in eastern-Fujian style.

雲霧繚繞的島群,以夢幻「藍眼淚」與閩東石頭聚落聞名。

Say it in English 🔊

Tap 🔊 to hear it — then try saying it yourself. 點 🔊 聽聽看,再開口說說看。

  • I'm from Matsu.我來自連江縣・馬祖。
  • Matsu is in Outlying Islands.連江縣・馬祖在臺灣離島。
  • Matsu is famous for Blue Tears.連江縣・馬祖以藍眼淚聞名。

What makes it special

在地特色

Blue Tears

藍眼淚

A magical natural glow that lights up the night sea with blue light.

Qinbi Village

芹壁聚落

A hillside village of granite houses — the best-preserved eastern-Fujian architecture.

Tunnels & Lighthouses

坑道與燈塔

Old military tunnels and historic lighthouses dot the coastlines.

Outer Islands

東莒與大坵

Quiet outer islands known for scenery and wild sika deer.
