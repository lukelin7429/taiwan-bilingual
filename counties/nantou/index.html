Central Taiwan · 中部

Nantou

南投縣

Taiwan's only landlocked county — misty mountains, alpine meadows and a shimmering lake at the island's very centre.

全台唯一不靠海的內陸縣,群山環繞、湖光山色,是寶島正中央。
