Eastern Taiwan · 東部

Yilan

宜蘭縣

A green, rainy land of steaming hot springs, ocean-view islands and lively night markets just over the mountains from Taipei.

好山好水的溫泉之鄉,山海相依、夜市熱鬧,是台北的後花園。

What makes it special

在地特色

Jiaoxi Hot Springs

礁溪溫泉

Rare lowland hot springs right in the heart of town.

Guishan Island

龜山島

A turtle-shaped volcanic island famous for whale watching.

Luodong Night Market

羅東夜市

One of Taiwan's most beloved night markets.

Forestry Heritage

林業文化

Once a timber hub for the Taipingshan forest, with railways telling its story.
