Eastern Taiwan · 東部

Taitung

臺東縣

A laid-back coastal county of Indigenous villages, drifting hot-air balloons and pristine offshore islands.

步調悠閒的海岸縣,原民部落、熱氣球與純淨離島交織。

What makes it special

在地特色

Luye Balloons

鹿野熱氣球

Home of Taiwan's colourful annual Hot Air Balloon Festival.

Indigenous Culture

原住民文化

A rich homeland for many tribes, celebrated through music and crafts.

Green Island

綠島

A volcanic island with a rare seawater hot spring and great diving.

Orchid Island

蘭嶼

Home of the seafaring Tao people, the 'flying-fish people'.
