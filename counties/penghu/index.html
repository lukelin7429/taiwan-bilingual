Outlying Islands · 離島

Penghu

澎湖縣

An island archipelago of black basalt cliffs, white coral beaches and the romantic twin-heart stone weir.

由群島組成的海上樂園,黑色玄武岩、白珊瑚沙灘與浪漫雙心石滬。

Tap 🔊 to hear it — then try saying it yourself. 點 🔊 聽聽看,再開口說說看。

  • I'm from Penghu.我來自澎湖縣。
  • Penghu is in Outlying Islands.澎湖縣在臺灣離島。
  • Penghu is famous for Basalt Columns.澎湖縣以玄武岩聞名。

What makes it special

在地特色

Basalt Columns

玄武岩

Dramatic black volcanic rock rising in columns along the coast.

Twin-Heart Stone Weir

雙心石滬

An ancient heart-shaped fish trap on Qimei, now a symbol of love.

Coral Sand Beaches

珊瑚沙灘

Crystal-clear water and beaches of crushed coral and shells.

Tianhou Temple

天后宮

Taiwan's oldest Mazu temple, at the heart of Magong city.
