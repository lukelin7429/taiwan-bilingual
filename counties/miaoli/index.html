Miaoli

苗栗縣

A mountain-and-strawberry county at the heart of Hakka Taiwan, famous for wood carving, hot springs and snowy tung blossoms.

山城與草莓之鄉,以木雕、溫泉、油桐花聞名的客家大縣。
