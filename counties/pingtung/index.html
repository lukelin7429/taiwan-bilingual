Southern Taiwan · 南部

Pingtung

屏東縣

The warm, sunny tip of Taiwan — tropical beaches, an old walled town and the island's richest fruit basket.

台灣溫暖陽光的最南端,熱帶海灘、古城牆與全島最豐盛的水果之鄉。

What makes it special

Kenting National Park

墾丁國家公園

Taiwan's top beach destination, loved for sunny sands and water sports.

Hengchun Old Town

恆春古城

A historic town with one of Taiwan's best-preserved old city walls (1879).

Fruit Basket

水果之鄉

A tropical region famous for mangoes, wax apples and sun-ripened fruit.

White Sand Bay

白沙灣

One of Kenting's most beautiful beaches, with soft white sand.
