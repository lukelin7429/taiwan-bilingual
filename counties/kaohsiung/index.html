Southern Taiwan · 南部

Kaohsiung

高雄市

Southern Taiwan's great harbour city, where ships, art warehouses, lakeside pagodas and a giant Buddha sit side by side along the water.

南台灣的大港都,海港、藝術倉庫、湖畔寶塔與大佛沿水岸並陳。

Tap 🔊 to hear it — then try saying it yourself. 點 🔊 聽聽看,再開口說說看。

  • I'm from Kaohsiung.我來自高雄市。
  • Kaohsiung is in Southern Taiwan.高雄市在臺灣南部。
  • Kaohsiung is famous for Love River.高雄市以愛河聞名。

What makes it special

在地特色

Love River

愛河

A scenic river through the city centre, lit up at night for evening cruises.

Pier-2 Art Center

駁二藝術特區

Old harbour warehouses turned into a colourful arts district.

Lotus Pond Pagodas

蓮池潭龍虎塔

A temple-ringed lake — enter the dragon's mouth, exit the tiger's for luck.

Fo Guang Shan

佛光山

A major Buddhist centre and museum with a giant golden Buddha.
