Northern Taiwan · 北部

Hsinchu County

新竹縣

A green, hilly county that is one of Taiwan's strongest heartlands of Hakka culture, with old streets, tea and mountain railways.

山巒翠綠的客家文化重鎮,有老街、擂茶與山城小火車。

What makes it special

在地特色

Neiwan Old Street

內灣老街

A mountain town reached by a branch railway, with an old theatre and cherry blossoms.

Beipu Old Street

北埔老街

One of Taiwan's most history-packed Hakka old streets.

Hakka Lei-cha

客家擂茶

A ground-tea drink of tea, sesame, peanuts and grains visitors can grind by hand.

Hakka Culture

客家文化

A living centre of Hakka language, food and customs.
