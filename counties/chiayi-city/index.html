Southern Taiwan · 南部

Chiayi City

嘉義市

A warm, walkable city where Japanese-era cypress houses, a baseball legend and the Tropic of Cancer all meet over a bowl of turkey rice.

溫暖好逛的城市,檜木老屋、棒球傳奇與北回歸線在此相遇。

What makes it special

在地特色

Hinoki Village

檜意森活村

Taiwan's largest cluster of Japanese-era cypress wooden houses, now shops and cafes.

KANO Baseball

KANO 棒球

Chiayi's farming-school team reached Japan's Koshien finals in 1931 — a true story turned film.

Tropic of Cancer

北回歸線

The Tropic of Cancer runs through the Chiayi area, marked by a tall sun-themed landmark.

Turkey Rice

火雞肉飯

Tender shredded turkey over warm rice — found on almost every corner.
