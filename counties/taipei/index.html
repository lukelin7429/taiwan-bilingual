Taipei

Taiwan's bustling capital, where a record-setting skyscraper, grand museums and lively night markets blend modern energy with deep tradition.

What makes it special

在地特色

Taipei 101

台北 101

A 508-metre skyscraper, once the tallest in the world, with a city-wide observation deck.

National Palace Museum

國立故宮博物院

Home to nearly 700,000 Chinese artworks, including the famous Jadeite Cabbage.

Longshan Temple

龍山寺

One of Taipei's oldest temples (1738), famous for its carvings.

CKS Memorial Hall

中正紀念堂

A grand white memorial on a vast public square.
