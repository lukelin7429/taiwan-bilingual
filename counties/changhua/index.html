What makes it special

在地特色

Baguashan Great Buddha

八卦山大佛

Changhua City's landmark — a towering Buddha with ridge-top hiking trails.

Lukang Old Town

鹿港古鎮

A Qing-era port — Longshan Temple ('Taiwan's Forbidden City') and old-street snacks.

Fan-shaped Roundhouse

扇形車庫

Taiwan's only surviving fan-shaped train depot, with its locomotive turntable.
