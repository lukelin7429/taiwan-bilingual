Central Taiwan · 中部

Yunlin

雲林縣

Taiwan's farming county — a sunny patchwork of rice fields and coffee hills that feeds the island and keeps its folk arts alive.

台灣的農業大縣,稻田與咖啡山交織,守護傳統技藝。

What makes it special

在地特色

Beigang Chaotian Temple

北港朝天宮

A grand 300-year-old Mazu temple, head temple for many across Taiwan.

Gukeng Coffee

古坑咖啡

Known as the home of Taiwanese coffee, grown on fragrant hills.

Budaixi Puppet Theatre

布袋戲

Yunlin is the heart of Taiwan's hand-puppet glove theatre.

Farm country

農業之鄉

Endless rice paddies make Yunlin one of Taiwan's key farming regions.
