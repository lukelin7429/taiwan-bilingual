From the Principal

校長室 · 西嶺國小的辦學理念
I.

Every Child Is a Gem

每個孩子都是寶
沈沈春木 校長

At Siling, we begin from one simple conviction: every child is a gem, and each one should be taught according to who they are. On a small mountain campus of just a few dozen students, that is not a slogan — it is something we can actually do, every single day.

在西嶺，我們從一個簡單的信念出發：每個孩子都是寶，每個孩子都應該因材施教。在一所只有數十名學生的山城小校裡，這不是口號——而是我們每一天都真正做得到的事。

Our wish for this school is captured in four words: a happy school under a sunny sky — Sunny Siling. We want children to feel safe and cared for, to read widely, to know and love their own local culture, and to grow in both character and confidence.

我們對這所學校的期盼，凝聚成八個字：幸福小學・陽光西嶺。我們希望孩子在這裡有安全感、被好好照顧，廣泛閱讀，認識並熱愛自己的在地文化，在品格與自信中一起成長。

From the lion-dance team and the kite workshop to the butterfly garden and our caring after-school support, everything at Siling exists for the same reason: so that every child on this plateau can find something to be proud of, and carry it for life.

從舞獅隊、風箏工坊，到蝴蝶園與溫暖的課後照顧，西嶺的一切都為了同一個理由而存在：讓這片台地上的每個孩子，都能找到值得驕傲的亮點，並受用一生。

"A happy school under a sunny sky."
幸福小學・陽光西嶺。 — 西嶺國小願景
