I.

Welcome

歡迎來到西嶺國小

A small school on the mountain plateau, with a hundred years of stories to tell.

一所坐落於八卦山台地上的山城小校，藏著一百年的故事。

Siling Elementary sits high on the Bagua Mountain plateau in Nantou City, surrounded by orchards, woodland, and butterflies. Founded a full century ago, it is a small country school — just a few dozen children — but one with an unusually big heart and a remarkable cultural inheritance.

西嶺國小坐落在南投市的八卦山台地上，四周是果園、林地與翩翩飛舞的蝴蝶。創校至今滿一百年，雖然是一所只有數十名孩子的偏鄉小學，卻有著格外溫暖的心，以及一份難得的文化傳承。

Here, every child is treasured. The school's belief — "every child is a gem; teach each one according to who they are" — turns small class sizes into a strength: real attention, real care, and the freedom to shine. Its vision says it best: a happy school under a sunny sky — Sunny Siling.

在這裡，每個孩子都是寶。學校的信念——「每個孩子都是寶、因材施教」——把小班小校化為優勢：真正的關注、真正的照顧，以及讓每個孩子發光的自由。學校願景說得最好：幸福小學・陽光西嶺。

Two proud teams give Siling its name far beyond the mountain: a lion-dance team that carries a century-old local tradition, and a kite team housed in Taiwan's only elementary-school kite museum.

兩支驕傲的校隊，讓西嶺的名字傳到了山下更遠的地方：一支承襲在地百年技藝的舞獅隊，以及一支擁有全台國小唯一風箏館的風箏隊。

II.

What Makes Siling

西嶺的特色
🦁
The Lion-Dance Team
舞獅隊

Founded in 2016, our team carries on the century-old local "Tongyi Hall Golden Lion" troupe — the eight moods of joy, anger, sorrow, and surprise. County champions in the Southern-Lion category, they have performed in Kasahara, Japan and at the Nantou Lantern Festival.

🪁
The Only Kite Museum
全台國小唯一風箏館

Siling is home to the only kite exhibition hall inside an elementary school in Taiwan, opened in 2002. Making a kite fly takes science, art, craft, terrain, weather, and teamwork — a whole curriculum on a single string.

🦋
A Butterfly Garden
生態蝴蝶園

On the green Bagua plateau, our butterfly garden turns the schoolyard into a living classroom, where children learn to observe, care for, and wonder at the natural world around them.

☀️
A Happy School
幸福小學

Great teaching, all-day care until 6 pm, free after-school tutoring, alumni scholarships, free clubs, reading and character education, and a caring school nurse — Siling's "seven treasures of happiness" wrap around every child.

Sunny Siling · 幸福小學 · 陽光西嶺
每個孩子都是寶 · 因材施教

Seven treasures of happiness — quality teaching · all-day care · free tutoring · scholarships · free clubs · reading & character · a caring nurse — so that on this mountain plateau, every child learns in the warmth of the sun.
