Ten Situations · 十大情境

Phrases for Every Classroom Moment

涵蓋課堂每個時刻的實用短句

These ten situations cover the full school day — from morning greetings to end-of-class farewells. Every phrase includes a Chinese translation and a 🔊 speaker button for pronunciation practice.

Situation 01

Greetings & Attendance

問候語與點名

Good morning, everyone!

大家早安！

Let me take attendance.

讓我來點名。

Please say "present" when I call your name.

叫到你的名字時請說「到」。

Who is absent today?

今天誰沒來？

Is everyone here?

大家都到了嗎？

Situation 02

Pre-Class Preparation

課前準備

Please take out your textbook.

請拿出課本。

Open to page twenty-four.

翻到第 24 頁。

Have your pencil and notebook ready.

準備好鉛筆和筆記本。

Please put away your phones.

請收起手機。

Let's get started!

我們開始吧！

Situation 03

Giving Instructions

給予指示

Listen carefully, please.

請仔細聽。

Repeat after me.

跟著我說。

Work with your partner.

跟你的夥伴一起做。

You have five minutes to finish.

你們有五分鐘完成。

Please write your answers on the board.

請把答案寫在黑板上。

Situation 04

Checking Understanding

確認理解

Does everyone understand?

大家都明白嗎？

Can you give me an example?

你能給我一個例子嗎？

Let me say that again, more slowly.

我再說一次，慢一點。

Do you have any questions?

你們有任何問題嗎？

What does this word mean?

這個詞是什麼意思？

Situation 05

Praise & Encouragement

稱讚與鼓勵

That's right! Well done!

答對了！做得好！

Excellent! I'm very proud of you.

太棒了！我非常以你為榮。

Good try! Let's try again.

嘗試得不錯！我們再試一次。

Keep going — you're almost there!

繼續加油——快到了！

I like the way you explained that.

我喜歡你解釋那件事的方式。

Situation 06

Classroom Rules

課堂規則

Please raise your hand before speaking.

發言前請舉手。

Please be quiet and pay attention.

請安靜並專心聽講。

No talking during the test.

考試時不可交談。

Eyes on me, please!

請看著我！

Respect your classmates at all times.

隨時尊重你的同學。

Situation 07

Group Activities

分組活動

Let's get into groups of four.

我們四人一組。

Each group needs a leader and a reporter.

每組需要一位組長和一位報告人。

Discuss your ideas with your group.

在組內討論你們的想法。

Now share your results with the class.

現在把你們的結果跟全班分享。

Great teamwork, everyone!

大家合作得很棒！

Situation 08

Transitions & Movement

轉換與移動

Please line up at the door.

請在門口排隊。

Move your desks into a circle.

把桌子移成圓形。

Walk, don't run, in the hallway.

在走廊要走路，不要跑。

Come back to your seats, please.

請回到你們的座位。

We're going to the computer lab today.

今天我們要去電腦教室。

Situation 09

Homework & Assessment

作業與評量

Tonight's homework is on page thirty.

今晚的作業在第 30 頁。

Please hand in your homework by Friday.

請在週五前交作業。

We will have a short quiz next week.

下週我們會有一個小考。

Please check your answers carefully.

請仔細檢查你的答案。

Time is up — put your pencils down.

時間到了——放下你的鉛筆。

Situation 10

End of Class

下課

That's all for today.

今天就到這裡。

Great work today, everyone!

今天大家表現得很棒！

Don't forget to study for the test.

別忘了複習備考。

See you next time! Goodbye!

下次見！再見！

Have a great day!

祝你今天愉快！

Practice Tip 學習小撇步

Listen to each phrase 3 times, then cover the Chinese and try to say it from memory. Repeat daily for two weeks and the phrases will stick!

每個短句聽 3 次，然後遮住中文自己試著說出來。每天練習兩週，這些短句就會變成反射動作！
