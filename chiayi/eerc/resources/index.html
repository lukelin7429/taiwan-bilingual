How to Use These Resources

如何使用本中心資源

👩‍🏫

For Teachers

Print phrase cards, copy announcement templates, or project vocabulary cards on your classroom screen. All resources are free and ready to use.

教師可列印短句卡、直接使用公告模板，或投影於教室螢幕。

🎒

For Students

Click the 🔊 speaker button on any phrase or word to hear it spoken aloud. Listen, repeat, and practice — your accent improves with every click.

點擊🔊鍵即可聆聽發音，不斷重複練習是進步最快的方法。

🏫

For Schools

Embed bilingual announcement templates directly into your school's morning broadcast. Consistent daily English exposure builds real fluency over time.

雙語公告模板可直接嵌入校園早播，日積月累建立英語語感。
