Morning Opening Broadcast

Good morning, [School Name] family!

早安，[學校名稱]大家庭！

各位老師、同學，祝大家學習愉快！

📻 Tips for Broadcasters · 廣播小技巧

Speak Slowly

The 🔊 button plays each phrase at a relaxed pace. Match that speed — don't rush. Your listeners need time to process both languages.

說慢一點，讓聽眾有時間同時吸收中英兩種語言。

Say It Twice

Say each line in English first, then in Chinese. Repetition helps the whole school community pick up vocabulary naturally over time.

先說英文，再說中文。反覆接觸是學語言最自然的方式。

Practice First

Click 🔊 on every line the night before your broadcast. Read along silently, then say it aloud. One practice run builds real confidence.

播報前一晚點 🔊 跟著練，一次演練就能大幅提升自信。
