About · 關於竹崎鄉

Getting to Know Zhuqi Township

Zhuqi Township sits at the foot of the Alishan mountain range, serving as the gateway between the Chiayi plains and the high mountain country. The famous Zhuqi Station (竹崎車站) is one of the oldest surviving wooden railway stations in Taiwan, built in 1910 during the Japanese colonial period.

From here, the Alishan Forest Railway begins its dramatic switchback climb into the mountains. The township is also known for its bamboo forests, tofu production, and the scenic Zhuqi Gorge (竹崎峽谷). Traditional tofu shops along the mountain roads use natural spring water to produce a silky, fresh tofu that has been a local specialty for generations.

竹崎鄉位於阿里山山脈山腳，是嘉義平原與高山地帶之間的門戶。著名的竹崎車站建於 1910 年日治時期，是台灣現存最古老的木造車站之一，阿里山森林鐵路從此展開壯觀的之字形攀山之旅。

本鄉以竹林、豆腐製作和竹崎峽谷的美景聞名。山路旁的傳統豆腐店以天然湧泉水製作出綿滑鮮嫩的豆腐，世代相傳成為在地特產。竹崎大橋與歷史車站區域全年吸引文化觀光旅客造訪。

Highlights · 特色亮點

What Makes Zhuqi Township Special

竹崎鄉的獨特魅力

🚉
Zhuqi Station
竹崎車站
Built in 1910, this wooden railway station is one of the oldest surviving stations in Taiwan. It marks the starting point of the famous Alishan Forest Railway switchback section — a feat of mountain engineering that still amazes visitors today.
建於 1910 年的木造車站是台灣現存最古老的車站之一，也是阿里山森林鐵路壯觀之字形路段的起點，這段山岳工程奇蹟至今仍令遊客嘆為觀止。
🌿
Bamboo Forests & Gorge
竹崎峽谷
The scenic Zhuqi Gorge winds through lush bamboo groves with walking trails and natural spring water features. Cool mountain air and the sound of rustling bamboo make this a beloved escape for hikers and nature lovers from across Taiwan.
竹崎峽谷蜿蜒穿越翠綠竹林，設有步道與天然湧泉景觀，涼爽的山風伴著竹葉聲響，吸引全台登山客與自然愛好者前來踏青。
🍽️
Mountain Spring Tofu
山泉豆腐
Roadside tofu shops famous across Chiayi use pure natural spring water to create silky, fresh tofu with a delicate flavor unlike anything found in the city. These family-run artisan shops have passed down their recipes for generations.
沿山公路旁的豆腐店聞名嘉義，以純天然湧泉水製作出綿滑鮮嫩、風味細膩的豆腐，這些家族經營的工藝小店已將祖傳食譜傳承數代。
🏔️
Gateway to Alishan
阿里山門戶
Every road to Alishan passes through Zhuqi, where the flat Chiayi plains give way dramatically to forested foothills and mountain ridges. The shift in landscape here is sudden and breathtaking — a natural threshold between two worlds.
所有通往阿里山的道路都經過竹崎，嘉義平原在此急劇轉換為林木茂密的山麓丘陵，景色的驟變令人屏息，彷彿踏入了截然不同的另一個世界。

Fun Facts · 趣味小知識

Did You Know?

你知道嗎？關於竹崎鄉的有趣事實

Zhuqi Station was built in 1910 and is one of the best-preserved wooden railway stations in Taiwan — it has appeared in many Taiwanese films and TV dramas.
竹崎車站建於 1910 年，是台灣保存最完好的木造車站之一，曾出現在許多台灣電影與電視劇中。
Chiayi County produces more bamboo than any other county in Taiwan, and Zhuqi's bamboo forests supply shoots for restaurants across the island.
嘉義縣的竹子產量居全台之冠，竹崎的竹林為全島各地餐廳供應竹筍。
The tofu shops along Mountain Road 18 near Zhuqi have been operating for over three generations — some use the same spring water source their great-grandparents used.
竹崎附近台18線山路旁的豆腐店已經營超過三代，有些店家至今仍使用曾祖父輩相同的泉水水源。
