About · 關於大埔鄉

Getting to Know Dapu Township

Dapu Township is the most remote and least populated township in Chiayi County, tucked deep into the southern reaches of the Central Mountain Range along the border with Tainan City. Its defining geographical feature is the Zengwen Reservoir (曾文水庫) — Taiwan's largest reservoir by capacity, built between 1967 and 1973, which inundated the original Dapu township center and displaced entire villages. The reservoir now provides drinking water for over 1.5 million people across Tainan and southern Chiayi County.

The township's remaining land is predominantly subtropical and tropical forest, including sections of Yushan National Park and protected wildlife corridors. The area is home to rare and endangered species including the Formosan black bear, sambar deer, Taiwan blue magpie, and mikado pheasant — Taiwan's national bird. Dapu has the darkest night skies in Chiayi County, making it an increasingly popular stargazing destination.

大埔鄉是嘉義縣最偏遠、人口最稀少的鄉鎮，深藏於中央山脈南段、鄰近台南市邊界。最具代表性的地理特色是曾文水庫——台灣容量最大的水庫，建於 1967 至 1973 年間，淹沒了大埔原有的鄉村中心，迫使多個村落遷移。水庫現供應逾 150 萬名台南及嘉義南部居民的飲用水。

鄉內其餘土地以亞熱帶及熱帶森林為主，包含玉山國家公園及受保護野生動物廊道的一部分。此地棲息著台灣黑熊、水鹿、台灣藍鵲與帝雉（台灣國鳥）等稀有瀕危物種。大埔的夜空是嘉義縣最黑暗的，因此成為越來越受歡迎的觀星目的地。

Highlights · 特色亮點

What Makes Dapu Township Special

大埔鄉的獨特魅力

💧
Zengwen Reservoir
曾文水庫
Taiwan's largest reservoir by storage capacity, the Zengwen Reservoir is surrounded by forested hills and shimmering blue water. Visitors come for boating, fishing, and peaceful lakeside walks — a stunning blend of engineering achievement and natural beauty.
曾文水庫是台灣容量最大的水庫，四周被林木翠嶺環繞、湖面碧藍清澈。遊客可在此乘船、釣魚，或沿湖畔步道漫步，感受人文工程與自然美景的完美交融。
🐻
Rare Wildlife & Protected Forests
稀有野生動物與保護林地
Dapu's forests are among Taiwan's most intact subtropical ecosystems. Within these protected corridors live the Formosan black bear, sambar deer, mikado pheasant, and Taiwan blue magpie — some of Asia's most iconic and endangered wildlife.
大埔的林地是台灣保存最完整的亞熱帶生態系之一。在這些受保護的野生動物廊道中，棲息著台灣黑熊、水鹿、帝雉與台灣藍鵲——亞洲最具代表性的珍稀物種。
🌟
Dark Sky Stargazing
暗空觀星
Far from city lights, Dapu offers some of the darkest skies in all of Chiayi County. On clear nights, the Milky Way stretches overhead in vivid detail — an increasingly rare experience in Taiwan's densely developed west coast.
遠離城市光害，大埔擁有嘉義縣最黑暗的夜空。晴朗夜晚，銀河清晰橫跨天際——這在台灣高度發展的西部地區已屬難得一見的體驗。
🛶
Reservoir Water Activities
水庫水上活動
Kayaking, paddle boating, and fishing on the Zengwen Reservoir draw outdoor recreation visitors year-round. Lakeside trails wind through forested hillsides, offering peaceful views of the reservoir and its mountain backdrop.
曾文水庫提供獨木舟、踩船及釣魚等多元水上活動，全年吸引戶外休閒遊客。環湖步道穿行林間山坡，讓人在靜謐中欣賞水庫與周圍山景。

Fun Facts · 趣味小知識

Did You Know?

你知道嗎？關於大埔鄉的有趣事實

The Zengwen Reservoir holds over 700 million cubic meters of water at full capacity — enough to fill over 280,000 Olympic swimming pools. It is the main water source for the entire Tainan metropolitan area.
曾文水庫滿水位時蓄水量超過 7 億立方公尺，足以灌滿逾 28 萬座奧運標準游泳池，是整個台南都會區的主要飲用水來源。
The Formosan black bear is Taiwan's largest native land animal — fewer than 1,000 individuals remain in the wild, mostly in the mountains of central and southern Taiwan, including Dapu's forests.
台灣黑熊是台灣體型最大的原生陸地動物——野外族群數量不足 1,000 隻，主要分布於台灣中南部山區，包括大埔的森林在內。
Dapu is one of only a handful of places in Taiwan where the Milky Way is easily visible — the township has fewer than 5,000 permanent residents and virtually no commercial lighting.
大埔是台灣少數能清楚看見銀河的地方之一——全鄉常住人口不足 5,000 人，幾乎沒有商業照明，讓夜空保持極度黑暗。
