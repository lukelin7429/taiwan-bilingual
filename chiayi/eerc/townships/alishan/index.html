About · 關於阿里山鄉

Getting to Know Alishan Township

Alishan Township is the crown jewel of Chiayi County, rising from subtropical valleys to alpine peaks that pierce a permanent sea of clouds. At its heart lies Alishan National Scenic Area, a 14,000-hectare reserve that draws over two million visitors annually. The famous Alishan Forest Railway — one of only three mountain railways in the world — winds 72 km from Chiayi City up to Zhushan at 2,451 meters, a route listed as a world engineering heritage candidate.

The township is also home to the Tsou (鄒族) indigenous people, who have inhabited these mountains for over 400 years. Their villages, language, millet-harvest festivals, and oral traditions form a living cultural heritage. Every February and March, rows of yoshino cherry trees explode into pink bloom along the mountain trails — a sight that has made Alishan's cherry blossom festival one of the most celebrated in all of Asia.

阿里山鄉是嘉義縣最耀眼的瑰寶，從亞熱帶河谷一路攀升至穿透雲海的高山山峰。核心區域阿里山國家風景區面積達 14,000 公頃，每年吸引超過 200 萬名遊客造訪。著名的阿里山森林鐵路是全世界三條登山鐵路之一，從嘉義市蜿蜒 72 公里爬升至海拔 2,451 公尺的祝山站，是世界工程遺產的候選路線。

本鄉也是鄒族原住民的家園，他們世居這片山地已逾四百年。鄒族的聚落、語言、小米收穫祭與口傳文化，共同構成了活生生的文化遺產。每年二、三月，山道兩旁的吉野櫻盛開如瀑，使阿里山賞櫻節成為全亞洲最受矚目的花季活動之一。

Highlights · 特色亮點

What Makes Alishan Township Special

阿里山鄉的獨特魅力

🌅

Sunrise at Zhushan Peak

祝山觀日出

Zhushan Station, at 2,451 m, is the highest point on the Alishan Forest Railway. Visitors board a special early-morning train in darkness to reach the summit just before dawn, where the sun erupts over a billowing sea of clouds — one of Taiwan's most iconic natural moments.

祝山站海拔 2,451 公尺，是阿里山森林鐵路的最高點。遊客在黎明前搭乘特別觀日列車前往山頂，靜待旭日從雲海中噴薄而出——這是台灣最具代表性的自然奇景之一。

🚂

Alishan Forest Railway

阿里山森林鐵路

Built in 1906 under Japanese rule, the Alishan Forest Railway climbs 2,216 meters using switchbacks, spirals, and 50 tunnels to navigate the rugged terrain. It is one of only three mountain railways in the world and remains an operating heritage line transporting both tourists and local residents.

阿里山森林鐵路建於 1906 年日治時期，以之字折返、螺旋路線和 50 座隧道攀越 2,216 公尺的高差。它是全世界三條登山鐵路之一，至今仍是連結山上居民與旅客的重要交通命脈。

🌸

Cherry Blossom Festival

阿里山花季

Each February and March, over 20,000 yoshino cherry trees burst into bloom across Alishan's hiking trails and slopes. The altitude creates a slightly later and longer bloom than low-elevation sakura, with pink blossoms against a backdrop of blue mountain ridges and white mist.

每年二至三月，超過兩萬棵吉野櫻在阿里山的步道和山坡上盛開。高海拔使花期比平地更晚且更長，粉色花海映著連綿山脈與白色雲霧，景色壯麗無比。

🏔️

Tsou Indigenous Culture

鄒族文化

The Tsou people (鄒族) have lived in the Alishan mountains for centuries. Their tribal villages in Dabang and Tefuye preserve traditional longhouses, millet cultivation, and the sacred Mayasvi warrior ceremony. Visitors can experience Tsou music, cuisine, and crafts through cultural tours.

鄒族世居阿里山山區已逾數百年。達邦與特富野部落保存著傳統集會所、小米農耕文化與神聖的瑪雅斯比祭典。遊客可透過文化導覽體驗鄒族音樂、飲食與工藝。

mist n.

雲霧；薄霧

"A thick mist rolls in every morning, covering the forest in silence."

每天清晨，濃霧滾滾而來，讓整片森林沉浸在靜謐之中。

indigenous adj.

原住民的；本土的

"The Tsou are the indigenous people of the Alishan mountains."

鄒族是阿里山山區的原住民族。

dawn n.

黎明；拂曉

"Hikers wake before dawn to catch the famous Alishan sunrise."

登山客在黎明前起床，只為欣賞著名的阿里山日出。

cedar n.

柏木；檜木

"Ancient cedar trees over 2,000 years old still stand in the Alishan forest."

阿里山森林中仍矗立著樹齡超過兩千年的古老檜木。

pilgrimage n.

朝聖；遠道參訪

"Every year, millions make a pilgrimage to Alishan to see the cherry blossoms."

每年數百萬人遠道前來阿里山，只為一睹櫻花盛開的美景。

switchback n.

之字形折返路

"The railway uses a switchback to reverse direction up the steep slope."

鐵路利用之字形折返路線，讓列車能在陡峭山坡上往返行駛。

ecology n.

生態；生態學

"Alishan's diverse ecology includes rare plants, birds, and cloud-forest insects."

阿里山豐富的生態涵蓋稀有植物、鳥類與雲霧林中的昆蟲。

Fun Facts · 趣味小知識

Did You Know?

你知道嗎？關於阿里山鄉的有趣事實

🌸 Alishan receives over 2 million visitors every year, making it one of Taiwan's top five tourist destinations.

阿里山每年吸引逾 200 萬名遊客，是台灣前五大觀光景點之一。

🌲 The giant Japanese red cypress trees in Alishan are estimated to be between 1,500 and 3,000 years old — older than most European cathedrals.

阿里山的日本紅檜巨木估計樹齡在 1,500 至 3,000 年之間，比大多數歐洲大教堂還要古老。

🚂 The Alishan Forest Railway is one of only three "mountain railways" in the world — the others are in India (Darjeeling) and Peru (Cusco).

阿里山森林鐵路是全球三條「登山鐵路」之一，另外兩條分別在印度大吉嶺和秘魯庫斯科。

⛅ Alishan has over 250 foggy days per year — the mist arrives so reliably that local farmers depend on it to water their tea and vegetable crops.

阿里山每年有超過 250 天的霧日——雲霧如此規律，當地農民甚至依賴它來滋潤茶園和蔬菜作物。
