Chiayi County · 嘉義縣

18 Townships

嘉義縣 18 個鄉鎮市

From Alishan's sacred peaks to the oyster beds of Dongshi — every township has its own story, ecology, and culture to explore.

🏔️ Mountain Regions · 山區鄉鎮

Where the Mountains Meet the Clouds

雲霧繚繞的山地鄉鎮

Chiayi County reaches from sea level to over 3,952 meters. These mountain townships are home to ancient forests, tea gardens, and indigenous heritage.

🌸

Alishan Township

阿里山鄉

Taiwan's most famous mountain township — cherry blossoms, sea of clouds, the historic Forest Railway, and the Tsou indigenous people.
🌲

Fanlu Township

番路鄉

The gateway to Alishan — persimmon orchards, Japanese cedar groves, and the mountain road that winds toward the clouds.
🍵

Meishan Township

梅山鄉

High-altitude tea plantations, breathtaking cloud-sea views, and the famous Meishan Old Street full of artisan shops.
🚂

Zhuqi Township

竹崎鄉

Where the Alishan Forest Railway begins its climb — bamboo crafts, tofu, and traditional foothills culture.
🦋

Zhongpu Township

中埔鄉

Durian orchards, butterfly ecological reserves, and subtropical valleys just south of Chiayi City.
🏞️

Dapu Township

大埔鄉

Remote mountain township beside Zengwen Reservoir — quiet forests, rare wildlife, and a sky full of stars.

🌾 Plains & Foothills · 平原丘陵

The Fertile Heartland

農業豐饒的平原與丘陵鄉鎮

The central and northern plains are the agricultural backbone of Chiayi County — pineapples, rice, sugarcane, and temples with centuries of history.

🏛️

Taibao City

太保市

The administrative capital of Chiayi County — home to the county government, National Chiayi University, and the Taiwan Sugar Museum.
🍍

Minxiong Township

民雄鄉

Taiwan's pineapple capital, home to two universities, the famous Ghost House, and some of Taiwan's best sausages.
🏮

Xingang Township

新港鄉

The spiritual heart of western Taiwan — the Fengtian Temple is one of the island's most visited Mazu pilgrimage sites.
🚉

Dalin Township

大林鎮

Historic railway town at the northern end of Chiayi County — sugarcane heritage, Tzu Chi Hospital, and traditional Hakka culture.
✈️

Shuishang Township

水上鄉

Chiayi Airport and the stunning Shuishang Lotus Pond — one of the most beautiful natural scenes in southern Taiwan.
🌾

Xikou Township

溪口鄉

A quiet agricultural township along the Puzi River — rice paddies, sweet potatoes, and folk-religion festivals.

🌊 Coastal Regions · 沿海鄉鎮

Life Along the Taiwan Strait

台灣海峽沿岸的漁村與鹽田

The western townships open onto the Taiwan Strait — fishing harbors, salt flats, tidal wetlands, and the freshest seafood in the island.

🦪

Dongshi Township

東石鄉

Taiwan's oyster capital — vast tidal flats, working fishing harbors, mangrove wetlands, and incredible fresh seafood culture.
🦐

Budai Township

布袋鎮

Historic salt production, shrimp and fish harbors, and the Budai Salt Museum — a window into Taiwan's salt-making past.
🌻

Liujiao Township

六腳鄉

Agricultural township with sprawling sunflower fields and the remnants of the Taiwan Sugar narrow-gauge railway network.
🏙️

Puzi City

朴子市

Second-largest city in Chiayi County — Peitian Temple, the Puzi River Art Village, and a thriving night market scene.
🍉

Yizhu Township

義竹鄉

Watermelon, peanut, and garlic farming in the southern coastal plains — a deep agricultural community with vibrant temple life.
🦌

Lucao Township

鹿草鄉

Named "Deer Grass" — once a haven for deer herds. Today a quiet farming township famous for garlic, sugar production, and folk temples.
