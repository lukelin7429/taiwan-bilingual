About · 關於東石鄉

Getting to Know Dongshi Township

Dongshi Township hugs the western coastline of Chiayi County along the Taiwan Strait, where vast tidal flats stretch for kilometers at low tide. It is Taiwan's most productive oyster-farming region, responsible for over 30% of the island's total oyster harvest. Rows of bamboo racks extend far into the intertidal zone, where Pacific oysters grow plump and sweet on the nutrient-rich tidal currents flowing in from the open sea.

Beyond oyster farming, Dongshi's wetland ecosystem is one of the most ecologically significant in Taiwan. The Dongshi Wetland is a vital stopover for migratory birds on the East Asian-Australasian Flyway, hosting over 200 recorded species. Mangrove forests along the tidal channels serve as nurseries for fish, shrimp, and crabs. The local fishing harbor still holds daily early-morning auctions where the night's catch is sold fresh off the boat.

東石鄉緊鄰台灣海峽的嘉義縣西部海岸，退潮時潮間帶延伸數公里。這裡是台灣牡蠣產量最高的鄉鎮，占全島牡蠣總產量逾三成。竹製蚵架成排延伸至潮間帶，太平洋牡蠣在豐沛的潮流中長得肥美鮮甜。

除了養蚵業，東石的濕地生態系也是台灣最重要的生態棲地之一。東石濕地是東亞澳洲線候鳥遷徙路線上的關鍵停棲地，已記錄到逾 200 種鳥類。沿潮溝分布的紅樹林是魚、蝦、蟹的重要育幼場。當地漁港至今仍每天清晨舉行現撈漁獲拍賣，維持著傳統漁村的生活節奏。

Highlights · 特色亮點

What Makes Dongshi Township Special

東石鄉的獨特魅力

🦪

Oyster Farming Capital

牡蠣養殖之鄉

Dongshi's tidal flats and clean offshore currents create ideal conditions for oyster cultivation. Local farmers manage thousands of bamboo racks in the intertidal zone, harvesting oysters by hand from small wooden boats. Dongshi oysters are prized for their large size, creamy texture, and clean ocean flavor.

東石潮間帶與清潔的外海水流為牡蠣養殖提供了絕佳條件。當地農民在潮間帶管理數千排蚵架，以小木船人工採收。東石蚵以體型碩大、口感細滑、海味純正而聞名。

🌊

Tidal Flat Ecology

潮間帶生態

At low tide, Dongshi's tidal flats become a feeding ground for thousands of shorebirds — black-faced spoonbills, egrets, and migratory sandpipers probe the mud for worms, clams, and small crabs. The flats also support sea lettuce, sea grass, and a rich community of marine invertebrates.

退潮時，東石廣闊的潮間帶成為數千隻水鳥的覓食場——黑面琵鷺、白鷺及候鳥磯鷸在泥灘上覓食蠕蟲、文蛤與小螃蟹。潮間帶也孕育著石蓴、海草及豐富的海洋無脊椎動物群落。

🐟

Dongshi Fishing Harbor

東石漁港

Dongshi Fishing Harbor is a working port where colorful fishing boats return each morning with fresh catches of grouper, sea bream, and squid. The harbor-side market opens at dawn for fish auctions attended by restaurant buyers from Chiayi City and beyond. Freshly steamed oysters and grilled seafood stalls make this a beloved food destination.

東石漁港是一座充滿活力的作業漁港，彩色漁船每天清晨滿載石斑、鯛魚與魷魚歸航。港邊市場在黎明開市，吸引嘉義市各大餐廳業者前來競標漁獲。現蒸鮮蚵與烤海鮮攤位更讓這裡成為遊客最愛的美食聚集地。

🌿

Mangrove Wetlands

紅樹林濕地

Dongshi's mangrove forests line the tidal channels with their distinctive stilt roots. These trees are among the most productive ecosystems on earth — filtering water, sequestering carbon, buffering storm surges, and providing shelter for juvenile fish, shrimp, and crabs before they move to open water.

東石的紅樹林以獨特的支柱根沿潮溝生長。紅樹林是地球上生產力最高的生態系之一，具有淨水、固碳、防浪等功能，同時為幼魚、蝦、蟹提供成長庇護所。

Fun Facts · 趣味小知識

Did You Know?

你知道嗎？關於東石鄉的有趣事實

🦪 Dongshi produces over 30% of Taiwan's total oyster output — more than any other single township in the country.

東石的牡蠣產量占全台總產量逾三成，居全台各鄉鎮之冠。

🐦 Over 200 species of birds have been recorded in Dongshi's wetlands, including the endangered black-faced spoonbill (黑面琵鷺).

東石濕地已記錄到逾 200 種鳥類，其中包括瀕危物種黑面琵鷺。

⚓ Dongshi has been a fishing community for over 300 years — its harbor was one of the earliest trading ports in southern Taiwan.

東石作為漁村已有逾 300 年歷史，其漁港是台灣南部最早的貿易港口之一。
