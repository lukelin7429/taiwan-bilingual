About · 關於梅山鄉

Getting to Know Meishan Township

Meishan Township climbs from the Chiayi plains into the misty foothills of the Central Mountain Range, spanning elevations from 100 to over 1,400 meters. This dramatic vertical range creates a mosaic of microclimates — from hot subtropical valleys where rice and vegetables grow, to cool high-altitude tea gardens where the daily temperature swing between day and night produces the aromatic complexity that makes Meishan oolong among the most prized in Taiwan.

The township's mountain scenery is spectacular. The famous "Meishan Cloud Sea" (梅山雲海) occurs most mornings from October to March, when a carpet of clouds fills the Chiayi basin below while the mountain peaks rise above them like islands. Mountain Road 18 — a winding highway connecting Meishan to the Alishan area — is considered one of Taiwan's most scenic drives, with hairpin turns overlooking valleys of tea, bamboo, and forest.

梅山鄉從嘉義平原一路延伸至中央山脈雲霧繚繞的山麓地帶，海拔高度從 100 公尺跨越至逾 1,400 公尺。這種劇烈的海拔落差形成多元的微氣候——從炎熱的亞熱帶河谷種植稻米蔬菜，到日夜溫差顯著的高山茶園，使梅山烏龍茶兼具複雜的香氣，成為台灣最珍貴的茶葉之一。

梅山的山景十分壯觀。著名的「梅山雲海」多出現於每年十月至三月的清晨，雲海鋪滿嘉義平原，山峰宛如島嶼從雲端浮現。縣道 18 號公路連結梅山與阿里山地區，途中迂迴蜿蜒的山路俯瞰茶園、竹林與原始森林，被視為台灣最美的公路景觀之一。

Highlights · 特色亮點

What Makes Meishan Township Special

梅山鄉的獨特魅力

🍵

High Mountain Tea Gardens

高山茶園

Meishan's Ruili and Ruifeng districts produce premium high-mountain oolong at elevations of 800–1,400 meters. The cool temperatures, morning mist, and mineral-rich soil give the tea a floral, buttery complexity. Tea farmers here follow precise seasonal harvest rhythms — spring and winter teas are the most sought-after.

梅山的瑞里與瑞峰地區在海拔 800 至 1,400 公尺生產頂級高山烏龍茶。涼爽氣溫、晨霧與富含礦物質的土壤賦予茶葉花香與奶油感的複雜風味。茶農嚴格依循季節採收節奏，春茶與冬茶最受市場追捧。

☁️

Meishan Cloud Sea

梅山雲海

Between October and March, the Chiayi basin fills with a sea of clouds each morning, visible from Meishan's observation platforms. As the sun rises, the cloud layer shifts and swirls in slow motion — a mesmerizing natural display that draws photographers and early risers from across Taiwan.

每年十月至三月，嘉義平原每天清晨都會形成雲海，從梅山各觀景台可清楚眺望。隨著太陽升起，雲層緩緩移動翻騰，形成令人著迷的自然奇景，吸引全台攝影迷與早起旅人慕名而來。

🛣️

Mountain Road 18 (Scenic Drive)

縣道 18 號景觀公路

County Road 18 winds from the foothills at Zhuqi through Meishan's tea villages before climbing toward Alishan. The road offers sweeping views of layered mountain ridges, tea terraces, and bamboo forests. Small roadside tea houses and lookout points make it perfect for a day of slow mountain driving.

縣道 18 號從竹崎山麓出發，蜿蜒穿越梅山茶鄉，再攀升至阿里山。一路上層疊山脊、茶梯與竹林景色盡收眼底。沿途小型茶館與觀景台讓這條公路成為享受山城慢旅的絕佳路線。

🏘️

Meishan Old Street

梅山老街

Meishan's historic market street retains traditional shop-house architecture from the Japanese colonial period. Local vendors sell handmade mochi (麻糬), bamboo shoots, pickled plums, and artisan teas. The street is particularly lively during temple festivals and harvest season.

梅山老街保存了日治時期的傳統街屋建築。在地攤販販售手工麻糬、竹筍、醃漬梅子與精製茶葉。每逢廟會節慶與農作收穫季節，老街更是人聲鼎沸、熱鬧非凡。

梅山烏龍茶屬半氧化茶，風味介於綠茶與紅茶之間。

terrain n.

地形；地勢

"The rugged mountain terrain of Meishan makes tea farming both challenging and rewarding."

梅山崎嶇的山地地形使種茶既具挑戰性，又充滿成就感。

山路上可遠眺嘉義平原的壯麗景色。

mist n.

霧氣；薄霧

"Morning mist settles in the tea gardens, keeping the leaves cool and tender."

清晨的霧氣籠罩茶園，使茶葉保持涼爽鮮嫩。

cultivate v.

栽培；種植

"Meishan farmers cultivate tea on steep hillside terraces."

梅山農民在陡峭的山坡梯田上栽培茶葉。

Fun Facts · 趣味小知識

Did You Know?

你知道嗎？關於梅山鄉的有趣事實

☁️ Meishan's cloud sea occurs on over 200 mornings per year — making it one of Taiwan's most reliable natural spectacles.

梅山雲海每年出現逾 200 個早晨，是台灣最穩定可見的自然奇景之一。

🍵 The name "Meishan" (梅山) means "plum mountain" — the area was once covered in wild plum trees that blossomed every spring.

「梅山」之名源自「梅花山」，昔日此地遍植野梅，每年春天繁花盛開。

🛣️ County Road 18 has over 170 sharp curves between Zhuqi and Alishan — earning it a reputation as one of Taiwan's most exciting mountain drives.

縣道 18 號從竹崎至阿里山間有逾 170 個急彎，被稱為台灣最刺激的山路之一。
