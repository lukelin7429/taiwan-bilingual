About · 關於義竹鄉

Getting to Know Yizhu Township

Yizhu Township lies in the southern part of Chiayi County, a flat, sun-drenched agricultural plain close to the border with Tainan City. Life here follows the quiet rhythms of the farming calendar — spring peanut planting, summer watermelon harvest, autumn sweet potato cultivation, and winter vegetable growing. The township's proximity to the coast and its sandy loam soil create ideal conditions for growing sweet, crisp watermelons that are sold across southern Taiwan.

Peanuts (花生) are the other signature crop of Yizhu. The township's sandy soil provides exactly the loose, well-drained medium that peanuts need to develop their full flavor. Local cooperative farms process peanuts into peanut oil, peanut butter, and the distinctive peanut brittle candy (花生糖) that is a traditional Taiwanese snack. Yizhu's rural pace and agricultural authenticity make it a living example of Taiwan's deep farming heritage.

義竹鄉位於嘉義縣南部，是鄰近台南市邊界的平坦、陽光充沛的農業平原。這裡的生活遵循著農業曆的靜謐節奏——春種花生、夏收西瓜、秋植番薯、冬種蔬菜。緊鄰海岸的地理位置與沙質壤土為種植甜脆西瓜創造了絕佳條件，西瓜銷售遍及台灣南部。

花生是義竹的另一項代表作物。本鄉沙質土壤提供了花生生長所需的疏鬆、排水良好的土質，使其風味更加飽滿。在地合作農場將花生加工成花生油、花生醬與傳統台灣零食花生糖。義竹的農村步調與農業純樸，是台灣深厚農耕傳統的活生生見證。

Highlights · 特色亮點

What Makes Yizhu Township Special

義竹鄉的獨特魅力

🍉
Watermelon Capital
西瓜產地
Yizhu's flat plain and sandy loam soil produce sweet, crisp watermelons from June to August. Local farms sell direct from roadside stands stacked high with giant melons, drawing visitors from across southern Taiwan who come for the freshest fruit straight from the field.
義竹平坦的平原與沙質壤土，每年六月至八月孕育出香甜脆口的西瓜。在地農場直接在路邊攤位販售，巨大的西瓜疊高如山，吸引台灣南部各地遊客前來品嚐最新鮮的田園果實。
🥜
Peanut Farming & Products
花生農業與製品
Yizhu's sandy soil is perfect for peanut cultivation, giving peanuts room to develop underground with excellent drainage. Local cooperative farms produce cold-pressed peanut oil, roasted peanuts, and traditional peanut brittle candy (花生糖) — a beloved Taiwanese snack with a satisfying crunch.
義竹沙質土壤非常適合花生種植，為地下生長的花生提供疏鬆、排水良好的環境。在地合作農場生產冷壓花生油、烘烤花生，以及傳統花生糖，是深受喜愛的台灣零食，口感酥脆令人回味無窮。
🌾
Sweet Potato Cultivation
番薯種植
Yizhu's autumn sweet potato harvest produces vibrant orange-fleshed varieties prized for their natural sweetness. Many are processed into traditional Taiwanese snacks including dried sweet potato strips and sweet potato chips. The crop is a testament to the township's year-round agricultural productivity.
義竹秋季番薯收穫產出鮮豔橘肉品種，以天然甜味著稱。許多番薯加工成傳統台灣零食，包括番薯乾與番薯片。這項作物展現了本鄉全年農業生產的豐沛活力。
🕌
Rural Temple Culture
農村廟宇文化
Village temples serve as community centers where generations of farming families gather for festivals, prayers for good harvests, and communal celebrations. These temples are the beating heart of Yizhu's social life, preserving rituals and traditions that connect modern residents to their agricultural roots.
村莊廟宇是社區中心，世代農家在此聚集，舉行節慶、祈求豐收，共同歡慶。這些廟宇是義竹社會生活的跳動之心，保存著儀式與傳統，讓現代居民與農耕根源緊密相連。

義竹農民在排水良好的沙質土壤中種植花生，讓根部保持健康。

Sandy loam is a type of soil that mixes sand and clay, making it great for growing many crops.
沙質壤土是混合沙和黏土的土壤類型，非常適合多種作物生長。

Families in Yizhu have cultivated the same fields for many generations, passing down their farming skills.
義竹家庭世代耕作同一片田地，代代相傳農耕技術。
harvest(n./v.)

The watermelon harvest in Yizhu happens in June and July, when the melons are at their sweetest.
義竹的西瓜收穫季節在六月與七月，此時瓜果最為香甜。
brittle(adj.)
酥脆的
Peanut brittle candy is a crispy, brittle treat made by mixing peanuts with hot caramelized sugar.
花生糖是將花生與熱焦糖混合製成的酥脆零食。
annual(adj.)
每年的
The annual watermelon season brings buyers and visitors to Yizhu from all over southern Taiwan.
一年一度的西瓜季節吸引台灣南部各地買家與遊客來到義竹。
rural(adj.)
農村的；鄉間的
Yizhu has a peaceful, rural atmosphere where you can see open fields stretching to the horizon.
義竹擁有寧靜的農村氛圍，放眼望去是延伸至地平線的開闊田野。

Fun Facts · 趣味小知識

Did You Know?

你知道嗎？關於義竹鄉的有趣事實

Yizhu watermelons are typically harvested in June and July — farmers know each melon is ripe by tapping it and listening for a hollow "thump" sound, a skill passed down through generations.
義竹西瓜通常在六月與七月採收——農民敲打西瓜，聆聽空洞的「咚」聲來判斷是否熟透，這項技術世代相傳。
A single peanut plant produces 25–50 individual peanuts underground — the nuts are actually legume seeds that grow from flowering stems that push into the soil after pollination.
一株花生植物可在地下結出25至50顆花生——這些果實其實是豆科種子，由授粉後向下插入土壤的花梗生長而來。
The name "Yizhu" (義竹) means "righteous bamboo" — the township was historically known for its bamboo groves before agricultural clearing made room for today's crops.
「義竹」意為「正義之竹」——本鄉歷史上以竹林聞名，後來農業開墾才為今日的農作物騰出空間。
