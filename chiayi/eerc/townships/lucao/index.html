About · 關於鹿草鄉

Getting to Know Lucao Township

Lucao Township takes its name from the Chinese words for "deer" (鹿, lù) and "grass" (草, cǎo) — a direct reference to the open grasslands that once supported large herds of Taiwan sambar deer and Formosan sika deer before the island's agricultural transformation. Today the township presents a quiet face of rice paddies, garlic fields, and small farming communities, but its name preserves the ecological memory of a wilder time.

The township contains the former Lujiao Sugar Factory (鹿草糖廠), one of the Taiwan Sugar Corporation's historic production facilities. Though the refinery itself no longer operates, its grounds have become a point of historical interest, and the surrounding farmland still produces sugarcane for specialty markets. Lucao is also known for its garlic production — together with neighboring Liujiao, it forms part of the "Chiayi garlic belt" that supplies a significant portion of Taiwan's total garlic crop.

鹿草鄉的地名來自「鹿」與「草」，直接記錄了台灣農業開發前這片平原曾有大批梅花鹿與水鹿出沒的生態記憶。今日本鄉呈現稻田、蒜田與小型農村社區交織的靜謐風貌，但其地名永久保存了那個更為野性的時代。

鄉內有台灣糖業公司的歷史生產設施鹿草糖廠舊址。糖廠雖已停止運作，其廠區已成為歷史人文景點，周邊農地仍為特殊市場生產甘蔗。鹿草也以蒜頭種植著稱，與相鄰的六腳鄉共同構成供應全台大量蒜頭的「嘉義蒜頭帶」。

Highlights · 特色亮點

What Makes Lucao Township Special

鹿草鄉的獨特魅力

🦌
Deer Heritage & Township Name
梅花鹿文化遺跡
Lucao's name commemorates the Taiwan sika deer that once grazed these plains. Historical records from the Dutch colonial era document massive deer herds roaming freely across the Chiayi region — a wildlife legacy that lives on in the township's identity.
鹿草鄉之名紀念曾在此平原吃草的台灣梅花鹿。荷治時期的歷史文獻記載了嘉義地區大批鹿群自由奔馳的景象，這份野生動物遺產至今仍活在鄉鎮的名字之中。
🏭
Lujiao Sugar Factory Heritage
鹿草糖廠遺址
The former Taiwan Sugar Corporation refinery tells the story of the sugarcane economy that once powered Chiayi County's rural communities. Its grounds and narrow-gauge railway network connected farms across a wide radius, shaping the landscape and livelihoods of generations.
台灣糖業公司的舊糖廠訴說著昔日驅動嘉義縣農村社區的甘蔗經濟故事。廠區與窄軌鐵道網絡連結了方圓數十公里的農田，形塑了數代人的土地與生計。
🧄
Garlic Belt Production
蒜頭帶農業
Together with neighboring Liujiao Township, Lucao is a key part of Chiayi County's famous garlic-growing region. The pungent crop flavors dishes across Taiwan — most of the harvest is gathered between April and June, filling the township air with a distinctive aroma.
鹿草與相鄰的六腳鄉共同構成嘉義縣著名的蒜頭種植區。這種氣味濃烈的作物為全台各地的料理增添風味，大部分蒜頭於四至六月間採收，收穫期間整個鄉鎮飄散著獨特的香氣。
🌾
Traditional Rice Farming
傳統稻米農業
Double-cropped rice paddies dominate Lucao's flat landscape, with planting in spring and autumn following patterns unchanged for centuries. The rhythm of the rice calendar — preparing fields, transplanting seedlings, and harvesting — remains central to township life.
雙期稻作主導著鹿草鄉的平坦地貌，春秋兩季的插秧遵循著數百年來不變的節奏。備田、插秧、收割的稻作日曆節奏，至今仍是鄉鎮生活的核心。

The old sugar refinery in Lucao once processed sugarcane from dozens of nearby villages.
鹿草的舊糖廠曾處理來自數十個鄰近村莊的甘蔗。
ritual(n.)
儀式
The seasonal ritual of transplanting rice seedlings brings families together every spring.
每年春天，插秧的季節性儀式將家人凝聚在一起。
ancestral(adj.)
祖先的
Many Lucao families still farm their ancestral land using traditional methods passed down for generations.
許多鹿草家庭至今仍在祖先的土地上以世代相傳的傳統方式耕作。
commemorate(v.)
紀念
The township's name was chosen to commemorate the wild deer that once lived here.
這個鄉鎮的名稱是為了紀念曾在此生活的野生鹿群而命名的。
heritage(n.)
遺產
The old sugar factory is an important part of Lucao's agricultural and industrial heritage.
舊糖廠是鹿草農業與工業遺產的重要組成部分。

Fun Facts · 趣味小知識

Did You Know?

你知道嗎？關於鹿草鄉的有趣事實

Taiwan once had one of the highest densities of wild deer in Asia — Dutch colonial records from the 1600s describe vast herds of sika deer across the Chiayi plains, where Lucao now stands.
台灣曾擁有亞洲最高密度的野生鹿群之一——荷治時期1600年代的文獻記載了嘉義平原（即今日鹿草所在地）大批梅花鹿群的景象。
The Lujiao Sugar Factory processed sugarcane grown across dozens of neighboring villages — its narrow-gauge railway network connected farms across a 50-kilometer radius.
鹿草糖廠處理了來自數十個鄰近村莊種植的甘蔗——其窄軌鐵道網絡連結了方圓50公里以內的農田。
Chiayi County, including Lucao, produces over 60% of Taiwan's total domestic garlic crop — most of it harvested between April and June each year.
包含鹿草在內的嘉義縣生產了台灣超過60%的國內蒜頭作物，大部分於每年四至六月間採收。
