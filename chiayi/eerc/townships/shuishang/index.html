About · 關於水上鄉

Getting to Know Shuishang Township

Shuishang Township lies just south of Chiayi City, straddling the transition from urban fringe to agricultural plain. Its most recognizable landmark is Chiayi Airport (嘉義水上機場), the region's primary civilian and military airfield, whose runways bisect the township's flat landscape. The airport has served Chiayi County since the Japanese colonial era, when it was established as a military aviation base in the 1930s.

Away from the airport, Shuishang presents a tranquil rural landscape of lotus ponds, orchid greenhouses, and rice paddies. The lotus ponds are particularly beautiful in summer (June–August) when pink and white lotus flowers bloom across the still water surfaces, attracting photographers and recreational visitors from Chiayi City. Shuishang is also an emerging center for orchid cultivation, with greenhouses producing Phalaenopsis orchids for domestic and export markets.

水上鄉緊鄰嘉義市南方，介於城市邊緣與農業平原之間。最具代表性的地標是嘉義水上機場——嘉義縣主要的民用與軍用機場，跑道橫貫本鄉平坦地景。機場自日治時代即服務嘉義縣，1930 年代以軍事航空基地的身分創設。

機場以外，水上呈現出蓮花池、蘭花溫室與稻田交織的恬靜農村景觀。蓮花池在夏季（六至八月）尤為迷人，粉紅與純白的蓮花盛開於靜謐水面，吸引攝影愛好者與休閒遊客從嘉義市專程前來。水上也是蘭花種植的新興中心，溫室生產蝴蝶蘭供應國內外市場。

Highlights · 特色亮點

What Makes Shuishang Township Special

水上鄉的獨特魅力

✈️
Chiayi Airport & Aviation History
嘉義水上機場
One of Taiwan's oldest operational airfields, originally built in the 1930s during the Japanese colonial era. The airport still serves both military and civilian flights, connecting Chiayi County to outlying islands and keeping the township at the heart of regional air travel.
臺灣最古老的現役機場之一，1930 年代日治時期建立，至今仍同時服務軍用與民用航班，連結嘉義縣與外島，是本鄉航空發展的歷史見證。
🌸
Lotus Pond Scenery
蓮花田景色
Thousands of lotus blossoms cover Shuishang's ponds from June to August, creating a stunning summer landscape of floating pink and white flowers against bright green lily pads. Visitors come early — the blooms open each morning before 10 a.m. and begin to close by early afternoon.
每年六至八月，數以千計的蓮花盛開於水上鄉的池塘，粉紅與純白花朵浮映於翠綠荷葉之上，是令人屏息的夏日美景。蓮花清晨十點前盛開，午後即漸漸合攏，賞蓮要趁早。
🌺
Orchid Cultivation
蘭花種植
Phalaenopsis orchids grown in Shuishang's state-of-the-art climate-controlled greenhouses are exported to flower markets around the world. The township is part of Chiayi County's thriving orchid industry — and Taiwan leads the entire globe in Phalaenopsis orchid exports.
水上鄉先進的恆溫溫室所培育的蝴蝶蘭外銷全球花卉市場，是嘉義縣蘭花產業的重要一環——而臺灣正是全球蝴蝶蘭最大出口國。
🌾
Irrigation Canal Network
水利灌溉渠道
A historic network of irrigation canals built during the Japanese colonial era continues to water Shuishang's rice fields today. These quiet, winding waterways also provide valuable habitat for freshwater birds and aquatic life, weaving together the township's agricultural heritage and natural environment.
日治時代建設的歷史灌溉渠道網絡至今仍滋養著水上的稻田。這些蜿蜒的水道同時也為淡水鳥類與水生生物提供棲地，將本鄉農業傳承與自然環境緊密交織。

Fun Facts · 趣味小知識

Did You Know?

你知道嗎？關於水上鄉的有趣事實

Chiayi Airport was originally built by Japanese military forces in the 1930s and has been in continuous operation for over 80 years — making it one of Taiwan's longest-serving airfields.
嘉義機場最初由日本軍方於 1930 年代建造，持續運作超過 80 年，是臺灣服役時間最長的機場之一。
Shuishang's lotus ponds attract tens of thousands of visitors every summer — the blossoms open each morning before 10 a.m. and begin to close by early afternoon.
水上鄉的蓮花池每年夏天吸引數萬名遊客前來——蓮花每天上午十點前盛開，午後便漸漸闔起。
Taiwan is the world's largest exporter of Phalaenopsis orchids, and Chiayi County — including Shuishang — is one of the top production regions for these beautiful flowers.
臺灣是全球最大的蝴蝶蘭出口國，而嘉義縣（包含水上鄉）正是蝴蝶蘭的頂尖產區之一。
