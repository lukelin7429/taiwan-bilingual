About · 關於太保市

Getting to Know Taibao City

Taibao City serves as the administrative capital of Chiayi County, housing the county government complex, courts, police headquarters, and numerous public agencies. Though compact in size, it plays an outsized role in the governance and economy of the entire county. The city's central location between Chiayi City and the coastal townships makes it a natural hub for public transportation and civic services.

Taibao is also home to National Chiayi University (NCYU), one of Taiwan's leading comprehensive universities with strengths in agriculture, science, and education. The university's sprawling campus brings thousands of students to the city each year, adding youthful energy to an otherwise administrative town. Nearby, the historic Puzi Station and Taiwan Sugar Museum preserve the region's sugarcane railway heritage.

太保市是嘉義縣的行政首府，設有縣政府、法院、警察局總部及各公共機關。雖然面積不大，卻在整個縣的治理與經濟運作上扮演舉足輕重的角色。太保介於嘉義市與沿海鄉鎮之間，地理位置優越，自然成為公共交通與公民服務的核心樞紐。

太保也是國立嘉義大學（NCYU）的所在地，這所以農業、科學與教育見長的綜合大學每年吸引數千名學生，為這座以行政功能為主的城市注入青春活力。附近的朴子站舊址與台灣糖業博物館更保存了這一帶珍貴的蔗糖鐵路文化遺產。

Highlights · 特色亮點

What Makes Taibao City Special

太保市的獨特魅力

🏛️

Chiayi County Government

嘉義縣政府

The Chiayi County Government complex relocated to Taibao from Chiayi City in 2003, anchoring Taibao as the county seat. The modern complex houses the county executive, county council, and administrative bureaus covering everything from education to environmental protection for all 18 townships.

嘉義縣政府於 2003 年從嘉義市遷至太保，奠定太保縣轄市的地位。現代化的縣府園區設有縣長辦公室、縣議會及各行政局處，統籌管理全縣 18 個鄉鎮市的教育、環保等各項業務。

🎓

National Chiayi University

國立嘉義大學

Founded in 1919 as an agricultural school, NCYU has grown into a comprehensive university with colleges of agriculture, science, education, management, arts, and music. Its Minxiong and Lantan campuses serve over 10,000 students and generate significant research in agricultural biotechnology and ecological sustainability.

國立嘉義大學前身為 1919 年創立的農業學校，現已發展為擁有農學、理學、師範、管理、人文藝術暨音樂等學院的綜合大學，兩校區逾一萬名在學生，在農業生技與生態永續領域深具研究實力。

🚃

Taiwan Sugar Museum

台灣糖業博物館

The former Puzi Station of Taiwan Sugar Corporation has been converted into an open-air museum showcasing the historic narrow-gauge sugar railway. Vintage locomotives, cane transport wagons, and photographic exhibits document the century-long era when sugarcane was Chiayi County's most valuable crop.

台糖朴子車站舊址已改建為露天博物館，展示昔日的小型蔗糖鐵路。老式蒸汽機車、甘蔗運輸車廂與歷史照片，訴說著甘蔗作為嘉義縣最重要作物的百年歲月。

🌆

Modern Urban Development

現代都市建設

Taibao has seen rapid development since becoming the county seat, with new hospitals, shopping centers, and residential communities sprouting around the government campus. The city's planned road grid and public spaces make it one of the most orderly and livable urban centers in Chiayi County.

自成為縣治所在地後，太保迅速發展，縣府園區周邊新建了醫院、商場與住宅社區。城市規劃整齊的路網與公共空間，使太保成為嘉義縣最宜居的城市之一。

Fun Facts · 趣味小知識

Did You Know?

你知道嗎？關於太保市的有趣事實

🏛️ Taibao became a city (市) in 1991 — upgraded from a township when its population exceeded 50,000 residents.

太保於 1991 年人口突破五萬後，由鄉改制升格為市。

🎓 National Chiayi University traces its origins to 1919 — making it over 100 years old, one of the oldest universities in southern Taiwan.

國立嘉義大學創立於 1919 年，迄今已逾百年，是台灣南部歷史最悠久的大學之一。

🚃 The Taiwan Sugar Railway once connected dozens of farms across Chiayi County — at its peak, the narrow-gauge network was over 3,000 km long.

台灣糖業鐵路曾連結嘉義縣各地農場，全盛時期小型鐵路網絡綿延逾 3,000 公里。
