About · 關於民雄鄉

Getting to Know Minxiong Township

Minxiong Township is one of the most populous and economically active townships in Chiayi County, benefiting from its position adjacent to Chiayi City and its role as a major university town. National Chiayi University's main Minxiong Campus (民雄校區) anchors a vibrant student community, with cafés, bookshops, and night market stalls catering to thousands of students. The presence of the university has made Minxiong a center of young energy and cultural activity in rural Chiayi.

The township's agricultural heritage centers on pineapple farming — the warm, well-drained soil of the Minxiong plains is ideal for growing sweet, fibrous pineapples that are processed into juice, jam, and the famous pineapple cake (鳳梨酥) filling. Minxiong also contains the famous "Ghost House" (民雄鬼屋), an abandoned colonial-era mansion that has become one of Taiwan's most talked-about haunted sites, generating a thriving ghost tourism industry.

民雄鄉是嘉義縣人口最多、經濟最活躍的鄉鎮之一，緊鄰嘉義市，且以大學城聞名。國立嘉義大學民雄校區錨定了充滿活力的學生社群，咖啡館、書店與夜市攤位服務著數千名學生，使民雄成為農村嘉義年輕文化的中心。

本鄉的農業傳統以鳳梨種植為主——民雄平原溫暖、排水良好的土壤非常適合種植甜美多汁的鳳梨，加工成果汁、果醬與著名的鳳梨酥內餡。民雄還有著名的「民雄鬼屋」，這棟日治時代廢棄的洋房成為台灣最受矚目的鬼屋景點之一，帶動了蓬勃的靈異觀光產業。

Highlights · 特色亮點

What Makes Minxiong Township Special

民雄鄉的獨特魅力

🎓
National Chiayi University — Minxiong Campus
國立嘉義大學民雄校區
One of Taiwan's leading comprehensive universities, the Minxiong Campus brings over 10,000 students to the township and drives a lively local economy of cafés, bookshops, and night market stalls — making Minxiong a rare hub of youthful energy in rural Chiayi.
國立嘉義大學民雄校區是台灣頂尖綜合大學之一，每年吸引逾萬名學生進駐，帶動咖啡館、書店與夜市等在地經濟，讓民雄成為農村嘉義少見的年輕活力重鎮。
🍍
Pineapple Farming Heritage
鳳梨農業傳統
Minxiong's warm, well-drained plains have produced sweet pineapples for generations. The fruit is processed locally into juice, jam, and the filling for pineapple cakes — one of Taiwan's most beloved and iconic souvenir foods gifted around the world.
民雄平原溫暖排水良好，數十年來盛產香甜鳳梨，在地加工成果汁、果醬，以及台灣最具代表性的伴手禮——鳳梨酥的內餡，聞名海內外。
👻
Minxiong Ghost House
民雄鬼屋
An abandoned Japanese-era mansion dating to the 1920s–1940s, the Minxiong Ghost House is one of Taiwan's most talked-about paranormal sites. It has been featured in countless TV documentaries and ghost-hunting shows, drawing curious visitors from across the island.
這棟建於日治時期（1920至1940年代）的廢棄洋房是台灣最受矚目的靈異景點，曾出現在無數電視紀錄片與捉鬼節目中，每年吸引全台好奇遊客慕名前來。
🌃
Night Market & Student Culture
夜市與學生文化
Minxiong's large university population has given rise to a lively night market, a thriving café scene, and street food stalls that blend rural Chiayi tradition with modern youth culture — creating a unique small-town atmosphere unlike anywhere else in the county.
龐大的大學生族群孕育出民雄熱鬧的夜市、蓬勃的咖啡文化與街頭美食，將農村嘉義傳統與現代青年文化巧妙融合，形成縣內獨一無二的小鎮氛圍。

Fun Facts · 趣味小知識

Did You Know?

你知道嗎？關於民雄鄉的有趣事實

Taiwan's pineapple cake (鳳梨酥) is the country's most popular souvenir food — and many of the best versions use pineapple paste made from Chiayi County pineapples like those grown in Minxiong.
台灣鳳梨酥是全國最受歡迎的伴手禮食品，許多頂級鳳梨酥使用的正是嘉義縣（包括民雄）所產鳳梨製成的果醬餡料。
The Minxiong Ghost House (民雄鬼屋) dates from the Japanese colonial era (1920s–1940s) and has been the subject of dozens of TV documentaries, ghost-hunting shows, and popular folklore narratives.
民雄鬼屋建於日治時代（1920至1940年代），已被數十部電視紀錄片、捉鬼節目及民間傳說所記錄，是台灣最具知名度的靈異地點。
National Chiayi University was founded in 1919 as an agricultural college — making it over 100 years old and one of the most historic universities in southern Taiwan.
國立嘉義大學創立於1919年，最初為農業學院，至今已逾百年歷史，是台灣南部最具歷史傳承的大學之一。
