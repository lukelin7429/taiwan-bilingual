About · 關於六腳鄉

Getting to Know Liujiao Township

Liujiao Township occupies the agricultural heart of Chiayi County's coastal plain, where flat, fertile land supports a rich mosaic of crops. The township is best known for two things: its spectacular winter sunflower fields, and its garlic production. Each December through February, farmers plant sunflowers between rice harvest and spring planting season, transforming the landscape into a sea of golden yellow. The Liujiao Sunflower Festival draws visitors from across Taiwan to walk through fields of towering, sun-facing blooms.

Liujiao's garlic is celebrated across Taiwan for its pungent aroma and concentrated flavor — the result of the coastal plain's mineral-rich soil and ideal growing conditions. The Shuangxi area of Liujiao is synonymous with premium garlic production. The township also preserves remains of the Taiwan Sugar Corporation railway, including the Shuangxi Sugar Factory, whose grounds have been converted into a family recreation park with vintage train rides.

六腳鄉位於嘉義縣沿海平原的農業腹地，平坦肥沃的土地養育了多樣豐富的作物。本鄉最著名的是冬季向日葵花田與蒜頭種植。每年十二月至二月，農民在稻米收割後、春耕前的空窗期種植向日葵，將廣袤大地染成金黃色海洋。六腳向日葵節吸引全台遊客徜徉在高聳的向陽花叢中。

六腳的蒜頭以濃郁的香氣和集中的風味聞名全台，這得益於海岸平原富含礦物質的土壤與絕佳的種植條件。蒜頭糖廠舊址已改建為家庭休閒公園，提供懷舊小火車搭乘服務。

Highlights · 特色亮點

What Makes Liujiao Township Special

六腳鄉的獨特魅力

🌻
Sunflower Fields
向日葵花田
Winter sunflower plantings transform Liujiao into a golden landscape every December through February. The annual festival brings thousands of visitors to photograph the blooms and stroll through rows of towering, sun-facing flowers.
每年十二月至二月，冬季向日葵將六腳染成金黃大地。一年一度的花節吸引大批遊客前來拍照，漫步在高聳的向陽花田之間。
🧄
Liujiao Garlic
六腳蒜頭
Liujiao garlic is prized across Taiwan for its strong flavor and aroma. The coastal plain's mineral-rich soil produces garlic with higher allicin content than most other regions, making it a favorite among chefs and home cooks alike.
六腳蒜頭以濃烈的風味與香氣享譽全台。沿海平原富含礦物質的土壤，讓這裡出產的蒜頭含有比其他地區更高的大蒜素，深受廚師與家庭主婦喜愛。
🚃
Shuangxi Sugar Factory Park
蒜頭糖廠親子園區
The former Taiwan Sugar Corporation factory has been reimagined as a family recreation park featuring vintage sugarcane train rides, an ice cream shop, and heritage exhibits that bring the sugar industry's history to life.
昔日的台灣糖業公司工廠已華麗轉身，成為充滿懷舊風情的親子園區，有復古甘蔗小火車、冰淇淋販賣部，以及重現糖業歷史的文物展示。
🌾
Fertile Agricultural Plains
肥沃農業平原
Liujiao's flat land produces rice, garlic, and seasonal vegetables in succession throughout the year, exemplifying Taiwan's highly efficient agricultural system where no field sits idle for long.
六腳平坦的土地全年輪番種植稻米、蒜頭與季節性蔬菜，充分展現台灣高效率農業體系的精髓——每一塊田都物盡其用、生生不息。

Fun Facts · 趣味小知識

Did You Know?

你知道嗎？關於六腳鄉的有趣事實

Liujiao's sunflower fields cover over 100 hectares during peak winter season — visitors can walk freely among the flowers, which are planted specifically to attract tourism during the agricultural off-season.
六腳的向日葵花田在冬季高峰期面積超過一百公頃，遊客可自由穿梭在花叢間，這些向日葵是農閒期間為吸引觀光而特別種植的。
Liujiao garlic contains some of the highest allicin levels of any garlic grown in Taiwan — a result of the specific mineral balance in the local coastal plain soil, which gives the garlic its signature strong aroma and health benefits.
六腳蒜頭的大蒜素含量位居全台之冠，這源於當地沿海平原土壤特有的礦物質平衡，賦予蒜頭標誌性的濃郁香氣與健康功效。
The Shuangxi Sugar Factory was established in 1906 and produced sugar for over 70 years — its narrow-gauge railway once connected to a network of over 80 km of sugarcane transport tracks across the Chiayi plain.
蒜頭糖廠創立於一九〇六年，生產砂糖逾七十年，其窄軌鐵路曾連結嘉義平原超過八十公里的甘蔗運輸路網。
