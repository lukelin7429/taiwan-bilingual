About · 關於大林鎮

Getting to Know Dalin Township

Dalin Township is located in the northern part of Chiayi County, positioned along the Western Taiwan Railway Line at a historic junction point. In the early 20th century under Japanese administration, Dalin became an important center for sugarcane processing — the Dalin Sugar Refinery (大林糖廠) operated for decades, processing cane from the surrounding farmland and contributing to the area's economic foundation. The factory's narrow-gauge railway network once spread across dozens of kilometers of cane fields.

Today, Dalin is known throughout Taiwan for Dalin Tzu Chi Hospital (大林慈濟醫院), a world-class medical center founded by Buddhist Tzu Chi Foundation in 2000. The hospital serves not only Dalin but the entire northern Chiayi region, drawing patients from across southern Taiwan with its combination of advanced medicine and compassionate care rooted in Buddhist values.

大林鎮位於嘉義縣北部，沿台灣西部縱貫鐵路線的一個重要交會點。日治時代初期，大林成為甘蔗加工的重要中心——大林糖廠運作數十年，處理周邊農地的甘蔗，奠定了地區的經濟基礎。糖廠的輕便鐵路網絡曾延伸數十公里。

今日大林因大林慈濟醫院而聞名全台。這座由佛教慈濟基金會於 2000 年創設的世界級醫療中心，服務大林及整個嘉義北區，以先進醫療結合佛教慈悲精神聞名，吸引台灣南部各地病患前來就醫。

Highlights · 特色亮點

What Makes Dalin Township Special

大林鎮的獨特魅力

🏥
Dalin Tzu Chi Hospital
大林慈濟醫院
A world-class Buddhist-founded hospital combining modern medicine with compassionate care. With over 1,000 beds and a regional trauma center, it has transformed access to healthcare for all of northern Chiayi County since opening in 2000.
由佛教慈濟基金會創設的世界級醫療中心，融合現代醫學與慈悲精神。擁有逾千張病床及區域急救中心，自 2000 年起大幅提升嘉義北區居民的醫療可近性。
🚉
Historic Railway Junction
歷史鐵道交會點
Dalin Station on the Western Railway has served passengers since Japanese colonial times. Positioned at a key junction, it remains a gateway connecting Chiayi City to the mountains and a living reminder of the town's role in Taiwan's transportation history.
大林車站自日治時代便是縱貫鐵路的重要節點，至今仍是連接嘉義市與山區的門戶，也是大林在台灣鐵路史上重要地位的活見證。
🌾
Sugarcane Heritage
甘蔗文化遺產
The former Dalin Sugar Refinery and its narrow-gauge cane-field railway tell the story of Taiwan's sugar industry, which once powered the island's entire economy. This sweet legacy shaped the landscape, culture, and community identity of Dalin for generations.
舊大林糖廠與其田間輕便鐵路，訴說著曾支撐台灣整體經濟的糖業故事。這段甜蜜的歷史遺產，世代形塑了大林的地景、文化與社區認同。
🍍
Pineapple & Agricultural Produce
鳳梨與農產品
Dalin's flat, fertile plains support thriving pineapple, corn, and vegetable farming. The township's agricultural output supplies markets across Chiayi County, keeping the rural heartbeat strong even as the town grows as a regional healthcare hub.
大林平坦肥沃的平原盛產鳳梨、玉米及各式蔬菜，農業產值供應嘉義縣各地市場，讓這座醫療重鎮仍保有濃厚的鄉村生命力。

Fun Facts · 趣味小知識

Did You Know?

你知道嗎？關於大林鎮的有趣事實

🏥 Dalin Tzu Chi Hospital was built on land donated by the Tzu Chi Foundation and became fully operational in 2000 — it was the first major hospital constructed in Chiayi County in decades.
大林慈濟醫院建於慈濟基金會捐贈的土地上，於 2000 年正式全面啟用，是數十年來嘉義縣首座新建的大型醫院。
🚉 The Dalin Sugar Refinery railway network once stretched over 60 km across the Chiayi plains, connecting dozens of cane farms to the central processing factory.
大林糖廠的鐵路網絡曾延伸超過 60 公里橫跨嘉義平原，將數十座甘蔗農場與中央加工廠連接起來。
🌾 The name "Dalin" (大林) means "great forest" — the township was originally covered by dense subtropical woodland before agricultural clearing in the 18th century.
「大林」意為「大森林」——這個鄉鎮在 18 世紀農業開墾之前，原本覆蓋著茂密的亞熱帶樹林。
