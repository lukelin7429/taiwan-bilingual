SDG 14 · Planet · 地球

Life Below Water

海洋生態

In one sentence 一句話

Protect the oceans, rivers, and the creatures that live in them.

保護海洋、河川與其中的生物。

Vocabulary 單字

English中文Definition · 釋義
ocean海洋the big salty water around lands.
陸地周圍的鹹水。
plastic塑膠a man-made material.
人造材料。
trash垃圾things thrown away.
丟掉的東西。
fish魚an animal that lives in water.
在水裡生活的動物。

Classroom Activity 課堂活動

Trace the trash

追蹤垃圾

Pick one piece of trash you produced today (wrapper, bottle, straw). Trace its possible journey: bin → truck → recycle plant or landfill → river → ocean? Draw the map.

選你今天產生的一件垃圾（包裝、瓶子、吸管）。畫出可能的旅程：垃圾桶 → 卡車 → 回收場/掩埋場 → 河 → 海？畫成地圖。

Discussion prompts 討論題

  • Taiwan is an island surrounded by sea. What sea creatures live near your coast?
  • Trace a piece of trash from your home — could it reach the ocean?
  • Design a poster to tell people not to litter at the beach.
