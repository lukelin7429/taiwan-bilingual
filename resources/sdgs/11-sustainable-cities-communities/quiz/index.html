Question 1

What is a 'community'?

什麼是「社區」？

Community = people sharing a place.

社區=共同生活的人。

Question 2

A 'sustainable city' is …

永續城市是……

Sustainable = lasts for the future.

永續=能長久延續。

Question 3

Parks are useful because they …

公園有用是因為……

Parks give nature + community space.

公園給大家綠地與聚會空間。

Question 4

Safer roads mean …

道路更安全代表……

Fewer crashes = safer for all.

更少車禍=大家更安全。

Question 5

Goal 11 is …

目標 11 是永續城鄉。
