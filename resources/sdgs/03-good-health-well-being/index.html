SDG 03 · People · 人類

Good Health & Well-being

健康與福祉

In one sentence 一句話

Stay healthy in body and mind — at every age.

在每個年齡都能身心健康。

Vocabulary 單字

English中文Definition · 釋義
health健康the good condition of body and mind.
身體與心理的良好狀態。
exercise運動physical activity that keeps you fit.
保持體能的身體活動。
sleep睡眠rest at night when your eyes close.
晚上閉眼休息。
doctor醫生a person trained to heal sick people.
受過訓練可以治療病人的人。

Classroom Activity 課堂活動

Weekly habit chart

每週習慣表

Draw a chart with 7 days × 3 habits: (1) sleep before 10 pm, (2) 20 minutes exercise, (3) eat fruit. Mark a check each day you did it. After a week, share what got easier.

畫一張 7 天 × 3 個習慣的表：(1) 晚上 10 點前睡、(2) 運動 20 分鐘、(3) 吃水果。做到就打勾。一週後分享哪一項變容易了。

Discussion prompts 討論題

  • What 3 habits keep you healthy at school?
  • How does your school help students who feel sad or stressed?
  • Plan a 10-minute daily exercise routine you can do at home.
