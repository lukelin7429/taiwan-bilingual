SDG 15 · Planet · 地球

Life on Land

陸地生態

In one sentence 一句話

Protect forests, fields, and the animals that share our home.

保護森林、田野與我們的動物鄰居。

Vocabulary 單字

English中文Definition · 釋義
forest森林many trees together.
許多樹聚在一起。
species物種a kind of animal or plant.
一種動物或植物。
habitat棲地where an animal lives.
動物生活的地方。
protect保護to keep safe.
保持安全。

Classroom Activity 課堂活動

Schoolyard bio-blitz

校園生態大調查

Walk the school yard for 20 minutes. List every living thing you see — trees, birds, insects, plants. Count species. Repeat in another season and compare.

在校園裡走 20 分鐘。記錄你看到的所有生物——樹、鳥、昆蟲、植物。算物種數。換個季節再做一次比較。

Discussion prompts 討論題

  • Name 5 trees or birds you can see in your school yard.
  • What's one animal that used to live near your county but is now rare?
  • Plant a seed at home and write a journal about how it grows.
