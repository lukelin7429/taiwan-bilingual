Thoughts by Grandfather

45 short spoken-word reflections on life, character and meaning — three to six minutes each. Good advanced listening practice, and a quiet pause for reflection.

45支生活省思短片,每支約三到六分鐘——適合作為進階英語聽力練習,也適合靜下心來反思。

Written and read by Leon La Couvée, a Taiwan-based English teacher and writer whose work explores freedom, character and what it takes to build a life worth living. Several reflections have a second, more cinematic reading — pick whichever version you'd like to hear.

由旅台英文老師暨作家 Leon La Couvée 撰寫並親自朗讀,探討自由、品格,以及如何活出值得的人生。部分主題附有第二支「電影版」朗讀,可自由選擇聆聽哪一版。 ｜ More of his writing ↗
