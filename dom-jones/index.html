Advocacy Partner · Dom Jones

From Compton to Taiwan.

Dom Jones — internationally recognized social advocate, educator and media personality from California — has chosen Taiwan as her home. Through My Culture Connect she brings a nationwide school assembly tour to Taiwan's campuses, built around three pillars: English education, global connections, and kindness.

Dom Jones(多姆・瓊斯)是來自美國加州的國際社會運動者、教育家與媒體人,目前長期定居台灣。她與人師教育協會合作,在全台校園展開巡迴學校集會——以英語教育、全球連結、善意與愛為三大主軸。

Born in the challenging streets of Compton to a mother of thirteen, Dom grew up in poverty and homelessness — and refused to be defined by it. She pursued higher education across the Midwest and Southern California in the humanities, communication studies, and women's studies.

With nearly 20 years in fitness, yoga and wellness advocacy, she is also the owner and CEO of Propel Cycle, Southern California's premier indoor cycling studio, and hosts The Dom Jones Show on Pho Bolsa TV, reaching millions across the US and Vietnam.

In 2022, Dom appeared on CBS's The Amazing Race (Season 34), sharing her message of empathy and cross-cultural understanding. As Government Affairs Ambassador for the United Nations Association — Orange County Chapter, she has dedicated her career to advancing the UN 2030 Agenda.

"Growing up without the love, safety and guidance every child deserves, Dom made a vow to become the love she did not receive. Her life mission is to give kindness, unity and hope to the world — especially to young people who need to know their voice and dreams matter." — Dom Jones

出生於美國加州康普頓街頭,是十三個孩子的母親撫養長大的孩子。Dom 在貧困與無家可歸的環境中成長,卻憑藉對教育的堅定信念,在美國中西部與南加州修讀了人文學、傳播學與女性研究。

近 20 年的健身、瑜伽與身心靈倡導經驗,使她深刻理解健康對每個人的意義。她同時是南加州頂級室內單車工作室 Propel Cycle 的執行長,並在 Pho Bolsa TV 主持《The Dom Jones Show》,跨國傳播至全球數百萬觀眾。

2022 年,她登上 CBS《The Amazing Race》第 34 季,向全球觀眾展現跨文化的理解與同理心。她也是聯合國協會橙縣分會的政府事務大使,長期推廣聯合國 2030 永續發展目標。

Our Mission校園巡迴的三大主軸

🗣️

English Education

英語教育

Real, lived English in every classroom. Dom speaks to students as a working American media personality — every word is something she'd actually say in California tomorrow, not textbook English.

把真實生活中的英語帶進每一堂課。Dom 以正在做媒體工作的美國人身份對學生說話——每一句都是她明天在加州會用到的英文,不是課本英文。

🌐

Global Connections

全球連結

From Compton to the UN to Taiwan. Dom shows students their campus is connected to Los Angeles, to Hanoi, to the UN — and that their voices matter on every one of those stages.

從康普頓、聯合國,到台灣。Dom 讓學生看見:他們的校園與洛杉磯、河內、聯合國是相連的——而他們的聲音,在每一個舞台上都很重要。

💛

Kindness

善意與愛

"Be the love you did not receive." Dom leads every assembly with empathy and unity, and the belief that every student's dreams matter — especially those who are told they don't.

「成為你不曾擁有的那份愛。」Dom 以同理心與團結帶領每一場集會,相信每一個孩子的夢想都很重要——尤其是那些被告知不重要的孩子。
