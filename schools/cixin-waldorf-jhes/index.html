About · 學校簡介

Not a typical public school — Taiwan's Waldorf pioneer.

Ci-Xin Waldorf School is not a conventional public school. It is Taiwan's first and best-known Waldorf (Steiner-inspired) school, born from founder Zhang Chun-shu's encounter with Waldorf education in Stuttgart, Germany, in 1990. What began in 1996 as early-childhood Waldorf education grew into a formal elementary division by 2000, and in August 2002 it became an officially registered public-private-partnership school, sharing a campus with Dongshan Elementary School's Xiangnan branch. The school extended to a full K-9 program in 2005, and by 2015 it had built out a complete 12-year continuous Waldorf curriculum — the first of its kind in Taiwan — with a secondary high-school campus in nearby Su'ao. This page covers the K-9 compulsory-education division, known in Chinese as "國中小."

慈心華德福並非一般公立學校，而是全台灣第一所、也是最具知名度的華德福（史代納）教育學校。其源起可追溯至創辦人張純淑女士於民國79年（1990年）在德國斯圖加特接觸華德福教育的經歷。學校自民國85年（1996年）以幼兒階段的華德福教育起步，至民國89年（2000年）發展出正式的小學部，並於民國91年（2002年）8月正式立案為公私協力學校，與冬山國小向南分校共用校地。民國94年（2005年）擴展為完整的國中小九年一貫課程，至民國104年（2015年）進一步建立全台第一套完整的十二年一貫華德福課程，並在鄰近的蘇澳設有高中部校區。本頁介紹的是義務教育階段的「國中小」部分。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 Taiwan's Waldorf Pioneer

全台華德福教育先驅

Ci-Xin was the first school in Taiwan to bring Waldorf (Steiner-inspired) education into the public system, growing from founder Zhang Chun-shu's 1990 encounter with the approach in Germany into a full public-private-partnership school by 2002.

📚 A Complete 12-Year Curriculum

全台首創十二年一貫課程

By 2015, the school had built the first continuous 12-year Waldorf curriculum in Taiwan, extending from kindergarten through senior high — a scale unmatched by any other Waldorf-inspired school in the country.

🎨 An Alternative to the Mainstream

實驗教育非傳統體制

As a public-private-partnership experimental school, Ci-Xin operates outside the standard public-school model, drawing families specifically seeking Waldorf pedagogy's emphasis on the arts, rhythm, and child development stages.

🌾 Grown From the Lanyang Plain

扎根蘭陽平原

Founded on the campus of Dongshan Elementary School, the school is rooted in the Lanyang Plain and has made Dongshan Township nationally known as a pioneering center for alternative and experimental education in Taiwan.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A national reputation grown from a local campus.

🌾

Founded on the campus of Dongshan Elementary School and rooted in the Lanyang Plain, Ci-Xin Waldorf School has given Dongshan Township a reputation across Taiwan as a pioneering center for alternative and experimental education — a distinctive counterpart to the township's more traditional public schools.
