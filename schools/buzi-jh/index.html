About · 學校簡介

A new school with an award-winning start.

Buzi Junior High School is a brand-new public school opening for the 115 school year (fall 2026) in Beitun District. Its first-phase campus construction won a 2024 National Excellence in Construction Award, marking the new school out even before it has opened its doors. The school takes its name from Buzi (廍子), and shares that name with the neighboring, well-established Buzi Elementary School.

廍子國中是位於北屯區的全新公立學校，預計於115學年度（2026年秋季）開學。其第一期校舍工程於民國113年（2024年）榮獲國家卓越建設獎，尚未正式開學便已備受肯定。校名「廍子」也與鄰近歷史悠久的廍子國小同名。

🍬 A Name From Taiwan's Sugarcane Past

地名源自製糖產業

The school's name, Buzi (廍), refers to old sugar-processing huts, tying it to Beitun's historic sugarcane-farming heritage.
