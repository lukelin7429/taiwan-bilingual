About · 學校簡介

Born inside a temple in the old salt flats.

Rixin Elementary School was founded in April 1912 as the "Anping Public School, Yancheng Branch," with its first classrooms set up inside the Yancheng Xuantian Shangdi Temple. It became independent in 1918 as "Tainan City Yancheng Public School," and was renamed Rixin Elementary in 1968. One of the oldest elementary schools in South District, the school celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2018 — the same year it began a full campus rebuild. Its original 1917 school building had been completed on the temple site before the school achieved independence the following year.

日新國小創立於1912年（明治45年）4月，初名「臺南市安平公學校鹽埕分校」，最初的教室設於鹽埕玄天上帝廟內。1918年（大正7年）獨立設校，定名「臺南市鹽埕公學校」，1968年（民國57年）更名日新國小。作為南區歷史最悠久的學校之一，學校於2018年（民國107年）舉辦建校百週年紀念，同年也展開全面校舍重建工程。1917年完工的原始校舍即建於廟埕之上，隔年學校便正式獨立。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛕 Born Inside a Temple

創立於玄天上帝廟內

Rixin's earliest classrooms were set up inside the Yancheng Xuantian Shangdi Temple in 1912, a direct link between the school and the neighborhood's traditional faith community.

💯 A Century Old in 2018

2018年建校百週年

The school marked its 100th anniversary in 2018, making it one of the two oldest elementary schools in South District.

🏗️ A Full Campus Rebuild

百週年同年展開全面重建

Rixin began a complete campus rebuild the same year it celebrated its centennial, modernizing its facilities while carrying forward over a century of history.

🧂 Rooted in the Old Salt Flats

鹽埕聚落的百年見證

Its founding inside Yancheng (鹽埕, "salt flat") ties the school to the old salt-flat, harbor-adjacent neighborhood that once defined this part of South District.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in the old salt-flat harbor district.

🧂

Rixin's founding inside Yancheng's Xuantian Shangdi Temple links it directly to the old salt-flat and harbor-adjacent Yancheng (鹽埕) neighborhood — part of the historic port-area geography that shaped South District long before the district's postwar housing developments arrived.
