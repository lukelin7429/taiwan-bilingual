About · 學校簡介

A century of history, told through a traditional house.

Wuling Elementary was founded in 1922 under Japanese colonial rule, originally as an educational institute for Aboriginal children — making it, alongside Haiduan Elementary, one of the two oldest schools serving Bunun Indigenous areas of Taitung County. The school has been renamed multiple times over the past century, settling on its current name in 1968. Today it maintains a dedicated Bunun Cultural Resource Center, and — notably — has built a traditional Bunun house on campus that is used for actual tribal ceremonies, not merely displayed as a museum piece.

武陵國小創立於1922年日治時期，最初為「蕃童教育所」，與海端國小並列台東縣布農族部落地區歷史最悠久的兩所學校。百年來歷經多次更名，1968年確立現今校名。學校目前設有專屬的布農文化資源中心，並在校園內建有一棟傳統布農家屋，供部落實際舉行祭儀使用，而非僅作為靜態展示。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏠 A Traditional House Used for Real Ceremonies

供實際祭儀使用的傳統家屋

The school built a traditional Bunun house on its campus that is genuinely used by the tribal community for ceremonies — a rare case of school infrastructure doubling as living cultural space.

📜 One of the Two Oldest Bunun-Area Schools

布農部落地區最早的兩所學校之一

Founded in 1922, Wuling shares the distinction — with Haiduan Elementary — of being one of the two oldest schools in Taitung County's Bunun Indigenous areas, having witnessed a full century of change.

📚 A Dedicated Bunun Cultural Resource Center

布農文化資源中心

The school's Cultural Resource Center actively documents and teaches Bunun tradition, giving students a structured way to engage with their own heritage inside the school day.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Yanping Township and the Bunun people.

🏔️

Wuling Elementary actively preserves Bunun tradition through its Cultural Resource Center and its on-campus traditional house, used for genuine tribal ceremonies — a concrete, living link between the school and the Indigenous mountain community of Yanping Township that surrounds it.
