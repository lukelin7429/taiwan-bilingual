Shanmei Elementary School

山美國民小學

A Tsou indigenous school in Shanmei village, home to nationally recognized Tsou folk singers and a landmark story of ecological recovery.

座落於鄒族山美（Saviki）部落，孕育全國知名鄒族古謠隊、也是生態復育傳奇之地的小學。

About · 學校簡介

Nearly a century in the Tsou village of Shanmei.

Shanmei Elementary was founded on February 23, 1929, during the Japanese colonial era — a founding date confirmed by the school's own 97th-anniversary publication. The school serves the Tsou (鄒族) village of Shanmei (also known by its Tsou name, Saviki), and places strong emphasis on Tsou indigenous-language education, including proficiency certification and vocabulary competitions. Its Tsou folk-song performance group has achieved national-level recognition, echoing the village's own renown for cultural tourism and ecological conservation.

山美國小創立於昭和4年（民國18年，1929年）2月23日日治時期，此創校年份由學校97週年紀念刊物確認。學校服務鄒族山美部落（鄒語名 Saviki），高度重視鄒語教育，包括語言能力認證與詞彙競賽。校內鄒族古謠隊表演團體已獲全國性肯定，呼應山美部落在生態保育與文化觀光方面的盛名。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎶 A National Award-Winning Tsou Choir

鄒族古謠表演團體

Shanmei's Tsou folk-song performance group has achieved national-level recognition, preserving traditional Tsou music through student performance.

鄒語認證與詞彙競賽

Students take part in indigenous-language proficiency certification and vocabulary competitions as part of the school's strong Tsou-language program.

🐟 A Village Known for Ecological Recovery

達娜伊谷生態復育

Shanmei village is nationally known for its post-disaster ecological recovery at the Tanayiku Nature Ecology Park, a source of local pride that the school's community shares in.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Shanmei, a Tsou village of rivers and recovery.

🐟

Shanmei village (also called Saviki) is renowned across Taiwan for its indigenous-led ecological conservation — most famously the fish conservation efforts along the Danayigu stream, now the Tanayiku Nature Ecology Park — following recovery from the 1999 earthquake and subsequent typhoons. It is also a major hub of Tsou cultural tourism. See the Chiayi county page for more on the region.
