About · 學校簡介

A neighbor to Nuannuan's oldest school.

Nuanjiang Elementary is located in Nuannuan District, directly adjacent to Nuannuan Elementary, one of Keelung's three century-old schools. Alongside its regular elementary classes, Nuanjiang runs an affiliated kindergarten, giving local families in this historic river-district neighborhood a continuous early-education option close to home.

暖江國小座落於基隆市暖暖區，緊鄰基隆市三所百年老校之一的暖暖國小。除國小部課程外，暖江國小也附設幼兒園，讓這個歷史悠久的河運地區居民能就近享有完整的學前及國小教育。
