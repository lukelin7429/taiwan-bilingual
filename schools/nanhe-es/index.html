About · 學校簡介

Half a century, and still growing wings.

Nanhe Elementary was founded in 1970 in a rural corner of Guanxi Township. The school marked its 50th anniversary with the opening of a new running track, an occasion attended by the county magistrate. Its most distinctive feature is a "campus butterfly ecosystem network" that weaves butterfly conservation directly into environmental education, alongside programs in arts enrichment, disaster prevention, and Hakka-language instruction.

南和國小創立於1970年，座落於關西鎮的鄉村地帶。學校於50週年校慶時啟用新跑道，並邀請縣長親臨剪綵。學校最具特色之處在於打造「校園蝴蝶生態網絡」，將蝴蝶保育直接融入環境教育，並開設藝術扎根、防災教育與客語教學等課程。
