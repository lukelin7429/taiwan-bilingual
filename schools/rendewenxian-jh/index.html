About · 學校簡介

A branch school born to reach Rende's harder-to-access villages.

Rende Wenxian Junior High School was formally established in August 1981 as “Tainan County Wenxian Junior High School.” It originated as the “Wenxian Branch of Rende Junior High School,” established in August 1976 on the west side of Bao'an Village, Rende Township, specifically to address poor internal transportation access for students in the district's interior. The school was renamed Tainan Municipal Rende Wenxian Junior High School after the 2010 Tainan county-city merger. Its first principal, Xu Qinzhang, built classrooms and improved facilities from 1981 until his death in office in 1984; the ninth principal, Chen Li-ru (appointed August 2019), introduced campus programs emphasizing virtue, gratitude, and practical life skills. The school's mission emphasizes character education and “balanced five-fold development” alongside community engagement.

仁德文賢國中正式設校於民國70年（1981年）8月，初名「台南縣立文賢國民中學」。前身為民國65年（1976年）8月設立的「台南縣立仁德國民中學文賢分部」，校址位於仁德鄉保安村西側，設立目的是為解決區內內部交通不便地區學生的就學問題。2010年台南縣市合併後改名為台南市立仁德文賢國中。首任校長許欽璋自1981年起興建教室、改善設施，直至1984年病逝於任內；第九任校長陳麗如（2019年8月上任）推動品德、感恩與生活實踐相關校園活動。學校辦學理念強調品格教育與五育均衡發展，並重視社區參與。
