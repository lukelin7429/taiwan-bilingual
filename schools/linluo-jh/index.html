About · 學校簡介

Built on land the sugar factory gave away.

Linluo Junior High School was founded in 1967 on 3.2 "jia" (甲) of state land donated by the Pingtung Sugar Factory for the school's construction. Today it enrolls around 336 students and staff across 13 classes, with about 40 staff members. The school fields a youth baseball team documented on the Taiwan Baseball Wiki and features a gymnasium that serves as a local landmark.

麟洛國中創校於民國56年（1967年），校地為屏東糖廠捐贈的3.2甲公有土地。現今全校約336名師生，共13班，教職員約40人。學校設有青少棒隊，戰績記錄於台灣棒球維基館，校內體育館更是在地知名地標。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where the turtles brought a kirin's luck.

🐢

Linluo Township is a small Hakka township that is part of the Liudui (六堆) cultural region's "front camp", where Hakka residents make up roughly 87% of the population. The township's name derives from a Qing-era legend of giant turtles found while digging irrigation channels — taken as an auspicious sign that "where there are turtles, there must be a kirin" (麟). See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
