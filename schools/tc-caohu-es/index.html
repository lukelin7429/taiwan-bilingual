About · 學校簡介

A school that began in a tobacco shed.

Caohu Elementary began in 1952 as a branch of Tucheng Elementary School and became independent in 1955. In its earliest days, with the school building not yet finished, classes were held in a borrowed tobacco-curing shed — a direct trace of Dali's mid-20th-century tobacco-farming economy before the area urbanized.

草湖國小於民國41年（1952年）以塗城國小分校形式創立，民國44年（1955年）獨立設校。創校初期因校舍尚未完工，曾借用一座菸樓作為臨時教室上課，直接見證了大里都市化之前、以菸葉種植為主的中期經濟型態。
