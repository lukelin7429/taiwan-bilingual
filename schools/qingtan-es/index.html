About · 學校簡介

From satellite classroom to independent school.

Qingtan Elementary opened in 1956 as a branch of Xindian Elementary, beginning as a satellite classroom before growing into an independent school. It takes its name from and sits near Qingtan (青潭), a scenic reservoir and dam area along the Xindian River, on the historic road toward Yilan.

青潭國小於民國45年（1956年）設立為新店國小分班，最初以衛星教室形式運作，其後獨立設校。校名源自並鄰近青潭——這是新店溪畔一處風景優美的水庫與攔河堰地區，位於通往宜蘭的歷史道路上。

Qingtan Elementary began in 1956 as a branch of Xindian Elementary, growing over time into a fully independent school.

🏞️ Named for a Scenic Reservoir

青潭水庫地景

🛣️ Along the Road Toward Yilan

Like several other schools in this part of Xindian, Qingtan sits on the historic route connecting the Taipei basin to Yilan.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A landscape of water and mountains.

💦

Qingtan Elementary takes its name from the scenic reservoir and dam area beside it, part of a string of water-and-mountain landscapes along the Xindian River corridor leading toward Yilan. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
