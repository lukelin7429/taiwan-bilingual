Kuihui Elementary School

奎輝國小

A riverside school on the Roma Highway whose Atayal-language motto, “Lokah ta kwara!”, means “Let's all be strong together.”

座落羅馬公路畔的溪岸學校，泰雅語校訓「Lokah ta kwara!」意為「讓我們一起堅強！」。

About · 學校簡介

Four settlements, one river, one small school.

Kuihui Elementary was founded on August 1, 1960. It sits at the 51.8 km marker of the Roma Highway (羅馬公路, Provincial Highway 118), right on the banks of the Dahan River, in a spot described as having especially scenic river views. Its catchment includes four Atayal settlements: Ksuno (嘎色鬧) and Upper, Middle, and Lower Kuihui. The school is small — about 6 classes, 64 students, and 32 staff — and its greeting and tagline, “Lokah ta kwara!”, is Atayal for roughly “Let's all be strong together!”

奎輝國小創立於民國49年（1960年）8月1日，位於羅馬公路（省道118線）51.8公里處，緊鄰大漢溪畔，以優美溪景聞名。學區涵蓋四個泰雅族部落聚落：嘎色鬧、奎輝上、中、下部落。學校規模不大，約6班、64名學生、32名教職員，校園問候語與標語「Lokah ta kwara!」為泰雅語，意為「讓我們一起堅強！」。
