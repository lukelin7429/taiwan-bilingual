Rixin Junior High School

日新國民中學

A public junior high school named for the classical ideal of daily renewal, built in a woodworking town near Taichung.

About · 學校簡介

Named for a classical ideal of renewal.

Rixin Junior High School was approved for construction on December 19, 1969, and broke ground on April 27, 1970, becoming the third junior high school built in Caotun Township. Its founding was championed by then-county-magistrate Lin Yang-kang, who played a key role in expanding junior-high education across Nantou County. The school's name, 日新 ("Daily Renewal"), is drawn directly from the Book of Rites, one of the Chinese classics, reflecting an educational philosophy of continual self-improvement.

日新國中於民國58年（1969年）12月19日獲准興建，並於民國59年（1970年）4月27日動土開工，是草屯鎮內第三所設立的國中。建校由時任南投縣長林洋港積極推動，對南投縣國中教育的普及貢獻良多。校名「日新」典出儒家經典《禮記》，取其「日新又新」的自我精進精神為辦學理念。

🏗️ Built at the County's Initiative

縣長推動建校

Approved in late 1969 and broken ground in April 1970, Rixin was the third junior high built in Caotun, championed by then-county-magistrate Lin Yang-kang as part of a broader push to expand secondary education across Nantou.

📖 A Name Drawn From the Classics

校名典出《禮記》

The school takes its name, 日新 ("Daily Renewal"), from the Book of Rites — a Confucian classic — expressing an educational philosophy centered on continual self-improvement and renewal.

🪑 Growing Up in a Woodworking Town

扎根木工家具之鄉

Students at Rixin grow up amid generations of local carpentry workshops and the timber trade that shaped Caotun's identity as one of Taiwan's best-known furniture-making centers.
