Fulin Elementary School

臺北市士林區福林國民小學

Founded in 1960 near the historic core of Shilin, known for its lush, tree-filled campus and more than 100 species of flowering plants.

About · 學校簡介

A green campus in the heart of Shilin.

Fulin Elementary School was founded by the Yangmingshan Management Bureau in 1960 in response to local demand, with 12 classrooms built. It opened for classes on September 1, 1961, under first principal Li Yuanhua, initially named "Xiangxian National School" (象賢國民學校). The founding name "Xiangxian" was later changed to "Fulin," tied to its location on Fuzhi Road. The school opened with students in grades one through four only, spanning 15 classes, and gradually expanded. Today the campus is known for its extensive mature tree cover and more than 100 species of flowering plants, used as a natural ecology-learning environment for students.

福林國小由陽明山管理局因應地方需求，於民國49年（1960年）創立，興建12間教室，於民國50年（1961年）9月1日正式開學，首任校長為李元華，初名「象賢國民學校」。創校名「象賢」後因位於福志路而改稱「福林」。創校之初僅招收一至四年級學生，共15班，此後逐步擴增。如今校園以茂密老樹及逾百種開花植物聞名，作為學生自然生態學習的場域。
