About · 學校簡介

A river-town school that became a branch campus.

Hean Elementary was founded in September 1952 as the "Shuilin National Elementary School Xixi Branch," serving families along the Beigang River in western Shuilin Township. In 1968 it was renamed Hean — literally "river safety" — a name reflecting its location beside the river's flood-prevention embankment. As of August 2016, the school operates as a branch campus of Shuicanlin Elementary School, continuing to serve local children under Shuicanlin's administration.

和安國小創立於民國41年（1952年）9月，最初為「水林國小西溪分班」，服務北港溪西側水林鄉西側的居民。民國57年（1968年）更名為「和安」，取「河流安瀾」之意，呼應學校緊鄰堤防道路的地理位置。自民國105年（2016年）8月起，學校成為水燦林國小和安分校，由水燦林國小統籌行政運作，持續服務地方學童。

Since August 2016, Hean has operated as a branch campus of nearby Shuicanlin Elementary School, part of a broader trend of small rural schools consolidating administration.

🌱 Rooted in a 1952 Branch Classroom

源自1952年分班

The school's history stretches back to 1952, when it began as a branch classroom of Shuilin National Elementary School serving the Xixi (western creek) area.

🍠 Farming Roots Along the River

河畔農田的孩子

Students here grow up in a farming community shaped by both the Beigang River and Shuilin Township's celebrated sweet potato cultivation.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Living with the river, farming the land.

Positioned along the flood-control embankment of the Beigang River, the school's very name ("river safety") reflects Shuilin Township's long relationship with managing water for its farmland, including its famous sweet potato fields — a history now carried on as a branch of Shuicanlin Elementary School.
