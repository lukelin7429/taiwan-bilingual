About · 學校簡介

From a branch school to a rural-education pioneer.

Emei Junior High opened in September 1956 as "Beipu Junior High School's Emei Branch," becoming fully independent on February 17, 1967, and taking its current name in 1968. In 2020 the school entered a new chapter, converting into a KIST (KIPP-inspired charter-style) public-private partnership school — the first of its kind in the Hsinchu/Miaoli area, formed through a three-way collaboration between the county government, the Chengzhi Education Foundation, and TSMC aimed at revitalizing rural education. Now branded "A School for Future Global Talents," it emphasizes bilingual education alongside a strong agriculture and tea-culture curriculum built in partnership with local farms and tea factories.

峨眉國中創立於1956年9月，最初為「北埔國中峨眉分校」，1967年2月17日正式獨立設校，1968年更為現名。2020年學校邁入嶄新篇章，轉型為KIST（公辦民營特許實驗學校）——這是竹苗地區首例，由縣政府、誠致教育基金會與台積電三方合作，致力於復興偏鄉教育。如今學校以「未來全球人才搖籃」為定位，強調雙語教育，並與在地農場及茶廠合作，發展扎實的農業與茶文化課程。
