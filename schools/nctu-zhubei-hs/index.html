About · 學校簡介

From provincial high school to a university-affiliated campus.

Founded in 1992 as "Taiwan Provincial Chu-pei Senior High School," the school became a national school in 2000 and, in August 2025, was formally transferred into the affiliation of National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU). As Zhubei's only national senior high school, it anchors the city's education pipeline, offering both academic and vocational tracks — general studies, information technology, business management, dance, athletics, and retail services. In 2007, the school built a swimming facility and history museum in memory of a student who died saving a child, and in 2021 it launched a bilingual experimental program reported to be the first of its kind among national senior highs in the Hsinchu/Miaoli region.

學校創立於1992年，原名「台灣省立竹北高級中學」，2000年改制為國立高中，並於2025年8月正式改隸國立陽明交通大學，成為其附屬學校。作為竹北市唯一的國立高中，本校長期扮演在地教育體系的核心角色，設有普通科與資訊科、商業經營科、舞蹈科、體育班、電子商務科等技職課程。2007年，學校為紀念一位捨身救童的學生興建游泳池與校史館；2021年更開辦雙語實驗班，據報導為新竹苗栗地區國立高中首開先例。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 Now Part of NYCU

改隸陽明交通大學

In August 2025, the school became an affiliated school of National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University — a major step that ties its senior high curriculum more closely to a leading research university.

🗣️ An Early Bilingual Program

雙語實驗班先驅

Launched in 2021, its bilingual experimental program is reported to be the first among national senior highs in the greater Hsinchu/Miaoli region.

🏊 A Pool Built in Memory

紀念性游泳館與校史館

The campus swimming facility and history museum, completed in 2007, honor a student who lost his life saving a child — a story woven into the school's identity.

🛠️ Academic and Vocational Tracks Side by Side

普通與技職並行

Students can choose from general academic studies or vocational programs in IT, business management, dance, athletics, and retail services, all on one campus.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring Zhubei's education pipeline.

🚄

As Zhubei's only national senior high school, this campus anchors the city's education pipeline just as Zhubei itself has grown into Hsinchu County's administrative and tech-commuter hub, drawing families relocating for jobs at nearby Hsinchu Science Park and around the High Speed Rail station.
