About · 學校簡介

A century of history, and a quiet ending.

Zhuwan Elementary's roots trace back to 1920, when it began as the "Zhugaowan" branch classroom under Japanese rule, and it took its current name, "Zhuwan National Elementary School," in 1968 — over a century of continuous history. Over the decades, it set up its own branches in turn, including Ximen (1951) and Heheng (1962, later independent as Heheng Elementary). But like many of Penghu's smallest outer-village schools, Zhuwan faced a steadily shrinking student population. Its final graduating class, in 2024, numbered just one student. At the end of that academic year, the school closed as an independent institution due to Taiwan's declining birthrate, and was merged into neighboring Chidong Elementary School as a branch campus.

竹灣國小校史可追溯至民國9年（1920年）日治時期設立的「竹篙灣」分教場，民國57年（1968年）定名為「竹灣國民小學」，走過百餘年歲月。歷年來曾陸續設立西門（1951年）、合橫（1962年，後獨立為合橫國小）等分校。然而，如同澎湖許多偏遠小校，竹灣國小學生人數逐年遞減，最後一屆畢業生（2024年）僅有1人。該學年度結束後，因少子化因素，竹灣國小走入歷史，正式併入鄰近的池東國小，成為其分校。
