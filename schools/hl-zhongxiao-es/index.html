Zhongxiao Elementary School

忠孝國小

About · 學校簡介

A small school in the heart of downtown.

Zhongxiao Elementary traces its roots to around 1961, when it began as a branch of Zhongzheng Elementary known as the "Senshi Branch" (千石分校), becoming its own school in 1963. Located in downtown Hualien City near the area's commercial district and a concentration of military and civil-servant households, Zhongxiao today runs a compact campus of about 17 classes, roughly 430 students, plus two kindergarten classes — a scale that has kept it close-knit even as the surrounding city has grown.

忠孝國小的歷史可追溯至約1961年，最初以中正國小「千石分校」的名義成立，於民國52年（1963年）獨立設校。校址位於花蓮市區，鄰近商業區與軍公教聚居地帶。目前全校約17班、學生約430人，另設2班幼兒園——規模雖小，卻在城市持續發展中維持著緊密的社區氛圍。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Branch That Became a School

千石分校獨立設校

Zhongxiao started as Zhongzheng Elementary's "Senshi Branch" around 1961 before becoming an independent school in 1963, part of a wave of postwar school splits in Hualien City.

🏙️ A Downtown, Close-Knit Campus

市中心的緊密校園

With around 17 classes and roughly 430 students plus two kindergarten classes, Zhongxiao has stayed a smaller, close-knit school even in the middle of Hualien City.

🏛️ Near the Civic and Commercial Core

鄰近商業與軍公教聚居區

The school's location near Hualien City's commercial district and its concentration of military and civil-servant families shapes much of its student community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Hualien's civic core.

🏙️

Zhongxiao sits within Hualien City's downtown core, where military, civil-servant and commercial life intersect — a snapshot of the county seat's postwar urban character. See the Hualien County schools page for more schools in the area.
