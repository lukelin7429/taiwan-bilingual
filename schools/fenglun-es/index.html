Fenglun Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A patient rise from branch campus to full school.

Fenglun Elementary opened in 1956 as a branch campus of Hexing Elementary School, becoming independent in 1962. Its early years were modest — for 25 years, until 1980, the school made do without a proper sports ground because of its small, poorly-equipped original site. Today the school has grown into a well-rounded campus with a dedicated school history room, an AV classroom, a nature classroom, and a computer lab, and its students have recently earned recognition in a county comics competition and the National Science Exhibition.

豐崙國小創校於民國45年（1956年），最初為和興國小的分校，於民國51年（1962年）獨立設校。建校初期條件簡樸——由於原始校地狹小、設備不足，學校在長達25年間（直到1980年）都沒有正式的運動場。如今學校已發展為設備完善的校園，設有專屬校史室、視聽教室、自然教室與電腦教室，學生近年也在縣級漫畫比賽與第66屆全國科展中獲得肯定。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Gradual growth in rural Pitou Township.

🌾

Fenglun Elementary grew from a small branch school with limited land into a full elementary school through decades of gradual expansion — a story that reflects the modest rural resources of Pitou Township 埤頭鄉 in the mid-20th century. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
