About · 學校簡介

The township's namesake elementary school.

Baoshan Elementary is the namesake school of Baoshan Township, likely tracing its origins to the Japanese colonial era in keeping with the founding pattern of other schools in the region, though precise founding records are not confirmed. The school relocated in 1953; its original site is now occupied by Baoshan Junior High's Jiguang Branch. Today, Baoshan Elementary also operates a branch campus, the Shanhu Branch (山湖分校), serving families near the Baoshan Reservoir.

寶山國小是寶山鄉的同名小學，創校時間雖未能確認，但依區域內其他學校的建校模式推斷，極可能源於日治時期。學校於民國42年（1953年）遷校，原址現為寶山國中濟光分校所在地。目前寶山國小亦設有山湖分校，服務寶山水庫周邊的家庭。

🏫 A Legacy Campus Site

原址現為濟光分校

Baoshan Elementary's original site, before a 1953 relocation, is now occupied by Baoshan Junior High's Jiguang Branch — a quiet link between the township's elementary and secondary education history.

🏞️ A Branch Campus by the Reservoir

山湖分校

The school operates the Shanhu Branch campus near the scenic Baoshan Reservoir, extending its reach to families in that part of the township.

🍵 The Township's Namesake School
