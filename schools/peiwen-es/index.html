About · 學校簡介

A school renamed four times, remembered for a pickle barrel.

Peiwen Elementary School's documented history begins in 1940, when it relocated to its current site as "Madou West Public School" (麻豆西公學校); an earlier, independent founding date has not been confirmed in available records. The school was renamed several times afterward: Madou West National School (1941), Madou No. 2 National School (1946), Madou Township Peiwen National School (1947), and finally Peiwen Elementary School in 1968. In 2020, Peiwen built a widely photographed "giant pickled-vegetable barrel" (大鹹菜桶) themed playground commemorating Madou's historic role as a center of Taiwanese pickled-mustard (鹹菜) production, complete with a terrazzo slide shaped like a barrel, a climbing wall, and a rope net, plus an on-campus "pickled-vegetable culture zone" featuring real oversized wooden barrels.

培文國小的可考校史始於民國29年（1940年），當時遷至現址並定名「麻豆西公學校」；更早的獨立創校年份於現有資料中尚未證實。此後歷經多次更名：麻豆西國民學校（1941年）、麻豆第二國民學校（1946年）、麻豆鄉培文國民學校（1947年），最終於民國57年（1968年）定名為培文國小。2020年，培文國小打造了廣受矚目的「大鹹菜桶」主題遊戲場，紀念麻豆作為台灣鹹菜醃製重鎮的歷史，設有仿鹹菜桶造型磨石子溜滑梯、攀爬牆與繩網，並附設「鹹菜文化區」，展示真實的大型木製醃缸。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Madou's other food heritage.

🥬

Peiwen's signature playground directly commemorates Madou's lesser-known identity — alongside its famous pomelos — as a historic hub of pickled-mustard-green (鹹菜) production, a concrete and non-generic tie between the school and its local food heritage.
