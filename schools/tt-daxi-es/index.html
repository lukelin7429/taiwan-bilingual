About · 學校簡介

A small school perched above the Pacific.

Daxi Elementary sits in Duoliang Village along the South-Link Highway, its campus perched on coastal cliffs looking straight out over the Pacific Ocean. It is one of Taitung's smallest schools, with enrollment typically in the range of 35 to 52 students plus a kindergarten class, and it serves a largely Paiwan Indigenous community, many of them from low-income households. Despite its size, the school offers baseball and Indigenous folk-song programs, and it has drawn national attention for milestones as intimate as a graduation ceremony with only a single incoming student.

大溪國小位於南迴公路旁的多良村，校園座落在面向太平洋的海岸懸崖上。這是台東最迷你的學校之一，學生人數通常僅35至52人，另加幼兒園班，服務對象以排灣族原住民及部分弱勢家庭子女為主。儘管規模小，學校仍開設棒球與原住民歌謠課程，甚至曾因僅有一名新生入學的畢業典禮而受到全國關注。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌊 A Campus Above the Ocean

俯瞰太平洋的校園

Daxi's cliffside setting along the South-Link Highway gives students an everyday view of the Pacific Ocean rarely matched by any school in Taiwan.

⚾ Baseball in a Tiny School

棒球與歌謠課程

Even with only a few dozen students, Daxi runs a baseball program alongside Indigenous folk-song instruction, keeping both sport and culture alive on a small campus.

🤝 A Close, Small Community

緊密的小型社區

With enrollment sometimes as low as a handful of students per grade, Daxi represents the intimate scale of education in Taiwan's remote coastal Indigenous villages.
