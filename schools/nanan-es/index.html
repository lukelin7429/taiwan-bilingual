Nan'an Elementary School

南安國小

About · 學校簡介

A landmark view above the harbor.

Nan'an Elementary sits on a hillside overlooking Nanfang'ao Bridge and harbor, with Pacific Ocean views on clear days. The school has been recognized as a Ministry of Education "Featured Excellence School" and "Excellent Ocean Education School," and its modern concrete-and-glass building has become a popular photography spot for visitors drawn to the harbor scenery.

南安國小座落於俯瞰南方澳大橋與漁港的山坡上，晴天時可遠眺太平洋。學校獲教育部評選為「教學卓越獎」及「海洋教育特色學校」，現代化的清水模與玻璃建築也成為遊客造訪港區時熱門的攝影景點。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌊 A National Ocean Education Model

海洋教育特色學校

The school has earned Ministry of Education recognition as an "Excellent Ocean Education School," reflecting a curriculum shaped by its harborside setting.

📸 A Landmark Building

熱門攝影景點

The school's modern concrete-and-glass architecture, framed by Nanfang'ao Bridge and the harbor, has made the campus itself a popular photography destination.

🏆 Teaching Excellence Recognition

教學卓越獎肯定

Nan'an has also been named an MOE "Featured Excellence School," a mark of distinction among Taiwan's public elementary schools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Inseparable from the harbor below.

🌉

Overlooking Nanfang'ao harbor, the school is inseparable from the fishing port views that define Su'ao — it has become a landmark photo spot precisely because of that setting.
