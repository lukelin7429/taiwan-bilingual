About · 學校簡介

A familiar campus under a restored name.

Hukou Junior High School traces its origins to 1953 as a branch of Hsinchu Middle School serving Hukou Township. For decades, it operated as "Zhongzheng Junior High School" (中正國中), sharing a campus and administration with Hukou Senior High as part of a single combined "complete middle school." In August 2025, the two divisions formally split into independent institutions, and the junior high took over the original Zhongzheng Road campus under its current, geographically named identity: Hukou Junior High School. Readers may still encounter references to "Zhongzheng Junior High" in older records — it is the same school. The school runs a bilingual education initiative and has a long record of strong results in the national Junior High Achievement Test.

湖口國民中學創校淵源可追溯至民國42年（1953年）新竹中學湖口分部。長年以來，本校以「中正國中」為名，與湖口高中共用校區與行政體系，同屬一所「完全中學」。民國114年（2025年）8月，兩部正式分立為獨立學校，國中部承接原中正路校區，並以現行地名「湖口國民中學」重新命名。舊資料中可能仍會看到「中正國中」的名稱，兩者實為同一所學校。學校推動雙語教育，並在國中教育會考中長期表現優異。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🪧 A Name Restored in 2025

2025年更名湖口國中

After decades as "Zhongzheng Junior High," the school formally split from its senior-high sibling in August 2025 and adopted its current geographically rooted name.

🗣️ A Bilingual Education Initiative

雙語教育計畫

The school runs a bilingual education initiative, part of a broader push across Hsinchu County schools to strengthen English-language instruction.

📈 Strong Exam Results

會考成績表現優異

The school has built a long-standing reputation for strong performance in the national Junior High Achievement Test (國中教育會考).

🏗️ Decades of Campus Growth

歷任校長擴建校舍

Successive principals have expanded the campus over the decades, adding a gymnasium, tennis courts, and science buildings to serve a growing student body.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Institutional roots near the Old Street.

🏮

The school sits in Hukou Township near the historic Old Street (老街) area, reflecting the town's long institutional continuity dating back to the Japanese-to-ROC transition era. See the Hsinchu County page for more on the region.
