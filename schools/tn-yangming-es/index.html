Yangming Elementary School

A small school on Shanhua's agricultural outskirts, carved out of Shanhua Elementary School in 1963 to serve the district's growing farmland fringe.

About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school on Shanhua's farmland fringe.

Yangming Elementary School was founded on August 1, 1963, as a branch of Shanhua Elementary School. Sitting on an 11,185-square-meter campus, the school serves a predominantly agricultural area on Shanhua's outskirts rather than the built-up town center, and passed its 50th anniversary in the early 2010s.

陽明國小創立於民國52年（1963年）8月1日，最初是善化國小的分校。校地面積約11,185平方公尺，服務範圍以農業地區為主，位於善化市街外圍而非市中心，並已於2010年代初期歡度建校50週年。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Carved from Shanhua's growing countryside.

🌾

Like Datong and Dacheng, Yangming Elementary was carved out of Shanhua Elementary as the district's farmland fringe grew enough population to need its own neighborhood school, part of a wave of new schools built as Shanhua's sugar-town economy expanded outward.
