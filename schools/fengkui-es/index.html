Fengkui Elementary School

風櫃國民小學

A seaside school at the southern tip of Penghu's main island, running a dedicated marine-life education program by the famous "sea roaring cave."

About · 學校簡介

A seaside school built around marine-life education.

Fengkui Elementary began in 1922 as a Japanese-era branch teaching post under Magong's "第二公學校" (now Wude Elementary), changing administrative parent schools several times during the colonial period. It became independent in 1946 as "馬公鎮風櫃國民學校," opening with just 2 classes and 86 students, and took its current name in 1968. Between 2001 and 2002 the school established a "Penghu County Marine Life Education Resource Center," and its buildings were rebuilt around 2006–2007. The school sits at the southern tip of Penghu's main island, right by the sea.

風櫃國小創立於日治大正11年（1922年），最初是馬公「第二公學校」（今五德國小前身）轄下的分教場，日治時期歷經數次改隸。民國35年（1946年）獨立設校，稱「馬公鎮風櫃國民學校」，創校時僅2班86名學生，民國57年（1968年）定名為現名。學校於2001至2002年間成立「澎湖縣海洋生物教育資源中心」，校舍並於2006至2007年間重建。學校座落於澎湖本島最南端，緊鄰大海。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🐠 A Marine Life Education Center

海洋生物教育資源中心

Established in 2001–2002, the "Penghu County Marine Life Education Resource Center" makes Fengkui a hub for ocean-focused learning across the county.

🌊 Home to the "Sea Roaring Cave"

風櫃聽濤

The Fengkui area is famous for its basalt sea cliffs and "sea roaring cave" (風櫃聽濤) blowhole, a dramatic natural feature right on the school's doorstep.

📍 Penghu's Southernmost Tip

澎湖本島最南端

Sitting at the southern tip of Penghu's main island, students grow up with the ocean as a daily backdrop and a living classroom.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where basalt cliffs sing with the sea.

🌋

Fengkui is famous across Penghu for its basalt sea cliffs and the "sea roaring cave" (風櫃聽濤) blowhole, where waves crashing through volcanic rock create a booming, resonant sound — a striking piece of the island's fishing and geological heritage that the school directly builds into its marine-education program.
