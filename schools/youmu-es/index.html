A tiny mountain school beside two major forest recreation areas, known for forest and ecological education.

About · 學校簡介

A tiny school between two forests.

Youmu Elementary was founded in 1965 and relocated to its current site in 1971. The school is nestled near the Manyueyuan and Daban-gen Forest Recreation Areas, two of the deepest mountain destinations accessible from Sanxia, and it has built a reputation for forest and ecological education that draws on its immediate surroundings.

有木國小創立於民國54年（1965年），民國60年（1971年）遷至現址。學校緊鄰滿月圓與大板根兩大森林遊樂區——這是三峽境內可及最深處的山林景點之一，並發展出以周邊環境為本的森林與生態教育特色，聞名於外。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 Forest and Ecological Education

森林生態教育特色

Youmu Elementary draws directly on its deep-mountain setting to deliver forest and ecological education, using the surrounding landscape as an outdoor classroom.

🏕️ Neighboring Two Forest Recreation Areas

緊鄰滿月圓、大板根遊樂區

The school sits beside the Manyueyuan and Daban-gen Forest Recreation Areas, two of the region's best-known ecotourism destinations.

🏫 A 1971 Relocation

1971年遷校現址

Founded in 1965, the school moved to its present site in 1971, where it remains today as a small anchor for its deep-mountain community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Deep in Sanxia's ecotourism country.

🌲

Nestled beside two major forest recreation areas, the school's identity is deeply tied to Sanxia's deep-mountain ecotourism geography, where hiking trails and old-growth trees draw visitors from across Taiwan. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
