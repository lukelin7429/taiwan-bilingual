About · 學校簡介

A school known by its Paiwan name.

Wangjia Elementary School serves Wangjia Village, a traditional Paiwan village in Laiyi Township still widely known by its Paiwan place name, "Vungalid" — a name the school itself uses on its Facebook page, reflecting a strong sense of indigenous cultural identity. The school runs an attached kindergarten and is supported by child-welfare outreach programs as a remote mountain-area school.

望嘉國小服務望嘉村，這是來義鄉一處傳統排灣族部落，至今仍普遍以排灣族地名「Vungalid」稱之——學校本身的臉書粉絲專頁也採用此名，展現濃厚的原住民文化認同。學校附設幼兒園，並作為偏遠山區學校，獲兒童福利聯盟等機構的關懷資源支持。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🪶 Known as Vungalid

Paiwan語地名Vungalid

The school and its village continue to go by the Paiwan place name "Vungalid" in everyday use, a marker of strong indigenous cultural identity.

🧒 An Attached Kindergarten

附設幼兒園

🤝 Support for a Remote Mountain School

偏遠山區學校關懷資源

As a remote mountain-area school, Wangjia Elementary receives outreach support from child-welfare organizations such as the Child Welfare League Foundation.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A Paiwan village that keeps its name.

🏔️

Wangjia, known in Paiwan as Vungalid, is a traditional Paiwan village in Laiyi Township — part of the township with the largest Paiwan indigenous population of any third-level administrative division in Taiwan. The continued use of the Paiwan place name reflects a strong indigenous cultural identity that carries through to the school itself. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
