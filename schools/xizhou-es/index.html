About · 學校簡介

A river-terrace school rooted in sandy-soil farmland.

Xizhou Elementary began in 1955 as a branch campus of Houlong Elementary School, established to serve children in Xizhou Village — an alluvial river-terrace community along the Houlong River. It became an independent school in 1959, and has continued to serve this farming village ever since, part of a coastal Hakka/Hoklo-Hakka township famed for its sandy-soil agriculture.

溪洲國小於1955年（民國44年）以後龍國小分校形式創立，服務後龍溪沖積河階台地上的溪洲聚落學童。1959年（民國48年）獨立設校，持續服務這個沙質土壤農業聞名的沿海客家、福佬客鄉鎮至今。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌊 Rooted on the Houlong River Terrace

後龍溪河階台地

The school serves Xizhou Village, an alluvial river-terrace community shaped by the Houlong River's seasonal floodplain — a distinctive geography within the township.

🍉 Heart of Watermelon and Peanut Country

西瓜花生之鄉

Houlong Township is known island-wide as Taiwan's watermelon and peanut growing region, and Xizhou's sandy alluvial soil sits right at the center of that farming tradition.

🏫 From Branch Campus to Independent School

從分校到獨立設校

Founded in 1955 as a branch of Houlong Elementary School, Xizhou gained independent status in 1959, reflecting the postwar growth of rural schooling in the area.

🌾 A Coastal Farming Community's School

沿海客家福佬客聚落

The school anchors a small coastal Hakka/Hoklo-Hakka farming village, continuing a long tradition of community-centered rural education in Houlong.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Taiwan's watermelon and peanut kingdom.

🍉

Xizhou village sits on the Houlong River floodplain in a township famed across Taiwan as the "watermelon and peanut kingdom" — its sandy coastal soil ideally suited to these crops. The area reflects the farming heritage of the coastal Hakka/Hoklo-Hakka community. See the Miaoli county page for more on the region.
