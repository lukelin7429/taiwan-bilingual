About · 學校簡介

Born from a growing town.

Xining Elementary was founded in 1960, splitting off from Qingshui Elementary as the town's population grew in the postwar decades. It stands among the newer of Qingshui's town-center elementary schools, part of a broader pattern in which the district's original elementary school subdivided repeatedly to keep pace with a growing student population.

西寧國小創立於民國49年（1960年），由清水國小分設而來，反映戰後數十年間清水市街人口成長的趨勢。學校是清水市區較新設立的小學之一，也是本區原始的清水國小歷經多次分校獨立過程中的一環。
