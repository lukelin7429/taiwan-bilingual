About · 學校簡介

A new campus for a new generation.

Donghu Elementary was established in 2018 under the name Jialin Elementary School, before being renamed the following year, in 2019. The school runs 48 general classrooms and 3 special-education classrooms, and moved into a brand-new campus completed in 2023, equipped with modern facilities — another new school born from Linkou New Town's continuing demographic boom.

東湖國小創立於民國107年（2018年），原名「佳林國小」，隔年（2019年）更名為現名。學校設有48班一般班級與3班特教班，並於2023年遷入設備完善的全新校舍——是林口新市鎮持續人口成長下，又一所應運而生的新學校。
