About · 學校簡介

Rebuilt, and remembering.

Minhe Elementary School serves Minhe Village in Shuili Township. Like many schools in the area, it was rebuilt after the devastating 1999 921 earthquake, and it maintains a "921 Reconstruction Record" documenting that rebuilding process — standing today as a marker of the township's recovery on the path travelers take en route to Sun Moon Lake.

民和國小服務水里鄉民和村。如同當地許多學校一樣，學校在1999年921大地震後歷經重建，並保存「921重建紀錄」，記錄下當時的重建歷程——如今矗立於旅客前往日月潭途中，見證著這個鄉鎮的重生。
