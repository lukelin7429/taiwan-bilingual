About · 學校簡介

A rural school built on character and community.

Nei'an Elementary began on March 31, 1939, as a branch of Tianzhong Public School with 6 classes, becoming independent just two years later, on March 31, 1941, as Neiwan National School with 10 classes. The school went through several renames before becoming Nei'an National School in 1953 and, finally, Nei'an Elementary School in 1969. Located in Xinzhuang Village in Tianzhong Township, the school won the 2018 Ministry of Education Teaching Excellence Gold Medal Award and has also been recognized as "Outstanding" in family education, with strong results in martial arts, music, and English competitions.

內安國小創立於民國28年（1939年）3月31日，最初作為田中公學校的分校，設有6班，兩年後的民國30年（1941年）3月31日獨立設校，定名為內灣國民學校，共10班。學校歷經數次更名，於民國42年（1953年）改稱內安國民學校，並於民國58年（1969年）定名為內安國民小學。校址位於田中鎮新庄里，曾獲2018年教育部教學卓越金質獎，並在家庭教育方面獲評「優等」，在武術、音樂與英語競賽中也表現優異。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥇 A National Teaching Excellence Gold Medal

教學卓越金質獎

Nei'an Elementary won the Ministry of Education's Teaching Excellence Gold Medal Award in 2018, a top national honor for its classroom innovation.

🧹 Character Built Into Daily Routines

品格教育融入日常

The school integrates character education into everyday activities like morning cleaning duties, reflecting its close-knit, community-minded rural character.

🥋 Strength Across Competitions

武術、音樂、英語競賽佳績

Students have achieved strong results in martial arts, music, and English competitions, showing a well-rounded approach to extracurricular development.

👨‍👩‍👧 An "Outstanding" Family Education Program

家庭教育優等評價

The school has been recognized as "Outstanding" in family education, underlining its efforts to connect classroom learning with home and community life.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A close-knit corner of Tianzhong.

Nei'an Elementary is a small, green-campus rural school in Xinzhuang Village, part of Tianzhong Township's close-knit, agricultural community — a setting that shapes its emphasis on everyday character education. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
