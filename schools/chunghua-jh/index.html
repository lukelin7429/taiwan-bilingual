Chunghua Junior High School

中華國民中學

A public junior high built on reclaimed marshland to serve Yilan City's growing population under nine-year compulsory education.

About · 學校簡介

Built from marshland into a growing campus.

Chunghua Junior High began in 1968 as a branch of Toucheng Middle School's Yilan campus and was formally renamed and established the following year, in 1969, becoming the third junior high school built in Yilan City to implement nine-year compulsory education. The original campus site was a low-lying, flood-prone marshy area, and early students and staff worked hard to develop the grounds into a usable school. Starting with just 8 classes, the school has since grown to 37 classes, including a dedicated sports class established in 2015.

中華國中前身為「宜蘭縣立頭城中學宜蘭分校」，成立於民國57年（1968年），隔年（1969年）正式更名獨立設校，是宜蘭市為推行九年國教而興建的第三所國中。創校初期校地為地勢低窪、容易積水的沼澤地帶，師生共同努力才將校園開墾建設起來。學校從最初僅8個班級，成長至今已有37班，並於2015年成立體育班。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏗️ Built From Reclaimed Marshland

沼澤地拓墾建校

The school's original campus was a flood-prone marsh that students and staff transformed by hand into usable grounds — a formative chapter in its history.

📈 From 8 Classes to 37

從8班成長至37班

What began as a small 8-class school has expanded nearly fivefold over the decades, reflecting the growth of Yilan City itself.

🏃 A Sports Class Since 2015

體育班

A dedicated sports class established in 2015 gives athletically talented students a specialized track within the school.

🏫 The Third School of Compulsory Education

九年國教第三所國中

Chunghua was the third junior high built in Yilan City specifically to implement Taiwan's nine-year compulsory education policy in the late 1960s.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A school shaped by its landscape.

🌾

Chunghua Junior High's origin story — a school literally built from reclaimed marshland — reflects the broader story of Yilan City's expansion into low-lying terrain during the late 1960s as compulsory education rolled out. See the Yilan County schools page for more on the region.
