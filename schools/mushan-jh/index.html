About · 學校簡介

A century-old lineage from an agricultural school.

Muzha Junior High School traces its lineage back to 1925, during the Japanese colonial era, when it was founded as the "Muzha Agricultural Supplementary School" (木柵農業補習學校) — making it one of the oldest schools in Wenshan District, despite operating today as a junior high. It was upgraded to an agricultural specialty school in 1931, relocated and renamed "Taipei County Muzha Junior Agricultural School" in 1949, and reorganized as "Taipei County Muzha Junior High School" in 1959.

The school celebrated its 91st anniversary in 2015 and marked its full 100th anniversary on November 9, 2024. Its origins as an agricultural school tie it directly to Muzha's historic farming and tea economy — the same economy that produced the Maokong tea district just up the hill.

木柵國中的校史可追溯至1925年（大正14年）日治時期創立的「木柵農業補習學校」，是文山區歷史最悠久的學校之一，儘管今日以國中型態辦學。該校於1931年升格為農業專修學校，1949年遷址並更名為「臺北縣木柵初級農業學校」，1959年改制為「臺北縣木柵初級中學」。

學校於2015年慶祝91週年校慶，並於2024年11月9日歡慶建校滿百年。其農業學校的出身，與木柵當地悠久的農耕與茶葉經濟直接相連——正是這片土地孕育出山上的貓空茶區。
