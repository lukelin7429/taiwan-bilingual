About · 學校簡介

Training Donggang's next generation of mariners.

National Donggang Marine & Fisheries Vocational Senior High School was founded in 1961 as a branch of Taiwan Provincial Kaohsiung Senior Fisheries Vocational School, becoming independent in 1968 as Taiwan Provincial Tung Kang Senior Fisheries Vocational School. It added a Maritime Department and took on its current focus in 1980, then transferred to national administration in 2000, becoming the "National" school it is today. As of the 2022–23 academic year, the school enrolled 794 students across 30 classes in six departments: Electronics, Home Economics, Marine Engineering, Aquaculture, Shipping Management, and Fishery Food Science — training students directly for the region's dominant fishing and maritime industries.

國立東港高級海事水產職業學校創立於民國50年（1961年），最初為台灣省立高雄水產職業學校分部，民國57年（1968年）獨立為「台灣省立東港水產職業學校」。民國69年（1980年）增設航海科並確立現今發展方向，民國89年（2000年）改隸國立，成為今日的「國立」學校。111學年度（2022-23年）全校共794名學生、30個班級，設有電子科、家事科、輪機科、水產養殖科、航運管理科、水產食品科六大科別，直接為東港地區主力的漁業與航運產業培育人才。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Educating a fishing port's workforce.

🎣

Donggang is one of Taiwan's most famous fishing ports, renowned for its bluefin tuna auctions and fresh seafood, and home to the triennial King Boat Burning Festival (迎王燒王船) centered on Donglong Temple. This vocational school trains students directly for the region's fisheries and maritime industries — its departments in aquaculture, marine engineering, and shipping management are a direct extension of the port town's economic backbone.
