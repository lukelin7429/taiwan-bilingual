Xiaogang Junior High School

小港國民中學

Founded on land leased from the Taiwan Sugar Corporation's local refinery, this junior high traces Siaogang's path from sugar-farming community to industrial district.

About · 學校簡介

A school rooted in the district's sugar-farming past.

Xiaogang Junior High began in 1956 as a branch campus of neighboring Linyuan Junior High, with its first classes held in borrowed classrooms at Xiaogang Elementary School. It became an independent school in 1958. Its permanent campus, built in 1957, sat on land leased from the Taiwan Sugar Corporation's Xiaogang sugar refinery — a direct link to the agricultural, sugar-growing economy that once defined this part of Kaohsiung before industrialization transformed the district.

小港國中創立於民國45年（1956年），最初為鄰近林園國中的分校，首批班級借用小港國小教室上課，於民國47年（1958年）獨立設校。其永久校舍建於1957年，用地租自台灣糖業公司小港糖廠——直接見證了小港在工業化改變地貌之前，曾是以蔗糖種植為主的農業經濟區。

Local Culture · 在地文化

From sugarcane fields to industrial zone.

🎋

Before Siaogang became home to Kaohsiung's airport, harbor, and heavy industry, much of the district's land — including Xiaogang Junior High's own campus site — belonged to the Taiwan Sugar Corporation's local refinery operations, a reminder of the sugar-farming economy that shaped this part of Kaohsiung for decades.
