About · 學校簡介

A community school in Chaozhou Town.

Chaodong Elementary School serves the children of Chaozhou Town, Pingtung County. As part of Chaozhou's broader network of neighborhood elementary schools, it provides a steady educational presence for local families within southern Pingtung's major market town and transportation hub.

潮東國小服務屏東縣潮州鎮的學童。作為潮州鎮眾多社區型國小網絡的一員，學校在這座屏南重要市鎮暨交通樞紐，為在地家庭提供穩定的教育據點。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Chaozhou, southern Pingtung's rail and market hub.

🚂

Chaodong Elementary sits within Chaozhou Town — southern Pingtung's major market town and transportation hub, historically shaped by the sugar industry and rail transport, including the Chaozhou Forest Railway heritage. The school's students share in this same regional identity and history.
