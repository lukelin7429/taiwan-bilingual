About · 學校簡介

The only junior high in Moon World country.

Tianliao Junior High School was founded around 1969, though available sources give slightly different years for its founding. It remains the only junior high school serving Tianliao District, a rural mountain community best known for its "Moon World" badlands terrain. The school has drawn outside attention for a locally themed reading and literacy program, featured in CommonWealth Magazine.

田寮國民中學創校於約民國58年（1969年）前後，各方資料對確切創校年份略有出入。學校是田寮區唯一的國中，這個山區鄉鎮以「月世界」惡地地形聞名。校內推行的在地閱讀推廣課程曾獲《天下雜誌》報導關注。

🏚️ Named for the Farmers' Field Huts

田寮地名由來

The district's name, Tianliao ("field huts"), recalls the simple shelters early farmers built while working these badlands during the Ming-Zheng era.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Taiwan's "Moon World."

🌕

Tianliao ("field huts") is named for the simple field shelters early Ming-Zheng-era farmers built here. The district is famous nationwide for its "Moon World" (月世界) badlands terrain, a name given in 1937 by a Japanese-era Kaohsiung governor. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
