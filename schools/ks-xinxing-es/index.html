Xinxing Elementary School

新興國小

A rapidly-growing postwar elementary school that pioneered special education for physically disabled children in the 1960s.

戰後迅速擴校的國小，1960年代率先開辦肢障兒童特殊教育班。

About · 學校簡介

Growing fast, and reaching further.

Xinxing Elementary opened in the spring of 1950 under founding principal Xu Zexiang. The school grew quickly in its first decade, expanding from 18 to 40 classes by 1961. In 1965 it established a special class for physically disabled children, and in 1968 it was designated a "hub school" for the surrounding area. The school shares its name and district with nearby Xinxing Senior High and Xinxing Junior High, together anchoring Kaohsiung's historic Xinxing commercial district.

新興國小創立於民國39年（1950年）春，首任校長為許則祥。學校在創校頭十年迅速成長，至民國50年（1961年）已由18班擴增為40班。民國54年（1965年）成立肢體障礙兒童特殊教育班，民國57年（1968年）獲指定為中心學校。學校與鄰近的新興高中、新興國中共享校名與行政區，共同座落於高雄歷史悠久的新興商圈。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📈 Rapid Postwar Growth

戰後迅速擴校

In just over a decade, Xinxing Elementary grew from 18 classes to 40, reflecting the rapid postwar expansion of Kaohsiung's urban population.

♿ An Early Special Education Class

肢障兒童特教班

In 1965, the school established a dedicated class for physically disabled children — an early commitment to inclusive education in the city.

🏙️ Anchoring a Historic Commercial District

新興商圈核心

Sharing its name with nearby Xinxing Senior High and Junior High, the school sits at the heart of Kaohsiung's historic Xinxing commercial district.
