Gukeng Combined Elementary and Junior High School

古坑國民中小學

The anchor K-9 school of Taiwan's coffee-growing heartland, formed by a 2015 merger of an elementary school dating to 1934 and a junior high dating to the late 1950s.

About · 學校簡介

Two schools, one campus, one coffee-growing community.

Gukeng Combined Elementary and Junior High School serves as the anchor educational institution of Gukeng Township. Its elementary division dates back to 1934 as a branch campus, while its junior high division originated as a branch of Dounan Junior High School before becoming independent around 1958–1959. The two divisions formally merged into a single combined K-9 institution in August 2015, unifying elementary and junior-high education under one administration. The elementary campus suffered severe damage in the catastrophic 921 earthquake of 1999, with 18 classrooms destroyed; it was rebuilt with support from the China Times and the Yihe Group. Today the school anchors education at the heart of Gukeng's coffee-farming community.

古坑國民中小學是古坑鄉的指標教育機構。其國小部前身可追溯至1934年設立的分校，國中部則源自斗南國中的分部，約於1958、1959年間獨立設校。兩部於2015年8月正式合併為國中小一貫的九年一貫學校，整合國小與國中教育於同一行政體系之下。國小部校舍曾在1999年921大地震中嚴重受損，18間教室全毀，後於中國時報與義和集團的協助下重建。如今，學校座落於古坑咖啡產業的核心地帶，持續肩負地方教育的重任。
