Sanguang Elementary School

新北市三重區三光國民小學

A small riverside school named for the sun, moon and starlight said to shine together on its Tamsui-riverbank site, now a designated Bilingual Pioneer School.

因基隆河畔「日光、月光、星光」交會而得名的小型學校，現為雙語領航學校。

About · 學校簡介

A small campus by the Tamsui River.

Sanguang Elementary School was established on April 1, 1951, splitting off from Sanchong National School to serve the riverside community near the Tamsui River and Zhongxiao Bridge. Its compact campus of about 1.87 hectares has, since the 2021 school year, been designated a Bilingual Pioneer School (雙語領航學校) — one of a select group of New Taipei elementary schools leading the district's bilingual education efforts. Alongside its bilingual programming, the school has also built distinctive fencing and saxophone programs for its students.

三光國小創立於民國40年（1951年）4月1日，由三重國民學校分設而出，服務基隆河與忠孝橋畔的社區。校地面積約1.87公頃，屬小型校園，自民國110學年度（2021年）起獲指定為「雙語領航學校」——新北市推動雙語教育的示範學校之一。除雙語課程外，學校也發展出擊劍與薩克斯風等特色社團。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for three kinds of light.

🌟

The school's name — "Three Lights" (日光、月光、星光, sun/moon/starlight) — comes from its riverside site, where sunlight, moonlight and starlight were said to shine together, directly invoking Sanchong's Tamsui-riverbank setting. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
