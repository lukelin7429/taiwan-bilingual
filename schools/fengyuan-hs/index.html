Fengyuan Senior High School

臺中市立豐原高級中等學校

A large academic senior high school whose fine-arts program, founded in 1984, was the first of its kind at any public high school in Taiwan.

一所大型高中，其於1984年成立的美術班，是全台公立高中中最早設立的美術資優班。

About · 學校簡介

A high school that teaches its own hometown.

Fengyuan Senior High School began in 1954 as a dispersal branch established to spread out student enrollment during a period of school crowding, becoming a fully independent senior high school in 1968. Today it enrolls roughly 1,500 students across about 45 classes, and is best known for its fine-arts talent program, founded in 1984 as the first public-school art program of its kind in Taiwan. The school also teaches a required "Fengyuan Studies" course, built specifically to root students in the history of their home district.

臺中市立豐原高級中等學校源起於民國43年（1954年）設立的疏散分部，後於民國57年（1968年）獨立設校。如今全校約有45班、1,500名學生，最為人稱道的是其於1984年成立的美術資優班——全台公立高中最早設立的美術班。學校並開設必修的「豐原學」課程，專為引導學生認識家鄉歷史而設計。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎨 Taiwan's First Public High-School Art Program

全台首創公立美術班

The school's fine-arts talent program, established in 1984, was the first art program at any public senior high school in Taiwan, and remains a signature strength today.

📖 A Required Course in Local History

必修「豐原學」

Every student takes a custom-built 'Fengyuan Studies' course, designed specifically to teach the history and character of the home district.

🏫 From Wartime Dispersal to a Major High School

從疏散分部到大型高中

Founded in 1954 as an enrollment-dispersal branch and made independent in 1968, the school has grown into one of Fengyuan's largest, with around 1,500 students.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A curriculum built around its own hometown.

📚

The school's mandatory "Fengyuan Studies" (豐原學) course is a rare example of a Taiwanese high school building its own local-history curriculum from scratch, reflecting Fengyuan's pride in its identity as Taichung's historic administrative and commercial center.
