About · 學校簡介

"Gangshan's founding school."

Gangshan Elementary School traces its origin to 1898, when it began as a branch of Fengshan National School serving the settlement of Akangtiam (阿公店), becoming an independent school the following year as "Akangtiam National School." As the oldest and longest-running school in the greater Gangshan area, it is known locally as "Gangshan's founding school" (岡山首學) — most postwar-era school principals in the surrounding region were once its students.

岡山國小創校可追溯至明治31年（1898年），最初為鳳山公學校在阿公店設立的分校，隔年（1899年）獨立設校，命名為「阿公店公學校」。作為大岡山地區歷史最悠久、持續辦學至今的學校，被地方尊稱為「岡山首學」——戰後周邊地區多數校長皆出身本校。

培育眾多校長的搖籃

🏘️ Named for Gangshan's Old Identity

阿公店公學校的由來

Its founding name, "Akangtiam National School," directly reflects Gangshan's pre-1920 name, Akangtiam — said to derive from an elderly man who ran a roadside teahouse on this old trade route.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for a roadside teahouse keeper.

🍵

The school's founding name, "Akangtiam National School," directly reflects Gangshan's pre-1920 name, Akangtiam (阿公店), said to derive from an elderly man ("阿公") who ran a roadside teahouse or inn on the historic trade route between Tainan and Zuoying.
