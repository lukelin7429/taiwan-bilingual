Meifeng Elementary School

美豐國民小學

A public elementary school in Dacheng Township that grew from a small branch campus into an independent school, now active in local-language and indigenous culture education.

位於大城鄉的公立國小，從小型分校成長為獨立學校，現致力於本土語言與原住民文化教育。

About · 學校簡介

From a small branch campus to a community anchor.

Meifeng Elementary began in 1952 as "Xigang National School Meifeng Branch," a satellite campus of Xigang National School. It became an independent school in August 1964 as Meifeng National School, under its first principal, Lin Wen-li. In 1968, when Taiwan implemented nine-year compulsory education, it was renamed Meifeng Elementary School, the name it carries today. The school has celebrated its 60th anniversary with a commemorative magazine and community sports day.

美豐國小創立於民國41年（1952年），最初名為「西港國民學校美豐分校」，是西港國民學校的分校。民國53年（1964年）8月獨立設校，定名「美豐國民學校」，首任校長為林文理。民國57年（1968年）隨九年國教實施，改名為「美豐國民小學」，沿用至今。學校曾出版六十週年校慶特刊，並舉辦社區運動會慶祝活動。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 From Branch to Independent School

從分校到獨立設校

Founded in 1952 as a branch of Xigang National School, Meifeng became fully independent in 1964 under its first principal, Lin Wen-li — a milestone still marked in the school's history.

🗣️ Local-Language and Indigenous Education

本土語言與原住民文化教育

The school actively promotes indigenous and local-language education programs, tying the campus into Dacheng Township's broader efforts at cultural and linguistic preservation.

🎊 Sixty Years of School Memory

六十週年校慶紀念

Meifeng marked its 60th anniversary with a commemorative magazine and a community sports day, bringing together generations of students, families, and neighbors.

Keeping local languages alive in Dacheng.

Meifeng Elementary plays an active role in local-language and indigenous culture education, part of a broader push across Dacheng Township — a coastal farming community in Changhua County — to preserve its linguistic and cultural heritage for the next generation.
