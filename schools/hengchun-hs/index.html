About · 學校簡介

Where the island's classrooms run out.

National Hengchun Vocational High School was established in 1955 as a vocational extension of a local junior middle school, then moved to its current campus in 1968 when the provincial government created eleven new senior high schools across Taiwan. Widely described as "Taiwan's southernmost high school," it now enrolls around 682 students across programs including General Studies, Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Data Processing, Tourism, Culinary Management/Technology, Information Technology, and a Comprehensive Vocational Skills track. The roughly 78,557-square-meter campus includes a school-run training hotel, giving tourism and hospitality students hands-on experience just minutes from Taiwan's most famous beach resort area.

國立恆春高級工商職業學校創立於民國44年（1955年），原為地方初中附設的職業補習班，於民國57年（1968年）台灣省政府增設十一所省立高中之際遷至現址。學校常被稱為「台灣最南端的高中」，目前約有682名學生，設有普通科、電機科、電子科、資料處理科、觀光科、餐飲管理/技術科、資訊科及綜合職能科等班別。校地約78,557平方公尺，並設有實習旅館，讓觀光餐飲科學生能就近在墾丁觀光門戶累積實務經驗。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏝️ Taiwan's Southernmost High School

最南端的高中

Perched at the tip of the Hengchun Peninsula, the school is widely known across Taiwan as the country's southernmost senior high, a point of local pride.

🏨 A Campus with Its Own Hotel

校內實習旅館

The school runs its own training hotel on campus, giving Tourism and Culinary Management students real front-desk and kitchen experience before graduation.

🎓 Eight Programs, One Campus

八大科別

From Electrical Engineering to Data Processing to Culinary Technology, around 682 students choose from a wide spread of technical and vocational tracks.

🧳 Feeding Kenting's Tourism Economy

接軌觀光產業

The Tourism and Hospitality departments train students to work directly in the resort and hotel trade that powers the Hengchun Peninsula's economy.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Inside Taiwan's historic walled town.

🏯

Hengchun Town is home to Taiwan's best-preserved walled old city, built in 1875, whose four historic gates still frame the streets of the town center. Hengchun also serves as the main gateway to Kenting National Park, the island's most famous beach and marine-tourism destination — which is exactly why the vocational school's Tourism, Culinary, and Hospitality programs exist: to train the workforce for the resorts, restaurants, and attractions that visitors encounter the moment they pass through the old city gates.
