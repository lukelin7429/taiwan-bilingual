Caotun Junior High School

草屯國民中學

A public junior high school with roots in a 1931 agricultural training institute, now a top academic and athletic performer in the area.

About · 學校簡介

From an agricultural institute to a top-ranked school.

Caotun Junior High School traces its institutional roots back to 1931, when it began as an agricultural youth training institute during the Japanese colonial era, later operating as a home-economics and agricultural vocational school. In more recent years the school has ranked first in academic performance among Caotun-area junior highs in the 115th-year (2026) entrance examinations, and its judo, wrestling, and volleyball teams earned national recognition in 2025.

草屯國中的歷史可追溯至民國20年（1931年）日治時期成立的農業青年講習所，其後曾以家事、農業職業學校的形式運作。近年來，該校在115學年度會考中，學業表現於草屯地區國中排名第一，柔道、角力與排球隊也於2025年獲得全國性殊榮。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 Roots in a 1931 Agricultural Institute

源自農業講習所

The school's history traces back to a 1931 agricultural youth training institute during the Japanese era, later evolving through home-economics and agricultural vocational education before becoming today's junior high.

🥇 Top Academic Performance Locally

會考成績區域第一

Caotun JH ranked first in academic performance among Caotun-area junior highs in the 115th-year entrance examinations, reflecting strong classroom instruction across subjects.

🥋 National Recognition in Combat Sports

柔道角力全國肯定

The school's judo and wrestling teams earned national-level recognition in 2025, alongside a strong volleyball program — a testament to its athletic training tradition.

🪑 An Artisan and Farming Heritage

木工農業雙重底蘊

Set in Caotun, a town long known for woodworking and furniture craftsmanship near Taichung, the school's agricultural and home-economics origins reflect the area's historic artisan and farming trades.
