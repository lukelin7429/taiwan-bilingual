About · 學校簡介

An open-campus school named for a housing complex.

Zhonglun Junior High takes its name from the Zhonglun public-housing complex, built on land once farmed by the Taiwan Sugar Corporation. Its campus, designed around an "open space" concept, won an architecture award in 2001, suggesting the school was built in the late 1990s. Today it remains one of Fongshan's smaller junior highs, sitting just across Fongshan Creek from the Chung Cheng Armed Forces Preparatory School, with around six classes and roughly 144 students as of 2024.

中崙國中校名取自建於原台糖甘蔗地上的中崙國宅社區。校園採「開放空間」設計，曾於民國90年（2001年）獲得建築獎項，推測建校時間約在1990年代末。學校規模較小，隔著鳳山溪與中正預校相望，截至2024年約有6班、144名學生。
