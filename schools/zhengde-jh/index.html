Zhengde Junior High School

正德國中

Tamsui's second junior high, opened in 1993 to meet a decade of population growth, with a second campus rooted in a 1971 branch school.

為因應淡水人口成長而於1993年創校，設有源自1971年分校的仙皓校區。

About · 學校簡介

Built for a growing district's second wave of students.

Zhengde Junior High School was formally approved in 1988 and opened its doors in 1993 with an inaugural class of 580 students, becoming Tamsui's second public junior high school as the district's population grew through the 1990s. The school also operates a second site, the Xianhao Campus, which began in 1971 as a branch of Tamsui Junior High, became independent in 1975, and was merged into Zhengde in 1994.

正德國中於民國77年（1988年）獲准設校，民國82年（1993年）正式招生，首屆即有580名學生，成為淡水地區因1990年代人口成長而增設的第二所公立國中。學校另設有仙皓校區，該校區源自1971年淡水國中的分校，1975年獨立設校，1994年併入正德國中。

👥 A Big First Class

兩校區合一

Zhengde operates a second site, the Xianhao Campus, which traces back to a 1971 branch of Tamsui Junior High before merging into Zhengde in 1994.

The school's founding responded directly to 1990s population growth in Tamsui that outgrew the capacity of the historic Tamsui Junior High.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A turning point in Tamsui's growth.

🏘️

Zhengde's founding marks a turning point in Tamsui's modern growth — as the old port town's population swelled through the 1990s, this was the first new junior high built to serve it. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
