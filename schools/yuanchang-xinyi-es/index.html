About · 學校簡介

A small school growing green.

Xinyi Elementary is located in Wukuai Village, right at the border between Yuanchang and Sihu townships in Yunlin County. It is a small school — six classes with 25 students in total — and has built a distinct identity around an ecological and organic campus, paired with disaster-education programs that give students practical, place-based learning.

信義國小位於雲林縣元長鄉五塊村，緊鄰元長鄉與四湖鄉的交界處。學校規模不大，共六班、學生25人，發展出以生態有機校園為特色的教學方向，並結合防災教育，讓學生獲得貼近生活的實用學習。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌿 An Ecological, Organic Campus

生態有機校園

Xinyi has developed its grounds into an ecological and organic campus, giving its small student body hands-on exposure to sustainable growing practices.

🚨 Disaster-Education Programs

防災教育

The school runs dedicated disaster-education programs, equipping students in this low-lying agricultural area with practical safety knowledge.

🤝 A Tiny, Tight-Knit School

迷你小校

With just 25 students across six classes, Xinyi offers an intimate learning environment where every child is known by name.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where two farming traditions meet.

🌾

Straddling the border of peanut-farming Yuanchang and rice-and-garlic-farming, fishing-adjacent Sihu townships, this small agricultural-community school embodies both townships' rural character. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
