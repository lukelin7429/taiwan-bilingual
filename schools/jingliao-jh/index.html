About · 學校簡介

Grown from a branch campus into an independent school.

Jingliao Junior High began in 1975 as a branch campus of Houbi Junior High in Changduanshu Village, initially borrowing classrooms at Jingliao Elementary School. It moved into its own partially completed campus the following year and became a fully independent school in 1979 as "Tainan County Jingliao Junior High School." Its home village sits directly adjacent to Jingliao — the rural community made internationally known by the 2004 documentary "Let It Be" (無米樂).

菁寮國中民國64年（1975年）創立，初為後壁國中分校，借用菁寮國小教室上課，隔年遷入尚未完工的自有校舍，民國68年（1979年）獨立設校，定名「臺南縣菁寮國民中學」。校址所在的長短樹里緊鄰菁寮，正是紀錄片《無米樂》所描繪的知名稻作社區。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Neighboring the "Let It Be" village.

🌾

Jingliao Junior High serves the Jingliao (菁寮) community at the heart of "Let It Be" (無米樂) country, where the 2004 documentary famously followed elderly farmers continuing traditional, low-mechanization rice cultivation. The school's own roots as a borrowed-classroom branch campus mirror the same story of a small farming community building its institutions from scratch.
