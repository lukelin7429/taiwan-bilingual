Zhengxin Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A young school for a growing neighborhood.

Zhengxin Elementary is one of the youngest elementary schools in Xinhua District, built to serve a growing residential area rather than the district's established old-street neighborhood. The Tainan County government appointed a preparatory-office director, drawn from Neijiao Elementary, in June 1997, and formally approved the school's name — "Tainan County Xinhua Town Zhengxin National Elementary School" — that August. Groundbreaking on the new campus followed on February 9, 1998.

正新國小是新化區最年輕的小學之一，為服務新興住宅區而設立，而非傳統老街周邊的既有社區。台南縣政府於民國86年（1997年）6月指派內角國小校長兼任籌備處主任，並於同年8月正式核定校名「台南縣新化鎮正新國民小學」。新校舍於民國87年（1998年）2月9日動工興建。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏗️ A Purpose-Built New Campus

全新規劃校舍

Groundbreaking for the school's own campus began on February 9, 1998, just months after the school's official approval, reflecting rapid response to local demand.

🏘️ Serving a Growing Neighborhood

服務新興社區

Rather than an established old-street district, the school was built to serve a growing residential population on Xinhua's newer fringe.

🆕 One of Xinhua's Youngest Schools

新化區年輕學校之一

Established in 1997, Zhengxin stands in contrast to Xinhua's many century-old schools, representing the district's more recent growth.

Xinhua's newer residential fringe.

Zhengxin Elementary represents Xinhua's more recently developed residential fringe, in contrast to the district's Japanese-era old-street core just a short distance away. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
