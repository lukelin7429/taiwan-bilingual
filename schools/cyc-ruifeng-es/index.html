About · 學校簡介

Tea arts and flower fragrance, high on the mountain.

Ruifeng Elementary was founded on September 1, 1958, in the Shengmaoshu area of Meishan Township. Its school motto emphasizes "tea arts and flower fragrance" (茶藝花香), directly reflecting the local high-mountain tea cultivation that surrounds the school. Ruifeng has partnered with the Taiwan-US Eco-Schools ecological education initiative, and runs annual arts-enrichment programs alongside bilingual programs including reader's theater and storytelling competitions.

瑞峰國小創立於民國47年（1958年）9月1日，位於梅山鄉生毛樹地區。校訓強調「茶藝花香」，直接呼應學校周邊的高山茶產業。瑞峰參與臺美生態學校夥伴計畫，並推行藝術深耕年度課程，以及包含讀者劇場與說故事比賽的雙語課程。

Local Culture · 在地文化

In the heart of the tea belt.

🍵

Ruifeng, in the Shengmaoshu area, sits in the heart of Meishan's famous high-mountain tea belt near Taihe and Ruili, and its "tea arts, flower fragrance" motto and eco-school partnership directly celebrate the surrounding tea gardens and forest trails, including the nearby Zhukeng Creek Trail (竹坑溪步道).
