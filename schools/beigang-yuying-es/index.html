About · 學校簡介

A branch campus in Fangou Village.

Yuying Elementary operates as a branch campus of Haoshou Elementary School, formally known as 好收國小育英分校, serving families in Fangou Village on the rural outskirts of Beigang Town. Its precise founding date is not confirmed in available records. As a small satellite campus, it extends the reach of its century-old parent school, Haoshou Elementary, to a more remote corner of the township.

育英國小是好收國小的分校，正式名稱為「好收國小育英分校」，服務北港鎮邊陲番溝里的家庭。確切創校年代目前尚無確切紀錄可考。作為小型的衛星分校，育英國小延伸了其百年母校好收國小的服務範圍，照顧到鄉鎮更偏遠角落的學童。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rural life within Chaotian Temple's orbit.

🛕

As a small branch school in Fangou Village, it serves families in Beigang's rural outskirts within the cultural orbit of the town's Chaotian Temple Mazu pilgrimage tradition. Even in this quieter corner of the township, the rhythms of farming life remain connected to the town's identity as a religious center. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
