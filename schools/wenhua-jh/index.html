About · 學校簡介

A friendly campus with championship alumni.

Wenhua Junior High was founded in 1968 under the motto "A Friendly Campus of Love and Wisdom." Its alumni include a national record-holding female hammer thrower and multiple players who represented Taiwan's national basketball team at the 2018 Asian Games. The school also operates the Wenhua Maker Center, giving students space for hands-on innovation projects.

文化國中創立於民國57年（1968年），以「仁愛智慧的溫馨校園」為校訓。校友中有全國紀錄保持人的女子鏈球選手，以及多位曾代表台灣參加2018年亞運籃球國手。學校並設有「文化自造中心」，提供學生動手實作、創新專題的空間。

🏅 A National Record Holder

Among the school's alumni is a female hammer thrower who set a national record, reflecting a track and field program that has produced elite talent.
