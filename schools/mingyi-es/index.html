About · 學校簡介

From a colonial-era branch to a major city school.

Mingyi Elementary was founded on April 1, 1933, during the Japanese colonial era, under the name "Karenkō-chō Karenkō Kōgakkō Kyū-Shinkō Branch Institute" (花蓮港廳花蓮港公學校舊新港分教場). After Taiwan's Retrocession, it was renamed Mingyi National School on January 25, 1946. Thanks to its location within a large and growing catchment area of downtown Hualien City, the school has expanded over the decades to become one of the largest elementary schools in eastern Taiwan.

明義國小創立於日治時期的昭和8年（1933年）4月1日，初名「花蓮港廳花蓮港公學校舊新港分教場」。台灣光復後，於民國35年（1946年）1月25日更名為明義國民學校。因位處花蓮市區廣大且持續成長的學區範圍內，學校歷經數十年發展，已成為東台灣規模最大的國小之一。
