About · 學校簡介

A Paiwan experimental school built on the seven good habits.

Danlu Elementary School was established in 1968 and, in 2018, was converted into a Paiwan Indigenous Experimental School (排灣族實驗學校), dedicated to cultivating children's leadership through indigenous pedagogy. The school has drawn Taiwanese media coverage for its “little archer” program, teaching students the “seven good habits” rooted in Paiwan cultural practice. It also maintains a long-term partnership with Teach For Taiwan, with TFT teachers accompanying students continuously from first grade through sixth-grade graduation.

丹路國小創立於民國57年（1968年），民國107年（2018年）轉型為排灣族實驗學校，致力以原住民族教育理念培養孩子的領導力。學校以「小小射不力」計畫廣受媒體報導，透過排灣族文化實踐教導學生「七個好習慣」。學校並與為台灣而教（TFT）長期合作，由TFT教師從一年級持續陪伴學生至六年級畢業。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Danlu, where Paiwan pedagogy shapes the whole school.

🏹

Shizi Township's Danlu community reflects the deep integration of Paiwan cultural pedagogy into formal schooling — the village's experimental-school status embodies a genuine, sustained approach to indigenous mountain education, built around leadership and the “seven good habits,” rather than a token curriculum add-on.
