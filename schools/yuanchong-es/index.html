Yuanchong Elementary School

圓崇國民小學

A school built at the former site of Wanqiao Station on the Alishan Forest Railway, with its own on-campus railway heritage room.

About · 學校簡介

Independence born from community advocacy.

Yuanchong Elementary began as a branch school of neighboring Lumen Elementary School, becoming independent in 1960 after local residents advocated for it. The campus sits at the entrance of the former Wanqiao (灣橋) station on the now-demolished section of the Alishan Forest Railway, and the school maintains a forest-railway history room to preserve this heritage. Its programs today include recorder ensemble, English storytelling, robotics and coding, and a bilingual reading initiative.

圓崇國小原為鄰近鹿滿國小的分校，因地方仕紳奔走爭取，於民國49年（1960年）獨立設校。校址位於已拆除的阿里山森林鐵路灣橋車站入口處，學校並設有林鐵相關校史文史室，保存這段鐵道記憶。目前推動直笛合奏、英語說故事、機器人與程式設計（Webduino、Scratch），以及雙語閱讀等多元課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚉 Built on Wanqiao Station

灣橋車站原址

The campus occupies the former entrance of Wanqiao Station on the Alishan Forest Railway, a physical link to the mountain railway's now-vanished route.

🗂️ A Railway History Room

林鐵校史文史室

Yuanchong preserves its unique heritage in an on-campus history and heritage room dedicated to the forest railway's story.

🤖 Robotics Meets Recorders

機器人程式與直笛合奏

Students take part in robotics and coding (Webduino, Scratch) alongside recorder ensemble and English storytelling/reader's theater — a broad mix for a small rural school.

🌱 Founded by the Community

仕紳奔走設校

The school's 1960 independence came about through local advocacy, giving it a founding story rooted directly in the community it serves.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Standing where the forest railway once ran.

🚂

Yuanchong's campus sits directly on the former site of Wanqiao Station on the historic Alishan Forest Railway, and the school actively preserves this vanished chapter of the line's history through its own heritage exhibit room. See the Chiayi county page for more on the region.
