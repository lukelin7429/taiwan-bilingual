About · 學校簡介

One of Pingtung's oldest girls' schools.

National Pingtung Girls' Senior High School was founded in 1932, during the Japanese colonial era, when Pingtung was developing as the administrative seat of the region. Today the school enrolls roughly 1,984 students across 48 classes, with regular, math-science honors (established 2003), and language honors (established 2005) tracks reserved for female students, while its music program, established in 1993, is coeducational. The school has been led since August 2020 by principal Yang Rongfeng.

國立屏東女子高級中學創校於昭和7年（1932年）日治時期，當時屏東正發展為地區行政中心。現有學生約1,984人，共48班。普通科、數理資優班（2003年設立）與語文資優班（2005年設立）皆僅招收女學生，而音樂班（1993年設立）則為男女兼收。現任校長楊榮豐自2020年8月上任。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 A Nine-Decade Legacy

近百年校史

Founded in 1932 under Japanese rule, the school is one of Pingtung City's oldest institutions for girls' education, tracing the city's growth as the region's administrative center.

🔬 Honors Tracks in Science and Language

數理與語文資優班

The math-science honors track (2003) and language honors track (2005) offer specialized academic pathways alongside the school's regular curriculum.

🎵 A Coeducational Music Program

男女兼收音樂班

Established in 1993, the music program is the school's only coeducational track, admitting male students alongside its otherwise all-female student body.

🏫 A Large Student Body

學生規模可觀

With about 1,984 students across 48 classes (16 per grade), it remains one of the larger senior high schools serving Pingtung City and the surrounding county.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Pingtung's historic urban core.
