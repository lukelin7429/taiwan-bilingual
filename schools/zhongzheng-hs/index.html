Zhongzheng Senior High School

臺北市立中正高級中學

A public senior high founded in 1946 as Shilin Junior High School, known for its Music Talent Class and a wind-band tradition dating back to 1986.

About · 學校簡介

From Shilin Junior High to Zhongzheng Senior High.

Zhongzheng Senior High traces its roots to 1946, when it opened as Taipei County Shilin Junior High School. The senior-high and junior-high divisions split into separate schools in 1968, and in 1975 — the year Chiang Kai-shek died — the senior-high took on its present name, drawn from his given name. To this day, Zhongzheng Senior High and its sister school, Zhongzheng Junior High, still share the same school song. The school has cultivated young musicians since establishing a Music Talent Class in 1985, and its Formosa Wind Symphony alumni band, founded in 1986 by the late director Liang Zhen-han, remains one of Taiwan's longest-running wind bands. More recently, the school has built out an AI curriculum track tied to its location within the Shilin-Beitou Technology Park.

中正高中創校於民國35年（1946年），初名「臺北縣立士林初級中學」。民國57年（1968年）高中部與初中部分家設校，民國64年（1975年）——正值蔣中正逝世之年——高中部更名為「中正高級中學」，沿用至今；高中部與姊妹校中正國中，至今仍共用同一首校歌。學校自民國74年（1985年）起設立音樂資優班，培育音樂人才；由已故指揮梁在漢創辦、成立於民國75年（1986年）的「震撼管樂團」校友樂團，是台灣歷史最悠久的管樂團之一。近年學校更配合校址所在的士林北投科技園區，發展人工智慧課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎺 The Formosa Wind Symphony

震撼管樂團

Founded in 1986 by the late director Liang Zhen-han, this alumni wind band is one of Taiwan's longest-running, keeping graduates connected through music long after they leave campus.

🎼 A Music Talent Class Since 1985

音樂資優班

Established in 1985 to cultivate gifted young musicians, the class has shaped the school's identity as a center for music education in northern Taipei.

💻 An AI Track in the Tech Park

人工智慧課程

Sitting inside the Shilin-Beitou Technology Park, the school has developed an AI-focused curriculum track that ties classroom learning to the surrounding tech corridor.

🎶 One School Song, Two Schools

共用校歌

Even after splitting into separate senior-high and junior-high schools in 1968, Zhongzheng Senior High and Zhongzheng Junior High still share the same school song from their shared origin.

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the edge of the volcanic foothills.

🌋

Zhongzheng Senior High sits at the edge of the Shilin-Beitou Technology Park, right where the city meets the foothills of the Yangmingshan volcanic massif. That same volcanic geology, running down through the hills toward central Beitou, is what feeds the hot springs that have made the district famous since the Japanese colonial era.
