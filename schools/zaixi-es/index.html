Zaixi Elementary School

載熙國小

A school with roots in the ROC Air Force community, named to honor a U-2 pilot who died in service, with a plaque penned by Chiang Ching-kuo.

About · 學校簡介

A name that honors a fallen pilot.

Zaixi Elementary traces its roots to the "Air Force Children's Elementary School," founded in Shanghai in 1946, which relocated to Taiwan in 1949 as the Nationalist government retreated. In August 1950, two divisions merged to form the "Air Force General Headquarters Xinzhu Elementary School" on the former campsite of the Air Force's 20th Brigade. On August 1, 1966, the school was renamed "載熙國小" to honor Wu Zaixi, an Air Force officer who died in a U-2 reconnaissance aircraft crash — the school's name plaque was personally written by then-Defense Minister Chiang Ching-kuo. The school became a municipal institution in 1982.

載熙國小的前身可追溯至民國35年（1946年）於上海成立的「空軍子弟小學」，民國38年（1949年）隨政府遷台。民國39年（1950年）8月，兩個分部合併成立「空軍總部新竹子弟小學」，校址位於空軍第20大隊舊營區。民國55年（1966年）8月1日，學校為紀念因U-2偵察機失事殉職的空軍軍官吳載熙，更名為「載熙國小」，校名匾額由時任國防部長蔣經國親筆題寫。民國71年（1982年）改制為市立學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

✈️ Named for a U-2 Pilot's Sacrifice

紀念U-2飛行員吳載熙

The school was renamed in 1966 to honor Wu Zaixi, an Air Force officer who died in a U-2 reconnaissance aircraft crash, giving the school a name tied directly to a moment of service and sacrifice.

✒️ A Plaque Written by Chiang Ching-kuo

蔣經國親題校名

The school's name plaque was personally written by then-Defense Minister Chiang Ching-kuo, a rare direct link between the school and a major figure in Taiwan's political history.

🎖️ Roots in the Air Force Children's School

源自空軍子弟小學

The school's lineage traces back to the Air Force Children's Elementary School founded in Shanghai in 1946, which relocated to Taiwan in 1949 and eventually settled permanently in Hsinchu.
