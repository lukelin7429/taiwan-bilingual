About · 學校簡介

A name from Taiwan's intelligence history.

Yunong Elementary School's planning was approved by the Taipei City Government in February 1974, with construction handled through neighboring Yusheng Elementary School and completed in October 1977. The school is named "Yunong" directly after Dai Li's (戴笠) courtesy name, honoring the Republic of China intelligence chief, because the area was formerly Military Intelligence Bureau land — a direct sister naming to the neighboring Yusheng Elementary School. The first principal was Xu Yuanyao, appointed by the Taipei City Department of Education. Four teaching buildings — "Zili," "Ziqiang," and "Zifa" Buildings, plus an activity center — are arranged in a "口" (mouth-shaped/quadrangle) layout around the athletic field, and an attached kindergarten was established in 1998.

雨農國小的興建計畫於民國63年（1974年）2月獲臺北市政府核准，工程委由鄰近的雨聲國小代辦，於民國66年（1977年）10月完工。校名「雨農」直接取自情報首腦戴笠的字，因校地原為軍統局用地，與鄰近的雨聲國小同樣以此命名致敬。首任校長為徐元耀，由臺北市教育局派任。校內「自立樓」、「自強樓」、「自發樓」等四棟教學大樓及活動中心，環繞運動場呈「口」字型配置，附設幼兒園則於民國87年（1998年）設立。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A Cold War-era layer of local history.

🕵️

Like neighboring Yusheng Elementary, this school's name and site both trace back to the former Republic of China Military Intelligence Bureau compound near Zhishan Rock, embedding a distinctive Cold War-era intelligence history into this corner of Shilin.
