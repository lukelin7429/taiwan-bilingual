About · 學校簡介

The mother school of Yilan's junior highs.

Toucheng Junior High School was founded in 1956 as Yilan County Toucheng Middle School. In its early years, the school established a branch campus in Yilan City — a branch that later split off to become today's independent Yilan Junior High School, making Toucheng Junior High the "mother school" behind one of the county's other major institutions. The school's mission centers on being "a school that cultivates local future leaders," and it runs digital learning platforms, exploration and outdoor education, public art initiatives, and transportation safety education.

頭城國中創立於民國45年（1956年），初名宜蘭縣立頭城中學。建校初期曾於宜蘭市設立分校，該分校後來獨立設校，即今日的宜蘭國民中學，頭城國中因此成為孕育宜蘭縣另一所重要國中的「母校」。學校秉持「培育在地未來領導者」的辦學使命，推動數位學習平台、探索與戶外教育、公共藝術，以及交通安全教育。
