Minglun Junior High School

About · 學校簡介

A school raised by its community.

Minglun Junior High was founded in 1968 in response to the nationwide introduction of nine-year compulsory education. It was initially named "Yuanxin Junior High" (員新國中) before being renamed to its current name, Minglun. Enrollment began that September under first principal Xiao Chuanming, with classes temporarily held elsewhere while the school's own buildings were completed. Sited at the foot of Baiguoshan (百果山麓), a local landmark hill, the school was founded through the direct sponsorship of local community gentry. Under principal Liu Mingzhao, who took office in 1977, the school's academic performance rose to become one of the leading schools in Changhua County.

明倫國中創校於民國57年（1968年），因應九年國民義務教育的實施而設立，初名「員新國中」，後更名為「明倫國中」。同年9月由首任校長蕭傳明主持招生，初期校舍尚未完工，暫借他處上課。學校座落於百果山麓，創校過程獲得地方士紳的直接贊助支持。民國66年（1977年）劉明昭校長到任後，帶領學校學業表現躍升，成為彰化縣內首屈一指的學校之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛰️ At the Foot of Baiguoshan

座落百果山麓

The campus sits at the base of Baiguoshan, a well-known local hill in Yuanlin, giving the school a distinctive setting within the city.

🤝 Community-Sponsored Founding

士紳贊助創校

Local community gentry directly sponsored and supported the school's establishment in 1968, reflecting strong grassroots investment in Yuanlin's education at the time.

📈 A Rise to Academic Prominence

學業表現躍升

Under principal Liu Mingzhao, who took office in 1977, the school's academic results climbed to rank among the best in Changhua County.

🏫 A Renamed Beginning

從員新到明倫

Originally named "Yuanxin Junior High," the school was renamed Minglun soon after its 1968 founding, a small piece of local history behind its current identity.
