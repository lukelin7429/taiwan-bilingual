About · 學校簡介

A tree that has outlived a century of students.

Ruimei Elementary dates back to 1905 under Japanese rule, cited as 115 years old in a 2020 source — making it one of the oldest schools in Ruisui Township. Its most distinctive feature is a nationally-recognized "most beautiful campus tree," a century-old black pine reportedly planted by the school's very first generation of students and teachers, which still stands on campus today as a living link to the school's founding. The school's international profile grew further when French artist "Monsieur Julien" visited as part of a global mural and painting project, and in 2021 it added a creative playground for younger students.

瑞美國小校史可追溯至明治38年（1905年），2020年資料顯示已有115年歷史，是瑞穗鄉歷史最悠久的學校之一。學校最引以為傲的特色，是一棵獲全國肯定的「最美校樹」——一棵據傳由創校第一屆師生親手栽種的百年黑松，至今仍矗立校園中，成為連結創校歷史的活見證。學校的國際知名度也因法國藝術家「朱利安先生」造訪並參與全球壁畫計畫而提升，並於民國110年（2021年）增設創意遊戲場，供低年級學童使用。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌲 A Century-Old Black Pine

全國最美校樹

Ruimei Elementary's black pine, reportedly planted by the school's founding students and teachers, earned national recognition as one of Taiwan's "most beautiful campus trees" — a living monument to the school's history.

🎨 A Visit From "Monsieur Julien"

法國藝術家壁畫計畫

The school welcomed French artist "Monsieur Julien" as part of a global mural and painting project, bringing an unexpected international creative exchange to this small rural campus.

🛝 A New Creative Playground

2021年創意遊戲場

Added in 2021, the school's creative playground gives younger students a modern, imaginative space to play, part of ongoing campus improvements.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Ruisui's hot spring and river country.

🌲

Ruimei Elementary sits within Ruisui Township, well known throughout Taiwan for its hot springs, papaya milk, and Xiuguluan River rafting — its century-old black pine standing as a quiet counterpoint to the township's more famous natural attractions.
