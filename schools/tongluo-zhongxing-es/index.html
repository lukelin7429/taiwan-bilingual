About · 學校簡介

A village school amid golden persimmon fields.

Tongluo Zhongxing Elementary is located in Zhongping Village, one of the villages that make up Tongluo Township in Miaoli County. The township is one of Taiwan's most recognized centers of Hakka persimmon-drying (柿餅) craft, and the school's community is closely tied to this seasonal agricultural tradition that draws visitors from across the island each autumn.

中興國小位於苗栗縣銅鑼鄉中平村。銅鑼鄉是台灣最知名的客家柿餅工藝重鎮之一，學校所在的社區與這項每逢秋季吸引全台遊客造訪的季節性農業傳統緊密相連。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📍 Rooted in Zhongping Village

位於中平村

The school serves the community of Zhongping Village, one of the rural villages that together make up Tongluo Township in Miaoli's hillside farming country.

🟠 Persimmon-Drying Heritage

柿餅工藝之鄉

Students grow up in a township celebrated across Taiwan for its Hakka persimmon-drying craft, an autumn tradition of laying out golden persimmons to dry that shapes local life.

🌾 A Hakka Farming Community

客家農村文化

As a small village school in Tongluo, Zhongxing reflects the rhythms of a rural Hakka farming township whose identity is inseparable from its agricultural calendar.
