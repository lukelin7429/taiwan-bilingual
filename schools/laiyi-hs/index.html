Pingtung County Laiyi Senior High School

屏東縣立來義高級中學

An Indigenous Paiwan experimental school at the center of a mountain community, combining junior and senior high divisions into one institution since 2002.

About · 學校簡介

One school, two divisions, a Paiwan community's anchor.

A preparatory office for the school was established in 1967, and it officially opened in August 1968 as Pingtung County Laiyi Junior High School. In 2002, the school was reorganized into a full comprehensive institution combining both junior high and senior high divisions — meaning that "來義國中" (Laiyi Junior High) is not a separate present-day school, but simply the junior-high division of this same institution. The school is designated as an Indigenous (Paiwan) experimental education school, with programs in technology, indigenous humanities, indigenous arts, and athletics talent cultivation, including two regular senior-high classes, one sports class, and an arts program added in 2004. Its school motto is 誠．正．勤．樸 (Sincerity, Righteousness, Diligence, Simplicity). Though administratively registered in Laiyi Township, the campus itself sits within neighboring Wanluan Township. Over its history the school also spun off two other junior highs that became independent institutions: Chunri Junior High (branch 1970, independent 1971) and Taiwu Junior High (branch 1973, independent 1977).

學校籌備處成立於民國56年（1967年），並於民國57年（1968年）8月正式創校，初名「屏東縣立來義國民中學」。民國91年（2002年），學校改制為兼具國中部與高中部的完全中學——也就是說，「來義國中」並非另一所現存學校，而是本校的國中部。學校為原住民（排灣族）實驗教育學校，設有科技、原住民人文、原住民藝術與體育人才培育等發展方向，包含兩班高中普通班、一班體育班，並於民國93年（2004年）增設藝術才能班。校訓為「誠．正．勤．樸」。學校雖登記於來義鄉，但校址實際座落於鄰近的萬巒鄉。歷史上，本校也曾分出春日國中（民國59年設分班，60年獨立）與泰武國中（民國62年設分班，66年獨立）兩校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Junior and Senior High, United

國中部與高中部合一

Since the 2002 reorganization, the former Laiyi Junior High School has operated as the junior-high division of this single comprehensive institution — there is no separate present-day "來義國中."

🪶 An Indigenous Experimental School

原住民實驗教育學校

Designated for Paiwan indigenous experimental education, the school builds its curriculum around technology, indigenous humanities, indigenous arts, and athletics talent cultivation.

🎨 An Arts Program Since 2004

2004年增設藝術才能班

Alongside two regular senior-high classes and a sports class, the school added a dedicated arts talent program in 2004, reflecting its focus on indigenous arts education.

🌱 A School That Grew Two More

分出兩所獨立學校

The school's branch-school program produced two now-independent institutions — Chunri Junior High (independent 1971) and Taiwu Junior High (independent 1977) — a mark of its role as an educational anchor for the region.

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the center of a Paiwan mountain community.

🏔️

The school sits at the center of a Paiwan indigenous mountain community in Laiyi Township, one of Pingtung's Paiwan-majority townships in the foothills of the Central Mountain Range. Laiyi is home to the largest Paiwan indigenous population of any third-level administrative division in Taiwan, and the school's designation as an Indigenous experimental school reflects that deep cultural setting. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
