About · 學校簡介

From a business junior college to a science and technology university.

Wufeng University of Science and Technology traces back to Wufeng Junior College of Business, established in September 1963 and approved for enrollment in October 1965. In August 2010, it was upgraded and renamed Wufeng University of Science and Technology. The school sits on a roughly 32.8-acre campus in Minsyong Township and today enrolls around 2,258 day-division students with 119 full-time faculty, organized across colleges spanning safety engineering, digital management, tourism and hospitality, and medical and health sciences.

吳鳳科技大學前身為吳鳳商業專科學校，創立於民國52年（1963年）9月，54年（1965年）10月奉准招生。民國99年（2010年）8月改制升格為吳鳳科技大學。校地約32.8英畝，座落於民雄鄉，目前日間部學生約2,258人，專任教師119人，設有安全工程、數位管理、觀光休閒、醫學健康等相關學院。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A name tied to a reassessed regional legend.

📜

The university's name is popularly linked to the Wu Feng legend — a once widely-taught folk story from the Chiayi/Alishan area about a Qing-era interpreter said to have sacrificed himself to end headhunting among the local Indigenous Tsou people. The legend has since been substantially reassessed and discredited by historians and Indigenous advocates as ethnocentric myth. The university's own current materials instead explain its name through classical literary allusions symbolizing harmony — but the name still carries the complex regional history of the Chiayi/Minsyong area. See the Chiayi County page for more on the region.
