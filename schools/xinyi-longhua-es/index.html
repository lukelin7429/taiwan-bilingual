About · 學校簡介

A rebuilt school on the road to Yushan.

Longhua Elementary sits in Shenmu (Sacred Tree) Village, deep in Xinyi Township — the mountain township that serves as the main gateway to Yushan National Park, home of Taiwan's tallest peak. After its original campus was damaged, the school was rebuilt to continue serving the small, remote community. Alongside its elementary classes, Longhua also runs a kindergarten program, making it a full early-childhood-through-elementary hub for local families.

隆華國小位於信義鄉神木村，深處通往玉山國家公園的山區道路旁，是台灣最高峰玉山的主要門戶鄉鎮之一。學校原有校舍曾經受損，後來重建，持續服務這個偏遠的山區社群。除了國小部課程外，隆華國小也附設幼兒園，為當地家庭提供從學前教育到國小教育的完整銜接。
