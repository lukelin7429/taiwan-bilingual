About · 學校簡介

From a private classics academy to a public school.

Zhonghu Elementary traces its origins to 1929, when it began as a private Chinese-classics academy known as a “hanwen shufang” (漢文書房) — an informal community school teaching classical Chinese texts, common in rural Taiwan before the public education system matured. It was reorganized and renamed Zhonghu Elementary School in 1948, and remains one of the smaller, older schools serving Yingge District today.

中湖國小的歷史可追溯至民國18年（1929年），最初以私塾「漢文書房」形式設立，教授漢文經典，是當時台灣鄉間常見的非正式社區教育型態。民國37年（1948年）改制並更名為中湖國民小學，至今仍是鶯歌地區歷史較悠久的小型學校之一。
