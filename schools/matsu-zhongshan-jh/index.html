About · 學校簡介

Named for Sun Yat-sen, reshaped over decades.

Zhongshan Junior High School traces its roots to Tanqi Elementary School, established in 1952, and was formally founded as "Zhongshan Junior High School" in 1968 at Banli Village to help implement nine-year compulsory education. It merged with Xingshi Elementary in 1974 to become "Zhongshan Junior-Elementary School," with the junior high division relocating to its current site in Qingbi Village in 1976 while the elementary section moved to Banli. Through a restructuring between 1980 and 1985, the school became the independent Zhongshan Junior High School it is today. The school's anniversary is celebrated on November 12 — the birthday of Sun Yat-sen, its namesake.

中山國民中學前身可追溯至民國41年（1952年）設立的塘岐國小，民國57年（1968年）在坂里村正式創設「中山國民中學」，以配合九年國民義務教育之推行。民國63年（1974年）與馨山國小合併，改稱「中山國民中小學」，國中部於民國65年（1976年）遷至現址青帆村，國小部則遷往坂里。經過民國69年至74年（1980–1985年）的改制，學校成為現今獨立的中山國民中學。學校校慶訂於11月12日，也就是校名由來——國父孫中山先生的誕辰。

🔄 Reshaped Through Decades of Reform

歷經多次改制

From its 1968 founding through a 1974 merger and a 1980-1985 restructuring, the school changed form repeatedly on the path to becoming today's independent junior high.

🏞️ A Campus in Scenic Qingbi Village

Local Culture · 在地文化

A Beigan school shaped by Matsu's era of change.

⛵

The school sits in scenic Qingbi (Chinbi) Village on Beigan Island, one of Matsu's most picturesque traditional harbor settlements. While no explicit frontline military history is recorded on the school's own site, its founding and restructuring took place during the same decades as Matsu's military governance era — the broader backdrop against which all of the archipelago's schools grew.
