About · 學校簡介

More than a century on the Lanyang Plain.

Wujie Elementary opened in 1911, during the Japanese colonial era, as the "Luodong Public School Wujie Branch." It was later renamed Wujie Public School and then Wujie National School, and recently passed its 110th anniversary. The school's motto, "五育齊全、結朋共學" — a wordplay built from the characters in the name Wujie — captures its emphasis on well-rounded development and learning together. Locally, the school is nicknamed "the pearl of the Lanyang Plain," a reference to an old camphor and parasol tree on its grounds. Today it runs an active brass band program and offers both Indigenous and Hokkien mother-tongue instruction.

五結國小創立於日治時期的民國前1年（1911年），最初名為「羅東公學校五結分校」，其後改稱五結公學校、五結國民學校，近年剛度過建校110週年。學校校訓「五育齊全、結朋共學」巧妙運用「五結」二字，展現學校對五育均衡發展與共學精神的重視。當地人暱稱這所學校為「蘭陽平原上的珍珠」，典故來自校園內一棵老樟樹與掌葉蘋婆樹的傳說。如今學校設有活躍的管樂團，並開設原住民族語與台語母語教學課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💎 The Pearl of the Lanyang Plain

蘭陽平原上的珍珠

The school's nickname comes from a legendary old tree on campus, a natural landmark that has become part of the school's local identity.

🎺 An Active Brass Band

管樂團活躍演出

Wujie Elementary maintains an active brass band program, giving students a hands-on outlet for music education alongside their regular studies.

🗣️ Mother-Tongue Instruction

族語與台語教學

The school offers both Indigenous and Hokkien mother-tongue instruction, supporting students in maintaining ties to their family languages.

📜 Over a Century of History

建校超過百年

Founded in 1911 under Japanese rule, Wujie Elementary recently passed its 110th anniversary, making it one of the oldest schools on the Lanyang Plain.
