About · 學校簡介

Over a century on Highway 11.

Sanmin Elementary traces its founding to 1905, during the Japanese colonial era, making it more than a century old and one of the founding schools of the Xingang/Chenggong fishing region. The school sits along Provincial Highway 11, the coastal road that links the towns of Taitung's east coast. In 2015, after enrollment at nearby Heping Elementary dropped too low to sustain an independent campus, Heping was absorbed into Sanmin as a branch campus.

三民國小創校於1905年（日治時期），至今已逾百年歷史，是新港（成功）漁鎮地區的創始學校之一。學校座落於臺9線／台11線沿海公路一帶，串聯臺東東海岸各鄉鎮。民國104年（2015年），鄰近的和平國小因學生人數過少，難以維持獨立校務，遂併入三民國小成為分校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Over a Century of History

逾百年校史

Founded in 1905 under Japanese rule, Sanmin is one of the oldest schools in the Xingang/Chenggong area, predating the region's modern fishing port by decades.

🛣️ Along the Coastal Highway

濱海公路上的學校

The school sits on Provincial Highway 11, the scenic coastal road running the length of Taitung's east coast, connecting it to neighboring fishing and farming communities.

🤝 A Merged Branch Campus

和平分校併入

In 2015, Sanmin absorbed the nearby Heping Elementary School as a branch campus after Heping's enrollment became too small to sustain, a common pattern for small rural schools along this coast.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A century in the Xingang fishing region.

🏘️

As a school founded in 1905 during the Japanese colonial era, Sanmin reflects the long history of settlement in the Xingang/Chenggong fishing region, long before the modern harbor was completed in 1932 and the town later took its present name.
