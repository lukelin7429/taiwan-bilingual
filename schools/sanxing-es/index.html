Sanxing Elementary School

三星國小

About · 學校簡介

A name that remembers Bali-sha.

Sanxing Elementary opened in 1909 during the Japanese colonial era as the "Luodong Public School Bali-sha Branch," becoming independent in 1913 as "Bali-sha Public School." It was renamed "Sanxing Public School" in 1921 and took its current name by 1968. The school now brands itself "百年三星" — "Century-old Sanxing" — and its historic Bali-sha (叭哩沙) name preserves the indigenous Kavalan-era place name for the area, predating the Chinese-language "Sanxing" designation.

三星國小創立於日治時期民國前3年（1909年），初名「羅東公學校叭哩沙分校」，民國2年（1913年）獨立設校，稱「叭哩沙公學校」。民國10年（1921年）更名「三星公學校」，至民國57年（1968年）前後定為現名。學校如今以「百年三星」自我標榜，其歷史名稱「叭哩沙」則保留了噶瑪蘭族時代此地的原住民地名，早於漢語「三星」之名。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💯 Proudly "Century-old Sanxing"

自我標榜「百年三星」

The school markets itself directly with the slogan "百年三星," making its centenarian history a core part of its identity in the community.

🗣️ A Name From the Kavalan Era

叭哩沙——噶瑪蘭語地名

Its original name, "Bali-sha" (叭哩沙), preserves the indigenous Kavalan-era place name for the region, a linguistic link to Yilan's pre-colonial history.

🏫 Multiple Renamings Across Eras

歷經多次更名

From "Bali-sha Public School" to "Sanxing Public School" to its current name, the school's several renamings trace the political and linguistic shifts of 20th-century Taiwan.

🧅 The Scallion Township's Namesake School

與蔥鄉同名

As the township's original central school, Sanxing Elementary directly invokes the area's signature crop and identity through its very name.
