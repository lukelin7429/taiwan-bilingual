About · 學校簡介

A century of teaching along the old rail corridor.

Da Du Elementary traces its history back roughly ninety years, likely to the Japanese colonial era around 1933–1934, based on a recent 90th-anniversary observance (the exact founding year is not firmly documented). The school sits along the Zhongfeng Road corridor in Hengshan Township near the Neiwan rail line, serving families connected to the area's old-street and farming communities. It runs an "English Village" immersion program and international exchange opportunities, alongside an attached preschool division and a library open to the wider community.

大肚國小校史已逾九十年，依照近年舉辦的建校90週年活動推算，創校時間可能落在日治時期約1933至1934年間（確切年份尚待考證）。學校座落於橫山鄉中豐路一帶，鄰近內灣鐵路支線，服務與當地老街及農村社區相連的家庭。學校設有「英語村」情境式學習課程與國際交流機會，並附設幼兒園與對社區開放的圖書館。

🏫 An English Village on Campus

音樂與美術競賽獲獎

Local Culture · 在地文化

Near the old branch line.

🚉

Da Du Elementary lies along the Zhongfeng Road corridor in Hengshan Township, close to the historic Neiwan branch railway and its famous old street — placing the school within a farming and tourism community shaped by more than a century of rail history.
