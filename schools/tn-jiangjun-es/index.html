About · 學校簡介

A century-old school named for a legendary general.

Jiangjun Elementary was founded in 1921 and celebrated its 100th anniversary on April 3, 2021, an occasion attended by Tainan Mayor Huang Wei-che, with even a 100-year-old alumnus returning to cut the anniversary cake. The school's student taiko drum team won the top "特優" honor at Tainan's 2025 (114學年度) traditional-arts competition for primary and secondary schools. The school takes its name from the same "General" (將軍) legend that names the district itself: local lore holds that Qing dynasty admiral Shi Lang, granted land here after defeating the Ming-loyalist Zheng regime in 1683, built his general's estate ("將軍府") in what became Jiangjun village — right where this century-old school now stands.

將軍國小創校於民國10年（1921年），2021年4月3日舉辦建校百年校慶，台南市長黃偉哲親臨現場，甚至有百歲校友返校切蛋糕慶祝。學校的太鼓隊在台南市114學年度中小學傳統藝術比賽中榮獲「特優」最高榮譽。學校校名與所在的「將軍」地名同源——相傳清代水師提督施琅於1683年擊敗明鄭政權後獲賜此地，在今日的將軍村建立「將軍府」，而這座百年老校正座落於此。
