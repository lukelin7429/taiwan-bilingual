About · 學校簡介

One of Luodong's founding schools.

Luodong Elementary traces its roots to 1898, when it opened during the Japanese colonial era as the "Yilan Mandarin Training Institute Luodong Branch." The school relocated to its current site in 1907 and is now recognized as one of Yilan County's official "century-old schools." In 2020, it completed a colorful "dream playground" renovation, and it maintains sister-school partnerships with schools in Japan, established in 2007, and Sweden, established in 2019.

羅東國小創校歷史可追溯至日治時期民國前14年（1898年），初名「宜蘭國語傳習所羅東分教場」。學校於民國前5年（1907年）遷至現址，如今是宜蘭縣官方認定的「百年老校」之一。民國109年（2020年）完成色彩繽紛的「夢想遊樂場」改建工程，並分別於民國96年（2007年）與民國108年（2019年）與日本及瑞典的學校締結姊妹校關係。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏆 A Designated Century-Old School

縣府認定百年老校

Founded in 1898, Luodong Elementary is officially recognized among Yilan County's century-old schools, among the oldest educational institutions in the region.

🌏 Sister Schools in Japan and Sweden

日本、瑞典姊妹校

International partnerships established in 2007 (Japan) and 2019 (Sweden) give students rare cross-cultural exchange opportunities for an elementary school.

🎡 A "Dream Playground" Renovation

夢想遊樂場改建

Completed in 2020, the school's colorful playground renovation brought a fresh, modern look to a campus over a century old.

🏘️ Relocated Early in Its History

1907年遷至現址

The school moved to its present site in 1907, just a few years after founding, and has remained there ever since as Luodong grew around it.

Local Culture · 在地文化

One of Luodong's very oldest institutions.

🏛️

As one of Luodong's very oldest schools, Luodong Elementary embodies the town's long history as an established population and administrative center on the Lanyang Plain.
