About · 學校簡介

The school that once linked four islands.

Wan'an Junior High was established in 1968 to serve four outer islands — Wang'an, Jiangjun, Dongji, and Qimei — initially operating with four branch classrooms across the archipelago. Its Qimei branch was transferred to Magong Junior High's administration in 1970; the Dongji branch closed in 1974; and the Jiangjun branch became a fully independent school (today's Jiangao Junior High) in 1978. In its early years the school grew quickly, from 4 classes and 149 students at founding to 13 classes and 452 students by 1977, reflecting how central it once was to schooling across this remote group of islands.

望安國中創立於民國57年（1968年），設立之初肩負望安、將軍、東吉、七美四座離島的國中教育，初期設有四個分部。民國59年（1970年）七美分部改隸馬公國中；民國63年（1974年）東吉分部裁撤；民國67年（1978年）將軍分部獨立設校（即今日的將澳國中）。創校初期發展迅速，由創校時的4班149人，成長至民國66年（1977年）的13班452人，可見其在這片離島群中曾扮演的核心教育角色。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring education on the Wan'an archipelago.

🛥️

Wan'an Township is a scattered group of remote outlying islands reachable only by boat. Wan'an Junior High's historical role coordinating schooling across Jiangjun, Dongji, and Qimei reflects how these fishing-island communities once had to share limited educational resources across the water.
