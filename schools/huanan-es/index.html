Huanan Elementary School

華南國民小學

A Huatan Township elementary school that grew from a small branch campus, now home to the township's only kindergarten and a growing multicultural curriculum.

從花壇鄉小型分校成長茁壯的國小，設有全鄉唯一的附設幼兒園，並發展多元文化課程。

About · 學校簡介

From an eight-class branch to a full campus.

Huanan Elementary began in 1960 as an eight-class branch school, "Huatan Branch School," covering just 0.3 hectares. It became a fully independent 12-class school on September 16, 1963, under its first principal, Shi Jiongyi, with a new gate, cement grounds, playground equipment, and a water tower on a larger 0.7456-hectare campus. In June 2012, it added the only kindergarten among Huatan Township's elementary schools, and it now offers multicultural and new-immigrant language courses, outdoor education, and summer English camps.

華南國小前身為1960年設立的「花壇分校」，僅有八班、佔地0.3公頃。民國52年（1963年）9月16日獨立設校，首任校長為施烱沂，佔地擴大為0.7456公頃，新建校門、水泥球場、遊樂設施與水塔，成為12班的完整學校。2012年6月，增設全花壇鄉國小中唯一的附設幼兒園，並開設多元文化與新住民語言課程（如越南語）、豐富的戶外教育及暑期英語營隊。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🧒 Huatan's Only School Kindergarten

全鄉唯一附設幼兒園

In June 2012, Huanan added the only kindergarten among Huatan Township's elementary schools, extending its educational reach to the community's youngest learners.

🌏 A Multicultural Classroom

新住民語言課程

The school offers multicultural and new-immigrant language courses, including Vietnamese, reflecting the diversity of families in the Huatan community.

🏕️ Outdoor Education and Summer Camps

戶外教育與英語營隊

Extensive outdoor education programs and summer English and potential-development camps give students hands-on learning beyond the regular classroom.

🎉 Six Decades and Growing

61週年校慶運動會

The school celebrated its 61st anniversary sports meet with a rich variety of talent and competition events, marking six decades of steady growth since 1963.
