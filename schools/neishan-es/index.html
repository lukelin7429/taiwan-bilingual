About · 學校簡介

Daxi's second-oldest school.

Neizha Elementary traces back to 1901, during the Japanese colonial era, when it opened as a branch of Dakekan Public School near the local Renan Temple (仁安宮). It became independent in 1925 as Neizha Public School, was renamed Neizha Public School again in 1947, and took its current name in 1967 — making it, at over 120 years old, Daxi District's second-oldest school. The campus maintains its own artifacts museum preserving local historical items, has earned Taiwan-US Green Flag Eco-School certification, runs a taiko-drumming arts program, and opened a new campus building, “Fengyun Building” (楓耘樓), in December 2024.

內柵國小創立於1901年（明治34年）日治時期，最初為大科崁公學校在仁安宮旁設立的「內柵分教場」，1925年獨立設校，1947年更名為內柵國民學校，1967年定名為內柵國民小學，創校逾120年，是大溪區第二古老的學校。校內設有「內柵國小文物館」，保存地方文物；並獲台美生態學校綠旗認證，推行太鼓藝術課程，新建校舍「楓耘樓」於2024年12月啟用。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Daxi's second-oldest school.

⛩️

Founded beside 仁安宮, the local temple, Neizha Elementary is a textbook example of how Daxi's rural hamlets historically organized around a shared temple before establishing their own schools — a pattern visible across many of the district's oldest communities.
