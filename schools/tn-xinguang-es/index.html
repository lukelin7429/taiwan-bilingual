Xinguang Elementary School

新光國小

A small public elementary school in an outlying Guanmiao hamlet, independent since 1966 with a stable, well-documented line of principals.

位於關廟外圍聚落的小型公立國小，1966年獨立設校，校史文獻記錄完整，歷任校長傳承穩定。

About · 學校簡介

A branch class that grew into its own school.

Xinguang Elementary School began life in 1947 as a branch class of Guanmiao Elementary School known as the "Gongli branch," became a full branch school in 1958, and gained full independence as Xinguang National School in 1966. Since independence, the school has had a long, well-documented succession of ten-plus principals, reflecting a stable multi-decade institutional history despite its small-town setting.

新光國小前身為民國36年（1947年）設立的關廟國小「公埔分班」，於民國47年（1958年）升格為分校，並於民國55年（1966年）獨立設校，命名為新光國民學校。獨立設校以來，歷經十餘任校長，校史文獻記錄完整，展現這所小型鄉間學校歷經數十年仍保持穩定發展的軌跡。

Local Culture · 在地文化

One thread in a patchwork of village schools.

Xinguang serves one of Guanmiao's outlying farming hamlets, part of the patchwork of small village schools — including Shenkeng and others — that grew out of Guanmiao Elementary as the district's bamboo-shoot-growing countryside was settled and its population spread into scattered hamlets.
