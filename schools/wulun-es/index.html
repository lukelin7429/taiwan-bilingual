About · 學校簡介

A school that grew with its community.

Wulun Elementary began as a small school beside the Dawulun Industrial Park before relocating to its current site in the 1990s as the surrounding community developed. The school celebrated its 72nd anniversary in 2025, suggesting founding roots reaching back to around 1953. Among its many extracurricular offerings, Wulun runs a dedicated "海洋課程" (Ocean Curriculum), connecting students to Keelung's maritime environment as part of their regular schooling.

武崙國小最初是位於大武崙工業區旁的小型學校，隨著周邊社區發展，於1990年代遷至現址。學校於2025年慶祝建校72週年，推算創校年代約在民國42年（1953年）前後。除多元社團活動外，學校也開設專屬的「海洋課程」，讓學生在日常學習中親近基隆的海洋環境。
