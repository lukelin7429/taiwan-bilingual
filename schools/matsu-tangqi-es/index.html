About · 學校簡介

The center school that once ran all of Beigan's education.

Tang Chi Elementary School was formally established in July 1952 as the educational center school for the entirety of Beigan Township, originally overseeing six branch schools at Houwo, Dazhu, Qiaozi, Qingbi, Banli, and Baisha. Those branch schools separated into independent institutions through 1960 and 1961, and the Houwo branch closed in 1970, consolidating its students at the main campus. Building reconstruction and new administrative offices were added between 1975 and 1986, an attached kindergarten opened in 1986, a new campus was completed in 2009, and the kindergarten expanded to accept children age two and older by 2014.

塘岐國民小學於民國41年（1952年）7月正式設立，作為北竿鄉的教育中心學校，最初統轄後澳、大澳、橋子、青帆、坂里、白沙六所分校。這些分校於民國49年至50年（1960–1961年）間陸續獨立設校，後澳分校則於民國59年（1970年）裁併，學生併入本校就讀。民國64年至75年（1975–1986年）陸續進行校舍重建與增建行政辦公室，民國75年（1986年）附設幼稚園成立，民國98年（2009年）完成新校舍，民國103年（2014年）幼兒園擴大招收2歲以上幼兒。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Once Oversaw Six Branch Schools

曾統轄六所分校

As Beigan Township's original center school, Tang Chi once ran six branch schools scattered across the island — Houwo, Dazhu, Qiaozi, Qingbi, Banli, and Baisha — before each grew into its own independent school.

🏗️ A New Campus in 2009

2009年完工新校舍

Following decades of building reconstruction from the 1970s through the 1980s, the school completed an entirely new campus in 2009.

🧒 Early Childhood Education Since 1986

附設幼兒園

An attached kindergarten opened in 1986 and later expanded in 2014 to accept children as young as age two, extending the school's role in the community.
