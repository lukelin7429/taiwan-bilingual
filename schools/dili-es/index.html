About · 學校簡介

A century of teaching, weaving, and roots.

Dili Elementary School was founded in 1923 and celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2023, making it one of the longest-standing schools in Xinyi Township's Bunun tribal school district. Located beside the Zhuoshui River in the northern part of the township, the school has built its curriculum around cultural preservation, teaching traditional Bunun weaving and dyeing arts alongside native-language recitation, and running "seeking ancestral roots" heritage programs that connect students to their tribal history.

地利國小創立於民國12年（1923年），並於2023年慶祝建校百年，是信義鄉布農族學區歷史最悠久的學校之一。學校位於鄉境北側的濁水溪畔，課程規劃著重文化傳承，將布農族傳統織布與染織技藝，以及族語朗讀融入教學，並推動「尋根」文化傳承活動，讓學生與部落歷史保持連結。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎊 A Full Century of Education

建校百年

Dili Elementary celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2023, a century of continuous service to this Bunun mountain community.

🧵 Weaving and Dyeing as Curriculum

傳統織布染織課程

Traditional Bunun weaving and dyeing arts are taught as part of the school's core curriculum, keeping tribal craft knowledge alive for a new generation.

🌱 Seeking Ancestral Roots

尋根文化傳承

The school's "seeking ancestral roots" heritage programs help students trace and understand their family and tribal history firsthand.

🗣️ Native-Language Recitation

族語朗讀教學

Native-language recitation is woven into daily learning, reinforcing Bunun linguistic tradition alongside the school's craft-based curriculum.

Located in a Bunun tribal school district beside the Zhuoshui River (濁水溪), Dili teaches traditional Bunun weaving and dyeing as part of its core curriculum, reflecting Xinyi's identity as Taiwan's Bunun heartland. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
