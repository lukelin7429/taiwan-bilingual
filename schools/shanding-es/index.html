Shanding Elementary School

山頂國小

Founded in 1965 to coincide with a military housing-relocation program, Shanding Elementary reflects Guishan's history as a settlement area for military dependents' villages.

About · 學校簡介

Built for a military housing plan.

Shanding Elementary was founded on August 1, 1965, established to coincide with the military's 'Dingxing Plan' (鼎興計畫), a housing and relocation program. A new 14-classroom building went up for bid in May 1966, students moved into the new campus that August, and full-day classes began by December 1966. The school was designated a 'dental-hygiene demonstration school' in 1971, and celebrated its 50th anniversary with a visit from the Taoyuan mayor.

山頂國小創校於1965年8月1日，因應軍方「鼎興計畫」眷舍安置而設立。1966年5月新建14間教室校舍發包，同年8月師生遷入新校舍，12月起全面實施全天上課。1971年獲指定為「牙齒衛生示範學校」，並曾於建校50週年時獲桃園市長蒞校祝賀。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏘️ Born From a Military Housing Plan

因鼎興計畫而設校

The school's founding in 1965 coincided directly with the military's 'Dingxing Plan' housing and relocation program, tying it to Guishan's mid-century military-village history.

🦷 A Dental-Hygiene Demonstration School

牙齒衛生示範學校

In 1971, Shanding was designated a dental-hygiene demonstration school, part of a broader public-health push in Taiwan's elementary schools at the time.

🏫 A Fast-Built Campus

快速興建的校舍

Within about a year of founding, a new 14-classroom building was bid, built, and occupied — students moved in by August 1966 and began full-day classes that December.
