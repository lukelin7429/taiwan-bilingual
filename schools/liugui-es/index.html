Liouguei Elementary School

六龜國民小學

A century-old school named for six turtle-shaped limestone hills, its campus decorated with ceramic murals of the Laonong River landscape.

因六座龜形石灰岩山丘得名的百年老校，校園以陶瓷壁畫描繪荖濃溪風光。

About · 學校簡介

Six turtles, one century-old school.

Liouguei Elementary was founded in 1912 under Japanese rule as a branch school of Fanshuliao Public School (蕃薯寮公學校六龜里分校). Its campus today blends Roman-column architecture with local motifs, and features ceramic murals depicting the 'Six Turtles' landscape and the Laonong River — the very landmarks that gave the district its name.

六龜國小創立於民國1年（1912年）日治時期，原為「蕃薯寮公學校六龜里分校」。現今校園融合羅馬柱式建築與在地元素，並以陶瓷壁畫描繪「六龜」地景與荖濃溪風光——正是為此地命名的地標。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🐢 Named for Six Turtle-Shaped Hills

六座龜形山丘命名

'六龜' (Six Turtles) refers to six turtle-shaped limestone hills along the Laonong River, long used as navigation landmarks — the same hills that give both the district and the school their name.

🏛️ Roman Columns Meet Local Motifs

羅馬柱式融合在地元素

The campus architecture blends Roman-column design with local motifs, an unusual pairing for a rural mountain-district school.

🎨 Ceramic Murals of the Laonong River

陶瓷壁畫描繪荖濃溪

Ceramic murals across the campus depict the 'Six Turtles' landscape and the Laonong River, keeping the district's founding landmarks visible to every student.

🍠 From a Sweet-Potato Hut to a School

蕃薯寮舊名典故

The old place-name 'Fanshuliao' (sweet-potato hut) reflects the area's origin as a small agricultural outpost before it grew into today's Liouguei.
