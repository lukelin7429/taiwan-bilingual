Taipei Municipal Renai Elementary School

臺北市立仁愛國民小學

Opened in 1959 to relieve crowding at nearby Jian'an Elementary, now set on Anhe Road just off the café-lined Yongkang Street district.

About · 學校簡介

Built to ease a neighboring school's overcrowding.

Renai Elementary was founded in August 1959 with just 3 classes and 144 students, established specifically to relieve overcrowding at nearby Jian'an Elementary School. Its site had been set aside as far back as a 1952 city land-use plan that also reserved land for what became Renai Junior High. In 1989 the school added sensory-integration classrooms, a central drinking-water system, and a resurfaced PU running track, and from 2006 to 2017 it ran a formal student-exchange partnership with an American international school.

仁愛國小創立於民國48年（1959年）8月，開校時僅3班、144名學生，設校目的是為紓解鄰近建安國小的擁擠情形。校地早在1952年市政府的土地規劃中即已預留，同一計畫也劃出了後來仁愛國中的用地。1989年，學校增建感覺統合教室、中央飲水系統，並重鋪PU跑道。2006年至2017年間，學校更曾與一所美國國際學校進行正式的學生交流合作。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A short walk from Yongkang Street.

☕

Located on Anhe Road just off the Yongkang Street café and boutique district, Renai Elementary serves one of Da'an's most walkable, café-dense residential neighborhoods — a pocket of the district where students grow up a few minutes' walk from some of Taipei's best-loved teahouses and bakeries.
