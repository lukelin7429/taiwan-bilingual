About · 學校簡介

A century in Beigang's farming outskirts.

Haoshou Elementary was founded on April 10, 1922, under Japanese rule as a public school, and was renamed Haoshou National School under ROC administration in 1950. Today the school operates a branch campus, Yuying Elementary, in the neighboring village of Fangou. Over its century-long history, it has remained rooted in Beigang's rural, agricultural outskirts, serving generations of local farming families.

好收國小創立於大正11年（1922年）4月10日，日治時期以公學校之姿設立，民國39年（1950年）更名為「好收國民學校」。如今學校於鄰近的番溝里設有育英分校。歷經百年校史，好收國小始終扎根於北港的農村邊陲地帶，服務世代務農的地方家庭。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A century feeding into Beigang's pilgrimage identity.

🛕

A century-old school in Beigang's farming outskirts, it has long fed into the town's broader identity as a Mazu pilgrimage hub centered on Chaotian Temple. Generations of families in Haoshou's rural catchment area have combined farming livelihoods with the religious and commercial life of the town center.
