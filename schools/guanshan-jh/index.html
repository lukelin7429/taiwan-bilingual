About · 學校簡介

From Guanshan Junior High to the "Dreamer" school.

Guanshan Junior High opened in 1968, its roots tracing back to the junior-high division of a provincial school branch established in 1955. Around 2016, the school reinvented itself, converting to an experimental education model and rebranding as "關山夢想家實驗國民中學" — Guanshan Dreamer Experimental Junior High School. The new curriculum emphasizes maker education and hands-on, project-based learning, including weekly coding classes for seventh and eighth graders, marking a significant departure from the standard junior-high format in this rice-farming town.

關山國中創立於民國57年（1968年），校史可追溯至民國44年（1955年）設立的省立學校分部之國中部。約在民國105年（2016年）前後，學校進行轉型，改採實驗教育模式，並更名為「關山夢想家實驗國民中學」。新課程強調自造者教育與動手實作、專題式學習，並為七、八年級學生開設每週程式設計課，在這座稻米之鄉走出一條有別於傳統國中的辦學之路。

🛠️ A Maker-Education Model

自造者教育課程

Since converting to an experimental model around 2016, the school has built its curriculum around maker education and hands-on, project-based learning.

💻 Weekly Coding for Every Student

七、八年級程式設計課

Seventh and eighth graders take a weekly coding class as a core part of the curriculum, an unusual commitment to computing education for a small-town junior high.

更名「夢想家」
