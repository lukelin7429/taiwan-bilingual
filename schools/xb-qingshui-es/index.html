About · 學校簡介

The clear-water community that gave its name to a district.

Qingshui Elementary originated in 1921 as a branch classroom of Tucheng Public School, becoming an independent school in 1943 during the final years of the Japanese colonial era. Named for the “clear water” stream area (清水坑) that gave this corner of Tucheng its identity, the school today runs bilingual-education initiatives and a string orchestra program, and served as the original namesake community for the later Qingshui Junior and Senior High School.

清水國小起源於大正10年（1921年）作為土城公學校的分教室，昭和18年（1943年）獨立設校，正值日治末期。學校因「清水坑」——這座溪流地區而得名，也是後來清水國中、清水高中命名的原始社區來源。學校現今推動雙語教育計畫並開設弦樂團課程。
