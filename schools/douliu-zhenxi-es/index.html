Zhenxi Elementary School

鎮西國民小學

A century-old school in the Yunlin county seat, tracing its roots to 1896 and known today as the county's only elementary school with a formal music program.

About · 學校簡介

A lineage stretching back to 1896.

Zhenxi Elementary traces its origins to the Yunlin Mandarin Training Institute (雲林國語傳習所), established in 1896 during the early years of Japanese rule — making it one of Yunlin County's oldest schools by lineage. Over more than a century, the school has grown into a modern institution serving the Douliu city center, distinguished today as the only elementary school in Yunlin County with a formal, dedicated music program, alongside a gifted-education track and a percussion ensemble.

鎮西國小的校史可追溯至1896年（明治29年）設立的雲林國語傳習所，是雲林縣歷史最悠久的學校之一。歷經百餘年發展，如今已成為服務斗六市區的現代化學校，也是雲林縣唯一設有正式音樂班的國小，並設有資優教育班與擊樂團。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Roots in an 1896 Training Institute

源自雲林國語傳習所

The school's lineage traces back to the Yunlin Mandarin Training Institute, founded in 1896 — placing it among the very oldest schools in Yunlin County.

🎵 Yunlin's Only Formal Music Program

全縣唯一音樂班

Zhenxi is the only elementary school in Yunlin County with a dedicated, formal music program, giving students structured training beyond standard classroom music lessons.

🥁 A Percussion Ensemble and Gifted Track

擊樂團與資優教育

Alongside its music program, the school runs a percussion ensemble and a gifted-education program, offering advanced pathways for students with particular strengths.

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the heart of the Yunlin county seat.

Zhenxi Elementary sits in Douliu City, the county seat of Yunlin — a role reflected in the school's deep civic roots and its century-plus of continuous service to the community at the administrative and cultural center of the county. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
