Wanggong Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A new school on old temple ground.

Wanggong Elementary opened on August 1, 2000, one of the newest schools in Linyuan District, following a multi-phase municipal land expropriation process that began in 1978. As of 2022, the school enrolled roughly 687 students across 29 classes with 54 teachers, and it serves the Guangying (廣應里) neighborhood among other parts of the district.

王公國小於民國89年（2000年）8月1日創校，是林園區最新設立的學校之一，其校地經歷自民國67年（1978年）起分階段的市地徵收過程。截至2022年，學校約有687名學生、29個班級、54位教師，服務範圍涵蓋廣應里等地區。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏗️ Two Decades of Land Preparation

歷經多階段徵地

The school's site took shape through a multi-phase municipal land expropriation process starting in 1978, more than two decades before the school itself opened in 2000.

⛩️ Named for a Folk Deity

以「王公」信仰命名

The school's name honors 'Wanggong,' a folk-deity title common in local temple names, reflecting Linyuan's coastal folk-religion traditions.

🏘️ Serving the Guangying Neighborhood

服務廣應里

Among the areas it serves is the Guangying (廣應里) neighborhood, one of several villages within Linyuan District.

📊 A Mid-Sized Student Body

中型學校規模

As of 2022, the school enrolled about 687 students across 29 classes with 54 teachers.
