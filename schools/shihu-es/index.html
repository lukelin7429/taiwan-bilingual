About · 學校簡介

Born from a lake's name and a growing neighborhood.

Shihu Elementary School was founded on March 30, 1978, under founding principal Huang Ming-jia, established as an offshoot of neighboring Dingjin Elementary School's rapidly growing catchment area. The school takes its name from 金獅湖 (Golden Lion Lake), a scenic reservoir and park landmark in the district's historic 覆鼎金 (Fudingjin) area. Enrollment grew quickly, reaching over 4,000 students by 1987 — a scale that reflected the fast urbanization of this part of Sanmin District in the 1970s and '80s.

獅湖國小創校於民國67年（1978年）3月30日，首任校長黃明嘉，因鄰近鼎金國小學區人口快速成長而分設。校名取自「金獅湖」——三民區歷史悠久的「覆鼎金」地區內一處風景優美的水庫公園地標。學校招生人數快速成長，至民國76年（1987年）已突破4,000人，反映出1970至80年代這一帶迅速都市化的軌跡。
