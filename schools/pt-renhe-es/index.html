About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school on Zhongshan Road.

Renhe Elementary School serves families along Zhongshan Road in Linbian Township, a coastal community on Pingtung's southwestern coast. The school maintains a dedicated history (學校沿革) page for its community, chronicling its role in local education. Like its neighbors across the township, Renhe Elementary is part of a community still defined by its recovery from historic flooding and its deep ties to coastal aquaculture.

仁和國小服務林邊鄉中山路一帶的居民，坐落於屏東西南沿海的社區。學校設有專屬的「學校沿革」頁面，記錄其在地方教育中的角色。與全鄉其他學校一樣，仁和國小所在的社區至今仍深受歷史性水患的影響，也與沿海養殖產業有著密不可分的關係。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Grouper country on the recovery road.

🐟

Linbian Township's economy centers on coastal aquaculture, especially grouper (石斑魚) farming, and the township carries the legacy of Typhoon Morakot's severe 2009 flooding, which submerged the area under one to two meters of water. Since then, the community has become known for its disaster-recovery resilience and eco-tourism initiatives. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
