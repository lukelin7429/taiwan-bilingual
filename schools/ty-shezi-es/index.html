Shezi Elementary School

社子國民小學

A tiny farmland school pivoting between Xinwu's inland town center, Fugang Railway Station, and Yong'an Fishing Harbor.

About · 學校簡介

A small school at a geographic crossroads.

Shezi Elementary was founded in 1970 and remains one of Xinwu's smaller schools, with about 57 students in one class per grade level plus a kindergarten class, taught by around 10 teachers. Its location is a geographic pivot point for the district: the Xinwu town center lies to the north, Fugang Railway Station to the south, and Yong'an Fishing Harbor to the west, with the school itself surrounded on all sides by farmland.

社子國小創立於1970年（民國59年），至今仍是新屋區規模較小的學校之一，全校約57名學生，每個年級一個班加上一個幼兒園班，共約10位教師任教。地理位置上，學校北望新屋市區，南接富岡車站，西臨永安漁港，四周皆為農田環繞，正處於新屋內陸農業與沿海漁業交會的地理樞紐。
