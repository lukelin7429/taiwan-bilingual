About · 學校簡介

Yilan's pioneering experiment in public-private education.

Founded in 2002, Yilan County Humanity Elementary and Junior High School was Taiwan's first publicly-funded, privately-operated (公辦民營) charter-style school — a landmark in the island's experimental education movement. Rather than teaching separate subjects in isolation, the school built its curriculum around theme-based, integrated learning that spans all nine grades from elementary through junior high in a single campus. In August 2024, the school came under direct administration by the Yilan County government following a change in its operating team, marking a new chapter in its two-decade history.

人文國中小創立於民國91年（2002年），是全台第一所公辦民營的實驗學校，為台灣實驗教育發展的重要里程碑。學校不採傳統分科教學，而是以主題式統整課程貫穿國小到國中共九個年級，國中部與國小部合一於同一校園。民國113年（2024年）8月，因辦學團隊異動，學校改由宜蘭縣政府直接經營管理，展開新的篇章。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Taiwan's First Public-Private School

全台首創公辦民營

Opened in 2002, the school was Taiwan's very first publicly-funded, privately-operated charter-style institution, paving the way for the island's broader experimental education movement.

🧩 Learning Organized Around Themes

主題式統整課程

Instead of standard subject silos, the school pioneered a theme-based, integrated curriculum that connects learning across disciplines rather than teaching them in isolation.

🔗 One Campus, Grades K Through 9

國中小九年一貫

Elementary and junior high divisions share a single campus and continuous educational philosophy, letting students move from early childhood through age fifteen without changing schools.

🔄 A New Chapter Under County Management

縣府直接經營新頁

Since August 2024, the Yilan County government has directly administered the school after a change in its operating team, continuing its experimental mission under public stewardship.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A modern experiment in Yilan's gateway town.

🌱

Located in Toucheng, Yilan's oldest Han settlement town, this experimental school carries forward the same pioneering, boundary-pushing spirit that first brought Han settlers into the region — reinventing what public education can look like nearly two centuries later.
