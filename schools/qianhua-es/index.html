About · 學校簡介

Relocated for a power plant, reinvented for bilingual learning.

Qianhua Elementary opened in 1907 as a branch of Jinbaoli Public School. In the 1970s, the school was forced to relocate when its original campus land was needed for the construction of the Jinshan Nuclear Power Plant — a direct encounter between a traditional farming and fishing hamlet and national energy infrastructure. In 2008, the school launched an "English Magic Academy" bilingual-immersion program, giving this small North Coast school an unusually strong English-learning identity for its size.

乾華國小創立於1907年，最初是金包里公學校的分校。1970年代，因原校地被徵用興建金山核能發電廠，學校被迫遷校——這是傳統農漁聚落與國家能源建設正面相遇的一段歷史。2008年，學校創辦「魔法英語學院」雙語沉浸式課程，讓這所北海岸小校在英語教學上發展出格外鮮明的特色。
