Daxi Elementary School

大溪國小

One of Taoyuan's most historic schools, sitting beside Daxi's famous Old Street where the district's tofu and soy-sauce trades once thrived.

桃園歷史最悠久的學校之一，緊鄰以豆腐、醬油產業聞名的大溪和平老街。

About · 學校簡介

Beside Daxi's Old Street.

Daxi Elementary traces back to 1897, when local gentry Lu Yingyang, Lu Jianbang, and others petitioned to establish a “Taipei Mandarin Training Institute Dakekan Branch” on the old Xinnan Street, formalized in 1898 as Dakekan Public School. Over 125 years old, it is one of Taoyuan's most historic schools. Its Japanese-era teachers' dormitory is a registered historic building under Taiwan's Cultural Heritage system, and the school has carried a succession of names tracking Taiwan's history before celebrating its 120th anniversary in 2017.

大溪國小的歷史可追溯至1897年（明治30年），由地方仕紳呂鷹揚、呂建邦等人於舊新南街發起申設「台北國語傳習所大嵙崁分教場」，1898年正式定名為大嵙崁公學校，創校逾125年，是桃園歷史最悠久的學校之一。校內的日式宿舍已登錄為文化資產保存的歷史建築，校名歷經多次變更，並於2017年舉辦建校120週年校慶。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Beside Daxi's Old Street.

🏮

Sitting directly beside Daxi's famous 和平老街 (Old Street) and 中央路, Daxi Elementary occupies the historic river-port core where the district's baroque shophouse architecture and its tofu and soy-sauce trades all developed, generations before the area became a tourist destination.
