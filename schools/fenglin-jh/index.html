About · 學校簡介

A school born at the hinge of two eras.

Fenglin Junior High evolved out of a Japanese-era vocational agricultural school around the transition from Japanese rule to Republic of China governance in 1945–46, formally taking the name "Fenglin Junior High" in 1968. Its early history carries a weighty and poignant connection to Fenglin's identity as a "town of doctors": physician Zhang Qi-lang served as acting principal of the predecessor agricultural school starting in November 1945, and his son Zhang Zong-ren later became principal of the reorganized junior high. Tragically, Zhang Qi-lang and two of his sons were executed during the 228 Incident — one of the darkest chapters in Taiwan's modern history — making the school's founding story inseparable from Fenglin's own.

鳳林國中的前身是日治時期的農業學校，在1945至1946年間台灣由日本統治過渡至中華民國政府治理之際逐步演變，並於民國57年（1968年）正式定名「鳳林國中」。其早期歷史與鳳林「醫生之鄉」的身分有著沉重而動人的連結：醫師張七郎自民國34年（1945年）11月起代理前身農業學校校長一職，其子張宗仁後來也成為改制後國中的校長。然而，張七郎與其兩名兒子後來在228事件中不幸罹難——這是台灣近代史上最黑暗的篇章之一，使這所學校的創校故事與鳳林鎮的歷史緊密交織，密不可分。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fenglin, the hometown of principals and doctors.

🎓

Fenglin Township (鳳林鎮) is a historic Hakka farming town famously known for producing an unusually high number of school principals and physicians — a legacy commemorated at the town's own "Principal's Dream Factory" (校長夢工廠) museum. This junior high's founding story, tied to the Zhang family of doctors, sits right at the heart of that identity.
