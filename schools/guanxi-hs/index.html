National Guanxi Senior High School

國立關西高級中學

A century-old school that grew from a Japanese-era agricultural training program into a comprehensive senior high in the heart of Hakka tea and persimmon country.

About · 學校簡介

A century of agricultural roots, now a comprehensive high school.

National Guanxi Senior High traces its history to March 1924, when it opened under Japanese rule as the "Taiwan Public Guanxi Agricultural Supplementary School," a two-year program training local farmers' children in agricultural skills. Over the following decades it evolved into a vocational school specializing in agriculture and forestry before converting into a national comprehensive senior high school in 2003. Today it enrolls roughly 861 students across general academic and vocational tracks including horticulture, animal husbandry, food processing, and home economics.

國立關西高中的歷史可追溯至1924年3月，日治時期設立的「臺灣公立關西農業補習學校」，招收當地農家子弟接受兩年制農業技能訓練。歷經數十年演變，逐步發展為以農林為專長的職業學校，並於2003年改制為國立綜合高級中學。現今全校約861名學生，橫跨一般學術科與園藝、畜產、食品加工、家政等職業類科。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 A Century in Agricultural Education

百年農業教育傳承

Founded in 1924 as a two-year agricultural supplementary school, the campus has carried an unbroken thread of farming and land-based education for a full century, now folded into a modern comprehensive curriculum.

🏛️ The Township Education Hall

鄉土教育館

A former principal's residence on campus has been repurposed into a "Township Education Hall," preserving local history for students and visitors alike.

🐄 Hands-On Vocational Tracks

多元職業類科

Alongside general academics, students can pursue horticulture, animal husbandry, food processing, and home economics — practical tracks tied directly to the surrounding farmland.

🍵 Rooted in Hakka Farm Country

客家農村的搖籃

The school's agricultural heritage mirrors the same land that produces Guanxi's famous dried persimmons and Fanlu red tea, connecting classroom learning to local industry.
