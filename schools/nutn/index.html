About · 學校簡介

From a normal school to a comprehensive university.

National University of Tainan (NUTN) traces its history to June 1899, when it was founded under Japanese rule as Tainan Normal School — one of Taiwan's oldest teacher-training institutions. After 1945 it was renamed Taiwan Provincial Tainan Normal School, then upgraded step by step: to Taiwan Provincial Tainan Junior Teachers' College (1962), Taiwan Provincial Tainan Teachers College (1987), and National Tainan Teachers College (1991) once it became a national institution. On August 1, 2004, it was upgraded to full university status and took its present name, completing its transition from a dedicated teacher's college into a comprehensive university.

國立臺南大學創校於1899年（明治32年），日治時期名為臺南師範學校，是台灣歷史最悠久的師資培育機構之一。1945年後更名為臺灣省立臺南師範學校，並逐步改制：1962年升格為臺灣省立臺南師範專科學校，1987年改制臺灣省立臺南師範學院，1991年改隸國立，改稱國立臺南師範學院。2004年8月1日升格為大學，正式定名為國立臺南大學，完成從師範學院到綜合大學的轉型。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Multiple Colleges

多元學院

NUTN is organized into colleges spanning Education, Humanities, Management, Science and Engineering, Arts, and Environmental Sciences and Ecology, reflecting its growth beyond its teacher-training origins.

📚 Two Campus Museums

校內兩座博物館

The campus houses the Bo Yang Museum and the Shiang Yu Museum, giving NUTN a distinctive cultural presence beyond its academic programs.

🌏 International Students Office

國際學生事務

NUTN's English website details a dedicated Chinese-language program and international student services, alongside scholarship information for incoming students.

🏛️ Notable Alumnus

傑出校友

Alumnus Tu Cheng-sheng served as Taiwan's Minister of Education from 2004 to 2008, reflecting the university's deep roots in national education policy.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A campus inside Tainan's old walled city.

🏮

NUTN's main campus sits within walking distance of Tainan Railway Station, inside the old walled-city core near the Confucius Temple and the Fucheng heritage district — placing Taiwan's oldest teacher-training institution at the historic heart of its oldest city.
