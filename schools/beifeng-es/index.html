About · 學校簡介

From a private study hall to a century-old school.

Beifeng Elementary School traces its origins to 1924, when it began as 社后書房, a traditional private study hall in the Shehou (社后) neighborhood — a direct link to the transition from Qing-era private schooling to modern public education in Xizhi. It became an independent public school in 1946 as Taipei County Beishan Elementary, taking its current name, Beifeng, in 1948. The school celebrated its 100th anniversary in May 2024, and has built a strong reputation in sports, including dodgeball and swimming, and canoeing — its canoe-sprint team was national champion from 2019 to 2021.

北峰國小校史可追溯至日治時期民國13年（1924年），前身為社后地區的私塾「社后書房」，直接見證了汐止從清代私塾走向現代公立教育的轉型過程。民國35年（1946年）獨立設校，定名「台北縣北山國民學校」，民國37年（1948年）改名為「北峰國民學校」。學校於民國113年（2024年）5月歡慶建校百週年，並在躲避球、游泳等運動項目表現優異，獨木舟競速隊更於民國108至110年（2019至2021年）連續三年榮獲全國冠軍。
