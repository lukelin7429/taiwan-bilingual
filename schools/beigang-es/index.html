About · 學校簡介

A school whose reach climbs into the hills.

Beigang Elementary School opened in 1921 as Beigang Public School, during the Japanese colonial era. Its catchment area is unusually large for an elementary school, stretching roughly 10 kilometers up into the foothills of Wuzhi Mountain, and it historically operated remote branch classes at Baliang and Hongnei to reach children living deep in the hills. This sprawling reach reflects Xizhi's transition from a dense river town into scattered upland hamlets as the district expanded into the mountains.

北港國小創立於日治時期民國10年（1921年），原名「北港公學校」。該校學區範圍在小學中相當罕見地廣大，向上延伸約10公里直達五指山腳下，過去曾在八連、烘內等偏遠地區設有分班，以服務散居山區的孩童。這種廣袤的學區反映了汐止從密集河岸市鎮向五指山麓零散山村擴展的歷史軌跡。
