About · 學校簡介

A university built for medical and health-technology education.

Central Taiwan University of Science and Technology was founded in 1966 (ROC 55) by Dr. Tien-Chi Chen, and reached full university status in 2005. The university specializes in medical and health-technology education, offering programs across three colleges to around 6,000 students.

中臺科技大學創立於民國55年（1966年），由陳天機博士創辦，於民國94年（2005年）升格為大學。學校以醫學及健康科技教育見長，設有三大學院，在學學生約6,000人。
