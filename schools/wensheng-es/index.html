Wensheng Elementary School

新北市板橋區文聖國民小學

About · 學校簡介

Named for the sage and his students.

Wensheng Elementary was established in 1984 as Banqiao's eleventh elementary school, with its first cohort of students moving into the new campus in September 1986. Its name honors Confucius (至聖) and his disciples Yan Hui and Kong Ji, part of a Confucian naming tradition. Today it is a mid-sized urban school of about 1,400 students, with a wind-band ensemble and a table-tennis team.

文聖國小創立於民國73年（1984年），是板橋第11所國小，首屆學生於民國75年（1986年）9月遷入新校舍就讀。校名取自「至聖」孔子及其弟子顏回、孔伋，承襲儒家命名傳統。現為約1,400名學生的中型都會學校，設有管樂團與桌球隊。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📯 A Wind-Band Ensemble

管樂團

Wensheng's wind-band ensemble gives students a structured music program within a mid-sized urban campus of around 1,400 students.

🏓 A Competitive Table-Tennis Team

桌球隊

The school fields a table-tennis team, one of several athletic programs balancing out its music offerings.

📖 Named for Confucius and His Disciples

孔子與弟子命名

The school's name honors Confucius (至聖) along with disciples Yan Hui and Kong Ji, situating it within a wider Confucian naming tradition among Taiwanese schools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A gateway between old Taipei and new Banqiao.

🚇

Sitting near Banqiao's northern edge close to the Jiangzicui MRT station, Wensheng's Confucian naming reflects the area's identity as a gateway between old Taipei and newly urbanizing Banqiao. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
