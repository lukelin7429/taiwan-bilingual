About · 學校簡介

A junior high in Taiwan's grouper country.

Linbian Junior High School sits in Guanglin Village, at the heart of Linbian Township — a coastal community whose fortunes have long been tied to aquaculture. The school maintains a dedicated history page on its official website, preserving its own institutional story even where detailed early records are hard to come by. Like the rest of the township, it has weathered the aftermath of severe flooding and remains a steady presence for local families.

林邊國中位於林邊鄉光林村，地處這個長期依賴養殖漁業維生的沿海鄉鎮核心地帶。學校官網設有專屬的校史頁面，保存了自身的辦學歷程。與全鄉一樣，學校也曾經歷嚴重水患的考驗，至今仍持續穩定服務當地學子與家庭。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Grouper country on the recovery road.

🐟

Linbian Township is a coastal aquaculture community renowned today for grouper (石斑魚) farming, and historically for tiger prawn farming. The township suffered severe flooding during Typhoon Morakot in 2009, when a breach linked to a railway-elevation project let the Linbian River flood the entire area to depths of one to two meters — and has since become known for its disaster-recovery spirit and eco-tourism efforts such as the Qifeng Wetland Park. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
