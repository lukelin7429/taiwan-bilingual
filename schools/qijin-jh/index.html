Cijin Junior High School

旗津國民中學

A junior high on Cijin Island — home to Taiwan's first HACCP-certified school kitchen and a karate team, on the sandbar considered the birthplace of Kaohsiung city.

座落於旗津島上的國中，擁有全台第一座通過HACCP認證的學校廚房與空手道隊，所在沙洲被視為高雄市的發源地。

About · 學校簡介

From the "Tenth Municipal Junior High" to Cijin.

Cijin Junior High School was founded in 1964 as "Municipal Tenth Junior High School," taking its current name in 1968. In 1985, a land transfer expanded the campus to roughly 50,057 square meters, and in 2001 the school opened Taiwan's first HACCP-certified school kitchen. It also fields a karate team.

旗津國民中學創校於民國53年（1964年），初名「市立第十初級中學」，民國57年（1968年）更名為旗津國中。民國74年（1985年）透過土地撥用擴大校地至約50,057平方公尺，並於民國90年（2001年）設立全台第一座通過HACCP認證的學校廚房，校內亦設有空手道隊。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🍱 Taiwan's First HACCP School Kitchen

全台首座HACCP認證廚房

In 2001, the school opened Taiwan's first school kitchen to earn HACCP food-safety certification, a distinction the school still holds up as a point of pride.

🥋 A Karate Team

空手道隊

The school runs a karate team, giving students a competitive martial-arts program alongside academics.

🏫 A Campus That Grew With the Island

1985年校地擴增

A 1985 land transfer expanded the campus to about 50,057 square meters, giving the school room to grow on an island with limited space.

🏛️ From "Tenth Municipal" to Cijin

從第十初中到旗津國中

Founded in 1964 as "Municipal Tenth Junior High School," the school took the name Cijin Junior High in 1968, tying its identity to the island it serves.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The birthplace of Kaohsiung city.

⚓

Cijin District is widely considered the birthplace of Kaohsiung city, home to the historic Qihou and Zhongzhou settlements at the mouth of old Takao Harbor, where Makatao Indigenous people lived some 400 years ago before Han Chinese settlement began in 1673. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
