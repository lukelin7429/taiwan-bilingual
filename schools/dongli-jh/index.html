Dongli Junior High School

東里國中

A hillside school overlooking rice fields, mountains on both horizons, at the southern edge of the East Rift Valley.

About · 學校簡介

From a branch campus to an independent hillside school.

Dongli Junior High began in 1974 as a branch of Fubei Junior High, serving students in Dongli Village before becoming fully independent on August 1, 1982. The school occupies a hillside site facing north over village farmland, with the Coastal Mountain Range visible to the east and the Central Mountain Range to the west — a striking, valley-framed setting. It shares Dongli Village with the century-old Dongli Elementary School, making the village a small but historic educational hub within Fuli Township.

東里國中於民國63年（1974年）以富北國中分部的形式創立，服務東里村的學生，並於民國71年（1982年）8月1日正式獨立設校。學校座落於山坡上，面北俯瞰村落農田，東側可見海岸山脈，西側則是中央山脈——形成縱谷環抱的壯闊景致。學校與擁有百年歷史的東里國小同在東里村，使這個村落成為富里鄉一個小而歷史悠久的教育重鎮。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fuli Township, rice country at the valley's edge.

🌾

Fuli Township is Hualien County's southernmost township, a rice-farming community near the Tropic of Cancer at the southern tip of the East Rift Valley. Dongli Junior High's outlook over farmland, framed by mountains east and west, captures the valley's classic agricultural landscape. See the Hualien schools page for the full township list.
