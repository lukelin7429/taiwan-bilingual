About · 學校簡介

From a continuing school to an AI innovation leader.

National Hualien Commercial Vocational Senior High School began in 1953 as the Hualien County Intermediate Commercial Vocational Continuing School and went through several transformations over the decades — becoming a junior vocational school in 1955, adding a senior division in 1958, converting to a provincial senior commercial school in 1968, and finally becoming a national school in 2000. Today it offers seven departments spanning commercial management, accounting and AI data processing, applied English, multimedia design, international trade, and applied Japanese. The school won a national gold award for innovative teaching in 2024 and placed first nationally in an AI innovation competition in 2025.

花蓮高商創立於1953年，初名「花蓮縣立初級商業職業進修學校」，數十年間歷經多次改制：1955年改為初級商業職業學校，1958年增設高級部，1968年改制為省立高級商業職業學校，民國89年（2000年）改隸國立。目前設有七個科別，涵蓋商業經營、會計資訊（含AI資料處理）、應用英語、多媒體設計、國際貿易與應用日語。學校於2024年榮獲教學創新全國金質獎，並於2025年在AI創新競賽中勇奪全國第一名。
