Guishan Junior High School

龜山國中

Named directly for Guishan District itself, this junior high sits near the century-old Guishan Elementary School from which many of the district's other schools originally branched off.

以龜山區為名的國中，鄰近百年老校龜山國小——龜山區多所國小的發源母校。

About · 學校簡介

Named for the district itself.

Guishan Junior High School began as a preparatory office at Shoushan Junior High in 2002, formally opened its doors to its first seventh-grade class in 2004, and reached municipal status in 2014. It now operates 39 classes across all three grades, including a diversified learning center, special-education classes, and a competitive athletics program launched in 2009 with dedicated baseball and shooting teams.

龜山國中前身為2002年設於壽山國中的籌備處，2004年正式招收第一屆七年級新生，2014年改制為市立學校。目前全校三個年級共39個班級，設有多元學習中心、特教班，並自2009年起成立棒球與射擊重點運動代表隊。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Near the district's oldest school.

🐢

Guishan Junior High is named directly for the district itself, and sits close to the older administrative core of the area — in the same broad catchment as the century-old Guishan Elementary School, the historical parent institution from which several of the district's other elementary schools originally branched off.
