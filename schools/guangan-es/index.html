About · 學校簡介

A small school with a big reading culture.

Guang'an Elementary School is a small school of about 6 classes and roughly 89 students in Wandan Township. Likely founded around 1964, it celebrated its 60th graduating class in the 2023-24 school year, marking roughly six decades of continuous operation. Despite its small size, the school has built a strong reading-promotion program: morning reading time, book recommendations, a “reading passport,” recognition for “reading experts,” and a mobile “Cloud and Water Bookmobile” (雲水書坊) service that brings books directly to students.

廣安國小是萬丹鄉一所小型學校，現有6班、約89名學生。學校可能創立於約民國53年（1964年），並於112學年度慶祝第60屆畢業典禮，顯示已有約一甲子的辦學歷史。儘管規模不大，學校在閱讀推廣上表現突出：晨讀活動、好書推薦、「閱讀護照」、「閱讀達人」表揚，以及將書籍直接送到學生面前的「雲水書坊」行動書車服務。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Wandan, land of lotus and red beans.

🪷

Guang'an Elementary sits within Wandan Township, Pingtung's most populous rural township, celebrated for its lotus-root and lotus-seed farming across a flat, water-rich agricultural plain. The rhythms of planting and harvest here have shaped village life for generations, forming the backdrop for this small school's six decades of reading-focused education.
