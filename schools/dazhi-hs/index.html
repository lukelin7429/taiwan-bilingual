About · 學校簡介

From a naval training ground to a city high school.

Dazhi Senior High School opened in 1964 as a junior high, built on the former site of a Taiwan Provincial Training Corps facility that had itself been a Japanese-era naval training ground constructed in 1932. It originally admitted only male students after formally converting to junior-high status in 1968, later becoming coeducational.

In the 1990s wave of school upgrades that created several of Taipei's "complete middle schools" (combining junior and senior high divisions), Dazhi became a full complete school in 1998 under a 1997 preparatory committee led by principal Yu Lin. The campus sits on Bei'an Road — the district's namesake corridor — connecting the school directly to the story of how Dazhi grew from isolated farmland into a modern Taipei neighborhood.

大直高中創校於1964年（民國53年），初期為國中部，校地前身為臺灣省訓練團設施，該處更早則是1932年興建的日治時期海軍訓練場。1968年正式改制為國中後，一度僅招收男學生，其後才改為男女兼收。

1990年代臺北市推動「完全中學」改制風潮，大直於1997年由校長游麟籌備、1998年正式改制為兼具國、高中部的完全中學。校園座落於北安路——中山區得名的重要道路，也將學校與大直地區從偏遠農地蛻變為現代臺北郊區的歷程緊密相連。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚓ A Japanese-Era Naval Site

日治海軍訓練場舊址

The campus occupies land first built as a Japanese naval training facility in 1932, later reused by a postwar provincial training corps before becoming a school in 1964.

🎓 A 1990s "Complete School"

1998年改制完全中學

Dazhi joined Taipei's 1990s wave of school upgrades, becoming a full junior-and-senior "complete middle school" in 1998 under a preparatory committee formed the year before.

🚹 From Boys-Only to Coed

從男校到男女兼收

Originally admitting only male students after its 1968 conversion to junior-high status, the school later opened its doors to female students as well.

🛣️ On the Namesake "Straight Road"

座落北安路

The campus sits on Bei'an Road, the modern extension of the old rural road that gave the Dazhi neighborhood ("big straight") its name.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The road that named a neighborhood.

🛤️

The Dazhi neighborhood takes its name ("大直" = "big straight") from the once-rural farm road running directly from Yuanshan to Mingshui Road and Bei'an Road that connected it to the city — the same road the school now sits on, embodying the area's transformation from isolated farmland into a modern Taipei suburb.
