Erchong Junior High School

About · 學校簡介

From a village branch school to a growing campus.

Erchong Junior High began in 1956 as a branch of Zhudong Junior High before becoming an independent school in 1962. Located in Erchong Village — an area of Zhudong that expanded alongside the township's commercial and residential growth — the school has kept pace with a rising student population: the county government has approved a self-funded expansion of roughly NT$390 million to accommodate continued enrollment growth. Today the campus runs 39 student clubs, from varsity volleyball to its long-running wind-band program.

二重國中創立於民國45年（1956年），原為竹東國中分部，民國51年（1962年）獨立設校。校址位於竹東鎮二重里——隨著竹東鎮商業與住宅發展而擴張的地區。因應學生人數持續成長，縣府已核准約新台幣3.9億元的自籌經費擴建計畫。目前全校共有39個社團，從排球校隊到歷史悠久的管樂團一應俱全。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎺 An Arts-Talent Wind Band

藝術才能音樂班（管樂）

Established in 2002, the school's arts-talent wind-band music program has given generations of students a dedicated track for musical training alongside their academic studies.

🏐 Thirty-Nine Student Clubs

39 個學生社團

The school runs 39 clubs in total, including a varsity volleyball team, giving students a wide range of extracurricular pursuits beyond the classroom.

🏗️ Growing to Meet Demand

因應成長的擴建計畫

The county government approved a roughly NT$390 million self-funded expansion for the campus, reflecting steady enrollment growth in this part of Zhudong.

Erchong Junior High sits in Erchong Village, part of Zhudong Township that has grown steadily alongside the wider town's development as a commercial and administrative hub for Hsinchu County's mountain townships.
