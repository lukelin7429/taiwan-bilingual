Xianxi Junior High School

線西國民中學

A public junior high on Changhua's coastline, founded in 1962 under a county-wide "one junior high per township" policy to serve the area's fishing-and-farming community.

座落於彰化縣海線的公立國中，創校於民國51年（1962年），源於「一鄉鎮一國中」政策，服務在地漁業與農業社區。

About · 學校簡介

Born from a "one junior high per township" policy.

Xianxi Junior High opened on August 1, 1962, under founding principal Huang Xiongwen, as part of a county-wide policy to establish one junior high school per township. It began with just 112 students, borrowing classrooms from neighboring Xianxi Elementary School before gaining independent official junior-high status on July 20, 1964. The school was reorganized as a "national junior high school" in 1968 under the nine-year compulsory education system, and has since marked major milestones including a new gate (1973), campus renovation (1996), track reconstruction (2007), and a new teaching building (2008) — recently celebrating its 60th anniversary.

線西國中於民國51年（1962年）8月1日創校，首任校長為黃雄文，因應縣府「一鄉鎮一國中」政策而設立，創校之初僅有112名學生，借用鄰近線西國小的教室上課。民國53年（1964年）7月20日獲准獨立設校。民國57年（1968年），隨九年國民義務教育實施，改制為國民中學。此後歷經新校門（1973年）、校園整建（1996年）、跑道重建（2007年）、新教學大樓（2008年）等重要建設里程碑，近年並慶祝創校60週年。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌊 Ocean-Spirited Youth

海派少年

The school describes its students as "ocean-spirited youth" (海派少年), reflecting its coastal setting, and emphasizes multi-path exploratory education to help them discover diverse futures.

🎓 University Partnership

彰師大合作學校

Xianxi Junior High partners with National Changhua University of Education, connecting classroom teaching with broader academic resources and training.

🏗️ Six Decades of Building

六十年校史建設

From a new gate in 1973 to a rebuilt track in 2007 and a new teaching building in 2008, the campus has steadily modernized across its 60-year history.

🏫 One of Two in Xianxi

全鄉唯一國中

Xianxi Township — Changhua County's smallest township — has only two elementary schools and this one junior high, making it a central hub for local secondary education.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xianxi, Changhua's smallest coastal township.

🎣

Xianxi Township is Changhua County's smallest township, a typical coastal farming-and-fishing community with a strong maritime character. Xianxi Junior High serves this small coastal community as its only junior high school, reflecting the tight-knit, seaside identity of the area. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
