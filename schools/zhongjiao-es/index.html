About · 學校簡介

A small school beside a famous bay.

Zhongjiao Elementary School began in 1952 as a branch of Jinshan Elementary School, becoming independent in 1954. It is a small, remote school within the North Coast National Scenic Area, sitting immediately adjacent to Zhongjiao Bay — a well-known surfing spot that draws visitors from across Taiwan.

中角國小於民國41年（1952年）以金山國小分校形式成立，民國43年（1954年）獨立設校。這是一所座落於北海岸國家風景區內的小型偏遠學校，緊鄰台灣知名衝浪勝地中角灣，吸引全台各地遊客前來。

北海岸國家風景區內

🌊 A Small School With Deep Roots

小校深根七十年

Beginning as a 1952 branch of Jinshan Elementary and becoming independent in 1954, the school has quietly served this coastal corner of Jinshan for over seven decades.
