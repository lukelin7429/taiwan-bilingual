Guangfu Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

Forty years serving a growing suburb.

Guangfu Elementary was established and formally named on April 22, 1985, after both Tucheng Elementary and Qingshui Elementary could no longer absorb the district's rising enrollment. Built on Guangfu Road in the former farming village of Bilin, the school recently marked its 40th anniversary, a milestone that reflects Tucheng's steady shift from agricultural township to modern Taipei-basin suburb.

廣福國小於民國74年（1985年）4月22日正式定名設校，起因是土城國小與清水國小已無法容納持續攀升的入學人數。學校座落於廣福路，位處昔日的農村聚落壁林，近期歡慶建校40週年，這個里程碑也反映出土城從農業鄉鎮逐步邁向現代台北盆地郊區的歷程。
