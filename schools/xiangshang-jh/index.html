About · 學校簡介

Built for a growing district in 1968.

Xiangshang Junior High School broke ground in April 1968 as "臺中市第十國民中學" (Taichung Municipal Tenth Junior High School) and welcomed its first students that September. It was renamed "向上" (Xiangshang) in 1971. The campus sits on Meicun Road, near the National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts, in one of West District's established arts-and-culture neighborhoods.

向上國中於民國57年（1968年）4月動工興建，初名「臺中市第十國民中學」，同年9月即招收首屆學生。民國60年（1971年）更名為「向上」。校址位於美村路上，鄰近國立臺灣美術館，座落於西區成熟的藝文廊帶內。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Ground to Classroom in One Year

當年動工當年招生

Construction began in April 1968 as the city's tenth junior high, and the school opened to its first students that same September — a fast turnaround for the era.

🎨 On Meicun Road's Arts Corridor

美村路的藝文廊帶

The campus sits on Meicun Road, close to the National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts and Green Way, placing it within West District's established arts-and-culture neighborhood.

📛 A 1971 Renaming

1971年更名

Originally known simply by number as "臺中市第十國民中學," the school took its present name "向上" in 1971, part of a broader trend of renaming numbered junior highs in the city.

Local Culture · 在地文化

West District's arts-and-culture corridor.

🖌️

Meicun Road runs through West District's arts corridor, home to the National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts and the tree-lined Green Way — a cultural neighborhood shared with several other West District schools. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
