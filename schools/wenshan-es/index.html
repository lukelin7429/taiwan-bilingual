Wenshan Elementary School

文山國民小學

About · 學校簡介

Built by the village, for the village.

Wenshan Elementary opened in August 1964 as a branch campus of Xinxing School, becoming fully independent in 1966 and taking its current name in 1968. The school owes its existence to the local gentry of Wenshan Village, who funded and helped build the schoolhouse themselves so village children would not have to travel far for their education. Yeh Kuo-pao served as the school's first independent principal in 1966.

文山國小創立於民國53年（1964年）8月，最初為新興國小的分班，民國55年（1966年）獨立設校，民國57年（1968年）改為現名。學校的誕生歸功於文山村地方仕紳自籌經費、協助興建校舍，讓村中孩童無須遠行就學。首任獨立設校校長為葉國寶（1966年）。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🤝 Funded by Local Gentry

仕紳自籌興建

Wenshan Elementary exists because local gentry from Wenshan Village pooled their own resources and labor to fund and build the schoolhouse for their community's children.

🏫 From Branch to Independent School

由分班獨立設校

The school began in 1964 as a branch of Xinxing School and became fully independent just two years later in 1966, under its first principal, Yeh Kuo-pao.

🌱 A Village Rooted in Self-Reliance

村落自立精神

The community-led founding story reflects a broader pattern across Xinpu Township, where rural villages have historically organized to build their own local schools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A village of self-reliance.

🏘️

Wenshan Elementary sits in Wenshan Village, part of Xinpu Township's rural Hakka countryside, where community self-organization — as seen in the school's own founding — has long shaped local life.
