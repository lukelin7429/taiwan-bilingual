Kaixuan Junior High School

About · 學校簡介

Purpose-built for a new district.

Kaixuan Junior High began with a preparatory office established on August 1, 2003, and was formally named and approved in 2006, enrolling its first students that school year. The school was built specifically to serve the new County Government Center district (縣政中心), an area planned to house roughly 12,000 residents, making it one of the newest and most recently established junior high schools in Yilan City.

凱旋國中籌備處成立於2003年8月1日，於民國95年（2006年）正式核准命名，並於當學年度開始招生。學校興建的目的是服務新開發的「縣政中心」地區，該地區規劃容納約12,000名居民，使凱旋國中成為宜蘭市最新設立的國中之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

🏛️ Serving the County Government Center

服務縣政中心地區

🆕 One of the City's Newest Schools

宜蘭市最新設立國中之一

3.03公頃現代化校園

Local Culture · 在地文化

Growing alongside a new district.

🏘️

Kaixuan Junior High's founding is tied directly to the development of Yilan's County Government Center district, a newly planned residential and administrative area — making the school itself a marker of the city's 21st-century growth. See the Yilan County schools page for more on the region.
