National Neipu Senior Agricultural-Industrial Vocational High School

國立內埔高級農工職業學校

One of the oldest vocational schools in the Liudui Hakka region, tracing back to a 1934 Japanese-era agricultural institute and today a designated Taiwan Indigenous Focus School.

About · 學校簡介

Nine decades of agricultural and industrial education.

National Neipu Senior Agricultural-Industrial Vocational High School traces its origins to 1934, when it opened as the "Takao Prefecture Puqiangshan Agricultural Lecture Institute" under Japanese colonial rule. It was renamed the Pingtung County Pingshan Agricultural Vocational School in 1961, and today offers a wide range of departments — including Farm Management, Agricultural Machinery, Food Processing, Wildlife Conservation, Mechanical Engineering, Automotive, Electrical Engineering, and Home Economics — across an 11.97-hectare campus. The school is officially designated a Taiwan Indigenous Focus School, reflecting Neipu's mixed Hakka and Indigenous student population.

國立內埔高級農工職業學校源起於1934年（昭和9年）日治時期設立的「高雄州埔羌山地農業講習所」，1961年更名為「屏東縣立屏山地農業職業學校」。學校現設有農場經營科、農業機械科、食品加工科、野生動物保育科、機械科、汽車科、電機科、家政科等多元科別，校地面積約11.97公頃。學校並獲指定為「臺灣原住民重點學校」，反映內埔鄉客家與原住民族群交融的學生組成。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚜 Nine Decades of Agricultural Roots

近九十年農業教育根基

Founded in 1934 as a Japanese-era agricultural lecture institute, the school has grown into a comprehensive vocational campus spanning agriculture, industry, and food science.

🔧 Eight Departments, One Campus

八大科別齊聚一校

Programs range from Farm Management and Agricultural Machinery to Food Processing, Wildlife Conservation, Mechanical Engineering, Automotive, Electrical Engineering, and Home Economics.

🦌 A Wildlife Conservation Program

野生動物保育科

Alongside traditional agricultural and industrial tracks, the school runs a Wildlife Conservation Department — an unusual offering among Taiwan's vocational high schools.

🌏 A Designated Indigenous Focus School

臺灣原住民重點學校

Officially designated a Taiwan Indigenous Focus School, the campus serves a student body that reflects Neipu Township's mixed Hakka and Indigenous communities.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Neipu's agricultural heritage.

🌾

As one of the oldest vocational institutions in the Liudui Hakka region, this school embodies Neipu Township's deep agricultural heritage of rice and tobacco farming, and its student body reflects the township's mixed Hakka and Indigenous population. Neipu is the "Hou Dui" (後堆, Rear Pile) of the Liudui confederation and home to National Pingtung University of Science and Technology, making the township a longtime center of agricultural learning in southern Taiwan.
