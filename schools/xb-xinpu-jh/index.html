Xinpu Junior High School

新北市立新埔國民中學

A Banqiao junior high known for its gifted art and dance programs, named for a longstanding local neighborhood.

板橋國中，以美術與舞蹈資優班聞名，因新埔地區而得名。

About · 學校簡介

Building gifted arts programs since the 1990s.

Xinpu Junior High School was founded in 1985 in the Xinpu neighborhood of Banqiao. The school added a gifted art program in 1990 and a gifted dance program in 1994, building a strong arts-education identity over the following decades. It was formally renamed a municipal school in December 2010.

新埔國中創立於民國74年（1985年），位於板橋新埔地區。學校於民國79年（1990年）增設美術資優班，民國83年（1994年）增設舞蹈資優班，逐步建立起藝術教育特色。民國99年（2010年）12月改制為市立學校。

🎨 A Gifted Art Program Since 1990

美術資優班

📍 Named for a Longstanding Neighborhood
