About · 學校簡介

Built for a zone on the move.

Daye Junior High was established in 1993 to serve Nantun's fast-developing 5th Redevelopment Zone, an area that had previously lacked a junior high school of its own. Its first students enrolled in 1996 as the surrounding neighborhood filled in with new housing. The school's founding traces the broader story of Nantun's transition from agricultural land into one of Taichung's expanding urban redevelopment zones during the 1990s.

大業國中創立於民國82年（1993年），是為了服務快速發展的南屯區五期重劃區而設立，當地過去並無國中，1996年首屆學生正式入學。學校的誕生見證了南屯從農業用地轉型為台中市擴張中都市重劃區的1990年代發展歷程。
