Xian'an Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A campus built on emigrant generosity.

Xian'an Elementary was founded in 1951 under the original name "Fengxian Elementary School." In 1957 it merged with Hou'an National School and was renamed "Xian'an National School," before formally taking its current name, "Kinmen County Jincheng Township Xian'an Elementary School," in 1968. Its 1965 campus was built with funding partly donated by Hong Zhongcheng, an overseas Kinmen emigrant based in Singapore — a reflection of Kinmen's long history as a "qiaoxiang," or hometown of the Chinese diaspora. A central kitchen facility was completed at the school in 2023.

賢庵國小創立於民國40年（1951年），初名「豐賢國小」。民國46年（1957年）與後垵國校合併，更名為「賢庵國校」，至民國57年（1968年）正式定名為「金門縣金城鎮賢庵國民小學」。民國54年（1965年）興建的校舍，部分經費由旅居新加坡的金門僑領洪忠誠捐助，見證金門作為華僑故鄉「僑鄉」的深厚歷史淵源。學校於民國112年（2023年）完成中央廚房設施。
