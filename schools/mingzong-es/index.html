About · 學校簡介

A small campus, big on the arts.

Mingzong Elementary is a rural school in Hunei District's Guanguan Village, serving around 460 students across 18 classes. The school maintains a dedicated calligraphy hall and supports a range of performing arts groups, including a taiko drum team, a recorder team, and a ukulele club — reflecting a community emphasis on both traditional Han cultural arts and contemporary music education. (The school's founding year is not documented in publicly available sources.)

明宗國小是位於湖內區公館里的鄉村學校，全校約18班、460名學生。學校設有專屬書法教室，並支持多個表演藝術社團，包括太鼓隊、直笛隊與烏克麗麗社，展現社區對傳統漢文化藝術與當代音樂教育的並重。（創校年份目前未見公開資料記載。）
