About · 學校簡介

A century in the old garrison hamlet.

Guoyi Elementary was founded in 1920, during the Japanese colonial era, in the hamlet of Guoyihou, one of Liuying's old settlement names. The school celebrated its 100th-anniversary sports day and celebration around 2020. Today it runs a dedicated family-education website and is listed among the remote and under-resourced partner schools supported by the Child Welfare League Foundation.

果毅國小創立於大正9年（1920年）日治時期，坐落於柳營老聚落「果毅後」。學校於2020年前後舉辦百週年校慶暨運動會。目前設有家庭教育網，並列名為兒童福利聯盟文教基金會扶助的偏鄉夥伴學校之一。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Liuying's frontier-garrison past.

🏯

The settlement name Guoyihou ("behind Guoyi") is tied to Liuying's Qing-era military-camp toponymy — the district's old name, Chamuying (查畝營), refers to land measured out for garrison troops. This links Guoyi Elementary to the area's frontier-settlement history, a layer of local identity that predates Liuying's later rise as a dairy-farming district.
