About · 學校簡介

A young school for a growing corner of Shulin.

Sanduo Elementary School was established in 1988 to serve the growing 三多, 三興, 三福, and 三龍 neighborhoods on Shulin's eastern edge, with formal enrollment beginning in August 1991. It remains one of the district's younger elementary schools and feeds directly into the neighboring Sanduo Junior High School.

三多國小創立於民國77年（1988年），為服務樹林東側日漸成長的三多、三興、三福、三龍等里而設立，於民國80年（1991年）8月正式招生。是樹林較年輕的小學之一，畢業生直接銜接鄰近的三多國中。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Growth on the district's eastern edge.

🏘️

Sanduo Elementary takes its name from and serves the "三多" cluster of neighborhoods on Shulin's eastern edge, part of the district's ongoing shift from an agricultural town into a denser residential suburb of New Taipei City. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
