About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school with an unusually rich club life.

Dingpu Elementary serves families across the Dingpu, Dingfu, and parts of the Zhongpu and Dongxiang neighborhoods of Xiangshan District. Having marked at least a 28th-anniversary celebration in recent years, the school traces its founding to roughly the early-to-mid 1990s. What stands out most about Dingpu today is the sheer range of after-school clubs on offer — from roller skating and diabolo to robotics, baking, clay crafts, orchestra, ukulele, rock climbing, swimming, and taekwondo — giving students an unusually broad set of ways to explore their interests beyond the regular curriculum.

頂埔國小服務範圍涵蓋香山區頂埔、頂福，以及中埔、東香部分地區的居民。學校近年曾舉辦至少28週年校慶活動，推算創校約在民國80年代初、中期。頂埔國小最鮮明的特色，是其極為豐富多元的課後社團——從直排輪、扯鈴到機器人、烘焙、黏土、管弦樂、烏克麗麗、攀岩、游泳與跆拳道，讓學生在正規課程之外，有更多元的探索空間。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🧗 An Unusually Broad Club Roster

多元社團陣容

Few elementary schools offer as wide a spread of after-school clubs as Dingpu — rock climbing and robotics sit alongside orchestra, baking, and taekwondo.

🎻 From Ukulele to Orchestra

烏克麗麗與管弦樂

Music runs through the club program, with both ukulele and full orchestra options open to students who want to explore performance.

🏘️ Serving Four Neighborhoods

跨里學區

The school's district spans Dingpu, Dingfu, and parts of Zhongpu and Dongxiang, making it a shared community hub for families across several neighborhoods.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xiangshan, Hsinchu's coastal Minnan heartland.

🦪

Xiangshan District is Hsinchu City's coastal district, long shaped by Minnan fishing and oyster-farming traditions and fringed by tidal wetlands. Its historic "mother school," Xiangshan Elementary, was founded in 1908 through the philanthropy of a local gentry family and gave rise to several of the district's neighborhood schools. See the Hsinchu City county page for more on the region.
