About · 學校簡介

A quiet school in the hills of Yongxing.

Yongxing Elementary School serves Yongxing Village, a hillside community in Shuili Township. Its remoteness reflects the rural mountain terrain surrounding the old logging routes near Checheng, and the school remains a modest but steady presence for the families of this quiet corner of the township.

永興國小服務水里鄉永興村，這是一個山區聚落。學校的偏遠位置正反映出車埕舊時林業運輸路線周邊的山區地形。學校雖規模不大，卻始終是這個寧靜鄉間角落家庭的重要依靠。
