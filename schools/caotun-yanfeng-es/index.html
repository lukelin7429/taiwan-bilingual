About · 學校簡介

One of Caotun's largest campuses.

Yanfeng Elementary School was founded around 1956 and has grown into one of the larger schools in Caotun Township, with roughly 1,141 students across 42 classes. The school runs the CORI (Concept-Oriented Reading Instruction) reading and writing curriculum alongside mBot robotics programs, giving students both strong literacy foundations and hands-on exposure to coding and engineering. Yanfeng also fields active volleyball and table-tennis teams.

炎峰國小創校於民國45年（1956年）左右，如今已發展為草屯鎮規模較大的學校之一，全校約1,141名學生，共42班。學校推動CORI（概念導向閱讀教學）讀寫課程，並搭配mBot機器人課程，讓學生兼具紮實的語文基礎與程式設計、工程實作的體驗。此外，排球與桌球隊伍表現活躍。
