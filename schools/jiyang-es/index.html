Jiyang Elementary School

吉洋國民小學

A small rural school opened in 1919 on donated farmland, still serving one of Meinong's smallest and most tight-knit communities.

About · 學校簡介

A century-old school on donated farmland.

Jiyang Elementary School opened on April 1, 1919, as a branch of Milong Public School called Shoujinliao Branch (瀰濃公學校手巾寮分校). The land for the school was donated by Nanlong Farm, and it opened with three classes and 87 students. Today Jiyang remains a very small rural school — as of 2022 it had just seven classes and 86 students — serving a close-knit farming community in Meinong District.

吉洋國小創立於1919年（大正8年）4月1日，最初為瀰濃公學校手巾寮分校，校地由南隆農場捐贈，開校時設3班、87名學生。如今吉洋國小仍是規模很小的農村學校——截至2022年僅有7班、86名學生——服務美濃區一個關係緊密的農業社區。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Founded on Donated Farmland

南隆農場捐地興學

The school's land was donated by Nanlong Farm when it opened in 1919, reflecting the close ties between local landowners and early rural education in Meinong.

🌾 A Century-Old, Still-Small Rural School

百年小校

Jiyang opened with just 3 classes and 87 students in 1919, and remains small today — 7 classes and 86 students as of 2022 — preserving an intimate rural-school character.

🏘️ Named for an Old Hakka Hamlet

「手巾寮」舊地名

The school's original name referenced Shoujinliao (手巾寮), an early Hakka hamlet tied to Meinong's traditional irrigation and farming settlement pattern.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for an old Hakka farming hamlet.

🏡

The school's original name referenced Shoujinliao (手巾寮), an early Hakka hamlet name in Meinong tied to the local irrigation and farming settlement pattern — a living link to the district's agricultural past. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
