About · 學校簡介

The newest school in a fast-growing district.

Xingao Elementary School is the newest school in this survey of Taiping District, established in 2018 and opened to students in 2020. It was built at a cost of roughly NT$211 million because neighboring Xinguang and Xinping elementary schools had reached full enrollment capacity — a direct product of Taiping's most recent housing boom in the Xinguang-Xinping area northeast of central Taichung.

新高國小是太平區內最新設立的學校，於民國107年（2018年）核准設立，民國109年（2020年）正式開學招生。建校經費約新台幣2.11億元，起因於鄰近的新光國小與新平國小均已達招生容量上限——這正是太平區近年在新光、新平一帶新一波房市榮景下的直接產物。
