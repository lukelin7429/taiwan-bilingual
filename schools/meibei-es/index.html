Meibei Elementary School

梅北國民小學

Founded in 2001 to relieve overcrowding at Meishan Elementary, this school channels the bamboo groves and tea gardens of Meibei Village into its bamboo music ensemble and tea-arts programs.

About · 學校簡介

A newer school for a growing township.

Meibei Elementary was founded on August 1, 2001, to relieve overcrowding and aging facilities at nearby Meishan Elementary, part of a broader government push for smaller schools and class sizes — making it one of the newest schools in this batch. As of the 2025 school year, the school has 12 regular classes plus 1 resource class, with 189-191 total students and 33 staff. Its guiding philosophy is summed up as "keep clean, keep order, be polite, be grateful, be well-read" (愛整潔.守秩序.有禮貌.會感恩.博學識).

梅北國小創立於民國90年（2001年）8月1日，為紓解鄰近梅山國小人數過多與校舍老舊的壓力而設立，也是政府推動小校小班制政策下的產物，是本批次中最年輕的學校之一。114學年度，全校共12個普通班加1個資源班，總學生數189-191人，教職員33人。學校秉持「愛整潔．守秩序．有禮貌．會感恩．博學識」的辦學理念。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎋 A Bamboo Music Ensemble

竹樂團

The school runs a distinctive bamboo music ensemble (竹樂團), turning the bamboo groves that surround Meibei Village into a source of musical instruments and performance.

🍵 A Tea-Arts Program

茶藝課程

Alongside its "Meibei Health Five Treasures" (梅北健康五寶) initiative, the school offers a tea-arts program drawing on the local tea gardens.

🌏 A Cross-Cultural World Tour Camp

環遊世界跨文化營

The school hosts a summer "cross-cultural world tour camp" (環遊世界跨文化營), broadening students' global awareness alongside its street dance club and other activities.
