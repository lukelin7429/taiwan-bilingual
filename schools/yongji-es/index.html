About · 學校簡介

A campus with clay in its foundations.

Yongji Elementary was established in 1998 on a site that had previously housed a brick-kiln factory and later a driving school — a location whose industrial past echoes Yingge's long history of clay-firing and ceramics production. The school was designated a “distinctive curriculum showcase school” for the 2024–25 school year, recognizing the strength of its specialty programs.

永吉國小創立於民國87年（1998年），校地前身曾為磚窯工廠，之後一度作為駕訓班用地——這段工業歷史恰好呼應了鶯歌長久以來的燒陶製磚產業背景。學校於113學年度（2024-25）獲選為特色課程展示學校，肯定其特色教學成果。
