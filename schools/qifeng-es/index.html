About · 學校簡介

A rebuilt campus by the wetlands.

Qifeng Elementary School serves Qifeng Village in Linbian Township, a low-lying coastal area that has faced ongoing land-subsidence and safety challenges. After its buildings were assessed as high-risk structures, the school underwent a major campus reconstruction reported at around NT$170 million. The school sits near the Qifeng Wetland Park, a roughly 9.5-hectare wetland at the edge of National Freeway No. 3 that forms part of the Dapeng Bay scenic area, and its official site includes a dedicated introduction page, "認識崎峰" ("Get to Know Qifeng"), describing the school and its community.

崎峰國小服務林邊鄉崎峰村，這是一處長期面臨地層下陷與安全隱憂的低窪沿海地區。因校舍被評估為高風險建築，學校近年進行了耗資約新台幣1.7億元的大規模重建工程。校址鄰近崎峰濕地公園，這片約9.5公頃的濕地位於國道三號末端，屬大鵬灣風景區的一部分；學校官網並設有「認識崎峰」專頁，介紹學校與在地社區。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Grouper country on the recovery road.

🐟

Linbian Township is known for coastal aquaculture, particularly grouper (石斑魚) farming, but it has also faced significant land subsidence and flood risk, most severely during Typhoon Morakot in 2009. In response, the township has invested in eco-tourism assets like the nearby Qifeng Wetland Park as part of its broader post-disaster resilience efforts. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
