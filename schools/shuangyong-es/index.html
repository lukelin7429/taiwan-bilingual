About · 學校簡介

A brand-new school made from two old ones.

Shuangyong Elementary School was created on August 1, 2022, by merging the former Yongchun Elementary School and Yongji Elementary School — the first school merger under Taipei's "Education-Oriented Development" (EOD) urban renewal policy. Note: this Yongji Elementary School is a distinct, elementary-level institution, separate from the still-independent Yongji Junior High School elsewhere in Xinyi District. The merged school's name was chosen through public i-Voting in May–June 2021: "Shuangyong" ("double Yong") won with 695 votes (40%), reflecting that both parent schools shared the character "永" (yong) and the wish for the merger to create a "win-win" for school and community. Under the city's EOD framework, its new campus — under construction in two phases from 2024 to 2029 on a 28,629-square-meter site — is planned to house 42 elementary classes, a 15-class attached kindergarten, a public daycare, a "lifelong learning" senior classroom, and a community arts center.

雙永國小於2022年（民國111年）8月1日由原永春國小與永吉國小合併而成，是台北市首座依「教育導向都市發展（EOD）」政策合併成立的學校。（附註：此處的「永吉國小」為國小層級的獨立學校，與信義區內仍獨立招生的永吉國中為不同機構。）校名於2021年5至6月透過全民i-Voting票選產生，「雙永」以695票（40%）獲選，取兩校皆有「永」字及合併後「雙贏」之意。依EOD政策規劃，新校舍將於2024至2029年分兩期於28,629平方公尺基地上興建，預計容納42班國小班級、15班附設幼兒園、公共托育中心、樂齡教室及社區藝文中心。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Redevelopment logic, applied to schools.

🏗️

As Taipei's first school created under the city's "Education-Oriented Development" policy, Shuangyong's 2022 merger is a direct, present-day continuation of the same redevelopment logic that transformed Xinyi from Songshan's rural periphery into a master-planned modern district anchored by Taipei 101 — this time applied to aging school buildings rather than the old munitions-depot land the Xinyi Planning District itself was built on.
