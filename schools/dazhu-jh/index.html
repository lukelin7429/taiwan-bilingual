Dazhu Junior High School

大竹國中

Serving the historic Dazhu settlement since 1958, home to a national rugby championship and a wide range of arts and sports programs.

About · 學校簡介

From branch campus to a national rugby champion.

Dazhu Junior High began in 1958 as a branch campus and became an independent junior high in 1962, converting to standard nine-year-compulsory-education status in 1968. Renamed to its current name in 2014, the school today enrolls about 1,137 students across 41 classes and 94 teachers, running wind-band, basketball, rugby, and dance/cheerleading programs alongside an attached preschool. Its rugby team won the National Zhongzheng Cup 10-a-side championship in 2009, a lasting point of local pride.

大竹國中於民國47年（1958年）以分校形式創立，民國51年（1962年）獨立設校，民國57年（1968年）配合九年國教改制。民國103年（2014年）更名為現名。學校現有41班、學生約1,137人、教師94人（民國114年統計），設有管樂、籃球、橄欖球及舞蹈啦啦隊等社團，並附設幼兒園。橄欖球隊於民國98年（2009年）榮獲全國中正盃十人制橄欖球錦標賽冠軍，是在地一大驕傲。

Local Culture · 在地文化

One of Luzhu's three historic settlement cores.

🌳

Dazhu Junior High serves the historic Dazhu (大竹) settlement, one of Luzhu District's three traditional core communities alongside Nankan and Kengzi (坑子). Several other Luzhu elementary schools — Luzhu, Xinxing, and Xinzhuang Elementary — all trace their own origins back to branch classrooms of the original Dazhu school, making Dazhu one of the district's true educational "mother" lineages. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
