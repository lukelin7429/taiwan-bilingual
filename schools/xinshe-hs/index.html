Xinshe Senior High School

臺中市立新社高級中學

A mountainside campus training students for Xinshe's flower and mushroom economy, rooted in a 1925 agricultural school.

About · 學校簡介

From an agricultural school to a mountain campus.

Xinshe Senior High traces its origins to 1925, when an agricultural supplementary school was established to train local farmers; it was reorganized into a full senior high school in 2010. Spread across roughly 15 hectares at about 500 meters elevation, the school offers both academic and vocational tracks — including data processing, business management, horticulture, and farm management — while preserving a registered historic Japanese-era faculty dormitory on campus.

新社高中的歷史源自1925年設立的農業補習學校，最初為訓練在地農民而設，2010年改制為完整的高級中學。校地約15公頃，海拔約500公尺，同時開設學術與技職課程，包括資料處理、企業管理、園藝與農場經營等科系，校園內並保存一棟登錄為歷史建築的日治時期教職員宿舍。

🌱 Roots in a 1925 Agricultural School

Local Culture · 在地文化

Training talent for the "back garden of Taichung."

🍄

Xinshe Senior High's horticulture and farm-management programs train students directly for the district's signature industries — the flower- and mushroom-growing economy that has earned Xinshe its reputation as "the back garden of Taichung." See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
