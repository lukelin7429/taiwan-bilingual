About · 學校簡介

A school that once reached deep into the hills.

Dahu Elementary School serves families in Yuanshan Township. For more than three decades, the school extended its reach into a more remote mountain community through a branch campus known as Shuanglian (雙連分校), which operated from 1957 until 1993. Though the branch eventually closed as the community it served changed, its history remains part of Dahu's own story of serving students across a spread-out rural township.

大湖國小服務員山鄉的家庭。學校曾透過「雙連分校」將教育觸角延伸至較偏遠的山區社區，該分校自1957年運作至1993年，長達三十餘年。雖然分校後來隨著服務社區的變遷而走入歷史，但這段歷史仍是大湖國小服務廣闊鄉村地區學生的重要篇章。
