About · 學校簡介

Anchoring the Kangning Road education corridor.

Taipei Municipal Minghu Junior High School was established on July 1, 1991, with Chen Shih-chang serving as its first principal. The school sits on Kangning Road, in the same educational corridor as Minghu Elementary, Nanhu Elementary, and Nanhu Senior High School — a cluster of schools serving the same fast-growing residential belt. Minghu also shares attendance-zone neighborhoods, including Huzhou, Jinhu, Kangning, Minghu, and Luzhou villages, with neighboring Sanmin Junior High School.

明湖國中創立於民國80年（1991年）7月1日，首任校長為陳士彰。學校座落於康寧路，與明湖國小、南湖國小及南湖高中同處一條教育廊帶，共同服務同一快速發展的住宅區域。明湖國中並與鄰近的三民國中共享葫洲里、金湖里、康寧里、明湖里、蘆洲里等學區里別。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛣️ Part of the Kangning Road Cluster

康寧路學校群

Minghu Junior High shares its Kangning Road corridor with Minghu Elementary, Nanhu Elementary, and Nanhu Senior High, forming a dense cluster of schools in the same residential belt.

跨五里共享學區

The school shares attendance-zone neighborhoods — Huzhou, Jinhu, Kangning, Minghu, and Luzhou villages — with neighboring Sanmin Junior High School.

👤 A 1991 Founding Under Its First Principal

首任校長陳士彰

Established July 1, 1991, the school opened under its first principal, Chen Shih-chang, serving the growing Kangning Road residential area from the outset.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Built on former hillside farmland.

🏘️

Minghu Junior High sits in Neihu's fast-growing Kangning Road residential belt, developed on former hillside farmland during the district's 1980s–90s population boom — one of several "湖" (lake)-named schools in the corridor that trace back to the district's original lake and marsh geography.
