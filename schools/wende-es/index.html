Wende Elementary School

新北市板橋區文德國民小學

About · 學校簡介

Culture and virtue, at scale.

Wende Elementary was established around 1989, part of a wave of schools built with Confucian-virtue names — 文德 means "culture and virtue" — as Banqiao rapidly expanded its elementary-school network into new postwar neighborhoods. Today it is one of the district's largest schools, with roughly 2,430 students, a concert/brass band founded in 2004, an ecological garden, and an evening supplementary school.

文德國小創校於約民國78年（1989年）前後，是板橋為因應戰後新社區快速發展而興建的一批「儒家美德」命名學校之一——「文德」即取「文化與德行」之意。學校現為板橋規模最大的國小之一，學生數約2,430人，設有成立於2004年的管樂團與生態園區，並附設補校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎺 A Brass Band Since 2004

2004年成立管樂團

Wende's concert and brass band, founded in 2004, has become a signature extracurricular program at one of Banqiao's largest elementary schools.

🌿 A Campus With Its Own Ecological Garden

生態園區

An on-campus ecological garden gives students hands-on exposure to plant and habitat science within a dense urban school.

🏫 One of Banqiao's Largest Schools

板橋規模最大國小之一

With roughly 2,430 students, Wende ranks among the largest elementary schools in Banqiao, reflecting the density of the neighborhoods it serves.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A name from Banqiao's school-building wave.

📜

Wende's name — "文德," culture and virtue — belongs to a wave of Confucian-virtue-named schools built across Banqiao's postwar new neighborhoods, a naming convention adopted as the district rapidly expanded its elementary-school network. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
