About · 學校簡介

The anchor school of the district seat.

Guantian Elementary School was founded in 1913 as a branch of Liujia Public School. It became independent in 1918 as "Guandian Public School," was renamed "Guantian Public School" in 1920, and took its current name, Guantian Elementary School, in 1968. Over a century old, it serves as the anchor elementary school of the district seat, located a short distance from the water-rail wetlands and water-chestnut paddies for which Guantian is best known.

官田國小創立於民國2年（1913年），前身為六甲公學校分校。民國7年（1918年）獨立設校，命名「官佃公學校」，民國9年（1920年）改名「官田公學校」，民國57年（1968年）定名為現今的官田國小。作為已逾百年歷史的學校，官田國小是官田區行政中心的核心國小，鄰近讓官田聞名的水雉溼地與菱角田。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Over a Century of History

逾百年校史

Founded in 1913 as a branch of Liujia Public School, Guantian Elementary became independent in 1918 and has served the district seat for well over a century.

🏛️ Renamed Through the Eras

歷經多次更名

The school carried the names "Guandian Public School" (1918) and "Guantian Public School" (1920) before settling on its current title in 1968.

行政中心核心學校

As the oldest school in the district center, Guantian Elementary anchors the educational life of the district's administrative seat.

🌰 Beside the Water-Chestnut Paddies

緊鄰菱角田

Local Culture · 在地文化

A hundred years among the water-chestnut fields.

🌰

As the oldest school in the district center, Guantian Elementary has educated generations of families whose livelihoods have long depended on the water-chestnut (菱角) paddies that, in turn, sustain the rare water rail (水雉) the district is now famous for protecting.
