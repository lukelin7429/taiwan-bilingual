Fu'an Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A small school with a century of history.

Fu'an Elementary School traces its history to April 1922, when it began as a branch teaching site of neighboring Sezi Elementary School called "Xizhoudi campus" (溪洲底校場). It became an independent school, "Taipei County Fu'an National School," in 1946, and took its current name, "富安國民小學," in 1968 under the nine-year compulsory education reform. Fu'an remains a small school by Taipei standards — as of the 2021-22 academic year, it had just 8 classes, 143 students, and 21 teachers — reflecting how education spread outward from older core schools into Shilin's outlying Sezi Island riverside farmland.

富安國小的歷史可追溯至大正11年（1922年）4月，當時為鄰近社子國小的分教場，稱為「溪洲底校場」。民國35年（1946年）獨立設校，稱「臺北縣立富安國民學校」，民國57年（1968年）九年國教改制後定名為「富安國民小學」。富安國小至今仍是臺北市規模較小的學校之一——110學年度僅有8班、學生143人、教師21人——見證了教育從士林核心老校逐步擴散至社子島河濱農地的歷程。

富洲里在地學校

Local Culture · 在地文化

Farmland at the river's edge.

🌾

Fu'an Elementary sits on the Sezi Island peninsula at Shilin's northwestern edge, a low-lying area shaped by the confluence of the Danshui and Keelung Rivers, historically farmland and flood-prone terrain distinct from the district's mountain and temple-market areas.
