About · 學校簡介

A village school by the sea.

Shande Elementary School serves the small coastal village of Xiding in Taixi Township. The school operates an attached kindergarten for the community's youngest learners, and offers an English-speaking showcase together with Taiwan Mother Language Day programming that keeps local language and culture alive alongside English learning.

尚德國小服務台西鄉溪頂村這處濱海小聚落。學校附設幼兒園，照顧社區裡最年幼的學童，並設有英語情境展示區，同時推動臺灣母語日活動，讓在地語言文化與英語學習並行不悖。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🧒 An Attached Kindergarten

附設幼兒園

Shande operates its own attached kindergarten, extending early-childhood education to the youngest members of this small coastal village.

🗣️ An English-Speaking Showcase

英語情境展示區

The school maintains an English-speaking showcase area, giving students a dedicated space to build everyday language confidence.

🎎 Taiwan Mother Language Day

臺灣母語日活動

Regular Mother Language Day programming keeps local Taiwanese and Hoklo heritage alive alongside the school's English-learning efforts.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A small village amid Taixi's coastal belt.

🦪

Situated in Taixi Township's coastal belt, the school's small-village setting reflects the area's fishing and oyster-farming livelihood, where generations of families have made their living from the sea. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
