Xincen Elementary School

新岑國小

A coastal elementary school known for horseshoe crab conservation education and robotics competitions, blending wetland ecology with modern STEM learning.

About · 學校簡介

Where horseshoe crabs meet robotics.

Xincen Elementary School was founded in September 1962, and today lives by its motto "健康創新 喜閱永續" (Health & Innovation, Joyful Reading & Sustainability). The school is distinctive for its horseshoe crab (鱟) conservation education and wetland/water-bird studies, drawing directly on Budai's coastal ecology, alongside robotics education with FLL (FIRST LEGO League) competition participation, percussion/drumming arts, and PBL energy education. It also runs an attached kindergarten and a maker/digital-creative classroom.

新岑國小創立於民國51年（1962年）9月，校訓為「健康創新 喜閱永續」。學校特色課程包括鱟保育教育及濕地水鳥生態研究，直接連結布袋濱海生態環境，同時推動機器人教育並參與FLL（國際奧林匹克機器人）競賽，另設有打擊樂藝術課程及PBL能源教育。學校並附設幼兒園及自造/數位創客教室。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🦀 Horseshoe Crab Conservation

鱟保育教育

Distinctive horseshoe crab (鱟) conservation education and wetland/water-bird studies connect students directly to the tidal mudflats that historically nursed horseshoe crab populations along Budai's coast.

🤖 Robotics & FLL Competitions

機器人與FLL競賽

Robotics education with FIRST LEGO League (FLL) competition participation gives students in this small coastal town a competitive STEM outlet.

🥁 Percussion & Drumming Arts

打擊樂藝術

A percussion/drumming arts program adds a performing-arts dimension to the school's broader emphasis on hands-on, creative learning.

🔧 A Maker & Energy-Education Classroom

自造與能源教育教室

A dedicated maker/digital-creative classroom and PBL energy education round out a curriculum that pairs local ecology with contemporary STEM skills.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Tidal mudflats and horseshoe crabs.

🦀

The horseshoe crab and wetland/water-bird curriculum directly reflects the tidal mudflats and lagoons around Budai, historically a nursery habitat for horseshoe crabs and migratory shorebirds tied to the area's oyster-farming geography. See the Chiayi county page for more on the region.
