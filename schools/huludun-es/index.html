Huludun Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A new school with an old name.

Huludun Elementary School was established in 1997 and admitted its first class in 2000, making it one of Fengyuan's newest campuses. Despite being brand new, the school chose a deliberately historic name: Huludun, the old Pingpu-derived town name by which Fengyuan was known before its modern name was adopted — a conscious nod to local heritage on a thoroughly modern campus.

葫蘆墩國小創立於民國86年（1997年），民國89年（2000年）招收首屆學生，是豐原地區最新設立的校園之一。雖為新校，校名卻刻意選用具歷史意涵的「葫蘆墩」——豐原在採用現今地名前，源自平埔族的舊稱——在現代化的校園中，向在地歷史文化致敬。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Reviving Fengyuan's oldest place name.

🏺

The name Huludun (葫蘆墩) is Fengyuan's own historic place name, rooted in the area's Pingpu Aboriginal-era history — and this new school's choice to revive it reflects a broader pride in Fengyuan's deep local heritage. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
