Hulu Elementary School

臺北市士林區葫蘆國民小學

About · 學校簡介

Overflow relief for a booming riverside area.

Hulu Elementary School was founded on August 1, 1975, specifically to relieve overcrowding at Sezi Elementary as the population of the "Hulu Island" (葫蘆島) area surged. The school takes its name from this "Hulu" (gourd-shaped) river island area of Sezi at Shilin's northwestern tip, between the Danshui and Keelung Rivers. One of the youngest elementary schools in the district, it was split off directly from Sezi Elementary alongside Bailing Elementary.

葫蘆國小創立於民國64年（1975年）8月1日，因應「葫蘆島」地區人口激增，為紓解社子國小過度擁擠而設立。校名取自社子地區「葫蘆」形河川沙洲，位於士林西北端、淡水河與基隆河交會處。作為士林區最年輕的小學之一，葫蘆國小與百齡國小同樣是從社子國小分出獨立設校。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎈 Named for a Gourd-Shaped Island

葫蘆島得名

The school is named for the "Hulu" (gourd-shaped) river island area of Sezi, formed at the confluence of the Danshui and Keelung Rivers.

📈 Built for a Population Boom

因人口激增而設校

Founded in 1975 specifically to relieve overcrowding at Sezi Elementary as the local population surged, tracing the rapid growth of Shilin's riverside communities.

👯 A Sister School to Bailing

與百齡國小同源

Hulu was split off directly from Sezi Elementary alongside Bailing Elementary, making the two schools historical siblings.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where two rivers meet.

🌊

Located on the Sezi/Hulu Island river-confluence area, Hulu Elementary's very founding — as overflow relief for the older Sezi Elementary — traces the rapid mid-20th-century population growth of Shilin's low-lying riverside farmland communities. See the Taipei county page for more on the region.
