About · 學校簡介

A name that carries a story of overseas generosity.

Chiaochien Elementary School serves families in Nantou City, and while detailed institutional records beyond its basic administrative structure are not readily available, the school's name carries a story worth knowing. The character 僑 ("overseas") in Chiaochien reflects a mid-20th-century tradition in which overseas Chinese communities donated funds to help build rural Taiwan schools — a pattern also seen at nearby Caotun's 僑光國小 (Chiaoguang Elementary School). This shared naming convention across Nantou County points to a broader history of diaspora philanthropy supporting local education.

僑建國小服務南投市在地家庭。目前除基本行政架構外，尚未取得更詳細的機構歷史資料，但校名本身即承載著一段值得認識的故事。「僑建」中的「僑」字反映了二十世紀中葉海外華僑社群捐資協助興建台灣鄉間學校的傳統，鄰近草屯的「僑光國小」也是同樣命名脈絡下的例子。這項南投縣內共通的命名傳統，展現出僑胞對在地教育發展的深遠貢獻。
