About · 學校簡介

From a junior high to a complete secondary school.

Sanchong Senior High School traces its history to 1962, when it opened as Taipei County Sanchong Junior High School. The school was upgraded into a complete secondary institution — combining junior high and senior high divisions — in 1998, took the name Sanchong Senior High School in 2001, and adopted its present English identity in 2010 following Taipei County's upgrade to New Taipei City. Today it enrolls roughly 2,134 students across 68 classes, offering art classes at both junior- and senior-high levels, a physical-education class, and a language-experimental class for senior-high students, alongside 28 student clubs. The campus sits near the intersection of four bridges linking Sanchong to Taipei, one of the district's oldest continuous educational sites.

三重高中的歷史可追溯至民國51年（1962年）創立的「臺北縣立三重初級中學」。民國87年（1998年）改制為國中部與高中部兼具的完全中學，民國90年（2001年）更名為「三重高級中學」，民國99年（2010年）配合臺北縣升格新北市而確立現名。現有學生約2,134人，共68班，設有國中部與高中部美術班、體育班，以及高中部語文實驗班，並有28個學生社團。校址鄰近連接三重與臺北的四座橋樑交會處，是三重歷史最悠久的教育場域之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 A Complete Secondary School

國中部與高中部兼具

Sanchong Senior High grew from a junior high into a full JH-through-SH institution in 1998, giving students a continuous six-year track under one roof.

🎨 Art and Language Tracks

美術班與語文實驗班

Dedicated art classes run at both the junior- and senior-high levels, complemented by a senior-high language-experimental class and a physical-education class.

🌉 At the Crossroads of Four Bridges

四橋交會的校園

The campus sits near where four bridges converge in central Sanchong, linking the school directly to the district's role as a gateway to Taipei.

🎭 28 Student Clubs

28個學生社團

A broad slate of 28 clubs gives students room to pursue interests beyond the classroom, from the arts to athletics.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Growing up alongside Sanchong.

🌉

Sitting at the "crossroads of four bridges" linking Sanchong to Taipei, the school has grown alongside the district's transformation from riverside farmland into a dense Taipei-metro suburb, serving as one of Sanchong's oldest educational anchors.
