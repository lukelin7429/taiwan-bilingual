Renmei Junior High School

仁美國民中學

A traditional municipal junior high on the Gaoshanding plateau that also runs Taiwan's first public "one-school, two-systems" Waldorf program, teaching both English and German.

位於高山頂台地的市立國中，同時開辦全台首創「公辦公營一校兩制」華德福實驗班，教授英語與德語兩種外語。

About · 學校簡介

Traditional roots, an alternative-education wing.

Renmei Junior High began in 1963 as a branch campus of Taoyuan County Yangmei Middle School at Puxin, becoming an independent school in 1966 under the name Taoyuan County Renmei Junior High. It took its current name after the 1968 nine-year compulsory education reform, and became a Taoyuan City school when the county was upgraded to a special municipality in 2014. In 2008 it broke new ground by converting some underused classrooms into a publicly run Waldorf-method experimental program — the first public school in Taiwan to operate a Waldorf track alongside a conventional one under a single roof.

仁美國中創立於民國52年（1963年），前身為桃園縣立楊梅中學埔心分部；民國55年（1966年）獨立設校，定名桃園縣立仁美初級中學；民國57年（1968年）九年國教實施後改制為桃園縣立仁美國民中學；民國103年（2014年）12月因桃園縣升格直轄市更名為桃園市立仁美國民中學。2008年，仁美國中首創「公辦公營」形式，將部分閒置教室改設華德福實驗國中小，是全台第一所與傳統學制並存的「一校兩制」公立華德福學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 Taiwan's First Public Waldorf Track

全台首創公辦公營華德福班

Since 2008, Renmei has run a publicly operated Waldorf-method experimental program alongside its conventional curriculum — a "one-school, two-systems" model that was the first of its kind in Taiwan.

🗣️ Two Foreign Languages, Waldorf-Style

同時教授英語與德語

The Waldorf classes follow anthroposophy-based curriculum design and teach both English and German, a rare dual-language offering for a Taiwanese public junior high.

🏅 A Designated Education Research Hub

教育領航研究中心

In 2009, the county government designated Renmei an education-leadership research center, recognizing its role in piloting alternative teaching approaches for other schools.

⛰️ On the Gaoshanding Plateau

位於高山頂地區

The school sits in Yangmei's "Gaoshanding" (high plateau) area, a historic Hakka farming district that has grown alongside the school's own decades of educational experimentation.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Trying something new in a traditional Hakka township.

🌾

Renmei's public Waldorf program opened an alternative path for families within Yangmei's traditional Hakka educational system, reflecting a district character that is inclusive and willing to experiment with new ideas even while rooted in long-standing customs.
