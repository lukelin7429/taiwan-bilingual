Dacun Elementary School

大村國民小學

About · 學校簡介

An old swallow-emblem school with a modern choir.

Dacun Elementary traces its roots to 1914, when it opened as a branch of Yuanlin Public School under the name "大庄分校." In 1916 it was renamed Yanwu Public School, echoing the township's historical place name "燕霧大莊" (Yanwu Dazhuang) — a heritage still reflected in the school's swallow (燕) emblem today. The school took its current name, Dacun National School, in 1941, and in recent years has become known nationally and internationally for both its choir and its English-immersion teaching.

大村國小創校於民國3年（1914年），最初為員林公學校的分校，稱作「大庄分校」。民國5年（1916年）更名為燕霧公學校，呼應大村舊地名「燕霧大莊」——這段歷史至今仍反映在學校以燕子為意象的校徽上。民國30年（1941年）改稱大村國民學校。近年來，學校在合唱與英語浸潤教學方面皆享有國內外知名度。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏆 Gold in Krakow

克拉科夫金牌合唱團

The school's choir won a Gold Diploma and the Grand Prix at the 2023 Krakow International Choir Competition in Poland — a remarkable achievement for a rural elementary school choir.

🐦 The Swallow's Legacy

燕子校徽的由來

The school's swallow (燕) emblem traces back to Dacun's old place name, "燕霧大莊" (Yanwu Dazhuang), a name the school itself carried for decades before its current one.

📖 A Reading Milestone

閱讀里程碑獎

Dacun received the Ministry of Education's 2024 Reading Milestone Award, recognizing its efforts to build a strong campus-wide reading culture.

🗣️ A Model for English Immersion

英語浸潤教學示範學校

The school is recognized as a demonstration school for immersive English teaching, making it a reference point for other schools developing bilingual programs.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Dacun's Yanwu heritage.

🐦

Dacun Township was historically known as "燕霧大莊" (Yanwu Dazhuang), and the school has carried that heritage forward through its swallow emblem and former name, Yanwu Public School — a small but enduring link between the campus and its township's past.
