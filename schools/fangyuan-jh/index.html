Fangyuan Junior High School

芳苑國民中學

A public junior high school that grew from two borrowed classrooms in 1962 into an independent campus, now home to a lively Food and Agriculture Festival.

About · 學校簡介

From two borrowed classrooms to an independent school.

Fangyuan Junior High was established in August 1962 as a branch of Erlin Junior High School, known then as "Erlin Junior High School Fangyuan Branch." It began with just two classes, borrowing space at the Fangyuan Township Office's Zhongshan Hall and at Fangyuan Elementary School. The Taiwan Provincial Government approved its independence on July 20, 1964, and its first principal, Huang Jing, took office on June 18, 1965.

芳苑國中創立於民國51年（1962年）8月，最初是二林初級中學的分部，稱為「二林初級中學芳苑分部」，僅設2個班級，借用芳苑鄉公所中山堂與芳苑國小的空間上課。台灣省政府於民國53年（1964年）7月20日核准獨立設校，首任校長黃景於民國54年（1965年）6月18日到職。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Starting With Two Classes

從兩個班級起家

Fangyuan Junior High opened in 1962 with just two classes, borrowing rooms at the township office's Zhongshan Hall and at Fangyuan Elementary School before becoming independent two years later.

🌾 Food and Agriculture Festival

食農嘉年華會

The school hosts a lively Food and Agriculture Festival (食農嘉年華會) that connects students with the coastal farming traditions of Fangyuan Township.

🐉 Dragon and Lion Dance Traditions

舞龍舞獅文化

Students take part in dragon and lion dance programs, keeping local folk traditions alive as part of campus life.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where farming and folk tradition meet the classroom.

🥁

Fangyuan Junior High's Food and Agriculture Festival and its dragon and lion dance programs directly connect campus life to Fangyuan Township's coastal farming economy and long-standing folk-tradition community. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
