Jungong Elementary School

軍功國民小學

A century-old school that survived the 1999 921 earthquake and still preserves a historic Japanese-era teacher's dormitory.

About · 學校簡介

Rebuilt after the 921 earthquake.

Jungong Elementary began in 1920 as Jungongliao Public School during the Japanese colonial era, taking its current name in 1969. The school's history took a dramatic turn in 1999, when the devastating 921 earthquake completely destroyed its campus; students were relocated temporarily while a new campus was built, reopening in 2001. Remarkably, a Japanese-era teacher's dormitory on the grounds survived and has since been registered as a Taichung historic building.

軍功國小創立於1920年（日治時期），最初名為軍功寮公學校，1969年改為現今校名。1999年，921大地震重創校園、幾乎全毀，學生一度遷校暫時上課，直到2001年新校舍重建完成後才復校。校內一棟日治時期教師宿舍奇蹟般倖存下來，如今已登錄為臺中市歷史建築。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏚️ A Surviving Japanese-Era Dormitory

登錄歷史建築的教師宿舍

A colonial-era teacher's dormitory still stands on campus, one of the few physical remnants of the school's founding decades, and is officially registered as a Taichung historic building.

🌏 Rebuilt After 921

921大地震後重建

The 1999 921 earthquake completely destroyed the original campus, forcing a temporary relocation before a rebuilt school reopened in 2001 — a dramatic chapter in the school's century-long story.

🏫 A Century Under Three Names

近百年校名沿革

From its 1920 founding as Jungongliao Public School to its current name adopted in 1969, the school's naming history spans the colonial era through to modern Taichung.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A physical record of two eras.

🏚️

The surviving Japanese-era teacher's dormitory and the school's dramatic 921-earthquake rebuilding make it a physical record of both Beitun's colonial past and Taiwan's most significant modern natural disaster. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
