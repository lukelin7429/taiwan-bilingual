About · 學校簡介

A baseball-loving school with a name worth clarifying.

Peinan (Beinan) Elementary is a public elementary school in Taitung City recognized for a competitive youth baseball team that has earned national-level placements — a point of pride reflecting the county's wider baseball tradition. The school also runs active traffic-safety and disaster-preparedness education programs for its students. It is worth noting that this is a separate, more recently founded school from the historically distinct 南王國小 (Nanwang Elementary), despite both institutions sitting within the same general area of Taitung City.

卑南國小是臺東市的公立國小，以青少棒隊聞名，曾獲全國級比賽名次——這也呼應了臺東縣悠久的棒球傳統。學校同時積極推動交通安全與防災教育。值得說明的是，本校與歷史悠久的「南王國小」是兩所不同的學校，雖然兩校皆位於臺東市大致相同的區域，但創校時間與沿革並不相同。
