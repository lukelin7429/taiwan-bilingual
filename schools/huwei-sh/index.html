National Huwei Senior High School

國立虎尾高級中學

A national senior high school in Huwei Town, formed by the 1968 merger of a girls' school founded in 1940 and a boys' school founded in 1946.

虎尾鎮的國立高中，由1940年創立的女中與1946年創立的高中，於1968年合併而成。

About · 學校簡介

Born from the merger of two historic schools.

National Huwei Senior High School was formed in 1968 through the merger of Taiwan Provincial Huwei Girls' Senior High School, established in 1940, and Huwei Senior High School, established in 1946. The combined school became a national-level institution in February 2000. Huwei's civic identity was shaped by its sugar-refining heritage, home to the historic Huwei Sugar Refinery, and the school has served generations of local families connected to that industrial legacy.

國立虎尾高級中學於民國57年（1968年）由台灣省立虎尾女子高級中學（創立於1940年）與虎尾高級中學（創立於1946年）合併而成。合併後的學校於民國89年（2000年）2月改制為國立高中。虎尾的城鎮認同深受糖業文化影響，是歷史悠久的虎尾糖廠所在地，學校世代服務著與這段工業歷史緊密相連的當地家庭。

🏫 A 1968 Merger of Two Schools

1968年兩校合併

Huwei Senior High combines the legacies of a 1940 girls' school and a 1946 boys' school, merged into one institution in 1968.

🎓 National-Level Status Since 2000

2000年改制國立

The school became a national-level institution in February 2000, reflecting its standing as one of Yunlin County's leading senior high schools.

🏭 Tied to Huwei's Sugar Heritage

與虎尾糖業歷史相連

Generations of local families connected to Huwei's sugar-refining industry have passed through the school, linking it closely to the town's industrial legacy.
