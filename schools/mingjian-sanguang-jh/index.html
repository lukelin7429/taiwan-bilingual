Sanguang Junior High School

三光國民中學

A rural junior high school among Mingjian's tea hills, known for a baseball program that gives rural students a path to athletic development.

About · 學校簡介

Character, reading, and rural athletic opportunity.

Sanguang Junior High School serves the rural villages of Mingjian Township, guided by a school motto that emphasizes character, reading, the arts, and vitality. The school offers gifted-education programs alongside a boarding dormitory, and it has built a particular reputation for its baseball program, which specifically supports students from rural farming communities in pursuing athletic development that might otherwise be out of reach.

三光國中服務名間鄉的鄉村聚落，校訓強調品格、閱讀、藝術與活力。學校設有資優教育班與住宿型宿舍，並以棒球隊聞名——該項目特別協助來自鄉村農家的學生發展運動專長，提供他們原本難以取得的發展機會。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚾ A Baseball Program for Rural Students

棒球隊扶助偏鄉學生

Sanguang's baseball program specifically supports students from rural farming villages in pursuing athletic development, opening doors that might otherwise be closed.

🏠 Boarding Dormitory Support

住宿型宿舍

The school offers a boarding dormitory, allowing students from Mingjian's more remote tea-farming villages to attend without a long daily commute.

🌟 Gifted Education Programs

資優教育班

Sanguang runs gifted-education programs alongside its general curriculum, extending opportunity to academically advanced students in a rural setting.

📚 Character, Reading, Arts, Vitality

品格、閱讀、藝術、活力

The school's motto — emphasizing character, reading, the arts, and vitality — shapes a curriculum aimed at well-rounded rural student development.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Among Mingjian's tea hills.

🍃

Set among Mingjian's rolling tea hills, Sanguang Junior High's rural-outreach baseball and dormitory programs serve students from the township's tea-farming villages around Songbolang, home to Taiwan's largest tea-growing township. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
