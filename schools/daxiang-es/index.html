Daxiang Elementary School

大鄉國民小學

A rural school on Puzi's outskirts with an active taekwondo team, guided by the motto 'Healthy, Happy, Creative Daxiang.'

About · 學校簡介

From a branch campus to a creative, active school.

Daxiang Elementary began in 1952 as a branch of Puzi Elementary School, becoming an independent school in 1976. It celebrated its 40th anniversary and held its 48th and 49th graduation ceremonies in 2024 and 2025. The school's motto, 'Healthy, Happy, Creative Daxiang' (健康快樂、創意大鄉), captures its emphasis on an active taekwondo team and multiple sports competitions alongside bilingual/English education, science exhibitions, and reading/literary competitions. An attached kindergarten rounds out its offerings.

大鄉國小前身為民國41年（1952年）成立的朴子國小大鄉分校，民國65年（1976年）獨立設校，曾舉辦建校40週年慶祝活動，並於2024、2025年分別舉行第48、49屆畢業典禮。校訓「健康快樂、創意大鄉」點出學校特色——活躍的跆拳道隊與多項運動競賽，並重視雙語/英語教育、科學展覽與閱讀/文學競賽，另附設幼兒園。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Puzi's agricultural hinterland.

🌾

A small, historically rural school in Daxiang Village on Puzi's outskirts, Daxiang Elementary represents the agricultural hinterland surrounding the historic market town of Puzi — distinct from the denser urban core near the old street and temple district, but no less a part of the city's identity.
