About · 學校簡介

From one classroom to a rebuilt campus.

Dongguang Elementary was founded in April 1922 as a branch classroom of Yuchi Public School, with just a single class. It became independent as Dongguang National School in 1941, established its own branch class in 1959 (which itself became independent in 1965), and took its current name in 1968. The school suffered severe damage in Taiwan's devastating 921 earthquake of 1999, prompting the Tzu Chi Foundation to fund its reconstruction. The new campus was completed and dedicated on November 24, 2001.

東光國小創立於1922年（大正11年）4月，最初為玉田公學校的分教場，僅有一個班級。1941年（昭和16年）獨立設校，定名東光國民學校，1959年（民國48年）設立分班（該分班於1965年獨立設校），並於1968年（民國57年）定名為東光國小。學校在1999年（民國88年）921大地震中嚴重受損，經慈濟基金會出資協助重建，新校舍於2001年（民國90年）11月24日完工啟用。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Single-Class Beginning

從單班分教場起家

Dongguang started in 1922 as a single-class branch classroom of Yuchi Public School, growing over decades into a full independent school.

🏗️ Rebuilt After the 921 Earthquake

921地震後重建

Severely damaged in the 1999 921 earthquake, the school's reconstruction was funded by the Tzu Chi Foundation, with the new campus completed in 2001.

🌱 A Branch That Grew Its Own Branch

分班再獨立設校

After becoming independent in 1941, Dongguang itself established a branch class in 1959, which went on to become its own independent school in 1965.
