Shengli Elementary School

勝利國民小學

A Pingtung City public elementary school founded in 1946, sitting beside the restored Japanese-era air force dependents' village now known as Shengli Star Village.

About · 學校簡介

A city-center school beside a heritage village.

Shengli Elementary was established on April 10, 1946, when the postwar government took over a Japanese-era elementary school following Retrocession — meaning its campus roots reach back into the Japanese colonial period even though its formal Republic of China founding date is 1946. As of the 2022–23 school year, the school served 571 students across 22 classes with 44 teachers, and its campus includes a library, playground, computer room, gymnasium, roller-skating area, and volleyball court. Shengli sits right beside the Pingtung Railway Station and the "Shengli Star Village," a former air force dependents' community whose restored Japanese-era dormitories have become one of the city's best-known heritage tourism sites.

勝利國小於民國35年（1946年）4月10日創校，係接收自日人小學而成立，因此校地歷史可追溯至日治時期，惟正式建校年份為戰後的1946年。111學年度全校共22班、571名學生、44位教師。校園設有圖書館、操場、電腦教室、體育館、直排輪場及排球場。學校緊鄰屏東火車站與「勝利星村」——一處由日治時期空軍眷舍修復而成、現已成為屏東市代表性文化觀光景點的眷村聚落。
