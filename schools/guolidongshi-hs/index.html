National Dongshi Senior High School

國立東石高級中學

A century-old school with roots in agricultural vocational training, now offering bilingual and math/science experimental classes near Taiwan's oyster-farming coast.

About · 學校簡介

From agricultural school to bilingual senior high.

National Dongshi Senior High School traces its history back to September 1, 1926, when it opened as Tainan Prefecture's Dongshi Agricultural Vocational School during the Japanese colonial era. Through a series of name and status changes, it became Taiwan Provincial Dongshi Senior High School in 1968 and was elevated to a national-level school in 2000. Despite its name, the campus sits in Puzi City rather than Dongshi itself. Today it offers full academic and vocational/technical tracks alongside bilingual experimental classes and math/science experimental classes, along with a maker lab, and recently celebrated a century of history.

國立東石高級中學的歷史可追溯至大正15年（1926年）9月1日創校的臺南州立東石農業職業學校，歷經多次更名與體制調整，於民國57年（1968年）改制為臺灣省立東石高級中學，並於民國89年（2000年）改隸國立。校名雖為「東石」，校址其實位於朴子市。現今學校兼具學術與技職雙軌，並開設雙語實驗班、數理實驗班及自造實驗室，近年更歡慶建校百年，人稱「百年東高」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 Agricultural Vocational Roots

農業職業教育起源

Founded in 1926 as an agricultural vocational school under Japanese rule, the school evolved through several name and status changes before becoming a full senior high in 1968, retaining strong vocational/technical tracks alongside academic ones.

🗣️ Bilingual & STEM Experimental Classes

雙語實驗班與數理實驗班

The school runs dedicated bilingual experimental classes (雙語實驗班) and math/science experimental classes (數理實驗班), plus a maker lab (自造實驗室) for hands-on project work.

🏅 Sports & Arts Scholarships

體育與藝術獎學金

Active sports and arts scholarship programs support students pursuing athletic and creative pathways alongside the school's academic and vocational tracks.

🎉 A Century of History

百年東高

Given its 1926 founding, the school marked its centennial with celebrations of "a century of splendor" (世紀風華), a milestone few Taiwan senior highs can claim.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in the Dongshi/Puzi coastal plain.

🦪

Despite bearing the Dongshi name, the school actually sits in Puzi City near Dongshi Township. Its agricultural-vocational origins reflect the long farming and fishing economy of the Dongshi/Puzi coastal plain, an area that has for generations relied on rice cultivation and oyster and fish aquaculture.
