Danei Junior High School

大內國中

Danei District's junior high, opened in 1968 on hilly terrain above the Zengwen River, and home to a Digital Opportunity Center serving the wider rural community.

大內區唯一國中，1968年創校，座落於曾文溪上游丘陵地帶，設有服務地方的數位機會中心。

About · 學校簡介

A hillside school serving rural Danei.

Danei Junior High School was established in August 1968, part of the nationwide rollout of nine-year compulsory education. The campus sits on hilly terrain, which the school itself describes as offering “a sea of green from the air,” and its vision statement centers on building a campus of “love and hope” (愛與希望). Beyond its regular classes, the school hosts a Digital Opportunity Center on the third floor of its computer classroom building, serving the wider rural Danei community's digital-access needs.

大內國中創立於民國57年（1968年）8月，是九年國教全面推行下設立的學校之一。校園座落於丘陵地帶，學校形容從空中俯瞰宛如「一片綠海」，並以「愛與希望」作為辦學願景核心。除一般班級教學外，學校在電腦教室3樓設有「大內數位機會中心」，服務周邊農村地區居民的數位近用需求。

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the edge of Danei's hillside pomelo country.

🍈

The school's hillside setting above the Zengwen River floodplain places it at the edge of Danei's pomelo orchards, reflecting the district's identity as a small, hilly farming community rather than a flatland town. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
