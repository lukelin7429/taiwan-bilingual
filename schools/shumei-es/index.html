Shumei Elementary School

述美國民小學

A century-old "General School" that celebrated 100 years with a round-Taiwan bike ride and now boasts the island's longest campus roller slide.

百年「將軍小學」，百年校慶曾舉辦環台千公里騎行活動，如今擁有全台最長的校園溜滑梯。

About · 學校簡介

A century of schooling, freshly renovated.

Shumei Elementary traces its history back to February 1921, though it went through multiple mergers and renamings over the following decades before taking its current name in 1970, in honor of General Meng Shu-mei. The school celebrated its 100th anniversary in November 2021 with festivities including a 1,000-kilometer round-Taiwan cycling event and tributes to notable alumni. A roughly NT$65 million campus renovation recently added what is described as Taiwan's longest campus roller slide.

述美國小的校史可追溯至民國10年（1921年）2月，其後歷經多次合併與更名，於民國59年（1970年）以孟述美將軍之名定為現名。學校於2021年11月舉辦建校百年校慶，活動包括一場環台千公里騎行以及表揚傑出校友。近年一項耗資約新台幣6,500萬元的校園整建工程，打造了號稱全台最長的校園溜滑梯。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎖️ Another of Kinmen's "General Schools"

又一所「將軍小學」

Shumei is one of six "General Schools" on Kinmen, named for the military officer — General Meng Shu-mei — who directed its construction during the martial-law era.

🎂 A Century Marked With a Long Ride

騎行千里慶百年

The school's 100th-anniversary celebrations in November 2021 included a 1,000-kilometer round-Taiwan cycling event honoring its century of history and notable alumni.

🛝 Taiwan's Longest Campus Roller Slide

全台最長校園溜滑梯

A roughly NT$65 million campus renovation recently installed what is described as Taiwan's longest roller slide, giving the century-old campus a playful new landmark.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A textbook Cold War-era "General School."

🎖️

Shumei is a textbook example of a Kinmen "General School" (將軍小學) of the Cold War frontline era, named for the military officer who directed its construction during the period of wartime martial administration. See the Kinmen county page for more on the region.
