About · 學校簡介

A school named after an old place, serving a farming district.

Wufu Elementary takes its name from the "Wulipai" (五里牌), a traditional place-name region in southwestern Tongxiao Township covering today's Wubei and Wunan villages. The school serves this farming community, part of a coastal district shaped by sandy soils well suited to sweet potato, peanut, and watermelon cultivation.

五福國小校名源自通霄鎮西南部的傳統地名「五里牌」，範圍涵蓋今日的五北里與五南里。學校服務這個農業聚落，屬於沙質土壤沿海地帶，適合種植地瓜、花生與西瓜等作物。
