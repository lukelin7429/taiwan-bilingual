About · 學校簡介

An award-winning history project from a small village school.

Dongrong Elementary began as a branch of Minsyong National School in January 1953, started enrolling grades 1-6 that September, became independent as "東榮國民學校" in September 1954, and was renamed "東榮國民小學" in 1968 under the nine-year compulsory education reform. Now with 70-plus years of history, the school runs STEAM and robotics programs using WeDo robotics and Scratch programming, and made a name for itself with its "National Radio Museum" digital project, which won a Gold Award in Taiwan's School Cyberfair competition in 2012 and a Silver Award at the international level in 2013. The school also emphasizes environmental education, reading promotion, and local-language (母語) instruction.

東榮國小的前身是民國42年（1953年）1月成立的民雄國校分校，同年9月開始招收一至六年級學生，於民國43年（1954年）9月獨立設校為「東榮國民學校」，並於民國57年（1968年）九年國教改制後更名為「東榮國民小學」。如今學校已有70餘年歷史，推動使用WeDo機器人與Scratch程式設計的STEAM教育，並以「國家廣播文物館」數位專題聞名，該作品於2012年榮獲全國學校Cyberfair競賽金獎，並於2013年獲得國際銀獎。學校也重視環境教育、閱讀推廣及母語教學。

國際Cyberfair銀獎

The school's "National Radio Museum" digital project won gold at Taiwan's School Cyberfair in 2012 and silver at the international level in 2013, a remarkable achievement for a small village school.
