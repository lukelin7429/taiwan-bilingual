About · 學校簡介

Movement, mentorship, and mountain trails.

Wenlan Elementary serves Truku Indigenous families in Wenlan Village, deep in the Xiulin Township mountains. The school is known for a distinctive "Active Living Dance" program that gets students moving, while outdoor experiential-education trails connect classroom learning to the surrounding landscape. Truku-language teaching assistants support language transmission on campus, and the Lanxin Education Foundation helps fund scholarships for disadvantaged students, easing the financial barriers that can affect families in this remote mountain community.

文蘭國小服務位於秀林鄉山區文蘭村的太魯閣族家庭。學校以獨具特色的「活力生活舞蹈」課程聞名，讓學生透過肢體律動學習；戶外體驗教育步道則將課堂學習與周邊自然環境連結起來。校內設有太魯閣族語教學助理，協助族語傳承；蘭馨教育基金會也提供獎學金，資助弱勢學生，減輕這個偏遠山區家庭可能面臨的經濟負擔。
