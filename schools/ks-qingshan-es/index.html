About · 學校簡介

From military-family classroom to neighborhood school.

Qingshan Elementary School was founded in the fall of 1950 as “聯合勤務總司令部附設高雄子弟學校,” a school established for the children of personnel of the Joint Logistics Command (聯勤總司令部). Its first classes were held in thatch-roofed buildings amid rice paddies just outside the Command's uniform factory, in what was then a rural stretch of Siaogang. The school took its current name, Qingshan, in 1979. Its origin as a military-dependents' school reflects the postwar military and industrial land use that shaped Siaogang's landscape well before its later urbanization.

青山國小創立於民國39年（1950年）秋，原名「聯合勤務總司令部附設高雄子弟學校」，是為聯勤總司令部人員子女設立的學校。首批班級的教室是位於稻田之間的茅草屋，就在聯勤被服廠外圍，當時的小港仍是一片鄉村地帶。學校於民國68年（1979年）改用現名「青山」。其誕生於軍眷子弟學校的背景，見證了小港在後來都市化之前，戰後軍事與工業用地並存的地貌。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where military installations once met rice paddies.

🎖️

Qingshan Elementary's founding as a school for Joint Logistics Command families is a window into Siaogang's postwar landscape, where military installations and industrial facilities like the Command's uniform factory sat alongside the district's rice paddies, decades before land reclamation and urbanization reshaped the area.
