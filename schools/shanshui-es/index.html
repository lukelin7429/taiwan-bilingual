Shanshui Elementary School

山水國民小學

A small school in one of Penghu's most scenic coastal villages, famous for its beach and basalt shoreline.

About · 學校簡介

From a branch school to an independent seaside campus.

Shanshui Elementary began in August 1958 as Wude Elementary's Suogang branch school, before becoming fully independent as Shanshui National Elementary in 1967. It took its current Chinese name in 1968, and was updated again in 1981 to reflect Magong Township's promotion to city status. Today the school serves the children of Shanshui Village, a coastal community on Penghu's south shore.

山水國小於民國47年（1958年）8月，最初以五德國小鎖港分校的形式成立，於民國56年（1967年）獨立設校，稱山水國民學校。民國57年（1968年）定名為現名，並於民國70年（1981年）馬公鎮升格為市後再次調整校名。如今學校服務澎湖南岸山水村的學童。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏖️ A Beachside Village Campus

海灘村落校園

Shanshui Elementary sits within one of Penghu's best-known coastal villages, famed for its sandy beach and dramatic basalt shoreline scenery.

🏫 A School That Grew Independent

由分校獨立設校

Originally a branch of Wude Elementary, Shanshui became a fully independent school in 1967, part of the steady expansion of schooling across Magong's coastal villages.

🌋 Volcanic Coastline on Its Doorstep

玄武岩海岸近在咫尺

Students grow up steps from Penghu's striking columnar basalt formations, a geological feature that defines much of the archipelago's southern coast.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where basalt meets the sea.

🌋

Shanshui Village is known across Penghu for its beach and volcanic basalt coastline, a landscape shaped by ancient lava flows that now draws beachgoers and tourists to Magong's south shore. See the Penghu county page for more on the region.
