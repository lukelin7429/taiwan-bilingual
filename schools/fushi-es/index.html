About · 學校簡介

The school at the mouth of Taroko Gorge.

Fushi Elementary serves Fushi Village, the community that sits historically at the very heart of what gave Taroko Gorge its name — the school is essentially located at the gorge's mouth. Alongside its academic program, Fushi has built a nationally competitive women's soccer program with recent championship wins, and runs a Truku Indigenous-language e-learning initiative that brings digital tools to bear on cultural language preservation.

富世國小服務富世村，這個部落在歷史上正是「太魯閣」一名的發源核心地帶——學校幾乎座落於太魯閣峽谷的入口處。除了學業教育之外，富世國小也發展出具全國競爭力的女子足球隊，近年屢獲冠軍佳績，並推動太魯閣族語數位學習計畫，運用科技工具協助族語文化的保存。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚽ A Championship Women's Soccer Team

全國性女子足球隊

Fushi fields a nationally competitive women's soccer program that has earned recent championship wins — a remarkable achievement for a small elementary school at the edge of Taroko Gorge.

💻 Truku Language E-Learning

太魯閣族語數位學習

The school runs a dedicated e-learning program for Truku Indigenous language, pairing digital tools with cultural and language preservation.

🚪 Gateway to Taroko Gorge

太魯閣峽谷的門戶村落

The school sits in Fushi Village, essentially at the mouth of Taroko Gorge — home to one of the most visited natural landmarks in Taiwan.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The village that gave Taroko its name.

🏞️

Fushi Village is historically the namesake heart of "Taroko" itself — the school sits essentially at the mouth of the gorge that draws visitors from around the world. It remains home to Truku Indigenous families whose connection to this landscape long predates the national park. See the Hualien county page for more on the region.
