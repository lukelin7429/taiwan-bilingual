About · 學校簡介

Giving Nangang its own high school.

Yucheng Senior High began as a preparatory office established on August 1, 1996, originally named the "市立南港高中籌備處." After the neighboring Nangang Junior High was upgraded to Nangang Senior High, the naming conflict led to a rename in 1998 to "臺北市立育成高級中學籌備處." The school opened for enrollment in 2002 under founding principal Chen Bi-du, created under Taipei's "one senior high per district" (區區有高中) policy since Nangang previously had no senior high of its own. It emphasizes a social-science-oriented curriculum, and its riverside campus along the Keelung River directly borders the Nangang Software Park, Nangang Vocational High School, and Nangang Elementary School.

育成高中的前身為民國85年（1996年）8月1日成立的「市立南港高中籌備處」，後因鄰近的南港國中升格為南港高中而產生校名衝突，民國87年（1998年）改名為「臺北市立育成高級中學籌備處」，並於民國91年（2002年）在首任校長陳碧渡帶領下正式招生。學校依台北市「區區有高中」政策設立，讓原本沒有專屬高中的南港區擁有自己的高中。育成高中著重社會組課程規劃，校址沿基隆河而建，直接與南港軟體園區、南港高工及南港國小為鄰。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Born From "One Senior High Per District"

區區有高中政策

Yucheng was created specifically to give Nangang District its first dedicated senior high school under Taipei's district-coverage policy.

📚 A Social-Science Focus

著重社會組課程

The school emphasizes a social-science-oriented curriculum, distinguishing it among Nangang's senior highs.

🌊 A Riverside Campus

沿基隆河而建

Yucheng's campus sits along the Keelung River, directly bordering the Nangang Software Park and neighboring Nangang Vocational High and Nangang Elementary.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Nangang's software and biotech corridor.

💻

Yucheng Senior High's riverside location, wedged between the software park and older vocational-industrial campuses, embodies Nangang's late-1990s and 2000s transformation from coal-mining and light-industrial land into a software and biotech corridor.
