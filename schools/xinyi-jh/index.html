About · 學校簡介

From the 3rd Municipal Junior Middle School to an experimental junior high.

Xinyi Junior High was founded in August 1961 as "Keelung Municipal 3rd Junior Middle School." In August 1968, when nine-year compulsory education was implemented nationwide across Taiwan, the school was renamed Keelung Municipal Xinyi Junior High School, and simultaneously designated an experimental junior high school — among the first cohort of schools nationwide to carry that designation.

信義國中創校於1961年（民國50年）8月，前身為「基隆市立第三初級中學」。1968年（民國57年）8月，隨著全國實施九年國民義務教育，學校改名為「基隆市立信義國民中學」，同時獲指定為實驗國民中學，是全國最早一批獲此指定的學校之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🧪 An Early Experimental Junior High

早期實驗國中之一

When nine-year compulsory education launched nationwide in 1968, Xinyi Junior High was simultaneously designated an experimental junior high school.

🏫 Keelung's Third Junior Middle School

基隆市立第三初中

The school opened in 1961 as Keelung Municipal 3rd Junior Middle School, part of the city's mid-century expansion of junior secondary education.

📅 A 1968 Turning Point

1968年的轉捩點

Like many Taiwanese schools of its generation, Xinyi Junior High took its current name in 1968, the year nine-year compulsory education began nationwide.
