About · 學校簡介

An agricultural school from the Japanese era.

National Beigang Agricultural and Industrial Vocational Senior High School was originally founded in April 1941 as "Taiwan Public Beigang Specialized Agricultural School" under Japanese colonial rule. It became a national institution in 2000, and today its roughly 12-hectare campus serves about 605 students across seven departments spanning agricultural and industrial fields. As one of the oldest schools in Beigang, it has shaped the vocational training of the town for over eight decades.

國立北港農工創立於日治時期昭和16年（1941年）4月，原名「臺灣公立北港農業專修學校」。民國89年（2000年）改制為國立學校，如今校地約12公頃，學生約605人，設有橫跨農、工領域的七個科別。作為北港鎮歷史最悠久的學校之一，超過八十年來持續培育地方青年的職業技能。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Agricultural training beside Mazu's temple.

🛕

As one of Beigang's oldest schools, it has trained generations of local youth in the agricultural trades that historically supported the town alongside its renowned Chaotian Temple pilgrimage economy. The school's vocational mission has long run parallel to Beigang's identity as a major Mazu pilgrimage center. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
