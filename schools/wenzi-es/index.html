About · 學校簡介

A village school named for the fish ponds around it.

Wenzi Elementary is located in Wenfeng Village, in Jiadong Township — a village whose name directly references the local "塭" (fish pond), part of Jiadong's long-standing salt-pond and fish-farming aquaculture industry. Like many of the smaller village schools in this coastal farming township, detailed enrollment and program records are limited, but the school continues to serve the families of Wenfeng Village as their local elementary school.

塭子國小位於屏東縣佳冬鄉塭豐村，村名中的「塭」字直接呼應當地悠久的魚塭養殖產業，也是佳冬鄉鹽田與養殖漁業傳統的一部分。與許多這座沿海農業鄉鎮的小型村落學校一樣，塭子國小目前公開可查的招生與課程資料有限，但學校仍持續作為塭豐村在地學童就讀的社區國小。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🐟 A Name Rooted in Fish Ponds

村名源於魚塭

The school takes its name from Wenfeng Village, where "塭" directly references the fish ponds that have long defined the local aquaculture economy.

🏘️ A Local Village Anchor

在地社區學校

As one of the smaller elementary schools in Jiadong Township, Wenzi Elementary continues to serve as the local school for Wenfeng Village families.

🌾 Part of Jiadong's Coastal Farming Belt

佳冬沿海農業帶

The school sits within Jiadong's coastal salt-pond and aquaculture zone, an area historically shaped by fish-farming rather than rice or fruit crops.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Jiadong's Hakka farming heritage.

🏡

Jiadong Township (佳冬鄉) is a historic Hakka farming community, home to the Xiao family mansion (built 1846) and a long tradition of salt-pond and fish-farming (虱目魚) aquaculture alongside dragon-fruit cultivation. Situated in Wenfeng Village, Wenzi Elementary sits at the heart of that aquaculture heritage — its very name reflecting the fish ponds that surround it.
