About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school reinvented as a Maker base.

Zhongxiao Elementary School in Yancheng District enrolls about 500 students, taught by 31 teachers, making it a mid-sized neighborhood school in downtown Kaohsiung. In recent years, the school has branded itself as a small-school "Maker learning base," running hands-on programs in fabric craft, 3D printing, drone operation, and coding — bringing contemporary STEM and design skills to a historic harbor-side community.

鹽埕區忠孝國小全校約500名學生、31位教師，是高雄市區一所中型的社區學校。近年來，學校以「小校自造基地」自我定位，推動布藝手作、3D列印、空拍機、程式設計等實作課程，將當代科技與設計技能帶入這個歷史悠久的港灣社區。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Serving Yancheng's historic harbor community.

🧂

Zhongxiao Elementary is one of several schools serving Yancheng's historic salt-flat-turned-harbor commercial district, a neighborhood that grew up around Kaohsiung's early 20th-century port development. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
