Guishan Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

The founding school of the entire district.

Guishan Elementary was founded on April 1, 1918, under Japanese colonial rule as 'Guilongkou Public School' (龜崙口公學校). It was renamed 'Guishan Public School' in 1929, converted to 'Guishan National School' in 1941, and took its present name, 'Guishan National Elementary School,' in 1968 with the extension of compulsory education. As the oldest public school in Guishan District, it is the historical parent school of at least four other elementary schools that split off as independent institutions over the 20th century: Dagang (1922), Longshou (1946), Fuyuan (1961), and Shoushan (1963).

龜山國小創立於日治時期1918年4月1日，初名「龜崙口公學校」；1929年更名「龜山公學校」，1941年改制為「龜山國民學校」，1968年隨九年國教實施更名為現名「龜山國民小學」。作為龜山區歷史最悠久的公立學校，本校是至少四所國小的發源母校——包括1922年獨立的大崗、1946年的龍壽、1961年的福源，以及1963年的壽山國小。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ The Oldest School in the District

龜山區歷史最悠久的學校

Founded in 1918 as Guilongkou Public School under Japanese colonial rule, Guishan Elementary is the oldest public school in the district by a wide margin.

🌳 Parent School to Four Others

四所學校的母校

Over the 20th century, Dagang (1922), Longshou (1946), Fuyuan (1961), and Shoushan (1963) elementary schools all split off from Guishan Elementary as independent institutions.

📛 Renamed Through Three Eras

歷經三次更名

The school's name changed with Taiwan's shifting eras — from Guilongkou Public School (1918) to Guishan Public School (1929), Guishan National School (1941), and finally Guishan National Elementary School (1968).

Local Culture · 在地文化

The root of Guishan's school network.

🏛️

As the origin institution for most of Guishan's older elementary schools, Guishan Elementary is effectively the founding node of the district's entire public education network — predating even the establishment of Guishan as a formally named administrative district.
