Chang Gung University

長庚大學

A private university founded by Formosa Plastics Group's Wang brothers, sitting alongside Chang Gung Memorial Hospital as the academic heart of Guishan's medical-education cluster.

由台塑集團王氏兄弟創辦的私立大學，緊鄰長庚紀念醫院，是龜山醫療教育重鎮的學術核心。

About · 學校簡介

Founded by the family behind Formosa Plastics.

Chang Gung University was founded in April 1987 as Chang Gung Medical College by brothers Wang Yung-ching and Wang Yung-tsai, founders of Formosa Plastics Group. They named the school after their father, Wang Chang-gung — the same namesake as the Chang Gung Memorial Hospital system the brothers had earlier established in his memory. In 1993 the school added Colleges of Engineering and Management alongside Medicine and was renamed Chang Gung College of Medicine and Technology. It was approved by Taiwan's Ministry of Education to become Chang Gung University in August 1997.

長庚大學於1987年4月由台塑集團創辦人王永慶、王永在昆仲創立，初名「長庚醫學院」，校名取自其父王長庚——與兄弟倆早年為紀念父親所創辦的長庚紀念醫院體系同名。1993年增設工學院與管理學院，與醫學院並列，更名為「長庚醫學暨工程學院」；1997年8月經教育部核准改制為長庚大學。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

⚕️ Three Colleges

三大學院

CGU is organized into three main colleges — Medicine (including Nursing, Traditional Chinese Medicine, Medical Biotechnology, Physical Therapy, and Radiological Sciences), Management, and Engineering.

🧬 Biosignatures Center

生物標誌中心

CGU runs an international Biosignatures Center jointly with Arizona State University, established in 2011 and focused on cancer prevention and early detection.

🏥 Tied to Chang Gung Memorial Hospital

長庚醫療體系

CGU's medical training is tightly integrated with the Chang Gung Memorial Hospital (CGMH) clinical system, one of Taiwan's largest hospital networks.

🏆 Research Reputation

研究表現

CGU sits in the 601–700 band of the ARWU 2023 global rankings and approximately #668 globally in the QS World Rankings 2026.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchor of Guishan's medical hub.

🏥

CGU sits at the heart of Guishan's "Chang Gung" campus cluster — anchored by Chang Gung Memorial Hospital, Taiwan's largest hospital system — a legacy of Formosa Plastics Group founder Wang Yung-ching's philanthropy that helped transform Guishan into one of Taiwan's major biomedical hubs.
