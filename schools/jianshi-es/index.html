Jianshi Elementary School

尖石國民小學

About · 學校簡介

A century-old school reclaims its original name.

Jianshi Elementary opened in 1918, during the Japanese colonial era, as the "Jiale Aboriginal Children's Education Institute," taking its present Chinese name in 1968. In 2019, as the school transitioned into an Atayal-culture-based experimental school, it restored the site's traditional Atayal place name, "Klapay," officially becoming Klapay National Experimental Elementary School. Although the school's own campus footprint is only 1.611 hectares, its district covers the entire township — all 528 square kilometers of it. Jianshi Township is home to roughly 9,500 residents, most of them Atayal, and this school — sharing the township's name — sits at the symbolic center of that community.

尖石國小創立於1918年（日治時期），最初名為「嘉樂蕃童教育所」，1968年更為現今中文校名。2019年，學校轉型為以泰雅族文化為本位的實驗學校，同時恢復當地傳統泰雅語地名「Klapay（卡拉排）」，正式定名為「klapay國立實驗國民小學」。學校本身校地僅1.611公頃，學區卻涵蓋全鄉528平方公里。尖石鄉居民約9,500人，多數為泰雅族，這所與鄉同名的學校，正座落於部落社群的象徵核心地帶。
