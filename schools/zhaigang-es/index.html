Zhaigang Elementary School

宅港國小

A century-old rural school whose new whale-shaped playground slide, unveiled for its centennial, replaced a beloved century-old elephant slide.

百年老校，建校百年之際以新的鯨魚溜滑梯取代陪伴幾代學童的大象溜滑梯。

About · 學校簡介

From elephant slide to whale slide, a century of memory.

Zhaigang Elementary opened in April 1922 as a satellite classroom of Xuejia Public School in Zhaizigang, formally becoming an independent school — Zhaizigang Public School — in 1939 under its first principal, Endo Fujio. The school reached its 100th anniversary in the early 2020s and marked its 104th anniversary on April 1, 2026.

宅港國小前身為學甲公學校於大正11年（1922年）4月在宅仔港設立的分教場，民國28年（1939年）獨立設校為宅子港公學校，首任校長為遠藤不二夫。學校於2020年代初歡慶建校百週年，並於2026年4月1日舉辦104週年校慶。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🐳 From Elephant Slide to Whale Slide

鯨魚溜滑梯取代大象溜滑梯

At its centennial in 2022–2023, the school retired its century-old elephant-shaped slide and unveiled a new whale-shaped slide, unveiled by then-Mayor Huang Wei-che — a playful nod to the district's coastal aquaculture life.

🎂 104 Years and Counting

2026年慶祝104週年校慶

Zhaigang celebrated its 104th anniversary on April 1, 2026, tracing its roots back to a 1922 satellite classroom of Xuejia Public School.

🏫 From Branch to Independent School

1939年獨立設校

What began as a satellite classroom of Xuejia Public School became an independent school in 1939 under its first principal, Endo Fujio.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A whale slide for a milkfish-farming coast.

🐋

Zhaigang's playground centerpiece — a whale-shaped slide that replaced its beloved century-old elephant slide — visually nods to the coastal, aquaculture-based life of the milkfish-farming villages the school serves. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
