Xiaoyang Elementary School

曉陽國民小學

A public elementary school in coastal Xianxi Township, named after the courtesy name of a former Changhua County magistrate and rooted in local civic history.

座落於線西鄉海線的公立小學，校名取自前彰化縣長呂世明先生的字號「曉陽」，承載地方公共歷史記憶。

About · 學校簡介

Named for a county magistrate, rooted in Xianxi's coast.

Xiaoyang Elementary began as an application submitted in September 1961 and approved that October, opening as a branch of Xianxi Elementary School in Dingli with 6 classes and 3 classrooms. It became an independent school on February 1, 1964, taking the name "Xiaoyang" after the courtesy name of former Changhua County Magistrate Lu Shiming. At independence, the school already had 12 classes and more than 600 students on a U-shaped campus of about 1.2 hectares, and its first graduating class was photographed in July 1965.

曉陽國小的設校申請提出於民國50年（1961年）9月，同年10月獲准，初期為線西國小頂犁分校，設6班3間教室。民國53年（1964年）2月1日獨立設校，校名「曉陽」取自前彰化縣長呂世明先生的字號。獨立設校時已有12班、學生600餘人，校園呈U字型，佔地約1.2公頃，首屆畢業生於民國54年（1965年）7月留下畢業照。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏷️ A Name With History

校名典故

The school's name, Xiaoyang, comes directly from the courtesy name of Lu Shiming, a former Changhua County Magistrate — a rare direct link between a local school and regional civic history.

📈 A Fast Start

快速成長

What began as a small branch school with 6 classes in 1961 had grown to 12 classes and over 600 students by the time it became independent just three years later, in 1964.

🏫 A U-Shaped Campus

U字型校園

Xiaoyang's original campus, spanning about 1.2 hectares, was laid out in a distinctive U-shape — a design that shaped the school's earliest years and first graduating class in 1965.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xianxi, a coastal farming-and-fishing township.

🎣

Xiaoyang sits in Dingli Village, part of Xianxi Township — a coastal farming-and-fishing community on Changhua's coastline. The school's name ties its identity directly to regional civic history, anchoring it in the everyday life of this small maritime township. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
