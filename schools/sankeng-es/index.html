About · 學校簡介

Named for three creeks, beside a century-old bridge.

Sankeng Elementary became independent in 2000, when its former branch campus status ended and it was established as a standalone school. The village name "三坑" ("three creeks") comes from the three streams — 鴨母溪, 蔗部溪, and 火劫尾溪 — that once flowed through the area. In January 2002 the school took over a second campus site, on reserved land formerly designated for Shimen Elementary. Its campus sits near Shimen Mountain and Shimen Reservoir, in a district known for tung-blossom (油桐花) season, live fish (活魚) restaurants, and the historic Sankeng Old Street with its century-old "red bridge" (紅橋).

三坑國小於民國89年（2000年）由分校獨立設校。「三坑」地名源自昔日流經當地的三條溪流：鴨母溪、蔗部溪與火劫尾溪。民國91年（2002年）1月，學校並接收原保留予石門國小的「文小三」用地作為第二校區。校園鄰近石門山與石門水庫，所在地區以油桐花季、活魚餐廳與擁有百年紅橋的三坑老街聞名。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💧 Named for Three Creeks

三坑得名由來

"三坑" means "three creeks," after the three streams that once flowed through the village: 鴨母溪, 蔗部溪, and 火劫尾溪.

🌉 Beside the Historic Red Bridge

百年紅橋

The school sits near Sankeng Old Street's century-old "red bridge" (紅橋), one of Longtan's best-known Hakka heritage tourist spots.

🌸 In Tung-Blossom Country

油桐花季

The surrounding area is known for its tung-blossom season, live-fish restaurants, and old-street tourism.

🏫 A Growing Second Campus

擴建第二校區

The school took over a second campus site, on former Shimen Elementary reserved land, in January 2002.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Beside Sankeng's old street and tung blossoms.

🌸

Sankeng Elementary sits directly adjacent to the Sankeng Old Street and bike-trail area, one of Longtan's best-known Hakka heritage tourist spots, tying the school to the district's tung-blossom and old-street cultural tourism identity. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
