Qingshui Senior High School

新北市立清水高級中學

A complete junior-and-senior high school named for Tucheng's “Clear Water” area, known for its dance-talent program and evening division.

因土城「清水」地區得名的完全中學，以舞蹈才能班與進修部聞名。

About · 學校簡介

From junior high to a complete secondary school.

Qingshui Senior High opened in 1989 as Taipei County Qingshui Junior High. It expanded into a complete junior-and-senior high school in 1996, and gained full senior-high status in 2000. Today it operates an evening division and participates in the “New Taipei Bilingual Strategic Alliance,” and it is especially noted for a dance-talent recruitment program that draws students with performing-arts ambitions.

清水高中創立於民國78年（1989年），初名「台北縣清水國中」。民國85年（1996年）擴增為完全中學，89年（2000年）正式升格為高級中學。學校目前設有進修部（夜間部），並參與「新北市雙語教育策略聯盟」，尤以舞蹈才能班的招生特色聞名，吸引許多有志於表演藝術的學生就讀。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for Tucheng's clear mountain stream.

💧

Named for and serving the Qingshui (“Clear Water”) area of Tucheng, the school's name references the clear mountain stream that historically defined this once-agricultural corner of the district. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
