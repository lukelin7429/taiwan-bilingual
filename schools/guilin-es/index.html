About · 學校簡介

A rural school that teaches its own hometown.

Guilin Elementary School lives by its motto, "打造一所充滿活力朝氣、人文薈萃的田園小學" (Building a vibrant rural elementary school rich in humanities). Its history includes a branch school, Xiazhuang Branch, established in September 1968 and dissolved in 2004, and an attached kindergarten founded in 1979 — evidence that the main school itself predates 1968 by some margin, though no confirmed founding date has been found. The school runs English-language programs including Cool English competitions and Wenguang International English Village activities, an active school chorus and musical performance program, transparent school-lunch nutrition tracking, and a distinctive social-studies unit titled "布袋鎮家鄉100問" (100 Questions About Our Hometown, Budai Township).

貴林國小校訓為「打造一所充滿活力朝氣、人文薈萃的田園小學」。校史可考的紀錄包括民國57年（1968年）9月設立、民國93年（2004年）裁撤的下庄分校，以及民國68年（1979年）成立的附設幼兒園，顯示本校創校時間應早於1968年，惟確切創校年份尚待考證。學校推動包括Cool English競賽及文光國際英語村等英語課程，並設有活躍的合唱及音樂表演活動、透明化的午餐營養公告，以及自編的「布袋鎮家鄉100問」社會科單元，帶領學生深入認識家鄉。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗺️ 100 Questions About Our Hometown

布袋鎮家鄉100問

A dedicated social-studies unit exploring local heritage, "100 Questions About Our Hometown, Budai Township," embeds the town's salt-and-fishing history directly into daily schoolwork.

🗣️ Cool English & International Village

Cool English與國際英語村

English-language programs including Cool English online competitions and Wenguang International English Village activities give students regular exposure to English beyond the textbook.

🎵 An Active School Chorus

合唱與音樂表演

An active school chorus and musical performance program brings the arts to life at this small rural school.

🍽️ Transparent Lunch Nutrition

午餐營養公開透明

Transparent school-lunch nutrition tracking gives parents a clear, ongoing view into what students eat each day.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Teaching the hometown, 100 questions at a time.

🧂

The "100 Questions About Our Hometown" unit is an explicit, dedicated local-culture curriculum teaching students about Budai's salt-and-fishing heritage, directly embedding the town's geography and history into schoolwork — a rare example of a school building its own hometown textbook from scratch.
