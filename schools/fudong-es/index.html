About · 學校簡介

A younger school for a growing neighborhood.

Fudong Elementary was founded on February 1, 1975, making it one of the younger schools in this part of Lingya District. Its name is part of a cluster of "福"-prefixed place and school names in eastern Lingya, reflecting the wave of residential development that reshaped the area through the 1970s.

福東國小創立於民國64年（1975年）2月1日，是苓雅區這一帶較年輕的學校之一。校名屬於苓雅區東側一系列以「福」字為首的地名與校名群組之一，反映1970年代該地區住宅開發浪潮下的地方發展脈絡。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏗️ Born From a 1970s Building Boom

誕生於1970年代開發浪潮

Founded in 1975, Fudong is one of the younger schools in eastern Lingya, established as the area's residential development accelerated through the decade.

🍀 Part of a "Fu"-Named Cluster

福字地名群組

The school's name joins a cluster of "福"-prefixed place and school names across eastern Lingya, a naming pattern tied to the district's mid-century growth.

🏫 A Relatively Young Neighborhood School

相對年輕的社區學校

Compared to many of its neighbors with roots in the Japanese colonial era, Fudong's 1975 founding makes it a distinctly postwar addition to the district.

Fudong belongs to a cluster of "福"-prefixed place and school names in eastern Lingya, a naming pattern tied to the wave of residential development that reshaped this part of the district through the 1970s. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
