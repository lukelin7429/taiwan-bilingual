About · 學校簡介

A Dazhuang-area school with a brand-new campus.

Nangang Junior High School was founded in 1968 (ROC 57) and serves the Dazhuang area of Nantou City, part of the county seat's residential expansion along the Bagua Mountain foothills. The school offers gifted, sports, and special-education classes, and around 2019-2020 completed a new NT$190 million campus building — a substantial investment reflecting the district's continued growth. Students at Nangang regularly place in county-level math, writing, and calligraphy competitions.

南崗國中創立於民國57年（1968年），服務南投市大庄地區，屬於縣治沿八卦山麓擴展的住宅發展區域之一。學校設有資優班、體育班與特教班，並於民國108-109年（2019-2020年）左右完成耗資1.9億元的新校舍建設，反映出該地區持續發展的規模。南崗國中學生經常在縣內數學、寫作、書法比賽中獲得佳績。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏗️ A NT$190 Million New Campus

1.9億元新校舍

The school completed a substantial new campus building around 2019-2020, funded with an investment of NT$190 million — one of the more significant recent facility upgrades in the county seat.

🖌️ Strength in Calligraphy and Writing

書法與寫作競賽表現優異

Students regularly place in county-level math, writing, and calligraphy competitions, reflecting the school's balanced focus on academic and artistic skills.

🌟 Gifted, Sports, and Special Education

資優、體育與特教並重

The school offers gifted, sports, and special-education classes, giving students in the Dazhuang area a range of tailored educational pathways.

🏘️ Serving a Growing District

服務成長中的社區

The school anchors the Dazhuang area of Nantou City, a residential district that continues to expand along the Bagua Mountain foothills.
