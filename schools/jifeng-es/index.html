Jifeng Elementary School

吉峰國民小學

About · 學校簡介

A school born from growth.

Jifeng Elementary was approved for establishment in 1996 and opened its doors in 1998, created specifically to relieve overcrowding at neighboring Qiaorong Elementary School as Wufeng's population grew. Despite being one of the district's youngest schools, its campus has become widely cited as one of Taichung's most scenic, a point of pride for a school still writing its own history.

吉峰國小於民國85年（1996年）獲准設校，民國87年（1998年）正式開學，設立目的是為紓解鄰近僑榮國小因霧峰人口成長而產生的過度擁擠問題。儘管是霧峰區歷史最年輕的學校之一，其校園卻被廣泛譽為台中市最具風景魅力的校園之一，也是這所仍在書寫自身歷史的學校的一項驕傲。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏞️ One of Taichung's Most Scenic Campuses

台中最美校園之一

Jifeng's campus is frequently cited as among the most scenic in Taichung, a distinction unusual for a school still in its first decades.

🌱 Founded to Ease Overcrowding

紓解鄰校擁擠而生

The school was created when neighboring Qiaorong Elementary became overcrowded, a direct response to Wufeng's growing school-age population in the 1990s.

🏫 One of Wufeng's Youngest Schools

霧峰最年輕的學校之一

Opening in 1998, Jifeng is among the newest of Wufeng's public elementary schools, reflecting the district's more recent suburban growth.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Wufeng's growth toward Dali.

🌱

Jifeng's creation to relieve overcrowding at older Wufeng schools reflects the district's rapid suburban growth toward neighboring Dali, part of Taichung's broader expansion in the late twentieth century. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
