About · 學校簡介

From junior high to complete senior high.

Sanyi Senior High School was founded in 1962 as a junior high school and transformed into a complete, six-year senior high school in 2013. The school is located in Sanyi Township, internationally famous for its woodcarving (木雕) craftsmanship — the township is home to the Sanyi Wood Sculpture Museum and a woodcarving tradition stretching back over a century.

三義高中創立於民國51年（1962年），原為國中，2013年升格為完全中學（含高中部）。學校座落於以木雕工藝聞名國際的三義鄉——鄉內設有三義木雕博物館，木雕傳統已延續超過一世紀。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 A Complete Six-Year High School

完全中學(國中+高中)

Upgraded from a junior high in 2013, Sanyi now offers a full six-year secondary education pathway from grades 7 through 12 under one roof.

🪵 In the "Wood Carving Art City"

木雕藝術之城

Sanyi Township is internationally renowned as Taiwan's premier woodcarving center, home to the Sanyi Wood Sculpture Museum and generations of master carvers.

🏛️ Over Six Decades of History

逾六十年校史

Founded in 1962, the school has served generations of Sanyi students through more than sixty years of growth and institutional change.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A century of woodcarving artistry.

🪵

Located in Sanyi Township, internationally renowned as Taiwan's "Wood Carving Art City," the school sits amid a century-old woodcarving craft tradition that began in 1918 with local artisan Wu Jin-bao. See the Miaoli county page for more on the region.
