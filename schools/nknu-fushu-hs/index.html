About · 學校簡介

From an experimental school to a full K–12 institution.

NKNU Affiliated Senior High School began in July 1978 as an experimental school affiliated with National Kaohsiung Normal University, initially covering elementary and junior-high grades only. It became a full "附屬高級中學" (affiliated senior high) in 1980, and took its current name in August 1989 when NKNU itself became a full university. The school today enrolls around 866 students across 24 classes, offers a dual-diploma partnership with Ontario's TVO Independent Learning Centre in Canada, and has maintained an active alumni association since 1998. Its campus sits on the former site of Kaihsiung Elementary School in Lingya District, an area that grew rapidly after World War II.

國立高雄師範大學附屬高級中學創立於1978年7月，最初為附屬於國立高雄師範大學的實驗學校，僅設國小、國中部。1980年升格為附屬高級中學，1989年8月隨高雄師範學院改制為大學而定名至今。現有約866名學生、24班，並與加拿大安大略省TVO獨立學習中心合作雙聯學制，自1998年起設有活躍的校友會。校址位於苓雅區，前身為凱旋國小舊址，該地區於二戰後迅速發展。
