Chongwen Elementary School

Chiayi City's oldest existing elementary school — founded in 1896, predating the Republic of China itself — with preserved Japanese-era heritage architecture and alumni who shaped Taiwan's art and dance.

About · 學校簡介

Chiayi's oldest school, 128 years and counting.

Chongwen Elementary traces its roots to 1896, when it began as the "Chiayi Japanese Language Training Institute" under Japanese colonial rule, becoming a formal public school in 1898 — years before the Republic of China was even founded in 1912. After Taiwan's 1945 retrocession to the ROC, it was renamed Chongwen National Elementary School. Today, at 128-plus years old, it stands as Chiayi City's longest-running elementary school, its campus still holding preserved heritage structures from its Japanese-era past, including a 1928 memorial clock tower and Taiwan's only remaining school broadcast tower.

崇文國小創校歷史可追溯至1896年，前身為日治時期的「嘉義國語傳習所」，1898年正式改制為公學校——早於1912年中華民國成立之前。1945年臺灣光復後，更名為崇文國民學校。如今建校已逾128年，是嘉義市歷史最悠久的國小，校園至今仍保存日治時期的古蹟建築，包括1928年落成的紀念鐘樓，以及全臺僅存的學校放送台（廣播台）。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🕰️ A 1928 Memorial Clock Tower

1928年紀念鐘樓

The campus preserves a memorial clock tower built in 1928, one of the most visible reminders of the school's century-long Japanese-era heritage.

📻 Taiwan's Only Surviving School Broadcast Tower

全臺僅存學校放送台

Chongwen's campus holds Taiwan's only remaining school broadcast tower (放送台), a rare surviving piece of school infrastructure from the Japanese colonial era.

🎨 Alma Mater of Painter Chen Cheng-po

畫家陳澄波校友

Renowned Taiwanese painter Chen Cheng-po graduated from the school in 1913 — one of several notable alumni who trace their early education back to this campus.

💃 Alma Mater of Choreographer Lin Hwai-min

雲門舞集創辦人林懷民校友

Cloud Gate Dance Theatre founder Lin Hwai-min, one of Taiwan's most celebrated choreographers, graduated from Chongwen in 1960, alongside 1935 graduate and industrialist Wang Yong-zai.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A living thread through Chiayi's cultural history.

🏛️

As Chiayi City's longest-running elementary school at over 128 years old, Chongwen is deeply woven into local identity, having educated generations of the city's most notable cultural and business figures — from painter Chen Cheng-po to Cloud Gate Dance Theatre's Lin Hwai-min. Its preserved Japanese-era clock tower and broadcast tower make the campus itself a small piece of living heritage. See the Chiayi City page for more on the region.
