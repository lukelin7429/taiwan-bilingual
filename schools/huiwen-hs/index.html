About · 學校簡介

A complete school built for a brand-new district.

Huiwen Senior High School opened in 2000 as a complete junior-and-senior high school serving students from grade 7 through grade 12 — purpose-built for Taichung's 7th Redevelopment Zone (七期), a newly planned district in Nantun. It was raised alongside its twin campus, Huiwen Elementary School, forming a deliberate “core cultural-education block” at the center of the new zone. Where much of Nantun's older identity centers on the historic Litoudian (犁頭店) old street a few kilometers away, Huiwen Senior High represents the district's thoroughly modern, master-planned face.

惠文高中創立於民國89年（2000年），是為台中市南屯區七期重劃區規劃興建的完全中學，招收國中部至高中部（七至十二年級）學生。學校與姊妹校惠文國小同時興建，共同構成七期重劃區核心的「文教專用區」。相對於南屯歷史悠久、以犁頭店老街為代表的舊聚落，惠文高中代表著這片新興重劃區的現代規劃樣貌。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 A Complete Six-Year Campus

國高中一貫的完全中學

Huiwen serves students continuously from junior through senior high on one campus, a structure less common than Taiwan's typical split junior-high/senior-high system.

🏙️ Twin School of a New District

與惠文國小同步興建

Built together with Huiwen Elementary School, the two campuses were placed at the center of the newly planned 七期 zone as its designated "cultural-education block."

🏮 A Modern Face for an Old District

新校園承接南屯舊底蘊

While Huiwen's own campus is entirely modern, it sits only a few kilometers from Nantun's centuries-old Litoudian (犁頭店) trading-post core, the district's original historic heart.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Nantun's old town and new zone, side by side.

🏮

Huiwen Senior High's birth alongside Huiwen Elementary anchored the newly planned 七期 zone as Nantun's modern educational and civic heart — a striking contrast with the district's much older Litoudian (犁頭店) old-street core, the Qing-era trading settlement that gave Nantun its original identity.
