Zhongli Commercial Senior High School

桃園市立中壢商業高級中等學校

One of Zhongli's oldest vocational high schools, training generations of workers for the district's role as Taoyuan's commercial and retail hub since 1954.

中壢區歷史最悠久的職業學校之一，自1954年創校以來，為桃園商業與零售重鎮培育了一代又一代的人才。

About · 學校簡介

A commercial-education anchor since 1954.

Founded in May 1954 as the Taoyuan County Zhongli Commercial Vocational School, on the former site of Yimin Middle School, this school became a provincial commercial high school in 1968 after its junior-high division ended, and turned municipal on January 1, 2018, following Taoyuan's upgrade to a special municipality. It now offers Data Processing, International Trade, and Business Management departments alongside a comprehensive high-school track, serving roughly 2,000 students across about 60 classes under the motto "公、誠、勤、毅" (fairness, sincerity, diligence, and perseverance).

學校創立於民國43年（1954年）5月，前身為桃園縣立中壢商業職業學校，校地原為義民中學所在地。1968年廢除初中部後改制為臺灣省立中壢高級商業職業學校，並於2018年1月1日隨桃園市升格為直轄市而改制為市立學校。現設有資料處理科、國際貿易科、商業經營科及綜合高中部，全校約2,000餘名學生、近60班，校訓為「公、誠、勤、毅」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💼 Business-Focused Departments

商科系所齊全

The school offers Data Processing, International Trade, and Business Management departments plus a comprehensive high-school track, serving roughly 2,000 students across about 60 classes.

🏫 Seven Decades of History

逾七十年校史

Founded in 1954 on the former site of Yimin Middle School, the school has operated continuously through provincial and now municipal governance since Taoyuan's 2018 upgrade.

🎓 A National College Partnership

曾設國立台北商專分班

From 1977, the school hosted a Zhongli branch of National Taipei College of Business's "Open College," extending post-secondary business education access locally.

🎭 Notable Alumni

傑出校友

Alumni include actor-singer Yen Ming-hao (言明澔), reflecting the school's broad reach beyond its core commercial-education mission.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Training talent for Taoyuan's commercial heart.

🛍️

As one of Zhongli's oldest vocational and commercial high schools, this school has trained generations of workers for the district's role as Taoyuan's commercial and retail hub, centered around the Zhongli/SOGO shopping district. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
