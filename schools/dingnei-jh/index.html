Dingnei Junior High School

碇內國民中學

A public junior high school first planned in 1990 and opened in 1997, now home to a career-exploration and experience demonstration center.

About · 學校簡介

Seven years in the planning, before the first bell rang.

Dingnei Junior High's story began well before it opened — land acquisition for the campus started as early as November 1990. A preparatory office was established on April 8, 1996, and founding principal Guo Qingshun was appointed in January 1997. The school enrolled its first cohort of five classes in August 1997, growing to nine classes in 1998 and ten in 1999. Today it hosts a career-exploration and experience demonstration center, giving students hands-on exposure to future career paths.

碇內國中的故事早在開學前多年便已展開——校地徵收工作最早可追溯至民國79年（1990年）11月。籌備處於民國85年（1996年）4月8日成立，首任校長郭清順於1997年1月獲聘。學校於1997年8月招收首屆5個班級新生，1998年增至9班，1999年增至10班。如今學校設有生涯試探暨體驗示範中心，讓學生能實際體驗未來各種職涯發展。

🧭 A Career-Exploration Center

生涯試探暨體驗示範中心

Dingnei hosts a dedicated career-exploration and experience demonstration center, giving students hands-on exposure to different career paths before graduation.

🏗️ Land Secured Seven Years Early

1990年即展開徵地

Campus land acquisition began as early as 1990, seven years before the school actually opened — an unusually long runway of planning behind its founding.

📈 Fast Early Growth

創校初期迅速擴班

Starting with five classes in 1997, the school grew to nine classes by 1998 and ten by 1999, reflecting strong early demand from the surrounding community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Nuannuan's historic river district.

🏞️

Dingnei Junior High sits in the Nuannuan District, a neighborhood historically tied to river trade along the Keelung River — a heritage shared with its elementary-school neighbor, Dingnei Elementary. See the Keelung county page for more on the region.
