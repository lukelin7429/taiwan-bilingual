About · 學校簡介

North Ruisui's own school.

Ruibei Elementary began in 1960 as a branch class of Ruisui Elementary School, was elevated to a branch school in 1962, and became fully independent in 1965. Its very name, "Ruibei" — meaning "north Ruisui" — reflects its origins as a satellite campus serving families in the northern part of Ruisui Township who lived some distance from the main Ruisui Elementary campus.

瑞北國小的前身是民國49年（1960年）設立的瑞穗國小分班，民國51年（1962年）升格為分校，民國54年（1965年）正式獨立設校。校名「瑞北」——意即「瑞穗之北」——正反映出其最初作為衛星校區的定位，服務居住於瑞穗國小主校區以北、距離較遠的家庭。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 From Branch Class to Independent School

分班到獨立設校

Ruibei Elementary's steady progression — branch class in 1960, branch school in 1962, full independence in 1965 — traces a typical but meaningful path for rural Taiwanese schools of the era.

🧭 A Name That Tells Its Story

「瑞北」地名由來

The name "Ruibei" (north Ruisui) directly reflects the school's founding purpose: serving families in the northern reaches of Ruisui Township closer to home.

🏫 A Sister to Ruisui Elementary

與瑞穗國小血緣相連

Ruibei's origins as an offshoot of Ruisui Elementary tie it closely to the township's central school, even as it has grown into its own independent institution.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Ruisui's hot spring and river country.

♨️

Ruibei Elementary sits within Ruisui Township, celebrated across Taiwan for its hot springs, papaya milk, and Xiuguluan River rafting — one of several small schools that together serve this scenic east-Hualien community's families.
