About · 學校簡介

A young school born from a growing neighborhood.

Pantao Elementary School opened its doors on August 1, 1999, after a preparatory office was formed that February. It began with 12 classes and 434 students, having split off from neighboring Jianguo Elementary School's catchment as local residential growth pushed enrollment beyond what the existing school could handle. Pantao is one of several Toufen City schools founded in the 1990s to keep pace with the city's rapid expansion.

蟠桃國小於1999年（民國88年）2月成立籌備處，同年8月1日正式開學，初期設有12班、學生434人。學校自鄰近的建國國小學區劃分獨立設校，因應當地人口成長帶來的入學壓力。蟠桃國小是頭份市在1990年代因城市快速擴張而陸續設立的學校之一。

🎒 A Fresh Start in 1999

1999年正式開學

Pantao opened in August 1999 with 12 classes and 434 students, a newly built school responding directly to Toufen's fast-growing population.

✂️ Split From Jianguo Elementary

自建國國小學區劃分

The school was carved out of Jianguo Elementary School's catchment area, part of a pattern of school-splitting that accompanied Toufen's residential boom.

🏗️ Part of a Wave of New Schools

1990年代設校潮的一員

Pantao's founding reflects the broader wave of new elementary schools established in Toufen during the 1990s to keep up with the city's growth.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A city drawing new families.

🌱

Like Jianguo, Pantao's founding reflects the wave of new residential development that has drawn Hakka and other families from across Taiwan into greater Toufen since the 1990s. See the Miaoli county page for more on the region.
