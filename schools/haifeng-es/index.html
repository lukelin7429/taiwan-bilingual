Haifeng Elementary School

海豐國民小學

A Pingtung City public elementary school rooted in the Haifeng community, one of the city's traditional agricultural-origin neighborhoods.

扎根於屏東市海豐社區的公立國小，海豐是市內傳統農業起源社區之一。

About · 學校簡介

Rooted in the Haifeng community.

Haifeng Elementary is located on Haifeng Street, taking its name from the Haifeng community it serves on the outskirts of Pingtung City. The neighborhood has agricultural roots typical of the city's traditional outlying districts, and the school itself has been noted on Taiwan tourism guides as a local point of interest, suggesting a notable campus or community role beyond its academic function.

海豐國小位於屏東市海豐街，校名取自其所服務的海豐社區，該地位於屏東市郊，具有典型的傳統農業起源背景。這所學校也曾被台灣旅遊指南列為在地景點之一，顯示其校園或社區角色具有一定的知名度，不僅止於教學功能。
