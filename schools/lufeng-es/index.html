About · 學校簡介

From one classroom to a hillside campus.

Lufeng Elementary began in 1952 as a single-class branch of Shalu Elementary, holding lessons in a village community hall. The school grew over the following decades and moved into a purpose-built campus on the Lufeng hillside in 1997. Its name places it within a cluster of Shalu place-names — Lufeng, Luliao, and Luyang — that share the upland "Lu-" prefix across the district's hilly terrain.

鹿峰國小創立於民國41年（1952年），最初以沙鹿國小單班分校的形式，借用村里活動中心上課。學校歷經數十年發展，於1997年遷入鹿峰山丘上新建的校地。校名與沙鹿地區「鹿寮」、「鹿陽」等地名同屬「鹿」字聚落群，共同見證此地丘陵地形的特色。

Lufeng Elementary's hillside setting places it within a distinctive cluster of Shalu place-names — Lufeng, Luliao, Luyang — all sharing the "Lu-" prefix that marks the district's upland terrain rising from the coastal plain. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
