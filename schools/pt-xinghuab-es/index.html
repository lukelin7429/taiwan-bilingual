About · 學校簡介

A distinct school with a near-homophonous name.

Xinghua Elementary School (興華國小) serves the children of Wandan Township, Pingtung County. It is worth noting that this school is a genuinely separate institution from the similarly named 興化國小 (also romanized “Xinghua”) elsewhere in Wandan Township — the two schools' Chinese names are near-homophones, but they have separate campuses, separate phone numbers, and separate school communities. Some online search results have mistakenly conflated the two schools' addresses, so visitors should treat address details with care and consult the school's official website directly.

興華國小服務屏東縣萬丹鄉的學童。值得注意的是，本校與萬丹鄉境內另一所校名近似的「興化國小」是兩所完全不同的學校——兩校中文校名讀音相近，但擁有各自獨立的校區、電話與社區。部分網路搜尋結果曾誤將兩校地址混淆，建議造訪者以學校官網資訊為準。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Wandan, land of lotus and red beans.

🪷

Xinghua Elementary sits within Wandan Township, Pingtung's most populous rural township, known for its lotus-root and lotus-seed cultivation on a flat, well-irrigated agricultural plain. Like its neighboring schools, it serves a community whose rhythms are shaped by this rich farmland.
