Fudan Elementary School

復旦國民小學

Founded in 1984 with its first classes held at a Hakka temple, and celebrated its 40th anniversary in 2024.

About · 學校簡介

Founded at a temple, before its own campus existed.

Fudan Elementary was officially founded on August 30, 1984, though classes actually began on August 1 at a borrowed campus — before its own buildings were ready, the school held its first classes at Xinming Elementary School inside the grounds of the Yimin Temple (義民廟), the region's most important Hakka "righteous martyrs" temple. Its first principal was Song Cheng-yuan. The school celebrated its 40th anniversary in 2024, with the Taoyuan mayor praising its diverse, student-centered programs.

復旦國小於1984年8月30日正式創校，實際上早在同年8月1日即已借用校地開學——在自有校舍完工前，首批班級借用義民廟境內的新明國小上課。義民廟是本地區最重要的客家「義民」信仰廟宇之一。首任校長為宋澄源先生。2024年，本校歡慶建校四十週年，桃園市長親臨致詞，讚揚學校多元、以學生為中心的辦學特色。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Born inside a Hakka temple's grounds.

🏮

Fudan Elementary's founding at the Yimin Temple — the region's most important Hakka "righteous martyrs" shrine — directly ties the school to the district's core Hakka religious and cultural heritage, a connection that persists even though the school now has its own dedicated campus on Guangping Street.
