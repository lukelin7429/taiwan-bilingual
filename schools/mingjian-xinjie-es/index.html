Xinjie Elementary School

新街國民小學

A century-old school with nearly 7,700 alumni, home to a mobile learning classroom and a national little-league baseball championship team.

About · 學校簡介

A century of alumni and a championship team.

Xinjie Elementary School was founded around 1922 and celebrated its 100th anniversary in March 2022, having educated roughly 7,699 alumni over the century. At the centennial celebration, the school unveiled a mobile-learning classroom, continuing its tradition of embracing new teaching tools. Shortly before the centennial, the school's baseball team won the national Chinatrust Cup little-league championship, a standout achievement for a century-old village school.

新街國小創校於大正11年（約1922年），並於2022年3月舉辦建校百年校慶，百年來培育近7,699位校友。校慶當日學校啟用行動學習教室，延續擁抱新教學工具的傳統。就在百年校慶前不久，該校棒球隊榮獲全國中信盃少棒錦標賽冠軍，對一所百年村校而言是相當亮眼的成就。
