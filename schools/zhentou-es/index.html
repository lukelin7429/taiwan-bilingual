Zhentou Elementary School

圳頭國小

A small hillside school on a scenic 9,075-square-meter campus, home to a Little Blue Whale puppet troupe and a close-knit community of about 35 students.

座落於9,075平方公尺風景優美校園的小型山區學校，擁有「小藍鯨」布偶劇團，全校約35位學生。

About · 學校簡介

A small, scenic school in Tongxiao's rural hills.

Zhentou Elementary began in 1958 as a branch class, becoming independent in 1962. Today it is a small rural school with around 35 students and 12 teachers, set on a 9,075-square-meter scenic campus. The school offers a Little Blue Whale puppet troupe, a dance troupe, and multi-domain enrichment activities including art, reading, diabolo, board games, and robotics.

圳頭國小於1958年（民國47年）以分班形式設立，1962年（民國51年）獨立設校。目前是一所小型鄉村學校，約有35位學生與12位教師，校園占地9,075平方公尺，風景優美。學校設有「小藍鯨」布偶劇團與舞蹈隊，並提供美術、閱讀、扯鈴、桌遊、機器人等多元領域的豐富學習活動。
