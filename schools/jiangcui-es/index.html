Jiangcui Elementary School

新北市板橋區江翠國民小學

Banqiao's second-oldest school, rebuilt over seven years and NT$750 million, with national-medal wrestling and judo teams.

板橋第二所學校，歷經七年、投入七億五千萬元重建，柔道與角力隊屢獲全國獎牌。

About · 學校簡介

A century old, rebuilt for a new one.

Jiangcui Elementary opened in 1919 as a branch campus of Banqiao Public School, becoming independent in 1921 as 江子翠公學校 — Banqiao's second school. Named for 江子翠, the river confluence where the Xindian and Dahan rivers meet the Tamsui River, the school underwent a seven-year, NT$750 million campus rebuild between 2009 and 2016. It now runs a bilingual/dual-language program and gifted programs in art and IT, alongside wrestling and judo teams that have won recent national medals.

江翠國小於1919年（日治時期）以板橋公學校江子翠分校身分成立，1921年獨立設校為「江子翠公學校」，是板橋第二所學校。校名源自江子翠——新店溪、大漢溪匯流入淡水河的三河交會之地。學校於2009年至2016年間歷經七年、耗資新台幣七億五千萬元的校舍重建工程，現設有雙語/雙軌教學班及美術、資訊資優班，柔道與角力隊近年屢獲全國獎牌。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥋 National-Medal Wrestling and Judo

柔道角力全國獎牌

Jiangcui's wrestling and judo teams have brought home recent national medals, a standout athletic tradition for an elementary school.

🏗️ A Seven-Year, NT$750 Million Rebuild

七年重建工程

Between 2009 and 2016, the school underwent a complete campus rebuild costing roughly NT$750 million — one of the larger elementary-school reconstructions in the district.

🌐 Bilingual and Gifted-Track Learning

雙語與資優教育

A bilingual/dual-language program runs alongside gifted programs in art and IT, giving students multiple specialized academic tracks.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for where three rivers meet.

🌊

Named for 江子翠, the confluence where the Xindian and Dahan Rivers meet the Tamsui River, the school's site was historically an important crossing point just across the water from Taipei's Wanhua District. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
