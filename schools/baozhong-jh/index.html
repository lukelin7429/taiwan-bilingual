About · 學校簡介

The only junior high for a nine-village township.

Baozhong Junior High is the sole junior high school serving Baozhong Township, drawing students from all nine of its villages. Sources vary on the exact founding date, placing it sometime between 1965 and 1972. Serving a largely agricultural community, the school has also taken on a supportive social role: in 2017, about 52% of its students came from disadvantaged families, reflecting both the challenges and the resilience of this rural farming township.

褒忠國中是褒忠鄉唯一的國民中學，招收全鄉九個村里的學生。關於創校年份，各方說法略有出入，約落在民國54年至61年（1965至1972年）之間。學校服務以農業為主的社區，也肩負重要的社會支持角色：2017年，全校約有52%的學生來自弱勢家庭，反映出這個農村鄉鎮所面對的挑戰與韌性。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Baozhong, home of Yunlin's pickled mustard greens.

🥬

As the sole junior high for Baozhong's nine farming villages, the school anchors a rural township whose economy runs on rice, peanuts, sweet potatoes, and garlic cultivation. Baozhong Township is also famous across Taiwan for suan cai (pickled mustard greens), a signature local specialty. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
