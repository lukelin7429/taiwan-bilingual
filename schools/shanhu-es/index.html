About · 學校簡介

A branch campus beside the reservoir.

A note on this listing: Shanhu is officially "Baoshan Elementary School's Shanhu Branch" (縣立寶山國小山湖分校) — a branch campus of Baoshan Elementary School, not a fully independent institution. Hsinchu County's school directory lists it as its own row, so we've built a page for it here, but in the interest of accuracy we want readers to know it operates under Baoshan Elementary's administration rather than as a stand-alone school.

The Shanhu Branch sits right beside the scenic Baoshan Reservoir and is classified as a remote/disadvantaged-area school. Despite its small size and branch status, it offers a distinctive program including woodworking and drama classes, and hosts Hsinchu County's first KKF natural farming education base — bringing a hands-on, sustainability-minded curriculum to this quiet corner of Baoshan Township.

關於本頁的說明：山湖國小正式名稱為「新竹縣立寶山國小山湖分校」，是寶山國小的分校，並非獨立設校。由於新竹縣學校名冊將其列為獨立一列，本頁因此為其建立介紹頁面；但為求資訊正確，特此說明該校實際上隸屬寶山國小管轄，而非獨立學校。

山湖分校緊鄰風景優美的寶山水庫，屬於偏遠／不利地區學校。儘管規模小且為分校身分，學校仍提供木工與戲劇等特色課程，並設有新竹縣首處KKF自然農法教育基地，為寶山鄉這處寧靜角落帶來動手實作、注重永續的課程內容。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 The County's First KKF Farming Base

首處KKF自然農法教育基地

Shanhu hosts Hsinchu County's first KKF (natural farming) education base, giving students hands-on experience with sustainable, chemical-free agriculture.

🎭 Woodworking and Drama

木工與戲劇課程

Despite its small size, the branch offers woodworking and drama programs — creative, hands-on outlets uncommon for a campus of its scale.

🏞️ A Home Beside the Reservoir

緊鄰寶山水庫

Located directly beside the Baoshan Reservoir, the school makes the most of its scenic, remote setting as a classified remote/disadvantaged-area campus.
