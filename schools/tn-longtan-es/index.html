Longtan Elementary School

臺南市永康區龍潭國民小學

Named for the "Dragon Pool" neighborhood it serves, this school's motto is "Foresight, Vitality, Gratitude," and its campus includes a swimming pool and attached kindergarten.

About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school named for its "Dragon Pool."

Longtan Elementary serves the Longtan (龍潭, "Dragon Pool") neighborhood of Yongkang District, though its exact founding year could not be confirmed from available public sources. (Note: an unrelated, same-named "龍潭國小" in Taoyuan's Longtan District, founded in 1899, should not be confused with this Tainan school.) The school's motto and tagline is "Foresight, Vitality, Gratitude" (遠見、活力、感恩). Its campus includes a swimming pool and dedicated playground equipment, both featured prominently in the school's own promotional materials. Like several other Yongkang schools, it uses only domestically-sourced pork and beef in its cafeteria and maintains an attached kindergarten.

龍潭國小服務永康區的龍潭地區，惟確切創校年份未能自公開資料查證。（註：桃園市龍潭區另有一所同名「龍潭國小」，創校於1899年，與本校並無關聯，請勿混淆。）學校校訓／標語為「遠見、活力、感恩」。校園設有游泳池與專用遊樂設施，均為學校宣傳材料中的重點特色。與永康區多所學校相同，午餐使用國產豬肉、牛肉，並附設幼兒園。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for a pond that shaped its village.

🐉

The school is named for the Longtan (龍潭, "Dragon Pool") neighborhood it serves — a place name referring to a body of water, consistent with the irrigation-pond and creek-side geography that shaped many of Yongkang's village names before urbanization.
