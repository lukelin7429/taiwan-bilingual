Yangmei Junior High School

楊梅國民中學

One of the district's oldest secondary-school lineages, founded in 1948 beside Guishan Park, with historic dormitory buildings now registered as municipal heritage.

創校於1948年，楊梅歷史最悠久的中等學校體系之一，緊鄰貴山公園，校內留有已登錄為歷史建築的日治後期宿舍群。

About · 學校簡介

Beside Guishan, the hill that changed its name.

Yangmei Junior High traces back to 1948, when it was founded as Taoyuan County Yangmei Middle School, adding a senior-high division in 1951. When nine-year compulsory education split junior and senior divisions apart in 1968, the junior division became Taoyuan County Yangmei Junior High (the senior division became today's Yangmei Senior High School), and the school took its current name, Taoyuan City Yangmei Junior High, in December 2014. The campus spans roughly 6.68 hectares next to Guishan Park, and several of its Japanese-colonial-to-postwar-era faculty dormitory buildings have been registered as Taoyuan City historic architecture.

楊梅國中創立於民國37年（1948年），原名「桃園縣立楊梅中學」；民國40年（1951年）增設高中部；民國57年（1968年）九年國教實施，初中部與高中部分立，初中部改制桃園縣立楊梅國民中學（高中部即後來的楊梅高中）；民國103年（2014年）12月更名桃園市立楊梅國民中學。校地廣達6.6761公頃，緊鄰貴山公園，校內留有多棟日治後期至戰後初期興建的校長與教職員宿舍群，其中部分建築已登錄為桃園市歷史建築。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Registered Historic Dormitories

歷史建築宿舍群

The campus preserves several principal and faculty dormitory buildings dating from the late Japanese colonial period into the early postwar years, several of which are now registered as Taoyuan City historic architecture.

🐢 From "Turtle Hill" to "Noble Hill"

龜山改名貴山的地方典故

The neighboring hill was once nicknamed "Turtle Hill" (龜山) for its shape; after local figures built the school there decades ago, it was renamed "Guishan" (貴山, "Noble Hill") — now a well-known local landmark.

🌳 A Sprawling 6.68-Hectare Campus

校地廣達6.6761公頃

At nearly 6.7 hectares, Yangmei Junior High has one of the largest campuses in the district, sitting directly beside Guishan Park.

🏫 Shared Roots with Yangmei Senior High

與楊梅高中同源

Both Yangmei Junior High and Yangmei Senior High trace back to the 1948-founded Taoyuan County Yangmei Middle School, making them one of Yangmei District's longest-running educational lineages.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A hill's name change tells Yangmei's story.

⛰️

Yangmei Junior High sits right beside Guishan Park, and the hill's own history — renamed from "Turtle Hill" to "Noble Hill" after the school was built there — is itself a small chapter in the story of Yangmei's local development. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
