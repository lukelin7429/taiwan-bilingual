Yongxing Elementary School

永興國民小學

A small rural school serving the Saisiyat Baishuo tribal settlement, known for its Yimin Temple cultural program and single-track cycling routes.

服務賽夏族百壽部落的鄉村小校，以義民廟文化課程及單車越野路線聞名。

About · 學校簡介

Serving Baishuo's Saisiyat and Hakka families.

Yongxing Elementary serves the Saisiyat "Baishuo" (百壽) tribal settlement area of Shitan Township, alongside its Hakka neighbors. The school runs a "Yimin Temple" cultural program that teaches students about Hakka culture and the local history of Shitan, and the area around the school is also known for single-track cycling tours along Provincial Route 3.

永興國小服務獅潭鄉賽夏族「百壽」部落一帶，也涵蓋周邊的客家鄰里。學校設有「義民廟」文化課程，教導學生認識客家文化與獅潭在地歷史，校園周邊也以沿台3線的單車越野路線聞名。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛕 The Yimin Temple Cultural Program

義民廟文化課程

A dedicated program built around the local Yimin Temple teaches students about Hakka culture and the layered local history of Shitan Township.

🚵 Single-Track Cycling on Route 3

台3線單車越野路線

The area around the school is known for single-track cycling tours along Provincial Route 3, drawing outdoor enthusiasts through the hills of Shitan.

🏘️ Serving the Baishuo Settlement

服務百壽部落

Yongxing serves the Saisiyat "Baishuo" (百壽) tribal settlement area, making it a school where indigenous and Hakka students learn side by side.

Serving the Saisiyat Baishuo tribal community alongside Hakka neighbors, and running programs centered on the local Yimin Temple, the school is a direct bridge between Shitan's Saisiyat and Hakka heritage. See the Miaoli county page for more on the region.
