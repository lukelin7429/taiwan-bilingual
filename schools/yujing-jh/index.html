Tainan Municipal Yujing Junior High School

玉井國中

Founded in 1946 as a branch of Xinhua Junior High, the school rebuilt after severe earthquake damage in 2010 with the help of a corporate donation, and today sits at the heart of Yujing's mango-growing basin.

About · 學校簡介

Rebuilt after an earthquake, rooted in the mango basin.

Yujing Junior High opened in May 1946 as a branch of Tainan County Xinhua Junior High School, located near today's Yujing fruit market and post office. It began with just two classes and 96 students under its first branch director, Chen Fu-zhen, and was renamed Tainan Municipal Yujing Junior High School after the 2010 Tainan county-city merger. On March 4, 2010, the school suffered serious damage in the Jiaxian earthquake; five teaching buildings were later demolished as unsafe, and the Formosa Plastics Group donated funds to rebuild three replacement buildings. Today the school is certified as a health-promoting campus with a dedicated native-language learning zone, and publishes its campus safety mapping and curriculum planning documents online.

玉井國中創校於民國35年（1946年）5月，前身為台南縣立新化初級中學玉井分校，校址鄰近今日玉井果菜市場與郵局，首任分校主任為陳福珍，開辦時僅2班96名學生。2010年台南縣市合併後改名為台南市立玉井國中。民國99年（2010年）3月4日甲仙地震重創校舍，忠孝、仁愛、信義、和平、文化等五棟教學大樓因結構安全問題陸續拆除，台塑集團捐款協助重建三棟校舍。學校現為健康促進學校，設有母語學習專區，並公開校園安全地圖與課程計畫等資訊。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏗️ Rebuilt After the 2010 Earthquake

震後重建校舍

The Jiaxian earthquake of March 2010 badly damaged five teaching buildings, which were later demolished; the Formosa Plastics Group donated funds to rebuild three replacement buildings, a striking example of corporate-aided recovery.

🌱 A Branch School That Became Its Own

分校獨立設校

Founded in 1946 as a branch of Xinhua Junior High, the school opened with just two classes and 96 students before growing into today's independent institution.

💚 Health-Promoting Campus

健康促進學校認證

The school is certified as a health-promoting school and maintains a dedicated native-language (mother tongue) learning zone for students.

📋 Transparent Campus Information

公開校園資訊

The school publishes public campus safety mapping and full curriculum planning documents online, reflecting a commitment to transparency for families in this remote district.

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the heart of the mango basin.

🥭

The school sits in the heart of Yujing's mango-growing basin, and its post-earthquake rebuilding — aided by a major corporate donation — reflects the resilience of this remote mountain township's public institutions. Students here grow up surrounded by the orchards that give Yujing its reputation as Taiwan's mango capital. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
