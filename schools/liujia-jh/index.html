Liujia Junior High School

六嘉國中

A Suantou Village junior high known for its harmonica and wind orchestras, competitive football and bocce teams, and strong reading-literacy program.

About · 學校簡介

Rooted in Suantou, Liujiao's historic hub.

Liujia Junior High School was founded on April 1, 1964, as a branch of Chiayi County's Yushan Middle School, temporarily housed at Suantou Elementary School before becoming independent that same year as Chiayi County Liujia Junior Middle School at its current site. It became Liujia Junior High School in July 1968 under Taiwan's nine-year compulsory education reform. The school maintains prominent harmonica and wind orchestras with county and national competition awards, fields competitive football and bocce (滾球) teams, and runs a strong environmental education curriculum alongside a comprehensive reading-literacy initiative.

六嘉國中創立於民國53年（1964年）4月1日，最初為嘉義縣立玉山中學分校，暫借蒜頭國小校舍上課，同年即獨立設校為嘉義縣立六嘉初級中學，落腳現址。民國57年（1968年）7月九年國教實施後改稱六嘉國民中學。學校擁有知名的口琴與管樂團隊，屢獲縣級及全國性競賽獎項，並組有具競爭力的足球與滾球隊，同時推動扎實的環境教育課程與全面性的閱讀素養計畫。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎼 Harmonica & Wind Orchestras

口琴與管樂團隊

The school maintains prominent harmonica and wind (管樂) orchestras that have earned county and national competition awards, a signature of its music program.

⚽ Football & Bocce Teams

足球與滾球隊

Competitive football (soccer) and bocce (滾球) teams give students strong athletic programs alongside the school's arts offerings.

🌱 Environmental Education Curriculum

環境教育課程

A strong environmental education curriculum includes student competitions, connecting classroom learning to Liujiao's surrounding farmland.

📚 Reading Literacy Initiative

閱讀素養計畫

A comprehensive reading-literacy initiative includes student talent competitions, part of a broader push for literacy across the school's programs.

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the historic heart of Liujiao.

🌾

Located in Suantou Village, the historic commercial and administrative center of Liujiao Township, the school sits amid the township's rice paddies and vegetable farms, tying its identity to the surrounding agricultural economy. See the Chiayi county page for more on the region.
