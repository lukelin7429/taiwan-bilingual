Liming Junior High School

黎明國中

A junior high whose name, "Dawn," reflects the optimism of Taiwan's 1960s–70s school-building era, rebuilt entirely on a new campus in 2014.

About · 學校簡介

From founding to a full campus rebuild.

Liming Junior High was founded in 1966 and took its current name, “Liming” (黎明, Dawn), in 1971 — part of a wave of optimistically named schools built during Taiwan's 1960s–70s education expansion. The school later underwent a full campus reconstruction, moving to a new address that was completed in October 2014.

黎明國中創立於民國55年（1966年），民國60年（1971年）更名為「黎明」，呼應台灣1960、70年代教育擴張時期學校命名的樂觀風氣。學校後來進行全面校舍重建，於2014年10月完成新址遷校工程。
