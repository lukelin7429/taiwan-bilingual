Jiangjun Elementary School

將軍國民小學

A resilient island school on Jiangjun Island, rebuilt three times to keep pace with the challenges of a remote fishing outpost.

About · 學校簡介

Rebuilt again and again for its island.

Jiangjun Elementary predates the modern campus that most visitors would recognize today — a new school building was constructed between 1968 and 1970, at the time becoming the largest school across all of Penghu's outer islands. The campus was rebuilt again in 1988, and once more in 1990–91. In 2012, an inspection found the building lacked sufficient earthquake resistance due to sand-contaminated concrete used in an earlier construction era, prompting the county government to fully demolish and rebuild it as a single-story structure — a reflection of just how difficult it is to keep infrastructure standing on Penghu's remote outer islands.

將軍國小校史早於現今大眾熟悉的校舍——新校舍於民國57至59年（1968–1970年）間興建，當時一度是澎湖離島規模最大的學校。校舍於民國77年（1988年）、以及民國79至80年（1990–91年）間再度重建。民國101年（2012年），檢測發現校舍因早期建材海砂含量過高，耐震強度不足，縣府因此全面拆除重建為單層建築——正是離島基礎建設維護不易的真實寫照。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A fishing island reachable only by boat.

⚓

Jiangjun Island is a small fishing island in the Wan'an archipelago, reachable only by boat. The repeated rebuilding of Jiangjun Elementary's campus over the decades reflects the practical challenges — and the community's persistence — in maintaining schooling on Penghu's remote outer islands.
