Tainan Changxing Elementary School

長興國小

Founded in 1918 as a temple-area branch school named for the Taizimiao (Crown Prince Temple), part of Rende's network of small farming-village schools that later urbanized.

1918年創校，因太子廟地區而得名的分教場，是仁德區眾多因廟宇聚落而生的小型學校之一，後隨都市化發展至今。

About · 學校簡介

A temple-village school that grew with its district.

Changxing Elementary School was established in 1918 as “Puqiangtou Public School's Taizimiao Branch” (埔姜頭公學校太子廟分教場). It became independent as “Taizimiao Public School” in 1921, was renamed “Taizimiao National School” in 1941, “Rende Township's Third National School” in 1946, “Changxing National School” in 1947, and finally “Changxing Elementary School” in 1968 under nine-year compulsory education. The school established a special-education class in September 1982, and its “Changxing Hall” was completed in June 1986. An attached kindergarten was added in August 2017.

長興國小創校於民國7年（1918年），初設「埔姜頭公學校太子廟分教場」。民國10年（1921年）獨立設校，改稱「太子廟公學校」，1941年改名「太子廟國民學校」，1946年改稱「仁德鄉第三國民學校」，1947年改名「長興國民學校」，1968年九年國教實施後定名「長興國民小學」。學校於民國71年（1982年）9月設立啟智班，「長興館」於民國75年（1986年）6月落成，並於民國106年（2017年）8月增設附設幼兒園。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛕 Named for the Crown Prince Temple

以太子廟為名

Named for the Taizimiao (Crown Prince Temple) area where it began as a branch school, Changxing represents Rende's network of small temple-adjacent farming-village schools.

🏛️ Changxing Hall Since 1986

長興館1986年落成

The school's dedicated “Changxing Hall” was completed in June 1986, adding a lasting piece of campus infrastructure still in use today.

💙 A Special-Education Class Since 1982

1982年設啟智班

The school established a special-education class in September 1982, extending its educational mission to a broader range of students.

👶 A Kindergarten Added in 2017

2017年增設幼兒園

An attached kindergarten was added in August 2017, keeping the century-old school responsive to the needs of local families.
