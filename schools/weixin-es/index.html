About · 學校簡介

A small school in an old settlement.

Weixin Elementary is a small coastal-village school serving Weixin Village in Yong'an District, in the area historically known as Zhuzigang. The village traces its settlement to the Ming-Zheng era, when troops under Prince Ningjing's retainers first established a community here, before it was renamed "Weixin" following the 2010 merger of Kaohsiung County and Kaohsiung City.

維新國小是服務永安區維新里的小型村莊學校，所在地區舊稱竹仔港。此地聚落可追溯至明鄭時期，寧靖王部屬曾在此屯墾定居，2010年高雄縣市合併後才改稱「維新」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚓ Rooted in Old Zhuzigang

竹仔港舊址

The school sits within Weixin Village, historically known as Zhuzigang ("bamboo harbor"), one of the older settlement names along this stretch of coast.

🏯 Ming-Zheng Era Origins

明鄭時期屯墾

The surrounding village's settlement history reaches back to the Ming-Zheng era, when troops under Prince Ningjing's retainers first farmed and settled the area.

🔤 Renamed After the 2010 Merger

2010年更名維新

The village — and the school with it — took its current name, Weixin, only after Kaohsiung County and Kaohsiung City merged in 2010.

🌊 A Small Coastal-Village Campus

濱海村莊小校

Like several of its Yong'an neighbors, Weixin remains a small school serving a close-knit coastal farming and fishing community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

From Zhuzigang to Weixin.

🎍

Weixin Village was originally called "Zhuzigang" (竹仔港); the name traces to the Ming-Zheng era, when troops under Prince Ningjing's retainers first settled the area, before it was renamed "Weixin" after the 2010 county-city merger. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
