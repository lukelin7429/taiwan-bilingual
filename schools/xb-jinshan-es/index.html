About · 學校簡介

A name older than "Jinshan" itself.

Jinshan Elementary School's history stretches back to 1898, when it opened as a branch of the Keelung Mandarin Training Institute under Japanese rule, before being renamed Jinbaoli Public School that same October. With more than 125 years of history, it is one of the oldest schools on Taiwan's North Coast. Its earliest name, "Jinbaoli" (金包里), is the historic Ketagalan-derived place name for Jinshan itself, tying the school directly to the district's Indigenous linguistic heritage.

金山國小的歷史可追溯至1898年（日治時期），最初為基隆國語傳習所分教場，同年10月更名為金包里公學校。校史逾125年，是北海岸歷史最悠久的學校之一。最初的校名「金包里」源自凱達格蘭族語，是金山地區的古地名，直接連結該地的原住民語言文化遺產。
