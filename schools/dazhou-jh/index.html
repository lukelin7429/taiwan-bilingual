About · 學校簡介

Founded on land shaped by a river.

Dazhou Junior High began in 1960 as a branch of Qishan Middle School based in nearby Xizhou, initially borrowing classrooms from Xizhou Elementary School before its own campus was completed. It became an independent school in 1963 under the former Kaohsiung County, as Dazhou Junior High School. The school takes its name from the local Dazhou and Xizhou area, land formed over time by the shifting meanders of the Qishan River.

大洲國中前身為民國49年（1960年）設立的「旗山中學溪洲分部」，初期借用溪洲國小教室上課，後遷入自建校舍。民國52年（1963年）獨立設校，定名為高雄縣立大洲初級中學。校名取自當地大洲、溪洲一帶地名，此地為旗山溪河道遷移沖積而成的土地。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Started in Borrowed Classrooms

借用教室起家

Before its own campus was built, the school's earliest students attended classes borrowed from neighboring Xizhou Elementary School.

🌊 Named for a River-Formed Landscape

旗山溪沖積地名

"大洲" and "溪洲" both describe land built up by the meandering Qishan River, tying the school's name directly to the local geography.

📜 More Than Six Decades of Independence

獨立設校逾一甲子

From a 1960 branch campus to full independence in 1963, the school has now served the Dazhou community for more than sixty years.
