About · 學校簡介

Where creativity sets sail.

Longgang Harbor Elementary School became independent in September 1963 as Chiayi County Dongshi Township Longgang National School, having previously operated from 1956 as a branch class known as "Sanjiang Branch Class," associated with the lineage that also produced Sanjiang Elementary School. Under the motto "Creative Longgang, Hope Sets Sail," the school has an attached kindergarten and is active in art, music, and science-fair competitions at the county level, alongside community-based learning initiatives and mobile-device usage guidelines for digital literacy.

龍港國小於民國52年（1963年）9月獨立設校，定名為嘉義縣東石鄉龍港國民學校，此前自民國45年（1956年）起以「三江分班」形式運作，與三江國小系出同源。學校秉持「創意龍港 希望啟航」的校訓，附設龍港幼兒園，並積極參與縣級美術、音樂與科展競賽，同時推動社區本位學習與行動載具使用規範，深化數位素養教育。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛵ "Creative Longgang, Hope Sets Sail"

創意龍港 希望啟航

The school's motto frames creativity and hope as guiding values, reflecting an outward-looking spirit in a small fishing-harbor community.

🎨 Art, Music & Science Fair Competitions

美術、音樂、科展競賽

Students are active in art, music, and science-fair competitions at the county level, giving the school a well-rounded competitive presence.

👶 Attached Kindergarten

龍港幼兒園

An attached kindergarten, Longgang Kindergarten, extends the school's role in the community to the youngest local children.

📱 Digital Literacy Guidelines

行動載具使用規範

Community-based learning initiatives and mobile-device usage guidelines support responsible digital literacy alongside the school's academic programs.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Dragon Harbor, on Dongshi's fishing coast.

🐉

"龍港" ("Dragon Harbor") sits directly on Dongshi's fishing harbor geography, with the local community's livelihood historically tied to inshore fishing and oyster cultivation. See the Chiayi county page for more on the region.
