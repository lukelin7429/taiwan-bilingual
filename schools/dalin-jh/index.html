Dalin Junior High School

大林國民中學

A public junior high school in the historic sugar-refinery town of Dalin, known for its award-winning music ensemble and strong academic results.

About · 學校簡介

A school forged alongside a sugar town.

Dalin Junior High traces its roots to 1913, when the Japanese-run Nitaka Sugar Manufacturing Company founded an elementary school for its employees' children at Dapolin. The school in its current form was formally founded in 1946 as "Tainan County Chiayi Girls' Junior High School," renamed after the Tainan/Chiayi county split in 1950, expanded into a full secondary school in 1957, and took its present name in 1968 under Taiwan's nine-year compulsory education reform. Today it enrolls around 515 students across 22 classes, including dedicated music and athletics talent classes, and its students have posted strong results on the national Basic Competency Test.

大林國中的歷史可追溯至1913年（大正2年），當時日資新高製糖株式會社在大莆林為員工子女設立小學。現今學校則正式創立於民國35年（1946年），初名「台南縣立嘉義女子初級中學」；民國39年（1950年）嘉義設縣後更名，民國46年（1957年）擴編為完全中學，並於民國57年（1968年）九年國教改制後定名為現名。學校目前約有515名學生、22個班級，並設有音樂及體育才藝班，學生在國中會考的表現亦十分優異。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎺 A Nationally Honored Ensemble

全國特優音樂社團

The school's marching and music ensemble has won "special excellence" at the national level, and students are also active in robotics and computational-thinking competitions.

📝 Strong Academic Results

會考成績亮眼

In the 2026 national Basic Competency Test, the school's top scorer achieved 5A8+ with a level-6 writing score, and 37 students scored 1A or above.

🎨 Talent Classes in Music and Sport

音樂與體育才藝班

Alongside its regular curriculum, the school runs dedicated art-talent classes with a music focus and athletics-talent classes, each spanning three classes per grade band.

🏭 Born of the Sugar Trade

與糖業共生

The school's own origin story, founded in the shadow of the Nitaka sugar refinery, is entwined with the sugar industry that built Dalin into a company town.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Growing up in a former sugar town.

🏭

Founded in the shadow of the Nitaka sugar refinery that built Dalin into a company town, the school's own origin story is entwined with the sugar industry that shaped the whole township. That legacy still runs through Dalin's streets and identity today, even as the town has grown to host Nanhua University and Dalin Tzu Chi Hospital.
