About · 學校簡介

Named for the man who watered the Taipei Basin.

Liugong Junior High School began with a preparatory office in 1978, was formally established in 1979 under founding principal Ye Jiandeng, and began enrolling students in 1980. The school is named to commemorate Guo Xiliu, the Qing-dynasty pioneer who built the Liugong Canal irrigation system that once watered much of the Taipei Basin — the campus itself sits on land created by filling in a former reservoir pond of that same canal system, donated by the Liugong Farmland Irrigation Association. The school today runs international-education exchanges, including a partnership with Institution Saint-Louis in France, alongside hands-on elective courses such as drone operation, folding-fan painting, and bubble-tea making.

瑠公國中籌備處於1978年（民國67年）成立，1979年（民國68年）由創校校長葉建登正式設校，1980年（民國69年）開始招生。校名為紀念清代開鑿瑠公圳、灌溉大台北盆地的水利先驅郭錫瑠而命名——校地即由瑠公農田水利會捐地，填平瑠公圳「中埤」舊址而成。學校現今推動國際教育交流，與法國聖路易中學（Institution Saint-Louis）締結姊妹校，並開設無人機操作、摺扇彩繪、珍珠奶茶調製等實作選修課程。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Built on the water that made Taipei possible.

💧

The school's very site is reclaimed land from a pond of the historic Liugong Canal (瑠公圳), the 18th-century irrigation network that made large-scale rice farming possible in the Taipei Basin. Sitting at the foot of Tiger Mountain, part of the Four Beasts Mountains range, the campus is a direct physical link between Xinyi's pre-urban agrarian past and its current dense development.
