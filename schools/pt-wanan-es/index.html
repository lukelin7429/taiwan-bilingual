Wan'an Elementary School

萬安國民小學

A Paiwan indigenous elementary school at the northern edge of Taiwu Township, at the foot of Dawu Mountain alongside the Wan'an River, with a catchment spanning five tribal settlements.

About · 學校簡介

A school district spanning five tribal settlements.

Wan'an Elementary School sits at the northern edge of Taiwu Township, at the foot of Dawu Mountain, alongside the Wan'an River (萬安溪). Its school district spans five tribal settlements across an unusually wide catchment area that crosses township lines: Wan'an, Anping, and Dali, all in Wan'an Village, Taiwu Township; Mashi, in Jiaping Village; and Chengzhilu, in Chengde Village, neighboring Wanluan Township. Virtually all students at the school are Paiwan indigenous children.

萬安國小位於泰武鄉北緣，北大武山腳下，緊鄰萬安溪。學區橫跨五個部落聚落，涵蓋範圍罕見地跨越鄉界：萬安、安平、達來（皆屬泰武鄉萬安村）、瑪士（屬佳平村），以及鄰近萬巒鄉成德村的成之路。學生幾乎全為排灣族原住民兒童。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗺️ A Five-Settlement, Cross-Township Catchment

跨鄉五部落學區

Wan'an Elementary's school district spans five tribal settlements — Wan'an, Anping, Dali, Mashi, and Chengzhilu — an unusually wide catchment area that even crosses into neighboring Wanluan Township.

⛰️ At the Foot of Dawu Mountain

北大武山腳下

The school sits at the northern edge of Taiwu Township, at the base of the sacred Dawu Mountain, alongside the Wan'an River — a landscape central to Paiwan spiritual life.

🪶 A Nearly Entirely Paiwan Student Body

學生幾乎全為排灣族

Virtually all of Wan'an Elementary's students are Paiwan indigenous children, reflecting the deep indigenous character of this mountain community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Taiwu, home of the internationally touring Ancient Ballads Choir.

🎵

Taiwu Township is a Paiwan indigenous mountain township in Pingtung, internationally known through the Taiwu Elementary Ancient Ballads Choir. Local traditional culture centers on the sacred Dawu Mountain, millet farming, and slate-house architecture. Wan'an Elementary's wide, five-settlement catchment at the northern foot of the mountain places its students at the very edge of this storied Paiwan homeland.
