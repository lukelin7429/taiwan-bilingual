About · 學校簡介

A valley school that built baseball champions.

Taiyuan Elementary was founded in 1929 during the Japanese colonial era under the name "Galabawan Public School," going through several renamings before settling on its current name in 1968. Tucked in the secluded Taiyuan valley of the Coastal Mountain Range, the school became a regional baseball powerhouse after founding its student team in 1983 — winning the East Region national qualifying title four years straight, from 1986 to 1989. Alumni Chang Tai-shan and Chang Chih-hao went on to become professional baseball stars, and the school has spawned several branch schools over the decades, including Beiyuan Elementary in 1957.

泰源國小創立於1929年（日治時期），初名「嘎嘮吧灣公學校」，歷經多次更名，1968年定名為現名。學校座落於海岸山脈幽靜的泰源溪谷，自1983年成立學生棒球隊後，成為區域棒球勁旅，連續四年（1986至1989年）榮獲東區代表權。校友張泰山與張志豪後來成為職棒明星，學校歷年來也分出多所分校，包括1957年獨立的北源國小。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚾ A Cradle of Professional Baseball

職棒人才搖籃

Taiyuan's student baseball team, founded in 1983, won the East Region national qualifying title four straight years (1986–1989) and produced pro stars Chang Tai-shan and Chang Chih-hao.

🏞️ A Nearly Century-Old Valley School

近百年溪谷老校

Founded in 1929, the school has anchored the secluded Taiyuan valley of the Coastal Mountain Range for nearly a century, through several eras and renamings.

🌱 A Root From Which Other Schools Grew

分校的起源

Over the decades, Taiyuan Elementary spawned several branch schools, including Beiyuan Elementary, which became independent in 1957, extending education further into the valley.

Local Culture · 在地文化

East Coast talent from a mountain valley.

Tucked in the secluded Taiyuan valley of the Coastal Mountain Range, Taiyuan Elementary draws on both its Indigenous and rural East Coast community roots and a proud baseball tradition — producing talent that has gone on to represent Taiwan at the highest level of the sport.
