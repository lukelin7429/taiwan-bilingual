Shipai Elementary School

彰化市石牌國民小學

About · 學校簡介

From a borrowed room to an independent campus.

Shipai Elementary began in 1954 as a temporary branch campus of Kuaiguan Elementary School, meeting in a community gathering space in Shipai Village. It became an independent school in September 1961 under its first principal, Shi Haican. From the 1990s onward the school modernized steadily, adding new sports courts, an elevator, and earthquake-reinforcement work, and in 2010 it formed a sister-school relationship with Xiwei Elementary School in Wuri Township, Taichung County.

石牌國小前身為民國43年（1954年）成立的快官國小分班，借用石牌里的公共集會場所上課。民國50年（1961年）9月獨立設校，首任校長為施海燦。自1990年代起，學校陸續進行現代化建設，包括新建運動場地、增設電梯與耐震補強工程，並於2010年與台中縣霧峰鄉喜峰國小締結姊妹校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏘️ Grown from the Community

從社區集會空間起家

The school began life in 1954 as a temporary branch campus meeting in a community gathering space, before becoming independent in 1961 — a grassroots start typical of the era.

🏗️ Decades of Modernization

持續現代化建設

Since the 1990s, Shipai has added new sports courts, an elevator, and earthquake-reinforcement upgrades, keeping its campus facilities current for students today.

🤝 A Sister School Across Taiwan

與喜峰國小締結姊妹校

In 2010, Shipai formed a sister-school relationship with Xiwei Elementary School in Wuri Township, Taichung County, connecting students across county lines.
