Yongle Elementary School

永樂國小

Sitting directly inside the historic Dadaocheng old street district, Yongle Elementary runs its own dedicated curriculum website teaching students the neighborhood's tea-trading heritage.

About · 學校簡介

A school split by a new road.

Yongle Elementary traces its lineage to the 1896-established Taipei Mandarin Training Institute, becoming independent on its current site in 1910 as Dadaocheng Girls' Public School (大稻埕女子公學校) — the earlier of two dates recorded for the school. The school was renamed Taipei Datong District Yongle National Elementary School in March 1989 after Taipei's administrative-district reorganization. It split from Taiping/Dadaocheng's original public school when Yanping N. Road was cut through the district in 1909, dividing the original school site in two — the east side became today's Taiping Elementary. Yongle now runs a dedicated "Dadaocheng" thematic curriculum website (大稻埕主題式網頁), explicitly teaching students about the historic tea- and dry-goods-trading street culture surrounding the school.

永樂國小校史可追溯至1896年設立的「台北國語傳習所」學脈，1910年於現址獨立設校，初名「大稻埕女子公學校」——此為校史記載的兩個創校年份中較早者。1989年3月，台北市行政區重劃後更名為「臺北市大同區永樂國民小學」。1909年因延平北路開闢，切割了太平／大稻埕原本共用的校地，東側部分後成為今日的太平國小。永樂國小目前設有專屬的「大稻埕主題式網頁」，明確教導學生認識學校周邊歷史悠久的茶葉與南北貨貿易街區文化。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Inside the historic old street.

🍵

Yongle Elementary runs its own dedicated "Dadaocheng" heritage educational website and sits directly inside the historic Dadaocheng old street district — arguably the strongest direct curricular tie to local tea-trade and old-street culture of any school in Datong District. Students here learn their neighborhood's merchant history as part of the regular school curriculum, not as an occasional field trip.
