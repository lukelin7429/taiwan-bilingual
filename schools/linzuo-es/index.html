About · 學校簡介

A branch school that spun off its own branch.

Linzuo Elementary was founded in 1942 as a Japanese-era branch school and became independent in 1945. The school later spun off its own branch, Mingde Elementary, in 1959. Linzuo's own school history notes that its Linzuo/Lindong catchment area is made up mostly of farming and fishing households, a description that captures the blended character of this part of Sihu Township.

林厝國小創立於1942年（日治時期）分校，於1945年獨立設校。學校後於民國48年（1959年）分出明德國小分校。林厝國小校史記載，林厝／林東學區居民多以農漁為業，正反映出四湖鄉這一帶農漁交織的地方特色。
