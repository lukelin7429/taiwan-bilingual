Luzhou's third secondary school, born from farmland redevelopment near Luzhou MRT Station and now one of the district's largest campuses.

About · 學校簡介

Growing with the MRT to Luzhou.

Lujiang Junior High was approved in 1998 and began enrolling students in 2002, making it Luzhou's third secondary school. It sits in the Nangangzi land-readjustment zone near Luzhou MRT Station, an area that transformed from farmland into dense residential blocks once rail access arrived. The school has since grown into one of Luzhou's largest campuses, with roughly 59 classes and around 2,700 students and staff.

鷺江國中於民國87年（1998年）核准設校，2002年正式招生上課，是蘆洲第三所中學。校址位於捷運蘆洲站附近的南港子重劃區，這一帶隨著捷運通車，由農地迅速轉變為密集的住宅街廓。學校如今已發展為蘆洲數一數二的大型校園，約有59班、近2,700名師生。
