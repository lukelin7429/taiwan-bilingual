Dacheng Junior High School

大成國民中學

Rebuilt by the Tzu Chi Foundation after near-total destruction in the 921 earthquake, now home to an international sister-school partnership.

About · 學校簡介

Built on forest land, rebuilt through disaster.

Dacheng Junior High was built on 2 hectares of nursery land granted by the Forestry Bureau, opening on September 9, 1968. Its buildings were nearly completely destroyed in the catastrophic 1999 921 earthquake, and the campus was rebuilt by the Tzu Chi Foundation, reopening in January 2002. In 2023, the school became a sister school with Izumi Junior High School in Kagoshima, Japan, and it also hosts a branch of Nantou Community University.

大成國中校地為林務局撥用之苗圃用地共2公頃，於民國57年（1968年）9月9日開校。校舍於1999年921大地震中近乎全毀，其後由慈濟基金會援建，於2002年1月復校開學。2023年與日本鹿兒島市伊集院中學締結姊妹校，校內亦設有南投社區大學分班。
