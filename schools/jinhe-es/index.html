About · 學校簡介

Beside a temple and a sports park.

Jinhe Elementary School was founded in 1984, located next to Yuantong Temple (圓通寺) and Jinhe Sports Park. The school's setting ties it directly to one of Zhonghe's most prominent Buddhist landmarks, anchoring it within the district's temple-centered old-town geography.

錦和國小創立於民國73年（1984年），校址緊鄰圓通寺與錦和運動公園。學校所在位置直接連結中和最具代表性的佛教地標之一，扎根於該區以廟宇為中心的舊城地理脈絡。
