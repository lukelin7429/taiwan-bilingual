Shenren Elementary School

伸仁國民小學

A public elementary school founded in 1969 to absorb the growing student population of rural Shengang Township.

創校於民國58年（1969年），為承接彰化縣伸港鄉快速成長人口而設立的公立國小。

About · 學校簡介

Born from a neighboring school's overflow.

Shenren Elementary traces its site preparation to 1967, with its school building completed the following year. The school formally opened in September 1969 as "Changhua County Shen Ren Elementary School," welcoming 731 students and 16 staff members transferred directly from neighboring Hsinkang (新港) Elementary School. First principal Tseng Ming-de took office that August, and the campus — covering nearly 6,900 square meters — has served the children of Shengang Township ever since.

伸仁國小的校地整地始於民國56年（1967年），隔年校舍完工。民國58年（1969年）9月，「彰化縣伸仁國民小學」正式開校，由鄰近的新港國小轉入731名學生與16位教職員。首任校長曾明德於同年8月到任，校地面積近6,889平方公尺，自此持續服務伸港鄉的學童。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A School Built to Meet a Need

應需而生的學校

Shenren was established as Shengang Township's population grew quickly in the late 1960s, giving local children a school of their own rather than a long commute to Hsinkang.

👥 A Ready-Made Community

現成的師生社群

The school opened with an entire student body and teaching staff transferred wholesale from Hsinkang Elementary — a rare founding story that gave Shenren an instant community from day one.

🌾 Rooted in Rural Shengang

扎根伸港鄉土

Located in Zengjia Village, the school remains closely tied to the farming and fishing communities of Shengang Township along Changhua's coastal plain.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Shengang Township's rapid growth years.

🏘️

Shenren's founding reflects a moment of rapid growth in Shengang Township's rural communities during the late 1960s, when the area's expanding population outgrew the capacity of its existing schools. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
