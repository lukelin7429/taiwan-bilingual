Erchong Elementary School

新北市三重區二重國民小學

About · 學校簡介

Over a century on the same ground.

Erchong Elementary School was founded on July 25, 1902, during Japanese colonial rule, as "Xingzhi Public School Touqian Branch Classroom" (興直公學校頭前分教場). It became an independent school in 1912 under the name "Erchongpu Public School," was renamed Erchong National School after Taiwan's 1945 retrocession, and adopted its current name in 1968. The school is designated one of Sanchong's demonstration elementary schools (示範國民小學) and celebrated its centennial in 2001 with a commemorative stele unveiled by then-President Chen Shui-bian, marking more than a century of continuous operation in the same general area.

二重國小創立於1902年（明治35年）7月25日，日治時期名為「興直公學校頭前分教場」。民國元年（1912年）獨立設校，改名「二重埔公學校」；民國34年（1945年）光復後改稱「二重國民學校」，民國57年（1968年）改為現名。學校為三重地區示範國民小學之一，於2001年舉辦建校百年慶祝活動，由時任總統陳水扁揭幕紀念碑，見證學校在同一地區逾百年的持續辦學歷史。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎉 A Presidential Centennial

總統揭幕百年紀念碑

The school's 2001 centennial celebration included a commemorative stele unveiled by then-President Chen Shui-bian, a rare honor for a district elementary school.

🏆 A Demonstration School

示範國民小學

Erchong is designated one of Sanchong's demonstration elementary schools, reflecting its long-standing role as a model within the district's education system.

🕰️ Over a Century of History

逾百年建校歷史

Founded in 1902 as a Japanese-era branch classroom, the school has operated continuously on the same general site for more than 120 years.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Erchongpu's old place-names.

📍

Its original name referencing "頭前" (Touqian) and later "二重埔" (Erchongpu) ties the school to the historic Han-Chinese settlement place-names of the Erchong floodway area predating modern Sanchong District boundaries. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
