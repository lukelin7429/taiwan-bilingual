Wumei Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A century-old Hakka village school with a reading reputation.

Wumei Elementary began in 1918 as a branch school under Japanese rule, becoming independent in 1921 — over 100 years of continuous schooling history. Today the school has about 12 classes (including a branch campus) and 143 students, with a strong reading and writing tradition that has ranked in the county's top five for three consecutive years.

烏眉國小於1918年（大正7年）以分校形式創立，1921年（大正10年）獨立設校，至今已有逾百年持續辦學歷史。目前學校（含分校）約有12個班級、143名學生，閱讀寫作傳統深厚，連續三年名列全縣前五名。
