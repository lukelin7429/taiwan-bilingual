About · 學校簡介

An aviation-academy school's long journey to Taipei.

Huaisheng Elementary School has an unusually long and winding history. It was founded in 1934 in Hangzhou's Jianqiao Xingcun (筧橋醒村) as the children's school of China's Central Aviation Academy (中央航空學校), then relocated repeatedly across China during wartime before re-establishing in Taipei in 1949. It was renamed "Huaisheng" ("懷生") by President Chiang Kai-shek personally in 1967, when the school came under Taipei City government administration.

The school moved from its original Da'an District site — which became Huaisheng Junior High School — to its present Andong Street site in Zhongshan District in 1972. Underground facilities and a railway overpass were completed in 1974, tying the school into central Taipei's mid-century wave of school construction as the city's population and infrastructure kept expanding northeast.

懷生國小的校史相當特殊且輾轉。1934年創立於中國杭州筧橋醒村，前身為中央航空學校的子弟學校，抗戰期間隨校多次遷徙，1949年（民國38年）方在臺北復校。1967年（民國56年）學校劃歸臺北市政府管轄，由蔣中正總統親自命名為「懷生」國小。

學校於1972年（民國61年）自原大安區校地（該址後成為懷生國中）遷至現址中山區安東街，並於1974年（民國63年）完成地下設施與鐵路陸橋工程，將學校納入臺北市中心區隨人口與基礎建設持續向東北擴張的戰後校舍興建浪潮之中。
