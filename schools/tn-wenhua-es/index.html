Wenhua Elementary School

文化國民小學

Established in 1996 to serve Guiren's growing Wenhua neighborhood, now home to a national-champion soccer team and doctoral-level teaching staff.

About · 學校簡介

Guiren's newest neighborhood school comes of age.

Wenhua Elementary was officially established on July 1, 1996, to serve Guiren District's growing Wenhua neighborhood. It initially borrowed classrooms from neighboring Guinan Elementary School while its own campus was under construction, moving in by January 1998 and graduating its first class in 1999. One of the district's newest elementary schools, it now enrolls roughly 1,057 elementary students plus kindergarten across about 39 classes, with teachers holding doctoral and master's degrees among its faculty.

文化國小於民國85年（1996年）7月1日正式設校，因應歸仁區文化里人口成長而設立。創校初期借用鄰近歸南國小教室上課，至民國87年（1998年）1月遷入自有校舍，民國88年（1999年）產出首屆畢業生。作為歸仁區最新設立的小學之一，目前約有1,057名國小學生外加幼兒園，共39班，師資中不乏具博士、碩士學位者。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚽ National Soccer Champions

全國足球冠軍隊

The school's soccer team, founded in 2002, won the 8th YAMAHA CUP national championship in 2017 — a standout result for the neighborhood school.

🏸 Badminton Team Titles

羽球隊全國冠軍

Its badminton program has recently taken both the boys' and girls' team titles at a national tournament, reflecting a strong overall athletics culture.

🎓 Highly Credentialed Teachers

博碩士師資

Among its roughly 1,057 students and 39 classes, the faculty includes teachers holding doctoral and master's degrees, part of the school's academic strength.

🏫 Born from a Neighbor's Classrooms

借教室起家

The school opened in 1996 borrowing classrooms from Guinan Elementary before completing its own campus in 1998 — a reminder of Guiren's fast-growing core.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Serving Guiren's growing residential core.

🏘️

As one of Guiren's newest elementary schools, Wenhua reflects the district's denser residential growth in its core neighborhoods, distinct from the older agricultural villages on Guiren's outskirts. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
