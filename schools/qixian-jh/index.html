About · 學校簡介

A historic feeder school on the move.

Qixian Junior High School was founded in 1969 in Kaohsiung's Qianjin District under first principal Guo Xingfang. It was historically known as one of the "375" trio — alongside Sanmin Junior High and Wufu Junior High — top feeder schools into Kaohsiung Senior High School and Kaohsiung Girls' Senior High School. Between 2009 and 2012, the school relocated to its current campus in Gushan District, and its original Qianjin site was decommissioned. Qixian now enrolls approximately 1,377 students.

七賢國中創立於民國58年（1969年），校址位於高雄市前金區，首任校長為郭興芳。該校在歷史上與三民國中、五福國中並稱「三七五」，是高雄中學與高雄女中的重要明星國中之一。2009年至2012年間，學校遷至現今鼓山區校址，原前金區校地隨後停用。七賢國中目前約有1,377名學生。

Local Culture · 在地文化

From Qianjin's 'Seven Sages' to Gushan's art district.

Although its name and founding tie to Qianjin's historic "Seven Sages" area, Qixian's relocation to Gushan's art-museum district between 2009 and 2012 reflects Kaohsiung's shifting urban development over the decades. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
