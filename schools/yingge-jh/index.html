About · 學校簡介

Yingge's original junior high school.

Yingge Junior High opened in 1946 as Taipei County Yingge Junior Middle School, becoming the district's original and, for decades, its only junior high school. As Yingge's population grew alongside its ceramics industry, enrollment eventually peaked at 102 classes before settling to roughly 70 today. The school has operated a night supplementary school since 1973, extending educational access to adult and working students in the community.

鶯歌國中創立於民國35年（1946年），初名「台北縣鶯歌初級中學」，長期是鶯歌地區唯一的國中。隨著鶯歌陶瓷產業帶動人口成長，班級數一度高達102班，現今約維持在70班左右。學校自民國62年（1973年）起附設補習學校，為社區中的成人與在職學生提供進修管道。
