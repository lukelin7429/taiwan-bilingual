About · 學校簡介

Where lessons grow from local tea leaves.

Xinyi Elementary School serves the children of Toufen City, in the heart of Miaoli County's Hakka country. The school is best known for a curriculum that connects students directly to the region's famed Oriental Beauty (Dongfang Meiren) tea, a program distinctive enough to have been featured on public television. Alongside this hometown-identity focus, students take part in Miaoli County's student music and dance competitions.

信義國小座落於苗栗縣客家庄核心地帶的頭份市，服務當地學童。學校最為人知的是連結學生與地方特產「東方美人茶」的家鄉茶課程，曾獲公共電視專題報導。除了深耕鄉土認同，學生也踴躍參與苗栗縣音樂舞蹈比賽。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🍵 A Hometown Tea Curriculum

家鄉茶課程

A signature program ties students to the region's celebrated Oriental Beauty tea, building local identity through hands-on learning about the crop that shapes the area's economy and culture.

📺 Featured on Public Television

公視專題報導

The school's tea program was distinctive enough to draw coverage from Taiwan's public television network, spotlighting how a small local school connects classroom learning to community identity.

🎶 Music and Dance on the County Stage

縣級音樂舞蹈競賽

Students regularly take part in Miaoli County's student music and dance competitions, giving them a public stage for performing arts training alongside their academic studies.
