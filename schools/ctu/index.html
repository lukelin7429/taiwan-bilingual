About · 學校簡介

Six decades of vocational and technical education.

Chien Kuo Technology University traces its history back to October 1965, when it was established as Chienkuo Commercial Junior College. The school was renamed and reorganized several times over the following decades — becoming an industrial junior college in 1974, then a combined industrial-and-commercial junior college in 1992 — before being upgraded to Chienkuo Institute of Technology in 1999. It reached full university status in 2004, taking its current name, Chienkuo Technology University.

建國科技大學創校於民國54年（1965年）10月，初名建國商業專科學校。歷經數次改制更名，民國63年（1974年）改為建國工業專科學校，民國81年（1992年）改為建國工商專科學校，民國88年（1999年）升格為建國技術學院，最終於民國93年（2004年）改制為大學，定名建國科技大學。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🏭 Four Colleges

四大學院

CTU offers programs across four colleges — Engineering, Management, Design, and Living Technology — alongside humanities and social science courses.

🏆 National Skills Champions

全國技能競賽冠軍

CTU students placed first nationally in the 2022 International Skills Competition (國際技能競賽), a notable point of institutional pride.

🚉 A Campus Near the Station

鄰近火車站

The campus sits just southeast of Changhua Railway Station, making it a convenient technical-education choice for the Changhua-Yunlin-Chiayi region.
