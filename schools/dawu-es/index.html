About · 學校簡介

A small school with a big energy focus.

Dawu Elementary is located in Dawu Township's main village, the small administrative and commercial center of this coastal Southern-Link community. With about six classes and roughly 128 students as of the 2022-23 school year, the school punches above its size: in 2025 it received recognition as an exemplary school for promoting energy education, backed by a dedicated "Energy Science Classroom." Alongside this, the school offers after-school programs, environmental education camps, and special-education support for its students.

大武國小位於大武鄉的主要聚落，是這個南迴海岸鄉鎮的行政與商業中心。全校約6個班級、114學年度學生人數約128人，表現卻不容小覷：2025年因推動能源教育成效卓著，獲選為績優學校，並設有專屬的「能源科學教室」。此外，學校也提供課後照顧、環境教育營隊及特教支持服務。
