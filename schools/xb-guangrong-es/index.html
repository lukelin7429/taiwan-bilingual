About · 學校簡介

A riverside school rebuilt for a new era.

Guangrong Elementary began preparations in 1974 and opened its doors in 1975, formed by splitting off 47 classes from the long-established Sanchong Elementary School as the district's population continued to swell. From its earliest years the school built a reputation as a tennis powerhouse, fielding a competitive team that traces back to 1975, and it later added a gifted-education program. Between 2020 and 2024 the campus underwent a complete rebuild, emerging with new academic buildings, a self-run student cafeteria, dedicated tennis courts, and rooftop solar panels.

光榮國小於民國63年（1974年）開始籌備，隔年（民國64年，1975年）正式招生，是三重國小為因應人口持續成長而分出47個班級所成立。學校自創校初期即建立起網球重鎮的名聲，校隊歷史可追溯至1975年，之後並設有資優教育班。民國109年至113年（2020至2024年）間，校園進行全面重建，落成新教學大樓、自營學生餐廳、專用網球場，以及屋頂太陽能板。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎾 A Tennis Powerhouse Since Day One

網球重鎮傳統

Guangrong's competitive tennis team dates back to the school's founding year of 1975 and remains one of its defining strengths today.

🏗️ Rebuilt From the Ground Up

2020至2024全面重建

A four-year rebuild (2020–2024) gave the school new academic buildings, a self-run cafeteria, dedicated tennis courts, and rooftop solar panels.

🌟 A Home for Gifted Learners

資優教育班

Alongside its athletic strength, Guangrong runs a gifted-education program for students who need a more advanced pace.

🌉 On the Banks of the Danshui River

基隆河畔校區

The school sits in Sanchong's northern riverside area, directly along the Danshui River that shaped the whole district's development.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The City of Seven Bridges.

🌉

Guangrong sits in Sanchong's northern riverside neighborhoods, directly along the Danshui River — the same waterway that gave rise to the district's nickname, the "City of Seven Bridges" (七橋之都), linking it to Taipei across the water. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
