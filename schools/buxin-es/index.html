Busin Elementary School

布新國小

One of Budai's newer schools, built in 1992 to ease overcrowding, now home to a swimming pool and a nationally competitive children's orchestra.

布袋鎮較年輕的學校之一，創立於民國81年（1992年）以紓解學區擁擠問題，現設有游泳池及曾在全國賽事亮相的兒童管弦樂團。

About · 學校簡介

A newer school built to serve a growing community.

Busin Elementary School was founded on August 1, 1992, when its first principal, Tsai Chung-chen, arrived to lead the new school; the completed campus opened in August 1994 with 12 classes, built specifically to relieve overcrowding at older schools in the Budai area. Enrollment has since fluctuated to around 10 classes. The school added a swimming pool in 1999, and its children's orchestra has gone on to compete nationally, while students' artwork has been featured in a world children's art exhibition. A school lunch program began in 2000, with a kindergarten class added the following year.

布新國小創校於民國81年（1992年）8月1日，首任校長蔡崇珍到任；新校舍於民國83年（1994年）8月正式啟用，開辦12班，主要目的是紓解布袋地區舊校學區擁擠的問題，其後班級數曾波動至10班左右。學校於民國88年（1999年）啟用游泳池，兒童管弦樂團曾在全國賽事中演出，學生美術作品也曾入選世界兒童美術展。民國89年（2000年）開辦學校午餐，隔年（2001年）增設幼兒園班級。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏊 A Campus Swimming Pool

校內游泳池

Opened in 1999, the school's swimming pool is a notable facility for a small township school, supporting water-safety education in a coastal area.

🎻 A Nationally Competitive Orchestra

全國賽事管弦樂團

Busin's children's orchestra has competed at the national level, a striking achievement for a relatively young and small rural elementary school.

🖼️ Student Art on the World Stage

作品躍上世界兒童美術展

Students' artwork has been selected for a world children's art exhibition, reflecting a school that nurtures creative talent well beyond the classroom.

🏗️ Built to Relieve Overcrowding

紓解學區擁擠而生

Opening with 12 classes in 1994, Busin was purpose-built to ease overcrowding at Budai's older schools, making it one of the newer additions to the township's education system.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Serving Budai's Wenchang and Cenhai communities.

🐟

As a relatively young school built to serve Budai's Wenchang/Cenhai residential area, Busin complements the town's older schools while drawing on the same fishing-and-aquaculture-based local community for its student body — a reminder that even Budai's newest school is rooted in the same coastal way of life.
