About · 學校簡介

A century of "Rainbow Kecuo."

Kecuo Elementary was founded in 1921 (the 10th year of the Republic of China), marking a full century of operation with its centennial celebration in 2021. Known locally by the affectionate nickname "彩虹客厝" (Rainbow Kecuo), the school has built its curriculum around three pillars: food-and-agriculture education, reading education, and character education — a combination that keeps it deeply connected to the farming village around it.

客厝國小創立於民國10年（1921年），並於民國110年（2021年）舉辦建校百年校慶。學校在地方上有著「彩虹客厝」的暱稱，課程以食農教育、閱讀教育與品格教育三大主軸為核心，讓學校與周邊農村社區保持緊密連結。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 Food-and-Agriculture Education

食農教育

Food-and-agriculture education sits at the core of Kecuo's curriculum, giving students direct, hands-on exposure to the farming economy that shapes Yuanchang Township.

📖 A Century-Long Reading Tradition

閱讀教育

Reading education is one of the school's three curricular pillars, reflecting a century-long commitment to literacy alongside its agricultural focus.

🌈 "Rainbow Kecuo"

彩虹客厝暱稱

Known locally as "彩虹客厝" (Rainbow Kecuo), the school carries a distinctive, colorful community identity that has stuck for generations.

🎂 A Full Century of History

建校百年

Founded in 1921, Kecuo celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2021, making it one of the longest-standing schools in Yuanchang Township.
