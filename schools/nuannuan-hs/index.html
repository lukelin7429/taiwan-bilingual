Nuannuan Senior High School

市立暖暖高級中學

About · 學校簡介

From a borrowed classroom to a complete high school.

Nuannuan Senior High traces its origin to April 1967, when preparations began for "基隆市立第七初級中學" (Keelung Municipal 7th Junior High School), which initially held classes at Ren'ai Elementary. In August 1968, as part of the nine-year compulsory education reform, it was renamed "基隆市立暖暖國民中學" (Keelung Municipal Nuannuan Junior High School). The school became a full junior-and-senior high school in September 1999, taking on the senior-high mission it carries today for Keelung's Nuannuan District.

暖暖高中的歷史始於民國56年（1967年）4月，「基隆市立第七初級中學」開始籌備，初期借用仁愛國小教室上課。民國57年（1968年）8月，因應九年國教改制，更名為「基隆市立暖暖國民中學」。民國88年（1999年）9月，學校升格為完全中學，兼辦高中部，成為今日服務暖暖區學子的完全中學。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Nearly Six Decades of History

近六十年校史

Founded in 1967 as Keelung's seventh junior high, the school has grown steadily for nearly six decades to become a full senior high institution.

📐 A 1999 Upgrade to Senior High

1999年升格完全中學

The school became a complete junior-and-senior high school in September 1999, expanding its mission to serve older students in the Nuannuan community.

🏠 Borrowed Beginnings

借用仁愛國小起步

The school's earliest classes were held in borrowed classrooms at Ren'ai Elementary before it established its own dedicated campus in Nuannuan District.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Serving the historic Nuannuan District.

🏞️

Nuannuan Senior High serves the historic Nuannuan District, once a river-trade hub along the Keelung River, alongside the district's other schools that share this heritage. See the Keelung county page for more on the region.
