About · 學校簡介

A splinter school from Qigu's historic parent campus.

Shulin Elementary began in August 1961 as a branch of Zhuqiao National School and became independent on August 1, 1970, as "台南縣七股鄉樹林國民小學," under founding principal Zhang Dengyuan. It is one of two branch schools — alongside Sangu Elementary — that split off from the historic Zhuqiao school as Qigu's population grew in the mid-20th century. Today it remains a small rural elementary serving Shulin village.

樹林國小創校於民國50年（1961年）8月，最初為竹橋國民學校的分校，民國59年（1970年）8月1日獨立設校，定名「台南縣七股鄉樹林國民小學」，首任校長為張登淵。與三股國小同為二十世紀中葉七股人口成長時，自歷史悠久的竹橋國小分出的兩所分校之一。至今仍是服務樹林村的小型偏鄉學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 Split Off From a Historic Parent School

分出自竹橋國小

Shulin began as a branch of the century-old Zhuqiao Elementary, becoming one of two schools — alongside Sangu — that split off from Qigu's historic parent campus as the district's population grew.

🏫 Independent Since 1970

1970年獨立設校

Under founding principal Zhang Dengyuan, Shulin gained full independence in August 1970, becoming its own school after nearly a decade as a branch campus.

🏘️ A Small Village School

小型村落學校

Shulin Elementary remains a small, close-knit rural school serving the families of Shulin village in Qigu District.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Qigu's postwar school fragmentation.

🌾

Shulin's lineage as a splinter campus of the historic Zhuqiao school reflects the postwar growth and later fragmentation of Qigu's dispersed farming and salt-worker villages into separate school catchments, a pattern shared with other small schools across this lagoon-and-salt-flat district.
