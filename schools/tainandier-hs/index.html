About · 學校簡介

Central and southern Taiwan's first middle school.

National Tainan Second Senior High School — known locally as "Nan-er-zhong" (南二中) — was founded on May 11, 1914, under Japanese rule as "Taiwan Governor-General's Tainan Middle School," the second public middle school established in Taiwan and the first in central or southern Taiwan. It was renamed several times over the following decades: Tainan Prefecture's Tainan First Middle School in 1922, Taiwan Provincial Tainan Second Middle School after the 1945 handover to the Republic of China government — a date the school now celebrates as its founding anniversary, December 8 — Taiwan Provincial Tainan Second Senior High School in 1961, and finally National Tainan Second Senior High School in 2000. Originally established mainly to educate Japanese settler youth during the colonial period, it is today, alongside Tainan First Senior High ("南一中"), one of the two oldest and most prestigious public boys' senior high schools in Tainan.

國立臺南第二高級中學，在地人稱「南二中」，於1914年（大正3年）5月11日創立，日治時期名為「臺灣總督府臺南中學校」，是臺灣第二所公立中學，也是中南臺灣第一所中學。歷經多次更名：1922年改為「臺南州立臺南第一中學校」，1945年12月8日（今日校慶紀念日）由中華民國政府接收後改稱「臺灣省立臺南第二中學」，1961年改為「臺灣省立臺南第二高級中學」，2000年（民國89年）定名為國立臺南第二高級中學。創校初期主要以教育日籍移民子弟為主，如今則與南一中並列臺南兩所歷史最悠久、最負盛名的公立男子高中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Central & Southern Taiwan's First Middle School

中南臺灣首所中學

Founded in 1914 as the second public middle school in all of Taiwan, Nan-er-zhong was the very first established anywhere in central or southern Taiwan.

📅 A Founding Day That Marks a Handover

12月8日校慶紀念1945年接收

The school now celebrates December 8 as its anniversary, commemorating the 1945 handover from Japanese to Republic of China administration.

🎓 One of Tainan's Two Great Boys' Schools

與南一中並列臺南兩大名校

Alongside Tainan First Senior High ("南一中"), Nan-er-zhong is one of the two oldest and most prestigious public boys' senior high schools in the city.

🏛️ Over a Century of Continuous History

跨越日治與戰後兩個時代

Renamed five times across more than a century, from a Japanese colonial middle school to today's national senior high, the school's name changes trace the arc of modern Taiwanese history itself.

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the historic core of old Tainan.

🏯

As one of Tainan's oldest secondary schools, Nan-er-zhong's North District campus sits near the historic core of the old walled city, in an area shaped by the temple- and railway-era development that gave North District its distinct identity through the Japanese colonial and postwar periods alike.
