Yong'an Experimental School

永安實驗中學

A small coastal junior high near Yong'an Fishing Harbor that transitioned to experimental-school status in 2025 in response to years of declining rural enrollment.

About · 學校簡介

A small coastal school charting a new path.

Yong'an Experimental School was founded in 1968 as Taoyuan County Yong'an Junior High, renamed a municipal junior high in 2014, and formally converted into "Yong'an Experimental School" starting the 2025 school year after the Ministry of Education approved the transition in 2024. The shift to experimental-school status was a direct response to years of declining enrollment in this remote coastal community — the school currently has about 158 students across nine classes, grades 7 through 9. It sits roughly 1.5 km from Yong'an Fishing Harbor, Taiwan's only fishing harbor with a majority-Hakka population, and has had 16 principals since its founding, with the current principal in office since 2019.

永安實驗中學創立於1968年（民國57年），初名「桃園縣立永安國民中學」，2014年更名為市立永安國中，並於教育部核准後，自2025學年度（民國114學年度）起正式轉型為「永安實驗中學」。轉型為實驗學校，是因應這個偏遠沿海社區多年來學生人數下滑的直接對策——目前全校僅約158名學生，共9個班級，涵蓋國中七至九年級。校址距永安漁港（全臺灣唯一客家人為主的漁港）約1.5公里，創校至今歷經16任校長，現任校長自2019年上任。
