About · 學校簡介

A new campus built on an old folk tradition.

Chiaoyong Elementary School sits just inside Hengchun's historic West Gate, within the old walled town that anchors Taiwan's southernmost major community. The school describes its own history as spanning roughly six decades. Its most visible recent milestone is a new three-story, 34-classroom campus building completed in September 2021 after a two-and-a-half-year construction project costing NT$144.06 million — and its design deliberately incorporates the moon guitar (月琴), the traditional instrument at the heart of Hengchun's folk-ballad heritage.

僑勇國小座落於恆春古城西門內側，位於台灣最南端聚落的古城牆範圍內。學校自述已有約一甲子的歷史。近年最顯著的里程碑，是一棟耗資新台幣1億4,406萬元、歷時兩年半（民國108年2月至110年9月）興建完成的三層樓新校舍，共34間教室，其設計特別融入了恆春民謠傳統核心樂器——月琴的意象。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Inside Taiwan's historic walled town.

🏯

Hengchun's historic walled city, built in 1875, still stands with its original gates, and Chiaoyong Elementary sits right by the old West Gate — close enough that its new building's architects chose to honor the region's moon guitar (月琴) folk-ballad tradition in the design itself. Hengchun remains the main gateway to Kenting National Park, so the school's students grow up at the crossroads of old-city heritage and Taiwan's busiest beach-tourism corridor.
