About · 學校簡介

From agricultural school to complete high school.

Linyuan Senior High School traces its roots to 1935, when it was founded under Japanese rule as 林園庄立林園農業專修學校, an agricultural vocational school training farming talent for the coastal Linyuan area. Over the following decades it was renamed and restructured many times, moving between vocational, junior-high, and senior-high status as Taiwan's education system evolved. The school stopped enrolling senior-high students between 1968 and 1996, operating as a junior high before resuming senior-high classes. Today it operates as a complete school with its own junior-high division under one administration, and became a Kaohsiung City-run school after the 2010 county-city merger.

林園高中創校於民國24年（1935年）日治時期，初名「林園庄立林園農業專修學校」，為訓練林園濱海地區農業人才而設。此後數十年間歷經多次更名與改制，隨台灣學制演變往返於職業學校、國中、高中之間。民國57年（1968年）至85年（1996年）間曾停止招收高中部學生，僅辦理國中部；其後恢復高中部招生。現今學校為涵蓋國中部的完全中學，並於2010年高雄縣市合併後改為市立學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 Rooted in Agricultural Education

農業教育起源

Founded in 1935 under Japanese rule as an agricultural vocational school, the school trained farming talent for Linyuan's coastal community long before becoming a complete high school.

🔄 Nearly Nine Decades of Change

近九十年沿革變遷

The school has been renamed and restructured many times, tracking Taiwan's shifting education system — from agricultural vocational school to junior high to today's complete high school.

⏸️ A 28-Year Pause in Senior-High Classes

高中部曾停辦28年

Between 1968 and 1996, the school operated as a junior high only, before resuming senior-high enrollment — an unusual gap in its long institutional history.

🏫 A Complete School With a Junior High Division

涵蓋國中部的完全中學

Today the school runs both senior and junior high divisions under one administration, having become a Kaohsiung City school after the 2010 county-city merger.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Linyuan Senior High was founded specifically to train agricultural talent for the farming and fishing villages along Kaohsiung's southern coastline — reflecting Linyuan District's long identity as a coastal community built on farming and fishing.
