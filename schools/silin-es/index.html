About · 學校簡介

Nearly a century, traced through every name change.

Silin Elementary School's predecessor, a branch of Chaozhou Public School called “Chaozhou Public School Silin Branch,” was established on April 1, 1931, in Silin Village. It became an independent school in 1936 under the name “Silin Public School,” graduating its first class on March 16, 1937. It was renamed “Silin National School” in 1941, and came under Pingtung County jurisdiction as “Pingtung County Chaozhou Town Silin National School” on October 1, 1950. The school took its current name, “Silin Elementary School,” on August 1, 1968, under the nine-year compulsory education reform. With nearly a century of continuous institutional history, Silin traces an unusually well-documented lineage through multiple name changes, mirroring the broader arc of Taiwan's modern education system.

四林國小前身「潮州公學校四林分教場」創立於昭和6年（1931年）4月1日，設於四林村。民國25年（1936年）獨立設校，稱「四林公學校」，並於民國26年（1937年）3月16日舉行首屆畢業典禮。民國30年（1941年）改稱「四林國民學校」，民國39年（1950年）10月1日改隸屏東縣，稱「屏東縣潮州鎮四林國民學校」。民國57年（1968年）8月1日九年國教改制時，定名為「四林國民小學」。近百年的連續校史，經多次更名，紀錄格外詳實，也映照出台灣現代教育制度的發展軌跡。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Chaozhou, southern Pingtung's rail and market hub.

🚂

Silin Village sits within Chaozhou Town — southern Pingtung's major market town and transportation hub, historically built on the sugar industry and rail transport, including the Chaozhou Forest Railway heritage. Silin Elementary's own century-long history runs parallel to this broader regional story.
