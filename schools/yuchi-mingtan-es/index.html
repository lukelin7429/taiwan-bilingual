About · 學校簡介

A small school beside Taiwan's most famous lake.

Mingtan Elementary School was established in 1952 and today serves a small community of roughly 34 students across six classes in Yuchi Township's Shuishe area, right beside Sun Moon Lake. The school celebrated its 70th anniversary in 2022, the same year it was designated a partial-domain bilingual school, and it maintains international sister-school exchanges with Kamikushige Elementary School in Japan and Ludong Elementary School in Changhua County.

明潭國小創立於民國41年（1952年），目前約有34名學生、6個班級，服務魚池鄉水社地區——就在日月潭畔。學校於2022年舉辦建校70週年慶祝活動，同年也獲指定為部分領域雙語學校，並與日本上崛之内小學校及彰化鹿東國小締結姊妹校，展開國際交流。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗣️ A Partial-Domain Bilingual School

部分領域雙語學校

Mingtan was designated a partial-domain bilingual school in 2022, folding English-medium instruction into select subjects for its students.

🎉 Seventy Years by the Lake

建校70週年

The school marked its 70th anniversary in 2022, celebrating seven decades of educating children in the Shuishe lakeside community.

🌏 Sister Schools Abroad

姊妹校國際交流

International exchanges with Kamikushige Elementary School in Japan and Ludong Elementary School in Changhua give Mingtan's small student body an outsized global connection.

🏫 A Close-Knit Small School

小而美的校園

With roughly 34 students across six classes, Mingtan offers the close teacher-student relationships characteristic of Taiwan's smallest rural elementary schools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Gateway to Sun Moon Lake's Thao culture.

🛶

Mingtan Elementary sits beside Sun Moon Lake's Shuishe area (水社), the gateway village for the lake's boat piers and for the indigenous Thao (邵族) culture that has called this lake home for generations. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
