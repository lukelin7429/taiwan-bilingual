About · 學校簡介

From five hamlets to the township's largest school.

Masai Elementary was founded in 1914 during the Japanese colonial era as a branch of Lize Public School, known then as "Lize Public School Masai Branch," before later being formally established as "Masai Public School." Originally serving students from five hamlets, it has grown into the largest elementary school in Su'ao Township today. The campus sits at the southern end of the Lanyang Plain, backed by mountains and facing the sea.

馬賽國小創立於日治時期民國3年（1914年），前身為「利澤公學校馬賽分校」，後正式獨立設校為「馬賽公學校」。創校初期服務五個聚落的學生，如今已成為蘇澳鎮規模最大的國小。校園座落於蘭陽平原南端，背山面海。
