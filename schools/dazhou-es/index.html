Dazhou Elementary School

大洲國小

A public elementary school in Sanxing Township with an open, wall-free campus fully integrated into its rural farming community.

About · 學校簡介

A campus without walls.

Dazhou Elementary sits in Dazhou Village, one of Sanxing Township's rural farming communities. Its exact founding date is not confirmed, though anniversary yearbooks marking the school's 96th and 97th years suggest a founding sometime in the late 1920s. The school is distinguished by its open, wall-free campus design, which blends directly into the surrounding agricultural village rather than standing apart behind a fence. It is currently on its 17th principal.

大洲國小座落於三星鄉的農村聚落大洲村。確切創校年代尚待考證，惟根據校慶96週年、97週年紀念冊推算，創校時間約在民國10年代後期（1920年代末）。學校以開放式、無圍牆的校園設計著稱，直接與周邊農村聚落融合，而非以圍牆區隔。現任校長為第17任。
