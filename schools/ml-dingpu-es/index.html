Dingpu Elementary School

苗栗縣竹南鎮頂埔國民小學

About · 學校簡介

A small rural school with a freshly renovated track.

Dingpu Elementary School has over 40 years of history according to school materials, though the exact founding date is not documented in available records. It is a small school, with just over 200 students and staff combined, serving the rural Dingpu (頂大埔) hill community within Zhunan Township. The school recently renovated its running track with full-coverage PU material, upgrading its athletic facilities for students.

頂埔國小依校方資料已有逾40年校史，惟確切創校年份未見於現有公開紀錄。學校規模小巧，全校師生合計僅約200餘人，服務竹南鎮頂大埔丘陵地區。學校近年將操場跑道翻修為全面PU材質，提升學生體育活動設施。
