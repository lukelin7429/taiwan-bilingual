Juren Junior High School

居仁國民中學

Opened in 1946 on the grounds of a former Japanese-era school, and briefly Taichung's "First Municipal Middle School" before its present name.

About · 學校簡介

A postwar school on a colonial-era campus.

Juren Junior High School opened its doors on September 18, 1946 as "臺中市立初級中學" (Taichung Municipal Junior Middle School), making use of part of the former Japanese-era Meiji School campus — today the site of Datong Elementary School — as its first home. The school briefly ran a senior-high division in 1949, was renamed "臺中市立第一中學" (Taichung Municipal First Middle School) in 1957, and took its present name, "居仁," in 1971.

居仁國中於民國35年（1946年）9月18日創校，初名「臺中市立初級中學」，最初借用日治時期明治小學校（即今日大同國小）部分校舍作為首個校址。民國38年（1949年）曾短暫設有高中部，民國46年（1957年）更名為「臺中市立第一中學」，民國60年（1971年）正式更名為「居仁」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Founded on a Colonial-Era Campus

創校於日治校舍

The school's first classes in 1946 were held in part of the former Japanese-era Meiji School building, today the site of Datong Elementary — a direct physical link to Taichung's colonial-to-postwar transition.

🎓 A Brief Senior-High Chapter

1949年短暫設高中部

For a short period in 1949, the school operated its own senior-high division before settling permanently into a junior-high-only structure.

📛 Two Names Before "居仁"

兩度更名的校史

The school carried the name "臺中市立第一中學" (Taichung Municipal First Middle School) from 1957 until its 1971 renaming to "居仁," tracing the shifting naming conventions of the postwar decades.

Layers of West District's educational history.

Juren's founding on the grounds of a Japanese-era school building directly links it to the layered colonial-to-postwar educational history of central West District, a heritage shared with several long-established neighboring schools. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
