About · 學校簡介

A young school built for a growing Tianmu.

San-Yu Elementary School was established in 1993, a relatively young school for the district, created to serve the rapidly growing Tianmu area. Located on Tianmu East Road in the same immediate area as Tianmu Junior High, the school's vision is built around three core values: health, harmony, and learning (健康、和諧、學習).

三玉國小創立於民國82年（1993年），是士林區較年輕的學校之一，為服務快速發展的天母地區而設立。學校位於天母東路，與天母國中位處同一區域，並以「健康、和諧、學習」三大核心價值作為辦學願景。

Local Culture · 在地文化

In the heart of international Tianmu.

🌐

San-Yu Elementary is located in Tianmu, one of Taipei's most internationally-flavored neighborhoods, shaped by decades of foreign and expatriate residents living in the surrounding streets. See the Taipei county page for more on the region.
