About · 學校簡介

A firefly-watching spot near Cihu.

Baiji Elementary traces its founding to September 14, 1901, sharing its lineage with the Dakekan Public School that also gave rise to Daxi Elementary. It took its current name on December 25, 2014, with Taoyuan's municipal upgrade. The school sits along 環湖路 near Cihu (慈湖), and the surrounding area is a well-known spot for firefly-watching in early summer.

百吉國小創校可追溯至1901年（明治34年）9月14日，與大溪國小同源自大嵙崁公學校體系，2014年（民國103年）12月25日隨桃園升格直轄市定名為現名。學校位於環湖路上，緊鄰慈湖，周邊地區是初夏知名的賞螢火蟲景點。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A firefly-watching spot near Cihu.

✨

Located near the Cihu (慈湖) area, Baiji Elementary sits within Daxi's most historically and touristically significant zone, home to the Chiang Kai-shek mausoleum and memorial parks, as well as the seasonal firefly displays that draw nature-watchers to the surrounding hills each summer.
