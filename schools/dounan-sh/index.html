About · 學校簡介

A complete secondary school serving Dounan.

Yunlin County Dounan Senior High School was founded in 1946 and today operates as a complete secondary school, teaching both junior and senior high students in Dounan Town. The campus features a national-standard PU running track along with dedicated training facilities for weightlifting and badminton, plus multiple specialized science labs and computer classrooms supporting its academic programs.

雲林縣立斗南高級中學創校於民國35年（1946年），現為完全中學，兼收斗南鎮的國中部與高中部學生。校內設有國家標準PU跑道，以及舉重、羽球等專項訓練場地，並配置多間專科實驗室與電腦教室，支援學校的學術課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Complete Secondary School

國高中一貫學制

Dounan Senior High teaches both junior and senior high students on one campus, giving Dounan Town a continuous six-year secondary education pathway.

🏃 National-Standard Athletics Facilities

國家標準運動設施

The campus includes a national-standard PU running track and dedicated training facilities for weightlifting and badminton.

🔬 Strong Science Infrastructure

多元科學實驗室

Multiple specialized science labs and computer classrooms give students hands-on facilities to support their coursework across subjects.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Dounan's produce trade.

🌾

As the senior high serving Dounan — Yunlin's major rice and vegetable transshipment market town — many students come from farming families connected to the town's produce wholesale trade. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
