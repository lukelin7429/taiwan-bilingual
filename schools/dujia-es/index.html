About · 學校簡介

A tiny school in Taiwan's largest single-surname village.

Duga Elementary began in 1948 as a branch of Hougang Elementary and became an independent school in 1959. It is a very small school — roughly 88 students and staff combined, across 6 classes, on a campus of about 2 hectares surrounded by fish ponds and farmland. Duga village itself is notable as the largest single-surname settlement in Taiwan, overwhelmingly populated by families named Qiu. At the school's 60th-anniversary celebration in 2020, about 80% of the distinguished alumni honored were also surnamed Qiu, directly reflecting the village's single-clan makeup.

篤加國小創校於民國37年（1948年），最初為後港國小的分校，民國48年（1959年）獨立設校。是一所規模極小的學校——全校師生合計約88人，共6班，校地約2公頃，四周環繞魚塭與農地。篤加村本身以台灣最大單姓聚落聞名，村內居民絕大多數姓邱。學校於2020年舉辦建校六十週年慶祝活動時，受表揚的傑出校友中約有八成同樣姓邱，直接反映了村落的單一宗族結構。

What makes this school worth knowing.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Taiwan's Largest Single-Surname Village

Duga village is described as the largest single-surname settlement in Taiwan, with the community overwhelmingly made up of families named Qiu — a fact directly reflected in the school's alumni rolls.

傑出校友八成姓邱

At its 2020 60th-anniversary celebration, roughly 80% of the school's honored distinguished alumni were surnamed Qiu, underscoring the deep, multi-generational bond between the school and its single-clan village.

🐟 A Tiny Campus Among Fish Ponds

魚塭環繞的小校園

With about 88 students and staff combined across 6 classes on a 2-hectare campus, Duga is one of the smallest schools in Qigu, surrounded by the fish ponds and farmland typical of the district.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Inseparable from the Qiu clan's presence.

🐟

Embedded in Taiwan's largest single-surname village, Duga Elementary's alumni rolls and community ties are inseparable from the Qiu clan's multi-generational presence amid Qigu's fish ponds and salt flats — a vivid example of how tightly rural Tainan schools are woven into their single village's identity.
