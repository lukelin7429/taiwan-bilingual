About · 學校簡介

A newer school with a name from the sea.

Qinghai Junior High was established in 1992 to relieve growing enrollment pressure at Qingshui Junior High, making it one of the newer secondary schools in the district. Its name — combining "Qing" (清, from Qingshui) and "Hai" (海, sea) — directly echoes the coastal geography that defines Qingshui's western edge along the Taiwan Strait.

清海國中設立於民國81年（1992年），為紓解清水國中日益增加的就學壓力而創辦，是本區較新設立的國中之一。校名結合「清」（取自清水）與「海」二字，直接呼應了清水區面向台灣海峽、地處濱海的地理特色。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Built to Ease Enrollment Pressure

紓解就學壓力而設

Qinghai Junior High opened in 1992 specifically to relieve growing enrollment pressure at neighboring Qingshui Junior High, part of the district's postwar school-building wave.

🌊 A Name Rooted in the Coast

校名呼應濱海地理

The school's name directly combines characters for Qingshui and the sea, tying its identity to the district's coastal geography along the Taiwan Strait.

🆕 One of the District's Newer Schools

清水區較新設校

As one of the more recently founded junior highs in Qingshui, the school reflects the district's continued population growth through the late 20th century.
