About · 學校簡介

A close-knit school with an uncertain namesake.

Wengyuan Elementary serves a small community in Daliao District with roughly 365 students and 31 teachers, alongside an attached kindergarten for the youngest local children. The origin of the place name "翁園" is not fully documented, though it may share a toponymic root with a 1915 branch-school name recorded in the history of nearby Yongfang Elementary — a possible but unconfirmed connection, and in any case a distinct institution.

翁園國小服務大寮區的一個小型聚落，全校約365名學生、31位教師，並附設幼兒園服務當地年幼的孩子。地名「翁園」的由來並無完整文獻記載，但可能與鄰近永芳國小校史中1915年的「翁公園分校」共享地名字根——這僅是可能但尚未證實的關聯，且無論如何都是彼此獨立的學校。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A name whose roots remain unclear.

🌱

The exact origin of the place name "翁園" is unclear, though local history hints it may relate to an old shrine or garden — and it may share a naming root with Yongfang Elementary's 1915 "翁公園分校," a link that remains unconfirmed and, in any case, describes a separate institution.
