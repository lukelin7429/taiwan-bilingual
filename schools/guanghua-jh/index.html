Guanghua Junior High School

About · 學校簡介

From a girls' school to a founding root of three schools.

Guanghua Junior High was founded in 1956 as "新竹縣立第一女子初級中學" (Hsinchu County First Girls' Junior High School) under founder Cao Jinying. It became coeducational and took its current name in 1968, alongside Taiwan's nine-year compulsory education reform, and became a municipal school in 1982. Over the following decades, two of its branch campuses grew into fully independent schools: Nanhua Junior High in 1983 and Sanmin Junior High in 1991.

光華國中創立於民國45年（1956年），初名「新竹縣立第一女子初級中學」，由創校校長曹金英女士所建立。民國57年（1968年），隨九年國教改制而改為男女合校並更名為現名，民國71年（1982年）改制為市立學校。此後，學校的兩個分部先後獨立設校：南華國中（1983年）與三民國中（1991年）。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

👩‍🎓 Founded as a Girls' Junior High

前身為女子初中

The school opened in 1956 as Hsinchu County First Girls' Junior High School under founding principal Cao Jinying, becoming coeducational only in 1968.

🏫 Root School to Two Sister Campuses

分出兩校的母校

Guanghua's branch campuses later became fully independent schools — Nanhua Junior High in 1983 and Sanmin Junior High in 1991 — making it a founding root within North District's school network.

🏙️ A Municipal School Since 1982

1982年改制市立

The school transitioned to municipal status in 1982, part of Hsinchu City's broader development as it grew from county to city administration.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A founding thread in North District's school network.

🌳

Guanghua's history is woven into the growth of North District's school network — its former branch campuses at Nanliao and elsewhere grew into the independent Nanhua and Sanmin Junior High Schools, spreading its founding legacy across the district. See the Hsinchu City page for more on the region.
