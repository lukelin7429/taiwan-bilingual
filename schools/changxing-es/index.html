About · 學校簡介

One of Qidu's youngest schools.

Changxing Elementary School was founded in June 1993, with its first principal appointed on August 1 of that year, making it one of the newest schools in Qidu District. Its activity center was completed the following year, in 1994. The school's guiding vision centers on the values of happiness, sincerity, innovation, and gratitude.

長興國民小學創校於民國82年（1993年）6月，首任校長於同年8月1日到任，是七堵區較年輕的學校之一。活動中心則於隔年民國83年（1994年）完工。學校秉持「快樂、誠實、創新、感恩」的辦學願景。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌟 A Vision of Happiness and Gratitude

快樂、誠實、創新、感恩

The school's guiding vision emphasizes happiness, sincerity, innovation, and gratitude, shaping its approach to student character education.

🏗️ A Modern, Purpose-Built Campus

近代規劃校園

Founded in 1993 with its activity center completed in 1994, Changxing Elementary was built as a modern, purpose-designed campus rather than growing out of an older branch school.

🆕 One of Qidu's Youngest Schools

七堵區較年輕的學校

Among Qidu District's eleven schools, many tracing back a century or more, Changxing stands out as one of the newest, giving it a distinct, contemporary character.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Qidu, a rail and river junction feeding Keelung's port.

🚆

Qidu District's identity is defined by its history as a rail and transport junction, distinct from the coastal fishing culture found in the city's other districts. See the Keelung county page for more on the region.
