About · 學校簡介

An indigenous-education anchor on the city's edge.

Guofu Elementary sits in the Guofu community on the western edge of Hualien City, where it has built a strong reputation for indigenous-language education. The school offers certification programs in both Amis and Sakizaya, two of the indigenous languages spoken across Hualien County, giving students a structured path to fluency and formal recognition of their heritage language skills.

國福國小位於花蓮市西側的國福社區，以原住民族語言教育見長。學校開設阿美族語與撒奇萊雅族語認證課程，讓學生能有系統地學習並取得族語能力的正式認證，深化對自身文化根源的認識。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗣️ Amis Language Certification

阿美族語認證課程

Guofu offers a structured Amis-language certification program, helping students build formal proficiency in one of Hualien's major indigenous languages.

🌾 Sakizaya Language Certification

撒奇萊雅族語認證課程

The school also runs a Sakizaya-language certification track, supporting one of Taiwan's smaller officially recognized indigenous peoples in passing on their language.

🏘️ Rooted in the Guofu Community

深耕國福社區

Located on Hualien City's western edge, the school serves as an educational anchor for the Guofu community, closely tied to local indigenous family life.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A mixed indigenous and Han city.

🪶

Guofu's Amis and Sakizaya language programs reflect Hualien City's mixed indigenous and Han population, part of a broader pattern across the county seat where multiple indigenous communities live alongside long-settled Han neighborhoods.
