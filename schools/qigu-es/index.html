About · 學校簡介

Qigu's oldest school, now its administrative anchor.

Qigu Elementary was founded in January 1921, the first elementary school established in Qigu District. It celebrated its 100th anniversary around 2021, inaugurating a new digital-learning building as part of the centennial. The school runs a kindergarten and today serves as the administrative anchor for Qigu's smaller rural campuses. Since 2025, it has also absorbed the once-independent Longshan Elementary School as a satellite campus, after Longshan's enrollment fell to about 22 students amid the depopulation of the district's fishing hamlets.

七股國小創校於民國10年（1921年）1月，是七股區第一所設立的小學。約於2021年慶祝建校百年，並啟用「全成數位教育館」作為百年校慶的一部分。學校設有附設幼兒園，現為七股區內多所偏鄉小校的行政母校。自2025年起，龍山國小併入本校，成為「七股國民小學龍山分校」，起因是隨著漁村人口外流，龍山國小學生數已降至約22人。

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the center of Qigu's salt and lagoon country.

🦩

As the oldest and largest school in Qigu, this campus sits at the center of a district shaped by salt production and aquaculture, with lagoons and tidal flats that host the famous black-faced spoonbill wetlands. Its recent absorption of the shrinking Longshan satellite campus mirrors the broader demographic decline of Qigu's remote fishing and salt-farming hamlets.
