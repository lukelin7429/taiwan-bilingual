Yuanli Zhongzheng Elementary School

中正國小

Founded during the Japanese colonial era to serve Yuanli's inland communities, now a compact ten-class school in the township's rush-weaving heartland.

About · 學校簡介

Founded so children wouldn't have to travel so far.

Yuanli Zhongzheng Elementary School was founded in 1944, during the final years of Japanese rule, as 苑裡南國民學校 ("Yuanli South National School"). It was established to serve students living in Yuanli's inland ("內區") area, who previously had to travel long distances to reach the nearest school. Today the school remains a compact institution of about 10 classes, continuing to serve the same inland communities more than eight decades later.

中正國小創立於1944年（昭和19年），日治時期名為「苑裡南國民學校」，設校初衷是為了服務苑裡「內區」的學童，讓他們不必再長途跋涉上學。如今學校仍維持約10班的規模，八十餘年來持續服務同一片內區社區。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Built to Shorten a Long Walk

縮短內區學童上學路

The school was founded in 1944 specifically to serve children in Yuanli's inland area, who had previously faced long journeys to reach the nearest school.

📏 A Compact Ten-Class School

十班規模小校

Zhongzheng today comprises about 10 classes, maintaining a close-knit scale that reflects its origins as a neighborhood school for inland Yuanli families.

🌾 In the Rush-Weaving Heartland

藺草編織的故鄉

The school sits within Yuanli Township, Taiwan's famous home of the sanjiao rush used to weave the renowned Dajia/Yuanli hats and mats.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Serving Yuanli's inland communities.

🌾

Yuanli Township is Taiwan's famous home of the sanjiao rush (三角藺草) used to weave the renowned Dajia/Yuanli hats and mats, and Zhongzheng Elementary's community heritage is tied directly to this craft tradition. See the Miaoli county page for more on the region.
