About · 學校簡介

A century-long educational lineage.

Nankan Junior High's lineage stretches back to 1924, when it opened as "Nankan Vocational Supplementary School" for graduates of the Nankan-Kengzi and Luzhu public schools, one of the oldest school lines in the district. Under Japanese rule it became an agricultural specialty school in 1932, and in 1959 converted to junior-high status. It briefly grew into a combined junior-senior high in 2000 before splitting into independent junior and senior high schools in 2011, taking its current name in 2014. Today it enrolls roughly 1,400 students across 59 classes.

南崁國中的校史可追溯至民國13年（1924年），當時創立「南崁實業補習學校」，招收南崁、坑子與蘆竹公學校畢業生。日治時期於昭和7年（1932年）改制為新竹州立南崁農業專修學校，民國48年（1959年）轉制為初級中學。民國89年（2000年）曾一度發展為完全中學（含高中部），民國100年（2011年）國中部與高中部分別獨立設校，民國103年（2014年）更名為現名。學校現有59班，學生約1,400人。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A living record of Luzhu's transformation.

🌾

Nankan Junior High's century-long evolution — from a farm-village vocational school, to an agricultural specialty school, to a full junior high — mirrors Luzhu District's own transition from a rice- and tea-farming township into an industrial and urban suburb of greater Taoyuan. The school sits in Wufu Village, named after Wufu Temple (五福宮), the spiritual and historic anchor of the old Nankan settlement. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
