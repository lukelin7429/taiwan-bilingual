About · 學校簡介

Over a century of names and history.

Lumen Elementary traces its origins to April 1909, when it opened as "Lumasan Public School" — a four-year school with four classes, under first principal Hosokawa Sutejiro, a Japanese educator. The school was renamed several times through Taiwan's changing eras: to "Lumasan National School" in 1941, "Zhuqi Township Second National School" in 1946 after Taiwan's retrocession, "Lumen National School" of Chiayi County's Zhuqi Township in 1950, and finally to its current name in 1968. With over a century of documented history — including its school emblem, flag, and anthem across generations of principals and alumni — the school celebrated its 79th graduating class in June 2024.

鹿滿國小創校於明治42年（1909年）4月，初名「鹿麻產公學校」，為四年制、四班規模的學校，首任校長為日籍教育家細川捨次郎。歷經數次更名：昭和16年（民國30年，1941年）改稱「鹿麻產國民學校」；台灣光復後，民國35年（1946年）2月改為「竹崎鄉第二國民學校」；民國39年（1950年）改為「嘉義縣竹崎鄉鹿滿國民學校」；民國57年（1968年）始定現名「鹿滿國民小學」。學校擁有逾百年的校史文獻，包括校徽、校旗、校歌，記錄了歷任校長與校友的傳承，並於2024年6月慶祝第79屆畢業生。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📜 Over a Century of Lineage

逾百年校史

Lumen's school emblem, flag, and anthem have been passed down across generations of principals and alumni since its 1909 founding as Lumasan Public School.

🎓 79 Graduating Classes and Counting

第79屆畢業生

The school celebrated its 79th graduating class in June 2024, a marker of its long, continuous role in educating Zhuqi's children.

💃 Dance and Athletics

舞蹈社與田徑競賽

Students take part in dance clubs and athletics including track and field and jump-rope, alongside the school's nutritious lunch program.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for the historic Lumasan settlement.

🦌

Lumen Elementary carries the name of the Japanese colonial-era settlement of "鹿麻產" (Lumasan), reflecting the deep agrarian and forestry-era history of the Alishan foothill region — one of the oldest continuously operating schools in Zhuqi Township.
