About · 學校簡介

The only high school for miles around.

National Yuli Senior High School was established in August 1968, carved out from the senior-high division of the former Yuli County Junior-Senior High School. As the only senior high school in southern Hualien County, it serves as the sole option for local students continuing beyond junior high without leaving the region. Originally an academic-track-only school, it began adding vocational programs from 1971 onward — including Business, Home Economics, and even Stonemasonry and Quarrying, reflecting the area's historical stone industry — later followed by Accounting, Beauty Care, and athletics tracks, before becoming a comprehensive high school by 2000.

國立玉里高中創立於民國57年（1968年）8月，由原玉里縣立初高中的高中部獨立而來。作為花蓮縣南區唯一的高級中學，本校是當地學生升學不必離鄉背井的唯一選擇。學校最初僅設普通科，自民國60年（1971年）起陸續增設職業類科，包括商業科、家事科，以及反映當地石礦產業歷史的採礦科；其後又增設會計科、美容科與體育班，並於民國89年（2000年）轉型為綜合高中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Southern Hualien's Only Senior High

花蓮南區唯一高中

As the sole senior high school in the region, Yuli High serves students across a wide stretch of the Hua-Tung Valley who would otherwise need to leave the area for school.

⛏️ A Program Born From Local Industry

因應在地產業設立採礦科

The school once ran a Stonemasonry and Quarrying program, a vocational track that directly reflected Yuli's historical stone and quarrying industry.

🔀 From Academic Track to Comprehensive High

綜合高中轉型

Starting as an academic-only school in 1968, Yuli High steadily added vocational programs over three decades before becoming a comprehensive high school in 2000.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring the Hua-Tung Valley's south.

🌾

Yuli Township 玉里鎮 is a rice-farming and hot-springs town in Hualien County's Hua-Tung Valley, near the Tropic of Cancer. As the only senior high school serving this stretch of the valley, Yuli High draws students from surrounding farming communities and townships, making it a regional hub for education well beyond Yuli itself.
