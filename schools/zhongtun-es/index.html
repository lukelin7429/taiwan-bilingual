About · 學校簡介

A century of schooling on a bridge-linked islet.

Zhongtun Elementary was founded in April 1922 as a branch of Gangwei Public School, becoming an independent school in March 1946. It added a branch campus at Dacang Island in 1955, which itself became independent twenty years later, in 1975. The school relocated to its current site in 1969 and took its present name in 1968, during the nationwide switch to nine-year compulsory education.

中屯國小創立於民國11年（1922年）4月，最初為港尾公學校的分校，於民國35年（1946年）3月獨立設校。民國44年（1955年）增設大倉分校，該分校於20年後、民國64年（1975年）獨立設校。學校於民國58年（1969年）遷至現址，並於民國57年（1968年）九年國教實施之際定名為現名。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 Over a Century of History

逾百年校史

Founded in 1922, Zhongtun Elementary has served generations of students on this small connecting islet between Baisha and Magong.

🌉 A Branch That Grew Independent

分校獨立設校的歷史

The school's 1955 branch campus at Dacang Island became fully independent two decades later in 1975, part of a broader pattern of school growth across Penghu's scattered islets.

🔗 A Bridge Between Two Districts

連接兩地的橋樑地帶

Zhongtun Village sits on a small islet connected by causeway between Baisha and Magong, giving the school a distinctive in-between geography.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Zhongtun's tidal flats and basalt coastline.

🌊

Zhongtun Village occupies a small islet linked by bridge and causeway between Baisha and Magong, historically tied to Penghu's tidal-flat fishing traditions and dramatic basalt-column coastal geology. See the Penghu county page for more on the region.
