About · 學校簡介

A new school for a new district.

Nanxing Junior High School is one of two brand-new schools — alongside sister school Nanxing Elementary School — set to open for Taichung's 115 academic year (fall 2026). The campus covers roughly 4.46 hectares next to Nanxing Park, with a design theme built around water features and green space, at an approximate construction budget of NT$358.4 million. The site sits within the redeveloped Taichung Railway Depot (台中機廠) special district, marking the transformation of former industrial rail land into a new residential and school zone in Beitun District. As the school has not yet opened, it does not have an official website; information so far comes only from a Facebook page and city government announcements.

南興國中是與姊妹校南興國小同步興建的兩所全新學校之一，預計於臺中市115學年度（2026年秋季）開學。校地約4.46公頃，緊鄰南興公園，設計以水景與綠地為主題，工程經費約新臺幣3億5,840萬元。校址座落於原「台中機廠」都市更新特定專用區內，象徵北屯區由鐵路工業用地轉型為新興住宅與學校用地。由於學校尚未正式開學，目前尚無官方網站，相關資訊僅見於臉書粉絲專頁與市府公告。
