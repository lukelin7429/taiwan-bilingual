Baoxi Elementary School

保西國民小學

One of Guiren District's oldest schools, founded in 1915 and known for a century-old banyan tree that still shades its campus.

歸仁區歷史最悠久的學校之一，創校於1915年，校園裡的百年老榕樹至今仍為師生遮蔭。

About · 學校簡介

Over a century under an old banyan tree.

Baoxi Elementary opened in 1915 as a branch of Guandimiao Public School serving the Mazu Temple community, during the Japanese colonial era. It was renamed "Mazu Temple Public School" in 1920, "Mazu Temple National School" in 1941, and took its current name, "Baoxi National Elementary School," in 1968. The campus is locally famous for a century-old banyan tree, cited in newspaper coverage of Taiwan's most memorable schoolyard corners, and in January 2018 the school celebrated its 103rd anniversary with the dedication of a new school building.

保西國小創立於日治時期大正4年（1915年），初為「關帝廟公學校媽祖廟分校」。大正9年（1920年）更名「媽祖廟公學校」，昭和16年（1941年）改稱「媽祖廟國民學校」，民國57年（1968年）確立現今校名「保西國民小學」。校園內百年老榕樹是地方著名地標，曾獲報章報導為台灣校園最難忘的角落之一；民國107年（2018年）1月1日，學校舉辦103週年校慶暨新校舍落成典禮。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 A Century-Old Banyan Tree

百年老榕樹

The campus's landmark banyan tree has been cited in newspaper coverage of Taiwan's most memorable schoolyard corners, a living link to the school's 1915 founding.

🏛️ One of Guiren's Oldest Schools

歸仁區資深老校

Founded in 1915 during the Japanese colonial era as a branch school, Baoxi has operated continuously through multiple renamings for over a century.

103週年新校舍落成

Local Culture · 在地文化

A living memory of pre-HSR Guiren.

🌳

As one of Guiren's oldest schools, Baoxi Elementary's century-plus history and its landmark old banyan tree reflect the district's pre-HSR agricultural village character — a quiet counterpoint to the transit-hub growth transforming much of Guiren since the mid-2000s.
