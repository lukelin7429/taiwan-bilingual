About · 學校簡介

One of the oldest schools bearing the Da'an name.

Da'an Elementary was founded in 1929, during Japanese rule, as “Da'an Public School.” It was renamed “Tomita National School” in 1941, reverted to the “Da'an” name after Taiwan's 1945 retrocession, and took its current formal name in 1967. Enrollment peaked in 1991 at 115 classes; today it has shrunk to about 43 classes with roughly 1,225 students and 98 teachers, reflecting the district's declining birth rate. The school opened one of the area's first public kindergartens in 1987.

大安國小創立於1929年（昭和4年）日治時期，初名「大安公學校」。1941年改稱「富田國民學校」，1945年台灣光復後恢復「大安」之名，1967年定為現名。學校在1991年達到115班的巔峰，如今因少子化影響已縮減至約43班、1,225名學生、98位教師（2023年數據）。1987年，學校更創設當地最早的公立幼兒園之一。

早於「大安區」正式命名
