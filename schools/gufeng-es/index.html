About · 學校簡介

A mountain school reaching out to its village.

Gufeng Elementary is located in Gufeng Village, in the Luntian area of Zhuoxi Township — one of several small, remote schools that anchor community life across this mountainous, Bunun-majority district. The school has been the subject of outreach and rural mentorship programs that bring extra academic and enrichment support to students who might otherwise have limited access to it, a common and valued form of support for Taiwan's most remote schools.

古風國小位於卓溪鄉古風村的崙天地區，是這個以布農族為主、地處深山的鄉鎮中，眾多小型學校之一，肩負著凝聚部落生活的角色。學校曾是外部關懷與偏鄉陪伴計畫的服務對象，為學生帶來額外的課業輔導與多元學習資源——這對台灣許多偏遠學校而言，是相當珍貴的支持。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Zhuoxi Township, a Bunun mountain home.

⛰️

Zhuoxi Township is a remote, mountainous district of Hualien County near Yushan National Park, home to a majority Bunun Indigenous population. Its eight elementary schools — and no junior high — reflect villages small enough that most students continue their education elsewhere after sixth grade.
