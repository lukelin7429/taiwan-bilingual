About · 學校簡介

Shiding's founding school.

Shiding Elementary School opened in 1904 as a branch of Shenkeng Public School (深坑公學校), becoming an independent school as Shiding Public School (石碇公學校) in 1909. It is one of New Taipei's recognized "century-old schools" and the oldest school in Shiding District, tracing the district's education history back to the Japanese-era settlement centered on the Jingmei River crossing that grew wealthy on tea and camphor trading.

石碇國小創立於1904年，最初為深坑公學校的分校，1909年獨立設校為「石碇公學校」。是新北市認定的「百年老校」之一，也是石碇區歷史最悠久的學校，見證了以景美溪渡口為中心、因茶葉與樟腦貿易而興盛的日治時期聚落發展歷程。
