Da'an Elementary School

大鞍國民小學

A school with roots in the Japanese colonial era, now a nationally recognized environmental school amid Zhushan's hillside tea gardens near Shanlinxi.

About · 學校簡介

From a language training center to a national environmental school.

Da'an Elementary traces its roots to 1931, when it began as a Japanese-era "national language training center" during the colonial period. It was formally reorganized as an elementary school in 1968. The school built a strong reputation for environmental education, winning Taiwan Province's top environmental-protection school award in 1995 and being named one of the nation's top-10 environmental schools in 1996. Today it runs tea-arts classes in partnership with local tea producers, tying the school directly to Zhushan's hillside tea-growing heritage.

大鞍國小的歷史可追溯至日治時期1931年（昭和6年）設立的「國語練習所」，於民國57年（1968年）正式改制為國民小學。學校在環境教育方面表現優異，曾於民國84年（1995年）榮獲台灣省環保績優學校首獎，並於民國85年（1996年）獲選為全國十大環保學校之一。如今學校與在地茶農合作，開設茶藝課程，將教育與竹山山區的製茶傳統緊密結合。
