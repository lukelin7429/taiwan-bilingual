About · 學校簡介

Global citizens on a farming plain.

Gengliao Elementary School was founded on February 1, 1946, according to official school records — though a Wikipedia source suggests possible Japanese-era origins around 1941, the school's own stated founding date is 1946. Under the motto "Global Citizen, Joyful Learning, Technology Exploration," the school has been recognized as an "Outstanding Reading School" at the county level, and is active in art, music, sports, and academic competitions with a strong honors program. It also maintains strong parent and family engagement programs alongside environmental and health education.

更寮國小依校方官方紀錄創立於民國35年（1946年）2月1日，惟部分維基百科資料指出可能可追溯至1941年前後之日治時期，仍以校方公告年份為準。學校秉持「地球公民 樂學展能 科技探索」的校訓，曾獲縣級「閱讀績優學校優等」肯定，並積極參與藝術、音樂、體育及學科競賽，設有完善的獎勵制度。學校亦長期推動家長與家庭參與計畫，並結合環境與健康教育。
