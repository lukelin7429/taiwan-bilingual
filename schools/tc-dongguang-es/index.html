Dongguang Elementary School

東光國民小學

About · 學校簡介

A young school for a growing neighborhood.

Dongguang Elementary held its opening ceremony on January 3, 2001, built specifically to serve the Dongguang neighborhood as Beitun District's residential development continued to push eastward. The school has grown into a compact community campus of roughly 33 classes and 791 students, complemented by a small attached kindergarten.

東光國小於2001年1月3日舉行開學典禮，設立目的是服務東光里一帶，因應北屯區住宅開發持續向東擴張。學校如今已發展為約33班、791名學生的社區型校園，並附設小型幼兒園。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📅 A January 2001 Opening

2001年1月開學典禮

Dongguang held its formal opening ceremony on January 3, 2001, making it one of the youngest schools among Beitun's established elementary campuses.

🧸 A Built-In Kindergarten

附設小型幼兒園

The campus includes a small attached kindergarten alongside its roughly 33 elementary classes, giving families a single site for early education through sixth grade.

🏘️ Named for Its Neighborhood

以東光里命名

The school takes its name directly from the Dongguang neighborhood it was built to serve, part of Beitun's steady eastward residential growth in the early 2000s.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Riding Beitun's eastward growth.

🧭

Named for and serving the Dongguang neighborhood, the school reflects Beitun District's continued eastward residential expansion in the early 2000s, part of a broader pattern of growth across northern Taichung. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
