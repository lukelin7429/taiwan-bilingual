About · 學校簡介

A small school rooted in the land.

Longtan Elementary School serves the farming community of Dongshi Township on Yunlin County's coastal plain. The school has built its identity around an "ecological organic campus" program that brings environmental and agricultural learning into daily school life, supported by an English-speaking showcase area and a library linked to the national reading system.

龍潭國小服務雲林縣濱海平原上的東勢鄉農業社區。學校以「生態有機校園」課程作為特色重點，將環境與農業教育融入日常校園生活，並設有英語情境展示區，圖書館也與全國閱讀系統相連結。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 An Ecological, Organic Campus

生態有機校園

Longtan runs a dedicated ecological/organic campus program, weaving environmental awareness and hands-on agricultural learning into the curriculum.

🗣️ An English-Speaking Showcase

英語情境展示區

The school maintains a dedicated English-speaking showcase area, giving students a visible, everyday space to practice the language.

📚 Reading Connected Nationwide

全國閱讀系統整合

Its library is integrated with the national reading system, linking students' reading habits to a wider island-wide network.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Dongshi's coastal farmland.

🌾

Located in Dongshi, a quiet coastal farming township of Yunlin, the school's ecological/organic campus programming reflects the area's agricultural, rural character — a community where daily life still follows the rhythms of the land. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
