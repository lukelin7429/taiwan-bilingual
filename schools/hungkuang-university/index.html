About · 學校簡介

From a nursing junior college to a health-sciences university.

Hungkuang University was founded in 1967 (ROC 56) by Dr. Wang Yuxuan as a nursing junior college, reaching full university status in 2003. That nursing and health-sciences heritage remains central to the university's identity today. Hungkuang enrolls more than 13,000 students, including around 700 international students, and won the National Sustainable Development Prize in 2017.

弘光科技大學創立於民國56年（1967年），由王玉雪博士創辦，初為護理專科學校，於民國92年（2003年）升格為大學。護理與健康科學傳統至今仍是弘光的核心特色。學校現有逾13,000名學生，其中包括約700名國際學生，並於民國106年（2017年）榮獲國家永續發展獎。

What makes this university worth knowing.

🩺 A Nursing and Health-Sciences Heritage

護理與健康科學傳統

Founded in 1967 as a nursing junior college by Dr. Wang Yuxuan, Hungkuang has carried that health-sciences focus through its 2003 upgrade to full university status.

🌱 A National Sustainable Development Prize

國家永續發展獎

Hungkuang was honored with Taiwan's National Sustainable Development Prize in 2017, recognition for its campus and institutional practices.

🌏 An International Student Community

國際學生社群

Among its more than 13,000 students, roughly 700 are international students, part of a growing international presence on campus.
