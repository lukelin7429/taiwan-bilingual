About · 學校簡介

Born from disaster relief and overseas Chinese generosity.

Qiaoping Elementary School was founded in response to a severe flood that struck central-southern Taiwan in 1959, funded partly by donations from overseas Chinese communities authorized by President Chiang Kai-shek. The school was officially formed on June 24, 1960, with its first principal, Gao Hongyou, taking office on August 1. Due to overcrowding, it relocated to Chiayi's Zhuzhu redevelopment zone between 1982 and 1984, with the new campus opening on October 14, 1984.

僑平國小因應民國48年（1959年）中南部嚴重水災而設立，部分經費來自蔣中正總統核定運用的海外華僑捐款。學校於民國49年（1960年）6月24日正式成立，首任校長高鴻猷於同年8月1日到任。因應學生數過多，學校於71至73年間（1982–1984年）遷至嘉義市竹圍重劃區，新校舍於73年（1984年）10月14日啟用。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌏 Founded on Overseas Chinese Generosity

僑胞捐款興學

The school's founding was directly tied to charitable donations from overseas Chinese communities following the severe 1959 flood — a connection reflected in the character “僑” (overseas Chinese) in its name.

🌊 Born of Disaster Relief

水災後重建設校

Qiaoping was established as part of the community's recovery from a major 1959 flood that struck central-southern Taiwan, embodying a strong rebuilding spirit.

🏗️ A 1984 Move to Zhuzhu

遷校竹圍重劃區

Due to overcrowding, the school relocated to Chiayi's Zhuzhu redevelopment zone in the early 1980s, with its current campus opening in October 1984.
