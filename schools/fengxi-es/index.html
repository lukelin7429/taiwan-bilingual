About · 學校簡介

A school that started by borrowing classrooms.

Fengxi Elementary School was established in August 1989, with its first classes initially held at nearby Fengshan Elementary School while its own campus was under construction; phase-one buildings were completed in May 1990. The school marked its 30th anniversary in 2018 and today runs 53 classes in total — 40 regular classes, four art-talent classes, two resource classes, four kindergarten classes, and three special-education classes — with the art-talent program running continuously since 1992. Fengxi is a genuinely separate institution from Fengshan Elementary School's brief 1941–1945 wartime name, "鳳山西國民學校," despite the similar sound.

鳳西國小創校於民國78年（1989年）8月，初期借用鄰近的鳳山國小教室上課，同時興建自己的校舍，第一期校舍於1990年5月完工。學校於2018年慶祝創校三十週年，現有53班，包括40個普通班、4個美術資優班、2個資源班、4個幼兒園班、3個特教班，其中美術資優班自1992年持續至今。儘管名稱相似，鳳西國小與鳳山國小1941至1945年間曾使用的舊名「鳳山西國民學校」實為兩所毫無關聯的獨立學校。
