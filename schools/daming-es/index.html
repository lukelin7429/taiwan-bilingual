About · 學校簡介

A village school in the Middle Pile.

Daming Elementary School serves Sizhou Village in Zhutian Township, a rural community historically tied to rice and tobacco farming within the Liudui Hakka region of Pingtung County. For decades, the school also operated a satellite campus, the Fengming Branch, in nearby Fengming Village from 1981 until it was merged into the main campus in 2020, reflecting the area's evolving rural population.

大明國小服務竹田鄉泗洲村，這是屏東縣六堆客家地區一個長年以稻米與菸葉種植為主的農村社區。學校過去也曾在鄰近的鳳明村設有「鳳明分班」，自民國70年（1981年）設立，直到民國109年（2020年）併入本校，反映了當地農村人口結構的演變。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in the Liudui Hakka heartland.

🏮

Zhutian Township is the "Zhong Dui" (中堆, Middle Pile) of the historic Liudui 六堆 Hakka militia confederation, formed by Qing-era Hakka settlers from Guangdong and Fujian to defend their farming communities. Once known as "Dunwu" (頓物) for its role as a rice-trading depot, the township's identity remains deeply tied to rice and, later, tobacco farming — the backdrop against which Daming Elementary's students grow up.
