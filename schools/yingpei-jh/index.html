About · 學校簡介

A small campus with an outsized bilingual focus.

Yingpei Junior High School was founded in 1999 (ROC 88), though some sources cite 2009 and the exact founding year remains unconfirmed. Located in Yingpei Village on the northern edge of Nantou City, the school serves a growing residential district shaped by the county seat's expansion toward the Bagua Mountain range. With about 342 students and 40 teachers, Yingpei has been selected as a preferred foundation school for disaster-prevention planning, received 2025 environmental education excellence recognition, and holds the "Three Virtues Campus" (三品校園) designation, with a strong emphasis on bilingual education.

營北國中創立於民國88年（1999年），惟部分資料記載為2009年，確切創校年份尚待確認。學校位於南投市北緣的營北里，服務隨縣治向八卦山脈擴展而成長的住宅區域。全校約342名學生、40位教師，曾獲選為防災規劃優先推動基礎學校，並於2025年獲頒環境教育傑出表現肯定，同時擁有「三品校園」認證，特別重視雙語教育。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗣️ A Strong Bilingual Education Focus

重視雙語教育

Yingpei places particular emphasis on bilingual education as part of its "Three Virtues Campus" (三品校園) designation, standing out among Nantou City's junior highs.

🌱 2025 Environmental Education Recognition

2025年環境教育傑出表現

The school received 2025 environmental education excellence recognition, reflecting an ongoing commitment to sustainability education.

🛡️ A Disaster-Prevention Foundation School

防災規劃基礎學校

Yingpei was selected as a preferred foundation school for disaster-prevention planning, a role that reflects both its facilities and community trust.

👥 A Small, Close-Knit Campus

小而緊密的校園

With about 342 students and 40 teachers, Yingpei maintains a smaller scale than many Nantou City schools, allowing more individualized attention.
