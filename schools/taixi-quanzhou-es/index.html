About · 學校簡介

Eighty years in a Quanzhou-settler village.

Quanzhou Elementary School takes its name from the old Quanzhou village in Niucuo, Taixi Township, founded around 1943. The school celebrated its 80th anniversary with commemorative video productions marking the milestone, and its students have excelled academically — taking first place overall in a language arts competition, with four gold, one silver, and two bronze medals.

泉州國小校名源自台西鄉牛厝村的舊泉州聚落，創校於約1943年（日治時期）。學校以紀念影片慶祝創校80週年，學生在語文競賽中表現優異，共奪得4金1銀2銅，並拿下團體總冠軍。
