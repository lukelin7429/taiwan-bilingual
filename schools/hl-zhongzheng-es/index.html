About · 學校簡介

A postwar school that grew a family of schools.

Zhongzheng Elementary opened in September 1956 as the first new elementary school built in Hualien City proper after Retrocession in 1945, aside from the four older "Ming-" schools that trace to the Japanese colonial era. It began modestly, with just 9 staff members and 344 students, and has since grown to roughly 2,000 students. Its rapid postwar growth led the school to spin off two branch campuses — one that became today's Zhongxiao Elementary, and another that became Xinyi Elementary — both of which later achieved independence.

中正國小創立於民國45年（1956年）9月，是花蓮市區在光復後（除了歷史更悠久、可追溯至日治時期的四所「明」字輩學校外）新設立的第一所國小。創校初期僅有教職員9人、學生344人，如今已成長為約2,000名學生的規模。因戰後人口快速成長，學校陸續分出兩所分校——一所後來成為忠孝國小，另一所成為信義國小——兩校日後皆獨立設校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Downtown Hualien's First Postwar School

戰後花蓮市第一所新設國小

Founded in 1956, Zhongzheng was the first entirely new elementary school built in downtown Hualien City after Taiwan's Retrocession, apart from the older Japanese-era "Ming-" schools.

🌳 A School That Grew Into a Family

分校獨立成校

Zhongzheng's postwar enrollment boom led it to spin off two branch campuses that later became fully independent schools: today's Zhongxiao Elementary and Xinyi Elementary.

📈 From 344 to Roughly 2,000

從344人成長至約2,000人

Starting with just 9 staff and 344 students at its founding, Zhongzheng has grown into one of the larger elementary schools in Hualien City, with an enrollment of roughly 2,000 students today.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Mirroring a city's postwar growth.

🏙️

Zhongzheng's expansion into branch schools mirrors Hualien City's rapid postwar population growth as the county seat, a period when the city's downtown core rapidly filled with new residents and new schools were needed to serve them.
