Xiatan Elementary School

下潭國民小學

A century-old school on the Bazhang River floodplain, teaching food-and-agriculture literacy rooted in the rice- and crop-farming life of rural Chiayi.

About · 學校簡介

A century of schooling by the Bazhang River.

Xiatan Elementary was established on April 24, 1921, during the Japanese colonial era, as Tainan Prefecture Xiatan Public School. The school marked its 90th anniversary with a commemorative issue in the early 2010s, and today runs an attached kindergarten alongside its elementary program. It is known locally for a strong food-and-agriculture literacy curriculum that draws on the government-supplied pork program in school lunches, as well as bilingual and English enrichment for middle-grade students and international exchange activities.

下潭國小創立於大正10年（1921年）4月24日的日治時期，時稱台南州下潭公學校。學校曾發行建校九十週年紀念特刊，如今除國小部外，並附設幼兒園。學校以扎實的食農教育著稱，結合校園午餐的公糧豬肉政策，並提供中高年級雙語與英語增能課程，以及國際交流活動。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rice country on the Bazhang River.

🌾

Xiatan Elementary sits in a classic farming settlement along the floodplain of the Bazhang River (八掌溪), in the southwest corner of Chiayi County's Chianan Plain. The school's food-agriculture curriculum is a direct reflection of the rice- and crop-farming life that has long defined its rural catchment, connecting classroom learning to the fields just outside the schoolyard.
