About · 學校簡介

A well-tended campus in sock country.

Qiaotou Elementary serves the children of Qiaotou Village in Shetou Township, a rural district in central Taiwan best known nationwide as the "hometown of socks" for its concentration of sock-knitting factories. The campus covers roughly two hectares and is planted with seasonal greenery around modern facilities. The school's motto, "Vibrant Qiaotou — Enjoying Love of Learning," reflects its focus on character cultivation, balanced learning, health promotion, and a strong reading culture.

橋頭國小服務彰化縣社頭鄉橋頭村的學童，社頭鄉在全台灣以「襪子的故鄉」聞名，聚集了大量織襪工廠。校園佔地約兩公頃，四季植栽環繞著現代化的教學設施。學校校訓「活力橋頭·樂學悅讀」，強調品格陶冶、五育均衡、健康促進與閱讀素養的培養。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Shetou, Taiwan's sock-making town.

🧦

Shetou Township is celebrated across Taiwan as the "hometown of socks" (襪子的故鄉), home to a dense concentration of sock-knitting and textile businesses that have defined the township's economy for generations. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
