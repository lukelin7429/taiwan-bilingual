About · 學校簡介

The township's namesake school.

Erlun Elementary is the central elementary school of Erlun Township, sharing the township's name and located in Lundong Village, the seat of the township's administrative offices. Its precise founding date is not confirmed in available records, but as the namesake school of the township, it has long served as a focal point for the local community's education. It sits within a Zhang-Zhou Hoklo farming township that has shaped the identity of the area for generations.

二崙國小是二崙鄉的核心小學，校名與鄉名相同，座落於鄉公所所在地崙東村。學校確切創校年代目前尚無確切紀錄可考，但作為鄉治核心學校，二崙國小長期以來一直是當地社區教育的重心，服務著一個世代由漳州福佬移民塑造地方認同的農業鄉鎮。

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the center of Erlun's farming township.

🌾

As the flagship elementary school in the Erlun township seat, it sits at the center of a community shaped by Zhang-Zhou Hoklo settlers and their garlic and rice farming traditions. Generations of local families have worked the fields surrounding Lundong Village, giving the township its distinctive agricultural identity. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
