Xing'an Elementary School

興安國民小學

A riverside East District school planned by Mayor Chang Po-ya, sitting beside the well-known Bazhang River near Junhui Bridge.

由市長張博雅規劃設立的東區學校，緊鄰知名的八掌溪、坐落於軍輝橋畔。

About · 學校簡介

Planned by a mayor, built beside a river.

Xing'an Elementary was planned in March 1985 by Mayor Chang Po-ya as a branch of Xuanxin Elementary School, and was formally named and made independent on April 1, 1987. Its first campus building was completed on November 24, 1989, with students initially borrowing classrooms at Xuanxin Elementary while construction finished. A second building was completed in January 1994, followed by further campus expansion through 1997.

興安國小的設校規劃始於民國74年（1985年）3月，由市長張博雅提出，原為宣信國小分校。民國76年（1987年）4月1日正式命名並獨立設校。第一期校舍於民國78年（1989年）11月24日完工，在此之前學生暫借宣信國小教室上課。第二期校舍於民國83年（1994年）1月完工，其後陸續擴建校舍至民國86年（1997年）。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏞️ A Riverside Setting

八掌溪畔校園

The school sits by the well-known Bazhang River near Junhui Bridge, giving it a distinct riverside setting within the East District community.

🏗️ Built in Two Phases

分期興建校舍

Campus construction unfolded in stages — a first building in 1989, a second in 1994, and further expansion through 1997 — reflecting steady, deliberate growth.

🏛️ Founded by Mayoral Initiative

市長規劃設校

The school's founding traces directly to a 1985 planning initiative by then-Mayor Chang Po-ya, who proposed it as a branch of Xuanxin Elementary.
