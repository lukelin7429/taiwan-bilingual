About · 學校簡介

A school renewed again and again.

Xinxing Elementary opened its doors on August 1, 1966. In 1972 it switched over to a school-district enrollment system, aligning it with Taiwan's broader shift toward zoned public schooling. The campus has been steadily modernized since: a new teaching building was completed in 2000, and the school's Culture Building later underwent a NT$230 million renovation — investments that mirror Beitun District's ongoing residential redevelopment.

新興國小於1966年8月1日創校。1972年改採學區制招生，跟上臺灣公立學校劃分學區的整體發展趨勢。校園也持續現代化：2000年完成新教學大樓，其後文化大樓更投入新台幣2.3億元進行整修——這些投資恰恰反映了北屯區持續進行的住宅更新。

🏗️ A NT$230 Million Renovation

文化大樓2.3億元整修

The school's Culture Building underwent a major NT$230 million renovation, one of the larger single investments among Beitun's older elementary schools.

🏫 A New Teaching Building in 2000

2000年新教學大樓落成

A new teaching building completed in 2000 marked a major step in the school's modernization, decades after its original 1966 founding.

🗺️ Joining the School-District System

1972年改採學區制

In 1972, Xinxing switched to enrolling students by residential school district, part of a nationwide shift that reshaped how children were assigned to local public schools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A mirror of Beitun's steady renewal.

🏘️

As one of Beitun's older mid-century schools, Xinxing's multiple building renewals mirror the district's steady residential redevelopment since the 1960s, from its original founding through the major renovations of recent decades. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
