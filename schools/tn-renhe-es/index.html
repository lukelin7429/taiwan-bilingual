About · 學校簡介

From a satellite branch to an independent school.

Renhe Elementary School was independently established on February 1, 1960, as “Tainan County Rende Township Renhe National School.” It originated as a 1955 branch class (牛稠分班) and 1956 branch school (牛稠分校) of Wenxian Elementary School, becoming fully independent in 1959 before its formal 1960 designation. The school was renamed “Renhe Elementary School” in 1968 under nine-year compulsory education. It briefly ran a special-education (啟智) class from 1980 until it was discontinued in the 2002 school year, and was approved to add an attached kindergarten (middle/large classes) in 1999. The school's meals use only domestically sourced pork and beef, and students recently placed in the Tainan City Karate Competition.

仁和國小獨立設校於民國49年（1960年）2月1日，初名「台南縣仁德鄉仁和國民學校」。前身為民國44年（1955年）文賢國小設立的「牛稠分班」，民國45年（1956年）升格為「牛稠分校」，於民國48年（1959年）完全獨立，隔年正式定名。1968年九年國教實施後改稱「仁和國民小學」。學校曾於1980年起開設啟智班，至2002學年度停辦；1999年獲准增設附設幼兒園（中、大班）。校園午餐全面採用國產豬肉與牛肉，學生近期並在台南市空手道錦標賽中獲獎。
