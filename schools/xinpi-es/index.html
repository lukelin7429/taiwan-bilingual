About · 學校簡介

From a gentry's land donation to 78 graduating classes.

Xinpi Elementary began in 1947 as a branch of Taibao Elementary School, becoming independent on August 1, 1948, and was renamed Xinpi Elementary School in 1968 under the nine-year compulsory education reform. Its initial campus of 0.7949 hectares was donated by local gentry Xu Shilai; the school has since grown to 2.2815 hectares following a 2005 land purchase. Starting with just 7 classes, 350 students, and 11 teachers, enrollment peaked at 21 classes and 1,130 students with 26 teachers in the mid-1960s. The school earned a top ('特優') honor in the 114th academic year health-promotion program and has a strong record in writing, recitation, speech, drawing, English listening, and dance competitions, celebrating its 78th graduation ceremony in 2026.

新埤國小前身為民國36年（1947年）成立的太保國小新埤分校，民國37年（1948年）8月1日獨立設校，民國57年（1968年）九年國教改制後定名新埤國小。創校初期校地0.7949公頃，由地方仕紳徐仕來捐贈，歷經民國94年（2005年）購地擴充，現有校地達2.2815公頃。創校之初僅7班、350名學生、11位教師，於民國50年代中期達到全盛時期的21班、1,130名學生、26位教師。學校於114學年度健康促進計畫獲得特優殊榮，並在作文、朗讀、演講、繪畫、英語聽力、舞蹈等競賽表現優異，2026年歡送第78屆畢業生。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A history that mirrors rural Chiayi's changes.

🌾

Founded through a local gentry donation to serve the Xinpi farming settlement of Taibao, Xinpi Elementary's history of enrollment rising to a thousand-plus students and later settling smaller mirrors broader rural depopulation trends seen across many of Chiayi County's agricultural townships.
