About · 學校簡介

Everyday English, wrapped in local tradition.

Wenxian Elementary serves Luchang Village within Xiluo Town, and has built its identity around consistent, everyday learning habits. The school runs an "English Day" activity alongside a daily "10-Minute English Speaking" program, giving students regular, low-pressure practice with the language. It also operates a digital reading library and offers puppet-theater courses, blending modern literacy tools with a distinctly Taiwanese performing art.

文賢國小服務西螺鎮鹿場里，多年來以持續、日常化的學習習慣建立特色。學校舉辦「英語日」活動，並推行每日「十分鐘英語口說」課程，讓學生能規律且輕鬆地練習英語。學校也設有數位閱讀圖書館，並開設布袋戲課程，將現代閱讀工具與台灣傳統表演藝術巧妙結合。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Along the old trade routes of Luchang Village.

🌉

Situated in Luchang Village of Xiluo Town, the school shares in the town's deep-rooted soy-sauce brewing heritage and its position along the historic trade routes near the Zhuoshui River. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
