Guoling Junior High School

桃園市立過嶺國民中學

Founded in 2009 alongside the nearby Taoyuan International Baseball Stadium, serving one of Zhongli's newest planned urban zones.

About · 學校簡介

A 21st-century school for a fast-growing zone.

Guoling Junior High opened on December 13, 2009, after a preparatory office was established in August 2005. Its campus totals 25,543 square meters across six merged land parcels within the Guoling urban-planning zone. The school opened in the same period as the nearby Taoyuan International Baseball Stadium (2009), both landmark developments of the newly built-up Guoling area. Its school district spans into neighboring Pingzhen, Yangmei, Guanyin, and Xinwu districts, reflecting the large catchment of this newly developed zone.

過嶺國中於2009年12月13日開校，籌備處早於2005年8月成立。校地面積達25,543平方公尺，由六筆合併地號組成，位於過嶺都市計畫區內。學校與鄰近的桃園國際棒球場同期於2009年落成，皆為過嶺新興地區的地標建設。學區更跨及平鎮、楊梅、觀音、新屋等鄰近行政區，反映此新興計畫區廣大的招生範圍。
