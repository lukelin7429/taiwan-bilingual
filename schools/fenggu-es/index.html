About · 學校簡介

A tiny school beneath the peak.

Fenggu Elementary was established in 1956 to serve the small mountain community beneath Jiujiu Peak, one of Wufeng's most striking natural landmarks. With only six classes, it remains one of the smallest and most remote elementary schools in the district, offering an intimate, close-knit education to the children of the surrounding hillside villages.

峰谷國小創立於民國45年（1956年），服務九九峰山腳下的小型山區社區——九九峰是霧峰最引人注目的自然地標之一。全校僅六個班級，是霧峰區最迷你、最偏遠的國小之一，為周邊丘陵村落的孩童提供緊密而親近的教育環境。
