About · 學校簡介

A link in the Nankan Elementary lineage.

Jinxing Elementary was established in 1990, with construction beginning September 10, 1991, specifically to relieve severe overcrowding at Nankan Elementary School, which had grown past 2,500 students amid Nankan's rapid urban development. The project was championed by then-county-magistrate Liu Bangyou and local representatives. In turn, Jinxing spun off its own branch campus in 1997, which became independent as Guangming Elementary in 1999 — making Jinxing part of the same branching lineage that runs back to Nankan Elementary.

錦興國小創立於民國79年（1990年），民國80年（1991年）9月10日動工興建，目的是紓解南崁國小因南崁快速都市發展、學生人數突破2,500人所產生的超額問題，由當時的縣長劉邦友與地方民代積極促成。錦興國小後於民國86年（1997年）分出自己的分校，該分校於民國88年（1999年）獨立設校為光明國小——使錦興國小同樣成為南崁國小分校系統的一環。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A record of Nankan's population boom.

🏙️

Jinxing Elementary is a direct product of Nankan's late-20th-century population boom, driven by the area's growing role as a commuter suburb near Taoyuan International Airport and the Taoyuan-Taipei industrial corridor. The school exists specifically because the historic Nankan Elementary could no longer hold the neighborhood's children — and its own later branch campus, Guangming Elementary, shows how that growth continued into the following decade. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
