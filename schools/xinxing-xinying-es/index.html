About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school beyond the historic core.

Xinxing Elementary's exact founding year is not confirmed in available records. The school's motto emphasizes care, respect, gratitude, and innovation, and its cafeteria uses only domestically sourced pork and beef. It holds AA-level website accessibility certification, and serves as one of several small neighborhood elementary schools filling out Xinying's residential districts beyond the historic rail-hub center.

新興國小確切創校年份尚待考證。學校校訓強調「關懷、尊重、感恩、創新」，營養午餐使用國產豬牛肉，網站並取得AA等級無障礙網頁標章。學校是服務新營住宅區的多所小型社區國小之一，位於歷史悠久的鐵道商業核心區之外。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💚 A Motto of Care and Gratitude

校訓：關懷、尊重、感恩、創新

The school's stated values — care, respect, gratitude, and innovation ('關懷、尊重、感恩、創新') — are prominently emphasized on its official site as guiding principles.

🐷 Cafeteria Sourced at Home
