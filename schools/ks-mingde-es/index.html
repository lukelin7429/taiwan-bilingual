Mingde Elementary School

高雄市左營區明德國民小學

Founded in 1957 on land donated by the Navy, an elementary school that later gave up part of its campus to found neighboring Lide Junior High.

About · 學校簡介

Navy land, and a school that gave land onward.

Mingde Elementary School was founded in 1957, with the Navy donating the land and the city government funding construction — a partnership reflecting Zuoying's identity as a major naval district. In 1974, 2.4 hectares of the school's land were split off to establish a new junior high school nearby, which was briefly even named Mingde Junior High before a naming conflict led to its being renamed Lide Junior High the following year — leaving the two schools with a documented shared origin.

明德國小創立於民國46年（1957年），校地由海軍捐贈，建校經費則由市政府支應——這項合作模式正反映出左營作為主要海軍軍區的地方特色。民國63年（1974年），學校劃出2.4公頃校地，用以設立鄰近的一所新國中，該校甚至一度沿用「明德國中」之名，直到隔年因校名衝突而改名為「立德國中」，兩校自此留下明確的共同起源。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚓ Built on Navy-Donated Land

校地由海軍捐贈

The Navy donated the land for Mingde Elementary's 1957 founding, while the city government funded construction — a partnership typical of Zuoying's naval-town history.

🔗 The School That Gave Birth to Lide Junior High

催生立德國中

In 1974, part of Mingde's own campus was split off to found a new junior high, which was even briefly named Mingde Junior High before becoming Lide Junior High.

🏘️ Rooted in a Military Housing Community

位於自立新村

The school's address in Zili New Village reflects its origins amid one of Zuoying's many postwar military housing communities.

🏫 Nearly Seven Decades of History

創校近七十年

Founded in 1957, Mingde has served Zuoying families for close to seven decades.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Land given by the Navy, for the Navy's families.

🏘️

Mingde Elementary's location in Zili New Village, and the Navy's donation of its founding land, tie it directly to Zuoying's identity as a district built around military housing communities for Navy families. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
