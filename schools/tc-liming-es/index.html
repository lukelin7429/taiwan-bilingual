About · 學校簡介

Fifty years at the edge of two Nantuns.

Liming Elementary opened around 1974, positioned at the edge of the 七期 redevelopment zone — both literally and figuratively at the border between old rural Nantun and the new high-rise district growing up around it. The school recently celebrated its 50th anniversary with a performance by Taiwan's renowned Cloud Gate Dance Theatre.

黎明國小約創立於民國63年（1974年），座落於七期重劃區邊緣，正是舊南屯農村與新興高樓林立地帶的交界地帶，無論在地理位置或象徵意義上皆然。學校近年舉辦五十週年校慶，邀請台灣知名的雲門舞集演出。
