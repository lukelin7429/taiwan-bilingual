Fengming Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A branch campus with roots in a neighboring school.

Fengming Elementary School began as a branch campus split off from nearby Fenglin Elementary School in October 1951. Its exact date of full independence as its own school is not well documented, but its origins tie it closely to Fenglin, with which it shares the “鳳” (phoenix) naming heritage common to several of Siaogang's coastal-district schools.

鳳鳴國小最初於民國40年（1951年）10月自鄰近的鳳林國小分出設立分校。其正式獨立設校的確切年份缺乏明確紀錄，但其起源與鳳林國小關係密切，並與小港沿海地區多所學校共享「鳳」字命名傳統。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of the coastal district's shared phoenix heritage.

🐦

Fengming Elementary shares its “Feng” (鳳, phoenix) name with several neighboring schools and communities in coastal Siaogang, part of a naming heritage tied to the historic Fongbitou and Dalinpu villages once found along this stretch of shoreline.
