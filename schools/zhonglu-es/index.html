About · 學校簡介

A century-old rural school with a mayoral visit.

Zhonglu Elementary School traces its founding to around 1922–1923, during the Japanese colonial era, with sources varying slightly on the exact year. The school celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2022–2023, an occasion attended by Kaohsiung Mayor Chen Chi-mai. Located about 4 km from central Alian along a rural road, the school today runs a baseball team alongside taiko drumming and violin programs, giving students a mix of athletic and musical extracurricular options.

中路國小創校年代約在大正11、12年間（1922-1923年），確切年份因史料而略有出入。學校於2022至2023年間舉辦建校百週年慶祝活動，高雄市長陳其邁亦到場出席。校址位於距阿蓮中心約4公里的鄉間道路旁，如今設有棒球隊，並開設太鼓與小提琴等音樂課程，讓學生兼具運動與音樂的課外學習選擇。
