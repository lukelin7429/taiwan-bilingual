About · 學校簡介

A small school with a world-stage softball team.

Sinfong Elementary is a public elementary school in Nantou City best known in recent years for its student softball team, which represented Taiwan at the 2024 IBA Softball Little League World Cup. Alongside its athletics program, the school runs marine education and green chemistry initiatives, giving students exposure to environmental science beyond the standard curriculum.

新豐國小是南投市的一所公立國小，近年來以學生壘球隊聞名，該校壘球隊曾代表台灣參加2024年IBA世界少棒壘球錦標賽。除體育表現外，學校也推動海洋教育與綠色化學等特色課程，讓學生在正規課程之外接觸環境科學領域。

世界少棒壘球錦標賽

Sinfong's student softball team competed in the 2024 IBA Softball Little League World Cup, a standout achievement for a Nantou City elementary school.

🌊 Marine Education Initiatives

海洋教育

The school runs marine education programs, connecting students in landlocked Nantou County to Taiwan's surrounding ocean environment and marine conservation topics.

Sinfong's green chemistry initiatives introduce students to environmentally conscious science practices as part of its broader push into environmental education.
