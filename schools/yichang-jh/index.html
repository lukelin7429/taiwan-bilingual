About · 學校簡介

Built on donated land, serving the whole community.

Yichang Junior High was officially established in 1968, with construction beginning on April 28, 1969, and classes opening that August. Part of the school's original campus — 2.3 hectares — was donated by neighboring Yichang Elementary School, a gesture that reflects the close-knit cooperation among Ji'an's schools as the township grew. Beyond its regular junior-high program, Yichang runs a student wind-band program with county and national competition recognition, operates an evening supplementary school for adult learners, and maintains a dedicated career-exploration center for students planning their next steps.

宜昌國中於1968年正式設立，1969年4月28日動工興建，同年8月開學。校園原始用地中有2.3公頃是由鄰近的宜昌國小捐贈，這份情誼也反映出吉安鄉發展過程中各校之間的緊密合作。除了正規國中課程外，宜昌國中也經營學生管樂團，屢獲縣賽與全國賽事肯定；並設有補校提供成人教育，以及生涯探索中心，協助學生規劃未來方向。
