About · 學校簡介

Founded to close the gap in a growing suburb.

Zhuwei Senior High School opened in 1996 as Zhuwei Junior High School, created because students living in the rapidly suburbanizing Zhuwei area faced a commute of more than five kilometers to Tamsui Junior High. The school was upgraded to a complete junior-senior high school in 2012, and its 4.6-hectare campus now sits between the Zhuwei and Hongshulin MRT stations, serving a neighborhood transformed by Taipei's northern rail corridor.

竹圍高級中學於民國85年（1996年）以「竹圍國民中學」之名創校，起因是快速郊區化的竹圍地區學生，通學至淡水國中的距離長達五公里以上。民國101年（2012年）升格為完全中學，4.6公頃的校地座落於竹圍站與紅樹林站之間，服務因捷運淡水線而蓬勃發展的社區。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚇 Between Two MRT Stations

座落雙捷運站之間

The campus sits directly between Zhuwei and Hongshulin MRT stations, making it one of the most transit-accessible schools in the district.

🏫 From Junior High to Complete School

從國中到完全中學

Founded in 1996 as a junior high, the school was upgraded to a full junior-senior high in 2012 to keep pace with local student demand.

🌆 Growing With the Suburbs

郊區化的見證

The school's founding reflects Zhuwei's rapid 1990s transformation from farmland into a dense residential corridor along the Tamsui River MRT line.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Growing with a commuter suburb.

🚇

Zhuwei's growth from a quiet riverside village into a commuter suburb accelerated with the opening of the Tamsui MRT line, and Zhuwei Senior High's founding is a direct product of that boom. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
