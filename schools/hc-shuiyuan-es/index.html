About · 學校簡介

A rural pocket of Hsinchu City, given over to ecology and music.

Shuiyuan Elementary was established in 1977 within a designated water-source protection zone on the edge of Hsinchu City, an area that has preserved a small rural and farming landscape even as the city has grown around it. The school's curriculum centers on ecology — students take part in rice planting, vegetable growing, and chicken raising alongside local farmers. Its Chinese orchestra has won the national top honor eight times, and a teacher-student micro-documentary from the school won first prize in the Ministry of Education's inaugural Education Golden Image Award.

水源國小創校於民國66年（1977年），座落於新竹市邊緣一處劃定的水源保護區內，即使城市持續發展，該地區仍保留一小片鄉村與農作地景。學校的校本課程以生態為核心，學生與在地農民一同參與種稻、種菜與養雞等活動。學校國樂團曾八度榮獲全國賽事最高榮譽，師生共同製作的微紀錄片也曾獲教育部首屆「教育金像獎」首獎。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎻 An Eight-Time National Champion Orchestra

八度全國冠軍國樂團

Shuiyuan's Chinese orchestra has won the national top honor eight times, making it one of the school's proudest and most enduring achievements.

🌾 Rice Paddies as Classrooms

稻田即教室

The school's ecology-based curriculum has students planting rice, growing vegetables, and raising chickens alongside local farmers from the surrounding community.

🎬 An Award-Winning Student Documentary

師生紀錄片獲獎

A teacher-student micro-documentary from the school won first prize in the Ministry of Education's inaugural Education Golden Image Award.

💧 Set Inside a Water-Source Protection Zone

座落於水源保護區

Because the school sits within a protected water-source zone at the edge of the city, it has preserved a small rural, farming landscape rarely found elsewhere in urban Hsinchu.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Farming traditions, protected by policy.

🌾

Shuiyuan Elementary works closely with the neighboring Shuiyuan (水源里) and Qianjia (千甲里) communities, weaving local farming traditions and water-cycle and ecological education into daily school life alongside village residents. See the Hsinchu City county page for more on the region.
