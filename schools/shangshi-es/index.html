About · 學校簡介

A green campus with a competitive edge.

Shangshi Elementary School opened in 1985 along Section 2 of Xitun Road, in a residential pocket that grew alongside the district's mid-1980s suburban infill. The school brands itself as a "green, shaded community school," has recently earned national-level recognition in dance competition, and now offers bilingual education initiatives.

上石國小於民國74年（1985年）創校，位於西屯路二段的住宅區域，隨西屯1980年代中期的郊區發展而興起。學校以「綠意盎然的社區學校」自我定位，近年在舞蹈競賽中獲得全國級肯定，並推動雙語教育。
