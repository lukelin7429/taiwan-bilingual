About · 學校簡介

A name that carries Indigenous history.

Anli Elementary School was founded in 1916 as a branch class under Japanese rule, becoming an independent school in 1918. Its original wood-frame brick school buildings and flag platform have survived well enough to be designated a municipal historic building in 2013. The school's name, Anli (岸裡), directly preserves the name of the historic Ansali Pingpu Aboriginal community — one of the most prominent Indigenous settlements in the history of the Taichung basin.

岸裡國小創立於1916年（日治時期），最初為分教場，1918年獨立設校。原有的木構造磚造校舍與升旗台保存良好，於2013年獲指定為市定歷史建築。校名「岸裡」直接承襲歷史上「岸裡社」平埔族聚落之名——台中盆地歷史上最重要的原住民部落之一。
