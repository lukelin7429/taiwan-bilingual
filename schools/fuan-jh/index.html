Fu'an Junior High School

臺北市立福安國民中學

Founded in 1979 in the Sezi area of far northern Shilin, a low-lying riverside neighborhood shaped by the Danshui and Keelung Rivers.

創校於1979年，位於士林最北端的社子地區，深受淡水河與基隆河交會影響的低窪河濱地帶。

About · 學校簡介

Serving the Sezi riverside community.

Fu'an Junior High School's preparatory office was established on August 31, 1979, under founding principal Wei Ruijin. The school has had a long line of successive principals since then, including Zhang Xiangxian, who took over in 1985 and is noted for driving early campus greening and beautification efforts. Located along Yanping North Road Section 7 in the Sezi (社子) area, in the far northern and western part of Shilin District, Fu'an sits in one of the district's more industrial, low-lying riverside neighborhoods.

福安國中籌備處於民國68年（1979年）8月31日成立，首任校長為魏瑞金。此後歷任多位校長，其中民國74年（1985年）接任的張湘賢校長以推動校園綠化美化著稱。學校位於士林區西北端的社子地區、延平北路七段，是士林較為工業化、地勢低窪的河濱社區之一。

Local Culture · 在地文化

On the low-lying edge of the district.

🌾

Fu'an Junior High is situated in the Sezi Island area at Shilin's western edge, a low-lying peninsula formed by the confluence of the Danshui and Keelung Rivers, historically flood-prone farmland now being redeveloped — a very different geography from the mountain and temple-market core of Shilin.
