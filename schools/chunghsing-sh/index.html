About · 學校簡介

Nantou County's only pure academic high school.

National Chung-Hsing Senior High School was founded in 1957 (ROC 46) in Chung-Hsing New Village, the planned garden city built as the seat of the Taiwan Provincial Government. Today it is Nantou County's only "pure" senior high school — offering a purely academic track without vocational programs — and serves about 1,182 students across 39 classes in day and evening divisions. Since 2004, the school has run dedicated gifted classes in math/science and language arts, and its 3.8-hectare campus remains woven into the administrative heritage of Chung-Hsing New Village.

國立中興高級中學創立於民國46年（1957年），座落於曾為台灣省政府所在地、以花園城市規劃聞名的中興新村。如今是南投縣唯一的「純高中」，不設職業類科，目前約有1,182名學生，分為39個班級（含日間部與進修部）。自民國93年（2004年）起，學校開設數理及語文資優班。校地面積3.8公頃，深植於中興新村的行政歷史脈絡之中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 The County's Only Pure High School

南投縣唯一純高中

Chung-Hsing is Nantou County's only senior high school that offers exclusively academic tracks, with no vocational departments — a distinct identity among the county's high schools.

🧮 Gifted Classes Since 2004

數理與語文資優班

The school established math/science and language-arts gifted classes in 2004, offering advanced pathways for high-achieving students within the county.

🏫 Day and Evening Divisions

日間部與進修部

With about 1,182 students across 39 classes in both day and evening divisions, the school serves a wide range of students and schedules.

🌳 A Garden-City Campus

花園城市校園

The school's 3.8-hectare campus sits within Chung-Hsing New Village, a planned garden city built for Taiwan's provincial government, giving it a distinctly spacious, orderly setting.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in a planned garden city.

🏛️

Located in Chung-Hsing New Village — the former Taiwan Provincial Government seat built as a garden city — the school reflects the area's planned, administrative heritage, distinct from Nantou City's older Hoklo/Hakka core. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
