National Caotun Commercial and Industrial Vocational Senior High School

國立草屯高級商工職業學校

A national vocational senior high school training students in commerce and industry, rebuilt on a new campus after the 1999 921 earthquake.

草屯鎮國立高職，培育商業與工業專業人才，於921大地震受損後遷建新校區。

About · 學校簡介

From a branch campus to a national vocational school.

The school was founded in 1955 as a Caotun branch of the Taichung Provincial Commercial Vocational School, becoming an independent institution in 1959. In 1974, it added a machine-work department and took on its current name. The 1999 921 earthquake badly damaged the campus, and the school relocated to a newly built campus completed in January 2004. It became a national school in 2000.

學校創立於民國44年（1955年），最初為台中省立商業職業學校草屯分部，於民國48年（1959年）獨立設校。民國63年（1974年）增設機工科，並改為現用校名。1999年921大地震重創校舍，其後遷至新建校區，於民國93年（2004年）1月完工啟用。學校於民國89年（2000年）改隸國立。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏭 Roots as a Commercial Vocational Branch

商業職業學校分部起家

Founded in 1955 as a Caotun branch of the Taichung Provincial Commercial Vocational School, the school became fully independent in 1959, laying the foundation for its vocational identity.

⚙️ Expanding Into Machine Work

增設機工科

In 1974, the school added a machine-work department and adopted its current commercial-and-industrial name, broadening its training beyond its commercial origins.

🏗️ Rebuilt After 921

921地震後遷建新校區

Badly damaged by the 1999 921 earthquake, the school relocated to a newly built campus completed in January 2004, part of Caotun's broader post-disaster rebuilding.

🎓 A National School Since 2000

2000年改隸國立

The school became a national institution in 2000, reflecting its growth from a small provincial branch into a full-fledged vocational senior high school.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Training Caotun's craftspeople.

🪑

As Caotun's dedicated commercial-and-industrial vocational school, 草屯商工 embodies the township's long identity as a woodworking and furniture-manufacturing town on Taichung's doorstep — training generations of students in the trades that have long powered the local economy.
