Huajiang Senior High School

臺北市立華江高級中學

A Wanhua senior high with roots as a 1945 night school, today known for a strong sports program, 34 student clubs, and a campus pool open to the public.

About · 學校簡介

A long chain of names, one steady campus.

Huajiang Senior High School opened on April 20, 1945 as Taipei Municipal Wanhua Junior High, a night school initially housed inside Laosong Elementary School. Over the following decades it went through an unusually long chain of renamings — Laosong Vocational Supplementary School (1947), Taipei Municipal Wanhua Commercial Vocational School (1958), Taipei Municipal Wanhua Girls' Junior High (1961), Taipei Municipal Huajiang Girls' Junior High (1968) — before becoming coeducational as Taipei Municipal Huajiang Junior High in 1991 and finally Taipei Municipal Huajiang Senior High in 1993. Today the school runs 34 student clubs across five categories, fields notably strong wind band, baseball, and honor guard teams, and recruits specifically for women's table tennis, handball, softball, and taekwondo. Its campus sports complex includes a 25-meter, 8-lane pool and a 180-seat international conference hall that is also rented out for public use.

華江高中創立於民國34年（1945年）4月20日，初名「臺北市立萬華初級中學」，為僅設夜間部的補校，最初借用老松國小校舍上課。此後歷經多次更名：老松初職（1947年）、臺北市立萬華商業職業學校（1958年）、臺北市立萬華女子初級中學（1961年）、臺北市立華江女子國民中學（1968年），民國80年（1991年）改為男女合校並更名「臺北市立華江國民中學」，民國82年（1993年）再改制為「臺北市立華江高級中學」。目前全校共有34個學生社團，橫跨五大類別，管樂隊、棒球隊與儀隊表現尤為突出，並專項招收女子桌球、手球、壘球與跆拳道選手。校內體育設施包含一座25公尺8水道游泳池，以及可對外租借的180人國際會議廳。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏷️ Six Names, One School

六度更名的校史

From a 1945 night school to today's coeducational senior high, Huajiang has carried six different names across eight decades — a living record of postwar Taiwan's changing education system.

🏊 A Campus Pool Open to the Public

對外開放的校園泳池

The sports complex's 25-meter, 8-lane swimming pool and 180-seat international conference hall are both available for public rental, making the campus a community sports resource as well as a school.

🎽 Recruiting for Four Sports

四項運動專項招生

Huajiang specifically recruits student athletes for women's table tennis, handball, softball, and taekwondo, alongside standout wind band, baseball, and honor guard teams among its 34 clubs.

Local Culture · 在地文化

On the riverside edge of old Bangka.

🌊

Huajiang Senior High sits on Xizang Road near the Xindian River dike, part of Wanhua's historic riverside trading edge that grew up alongside the old Bangka (艋舺) settlement, also known as Monga. A few kilometers north, Longshan Temple (龍山寺) and the nearby Herb Lane (青草巷) remain the spiritual and commercial anchors of that original settlement — the same riverside trade that built Bangka also shaped the low-lying district where this school now stands.
