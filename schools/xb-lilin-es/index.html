About · 學校簡介

A castle for a second wave of families.

Lilin Elementary was established in 1995, with enrollment beginning in 1998, built to relieve overcrowding at nearby Liyuan Elementary School as Linkou New Town's second wave of growth took hold in the 1990s. The school is known for its distinctive castle-themed campus design and maintains a focus on arts and IT education.

麗林國小創立於民國84年（1995年），民國87年（1998年）開始招生，興建目的是為紓解鄰近麗園國小的就學壓力，正值林口新市鎮1990年代第二波發展浪潮。學校以獨特的城堡造型校園聞名，並著重藝術與資訊科技教育。

Lilin Elementary is a direct product of Linkou New Town's second wave of growth in the 1990s, built as a 'sister school' to relieve overcrowding at nearby Liyuan Elementary. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
