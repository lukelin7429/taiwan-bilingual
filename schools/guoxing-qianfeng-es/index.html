About · 學校簡介

A small school rooted in Hakka farm life.

Qianfeng Elementary is a small rural school serving Qiangou Village in Guoxing Township, one of Taiwan's few Hakka-majority mountain communities. The school runs active Hakka-language and cultural education programs, and gives students hands-on agricultural experience through activities like plum picking and traditional food-making, connecting classroom learning to the farming rhythms of the surrounding countryside.

乾峰國小是位於國姓鄉乾溝村的小型學校，服務台灣少數客家人口占多數的山區社區。學校積極推動客語文化教育，並透過採梅、傳統米食製作等活動，讓學生親身體驗在地農村生活節奏，將課堂學習與周邊農村環境緊密連結。
