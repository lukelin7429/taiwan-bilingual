About · 學校簡介

From a branch classroom to its own school.

Gongming Elementary began in 1960 as a branch campus of nearby Gongguan Elementary School, established to serve children in the Gongming area of Shalu District. It became a fully independent school in 1968, part of a broader mid-century pattern in which Shalu's rural school districts were subdivided to keep pace with population growth.

公明國小於民國49年（1960年）以公館國小分校的形式成立，服務沙鹿區公明地區的學童，並於民國57年（1968年）正式獨立設校。這段歷程反映了沙鹿地區在戰後農村人口成長下，學區逐步分割設立新校的普遍模式。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 From Branch to Independent School

從分校到獨立設校

Gongming Elementary started life in 1960 as a branch of Gongguan Elementary before becoming fully independent eight years later, in 1968.

🏘️ A Product of Rural Growth

農村人口成長下的分校設立

Its founding reflects Shalu's mid-century pattern of subdividing school districts as rural population grew, a story shared with several neighboring elementary schools.

📍 Rooted in the Gongming Neighborhood

扎根公明社區

The school takes its name from and continues to serve the Gongming area of Shalu District where it was first established.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A pattern of growth across Shalu.

🏘️

Like several of its neighbors, Gongming Elementary's split from an older parent school traces Shalu's mid-20th-century growth — as rural hamlets filled with families, one classroom became a branch, and one branch eventually became a school of its own.
