Datong Elementary School

大同國民小學

One of Taiwan's oldest schools, founded in 1899 — its front building is a registered Taichung historic building, unique among local elementary campuses.

About · 學校簡介

Over 120 years at the heart of West District.

Datong Elementary School was founded in 1899 as "臺中小學校" (Taichū Elementary School), during the early years of Japanese colonial rule, making it one of Taiwan's oldest continuously operating schools. In 2004, its front building was registered as a Taichung historic building — the only elementary campus in the local area with such a designation, a recognition of its architectural and historical significance.

大同國小創立於1899年（日治初期），初名「臺中小學校」，是台灣歷史最悠久、持續辦學至今的學校之一。民國93年（2004年），其前棟校舍登錄為台中市歷史建築，是當地唯一獲此殊榮的國小校園，肯定了其建築與歷史價值。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ A Registered Historic Building

登錄歷史建築

The school's front building was registered as a Taichung historic building in 2004 — the only elementary campus locally to hold this designation.

📜 More Than a Century of History

120餘年校史

Founded in 1899 as "臺中小學校," Datong is one of Taiwan's oldest schools, with more than 120 years of continuous operation through colonial and postwar eras alike.

🏫 A Cradle for Later Schools

孕育後世學校的搖籃

Part of the historic Datong campus once housed the earliest classes of Juren Junior High in 1946, linking this century-old elementary school directly to the founding of a neighboring junior high.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A living link to Japanese-era Taichū City.

🏯

As a 120+ year-old former "Meiji Elementary School," Datong is a living link to West District's identity as the administrative and educational heart of Japanese-era Taichū City. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
