About · 學校簡介

A name reinterpreted for a growing district.

Shuyi Elementary opened in 2002 to relieve overcrowding at Heping, Xinyi, and Guoguang elementary schools amid South District's rapid growth. Named after Shuyi Village, the school's name has since been reinterpreted locally as meaning "cultivating people, upholding righteousness." It runs a dedicated bilingual program alongside a traditional Chinese orchestra.

樹義國民小學於民國91年（2002年）創立，為紓解南區快速成長期間和平、信義、國光等鄰近國小的擁擠壓力而設。校名取自樹義里，地方後來將其詮釋為「樹人立義」之意。學校設有專屬雙語課程，並成立國樂團。
