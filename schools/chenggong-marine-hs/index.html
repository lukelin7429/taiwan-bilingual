National Chenggong Commercial and Marine Products Vocational School

國立成功商業水產職業學校

The only senior high school on this stretch of Taitung's east coast, built around the region's identity as one of Taiwan's great tuna and billfish fishing ports.

臺東海岸線這一段唯一的高中職學校，扎根於這裡身為台灣鮪魚與旗魚重要漁港的產業傳統。

About · 學校簡介

The only high school on this coast.

The school's preparatory office was established in 1988, and it formally opened in 1990 as a provincial vocational school, becoming a national school in 2000 and converting to a comprehensive high school in 1997. It stands as the only senior high school east of the Central and Coastal Mountain Range along this section of coastline, giving students from Chenggong and its neighboring townships a place to continue their education without leaving the region. The campus spans roughly 6.8 hectares and originally offered five departments, including a branch on Orchid Island focused on aquaculture and marine product processing.

學校籌備處成立於民國77年（1988年），民國79年（1990年）正式成立為省立職業學校，民國89年（2000年）改隸國立，民國86年（1997年）並改制為綜合高中。作為這段海岸線唯一的高中職，學校讓成功鎮及鄰近鄉鎮的學子得以就近升學。校地約6.8公頃，創校初期設有五個科別，其中一科曾在蘭嶼設有分部，專攻水產養殖與水產加工。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎣 Rooted in the Fishing Industry

扎根漁業

The school's historic strength in aquaculture and marine product programs directly reflects Chenggong's identity as one of Taiwan's major tuna and billfish fishing ports, long known by its older name, Xingang, or "New Harbor."

🛶 A Canoe and Indigenous Dance Club

獨木舟社與原住民舞蹈社

Alongside its academic departments, the school runs a canoe club and an Indigenous dance club, connecting student life to the coastal and Amis Indigenous culture of the surrounding region.

⚾ A Baseball Team

棒球隊

The school fields a baseball team, part of a broader athletics tradition that runs through many schools along this section of the Taitung coast.

🏠 On-Campus Dormitories

學生宿舍

Because it draws students from a wide, sparsely populated coastal catchment area, the school provides dormitories so students from farther townships can board on campus.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Chenggong, a fishing port town.

🐟

Chenggong Town was long known as Xingang (新港, "New Harbor") before being renamed in 1946, and its fishing harbor, completed in 1932, remains one of the largest on Taiwan's east coast, especially prominent for tuna and billfish. The town is also home to a substantial Indigenous Amis population with deep ties to the sea.
