About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school known for its playground.

Qiaode Elementary School serves the children of Fangliao Township in Pingtung County. The school is best known locally for its inclusive, universal-design playground (共融遊戲場) — built so that children of all abilities can play together — which has become a recognized feature and small local attraction within the township.

僑德國小服務屏東縣枋寮鄉的學童。校內設有共融遊戲場，讓不同能力的孩子都能一起玩耍，是地方上頗具知名度的特色設施。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛝 An Inclusive Playground

共融遊戲場

The school's universal-design playground is built so that children of all abilities can play together, and has become a recognized local feature within Fangliao Township.

🏫 A Neighborhood Elementary School

社區型國小

As one of several elementary schools serving Fangliao Township, Qiaode Elementary anchors its immediate neighborhood, giving local children a nearby place to learn and grow.

🐟 Rooted in Fangliao's Coastal Economy

扎根枋寮漁鄉

Students here grow up alongside Fangliao's fishing port and aquaculture ponds, part of a township economy built on grouper, shrimp, and other high-value seafood.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fangliao, a fishing and aquaculture township.

🐟

Fangliao Township is known for its fishing port (枋寮漁港) and extensive aquaculture ponds raising grouper, whiteleg shrimp, and other high-value seafood, alongside mango and wax-apple (蓮霧) orchards along the coastal plain. It is also the starting point of the scenic South-Link Railway toward Kenting — giving Qiaode Elementary's students a childhood shaped by both sea and rail.
