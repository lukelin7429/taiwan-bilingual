About · 學校簡介

Six arts, fragrance of books.

Liumei Elementary School traces back to April 1919, when it was established as "Liudouwei Branch School," a branch of Suantou Public School, during the Japanese colonial era; it became a formally recognized school in 1920. Under the motto "Six Arts, Fragrance of Books, Beautiful Childhood Heart," the school emphasizes a classical "six arts" curriculum concept alongside a strong reading culture. Students use digital learning platforms including PaGamO, Junyi Academy, and LearnMode, and have had articles published in local newspapers while winning awards in English proficiency, calligraphy, and reading contests.

六美國小校史可追溯至大正8年（1919年）4月創立的「六斗尾分校」，隸屬蒜頭公學校，屬日治時期學制；於1920年正式立校。學校秉持「六藝書香 美麗童心」的校訓，強調傳統六藝精神與扎實的閱讀文化，並運用因材網、均一教育平台、學習吧等數位學習平台。學生作品曾刊登於地方報紙，並在英語能力、書法與閱讀競賽中屢獲獎項。

As a former branch of Suantou's public school system, Liumei's history mirrors Liujiao Township's rice- and vegetable-farming village network that grew up around Suantou's commercial center. See the Chiayi county page for more on the region.
