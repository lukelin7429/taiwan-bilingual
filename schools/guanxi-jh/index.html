About · 學校簡介

From seven borrowed classrooms to Guanxi's main junior high.

Guanxi Junior High opened in August 1955 as a branch of Xinpu Junior High, under founding principal Chen Cong-jie. It started with just seven classes and 355 students, borrowing classroom space from neighboring Guanxi Elementary School before becoming fully independent in 1956 and taking its current name in 1968. The school grew steadily, reaching 29 classes by 1968 and building its own athletics track, and today runs an arts talent class alongside a library named "Pine Ridge Study" (松嶺書坊).

關西國中創立於1955年8月，最初為新埔國中分校，首任校長為陳琮傑。創校時僅有7個班、355名學生，借用鄰近的關西國小教室上課，於1956年正式獨立設校，1968年更為現名。學校穩步成長，1968年已擴增至29班並建有自己的操場，現設有美術資優班，並設有名為「松嶺書坊」的圖書館。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Built From Borrowed Classrooms

從借用教室起步

The school opened with only seven classes and 355 students, borrowing classroom space from neighboring Guanxi Elementary before growing into an independent campus with its own athletics track.

🎨 An Arts Talent Class

美術資優班

A dedicated arts talent program gives students with artistic ability a specialized track alongside the general academic curriculum.

📖 Pine Ridge Study Library

松嶺書坊

The school's library carries the evocative name "Pine Ridge Study" (松嶺書坊), reflecting the hillside setting of Guanxi Township.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Heart of the historic old town.

🏮

As Guanxi's principal junior high, it draws students from the historic Hakka old town and surrounding farming villages known for persimmons and red tea.
