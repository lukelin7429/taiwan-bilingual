About · 學校簡介

A brand-new campus built to green standards.

Jiafeng Elementary was approved in 2019, broke ground in May 2021, opened its first phase in August 2022, and was fully completed in November 2024. It holds the distinction of being Hsinchu County's first Diamond-level green building school campus — the highest tier in Taiwan's green building certification system. The school was built specifically to relieve chronic overcrowding in existing Zhubei schools, with a campus spanning about 2.3 hectares and a construction cost of roughly NT$1.27 billion.

嘉豐國小於2019年奉准設立，2021年5月動工，2022年8月首期啟用，2024年11月全面完工。學校是新竹縣第一所鑽石級綠建築學校——台灣綠建築標章制度中的最高等級。學校興建的主要目的是紓解竹北既有學校長期額滿的問題，校地約2.3公頃，建設經費約新台幣12.7億元。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💎 A Diamond-Level Green Campus

全縣首座鑽石級綠建築

Jiafeng is Hsinchu County's first Diamond-level green building school — the top tier of Taiwan's green building certification, reflecting an ambitious sustainability standard.

💰 A NT$1.27 Billion Investment

耗資12.7億元興建

The roughly NT$1.27 billion construction cost across a 2.3-hectare campus reflects the scale of investment Hsinchu County has made to keep pace with Zhubei's growth.

🏗️ Purpose-Built to Relieve Overcrowding

紓解學校額滿問題

The school exists specifically to ease chronic overcrowding at other Zhubei elementary schools, absorbing demand from the city's fast-growing population.
