Zhongshan Junior High School

中山國民中學

The alma mater of Olympic badminton doubles champions Wang Chi-lin and Lee Yang, whose gold-medal run is honored in the campus's "Lin-Yang Hall."

About · 學校簡介

Home to Olympic champions.

Zhongshan Junior High opened on August 1, 1978, after a preparatory office was established the previous year under founding director Wang Xuepin. The school sits beside the MRT Zhongshan Junior High Station on the Wenhu (Brown) Line, in the residential heart of Songshan built up after Taiwan's 1968 nine-year compulsory education reform. New campus buildings completed between 2018 and 2021 — named "迎曦樓," "御風樓," "寰宇樓," and "捷思樓" — modernized the school's facilities.

中山國中於民國67年（1978年）8月1日創校，前一年（民國66年）即由首任主任王學平成立籌備處。學校緊鄰捷運文湖線中山國中站，位於松山區因民國57年（1968年）九年國教改革而發展起來的住宅核心地帶。民國107年至110年（2018–2021年）間陸續完工的「迎曦樓」、「御風樓」、「寰宇樓」與「捷思樓」，為校園帶來全新面貌。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏸 Home of "Lin-Yang" Olympic Gold

麟洋配的母校

Zhongshan is the alma mater of Wang Chi-lin and Lee Yang, who won back-to-back Olympic badminton doubles golds at Tokyo 2020 and Paris 2024 — honored in the campus's "Lin-Yang Hall" badminton gym, opened in 2021.

🛣️ A Street Named for Its Champions

麟洋路命名紀念

In 2024, the lane in front of the school was given the honorary alias "麟洋路" (Lin-Yang Road) to commemorate the pair's Olympic record.

🚇 Steps from the MRT

緊鄰捷運站

The campus sits directly beside MRT Zhongshan Junior High Station on the Wenhu (Brown) Line, making it one of the most accessible schools in Songshan.

🏗️ A Freshly Rebuilt Campus

全新校舍

Four new buildings completed between 2018 and 2021 gave the school modern classrooms and facilities, including the badminton hall named for its Olympic alumni.
