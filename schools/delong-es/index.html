Delong Elementary School

德龍國民小學

A small rural school known for a music tradition in which nearly half its students learn harmonica and recorder, now slated for relocation as the nearby Longtan Science Park expands.

About · 學校簡介

A small school with a big musical tradition.

Delong Elementary was founded in 1982 in a quiet, rural part of Longtan District. It is a small school of roughly 300-plus students, notable for a music tradition in which nearly half the student body learns harmonica and recorder starting from the mid-elementary grades. The school is slated for eventual relocation to the "Longke Phase 3" (龍科三期) resettlement area under a "build-first-then-move" plan, with a new sister campus, Meilong Elementary, planned nearby — a reflection of the ongoing development pressure brought by the expanding Longtan Science Park.

德龍國小創立於民國71年（1982年），位於龍潭區一處寧靜的鄉村地帶。全校學生約300餘人，規模不大，但音樂傳統深厚——近半數學生自中年級起學習口琴與直笛。學校因龍潭科學園區擴建，未來將依「先建後遷」模式遷至「龍科三期」安置區，並規劃鄰近設立姊妹校「梅龍國小」，反映出當地因科技產業發展而面臨的變遷壓力。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A rural school reshaped by the science park.

🏗️

Delong Elementary's planned relocation is tied directly to the expansion of the Longtan Science Park (龍潭科學園區), illustrating how Longtan's rural school communities are being reshaped by the district's semiconductor and tech-industry growth. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
