Ziqiang Junior High School

桃園市立自強國民中學

Founded in 1980 on reclaimed pond land in the Neili area, tracking Zhongli's conversion from farmland to residential and school infrastructure.

創校於1980年，校地為內壢地區填平的池塘用地，見證了中壢從農漁地轉型為住宅與學校用地的過程。

About · 學校簡介

Built on reclaimed pond land in Neili.

Ziqiang Junior High was founded on August 6, 1980. It initially borrowed classroom space at Zili Elementary School before its own campus — built on a former pond site of roughly 2.3 hectares — was completed in December 1981. Located in the Neili/Ziqiang Li area of Zhongli, the school became municipal on December 25, 2014.

自強國中創立於民國69年（1980年）8月6日，初期借用自立國小教室，直到1981年12月自有校舍完工，校地約2.3公頃，原為魚塭池塘用地。學校位於中壢內壢／自強里一帶，2014年12月25日改制為市立學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🐟 Built on a Former Pond

校地原為池塘

The school's roughly 2.3-hectare campus was built on a former pond site, completed in December 1981 after the school opened by borrowing classrooms elsewhere.

🏫 A Neili-Area Institution

位於內壢／自強里

Located in the Neili/Ziqiang Li area of Zhongli, the school has served this urbanizing corner of the district since 1980.

🏛️ Municipal Since 2014

2014年改制市立

The school transitioned to municipal governance on December 25, 2014, as part of Taoyuan's citywide upgrade to a special municipality.

Local Culture · 在地文化

From aquaculture land to schoolyard.

🐟

Built on reclaimed pond land in the Neili area, this school's history tracks the conversion of Zhongli's agricultural and aquacultural land into residential and school infrastructure as the district urbanized. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
