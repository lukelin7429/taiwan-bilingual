Chunri Elementary School

春日國小

About · 學校簡介

A miniature Paiwan culture park in school form.

Chunri Elementary School traces its origin to February 1945, when it was established during the Japanese colonial era as an indigenous children's education center; it was renamed Chunri National School in December 1945 after Taiwan's retrocession. The school is regarded as one of the “must-visit” elementary schools in southern Taiwan, largely because its library takes the form of a traditional Paiwan tribal granary (穀倉), giving it a naturally lit, cafe-like appearance often described as resembling a miniature Paiwan culture park. As of the 2018 school year, indigenous — mostly Paiwan — students made up 99% of its 66-student enrollment. The school also fields a strong judo program and an award-winning traditional-song group.

春日國小前身為昭和20年（1945年）2月成立的「蕃童教育所」，同年12月台灣光復後改稱春日國民學校。學校因圖書館以傳統排灣族穀倉造型設計而聞名，採光良好、宛如咖啡廳，常被譽為南台灣「必訪小學」之一，猶如迷你排灣族文化園區。截至107學年度，原住民（主要為排灣族）學生占全校66名學生的99%。學校柔道隊表現優異，古謠隊也屢獲全國大獎。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏺 A Library Shaped Like a Granary

穀倉造型圖書館

The school's library takes the form of a traditional Paiwan tribal granary, naturally lit and cafe-like in feel — a design so distinctive it draws visitors from across southern Taiwan.

🥋 A National Judo Powerhouse

全國柔道勁旅

Chunri Elementary's judo program won 6 gold, 5 silver, and 5 bronze medals at the 2024 National Judo Championship, a remarkable haul for such a small school.

🎶 An Outstanding Traditional-Song Group

古謠隊特優

The school's traditional-song (古謠) group achieved “outstanding” rank in the National Teachers and Students Indigenous-Language Folk Song category in both 2022 and 2023.

🌾 99% Indigenous Enrollment

原住民學生占99%

As of the 2018 school year, indigenous students — overwhelmingly Paiwan — made up 99% of the school's 66-student enrollment, reflecting Chunri's identity as a Paiwan mountain community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Chunri, a living Paiwan culture park.

🏺

Chunri Township is a Paiwan indigenous mountain township in the deep south of Pingtung, along the route toward the Jinshuiying Historic Trail. Paiwan granary architecture, traditional song, and communal ceremony are actively woven into daily school life here — nowhere more visibly than in Chunri Elementary's granary-shaped library, a design that turns the school itself into a piece of living Paiwan heritage.
