About · 學校簡介

A school in "Bountiful Fishing" village.

Haibin Elementary School is located in Fengyu Village — its name literally meaning "Bountiful Fishing" — a fishing community within Donggang Town. The village's name itself speaks directly to the maritime livelihood that has long defined this part of Pingtung County's coast, and Haibin Elementary serves the families who call this fishing neighborhood home.

海濱國小位於東港鎮豐漁里——「豐漁」二字本身即意指漁獲豐收——是一處漁村社區。這個村里的名稱本身便直接道出屏東沿海此地長久以來以漁業為生的生活樣貌，海濱國小服務的正是這個漁村社區的居民與孩子。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎣 A Name That Means "Bountiful Fishing"

豐漁里的意涵

The school's home village, Fengyu, translates to "bountiful fishing" — a name that captures the maritime identity of the surrounding community.

🌊 A Coastal Neighborhood School

沿海社區學校

As the local elementary school for Fengyu Village, Haibin Elementary anchors education for one of Donggang's fishing communities.

⚓ Part of Donggang's Fishing Fabric

東港漁業版圖的一角

Like the rest of Donggang Town, the school's community is closely tied to the fishing industry that has shaped the area for generations.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A fishing port with a royal tradition.

🎣

Haibin Elementary sits in Donggang Town's Fengyu ("Bountiful Fishing") Village, directly reflecting the town's identity as a major fishing port famous for bluefin tuna. The town is also home to the triennial King Boat Burning Festival (迎王燒王船), centered on Donglong Temple and recognized as national important intangible cultural heritage since 2011. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
