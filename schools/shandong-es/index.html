Shandong Elementary School

Named after its home village, Shandong Elementary grew from a 1948 branch classroom of Dalun Elementary into an independent school within a decade.

About · 學校簡介

A daughter school named for its village.

Shandong Elementary began in 1948 as a branch classroom of Dalun Elementary School, becoming an independent school in 1956 under the name "Taoyuan County Zhongli Town Shandong Elementary." The school takes its name from Shandong Village (山東里), the local administrative area it serves. It has marked a 60th-anniversary celebration, complete with an archive of all its past principals maintained on a dedicated anniversary website.

山東國小前身為民國37年（1948年）設立的大崙國小分班，於民國45年（1956年）獨立設校，定名為「桃園縣中壢鎮山東國民學校」。校名取自其所在地山東里。學校曾舉辦六十週年校慶，並建置專屬紀念網站，收錄歷任校長名錄。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Grown out of a neighboring school.

📍

Like several of Zhongli's mid-century schools, Shandong Elementary grew out of a larger neighboring school — in this case Dalun Elementary — as the district's population spread into new villages after the war. Its name simply reflects the place it has always served, Shandong Village, though the deeper story of how that village itself came to be named is not something this profile can confirm.
