About · 學校簡介

A hundred-year-old village school with heart.

Xingzhong Elementary was established in 1916 as a branch school and celebrated its 109th anniversary during the 2024 academic year, giving it over a century of history in Minsyong Township. The school runs a dedicated art class program called "藝啟感動-興中美術班" (Art Inspires Emotion), alongside indigenous-language instruction, English, calligraphy, and reading competitions. It also maintains track and field and volleyball facilities as well as school garden spaces, rounding out a well-balanced program of arts, language, sport, and hands-on learning.

興中國小創立於民國5年（1916年），最初為分校，於2024學年度慶祝建校109週年，在民雄鄉已有超過百年歷史。學校設有名為「藝啟感動－興中美術班」的專屬美術課程，並推動本土語言、英語、書法及閱讀競賽課程。校園也設有田徑與排球運動設施，以及校園菜圃空間，展現藝術、語言、體育與實作學習並重的均衡教育。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A century-old outpost of the Japanese colonial era.

🏞️

Founded in 1916 during the Japanese colonial era, Xingzhong Elementary is one of several century-old branch schools — alongside Xiulin and Minsyong Elementary — founded in Minsyong's outlying villages, reflecting the township's long agricultural settlement history predating its modern university-town identity.
