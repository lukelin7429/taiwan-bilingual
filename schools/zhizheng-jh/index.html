About · 學校簡介

One of the city's newer junior highs.

Zhizheng Junior High School was founded in 1982, decades after Pingtung City's other established junior highs — Zhongzheng, Mingzheng, Gongzheng, and Datong — to absorb the city's continued population growth. Located in the Beixing Village (北興里) area of Pingtung City, it remains one of the newer additions to the county seat's secondary school system.

至正國中創立於民國71年（1982年），晚於屏東市既有的中正、明正、公正、大同等國中數十年，主要為因應屏東市持續增長的人口而設立。校址位於屏東市北興里，是縣立中學系統中較晚成立的學校之一。
