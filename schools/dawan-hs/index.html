Dawan Senior High School

臺南市立大灣高級中學

A complete secondary school of about 2,300 students, founded in 1983 and named after Dawan, one of Yongkang District's oldest settled areas.

創校於1983年、學生約2,300人的完全中學，以永康區歷史最悠久的聚落之一「大灣」為名。

About · 學校簡介

From a county junior high to a complete secondary school.

Established on August 1, 1983 as "Tainan County Dawan Junior High School," the school became a "complete secondary school" (完全中學) by adding a senior-high division — designated in 2001 and fully implemented in 2003. It took its current name in 2010 after the Tainan county-city merger. Today the school enrolls roughly 2,300 students across 60 junior-high and 18 senior-high classes on a 54,048-square-meter campus equipped with a track and facilities for basketball, volleyball, badminton, and archery.

學校於1983年8月1日創立，初名「臺南縣大灣國民中學」，後於2001年獲准增設高中部、2003年正式實施，成為「完全中學」。2010年臺南縣市合併後更為現名。目前全校約2,300名學生，國中部60班、高中部18班，校地面積54,048平方公尺，設有田徑場及籃球、排球、羽球、射箭等運動設施。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Complete Secondary School

完全中學

Originally a junior high, the school added a senior-high division in 2001 and fully implemented it in 2003, now spanning both junior and senior high in a single campus.

🎯 A Well-Equipped Athletics Campus

多元運動設施

The 54,048-square-meter campus includes a running track and dedicated facilities for basketball, volleyball, badminton, and archery.

🏘️ Named After Yongkang's "Great Bay"

大灣地名由來

The school takes its name from Dawan (大灣), one of Yongkang's oldest settled areas — a name that recalls the district's early-Qing-era proximity to tidal creeks before land reclamation pushed the coastline west.

📈 Post-Merger Renaming

縣市合併更名

Founded in 1983 as a Tainan County junior high, the school was renamed to its current title in 2010 following the merger of Tainan County and Tainan City.
