About · 學校簡介

Ancient ground, and a home for displaced students.

Dayuan Elementary was founded in 1903, during the Japanese colonial era, as a branch facility, becoming a fully independent school in 1906. It is the largest elementary school by campus area in Taoyuan City, and its grounds sit within the Dayuan Jianshan Archaeological Site (大園尖山遺址), designated a county-protected heritage site in 2008. The school built a new main gate in 2003, reoriented to face Zhongshan South Road, with continued facility modernization — including digital-learning upgrades — through 2022. In recent years, Dayuan Elementary has also absorbed transferred students from several nearby elementary schools closed in 2024 due to the Taoyuan Aerotropolis project: Shalun, Zhentou, Houcuo, and Chenkang.

大園國小創立於1903年（明治36年）日治時期，最初為分教場性質，1906年（明治39年）正式獨立設校。學校是桃園市面積最大的國小，校地座落於「大園尖山遺址」之上，該遺址於2008年（民國97年）指定為縣定古蹟。學校於2003年新建正門，改朝向中山南路，並持續進行設施現代化，包括至2022年的數位學習升級工程。近年來，大園國小也接收了多所因桃園航空城計畫於2024年廢校的鄰近小學轉學學童，包括沙崙、圳頭、後厝與陳康國小。

Anchoring both the ancient past and the modern airport.
