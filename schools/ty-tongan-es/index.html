Tong-An Elementary School

同安國民小學

A large neighborhood elementary school east of Taoyuan's old city core, known for its Hakka-language program and a student art exhibition now in its 19th year.

About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood anchor east of downtown Taoyuan.

Tong-An Elementary is one of the larger public elementary schools in Taoyuan District, enrolling roughly 1,244 students across 49 classes as of the 2022 school year. Sitting on Tongde 11th Street in the dense residential grid just east of Taoyuan's historic city core, it is part of a cluster of neighborhood schools — Tong-An, Tong-De, Ciwen, and Huiji — that grew up around the district's old administrative center as the city urbanized through the postwar decades. Alongside its regular curriculum, the school runs a gifted-education program and bilingual-education initiatives.

同安國小是桃園區規模較大的公立國小之一，111學年度全校約有1,244名學生、49班。校址位於桃園舊城區以東、住宅密集的同德十一街，與同德、慈文、會稽等鄰近小學同屬因應戰後城市擴張而在舊行政核心周邊陸續設立的社區學校群。校內除一般課程外，另設有資優班及雙語教育等措施。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎤 A Strong Hakka-Language Program

客語教學特色

The school's "Good Health Team" placed second nationally in the 2025 National Hakka Arts and Culture Competition, reflecting a sustained investment in Hakka-language education.

🎨 A 19-Year Student Art Exhibition

歷史悠久的學生美展

An annual student art exhibition, now in its 19th year, gives every grade a platform to show off classroom artwork — one of the school's longest-running traditions.

🌟 Gifted and Bilingual Programs

資優班與雙語教育

Alongside its general classes, Tong-An runs a gifted-education program (資優班) together with bilingual-education and Indigenous-language initiatives.

🏫 One of Taoyuan District's Larger Schools

桃園區大型學校

With about 1,244 students across 49 classes and 91 teachers (2022 figures), Tong-An is one of the bigger elementary schools serving Taoyuan District's dense eastern neighborhoods.
