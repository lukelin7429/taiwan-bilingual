About · 學校簡介

A small school with a big heart on the county border.

Neiwan Elementary is a small rural school with about 60 students and 12 teachers, serving the Neiwan area of Zhuolan Township on the border between Miaoli and Taichung. The school also maintains an affiliated Baifan Branch. Despite its small size, Neiwan has produced notable student work — including a piece that won recognition in the Hsin Yi Children's Animation Award.

內灣國小是一所小型鄉村學校，全校約60名學生、12位教師，服務位於苗栗、台中交界卓蘭鎮內灣地區的學童。學校並設有附屬的白布帆分校。儘管規模不大，內灣國小仍培育出優秀學生作品——曾有作品在信誼兒童動畫獎中獲得肯定。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Among Zhuolan's mountain-fruit orchards.

🍐

Located in Zhuolan's Neiwan area on the Miaoli-Taichung border, the school belongs to a township where Hakka and Hoklo mountain-farming communities have long cultivated the high-elevation pears and fruit orchards that define local Zhuolan livelihoods.
