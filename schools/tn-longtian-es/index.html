About · 學校簡介

A school of many names, one famous graduate.

Longtian Elementary School was founded in 1941 as a branch classroom and became a fully independent school in 1945. Through its history it carried several names — Guantian National School's No. 2 branch, then "Fanzitian National School," then "Guantian Central National School" — before settling on Longtian. The school counts former President of the Republic of China Chen Shui-bian (陳水扁) among its alumni, an 18th-graduation-class graduate who grew up in nearby Xizhuang Village in Guantian. Beyond academics, Longtian emphasizes track and field as its core athletics program, and also runs a recorder ensemble, choir, and children's rhythm band for arts education.

隆田國小創立於民國30年（1941年），最初為分教場，民國34年（1945年）正式獨立設校。校名歷經多次更迭，曾為官田國民學校第二分校、「番仔田國民學校」、「官田中心國民學校」，最後定名隆田國小。前中華民國總統陳水扁為該校第18屆畢業校友，他自幼在鄰近的官田西莊里成長。除學業外，隆田國小以田徑為核心體育項目，並設有直笛隊、合唱團與兒童節奏樂隊等藝術教育活動。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Water chestnuts and a famous graduate.

🌰

The school sits in Guantian's water-chestnut-farming heartland — a crop so central to local identity that coverage of the school often notes the district's fame for the crop alongside its most famous alumnus, former President Chen Shui-bian. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
