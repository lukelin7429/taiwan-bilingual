Caohu Junior High School

草湖國民中學

A public junior high school in Fangyuan Township that grew out of a borrowed classroom in 1962, on land whose community roots stretch back to a private academy founded in 1931.

位於芳苑鄉的公立國中，自1962年借用教室起步，其社區教育根源可追溯至1931年創立的私塾。

About · 學校簡介

Ninety years of community education in Caohu.

Caohu Junior High began in August 1962 as a branch of Erlin Junior High School, with Huang Tengbiao appointed as preparatory director and classes borrowed from Caohu Elementary School. It became an independent school, Caohu Junior High School, in the winter of 1964, and was renamed Changhua County Caohu Junior High School in September 1968. The school shares its historical roots with neighboring Caohu Elementary, whose site traces back to a private academy founded in 1931.

草湖國中創立於民國51年（1962年）8月，最初是二林初級中學的分部，由黃騰標擔任籌備主任，借用草湖國小教室上課。民國53年（1964年）冬天獨立設校，定名「草湖初級中學」，民國57年（1968年）9月改名為「彰化縣立草湖國民中學」。學校與鄰近的草湖國小共享歷史淵源，兩校的社區教育根源皆可追溯至1931年創立的私塾。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Born From Borrowed Classrooms

借教室起家的辦學歷程

Caohu Junior High began in 1962 using classrooms borrowed from Caohu Elementary School, a modest starting point that grew into a fully independent school within two years.

📜 Roots Stretching to 1931

淵源可溯至1931年私塾

The school's site is connected to Caohu's earliest community-founded private academy, "草湖私古軒," established in 1931 — linking it to over ninety years of grassroots educational effort in the area.

👨‍👩‍👧 A Legacy of Local Families

陳氏家族的辦學傳統

Caohu's educational history was shaped by local Chen-family founders whose grassroots efforts helped establish schooling in the area long before the modern junior high was formed.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A century of grassroots schooling in Caohu.

Caohu's educational tradition stretches back to 1931, when local Chen-family community leaders founded a private academy on land connected to today's school — over ninety years of grassroots educational effort in this corner of Fangyuan Township.
