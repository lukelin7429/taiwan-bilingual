About · 學校簡介

The first national university reborn in Taiwan.

National Chengchi University (NCCU) was founded in 1927 in Nanjing, China, as the Central School of Party Affairs, training government and party officials under founding president Chiang Kai-shek. In 1946 it merged with the Central Cadres Institute to form National Chengchi University, and was placed under the Ministry of Education the following year. The university was suspended after the Republic of China government relocated to Taiwan in 1949, then reopened in 1954 in Taipei's Muzha area — now Wenshan District — as the first national university re-established in Taiwan after the Chinese Civil War. It has since grown into one of Asia's leading centers for humanities and social science research, particularly strong in law, journalism, international affairs, and business.

國立政治大學創立於1927年，前身為南京的中央黨務學校，由創校校長蔣中正主持，培育黨政人才。1946年與中央幹部學校合併，定名為國立政治大學，隔年改隸教育部。1949年中華民國政府遷台後一度停辦，1954年在台北市木柵（今文山區）復校，成為國共內戰後在台灣復校的第一所國立大學。此後政大逐步發展為亞洲人文與社會科學研究的重鎮，尤以法律、新聞傳播、國際事務與商管領域見長。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Twelve Colleges

十二個學院

NCCU spans twelve colleges — including Law, Communication, International Affairs, Foreign Languages and Literature, and Global Banking and Finance — with 34 departments and 48 graduate institutes.

⚖️ Training Ground for Taiwan's Elite

政經法界搖籃

Alumni include Vice President Vincent Siew and Examination Yuan President Wu Jin-lin; both Presidents Ma Ying-jeou and Tsai Ing-wen taught as law faculty here.

🌐 Extensive International Network

廣泛國際交流網絡

NCCU maintains partnerships with 496+ universities worldwide, offers 800+ English-taught courses, and hosts over 3,000 overseas students.

📰 Strength in Journalism and Diplomacy

新聞與外交強項

Long recognized for its Colleges of Communication and International Affairs, NCCU has trained much of Taiwan's diplomatic, legal, and media elite.
