About · 學校簡介

From farmers' school to comprehensive high school.

National Hsin-Feng Senior High School traces its roots to March 27, 1929, when community leaders from five local townships established an agricultural supplementary school, "Guiren Agricultural Supplementary School," to train the district's farming workforce. The school grew into a full senior high school in 1957 under provincial administration, and later took on its current national designation around 2000. Today it sits on a large, roughly 8-hectare campus — unusually spacious for its enrollment of about 1,000 to 1,200 students — offering both an academic track and vocational programs in advertising design, business services, information technology, and applied English, alongside an evening continuing-education division.

新豐高中創校於民國18年（1929年）3月27日，由地方五個鄉鎮的仕紳共同籌設「歸仁農業補習學校」，培育當地農業人才。民國46年（1957年）改制為完整的省立高級中學，約於2000年前後確立現今的國立高中校名。學校校地約8公頃，相對於約1,000至1,200名學生的規模而言顯得格外寬闊，同時開設普通科與廣告設計、商業經營、資訊、應用英語等職業類科，並設有進修部。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 Born as a Farmers' School

起源於農業補習學校

Founded in 1929 by leaders of five local townships to train Guiren's agricultural workforce, the school's roots run deep into the district's farming past, long before it became a rail-transit hub.

🏫 A Spacious, Comprehensive Campus

校地寬闊的綜合型高中

An 8-hectare campus serves about 1,000–1,200 students with both an academic track and vocational programs spanning advertising design, business services, IT, and applied English.

🏆 National Sporting Titles

全國運動賽事榮譽

The school's athletic history includes a 1996 national championship in rugby-style football and a 2000 national badminton men's singles title.

🌙 Evening Continuing Education

進修部

Alongside its day programs, the school runs an evening continuing-education division, extending access to secondary education for adult and working students in the district.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Guiren, from farming township to rail gateway.

🚄

Guiren District was long a quiet farming township before the mid-2000s, when the arrival of the Tainan High Speed Rail Station and proximity to Tainan Airport transformed it into a transit-development zone. National Hsin-Feng's century-old agricultural origins predate that transformation entirely — its campus now sits near the very rail corridor that reshaped the district around it.
