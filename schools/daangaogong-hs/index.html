About · 學校簡介

One of Taipei's oldest vocational schools.

Da'an Vocational High School was founded on April 18, 1940, during the Japanese colonial era, as the Taipei Commercial-Industrial Continuation School, teaching machinery, electrical, and commercial trades. It took on the “Da'an” name in September 1981 and has since expanded to eleven departments. Sitting directly beside Taipei MRT Da'an Station, the school occupies some of the busiest real estate in the district. Starting in 2025, it launched a “3+2” collaborative automation-engineering program with National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (NTUST).

大安高工創立於1940年（日治時期），初名「臺北商工專修學校」，教授機械、電機、商業等科別。民國70年（1981年）9月更名為「大安」，此後陸續擴展至十一個科別。校址就在捷運大安站旁，位處大安區最繁忙的商業地帶之一。自民國114年（2025年）起，更與台灣科技大學合作推出「3+2」自動化工程銜接專班。

🏭 One of Taipei's Oldest Vocational Schools

Founded in 1940 as the Taipei Commercial-Industrial Continuation School, the school has grown from teaching basic trades into eleven departments spanning machinery, electrical, and commercial fields.

🔧 A Working-Class Counterpoint

白領區裡的技職學府

Local Culture · 在地文化

A technical counterweight in a white-collar district.

🚇

Occupying prime real estate steps from MRT Da'an Station, in the middle of the district's busiest retail and office corridor, Da'an Vocational High School gives Da'an's identity a technical, working-class counterweight to the university faculty and white-collar households that surround it — proof that the district's story isn't only about academia.
