Ruiyuan Elementary School

瑞原國民小學

A century-old "railway school" founded in 1922, its front gate sitting right beside the main north-south line and its grounds surrounded on three sides by farmland.

創設於1922年的百年鐵道特色小學，正門緊臨縱貫鐵路，三面為農田環繞，是楊梅著名的百年小學。

About · 學校簡介

A century of learning beside the tracks.

Ruiyuan Elementary was established in April 1922, giving it more than a century of history. Local history writers have documented it under the tag "#118 Ruiyuan Elementary 1922 — Yangmei, Taoyuan — a century-old railway school," a nod to its front gate sitting directly beside Taiwan's main north-south rail line, with farmland surrounding the campus on three sides. The school covers a total area of 26,586 square meters and today runs six elementary classes and one kindergarten class, serving roughly 95 students — a genuinely small school with a genuinely long history.

瑞原國小創設於民國11年（1922年）4月，至今已逾百年校史。地方文史紀錄稱其為「＃118 瑞原國小 1922｜桃園楊梅｜百年小學｜鐵道特色小學」，正門緊臨縱貫鐵路，三面為農田環繞。校地總面積26,586平方公尺，現有國小部六班、幼稚園一班，學生人數約95人，屬小型學校。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A century of witness to a railway-built town.

🚂

Sitting right beside the main north-south railway, Ruiyuan Elementary has spent over a century as an education landmark of Yangmei's "railway Hakka village" Shuimei area, witnessing how the arrival of the railway shaped this settlement's growth. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
