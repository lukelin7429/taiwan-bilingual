About · 學校簡介

From the 'Seventh Junior High' to a complete school.

Gushan Senior High School was founded in 1958 as Kaohsiung Municipal Seventh Junior High School (高雄市立第七初級中學). It was renamed Kaohsiung Municipal Gushan Junior High School in 1968, became a complete junior-and-senior-high school in 1997, and took its current name, Gushan Senior High School, in October 2000. The school today serves roughly 1,050 students on a campus of about 5.2 hectares at the western edge of Kaohsiung City.

鼓山高中創立於民國47年（1958年），初名「高雄市立第七初級中學」。1968年更名為「高雄市立鼓山國民中學」，1997年改制為完全中學，並於2000年10月更名為現今的「鼓山高級中學」。學校目前約有1,050名學生，校地面積約5.2公頃，座落於高雄市西側。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🔢 From 'Seventh Junior High' to Today's Name

從「第七初中」到現名

The school's original 1958 name, Kaohsiung Municipal Seventh Junior High School, reflects the numbered-school system used during Kaohsiung's rapid postwar educational expansion.

🏫 A Complete Junior-and-Senior-High Campus

完全中學

Since becoming a complete school in 1997, Gushan has offered a continuous 7–12 education on a single campus of about 5.2 hectares.

👨‍🎓 Serving About 1,050 Students

約1,050名學生

The school today enrolls roughly 1,050 students across its junior and senior high divisions at the western edge of Kaohsiung City.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A marker of Kaohsiung's postwar growth.

🏙️

Gushan Senior High's original 1958 name — "Seventh Junior High" — reflects Kaohsiung's rapid postwar school-numbering expansion, as the growing city opened one numbered junior high after another to keep pace with its population boom. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
