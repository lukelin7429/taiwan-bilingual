About · 學校簡介

A small school with a five-unit heritage curriculum.

Dingshe Elementary was established in 1962, serving Kengzi Village (坑子村) with an enrollment of around 170 students. The school has built a distinctive local-culture (鄉土教學) curriculum around five themed trails and units: "Camphor-wood Old House Memories," "Nostalgic Old Houses," "Fragrant Flowers and Tea, Lovely Hometown," "Pangolin Trail Tour," and "Beautiful Kengzi."

頂社國小創立於民國51年（1962年），服務坑子村，學生人數約170人。學校發展出獨具特色的鄉土教學課程，共分五大主題單元：「樟木老屋記憶」、「懷舊古厝」、「花香茶鄉，可愛家園」、「穿山甲步道之旅」及「美麗的坑子」。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Luzhu's "back garden," taught by its own school.

🏡

Dingshe Elementary is located in Kengzi (坑子), sometimes called Luzhu's "back garden," near the historic Kengzi Old Street. The school's entire local-culture curriculum is explicitly organized around teaching students the old houses, trails, and tea-growing heritage of this historic settlement — one of the district's three traditional cores alongside Nankan and Dazhu. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
