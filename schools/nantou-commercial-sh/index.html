About · 學校簡介

Training the county's commercial workforce since 1955.

National Nantou Commercial High School was established in 1955 (ROC 44) and has grown into the county's leading vocational-commercial school. About 1,480 students study in day programs across eight departments — including Applied Foreign Languages, Accounting, Data Processing, International Trade, and Tourism — preparing them directly for careers in business and service industries. In 2013, the school received the Ministry of Education's top national school-evaluation rating, a strong endorsement of its vocational training programs.

國立南投高商創立於民國44年（1955年），發展至今已成為南投縣首屈一指的商業職業學校。目前約有1,480名學生就讀日間部，分屬應用外語、會計、資料處理、國際貿易、觀光等八個科別，直接培養學生投入商業與服務業所需的專業能力。民國102年（2013年），學校榮獲教育部評鑑最高等級肯定，充分展現其職業教育成效。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏅 Top National Evaluation Rating

教育部最高評鑑

In 2013, the school received the Ministry of Education's top national school-evaluation rating, a strong endorsement of the quality of its vocational-commercial programs.

🌐 Eight Vocational Departments

八大職業類科

Departments span Applied Foreign Languages, Accounting, Data Processing, International Trade, and Tourism among others, giving students a wide choice of business-focused career paths.

💼 Training the Local Workforce

培育在地商業人才

As the county's leading vocational-commercial school, it trains much of the local workforce that serves Nantou City's role as the county's administrative and commercial hub.

👥 A Large Student Body

約1,480名學生

With about 1,480 students in day programs, the school is one of the larger senior high institutions in Nantou City, reflecting strong demand for vocational-commercial education.
