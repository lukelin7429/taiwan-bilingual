Dali Elementary School

大里國民小學

A small school near the historic Caoling Pass trail, rebuilt in 2025 with a "terraced rice field" design offering panoramic Turtle Island views.

About · 學校簡介

From a branch campus to an award-winning building.

Dali Elementary began in 1964 as a branch campus of Daxi Elementary School before becoming independent. Located in Shicheng Village near the foot of Caoling Pass, at the historic site known as "大里簡," the school has run night-tutoring programs for rural children since 2008. In July 2025, it completed a brand-new campus building — a NT$139 million, three-story, nine-classroom structure designed with a "terraced rice field" motif that offers panoramic views of Turtle Island, drawing wide press coverage.

大里國小前身為民國53年（1964年）設立的大溪國小大里分班，後獨立設校。校址位於石城里、草嶺古道山腳下的歷史聚落「大里簡」。學校自民國97年（2008年）起推動偏鄉夜間課輔。民國114年（2025年）7月，斥資新台幣1.39億元興建的全新校舍落成，三層樓、九間教室，以「梯田」意象設計，可俯瞰龜山島全景，廣受媒體報導。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 A "Terraced Rice Field" Campus

梯田意象新校舍

Completed in July 2025 at a cost of NT$139 million, the school's new three-story, nine-classroom building was designed around a terraced rice field motif.

🐢 Panoramic Turtle Island Views

龜山島全景視野

The new campus offers sweeping views of Turtle Island, a feature that drew widespread media attention when the building opened.

🥾 Beside the Historic Caoling Pass

緊鄰草嶺古道

The school sits near the historic Dali Tiangong Temple area at the foot of Caoling Pass, the old trail early Han pioneers used entering Yilan from the north.

📚 Night Tutoring for Rural Students

偏鄉夜間課輔

Since 2008, the school has offered night-tutoring programs supporting rural children's education beyond the regular school day.
