About · 學校簡介

A small school in the heart of Sicao.

Zhenhai Elementary was founded in 1951 in Sicao Village (四草里) — the same village that gives its name to the famous Sicao Green Tunnel mangrove waterway. The school serves the Sicao and Yantian ("Salt Field") villages, directly naming the historic salt-pan industry of the area in its school-district boundary. It is a small rural school with 6 elementary classes plus 1 kindergarten class, 24 staff, and a campus area of 9,844 square meters.

鎮海國小創立於1951年（民國40年），位於四草里——正是知名「四草綠色隧道」紅樹林水道所在的同一里。學校學區涵蓋四草與鹽田里，校名及學區劃分直接呼應當地歷史悠久的鹽業。這是一所小型鄉村學校，設有6個國小班級及1個幼兒園班，教職員24人，校地面積9,844平方公尺。

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the heart of Annan's ecological heritage.

🌊

Sitting in Sicao Village on "Dazhong Road," the school is a short distance from the Sicao Green Tunnel mangrove wetlands and the historic Sicao salt pans, placing it at the heart of Annan's signature ecological and salt-heritage landscape. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
