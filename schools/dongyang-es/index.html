Dongyang Elementary School

東陽國小

A small rural school serving outlying milkfish-pond hamlets of Xuejia, open to students from outside its normal zone since 2011.

服務學甲區虱目魚養殖聚落的小型鄉村學校，自2011年起開放自由入學。

About · 學校簡介

A branch school that grew independent, then opened its doors wider.

Dongyang Elementary began in July 1957 as the 'Xinrong Branch' of Xuejia Elementary School, becoming independent in 1961 as Tainan County Xuejia Township Dongyang Elementary School. A short-lived branch classroom in Wenhua Meihe was discontinued in 1972. In December 2011 the school was designated a 'free enrollment' school, letting students from outside its normal catchment zone attend.

東陽國小前身為學甲國小於民國46年（1957年）7月設立的「新榮分校」，民國50年（1961年）獨立設校為台南縣學甲鄉東陽國民小學。學校曾設有文化美和分校，於民國61年（1972年）裁撤。民國100年（2011年）12月起，學校被列為「自由入學」學校，開放學區外學生就讀。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Serving Xuejia's outlying milkfish hamlets.

🐟

Dongyang serves outlying, milkfish-pond hamlets of Xuejia District away from the town center — a small rural school in a district whose economy and culture are built around milkfish aquaculture. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
