About · 學校簡介

A century of schooling beside the canal.

Yuandou Elementary traces its roots to 1921, when it opened as a Japanese-era branch school known as "丈八斗分教場," part of Erlin Public School. After Taiwan's 1945 retrocession it was renamed Yuandou National School, and took its present name, Yuandou Elementary School, in 1968. A junior high division (Yuandou Junior High) was split off as a separate school in 1983. In 2018, the elementary and junior high were reunited under a "top school" pilot program, making it Changhua County's first combined elementary-junior high school, formally known as Yuandou Combined Elementary and Junior High School (原斗國民中小學). This site continues to catalogue it under its elementary-school grades.

原斗國小的歷史可追溯至民國10年（1921年），前身為日治時期二林公學校的「丈八斗分教場」。1945年台灣光復後改稱「原斗國民學校」，民國57年（1968年）定名為「原斗國小」。民國72年（1983年），原斗國中自本校分出獨立設校。到了民國107年（2018年），透過「特色領航學校」計畫，國中部與國小部再度合併，成為彰化縣第一所國中小合併學校「原斗國民中小學」。本網站仍以其國小學制介紹此校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏯 A Century of Continuous Schooling

跨越百年的辦學歷史

The school's history stretches back to a 1921 Japanese-era branch classroom, making it one of the older educational institutions in the Erlin area, with roots older than Taiwan's 1945 retrocession.

🔗 Reunited After 35 Years

國中小合併辦學

After splitting off its junior high division in 1983, the school was reunited with it in 2018 under a county pilot program, becoming Changhua's first combined elementary-junior high campus and giving the community a single school spanning preschool through junior high.

💧 A Home Beside the Canal

莿仔埤圳畔的學校

Located next to the historic Cizaipi Canal, the school sits at the center of Yuandou village — historically known as "Bridgehead" (Qiaozaitou) — serving as the area's educational and community hub.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Yuandou village, once known as "Bridgehead."

🌾

Yuandou village was historically called "Bridgehead" (橋仔頭), and the school beside the century-old Cizaipi Canal (莿仔埤圳) has long served as the community's central gathering point for education, spanning generations of local families. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
