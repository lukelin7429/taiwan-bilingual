Yonggang Elementary School

永港國民小學

A small village school near Taiwan's southernmost point, ringed by mountains and surrounded by sea on three sides.

About · 學校簡介

Near the southernmost point in Taiwan.

Yonggang Elementary School sits in Yongjing Village, close to Taiwan's southernmost point, in a landscape described as surrounded by sea on three sides — the Pacific Ocean to the east, the Bashi Channel to the south, and the Taiwan Strait to the west — and ringed by mountains including Laofo Mountain, Lide Mountain, and Harbor Mountain. It is a small school: as of the 2022–23 academic year it had 6 classes and 45 students, supported by 14 teachers and 2 staff. Campus facilities include a library, playground, computer classroom, and gymnasium.

永港國小位於永靖村，鄰近台灣最南端，地處三面環海之地——東臨太平洋、南濱巴士海峽、西接台灣海峽，並被老佛山、里德山、港仔山等山脈環繞。這是一所小型學校，111學年度計有6個班級、45名學生，由14位教師與2位職員支持。校內設有圖書館、操場、電腦教室與體育館等設施。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where the hawks fly and the old songs echo.

🦅

Yonggang Elementary sits in Manzhou Township near Taiwan's southern tip, right along the flyway watched each autumn as thousands of gray-faced buzzard hawks (灰面鵟鷹) pass overhead on their annual migration south. The area also falls within the cultural region credited as the birthplace of "思想起" (Thinking Of), the classic Hengchun-Manzhou folk ballad sung to moon-guitar accompaniment — heritage that surrounds this small coastal-and-mountain school on every side. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
