About · 學校簡介

The school of the township's busiest village.

Xianji Elementary is located in Xianji Village, which has the highest population density in all of Xinyuan Township — about 1,160 people per square kilometer. Detailed enrollment and program records beyond this fact and the school's address are limited, but the school continues to serve as the local elementary school for this comparatively bustling corner of an otherwise rural, farming-oriented township.

仙吉國小位於仙吉村，是新園鄉人口密度最高的村落，每平方公里約有1,160人。除此之外，目前公開可查的招生與課程資料有限，但學校仍持續作為這個在整體以農業為主的鄉鎮中相對熱鬧角落的在地國小。

Local Culture · 在地文化

The alluvial plain of the Gaoping River.

🌾

Xinyuan Township (新園鄉) sits on an agricultural plain formed by alluvial deposits of the Gaoping River and Donggang River, known for rice, banana, and fruit farming, plus fish, shrimp, and eel aquaculture near the river mouth. Xianji Elementary serves the township's most densely populated village, a small but lively pocket within this farming landscape. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
