Zhongxiao Elementary School

臺北市中正區忠孝國民小學

A public elementary school whose construction was delayed for years by a city sanitation garage occupying its site, finally reaching full grade coverage in 1978.

About · 學校簡介

A school that shared its grounds with garbage trucks.

Zhongxiao Elementary School's site was secured in 1968, with construction contracted in late 1970 — but the project was repeatedly delayed for years because a Taipei City sanitation department vehicle-maintenance garage occupying the site was slow to relocate. Students reportedly shared the unfinished, cramped grounds with garbage trucks for a period in the mid-1970s, until the garage fully vacated in 1976. The school only reached full K–6 grade coverage by 1978, graduating its first sixth-grade class the following year. Its name — "忠孝," loyalty and filial piety — echoes the Confucian-virtue street-naming scheme (Zhongxiao, Ren'ai, Xinyi, Heping) imposed on Taipei's road grid after 1945.

忠孝國小校地於民國57年（1968年）確定，民國59年（1970年）底發包興建，惟因原址上的臺北市政府清潔隊車輛維修車庫遷移緩慢，工程延宕多年。據傳1970年代中期，學生曾在未完工、擁擠的校地上與垃圾車共處一段時間，直至民國65年（1976年）車庫才完全遷出。學校至民國67年（1978年）始達成國小六年級完整學制，翌年首屆六年級畢業。校名「忠孝」呼應1945年後台北市街道命名沿用的儒家倫理命名系統（忠孝、仁愛、信義、和平）。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A name from Taipei's postwar street grid.

🛣️

The school's name echoes the Confucian-virtue street-naming scheme — Zhongxiao, Ren'ai, Xinyi, Heping — imposed on Taipei's road grid after 1945, a naming convention closely associated with Zhongzheng District's postwar administrative planning. See the Taipei county page for more on the region.
