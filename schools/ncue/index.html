About · 學校簡介

From teacher's college to research university.

National Changhua University of Education (NCUE) was formally established in 1971 as the Taiwan Provincial College of Education, at a time when Taiwan needed a dedicated institution to train secondary-school teachers. It was renamed twice — in 1980 and again in 1989 — before taking its current name and status as a national university. Today NCUE spans two campuses near Changhua Railway Station (Jin-De and Bao-Shan) and has grown well beyond its teacher-training roots into a comprehensive research university with seven colleges.

國立彰化師範大學創立於民國60年（1971年），前身為台灣省立教育學院，成立之初肩負培育中等學校師資的任務。歷經兩次改制（1980年、1989年），逐步發展為今日的國立大學。現今彰師大擁有進德、寶山兩個校區，皆鄰近彰化火車站，並已從師資培育學校成長為擁有七個學院的綜合型研究大學。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Seven Colleges

七大學院

NCUE is organized into seven colleges — Education, Arts, Science, Social Science and Physical Education, Management, Technology and Vocational Education, and Engineering.

🍎 Taiwan's Teacher-Training Heritage

師資培育傳統

Its Center for Teacher Education and Professional Development carries forward NCUE's historic role as Taiwan's premier institution for training secondary-school teachers.

🌏 International Master's Programs

全英語碩士學程

NCUE offers International Master's Programs in Engineering and in Materials/Biological Technology and Science Education, alongside dual-degree and exchange pathways for international students.

🏆 Internationally Ranked

國際排名肯定

NCUE appears in the QS World University Rankings (#256 in Eastern Asia) and was ranked #75 worldwide for Quality Education in the 2024 Times Higher Education Impact Rankings.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A campus at the heart of Changhua City.

🚉

Both of NCUE's campuses sit near Changhua Railway Station, making the university an easy stop for visitors exploring the city's historic core, including the nearby Bagua Mountain Great Buddha. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
