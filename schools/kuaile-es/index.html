Happy Elementary School

快樂國民小學

A young Taoyuan District school whose very name was chosen by student vote, and one of the earliest schools in the district certified as a Bilingual Innovation School.

校名由學生票選而來的年輕學校，是桃園區最早獲認證的雙語創新學校之一。

About · 學校簡介

The school students named themselves.

Happy Elementary began in 2003 as a branch campus of Hui-Ji Elementary, called "會稽國小快樂分校," and became an independent school in 2004. Its unusual name was chosen by student vote while it was still a branch campus — a rare origin story among Taiwan's public schools. One of the newest schools in Taoyuan District, it was created purely to absorb residential growth along Dayou Road, illustrating how the district's historic core has kept spinning off brand-new campuses as its population has continued to densify into the 21st century.

快樂國小前身為民國92年（2003年）設立的「會稽國小快樂分校」，於民國93年（2004年）獨立設校。其校名在仍為分校時期即由學生票選產生，在台灣公立學校中相當罕見。作為桃園區最年輕的學校之一，快樂國小的成立純粹是為了因應大有路一帶住宅人口成長，反映出桃園歷史核心區隨人口持續密集化，至21世紀仍不斷分出新校的發展軌跡。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗳️ A Name Chosen by Students

學生票選校名

The school's name, "快樂" (Happy), was chosen by student vote while it was still a branch campus of Hui-Ji Elementary — a distinctive origin story among Taiwan's schools.

🌐 An Early Bilingual Innovation School

雙語創新學校認證

Officially certified as a Taoyuan City "Bilingual Innovation School" since the 2017 school year, with foreign teachers co-teaching PE, drama, and visual arts in lower and middle grades.

🔬 A Bilingual STEAM Curriculum

雙語STEAM課程

The school runs a bilingual STEAM curriculum alongside a wide range of after-school clubs, including soccer, dance, robotics, inline skating, ukulele, and taekwondo.

🌱 One of the District's Youngest Schools

桃園區最年輕的學校之一

Established as an independent school only in 2004, Happy Elementary is among the newest additions to Taoyuan District's public-school map.
