Fugang Elementary School

富岡國民小學

A mid-sized community elementary school at the core of Yangmei's historic "railway town" of Fugang, near the train station and old Hakka street.

About · 學校簡介

The community school of Fugang's railway town.

Fugang Elementary is very likely one of the oldest schools in the area — neighboring Fugang Junior High is known to have split off from an earlier "Fugang branch" of Yangmei Middle School in 1966, suggesting Fugang Elementary itself predates that. As of 2023 the school enrolled about 522 students taught by 35 teachers, and its earlier website featured a dedicated "Fugang Digital School History Hall," a sign of how much the school values preserving its own history, though detailed archival records were not accessible in this research.

富岡國中已知1966年由「楊梅中學富岡分部」獨立設校，富岡國小創校時間應更早，但未查得確切數字。2023年統計約522名學生、35名教師。舊網站設有「富小數位校史館」專頁，顯示學校重視校史保存，但詳細沿革內容未能於本次檢索中完整取得。

👥 A 522-Student Community Anchor

The heart of a lasting railway community.

Located at the core of the famous "Fugang railway town," near the historic train station and old Hakka street, Fugang Elementary is one of the longest-standing community schools in this well-preserved corner of Yangmei District. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
