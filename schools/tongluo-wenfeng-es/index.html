Wenfeng Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

From borrowed classrooms to an independent school.

Wenfeng Elementary began life in 1966 as a three-classroom branch school known as "Zhangshu Branch" (樟樹分校), initially borrowing classroom space from nearby Tongluo Elementary School. Once its own building was completed in February 1967, it became independent as "Wenfeng National School" (文峰國民學校). The school today serves Zhangshu (Camphor Tree) Village, in the heart of Tongluo's Hakka persimmon-drying region.

文峰國小的前身是民國55年（1966年）成立的三間教室分校「樟樹分校」，初期借用鄰近的銅鑼國小教室上課。民國56年（1967年）2月，自有校舍完工後正式獨立設校，更名為「文峰國民學校」。學校現今服務樟樹村社區，座落於銅鑼鄉客家柿餅之鄉的核心地帶。

🌳 Named for the Camphor Tree Village

樟樹村的學校

Located in Zhangshu (Camphor Tree) Village, the school's name and setting reflect a small rural community defined by farming and local place-names.
