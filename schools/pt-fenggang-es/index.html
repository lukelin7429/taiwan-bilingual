About · 學校簡介

At the crossroads of the coastal highway.

Fengkang Elementary School serves Fengkang (“Maple Harbor”), the southernmost of Fangshan Township's three main settlements, sitting at the junction of Provincial Highways 9 and 26 — a historic gateway where the coastal road meets the route into the South Cross-Island Highway corridor toward Taitung. The school operates an attached kindergarten open on weekdays, and has taken part in local folk-song and heritage preservation activities.

楓港國小服務枋山鄉三大聚落中位置最南端的楓港，坐落於台9線與台26線交會的要衝——這裡是濱海公路通往南橫公路走廊、前往台東的歷史門戶。學校附設幼兒園（平日開放），並曾參與地方民謠與文化保存活動。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fengkang, gateway to the South Cross-Island Highway.

🛣️

Fengkang (Maple Harbor) is the southernmost of Fangshan Township's three main settlements, sitting at the junction of Provincial Highways 9 and 26 — the historic gateway where the coastal road meets the route into the South Cross-Island Highway corridor toward Taitung. Like the rest of Fangshan, the area is renowned for Irwin mango cultivation, plus custard apple, wax apple, and onion farming sold roadside during harvest season.
