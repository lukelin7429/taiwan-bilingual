About · 學校簡介

A junior college with a civic, community-health origin.

Tzu Hui Institute of Technology was founded in August 1964 as the Pingtung County Private Tzu Hui Vocational School of Nursing and Midwifery (屏東縣私立慈惠護理助產職業學校) by Li-Yan Tsai, a former Pingtung County councilor, with the explicit goal of improving community health through nursing training. It was upgraded and renamed Tzu Hui Institute of Technology in August 2000. One nuance worth noting for accuracy: despite its English name "Institute of Technology," the school's official Chinese name uses 專科學校 ("junior college"), meaning it is technically a junior/technical college rather than a full bachelor's-degree-granting university — a different tier from the other universities profiled alongside it in this county. The school sits on the border of Donggang and Nanzhou in Nanzhou Township and offers eight teaching departments, including Nursing, Early Childhood Care, and Physical Therapy.

慈惠醫護管理專科學校創立於民國53年（1964年）8月，前身為「屏東縣私立慈惠護理助產職業學校」，由曾任屏東縣議員的蔡李阿延創辦，宗旨為透過護理教育提升地方社區健康。民國89年（2000年）8月改制升格為慈惠醫護管理專科學校，即現今校名。值得留意的是，雖然英文校名譯為「Institute of Technology」，但校名本身即為「專科學校」，屬於專科層級，而非授予學士學位的大學——與本縣其他所介紹的大學屬於不同學制層級。學校位於南州鄉，鄰近東港與南州交界處，設有護理科、幼兒保育科、物理治療科等八個科系。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏥 Eight Teaching Departments

八大科系

Tzu Hui offers eight teaching departments, including Nursing (護理科), Early Childhood Care (幼兒保育科), and Physical Therapy (物理治療科).

🎗️ Community-Founded Mission

地方創辦背景

The school was founded by a former Pingtung County councilor specifically to address local community healthcare training needs — a distinctly civic origin story.

🎓 A Junior College, Not a University

專科學校層級

Despite its English name, Tzu Hui's official Chinese designation is 專科學校 (junior college) — it operates at a different academic tier from the bachelor's-degree-granting universities elsewhere in Pingtung County.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A civic tradition of local, need-driven schools.

🏘️

Tzu Hui's local-culture tie is civic rather than agricultural: it reflects Pingtung's grassroots tradition of locally founded vocational schools addressing community needs — here, nursing and health training initiated by a county councilor — rather than the county's farming economy.
