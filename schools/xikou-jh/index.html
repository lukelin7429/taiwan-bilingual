Xikou Junior High School

溪口國民中學

The only public junior high in Xikou Township, born from a 1960s push to give the small farming township its own secondary school.

溪口鄉唯一的公立國中，源於1960年代地方爭取設立中學的努力。

About · 學校簡介

A secondary school born of local advocacy.

Xikou Junior High began in 1965 as a branch of Minsyong Junior High, became an independent school in August 1967, and took its current name in 1968 under the nine-year compulsory education reform. Guided by the motto "Innovation, Diversity, Adaptability, Excellence," the school runs vocational skills competitions with recent achievements in drone maintenance and invention exhibitions, and it recently completed a gender-friendly restroom design project. School lunches feature domestically sourced pork and beef.

溪口國中於民國54年（1965年）以民雄初級中學溪口分部之姿起步，民國56年（1967年）8月獨立設校，並於民國57年（1968年）九年國教改制後定名為現名。學校秉持「創新、多元、適性、卓越」的辦學理念，積極參與國中技藝競賽，近年在無人機維修與發明展方面表現突出，並完成114年度性別友善廁所設計專案。學校午餐採用國產豬肉與牛肉。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚁 Drone Maintenance Skills

無人機維修技藝

Students compete in the county's vocational skills competition, with recent recognition in drone maintenance and invention-exhibition categories.

🚻 A Gender-Friendly Campus

性別友善廁所設計

The school recently completed a gender-friendly restroom design project, part of a broader push toward an inclusive campus environment.

🎯 Innovation, Diversity, Excellence

Local Culture · 在地文化

Won through years of local advocacy.

🌾

Founded in the mid-1960s as part of the countywide "one town, one junior high" policy, Xikou Junior High reflects the township's identity as a small, historically underserved agricultural community that only gained its own secondary school after years of local advocacy.
