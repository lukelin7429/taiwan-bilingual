Gaolang Elementary School

高朗國小

A public elementary school in Gaolang Village, home to the "Gaolang No.1" jujube, a prized local variety of Indian jujube.

About · 學校簡介

The school of a prized jujube village.

Gaolang Elementary School serves Gaolang Village in Yanpu Township, a village known across Taiwan for producing "Gaolang No.1" jujube (印度棗高郎一號) — a large, premium variety of Indian jujube grown by local farmers alongside mango and flowers.

高朗國小服務鹽埔鄉高朗村，該村以出產「印度棗高郎一號」聞名全台——這是一種果實碩大、品質優良的印度棗品種，當地農民與芒果、花卉一同栽種。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🍎 Home of "Gaolang No.1"

印度棗高郎一號

Gaolang Village, where the school is located, is renowned for growing "Gaolang No.1," a large, premium variety of Indian jujube prized across Taiwan.

🏘️ A Village-Named School

同名村落學校

Sharing its name with Gaolang Village, the school is a small but central part of the local farming community's identity.

🥭 Mango and Flower Country

芒果與花卉之鄉

Beyond its signature jujube, Gaolang Village's farms also grow mango and flowers, part of Yanpu Township's diverse agricultural economy.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The village that grows a famous jujube.

🍇

Gaolang Village is noted for producing "Gaolang No.1" jujube (印度棗高郎一號), a large, premium variety of Indian jujube, alongside mango and flower farming across Yanpu Township. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
