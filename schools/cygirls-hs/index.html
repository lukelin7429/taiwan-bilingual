About · 學校簡介

Chiayi's oldest girls' school, now open to all.

National Chiayi Girls' Senior High School traces its roots to April 1, 1922, when it opened as the “Tainan Prefecture Chiayi Higher Girls' School” under Japanese rule — making it the oldest girls' school in the Chiayi area. It became a provincial institution after 1945 and was elevated to national senior-high status in 2000. In a historic shift, the school began admitting male students for the first time in 2025, with 30 boys enrolled in specialized programs.

國立嘉義女子高級中學創校於民國11年（1922年）4月1日，前身為日治時期的「臺南州立嘉義高等女學校」，是嘉義地區歷史最悠久的女子學校。戰後改為省立學校，並於民國89年（2000年）改制為國立高中。2025年起首度招收男學生，於特定學程中招收30名男學生，寫下學校歷史新頁。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ A Century of History

百年校史

Founded in 1922 as a prefectural girls' school under Japanese rule, the school has educated generations of Chiayi women through a full century of Taiwan's changing eras.

🚻 Opening to Male Students

首度招收男學生

In 2025, the school admitted its first male students — 30 boys in specialized programs — a landmark change after over a hundred years as a girls' school.

🎓 National Senior High Status

改制國立高中

Originally a provincial school after 1945, it was elevated to national senior-high status in 2000, reflecting its standing as one of Chiayi's leading academic institutions.

🌸 A School Motto Built on Character

校訓精神

The school's motto emphasizes sincerity, integrity, humaneness, and continuous self-improvement — values instilled across generations of graduates.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A landmark of Chiayi's education history.
