About · 學校簡介

An award-winning school between old town and Tianmu.

Lanya Junior High School held its first day of classes on September 5, 1977, and takes its name from its location near the historic "Nanya" (湳雅) area of Shilin. Today it runs 42 regular classes plus 7 special classes — including gifted math/science programs — serving roughly 1,100 students, with facilities that include an indoor heated swimming pool. The school was selected for Taipei City's "Spotlight Program" (亮點計畫) starting in December 2016, and won the Ministry of Education's National Teaching Excellence Award (Gold) in 2020.

蘭雅國中於民國66年（1977年）9月5日正式開學，校名源自士林舊地名「湳雅」一帶。目前設有普通班42班、特殊班7班（含數理資優班），學生約1,100人，並設有室內溫水游泳池等設施。學校於民國105年（2016年）12月獲選臺北市「亮點計畫」，並於民國109年（2020年）榮獲教育部教學卓越獎金質獎。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥇 National Teaching Excellence Gold Award

教學卓越獎金質獎

Lanya won the Ministry of Education's National Teaching Excellence Award (Gold) in 2020, one of the top honors in Taiwan's secondary education system.

🏊 An Indoor Heated Pool

室內溫水游泳池

Among a large campus of 42 regular classes and 7 special classes, Lanya's facilities include an indoor heated swimming pool serving roughly 1,100 students.

✨ A Taipei "Spotlight Program" School

臺北市亮點計畫

Selected for Taipei City's "Spotlight Program" starting in December 2016, recognizing the school's distinctive programs and achievements.

🏘️ Named for a Historic Place

湳雅舊地名

The school is named for its location near the historic "Nanya" (湳雅) area, a name that lives on in nearby Lanya Elementary School as well.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Between Old Street and Tianmu.

🏘️

Located in the Lanya (Nanya) area between Shilin's old town and Tianmu, the school sits in a dense residential zone that grew up as the district urbanized outward from its historic Old Street core toward the more international Tianmu neighborhood. See the Taipei county page for more on the region.
