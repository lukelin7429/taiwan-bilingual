Chongde Junior High School

崇德國民中學

A public junior high school in Huwei Town, home to an award-winning choir and band near the town's sugar-refinery heritage zone.

About · 學校簡介

A Huwei school with a strong musical voice.

Chongde Junior High School was officially established on August 1, 1970, in Lianshi Village, Huwei Town. Over the decades it has built a reputation well beyond academics, with its choir taking first place at the national Hakka folk song competition and its band and flag team earning excellence awards at a national music competition — a track record that has made performing arts a defining part of student life here.

崇德國中創立於民國59年（1970年）8月1日，校址位於虎尾鎮廉使里。多年來，學校在課業之外也建立起亮眼的音樂成績：合唱團曾在全國客家歌謠比賽中奪得第一名，管樂團與旗隊也在全國音樂比賽中榮獲優等獎，讓表演藝術成為校園生活的重要特色。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Growing up beside the sugar refinery.

🏭

Chongde Junior High sits in Lianshi Village, close to Huwei's historic sugar-refinery heritage zone. The Huwei Sugar Refinery has long anchored the township's economy, and the school's own growth mirrors the industrial-era expansion the refinery brought to the surrounding community.
