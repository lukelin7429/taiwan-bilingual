About · 學校簡介

From Qing-dynasty Nanjing to the Sun Moon Lake foothills.

National Chi Nan University (NCNU) traces its roots to 1906, when 暨南學堂 was founded in Nanjing during the late Qing Dynasty as one of China's earliest schools for overseas Chinese students. The institution's name and mission were revived in Taiwan in 1995 (ROC 84), when the university was re-established on a 150-hectare campus in Puli Township, Nantou County — making it, over a century after its founding, the county's only university. Today NCNU serves about 6,556 students, including 4,802 undergraduates and 1,754 graduate students, and is ranked among Taiwan's leading universities for research output.

國立暨南國際大學的歷史可追溯至1906年（清光緒年間），當時「暨南學堂」於南京創立，是中國最早招收僑生的學校之一。這段歷史與精神於民國84年（1995年）在台灣復校延續，校址選定於南投縣埔里鎮，佔地150公頃，也讓暨大成為南投縣唯一的大學。現今暨大擁有約6,556名學生，其中大學部4,802人、研究所1,754人，並在台灣各大學研究表現排名中名列前茅。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Six Colleges

六大學院

NCNU is organized into six colleges — Humanities, Education, Science and Technology, Management, the Water Shalien College, and Nursing and Health & Welfare — spanning a broad academic range for a mid-sized national university.

🏞️ A 150-Hectare Mountain Campus

150公頃校園

The Puli campus spans 150 hectares in the foothills near Sun Moon Lake and the Bagua Mountain range, one of the largest and most scenic university grounds in Taiwan.

📜 A Century-Old Lineage

百年歷史脈絡

NCNU's institutional roots reach back to 1906, when 暨南學堂 was established in Nanjing to educate overseas Chinese students — a legacy the university carries into its modern research mission.

🔬 Strong Research Output

研究表現優異

Despite its relatively small size and rural setting, NCNU is consistently ranked among Taiwan's top universities for research output across its six colleges.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring academic life near Sun Moon Lake.

🏔️

As the county's only university, NCNU anchors Puli Township's academic and cultural life in the Sun Moon Lake foothills region near the Bagua Mountain range — a setting that has made the campus and the surrounding town a hub for students and researchers across central Taiwan.
