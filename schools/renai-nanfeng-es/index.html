About · 學校簡介

A tribal school for the Seediq community.

Nanfeng Elementary identifies as a tribal education institution serving the Seediq community of Ren'ai Township. The school's website features dedicated "Seediq Culture" sections covering language and traditional skills, reflecting a curriculum built around preserving and passing on Seediq heritage. Students take part in archery and Indigenous cultural heritage competitions, carrying these traditions into active, hands-on practice.

南豐國小定位為服務賽德克族社群的民族教育學校。校方網站設有專屬「賽德克文化」專區，涵蓋族語與傳統技藝教學，課程設計以保存與傳承賽德克文化為核心。學生並參與射箭與原住民文化傳承相關競賽，將傳統技藝落實於實際練習中。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Carrying forward Seediq culture and history.

🏹

A Seediq tribal school in Ren'ai Township, Nanfeng actively teaches Seediq language and traditional archery, carrying forward the culture of the people central to the 1930 Wushe Incident — a defining, still-resonant chapter in the community's history of resistance and resilience.
