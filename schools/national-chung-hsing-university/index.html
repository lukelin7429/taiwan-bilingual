National Chung Hsing University

國立中興大學

One of Taiwan's oldest and top public research universities, renowned for agriculture, forestry and life sciences, spanning 11 colleges including Veterinary Medicine and Medicine.

About · 學校簡介

From an agronomy academy to an eleven-college research university.

National Chung Hsing University traces its roots to 1919, when it was founded during the Japanese colonial era as an agronomy and forestry academy. It became a national university in 1971. Today NCHU is one of Taiwan's oldest and top public research universities, renowned for its programs in agriculture, forestry, and life sciences, and spans 11 colleges — including Veterinary Medicine and Medicine — with more than 100 courses taught in English for its roughly 16,000 students.

國立中興大學創立於1919年（日治時期），前身為農林專門學校。民國60年（1971年）升格為國立大學。現今中興大學是台灣歷史最悠久、頂尖的公立研究型大學之一，以農業、林業及生命科學研究見長，設有十一個學院——包括獸醫學院與醫學院——並開設逾百門全英語授課課程，服務約16,000名學生。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🌾 A Century of Agricultural Research

百年農業研究傳統

Founded in 1919 as an agronomy and forestry academy, NCHU remains one of Taiwan's leading centers for agriculture, forestry, and life-sciences research.

🏥 Eleven Colleges, Including Medicine & Veterinary Medicine

十一學院涵蓋醫學與獸醫

NCHU spans 11 colleges, a breadth unusual for a university with agricultural roots — including full colleges of Veterinary Medicine and Medicine.

🌏 100+ English-Taught Courses

逾百門全英語課程

More than 100 courses are taught in English, supporting the international students who study at this national research university.

🎓 A National University Since 1971

1971年升格國立大學

NCHU reached national-university status in 1971, building on more than a century of institutional history that stretches back to 1919.
