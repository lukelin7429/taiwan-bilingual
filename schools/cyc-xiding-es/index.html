Siding Elementary School

隙頂國民小學

A tiny high-mountain school on the Alishan Highway, sited by a lottery in 1949 and known today for its sea-of-clouds views and tea-arts certification program.

位於阿里山公路上的迷你高山小學，1949年以抽籤方式選定校址，如今以雲海美景與茶藝認證課程聞名。

About · 學校簡介

Sited by a coin toss, high on the Alishan road.

Siding Elementary was established in 1949, after a site competition between the Siding and Longtou communities was resolved by lottery — Siding residents agreed to build and maintain the connecting road to Longtou in exchange for the school being sited on their land. Today it remains a very small rural school: as of the 2025 school year, it enrolls only 48 elementary students across grades 1 through 6, plus 14 kindergarteners. Its school motto, "隙間鍾秀氣 頂上育英才," reflects its perch high on the mountain.

隙頂國小創立於民國38年（1949年），當年隙頂與龍頭兩個社區為爭取設校地點以抽籤方式決定，隙頂居民並承諾修築、維護通往龍頭的聯絡道路以換取設校。至今仍是規模極小的山區學校：114學年度全校僅48名國小學生（一至六年級），另有14名幼兒園學生。校訓「隙間鍾秀氣 頂上育英才」，正呼應了學校座落山頂的地理特色。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🍵 A Certified Tea-Arts Program

茶藝認證課程

The school offers a certified tea-arts program (茶藝認證) that directly reflects the high-mountain tea industry surrounding the campus.

☁️ Sea-of-Clouds Scenery

著名雲海景點

Siding is one of the most famous sea-of-clouds (雲海) viewpoints on the Alishan Highway, giving the school one of the most scenic settings of any elementary school in Chiayi County.

🤝 A School Built on a Community Bargain

抽籤定校址的歷史

The school's very location traces to a 1949 lottery between two competing communities, with Siding residents agreeing to build a connecting road in exchange for hosting the school.

Local Culture · 在地文化

High-mountain tea and clouds.

🍃

Siding is one of the most famous tea- and cloud-viewing spots on the Alishan Highway, located in Gongtian Village at high elevation on the approach road to Alishan. The school's tea-arts certification program directly reflects the surrounding high-mountain tea (高山茶) plantations that define the local economy. See the Chiayi County page for more on the region.
