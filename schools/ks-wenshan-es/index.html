About · 學校簡介

Seventy years of steady growth.

Wenshan Elementary School broke ground in December 1952 and formally opened in 1953. Over more than seven decades, the school has been led by 14 principals to date, and today it serves a community of more than 500 students across 21 regular classes, plus special education, resource, and kindergarten classes. Its name, "文山" ("culture/mountain"), places it within a postwar wave of Fengshan schools drawing on Confucian and literary naming traditions.

文山國小於民國41年（1952年）12月動土，民國42年（1953年）正式創校。逾七十年來，歷經14任校長，如今全校學生逾500人，共21個普通班，另設有特教班、資源班與幼兒園班。校名「文山」延續了鳳山戰後一批以儒家、文學傳統命名學校的風氣。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Fengshan's postwar naming wave.

📖

Wenshan Elementary is one of a cluster of Fengshan schools whose names begin with "文" (culture/literature) — 文山, 文德, and 文華 — reflecting the postwar school-building boom that shaped this part of the district. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
