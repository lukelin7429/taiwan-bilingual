About · 學校簡介

A school woven into a historic fishing village.

Neihu Junior High is located in the historic Neihu coastal community of Xiangshan District, a traditional Minnan (Hoklo) fishing and oyster-farming settlement (its affiliated elementary school dates to 1918, reflecting the area's long educational history). The school has built a school-based curriculum theme called "閱讀玩家" (Reading Player), centered on local Neihu history, land, and people. Old Neihu Street nearby still preserves heritage "over-street buildings," a historic doctor's house, and Minnan-style architecture that reflect the area's former prosperity.

內湖國中位於香山區歷史悠久的內湖社區，這裡是傳統的閩南漁村與蚵農聚落（鄰近的內湖國小創校可追溯至民國7年〔1918年〕，反映此地深厚的教育歷史）。學校發展出以「閱讀玩家」為主題的校本課程，內容圍繞內湖在地的歷史、土地與人物。附近的內湖老街至今仍保留著「亭仔腳」街屋、歷史悠久的醫生宅第，以及閩南式建築，見證這片土地昔日的繁榮風貌。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📖 A "Reading Player" Local Heritage Curriculum

閱讀玩家校本課程

The school's signature "閱讀玩家" (Reading Player) curriculum uses local Neihu history, land, and people as its central teaching theme, connecting classroom learning directly to the community outside.

🦪 In a Historic Fishing and Oyster Village

閩南漁村蚵農聚落

Neihu Junior High sits within a traditional Minnan (Hoklo) fishing and oyster-farming settlement, giving students a living connection to one of Hsinchu's older coastal industries.

🏘️ Near a Heritage Old Street

鄰近內湖老街

Old Neihu Street, near the school, still preserves heritage "over-street buildings," a historic doctor's house, and Minnan-style architecture reflecting the area's former prosperity.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Teaching through Neihu's fishing-village identity.

🦪

The school's curriculum is built around the identity of Neihu's historic Minnan fishing and oyster-farming village, using local heritage — including the nearby Old Neihu Street with its heritage buildings — as a teaching theme. See the Hsinchu City page for more on the region.
