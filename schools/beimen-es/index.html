Beimen Elementary School

桃園市桃園區北門國民小學

Named for the historic North Gate of old walled Taoyuan City, this public elementary school runs five signature curricular tracks and a wide range of after-school clubs.

About · 學校簡介

Five tracks, one heartfelt vision.

Beimen Elementary was founded in 1985. The school runs five signature curricular tracks — character education, local history and culture, science, art, and international education — and offers a wide range of after-school clubs, including Go, inline skating, diabolo, electronic keyboard, taekwondo, robotics, soccer, dominoes, dance, and martial arts. As of the 2022/2023 school year, the school had about 733 students, and its vision statement — "愛在北門、從心啟航" (Love at Beimen, setting sail from the heart) — captures its student-centered approach.

北門國小創立於民國74年（1985年）。學校開設五大特色課程——品格教育、鄉土文化、科學、藝術與國際教育，並提供豐富多元的社團活動，包括圍棋、直排輪、扯鈴、電子琴、跆拳道、機器人、足球、骨牌、舞蹈與武術等。2022至2023學年度資料顯示，全校約733名學生。學校願景「愛在北門、從心啟航」，展現以學生為中心的教育理念。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for the old city's North Gate.

🚪

Its name, "Beimen" (North Gate), directly recalls the North Gate of old walled Taoyuan City, one of the four historic gates — along with the South Gate, home to nearby Nanmen Elementary — that once marked the boundary of the old town center around today's Jingfu Temple and downtown commercial streets.
