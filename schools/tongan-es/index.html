About · 學校簡介

A branch of Nanzhou's educational family tree.

Tong'an Elementary School split off from Nanzhou Elementary School's original catchment area around 1958, as the older school's territory — once spanning eight villages — was divided to bring education closer to more of Nanzhou Township's farming communities. The school is recognized by the Pingtung County Education Department as a "concept/feature school" (理念特色學校), reflecting a distinct educational approach within the county's rural elementary system.

同安國小約於民國47年（1958年）自南州國小原學區分出設立，當時南州國小學區原涵蓋8個村里，經劃分後讓南州鄉更多農業社區的孩童能就近就學。本校獲屏東縣教育處列為「理念特色學校」，在縣內鄉村國小教育體系中具有特色定位。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Sugar-cane roots, watermelon present.

🍉

Nanzhou Township's economy has long revolved around sugar-cane cultivation and the historic Taisugar sugar refinery that once anchored the community (then known as Xizhou). Today, the fields around Tong'an Elementary are just as likely to be growing watermelon, one of the township's key modern crops. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
