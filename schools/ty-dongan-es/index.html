About · 學校簡介

An airship-branded school with big ambitions.

Dong'an Elementary sits in the 官路缺 (Guanluque) area of Pingzhen, the same neighborhood as its sister institution Dong'an Junior High, tied to the same historic official-road place name in the district's inland geography. The school's motto and slogan — "東安飛行船啟動，航向世界！" ("Dong'an Airship, launch — sailing toward the world!") — captures an outward-looking educational spirit. Students have recently been recognized in the Pingzhen District language competition, the 66th Taoyuan City Elementary/Middle School Science Exhibition, tooth-brushing observation contests, and unicycling championships, and the school runs a health-promotion "center school" initiative covering tobacco and betel-nut harm prevention and healthy body weight.

東安國小位於平鎮區官路缺地區，與姊妹校東安國中同屬一地，地名源自平鎮內陸地理中一條歷史悠久的官道。學校的校訓標語「東安飛行船啟動，航向世界！」展現出向外拓展的教育精神。學生近年在平鎮區語文競賽、第66屆桃園市中小學科學展覽會、潔牙觀摩賽及獨輪車錦標賽中屢獲佳績，學校並推動健康促進中心學校計畫，涵蓋菸檳防制與健康體位等議題。
