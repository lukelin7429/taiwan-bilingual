About · 學校簡介

A name that spans two districts.

Xintai Junior High School opened in 1978 on the boundary between Xinzhuang and neighboring Taishan District — a location reflected in its name, which fuses "新" from Xinzhuang and "泰" from Taishan. Known informally as the "orchid campus," the school has built a reputation for bilingual-campus initiatives and reading and digital-learning programs, alongside varsity teams in baseball, taekwondo, and tennis.

新泰國中創立於民國67年（1978年），校址位於新莊與泰山兩區交界，校名即取「新」莊與「泰」山二字而成。學校有「蘭花校園」之美稱，推動雙語校園、閱讀與數位學習等計畫，並設有棒球、跆拳道、網球代表隊。
