Zhuangwei Junior High School

壯圍國民中學

A public junior high school in the heart of Zhuangwei's coastal farmland, home to a competitive sports program and a technology center serving the wider township.

座落於壯圍鄉沿海農業地帶核心的公立國中，設有體育班，並附設服務全鄉的「壯圍科技中心」。

About · 學校簡介

A campus for the whole township to share.

Zhuangwei Junior High School was founded in 1957 to serve the growing farming communities of Zhuangwei Township on Yilan's coastal plain. Beyond its regular classes, the school runs a competitive sports class that recruits and trains student-athletes, and it hosts the Zhuangwei Technology Center, a resource open to the wider township rather than just its own students. Its guiding motto — "warm campus, health & safety, diverse development" — sums up a school that sees itself as a hub for the whole community, not just its enrolled students.

壯圍國中創立於民國46年（1957年），服務宜蘭沿海平原上逐漸發展的壯圍鄉農業社區。除一般班級外，學校設有體育班，招募並培訓學生運動員，並附設「壯圍科技中心」，開放給全鄉居民使用，而不僅限於在校學生。校訓「溫馨校園、健康安全、多元發展」正說明了這所學校自我定位為服務全鄉的教育樞紐。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏃 A Competitive Sports Class

體育班培訓計畫

The school actively recruits and trains student-athletes through a dedicated sports class, giving young competitors from the township a path to develop their talent.

💻 A Technology Hub for the Township

壯圍科技中心

The school hosts the Zhuangwei Technology Center, extending its facilities and programs beyond its own students to serve the broader community.

🌉 Near the Zhuangwei Bridge

鄰近壯圍橋

The campus sits close to the Zhuangwei Bridge, at the center of the township's flat rice- and peanut-farming landscape.

🌱 A Motto of Balance

溫馨校園、健康安全、多元發展

The school's guiding motto — a warm campus, health and safety, and diverse development — reflects its holistic approach to raising well-rounded students.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Zhuangwei's coastal farmland.

🥜

Zhuangwei Township is a flat, coastal stretch of the Lanyang Plain known for its rice and peanut farming. The school's location near the Zhuangwei Bridge places it at the heart of this agricultural community. See the Yilan county page for more on the region.
