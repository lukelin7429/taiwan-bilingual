About · 學校簡介

The township's flagship elementary school.

Mingjian Elementary School is the central elementary school of Mingjian Township, with roughly 45 teachers, 4 staff, and about 466 students. Located at the heart of the township, the school maintains a dedicated environmental-education program alongside its general curriculum, serving as the flagship school for the community's youngest students.

名間國小是名間鄉的中心國小，約有45位教師、4位職員、466名學生。學校座落於鄉的中心地帶，除一般課程外，另設有專屬的環境教育計畫，作為社區最年幼學子的旗艦型學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 The Township's Central School

名間鄉中心國小

As the flagship elementary school of Mingjian proper, the school serves roughly 466 students with a staff of about 45 teachers and 4 support staff.

🌿 A Dedicated Environmental Program

專屬環境教育計畫

Mingjian Elementary maintains its own environmental-education program, giving students hands-on engagement with sustainability alongside their regular studies.

📍 At the Heart of the Township

座落鄉中心地帶

The school's central location places it at the geographic and civic heart of Mingjian Township, serving as a focal point for the community's families.

🍵 In Taiwan's Tea Capital

台灣茶鄉核心

Situated in Taiwan's largest tea-growing township, the school's students grow up surrounded by the Songbolang tea gardens that define the local economy.

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the center of Songbolang tea country.

🍃

As the flagship elementary school of Mingjian proper, this school sits at the center of Taiwan's largest tea-growing township, famous for Songbolang evergreen tea. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
