About · 學校簡介

A small junior high for Dahu's farming families.

Nanhu Junior High began in 1970 as the "Nanhu Branch" of Dahu Junior High School, becoming fully independent in 1974. With around 113 students, it is a small school that draws its student body from a wide catchment of six elementary schools — including Nanhu, Lilin, Dongxing, Wurong, and Zhuolan's Jingshan and Pinglin — serving mostly the children of hardworking local farming families in Taiwan's strawberry heartland.

南湖國中前身為民國59年（1970年）成立的「大湖國中南湖分校」，於民國63年（1974年）正式獨立設校。全校約113名學生，招生範圍廣及南湖、栗林、東興、五榮，以及卓蘭的景山、坪林等六所國小畢業生，學生多來自辛勤耕作的在地農家。
