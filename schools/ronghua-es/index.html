About · 學校簡介

A small school in Jianxing Village.

Ronghua Elementary serves Jianxing Village, a small farming community in Neipu Township's "Rear Pile" of the Liudui Hakka region. As of 2022 statistics, the school had about 60 students and 12 teachers — a roughly 1-to-5 teacher-student ratio that reflects the broader rural depopulation trend affecting small Liudui-area villages, while allowing for closely personalized instruction.

榮華國小服務內埔鄉建興村，這是六堆客家「後堆」內的一個小型農村社區。根據2022年統計資料，學校約有60名學生與12位教師，師生比約為1比5，反映出六堆地區鄉村人口外流的普遍趨勢，也讓學校得以提供更貼近個別學生的教學。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A small farming village in the Rear Pile.

🏮

Ronghua Elementary serves Jianxing Village, a small farming community in Neipu Township — the "Hou Dui" (後堆, Rear Pile) of the Liudui Hakka confederation. The school's modest enrollment reflects the broader rural depopulation trend affecting Liudui-area villages, even as the community's Hakka farming heritage endures.
