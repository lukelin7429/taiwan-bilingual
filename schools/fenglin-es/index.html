About · 學校簡介

At the heart of the "hometown of principals."

Fenglin Elementary traces its history back to around 1920, during the Japanese colonial era, making it more than a century old and one of the oldest schools in Fenglin Township. The school fields active karate, taekwondo, and soft-tennis teams with strong community engagement. It sits at the heart of a town celebrated nationwide as the "hometown of principals" — Fenglin has produced more than 100 alumni who went on to become school principals across Taiwan, a legacy now commemorated at the nearby "Principal's Dream Factory" museum.

鳳林國小校史可追溯至約民國9年（1920年）日治時期，逾百年歷史，是鳳林鎮歷史最悠久的學校之一。學校設有活躍的空手道、跆拳道與軟式網球隊，深獲社區支持。學校座落於以「校長之鄉」聞名全台的鳳林鎮核心地帶——鳳林鎮已培育出逾百位校友日後擔任台灣各地學校校長，這份傳承如今在鎮上的「校長夢工廠」文物館中被永久紀念。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fenglin, the hometown of principals.

🏆

Fenglin Township is celebrated across Taiwan as the "hometown of principals" (校長之鄉) — an unusually high concentration of its sons and daughters went on to lead schools nationwide, a legacy honored at the town's own "Principal's Dream Factory" (校長夢工廠) museum. Fenglin Elementary sits right at the center of that celebrated Hakka farming-town identity.
