About · 學校簡介

From a Japanese-era agricultural school to a national technical campus.

Taoyuan Nonggong traces its roots to 1938, when it was founded under Japanese rule as the "Shinchiku Prefectural Taoyuan Agricultural School." It became a national (國立) institution in 2000, and in 2016 was brought under the wing of National Taipei University of Technology, giving it its current name. The campus is unusually large, spanning roughly 251,588 square meters and dotted with historic sculptures and monuments, and as of 2017 the school enrolled about 2,772 students across 83 classes. It offers five major vocational tracks — agriculture, mechanical, chemical, electrical, and service/commerce — and has been led by principal Yang Qing-shan since August 2024.

桃園農工創校於1938年日治時期，最初名為「新竹州立桃園農業學校」。民國89年（2000年）改制為國立學校，民國105年（2016年）併入國立臺北科技大學體系，成為現名。校地廣達約251,588平方公尺，園區內留有多座歷史雕塑與紀念物；2017年資料顯示全校約有2,772名學生、83個班級，開設農業、機械、化工、電機與服務類等五大職業群科。現任校長為楊青山，自2024年8月上任。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚜 Deep Vocational Roots

深厚的職業教育根基

Founded in 1938 as an agricultural school, the campus now runs five major vocational tracks — agriculture, mechanical, chemical, electrical, and service/commerce — reflecting nearly a century of technical education.

🏛️ A University-Affiliated Campus

科技大學附屬校園

Nationalized in 2000 and affiliated with National Taipei University of Technology in 2016, the school gives students a direct pathway between vocational senior-high training and higher technical education.

🌳 One of Taoyuan's Largest Campuses

桃園區規模最大校園之一

The school's grounds cover roughly 251,588 square meters — dotted with historic sculptures and monuments — making it one of the largest school sites in Taoyuan District.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A living record of Taoyuan's farming past.

🌾

Originally established as a Japanese-era agricultural school, Taoyuan Nonggong's very founding is a reminder that today's built-up Taoyuan District — now the administrative and commercial core of Taoyuan City — was once the farming heartland its students trained to work.
