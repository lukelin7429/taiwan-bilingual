About · 學校簡介

A small school beside a living wetland.

Gangnan Elementary opened in 1965 as a branch of Hulin National School, becoming Hsinchu City Gangnan Elementary School after the city's 1982 upgrade to provincial-city status. It sits near Hsinchu's scenic 17-kilometer coastal bikeway and a popular bird-watching area, with unique coastal wetland ecology — mangroves, fiddler crabs, mudskippers and egrets — that the school uses directly for ecological education. With about 6 classes and 120 students, Gangnan recently marked its 58th anniversary with a major renovation project that rebuilt its old campus buildings. Its attached kindergarten specializes in environmental education, extending that same outdoor-learning spirit to its youngest students.

港南國小於民國54年（1965年）以虎林國民學校分校身分設立，新竹市於1982年升格省轄市後改為現名。學校鄰近新竹17公里海岸自行車道及賞鳥勝地，擁有紅樹林、招潮蟹、彈塗魚與白鷺等獨特濱海濕地生態，並直接作為生態教育教材。目前約有6班、120名學生，並於創校58週年之際完成舊校舍拆除重建的大型整修計畫。附設幼兒園以環境教育為特色，將戶外學習精神延伸至最年幼的學生身上。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🦀 A Mangrove Wetland Next Door

紅樹林濕地生態

The school sits beside a coastal wetland home to mangroves, fiddler crabs, mudskippers and egrets — a hands-on ecology lesson within walking distance of the classroom.

🚴 On Hsinchu's 17km Coastal Bikeway

17公里海岸自行車道

Gangnan is close to Hsinchu's popular 17-kilometer coastal bikeway and a bird-watching area, connecting the school to one of the city's best-loved outdoor destinations.

🏗️ A Rebuilt Campus at 58

58週年校舍重建

To mark its 58th anniversary, the school's old buildings were demolished and rebuilt as part of a major renovation project, refreshing the campus for a new generation.

🌱 Environmental Education from Kindergarten

附幼環境教育

Its attached kindergarten specializes in environmental education, giving even the youngest students an early introduction to the wetland ecosystem next door.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xiangshan, Hsinchu's coastal Minnan heartland.

🦪

Gangnan Elementary is embedded in one of Xiangshan District's coastal wetland fishing communities, where Minnan fishing and oyster-farming traditions meet a rich tidal ecosystem of mangroves and mudflats. The district's historic "mother school," Xiangshan Elementary, was founded in 1908 through the philanthropy of a local gentry family. See the Hsinchu City county page for more on the region.
