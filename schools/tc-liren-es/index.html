About · 學校簡介

A school born from a growing neighborhood.

Liren Elementary School was established in 1988 to serve North District's continuing population growth. In its first year, the school borrowed classrooms from nearby Jianxing Elementary School while its own campus was under construction, before moving into its permanent home in 1989. The school's founding reflects North District's ongoing postwar pattern of subdividing school catchments as neighborhoods grew denser.

立人國小創立於民國77年（1988年），為服務北區持續成長的人口而設立。創校首年，學校借用鄰近健行國小教室上課，直至民國78年（1989年）新校舍落成後才遷入現址。學校的成立延續了北區戰後隨社區發展逐步細分學區的模式。
