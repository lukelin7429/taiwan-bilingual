Nanmen Junior High School

臺北市立南門國民中學

A large public junior high near the Botanical Garden, built on the site of a Japanese-era school and a postwar police academy.

座落於植物園附近的大型公立國中，校地前身歷經日治時期學校與戰後警察學校。

About · 學校簡介

A campus with a layered history.

Nanmen Junior High School opened in September 1968. Its campus was originally the site of the Japanese-era "Chengnan Elementary School" for Japanese children living south of Taipei's old city wall; after 1945 the site served for roughly two decades as the Taiwan Provincial Police School before being reclaimed by the city for the new junior high. The large campus — over 9,600 ping — grew in stages from 1968 to 1980 to include six classroom buildings (named Zhongxiao, Ren'ai, Xinyi, Heping, Dade, and Qinyun) plus an activity center. Alongside general classes, the school offers dedicated music-class and PE-class tracks. A same-address elementary school, South Gate Elementary, shared administration with it until splitting into its own independent school in 1989.

南門國中創立於民國57年（1968年）9月。校地原為日治時期專供日籍學童就讀的「城南小學校」，1945年後曾作為台灣省警察學校使用近二十年，後由市府收回興建國中。校園規模逾9,600坪，1968年至1980年間分階段興建忠孝、仁愛、信義、和平、達德、琴韻六棟教學大樓及活動中心。本校除一般班級外，另設有音樂班與體育班。與本校同址的南門國小曾與本校同一校長共同管理，直至1989年才獨立設校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Century of Layered Use

校地歷史層層堆疊

The campus site moved from a Japanese-era elementary school to the Taiwan Provincial Police School for roughly two decades after 1945, before becoming a junior high in 1968.

🏛️ Six Named Classroom Buildings

六棟教學大樓

Built in stages from 1968 to 1980 across more than 9,600 ping, the campus includes six classroom buildings — Zhongxiao, Ren'ai, Xinyi, Heping, Dade, and Qinyun — plus an activity center.

🎵 Dedicated Music and PE Tracks

音樂班與體育班

Alongside its general classes, the school offers dedicated music-class and PE-class tracks for students pursuing focused training in those areas.

🤝 A Shared History with Nanmen Elementary

與南門國小同源

Nanmen Junior High shared one principal and administration with the same-address Nanmen Elementary School until the elementary school became fully independent in 1989.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in the old South Gate neighborhood.

🚪

Built on the grounds of a former police academy in the old "South Gate" (南門) neighborhood near the Botanical Garden, the school reflects the area's layered use — Qing-era city gate, Japanese-era school, postwar police training ground, then modern junior high.
