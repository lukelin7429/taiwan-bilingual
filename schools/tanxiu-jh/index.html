Tanxiu Junior High School

潭秀國民中學

A junior high school founded in 1992 so Tanzi's children would no longer have to cross a rail line and highway to reach class.

創校於民國81年，讓潭子的孩子不必再穿越鐵路與公路才能上學。

About · 學校簡介

Founded for safety, quickly proven in competition.

Tanxiu Junior High opened in 1992 to relieve overcrowding at Tanzi Junior High as the district industrialized and grew, and specifically so that children no longer had to cross a rail line and highway that cut through Tanzi just to reach school. Only a year after opening, the school hosted the 1993 Taiwan Region Middle School Athletic Meet.

潭秀國中創立於民國81年（1992年），設校目的是紓解隨著潭子地區工業化而日益擁擠的潭子國中，同時讓孩童不必再穿越貫穿潭子的鐵路與公路才能到校。開校僅一年，學校便主辦了民國82年（1993年）台灣區中學運動會。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Built alongside Tanzi's industrial growth.

🛤️

Tanxiu Junior High's founding is directly tied to Tanzi's industrialization in the early 1990s, an era when the district's growing population and its dangerous rail-and-highway crossing prompted a new school to be built for local children's safety.
