Xiulin Elementary School

秀林國民小學

Once on the brink of closure with only about 20 students, this small school reinvented itself through unicycling and became a nationally featured "miracle school."

About · 學校簡介

A "miracle school" turnaround, one wheel at a time.

Xiulin Elementary nearly faced closure before 2015, when enrollment had dropped to only about 20 students. A determined principal turned the school around by making unicycling its signature program, drawing new interest from families well beyond the local catchment area. Today the school enrolls about 76 students, with 59 of them commuting in from outside the neighborhood — a remarkable reversal that was featured in CommonWealth Magazine as a "miracle school" turnaround story.

秀林國小在民國104年（2015年）之前一度僅剩約20名學生，瀕臨裁併危機。校長以推廣獨輪車作為學校特色，成功吸引學區外家庭的關注。如今學校在學人數已回升至約76人，其中59人是從學區外通勤而來，這段翻轉歷程曾獲《天下雜誌》以「奇蹟小學」為題專文報導。
