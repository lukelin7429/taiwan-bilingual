Songlin Elementary School

松林國民小學

A 21st-century Xinfeng Township school built to ease overcrowding, standing on the historic site of a century-old brick-kiln industry.

About · 學校簡介

A new school on old industrial ground.

Songlin Elementary School was established in 2003 and began enrolling students in 2005, created specifically to relieve overcrowding at nearby Shanqi Elementary School as the local population grew. The school's site has a distinctive industrial past: it formerly hosted red-brick kiln factories dating back over a century, an industry that boomed from 1931 onward thanks to the nearby Xinfeng train station. Today, Songlin stands as a modern school built on ground that once powered the area's early 20th-century economy.

松林國小成立於民國92年（2003年），並於民國94年（2005年）開始招生，設校目的是為紓解鄰近山崎國小因當地人口成長所產生的過度擁擠問題。學校校地擁有獨特的產業歷史——此地曾是超過百年歷史的紅磚窯廠所在地，該產業自昭和6年（1931年）起因鄰近新豐車站而蓬勃發展。如今，松林國小是一所建於曾經帶動當地20世紀初期經濟的土地上的現代化學校。
