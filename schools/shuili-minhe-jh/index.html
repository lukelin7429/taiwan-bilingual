About · 學校簡介

An independent school for Minhe Village.

Minhe Junior High School was established as an independent junior high on August 1, 1977. It serves Minhe Village on the outskirts of Shuili Township, a community historically tied to the timber and logging trade that once moved through the township toward the Checheng depot. The school maintains active choir and taekwondo teams.

民和國中於1977年（民國66年）8月1日獨立設校，服務水里鄉外圍的民和村，這裡的社區歷史與早年經車埕集散地轉運的木業貿易密不可分。學校設有活躍的合唱團與跆拳道隊。
