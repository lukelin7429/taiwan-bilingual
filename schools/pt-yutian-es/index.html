Yutian Elementary School

玉田國民小學

A public elementary school in Yutian Village, now paired with a major new non-profit kindergarten built to serve the surrounding farming community.

位於玉田村的公立國小，校內新建大型公共化幼兒園，服務周邊農業社區的幼兒。

Grades1–6 · Elementary

About · 學校簡介

A campus expanding to serve its youngest neighbors.

Yutian Elementary serves a school district covering Yutian, Chaocun, and Daping villages, where most residents work in agriculture — distinct from a same-named school in Yilan County. Around 2023–24, a new non-profit (公共化) kindergarten was completed on campus: a two-story building with 10 classrooms, built on an NT$82.69 million budget, with capacity for 6 classes and 152 children aged 2 through pre-K, operated by the Taiwan Early Childhood Education Professional Promotion Association.

玉田國小學區涵蓋玉田、朝村、大平等村，居民多以農業為生——與宜蘭縣同名的玉田國小並非同一所學校。約在2023至2024年間，校內完成一座新建的公共化幼兒園：兩層樓建築、共10間教室，斥資新台幣8,269萬元興建，可容納6班、152名2歲至學齡前幼兒，由台灣幼兒教育專業促進協會承辦營運。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏗️ A Major New Kindergarten

公共化幼兒園新建工程

A new two-story, 10-classroom non-profit kindergarten was completed on campus around 2023–24, built on an NT$82.69 million budget with room for 152 young children.

👶 Serving Ages 2 Through Pre-K

2歲至學齡前

The new kindergarten facility, operated by the Taiwan Early Childhood Education Professional Promotion Association, extends the school's reach to children as young as age 2.

🗺️ A Three-Village School District

跨玉田、朝村、大平三村

The school district covers Yutian, Chaocun, and Daping villages, where residents are mostly engaged in agriculture, making the school a shared anchor for these farming communities.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Investing in the next generation of farm families.

🌾

Located in Yutian Village, within the agricultural plains adjacent to Cishan known for pig's-knuckle, betel-nut, and rice farming, Yutian Elementary's new kindergarten reflects a broader investment in early childhood education for rural communities — ensuring that farming families in Ligang Township have access to quality early care close to home.
