Shizi Junior High School

獅子國中

About · 學校簡介

Independent since 1970, between sea and mountains.

Shizi Junior High School became an independent school in 1970, having previously operated as the Shizi branch campus of Mudan Junior High. Its campus, spanning 1.608 hectares, sits beside Fengkang Creek along Provincial Highway 9, close to both sea and mountains, near the entrance to the South Cross-Island Highway route toward Taitung and not far from Kenting National Park. The school has had 14 principals since gaining independence, and lies within Paiwan indigenous traditional territory, as Shizi Township is one of the Paiwan distribution areas of southern Taiwan.

獅子國中於民國59年（1970年）獨立設校，此前為牡丹國中獅子分校。校地面積1.608公頃，緊鄰台9線旁的楓港溪，依山傍海，鄰近通往台東的南橫公路入口，也靠近墾丁國家公園。獨立設校以來歷經14任校長。獅子鄉為南台灣排灣族分布地區之一，學校即座落於排灣族傳統領域範圍內。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Shizi, a Paiwan mountain corridor to the east coast.

🏔️

Shizi (Lion) Township is a Paiwan indigenous mountain township straddling the route of the South Cross-Island Highway toward Taitung, with communities perched along the mountainous corridor between the Hengchun Peninsula and the East Coast. As one of southern Taiwan's Paiwan distribution areas, Shizi Junior High's students carry forward this mountain heritage even as the school's location places it at a literal crossroads between sea, mountains, and two coasts.
