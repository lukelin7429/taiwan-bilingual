Jimei Elementary School

集美國民小學

About · 學校簡介

Young, large, and named for its own street.

Jimei Elementary was established in 1995, taking its name from the street it sits on, Jimei Street — making it one of Sanchong's youngest schools, founded decades after the district's historic core schools, as residential growth continued expanding through the 1990s. It has also grown into one of the district's largest campuses, enrolling around 3,700 students as of 2018. The school maintains specialized rooms for music, art, science, karaoke and singing, and rhythm instruction, and runs active bilingual programming through English Day events alongside Indigenous-language classes.

集美國小成立於民國84年（1995年），校名取自所在的集美街——是三重較晚設立的學校之一，反映1990年代住宅發展持續向外擴張，遠晚於三重歷史核心區的老校。學校也發展成三重規模最大的校園之一，截至2018年約有3,700名學生。校內設有音樂、美術、科學、歡唱歌唱及節奏教學等專科教室，並透過英語日活動及原住民族語課程積極推動雙語與多元文化學習。
