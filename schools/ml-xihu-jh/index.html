Xihu Junior High School

西湖國民中學

A public junior high school in Longdong Village, born as a branch campus and independent since 1968, near a historic Hakka mansion.

位於龍洞村的公立國中，前身為分校，民國57年（1968年）正式獨立設校，鄰近歷史悠久的客家古宅。

About · 學校簡介

Grown from borrowed classrooms into an independent school.

Xihu Junior High began in 1966 as a branch of Wenlin Junior High School, initially using classrooms borrowed from Xihu Elementary School. Two years later, in August 1968, it became a fully independent junior high school in its own right. Today the school continues to serve students from Longdong Village and the surrounding Xihu Township community.

西湖國中創立於民國55年（1966年），最初以文林國中分校形式成立，借用西湖國小的教室上課。民國57年（1968年）8月，學校正式獨立設校。至今持續服務龍洞村及西湖鄉周邊社區的學子。

🏫 From Borrowed Classrooms to Independence

從分校到獨立設校

Xihu Junior High started life in 1966 as a branch of Wenlin Junior High, using borrowed classrooms at Xihu Elementary before becoming fully independent in 1968.

📍 Rooted in Longdong Village

扎根龍洞村

The school has long served the community of Longdong Village, giving generations of local families a nearby option for junior high education.

🏛️ Neighboring a Hakka Landmark

鄰近客家古宅

Students grow up near the historic Liu Family Mansion, giving the school community a living connection to Xihu Township's Hakka architectural heritage.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Alongside a grand Hakka homestead.

🏛️

Xihu Township is home to the historic Hakka Liu Family Mansion (劉恩寬古宅), a grand three-hall compound with green glazed tiles and swallow-tail roof ridges reflecting Hakka literary and architectural heritage — a heritage the school's students grow up alongside.
