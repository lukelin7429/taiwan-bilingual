About · 學校簡介

A name that honors the sages.

Sixian Elementary was founded in 1979 to serve Xinzhuang's residents along Zili Street. Its name, meaning roughly "contemplating the sages," draws on the district's long-standing scholarly and Confucian heritage. Today the school runs a dedicated gifted-education program, an evening supplementary school, and STEAM and robotics maker-education activities, alongside active scouting groups and multilingual learning programs.

思賢國小創立於民國68年（1979年），服務新莊自立街一帶的居民。校名意為「思慕賢者」，呼應當地源遠流長的文昌儒學傳統。學校目前設有資賦優異教育班、補校夜間課程，以及STEAM與機器人自造教育活動，並積極推動童軍團與多語言學習方案。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xinzhuang's Confucian and scholarly roots.

📜

Sixian's name recalls Xinzhuang's historic scholarly tradition, centered on the Wenchang Temple (文昌祠) in the district's old town — a reminder that education and reverence for learning have deep roots here. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
