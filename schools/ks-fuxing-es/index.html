About · 學校簡介

A name from a naming wave.

Fuxing Elementary School's precise founding date is not documented in currently available sources, though indirect evidence from neighboring Shijia Elementary's history suggests it was established sometime in the 1950s or 60s, during Cianjhen's early postwar growth. As of the 2022-23 school year, the school enrolled 449 students across 22 classes. Its name, "復興" ("Revival" or "Restoration"), was a common choice for Taiwanese schools founded in the years after 1949.

復興國小確切創校年份目前尚無法從公開資料中確認，惟從鄰近獅甲國小的校史間接推估，可能設立於1950至60年代前鎮戰後初期發展階段。112學年度（2022-23）全校共22班、449名學生。校名「復興」是1949年後台灣許多學校常見的命名方式之一。
