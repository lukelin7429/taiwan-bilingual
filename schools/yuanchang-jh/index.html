Yuanchang Junior High School

元長國民中學

The only junior high school in Yuanchang Township, drawing students from its peanut- and pig-farming villages into art-talent and gifted-education programs.

元長鄉唯一一所國中，將花生與養豬產業村落的學子帶入美術班與資優教育的殿堂。

About · 學校簡介

The township's only junior high.

Yuanchang Junior High traces its origins to 1957, when it began as a branch of Beigang Junior High School across the county line in Chiayi. It became an independent school in 1961 and took its current name in 1968. Today it is the only junior high school serving all of Yuanchang Township, offering an art-talent class, a gifted-education track, and an English oral-fluency program that draw students from across the township's farming villages.

元長國中的歷史可追溯至民國46年（1957年），最初是嘉義縣北港國中的分校，於民國50年（1961年）獨立設校，並於民國57年（1968年）更為現名。如今是元長鄉唯一的國中，設有美術班、資優教育班，以及英語口語表達課程，吸引來自鄉內各農村的學子就讀。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Peanut and pig country, Yunlin's Yuanchang.

🥜

As the sole junior high serving all of Yuanchang Township, the school draws students from the township's peanut- and pig-farming villages into its arts and gifted-education programs — a reminder that Yuanchang is one of Yunlin's leading peanut-growing districts, its rural economy long anchored in peanut cultivation and pig raising.
