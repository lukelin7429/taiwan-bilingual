Datan Elementary School

大潭國民小學

A small farming-community school founded around 1943, named for Guiren's Datan area and its landmark neighborhood temple.

About · 學校簡介

Health, gratitude, and innovation.

Datan Elementary was founded around 1943, taking its name from Guiren's Datan area. The school marked its 80th anniversary with a community sports day on March 26, 2023, and maintains a dedicated school-history microsite documenting its story. It operates under the motto "Health, Gratitude, Innovation" (健康、感恩、創新), serving the children of a small farming community in Guiren District.

大潭國小創校約於民國32年（1943年），校名取自歸仁區大潭地區。民國112年（2023年）3月26日，學校舉辦社區運動會慶祝80週年校慶，並設有校史專屬網站記錄其發展歷程。校訓為「健康、感恩、創新」，服務歸仁區這個小型農村社區的學童。

Named for the Datan area of Guiren, this small farming-community school shares its neighborhood with the Zhaode Temple (昭德宮), a local landmark that remains a fixture of community life alongside the school. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
