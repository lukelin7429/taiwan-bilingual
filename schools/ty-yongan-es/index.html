Yong'an Elementary School

永安國民小學

Sharing its historic name with nearby Yong'an Fishing Harbor, this school has spun off two branch campuses of its own since the 1960s.

About · 學校簡介

Renamed for luck, and a parent school in its own right.

Yong'an Elementary began in 1920, under Japanese rule, as a teaching branch of Dapo Public School at Kantoucuo (大坡公學校崁頭厝分教場), becoming independent as Kantoucuo Public School in 1929. After Taiwan's 1945 retrocession it was renamed Kantoucuo National School, then Yong'an National School in 1959 because the old name was considered inauspicious, and finally took its current name of Yong'an Elementary School in 1968. The school in turn spun off two branch schools of its own in the 1960s — Beihu (1960) and Bengang (1962) — both of which later became independent schools. Its old name "崁頭厝" shares the same root as "崁頭屋港," the historic name of nearby Yong'an Fishing Harbor.

永安國小創立於1920年（大正9年）日治時期，最初是大坡公學校崁頭厝分教場，1929年（昭和4年）獨立設校為「崁頭厝公學校」。1945年臺灣光復後改名為崁頭厝國民學校，1959年（民國48年）因舊名被認為不吉利而更名為永安國民學校，並於1968年（民國57年）定名為今日的永安國小。學校在1960年代也分出了自己的兩所分校——北湖（1960年）與笨港（1962年）——之後皆各自獨立設校。其舊名「崁頭厝」與鄰近永安漁港的歷史舊名「崁頭屋港」同源。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚓ Sharing a Name With the Harbor

與永安漁港同源舊名

The school's pre-1959 name, "崁頭厝," shares its root with "崁頭屋港," the historic name of nearby Yong'an Fishing Harbor.

🌱 A Parent School to Two Others

分出北湖、笨港兩校

Yong'an Elementary spun off its own Beihu (1960) and Bengang (1962) branch campuses, both of which later became independent schools.

📛 A Renamed-for-Luck School

因舊名不吉利而改名

The school's 1959 renaming from "Kantoucuo" to "Yong'an" ("Peaceful and Secure") came because the old name was considered inauspicious.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Sharing its name with a Hakka fishing harbor.

⚓

Yong'an Elementary's lineage is directly tied to Yong'an Fishing Harbor — Taiwan's only Hakka-majority fishing harbor — sharing its pre-1959 place name with the harbor itself, and anchoring the school firmly in the district's coastal fishing heritage.
