About · 學校簡介

From a mountain branch school to a life-education pioneer.

Maling Elementary School was founded in 1920 as the "Shuifanjiao Public School Maling Branch," making it over a century old and one of the oldest schools deep in Qidu District's forested mountains. Since 2016, it has operated as Keelung's first publicly-run, privately-managed experimental school, in partnership with the Bliss and Wisdom Foundation, centered on a life-education curriculum. Its campus today includes an insect museum, an artifact museum, an organic farming garden, an ecological pond, and even a wood-fired pizza oven used for hands-on teaching.

瑪陵國民小學創校於民國9年（1920年），初名「水返腳公學校瑪陵分校」，是深藏於七堵山林間逾百年歷史的老校之一。自民國105年（2016年）起，本校與福智文教基金會合作，成為基隆市首間公辦民營實驗小學，以生命教育為核心課程。校園設有昆蟲館、文物館、有機農園、生態池，甚至還有一座供教學使用的柴燒披薩窯。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌲 Keelung's "Most Beautiful Forest School"

基隆市「最美森林小學」

Dubbed Keelung's most beautiful forest elementary school, Maling sits deep in Qidu's mountains, drawing on local ecology rather than the city's coastal identity.

🎓 Keelung's First Public-Private Experimental School

基隆首間公辦民營實驗小學

Since 2016, Maling has been run in partnership with the Bliss and Wisdom Foundation as Keelung's first publicly-run, privately-managed experimental school, with a life-education curriculum.

🐞 Museums and an Organic Garden

昆蟲館、文物館與有機農園

The campus features an insect museum, an artifact museum, an organic farming garden, and an ecological pond, giving students hands-on access to the natural and cultural world around them.

🍕 A Wood-Fired Teaching Oven

柴燒披薩窯教學

A wood-fired pizza oven on campus is used as a hands-on teaching tool, part of the school's broader life-education approach to learning through direct experience.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Deep in Qidu's mountains, once a mining frontier.

🌲

Maling sits in the forested mountains of Qidu District, an area that, like neighboring communities, has historical ties to the district's inland coal-mining past — a resource-economy counterpart to Keelung's coastal port and fishing trade. Today the school draws its identity more from the surrounding forest ecology than from that mining history. See the Keelung county page for more on the region.
