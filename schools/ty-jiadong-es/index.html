Jiadong Elementary School

Named for the Bishopwood tree once used to mark village boundaries — a trace of Bade's pre-industrial, agrarian past.

About · 學校簡介

Named for a village-boundary tree.

Jiadong Elementary opened on October 1, 1947, as a branch school before becoming independent on August 1, 1953, as Jiadong Public School, taking its current name in 1968 with the introduction of nine-year compulsory education. The school maintains a detailed history archive on a dedicated website tracing its founding and evolution.

茄苳國小於1947年（民國36年）10月1日設立「茄苳分校」，1953年（民國42年）8月1日獨立設校，定名為茄苳國民學校，1968年隨九年國教實施更名為茄苳國民小學。學校設有專屬網站，詳細記錄創校以來的沿革歷史。

🌳 A Name Rooted in Old Village Geography

地名源自聚落界標

“茄苳” (Bishopwood) trees once marked village boundaries in old Taiwanese hamlets — the school's name preserves that pre-industrial place-naming tradition.

📜 A Detailed School-History Archive

詳實的校史紀錄

🏫 Independent Since 1953

1953年獨立設校

Founded as a branch in 1947, the school became fully independent six years later, taking its current name in 1968.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for a village-boundary tree.

🌳

Named for the 茄苳 (Bishopwood) tree, a common village-boundary marker in old Taiwanese hamlets, Jiadong Elementary's very name is a trace of Bade's pre-industrial agrarian place-naming — a linguistic fingerprint of the district's farming-village origins.
