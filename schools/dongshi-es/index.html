About · 學校簡介

The anchor school of Dongshi's township seat.

Dongshi Elementary serves as the township-seat elementary school for Dongshi, anchoring the administrative center of this farming community. The school has modernized its internal operations with an internet-phone (VoIP) extension system connecting its administrative departments, reflecting a practical approach to keeping a rural school office running efficiently.

東勢國小是東勢鄉治所在地的核心小學，服務這個農業社區的行政中心。學校也在內部營運上導入網路電話（VoIP）分機系統，串連各行政單位，展現出鄉村學校在提升行政效率上的務實作法。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Dongshi, Taiwan's garlic and carrot heartland.

🧄

As the anchor elementary school of Dongshi Township's seat, Dongshi Elementary sits at the center of a farming community famed for garlic and carrot production, with roughly nine in ten of Taiwan's garlic bulbs grown in Yunlin. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
