About · 學校簡介

A school named for looking ahead.

Ruifeng Junior High began in 1973 as a branch of Cianjhen Junior High, formed to absorb the district's rapidly growing student population, and took its current name in August 1974. Its motto, "前瞻瑞豐．邁向巔峰" ("Looking ahead with Ruifeng, climbing toward the peak"), reflects the ambitions of a school built during Cianjhen's era of explosive growth. The name "瑞豐" ("auspicious abundance") is shared by several streets and schools built across the district during the same 1970s boom.

瑞豐國中於民國62年（1973年）以前鎮國中分校形式成立，以因應前鎮地區急遽增加的學生人口，民國63年（1974年）8月更名為瑞豐國中。校訓「前瞻瑞豐．邁向巔峰」呼應了這所誕生於前鎮爆發性成長年代學校的期許。「瑞豐」（意為吉祥豐盛）之名也為區內多條街道與學校於1970年代人口成長期共同採用。
