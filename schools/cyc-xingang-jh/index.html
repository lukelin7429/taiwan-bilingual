Xingang Junior High School

新港國中

A public junior high school with deep roots in Xingang's folk-performance traditions, from Chinese orchestra to a championship lion dance team.

About · 學校簡介

A junior high steeped in performance traditions.

Xingang Junior High traces its educational lineage back to 1898, making Xingang home to one of Chiayi County's oldest school histories, though the junior high in its present form was organized later in the postwar period. Today the school is best known for its arts-talent programs: its Chinese orchestra and visual arts classes have won numerous competitions, and its recorder ensemble captured five consecutive county championships before representing Chiayi County at the national level. The school's football team has also claimed a national championship, and its lion dance team won a top "special excellence" award in 2025.

新港國中的教育淵源可追溯至1898年，是嘉義縣歷史最悠久的學校系統之一，不過現今形式的國中則於戰後時期才正式設立。學校以藝術才能班聞名，國樂班與美術班屢獲競賽佳績，直笛團更曾連續五年蟬聯縣賽冠軍，並代表嘉義縣參加全國賽。此外，足球隊曾榮獲全國冠軍，獅陣隊也在2025年獲得縣賽特優成績。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎶 A County Champion Recorder Ensemble

直笛團五連霸

The school's recorder ensemble won five consecutive county championships and went on to represent Chiayi County at the national level, a rare feat for a rural junior high.

🦁 A Special-Excellence Lion Dance Team

獅陣隊榮獲特優

In 2025, the school's lion dance team earned the top "特優" rating in county competition, keeping alive a folk-performance art closely tied to Xingang's temple culture.

⚽ A National Championship Football Team

足球隊全國冠軍

Xingang Junior High's football team has won a national championship, showing the school's strength extends well beyond its celebrated arts programs.

🎻 Chinese Orchestra and Visual Arts

國樂與美術才能班

Dedicated Chinese orchestra (國樂) and visual arts classes give students a formal track for artistic talent, backed by a long record of competition wins.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Echoes of the Matsu pilgrimage.

🦁

Xingang Township is home to Fengtian Temple (奉天宮) and its famous Baan-kau (刈火) Matsu pilgrimage, one of Taiwan's most celebrated folk-religious festivals. Xingang Junior High's strong folk-performance traditions — its lion dance team and music programs — directly echo the temple-centered festival culture that defines the township.
