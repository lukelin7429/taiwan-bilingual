About · 學校簡介

Built to relieve three overcrowded schools.

Xin-Pu Elementary was planned under then-Tong-An Elementary principal Lü Zheng-nan beginning August 1, 2004, and officially opened for enrollment on August 1, 2005 under founding principal Zhao Guan-rong. It was created specifically to relieve overcrowding at three neighboring schools — Tong-An, Tzu-Wen, and Hui-Ji — as the surrounding residential population grew. Now on its fourth principal, Tsai Ming-han, the school is branded a "雙語亮點學校" (Bilingual Spotlight School) known for an immersive English-teaching approach.

新埔國小的籌設工作於民國93年（2004年）8月1日起由時任同安國小校長呂正南負責推動，民國94年（2005年）8月1日正式招生開學，首任校長為趙冠榮。設校目的是為紓解同安、慈文、會稽三校因周邊人口成長而面臨的爆滿壓力。學校現任第四任校長蔡明翰，並獲認證為「雙語亮點學校」，以沉浸式英語教學聞名。

🥾 Tracing a Stream to Its Source

行旅・開根how戶外教育

The school's "行旅・開根how" outdoor-education program takes students to trace a local stream from its source and explore nearby hills — a program featured by Taiwan's Ministry of Education.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Subdividing an old school catchment.

🏘️

Xin-Pu Elementary is a textbook example of how the historic Taoyuan District core kept subdividing its old school catchments — it exists purely because the neighborhoods around the old 同安/慈文/會稽 zone outgrew their capacity. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
