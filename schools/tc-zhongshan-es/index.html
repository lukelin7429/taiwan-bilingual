About · 學校簡介

From 'Dongshi Corner' to a modern curriculum.

Zhongshan Elementary opened in 1916 under Japanese rule as one of the original schools serving "Dongshi Corner" (東勢角), the historic core of what would become today's timber-trading hill town. Its four-story building was totally destroyed in the 1999 921 earthquake, forcing a full rebuild. Now well over a century old, the school has moved firmly into the present with a curriculum that includes AI, robotics, and 3D-printing instruction.

中山國民小學於1916年日治時期創立，是最早服務「東勢角」地區（今日木材貿易山城的歷史核心）的學校之一。1999年921大地震摧毀了校內四層樓建築，校方因此進行全面重建。如今建校已逾百年，學校也積極邁向未來，開設AI、機器人與3D列印等課程。
