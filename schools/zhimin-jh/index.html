About · 學校簡介

A close-knit school in Yuanli's Old Settlement village.

Zhimin Junior High is a small-scale public junior high school located in Jiushe ("Old Settlement") Village in Yuanli Township. With roughly 170 students and about 30 teachers, the school offers a close-knit learning environment typical of Miaoli's smaller rural junior highs, serving families in this historically significant corner of the township.

致民國中是一所位於苑裡鎮舊社里的小型公立國中，全校約170名學生、30位教師，提供緊密的學習環境，服務苗栗縣這座歷史意義深厚的鄉鎮聚落。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Small, Close-Knit Campus

小校精緻教學

With approximately 170 students and 30 teachers, Zhimin offers the kind of close teacher-student relationships often found at Miaoli's smaller rural junior highs.

🪶 Roots in Daoka Heritage

道卡斯族歷史根源

The school sits in Jiushe Village — literally "Old Settlement" — an area with historical roots tracing to the Daoka (道卡斯) indigenous community of the Yuanli plain.

🌾 Part of Rush-Weaving Country

藺草編織的故鄉

Like the rest of Yuanli Township, the school's community shares in the area's renowned Hakka and Hoklo rush-weaving craft heritage.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Jiushe Village's layered history.

🪶

Zhimin Junior High is located in Yuanli's Jiushe (舊社) Village — literally "Old Settlement" — an area with historical roots connected to the Daoka (道卡斯) indigenous community, set within the wider township renowned for its Hakka and Hoklo rush-weaving craft heritage.
