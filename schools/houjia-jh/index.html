Houjia Junior High School

後甲國中

Founded in 1968 as part of Taiwan's nationwide nine-year compulsory education rollout, now Tainan's second-largest municipal junior high by enrollment.

About · 學校簡介

Part of the 1968 education reform wave.

Houjia Junior High School was established in 1968 as part of Taiwan's nationwide rollout of nine-year compulsory education, taking its name from the "Houjia" land section where it was built. Preparatory work began on November 15, 1967 under Chen Fengjin, then-principal of Zhongxiao Junior High, who was loaned as founding-committee director. Campus groundbreaking took place on January 26, 1968, with construction completed by August 15 that same year. In 1991, the school acquired additional land, expanding its campus from roughly 14,000 ping to over 18,000 ping.

後甲國中創校於1968年（民國57年），是台灣九年國民義務教育全面推行下的產物，校名取自校地所在的「後甲」段地名。籌備工作始於1966年（民國56年）11月15日，由時任忠孝國中校長陳逢金借調擔任籌備處主任。1968年1月26日校地動土，同年8月15日校舍完工。1991年學校再徵收部分土地，校地面積由約14,000坪擴增至逾18,000坪。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Tainan's Second-Largest Junior High

臺南市第二大國中

Houjia is currently Tainan's second-largest municipal junior high school by enrollment, after Fuxing Junior High.

🛠️ A Regional Career-Exploration Hub

區域生涯探索中心

The school runs a regional career-exploration center that hosts summer vocational-exploration camps for elementary and junior-high students from across the city.

🏗️ Built in the 1968 Reform Wave

1968年教改設校潮

Houjia was part of the wave of schools built citywide during the 1968 nine-year-education reform that reshaped Tainan's school map beyond the old walled city.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Tainan's mid-century expansion.

🏗️

Located on Linsen Road in the East District's mid-century urban expansion zone, the school is part of the wave of schools built citywide during the 1968 nine-year-education reform that reshaped Tainan's school map far beyond the old walled city. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
