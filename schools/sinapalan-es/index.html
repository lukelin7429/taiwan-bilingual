About · 學校簡介

A small school with a globally recognized choir.

Sinapalan Elementary School serves a Bunun tribal community in Xinyi Township and has built a distinctive reputation through its choir, which won a gold award at the 2025 Tokyo International Choir Competition — carrying Bunun musical tradition onto an international stage. Beyond music, the school actively teaches the Bunun language, with students competing in Bunun-language recitation contests and the National Bunun Tribal Sports Games, and it maintains an indigenous-language "E-Park" digital learning resource to support ongoing language study.

希娜巴嵐國小服務信義鄉一處布農部落社群，並以合唱團建立了獨特聲譽——該團在2025年東京國際合唱大賽中榮獲金獎，將布農族的音樂傳統帶上國際舞台。除了音樂，學校也積極推動布農語教學，學生參與布農語朗讀比賽及全國布農族傳統運動會，並設有原住民語「E大」數位學習資源，支持族語學習持續進行。
