Neili Senior High School

桃園市立內壢高級中等學校

Founded in 1999 as the last national-level senior high built in Taoyuan before municipalization, serving the commuter and manufacturing hub of Neili.

About · 學校簡介

Serving the Neili commuter corridor since 1999.

Established in 1999 as Taiwan Provincial Neili Senior High School — with a preparatory office set up in 1996 at Wuling Senior High School — this was the most recently founded national-level senior high in Taoyuan prior to the county's 2018 municipalization, when it transitioned to municipal governance. The school has held a sister-school relationship with Tamagawa High School in Shiga Prefecture, Japan, since November 2015. It enrolls around 2,013 students across 57 classes (2024 figures), with competitive teams in swimming, basketball, baseball, and volleyball.

學校創立於民國88年（1999年），前身為臺灣省立內壢高級中學，1996年於武陵高中設立籌備處。這是桃園縣2018年升格為直轄市前最晚設立的省／國立高中，升格後隨即改制為市立學校。自2015年11月起與日本滋賀縣玉川高等學校締結姊妹校。現有學生約2,013人、57班（2024年統計），並設有游泳、籃球、棒球、排球等競技隊伍。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🇯🇵 A Japan Sister-School Tie

日本姊妹校交流

Since November 2015, the school has held a sister-school relationship with Tamagawa High School in Shiga Prefecture, Japan, supporting cross-cultural student exchange.

🏊 Strong Athletic Program

游泳、籃球、棒球、排球隊

The school fields competitive teams in swimming, basketball, baseball, and volleyball, serving a student body of roughly 2,013 students across 57 classes (2024 figures).

🎒 Student-Led Policy Change

學生爭取鬆綁書包規定

In 2021, students successfully petitioned to lift restrictions on non-regulation backpacks and mandatory uniform-wearing at school entry — a notable example of student advocacy.

🏫 Taoyuan's Last Pre-Municipal High School

升格前最後設立的國立高中

Founded in 1999, this was the most recently established national-level senior high in Taoyuan before the county's 2018 upgrade to a special municipality.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring the Neili rail corridor.

🚆

The school serves the Neili (內壢) sub-center of Zhongli, a dense residential and industrial area straddling the rail line — reflecting Zhongli's role as a commuter and manufacturing hub along the Taoyuan rail corridor. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
