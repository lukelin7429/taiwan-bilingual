Tainan Municipal Shalun International Senior High School

臺南市立沙崙國際高級中等學校

Founded as Shalun Junior High in 2005 to serve Guiren's booming HSR district, converted in 2025 into Tainan's first public K-12 bilingual school.

About · 學校簡介

From a rail-district junior high to Tainan's first K-12 bilingual school.

This school's story begins on May 2, 2005, when Tainan County established "Shalun Junior High School" (沙崙國民中學) to absorb the population growth arriving with the new Tainan High Speed Rail station in Guiren District. Founding principal Chen Renzheng, appointed in 2002 to prepare the school, led it from its 2005 opening through 2007; principals Zhang Mingxian, Chen Lizu, and Chen Bocang followed in succession. In 2010, after the Tainan county–city merger, it became "Tainan Municipal Shalun Junior High School." In November 2024, the Tainan City education board approved a major transformation: converting the junior high into a full kindergarten-through-senior-high institution. The relaunch took effect for the 2025 school year, when the school reopened as "Tainan Municipal Shalun International Senior High School" — billed as Taiwan's first public K-12 school, with roughly 15 classes spanning kindergarten, elementary, junior-high, and senior-high divisions on a 5.18-hectare campus inside the Tainan HSR Special District. The school operates bilingually in Chinese and English, a deliberate response to the international and professional population the rail line has drawn to Guiren.

本校故事始於民國94年（2005年）5月2日，台南縣政府為承接台南高鐵歸仁站帶來的新興人口，設立「沙崙國民中學」。民國91年（2002年）獲聘籌備的創校校長陳仁政，自2005年開校起帶領學校至2007年，其後歷經校長張明賢、陳麗足、陳柏蒼相繼接掌。民國99年（2010年）縣市合併後，更名為「臺南市立沙崙國民中學」。民國113年（2024年）11月，台南市教育局核定重大轉型計畫，將國中部改制為涵蓋幼兒園至高中的完全一貫學校。改制自2025學年度起生效，學校以「臺南市立沙崙國際高級中等學校」之名重新啟動——號稱全台第一所公立幼兒園至高中一貫學校，開校時約有15班，涵蓋幼兒園、國小、國中、高中各部，校地約5.18公頃，位於台南高鐵特定區內。學校採中英雙語教學，是回應高鐵沿線國際化、專業人口移入所做的刻意規劃。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 Taiwan's First Public K-12 School

全台首創公立幼兒園至高中一貫學校

The 2025 conversion made this Taiwan's first public school spanning kindergarten through senior high under one administration — roughly 15 classes across all four divisions from day one.

🌐 Built to Be Bilingual

雙語辦學定位

The school operates in Chinese and English by design, a direct response to the international and professional population that the Tainan HSR station has brought to Guiren.

🚄 A School Born of the HSR Boom

高鐵特定區的產物

Its very existence traces back to 2005, when it opened as a junior high specifically to serve the population influx following the arrival of the Tainan High Speed Rail line.

🏫 One Institution Across Two Names

沙崙國中即為現今高中部前身

The former "Shalun Junior High School" (沙崙國中) is not a separate school — it is the same institution before its November 2024 approval to convert and rename into today's K-12 international senior high.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Guiren's rail gateway, in miniature.

🚄

Like the district around it, this school's history is a direct product of the Tainan High Speed Rail Station reshaping Guiren from a farming township into a transit-development zone. It opened in 2005 to absorb the housing boom that followed the rail line, and its 2025 conversion into a K-12 bilingual school reflects the same forces two decades on — an international, professional community now settled around the HSR Special District.
