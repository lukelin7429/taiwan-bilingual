About · 學校簡介

Built on reclaimed military land.

Sanduo Junior High opened in 2004 on a 2.81-hectare campus in eastern Shulin, on land that had previously served as the Guanghua Army Camp (光華營區) before the Ministry of National Defense donated it for educational use in 2003. As one of the newest junior high schools in the district, it serves the fast-growing residential neighborhoods on Shulin's eastern edge, alongside its namesake Sanduo Elementary School.

三多國中創立於民國93年（2004年），校園佔地2.81公頃，原為「光華營區」的軍事用地，民國92年（2003年）由國防部撥用改作教育用途。作為樹林較新設立的國中之一，服務樹林東側快速發展的住宅社區，並與同名的三多國小相銜接。

樹林新設國中

Local Culture · 在地文化

Land reclaimed for a new generation.

🪖

The reuse of the former Guanghua Army Camp for Sanduo Junior High mirrors a broader pattern across postwar Shulin, where land once devoted to military and agricultural use has steadily been repurposed for residential and educational development as the district urbanized.
