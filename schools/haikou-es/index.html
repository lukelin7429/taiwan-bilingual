About · 學校簡介

A coastal school growing its own food-and-farming identity.

Haikou Elementary School serves about 21 classes and roughly 484 students in Zhunan's coastal Haikou community. True to its name — 'Haikou' means river mouth or coastal opening — the campus includes an ecological pond (生態池) and a 'Haikou Food & Farming Park' (海口食農園區) that supports farm-to-table learning. The school places strong emphasis on character education and school-wide reading promotion.

海口國小現有約21班、484名學生，服務竹南鎮沿海的海口社區。校名如其地——「海口」意指河口或濱海開口——校園內設有生態池與「海口食農園區」，推動食農教育。學校同時重視品格教育與全校性閱讀推廣活動。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 The Haikou Food & Farming Park

海口食農園區

A dedicated 'Haikou Food & Farming Park' (海口食農園區) gives students hands-on, farm-to-table learning experiences tied to the school's coastal setting.

🐟 An On-Campus Ecological Pond

生態池

The campus includes an ecological pond (生態池) that supports environmental education and connects students to local wetland ecology.

📖 Character and Reading Promotion

品格與閱讀推廣

Haikou places strong emphasis on character education alongside a school-wide reading-promotion program for its roughly 484 students.
