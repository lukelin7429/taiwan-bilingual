Yong-Ren Senior High School

臺南市立永仁高級中學

A complete secondary school founded in 1975 that opened by borrowing classrooms from a neighboring elementary school — today known locally for its girls' basketball program.

About · 學校簡介

An improvised start that grew into a complete secondary school.

Approved for establishment on August 1, 1975, the school opened with just 7 classes, temporarily borrowing classrooms from neighboring Fusing (復興) Elementary School because its own buildings weren't yet ready. It moved into its permanent campus in September 1976 with 14 classes, and became a "complete secondary school" — combining junior- and senior-high divisions — in the 2005 school year. Today the school is a local point of pride for its girls' basketball program: the junior-high squad, started in 2002, took national runner-up-tier honors in only its second year, and the senior-high squad debuted in the HBL national high-school league in 2011, placing 6th in its first year.

學校於1975年8月1日奉准設校，開校時僅7班，因校舍尚未完工，暫借鄰近的復興國小教室上課，於1976年9月遷入現址，當時已擴增為14班。94學年度（2005年）增設高中部，成為兼具國中、高中的「完全中學」。學校女子籃球隊是地方一大亮點：國中部球隊自2002年成軍，第二年即獲全國準亞軍級佳績；高中部球隊則於2011年首度打入全國高中籃球聯賽（HBL），首年即拿下第六名。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Built to keep up with a booming suburb.

🏘️

The school's improvised start — borrowing a neighboring school's classrooms while its own were built — is typical of how Yongkang's schools multiplied rapidly in the 1970s–90s to keep pace with the district's fast population growth as Tainan's urban core expanded northward.
