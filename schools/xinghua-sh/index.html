Hsing Hua Senior High School

苗栗縣立興華高級中學

A more than 50-year-old public school in Toufen offering junior high, senior high, and vocational divisions, with award-winning arts and athletics classes.

About · 學校簡介

More than 50 years serving the Toufen-Zhunan corridor.

Hsing Hua Senior High School opened in 1968 as Hsing Hua Junior High School. It became a complete middle school in 1997 and added a senior high division in 2001, later expanding further to include a vocational (高職部) track alongside its junior and senior high programs. Over more than 50 years, the school has built special classes in art (美術班) and athletics (體育班), and its 2024 college entrance exam (學測) results set a new school record.

興華高中創立於民國57年（1968年），初期為興華國民中學。民國86年（1997年）升格為完全中學，民國90年（2001年）增設高中部，其後又擴增高職部，形成國中部、高中部與高職部並存的完整體系。逾50年的辦學歷程中，學校陸續設立美術班與體育班等特色班別，民國113年（2024年）學測成績更創下歷年新高。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📜 Over 50 Years of History

逾半世紀辦學歷史

Founded in 1968 as a junior high school, Hsing Hua has grown over more than five decades into one of Miaoli's more established county-run secondary schools.

🎨 Arts and Athletics Special Classes

美術班與體育班

The school offers dedicated arts (美術班) and athletics (體育班) special classes, broadening its academic offerings beyond the standard curriculum.

📈 A Record College Entrance Exam Year

學測成績創新高

The 2024 college entrance exam (學測) results set a new school record, reflecting steady academic improvement.

🏫 Three Divisions Under One Roof

國中、高中、高職三部合一

Hsing Hua now spans junior high, senior high, and vocational (高職部) divisions, making it one of the more comprehensive secondary campuses in the Toufen-Zhunan area.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A long-established anchor in the Toufen-Zhunan corridor.

🏘️

As a long-established school in the densely populated Toufen-Zhunan Hakka corridor, Hsing Hua reflects the area's transition from traditional Hakka hillside farming communities to a bustling township hub. See the Miaoli county page for more on the region.
