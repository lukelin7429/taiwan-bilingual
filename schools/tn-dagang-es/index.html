About · 學校簡介

A name rooted in North District's harbor history.

Dagang Elementary School was founded on August 1, 1988, to serve the Dagangliao (大港寮) community in North District. Its opening involved first principal Lin Xin-xian, along with then-Tainan mayor Lin Wen-xiong and the chair of the school's parents' association. Today the school's branding emphasizes "quality bilingual education, sailing toward the world" (優質雙語航向國際), reflecting an active, ongoing bilingual-education program.

大港國小於1988年（民國77年）8月1日創立，為服務北區大港寮社區而設。開校典禮由首任校長林信賢主持，並有時任臺南市長林文雄與學校家長會會長共同出席。學校目前以「優質雙語航向國際」作為辦學特色，持續推動雙語教育。
