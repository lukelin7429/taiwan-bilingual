About · 學校簡介

Over 125 years of teacher training in Da'an.

National Taipei University of Education (NTUE) traces its origin to 1896, when it was founded as Zhishanyan School — one of the very first modern schools established in Taiwan, at the start of Japanese colonial rule. Renamed Taihoku Normal School in 1897, it became the Taiwan Governor-General's Office Taipei Normal School in 1920, split into two normal schools in 1927, and merged back together in 1943. After 1945 it was renamed Taiwan Provincial Normal School, then restructured several more times — becoming a junior teachers college in 1961, a normal college in 1987, and passing to national administration as National Taipei Teachers College in 1991 — before being elevated to full university status in 2005 under its current name. More than 100,000 alumni have passed through the institution, which remains centered on teacher training while having broadened into humanities, arts, and science.

國立臺北教育大學校史可追溯至1896年創立的芝山巖學堂，是日治時期初期台灣最早的現代學校之一。1897年更名為台北師範學校，1920年改為台灣總督府台北師範學校，1927年一度分為兩校，1943年再度合併。1945年後更名為台灣省立台北師範學校，其後歷經多次改制：1961年改為台灣省立台北師範專科學校，1987年升格為台灣省立台北師範學院，1991年改隸國立並更名國立台北師範學院，2005年正式升格為大學，即現今校名。百年來培育逾十萬名校友，至今仍以師資培育為核心，同時發展出人文、藝術與科學等領域。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Three Colleges

三個學院

NTUE is organized into the Colleges of Education, Humanities and Arts, and Science, with 16 departments and 18 graduate schools and programs, including three doctoral programs.

🏫 Taiwan's Oldest Continuous School Site

全台歷史最久校址之一

Descended from Zhishanyan School, founded in 1896, its Heping East Road campus is one of the oldest continuously operating educational sites in Taipei.

👩‍🎓 100,000+ Alumni

逾十萬校友

More than 100,000 graduates have gone on to careers in education, literature, history, art, law, politics, and finance across Taiwan.

🌏 English Content Across Subdomains

跨子站英文內容

English-language content is confirmed on NTUE's main site as well as academic subdomains covering university history and current admissions news.
