Pingxing Elementary School

平興國民小學

A school in the heart of Pingzhen's urban core, guided by a motto embracing liveliness, excellence, and a global outlook.

About · 學校簡介

A school with a global-minded motto.

Pingxing Elementary sits along the Huannan Road / Guangtai Road corridor, part of the cluster of schools serving Pingzhen's built-up urban core, alongside its sister institution Pingxing Junior High. The school's motto — "活潑、卓越、倫理心、人文、科學、國際觀" (Lively, Excellent, Ethical, Humanistic, Scientific, Globally-minded) — captures a broad educational philosophy spanning character, academics, and global awareness. Students regularly take part in Pingzhen-district track and field selections, as well as multicultural (new-resident) storytelling and singing competitions.

平興國小位於環南路、廣泰路一帶，與姊妹校平興國中同屬服務平鎮都會核心地帶的學校群。校訓「活潑、卓越、倫理心、人文、科學、國際觀」，涵蓋品格、學術與國際視野等多面向教育理念。學生經常參與平鎮區田徑選拔賽，以及新住民多元文化說故事、歌唱比賽等活動。

Pingxing Elementary's multicultural storytelling and singing events for new-resident families reflect Pingzhen's evolving demographic makeup — a district whose original Hakka farming communities have, over the decades, welcomed new residents from across Taiwan and beyond.
