About · 學校簡介

From Taiwan Provincial College of Law and Business to a national university.

National Taipei University was founded in 1949 (民國38年) as the Taiwan Provincial College of Law and Business, and was renamed National Taipei University in 2000. It has built the strongest national reputation in Law and Business among Taiwan's public universities, and today its main campus sits on 54 hectares in Sanxia District, supplemented by a satellite campus in Taipei's Zhongshan District. The university's main website defaults to Chinese; its English-language presence online is currently limited to a thin recruitment microsite rather than a full parallel English site, so most in-depth information about the university is only available in Chinese.

國立臺北大學創立於民國38年（1949年），前身為「台灣省立法商學院」，於民國89年（2000年）改制更名為國立臺北大學。該校在法律與商管領域享有全國性的優良聲譽，現今主校區位於三峽區，佔地54公頃，另於台北市中山區設有分部。校方官網目前仍以中文為主，英文網頁僅提供簡易的招生介紹，尚未建置完整的英文版官網，因此大部分詳細資訊仍僅提供中文版本。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

⚖️ Strongest in Law and Business

法律商管見長

Among Taiwan's public universities, NTPU holds the strongest national reputation in Law and Business, carrying forward its origins as the Taiwan Provincial College of Law and Business.

🌏 An English-Taught College

全英語學院

NTPU's International College of Sustainability Innovations offers programs taught entirely in English, even though the university's main website is not.

🏞️ A 54-Hectare Sanxia Campus

三峽校區佔地54公頃

The main Sanxia campus spans 54 hectares, supplemented by a satellite campus in Taipei's Zhongshan District.

🏆 Internationally Ranked

國際排名肯定

NTPU appears in the QS World University Rankings at 1201–1400.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A campus in historic Sanxia.

The main campus sits in Sanxia District, known for its centuries-old Sanxia Old Street and the ornately carved Sanxia Zushi Temple (三峽祖師廟). See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
