About · 學校簡介

Fifty years on the edge of the algal reef coast.

Caoluo Junior High began as a preparatory office in 1972 and formally opened in 1975. The school marked its 50th anniversary with a sports meet in January 2026. Its motto, “Health, Happiness, Growth, Excellence” (健康、快樂、成長、卓越), reflects a campus culture built around well-rounded student development, from literacy programs to competitive sports and science teams.

草漯國中民國61年（1972年）成立籌備處，民國64年（1975年）正式創校。民國115年（2026年）1月以運動會慶祝建校五十週年。校訓「健康、快樂、成長、卓越」，展現學校在閱讀素養、體育與科學競賽等多方面培育學生的辦學理念。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🪸 Steps from the Algal Reef

鄰近藻礁保護區

The campus sits close to the Caota Sand Dunes (草漯沙丘) and the Taoyuan Algal Reef (桃園藻礁), one of Taiwan's most nationally significant coastal ecological sites.

📰 A Reading and Literacy Campus

三好校園閱讀報紙計畫

The school is active in “three-goodness campus” (三好校園) programs, notably a newspaper-reading literacy initiative that builds reading habits alongside classroom study.

🥋 Competitive Teams Beyond the Classroom

科展、跆拳道與水上運動

Students compete in the science fair, taekwondo, and synchronized swimming — a breadth of extracurricular strength for a mid-sized district junior high.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Bordering Taoyuan's algal reef coastline.

🪸

The campus sits within walking distance of Taoyuan's protected algal reef coastline, tying the school directly to one of Guanyin's best-known environmental landmarks — a coastal ecosystem that has drawn national attention to conservation efforts in the district.
