Dingliu Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A parent school with over 5,000 alumni.

Dingliu Elementary School began after local residents petitioned for a school in January 1926; approval followed in March 1926 as the Dingliu Annex of Zhongpu Public School. It became independent as Dingliu Public School in April 1935 once enrollment grew sufficiently — the year commonly cited as its formal founding. Renamed after Taiwan's 1945 Retrocession, the school spun off a branch in 1957 that became independent Hemu Elementary School in 1962, and adopted its current name in August 1968. The school celebrated its 85th anniversary in 2020, reporting over 5,000 alumni. Today it runs a youth orchestra, children's band, multiple dance and performance clubs, an idiom-learning curriculum module, and extensive Microsoft Teams-based digital infrastructure, alongside an attached kindergarten.

頂六國小起源於民國15年（1926年）1月，地方居民請願設校，同年3月獲准成立中埔公學校頂六分教場。民國24年（1935年）4月因學生人數增長而獨立設校，定名頂六公學校，通常以此年視為正式創校。民國35年（1946年）台灣光復後更名，民國46年（1957年）分出和睦分班，後於民國51年（1962年）獨立為和睦國小，民國57年（1968年）8月定名嘉義縣中埔鄉頂六國民小學。學校於民國109年（2020年）舉辦建校85週年慶，校友人數已逾5000人。現今學校設有少年管弦樂團、兒童樂隊、多個舞蹈與表演社團、「學學成語」課程模組，並建置完善的Microsoft Teams數位教學基礎設施，同時設有附設幼兒園。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎉 85 Years and 5,000+ Alumni

建校85週年、逾5000校友

The school celebrated its 85th anniversary in 2020, reporting more than 5,000 alumni across many sectors — a testament to its long institutional reach.

🎼 Youth Orchestra and Children's Band

少年管弦樂團與兒童樂隊

A youth orchestra, children's band, and multiple dance and performance clubs give students rich opportunities in music and the performing arts.

📚 Idiom-Learning Curriculum

學學成語課程

A dedicated "學學成語" (idiom-learning) curriculum module helps students build deeper fluency in Chinese language and cultural literacy.

💻 Microsoft Teams Digital Classrooms

Teams與Office 365數位教學

Extensive digital infrastructure, including Microsoft Teams-based online classrooms and Office 365 integration, supports modern instruction.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A parent school for the surrounding farmland.

🎋

One of the older schools in Zhongpu Township, its founding is tied directly to the growth of nearby farming hamlets producing bamboo shoots and citrus, with Hemu Elementary School later spun off from it. See the Chiayi County page for more on the region.
