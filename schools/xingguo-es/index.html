About · 學校簡介

Thirty years, marked with a parade.

Xingguo Elementary's exact founding date has not been separately confirmed, but a 30th-anniversary parade celebration held in October 2024 — titled "興國30 精采充實" — places its establishment around 1994. The school's motto, "孝誠、勤樸" (filial piety and integrity, diligence and simplicity), reflects a traditional values-based approach to education. Like several other Zhongli schools founded in the 1990s, Xingguo's establishment reflects the district's continued population growth through that decade.

興國國小確切創校年份尚無法完全確認，但依民國113年（2024年）10月舉辦、名為「興國30 精采充實」的三十週年街區遊行慶祝活動推算，創校約在民國83年（1994年）前後。學校校訓「孝誠、勤樸」，展現重視傳統品格教育的辦學方向。與中壢多所1990年代設立的學校相同，興國國小的創校反映了該十年間地區人口持續成長的趨勢。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎉 A Neighborhood Street Parade

街區遊行慶祝

Xingguo marked its 30th anniversary in October 2024 with a full neighborhood parade celebration, "興國30 精采充實" — a notably community-facing way to mark a school milestone.

📜 A Traditional-Values Motto

孝誠勤樸校訓

The school's motto, "孝誠、勤樸" — filial piety and integrity, diligence and simplicity — anchors its approach to character education.

📈 Born of 1990s Growth

1990年代人口成長下設立

Like several other Zhongli schools founded around the same time, Xingguo's establishment in the early-to-mid 1990s reflects the district's continued population growth through that decade.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of a 1990s wave of new schools.

📈

Xingguo Elementary's founding in the early-to-mid 1990s places it among a cluster of Zhongli schools established during that decade's continued population growth — a period when the district kept needing new schools even though its oldest institutions had already been serving families for generations.
