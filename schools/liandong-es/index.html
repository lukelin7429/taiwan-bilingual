Liandong Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A school whose enrollment tracks an industry's history.

Liandong Elementary began as a branch school in 1948 and became independent in 1955. Located in Shuinandong on Ruifang's scenic "Golden Waterfall" coast, its enrollment swelled to nearly 1,000 students during the peak of the nearby Liyue Copper Refinery, then collapsed to just 11 students by 2020 after the refinery closed in 1990 — a dramatic reflection of the rise and fall of Taiwan's gold and copper mining industry.

濂洞國小1948年以分校形式創立，1955年獨立設校。學校位於瑞芳知名的「黃金瀑布」海岸的水湳洞，學生人數曾在鄰近的濂洞煉銅廠鼎盛時期高達近千人，1990年煉銅廠關閉後，人數銳減至2020年僅剩11人——鮮明呈現了臺灣金銅礦業興衰的歷史軌跡。
