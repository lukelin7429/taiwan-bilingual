About · 學校簡介

A hub for environmental and disaster-prevention education.

Xingjia Elementary School was founded in 1993 and celebrated its 33rd founding anniversary in late 2025. In the 110th academic year (2021), the school had 48 classes and 1,281 students. It serves as a designated center school for Comprehensive Studies, Disaster Prevention Education, and Environmental Education in Chiayi City, and is home to both a “Reading Dream Hall” and an Energy Education Center.

興嘉國小創立於民國82年（1993年），2025年底慶祝建校33週年。110學年度（2021年）全校共48班、1,281名學生。學校為嘉義市指定的綜合活動、防災教育及環境教育中心學校，並設有「閱讀夢工場」與能源教育中心。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 A Designated Environmental Education Center

環境教育中心學校

Xingjia Elementary serves as one of Chiayi City's designated center schools for Environmental Education, embedding sustainability into its curriculum.

🚨 Leading Disaster Prevention Education

防災教育中心學校

The school is also a designated hub for Disaster Prevention Education, training students and the wider community in civic resilience.

📖 The Reading Dream Hall

閱讀夢工場

A dedicated “Reading Dream Hall” anchors the school's reading culture, alongside its Energy Education Center for hands-on science learning.

🏫 A Sizeable West District School

西區大型學校

As of the 110th academic year, the school had 48 classes and 1,281 students, making it one of the larger elementary schools in the West District.
