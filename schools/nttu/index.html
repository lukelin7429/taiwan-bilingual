About · 學校簡介

From a teacher-training school to the county's own university.

National Taitung University's roots go back to 1948, when it opened as "臺灣省立臺東師範學校" (Taiwan Provincial Taitung Normal School). It became Taitung Normal Junior College in 1967, was upgraded to Taitung Normal College in 1987, gained national status in 1991, and finally became a full comprehensive university in 2003. Today NTTU offers programs across colleges of humanities, science and engineering, and education, along with graduate and doctoral programs, serving approximately 3,668 students. The university maintains a dedicated English-language site for international students and holds exchange agreements with partner universities in Japan, South Korea, the United States, Russia, and mainland China, promoting itself as a "Green International University."

國立臺東大學的歷史可追溯至1948年，創校時名為「臺灣省立臺東師範學校」。1967年改制為臺東師範專科學校，1987年升格為臺東師範學院，1991年改隸國立，2003年正式轉型為綜合大學。目前設有人文學院、理工學院、教育學院，並提供碩博士班課程，在學學生約3,668人。學校設有專屬的國際學生英文網站，並與日本、韓國、美國、俄羅斯及中國大陸的夥伴大學簽有交流協議，並以「綠色國際大學」自我定位。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 A Three-College Comprehensive University

人文、理工、教育三學院

NTTU offers degree programs across colleges of humanities, science and engineering, and education, plus graduate and doctoral programs, having become a full comprehensive university in 2003.

🌱 A "Green International University"

綠色國際大學

The university brands itself as a "Green International University," reflecting both its environmental positioning and its exchange agreements with partner institutions in Japan, South Korea, the US, Russia, and mainland China.

📖 From Normal School to University

師範學校演變史

Tracing back to a 1948 teacher-training school, NTTU's evolution through normal junior college, normal college, and finally comprehensive university status in 2003 reflects the broader transformation of Taiwan's higher-education system.

🌏 A Global Student Body, By Design

國際學生英文網站

With a dedicated English-language site for prospective and current international students, the university actively positions itself to welcome students from beyond Taiwan.

As the county's only national university, NTTU anchors Taitung City's role as the educational hub of the county, drawing students region-wide — including from Indigenous Puyuma and Amis communities — into its programs. See the schools directory for more Taitung County listings.
