Zhongshan Experimental Elementary School

忠山實驗小學

A small hill school reinvented in 2019 as New Taipei's first city-run experimental elementary school, with outdoor and civic-literacy learning.

About · 學校簡介

From a hill-country branch to a citywide experiment.

Zhongshan Experimental Elementary School (忠山實驗小學) opened in 1957 as a branch of Xingren Elementary School and became independent in 1964. In 2019, it was converted into New Taipei City's first publicly run experimental elementary school under the Experimental Education Act, adopting an outdoor-education and civic-literacy curriculum and opening enrollment to students citywide, regardless of district. Note that its Chinese name uses the character 忠 ("loyal"), not 中 — the two happen to share the same romanization, "Zhongshan," as an unrelated Zhongshan Elementary School elsewhere in New Taipei.

忠山實驗小學於民國46年（1957年）以興仁國小分校形式創立，民國53年（1964年）獨立設校。民國108年（2019年）依《實驗教育三法》轉型為新北市首所公辦公營實驗小學，推動戶外教育與公民素養課程，並開放全市學童跨學區報名就讀。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 New Taipei's First Public Experimental School

全市首所市立實驗小學

In 2019, the school became New Taipei City's first publicly run elementary school under the Experimental Education Act, pioneering a citywide model for alternative public education.

🥾 Outdoor and Civic-Literacy Learning

戶外與公民素養課程

The school's experimental curriculum emphasizes hands-on outdoor education and civic literacy, a departure from the standard textbook-based approach.

🌍 Open to the Whole City

跨學區開放報名

Unlike most Tamsui elementary schools, Zhongshan Experimental accepts students from anywhere in New Taipei City, not just its immediate neighborhood.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Reinventing a small rural school.

🏫

The school's 2019 transformation into an experimental school reflects a broader effort across Tamsui's hill country to revitalize small rural schools facing declining local enrollment by reinventing them as citywide destinations. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
