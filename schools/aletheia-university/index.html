About · 學校簡介

From Mackay's Oxford College to a modern university.

Aletheia University's history reaches back to 1882 — decades before the Republic of China era in Taiwan — when Canadian Presbyterian missionary George Leslie Mackay founded Oxford College (牛津學堂) in Tamsui, one of the oldest institutions of higher education anywhere in Taiwan. The school was reorganized as a four-year college in 1994 and formally renamed Aletheia University in 1999. Its historic Oxford College building, Li-Chi Hall, still stands on campus today and is preserved as a national historic monument. The university now offers programs across four colleges plus a Chinese Language Center.

真理大學的歷史可追溯至1882年——早於台灣進入中華民國時期數十年——當時加拿大長老教會宣教士馬偕博士（George Leslie Mackay）在淡水創立「牛津學堂」，是台灣歷史最悠久的高等教育機構之一。該校於民國83年（1994年）改制為四年制學院，並於民國88年（1999年）正式更名為「真理大學」。校內至今仍保存牛津學堂的歷史建築「理學堂大書院」，並列為國定古蹟。現今真理大學設有四個學院及一所華語中心。
