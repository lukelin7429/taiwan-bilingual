Erlun Junior High School

二崙國民中學

A public junior high school beside the Zhuoshui River, with a national championship-winning harmonica ensemble and a strong athletics program.

座落於濁水溪畔的公立國中，口琴合奏團榮獲全國冠軍，田徑成績同樣亮眼。

About · 學校簡介

Music and athletics on the Zhuoshui plain.

Erlun Junior High was founded in 1968, as part of the nationwide push to establish universal junior high education across Taiwan. Set on a campus of roughly 4 hectares beside the Zhuoshui River, the school has built two standout programs: its harmonica ensemble has won top honors in a national competition, and its athletics teams earned six gold medals at the 2026 national athletics meet.

二崙國中創立於民國57年（1968年），呼應台灣全面推動國民中學義務教育的政策。校地約4公頃，緊鄰濁水溪畔，發展出兩項亮眼特色：口琴合奏團在全國比賽中榮獲最高榮譽，田徑隊也在民國115年（2026年）全國運動會上勇奪六面金牌。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rice, melons, and Hakka heritage.

🌾

Erlun Township is a major rice- and melon-growing area along the Zhuoshui River plain and home to Taiwan's Zhao'an Hakka community — a heritage the school's students carry forward. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
