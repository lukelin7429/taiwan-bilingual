About · 學校簡介

Geology, turned into a classroom.

Shijiao Elementary began as a branch school in 1951 and became independent in 1953, sitting at the foot of the Xueshan (Snow Mountain) range on Dongshi's eastern edge. The school has built and maintains its own fossil museum, home to more than 20,000 specimens, turning its location amid the Dajia River gorge's dramatic geology into a hands-on teaching resource.

石角國民小學於民國40年（1951年）以分校形式設立，民國42年（1953年）獨立設校，座落於東勢區東側雪山山脈山麓。學校自設化石博物館，館藏標本超過兩萬件，將大甲溪峽谷壯麗的地質景觀轉化為第一手的教學資源。
