About · 學校簡介

A later addition to an old lineage.

Guanmiao Junior High School was established around 1968, when Taiwan extended compulsory education to nine years — though an exact founding date has not been confirmed in available records. The school is closely tied to its much older sister institution, Guanmiao Elementary School, which traces its roots to 1899 as a schoolhouse inside the district's Guandi Temple, the very temple that gives Guanmiao ("Guan[di] Temple") District its name. The junior high keeps a dedicated school-history microsite documenting its own development, and its campus sits in the historic district seat, close to the temple itself.

關廟國中約創立於民國57年（1968年）九年國教實施前後，確切創校年份於現有資料中尚未證實。學校與歷史悠久的姊妹校關廟國小淵源深厚——關廟國小可追溯至1899年設於關帝廟內的學堂，正是「關廟」地名的由來。關廟國中設有專屬校史網頁，記錄學校本身的發展歷程，校址就位於關廟老街區，鄰近關帝廟。

🏫 A Twentieth-Century Addition

Guanmiao Junior High grew out of the same educational lineage as the district's 1899-founded Guanmiao Elementary, becoming a later addition once nine-year compulsory education began.

鄰近關帝廟

🎋 Rooted in Bamboo-Shoot Country

扎根綠竹筍之鄉

Most students come from farming or noodle-making households in a district whose economy and identity are built on bamboo-shoot cultivation and the noodles that share Guanmiao's name.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Bamboo shoots and noodles, town and school.

🎋

Guanmiao District's economy and identity for generations have centered on bamboo-shoot (綠竹筍) farming and the production of Guanmiao noodles (關廟麵), which take the town's own name. Most students at the junior high grow up in farming or noodle-making families, so the rhythms of the bamboo groves and noodle workshops that ring the town are part of daily life for the whole school community.
