About · 學校簡介

An arts-feeder school in Beilun Village.

Fuxing Elementary, located in Beilun Village within Xingang Township, dates to around 1957, inferred from a 57th-anniversary sports day held in December 2014. The school has built a strong reputation for visual arts and beauty education, with students regularly entering county art competitions, and it serves as a key feeder school for Xingang Junior High's visual-arts talent program. It also maintains a dedicated indigenous-language education network, runs multi-day outdoor education trips as far as Miaoli County, and has an attached kindergarten.

復興國小位於新港鄉北崙村，創校年代約在民國46年（1957年）前後，推估自2014年12月舉辦的建校57週年運動會。學校以美術與美感教育見長，學生經常參加縣內美術競賽，也是新港國中美術才能班的重要來源學校。此外，學校設有本土語言教育專網，並辦理多日戶外教育旅行（遠至苗栗縣），並附設幼兒園。

What makes this school worth knowing.

Fuxing's strong visual-arts and beauty-education program regularly sends students on to Xingang Junior High's visual-arts talent program.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Xingang's arts-education pipeline.

🖌️

Located in Beilun Village within Xingang Township — home to the famous Fengtian Temple (奉天宮) and its Matsu pilgrimage — Fuxing Elementary's visual-arts emphasis feeds directly into the township's noted local arts-education pipeline, alongside schools like Xingang Junior High.
