Houcuo Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

68 years serving a village, ended by land expropriation.

Houcuo Elementary was founded in 1956 and served the farming community of Houcuo Village in Dayuan District for 68 years. As one of several Dayuan elementary schools abolished in 2024 due to Taoyuan Aerotropolis land expropriation, the school stopped accepting new students in April 2024 and was formally closed at the end of July that year. We keep this page as a historical record of a school that anchored its village community for nearly seven decades before land expropriation for Taiwan's airport-expansion megaproject brought its story to a close.

後厝國小創立於1956年（民國45年），服務大園區后厝村的農村社區長達68年。作為2024年因桃園航空城土地徵收而廢校的幾所大園國小之一，學校自2024年4月起停止招收新生，並於同年7月底正式廢校。我們保留此頁面，作為這所服務村落社區近七十年的學校歷史紀錄，直到因台灣機場擴建重大建設案的土地徵收，為它的故事畫下句點。
