About · 學校簡介

Serving Qianfeng since the early 1950s.

Qianfeng Elementary opened on September 1, 1950, as "Kaohsiung County Gangshan Town Qianfeng National School," taking its current name, Qianfeng National Elementary School, in 1968. Today the school has 34 classes, 785 students, and 65 teachers, serving families in the historic Qianfeng neighborhood of Gangshan District.

前峰國小創立於民國39年（1950年）9月1日，初名「高雄縣岡山鎮前峰國民學校」，民國57年（1968年）改為現名「前峰國民小學」。學校現有34個班級、785名學生、65位教師，服務岡山區歷史悠久的前峰社區。

⚔️ A Neighborhood Name From a Garrison

前鋒鎮右營的地名淵源

Local Culture · 在地文化

Living on a soldier-farmer's namesake.

⚔️

Qianfeng Elementary shares the Qianfeng (前峰) place name rooted in the Ming-Zheng era military farming garrison "前鋒鎮右營," whose second character was later changed for numerological reasons. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
