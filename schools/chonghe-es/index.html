Chonghe Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

From a temple branch school to an A-class bilingual campus.

Chonghe Elementary School was founded in April 1919 as a branch of Guanmiao Public School, originally housed inside Shenkeng Temple. It was renamed and relocated several times before becoming an independent school in 1942 as "Guedong National School," taking its current name, Chonghe, in 1953. The school is designated an "A-class" bilingual-education school, devoting roughly a third of its weekly class time to English instruction, supplemented by a mobile "English activity vehicle" that brings interactive lessons to students. It is also a designated Hakka-language feature school, running a taiko drumming team, taichi, and a track-and-field program. In 1965, Chonghe established its own branch school in Budai Village, which became independent in 1976 as Wenhe Elementary School.

崇和國小創立於大正8年（1919年）4月，最初為關廟公學校的分校，設於深坑寺內。歷經多次改名與遷校，於民國31年（1942年）獨立設校，命名為「龜洞國民學校」，並於民國42年（1953年）改名為崇和國小。學校獲評定為「A級」雙語教育學校，每週約三分之一課程時間以英語授課，並配有「英語活動車」到校提供互動式英語課程。崇和亦為客語特色學校，設有太鼓隊、太極拳與田徑隊。1965年，崇和曾於布袋里設立分校，該分校於1976年獨立設校，即今日的文和實小。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A school that split to serve scattered hamlets.

🎋

Chonghe's own history of branching off village schools — including what became Wenhe Elementary — mirrors the scattered hamlet settlement pattern typical of Guanmiao's farming communities, where hilly terrain and dispersed villages long required local branch classrooms before children could reach a central school.
