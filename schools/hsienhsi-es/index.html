Xianxi Elementary School

線西國民小學

A century-old public elementary school in a small coastal farming-and-fishing township, now developing a bilingual and technology focus for its students.

About · 學校簡介

Over a century serving Changhua's coastline.

Xianxi Elementary was founded in 1909 as a branch of Hemei-Xian public school, making it more than a century old. The school has undergone several name changes through Taiwan's changing eras and marked its centennial with a special anniversary publication. Today it serves the children of Xianxi Township — Changhua County's smallest township — and has developed a bilingual/technology-focused emphasis for its students.

線西國小創校於1909年（明治42年），最初為和美線公學校下見口分校。歷經數次更名，並於建校百週年時發行紀念特刊。如今持續服務彰化縣線西鄉——全縣面積最小的鄉鎮——的學童，並發展出雙語科技特色教育（線西雙語科技小學）。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A small coastal community with a maritime character.

🎣

Xianxi Township is Changhua County's smallest township, a typical coastal farming-and-fishing community with a strong maritime character. The township has just two elementary schools and one junior high, meaning Xianxi Elementary plays an outsized role in the daily life of this small seaside community.
