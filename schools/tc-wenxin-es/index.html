About · 學校簡介

Built for a booming corridor.

Wenxin Elementary was approved and broke ground in 1990, opening its doors to students two years later in 1992. The school was built inside Taichung's 4th Urban Land Readjustment Zone, along the busy Wenxin Road corridor — one of the major north-south arteries that reshaped Beitun District from farmland into dense residential neighborhoods during the 1990s.

文心國小於1990年獲准設校並動工，1992年正式招生開學。校址位於臺中市第四期市地重劃區內，緊鄰文心路——這條1990年代將北屯區從農田轉變為密集住宅區的重要南北向幹道之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛣️ On Taichung's Busiest Artery

緊鄰文心路要道

The campus sits along Wenxin Road, one of Taichung's most heavily traveled north-south corridors, connecting Beitun District to the heart of the city.

🏗️ A Product of Land Readjustment

第四期市地重劃區產物

Wenxin Elementary was purpose-built inside Taichung's 4th Urban Land Readjustment Zone, part of a citywide 1990s effort to convert farmland into planned residential districts.

📆 A Two-Year Build

從動工到開學兩年

Approved and broken ground in 1990, the school welcomed its first students just two years later in 1992, a relatively fast turnaround for a new public school of its era.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Taichung's 1990s transformation.

🏙️

The school's founding is directly tied to Taichung's 1990s urban land-readjustment boom, which transformed Wenxin Road into one of the city's major arteries and reshaped Beitun District's landscape in the process. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
