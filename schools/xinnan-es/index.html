Xinnan Elementary School

新南國民小學

A Zhuangwei elementary school with an attached kindergarten, active in local and mother-tongue language education and environmental learning.

About · 學校簡介

Language and land, from an early age.

Xinnan Elementary School serves families in Zhuangwei Township with an attached kindergarten that gives young children an early start alongside their older peers. The school is especially active in local and mother-tongue language education, helping students build a connection to Taiwan's linguistic heritage from a young age, and it pairs this with a strong environmental education program that grounds classroom learning in the surrounding landscape.

新南國小服務壯圍鄉的家庭，附設幼兒園，讓年幼的孩子能與年長的同儕一同從小扎根學習。學校積極推動本土語言教育，協助學生從小建立與台灣語言文化的連結，並搭配扎實的環境教育課程，將課堂學習與周邊自然環境緊密結合。
