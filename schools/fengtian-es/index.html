About · 學校簡介

Two thatched classrooms, funded by parents.

Fengtian Elementary began in 1953 as "Likavung National School's Yanliao Branch," built with two thatched-roof classrooms funded directly by parents in the community. It grew into a full branch school in 1957 with four classes, and became independent in 1959 with six classes and 235 students. The school was renamed in 1968, and again with a "Taitung City" prefix in 1975 following administrative changes. It has been designated an Indigenous-focus school since 2014.

豐田國小創立於民國42年（1953年），最初為「利嘉國民學校煙寮分班」，由家長集資興建兩間茅草屋教室。民國46年（1957年）升格為分校，設有4班；民國48年（1959年）獨立設校，共6班235名學生。民國57年（1968年）更名，民國64年（1975年）行政區劃調整後再冠上「台東市」字樣。自民國103年（2014年）起為原住民重點學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏚️ Built by Parents, By Hand

家長集資興建茅草教室

The school's very first classrooms — two thatched-roof rooms in 1953 — were funded directly by parents in the community, a grassroots founding story typical of rural Taitung.

📈 A Steady Climb to Independence

從分班到獨立設校

Fengtian grew step by step — from a branch in 1953, to a full branch school with four classes in 1957, to independence in 1959 with six classes and 235 students.

🪶 An Indigenous-Focus School

2014年列為原住民重點學校

Since 2014, Fengtian Elementary has been designated an Indigenous-focus school, shaping its curriculum and campus life around its Indigenous student community.
