About · 學校簡介

Born inside the temple that named the district.

Guanyin Elementary was founded in 1905, seven years before the founding of the Republic of China, under Japanese colonial rule — making it the oldest school in this batch. It opened with only about 60 students (roughly 50 boys and 10 girls); classes were initially held in a rented room at the Ganquan Temple (甘泉寺, also known as Furen Temple) in Shiguanyin, before a permanent campus was built. The school underwent major reconstruction from 1995 to 2001 after a 1994 structural (rebar) defect was discovered in the original buildings, added an on-campus preschool program in 2015, and celebrated its 120th anniversary in 2025.

觀音國小創立於民前7年（1905年），即日治時期、中華民國成立前七年，是本批學校中歷史最悠久者。創校時僅約60名學生（約50名男生、10名女生），最初借用石觀音甘泉寺（又稱蘆竹富仁廟）一間廂房上課，其後才興建正式校舍。學校於1994年發現原有校舍鋼筋結構缺失，遂於1995至2001年間進行大規模重建；2015年增設附設幼兒園；2025年歡慶建校120週年。
