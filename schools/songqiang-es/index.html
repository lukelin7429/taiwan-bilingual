Songqiang Elementary School

松強國民小學

Taichung's newest elementary school, purpose-built in a fast-growing land-readjustment zone to ease chronic overcrowding nearby.

About · 學校簡介

The newest school in northern Taichung.

Songqiang Elementary broke ground in September 2022 and welcomed its first students in 2025, making it the newest school in this batch of Beitun profiles. Built on a roughly 3.5-hectare campus in the 14th Urban Land Readjustment Zone, the school exists specifically to relieve chronic overcrowding at nearby Renmei Elementary, a direct response to one of the district's most intense recent housing booms.

松強國小於2022年9月動工，2025年正式招收第一屆學生，是這一批北屯學校介紹中最年輕的一所。校園座落於第14期市地重劃區內，面積約3.5公頃，設校目的是紓解鄰近仁美國小長年爆滿的就學壓力，直接回應了北屯區近年最猛烈的一波住宅開發熱潮。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🆕 The Newest School in the District

最新設立的學校

Songqiang enrolled its very first students in 2025, making it the youngest elementary school among its Beitun neighbors and a fresh addition to the district's education map.

🏗️ Built to Relieve Overcrowding

為紓解仁美國小爆滿而生

The school was purpose-built to take pressure off nearby Renmei Elementary, whose enrollment had become chronically overcrowded amid the neighborhood's rapid growth.

📐 A 3.5-Hectare Campus

3.5公頃校地

Groundbreaking took place in September 2022 for a roughly 3.5-hectare campus, giving the new school a generous footprint by comparison with many older, land-constrained schools nearby.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A response to Beitun's newest housing boom.

🏗️

Songqiang's creation is a direct response to Beitun's most explosive recent housing boom — the 14th Urban Land Readjustment Zone — where new residential towers have risen faster than existing schools could absorb the incoming students. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
