About · 學校簡介

An old mountain school, reborn in the forest.

Chajiao Elementary traces its roots to a 1922 private school, "大有義塾," and gained independent public status in 1941 during the Japanese colonial era — giving it over 80 years of history. In school year 113 (2024), the school was converted into Taiwan's first public forest experimental elementary school, adopting a distinctive "悠森學" (Forest Learning) curriculum built on four core strands: humanities, ecology, outdoor education, and action-based forest learning. The campus sits in the Dabaqi Creek watershed, a mountainous area historically home to Atayal indigenous and Hakka logging communities, and its forest-based curriculum directly reflects that terrain and history.

插角國小的歷史可追溯至大正11年（1922年）的私塾「大有義塾」，昭和16年（1941年）日治時期取得獨立公學校地位，至今已逾80年校史。113學年度（2024年）學校轉型為全台第一所公立森林實驗小學，發展出獨特的「悠森學」課程，以人文、生態、戶外與行動四大核心主軸為架構。校園座落於大豹溪流域，這片山區歷史上曾是泰雅族原住民與客家伐木聚落所在地，學校的森林本位課程正直接反映了這片土地的地形與歷史。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌲 Taiwan's First Public Forest Experimental School

全台首創公立森林實驗小學

Converted in 2024, Chajiao became Taiwan's first public elementary school built entirely around a forest-based experimental curriculum — a distinctive milestone among the district's schools.

🥾 Four Strands of "Forest Learning"

悠森學四大核心主軸

The school's "悠森學" curriculum organizes learning around humanities, ecology, outdoor education, and hands-on action — all set against its surrounding mountain landscape.

🏞️ Rooted in the Dabaqi Creek Watershed

大豹溪流域校園

The campus sits in the Dabaqi Creek watershed, an area with deep Atayal indigenous and Hakka logging history that now shapes the school's outdoor curriculum.

🏛️ Over 80 Years of History

逾80年校史

With roots tracing back to a 1922 private school and independent status since 1941, Chajiao brings more than eight decades of local educational history into its new forest-school identity.
