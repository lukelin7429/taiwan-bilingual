About · 學校簡介

A small school in a farming village.

Dongcheng Elementary is a small public school located in Meinong Village, a farming community in Beinan Township, with about six classes and roughly fifty students. Detailed institutional history is limited in available records, but the school is an active participant in Taitung County's environmental education resource network, including efforts tied to the county's "net-zero" sustainability push — a modest but concrete example of how even a small rural school plays a role in wider county-level initiatives.

東成國小是位於卑南鄉美農村的一所小型公立學校，全校約6班、50名學生。目前可查考的校史資料有限，但學校積極參與台東縣環境教育資源網絡，包括縣府推動的「淨零」永續政策相關活動——展現出即使是一所小型鄉村學校，也能在縣級整體政策中扮演實質角色。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 Part of a County-Wide Net-Zero Network

Dongcheng Elementary takes part in Taitung County's environmental education resource network, including activities connected to the county's broader "net-zero" sustainability efforts.

🌾 Rooted in Meinong Village Farming Life

扎根美農村農業生活

Located in Meinong Village, the school is closely tied to the rhythms of the surrounding farming community, a common pattern among Beinan Township's smaller rural schools.

🏫 A True Small-Village School

典型小型農村小學

Dongcheng Elementary sits in Meinong Village, one of the many small farming communities that make up Beinan Township alongside its more widely known Puyuma Indigenous villages and the archaeologically significant Beinan site.
