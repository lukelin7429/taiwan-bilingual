About · 學校簡介

The township's central, historic elementary school.

Guanxi Elementary is the township's oldest and central flagship elementary school — so central, in fact, that it once hosted Guanxi Junior High's very first classes in 1955, before that school had its own campus. Today it runs a baseball team and a range of after-school clubs, alongside a distinctive Chinese-classics curriculum built around a "Tang Poetry and Song Lyrics" recitation system and calligraphy instruction, complemented by English, Hakka, and Hoklo language programs.

關西國小是關西鎮歷史最悠久、地位最核心的指標性小學——甚至在1955年，尚未擁有自己校舍的關西國中曾借用此校教室開辦首屆班級。如今學校經營棒球隊與多元課後社團，並發展出以「唐詩宋詞」吟誦系統與書法教學為核心的國學特色課程，輔以英語、客語與閩南語課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📜 A Tang Poetry Recitation System

唐詩宋詞吟誦

A distinctive Chinese-classics curriculum built around reciting Tang poetry and Song lyrics, paired with calligraphy instruction, gives students a deep grounding in classical literary heritage.

⚾ A Baseball Team and Clubs

棒球隊與課後社團

Beyond academics, the school fields a baseball team and offers a variety of after-school clubs, rounding out student life on this central campus.

🗣️ Three Languages Alongside Mandarin

英語、客語、閩南語並行

English, Hakka, and Hoklo language programs reflect the school's commitment to Guanxi's linguistic diversity alongside its classical Chinese curriculum.

🏫 A School That Hosted Another School

曾借出教室給關西國中

Guanxi Elementary once loaned its classrooms to a fledgling Guanxi Junior High in 1955, a small but telling piece of the township's educational history.
