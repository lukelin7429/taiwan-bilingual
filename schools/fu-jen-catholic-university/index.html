About · 學校簡介

From Beijing in 1925 to a Xinzhuang campus reborn.

Fu Jen Catholic University traces its founding to 1925 (民國14年), when it was established in Beijing by the Societas Verbi Divini (the Society of the Divine Word). After the university's mainland operations ended amid the political upheavals of the mid-20th century, it was re-established in Taiwan in 1961, with its present Xinzhuang campus opening in 1963. Today Fu Jen is organized into twelve colleges and remains the only Catholic university in the Chinese-speaking world placed under the direct authority of the Vatican's Congregation for Catholic Education, a distinction that shapes its identity within Taiwan's private higher-education landscape.

輔仁大學創立於民國14年（1925年），由聖言會（天主教聖言會）在北京創辦。歷經20世紀中葉的政局變動，大陸時期校務中斷後，於民國50年（1961年）在台灣復校，現今新莊校區則於民國52年（1963年）啟用。現今輔大設有十二個學院，並且是全球華語世界中唯一直屬梵蒂岡天主教教育部管轄的天主教大學，此一特殊地位也形塑了輔大在台灣私立高等教育中的獨特定位。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Twelve Colleges

十二學院

Fu Jen spans twelve colleges, including Medicine, Law, Management, Foreign Languages, and Textiles & Clothing, among others.

⛪ A Vatican-Affiliated University

直屬梵蒂岡管轄

Fu Jen is the only Catholic university in the Chinese-speaking world under the direct authority of the Vatican's Congregation for Catholic Education.

🏥 Its Own Teaching Hospital

自設教學醫院

Fu Jen opened its own teaching hospital in 2017, extending its College of Medicine into clinical training and care.

🏆 Internationally Ranked

國際排名肯定

Fu Jen appears in the QS World University Rankings at 1201–1400 (2024), reflecting its scale and research standing among Taiwan's private universities.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchored in one of New Taipei's oldest settlements.

🏮

The Xinzhuang campus sits in Xinzhuang District, one of the earliest-settled areas of the greater Taipei basin, known for the centuries-old Xinzhuang Old Street and its historic Fuyou Temple (慈祐宮). See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
