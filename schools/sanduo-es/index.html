About · 學校簡介

A quiet school in Sankuai Village.

Sanduo Elementary is a public elementary school located in Sankuai Village, Jiuru Township — a plains agricultural area in the northwest of Pingtung County. Note that this school is distinct from the similarly-named Sanduo schools found in New Taipei City. Detailed institutional records for Sanduo Elementary are limited beyond its location and contact information, but the school continues to serve the farming families of Sankuai Village as part of Jiuru's local education network.

三多國小是位於屏東縣九如鄉三塊村的公立國小，地處屏東縣西北部以農業為主的平原地帶。值得留意的是，本校與新北市同名的「三多國小」並非同一所學校。目前關於三多國小的公開資料除了地址與聯絡方式外較為有限，但學校持續作為九如鄉在地教育網絡的一環，服務三塊村的農家子弟。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Betel-nut and rice country.

🌾

Sanduo Elementary sits in Sankuai Village, part of Jiuru Township's plains agricultural belt in northwestern Pingtung County, an area long known for its betel-nut and rice farming. The school's rhythms are still shaped by this agricultural calendar, connecting its students to a farming heritage that stretches back generations in the area.
