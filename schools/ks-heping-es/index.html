Heping Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

Built for a growing city.

Heping Elementary was founded on August 1, 1985, one of the newer elementary schools in Gangshan District. It opened as the district's urban population expanded through the 1980s, giving it a name rooted in the aspirational ideal of "peace" (和平) rather than a historic local place name, as many of its older neighboring schools carry.

和平國小創立於民國74年（1985年）8月1日，是岡山區較新設立的國小之一。學校成立於1980年代岡山都市人口擴張之際，校名採用「和平」這個理想化的名稱，而非如許多鄰近老校般沿用地方傳統地名。

🏙️ Born of Gangshan's 1980s Growth

1980年代都市擴張中誕生

Founded in 1985, Heping Elementary is one of the newer elementary schools in the district, established to serve Gangshan's expanding urban population.
