Liuying Junior High School

柳營國中

Founded in 1968 to serve Taiwan's nine-year compulsory education reform, sitting in the heart of Liuying's dairy country.

About · 學校簡介

Built for the nine-year education reform.

Liuying Junior High was founded in 1968 as part of Taiwan's rollout of nine-year compulsory education. Former Yanshui Junior High principal Lin Hui oversaw the founding work, and the campus site was chosen between Taikang and Dongsheng villages. When the first new-student orientation was held that September, the school buildings were still unfinished, so the event had to be held on loan at the Dongsheng Village community hall. Today the school runs bilingual-education teaching communities with public observation lessons for teachers, alongside a dual-track vocational partnership program with technical high schools.

柳營國中創校於民國57年（1968年），是九年國民義務教育推行下設立的學校。籌備工作由前鹽水國中校長林煇主持，校地選定於太康、東昇兩里之間。同年9月舉辦第一屆新生訓練時，校舍尚未完工，只能商借東昇村中山堂舉行。如今學校推動雙語教育教學社群，並辦理公開觀課，同時與技術型高中合作雙軌並進的技職試探課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗣️ Bilingual Teaching Communities

雙語教育教學社群

The school runs dedicated bilingual-education teaching communities and holds public observation lessons so teachers can learn from one another.

🛠️ A Dual-Track Vocational Path

技職雙軌合作方案

Students can take part in a dual-track vocational partnership program run jointly with technical high schools, giving them an early look at trade pathways.

🐄 Locally Sourced School Lunches

全採國產豬牛肉

The school lunch program uses only domestically sourced pork and beef, a small but deliberate nod to the surrounding farm economy.

🏫 Born on Borrowed Ground

中山堂裡的第一堂課

Its very first new-student orientation in 1968 was held at a village community hall because the school buildings weren't finished yet.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Liuying's dairy heartland.

🐄

Liuying Junior High sits in the heart of Liuying's dairy country, part of Taiwan's foremost cattle-farming district, and not far from the historic Liu-clan mansion whose 19th-century rise helped shape the area's identity. The district's character today is a blend of that pastureland economy and its gentry-family history — two threads that run through nearly every school in Liuying.
