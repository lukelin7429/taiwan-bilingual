About · 學校簡介

A new campus after a concrete-quality scare.

Bihua Junior High School was established in 1977 as Sanchong's fourth junior high, built to absorb the district's continuing population growth. In more recent years its original buildings were found to contain high-chloride "sea sand" concrete, a defect affecting a number of older Taiwanese school buildings, prompting a full relocation. The school moved into a newly built campus dedicated on April 25, 2021, and today enrolls more than 1,300 students across roughly 45 classes.

碧華國中創立於民國66年（1977年），為三重第四所國中，設校目的在於承接當時持續成長的人口需求。近年舊校舍因發現含氯量過高的「海砂屋」結構問題——這也是台灣部分老舊校舍常見的通病——校方因此進行整體遷校。新校園於民國110年（2021年）4月25日啟用，學校現有學生1,300餘人，約45班。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A river-crossing location into Taipei.

🌉

The school sits at Sanchong's northeast edge along the Tamsui River, directly across from New Taipei Senior High and next to Chongyang Bridge into Taipei's Shilin district — a location tying it to the river-crossing geography that shaped Sanchong's growth as a Taipei satellite.
