Qionglin Junior High School

芎林國民中學

A junior high school founded through community advocacy and land donated by a mountain temple, teaching traditional bamboo weaving today.

About · 學校簡介

Born from local advocacy and donated land.

Qionglin Junior High School opened in August 1946 as Hsinchu County Qionglin Junior Middle School, founded through the advocacy of local leader Tang Tian-cai, with support from Zou Qing-zhi and Zou Di-zhi. A mountain temple trust donated 2.5 jia of land for the campus in 1947. The school began with just 2 classes and 110 students, initially borrowing rooms from neighboring Qionglin Elementary School. Today it continues to teach traditional bamboo weaving as part of its curriculum.

芎林國中創立於民國35年（1946年）8月，初名新竹縣立芎林初級中學，由地方仕紳鄧添財倡議，鄒清之、鄒帝之等人協助促成。民國36年（1947年）山區廟產信託捐贈2.5甲土地作為校地。創校初期僅2班、110名學生，借用鄰近的芎林國小教室上課。學校至今仍將傳統竹編技藝納入課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎍 A Living Bamboo-Weaving Tradition

傳統竹編技藝課程

Qionglin Junior High teaches traditional bamboo weaving as part of its curriculum, keeping alive a craft historically tied to the township's rural economy.

🙏 Founded on Temple-Donated Land

山區廟產捐地

A mountain temple trust donated 2.5 jia of land in 1947 to help establish the school, reflecting the close ties between local religious institutions and community education.

🤝 Built by Community Advocacy

仕紳倡議創校

The school's founding was driven by local leader Tang Tian-cai and supporters Zou Qing-zhi and Zou Di-zhi, whose advocacy secured the school's establishment in 1946.
