Jiana Elementary School

加拿國民小學

A small farming-community school whose Bunun name means "flat mountainside," dedicated to raising future stewards of Bunun culture.

About · 學校簡介

A name rooted in the land.

Jiana Elementary takes its name from a Bunun-language term meaning "flat mountainside," describing the terrain of the small farming village it serves against the Central Mountain Range. It is a small school — around six classes and just over 40 students — where roughly 95% of families make their living through agriculture. The school's guiding motto emphasizes sustainable development and cultivating the next generation of stewards for Bunun culture.

加拿國小校名源自布農語，意指「平坦的山腰地」，貼切描繪出這座緊鄰中央山脈的小型農村聚落地形。學校規模小巧，約六班、40餘名學生，村內約95%家庭以務農維生。學校秉持永續發展的辦學理念，致力培育布農文化的未來傳承者。
