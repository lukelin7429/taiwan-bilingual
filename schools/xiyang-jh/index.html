Xiyang Junior High School

溪陽國民中學

A public junior high school in Xizhou Township, built and expanded within a single remarkable year in 1970.

座落於溪州鄉的公立國中，於民國59年（1970年）短短一年內快速建校並擴充規模。

About · 學校簡介

Built fast to keep pace with a growing township.

Xiyang Junior High was founded on January 8, 1970, initially as "Changhua County Chenggong Junior High School," and renamed Xiyang Junior High School that same year, on July 24. Under its first principal, Huang Wen-lan, who took office that July, students began by borrowing classrooms at nearby Chenggong Elementary School while their own campus was under construction. The school moved to its current site on December 2, 1970, with just six classrooms, an office, a crafts room, and a water tower — and by the following summer, growing enrollment had already prompted the construction of six more classrooms plus a science lab and library.

溪陽國中創立於民國59年（1970年）1月8日，最初名為「彰化縣立成功初級中學」，同年7月24日更名為溪陽國民中學。首任校長黃文蘭於同年7月就任，學生最初借用鄰近成功國小的教室上課，同時校舍仍在興建中。學校於1970年12月2日遷入現址，當時僅有六間教室、一間辦公室、一間工藝教室與一座水塔；到隔年夏天，因學生人數成長，已再增建六間教室及一間科學實驗室與圖書館。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚡ Founded and Renamed in One Year

一年內建校並更名

The school was established in January 1970 and renamed Xiyang Junior High just months later that July — a rapid founding that reflects the urgency of the moment.

🏗️ From Six Classrooms to a Growing Campus

從六間教室快速擴建

The school moved into its own site in December 1970 with just six classrooms and a water tower, adding six more classrooms, a science lab, and a library within a year.

🏫 Borrowed Classrooms to Start

借用成功國小教室起步

Before its own campus was ready, the school's first students studied in borrowed classrooms at Chenggong Elementary School under founding principal Huang Wen-lan.
