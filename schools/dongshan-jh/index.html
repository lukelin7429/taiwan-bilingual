About · 學校簡介

Nearly seventy years at the town's center.

Dongshan Junior High traces its roots to 1956, when it opened as a branch of Luodong Middle School (羅東中學冬山分校), borrowing classroom space in the chapel and hall of neighboring Dongshan Elementary School. Its first principal, Chen Hua-cong, was appointed that same year. The school became fully independent in 1959 and took its present name in 1968. Nearly seventy years on, it remains a fixture of Dongshan Township's town center, near the Dongshan River.

冬山國中的歷史可追溯至民國45年（1956年），最初作為羅東中學冬山分校成立，借用鄰近冬山國小的禮堂與教室上課。首任校長陳華聰於同年上任。學校於民國48年（1959年）獨立設校，並於民國57年（1968年）確立現名。近七十年來，學校始終座落於冬山鄉行政與文化中心，緊鄰冬山河畔。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Born From a Borrowed Hall

借址辦學的起點

The school began life in 1956 borrowing the chapel and classrooms of Dongshan Elementary before becoming fully independent just three years later in 1959.

🕰️ Nearly Seven Decades of History

近七十年辦學歷程

Continuously operating since the 1950s, the school has served generations of Dongshan Township families through Taiwan's changing eras.

📍 At the Township's Center

冬山鄉行政文化核心

At the heart of Dongshan Township.

Dongshan Junior High sits in the town center of Dongshan Township, the administrative and cultural heart of the area, near the scenic Dongshan River that gives the township its name and its identity as a hub of the Lanyang Plain.
