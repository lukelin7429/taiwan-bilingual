About · 學校簡介

Finding a voice through storytelling.

Yongding Elementary is a rural school serving families in Erlun Township. Its precise founding date is not confirmed in available records, but the school is known for hosting storytelling competitions for its 4th and 5th grade students, encouraging young learners to build confidence in public speaking and narrative expression. Like many small schools in the township, it serves a farming community shaped by generations of local agriculture.

永定國小是一所服務二崙鄉家庭的鄉村小學。確切創校年代目前尚無確切紀錄可考，學校以舉辦四、五年級學生說故事比賽聞名，鼓勵年輕學子培養公開表達與敘事能力的自信。與鄉內許多小型學校一樣，永定國小服務著一個世代務農的社區。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📖 Storytelling Competitions

說故事比賽

The school hosts storytelling competitions for 4th and 5th grade students, building public-speaking confidence and a love of narrative.

🎤 Finding Young Voices

培養學生自信表達

The competitions give upper-elementary students a stage to develop expression and confidence beyond the standard classroom curriculum.

🧑‍🌾 Rooted in Erlun's Fields

扎根二崙農村

Students at Yongding grow up in Erlun's rural villages, where garlic and rice farming still shape daily community life.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A farming village's rural rhythm.

🧄

Rooted in Erlun's rural Zhang-Zhou Hoklo villages, the school's community livelihood centers on the garlic and rice farming that defines the township. This agricultural backdrop shapes the pace of daily life for the families the school serves. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
