Yilan County Nan'ao Senior High School

宜蘭縣立南澳高級中學

The only combined junior-and-senior secondary school in Nan'ao Township, keeping local Atayal Indigenous students in their home community through all six years of secondary education.

南澳鄉唯一涵蓋國中與高中部的完全中學，讓當地泰雅族學生無需離鄉即可完成六年中等教育。

About · 學校簡介

The only path to senior high without leaving home.

The school began in 1962 as a branch class of Su'ao Junior High, became an independent branch school in 1966, and was established as a fully independent "Nan'ao Junior High School" in 1968. On August 1, 2000, it was upgraded to a complete school (完全中學) spanning both junior high and senior high divisions. Located directly on the Suhua Highway, it is the only combined junior-and-senior secondary school serving Nan'ao Township — the sole option for local students to attend senior high without leaving the township. Its student population is predominantly Atayal Indigenous.

學校於民國51年（1962年）以蘇澳國中分班形式創立，民國55年（1966年）獨立為分校，民國57年（1968年）正式獨立設校為「南澳國民中學」。民國89年（2000年）8月1日，學校改制為橫跨國中與高中部的完全中學。校址位於蘇花公路旁，是南澳鄉唯一涵蓋國中與高中部的學校，也是當地學生無需離鄉即可就讀高中的唯一選擇。學生以泰雅族原住民為主。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 The Only Senior High in Nan'ao

南澳鄉唯一高中部

As the sole senior high institution serving the township, the school lets local students complete all six years of secondary education without relocating away from home.

🏫 A Complete School Since 2000

2000年改制完全中學

Upgraded on August 1, 2000 to combine junior and senior high divisions under one administration, the school now spans grades 7 through 12.

🛣️ On the Suhua Highway

緊鄰蘇花公路

Located directly on Taiwan's famous coastal Suhua Highway, the campus sits at a geographic crossroads between Yilan's coast and its mountainous interior.

🧡 A Predominantly Atayal Student Body

學生以泰雅族為主

Serving a predominantly Atayal Indigenous student population, the school anchors secondary education for Nan'ao's Indigenous mountain communities.
