About · 學校簡介

Born from a population boom, built for performing arts.

Anhe Elementary was established in 1990 as Tucheng's population surged during the district's industrial and transportation boom, easing overcrowding at nearby Qingshui Elementary School. The school has since built a strong dance team, a lion dance troupe, and an English-drama program, giving students a range of performing-arts outlets alongside their core studies.

安和國小創立於民國79年（1990年），當時土城正值工業與交通建設帶動的人口激增期，為紓解鄰近清水國小的就學壓力而設校。學校日後發展出優秀的舞蹈隊、舞獅隊與英語戲劇課程，為學生提供多元的表演藝術學習管道。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Born from Tucheng's 1990s boom.

Anhe Elementary's creation directly tracks Tucheng's 1990s industrial and transportation boom, which pulled in enough new residents to require splitting off a new elementary school from Qingshui Elementary. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
