About · 學校簡介

Whole-person education at the county seat.

Guangming Elementary opened in 2001 within Zhubei's county-government district, one of the schools built to serve the area's rapidly growing population. The school emphasizes whole-person education and reading promotion, and it maintains a notably full music program — a choir, string orchestra, wind band, and percussion ensemble all operate alongside the regular curriculum. Each year, the school hosts a community arts event known as "Art and Music Night," bringing families and neighbors onto campus for student performances.

光明國小於2001年在竹北縣治區成立，是為服務當地快速成長人口而興建的學校之一。學校強調全人教育與閱讀推廣，並經營相當完整的音樂社團——合唱團、絃樂團、管樂團與打擊樂團皆同時運作。學校每年舉辦「藝術與音樂之夜」社區藝文活動，邀請家長與鄰里共同欣賞學生演出。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎻 A Full Music Program

合唱團、絃樂團、管樂團

Guangming runs a choir, string orchestra, wind band, and percussion ensemble side by side — an unusually complete set of music offerings for an elementary school.

🎨 Art and Music Night

年度社區藝文活動

The school's annual "Art and Music Night" invites the surrounding community onto campus to enjoy student performances, turning school events into neighborhood gatherings.

📖 A Focus on Whole-Person Education

全人教育與閱讀推廣

Beyond academics, the school emphasizes whole-person development and active reading promotion as core parts of its educational philosophy.
