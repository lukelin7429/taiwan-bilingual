About · 學校簡介

An island's own university, built from a branch campus.

National Penghu University of Science and Technology (NPU) began on August 1, 1991, as a branch campus of Kaohsiung Marine College, established to bring higher education to Taiwan's outlying island county. It became fully independent in 1995 as the National Penghu Institute of Maritime Technology, was upgraded to junior college status in 2000, and became a full university on August 1, 2005. Today NPU stands as the first and only university of science and technology on any outlying island of Taiwan — and the first university anywhere in the Taiwan Strait region — serving around 2,400 students from its campus in Magong City.

國立澎湖科技大學創立於民國80年（1991年）8月1日，最初為高雄海事專科學校的分部，目的是為澎湖這個離島縣帶來高等教育。民國84年（1995年）獨立設校，定名為「國立澎湖海事管理專科學校」，民國89年（2000年）改制為專科學校，民國94年（2005年）8月1日升格為完整大學。如今澎湖科大是台灣離島第一所、也是唯一一所科技大學，同時也是台灣海峽區域第一所大學，現有約2,400名學生於馬公市校區就讀。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Four Colleges

四大學院

NPU is organized into four colleges — Marine Resources and Engineering, Tourism and Leisure, Humanities and Management, and General Education/Liberal Arts — spanning roughly 12 departments, 1 division, and 5 graduate institutes and master's programs.

💨 Taiwan's First University Wind Turbine Park

全國首座大學風力發電園區

NPU operates Taiwan's first university-based wind turbine park and an international testing center for small and mid-size wind turbines — putting Penghu's famously strong winds to research use.

🏄 An Olympic Windsurfing Tradition

奧運帆船運動傳統

The university is renowned for its windsurfing athletics program, having produced multiple Olympic windsurfing competitors representing Taiwan.

🌊 Built Around Penghu's Fishing-Island Economy

紮根海島產業

As the island county's only university, NPU's programs in marine resources, fisheries, and tourism/leisure are directly built around Penghu's fishing-island geography and economy.

Local Culture · 在地文化

An island economy on campus.

🌬️

As the only university in Taiwan's outlying island county, NPU's marine resources, fisheries, and tourism/leisure programs are directly built around Penghu's fishing-island geography and economy, while its wind turbine research park turns the county's famously strong monsoon winds into a subject of study.
