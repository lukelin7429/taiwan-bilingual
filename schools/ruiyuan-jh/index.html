About · 學校簡介

Founded on donated orchard land.

Ruiyuan Junior High traces its roots to 1968, when it began as the Ruiyuan branch of Luye Junior High. After local advocates petitioned Taiwan's Provincial Governor Huang Chieh for approval, the school became fully independent the following year, in 1969. A local benefactor, Chien Hsi-tung, donated 1.8 hectares of his own plum orchard as the school site — a gift that gave the community's children a school of their own. The school today runs a "zero-rejection" enrollment hotline, reflecting a long-standing commitment to inclusive admissions.

瑞源國中的前身可追溯至民國57年（1968年）「鹿野國民中學瑞源分部」，經地方人士向臺灣省政府主席黃杰陳情爭取後，於隔年（1969年）正式獨立設校。地方仕紳簡錫桐先生慨然捐出自家1.8公頃的梅園作為校地，成就了這所屬於在地孩子的學校。學校目前設有「零拒絕」入學專線，展現長期以來對融合教育的重視。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 Built From a Farmer's Gift

梅園捐地興學

The school's campus sits on 1.8 hectares once part of a plum orchard, donated outright by local benefactor Chien Hsi-tung so the community could have its own junior high.

✊ A School Won by Petition

地方陳情爭取設校

Independence from Luye Junior High in 1969 came only after local advocates successfully petitioned the provincial governor — a grassroots campaign that shaped the school's origins.

🤝 A Zero-Rejection Commitment

零拒絕入學專線

Ruiyuan runs a dedicated "zero-rejection" enrollment hotline, ensuring every child in the area has a path into the school regardless of circumstance.

🍵 Near Taitung's Tea Highlands

鄰近大原茶區

Situated in Luye's Dayuan area near the township's tea-growing highlands, the school sits at the edge of one of Taitung's most productive agricultural landscapes.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Luye Township, land of balloons and tea.

🎈

Luye Township 鹿野鄉, on Taitung's East Rift Valley, is famous across Taiwan for its annual hot-air balloon festival and its high-altitude tea-growing plateau. Ruiyuan Junior High serves the farming community of Ruiyuan Village near this scenic highland region.
