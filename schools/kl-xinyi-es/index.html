About · 學校簡介

Where Keelung's modern education history begins.

Xinyi Elementary's origins trace to the "Keelung Japanese Language Institute," founded September 16, 1896 — one of the earliest modern schools established in Keelung, and regarded by many as the starting point of the city's modern education history. The school was renamed multiple times under Japanese rule, then in 1945 merged with the neighboring Futaba National School to form "Ren'ai No.1 National School." In 1947 it split back apart to become Xinyi National School, and it took its current name, Xinyi Elementary, in 1968.

信義國小的前身「基隆國語傳習所」創立於1896年（明治29年）9月16日，是基隆最早的現代學校之一，也被視為基隆現代教育史的起點。學校在日治時期歷經數次更名，1945年（民國34年）與鄰近的雙葉國民學校合併為「仁愛第一國民學校」，1947年（民國36年）再度分校，成為信義國民學校，並於1968年（民國57年）定名為信義國小。
