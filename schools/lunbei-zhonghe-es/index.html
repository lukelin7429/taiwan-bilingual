About · 學校簡介

From a borrowed living room to a full campus.

Zhonghe Elementary School was founded on September 1, 1954 as Fengrong Elementary School's "草湖分校" branch classroom, with lessons originally taught in a borrowed private residence in Caohu Village. The school has since grown into a full campus that runs a wood ball team and English-language programs for its students.

中和國小創立於1954年9月1日，最初為豐榮國小的「草湖分校」分教室，最早借用草湖村一戶民宅授課。學校如今已發展為完整校區，設有木球隊與英語課程。

🏑 A Wood Ball Team

The school fields an active wood ball team, a distinctively Taiwanese sport that gives students a competitive outlet.

🗣️ English-Language Programs

英語課程

Zhonghe runs dedicated English-language programs, building language skills for its students in this garlic-farming township.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Caohu Village's garlic country.

🧄

Located in Caohu Village within Lunbei Township's traditional Zhang-Zhou Hoklo garlic-farming community, the school's history began in the same soil its families still work. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
