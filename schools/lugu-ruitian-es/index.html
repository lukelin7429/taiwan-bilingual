Ruitian Elementary School

瑞田國民小學

A small forest school rebuilt after the 1999 921 earthquake with Lions Club International support, known for its farm-teaching program.

921大地震後由國際獅子會協助重建的森林小校，以農場教學課程聞名。

About · 學校簡介

Rebuilt with a hands-on farming spirit.

Ruitian Elementary School was founded in 1952 in a village originally called Lu-yan, which was renamed Ruitian in 1959 — the school taking on the same new name. The campus was destroyed in the catastrophic 1999 921 earthquake and rebuilt with support from Lions Club International District 300A. Today Ruitian is known as a small forest school with dedicated farm-teaching areas, giving students direct, hands-on experience with agricultural education.

瑞田國小創立於民國41年（1952年），所在村落原名「鹿寮」，於民國48年（1959年）更名為「瑞田」，學校亦隨之更名。原校舍在民國88年（1999年）921大地震中損毀，後由國際獅子會300A區協助重建。如今瑞田是一所小型森林學校，設有專屬農場教學區，讓學生直接透過實作體驗農業教育。

🌾 Dedicated Farm-Teaching Areas

農場教學專區

Ruitian maintains dedicated farm-teaching areas on campus, giving students hands-on agricultural education rooted in the land their families work.

🤝 Rebuilt by International Goodwill

獅子會協助重建

After the 1999 921 earthquake destroyed the campus, Lions Club International District 300A stepped in to help rebuild the school for the community.

🏷️ A Village That Renamed Itself

村名與校名同步更改

The school shares its story with its village — originally called Lu-yan, it was renamed Ruitian in 1959, and the school took on the same fresh identity.
