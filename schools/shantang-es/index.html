Shantang Elementary School

善糖國小

A former Taiwan Sugar Corporation company school built beside the historic Shanhua Sugar Refinery, whose entire curriculum is organized around the history and memory of sugar.

緊鄰善化糖廠而建的前台糖員工子弟學校，全校課程皆圍繞「糖」的歷史與記憶設計。

About · 學校簡介

A school built by, and for, the sugar refinery.

Shantang Elementary School was founded in 1947 as “the Taiwan Sugar Corporation Wanli Refinery Employees' Children's School” (臺灣糖業公司灣裡糖廠員工子弟學校), an unusual origin among Tainan's public elementary schools even today. It took its present name, Shantang Elementary School, in 1968. Built in Minnan-style architecture directly adjacent to the historic Shanhua Sugar Refinery and today's Shantang Cultural Park, the campus features a large slide and sand pit shaped by that industrial-town setting.

善糖國小創立於民國36年（1947年），初名「臺灣糖業公司灣裡糖廠員工子弟學校」——這樣的起源在今日台南公立國小中相當罕見。民國57年（1968年）改為現名「善糖國民小學」。校舍以閩南式建築緊鄰善化糖廠與今日的善糖文化園區而建，校園內設有大型溜滑梯與沙坑，處處可見糖廠聚落的生活痕跡。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏭 Born From the Refinery Workforce

源自台糖員工子弟學校

The school began life in 1947 as a company school for the children of Taiwan Sugar Corporation's Wanli refinery workers, unusual among Tainan public elementary schools.

🍬 A Curriculum Built Around Sugar

課程三大主軸：糖史、糖廠建築、童年記憶

Its school-based curriculum is organized entirely around “sugar”: the history of sugarcane refining, the architectural heritage of the refinery buildings, and childhood memories of sweets.

🏘️ Next Door to the Historic Refinery

緊鄰善化糖廠文化園區

Built in Minnan-style architecture beside the Shanhua Sugar Refinery and Shantang Cultural Park, the campus includes a large slide and sand pit reflecting its industrial-town setting.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The most literal expression of Shanhua's sugar heritage.

🎋

Of all the schools in this district, Shantang Elementary is the most literal embodiment of Shanhua's sugar-refinery heritage — a school born out of the refinery workforce itself, sitting next door to the historic Shanhua Sugar Refinery, and still teaching sugarcane history as its core curriculum today.
