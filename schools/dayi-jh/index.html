Dayi Junior High School

高雄市立大義國民中學

A junior high near Lotus Pond and the old Fongshan County walled city, whose marching band has been a fixture of local celebrations since 1996.

About · 學校簡介

A marching band beside the old walled city.

Dayi Junior High School was founded in 1970 and moved into its permanent campus two years later, in 1972. Since 1996, its wind and marching band has performed regularly at local celebrations near the Lotus Pond scenic area, becoming something of a neighborhood fixture. The school sits close to Lotus Pond (蓮池潭) and the East Gate of the old Fongshan County walled city, placing it at the heart of Zuoying's historic core.

大義國中創立於民國59年（1970年），民國61年（1972年）遷入現址永久校區。自民國85年（1996年）起，學校管樂儀隊經常在蓮池潭風景區周邊的地方慶典中演出，已成為社區活動的固定班底。校址鄰近蓮池潭與鳳山縣舊城東門，正位於左營歷史核心地帶。

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the heart of Zuoying's historic core.

🏯

Dayi sits within Zuoying's historic core, close to both Lotus Pond (蓮池潭) and the East Gate of the old Fongshan County walled city — remnants of the Qing-dynasty county seat that once stood here, long before the district became a Navy town. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
