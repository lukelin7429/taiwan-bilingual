Jiude Elementary School

A 1992 elementary school bordered by the Taichung EPA office, Wuri Train Station, and Mingdao High School, near the Changhua boundary.

About · 學校簡介

A crossroads campus near the county line.

Jiude Elementary School was established in 1992, with student enrollment beginning the following year. The campus is bordered by the Taichung Environmental Protection Administration office, Wuri Train Station, Mingdao High School, and Nantun District, placing it in a busy transit-and-institution corridor straddling the Taichung–Changhua boundary.

九德國小創立於民國81年（1992年），隔年開始招生。校址周邊緊鄰台中市環保局、烏日火車站、明道高中與南屯區，座落於台中與彰化交界的交通與機構匯集廊帶。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚉 Beside Wuri Train Station

緊鄰烏日火車站

The campus borders Wuri Train Station, giving the school community direct proximity to one of the district's main rail transit points.

🏫 Neighbors Include a Major High School

鄰近明道高中

Jiude sits near Mingdao High School and the Taichung EPA office, part of a cluster of institutions along this stretch of Wuri District.

🗺️ On the Taichung–Changhua Line

台中彰化交界

Its location straddling the boundary between Taichung City and Changhua County places the school at the edge of two administrative regions.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Wuri, a gateway between city and county.

🚉

Jiude's location straddling the Taichung–Changhua boundary reflects Wuri's historic role as the gateway and transit link between Taichung City and Changhua County. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
