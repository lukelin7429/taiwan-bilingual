About · 學校簡介

A school rooted in Xiangyang's farmland.

Wujia Elementary School serves the Wujia (五甲) and Xiangyang (香洋) area of Guanmiao District, a rural farmland belt where bamboo-shoot cultivation is a major livelihood for local families. An exact founding date has not been confirmed in available records. The school is linked to "走讀香洋" (Reading Xiangyang), a community history website documenting the surrounding locale, and keeps an attached kindergarten alongside an active YouTube and social-media presence for recruitment and community outreach.

五甲國小位於關廟區五甲、香洋一帶的農村地區，當地居民多以種植綠竹筍維生。學校確切創校年份於現有資料中尚未查明。學校與地方社區歷史網站「走讀香洋」關係密切，並附設幼兒園，同時透過YouTube等社群媒體積極經營招生與社區交流。

Wujia Elementary sits within the Wujia/Xiangyang area of Guanmiao, documented through the school-linked "走讀香洋" (Reading Xiangyang) community history project.
