Jianshan Junior High School

尖山國民中學

A mid-sized junior high founded to relieve crowding at Yingge Junior High, known today for its baseball, wrestling, and track programs.

About · 學校簡介

Founded to ease growing pains in a booming pottery town.

Jianshan Junior High was established in 1989 as Yingge's ceramics industry and highway-driven economy fueled rapid population growth, easing overcrowding at the district's original Yingge Junior High. The school appointed its first formal principal in 1994 and has since grown into a mid-sized campus of 14 regular classes plus 3 physical-education classes, with an attached kindergarten. It has built a strong athletic reputation, particularly in baseball, wrestling, and track and field.

尖山國中創立於民國78年（1989年），當時鶯歌因陶瓷產業與公路建設帶動人口快速成長，原有的鶯歌國中已不敷使用，因而設校紓解就學壓力。學校於民國83年（1994年）任命首任正式校長，如今發展為中型學校，設有14個普通班及3個體育班，並附設幼兒園。學校在棒球、角力、田徑等運動項目上表現優異，建立起穩固的體育傳統。
