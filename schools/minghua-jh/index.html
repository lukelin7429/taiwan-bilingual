Minghua Junior High School

明華國民中學

One of Kaohsiung's newest junior highs, built in stages through the 2000s to serve the fast-growing Art Museum residential district.

About · 學校簡介

Built for a fast-growing new district.

Minghua Junior High School was founded in 2001. It borrowed classroom space from nearby Dayi Junior High School in 2003 for its first intake of students, before its own phase-one buildings opened in 2004 and phase-two buildings followed in 2006. With about 1,703 students, Minghua is large for a junior high, and its campus includes an underground three-story activity center. The school was built specifically to serve the rapidly developed Art Museum and Mingcheng residential district in Gushan.

明華國中創立於民國90年（2001年）。2003年首屆招生時，暫借鄰近大義國中校舍上課，第一期校舍於2004年完工，第二期則於2006年完工。學校目前約有1,703名學生，規模在國中中屬偏大型，校園並設有地下三層活動中心。明華國中的興建，正是為了服務鼓山區快速發展的美術館與明誠特區。

🏗️ Built in Stages Through the 2000s

分兩期興建

After borrowing classrooms in 2003, Minghua's own campus was built in two phases, completing phase-one buildings in 2004 and phase-two in 2006.

👨‍🎓 A Large Student Body for a Junior High

約1,703名學生

With approximately 1,703 students, Minghua is notably large for a junior high, reflecting strong demand in its fast-growing residential district.

🏢 An Underground Three-Story Activity Center

The campus includes an underground three-story activity center, a modern facility reflecting the school's relatively recent construction.

🖼️ Serving the Art Museum District

服務美術館特區

Local Culture · 在地文化

As one of Kaohsiung's newest junior highs, Minghua was built specifically to serve the rapidly developed Art Museum and Mingcheng residential district — a marker of Gushan's continued urban growth into the 21st century. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
