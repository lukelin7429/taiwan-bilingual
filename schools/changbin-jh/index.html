Changbin Junior High School

長濱國民中學

A remote coastal junior high where 9th graders build and sail a traditional boat as a coming-of-age rite, alongside award-winning maker education.

偏遠海岸國中，九年級生以造舟出航作為成年禮，同時也在科技創客教育屢獲國際獎項。

About · 學校簡介

From handmade boats to international invention awards.

Changbin Junior High serves students across this remote township on Taitung's northern east coast. The school houses a Maker Education and Technology Center, and its students won gold medals at the 2025 Nuremberg International Invention Exhibition — a striking achievement for a small rural school. Reflecting the linguistic diversity of its community, the school offers Indigenous and minority language instruction in Bunun, Vietnamese, Minnan, Kavalan, and Taiwan Sign Language, alongside its core curriculum.

長濱國中服務臺東東海岸北段這處偏遠鄉鎮的學子。校內設有創客教育科技中心，學生曾在2025年德國紐倫堡國際發明展中榮獲金牌——對一所偏鄉小校而言相當難得。學校也反映在地語言的多樣性，開設布農語、越南語、閩南語、噶瑪蘭語及臺灣手語等語言課程，與核心課程並行。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛶 A Boat-Building Coming-of-Age Rite

造舟出航成年禮

Ninth graders take part in a traditional boat-building and sailing program as a rite of passage — a direct expression of Changbin's Amis maritime heritage along Taiwan's east coast.

🏆 International Invention Award Winners

紐倫堡發明展金牌

Students from the school's Maker Education and Technology Center won gold medals at the 2025 Nuremberg International Invention Exhibition, a remarkable result for a school in one of Taitung's most remote townships.

🗣️ Five Languages of the Community

多元語言課程

The school offers instruction in Bunun, Vietnamese, Minnan, Kavalan, and Taiwan Sign Language, reflecting the layered, multiethnic makeup of the communities it serves.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A maritime rite of passage.

🌊

The school's canoe-building rite of passage for graduating ninth graders directly reflects Changbin's Amis maritime heritage along Taiwan's east coast, connecting a modern educational milestone to a much older relationship between the township's Indigenous communities and the sea.
