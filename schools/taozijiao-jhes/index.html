Taozijiao Combined Junior High and Elementary School

桃子腳國民中小學

A combined K-9 school built inside the planned "Beida Special District" new town, named for a rural place-name that predates the development around it.

座落於「北大特區」新興市鎮內的國中小學，校名源自開發前的古地名，保留樹林早期的鄉土記憶。

About · 學校簡介

One school, nine grades, inside a brand-new town.

Taozijiao Combined Junior High and Elementary School opened in 2001 as a junior high, adding its elementary division the following year to become a single K-9 institution. Its 5.2-hectare campus, with 48 elementary classes and 21 junior-high classes, sits inside the "Beida Special District" (北大特區), a large planned new town built around National Taipei University's Sanxia campus that has transformed the surrounding farmland into dense modern housing. As a combined institution, the school appears here as a single page even though some directories may list its junior-high and elementary divisions separately.

桃子腳國中小創立於民國90年（2001年），初設國中部，隔年（2002年）增設國小部，成為涵蓋國小、國中的九年一貫學校。校地5.2公頃，設國小部48班、國中部21班，位於環繞台北大學三峽校區興建的大型新市鎮「北大特區」內，周邊原為農田，如今已發展成密集的現代化住宅區。提醒：本校為單一行政體制下的國中小合校，部分名錄可能將國小部與國中部分別列出，實為同一所學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🍑 Named for the Old "Peach Tree Foot"

因「桃子腳」古地名得名

The school's name preserves "桃子腳莊," an old place-name recalling a legend of three peach trees where travelers once rested — a rural memory kept alive amid a fully modern new town.

🏙️ Anchoring the Beida Special District

北大特區的教育樞紐

Built inside the planned new town surrounding National Taipei University's Sanxia campus, the school serves a rapidly growing population of young families in newly built housing.

🏫 One School, Nine Grades

一貫九年，一校到底

Established as a junior high in 2001 and expanded with an elementary division in 2002, the school now runs grades 1 through 9 as a single combined institution.

🌳 From Farmland to New Town

從農田到新市鎮

Its 5.2-hectare campus reflects the same transformation as its surroundings: land once devoted to agriculture now supports a 48-classroom elementary division and a 21-classroom junior-high division.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A rural memory inside a modern new town.

🍑

The name "Taozijiao" (桃子腳) preserves a pre-development place-name — local legend holds that three peach trees once stood here, offering shade to travelers passing through what was then open farmland. That rural memory now sits at the center of the Beida Special District, one of Shulin's largest and newest planned communities. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
