About · 學校簡介

A school built on layered history.

Longgang Elementary School existed as "Lizilun National School" from October 1945, at Taiwan's retrocession, before being renamed Longgang National School in January 1951. It became Longgang Elementary School in September 1968 under Taiwan's nine-year compulsory education reform. The campus totals 19,966 square meters, including a 3,786-square-meter area containing a Pingpu (Plains Indigenous) archaeological site on a rear hill — a notable heritage feature. The school's lineage also includes a former branch, Wangliao Branch, established in 1951 and made independent in 1960 as today's Wangliao Elementary School.

龍崗國小自民國34年（1945年）10月台灣光復起，初名「栗子崙國民學校」，於民國40年（1951年）1月改稱龍崗國民學校，並於民國57年（1968年）9月九年國教實施後改稱龍崗國小。校地總面積19,966平方公尺，其中3,786平方公尺後方山丘設有平埔族考古遺址，是校內一項珍貴的文化資產。學校校史亦包含民國40年（1951年）設立、於民國49年（1960年）獨立為今日網寮國小的網寮分校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏺 A Pingpu Archaeological Site

平埔族考古遺址

A 3,786-square-meter area on a rear hill of the campus contains a Pingpu (Plains Indigenous) archaeological site, a rare heritage feature for an elementary school campus.

📰 Student Journalism Programs

小小播報員與龍崗小記者

Programs like "小小播報員" (Little Broadcasters) and "龍崗小記者" (Longgang Junior Reporters) give students hands-on experience in reporting and public speaking.

🎵 Recorder Ensemble Competitions

直笛隊競賽

An active music program, including a recorder ensemble that competes in county competitions, forms a core part of the school's arts curriculum.

🌏 International Education & Disaster Drills

國際教育與防災演練

The school is also strong in international education and regularly runs disaster-prevention drills as part of its safety curriculum.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Layers of history beneath a fishing township.

🏺

The campus's Pingpu Indigenous archaeological site connects the school directly to the pre-Han indigenous history of the Dongshi coastal plain, layered beneath its now Han fishing-and-farming community. See the Chiayi county page for more on the region.
