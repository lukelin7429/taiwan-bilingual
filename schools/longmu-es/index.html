About · 學校簡介

A hillside school with a hidden forest.

Longmu Elementary School became independent in 1971, growing out of the Longmu branch campus of Dashu Elementary School. Its hillside campus at the foot of the hills in Longmu ("dragon eye") Village has earned it the local nickname "green forest elementary school" (綠森林小學), thanks to an ecology pond and a hidden forest park tucked behind the school — a minor tourist attraction in its own right. The village and school take their name from a local geographic landform said to resemble a dragon's eye.

龍目國小於1971年（民國60年）自大樹國小的龍目分校獨立設校。校園位於龍目里的丘陵山腳，因擁有生態池與隱身校園後方的森林公園，在地人暱稱其為「綠森林小學」，也成為當地小型觀光景點。「龍目」之名源自當地一處狀似龍眼的地形景觀。
