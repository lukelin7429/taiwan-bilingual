Changan Junior High School

彰化市立彰安國民中學

A comprehensive public junior high in the heart of Changhua City, founded to ease overcrowding — now known for its dance, visual arts, and athletics programs.

位於彰化市中心的公立完全中學，創校初衷是紓解市區國中就學壓力，如今以舞蹈、美術、體育班聞名。

About · 學校簡介

Built to give Changhua City room to grow.

Changan Junior High was established on August 1, 1968, at a time when Changhua City had only a single county junior high school and badly needed more classroom space. Its first class enrolled 470 students across nine sections, initially borrowing facilities before the school moved onto its own permanent campus. Today it has grown into a comprehensive school of roughly 35 classes and about 838 students, offering specialized tracks in dance, visual arts, and athletics alongside its regular academic program.

彰安國中創立於民國57年（1968年）8月1日，當時彰化市僅有一所縣立國中，急需增設學校紓解就學壓力。首屆招收九個班、470名學生，初期借用他校場地上課，後遷入現址。如今學校已發展為約35班、838名學生的完全中學，除一般學科教育外，並設有舞蹈班、美術班與體育班等特色課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Born to Ease the Squeeze

為紓解就學壓力而生

Changan was founded in 1968 specifically to give Changhua City a second junior high school, opening with nine classes of 470 students who initially studied in borrowed facilities.

💃 A Home for Dance and Art

舞蹈班與美術班

Beyond its regular academic classes, the school runs dedicated dance and visual arts tracks, giving students a path to develop specialized talents alongside their core studies.

🏃 An Athletics Program

體育班特色課程

A dedicated athletics class rounds out Changan's specialized offerings, reflecting the school's identity as a comprehensive campus serving a wide range of student interests.

🌆 Growing with the City

與彰化市共同成長

From a nine-class start to roughly 35 classes and 838 students today, Changan's growth has tracked Changhua City's development as a central Taiwanese commercial hub.
