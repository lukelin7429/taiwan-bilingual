About · 學校簡介

The only school with a true sea view.

Baisha Elementary began in 1954 as a branch class of Liuqiu National School, was upgraded to a full branch school in 1955, became independent as "Baisha National School" in 1958, and took its current name in 1968. The school sits in Zhongfu Village, historically known as "Zhong'aozi" — a small bay that once served as a boat dock at the center of Baisha Beach. What sets it apart is its setting: Baisha is known as Xiaoliuqiu's only school with a true oceanfront view, where students can see the ocean and fishing boats directly from campus, with beach shower facilities nearby. With about 112 students, 25 staff and employees, and 7 classes, the school has been featured on public television's children's programming, "台灣囡仔讚" (Taiwan's Wonderful Kids), as a showcase island school.

白沙國小創校於1954年（民國43年），最初為琉球國民學校分校班，1955年（民國44年）升格為分校，1958年（民國47年）獨立設校為「白沙國民學校」，1968年（民國57年）更為現名。學校位於中福村，該地舊稱「中澳仔」，曾是白沙海灘中心的一處小港灣船隻停靠處。這所學校最特別之處在於其地理位置：白沙國小被稱為小琉球唯一真正面海的學校，學生在校園內就能直接望見大海與漁船，附近還設有海灘沖水設施。全校約有112名學生、25位教職員、7個班級，並曾登上公共電視兒童節目《台灣囡仔讚》，作為離島特色學校的代表。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Taiwan's only coral-reef island township.

🐢

Xiaoliuqiu (琉球鄉) is Taiwan's only coral-reef island township, reachable only by ferry from Donggang, with an economy built on tourism, snorkeling, and traditional fishing. Called "Xiaoliuqiu's only seaview school," Baisha Elementary faces the ocean and fishing boats directly from campus — a vivid emblem of the island's coral-reef and fishing culture.
