About · 學校簡介

Taiwan's first national arts high school.

National Xingang Senior High School of Arts was officially established on August 1, 2006, as Taiwan's first national ("國立") senior high school dedicated entirely to the arts. Its preparatory office was approved in 2001, and an unveiling ceremony was held in December 2003, presided over by then-President Chen Shui-bian. While its purpose-built Xingang campus was under construction, the school was temporarily housed at National Chiayi University's Linsen campus, finally moving into its permanent home in 2008. Today it remains an intentionally small school, with roughly 217 to 259 students across about 15 classes studying music, fine arts, dance, and drama.

國立新港藝術高級中學於民國95年（2006年）8月1日正式設立，是全台第一所以藝術為主軸的國立高級中學。籌備處於民國90年（2001年）獲准成立，並於民國92年（2003年）12月舉行揭牌典禮，由時任總統陳水扁主持。校舍興建期間，師生暫借國立嘉義大學林森校區上課，直到民國97年（2008年）才遷入新港的正式校地。學校規模精巧，全校約217至259名學生、15個班級，分設音樂、美術、舞蹈、戲劇科。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎨 Taiwan's First National Arts High School

全台首創國立藝術高中

Established in 2006, this is Taiwan's first national senior high school built specifically around an arts curriculum, offering dedicated tracks in music, fine arts, dance, and drama.

🏛️ A Campus Built for Aesthetics

美感教育的校園設計

The purpose-built Xingang campus, opened in 2008, was designed with an emphasis on art-integrated architecture, and has been featured in case studies on aesthetic campus design.

🎭 A Small, Focused Community

精巧的小校規模

With roughly 15 classes and around 250 students, the school maintains an intimate scale that supports close mentorship between students and their arts faculty.

🙏 Rooted in Temple-Arts Heritage

扎根廟宇工藝傳統

Located in a township famous for its folk-art and temple-arts traditions, the school channels this local heritage of embroidery, temple painting, and performance into a formal arts curriculum.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xingang, home of the Matsu pilgrimage.

🏮

Xingang Township is the seat of the famous Fengtian Temple (奉天宮) and its annual Matsu pilgrimage (刈火), one of Taiwan's most important folk-religious festivals. This deep well of folk-art and temple-arts heritage — embroidery, temple painting, and ritual performance — runs directly through the township, and National Xingang Senior High School of Arts channels that same creative energy into a formal, modern arts education.
