About · 學校簡介

A small rural school on the lagoon coast.

Xingang Elementary is a small rural school serving Xingang and Yantian villages on Yong'an District's lagoon coast. As of 2022 it enrolled just 37 students across 6 classes, taught by 12 teachers — a scale typical of the district's smaller coastal communities. Despite its size, the school runs a baseball team, a roller-skating club, and a traditional silk-and-bamboo music ensemble.

新港國小是服務永安區潟湖沿岸新港里與鹽田里的小型鄉村學校。截至民國111年（2022年），全校僅37名學生、6個班級、12位教師，反映出當地沿海小型社區的規模。儘管校園不大，學校仍設有棒球隊、直排輪社，以及傳統絲竹樂團。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚾ A Baseball Team

棒球隊

Despite its small enrollment, the school fields its own baseball team, a point of pride for the tight-knit lagoon-coast community.

⛸️ Roller-Skating Club

直排輪社

A roller-skating club gives students another outlet for physical activity alongside baseball, on a campus otherwise defined by its small size.

🎻 A Silk-and-Bamboo Ensemble

絲竹樂團

Students take part in a traditional silk-and-bamboo (絲竹) music ensemble, playing classical Chinese string and wind instruments — a cultural program uncommon for a school this size.

🌾 A School Built for a Small Community

迷你小校

With around 37 students in 2022, Xingang is one of the smallest elementary schools on Yong'an's coast, giving every student close attention across its handful of classes.

Local Culture · 在地文化

On the historic Miluogang wetlands.

🎋

Xingang and neighboring Yantian villages sit within the historic "Miluogang" (彌羅港) wetland area, whose name is thought to derive from a Siraya or Makatao Indigenous word meaning "bamboo." See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
