National Guanshan Vocational Senior High School

國立關山高級工商職業學校

About · 學校簡介

From a junior-high branch to a national vocational school.

Guanshan Vocational Senior High traces its origins to 1955, when it opened as a branch of the Provincial Taitung Middle School. Commerce and home economics programs were added in 1969, followed by architecture and refrigeration programs in 1974, gradually building the school's identity as a technical and vocational institution. It became an independent provincial vocational school in 1978 and was elevated to national status in 2000. Between 1996 and 2013, the school briefly ran a comprehensive high school track alongside its vocational programs. Today its campus spans roughly 5.1 hectares in Guanshan Town, at the gateway to Taitung's East Rift Valley.

關山工商創校於民國44年（1955年），最初為「省立台東中學關山分部」。民國58年（1969年）增設商業與家事科，民國63年（1974年）再增設建築與冷凍科，逐步奠定學校技職教育的定位。民國67年（1978年）獨立為省立高職，民國89年（2000年）改隸為國立高職。民國85年至102年（1996–2013年）間，學校曾一度並行綜合高中學程。如今校園面積約5.1公頃，座落於臺東縱谷入口的關山鎮。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏗️ Seven Decades of Growth

七十年校史沿革

From a 1955 branch campus to a national vocational school in 2000, Guanshan Vocational's history traces the broader evolution of Taiwan's technical education system.

🛠️ A Range of Technical Programs

商業、家事、建築、冷凍科

Over the decades the school built out programs in commerce, home economics, architecture, and refrigeration — a broad technical curriculum for the East Rift Valley region.

🎓 A Brief Comprehensive High School Era

綜合高中學程

From 1996 to 2013, the school ran a comprehensive high school track alongside its vocational offerings, giving students an additional academic pathway.

🚴 Gateway to the Rift Valley

縱谷入口的校園

Located in Guanshan Town, the school sits at the gateway to the East Rift Valley's rice-farming region and the famous Guanshan bicycle loop path.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Guanshan Town, the bicycle-loop capital of the valley.

🚴

Guanshan Town 關山鎮 is famous across Taiwan for its scenic bicycle loop path, which circles the town through rice paddies and farmland, and for its long tradition of rice farming in the East Rift Valley.
