About · 學校簡介

A school founded for Taiwanese students.

National Tainan First Senior High School, widely known as Tainan First Senior High or "臺南一中," was approved for founding on April 1, 1922 and opened on May 1 of that year as the "Tainan Prefectural Second Middle School" — established specifically to serve Taiwanese students in southern Taiwan, in contrast to schools that primarily served Japanese students. After 1945 it was renamed "Taiwan Provincial Tainan First Middle School," operating as a full six-year school covering both junior and senior high. In 2000, following a national-level reorganization, it took its current name and became a senior-high-only institution. Over a century after its founding, the school is widely regarded as one of Taiwan's most academically elite public high schools, with a large and influential alumni network known as the "竹園" (Bamboo Garden) alumni culture.

國立臺南第一高級中學，一般俗稱「臺南一中」，於1922年（大正11年）4月1日獲准設校，同年5月1日正式開學，初名「臺南州立臺南第二中學校」，創校宗旨即是專為招收台灣籍學生而設，有別於當時主要招收日籍學生的學校。戰後（1945年）改稱「臺灣省立臺南第一中學」，為完整六年制（含國中部與高中部）學校。2000年精省後改為現名，並轉型為純高中部。歷經百餘年，該校至今仍被公認為台灣學術聲望最高的公立高中之一，擁有龐大且具影響力的校友網絡，即所謂的「竹園」校友文化。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎋 The "Bamboo Garden" Alumni Culture

竹園校友文化

The school's large and influential alumni network is known as the "竹園" (Bamboo Garden) culture, reflecting over a century of continuous academic reputation.

🎓 Founded for Taiwanese Students

創校即招收台籍學生

Established in 1922 specifically to serve Taiwanese students in southern Taiwan, the school's founding purpose set it apart from schools originally intended for Japanese students, such as Tainan First Girls'/Boys' schools.

📖 An Active School-History Museum

校史館網站

The school maintains a dedicated school-history museum website documenting its Japanese-era founding and evolution over the past century.

🚪 Near the Old Great East Gate

鄰近大東門

The campus sits at the East Gate roundabout area on Minzu Road, near the historic Great East Gate (大東門) that once marked the eastern boundary of walled Tainan.

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the gateway between old and new Tainan.

🚪

Located at the East Gate roundabout on Minzu Road, near the historic Great East Gate (大東門) that once marked the eastern boundary of walled Tainan, the campus sits right at the transition point between the old walled city and the newer East District/NCKU area.
