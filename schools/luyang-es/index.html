About · 學校簡介

Taichung's newest school, named for a swordsmith.

Luyang Elementary opened in August 2025, making it the newest elementary school in Taichung City. It was built to relieve overcrowding caused by Shalu District's ongoing population boom. The school takes its name from — and sits beside — the historic residence of Chen Tian-yang, a traditional swordsmith whose craft is part of Shalu's local heritage.

鹿陽國小於2025年8月啟用，是目前台中市最新設立的國小，興建目的是為紓解沙鹿地區人口持續成長所帶來的就學壓力。校名取自並緊鄰傳統刀劍匠師陳天陽的故居，將學校與在地工藝傳承緊密相連。
