About · 學校簡介

Grown from a branch campus into its own school.

Mingde Elementary originated in 1959 as a branch of Linzuo Elementary School, before later becoming an independent school in its own right. It is located in Xiweicuo (溪尾村), a farming hamlet within Sihu Township, giving families in this part of the countryside a school within easy reach.

明德國小創始於民國48年（1959年），最初為林厝國小的分校，後獨立設校。學校位於四湖鄉的農村聚落溪尾村，讓當地家庭的孩童就近入學。

扎根溪尾村

Located in the farming hamlet of Xiweicuo, the school gives this part of rural Sihu Township its own accessible elementary education.

🌾 Part of a Wider Farming Network

農村教育網絡的一環

As a branch-turned-independent school, Mingde continues a pattern seen across Sihu, where rural farming communities built their own local schools over time.
