About · 學校簡介

Over 120 years serving all of Checheng.

Checheng Elementary School was established on February 8, 1901, as Checheng Public School under preparation by Hamuro Choukyo, making it the only elementary school serving all of Checheng Township, with over 120 years of continuous history. The school currently has 33 classes and 652 students, plus two branch campuses — the Hot Spring branch (溫泉分校) and the Sheliao branch (射寮分校) — and an attached kindergarten. In 1902 the school established a Fengkang branch school, which became a full branch campus in 1906 and later evolved into today's separate Fengkang Elementary School in Fangshan Township.

車城國小創立於明治34年（1901年）2月8日，由羽室長享籌辦，原名車城公學校，是車城鄉唯一的國小，擁有超過120年的連續校史。學校現有33班、652名學生，並設有溫泉分校與射寮分校兩個分校，以及附設幼兒園。明治35年（1902年）本校設立楓港分教場，明治39年（1906年）升格為分校，後來演變為今日枋山鄉獨立設校的楓港國小。

🏫 Over 120 Years of History

🌡️ Two Branch Campuses

Local Culture · 在地文化

Checheng, gateway to Kenting and home to Fu'an Temple.

⛩️

Checheng is the historic gateway to Kenting National Park and site of Fu'an Temple (福安宮, founded 1662) — one of Taiwan's most revered Earth God temples, famed for its towering incense furnace (nicknamed the “money-counting machine” by locals) and a pair of massive stone lions. The temple also preserves stone tablets referencing the 1867 Rover Incident, when General Liu Mingdeng helped mediate between the U.S. and Taiwanese indigenous communities.
