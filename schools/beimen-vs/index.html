National Beimen Vocational High School of Agriculture and Industry

國立北門高級農工職業學校

A vocational senior high school dating to 1936, whose agricultural, industrial and food-processing programs grew directly out of the farming and fishing economy of the Beimen coastal plain.

創校於1936年日治時期的高職，農業、工科與食品加工等科別，皆源自北門沿海地區的農漁業經濟。

About · 學校簡介

Vocational roots in the coastal farming and fishing economy.

National Beimen Vocational High School was founded on June 1, 1936 under Japanese rule as the "Beimen National Agricultural Specialized School." After 1945 it was reorganized as Tainan County Beimen Junior Agricultural Vocational School, became a provincial vocational school in 1968, and attained national (國立) status in 2000. Its technical departments in machinery, electrical engineering, electronics, civil engineering, food processing, animal husbandry, and landscaping trace directly back to the agricultural and aquaculture economy of the surrounding Beimen and Jiali coastal plain.

國立北門高級農工職業學校創立於昭和11年（民國25年，1936年）6月1日，初名「北門國民農業專修學校」。民國34年（1945年）後改制為「臺南縣立北門初級農業職業學校」，民國57年（1968年）改為省立農工職業學校，民國89年（2000年）改隸國立。學校設有機械、電機、電子、土木、食品加工、畜牧、造園等科，皆與北門、佳里沿海一帶的農漁業經濟發展密切相關。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🔧 Seven Technical Departments

七大專業科別

Programs span machinery, electrical engineering, electronics, civil engineering, food processing, animal husbandry, and landscaping — a curriculum built for the region's agricultural and coastal economy.

🌏 International Sister Schools

國際姊妹校

The school maintains sister-school partnerships with institutions in Thailand and Israel, giving students exposure beyond Taiwan.

🥋 Competitive Athletics

體育代表隊

Teams compete in soft tennis, volleyball, track and field, and taekwondo, representing the school at regional and national meets.

🌾 Grown From the Coastal Economy

扎根農漁經濟

Founded in 1936 to train the Beimen coastal region's workforce, the school's agricultural and food-processing tracks remain tied to its farming and fishing roots.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Jiali, market town of the coastal plain.

⛩️

Jiali District has long served as the market town for Tainan's northern coastal plain, centered on Jinluan Temple (金唐殿), one of the region's key Wangye "King Boat Festival" temples and a focal point of Siraya Pingpu ancestor-spirit (阿立祖) worship in nearby hamlets. This vocational school's agricultural and food-processing curriculum grew directly out of the salt-farming, rice-growing and oyster-culture economy of the Beimen coastline that Jiali has served as an inland hub for generations.
