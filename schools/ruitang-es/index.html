Ruitang Elementary School

瑞塘國民小學

A newer public elementary school opened in 1998 to serve rising enrollment in Yangmei's Puxin area, with a 2017 expansion adding an activity center and specialty classrooms.

1998年設立的新興國小，因應埔心地區學生人數增加而設立，2017年增建活動中心及專科教室。

About · 學校簡介

Built for Puxin's growing school-age population.

Ruitang Elementary received Ministry of Education funding approval on July 4, 1998, and was formally established by the Taoyuan County government just three weeks later, on July 24, under the original name "Taoyuan County Yangming Elementary School Ruitang Branch." It is one of Yangmei's more recently established elementary schools, founded specifically to absorb rising student numbers in the Puxin area. In 2017, the Taoyuan City government built a new activity center and additional arts and specialty classrooms to address growing space constraints on campus.

瑞塘國小於民國87年（1998年）7月4日經教育部核准設校經費，同年7月24日由桃園縣政府成立，原名「桃園縣楊明國民小學瑞塘分校」。是楊梅區設校時間較晚的小學之一，因應埔心地區學生人數增加而設立。桃園市政府於2017年興建活動中心及附屬藝文、專科教室，因應校園空間不足問題。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏗️ Founded to Meet Rising Enrollment

因應學生人數增加而設立

Ruitang was established specifically to absorb growing student numbers in the Puxin area, a direct response to local population pressure in the late 1990s.

🎨 A 2017 Activity Center and Arts Wing

2017年增建活動中心與藝文教室

The Taoyuan City government funded a new activity center plus additional arts and specialty classrooms in 2017, expanding the campus to keep pace with demand.

🌱 Started as a Branch of Yangming Elementary

原為楊明國小瑞塘分校

Ruitang began life as a branch campus of Yangming Elementary before gaining its own independent identity in 1998, part of Yangmei's broader pattern of splitting schools as neighborhoods grow.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A marker of Puxin's fast urbanization.

🏘️

Ruitang Elementary's establishment reflects the rapid urbanization the Puxin area of Yangmei has undergone over the past two to three decades, driven in part by nearby transportation development. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
