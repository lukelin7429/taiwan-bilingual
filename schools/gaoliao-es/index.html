About · 學校簡介

A name that remembers the hunters who came first.

Gaoliao Elementary opened in September 1961 as a branch of Guanshan (Guanyin) Elementary and became independent on August 1, 1964, making it one of the newer, smaller schools in Yuli Township. The place name "Gaoliao" itself carries history: it derives from Amis Indigenous hunters who built hunting huts (寮, liao) in this area before Taiwan's 1945 retrocession from Japanese rule — a naming legacy that ties the modern school directly to the land's earlier Indigenous use.

高寮國小於民國50年（1961年）9月以關山（觀音）國小分校形式設立，民國53年（1964年）8月1日獨立設校，是玉里鎮較晚成立的小型學校之一。「高寮」這個地名本身即承載著歷史：其名源自阿美族獵人在1945年台灣光復前於此地搭建獵寮（「寮」）的傳統，將這所現代學校與這片土地更早的原住民使用歷史直接連結在一起。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A place-name tied to Amis heritage.

🏕️

Gaoliao sits within Yuli Township 玉里鎮, a rice-farming and hot-springs town in Hualien's Hua-Tung Valley near the Tropic of Cancer. The village's own name directly ties this community to its Amis Indigenous heritage, a reminder that the rice fields and quiet streets of today's Gaoliao sit on ground once used for traditional hunting.
