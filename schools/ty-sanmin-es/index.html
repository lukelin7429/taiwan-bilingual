About · 學校簡介

A boundary school on the North Cross-Island Highway.

Sanmin Elementary became independently established in 1952. It sits directly on the North Cross-Island Highway (北橫公路) at the boundary between Fuxing and neighboring Daxi District — its catchment spans both Sanmin Village (Fuxing) and Xinfeng Village (Daxi), crossing township lines. The campus is small, just 10,454 square meters, with 6 regular classes plus 1 resource class, and added a mixed-age kindergarten class starting in the 2001 academic year.

三民國小民國41年（1952年）獨立設校，座落於北橫公路上、復興區與鄰近大溪區的交界，學區橫跨復興區三民里與大溪區新峰里，跨越鄉鎮界線。校地僅10,454平方公尺，設有6個普通班及1個資源班，並自民國90學年度（2001年）起增設混齡幼兒園班。
