About · 學校簡介

The only school of its kind in Taiwan.

Danan Elementary — now rebranded the Darumak Ethnic Experimental Elementary School — holds a singular place among Taiwan's schools: it is the only one in the entire country serving the Taromak (達魯瑪克) people, a community of the East Rukai, whose Indigenous heritage is distinct from both the Tao of Orchid Island and the Puyuma more typically associated with Beinan Township. The school dates to the Japanese colonial era, with its founding placed somewhere around 1921–1926 (the school has celebrated a centennial, though the precise founding year is not firmly documented). Today, more than 85 percent of the surrounding population is Taromak East Rukai, and the school's student body is itself nearly 100 percent Indigenous — a proportion that led to its recognition as Taiwan's fifth Indigenous experimental elementary school.

大南國小——現已更名為達魯瑪克民族實驗小學——在全台灣的學校中占有獨特地位：它是全國唯一服務達魯瑪克族（East Rukai，東魯凱族的一支）的學校，其原住民文化背景既不同於蘭嶼的達悟族，也有別於卑南鄉更常見的卑南族。學校創立於日治時期，確切年份約落在1921至1926年間（校方曾舉辦創校百年慶，但精確創校年份尚無明確文獻記載）。如今，學校周邊人口逾85%為達魯瑪克東魯凱族，全校學生幾乎百分之百為原住民，這樣的比例也讓該校成為台灣第五所原住民族實驗小學。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌏 Taiwan's Only Taromak School

全台唯一服務達魯瑪克族的學校

Danan Elementary is the only school anywhere in Taiwan dedicated to serving the Taromak (East Rukai) people, making it a singular institution within the national education system.

🏫 Taiwan's Fifth Indigenous Experimental Elementary School

全國第五所原住民族實驗小學

With a student body that is nearly 100 percent Indigenous, the school earned recognition as Taiwan's fifth Indigenous experimental elementary school, now operating under the name Darumak Ethnic Experimental Elementary School.

🏔️ A Century-Old Institution

歷史逾百年

Founded during the Japanese colonial era roughly a century ago, the school has celebrated its centennial while continuing to serve the same Taromak community across generations.

🌾 A Community That Is 85% Taromak

周邊人口逾85%為達魯瑪克族

Over 85 percent of the population surrounding the school is Taromak East Rukai, making the school a direct and deeply embedded reflection of the community it serves.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Beinan's unusual Indigenous diversity.

🏔️

This is Taiwan's only school for the Taromak (East Rukai) community — a group distinct from the Tao (Yami) of Orchid Island and from the Puyuma more typically found across Beinan Township. Its presence highlights the unusual Indigenous diversity within a single township: Beinan is home to Puyuma communities, the archaeologically significant Beinan site, and this singular East Rukai enclave, all within the same administrative area.
