About · 學校簡介

A small campus in the hills near Longevity Bridge.

Shengxian Elementary was founded in April 1941 (Showa 16), during the Japanese colonial era, to serve the Shengxian hill hamlet in Dongshan District. It remains a small campus — about seven classes and roughly 108 students on a 28,636-square-meter site, with around 15 staff. The school was once the parent school of a branch campus at Donghe, which later became independent as Kabuasua (Jibeishua) Elementary. It maintains an active alumni association.

聖賢國小創校於昭和16年（民國30年，1941年）4月，日治時期即已設立，服務東山區聖賢山區聚落。學校規模小巧，全校約七個班、學生約108人，校地面積約28,636平方公尺，教職員約15人。學校過去曾為東河分校的母校，該分校後來獨立設校，成為今日的吉貝耍國小。校內設有活躍的校友會組織。

The school serves the Shengxian hill hamlet near the Toucheng Longevity Bridge (頭橋長壽橋), a scenic suspension bridge that is one of the most striking entry points into Dongshan District's famed coffee-growing highlands. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
