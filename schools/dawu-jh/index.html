About · 學校簡介

The junior high for six feeder schools.

Dawu Junior High serves as the central junior high school for Dawu Township, a small coastal community at Taitung's southern end along the Southern-Link Railway and Highway. It draws students from six elementary feeder schools across the township, and it has built a well-rounded record of student achievement spanning basketball, judo, soccer, dance, reading literacy, and programming competitions. The school is also active in disaster-prevention education and environmental sustainability certification, reflecting the practical realities of life along Taiwan's exposed southeastern coast.

大武國中是大武鄉的核心國中，這個小型海岸鄉鎮位於台東南端，緊鄰南迴鐵路與南迴公路。學校招收全鄉六所國小的畢業生，學生在籃球、柔道、足球、舞蹈、閱讀素養及程式設計競賽等領域皆有優異表現。學校也積極推動防災教育與環境永續認證，反映出台灣東南沿海生活的現實需求。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏀 Well-Rounded Student Achievement

多元競賽表現

Students have earned recognition across basketball, judo, soccer, dance, reading literacy, and programming — an unusually broad spread of achievement for a small township school.

🌪️ Disaster-Prevention Education

防災教育

Given its exposed coastal location, the school places real emphasis on disaster-prevention education, preparing students for the realities of life on Taiwan's southeastern coast.

🌱 Environmental Sustainability Certification

環境永續認證

Dawu Junior High is active in environmental sustainability certification programs, connecting classroom learning to the health of the coastal environment around it.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Dawu, on the Southern-Link coast.

Dawu Junior High serves Dawu Township, a small coastal community at Taitung's southern tip where the Southern-Link Railway and Highway connect the county to Pingtung, and where Paiwan Indigenous heritage runs deep.
