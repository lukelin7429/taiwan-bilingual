Schools · Nan'ao Township 南澳鄉

Jinyue Elementary School

金岳國小

An Indigenous-focus elementary school in the Atayal Jinyue community, offering mother-tongue instruction and home to an award-winning Atayal singing troupe.

座落於泰雅族金岳部落的原住民重點學校，開設母語課程，並擁有屢獲佳績的泰雅族歌謠團。

About · 學校簡介

A community school in a living Atayal village.

Jinyue Elementary is located in the Atayal Jinyue community, about a ten-minute drive from Nan'ao Train Station across the Jinyue Bridge. Designated an Indigenous-focus school, it offers foreign-language and mother-tongue instruction as part of its regular curriculum. The school's Atayal singing troupe has won top honors in Yilan County's Indigenous-language competition, reflecting strong community investment in passing on language and song.

金岳國小位於泰雅族金岳部落，從南澳火車站開車跨越金岳大橋約十分鐘車程。學校經指定為原住民重點學校，於正規課程中開設外語與族語教學。學校的泰雅族歌謠團曾在宜蘭縣原住民族語比賽中榮獲佳績，展現部落對語言與歌謠傳承的深厚投入。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎤 An Award-Winning Singing Troupe

泰雅歌謠團獲縣賽佳績

The school's Atayal singing troupe won top honors in Yilan County's Indigenous-language competition, a mark of the community's active language and music preservation.

🗣️ Mother-Tongue Instruction

族語與外語課程

As a designated Indigenous-focus school, Jinyue integrates mother-tongue and foreign-language instruction directly into its regular curriculum.

🌉 A Bridge Into the Village

跨越金岳大橋的部落

Reached by crossing the Jinyue Bridge about ten minutes from Nan'ao Train Station, the school sits within a close-knit Atayal community with its own distinct village life.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A living Atayal community.

🍽️

The Jinyue community is also home to the well-known "Sayun's Kitchen" Atayal restaurant, reflecting how the school anchors a living Atayal cultural community within Nan'ao's mountains.
