About · 學校簡介

A century in the heart of old downtown.

Guangfu Elementary opened in 1919 as “Taichung No. 2 Ordinary Higher Elementary School” (台中第二尋常高等小學校), taking its current name, Guangfu (光復, meaning “Retrocession”), after Taiwan's return to Republic of China rule in 1945. Now over a century old, the school sits on Sanmin Road in the heart of Taichung's original Japanese-era planned downtown, near the old Taichung Prefectural Government Hall complex and the train station. Its early Little League baseball program produced several professional players, and its school district today covers seven historic Central District neighborhoods.

光復國小於1919年（大正8年）創校，初名「台中第二尋常高等小學校」，1945年台灣光復後更名為光復國小。學校已逾百年歷史，校址位於三民路，正是台中日治時期規劃市中心的核心地帶，鄰近舊台中州廳與台中車站。學校早期的少棒隊培育出多位職業選手，學區至今涵蓋中區七個歷史悠久的里。
