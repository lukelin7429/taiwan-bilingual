About · 學校簡介

One of Taichung's largest vocational campuses.

Dajia Industrial Vocational Senior High School traces its roots to 1937, when it opened during the Japanese colonial era as the Dajia Agricultural Citizens' School. It has since grown into one of Taichung's two largest high-school campuses, enrolling roughly 1,300 day-division students plus another 500 in evening classes. Today the school offers technical tracks in machinery and manufacturing, electrical and electronics, and civil construction and architecture, alongside a special-education culinary program.

大甲工業高級中等學校前身可追溯至1937年（日治時期）創立的大甲農業補習學校，如今已發展為臺中市兩大高中職校園之一，日間部約1,300名學生，夜間部另有約500人。學校目前設有機械製造、電機電子、土木建築等技術群科，並開設特教班烘焙餐飲課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏭 One of Taichung's Largest Campuses

臺中規模最大校園之一

With around 1,300 day-division students and another 500 studying in the evening, Dajia Industrial ranks among the two biggest high-school campuses in Taichung City.

⚙️ Four Technical Tracks

四大技術群科

Students choose among machinery and manufacturing, electrical-electronics, and civil construction and architecture programs, plus a special-education culinary and food-service track.

🌾 Agricultural Roots

起源於農業補習學校

Opening in 1937 as an agricultural citizens' school, the campus reflects Dajia's long identity as a farming community even as it now trains students in industrial trades.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Dajia's farming heritage.

🙏

Dajia town is anchored by the historic Jenn Lann Temple (鎮瀾宮) and its Mazu pilgrimage, one of Taiwan's largest annual religious processions, alongside a centuries-old rush-weaving craft tradition. The school's own founding as an agricultural institute reflects the farming heritage that still runs through the district. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
