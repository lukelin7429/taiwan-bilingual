About · 學校簡介

One of the district's biggest neighborhood schools.

Tong-De Elementary is among the largest elementary schools in Taoyuan District — as of the 2022 school year it had 55 classes, 1,565 students, and 97 teachers. Located on Tongde 6th Street in the eastern residential district that grew up alongside neighboring Tong-An Elementary, the school offers a creativity gifted class (創造力資優班) and a bilingual curriculum, and has built its own branded trilingual-education initiative, "同德新三語" (New Trilingual). Students have recently won robotics-competition awards, and staff have received Taiwan's national Teaching Excellence Award (師鐸獎).

同德國小是桃園區規模最大的國小之一，111學年度共有55班、1,565名學生、97位教師。校址位於同德六街，與鄰近的同安國小同屬因戰後城市擴張而興起的東側社區學校群。校內設有創造力資優班與雙語課程，並發展出自有品牌「同德新三語」教育計畫。學生近年在機器人競賽中屢獲佳績，教師亦曾獲頒師鐸獎。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗣️ The "New Trilingual" Initiative

同德新三語教育

A self-branded "同德新三語" (New Trilingual) education program sits alongside the school's bilingual curriculum, reflecting a sustained institutional focus on language learning.

💡 A Creativity Gifted Class

創造力資優班

Tong-De runs a dedicated "creativity gifted class" (創造力資優班) for students who show strong creative-thinking ability, one of several specialized programs on campus.

🤖 Robotics-Competition Winners

機器人競賽獲獎

Students recently won awards at robotics competitions in 2026, and school staff have separately been honored with Taiwan's national 師鐸獎 (Teaching Excellence Award).

🏫 One of Taoyuan's Largest Schools

桃園區大型學校

With 55 classes and over 1,500 students as of 2022, Tong-De is one of the biggest elementary schools serving Taoyuan District's dense eastern neighborhoods.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Taoyuan's postwar school cluster.

🏘️

Tong-De Elementary sits in the same eastern residential district as neighboring 同安國小, part of the postwar expansion of neighborhood schools that radiated outward from Taoyuan District's historic city-center commercial core as the population grew. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
