About · 學校簡介

A small school keeping Hakka craft alive.

Wugu Elementary serves around 208 students and 22 teachers in Wugu Village, at the center of Gongguan Township's rice-farming heartland. As a designated Hakka-language feature school, it runs Hakka certification camps and hands-on weaving and pottery courses that keep local Hakka craft traditions alive for a new generation. Beyond the classroom, students take part in a lively mix of clubs, including a cup-stacking team, basketball, a dragon dance team, and Tangshou-do martial arts.

五穀國小位於五谷村，座落於公館鄉稻米之鄉的核心地帶，全校約208名學生、22位教師。學校是客語特色學校，開設客語認證營，以及編織、陶藝等實作課程，將在地客家工藝傳承給下一代。除了課堂學習，學生也參與多元社團，包括疊杯隊、籃球隊、龍舞隊，以及唐手道武術。

🧶 Hakka Weaving and Pottery
