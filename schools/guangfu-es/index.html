About · 學校簡介

A rare "small school" with a long lineage.

Guangfu Elementary opened on September 1, 1899, as "Yilan Small School" (宜蘭小學校) — one of only seven "small schools" (小學校) established islandwide in the early Japanese colonial period specifically for the children of Japanese settlers, and the only one in what was then Yilan Prefecture. It was renamed Yilan County Guangfu National School in October 1950 and took its current name in September 1968. Over the decades, several of its satellite branch schools grew into fully independent schools of their own, including institutions now found in Suao, Luodong, Sanxing, and Toucheng. By the 2020–21 school year, the school had graduated 108 cohorts totaling more than 20,000 students across both the Japanese colonial and postwar eras.

光復國小創立於1899年9月1日，日治時期名為「宜蘭小學校」，是當時全台僅設立的七所「小學校」之一（專供日籍學童就讀），也是宜蘭廳唯一的一所。1950年10月更名為「宜蘭縣光復國民學校」，1968年9月定名為現名。歷年來多個分校陸續獨立設校，包括今日蘇澳、羅東、三星、頭城等地的學校。截至2020至2021學年度，全校已培育108屆畢業生，橫跨日治與戰後兩個時代，總數超過兩萬人。
