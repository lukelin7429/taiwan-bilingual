About · 學校簡介

A countryside school built for environmental learning.

Houzhuang Elementary is located in Qilao Village, Jiuru Township, and is often described as a rural-type countryside school well suited to combining humanities education with environmental education, given its natural, unspoiled setting. The school currently has 24 staff and teachers serving 157 students across 6 classes, with a compact campus of roughly 207 square meters according to one directory listing. The school maintains a dedicated history page on its official website, though its exact founding date could not be confirmed from available sources.

後庄國小位於九如鄉耆老村，常被形容為適合結合人文教育與環境教育的鄉村型學校，得益於其自然純樸的環境。學校目前共有24位教職員，服務157名學生，分為6個班級，根據一份名錄資料，校地面積約207平方公尺。學校官網設有專屬沿革頁面，惟確切創校年份目前未能從公開資料中查證。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A rural school in the farming belt.

🌾

Houzhuang Elementary sits within Jiuru Township's plains agricultural belt, an area shaped by the region's long history of betel-nut and rice-farming. Its unspoiled countryside setting is part of what makes the school well suited to environmental and humanities-based education, connecting classroom learning directly to the surrounding rural landscape.
