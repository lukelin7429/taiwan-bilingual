About · 學校簡介

A steady presence in Xinle Village.

Xinle Elementary began in 1953 as a branch class of Jianshi National School serving Xinle Village, was upgraded to a branch school in 1955, and became fully independent in 1958, taking its present name in 1968. An attached kindergarten class was added in 1986. In more recent years, the school undertook a full demolition and rebuild of its complex and gymnasium building. Like its neighboring schools across Jianshi Township, Xinle Elementary serves the area's Atayal Indigenous community.

新樂國小前身為1953年設立的「尖石國小新樂分班」，1955年升格為分校，1958年正式獨立設校，1968年更為現名，1986年附設幼兒園。近年來，學校也進行了活動中心與體育館建築的全面拆除重建工程。與尖石鄉其他鄰近學校一樣，新樂國小服務當地的泰雅族原住民社區。
