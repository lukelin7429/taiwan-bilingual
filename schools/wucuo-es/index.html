About · 學校簡介

A mini school beside the airport.

Wucuo Elementary was founded in 2000 on Aofeng Hill, just beside Taichung International Airport. The school describes itself as a "mini school," reflecting its small size and close-knit community, and has placed particular emphasis on bilingual and dual-language education for its students.

吳厝國小創立於民國89年（2000年），校址位於鰲峰山上，緊鄰台中國際機場。學校自稱為「迷你學校」，反映其小巧精緻的規模與緊密的校園社群，並致力推動雙語與雙軌教育。
