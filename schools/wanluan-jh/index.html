About · 學校簡介

At the heart of Wanluan Township.

Wanluan Junior High School is located in Wanhe Village, in the administrative and commercial heart of Wanluan Township. The school fields a notable badminton team with its own dedicated following, and shares its township with Wanluan Elementary School in the community center.

萬巒國中位於萬和村，處於萬巒鄉行政與商業中心地帶。學校擁有頗具知名度的羽球隊，並與萬巒國小同樣座落於鄉治中心區域。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Home of Wanluan pig's-knuckle.

🐷

Wanluan Township is a Hakka township world-famous for its Wanluan pig's-knuckle (萬巒豬腳) cuisine street. The dish's fame grew nationwide after President Chiang Ching-kuo was photographed eating it at a Wanluan stall in the 1970s–80s, and the pig's-knuckle street's paving today reflects Hakka cultural motifs such as blue shirts, betel nuts, and pig's knuckles. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
