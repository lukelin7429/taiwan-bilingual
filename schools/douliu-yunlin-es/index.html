Yunlin Elementary School

雲林國民小學

A modern school in the Yunlin county seat, built to relieve overcrowding and home to Taiwan's first Yunlin International English Village.

座落於雲林縣治斗六市的現代化學校，因應學區人口成長而設立，設有全台首創的雲林國際英語村。

About · 學校簡介

Built to meet a growing county seat's needs.

Yunlin Elementary was established in 1994 on designated "Wenqi" reserved land, created specifically to relieve overcrowding at other schools in the fast-growing Douliu city center. In the years since, the school has built a distinctive identity around English-language learning, operating Taiwan's first Yunlin International English Village — an immersive English-learning facility — and earning a Ministry of Education Teaching Excellence Team Silver Award for its work.

雲林國小創立於1994年（民國83年），校地取自縣府劃定的「文旗」保留用地，設校目的在於紓解斗六市區學校日益擁擠的就學壓力。多年來，學校致力發展英語教育特色，設有全台首創的「雲林國際英語村」沉浸式英語學習場域，並榮獲教育部教學卓越團隊銀質獎的肯定。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌍 Taiwan's First International English Village

全台首創國際英語村

Yunlin Elementary operates Taiwan's first Yunlin International English Village, an immersive facility that lets students practice English in simulated real-world settings.

🏅 A Ministry-Recognized Teaching Team

教學卓越團隊銀質獎

The school's teaching staff won a Ministry of Education Teaching Excellence Team Silver Award, a mark of recognition for its instructional innovation.

🏗️ Built to Serve a Growing City

因應人口成長設校

Founded in 1994 on designated reserve land, the school was purpose-built to relieve overcrowding as Douliu's county-seat population grew.
