About · 學校簡介

A university built to serve overseas-Chinese students.

Overseas Chinese University was founded in 1964 (ROC 53), historically oriented toward serving students of overseas-Chinese background. Today the university offers programs across management, computer science, engineering, design, law, and health sciences, enrolling around 5,300 students.

僑光科技大學創立於民國53年（1964年），歷史上以服務僑生為重要辦學方向。現今學校開設管理、資訊、工程、設計、法律及健康科學等學程，在學學生約5,300人。

🌏 A History of Serving Overseas-Chinese Students

僑生教育傳統

Since its 1964 founding, Overseas Chinese University has been historically oriented toward serving students of overseas-Chinese background, a distinctive institutional identity in Taiwan's higher-education landscape.

🎓 A Focused, Mid-Sized Student Body

中型精緻學生規模
