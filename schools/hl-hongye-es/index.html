Hongye Elementary School

紅葉國民小學

A 90-year-old Truku village school in Hualien, whose traditional dance troupe has carried its name to national honors.

About · 學校簡介

A small mountain school, not to be confused with its Taitung namesake.

Hongye Elementary traces its roots to January 1934, when it opened under Japanese rule as an "educational institute for southern-savage children" (悅付南蕃童教育所) — the language of the era's colonial administration. Renamed several times over the decades, including a 1958 change to avoid duplicating a Taipei County township name, the school celebrated its 90th anniversary in the 2020s. Today it is a small campus of about six classes and roughly three dozen students, serving the Truku Indigenous community of Hongye Village. Note: Taiwan has three separate elementary schools named "Hongye" (紅葉國小) — in Taitung, Hualien, and Nantou. This school, in Wanrong Township, Hualien, is distinct from the Taitung Hongye Elementary famous for its 1968 Little League baseball upset over a visiting Japanese team.

紅葉國小的歷史可追溯至日治時期民國23年（1934年）1月25日設立的「悅付南蕃童教育所」。歷經數次更名，包括民國47年（1958年）為避免與台北縣某鄉鎮同名而改名，學校已慶祝建校90週年。目前全校約6班、37名學生，服務紅葉村的太魯閣族部落。特別說明：全台灣共有三所「紅葉國小」——分別位於台東、花蓮（本校）與南投。本校與因1968年紅葉少棒隊擊敗來訪日本球隊而聞名全台的台東紅葉國小並非同一所學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💃 A Nationally Recognized Dance Troupe

傳統舞蹈隊揚名全國

Hongye's traditional Indigenous dance troupe has won honors at the national level, giving this small mountain school an outsized profile in Truku cultural performance.

🏛️ Ninety Years of History

建校九十週年

Founded in 1934 under Japanese rule and renamed multiple times since, the school has marked its 90th anniversary — a long institutional memory for such a small campus.

🗣️ Keeping Truku Alive

太魯閣族語傳承

The school promotes Truku-language instruction alongside its cultural programs, reinforcing community ties in this small Truku village.

Local Culture · 在地文化

One name, three schools, one distinct village.

🍁

Hongye Village sits within mountainous Wanrong Township 萬榮鄉, a Truku Indigenous community in Hualien County's Central Mountain Range foothills. While the name "紅葉" (Red Leaf) is most famous internationally through a different Hongye Elementary in Taitung County — home to the celebrated 1968 Little League baseball team — this Hualien school is an entirely separate institution with its own century-spanning Truku community history.
