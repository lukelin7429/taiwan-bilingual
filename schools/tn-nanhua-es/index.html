About · 學校簡介

A century-old school tracing the settlement of the Nanhua basin.

Nanhua Elementary School was founded on April 1, 1909, as “Tapani Public School Nanzhuang Branch.” It became independent as “Nanzhuang Public School” in 1914, was renamed “Inner Xinhua Public School” the same year, then “Nanhua Public School” in 1921 — at which point it also established the “Beiliao Branch School” that later became today's Beiliao Elementary School. The school became “Nanhua National School” in 1941, relocated to its present site in 1946 after World War II, and was renamed “Nanhua Elementary School” in 1968. Classified as a special remote (極偏) school with over a century of history, it now enrolls about 78 students across 6 classes on a campus covering roughly 14,751 square meters, and established an attached self-governing kindergarten in 1973.

南化國小創校於明治42年（1909年）4月1日，初設「噍吧哖公學校南莊分校」。民國3年（1914年）獨立設校，改稱「南莊公學校」，同年又改名「內新化公學校」，1921年再改名「南化公學校」，並於此時設立「北寮分教場」，即今日北寮國小的前身。1941年改稱「南化國民學校」，二戰後於1946年遷至現址，1968年定名「南化國民小學」。學校被列為「極偏」偏遠學校，校史逾百年，現有學生約78人、6班，校地約14,751平方公尺，1973年設立附設自治幼兒園。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 The Founding School of the Basin

南化盆地的源起學校

As the founding school from which Beiliao Elementary later branched off in 1921, Nanhua Elementary's century-plus history traces the settlement of the Nanhua basin around what is now the Nanhua Reservoir watershed.

🏔️ A Special Remote-Designated School

列為極偏遠學校

Classified as a special remote (極偏) school, Nanhua Elementary serves one of Tainan's most sparsely populated mountain districts with just 78 students across 6 classes.

🏗️ Relocated After World War II

戰後遷校

The school moved to its present site in 1946, after the war, before taking its current name in 1968 under nine-year compulsory education.

👶 A Kindergarten Since 1973

1973年設幼兒園

The school established an attached self-governing kindergarten in 1973, extending its role as the educational anchor of the Nanhua basin.
