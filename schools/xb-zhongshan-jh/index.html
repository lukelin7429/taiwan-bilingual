Zhongshan Junior High School

新北市立中山國民中學

A Banqiao junior high whose first campus is now the New Taipei City Government building.

About · 學校簡介

A school that moved twice to find its home.

Zhongshan Junior High School was established in 1981, named in honor of Dr. Sun Yat-sen (孫中山). Its original campus, at No. 233, Sec. 1, Zhongshan Rd., is today the site of the New Taipei City Government building. The school relocated in 1997 to a former Zhongxiao Elementary School site, then moved again in 1999 to its permanent campus — built on the site of a former CPC (state oil company) fuel depot.

中山國中創立於民國70年（1981年），為紀念孫中山先生而命名。原校址位於中山路一段233號，現址已成為新北市政府大樓。學校於民國86年（1997年）遷至原忠孝國小校地，民國88年（1999年）再遷至現今的永久校區——原為中油油庫用地。

🇨🇳 Named for Dr. Sun Yat-sen

以孫中山命名

The school takes its name, 中山, in honor of Dr. Sun Yat-sen, founding father of the Republic of China.

🚚 A School on the Move

兩度遷校

The school relocated twice within two decades — first to a former elementary school site in 1997, then to its permanent campus in 1999 — before settling into its current home.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Tracing Banqiao's industrial-to-administrative shift.

The school's relocations trace Banqiao's transformation from an industrial and oil-depot area into today's New Taipei City government and administrative core. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
