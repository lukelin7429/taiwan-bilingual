About · 學校簡介

A reservoir-watershed school built around ecology.

Ruifeng Elementary School's own site references a school-history section but does not display a specific founding year; the earliest concrete date on record is 1998, when the school held its first outdoor-education activity. In 2000, the school was selected as a Tainan small-class-size (小班教育) experimental school, building its curriculum around the surrounding community ecology. Located in the catchment area upstream of Nanhua Reservoir, the school runs environmental-education, bilingual, and family-education programs, and provides an attached kindergarten and online learning platforms despite its remote location.

瑞峰國小官網雖設有「學校沿革」專區，但未明確標示創校年份；現存最早的具體紀錄為1998年學校首次辦理戶外教育活動。民國89年（2000年）獲選為台南市小班教育實驗學校，課程設計以周邊社區生態環境為核心。學校位於南化水庫上游集水區，辦理環境教育、雙語及家庭教育等課程，並設有附設幼兒園及線上學習平台，即使地處偏遠仍提供完整的教育資源。

🌿 A Small-Class Ecology Curriculum

小班教育生態課程

Selected in 2000 as a Tainan small-class-size experimental school, Ruifeng built its curriculum around the surrounding community ecology — a model tailored to its remote mountain setting.

💧 Upstream of Nanhua Reservoir

南化水庫上游集水區

Sitting directly in the watershed that feeds Nanhua Reservoir, the school's curriculum is explicitly built around the surrounding mountain ecology and water-catchment environment.
