About · 學校簡介

A well-equipped school in a historic corner of Fengshan.

Wenhua Elementary School enrolls around 915 students across 32 classes, taught by 52 teachers. Its facilities include dedicated audiovisual, music, computer, and nature-study classrooms. The school serves the historic 五甲 (Wujia) area of Fengshan — a place name dating back to Qing-dynasty land-measurement units — and draws students from the Wenfu, Wenhua, and Wenheng neighborhoods.

文華國小全校約915名學生、32班、52位教師，設有視聽教室、音樂教室、電腦教室與自然教室等專科教室。學校服務鳳山歷史悠久的五甲地區——這個地名源自清代的土地丈量單位——並招收文福里、文華里、文衡里等鄰近里別的學生。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Serving the historic Wujia area.

🏘️

Wenhua Elementary serves the 五甲 (Wujia) area of Fengshan, a place name with roots in Qing-dynasty land-measurement units — a small linguistic trace of how the district's farmland was once parceled and recorded. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
