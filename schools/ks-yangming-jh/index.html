About · 學校簡介

Kaohsiung's largest junior high, by the freeway.

Yangming Junior High School broke ground on July 1, 1988, with then-Kaohsiung mayor Su Nan-cheng presiding over the ceremony. The school sits near the boundary between Sanmin and Fengshan districts, alongside National Freeway No. 1, and has historically been the largest school by enrollment anywhere in the Kaohsiung area — a scale that reflects the rapid growth of the surrounding neighborhood in the years after its founding.

陽明國中於民國77年（1988年）7月1日動土興建，由時任高雄市長蘇南成主持動土典禮。學校座落於三民區與鳳山區交界處，緊鄰國道一號，歷史上曾是高雄地區學生人數最多的學校之一，反映出創校後周邊社區快速發展的軌跡。
