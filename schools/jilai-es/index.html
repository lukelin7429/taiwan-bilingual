Jilai Elementary School

A rural elementary school that served Shanlin District for 77 years before formally closing in August 2024, amid post-disaster population change.

About · 學校簡介

A historical record of a school that has closed.

Jilai Elementary formally closed in August 2024 after 77 years of operation, and this page is presented as a historical record rather than a profile of an active school. The closure followed a severe decline in enrollment tied to the community's resettlement after the 2009 Typhoon Morakot disaster, as residents moved to the nearby Da'ai village, drawing students toward what became the Banahua Tribal School. Jilai Elementary's history is part of the same story of post-disaster community change that reshaped this corner of Shanlin District.

集來國小已於2024年8月正式停辦，走過77年歷史，本頁作為該校的歷史紀錄呈現，而非現行招生學校的介紹。停辦原因與2009年莫拉克風災後的社區遷移密切相關——居民陸續遷往鄰近的大愛村，學童也隨之轉往後來成立的巴楠花部落中小學就讀。集來國小的歷史，是杉林區這片土地上災後社區變遷故事的一部分。
