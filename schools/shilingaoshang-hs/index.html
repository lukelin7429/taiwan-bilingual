Shilin Senior High School of Commerce

臺北市立士林高級商業職業學校

One of Taipei's oldest and most prominent public commercial high schools, tracing its lineage to three 1945-era junior commercial schools consolidated in 1951.

臺北市歷史最悠久、最具代表性的公立商業高中之一，前身可追溯至1945年成立的三所初級商業職業學校，於1951年合併創校。

About · 學校簡介

A flagship of Taipei's commercial education.

Shilin Senior High School of Commerce was established in 1951 as "Taipei Municipal Junior Commercial Vocational School," formed by merging three former junior commercial schools — Taiping, Laosong, and Songshan — along with the Ximen supplementary school. The school gained a senior division in 1954 and moved to its current Shilin campus in 1970. Over the decades it has become one of Taipei's most prominent public commercial/vocational high schools, with alumni-turned-principals going on to lead other flagship municipal high schools across the city.

士林高商創立於民國40年（1951年），初名「臺北市立初級商業職業學校」，由太平、老松、松山三所初級商業學校及西門補習學校合併而成。民國43年（1954年）增設高級部，民國59年（1970年）遷至現址士林。多年來已成為臺北市最具代表性的公立商業職業學校之一，多位傑出校友出任其他臺北市指標高中的校長。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ A Merger of Three Historic Schools

三校合併創校

The school traces its roots to three separate junior commercial schools founded in 1945 — Taiping, Laosong, and Songshan — consolidated into a single institution in 1951.

🎓 A Training Ground for Principals

名校校長搖籃

Multiple alumni have gone on to lead other flagship Taipei public high schools, including Chenggong, Heping, Zhongzheng, Huajiang, and Jingmei Girls', reflecting the school's standing within the municipal system.

🎪 Shishang April Days

士商四月天商業季

The school hosts an annual "Shishang April Days — Business Season" carnival and fair that opens its commercial-education programs to the wider public.

🏙️ Rooted in Old Shilin

扎根士林老街

Sited on Shishang Road just downhill from the historic Shilin Old Street and temple market, the school has long trained generations of local commercial and retail talent.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Downhill from Shilin's night-market economy.

🏮

Shilin Senior High School of Commerce sits in the heart of old Shilin, just downhill from the historic Shilin Old Street and temple market district — the neighborhood whose famous night-market economy the school's commercial curriculum has long fed with trained talent.
