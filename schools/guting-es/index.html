Guting Elementary School

古亭國民小學

A resilient Zhuangwei elementary school that survived a devastating typhoon, pioneered school lunches in Yilan County, and later won a national teaching award.

歷經颱風重創仍屹立不搖的壯圍老校，是全宜蘭縣最早推行營養午餐的學校，並曾榮獲教育部教學卓越金質獎。

About · 學校簡介

Formed from three villages, tested by a typhoon.

Guting Elementary School was formed in 1937, during the Japanese colonial era, through the merger of three earlier village schools — Tuwei, Guoling, and a Dafu branch classroom — under the original name "Guting Main Public School." The school survived catastrophic damage from Typhoon Oper in 1972 and rebuilt to become a pioneer for the whole county: in 1982, it was the first school in Yilan County to implement a school lunch program. Its efforts were recognized nationally in 2012 with the Ministry of Education's Teaching Excellence Gold Award, and the school has run an attached kindergarten since 1997.

古亭國小於1937年（昭和12年，日治時期）由「土圍公學校」、「過嶺公學校」與「大福分教場」三所學校合併而成，最初名為「古亭本公學校」。學校歷經1972年歐珀颱風的重創，浴火重生後成為全縣先驅：民國71年（1982年），古亭國小是全宜蘭縣第一所實施營養午餐的學校。這份努力於民國101年（2012年）獲教育部教學卓越金質獎肯定，學校並自1997年起附設幼兒園。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🍱 First School Lunch in Yilan County

全縣首創營養午餐

In 1982, Guting became the first school in all of Yilan County to implement a school lunch program, a pioneering step for student nutrition countywide.

🏅 A National Teaching Award

教學卓越金質獎

The school won the Ministry of Education's Teaching Excellence Gold Award in 2012, a mark of national recognition for its educational programs.

🌪️ Rebuilt After Disaster

走過颱風重創

Guting survived catastrophic damage from Typhoon Oper in 1972, rebuilding to become one of the township's most resilient institutions.

🏘️ Born From Three Villages

三校合併而生

The school's 1937 founding merged three earlier village schools into one, a lineage that still shapes its identity as a community anchor today.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A community school through and through.
