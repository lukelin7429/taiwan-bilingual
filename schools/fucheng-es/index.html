About · 學校簡介

An elementary school for a growing neighborhood.

Fucheng Elementary School was established on October 16, 1966, according to the school's own records, to meet the needs of the rapidly growing population in Fengshan's 五甲 (Wujia) area. It is worth noting that Fucheng Elementary is an entirely separate institution from the unrelated 高雄市立福誠高級中學 (Fucheng Senior High School), which was founded later, in 1993, in the same general area — the shared name is a frequent source of confusion.

根據學校本身的沿革記載，福誠國小創立於民國55年（1966年）10月16日，因應鳳山五甲地區人口快速成長而設立。值得留意的是，福誠國小與同一區域內、於1993年創校的高雄市立福誠高級中學為完全不同的兩所學校——兩校名稱相近，常造成混淆。
