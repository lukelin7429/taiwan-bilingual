About · 學校簡介

A coastal school with a nationally unique identity.

Fushan Elementary began in 1950 as a branch of Fugang Elementary School and became fully independent in 1972. In 2017 it took on a distinction unmatched anywhere else in Taiwan, becoming the country's only public "International Education"-themed experimental elementary school. The campus sits along Beinan Township's Pacific coastline, between the distinctive Xiaoyeliu rock formations and the popular Shanyuan Beach, an area otherwise known chiefly as a scenic tourist destination. The school district itself covers several small Indigenous fishing and farming hamlets, and the "International Education" identity draws partly from the school's role serving these small coastal communities.

富山國小於1950年以富岡國小分校形式創立，1972年正式獨立設校。2017年，學校取得全台獨一無二的定位——成為全國唯一以「國際教育」為主題的公立實驗小學。校園位於卑南鄉太平洋海岸線，介於知名的小野柳岩石地景與熱門的杉原海灘之間，該區域向來以觀光景點聞名。學校學區涵蓋數個小型原住民漁業與農業聚落，其「國際教育」的定位有部分即源自服務這些沿海小型社區的角色。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌐 Taiwan's Only "International Education" Public Elementary School

全台唯一國際教育主題公立實驗小學

Since 2017, Fushan has held a distinction unmatched anywhere else in Taiwan as the country's only public elementary school built around an "International Education" experimental theme.

🏖️ Between Two Coastal Landmarks

介於小野柳與杉原海灘之間

The campus sits between the Xiaoyeliu rock formations and Shanyuan Beach, two of the most visited scenic spots along Taitung's coastline, placing the school in a genuinely striking natural setting.

🎣 Serving Small Coastal Indigenous Hamlets

學區涵蓋沿海原住民聚落

The school district covers several small Indigenous fishing and farming hamlets along the coast, connecting its international-education mission directly to the needs of these close-knit communities.
