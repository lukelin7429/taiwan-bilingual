About · 學校簡介

A Hakka-language school with a green campus.

Shanghu Elementary is a designated Hakka-language school in Shanghu Village, Yangmei District, with an attached kindergarten. The school has driven a "Green Pine, Green Campus" (黑松綠色校園) ecological education initiative and, in 2024, performed strongly in the Hakka Affairs Council's National Hakka Arts and Culture Competition for schools, earning recognition in the spoken-arts and folk-song categories. It has also received recognition for its agricultural education programs and teaching excellence.

上湖國小是楊梅區的客語特色學校，設有附設幼兒園。學校推動「黑松綠色校園」生態環境教育計畫，2024年參加客家委員會全國中小學客家藝文競賽，於口說藝術與歌謠演唱項目表現優異，也曾獲農業教育推動與教學卓越相關肯定。
