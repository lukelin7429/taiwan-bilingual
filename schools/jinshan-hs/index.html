About · 學校簡介

The North Coast's first complete school.

Jinshan High School was founded in 1946, becoming the North Coast's first complete school combining junior and senior high divisions. A senior-high division was added in 1996, and the school became a comprehensive high school in 2001, offering tracks in academics, e-commerce, business management, food and beverage, and tourism. Since 2000, the school has run a distinctive Indigenous Arts & Skills Class, teaching Indigenous language, music and dance, and history — a program that reflects the cultural diversity of Taiwan's North Coast. As the sole complete high school serving the region, Jinshan High School anchors secondary education for a coastal township historically built on hot-spring tourism, fishing, and rice farming.

金山高中創立於民國35年（1946年），是北海岸第一所結合國中與高中部的完全中學。民國85年（1996年）增設高中部，民國90年（2001年）轉型為綜合高中，設有學術、電子商務、企業管理、餐旅與觀光等學程。自2000年起，學校開設別具特色的原住民藝能班，教授原住民族語言、音樂舞蹈與歷史文化，反映北海岸地區的多元文化面貌。作為服務全區唯一的完全中學，金山高中承擔了這個以溫泉觀光、漁業與稻作聞名的海濱鄉鎮之中等教育重任。
