Xiufeng High School

秀峰高中

A complete junior-and-senior high school by Xizhi Train Station, born from a 1955 partnership among five of Taipei's leading schools.

About · 學校簡介

Five schools, one branch, six decades of growth.

Xiufeng High School traces its roots to 1955, when it opened as a shared Xizhi branch campus jointly run by five elite Taipei-area schools — a reflection of Xizhi's historic role as a commuter gateway feeding students into Taipei's top schools. It became an independent junior high in 1964 and expanded into a complete school, adding a senior-high division, in 1997. Today, sitting right beside Xizhi Train Station, its senior-high division offers general academic, international-application, science-and-technology, and PE-specialty tracks.

秀峰高中源於民國44年（1955年）由台北五所知名學校共同設立的汐止分部，反映了汐止長期作為台北通勤門戶、輸送學生進入台北頂尖學校的歷史角色。民國53年（1964年）獨立設立國中部，並於民國86年（1997年）增設高中部，成為完全中學。如今校址緊鄰汐止火車站，高中部設有普通、國際應用、科技與體育等多元學程。
