About · 學校簡介

Anchoring education in a hard-hit mountain township.

Guoxing Junior High School serves as the parent school for the area, having once operated a branch campus in Beimei before that school became independent. The school received a special award for disaster-education program design in 2022, a distinction rooted in the region's hard-won experience recovering from the 1999 921 earthquake. In recent years, the county government has also conducted an on-site inspection of the school's aging buildings to identify needed repairs.

國姓國中為國姓鄉的核心學校，過去曾設有北梅分校，其後獨立設校。該校於2022年榮獲防災教育課程設計特別獎，此一殊榮與地區歷經1999年921大地震後艱辛復原的經驗密切相關。近年縣政府也曾對校舍老舊建築進行現場勘查，評估修繕需求。
