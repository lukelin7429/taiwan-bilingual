Chongqing Junior High School

新北市立重慶國民中學

A Banqiao junior high named for mainland China's wartime capital, known for its music-class program.

About · 學校簡介

A retrocession-era name, a music-education legacy.

Chongqing Junior High School was founded in 1974 under its first principal, Ma Zong-yun. The school offers a dedicated music-class program (音樂班) and an attached kindergarten, building a distinctive arts-education identity within Banqiao District. Note: the school's name is romanized “Chongqing,” not “Zhongqing.”

重慶國中創立於民國63年（1974年），首任校長為馬宗芸。學校設有音樂班及附設幼兒園，在板橋區發展出獨特的音樂教育特色。（註：校名羅馬拼音為「Chongqing」，而非「Zhongqing」。）

Local Culture · 在地文化

A name tied to retrocession-era identity.

🗺️

Named “重慶” (Chongqing) after mainland China's wartime capital, the school's name is part of a wave of 1970s-era school naming that invoked mainland place names as part of the ROC's retrocession-era national identity. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
