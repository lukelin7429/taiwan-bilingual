About · 學校簡介

A century witnessing Danan's growth.

Dacheng Elementary began in 1923 as a branch school of Bade Elementary serving the Danan (大湳) farming hamlet, becoming independent in 1928 and taking its current name in 1968. The school marked its 100th anniversary in 2023 with Taoyuan's mayor in attendance, who praised it for having “witnessed Bade's development” (見證八德發展) over the past century.

大成國小創立於1923年（大正12年），原為八德國小在大湳地區設立的分教場，1928年獨立設校，1968年定名為大成國民小學。學校於2023年舉辦建校百年校慶，桃園市長親臨現場，讚譽學校「見證八德發展」的百年歷程。
