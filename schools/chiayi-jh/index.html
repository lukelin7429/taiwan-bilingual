About · 學校簡介

Nearly a century of service to Chiayi's East District.

Chiayi Junior High School traces its origin to 1933, when the Chiayi Girls' Technical Institute was established after Chiayi Elementary School's girls' continuing-education program was discontinued. The school relocated to its present campus at Shanzaiding in 1939, and operated as a girls-only institution through several name changes before becoming coeducational around 1950. It became a municipal junior high in 1982, when Chiayi City was upgraded to provincial-city status.

嘉義國中源起於1933年（昭和8年）設立的嘉義女子技藝講習所，係因嘉義公學校女子繼續科停辦而設。民國28年（1939年），該校遷至現址山仔頂。歷經數次更名，原為女校，約於民國39年（1950年）改為男女合校。民國71年（1982年）嘉義市升格為省轄市後，改隸市立國中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Near-Century Origins

源起1933年女子技藝講習所

Tracing back to the 1933 Chiayi Girls' Technical Institute, the school has continuously served the community for nearly a hundred years across several eras and names.

🎀 From Girls' School to Coeducational

由女校轉為男女合校

Originally a girls'-only institution, the school became coeducational around 1950, reflecting the broader postwar transformation of Taiwan's education system.

🌱 A Symbolic School Emblem

校徽「青苗」與「五瓣」的意涵

The school's emblem, featuring "green seedlings" and "five petals," symbolizes nurturing local citizens toward balanced moral, intellectual, physical, social, and aesthetic development.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A near-century of civic identity.

🌱

Since relocating to Shanzaiding (山仔頂) in 1939, the school has been rooted in this East District neighborhood, its emblem's "green seedlings" and "five petals" reflecting an educational philosophy of nurturing well-rounded citizens for the community. See the Chiayi City page for more on the region.
