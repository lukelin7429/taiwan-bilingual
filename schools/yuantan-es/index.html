About · 學校簡介

A small school named for a natural landform.

Yuantan Elementary opened in 1937 as a branch of Qishan No. 1 Public School, becoming an independent school in 1940. It remains a small rural school serving the Yuantan community in northern Cishan District. The name "圓潭," meaning "round pool," likely refers to a natural pool or pond landform in the area.

圓潭國小於1937年（昭和12年，日治時期）設立為旗山第一公學校分教場，1940年獨立設校，至今仍是服務旗山北部圓潭社區的小型鄉村學校。校名「圓潭」意為「圓形水潭」，推測與當地一處天然水潭或池塘地形有關。
