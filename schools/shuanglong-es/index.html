About · 學校簡介

Double dragons guarding a pearl.

Shuanglong Elementary was founded in 2005 and celebrated its 20th anniversary in March 2025. At the anniversary event, Taoyuan Deputy Mayor Su Jun-bin explained the school's name, "雙龍" ("Double Dragon"), as symbolizing "雙龍護珠" (double dragons guarding a pearl) — with one "dragon" representing school leadership, the PTA, and civic supporters, and the other representing parents as the children's strongest backing. The school's facilities include a library, the "Longteng Hall" (龍騰館) gymnasium, and an upgraded running track, all funded through municipal support. It is one of Longtan's newest and smaller elementary schools, reflecting continued residential growth in the area near the science park.

雙龍國小創立於民國94年（2005年），並於2025年3月舉辦建校二十週年慶典。桃園市副市長蘇俊賓在慶典上詮釋校名「雙龍」的意涵為「雙龍護珠」——一龍象徵學校、家長會與地方仕紳的支持，另一龍則象徵家長作為孩子最堅實的後盾。校內設施包括圖書館、「龍騰館」體育館及升級後的跑道，皆由市府經費挹注。學校是龍潭最年輕、規模較小的國小之一，反映當地鄰近科學園區持續增加的居住人口。
