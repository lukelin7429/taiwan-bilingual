About · 學校簡介

A hillside school in the Shangjiao area.

Shangjiao Elementary is the sister institution to Shangjiao Junior High School, located in the same hillside Shangjiao area of Luzhu near the foothills of the Linkou Tableland. An exact founding date was not confirmed in the sources checked for this page, though the school has reached its 59th graduating class in recent years, suggesting a founding roughly in the mid-20th century.

山腳國小是山腳國中的姊妹學校，同樣座落於蘆竹丘陵地帶的山腳地區，鄰近林口台地山麓。本頁撰寫時查證所得資料未能確認確切創校年份，惟學校近年已產出第59屆畢業生，推估創校年代約在二十世紀中期。

Local Culture · 在地文化

The quieter, inland face of Luzhu.

⛰️

Like the adjoining junior high, Shangjiao Elementary serves the inland, hillier "foot of the mountain" part of Luzhu District — physically and culturally distinct from the flat, airport-adjacent Nankan core. This quieter, tea- and orchard-farming corner of the district offers a different face of Luzhu from the fast-growing suburban neighborhoods closer to Taoyuan International Airport. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
