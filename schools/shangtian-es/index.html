About · 學校簡介

A small school in the hills of Shangtian.

Shangtian Elementary sits in Shangtian Village on the southern side of Yangmei District, a rural farming community set among the district's hilly plateau terrain. The school's guiding vision is captured in its motto — "gratitude and cherishing blessings, healthy growth, excellence and innovation" — and it continues to serve the children of this small agricultural settlement south of Yangmei's town center.

上田國小位於楊梅區上田里，屬於楊梅南側農業聚落型小學，地處楊梅丘陵台地間。校訓／願景為「感恩惜福、健康成長、卓越創新」，持續服務這個位於楊梅市街南側的小型農村聚落。
