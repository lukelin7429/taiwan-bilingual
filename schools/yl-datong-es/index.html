About · 學校簡介

The school that shares the township's name.

Datong Elementary is thought to date to around 1920, during the Japanese colonial era, according to third-party sources, though its earliest records are not directly confirmed. As the elementary school carrying the township's own name, it sits along Provincial Highway 7 and today enrolls fewer than 100 students — a small, close-knit rural school known for its distinctive rainbow-colored running track. To reach the dispersed mountain settlements of Datong Township, the school operates two branch campuses, Songluo and Leshui, extending its reach further into remote terrain.

根據第三方資料，大同國小的歷史約可追溯至日治時期的民國9年（1920年）左右，惟早期紀錄未經直接證實。作為以鄉命名的國小，校址位於台7線省道旁，目前全校學生不足百人——是一所小而緊密的山區小校，以色彩繽紛的彩虹跑道聞名。為服務大同鄉分散各處的山區聚落，學校設有松羅、樂水兩所分校，將服務範圍延伸至更偏遠的山區。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌈 A Rainbow-Colored Running Track

彩虹跑道

The school's playground features a distinctive rainbow-colored running track, a small but memorable touch of color at this rural mountain campus.

🏘️ Two Branch Schools Reaching Further

松羅、樂水兩分校

Datong Elementary runs two branch campuses, Songluo and Leshui, allowing it to serve children from mountain settlements too remote for a single central campus.

🏫 A Small School With a Big Name

以鄉為名的小校

Enrolling fewer than 100 students, Datong Elementary carries the same name as the township itself, reflecting its role as the community's original, namesake school.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Serving Datong's dispersed mountain villages.

🏔️

As the township's namesake elementary school, Datong Elementary serves the Atayal community of Datong through a main campus plus two remote branch schools reaching deep into the mountains. See the Yilan county page for more on the region.
