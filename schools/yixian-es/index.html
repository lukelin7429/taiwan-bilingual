Yixian Elementary School

臺北市立逸仙國民小學

A public elementary school renamed in 1973 to honor Sun Yat-sen (孫逸仙) — the only school in Taiwan carrying the name "Yixian."

About · 學校簡介

The only "Yixian" school in Taiwan.

Preparation for Yixian Elementary began in February 1957 under the planned name "Datun Elementary School," but the school actually opened as "Zhongshan Elementary School." It was renamed 逸仙國小 in March 1973 by the Taipei City Department of Education, honoring Sun Yat-sen (孫逸仙) — making it the only school in Taiwan carrying that name. Its first principal was Wang Fang-lan, succeeded by Jin Xin-hua in the school's 55th academic year.

逸仙國小的籌設始於民國46年（1957年）2月，原規劃校名為「大屯國小」，實際開校時則稱「中山國小」。民國62年（1973年）3月，臺北市教育局將其更名為「逸仙國小」，以紀念孫逸仙——使其成為全台灣唯一以「逸仙」命名的學校。首任校長為王芳蘭，第二任校長金新華則於第55學年度接任。

🇹🇼 Taiwan's Only "Yixian" School

全台唯一逸仙國小

Renamed in 1973 to honor Sun Yat-sen (孫逸仙), Yixian Elementary is the only school anywhere in Taiwan carrying this particular name.

🔄 Three Names in Sixteen Years

十六年三易校名

Planned as "Datun Elementary," opened as "Zhongshan Elementary," and finally renamed "Yixian Elementary" in 1973 — the school's naming history reflects the shifting priorities of its era.

👩‍🏫 A Founding Principal's Legacy

首任校長王芳蘭

Wang Fang-lan served as the school's first principal from its 1957 founding preparations, laying the groundwork later carried on by successor Jin Xin-hua.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Beitou's residential fabric.

Yixian Elementary serves a residential neighborhood of Beitou District, in the same volcanic-hills corridor of northern Taipei that has shaped the district's identity since the Japanese colonial era. See the Taipei county page for more on the region.
