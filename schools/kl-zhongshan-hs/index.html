About · 學校簡介

One school, two divisions, six decades.

Keelung Municipal Zhongshan High School was founded in August 1962. On April 22, 1999, it was restructured into a "complete school" (完全中學) spanning both junior high and senior high divisions, and in 2000 it took its current official name, 基隆市立中山高級中學. Note: this school appears twice in some school directories — once as a junior high and once as a senior high — because it is a single institution offering both divisions under one administration.

市立中山高中創立於民國51年（1962年）8月。民國88年（1999年）4月22日，學校改制為橫跨國中與高中部的「完全中學」，並於民國89年（2000年）確立現今校名「基隆市立中山高級中學」。提醒：本校在部分學校名錄中會分別以「國中」與「高中」兩筆資料列出，實為同一所學校，僅為單一行政體制下同時開設國中部與高中部。

🏫 A Complete School Since 1999

1999年改制完全中學

On April 22, 1999, the school was restructured to combine junior high and senior high divisions under one roof, spanning grades 7 through 12.

🏛️ Over Six Decades of History

創校逾一甲子

Founded in 1962, the school has served Zhongshan District students for more than sixty years under evolving names and structures.

📛 One School, Not Two

同一所學校，非兩校

The school took its current official name in 2000, and though it may appear listed separately as a junior high and a senior high, it is a single institution.
