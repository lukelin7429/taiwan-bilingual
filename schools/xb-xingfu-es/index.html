About · 學校簡介

A school emblem born from fireflies and lotus.

Xingfu Elementary began in 1954 as a branch of Jiabao Elementary School, taking its present name in 1973. The school is located in Xiafu village, one of Linkou's old agrarian hamlets, whose rural character inspired an early teacher to design the school's emblem — depicting fireflies flickering near a lotus pond, a poetic image that still represents the school today.

興福國小於民國43年（1954年）以嘉寶國小分校形式設立，民國62年（1973年）更名為現名。學校位於下福村——林口區古老的農業聚落之一，其鄉村風貌啟發了一位早期教師設計出校徽：描繪螢火蟲在蓮花池畔閃爍的詩意畫面，至今仍代表著這所學校。
