Siwei Elementary School

四維國民小學

Founded in 1961 to serve a military-dependents' community, today a mid-sized public elementary school in Yangmei's Puxin area.

About · 學校簡介

Founded for a military community, grown with Puxin.

Siwei Elementary opened in August 1961, originally established as a dedicated school for a nearby military-dependents' village, with most of its early students being children of military families. Today the school enrolls around 550 students across 21 elementary classes and 3 kindergarten classes, and continues to serve Yangmei's Puxin area, particularly the Guanghua Village neighborhood, under the vision of "joy, creativity, and vitality."

四維國小創校於民國50年（1961年）8月，早期為軍眷區專屬小學，初期學生多為軍眷子弟。現有國小21班、幼兒園3班，學生約550人，願景為「喜悅、創意、有活力」，持續服務楊梅埔心地區（光華里一帶）的學童。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎖️ Roots in a Military-Dependents' Village

軍眷區專屬小學起源

Siwei was originally built to serve a nearby military-dependents' community, with its earliest cohorts made up largely of children from military families — a distinctive origin story among Yangmei's schools.

✨ "Joy, Creativity, Vitality"

喜悅、創意、有活力

The school's guiding vision emphasizes joyful, creative, and energetic learning, shaping its approach to whole-child education for around 550 students.

🏫 A Mid-Sized Puxin-Area School

21班規模，服務埔心地區

With 21 elementary classes plus kindergarten, Siwei is a solidly mid-sized school serving the Guanghua Village neighborhood of Yangmei's Puxin area.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where postwar migrants met an old Hakka community.

🏘️

Siwei Elementary's founding to serve a military-dependents' community is a marker of how postwar migration blended with Yangmei's established Hakka village life in the Puxin area, part of the district's broader story of integration and growth. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
