Xinxing Elementary School

新興國小

About · 學校簡介

A small school with a Hakka heart.

Xinxing Elementary School is a six-class elementary school with an attached kindergarten, located in a Hakka community that the school actively supports through Hakka-culture promotion. Its campus sits at the foot of Chepinglong Mountain, nestled between two streams that feed toward the Zhuoshui River. A new school building was completed in March 2013.

新興國小是一所六班制小學，附設幼兒園，座落於客家聚落之中，學校積極推廣客家文化。校園位於車坪林山腳，坐落在兩條匯流入濁水溪的溪流之間。新校舍於2013年3月完工啟用。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏮 Promoting Hakka Culture

推廣客家文化

Located in a Hakka community, Xinxing actively promotes Hakka culture and language, giving students a strong connection to their local heritage.

💧 Nestled Between Two Streams

兩溪之間的校園

The school sits at the foot of Chepinglong Mountain, nestled between two streams that feed toward the Zhuoshui River — a quiet, water-shaped setting typical of the area.

🏫 A Small, Close-Knit Campus

六班制小校園

As a six-class elementary school with an attached kindergarten, Xinxing offers a small, close-knit learning environment, with a new school building completed in 2013.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A Hakka village on the way to the timber trade.

🏮

Nestled between two streams that feed toward the Zhuoshui River, the school's Hakka-heritage community reflects the mixed settler history that grew up around Shuili's timber trade. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
