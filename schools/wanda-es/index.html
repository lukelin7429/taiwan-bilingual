About · 學校簡介

A bilingual school on the Jiala riverside.

Wanda Elementary School was founded in 1976 and takes its name from Wanda Road, which runs through southern Wanhua's Jiala area near the boundary with Zhonghe across the Xindian River. The school is now designated a "Bilingual Education Elementary School" (萬大雙語教育學校), with an active push toward bilingual instruction and international-exchange programming. Detailed early history beyond the founding year is limited in publicly available sources — the school's own website would hold fuller archival detail for those researching further.

萬大國小創立於民國65年（1976年），校名取自貫穿萬華南側加蚋仔地區、緊鄰新店溪對岸中和邊界的萬大路。學校現已指定為「萬大雙語教育學校」，積極推動雙語教學與國際交流課程。除創校年份外，公開資料中關於學校早期歷史的詳細記載較為有限，若欲進一步查考，建議參考學校官方網站的完整校史資料。

Local Culture · 在地文化

On the historic riverside boundary of Jiala.

🌊

Wanda Elementary takes its name from Wanda Road in southern Wanhua's Jiala area, bordering Zhonghe across the Xindian River — part of the district's historic riverside boundary zone. Jiala's identity has always been tied to the older Bangka (艋舺) settlement to the north, whose core around Longshan Temple (龍山寺) and Herb Lane (青草巷) remains the spiritual and commercial anchor for this end of Wanhua as well.
