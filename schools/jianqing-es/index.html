Jianqing Elementary School

見晴國民小學

A foothill school where Truku archery and wooden xylophone music sit alongside drawing, calligraphy, and hide crafting.

座落於山麓的學校，太魯閣族傳統射箭與木琴音樂，和繪畫、書法、皮雕課程並行發展。

About · 學校簡介

Four values, expressed in Truku.

Jianqing Elementary sits north of Wanrong village along the western foothills of the Hua-Tung Valley. The school displays its four core educational values — Initiative, Exploration, Diversity, and Innovation — bilingually in Truku, grounding its modern teaching philosophy in the language of the community it serves. Alongside a full arts-and-humanities program covering drawing, calligraphy, and leather and hide crafting, the school promotes traditional archery and instruction in the traditional wooden xylophone, an instrument tied closely to Truku musical heritage.

見晴國小位於萬榮村以北、花東縱谷西側山麓地帶。學校以太魯閣族語呈現其四大核心教育價值——主動、探索、多元、創新，將現代教育理念根植於在地族語文化之中。除了完整的藝文課程（含繪畫、書法、皮革工藝）外，學校也推廣傳統射箭與太魯閣族傳統木琴教學，延續與部落音樂文化緊密相連的技藝。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎵 Traditional Wooden Xylophone

太魯閣族傳統木琴

Students learn to play the traditional wooden xylophone, an instrument central to Truku musical tradition, keeping a distinctive local sound alive in the classroom.

🏹 Archery Alongside the Arts

射箭與藝術並重

Traditional archery training runs alongside a full arts program of drawing, calligraphy, and leather and hide crafting — a balance of physical skill and creative craft.

🔤 Core Values, Spoken in Truku

四大核心價值以族語呈現

The school's four guiding values — Initiative, Exploration, Diversity, Innovation — are displayed bilingually in Truku, making language visibility part of everyday school life.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Foothill village, mountain heritage.

🎋

Jianqing Village sits along the western edge of the Hua-Tung Valley within Wanrong Township 萬榮鄉, a mountainous Truku Indigenous community. The school's pairing of traditional archery and wooden xylophone instruction reflects skills once essential to daily and ceremonial Truku life, now preserved through the next generation of students.
