Tianzhong Senior High School

田中高級中學

A public senior high school deeply rooted in Taiwan's "rice granary" township, offering specialized semiconductor and sports programs alongside a 70-year history.

About · 學校簡介

From junior high roots to a 70-year legacy.

Tianzhong Senior High traces its origins to preparatory approval granted in 1955, and was officially established on October 28, 1957 as Changhua County Tianzhong Junior Middle School. It became a full junior high school in 1968 and added a supplementary school in 1979. A preparatory office for a senior-high division opened in 2009, and the school officially became Changhua County Tianzhong Senior High School in 2011 — celebrating its 70th anniversary as an institution. Today it offers specialized programs including a semiconductor experimental class and a sports program, deeply connected to Tianzhong Township's identity as Taiwan's "rice granary."

田中高中的前身於民國44年（1955年）獲准籌設，並於民國46年（1957年）10月28日正式成立為彰化縣立田中初級中學。民國57年（1968年）改制為完全國中，民國68年（1979年）附設補校。民國98年（2009年）成立高中部籌備處，於民國100年（2011年）正式改制為彰化縣立田中高級中學，適逢建校70週年。學校目前設有半導體實驗班與體育班等特色課程，與田中鎮作為台灣「米倉」的地方特色緊密相連。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💻 A Semiconductor Experimental Class

半導體實驗班

Tianzhong Senior High offers a specialized semiconductor experimental class, connecting students to one of Taiwan's most important industries even before university.

🏃 A Dedicated Sports Program

體育班特色課程

The school's sports program gives student-athletes a structured path to compete and train, complementing its academic and technical tracks.

🎓 From Junior High to Senior High

從國中到高中的轉型

Founded as a junior middle school in 1957, the school only became a full senior high in 2011 — a transformation reflecting decades of steady institutional growth.

🌾 Deep Roots in Local Life

深耕在地

Local Culture · 在地文化

Tianzhong, Taiwan's rice granary.

🌾

Tianzhong Township is known across Taiwan as the island's "rice granary" (台灣米倉), and is home to the famous Tianzhong Marathon, which runs through its rice paddies. The school's vision of "深耕在地" (deep local roots) draws directly on this rice-farming heritage. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
