Ren-ai Elementary School

仁愛國民小學

An elementary school founded in 1956 specifically to absorb overflow students from the overcrowded Jiali Elementary School, reflecting the town's postwar population boom.

1956年創校，為紓解佳里國小學生數過多而設立，見證佳里市街戰後人口成長。

About · 學校簡介

Built to relieve an overcrowded neighbor school.

Ren-ai Elementary was approved for establishment in August 1956 as "Ren-ai National School," with founding principal Chen Xiquan taking office that September and a founding ceremony held on December 23, 1956. The school's first graduating class went out in 1959, and it was renamed Ren-ai Elementary School in 1968. It was established specifically to absorb overflow enrollment from the overcrowded Jiali Elementary School, taking over its second-, third-, and fourth-grade classes at founding.

仁愛國小於民國45年（1956年）8月奉准設立，初名「仁愛國民學校」，首任校長陳西泉於同年9月到任，並於同年12月23日舉行創校典禮。民國48年（1959年）首屆畢業生畢業，民國57年（1968年）改稱「仁愛國民小學」。本校設立目的在於分擔佳里國小學生數過多的壓力，創校時即承接該校二、三、四年級部分班級學生。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Jiali, market town of the coastal plain.

⛩️

Ren-ai Elementary's founding illustrates Jiali town's post-war population boom as the district's traditional commercial center expanded. The wider district remains centered on Jinluan Temple (金唐殿), one of the Tainan coastal plain's key Wangye "King Boat Festival" temples, and on the Siraya Pingpu ancestor-spirit (阿立祖) worship traditions found in the surrounding hamlets.
