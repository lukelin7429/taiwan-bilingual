About · 學校簡介

A century of business education in South District.

National Tainan Commercial & Vocational Senior High School, known locally as "Nanshang" (南商), traces its roots to 1921, when it opened as the Public Tainan Commercial Supplementary School and was formally organized the following year. Over a century later it remains one of the oldest vocational commercial schools in southern Taiwan, with a campus of about 3.3 hectares enrolling roughly 1,659 day-division students across 51 classes plus 143 evening-division students in 12 classes. The school has long specialized in accounting, business management, and international trade tracks, and in November 2022 it opened the first civic sports center built inside a national vocational high school, complete with a climbing wall and fitness studio open to the surrounding community.

國立臺南高級商業職業學校，在地人稱「南商」，源自1921年（大正10年）創立的「公立臺南商業補習學校」，隔年正式立案。歷經百餘年，至今仍是南台灣歷史最悠久的商業職業學校之一，校地約3.3公頃，日間部約1,659名學生、51班，另有夜間部143名學生、12班。學校長期專精於會計、企業管理與國際貿易等商科領域，並於2022年11月啟用全國高職首座對外開放的市民運動中心，設有攀岩牆與健身教室，服務鄰近社區。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💼 Over a Century of Business Education

百年商科教育

Founded in 1921 as a commercial supplementary school, Tainan Commercial is one of the oldest vocational business schools in southern Taiwan, having trained generations of accountants, managers, and traders.

🧗 A Sports Center Open to the Community

全國首座對外開放的高職市民運動中心

In November 2022 the school opened the first civic sports center built inside a national vocational high school, with a climbing wall and fitness studio welcoming neighborhood residents alongside students.

📊 Specialized Vocational Tracks

會計、企業管理、國際貿易專業科別

Students choose among accounting, business management, and international trade programs, reflecting the school's long focus on preparing graduates for Tainan's commercial workforce.

🏫 A Large, Active Campus

日夜間部並行的活絡校園

The roughly 3.3-hectare campus supports both a day division of about 1,659 students and an evening division of 143 students, keeping the school a busy hub of vocational learning.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchored in Tainan's old canal and harbor trade.

🚢

Tainan Commercial sits on Jiankang Road in South District, near the site of the old Tainan Canal and harbor district that once linked the city to the sea. For over a century the school has trained the clerks, accountants, and merchants who kept this port-adjacent commercial world running — a living link to South District's harbor-trade heritage.
