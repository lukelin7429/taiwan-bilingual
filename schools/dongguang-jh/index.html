Dongguang Junior High School

東光國民中學

A public junior high school in Luodong Township, home to the town's only art gifted-and-talented class since 1994.

About · 學校簡介

From branch school to arts pioneer.

Dongguang Junior High began in 1957 as a branch of Luodong Junior High, gaining independence in 1963 under the name Dongguang Junior Middle School and adopting its current name in 1968. In 1994, it became the first school in Yilan County to establish an art gifted-and-talented class — a program that remains the only one of its kind in Luodong Township. The school has also operated a supplementary evening school since 1986, and its old school gate has been preserved and recognized as a heritage landmark.

東光國中創立於民國46年（1957年），最初為羅東國中分校，民國52年（1963年）獨立設校，初名「東光初級中學」，民國57年（1968年）更為現名。民國83年（1994年），該校成為宜蘭縣第一所設立美術資優班的學校，至今仍是羅東鎮唯一的美術資優班。學校自民國75年（1986年）起附設補校，其保存下來的舊校門也已被列為文化資產地標。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎨 Yilan's First Art Gifted Class

全縣首創美術資優班

Established in 1994, Dongguang's art gifted-and-talented class was the first of its kind in Yilan County and remains the only such program in Luodong Township.

🚪 A Heritage School Gate

保存的舊校門地標

The school's original entrance gate has been preserved and recognized as a heritage landmark, standing as a physical link to its founding decades.

🌙 A Long-Running Evening School

附設補校

Since 1986, the school has operated a supplementary evening program, extending educational access to adult and working students in the community.

🏫 Six Decades of Growth

近七十年校史

From a 1957 branch campus to an independent junior high with a nationally recognized arts program, Dongguang has grown alongside Luodong's expanding population.

Local Culture · 在地文化

An arts-minded corner of Luodong.

🖼️

Dongguang's art-focused gifted program contributes to Luodong's cultural identity as a town known for creative and performing arts beyond its commercial reputation.
