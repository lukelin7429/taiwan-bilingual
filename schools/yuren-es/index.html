About · 學校簡介

Half a century of calligraphy tradition.

Yuren Elementary School celebrated its 50th founding anniversary in 2022, placing its origins around 1972. The school is well known locally for promoting calligraphy culture, having held an annual school-wide Spring Couplet (春聯) calligraphy writing event for more than seven consecutive years, drawing over 400 students and teachers each time.

育人國小於2022年慶祝建校50週年，推算創校約在民國61年（1972年）。學校長期推廣書法文化，連續七年以上舉辦全校性的「春聯」書法比賽活動，每次吸引超過400位師生共同參與。
