Datong Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A branch school that became a digital-learning model.

Datong Elementary School began in September 1954 as the "Jinxue Elementary School Dalin Branch" (進學國校大林分校) under branch director Xu Ben, becoming an independent school in September 1955 as "Tainan City East District Datong National School." Its modern reputation, however, rests on a long run of technology-education partnerships: in 2006 it became a National Tainan University collaborative development school hosting NUTN's Datong Experimental Center, in 2008 it partnered with Microsoft Taiwan on a "school of the future" initiative, and in 2014 it partnered with Samsung Taiwan to establish a "SMART School" smart classroom. The school is recognized as a Ministry of Education "Reading Cornerstone" school and character-education model school, and won MOE awards for tech-integrated teaching and mobile learning in 2017, followed by selection for the 2018 MOE digital-learning deepening program.

大同國小前身為1954年（民國43年）9月成立的「進學國校大林分校」，首任分校主任為許本，1955年（民國44年）9月獨立設校，定名為「臺南市東區大同國民學校」。學校現今聲名主要來自一系列教育科技合作：2006年成為國立臺南大學合作發展學校，校內設有南大大同實驗中心；2008年與台灣微軟合作推動「未來學校」計畫；2014年與台灣三星合作建置「SMART School」智慧教室。學校並獲教育部認證為「閱讀磐石」學校及品格教育典範學校，2017年獲教育部科技融入教學暨行動學習獎項，2018年入選教育部數位學習深耕計畫。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💻 A Decade of Ed-Tech Partnerships

跨產業教育科技合作

From Microsoft Taiwan's "school of the future" (2008) to Samsung's "SMART School" (2014), the school has built a long string of ministry-level education-technology recognitions spanning 2006–2018.

📖 A Ministry "Reading Cornerstone" School

閱讀磐石認證學校

Recognized by the Ministry of Education as a "Reading Cornerstone" school and character-education model school, reflecting a strong emphasis on literacy and values education.

🎓 A University Collaborative Campus

南大合作發展學校

Since 2006, the school has hosted National Tainan University's Datong Experimental Center as a collaborative development school.

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the heart of the district's civic corridor.

🏛️

Located near the Tainan Municipal Cultural Center along Zhonghua East Road, the school sits in the heart of the district's postwar civic and cultural development corridor, distinct from — but complementary to — the historic core to the west. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
