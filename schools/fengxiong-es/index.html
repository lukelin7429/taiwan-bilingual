Fengxiong Elementary School

鳳雄國小

One of the smallest schools in Yanchao District, part of a family of schools that all trace back to Hengshan Elementary's branch network.

About · 學校簡介

A small school with a wide reach.

Fengxiong Elementary opened in 1951 as a branch class of Hengshan Elementary School, becoming independent in 1955. It remains one of the smallest schools in Yanchao District, with just 6 classes, around 84 students, and 11 teachers, and it operates a community reading station that serves the wider neighborhood.

鳳雄國小創立於民國40年（1951年），原為橫山國民學校「鳳雄分班」，民國44年（1955年）獨立設校。學校目前僅有6班、約84名學生、11位教師，是燕巢區規模最小的學校之一，並設有社區閱讀站服務鄰里。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📖 A Reading Station for the Community

社區閱讀站

Fengxiong hosts a community reading station, extending its role beyond the classroom to serve neighbors of all ages in this small rural village.

🌾 One of Three Sibling Schools

橫山分校體系

Along with Hengshan and Shenshui Elementary, Fengxiong descends from Hengshan Elementary's mid-20th-century branch network — a trio of schools born from one mother school.

🏫 A Very Small, Close Community

全區最小規模之一

With just 6 classes and around 84 students, Fengxiong offers an intimate, close-knit learning environment typical of Yanchao's rural villages.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A family of schools from one root.

🌾

Fengxiong, along with Hengshan and Shenshui Elementary, is part of a small family of schools descended from Hengshan Elementary's mid-20th-century branch network — a pattern of school-splitting common across rural Yanchao District. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
