About · 學校簡介

A Japanese-era campus straddling two towns.

Songmei Elementary was established on April 7, 1913, as the 'Nanshizhu branch' of Puzi Public School, petitioned into being by regional leaders including the district chiefs of both Budai and Puzi. It went through several post-war renamings — Tainan County Yizhu Township 2nd National School in 1946, Songmei National School later that year, transfer to Chiayi County in 1950, reassignment to Puzi Township in 1962, and Songmei Elementary School in 1968. The campus includes a Japanese-era principal's residence completed in 1915, registered as a Chiayi County historic building in 2004. The school recently completed a new PU running track, celebrated its 108th founding anniversary under the motto '松梅2.0~喜迎100' (marking its approach to a full century under its current identity), and runs a dedicated indigenous/native-language education website alongside strong environmental and lunch education programs.

松梅國小創立於大正2年（1913年）4月7日，最初為樸仔腳公學校南勢竹分校，由布袋與朴子兩地區長共同請設。戰後歷經多次更名：民國35年（1946年）改稱台南縣義竹鄉第二國民學校，同年稍後改稱松梅國民學校，民國39年（1950年）改隸嘉義縣，民國51年（1962年）改隸朴子鎮，民國57年（1968年）定名松梅國小。校園內保有大正4年（1915年）完工的日治時期校長官舍，於民國93年（2004年）登錄為嘉義縣定古蹟。學校近期完成新建PU跑道，並以「松梅2.0～喜迎100」為主題慶祝建校108週年，象徵即將邁向百年，另建置本土語言教育網站，並推動扎實的環境教育與午餐教育課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏯 A Registered Historic Building

校長官舍：縣定古蹟

The campus's Japanese-era principal's residence, completed in 1915, was registered as a Chiayi County historic building in 2004 — a rare architectural landmark on an elementary school campus.

🤝 Founded by Two Districts Together

布袋、朴子共同請設

The school's founding was jointly petitioned by leaders of both Budai and Puzi districts, reflecting its position straddling their historic administrative boundary.

🏃 A New Running Track

新建PU跑道

A newly completed PU running track gives students modern athletic facilities on this century-old campus.

💯 Approaching a Full Century

松梅2.0～喜迎100

Under the motto '松梅2.0~喜迎100,' the school marked its 108th anniversary while looking ahead to its full centennial.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where colonial-era roots meet coastal Chiayi.

🏯

Straddling the historic boundary between Budai and Puzi's administrative districts — its founding jointly petitioned by leaders of both towns — Songmei Elementary's Japanese-era heritage building reflects the deep colonial-period roots of education across Chiayi County's coastal salt, aquaculture, and market towns.
