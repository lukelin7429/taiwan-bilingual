About · 學校簡介

Outdoor education meets a bilingual track.

Zhongfu Elementary opened on August 1, 1999, one of the newest schools to split off from historic Xinjie Elementary as Zhongli's population continued expanding through the late 1990s. The school has built a distinctive identity around outdoor education, running programs that include tree-climbing (攀樹) alongside broader environmental education, and it offers a bilingual/English certification track alongside cultural-heritage programming. Its school motto — Gratitude, Inclusiveness, Simplicity, Persistence, Innovation (感恩、包容、樸實、堅持、創新) — reflects a holistic approach to student development.

忠福國小創校於民國88年（1999年）8月1日，是新街國小為因應中壢地區1990年代末人口持續成長而分出的新校之一。學校以戶外教育為特色，開設攀樹等課程並結合環境教育，同時提供雙語／英語認證課程與文化傳承活動。校訓「感恩、包容、樸實、堅持、創新」展現學校對學生全人發展的重視。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 Tree-Climbing as Outdoor Education

攀樹戶外教育

Zhongfu runs a distinctive tree-climbing (攀樹) program as part of its outdoor and environmental education offerings, uncommon among elementary schools in the district.

🗣️ A Bilingual Certification Track

雙語認證課程

Alongside cultural-heritage programming, the school offers a bilingual/English certification track for students.

👨‍👩‍👧 A Daughter School of Xinjie

新街國小分校

Zhongfu is one of several schools that split off from historic Xinjie Elementary as Zhongli's urban core kept expanding into the 1990s.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Still spinning off new schools in the 1990s.

🌳

Zhongfu Elementary is a "daughter school" of Xinjie Elementary, one of Zhongli's oldest institutions — a lineage shared with Neili and Xinyi elementary schools. Its 1999 founding shows that Zhongli's core urban schools kept spinning off new campuses well into the late 20th century, decades after the district's earliest schools were established, as the population never stopped growing.
