Liyu Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

Named for the carp in the river.

Liyu Elementary School was founded in 1955 in Zhushan Township, Nantou County, taking its name from a fish-shaped sandbar — 鯉魚, meaning "carp" — that sits in the nearby Qingshui River. The school has built a reputation for creative, hands-on classroom teaching, earning a place on Parenting magazine's 2019 "Education Innovation 100" list, and its students have gone on to win a Sony science-competition championship.

鯉魚國小創立於民國44年（1955年），位於南投縣竹山鎮，校名源自附近清水溪中一塊形似鯉魚的沙洲。學校以創意動手實作教學聞名，曾入選親子天下2019年「教育創新100」，學生更曾榮獲Sony科展競賽冠軍。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🐟 A Name From the River

鯉魚沙洲命名由來

The school takes its name from a carp-shaped sandbar (鯉魚) in the nearby Qingshui River, tying its identity to the waterway that runs through the community it serves.

💡 Recognized for Education Innovation

教育創新100

Liyu was selected for Parenting magazine's 2019 "Education Innovation 100" list, honoring its creative, hands-on approach to classroom teaching.

🏆 Sony Science Champions

Sony科展冠軍

Students from this small rural school have won a Sony science competition championship, a notable achievement for a school of its size.

🎋 Rooted in Bamboo Country

竹鄉子弟

Set amid the bamboo-forest hills of Zhushan Township, the school's rural setting reflects the industry and landscape that shape daily life for its students' families.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Zhushan, Taiwan's bamboo capital.

Located in Zhushan Township, world-famous for its bamboo craft, Liyu Elementary's rural setting sits amid the bamboo-forest hills that define the township's identity and local economy. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
