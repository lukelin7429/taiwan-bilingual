About · 學校簡介

A newer school on an old road.

Dong'an Junior High was founded in 1999, after a preparatory office was established in August 1996. The school is located in the 官路缺 (Guanluque) area of Pingzhen — a neighborhood name that references a historic Qing-era official route through the hills, tying the school to the district's old inland transportation geography. It became a municipal institution in 2014 following Taoyuan County's upgrade to special-municipality status. Detailed enrollment and award records from the school's founding era are not well documented in available sources, but it remains one of the newer junior highs serving Pingzhen's eastern neighborhoods.

東安國中創立於1999年，1996年8月即成立籌備處。校址位於平鎮區官路缺地區——此地名源自清代穿越丘陵地帶的官方道路，見證了平鎮早期的內陸交通地理。2014年桃園縣升格直轄市後，本校改制為市立學校。學校創校初期的詳細招生與獲獎紀錄目前尚無完整史料可考，但至今仍是服務平鎮東側社區的重要國中之一。
