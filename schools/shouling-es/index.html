Shouling Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

Named for the man who donated the land.

Shouling Elementary School began on August 1, 1952, as a branch campus of Mituo National School called Wenji Branch School. It became independent on September 1, 1954, as Wenji National School, before being renamed "Shouling" on August 1, 1958, to honor Zhang Shouling, the local benefactor who donated the land the school stands on. Unlike most schools in the area, whose names reference places or geography, Shouling's name commemorates an individual act of private philanthropy.

壽齡國小創立於民國41年（1952年）8月1日，最初為彌陀國校分校，名為「文吉分校」。民國43年（1954年）9月1日獨立設校，定名「文吉國民學校」，民國47年（1958年）8月1日改名為「壽齡」，以紀念捐地興學的地方仕紳張壽齡。與本地多數以地名命名的學校不同，壽齡國小的校名紀念的是一位個人的公益善舉。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎗️ Named for a Land-Donating Benefactor

紀念捐地仕紳

The school was renamed "Shouling" in 1958 to honor Zhang Shouling, the local benefactor who donated the land it stands on — a rare example of a school named for an individual rather than a place.

🏫 A Branch School That Grew Independent

分校獨立設校

Shouling Elementary began in 1952 as Mituo National School's Wenji Branch, becoming a fully independent school two years later in 1954.

📛 Two Names in Six Years

六年間兩度更名

The school carried the name "Wenji" for four years before its 1958 renaming to "Shouling," reflecting the community's wish to formally honor its benefactor.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A rare school named for a private benefactor.

🎗️

Shouling Elementary is a rare example among Mituo's schools of a name honoring a private philanthropist rather than a place name — commemorating Zhang Shouling, who donated the land for the school. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
