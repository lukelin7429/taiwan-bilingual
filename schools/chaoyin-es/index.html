Chaoyin Elementary School

潮音國民小學

About · 學校簡介

Too many students, not too few.

Chaoyin Elementary traces its origins to a teaching classroom established by Dayuan National School on September 1, 1950, becoming independent as Chaoyin National School in February 1956. Unlike the steady depopulation facing most of Dayuan's coastal schools, Chaoyin faces the opposite problem: it is now severely overcrowded, with enrollment surging due to new residential and commercial development near the campus. To relieve the pressure, the Taoyuan City government is building an entirely new school, tentatively called "航科國小" (Aviation-Science Elementary) — its preparatory office was slated to open in August 2025, with the new campus expected to finish around 2027 and begin enrollment around 2028.

潮音國小前身為大園國民學校於1950年（民國39年）9月1日設立的教學班，1956年（民國45年）2月獨立設校，稱「潮音國民學校」。與大園多數沿海學校面臨的人口外流恰恰相反，潮音國小如今嚴重超額，因鄰近新建住宅與商業開發案而學生人數暴增。為紓解壓力，桃園市政府正興建一所全新學校，暫定名為「航科國小」，籌備處預計於2025年8月成立，新校區預計2027年完工，2028年前後開始招生。
