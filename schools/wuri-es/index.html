About · 學校簡介

A century-old school that named its district.

Wuri Elementary School was founded in 1915 as a branch campus of "犁頭店公學校" (Litoudian Public School), during the Japanese colonial era. It became independent in 1919 as "烏日公學校." Now over a century old, the school serves roughly 740 students across 30 regular classes plus 2 special-education classes, making it a substantial anchor institution for the district that shares its name.

烏日國小創立於1915年（日治時期），最初為「犁頭店公學校」的分校，民國8年（1919年）獨立設校，改稱「烏日公學校」。如今建校已逾百年，全校約740名學生，設有30個普通班及2個特教班，是與本區同名的重要據點學校。
