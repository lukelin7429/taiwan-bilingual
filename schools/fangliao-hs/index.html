Fangliao Senior High School

枋寮高級中學

The only “complete school” (junior and senior high combined) in southern Pingtung, built around a school-wide reading-literacy program that runs from grade 7 through grade 12.

屏南唯一的完全中學，國中部與高中部合一，並以貫穿七年級到十二年級的全校閱讀素養課程為特色。

About · 學校簡介

One campus, junior high through senior high.

Fangliao Senior High School traces its roots to 1964, when it opened as a branch of Donggang Junior High. It became an independent junior high school in 1966, was renamed Fangliao National Junior High in 1968 under Taiwan's nine-year compulsory education reform, and was upgraded to full senior-high status in February 2001. Today it is the only “complete school” (完全中學) in southern Pingtung County, meaning its junior-high division (國中部, grades 7–9) and senior-high division (高中部, grades 10–12) share one campus and one administration. The senior-high division runs nine general classes plus three athletics/PE special classes, while the junior-high division has 21 general classes, two special-education classes, and one home-schooling itinerant class. Note: some directories list “枋寮國中” (Fangliao Junior High) separately, but it is simply this school's junior-high division, not a distinct institution.

枋寮高中創校於民國53年（1964年），最初是東港國中枋寮分部。民國55年（1966年）獨立設校為枋寮國中，民國57年（1968年）因九年國教改制更名為枋寮國民中學，並於民國90年（2001年）2月奉准增設高中部，成為屏南地區唯一的完全中學。高中部設有普通班9班及體育班3班，國中部則有普通班21班、特教班2班及巡迴輔導班1班。提醒：部分名錄會將「枋寮國中」單獨列出，但實際上僅是本校國中部，並非另一所學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📖 A School-Wide Reading Program

全校閱讀素養課程

A three-stage reading curriculum — “reading guidance,” “reading literacy,” and “reading step by step in Fangliao” — runs across junior-high grades 7–9 and feeds directly into senior-high elective courses.

🏫 Southern Pingtung's Only Complete School

屏南唯一完全中學

Fangliao Senior High is the sole junior-and-senior-high combined institution in the southern part of Pingtung County, giving students a continuous six-year path from grade 7 through grade 12 on one campus.

🏃 Athletics Alongside Academics

體育班與特教資源

Beyond its general classes, the senior-high division runs three dedicated athletics/PE classes, while the junior-high division supports students through two special-education classes and a home-schooling itinerant program.

🚄 Gateway to the South-Link Railway

南迴鐵路門戶

The school sits in Fangliao, the starting point of the South-Link Railway and its nostalgic “Blue Train,” and a major grouper- and whitebait-fishing and aquaculture hub along Taiwan's southern coast.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fangliao, gateway to the South-Link Railway.

🚄

Fangliao Township sits at the junction of Provincial Highway 1 and Highway 17, marking the starting point of the South-Link Railway (南迴鐵路) and its nostalgic “Blue Train” (藍皮解憂號) toward Taitung and Kenting. The township is also one of Taiwan's major fishing and aquaculture centers, famous for grouper (石斑魚) and whitebait (魩鱙魚) — an identity the school's students grow up alongside every day.
