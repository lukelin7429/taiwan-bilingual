About · 學校簡介

Nearly a century of agricultural education.

Founded in 1937 as "Taichung Prefecture Agricultural School" (臺中州立農業學校) under Japanese colonial rule, this school has for nearly a century trained generations of students in Taiwan's agricultural trades — reflecting central Taiwan's long-standing role as one of the island's major agricultural production regions. In 2014, the school became formally affiliated with National Chung Hsing University, adopting its present name and deepening ties between secondary agricultural vocational education and the university's agricultural and life-sciences programs. Today the school's Forestry Department manages nearly 100 hectares of practice forest, giving students hands-on training that few vocational high schools in Taiwan can match. Though closely tied to National Chung Hsing University, the school itself remains a senior vocational high school, not a university campus.

本校創立於1937年（昭和12年），原名「臺中州立農業學校」，近百年來培育無數農業人才，體現中台灣長期作為全台重要農業生產地區的角色。民國103年（2014年）起，學校正式成為國立中興大學附屬學校，並更名為現名，深化中等農業職業教育與大學農業暨生命科學相關科系的連結。目前學校森林科管理近百公頃實習林地，提供學生難得的實作訓練機會，在全台職業高中中相當罕見。雖與國立中興大學關係密切，本校仍是一所高級職業學校，並非大學校區。
