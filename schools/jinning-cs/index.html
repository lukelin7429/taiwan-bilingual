About · 學校簡介

One school, three stages, one Jinning community.

Jinning Junior High/Elementary School was founded in the fall of 1966 as "Kinmen County Jinning Junior High School," opening with just 157 students taught in rented houses in Panshan Village while permanent buildings were still under construction. Those buildings were completed the following year. In 1968, when nine-year compulsory education began nationwide, the school merged with the nearby Anmei School (itself formed the year before) and absorbed a branch of Guning Elementary, taking on its current name and combined structure. Today it is the only school in Kinmen County that brings junior high, elementary, and kindergarten together under one administration, and it has celebrated its 55th anniversary as a fixture of the Jinning community.

金門縣立金寧國民中小學創校於民國55年（1966年）秋季，初名「金門縣立金寧初級中學」，開辦時僅有157名學生，借用盤山村民宅上課，隔年校舍始正式完工。民國57年（1968年）九年國民義務教育開始實施，學校與安美學校（前一年始設）合併，並併入古寧國小分班，逐步發展為現今國中、國小合一的組織型態，也是金門縣唯一將國中、國小、幼兒園整合於同一行政體系下的學校。學校已歡慶55週年校慶，是金寧鄉在地重要的教育機構。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Kinmen's Only Combined School

全金門唯一國中小合一學校

Jinning JH/ES is the sole school in Kinmen County that unites a junior high, an elementary school, and a kindergarten under one shared administration.

🏘️ Built From Village Schools

整併多所鄉村學校

The school grew through a series of mergers and absorptions — starting from rented houses in Panshan Village, joining with Anmei School, and taking in a branch campus from Guning Elementary as enrollment expanded.

🎉 55 Years of History

55週年校慶

Founded in 1966, the school has marked its 55th anniversary, reflecting six decades of continuous service to Jinning Township families.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anqi, in the shadow of Kinmen's frontline history.

🎖️

The school sits in Anqi, Jinning Township — an area of Kinmen shaped by the island's frontline military history, having absorbed several small village schools that were consolidated during the post-war and artillery-battle era. See the Kinmen county page for more on the region.
