Dengfa Elementary School

登發國小

Named for pro-democracy pioneer Yu Dengfa, this school was founded in 1992 to ease overcrowding as Renwu's Chengcing Lake area urbanized.

以民主前輩余登發之名命名，1992年為紓解澄清湖特區都市化帶來的就學壓力而設立。

About · 學校簡介

Named for a democracy pioneer, built for a growing district.

Dengfa Elementary was established on August 1, 1992, and began enrollment in August 1993 with 18 classes transferred from an overcrowded Wannei Elementary School. It is named after Yu Dengfa (余登發, 1904–1989), a pro-democracy pioneer and the fourth magistrate of Kaohsiung County. The school grew well beyond its originally planned 24 classes as the surrounding Chengcing Lake Special Zone urbanized rapidly.

登發國小成立於1992年（民國81年）8月1日，1993年8月開始招生，最初18個班級是由過度擁擠的灣內國小轉入。學校以民主前輩、高雄縣第四任縣長余登發（1904–1989）之名命名。隨著澄清湖特定區快速都市化，學校規模遠超原先規劃的24班。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Urbanization around Chengcing Lake.

🏙️

Dengfa Elementary was built because the Chengcing Lake Special Zone converted rapidly from farmland into a dense commercial and residential area, overwhelming the capacity of neighboring Wannei Elementary School. See the Kaohsiung County/City page for more on the region.
