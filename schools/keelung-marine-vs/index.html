About · 學校簡介

From a colonial-era training institute to a university-affiliated high school.

This school's roots go back to 1936, when the Japanese colonial government established the Taiwan Governor-General's Water Products Training Institute (臺灣總督府立水產講習所) to train workers for the fishing industry around Keelung's harbor. It was upgraded to senior vocational school status in 1953 and rebranded as a maritime vocational institution in 1970, long known simply as "Keelung Maritime and Fisheries Vocational School" (國立基隆海事水產職業學校). The school came under national government administration in 2000, and in 2020 it entered into an affiliation with National Taiwan Ocean University, adopting its current official name: National Taiwan Ocean University Affiliated Keelung Maritime Senior High School (國立臺灣海洋大學附屬基隆海事高級中等學校). Its entire mission traces Keelung's identity as a fishing and shipping port, training students in navigation, marine engineering, and fisheries for the local maritime industry.

本校歷史可追溯至1936年（昭和11年），日本殖民政府設立「臺灣總督府立水產講習所」，為基隆港周邊的漁業訓練人才。民國42年（1953年）升格為高級職業學校，民國59年（1970年）轉型為海事類職業學校，長期以「國立基隆海事水產職業學校」之名為人熟知。民國89年（2000年）改隸國立，民國109年（2020年）與國立臺灣海洋大學建立附屬關係，正式更名為現今的「國立臺灣海洋大學附屬基隆海事高級中等學校」。學校的整體使命緊扣基隆作為漁業與航運港口的城市定位，為在地海事產業培育航海、輪機與漁業人才。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚓ Nearly 90 Years of Maritime Training

近九十年海事教育歷史

Founded in 1936 as a colonial-era water products training institute, the school has trained generations of sailors, marine engineers, and fisheries workers for Keelung's port economy.

🎓 A New University Affiliation

2020年起附屬海大

Since 2020, the school has been affiliated with National Taiwan Ocean University, adopting its current name and strengthening ties between vocational maritime training and higher education.

🐟 A Fisheries and Navigation Legacy

航海與水產專業傳承

The school moved from senior vocational status in 1953 to a dedicated maritime institution in 1970, continuously adapting its programs to serve Taiwan's fishing and shipping industries.

🏛️ Now a National School

2000年改隸國立

The school transitioned to national government administration in 2000, two decades before its affiliation with National Taiwan Ocean University.
