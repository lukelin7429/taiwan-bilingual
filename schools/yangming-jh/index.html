Yangming Junior High School

楊明國民中學

A mid-sized municipal junior high founded in 1994 as Yangmei grew, now home to a strong wrestling team and award-winning aesthetic-education courses.

1994年因楊梅人口成長而設立的中型國中，角力隊表現優異，並發展出多項獲獎的美感教育特色課程。

About · 學校簡介

Built for a growing, urbanizing Yangmei.

Yangming Junior High's preparatory office was established at Ruiyuan Junior High in 1994, and the school opened in 1996 to its first cohort of seventh-graders as Taoyuan County Yangming Junior High. It took its current name, Taoyuan City Yangming Junior High, in December 2015. The campus covers about 3.2 hectares across 36 classes, making it a mid-sized school for the district, and its founding in the 1990s reflects Yangmei's shift from a traditional Hakka farming township toward a more urbanized community with a growing school-age population.

楊明國中於民國83年（1994年）在瑞原國中成立桃園縣立楊明國民中學籌備處；民國85年（1996年）正式成立，招收第一屆國一新生；民國104年（2015年）12月更名桃園市立楊明國民中學。校地約3.2公頃，規模達36班，屬楊梅區中型國中。1990年代因楊梅人口成長而增設，反映楊梅從傳統客庄逐步都市化、學齡人口擴張的發展軌跡。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🤼 A Strong Wrestling Program

角力隊表現優異

Yangming's wrestling team turned in standout results at the 114th National Intercollegiate Athletic Games, one of the school's marquee competitive-sports achievements.

✨ Award-Winning Aesthetic Education

跨領域美感教育卓越領航

The school takes part in the national Cross-Disciplinary Aesthetic Education Excellence program, developing original courses like "A Thousand Miles to Meet, Drawn in a Circle" and "Light and Shadow Magician: 3D Geometric Lanterns."

🏫 A Mid-Sized, 36-Class Campus

校地3.2公頃，規模36班

With about 3.2 hectares of campus and 36 classes, Yangming sits solidly in the mid-size range among Yangmei's junior highs, having grown alongside the district's 1990s population boom.
