About · 學校簡介

From junior high roots to the county's flagship high school.

National Nantou Senior High School was founded in 1946 (ROC 35) as Taichung County Nantou Junior High School, becoming a national senior high school in 2000. Today it sits at the administrative and educational heart of the county seat, drawing about 1,310 students from across Nantou's Bagua Mountain foothill townships to a 92,000-square-meter campus with dormitories and dining facilities. The school offers both general academic and vocational tracks, including architecture, CAD, electronics, applied English, and e-commerce.

國立南投高中創立於民國35年（1946年），前身為台中縣立南投初級中學，於民國89年（2000年）改制為國立高中。如今座落於南投市治理與教育核心地帶，吸引來自八卦山麓各鄉鎮的學生，全校約1,310人，校地達92,000平方公尺，設有宿舍與餐廳設施。學校兼設普通科與職業類科，包括建築、電腦繪圖、電子、應用英語、電子商務等。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏆 World Tug-of-War Champions

拔河隊世界冠軍

The school's tug-of-war team won the youth 560kg category world championship in the Netherlands in 2016 — a standout achievement for the county seat's flagship high school.

🏗️ Academic and Vocational Tracks

普通科與職業類科並設

Beyond its general academic program, the school offers vocational departments in architecture, CAD, electronics, applied English, and e-commerce, broadening students' career pathways.

🏫 A 92,000 sq. m Campus

92,000平方公尺校園

The campus spans 92,000 square meters with dormitories and dining facilities, supporting students who commute from townships across the county.

📍 The County Seat's Educational Heart

縣治教育核心

As Nantou City's flagship general senior high, the school draws students from across the Bagua Mountain foothill townships to the administrative center of the county.
