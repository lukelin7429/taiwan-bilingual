About · 學校簡介

A school moved by a reservoir, reborn as a charter school.

Neiweng Elementary began in 1950 as a branch classroom of Minhe Elementary, gaining independence in 1959. Construction of the Renyi Reservoir forced the school to relocate — after years of planning from 1979 to 1985, the campus moved to its current site in 1987. In 2024, Neiweng became the second public-private partnership (KIST) experimental school in Chiayi County, opening a new third-generation campus building that cost nearly NT$100 million. Its motto, "cultivate learning, build confidence and character" (耕讀內甕 自信有品), reflects a strong focus on project-based learning, AI literacy, and environmental and reading education.

內甕國小前身為民國39年（1950年）設立的「民和國民學校內甕分教室」，民國48年（1959年）獨立設校。因仁義潭水庫興建，歷經民國68至74年（1979-1985年）規劃後，於民國76年（1987年）遷至現址。2024年，內甕轉型為嘉義縣第二所公辦民營KIST實驗小學，並啟用斥資近新台幣一億元興建的第三代校舍。校訓「耕讀內甕 自信有品」，展現學校在專題式學習、AI素養與環境閱讀教育上的用心。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏗️ A Brand-New Third-Generation Campus

第三代校舍啟用

The school's current campus building, opened in June 2024 at a cost of nearly NT$100 million, is the third generation of buildings the school has occupied since its 1987 relocation.

🎓 A KIST Charter-Style Transformation

公辦民營實驗學校

In 2024, Neiweng converted into a KIST (公辦民營) experimental education school, becoming the second such public-private partnership school in Chiayi County.

🌊 Built Beside a Reservoir

緊鄰仁義潭水庫

The construction of Renyi Reservoir literally forced the school to relocate in 1987, permanently tying its history to the reservoir's water-resource geography.

🌱 Project-Based, AI-Literate Learning

PBL與AI素養並重

Under the motto "cultivate learning, build confidence and character," the school emphasizes project-based learning, AI literacy, and environmental and reading education.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Life beside Renyi Reservoir.

💧

Located beside Renyi Reservoir (仁義潭) in Fanlu Township's Neiweng area, the school's history is inseparable from the reservoir's construction, which forced the original campus to relocate to its present site. That direct link between the school and the surrounding water-resource geography still shapes how the community understands its own local landscape.
