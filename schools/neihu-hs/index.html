內湖高級中學

About · 學校簡介

Built before its own building was ready.

Taipei Municipal Neihu Senior High School was established on August 1, 1988, becoming the first senior high school that Taipei City founded directly after it was elevated to special-municipality status. The city selected the school's Wende Road site in 1986, and Lin Hui, previously principal of Jieshou Junior High School, served as its first principal. When the first cohort of 18 classes enrolled in September 1988, the campus itself was not yet finished, so students spent their first year borrowing classroom space from neighboring Sanmin Junior High and Xihu Junior High before moving onto the completed Neihu campus in September 1989.

內湖高中創立於民國77年（1988年）8月1日，是臺北市升格為直轄市後首度直接創辦的高級中學。臺北市於民國75年（1986年）選定文德路現址作為校地，首任校長林煇原為介壽國中校長。民國77年9月首屆18班學生入學時，校舍尚未完工，學生們曾借用鄰近的三民國中與西湖國中教室上課一年，直到民國78年（1989年）9月才遷入完工的內湖校區。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Growing up with a fast-urbanizing district.

🏙️

Neihu Senior High was founded the same decade that Neihu was rapidly converting from farmland and marshland into a formal Taipei district with its own dedicated network of secondary schools. The fact that its own building was still unfinished when its first students arrived is a small, vivid snapshot of just how quickly the district was urbanizing in the late 1980s.
