About · 學校簡介

The oldest school in the district, named for a historic uprising.

Yujing Elementary School traces its roots to January 12, 1906, when it was established as the six-year “Tapani Public School” (噍吧哖公學校); classes began that April 2 in a borrowed local temple. Its origins stretch back further still, to a 1898 opening ceremony for a National Language Practice Institute branch in the Tapani area. The school was renamed several times through the 20th century — “Yujing Public School” in 1921, then through further renamings until it became “Yujing Elementary School” in 1968 under nine-year compulsory education. Designated a model school by the former Tainan County Government in 1958, it today serves about 405 students across 25 classes, including special education, with 48 teachers and 17 staff drawing from eight villages across Yujing District.

玉井國小創校於明治39年（1906年）1月12日，初設六年制「噍吧哖公學校」，同年4月2日借用地方廟宇開學上課，校史更可追溯至1898年噍吧哖地區「國語傳習所」分教場的開設典禮。歷經多次更名：1921年改稱「玉井公學校」，1941年「玉井西國民學校」，1946年「玉井鄉第一國民學校」，1947年「玉井國民學校」，1968年九年國教實施後定名「玉井國民小學」。1958年獲前台南縣政府評選為示範學校。現有學生約405人（含幼兒園）、25班（含特教班），教師48人、職員17人，學區涵蓋玉井區八個里。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 The Oldest School in Yujing District

玉井區最古老學校

Tracing back to 1906 (with roots to a 1898 ceremony), Yujing Elementary is the district's oldest school, founded under the name “Tapani Public School.”

📜 A Name Tied to History

校名承載歷史記憶

The school's original name, 噍吧哖 (Tapani), is the historic name of Yujing tied to the 1915 Tapani Incident, a major anti-colonial uprising — making the school's century-plus history inseparable from the town's own identity.

🏅 A Model School Since 1958

1958年獲選示範學校

Designated a model school by the former Tainan County Government in 1958, the school has long held a leading role among the district's institutions.

🗺️ Serving Eight Villages

服務全區八里

With about 405 students, 48 teachers, and 25 classes including special education, the school draws children from eight villages spread across Yujing District.
