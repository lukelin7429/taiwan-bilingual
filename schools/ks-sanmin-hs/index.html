Sanmin Senior High School

高雄市立三民高級中學

A relatively young public senior high school built around elite athletics — its baseball program has sent roughly thirty players into the professional ranks, including MLB pitcher Tseng Jen-ho.

About · 學校簡介

A young school, a fast-built athletics reputation.

Sanmin Senior High School opened in July 1996 with an inaugural intake of ten classes, making it one of the newer public senior highs in Kaohsiung's Sanmin District. Within a year of opening, the school founded a baseball team that has gone on to produce roughly thirty professional players, including MLB pitcher Tseng Jen-ho, alongside elite classes in tennis, fencing, and golf. (It is a separate institution from the similarly named senior high school in New Taipei City.)

高雄市立三民高級中學創校於民國85年（1996年）7月，首屆招收十個班級，是三民區較晚設立的公立高中之一。創校隔年（1997年）即成立棒球隊，至今已培育出近三十位職業選手，包括旅美投手曾仁和，此外也設有網球、擊劍、高爾夫球等體育菁英班。（與新北市立三民高級中學為不同的學校。）

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚾ A Pipeline to the Pros

職棒選手搖籃

Founded in 1997, just a year after the school itself, the baseball team has produced roughly thirty professional players — most notably MLB pitcher Tseng Jen-ho.

🤺 Elite Sport Beyond Baseball

網球、擊劍、高爾夫菁英班

Alongside baseball, the school runs dedicated classes for tennis, fencing, and golf, giving student-athletes a range of paths into competitive sport.

🏔️ Rooted in Fudingjin

覆鼎金地名淵源

The campus sits in the historic 覆鼎金 (Fudingjin) area, sharing its "鼎金" place-name root with several other schools in this part of Sanmin District.

🎓 Ten Classes to a Full Campus

從十班起家

Opening with just ten classes in 1996, the school built its identity and reputation quickly, largely on the strength of its athletics programs.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Sanmin District's "overturned tripod" hill.

🏔️

The school sits in the 覆鼎金 (Fudingjin) area, named for a hill said to resemble an overturned bronze tripod. Several other schools on this list — including the Dingjin schools — carry the same "鼎金" place-name root. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
