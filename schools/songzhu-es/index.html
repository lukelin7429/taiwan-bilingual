Songzhu Elementary School

松竹國民小學

One of Beitun's oldest schools, born in 1904 as a temple classroom and now blending coding clubs with lion-dance and diabolo teams.

北屯區歷史最悠久的學校之一，1904年源起於廟宇教室，如今兼具程式設計與舞獅、扯鈴等傳統技藝社團。

About · 學校簡介

From a temple classroom to a modern campus.

Songzhu Elementary's history stretches back to 1904, when it began as a branch classroom of Dayan Public School held inside the Sizhangli Wenchang Temple. It was renamed Sanfenpu Public School in 1913, then Beitun Public School in 1921, before finally taking its current name in 1968. Today it is one of the oldest and largest schools in this Beitun batch, with roughly 55 classes and about 1,469 students, blending traditional folk sports with modern digital-age clubs.

松竹國小的歷史可追溯至1904年，最初是大雅公學校的分教場，設於四張犁文昌廟內上課。1913年改名三份埔公學校，1921年再改名北屯公學校，1968年正式定名為現今校名。如今學校規模為北屯區這批學校中歷史最悠久、規模最大者之一，約55班、1,469名學生，融合傳統民俗技藝與現代數位社團。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Born Inside a Local Temple

起源於文昌廟教室

The school's earliest classes in 1904 were held inside the Sizhangli Wenchang Temple, tying its origins directly to one of the district's most historic religious sites.

💻 Coding Meets Tradition

程式設計與傳統技藝並行

Students take part in unplugged coding and Scratch programming alongside AI morning clubs, while also training in badminton, table tennis, abacus, diabolo, lion dance, and track.

🏫 A Century of Renaming

歷經多次更名

Over six decades the school carried three different names — Sanfenpu, Beitun, and finally Songzhu in 1968 — a naming history that traces Taiwan's shifting eras of administration.

🦁 A Large, Active Campus

約55班大型校園

With roughly 55 classes and nearly 1,500 students, Songzhu ranks among the larger elementary schools profiled in this batch of Beitun schools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in the district's oldest place-name.

⛩️

Its roots as a 1904 branch classroom held inside the Sizhangli Wenchang Temple (四張犁文昌廟) tie the school directly to the district's oldest place-name and folk-religious heritage, a rare living link to pre-colonial Beitun. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
