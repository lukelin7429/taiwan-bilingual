About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school in tea country.

Mingjian Zhongshan Elementary School serves families across Mingjian Township, part of Taiwan's largest tea-producing region. As a community elementary school, it provides local children with their foundational education close to home, within a township renowned for the Songbolang evergreen tea gardens that surround its villages.

中山國小服務名間鄉各聚落的家庭，該鄉是台灣最大的茶葉產區。作為一所社區型國小，學校讓當地孩童能就近接受基礎教育，座落於以松柏嶺常青茶園聞名的鄉鎮。
