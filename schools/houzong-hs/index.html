Taichung Municipal Houzong High School

臺中市立后綜高級中學

A combined junior-and-senior high with nearly 90 years of agricultural-vocational roots in Houli's farming heartland.

About · 學校簡介

One school, two divisions, nine decades of roots.

Houzong High School traces its origins to 1935, during the Japanese colonial era, when it opened as Houli Agricultural Citizens' School. It was reorganized as a complete school spanning both junior high and senior high divisions in 1999, taking its current name. Note: this school is a single institution combining junior high and senior high under one administration — with roots in agricultural-vocational education dating back nearly 90 years, reflecting Houli's long identity as a farming and orchard district.

后綜高中創校可追溯至日治時期的1935年，初創時為「后里農業公民學校」。學校於民國88年（1999年）改制為橫跨國中與高中部的完全中學，並確立現今校名。提醒：本校為單一行政體制下同時設有國中部與高中部的完全中學。其近90年的農業職業教育根基，反映了后里長期以來作為農業與果園之鄉的地方特色。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 Nearly 90 Years of Agricultural Roots

近90年農業教育根基

Tracing back to 1935 as Houli Agricultural Citizens' School, the school's vocational-agricultural origins run nearly 90 years deep, tied to the district's farming economy.

🏫 A Complete School Since 1999

1999年改制完全中學

In 1999, the school reorganized into a complete school spanning both junior high and senior high divisions, grades 7 through 12, under one administration.

🍊 Rooted in Houli's Orchard Economy

扎根后里果園經濟

The school's agricultural-vocational beginnings mirror Houli's historical identity as a farming and orchard district in central Taiwan.

📛 One School, Two Divisions

一校兩部

Like several complete schools in Taiwan, Houzong combines junior high and senior high under a single institution, sometimes listed separately in directories despite being one school.
