About · 學校簡介

A school with a history we could not fully confirm.

Xingzhong Elementary School is located in Gaozhong Village (高中里) in Kaohsiung's mountainous Taoyuan District, an area home to a Hla'alua (拉阿魯哇族) Indigenous community according to Indigenous-affairs sources. We were unable to confirm the school's founding date or most operational details — available research shows only its address, along with a related complication: sources on nearby Jianshan Elementary School describe a branch campus called 興中分班 (Xingzhong Branch) that was merged into Jianshan in 1970 and ceased to exist separately, yet a school by this same name serves the community today. No source we found explains how or when the present-day Xingzhong Elementary was established or re-established, so this page presents only what could be confirmed and does not assert a continuous history bridging that gap.

興中國小位於高雄市桃源區高中里，根據原住民族相關資料，當地為拉阿魯哇族社群所在地。我們未能確認學校的創校年份與大部分營運資訊——目前查得的資料僅有校址；另外，鄰近建山國小的相關史料提到，1970年一處名為「興中分班」的分校併入建山國小、不再獨立存在，但今日社區中仍有一所同名的「興中國小」在運作。目前查無資料說明現今興中國小的成立或重新設立經過，因此本頁僅呈現可確認之資訊，不對此段歷史空白做出推論性的連續性敘述。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏔️ A Small School in Gaozhong Village

位於高中里

Xingzhong Elementary serves Gaozhong Village (高中里) in the mountains of Kaohsiung's Taoyuan District, one of the smaller communities along the South Cross-Island Highway corridor.

🪶 Home to a Hla'alua Community

拉阿魯哇族社群所在地

According to Indigenous-affairs sources, Gaozhong Village is home to a Hla'alua (拉阿魯哇族) community — one of Taiwan's officially recognized Indigenous peoples, concentrated in this part of Kaohsiung's mountains.

📋 A History We Could Not Fully Confirm

校史尚待查證

Available sources leave gaps in this school's history, including its founding date and its exact relationship to an earlier same-named branch campus that other records describe as merged away in 1970. This page presents only what could be confirmed, honestly.
