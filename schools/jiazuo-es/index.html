About · 學校簡介

A century witness, by local reporting.

Jiazuo Elementary School is designated a Hakka-language feature school and runs an attached kindergarten. A PeoPo citizen-journalism feature titled "百年見證/佳佐國小" ("100-Year Witness") suggests the school may be over a century old, though an exact founding date has not been confirmed. The school also houses a branch of the Wanluan Township public library.

佳佐國小獲指定為客語特色學校，並附設幼兒園。公民新聞平台PeoPo一篇題為「百年見證／佳佐國小」的報導，暗示學校校齡可能已逾百年，惟確切創校年份尚待考證。校內亦設有萬巒鄉立圖書館佳佐分館。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Hakka heritage and betel-nut country.

🐷

Wanluan Township is a Hakka township famous nationwide for its pig's-knuckle (豬腳) cuisine and its betel-nut farming heritage, giving Jiazuo Elementary's Hakka-language designation a natural home in the surrounding community. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
