About · 學校簡介

A campus that wears its culture openly.

Bihou Elementary was founded in 1911 during the Japanese colonial era as the "Bihou Aboriginal Education Institute." Today, over 90% of both faculty and students are Atayal Indigenous. The campus architecture features Atayal totem motifs — diamond shapes, blue waves, a watchtower-style spire, and an interlocking peaked roof — making the buildings themselves a form of cultural expression. The school is also home to the Bihou Atayal Dance Troupe.

碧候國小創立於日治時期民國前1年（1911年），初名「碧候蕃童教育所」。如今師生逾九成為泰雅族原住民。校園建築融入泰雅族圖騰意象——菱形紋、藍色波浪、瞭望塔造型尖頂、以及交疊式人字屋頂——使建築本身成為一種文化展現。學校也是碧候泰雅舞蹈團的所在地。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Architecture as Cultural Expression

建築融入泰雅圖騰

The campus buildings incorporate Atayal diamond patterns, blue wave motifs, a watchtower-style spire, and an interlocking peaked roof — making the architecture itself a cultural statement.

🧡 A Community of Over 90% Atayal

師生逾九成泰雅族

With more than 90% of both students and faculty identifying as Atayal Indigenous, Bihou functions as a genuine community school for the local tribe.

💃 The Bihou Atayal Dance Troupe

碧候泰雅舞蹈團

The school hosts its own dedicated dance troupe, giving students a structured, ongoing outlet for performing and preserving Atayal dance traditions.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A symbolic home on the alluvial plain.

🏔️

Nestled at the foot of the mountains on the Nan'ao alluvial plain, Bihou is one of the most architecturally symbolic Atayal schools, with its very buildings encoding Atayal cultural motifs.
