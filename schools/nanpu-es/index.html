About · 學校簡介

Over a century on Feiyan Mountain.

Nanpu Elementary School opened in 1915, in Taiwan's Japanese colonial era, as a satellite classroom of Sanwan Public School serving "Da-Nanpu." It became the independent "Da-Nanpu Public School" in 1922, was renamed a national school in 1941, and took its current name, Nanpu Elementary School, in 1968. The school's original catchment area was administratively transferred from Sanwan to Nanzhuang Township in 1921. Today, with over a century of history, the school sits on "Feiyan Mountain" (飛燕山) in Nanpu village, an area known for its scenic terraced rice paddies and eco-tourism.

南埔國小創校於1915年（大正4年）日治時期，最初為三灣公學校的「大南埔」分教場。1922年獨立設校為「大南埔公學校」，1941年改制為國民學校，1968年（民國57年）正式更名為南埔國民小學。學校原本的招生範圍在1921年由三灣行政劃分至南庄鄉。如今，這所歷史逾百年的學校座落於南埔村的「飛燕山」，該地以優美的梯田景觀與生態旅遊聞名。
