Yunhai Elementary School

雲海國小

Named for the "sea of clouds" that rolls through this mountain village on the old Beiyi Highway, one of Shiding's most scenic school sites.

因高地雲海奇景而得名，座落於北宜公路上的坑內山村，是石碇風景最獨特的校址之一。

About · 學校簡介

Named for a mountain weather phenomenon.

Yunhai Elementary School began in 1920 as a detached classroom of Shiding Public School (石碇公學校小格頭分離教室), located in the mountain village of Getou (小格頭) on the old Beiyi Highway. The school takes its name directly from the "sea of clouds" (雲海) phenomenon famously visible here during seasonal weather changes, and in 1997 it absorbed Yong'an Elementary School as a branch campus.

雲海國小起源於1920年，最初為「石碇公學校小格頭分離教室」，位於北宜公路上的小格頭山村。校名直接取自此地在季節交替時節可見的著名「雲海」奇景，民國86年（1997年）更將永安國小併入，成為其分校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

☁️ Named for a Mountain Weather Phenomenon

因雲海奇景得名

The school's name commemorates the striking "sea of clouds" visible in this mountain village during seasonal weather changes, a phenomenon unique to Shiding's high terrain.

🛣️ On the Historic Beiyi Highway

座落北宜公路

Located in the mountain village of Getou (小格頭), the school sits directly on the old Beiyi Highway, the historic road linking Taipei to Yilan through Shiding's mountains.

🤝 Absorbing a Neighboring School

併入永安國小

In 1997, Yunhai Elementary took on Yong'an Elementary School as a branch campus, consolidating education for this sparsely populated mountain area.

🏫 A Century of Mountain Village Education

山村教育逾百年

Tracing its roots to 1920 as a detached classroom, the school has anchored education in this remote highland community for over a century.
