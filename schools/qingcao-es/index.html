Qingcao Elementary School

青草國民小學

A quiet, green rural school beside the mouth of the Zengwen River, established in 1981 and surrounded by farmland and birdlife.

1981年設校，座落於曾文溪出海口附近的寧靜綠色校園，四周環繞農田與豐富鳥類生態。

About · 學校簡介

A green campus at the river's mouth.

Qingcao Elementary broke ground in August 1981, in the northwest corner of Tucheng, right beside the mouth of the Zengwen River (曾文溪), surrounded by farmland. The school takes its name from neighboring Qingcao Village, flanked by Shalun Community to the east and Qingcao Village to the west, and official materials describe it as a quiet, green campus with abundant birdlife amid the surrounding farmland.

青草國小於1981年（民國70年）8月動工興建，位於土城西北角、曾文溪出海口附近，四周環繞農田。校名取自鄰近的青草里，東鄰砂崙社區、西接青草里。校方資料形容此校為一所寧靜、綠意盎然、鳥類生態豐富的校園，坐落於一片農田之中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🐦 Abundant Birdlife

豐富的鳥類生態

Surrounded by farmland near the Zengwen River estuary, the campus is described in official materials as home to abundant birdlife.

🌊 At the Mouth of the Zengwen River

座落於曾文溪出海口

The school sits right beside where the Zengwen River meets the sea, part of Annan's coastal estuarine landscape.

🌾 A Farmland Setting

農田環繞的校園

Flanked by Shalun Community and Qingcao Village, the school's quiet, green setting is defined by the surrounding agricultural land.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where the river meets the sea.

🌊

Sitting where the Zengwen River meets the sea, the school is close to Annan's estuarine wetlands and fish-farming zones, part of the same coastal ecosystem that includes the Sicao mangrove tunnels further south. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
