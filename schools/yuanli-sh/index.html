Yuanli Senior High School

苗栗縣立苑裡高級中學

A county-run comprehensive senior high with over 90 years of history, tracing back to a Japanese-era agricultural vocational school in the heart of Taiwan's rush-weaving country.

About · 學校簡介

A 90-year lineage, from agricultural school to comprehensive high.

Yuanli Senior High School traces its roots back to 1932, when it was established under Japanese rule as Shinchiku Prefecture Yuanli Agricultural Vocational School. Over more than nine decades it has passed through multiple name and status changes, reflecting the shifting shape of Taiwan's education system across the late colonial period and the postwar decades. It became a comprehensive senior high school — with both an academic and a vocational track, plus a junior-high division — in 1997, and was further reorganized as a regular senior high with vocational programs and a junior-high division in 2019.

苗栗縣立苑裡高級中學的校史可追溯至1932年（昭和7年），創校時為新竹州苑裡農業職業學校，歷經數次改制與更名，走過九十餘年歲月。民國86年（1997年）改制為兼具普通與職業類科的完全中學，並設有國中部；民國108年（2019年）再度調整為設有職業類科與國中部的普通型高級中學。

Note: Yuanli Senior High School (苗栗縣立苑裡高級中學, this page) is a separate institution from the similarly named "National Yuanli Senior High School" (ylsh.mlc.edu.tw) — the two should not be confused.

提醒：苗栗縣立苑裡高級中學（本頁介紹）與另一所校名相近的「國立苑裡高級中學」（ylsh.mlc.edu.tw）為不同學校，請勿混淆。

🏫 Over 90 Years of School History

🌾 Heart of Rush-Weaving Country

Local Culture · 在地文化

Yuanli, home of rush-weaving craft.

🌾

Yuanli Township is known nationwide for its rush-weaving handicrafts (藺草編織), a Hakka and Hoklo tradition of weaving the local sanjiao rush into hats and mats. As the county-run senior high at the center of the township, Yuanli Senior High shares its neighborhood with this centuries-old craft heritage. See the Miaoli county page for more on the region.
