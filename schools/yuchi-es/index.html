About · 學校簡介

The township's namesake school, 120 years strong.

Yuchi Elementary School, founded around 1904, is one of the oldest schools in Yuchi Township and recently celebrated its 120th anniversary — a milestone reflecting its deep roots as the township seat's namesake school. Beyond academics, Yuchi Elementary operates an attached kindergarten serving the youngest children in the community, and its students have brought home medals in shot put and other events at the county athletic meet.

魚池國小創立於約1904年，是魚池鄉歷史最悠久的學校之一，近年慶祝建校120週年——見證其作為鄉治所在地代表學校的深厚根基。除了學術教育，魚池國小附設幼兒園，服務社區內最年幼的孩子，學生也曾在縣運動會的鉛球等項目中獲得獎牌。

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the center of tea country.

🍃

As the township seat's namesake school, Yuchi Elementary sits at the center of Yuchi's world-renowned Sun Moon Lake assam black tea growing region, a landscape that has shaped the local economy and culture for generations. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
