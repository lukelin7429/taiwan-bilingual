Qiao'ai Elementary School

僑愛國小

Built on land donated by local gentry in 1957, with a name reflecting mid-20th-century resettlement history along the Bade-Daxi corridor.

校地由地方仕紳於1957年捐贈，校名見證二十世紀中葉的僑民安置歷史，位於連結八德與大溪的介壽路。

About · 學校簡介

Land donated by local gentry.

Qiao'ai Elementary began in August 1957 as “Bade School Qiao'ai Branch” (renamed Yuanshulin Public School Qiao'ai Branch the same year), becoming independent in August 1959 as Qiao'ai Public School. The land for the school was donated by local gentry in October 1957, and the new school building was completed in June 1958. The school is listed in Taiwan's Indigenous Peoples reference encyclopedia, reflecting a notable Indigenous-student community.

僑愛國小於1957年（民國46年）8月設立為「八德國校僑愛分校」，同年改隸為員樹林國民學校僑愛分校，1959年（民國48年）8月獨立設校，定名為僑愛國民學校。校地由地方仕紳於1957年10月捐贈，新校舍於1958年6月落成。學校亦收錄於臺灣原住民族百科全書，顯示校內有一定規模的原住民學生族群。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Land donated by local gentry.

🛣️

Qiao'ai Elementary sits on 介壽路, a main corridor linking Bade and Daxi districts, and its name — roughly “Overseas Love/Loyalty” — reflects mid-20th-century resettlement history in the area, when new communities were being established across this part of Taoyuan.
