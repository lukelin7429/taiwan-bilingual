Fuhe Junior High School

福和國民中學

Founded to relieve an overcrowded Yonghe Junior High, Fuhe opened as a girls-only school before returning to co-ed enrollment in 1993.

為紓解永和國中人數過多而創立，創校初期為女子學校，1993年起恢復男女合校。

About · 學校簡介

Born from a 1970s population boom.

Fuhe Junior High was founded in 1979, with its first classes beginning in September 1980, as Yonghe's population grew rapidly through the 1970s. It opened as a girls-only school before returning to co-ed enrollment in 1993, and it shares the same odd/even household-ID district split with neighboring Yonghe Junior High.

福和國中創立於民國68年（1979年），首屆學生於民國69年（1980年）9月入學，正值永和地區人口於1970年代快速成長之時。創校初期為女子學校，民國82年（1993年）起恢復男女合校，並與鄰近的永和國中共同以身分證字號單雙數劃分學區。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

👧 Originally a Girls-Only School

創校初期為女校

Fuhe opened in 1980 as a girls-only junior high before returning to co-ed enrollment in 1993, mirroring the same shift seen at neighboring Yonghe Junior High.

📈 Founded to Relieve a Population Boom

紓解人口壓力而設

The school's 1979 founding directly answered Yonghe's rapid 1970s population growth, which had outgrown the district's first junior high within a decade.

🔢 Sharing an ID-Number District Split

共用單雙號學區制度

Like Yonghe Junior High, Fuhe draws its students by the last digit of their household-registration ID number — a shared local zoning arrangement between the two schools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A second school for a booming bedroom town.

🏘️

Fuhe's founding directly reflects 1970s Yonghe's population boom as a cross-river bedroom community for Taipei, which outgrew its first junior high within a decade. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
