About · 學校簡介

A name marking the old town's northern edge.

Zhenbei Elementary School's name, "鎮北" ("north of the town"), reflects its position on the northern edge of the historic walled town of Fengshan. The school today comprises 29 classes in total: 23 regular classes, four kindergarten classes, one special-education class, and one itinerant preschool outreach class — a comprehensive range of programs for a single campus.

鎮北國小校名「鎮北」意指座落於鳳山古城北側。學校現有29班，包括23個普通班、4個幼兒園班、1個特教班，以及1個巡迴輔導學前班——在單一校園中提供相當完整的班別規劃。
