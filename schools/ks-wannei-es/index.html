About · 學校簡介

A river-bend village school, freshly rebuilt.

Wannei Elementary opened on April 20, 1953, as a branch of Renwu Elementary School, serving the village of Wannei — a name meaning "inside the bend," referencing a bend in a local waterway. In January 2022, the school relocated to a brand-new campus that combines childcare, kindergarten, and elementary facilities in one complex, serving around 354 students.

灣內國小創立於1953年（民國42年）4月20日，最初為仁武國小分校，服務「灣內」——意指水道彎曲之處——一帶的學童。2022年1月，學校遷入一座全新校園，將托嬰、幼兒園與國小整合於同一園區，目前約有354名學生。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A name shaped by the water.

🌊

The village of Wannei (灣內, "inside the bend") takes its name from a bend in a local waterway, a common naming pattern for early Taiwanese agricultural settlements built along rivers and irrigation channels. See the Kaohsiung County/City page for more on the region.
