Chenggong Elementary School

成功國民小學

A Huxi Township school whose village and name changed together after the completion of a local reservoir.

About · 學校簡介

A school that grew with its village.

Chenggong Elementary was founded in November 1964, originally named "Gangdi National School," to serve children of Gangdi Village who previously had to travel far to attend Shagang Elementary School. It was renamed "Gangdi National Elementary School" in 1968, then took its current name, "Chenggong Elementary," in 1974 — the same year Gangdi Village itself was renamed Chenggong Village following completion of the Chenggong Reservoir. Enrollment has declined over time, from 217 students in 1967 to about 49 students by 2008.

成功國小創立於民國53年（1964年）11月，初名「港底國民學校」，設校目的是服務港底村的學童，過去他們須遠赴沙港國小就讀。民國57年（1968年）改名「港底國民小學」，民國63年（1974年）隨著港底村因成功水庫完工而更名為成功村，學校也同步改名為「成功國民小學」。在學人數逐年減少，從民國56年（1967年）的217人降至民國97年（2008年）約49人。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏞️ Named After a Reservoir Milestone

因水庫完工而更名

The school and its village were both renamed Chenggong in 1974 following completion of the Chenggong Reservoir — a mid-century water-infrastructure milestone for the community.

🚸 Founded to Shorten Students' Commute

為縮短學童通學距離而設校

The school was established so that children of Gangdi Village no longer had to travel far to attend Shagang Elementary School.

🏘️ A Village and School's Shared History

村校共同的歷史軌跡

The renaming of both village and school in the same year reflects how closely this small elementary school is woven into its community's story.
