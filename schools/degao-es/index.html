About · 學校簡介

A farmland school with a baseball tradition.

Degao Elementary opened in 1950 as a branch of Guanshan Elementary School, becoming fully independent in 1954. The school serves a community of mostly Indigenous and Hakka farming families living between Guanshan Town and neighboring Haiduan Township. Over the years, it has built a strong reputation for its baseball education program, a sport with deep roots throughout Taitung County's Rift Valley communities.

德高國小成立於民國39年（1950年），最初為關山國小分校，民國43年（1954年）獨立設校。學校服務關山鎮與鄰近海端鄉之間，以原住民與客家農村家庭為主的社區。多年來，學校在棒球教育方面建立了良好聲譽，這項運動在臺東縱谷各社區有著深厚的根基。

The school's student body reflects the mixed Indigenous and Hakka farming heritage of the flat farmland between Guanshan and Haiduan.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Guanshan Town, the bicycle-loop capital of the valley.

🚴

Degao sits on flat farmland at the northern edge of Guanshan Town 關山鎮, part of the rice-growing plain that lines the township's famous bicycle loop route — one of Taiwan's most scenic cycling paths.
