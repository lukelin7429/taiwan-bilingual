Lijia Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

Music and forest trails in Likavung village.

Lijia Elementary serves Lijia village in Beinan Township, whose traditional name, Likavung, reflects the area's Puyuma Indigenous heritage. Since 1999 the school has held an annual on-campus music concert, a tradition now running for more than two decades that has become a fixture of community life. The village itself is known beyond the school as the gateway to the Lijia Forest Trail, a popular hiking and nature destination that draws visitors from across Taitung to the scenic hills surrounding the campus.

利嘉國小服務卑南鄉利嘉村，其傳統地名「Likavung」反映出此地深厚的卑南族原住民淵源。自1999年起，學校每年舉辦校園音樂會，這項傳統已延續超過二十年，成為社區生活的重要一環。利嘉村本身也因鄰近知名的利嘉林道而聞名，這條熱門的健行步道吸引全台東各地遊客造訪校園周邊的優美山林。
