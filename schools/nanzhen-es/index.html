Nanzhen Elementary School

南鎮國民小學

A public elementary school in Tianwei Township with a national Reading Rock Award and an eight-year recorder-ensemble winning streak.

座落於田尾鄉的公立小學，曾獲教育部「閱讀磐石獎」，直笛隊更蟬聯八年縣賽冠軍。

About · 學校簡介

A reading champion in southern Tianwei.

Nanzhen Elementary was established in 1949 and celebrated its 70th anniversary around 2018, serving generations of children in Nanzhen Village, Tianwei Township. The school has built a strong reputation for reading education, earning the Ministry of Education's national "Reading Rock" Award, while also excelling in music and sport — its recorder ensemble has won the county championship eight years running, and its sixth-graders have taken county titles in soft korfball.

南鎮國小創校於民國38年（1949年），並於約2018年舉辦建校七十週年慶祝活動，長年服務田尾鄉南鎮村的學童。學校在閱讀教育方面表現亮眼，曾獲教育部「閱讀磐石獎」的肯定，音樂與體育也同樣出色——直笛隊已連續八年蟬聯縣賽冠軍，六年級學生更在軟式合球項目奪得縣冠軍。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📖 A National Reading Rock Award

教育部閱讀磐石獎

Nanzhen Elementary has been recognized with the Ministry of Education's "Reading Rock" Award, a national honor for outstanding school reading programs.

🎼 Eight Straight Years of Champions

直笛隊八連霸

The school's recorder ensemble has won the county double championship for eight consecutive years — a remarkable streak for a small-town elementary school.

🏐 County Korfball Champions

合球縣冠軍

Sixth-graders at Nanzhen have won county championships in soft korfball in both the boys' and girls' divisions, reflecting a strong all-round sports program.

🏘️ Seven Decades in Nanzhen Village

深耕南鎮村七十載

Founded in 1949 and marking its 70th anniversary in 2018, the school has been the educational anchor of Nanzhen Village for generations of local families.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The village south of old Zhenping.

🏞️

The school takes its name from Nanzhen Village 南鎮村, whose name comes from lying south ("南") of the old settlement of Zhenping — first settled by Hakka and Hoklo migrants from Guangdong and Fujian during the late Ming and early Qing era. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
