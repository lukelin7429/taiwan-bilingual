About · 學校簡介

A small school active well beyond the classroom.

Yuemei Elementary serves Yuemei Village in Guanshan Town, part of the township's broad rice-farming plain along the East Rift Valley. Despite its small size, the school is active in art competitions, language contests, and environmental education programs, giving students regular opportunities to compete and learn beyond the standard curriculum. It also runs a campus lunch platform and a student information portal to support daily school operations.

月眉國小服務關山鎮月眉村，位於臺東縱谷稻米產區的一部分。儘管規模不大，學校積極參與美術競賽、語文比賽與環境教育活動，讓學生在正規課程之外也能定期參與競賽與學習。學校並設有校園午餐平台與學生資訊系統，支援日常校務運作。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Guanshan Town, the bicycle-loop capital of the valley.

🚴

Yuemei Village sits within Guanshan Town's 關山鎮 rice-farming plain, part of the East Rift Valley landscape famous for its scenic bicycle loop path and long tradition of rice cultivation.
