About · 學校簡介

One of the district's youngest schools.

Dahu Elementary was established in 2002, making it one of the youngest elementary schools in Guishan District. It was built to serve the growing residential population of the Daping-ding (大坪頂) plateau area, and its core educational values — proactive, responsible, appreciative, and grateful — reflect a school culture built for a fast-growing new community. The campus sits near Dahu Memorial Park, known locally for its wisteria blooms and historic irrigation ponds (陂塘) characteristic of the Guishan plateau's farming past.

大湖國小創校於2002年，是龜山區校齡最年輕的國小之一，設校目的為服務大坪頂台地日益成長的新興社區。學校核心價值為「積極、負責、欣賞、感恩」，反映出為快速成長的新社區所打造的校園文化。校園鄰近大湖紀念公園，以紫藤花開與具龜山台地農業歷史特色的陂塘聞名。
