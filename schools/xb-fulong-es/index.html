About · 學校簡介

A beach-town school beside Taiwan's best-known shoreline festival.

Fulong Elementary became an independent school in 1956, growing out of a branch class that dated back to 1946. Located just steps from famous Fulong Beach, the school shares its neighborhood with the annual Ho-hai-yan Rock Festival and a well-known sand sculpture festival, both of which draw large crowds to the area each year.

福隆國小於民國45年（1956年）獨立設校，前身為1946年設立的分班。學校緊鄰知名的福隆海水浴場，鄰近每年舉辦的貢寮國際海洋音樂祭與沙雕藝術季，每年吸引大量遊客造訪這片海岸。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏖️ Steps from Fulong Beach

緊鄰福隆海水浴場

The school sits just steps from Fulong Beach, one of Taiwan's premier stretches of sand and its Northeast Coast's most recognized destination.

🎸 Neighbor to a Major Music Festival

音樂祭鄰校

Fulong Beach hosts the annual Ho-hai-yan Rock Festival, one of Taiwan's best-known outdoor music events, held right in the school's neighborhood.

🏗️ Independent Since 1956

1956年獨立設校

Growing out of a branch class dating to 1946, the school became fully independent in 1956 and has served this beach community for nearly seven decades since.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Living beside Taiwan's most famous beach.

🏄

The school's fortunes rise and fall with Fulong's identity as Taiwan's premier beach and surfing destination on the Northeast Coast, host each year to the Ho-hai-yan Rock Festival and a sand sculpture festival. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
