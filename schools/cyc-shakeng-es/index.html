Shakeng Elementary School

沙坑國民小學

A small remote-area school that grew from a single-class branch campus into an independent school with carefully preserved records of its own history.

About · 學校簡介

From a one-class branch to an independent school.

Shakeng Elementary began in September 1955 as a single-class branch of nearby Yiren Elementary School, expanding to three classes by 1957. It was approved as an independent school in August 1960, with a formal independence ceremony held in October 1963, and took its current name in 1968 under the nine-year compulsory education reform. Designated a remote-area school, Shakeng maintains a campus tree information platform, accessibility maps for campus safety, and detailed records of its past principals and school history.

沙坑國小前身為民國44年（1955年）9月設立、僅一班的「沙坑分班」（義仁國小分班），民國46年（1957年）擴增為三班；民國49年（1960年）8月1日獲准獨立設校，並於民國52年（1963年）10月30日舉行獨立設校典禮，民國57年（1968年）因應九年國教改制更為現名。學校被列為「偏遠地區」學校，設有校園樹木資訊平台與校園安全無障礙地圖，並詳細保存歷任校長與校史紀錄。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A hamlet school on the mountain approach.

🏘️

Shakeng is one of the small remote mountain hamlets scattered through Zhuqi's interior — communities that historically grew tea and farmed the foothills on the approach to Alishan. Shakeng Elementary represents this pattern of tiny, tightly-knit rural schools. See the Chiayi county page for more on the region.
