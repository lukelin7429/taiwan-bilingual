About · 學校簡介

A brand-new campus built for a booming science-park suburb.

Xiaoxin Elementary School's exact founding year is not confirmed in available records, but its recent history is well documented: on its historic site at Xiaoxin Li, the school was a small campus of about 56 students and staff across 6 classes on roughly 3,374 square meters. Rapid population inflow from the Shanhua-Nanke special zone, tied to the nearby Southern Taiwan Science Park, drove a NT$340.286-million relocation and rebuilding project, and the school reopened on a new campus in Liantan Li on April 27, 2021. Its curriculum now emphasizes reading, information technology, and international education, with plans to build out SDG and environmental education as a designated “soil and water conservation” seed school.

小新國小確切創校年份於現有資料中尚無法確認，但其近年發展脈絡清楚可考：原校址位於小新里，規模不大，約有學生教職員56人、6個班級，校地約3,374平方公尺。因鄰近南部科學園區、善化南科特定區（LM區）人口快速移入，學校進行了耗資新台幣3.4億286萬元的遷校重建工程，並於民國110年（2021年）4月27日正式遷入蓮潭里新校區。現行校本課程以閱讀、資訊科技與國際教育為主軸，並規劃納入SDGs與環境教育，發展為水土保持特色種子學校。
