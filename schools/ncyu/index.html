About · 學校簡介

Taiwan's first successful university merger.

National Chiayi University (NCYU) was formed on February 1, 2000, through the merger of National Chiayi Normal College (founded 1957) and National Chiayi Institute of Technology, whose agricultural and forestry-school roots trace back to 1919. The merger is cited as the first successful case of university consolidation in Taiwan's higher education history. Today NCYU operates seven colleges — Teacher Education, Humanities and Arts, Management, Agriculture, Engineering/Science, Life Sciences, and Veterinary Medicine — spanning 39 departments, 43 master's programs, and 7 doctoral programs across four campuses in and around Chiayi.

國立嘉義大學（NCYU）於民國89年（2000年）2月1日，由國立嘉義師範學院（創於1957年）與國立嘉義技術學院（其農林教育根源可追溯至1919年）合併成立，是台灣高等教育史上第一個成功合併的大學案例。現今嘉義大學設有師範學院、人文藝術學院、管理學院、農學院、工程/理學院、生命科學院、獸醫學院共七大學院，橫跨39個系所、43個碩士班與7個博士班，並在嘉義市周邊設有四個校區。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Seven Colleges

七大學院

NCYU is organized into seven colleges — Teacher Education, Humanities and Arts, Management, Agriculture, Engineering/Science, Life Sciences, and Veterinary Medicine — with 39 departments, 43 master's programs, and 7 doctoral programs.

🤝 Taiwan's First Successful University Merger

台灣首例大學合併

NCYU's 2000 formation, merging National Chiayi Normal College and National Chiayi Institute of Technology, is cited as the first successful case of university consolidation in Taiwan's higher education history.

🌾 Roots Going Back to 1919

溯源1919年

The university's agricultural and forestry education lineage traces back to 1919, giving NCYU one of the longer institutional histories among Taiwan's national universities.

🏫 Four Campuses Around Chiayi

四大校區

NCYU spans four campuses — Lantan, Linsen, Minsyong, and Sinmin — anchoring the university's presence across the Chiayi region, with over 113,000 alumni.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring Chiayi's East District academic community.

🎓

As Chiayi's only comprehensive national university, NCYU anchors the city's East District academic community and supplies teachers, agricultural specialists, and veterinarians serving the wider Chiayi region. See the Chiayi City page for more on the region.
