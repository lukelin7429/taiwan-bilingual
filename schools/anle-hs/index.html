Anle Senior High School

市立安樂高級中學

A public senior high school with roots as one of Keelung's original 1960s junior highs, now recognized nationally for its ocean-themed curriculum.

前身為基隆市1960年代創立的初級中學之一，如今以海洋主題課程聞名全國的公立高級中學。

About · 學校簡介

From junior high to a full high school, six decades on.

Anle Senior High traces its history to 1966, when it was founded as "Keelung Municipal 6th Junior High School." Under the nine-year compulsory education reform of 1968, it was renamed first Jiande and then Anle Junior High. The school grew into a full junior-and-senior high school by 1999–2000, taking its current name in November 2000. Today it is recognized nationally for a marine-education program that takes students out of the classroom and onto Keelung's coastline.

安樂高中的歷史可追溯至1966年（民國55年），創校時名為「基隆市立第六初級中學」。民國57年（1968年）九年國教改制後，先後更名為建德國中、安樂國中。學校於1999至2000年間發展為完全中學，並於2000年11月正式定名為現名。如今學校以海洋教育課程聞名全國，帶領學生走出教室、走向基隆的海岸線。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌊 An Award-Winning Ocean Curriculum

海洋教育課程獲全國銀質獎

The school's ocean-themed course won a national Teaching Excellence Silver Award, built around field study at Keelung's coastline and fishing communities.

🎣 Fieldwork at Badou and Daping

八斗子與大坪海岸實地學習

Students travel to the Badou fishing village and Daping coast to experience Keelung's maritime and fishing culture firsthand, rather than learning about it only from textbooks.

🏫 Six Decades of Institutional History

歷經改制的六十年校史

Founded in 1966 as a junior high, the school was renamed twice and upgraded to a full senior high school by 2000 — a history that mirrors Taiwan's own education reforms.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Learning from Keelung's working coastline.

⚓

Anle's flagship marine curriculum sends students directly into Badou fishing village and the Daping coast, engaging Keelung's harbor and fishing-community heritage as living classrooms rather than distant subject matter. See the Keelung county page for more on the region.
