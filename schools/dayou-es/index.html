Dayou Elementary School

大有國小

A rare individually-endowed public school, founded in 1962 through the philanthropy of a local doctor, Dr. Weng Dayou, for the farming community of Shitoucuo.

About · 學校簡介

A school built on one doctor's generosity.

Dayou Elementary School owes its founding to Dr. Weng Dayou, a local physician and philanthropist who donated funds to build a school for the Shitoucuo community, matched by government construction funding — a rare case of an individually-endowed public school in Taiwan. The first preparatory meeting was held March 15, 1962, with approval following in April; a two-story campus building was completed in June 1963, and the school opened its doors on September 1, 1963 under founding principal Chen Dingqi. Today the school's curriculum includes Hakka language instruction, a "Dayou Food and Agriculture" education program, and environmental and traffic safety education initiatives.

大有國小的創建，歸功於地方醫師暨慈善家翁大有醫師，他捐資為石頭厝社區興學，並獲政府配合撥款興建校舍——這在台灣是罕見由個人捐建的公立學校案例。民國51年（1962年）3月15日召開首次籌備會議，同年4月獲准設校；兩層樓校舍於民國52年（1963年）6月完工，學校於同年9月1日開學，首任校長為陳丁奇。現今學校課程包含客語教學、「大有食農」教育課程，以及環境教育與交通安全教育等推廣活動。
