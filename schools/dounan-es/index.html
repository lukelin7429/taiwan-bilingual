About · 學校簡介

Dounan's oldest and largest elementary school.

Dounan Elementary School traces its history to 1900, when it was founded as Talibu Public School under Japanese rule — making it one of Yunlin County's oldest schools. The campus preserves a 1945 Japanese-style dormitory, registered as a historic building in 2011, which now serves as the "Dounan Cultural Pavilion" for calligraphy and art exhibits. As the town's oldest and largest elementary school, it sits at the historic heart of Dounan.

斗南國小創校於明治33年（1900年），前身為「他里霧公學校」，是雲林縣歷史最悠久的學校之一。校內保存一棟1945年興建的日式宿舍，於2011年登錄為歷史建築，現作為「斗南文化館」，展示書法與藝術作品。作為斗南鎮歷史最悠久、規模最大的國小，學校座落於這座城鎮的歷史核心地帶。
