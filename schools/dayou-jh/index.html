Dayou Junior High School

桃園市立大有國民中學

A public junior high established in 2003 as Taoyuan District's growing population spilled into newer residential neighborhoods.

About · 學校簡介

Founded to serve a growing district.

Dayou Junior High's preparatory office was established on August 1, 2002, hosted at nearby Qingxi Junior High, before the school formally opened in 2003, admitting nine first-year classes. It was renamed to Taoyuan Municipal Dayou Junior High School in the December 2014 county-to-city reorganization. Like several other junior highs spun off from older schools via preparatory offices, Dayou was founded as part of a broader wave of new campuses built to serve Taoyuan District's expanding population through the 1990s and 2000s.

大有國中籌備處於民國91年（2002年）8月1日成立，暫設於鄰近的青溪國中，並於民國92年（2003年）正式設校，首屆招收九個班級。2014年12月隨桃園縣升格直轄市，正式更名為桃園市立大有國民中學。與其他多所自籌備處分出設立的國中相同，大有國中的創立是1990至2000年代桃園區人口成長、新校陸續設立浪潮的一部分。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Nine-Class Beginning

九班起家

Dayou opened in 2003 with nine first-year classes after two years as a preparatory office hosted at Qingxi Junior High, reflecting the careful planning behind Taoyuan's newer schools.

📈 Part of a Growth Wave

人口成長浪潮下的新校

The school is one of several Taoyuan District junior highs founded through preparatory offices in the 1990s and 2000s to keep pace with expanding residential neighborhoods.

🏙️ A Municipal-Era Rename

升格直轄市後更名

Renamed to Taoyuan Municipal Dayou Junior High School after the December 2014 county-to-city upgrade, marking its place in the city's modern administrative era.
