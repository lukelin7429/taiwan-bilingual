Jianshi Junior High School

尖石國民中學

The sole junior high school serving all of Jianshi Township — Hsinchu County's largest and most mountainous district — with an Atayal Experimental Education program at its core.

About · 學校簡介

One school, an entire mountainous township.

Jianshi Junior High opened in 1956 as a branch class of Hsinchu County Zhudong Junior High School, becoming fully independent around 1969–1970. Its current campus buildings were completed in June 2011. As the only junior high school in Jianshi Township — a district covering 528 square kilometers, roughly 37% of Hsinchu County's total land area, and home to about 9,500 residents, most of them Atayal Indigenous people — the school serves students from across the township's scattered mountain communities. It was designated an Indigenous-focus key school in 1998, and since 2013 has developed an Atayal-based curriculum that formally became the Atayal Experimental Education program in 2020.

尖石國中於1956年（民國45年）以「新竹縣竹東國中尖石分班」設立，約於1969至1970年間正式獨立設校，現有校舍於2011年6月完工啟用。作為尖石鄉唯一的國中，服務範圍涵蓋全鄉528平方公里、約占新竹縣總面積37%的廣大山區，鄉內約9,500名居民多為泰雅族原住民。學校自1998年起獲指定為原住民重點學校，自2013年發展泰雅族本位課程，並於2020年正式轉型為「泰雅族實驗教育」學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏔️ One School, 528 Square Kilometers

全鄉唯一國中·528平方公里學區

As Jianshi Township's only junior high, the school's district spans 528 square kilometers — about 37% of Hsinchu County's entire area — drawing students from across the mountains.

🌿 Atayal Experimental Education

泰雅族實驗教育

Since formalizing its Atayal Experimental Education program in 2020 — building on an Atayal-based curriculum developed from 2013 — the school has centered Atayal language, history, and culture in daily learning.

🏫 A Modern Campus in the Mountains

2011年落成新校舍

The school's current campus buildings were completed in June 2011, providing updated facilities for students traveling in from Jianshi's remote villages.

🎖️ A Long-Standing Indigenous-Focus School

原住民重點學校

Designated an Indigenous-focus key school since 1998, Jianshi Junior High has spent decades building programs tailored to its predominantly Atayal student body.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Jianshi, Hsinchu County's mountain heartland.

🦅

Jianshi Township (尖石鄉) is Hsinchu County's largest and most mountainous district, home to a predominantly Atayal Indigenous population spread across remote settlements including Smangus and Cinsbu. The school's experimental curriculum reflects a broader, deliberate effort across the township's schools to sustain Atayal language and cultural knowledge for the next generation.
