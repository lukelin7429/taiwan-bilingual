About · 學校簡介

Still known by its old name, "Qian-Dabu."

Dongyuan Elementary traces its roots to March 1915 (Taisho 4), when it opened as 番社庄前大埔公學校. It was renamed several times through the twentieth century — to 前大埔公學校 in 1922, 東原國民學校 in 1947, and formally reorganized as Tainan County Dongshan Township Dongyuan Elementary School in 1960. The school once operated a satellite branch campus at Nanshi (南勢分校) from 1956 to 1974, which closed for lack of enrollment. Marking 110 years of history around 2025, the school is still widely known locally by its old place name, "Qian-Dabu" (前大埔, "front big embankment").

東原國小校史可追溯至大正4年（1915年）3月設立的番社庄前大埔公學校。歷經多次更名：1922年改稱前大埔公學校、1947年改稱東原國民學校，1960年正式改組為台南縣東山鄉東原國民小學。學校曾於1956年至1974年間設有南勢分校，後因招生不足而裁撤。學校於2025年前後屆滿110週年校慶，至今地方上仍習慣以舊地名「前大埔」稱呼這所學校。
