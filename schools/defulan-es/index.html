About · 學校簡介

Restoring a place-name through community vote.

Defulan Elementary School traces its origins to 1916, when it opened under Japanese colonial rule as "Kyuryōsei Indigenous-Children's Education Post" (久良栖蕃童教育所). For decades afterward it was known as Bo'ai Elementary School. In September 2022, following a community vote, the school was renamed "德芙蘭" (Devilan), restoring the original Atayal place-name to a school whose Japanese-era and Mandarin names had, over the twentieth century, obscured its Indigenous identity. The school also operates a branch campus near the Guguan hot springs.

德芙蘭國小創校可追溯至1916年（日治時期），初名「久良栖蕃童教育所」。此後數十年間，學校一直以「博愛國小」之名為人所知。2022年9月，經部落公投決議，學校更名為「德芙蘭」，將原本的泰雅族地名恢復，找回這所歷經二十世紀日治與國語名稱雙重覆蓋、逐漸模糊的原住民身分認同。學校並在谷關溫泉區附近設有分校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗳️ A Name Restored by Community Vote

部落公投更名

In September 2022, the community voted to rename the school from Bo'ai to Defulan, explicitly restoring an Atayal place-name that had been set aside for decades.

🏔️ "A Place of Good Mountains and Water"

「山明水秀，適合人居」

According to the school's own stated rationale, "Devilan" is Atayal for a place with good mountains and good water, fit for people to live — the meaning behind the restored name.

♨️ A Branch Campus Near Guguan

谷關分校

The school operates a branch campus near the Guguan hot springs, extending its reach to families living further along the mountain valley.
