Beida Senior High School

北大高級中學

New Taipei's first public high school built around bilingual education, anchoring the fast-growing Beida university town.

About · 學校簡介

A new school built for a new town.

Beida Senior High traces its roots to 2010 as Longpu Junior High, becoming an independent senior high in 2013 and opening its own junior-high wing in 2015 — a complete 7-through-12 school. It was established as New Taipei's first public high school to emphasize bilingual education, and it now serves roughly 1,075 students across 30 classes. The campus is marked by its landmark "Kite Tower," whose mosaic tilework echoes the shape of the nearby Yanzhan mountain, and the school offers experimental language and math tracks that require entrance exams.

北大高中的前身可追溯至民國99年（2010年）成立的龍埔國中；民國102年（2013年）獨立設立高中部，民國104年（2015年）再增設國中部，成為完整的七至十二年級學校。學校是新北市第一所以雙語教育為特色的公立高中，目前約有1,075名學生、30個班級。校園以造型呼應鄰近鳶山山形的馬賽克拼貼地標「風箏塔」聞名，並設有需經入學考試甄選的語文與數理實驗班。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌐 New Taipei's First Bilingual-Focused High School

新北市首創雙語特色高中

Beida Senior High was established as New Taipei City's first public high school built around bilingual education, positioning it at the front edge of the region's language-learning push.

🪁 The Kite Tower Landmark

風箏塔地標建築

The campus's signature "Kite Tower" uses mosaic tilework that echoes the silhouette of the nearby Yanzhan mountain, giving the school a distinctive visual identity tied to its landscape.

📐 Competitive Experimental Tracks

語文與數理實驗班

Students can apply through entrance exams to specialized language and math experimental tracks, alongside the general curriculum across its complete 7–12 program.

🏫 A Complete 7–12 Campus

完整七到十二年級學校

Grown from a 2010 junior high into a full junior-and-senior-high campus of about 1,075 students in 30 classes, the school reflects the rapid growth of its surrounding district.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rising with the Beida university town.

🏙️

The school's rapid growth tracks the rise of the Beida (Taipei University) Special District, one of Sanxia's most transformative modern developments — a planned new town built around the campus of National Taipei University. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
