Qingxi Junior High School

桃園市立青溪國民中學

A public junior high founded in 1968 beside Nankan Creek and Tiger Head Mountain, recognized for strong music, PE, and science programs.

About · 學校簡介

A riverside, hillside campus with broad strengths.

Qingxi Junior High was founded on August 1, 1968. The school sits beside Nankan Creek (南崁溪) with Tiger Head Mountain (虎頭山) as its neighbor, and it is recognized for strong programs in music, physical education, industrial arts, science education, and Chinese-language education. It was renamed to Taoyuan Municipal in the December 2014 reorganization, and its preparatory-office role for several later schools — including Jianguo, Dayou, and others — reflects its long-standing place as one of the district's established junior highs.

青溪國中創立於民國57年（1968年）8月1日。校址緊鄰南崁溪，並以虎頭山為鄰，音樂、體育、工藝、科學教育與國語文教育表現出色，廣受肯定。2014年12月隨桃園縣升格直轄市，正式更名為桃園市立青溪國民中學。曾多次擔任建國、大有等後設國中的籌備處，體現其作為區內歷史悠久國中的地位。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎵 Strong Music and Arts Programs

音樂與工藝教育出色

Qingxi is recognized for its programs in music, industrial arts, and Chinese-language education, giving students a well-rounded curriculum beyond core academics.

🏃 A Physical Education Tradition

體育教育傳統

Alongside its arts strengths, the school is noted for strong physical education programming, part of a well-rounded educational identity.

🏫 A Founding Host for Newer Schools

孕育多所新設國中

Founded in 1968, Qingxi has hosted the preparatory offices of several younger junior highs in the district, underscoring its long institutional history.
