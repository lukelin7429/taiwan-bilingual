Anqing Elementary School

安慶國民小學

Founded directly by the Huwei Sugar Refinery's own director to educate sugar-workers' children — one of the clearest living links to the town's sugar heritage.

About · 學校簡介

A school built by the sugar refinery itself.

Anqing Elementary School was founded directly in 1947 by Taiwan Sugar Corporation's first branch manager and the Huwei Sugar Refinery's own director, originally named "Tainan County Private Taisugar First Elementary School" to educate the children of sugar-refinery workers. It was renamed Anqing in 1950. With a lineage tracing back even earlier to 1909, the school stands as one of the clearest living links to Huwei's sugar-industry heritage.

安慶國小由台灣糖業公司首任分廠經理暨虎尾糖廠廠長於1947年直接創辦，最初命名為「台南縣私立台糖第一國民學校」，專為糖廠員工子弟設立。1950年更名為安慶國小。學校的淵源甚至可追溯至更早的1909年，是虎尾糖業歷史最鮮明的見證之一。
