About · 學校簡介

Health, happiness, and growth.

Guanghua Elementary began in 1970 as a branch class of Daoxiang Elementary, was upgraded to a branch school in 1972, and became fully independent in 1977. Guided by the motto "Health, Happiness, Growth," the school briefly operated a satellite branch in the Yanliao area of neighboring Shoufeng Township from 1989 to 1993, an unusual arrangement that has since been dissolved — a small but telling example of how Hualien's rural schools have adapted their footprint to follow where students actually live.

光華國小於1970年以稻香國小分班形式創立，1972年升格為分校，1977年正式獨立設校。學校秉持「健康、快樂、成長」的校訓，並曾於1989年至1993年間，在鄰近壽豐鄉鹽寮地區設立衛星分校，這項特殊安排如今已不復存在——這也是花蓮鄉間學校因應學童居住地變化而調整佈局的一個小例子。
