About · 學校簡介

At the center of the Pingtung Plain.

Linluo Elementary School serves the children of Linluo Township, a small Hakka community sitting at the center of the Pingtung Plain. As part of the Liudui cultural region's historic "front camp," the township is home to a Hakka population of roughly 87%, and the school maintains an active Facebook page to keep its community connected.

麟洛國小服務麟洛鄉的學童，該鄉為位於屏東平原中心的小型客家聚落。作為六堆文化區「前堆」的一部分，全鄉客家人口約佔87%，學校並經營活躍的臉書粉專與社區保持聯繫。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where the turtles brought a kirin's luck.

🐢

Linluo Township is a small Hakka township within the Liudui (六堆) cultural region's "front camp," where Hakka residents make up approximately 87% of the population. The township's name traces to a Qing-era legend of giant turtles found while digging irrigation channels — an auspicious sign said to herald a kirin (麟). See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
