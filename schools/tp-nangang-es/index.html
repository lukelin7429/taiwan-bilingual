About · 學校簡介

From fertilizer plant to trade park.

Nangang Elementary opened on April 1, 1914, as "錫口公學校南港分校," a branch of Songshan's Xikou Public School created after local leaders petitioned the colonial government in 1913 because students had to travel too far to Xikou. It became independent as "南港公學校" on March 31, 1918, and was renamed "南港國民小學" in 1968 when Nangang Township joined Taipei City. The oldest elementary school in Nangang District, it celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2014 and spun off two later-independent schools — a 1945 branch that became today's Yucheng Elementary, and a 1955 branch that became today's Jiuzhuang Elementary. Construction from 2007 to 2010 relocated the school to an entirely new campus within the Nangang Trade & Exhibition Park redevelopment zone, on land carved out of the former Taiwan Fertilizer Company Nangang plant site.

南港國小於大正3年（1914年）4月1日創校，最初為「錫口公學校南港分校」，因當地士紳於1913年向殖民政府陳情——學童就讀錫口公學校路途過遠——而設立。民國7年（1918年）3月31日獨立設校，定名「南港公學校」，民國57年（1968年）南港鎮併入台北市後改名「南港國民小學」。作為南港區歷史最悠久的小學，南港國小於民國103年（2014年）歡慶建校百年，並曾分出兩所後來獨立的學校——1945年設立的分校（今玉成國小）與1955年設立的分校（今舊莊國小）。民國96年至99年（2007–2010年）間，學校遷至南港展覽館重劃區內的全新校地，該地原為台灣肥料公司南港廠舊址。
