National Chutung Senior High School

國立竹東高級中學

Zhudong Township's flagship public high school, built on the former grounds of a Japanese-era Shinto shrine and serving as the educational capstone for Hsinchu County's mountain townships.

About · 學校簡介

From shrine grounds to a mountain hub's flagship school.

National Chutung Senior High traces its roots to 1946, when it opened as a branch of Hsinchu Junior High before becoming independent in 1948 and being elevated to a provincial senior high school in 1961. In 1951, the campus moved to its present site at the foot of Fengyang Mountain — the former grounds of the Japanese colonial-era Zhudong Shinto Shrine. Today the school spans roughly 66,407 square meters, runs both day and evening divisions for around 1,700 students, and stands as sister institution to nearby Zhudong Junior High.

國立竹東高中前身可追溯至民國35年（1946年），最初為新竹中學的分部，民國37年（1948年）獨立設校，民國50年（1961年）升格為省立高中。民國40年（1951年），校址遷至現址——鳳凰山麓，也就是日治時期竹東神社的舊址。如今校地約66,407平方公尺，設有日間部與夜間部，學生約1,700人，並與鄰近的竹東國中為姊妹校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛩️ A Campus on Shrine Grounds

神社舊址上的校園

The school's present campus at the foot of Fengyang Mountain occupies the former site of the Japanese colonial-era Zhudong Shinto Shrine, relocated there in 1951 — a physical link between the town's colonial past and its educational present.

🎓 Day and Evening Divisions

日間部與夜間部並設

Spanning roughly 66,407 square meters, the school serves about 1,700 students across both day and evening divisions, making it one of the larger secondary campuses in the region.

🏔️ The Region's Educational Capstone

山區鄉鎮的升學終點

As Zhudong's only senior high school, it serves as the educational hub and capstone for students from Hsinchu County's surrounding mountain townships, including Wufeng, Jianshi, and Beipu.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Zhudong, the mountain gateway's hub town.

🏙️

As Zhudong's flagship public high school, National Chutung Senior High embodies the town's long-standing role as the educational and administrative hub for Hsinchu County's mountain townships — including Wufeng, Jianshi, and Beipu — whose students have historically come down to Zhudong to complete their secondary and senior-high education.
