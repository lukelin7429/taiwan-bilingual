Hsin Chuang Senior High School

高雄市立新莊高級中學

A senior high school in Zuoying's "Xinzhuang" sub-area, offering a bilingual science class alongside archery, tennis, and roller hockey teams.

位於左營「新莊」次分區的高中，開設雙語理科班，並設有射箭、網球、直排輪曲棍球隊。

About · 學校簡介

Bilingual science in a growing "new village."

Hsin Chuang Senior High School's preparatory office was established in 1992, and the school formally opened on July 1, 1995. It has grown to serve roughly 1,492 students as of 2023. Since 2006, the school has fielded teams in archery, tennis, and roller hockey, and in 2021 it introduced a bilingual science class, reflecting Kaohsiung's push toward bilingual education. The school takes its name from Xinzhuang ("new village"), the sub-area of Zuoying where it is located.

新莊高中籌備處成立於民國81年（1992年），並於民國84年（1995年）7月1日正式創校。截至2023年，全校學生約1,492人。自民國95年（2006年）起，學校成立射箭、網球、直排輪曲棍球隊，並於民國110年（2021年）開設雙語理科班，呼應高雄市推動雙語教育的政策方向。校名取自左營「新莊」次分區，即學校所在地。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏹 Archery, Tennis, and Roller Hockey

射箭、網球、直排輪曲棍球隊

Since 2006, the school has built varsity teams in archery, tennis, and roller hockey — a distinctive combination for a public senior high.

🔬 A Bilingual Science Class

雙語理科班

Since 2021, Hsin Chuang has offered a bilingual science class, part of Kaohsiung's broader push toward bilingual education under Taiwan's 2030 policy.

🏘️ Named for Zuoying's "New Village"

校名源自「新莊」

The school takes its name from Xinzhuang, the "new village" sub-area of Zuoying where the campus sits.

📈 A Growing Student Body

學生人數持續成長

Established in 1995, the school has grown to serve close to 1,500 students, making it one of the district's larger senior highs.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Zuoying's newer residential face.

🏘️

Xinzhuang ("new village") is a growing residential sub-area within Zuoying, distinct from the district's older naval-town core — a reminder that Zuoying today is a mix of historic military neighborhoods and newer suburban development. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
