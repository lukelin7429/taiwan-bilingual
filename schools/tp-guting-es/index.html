About · 學校簡介

Home to an early music-talent program.

Guting Elementary was founded in 1952. In August 1979, National Taiwan Normal University partnered with the Ministry of Education to establish an experimental music education class (音樂教育實驗班) at the school — one of Taiwan's earliest formal school-based music-talent programs. The school shares its “Guting” name with the neighboring but administratively separate Guting Junior High.

古亭國小創立於民國41年（1952年）。1979年8月，國立台灣師範大學與教育部合作，於本校成立「音樂教育實驗班」，是全台灣最早的校內音樂資優教育計畫之一。學校與鄰近但行政上各自獨立的古亭國中共用「古亭」之名。
