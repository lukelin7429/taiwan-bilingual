Zhongxiao Elementary School

忠孝國民小學

A public elementary school in Puli's Niuyan Village, known for its string orchestra and language enrichment programs.

About · 學校簡介

A village school with a musical streak.

Zhongxiao Elementary sits in Niuyan Village on the outskirts of the Puli basin, serving families in this quieter corner of the township. The school is best known locally for its string orchestra program and its language enrichment offerings, and it operates an attached kindergarten that eases the transition into formal schooling for the youngest children in the community.

忠孝國小座落於埔里盆地邊緣的牛眠里，服務這個較為寧靜的鄉鎮角落。學校在地方上以弦樂團與語文增能課程著稱，並附設幼兒園，協助社區幼童順利銜接正式就學階段。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎻 A String Orchestra Program

弦樂團特色課程

Zhongxiao offers a string orchestra program that gives students in this rural village access to instrumental music training not always available at small schools.

📖 Language Enrichment

語文增能課程

The school runs dedicated language enrichment programming, reflecting an emphasis on literacy and communication skills alongside its core curriculum.

🧸 An Attached Kindergarten

附設幼兒園

An on-campus kindergarten allows the youngest children in Niuyan Village to begin their education close to home before moving into elementary grades.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Puli, land of paper and orchids.

🌸

Set in Puli's Niuyan Village within the flower- and orchid-growing basin at the geographic heart of Taiwan, the school is part of an area also known for its papermaking heritage. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
