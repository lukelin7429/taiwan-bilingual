About · 學校簡介

Independent only since 1997.

Ruixiang Elementary began in August 1975 as a branch school with six classes under founding head Zhang Chou-mei, becoming independent on August 1, 1997, as Taoyuan County Daxi Township Ruixiang Elementary School — making it one of Daxi's more recently independent schools.

瑞祥國小於1975年（民國64年）8月設立為「瑞祥分校」，首任分校主任張綢妹，創校時僅6班，1997年（民國86年）8月1日獨立設校，定名為桃園縣大溪鎮瑞祥國民小學，是大溪區較晚獨立設校的學校之一。
