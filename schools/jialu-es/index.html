Jialu Elementary School

加祿國小

A Fangshan Township elementary school renovated into one of Taiwan's most scenic campuses, sitting less than 10 meters from the coastline and nicknamed the “whale elementary school.”

About · 學校簡介

The “whale elementary school” by the sea.

Jialu Elementary School was founded on April 1, 1945, near the end of the Japanese colonial era, as a branch site of Fengkang School. Just months later, following Taiwan's retrocession, it became independent as Kaohsiung County Fangshan Township Jialu National School on December 1, 1945. The school has since become widely known across Taiwan media as one of the country's “most beautiful schools” — its old school building was renovated into a strikingly scenic campus, nicknamed the “whale elementary school,” sitting less than 10 meters from the coastline with an open ocean view. It operates two campuses: the main Jialu campus and a Fangshan branch campus.

加祿國小創立於昭和20年（1945年）4月1日，日治末期作為楓港學校的分教場；同年台灣光復後，於12月1日獨立設校，改稱高雄縣枋山鄉加祿國民學校。近年因舊校舍改建為景觀優美的校園，緊鄰海岸不到十公尺、坐擁無敵海景，被台灣媒體譽為全台「最美麗學校」之一，並有「鯨魚國小」之暱稱。學校設有加祿本校與枋山分校兩個校區。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🐋 The “Whale Elementary School”

鯨魚國小

Jialu Elementary's renovated old building has made it a media favorite as one of Taiwan's most beautiful schools, earning the nickname “whale elementary school” for its striking coastal campus design.

🌊 Ten Meters from the Ocean

緊鄰海岸不到十公尺

The campus sits less than 10 meters from the coastline, giving students and visitors an open ocean view rare among Taiwan's elementary schools.

🥭 In the Heart of Mango Country

芒果王國核心

Fangshan Township, home to Jialu Elementary, is known across Taiwan as the “mango kingdom” for its prized Irwin mangoes, grown along the island's narrowest, longest township.

🏫 Two Campuses, One School

本校加分校

Jialu Elementary operates both its main Jialu campus and a separate Fangshan branch campus, serving communities across the township's coastal strip.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fangshan, Taiwan's mango kingdom.

🥭

Fangshan Township is Taiwan's most famous mango-growing region, nicknamed the “mango kingdom” — Irwin (愛文) mangoes here benefit from long sun exposure and large day-night temperature swings, producing exceptionally sweet, high-quality fruit sold April through July. The township is Taiwan's narrowest and longest, just 500 meters to 1 kilometer wide but 30 kilometers long, comprising the three settlements of Fengkang, Fangshan, and Jialu.
