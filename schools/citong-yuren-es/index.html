Yuren Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A village school with a performance tradition.

Yuren Elementary serves Ganchuo Village within Citong Township, and has built a reputation locally for its performing arts. The school maintains a Chinese orchestra and a war-drum performance team, giving students an outlet in traditional music and rhythm, while a badminton team offers a competitive athletic complement to campus life.

育仁國小服務莿桐鄉甘厝村，在地方上以表演藝術見長。學校設有國樂團與戰鼓表演隊，讓學生在傳統音樂與節奏中找到發揮空間，同時也組有羽球隊，為校園生活增添競技體育的一面。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎻 A Chinese Orchestra Tradition

國樂團

Yuren maintains a Chinese orchestra, giving students hands-on experience with traditional instruments and ensemble performance.

🥁 A War-Drum Performance Team

戰鼓表演隊

The school's war-drum team brings a powerful, rhythmic performance tradition to school events, a distinctive feature among Yunlin's smaller village schools.

🏸 A Competitive Badminton Team

羽球隊

Alongside its arts programs, Yuren fields a badminton team, giving students a competitive outlet in one of Taiwan's most popular school sports.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Ganchuo Village, in the heart of pomelo country.

🍈

Located in Ganchuo Village within Citong Township, the school's rural setting is part of the pomelo (文旦)- and betel-nut-farming landscape that defines the township's agricultural character. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
