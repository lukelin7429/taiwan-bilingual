Xialun Elementary School

下崙國民小學

An 80-plus-year-old public elementary school in Kouhu Township's milkfish-farming coastal belt, marking its 80th anniversary with a student street procession.

About · 學校簡介

Eight decades on Yunlin's flood-prone coast.

Xialun Elementary was founded on April 1, 1938, as the "Kouhu National School Xialun Branch," becoming an independent school on April 1, 1943. In 2018, the school celebrated its 80th anniversary with a lively student-performance street procession through the community. Over its long history, the school has weathered the coastal region's turbulent natural history, including the devastating floods that have periodically struck Kouhu Township.

下崙國小創立於昭和13年（1938年）4月1日，最初為「口湖公學校下崙分校」，昭和18年（1943年）4月1日正式獨立設校。民國107年（2018年），學校舉辦80週年校慶，以學生表演踩街遊行的方式歡慶。悠久的校史見證了這片沿海地區歷經多次天災的滄桑，包括曾重創口湖鄉的水患。
