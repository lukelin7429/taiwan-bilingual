About · 學校簡介

Decades of dance in a farming community.

Qiaoshan Elementary School serves families in a rural Hakka farming area of Toufen City. The school is best known for a long-running dance program established in 1990, created specifically to bring broader arts and cultural exposure to children in the countryside. The program has since earned recognition from National Taiwan Normal University's arts-education resource network, a mark of its lasting quality.

僑善國小服務頭份市客家農村地區的學童。學校最具代表性的是一項創立於1990年（民國79年）的長期舞蹈課程，當初設立的目的正是要為鄉村孩童帶來更廣泛的藝術與文化薰陶。此課程後來獲得國立臺灣師範大學藝術教育資源網的肯定，展現其長年累積的品質。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💃 A Dance Program Since 1990

1990年創立舞蹈班

Established in 1990 to widen rural children's access to the arts, the school's dance program has run continuously for over three decades, becoming a defining feature of the school.

🎓 Recognized by a National Arts Network

師大藝術教育資源網肯定

The program has been recognized by National Taiwan Normal University's arts-education resource network, a signal of its quality and its role as a model for rural arts programming.

🌾 Serving a Rural Hakka Community

扎根客家農村

The school sits outside central Toufen in a rural Hakka farming area, and its arts programming was built specifically to close the cultural-access gap for children living there.
