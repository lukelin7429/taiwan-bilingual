About · 學校簡介

Zhongli's oldest school, over a century of memory.

Zhongli Elementary was founded on December 26, 1898, under Japanese rule, making it the oldest school in this batch — it was formally named Zhongli Public School in 1900. Over the following decades the school was renamed many times: Nanshi Public School (1916), Dalun Public School (1926), Zhongli No. 1 Public School (1936), Xingnan National School (1941), Zhongli National School (1947), Zhongli National Elementary School (1968), and finally Taoyuan City Zhongli District Zhongli National Elementary School (2014). The school celebrated its 124th anniversary with a new school-building dedication attended by two Taoyuan mayors.

中壢國小創立於明治31年（1898年）12月26日，是本批次中歷史最悠久的學校，1900年正式定名為中壢公學校。此後歷經多次更名：南勢公學校（1916年）、大崙公學校（1926年）、中壢第一公學校（1936年）、興南國民學校（1941年）、中壢國民學校（1947年）、中壢國民小學（1968年），並於2014年改名為桃園市中壢區中壢國民小學。學校曾舉辦124週年校慶暨新校舍落成典禮，兩位桃園市長皆出席。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Zhongli's Oldest School

中壢歷史最悠久的學校

Founded in 1898, this school predates even the Japanese colonial cadastral reorganization of the town, giving it the longest continuous history among Zhongli's schools.

🏯 A Restored 1917 Dormitory Complex

壢小故事森林

The school preserves a set of Japanese-era teachers' dormitories built in 1917, listed as a Taoyuan historical building since March 2015 and restored and reopened in 2019 as "壢小故事森林" (Lek Elementary Story Forest), a public reading and children's cultural space that won the 2019 National Excellent Construction Award (Gold, Best Environment & Culture category).

🏗️ A Rare Two-Story Platform

罕見雙層平台建築

The black-tile-and-red-brick dormitory complex includes a rare two-story platform, a distinctive architectural feature among surviving Japanese-era school buildings in Taiwan.

🎉 A 124-Year Legacy

124週年校慶

The school celebrated its 124th anniversary with a new school-building dedication ceremony attended by two Taoyuan mayors, underscoring its civic significance.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring downtown Zhongli's century-old identity.

🏯

As Zhongli's oldest school, its restored 1917 teacher-dormitory complex is now a public heritage site that anchors downtown Zhongli's identity as a century-old rail and market town at the heart of Taoyuan. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
