About · 學校簡介

A campus atop layers of history.

The Affiliated Senior High School of National Chi Nan University traces its earliest roots to a 1943 girls' vocational school, and formally began as Puli Middle School's senior high division in 1958. It became the independent provincial "Puli Senior High School" in 1968, and was affiliated with National Chi Nan University in 2001. The campus suffered severe damage in the 1999 921 earthquake and was reconstructed between 2000 and 2004. Notably, it sits atop the Damaling Neolithic archaeological site, and today operates comprehensive, vocational, and evening-school divisions.

國立暨南國際大學附屬高級中學校史最早可溯及1943年的女子家政學校，1958年正式以「埔里中學」高中部形式設立，1968年獨立為省立埔里高級中學，2001年改隸暨南國際大學附屬。校舍於1999年921大地震中嚴重受損，於2000至2004年間重建。學校座落於「大馬璘」新石器時代考古遺址之上，現設有普通科、職業類科與夜間部。
