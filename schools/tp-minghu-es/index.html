About · 學校簡介

An underground pool and a teaching pond.

Taipei Municipal Minghu Elementary School was founded in 1987 on Kangning Road, in the cluster of "湖" (lake)-named schools that trace back to Neihu's original lake and marsh geography. As of the 2021–22 academic year, the school had 62 classes, 1,683 students, and 120 teachers. Its facilities include an underground swimming pool and an aquatic-plant teaching pond, alongside astronomy and robotics/coding clubs that give students hands-on exposure to both natural science and technology.

明湖國小創立於民國76年（1987年），位於康寧路，屬於一系列源自內湖原始湖沼地形的「湖」字校名學校群之一。截至110學年度（2021年），全校共62班、1,683名學生、120位教師。校內設施包括地下游泳池與水生植物教學池，並設有天文社與機器人程式社，讓學生同時接觸自然科學與科技領域的實作學習。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏊 An Underground Swimming Pool

地下游泳池

Minghu's campus includes an underground swimming pool, a notable facility that gives students year-round access to swimming instruction.

🪷 An Aquatic-Plant Teaching Pond

水生植物教學池

An on-campus aquatic-plant teaching pond gives students direct, hands-on exposure to wetland ecology as part of their science education.

🤖 Astronomy and Robotics Clubs

天文社與機器人社

The school runs both astronomy and robotics/coding clubs, extending science learning beyond the standard classroom curriculum.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Neihu's lake-named school cluster.

🪷

Located on Kangning Road, Minghu Elementary sits in the cluster of "湖" (lake)-named schools that trace back to the district's original lake and marsh geography — a naming pattern shared with neighboring Nanhu, Xihu, and Xinhu schools across Neihu. See the Taipei county page for more on the region.
