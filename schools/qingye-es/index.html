About · 學校簡介

A Rukai enclave within Paiwan territory.

Qingye Elementary, founded in 1977, serves the only Rukai-tribe village within Sandimen Township — a township otherwise made up mostly of Paiwan communities. The village's residents relocated collectively from the old Dawu settlement in neighboring Wutai Township more than 70 years ago, and most families today work in agriculture. Including kindergarten, the school has 7 classes, and its student body is 100 percent Rukai. Its Rukai Ballad Choir (魯凱歌謠隊) has won the national folk-song competition for six consecutive years and has performed abroad in Italy and Canada.

青葉國小創立於民國66年（1977年），是三地門鄉境內唯一的魯凱族部落學校——該鄉其餘村落多為排灣族聚落。青葉部落居民約70多年前自鄰近霧台鄉的大武部落集體遷徙至此，多數居民從事農業。學校（含幼兒園）共有7個班級，全校學生皆為魯凱族。校內的魯凱歌謠隊曾連續六年獲得全國民謠比賽冠軍，並曾赴義大利、加拿大演出。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rukai songs, weaving, and migration memory.

🎶

Qingye is a Rukai indigenous enclave set within a township that is otherwise predominantly Paiwan — a reminder that Sandimen's mountain communities are more culturally layered than a single label suggests. The school's curriculum is built around preserving Rukai oral songs, weaving, and the migration history that brought the community here from its ancestral village in the Wutai mountains, keeping that heritage alive through the internationally recognized Rukai Ballad Choir.
