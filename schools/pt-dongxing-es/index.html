About · 學校簡介

Sunlight, ocean, and a growing sprout.

Dongxing Elementary School serves Neiguandi Village in Donggang Town. As of the 2021–22 academic year, the school had 19 classes with 522 students and 31 faculty and staff. Its school emblem is designed around the theme of "sunlight, ocean, and a green sprout" (陽光、海洋、綠苗), symbolizing new growth nourished by sunlight against a marine-culture backdrop — a direct visual nod to the school's coastal setting. The school's motto and branding, "陽光活力健康有禮" ("Sunny, energetic, healthy, courteous"), carries that same spirit into daily school life.

東興國小服務東港鎮內關帝里。110學年度（2021-22年）全校共19班、522名學生、31位教職員。校徽以「陽光、海洋、綠苗」為設計主題，象徵在陽光滋養下、於海洋文化背景中茁壯成長的新芽，直接呼應學校的沿海地理位置。校訓與品牌形象「陽光活力健康有禮」，也將這股精神延伸到日常校園生活中。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A fishing port with a royal tradition.

🎣

Neiguandi Village's economy and identity, like the rest of Donggang Town, are built on the fishing port and the bluefin tuna trade — a connection made explicit in Dongxing Elementary's own emblem, which draws directly on local marine culture. Donggang Town is also home to the triennial King Boat Burning Festival centered on Donglong Temple. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
