Shekou Elementary School

社口國小

A public elementary school founded in 1942 as a colonial-era annex, known today for its taiko drumming and a decades-old student magazine.

About · 學校簡介

From colonial-era annex to a school with its own magazine.

Shekou Elementary School was founded in April 1942, during the Japanese colonial era, as the Shekou Annex of Zhongpu Public School. It became an independent school, Zhongpu Township's Fourth National School, in November 1945, was renamed Shekou National School in January 1947, and became Shekou Elementary School in August 1968 under the nine-year compulsory education reform. Today the school runs an attached kindergarten, features music programs with recorder and Japanese taiko (和太鼓) drum performances, publishes a school magazine called "飛躍社口" (Flying Shekou), and offers indigenous/native language education and environmental education programs.

社口國小創立於昭和17年（民國31年，1942年）4月，日治時期名為中埔公學校社口分教場。民國34年（1945年）11月獨立設校，改稱中埔鄉第四國民學校，民國36年（1947年）1月更名社口國民學校，民國57年（1968年）8月九年國教實施後改制為社口國民小學。現今學校設有附設幼兒園，音樂課程包含直笛與和太鼓演出，並發行校刊「飛躍社口」，同時推動原住民族語教育與環境教育課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥁 Taiko Drum Performances

和太鼓演出

Music programs featuring recorder and Japanese taiko (和太鼓) drum performances give students a distinctive performing-arts outlet.

📰 Flying Shekou Magazine

校刊「飛躍社口」

The school publishes its own magazine, "飛躍社口" (Flying Shekou), giving students a platform to showcase their writing and school life.

🌏 Indigenous Language and Environmental Education

原住民族語與環境教育

Indigenous/native language education and environmental education programs round out the school's culturally responsive curriculum.

👶 Attached Kindergarten

附設幼兒園

An attached kindergarten extends the school's reach to the youngest children of Longxing Village.

Local Culture · 在地文化

In the citrus belt near the Alishan foothills.

🍊

Located in Longxing Village of Zhongpu Township, the school is part of the township's traditional agrarian belt growing bamboo shoots and citrus fruit near the base of the Alishan foothills. See the Chiayi County page for more on the region.
