Longteng Branch School, Yuying Elementary School

育英國小龍騰分校

A tiny forest campus of about 19 students near the famous Longteng Broken Bridge, at the heart of Sanyi's mountain railway heritage.

About · 學校簡介

A forest school beside a broken bridge.

Longteng Branch School is a very small, remote campus of Yuying Elementary School, with only about 19 students. Set within a scenic, richly forested part of Sanyi Township, the school makes the most of its small size and natural surroundings, running Hakka theater and opera programs and hosting an annual student creative-works exhibition. It also partners with the nearby Sanyi Huoyanshan Ecology Museum for hands-on plant and ecology field lessons.

育英國小龍騰分校是一所規模極小的偏遠分校，全校學生僅約19人。校園座落於三義鄉風景秀麗、林木蒼鬱的山區，善用小校優勢與自然環境，發展客家戲曲課程，並每年舉辦學生創作成果展。學校也與鄰近的三義火炎山生態館合作，帶領學生進行植物與生態的實地學習。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌲 A Tiny Campus in the Forest

森林中的迷你校園

With roughly 19 students, Longteng Branch School is one of the smallest campuses in the area, set among the richly green, scenic hills of Sanyi Township.

🎭 Hakka Theater and Opera

客家戲曲課程

The school develops Hakka theater and opera programs for its students, keeping a distinctive local performing-arts tradition alive on a very small campus.

🖼️ An Annual Creative-Works Show

學生創作成果展

Every year the school hosts a student creative-works exhibition, giving its small community of learners a chance to showcase what they've made and learned.

🌿 Ecology Lessons Next Door

火炎山生態館合作

Through a partnership with the nearby Sanyi Huoyanshan Ecology Museum, students take part in field lessons on local plants and ecology just beyond the classroom.

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the heart of Sanyi's mountain railway heritage.

🚂

Located in Longteng Village near the famous Longteng Broken Bridge and the Shengxing Old Railway line, this tiny forest school sits at the heart of Sanyi's mountain railway heritage — the same heritage that also gave rise to the township's renowned woodcarving culture.
