About · 學校簡介

Engineering talent since the colonial era.

National Hualien Industrial Vocational Senior High School was founded in 1940 under Japanese rule as Hualien Harbor Prefectural Industrial School, with departments in mechanical, electrical, and chemical engineering. It became a national school in 2000 and is counted among Taiwan's historic "Eight Great Provincial Industrial Schools" — a group of foundational technical schools that trained much of the island's postwar industrial workforce. The campus retains a designated historic architectural group from the American-aid era, recognized in 2016, and the school continues under the motto "Integrity, Simplicity, Diligence, Perseverance."

花蓮高工創立於1940年日治時期，初名「花蓮港廳立工業學校」，設有機械、電機、化工等科別。民國89年（2000年）改隸國立，並名列台灣歷史悠久的「省立八大工校」之一——這批基礎工業學校培育了戰後台灣大部分的產業技術人力。校園內保存美援時期的歷史建築群，於2016年獲得指定，學校至今仍秉持「誠、樸、勤、毅」校訓辦學。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏭 One of the "Eight Great Provincial Industrial Schools"

省立八大工校之一

The school belongs to a historic group of foundational technical schools that shaped Taiwan's postwar industrial workforce, giving it a distinguished place in the island's vocational education history.

🏛️ Historic American-Aid Era Buildings

美援時期歷史建築群

A designated historic architectural group from the American-aid era, officially recognized in 2016, still stands on campus as a physical record of Taiwan's postwar development.

⚙️ Three Founding Engineering Departments

機電化工三科起家

Opened in 1940 with mechanical, electrical, and chemical engineering departments, the school's founding curriculum reflected the technical needs of a developing port city.

💪 Integrity, Simplicity, Diligence, Perseverance

誠樸勤毅校訓

The school's enduring motto reflects the hands-on, disciplined culture typical of Taiwan's classic industrial vocational schools.
