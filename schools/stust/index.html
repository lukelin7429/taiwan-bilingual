About · 學校簡介

From an engineering junior college to a science and technology university.

Southern Taiwan University of Science and Technology (STUST) was founded in 1969 as Nan-Tai Junior College of Engineering, established by local political consultant San-lian Wu together with academics Wen-Ping Hsin, Xiu-qi Wu, and Li-tang Chang, in response to a government push for private institutions to train industrial and business talent. It expanded into Nan-Tai Junior College of Engineering and Business in 1990, was upgraded to Nan-Tai Institute of Technology in 1996, and became Southern Taiwan University of Technology in 1999, adding graduate schools in 2000 and doctoral programs in 2002. It was renamed Southern Taiwan University in 2007, and took its current name in 2012.

南臺科技大學創立於1969年，前身為南台工業專科學校，由地方政治顧問吳三連偕同辛文炳、吳修齊、張麗堂等學者共同創辦，回應政府推動私立學校培育工商人才的政策。1990年擴大為南台工商專科學校，1996年升格為南台工業技術學院，1999年改名南台科技大學前身南台技術學院，2000年設立研究所，2002年增設博士班。2007年更名南台大學，2012年改為現名南臺科技大學。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Engineering and Business Roots

工商起家

STUST's colleges span Engineering, Business and Management, Humanities and Social Sciences, Digital Design, and Smart Health, reflecting its growth from an engineering-focused junior college into a comprehensive university.

🌏 English-Taught Degree Programs

全英語學位學程

STUST offers six English-taught programs for international students, including a Global MBA, Mechanical Engineering (MS/PhD), Electrical Engineering (MS/PhD), Computer Science and Information Engineering (MS), Semiconductor and Electro-Optical Engineering (MS), and International Business (BBA).

👥 A Large Private University

大型私立大學

With approximately 18,000 students, 800-plus faculty, and over 170 international students, STUST is one of the larger private universities in the Tainan area.

📖 English Content Lives in the Subpages

英文內容詳見子頁面

STUST's English homepage is fairly minimal, but its linked subpages — including History, Academics, and Organization — carry genuine institutional detail, alongside a separate Office of International Affairs site listing degree programs.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A campus that grew with Yongkang.

🏭

STUST's growth from a 1969 engineering junior college into a full science-and-technology university tracks the postwar industrialization of Yongkang — a district that grew from a rural periphery into one of greater Tainan's dense manufacturing and residential suburbs.
