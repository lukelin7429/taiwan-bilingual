Taiwu Elementary School

泰武國民小學

Home of the internationally acclaimed Taiwu Ancient Ballads Choir, rebuilt after Typhoon Morakot as Taiwan's first fully post-disaster-rebuilt school, praised by scholars as “the world's most beautiful elementary school.”

About · 學校簡介

Rebuilt as the world's most beautiful elementary school.

Taiwu Elementary School was formally established in 1968 at its original mountainside campus, merging with the Jiaxing branch school; its history traces back further to an earlier Japanese-era school presence in the mountains. The school is home to the internationally touring Taiwu Elementary Ancient Ballads Choir (泰武古謠傳唱), formed in 2004 by five students under teacher Chalaw Chamak and formally established as a troupe in 2006. The choir has released multiple albums, been nominated for and won Golden Melody Awards for Best Indigenous Language Album, and performed internationally to widespread acclaim, including praise from an Australian arts curator. The school's original campus was devastated by Typhoon Morakot in 2009 and relocated in stages — first to Jiaping Village, then Taiwu Junior High, then Jiaxing Village — before rebuilding permanently at the base of the mountain in the new Kucapungane/Wulaljuc (吾拉魯滋) permanent-housing settlement, completed in August 2011. It was the first school campus in Taiwan fully rebuilt after the Morakot disaster, constructed with support from the BenQ/AU Optronics Cultural and Educational Foundation. It became Pingtung's first school with a fully solar-panel roof, and Japanese civil engineering scholars praised it as “the world's most beautiful elementary school.” The campus deeply integrates Paiwan culture: an attached kindergarten built in traditional slate-house style, corridors decorated with hundred-pace-snake motifs, pottery and sun-themed artwork, and the three sacred Paiwan colors — yellow, green, and red — symbolizing respect, harmony, and blessing. Historically the school has been very small, with around 60-plus students, reflecting its remote mountain location closest to the Paiwan sacred peak, Dawu Mountain.

泰武國小於民國57年（1968年）在原山區校址正式設校，與佳興分校合併；其校史則可追溯至更早的日治時期山區教育據點。學校是享譽國際的「泰武古謠傳唱」所在地，該傳唱團於民國93年（2004年）由五名學生在查馬克老師帶領下組成，民國95年（2006年）正式成立為表演團體。傳唱團發行多張專輯，曾入圍並榮獲金曲獎最佳原住民語專輯獎，並多次赴海外巡演，獲得澳洲藝術策展人等各界高度讚譽。原校舍於民國98年（2009年）遭莫拉克風災重創，先後遷至佳平村、泰武國中、佳興村等地暫時安置，最終於民國100年（2011年）8月在山腳下新建的「吾拉魯滋」永久屋基地重建完成，是台灣第一座完全重建完成的災後校園。校舍興建獲明基友達文教基金會支持，是屏東縣第一所全太陽能板屋頂的學校，並獲日本土木工程學者譽為「世界最美麗的小學」。校園設計深度融入排灣族文化：附設幼兒園採傳統石板屋建築，走廊裝飾百步蛇圖騰、陶壺與太陽意象，並運用象徵尊重、和諧與祝福的排灣族三聖色——黃、綠、紅。學校歷來規模很小，約六十餘名學生，反映其地處最靠近排灣聖山北大武山的偏遠山區位置。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎶 The Internationally Acclaimed Ancient Ballads Choir

泰武古謠傳唱團

Formed in 2004 by five students and formally established in 2006, the Taiwu Elementary Ancient Ballads Choir has won Golden Melody Awards and performed internationally, carrying Paiwan traditional song to the world.

🏗️ Taiwan's First Fully Rebuilt Post-Morakot Campus

全台首座災後重建完成校園

Devastated by Typhoon Morakot in 2009 and relocated three times, the school was rebuilt permanently at the Wulaljuc settlement in August 2011 — the first campus in Taiwan fully rebuilt after the disaster.

🌍 “The World's Most Beautiful Elementary School”

校園融入排灣文化元素

Local Culture · 在地文化

Taiwu, where a rebuilt school carries a nation's song.

🎵

Taiwu Township is a Paiwan indigenous mountain township where Paiwan make up about 94% of the population, centered on the sacred Dawu Mountain (北大武山); traditional life is built around millet farming and slate-house (石板屋) architecture. The township is internationally known through the Taiwu Elementary Ancient Ballads Choir — a major source of local pride — and its rebuilt campus in the Wulaljuc permanent-housing settlement stands as a living monument to Paiwan resilience after Typhoon Morakot, blending post-disaster reconstruction with deeply rooted cultural design.
