About · 學校簡介

Founded by farmers who wanted a closer school.

Jinshui Elementary was founded in 1940 as the "Zaoqiao Public School Jinshui Annex," a direct response to the hardship faced by local farming families, whose children had to walk one to two hours over mountainous terrain to reach the nearest school. Community leaders campaigned to establish the branch school, and it proved successful enough that a second class was added just one year after founding, reflecting strong local demand for accessible education.

錦水國小創校於1940年（昭和15年／民國29年），最初名為「造橋公學校錦水分教場」，設校起因於當地農家子弟每日須翻山越嶺步行一至二小時才能抵達最近的學校。地方領袖積極奔走請願，促成分校成立，設校僅一年後便增設第二班，顯示當地對就近就學的殷切需求。
