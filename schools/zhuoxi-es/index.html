About · 學校簡介

The heart of the township's administrative center.

Zhuoxi Elementary sits in Zhuoxi Village, the namesake settlement and administrative center of Zhuoxi Township. The school includes an attached kindergarten and, like its neighbors across the township, serves a predominantly Bunun Indigenous student body. Alongside archery, the school is known for keeping alive Pasibutbut (八部合音), the Bunun's extraordinary eight-part polyphonic chant recognized internationally as one of the world's most distinctive vocal traditions.

卓溪國小座落於卓溪村——卓溪鄉的同名聚落與行政中心。學校附設幼兒園，如同全鄉其他學校，學生以布農族原住民為主。除了射箭活動，學校也致力傳承「祈禱小米豐收歌」（Pasibutbut，八部合音）——這項布農族獨特的複音吟唱傳統，在國際上被視為世界最具特色的人聲藝術之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎵 Keepers of Pasibutbut

祈禱小米豐收歌傳承

Students learn Pasibutbut, the Bunun's famed eight-part harmonic chant traditionally sung to pray for a millet harvest — a vocal tradition studied by ethnomusicologists worldwide.

🏹 Archery Alongside Academics

結合射箭活動

As at other Zhuoxi schools, students practice archery as a living connection to Bunun hunting heritage, woven into the regular school calendar.

📍 The Township's Namesake Village

卓溪鄉行政中心

Located in Zhuoxi Village itself, the school sits at the administrative heart of the township that shares its name.

🧒 A Kindergarten Under One Roof

附設幼兒園

An attached kindergarten means the school serves families from their children's earliest years through elementary graduation.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Zhuoxi Township, a Bunun mountain home.

⛰️

Zhuoxi Township is a remote, mountainous district of Hualien County near Yushan National Park, home to a majority Bunun Indigenous population. Its eight elementary schools — and no junior high — reflect villages small enough that most students continue their education elsewhere after sixth grade.
