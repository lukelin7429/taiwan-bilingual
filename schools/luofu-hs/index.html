Luofu Senior High School

桃園市立羅浮高級中等學校

Taiwan's only public complete Indigenous experimental high school, built so Fuxing's Atayal students can complete a full K-12 pathway without leaving the mountains.

About · 學校簡介

One mountain, one complete Indigenous high school.

Luofu Senior High traces back to a 2013 preparatory office (“桃園縣立原住民實驗高中籌備處”). Campus construction began soon after, and the school was formally dedicated on May 31, 2019, before enrolling its first students in 2021 under its current name. It is Taiwan's only public complete Indigenous experimental high school (原住民族完全中學) — a distinction that matters in a city where the Indigenous population (60,000+) is the third-largest of any county or city in Taiwan, after Hualien and Taitung, and where Fuxing District itself (population roughly 11,000) is majority Atayal. In 2022, the nearby Jieshou Junior High (介壽國中), which sat on a slope with geological safety concerns, was merged into Luofu as its junior-high division — making Luofu a genuine six-year complete school with both a junior-high and senior-high division under one administration. It offers vocational tracks in culinary management (餐飲管理) and tourism business (觀光事業) alongside academic pathways.

羅浮高中的前身可追溯至民國102年（2013年）成立的「桃園縣立原住民實驗高中籌備處」。校舍其後動工，並於民國108年（2019年）5月31日舉行揭牌典禮，民國110年（2021年）首屆學生入學，同年正式定名「桃園市立羅浮高級中等學校」。羅浮高中是全國唯一的公立原住民族完全中學——這在桃園別具意義，桃園原住民人口逾6萬人，居全國縣市第三（僅次於花蓮、台東），復興區本身（人口約1.1萬人）更是以泰雅族為主的鄉鎮。民國111年（2022年），坐落於坡地、有地質安全疑慮的介壽國中併入羅浮高中，成為其國中部，使羅浮高中成為名副其實、國中部與高中部一貫的六年完全中學。學校並設有餐飲管理與觀光事業兩科技職課程，提供學術與技職雙軌選擇。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏔️ Taiwan's Only Complete Indigenous High School

全國唯一原住民族完全中學

Luofu is Taiwan's only public complete Indigenous experimental high school (原住民族完全中學), serving a district where Fuxing's roughly 11,000 residents are majority Atayal.

🤝 A 2022 Merger with Jieshou Junior High

2022年併入介壽國中

In 2022, 介壽國中 (Jieshou Junior High), which sat on a slope with geological safety concerns, was merged into Luofu as its junior-high division — making Luofu a genuine six-year complete school covering grades 7 through 12.

🍽️ Culinary and Tourism Vocational Tracks

餐飲與觀光技職課程

Alongside academics, the school offers vocational tracks in culinary management (餐飲管理) and tourism business (觀光事業), aimed at both academic and vocational pathways for Indigenous students.

🏕️ Built to Keep Education in the Mountains

留在部落完成十二年教育

The school was built specifically to let Fuxing's Atayal students complete a full K-12 pathway locally, preserving and transmitting Atayal culture rather than requiring a commute out of the mountains for high school.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A school built for Atayal Fuxing.

🏔️

Luofu Senior High was built specifically to let Fuxing's Atayal students complete a full K-12 pathway locally, preserving and transmitting Atayal culture rather than requiring a commute out of the mountains for high school — a mission that took on added weight in 2022 when the school absorbed the former Jieshou Junior High as its junior-high division.
