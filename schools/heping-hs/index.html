About · 學校簡介

Da'an's only public senior high school.

Heping Senior High traces back to April 1949, when its predecessor, the Taipei Municipal Junior Agricultural Vocational School, was founded; it became a senior high on July 1, 1994. As the only public senior high located within Da'an District proper, it is a “complete school” spanning both junior and senior divisions, with roughly 1,386 students, 40 classes, and 181 staff. Its campus site was actually reserved back in 1941 for a Japanese-era school, Taihoku Prefecture's 4th Middle School, a plan shelved by the outbreak of World War II — so the school now marks its founding anniversary on the day ground was finally broken, May 3.

和平高中創校可追溯至民國38年（1949年）4月，前身為「臺北市立初級農業職業學校」；民國83年（1994年）7月1日增設高中部並更名為和平高中。作為大安區境內唯一一所市立高中，學校為涵蓋國中與高中部的「完全中學」，目前約有1,386名學生、40個班級及181位教職員。校地其實早在1941年日治時期便已預留，原規劃興建「臺北州立第四中學校」，因二戰爆發而擱置，因此學校特以動土典禮的5月3日作為校慶日。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Da'an's Only Public High School

大安區唯一市立高中

As the sole public senior high seated inside Da'an District proper, Heping draws heavily from the same neighborhood families whose children go on to NTU and other nearby universities.

🌸 A Campus Waiting Since 1941

塵封的1941年建校計畫

The school's grounds were reserved as far back as 1941 for a Japanese-era middle school that was shelved by WWII, and the site wasn't finally used until Heping's predecessor broke ground — a history the school now honors as its founding day.

🏀 HBL Basketball Tradition

HBL籃球傳統

Heping fields a men's basketball team, established in 1992, that competes in Taiwan's High School Basketball League (HBL).

🌿 Toward a “Future School”

邁向「未來學校」

Known for its lotus pond and green, digital-campus branding, Heping is currently building a new campus building slated for completion around 2026.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Da'an's academic bridge to NTU.

🎓

As the only public senior high located within Da'an District itself, Heping High sits among the same tree-lined streets, café culture, and university-adjacent neighborhoods — near Da'an Forest Park and within reach of National Taiwan University — that make the district Taipei's premier address for education-driven living, drawing heavily from local families whose children go on to NTU and other nearby universities.
