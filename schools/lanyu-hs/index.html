Lanyu High School

蘭嶼中學

The only secondary school on Orchid Island, and Taiwan's first fully-integrated Tao Indigenous experimental secondary school, spanning junior and senior high under one roof.

About · 學校簡介

One island, one school, two divisions.

Lanyu High School was founded in 1969 as Lanyu Junior High School, serving the children of Orchid Island — home to the Tao (also known as Yami) people, Taiwan's only Austronesian island-dwelling Indigenous community, culturally and linguistically distinct from Taiwan's other fifteen recognized Indigenous peoples. In 1997 the school was restructured into a "complete school" (完全中學), adding a senior high division so that Tao students could complete secondary education without leaving the island. As of 2024 it enrolls roughly 77 students across six classes in junior high and about 38 students across three classes in a comprehensive senior-high track. Since 2018 the school has been transitioning into Taiwan's first fully-integrated junior-and-senior-high Tao Indigenous experimental education program, with full implementation from 2020 — meaning Tao language, ocean-canoe and flying-fish traditions, and land-based culture are now woven into the curriculum across all six grades, from age twelve through eighteen.

蘭嶼高中前身為創立於民國58年（1969年）的蘭嶼國中，服務蘭嶼島上的達悟族（雅美族）學子——達悟族是台灣唯一的南島語系離島原住民族，文化與語言皆與台灣其他十五族原住民族截然不同。民國86年（1997年），學校改制為橫跨國中與高中部的「完全中學」，讓達悟族學生無需離島即可完成中等教育。截至2024年，國中部約有6班77名學生，高中部（綜合高中）約有3班38名學生。自2018年起，學校逐步轉型為台灣第一所國中部與高中部完整整合的達悟族實驗教育學校，並於2020年全面實施——達悟族語、拼板舟與飛魚文化、土地倫理等內容，如今貫穿六個年級、十二歲到十八歲學生的完整課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛶 Taiwan's First Integrated Tao Experimental School

全台首創達悟族完全中學實驗教育

Since full implementation in 2020, Lanyu High has run Taiwan's first Tao Indigenous experimental curriculum spanning both junior and senior high, teaching Tao language and culture continuously from grade 7 through grade 12.

🏠 A Campus Built in Tao Architectural Style

融入達悟族地下屋建築特色

A 2007 campus redesign incorporated the traditional Tao semi-underground house style, and a Tao ethnobotanical garden was added in 2019 to bring plant knowledge into daily student life.

🐟 Flying Fish and Canoe Traditions in the Classroom

飛魚文化與拼板舟傳承課程

Beyond academics, students learn the ocean-canoe and flying-fish traditions that have shaped Tao life for generations, connecting classroom learning directly to the rhythms of the surrounding Pacific.

🏝️ The Only Secondary School on the Island

全島唯一中等學校

As the sole junior-and-senior-high institution serving Orchid Island, the school carries outsized importance for every Tao family on Lanyu whose children reach secondary-school age.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Orchid Island and the Tao people.

🌊

Lanyu Township — Orchid Island — is home to the Tao (Yami) people, Taiwan's only Indigenous community traditionally living on an offshore island rather than the main island, with a culture built around plank-canoe (tatala) building, flying-fish-season rituals, and a distinct Austronesian language unlike any of Taiwan's other fifteen recognized Indigenous peoples. Lanyu High's experimental curriculum is one of the clearest expressions of that heritage in the island's public life today.
