About · 學校簡介

Rende's original junior high, at the suburban core.

Rende Junior High School is the district's original junior high school, predating the later Wenxian branch school that would eventually become its own independent institution. A confirmed founding date is not available from public sources, but the school has long served the built-up suburban core of Rende District closest to Tainan's urban center. Today it offers a bilingual-learning initiative with its own dedicated site, plus a self-built education and technology center, and fields multiple athletic teams including taekwondo, softball, badminton, and basketball. The school maintains active honors listings recognizing student achievement in academics, sports, and reading competitions.

仁德國中是仁德區最早設立的國中，早於後來獨立設校的文賢分部（今仁德文賢國中）。校方公開資訊中未載明確切創校年份，但學校長期以來服務仁德區鄰近台南市區核心的密集住宅區。學校現推動雙語學習計畫並設有專屬網站，並自建教育科技中心，同時設有跆拳道、壘球、羽球、籃球等多支運動代表隊。學校持續公告學生在學術、體育及閱讀競賽方面的獲獎榮譽。
