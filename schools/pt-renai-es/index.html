About · 學校簡介

From a girls' school to the county's largest.

Renai Elementary School was founded on April 1, 1924, as a girls' public school, splitting off 259 female students from Pingtung Public School into seven classes. It became coeducational in 1937 as Pingtung City Ōmiya Public School, and took its current name, Renai National Elementary School, in 1950. Today it is the elementary school with the most students and teachers in Pingtung County. In 1987, the school established a "Music Gifted Experimental Class" with support from local community leaders and government. It recently celebrated its 100th anniversary, with alumni including former Premier Su Tseng-chang returning for the celebration.

仁愛國小創立於民國13年（1924年）4月1日，最初為女子公學校，由屏東公學分出259名女學生，編為7個班級。民國26年（1937年）改為男女兼收，更名為「屏東市大宮公學校」，民國39年（1950年）定名「屏東縣屏東市仁愛國民小學」。現為屏東縣師生人數最多的國小。民國76年（1987年）在地方仕紳與政府支持下設立「音樂資賦優異實驗班」。學校近年慶祝建校百週年，校友前行政院長蘇貞昌亦返校參與慶典。
