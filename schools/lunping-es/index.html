About · 學校簡介

A satellite campus that grew into its own school.

Lunping Elementary began as a branch campus of Xinpo Elementary (新坡國小) in August 1975, becoming independent as “Taoyuan County Guanyin Township Lunping Elementary School” in August 1983. Its first principal after independence, Liang Chi-yun (梁杞雲), took office that September. The school added a PU running track, an audio-visual classroom, and a school-history room by May 1997, and began its school lunch program in November 1995.

崙坪國小民國64年（1975年）8月以新坡國小分班之姿創立，民國72年（1983年）8月獨立設校，定名「桃園縣觀音鄉崙坪國民小學」。獨立設校後首任校長梁杞雲同年9月到任。學校於民國86年（1997年）5月完成PU跑道、視聽教室及校史室等設施，並於民國84年（1995年）11月開辦營養午餐。
