About · 學校簡介

A village school known for its ball team.

Jiayi Elementary serves a Paiwan indigenous student population across Majia Township's Lianshan, Jiayi, and Paiwan villages, plus part of neighboring Wanluan Township. As of the 2022–23 school year, the school had 10 classes, 153 students, 22 teachers, and 2 staff, with its own cafeteria and an attached kindergarten. Beyond academics, the school runs baseball, volleyball, archery, choir, and painting programs — its youth baseball team (佳義國小少棒隊) is well known enough to be documented in the Taiwan Baseball Wiki.

佳義國小服務瑪家鄉連山村、佳義村、排灣村及萬巒鄉部分地區的排灣族學童。截至111學年度，全校共有10個班級、153名學生、22位教師、2位職工，設有自設廚房及附設幼兒園。除了學科教學，學校也推動棒球、排球、射箭、合唱與繪畫等課程——校內的佳義國小少棒隊小有名氣，並被收錄於台灣棒球維基館。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚾ A Documented Youth Baseball Team

佳義國小少棒隊

The school's youth baseball team is notable enough to be documented in the Taiwan Baseball Wiki, a point of pride for a small Paiwan village school.

🎨 A Wide Range of Extracurriculars

排球、射箭、合唱、繪畫課程

Alongside baseball, students can take part in volleyball, archery, choir, and painting programs, giving the school a well-rounded activity offering for its size.

🍽️ A Self-Contained Campus

自設廚房與附幼

The school operates its own cafeteria and an attached kindergarten, making it a self-contained educational hub for the surrounding villages.

🗺️ A Multi-Village Catchment

跨村跨鄉學區

The school district covers Majia Township's Lianshan, Jiayi, and Paiwan villages plus part of neighboring Wanluan Township, reflecting its role as a shared community anchor.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Paiwan Majia.

🏔️

Jiayi Elementary serves a Paiwan indigenous student population in Majia Township, home to the Taiwan Indigenous Peoples Culture Park and rooted in Paiwan mountain-agriculture and ancestral village traditions. The school's mix of sports, arts, and academics reflects a community balancing modern extracurricular life with deep indigenous roots.
