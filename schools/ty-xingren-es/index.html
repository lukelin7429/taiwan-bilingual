About · 學校簡介

An English-featured school near the airport.

Xingren Elementary has been certified as one of Taoyuan's "English-featured quality schools" (英語特色優質學校), a designation the school pursued specifically because of its proximity to Taoyuan International Airport. It runs an English-immersion program that includes a student-run English news broadcast, along with partnerships with nearby universities and English cram schools. Beyond English education, the school offers an unusually wide range of after-school clubs, from table tennis and Chinese martial arts to diabolo, inline skating, taekwondo, swimming, and scouting. Exact records of its founding year were not available for this profile.

興仁國小獲認證為桃園市「英語特色優質學校」，學校特別提到，這項特色的推動與鄰近桃園國際機場的地緣位置有直接關係。學校推動英語沉浸式課程，包含學生自製英語新聞播報，並與鄰近大學及英語補習班合作。除英語教育外，學校也開設多元社團，涵蓋桌球、國術、扯鈴、直排輪、跆拳道、游泳、籃球、足球、田徑、寫作、繪畫與童軍等。本頁尚未能確認其確切創校年份。
