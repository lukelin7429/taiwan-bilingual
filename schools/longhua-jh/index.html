Longhua Junior High School

高雄市立龍華國民中學

Founded in 1983 to relieve overcrowding in booming Sanmin District, a junior high that grew nearly twentyfold from its opening class size.

1983年為紓解三民區人口成長壓力而創校，班級數自四班成長至近二十倍。

About · 學校簡介

Built fast to keep pace with a booming district.

Longhua Junior High School was approved for establishment on August 30, 1982, and began enrolling students in September 1983. It was founded specifically to relieve overcrowding driven by rapid population growth in neighboring Sanmin District. The school grew dramatically from its original four classes to a peak of 78 classes, reflecting the pace of urban development in Zuoying and its surrounding districts during the 1980s and 1990s.

龍華國中於民國71年（1982年）8月30日獲准設立，民國72年（1983年）9月開始招生。創校目的是為紓解鄰近三民區人口快速成長所帶來的就學壓力。學校班級數從創校時的4班，一路成長至最多78班，反映出1980至1990年代左營及周邊地區都市發展的速度。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📈 A Nearly Twentyfold Growth

班級數成長近二十倍

Longhua grew from just 4 classes at its 1983 opening to a peak of 78 classes, one of the most dramatic expansions among Zuoying's junior highs.

🏙️ Built to Relieve Overcrowding

為紓解三民區就學壓力而設

The school was established specifically to absorb students from rapidly growing Sanmin District, easing pressure on existing schools nearby.

📅 Approved in 1982, Opened in 1983

1982年核准，1983年招生

Longhua was approved for establishment in August 1982 and began enrolling students the following September.

🏫 A Product of 1980s Urban Growth

Local Culture · 在地文化

A record of Kaohsiung's fast urban growth.

🏙️

Longhua's founding to relieve overcrowding from booming Sanmin District, and its rapid growth from 4 to 78 classes, is a direct record of how fast Kaohsiung's urban population expanded through the 1980s and 1990s. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
