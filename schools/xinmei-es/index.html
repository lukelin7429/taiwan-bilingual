Xinmei Elementary School

新美國民小學

A Tsou indigenous school raising "Tsou-praised" children through language certification, an ancient-ballad choir, and traditional hunting education.

About · 學校簡介

Raising children who make the tribe proud.

Xinmei Elementary was founded in 1961 in the Tsou (鄒族) village of Xinmei. Its school motto emphasizes raising "鄒讚" (Tsou-praised) — disciplined, excellent children — carrying strong Tsou cultural branding through every part of school life. Xinmei offers Tsou-language certification courses, maintains the "Somolosolo 鄒族古謠隊" ancient-ballad choir, and runs cultural and traditional hunting-education outdoor activities, while its students regularly take part in county-level science, athletics, and arts competitions.

新美國小創立於民國50年（1961年），位於鄒族新美部落。校訓強調培育「鄒讚」——具紀律、優秀的孩子，展現濃厚的鄒族文化色彩。學校開設鄒語認證課程，並經營「Somolosolo鄒族古謠隊」，同時推動文化與傳統狩獵教育戶外活動，學生也經常參與縣級科學、體育與藝術競賽。
