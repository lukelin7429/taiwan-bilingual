Dachi Elementary School

大池國民小學

A small, well-greened elementary school near the historic Erkan settlement, one of Penghu's best-preserved traditional villages.

緊鄰澎湖保存最完整的傳統聚落之一——二崁——的小型綠意校園。

About · 學校簡介

A green campus at a village crossroads.

Dachi Elementary began in 1964 as the "Dachi Branch" of nearby Chidong Elementary School, opening with just two classes, and became a fully independent school in 1970. It sits at the junction of Dachi, Erkan, and Chidong villages, and serves only Dachi Village's children — a small, single-community school known for its extensive plantings of araucaria (Norfolk pine) trees, which give the campus an unusually green, shaded feel for the outer islands.

大池國小創立於民國53年（1964年），原為池東國小「大池分校」，初設兩班，民國59年（1970年）獨立設校。學校位於大池、二崁、池東三村交界，僅服務大池村的學童，是單一村落的小型學校，校園內廣植南洋杉，綠意盎然，在離島校園中頗為少見。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌲 A Campus Full of Norfolk Pines

南洋杉綠意校園

Dachi Elementary is known for its extensive plantings of araucaria (Norfolk pine) trees, giving the school an unusually shaded, green campus for Penghu's windswept outer islands.

🏫 From Branch Class to Independent School

從分校到獨立設校

The school began in 1964 with just two classes as a branch of Chidong Elementary, becoming fully independent in 1970.

🏘️ At a Three-Village Crossroads

三村交界處

Located where Dachi, Erkan, and Chidong villages meet, the school nonetheless serves only Dachi Village's own children, keeping its community tight-knit.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Near Erkan, a living heritage village.

🏛️

Dachi Elementary sits close to Erkan (二崁), one of Penghu's best-preserved traditional Minnan settlements, famous for its coral-and-basalt stone houses. The school serves a small traditional Xiyu farming and fishing community shaped by this same architectural heritage. See the Penghu county page for more on the region.
