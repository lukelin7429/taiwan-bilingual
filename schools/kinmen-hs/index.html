National Kinmen Senior High School

About · 學校簡介

A school shaped by war and rebuilt on principle.

National Kinmen Senior High School was founded in 1951 as "Fukien Provincial Kinmen Middle School," formed by merging two existing private junior high schools. Its early history was defined by the realities of life on Taiwan's frontline: in 1954 the school relocated to avoid bombardment during the "9-3 Artillery Battle," and in October 1958, following the intense "8-23 Artillery Battle," 921 students were evacuated to Taiwan for their safety. The school reopened at its present site in 1960, was renamed Fukien Provincial Kinmen Senior High School in 1972, and became National Kinmen Senior High School in 1984. Vocational programs, first established in 1968, continue to run alongside the academic track today.

國立金門高級中學創校於民國40年（1951年），由兩所私立初級中學合併而成，初名「福建省立金門中學」。建校初期即深受戰地生活影響：民國43年（1954年）因「九三炮戰」而遷校避難；民國47年（1958年）「八二三炮戰」爆發後，921名學生於同年10月疏遷至台灣本島。學校於民國49年（1960年）在現址復校，民國61年（1972年）更名為「福建省立金門高級中學」，民國73年（1984年）改制為「國立金門高級中學」。自民國57年（1968年）設立的職業類科，至今仍與普通科並行。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚢 The 1958 Evacuation

八二三炮戰疏遷

Following the "8-23 Artillery Battle" in 1958, 921 students were evacuated to Taiwan for their safety — one of the most dramatic episodes in the school's history.

🏛️ Formed From Two Schools

兩校合併創立

The school was founded in 1951 through the merger of two existing private junior high schools, becoming Kinmen's provincial-level secondary school.

🎓 Academic and Vocational Tracks

普通科與職業類科並行

Vocational programs established in 1968 continue to run alongside the school's academic senior-high track, giving students multiple pathways after graduation.
