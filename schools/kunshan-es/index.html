Kunshan Elementary School

臺南市永康區崑山國民小學

Named after neighboring Kun Shan University, this school is known for its open, "not a single barred window" campus design and its own private educational foundation.

校名源自鄰近的崑山科技大學，以「全校無鐵窗」的開放式校園設計聞名，並設有自己的文教基金會。

About · 學校簡介

An open campus named for its university neighbor.

Kunshan Elementary is named after the neighboring "崑山里" (Kunshan Village), which itself is named for nearby Kun Shan University (崑山科技大學). The school's own materials describe it as growing over the decades from just 6 classes to more than 90, though an exact founding date could not be confirmed from available sources. The campus is known for a distinctive "田"-character (field-shaped) double-corridor classroom design, and the school states it has "not a single barred window" campus-wide — an intentional open-campus design choice. Unusually among Tainan's 170-plus elementary schools, Kunshan operates its own private educational foundation (財團法人文教基金會). Its "成德館" activity center includes a 198-seat international conference hall and four tennis courts, and the school was recognized as a top-performing school for oral/dental health education in the 2025 school year.

崑山國小校名源自鄰近的「崑山里」，而崑山里之名又源自鄰近的崑山科技大學。學校自述班級數歷經數十年由6班成長至逾90班，惟確切創校年份未能自公開資料查證。校園以「田」字型雙走廊教室設計為特色，並自稱「全校無鐵窗」，是有意為之的開放式校園設計。崑山國小在全台南市170餘所國小中，罕見地設有自己的財團法人文教基金會。校內「成德館」活動中心設有198席國際會議廳及四座網球場，並於114學年度（2025年）獲評為口腔／牙齒保健教育績優學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🪟 Not a Single Barred Window

全校無鐵窗

The school's "田"-character double-corridor classroom design and its "not a single barred window" campus is an intentional, unusually open design choice for a Taiwanese elementary school.

🏛️ Its Own Private Foundation

自設文教基金會

Kunshan Elementary operates its own private educational foundation, reportedly a rarity among Tainan's 170-plus elementary schools.

🎾 A Conference Hall and Four Tennis Courts

成德館設施

🦷 Recognized for Dental Health Education

口腔保健績優

The school was recognized as a top-performing school for oral and dental health education in the 2025 school year.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of a university-anchored neighborhood.

🎓

The school and its namesake village sit adjacent to Kun Shan University, part of the private-university cluster that has helped urbanize Yongkang's northern edge since the 1960s–80s. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
