About · 學校簡介

Over a century at the heart of Shengang.

Shengang Elementary School opened its first classes in 1902 inside Shunji Temple, a local folk-religion temple, linking the school's origin directly to the district's traditional village religious life. Now more than 120 years old, it is Shengang's oldest and most central school. In 1935, the campus was struck by the devastating Central Taiwan earthquake, which collapsed eight classrooms and caused 189 student casualties — a tragedy the school has carried in its long institutional memory ever since.

神岡國小於1902年在順天宮（地方民間信仰廟宇）內開始上課，本校創校淵源因此與地方傳統村落宗教生活緊密相連。至今逾120年校史，是神岡地區歷史最悠久、位居核心的學校。1935年，校園受臺灣中部大地震重創，8間教室倒塌、189名學生罹難或受傷，這場悲劇長久留存於學校的歷史記憶中。
