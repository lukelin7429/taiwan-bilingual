About · 學校簡介

Built for a new university town.

Longpu Elementary's name was approved in 2001, and its preparatory office opened in 2008, with full grades 1–6 enrollment reached by 2012. The school was established as part of the broader "Taipei University Special District" plan, conceived alongside the founding of National Taipei University together with two elementary and two junior-high schools. Today Longpu runs a bilingual experimental education program, reflecting its identity as a school built for a new, internationally minded university town.

龍埔國小校名於民國90年（2001年）核定，籌備處於民國97年（2008年）成立，至民國101年（2012年）達到一至六年級全額招生。學校是「臺北大學特定區」整體計畫的一環，隨國立臺北大學設校同步規劃，與另外一所國小、兩所國中共同納入計畫。如今龍埔國小推動雙語實驗教育課程，展現其做為一所因應新興、具國際視野大學城而生的學校定位。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Born from a university town's creation.

🎓

Longpu Elementary's creation is directly tied to the establishment of National Taipei University and the transformation of Sanxia's Beida area into a modern university town, one of the district's most significant recent chapters. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
