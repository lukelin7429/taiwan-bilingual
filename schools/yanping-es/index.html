About · 學校簡介

A century-old name in a brand-new building.

Yanping Elementary was founded on April 1, 1939, during the Japanese colonial era, as Kawai (河合) Public School, named for the nearby Kawai-chō district. Students initially boarded at Yuanshan National School before the dedicated campus site — between the old Taiping-chō and Dalongdong-chō areas — was requisitioned in 1940, and the school moved there in 1941, becoming "河合國民學校." It was renamed "延平國民學校" after WWII, then "延平國民小學" in 1968 under the nine-year compulsory education reform. The school underwent a full campus demolition and rebuild starting in 2017, with students relocated temporarily to a satellite campus at Lanzhou Junior High during construction; the new four-story-above, two-story-below building, including a city-operated underground parking structure, was completed in 2021.

延平國小創校於1939年（昭和14年）4月1日日治時期，初名「河合公學校」，因鄰近河合町而得名。學生最初寄讀於圓山國民學校，直到1940年徵收位於太平町與大龍峒町之間的校地，1941年遷入現址，更名「河合國民學校」。二戰後更名「延平國民學校」，1968年九年國教實施後改稱「延平國民小學」。學校自2017年起進行全面拆除重建，施工期間學生暫遷至蘭州國中衛星校區上課；地上四層、地下二層（含市府經營之地下停車場）的新校舍於2021年完工。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Between two historic neighborhoods.

🏮

Yanping Elementary sits between the historic Taiping-chō and Dalongdong neighborhoods, two of the core sub-areas that define old Datong District. Its very campus site was carved out of the boundary between these two communities during the Japanese colonial era, making the school a literal meeting point of Datong's historic geography.
