Youchang Junior High School

About · 學校簡介

A school that started by borrowing its neighbor's classrooms.

Youchang Junior High opened in September 1973, with its inaugural class of six sections and 317 students initially borrowing classrooms from neighboring Youchang Elementary School before its own campus was ready. The roughly 3.5-hectare campus includes teaching buildings named for the traditional virtues of benevolence, righteousness, and peace ("仁愛," "信義," "和平"), alongside buildings named "莊敬," "自強," and "勤學." The school operates under the motto "Propriety, Righteousness, Integrity, and Shame" (禮義廉恥), and its alumni include singer Yoga Lin (孔令奇), who appeared on the cover of the 2008 yearbook, and Chan Shun-kuei, a former deputy administrator of Taiwan's Environmental Protection Administration.

右昌國中創立於民國62年（1973年）9月，首屆招收一年級6班共317名學生，初期借用右昌國小教室上課。校地約3.5公頃，設有以「仁愛」、「信義」、「和平」為名的教學大樓，以及「莊敬」、「自強」、「勤學」等棟。校訓為「禮義廉恥」，知名校友包括歌手孔令奇（曾為2008年畢業紀念冊封面人物）及前行政院環境保護署副署長詹順貴。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A name shared with its elementary neighbor.

🏘️

The school's name, "Youchang" (右昌), descends from the Qing-dynasty village name "右沖庄" — a name it shares with neighboring Youchang Elementary School, both rooted in the same historic settlement in northern Nanzih. See the Kaohsiung county page for more.
