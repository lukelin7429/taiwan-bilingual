About · 學校簡介

Anchoring Baihe since "Diamboakou."

Baihe Elementary's roots reach back to 1898, when it opened as the "Chiayi Japanese-Language Training Institute, Diamboakou Branch Classroom," under Baihe's historic pre-Japanese-era place name, "Diamboakou" (店仔口). It was renamed "Diamboakou Public School" later that year, then "Baihe Public School" in 1914. After the war it became "Tainan County Baihe Town No. 1 National School" (1946), then "Baihe Town Baihe National School" (1947), and took its current name under nine-year compulsory education in 1968, with its current municipal-district name following the 2010 Tainan city-county merger. Sitting on Sanmin Road in Baihe's town center near the Baihe Reservoir area, it is one of the oldest schools in the district.

白河國小前身為明治31年（1898年）設立的「嘉義國語傳習所店仔口分教場」，「店仔口」即白河在日治以前的舊地名。同年更名「店仔口公學校」，大正3年（1914年）改名「白河公學校」。戰後歷經「臺南縣白河鎮第一國民學校」（1946年）、「白河鎮白河國民學校」（1947年），民國57年（1968年）九年國教後改為現名，民國99年（2010年）縣市合併後改為市轄區名。校址位於白河市中心三民路、鄰近白河水庫一帶，是區內歷史最悠久的學校之一。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Baihe, land of lotus and hot springs.

🪷

Baihe District is known across Taiwan for its lotus fields, celebrated each summer at the Baihe Lotus Festival (白河蓮花節), and for the Guanziling (關子嶺) hot-springs resort in its mountainous east. As Baihe's original, oldest school under its historic "Diamboakou" name, Baihe Elementary has anchored the town center through the district's transitions from rice and sugar farming to today's lotus-farming and hot-springs tourism economy.
