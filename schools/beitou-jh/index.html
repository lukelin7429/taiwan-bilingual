Beitou Junior High School

臺北市立北投國民中學

A public junior high founded in 1946, sitting on Hot Spring Road in the heart of Beitou's historic hot-spring resort district.

About · 學校簡介

A postwar school on Hot Spring Road.

Beitou Junior High was founded in June 1946, petitioned into being by local education advocates building on a Japanese-era elementary institution. For a period around 1973–74 the school operated as a girls-only institution, shortly before the surrounding region was transferred to Taipei City administration in 1974. Its softball team, established in 2011, went on to win the regional league in the 107th academic year, and the school's original 1946 founding-cornerstone monument has since been designated a protected cultural heritage asset, marking it as part of Taiwan's postwar education history.

北投國中創校於民國35年（1946年）6月，由地方教育人士倡議設立，前身可追溯至日治時期的一所小學機構。民國62至63年（1973–74）間，學校曾一度改制為女子中學，隨後該地區於民國63年（1974年）劃入臺北市行政管轄。成立於民國100年（2011年）的壘球隊，曾於107學年度贏得分區聯賽冠軍；校內民國35年（1946年）的開校紀念碑，如今已被列為文化資產保存標的，見證台灣戰後教育發展史。

Local Culture · 在地文化

On Hot Spring Road.

♨️

Beitou Junior High's own address places it directly on Wenquan ("Hot Spring") Road, right inside Beitou's historic hot-spring resort district — the geothermal valley that has drawn bathers to the area since the Japanese colonial era. See the Taipei county page for more on the region.
