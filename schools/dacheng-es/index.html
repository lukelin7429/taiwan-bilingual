About · 學校簡介

From near-closure to a national arts model.

Dacheng Elementary School was founded in 1960. Facing enrollment as low as about 20 students, the school reinvented itself as Taiwan's first arts experimental elementary school (全台第一所藝術實驗小學) and Pingtung County's third privately-managed public school (公辦民營), reversing a trend toward closure. It now partners with Taipei National University of the Arts and Cloud Gate Dance Theatre for arts resources and curriculum, drawing national media attention for how a tiny rural school gained access to major Taipei-based cultural institutions. It is also recognized as an outstanding Hakka-culture elementary school (屏東優良客家小學) in local branding.

大成國小創校於民國49年（1960年）。面對曾低至約20人的招生人數，學校轉型為全台第一所藝術實驗小學，也是屏東縣第三所公辦民營學校，扭轉了原本走向裁併的趨勢。如今學校與國立臺北藝術大學及雲門舞集合作，取得藝術資源與課程支持，這所偏鄉小校如何獲得台北重要文化藝術機構資源的故事，也引發全國媒體關注。學校也在地方品牌推廣中被列為「屏東優良客家小學」。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Hakka farming roots, reborn through the arts.

🌾

Xinpi Township is a Hakka farming township with a history rooted in sugarcane and betel-nut cultivation. Dacheng's rural setting and small size made it a candidate for the arts-experimental transformation that has since put it on the national map. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
