About · 學校簡介

A mountainside school built on clear water.

Qingshui Elementary sits at the foot of Bagua Mountain in Shetou Township, in an area long known for its notably pure water quality — the source of the school's name, which literally means "clear water." Its educational philosophy is built on the values of sustainability, gratitude, and the humanities, and its setting places it close to local landmarks including Qingshui Yansi Temple, Qingsheng Palace, the Eighteen Bends Ancient Trail, and the Changqing Cycling Path.

清水國小位於社頭鄉八卦山腳下，當地因水質格外清澈而聞名，這也是校名「清水」的由來。學校的教育理念建立在永續、感恩與人文精神之上，校園鄰近清水岩寺、清聖宮、十八彎古道與長青自行車道等在地景點。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛰️ At the Foot of Bagua Mountain

八卦山腳下的校園

The school's location at the base of Bagua Mountain gives it a naturally scenic setting, long recognized locally for the clarity and purity of its water.

🌱 Sustainability and Gratitude

永續與感恩教育

The school builds its educational philosophy around sustainability, gratitude, and the humanities, weaving these values through everyday classroom life.

🛕 Neighbor to Qingshui Yansi Temple

鄰近清水岩寺

Students grow up beside Qingshui Yansi Temple and Qingsheng Palace, centers of local Taoist culture, along with the scenic Eighteen Bends Ancient Trail.

🚲 On the Changqing Cycling Path

緊鄰長青自行車道

The nearby Changqing Cycling Path connects the school to the surrounding hillside trails, linking outdoor recreation with everyday school life.
