About · 學校簡介

Named for a life of quiet service.

Huaren Elementary began in 1959 as a branch class of Yichang Elementary, was upgraded to a branch school in 1964, and became fully independent in 1968. The school takes its name from Qiu Huaren (1885–1953), a local figure who resisted Japanese colonial police, fled to Ji'an, and spent years teaching farming techniques and Chinese literacy to local youth. After Taiwan's retrocession, Qiu continued his community service work until his death at the age of 81 — a legacy of quiet, sustained dedication that the school's name now carries forward.

化仁國小於1959年以宜昌國小分班形式創立，1964年升格為分校，1968年正式獨立設校。學校校名取自邱化仁（1885–1953），這位當地志士曾為躲避日本殖民警察而逃至吉安，多年來教導當地青年農業技術與漢文識字。台灣光復後，邱化仁持續投入社區服務工作，直至81歲辭世——這份默默耕耘的精神，如今由校名延續傳承。
