About · 學校簡介

A child of Zhonghe's growth years.

Fuxing Elementary School was established around 1978 as one of several schools spun off from the historic Zhonghe Elementary School, as the district's population outgrew its original "mother school." The school holds an annual anniversary sports meet, part of a school culture built around its founding story as a child of Zhonghe's postwar growth.

復興國小約創立於民國67年（1978年），是中和國小因學區人口成長而分設出的多所學校之一。學校每年舉辦校慶運動會，延續著身為中和戰後人口成長潮產物的辦學脈絡。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Absorbing a population boom.

Fuxing Elementary is part of a wave of schools founded to absorb Zhonghe's 1970s–80s population boom, as the district's original mother school could no longer hold all its students. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
