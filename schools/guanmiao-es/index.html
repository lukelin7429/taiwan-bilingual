Guanmiao Elementary School

關廟國小

The oldest and largest school in Guanmiao District, founded in 1899 inside the Guandi Temple that gave the district its name, and the "mother school" of several village schools nearby.

About · 學校簡介

Founded inside the temple that named the district.

Guanmiao Elementary School was founded in 1899, initially as "Guandi Temple Public School" (關帝廟公學校), using the Guandi Temple itself as its first schoolhouse — a direct link to the temple that gave Guanmiao District its name. It was renamed multiple times through the Japanese and postwar periods, becoming Guanmiao Public School in 1921, Guanmiao National School in 1941, and taking its current name, Guanmiao Elementary School, in 1968. New buildings on the current site were completed in 1931. As the oldest and largest school in the district, it is effectively the "mother school" from which several other Guanmiao-area elementary schools, including Xinguang and Shenkeng, originally split off as branch classrooms.

關廟國小創立於明治32年（1899年），初名「關帝廟公學校」，最初借用關帝廟作為校舍——與為關廟區命名的關帝廟有直接淵源。歷經日治與戰後多次更名：大正10年（1921年）改名關廟公學校，昭和16年（1941年）改為關廟國民學校，民國57年（1968年）定名為現在的關廟國小。現址新校舍於民國20年（1931年）落成。作為全區歷史最悠久、規模最大的學校，關廟國小可謂多所關廟地區國小的「母校」，包括新光國小與深坑國小，最初皆是由關廟國小分出的分校。

Local Culture · 在地文化

The temple that gave Guanmiao its name.

⛩️

Its founding inside the Guandi Temple directly ties the school to the temple worship of Guan Yu that gives Guanmiao District (literally "Guan[di] Temple District") its name — arguably the most concrete school–place-name connection anywhere in the district.
