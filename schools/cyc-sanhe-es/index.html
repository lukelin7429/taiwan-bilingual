About · 學校簡介

A community elementary school in Dalin Township.

Sanhe Elementary School (formally 嘉義縣大林鎮三和國民小學) is a public elementary school serving Dalin Township in Chiayi County. The school maintains an active official website with regularly updated news and photo galleries, and participates in county-level systems for cloud-based attendance management, school lunch administration, and online learning. Recent school activities documented on its site include a spring hiking outing for younger grades and a Lantern Festival performance for the local community.

三和國小（全名：嘉義縣大林鎮三和國民小學）是一所服務嘉義縣大林鎮學童的公立國民小學。學校設有持續更新的官方網站，並使用縣府雲端到課系統、營養午餐管理平台與線上學習平台等系統。校網近期紀錄的活動包括低年級「拜訪春天」健行活動，以及大林鎮元宵節社區表演。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥾 Seasonal Outdoor Learning

四季戶外學習

The school runs seasonal outdoor activities for its students, including a "Visiting Spring" hiking event for lower-grade classes.

🏮 Community Lantern Festival Ties

參與元宵節社區活動

Students have taken part in Dalin Township's Lantern Festival (元宵節) performances, connecting the school to the wider township's cultural calendar.

💻 Connected to County Digital Systems

縣府數位系統整合

Sanhe Elementary's website links to Chiayi County's cloud attendance management, school lunch fund platform, and online learning portal.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Dalin Township's community life.

🌾

Sanhe Elementary sits within Dalin Township, a central Chiayi County community also home to Nanhua University — the township blends rural agricultural life with a growing higher-education presence. See the Chiayi County page for more on the region.
