About · 學校簡介

Blending craft, folk performance, and modern skills.

Jianguo Elementary was established in 1989 in Yingge District, the town known across Taiwan as “Jingdezhen of Taiwan” for its centuries-old ceramics industry. The school has built a distinctive set of specialty programs, including information technology education, ceramic arts, a wind and marching band, lion dance, and diabolo (扯鈴) performance — blending modern tech skills with traditional craft and folk-performance training.

建國國小創立於民國78年（1989年），位於有「台灣景德鎮」之稱的鶯歌區。學校發展出多項特色課程，包括資訊科技教育、陶藝、管樂與行進樂隊、舞獅，以及扯鈴表演，將現代科技能力與傳統工藝、民俗技藝訓練融合並重。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏺 Ceramic Arts on Campus

校園陶藝課程

Reflecting Yingge's identity as “Taiwan's Jingdezhen,” Jianguo teaches ceramic arts alongside its core curriculum, connecting students to the district's craft traditions.

🥁 A Marching Band and Lion Dance Troupe

管樂隊與舞獅隊

The school fields both a wind and marching band and a lion dance troupe, giving students hands-on experience in music and traditional folk performance.

🪀 Diabolo (扯鈴) Performance

扯鈴特色表演

Students train in diabolo (扯鈴), a traditional Chinese performance skill that has become one of the school's signature extracurricular activities.

💻 Information Technology Education

資訊科技教育

Alongside its arts and folk-performance programs, the school emphasizes information technology education, preparing students with practical digital skills.
