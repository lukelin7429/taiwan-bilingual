Taixing Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

An "Elementary School in the Clouds."

Taixing Elementary is known locally as the "Elementary School in the Clouds" (雲端上的小學) for its dramatic mountain setting. Since 2018, it has operated as an Atayal indigenous experimental education school, implementing a curriculum rooted in Atayal ethnic education. In 2020, the school completed a new teaching building whose design incorporates motifs drawn from the "Three Treasures of the Atayal," physically embedding indigenous cultural identity into the campus itself.

泰興國小因其壯麗的山區景色而有「雲端上的小學」之美稱。自2018年起，學校轉型為泰雅族原住民實驗教育學校，實施以泰雅族民族教育為核心的課程。2020年完工的新教學大樓，其建築設計融入「泰雅三寶」的文化元素，將原住民文化認同實際融入校園建築之中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

☁️ The Elementary School in the Clouds

雲端上的小學

Perched in Taian's mountains, the school earned its poetic local nickname from its scenic, cloud-wrapped setting — one of Miaoli's most striking school locations.

🪶 Atayal Indigenous Experimental Education

泰雅族實驗教育

Since 2018, Taixing has operated as a full Atayal indigenous experimental school, building its curriculum around Atayal ethnic education rather than treating it as a supplement.

🏛️ A Building Shaped by the "Three Treasures"

融入泰雅三寶意象

The new 2020 teaching building was designed around motifs of the "Three Treasures of the Atayal," turning the campus architecture itself into a lesson in cultural heritage.
