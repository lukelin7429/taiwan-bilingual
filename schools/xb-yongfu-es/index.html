About · 學校簡介

A newer school built around sustainability.

Yongfu Elementary was approved in March 1984 and formally established that August, making it one of Sanchong's newer schools, founded as the district's residential growth pushed further out from its historic core. The school has built its identity around sustainability and international education, organizing student projects around the UN's Sustainable Development Goals and integrating STEAM and technology learning throughout the curriculum. An active English reader's-theater program adds a language-learning dimension to student life.

永福國小於民國73年（1984年）3月10日獲准籌設，同年8月1日正式成立，是三重較晚設立的學校之一，反映當時三重住宅發展持續向外擴張、超越原本的核心區域。學校以永續發展與國際教育為特色，帶領學生圍繞聯合國永續發展目標（SDGs）進行專題製作，並將STEAM與科技教育融入課程之中。積極推動的英語讀者劇場活動，也為學生生活增添語言學習的面向。

🌍 SDGs in the Classroom
