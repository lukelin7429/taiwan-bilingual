About · 學校簡介

A community anchor in southern Yilan City.

Fuxing Junior High School was approved for establishment in 1968 as part of Taiwan's rollout of nine-year compulsory education, with its first building completed and occupied the following year. Located in the center of southern Yilan City near the county cultural center, the school created a Music Gifted Class in 1994 and established Yilan County's first school teachers' association in 1996. In 2000, it hosted the founding ceremony of Yilan Community College (宜蘭社區大學) on its campus. The school's motto — "Public-spirit, Sincerity, Diligence, Perseverance" (公、誠、勤、毅) — reflects its long-standing values.

復興國中於民國57年（1968年）核准設立，為配合九年國教政策而興建，隔年（1969年）完成首棟校舍並啟用。學校位於宜蘭市南側中心地帶，鄰近縣立文化中心，1994年成立音樂資優班，1996年成立宜蘭縣第一個教師會。2000年，宜蘭社區大學的成立大會即於本校校園舉行。校訓「公、誠、勤、毅」體現學校長久以來的辦學精神。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎵 A Music-Gifted Class Since 1994

音樂資優班

Established in 1994, the school's Music Gifted Class has given musically talented students a dedicated track for three decades.

🎓 Birthplace of Yilan Community College

宜蘭社區大學創校地

Yilan Community College held its founding ceremony on this campus in 2000, linking Fuxing Junior High to the county's adult and lifelong-learning movement.

🤝 Yilan County's First Teachers' Association

宜蘭縣第一個教師會

In 1996, the school established Yilan County's first school-level teachers' association, a milestone in local educator organizing.

🏛️ Near the County Cultural Center

鄰近縣立文化中心

Its location in central southern Yilan City, close to the county cultural center, places students near a hub of local arts and civic life.
