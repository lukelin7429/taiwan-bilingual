About · 學校簡介

Twin lakes, twin villages, one small school.

Shuanghu Elementary began in August 1952 as Qimei Elementary's "West Lake Branch," was reorganized as the "East-West Lake Branch School" in 1955, and became a fully independent school in August 1959, taking its current name in 1968. Its name, "Shuanghu" ("Twin Lakes"), reflects its role serving both Donghu (East Lake) and Xihu (West Lake) villages. Enrollment peaked in 1982 at 55 students and has declined steadily since, a pattern common to many of Penghu's small outer-island schools — but the school has continued on, holding its 62nd graduation ceremony in recent years.

雙湖國小創立於民國41年（1952年）8月，原為七美國小「西湖分校」，民國44年（1955年）改制為「東西湖分校」，民國48年（1959年）8月正式獨立設校，民國57年（1968年）定名為「雙湖國民小學」。校名「雙湖」取自其服務東湖、西湖兩村之意。學生人數於民國71年（1982年）達到高峰55人，此後逐年遞減，這也是澎湖許多離島小校共同面臨的處境——但學校仍持續辦學，近年舉辦了第62屆畢業典禮。
