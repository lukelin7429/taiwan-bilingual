About · 學校簡介

A village named for the field that built its school.

Xuetian Elementary carries one of the most distinctive founding stories in Fuli Township. As early as 1904, villagers from Fuli's two largest settlements jointly reclaimed a stretch of wasteland and farmed it communally, using the harvest income specifically to fund the construction of a local public school. The practice left such an impression that in 1946 the settlement itself was renamed "Xuetian" — literally "school field." The school opened as a branch class in 1947, became a full branch campus in 1949, and gained full independence in August 1956, taking its present name in 1968. Today it sits at Hualien County's southernmost point and holds a designated focus on Indigenous education.

學田國小的創校故事，在富里鄉眾多學校中格外特別。早在明治37年（1904年），富里兩大聚落的居民便共同開墾荒地並集體耕作，將收穫所得專門用於興建地方公學校。這項傳統影響深遠，以致民國35年（1946年），這個聚落本身便被命名為「學田」——字面意思即為「用來辦學的田地」。學校於民國36年（1947年）以分班形式成立，民國38年（1949年）升格為分校，民國45年（1956年）8月正式獨立設校，民國57年（1968年）定名為現名。如今學校位於花蓮縣最南端，並設有原住民教育重點班。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fuli Township, rice country at the valley's edge.

🌾

Fuli Township is Hualien County's southernmost township, a rice-farming community near the Tropic of Cancer at the southern tip of the East Rift Valley. Xuetian's communal "school field" origin captures the cooperative, land-rooted spirit of this farming region better than almost any other local story.
