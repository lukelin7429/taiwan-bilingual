Xianglin Elementary School

香林國民小學

Sitting at 2,195 meters elevation near Alishan's historic scenic area, this small school proudly calls itself Taiwan's "highest institute of learning."

海拔2,195公尺，自稱「全國最高學府」的阿里山小校。

About · 學校簡介

The "highest institute of learning."

Xianglin Elementary traces its origins to 1914, when it opened as "Alishan Ordinary Elementary School" for the children of Japanese settlers, later expanding with a higher-elementary division in 1916. The school was reorganized and renamed several times over the following decades — becoming "Alishan National School" in 1941, renamed again in December 1945, then "Xianglin National School" in May 1947, before settling on its current name in 1968. Enrollment peaked at over 400 students in the 1950s; today it is a very small, extremely remote mountain school, self-branded as Taiwan's "highest institute of learning" — a well-known pun on its 2,195-meter elevation.

香林國小前身可追溯至1914年設立的「阿里山尋常小學校」，最初為日籍移民子弟設校；1916年增設高等科，改稱「阿里山尋常高等小學校」。歷經數十年多次改制更名——昭和16年（民國30年，1941年）改為「阿里山國民學校」，民國34年（1945年）12月再度更名，民國36年（1947年）5月改為「香林國民學校」，最終於民國57年（1968年）定為現名。1950年代學校人數曾逾400人，如今則是極為偏遠的小型山區學校，自詡為「全國最高學府」——巧妙呼應其海拔2,195公尺的地理位置。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛰️ Taiwan's Highest Institute of Learning

全國最高學府

At 2,195 meters elevation, Xianglin's self-branded title as the "highest institute of learning" is both literally true and a well-loved local pun.

📖 A Well-Loved Reading Room

亮眼的閱讀書軒

The school's on-campus reading room has earned recognition as a standout feature — a bright spot in this tiny, extremely remote mountain school.

🌱 Environmental Certification

環境教育認證系統

Xianglin holds Environmental Education Certification, building its "生活" (living skills) and "生命" (life understanding) character-education themes around its unique mountain-top setting.

📉 From 400 Students to a Handful

從400人到極偏小校

Enrollment peaked at over 400 in the 1950s; today the school serves a much smaller community, reflecting the changing population of the high mountains.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where forestry history meets sunrise tourism.

🌄

Located at Alishan's main scenic area near the historic Alishan Forest Railway terminus and the famous sunrise-viewing tourist area, Xianglin Elementary embodies the intersection of Japanese-era forestry development and the modern Alishan tourism and railway culture that draws visitors from around the world.
