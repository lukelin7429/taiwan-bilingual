About · 學校簡介

An early post-war school with a wide influence.

Lukang Junior High is one of the earliest post-war junior high schools in the area, with its founding principal Ye Zhi-ting serving from 1946 to 1948. The school has had 13 principals across its history, including Wu Ying-si (1979–1987), its first female principal. Historically, Lukang Junior High served as the administrative "parent" school for Hsiushui Junior High School, which began as one of its branch classes in the early 1960s before growing into a fully independent school. Today the school maintains an active alumni association and social media presence.

鹿港國中是戰後地區最早設立的國中之一，創校校長葉志挺自民國35年（1946年）至民國37年（1948年）任職。學校歷經13任校長，其中吳英絲校長（1979-1987年在任）為該校首位女性校長。歷史上，鹿港國中曾是秀水國中的行政「母體」學校——秀水國中最初於1960年代初期以其分班形式起步，後來才發展成為獨立學校。如今學校仍設有活躍的校友會與社群媒體專頁。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Founding Institution

秀水國中的母體

Hsiushui Junior High School began as one of Lukang Junior High's branch classes in the early 1960s, reflecting Lukang's historical role as an educational center for the wider Changhua coastal region.

👩‍🏫 A First for Women's Leadership

首位女性校長

Principal Wu Ying-si led the school from 1979 to 1987, becoming its first female principal across 13 principals in the school's history.
