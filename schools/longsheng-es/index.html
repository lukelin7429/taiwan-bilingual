Longsheng Elementary School

隆聖國小

A public elementary school that grew from a small branch class in Neiliao into a fully independent school, expanding from 6 to 18 classes within a few years.

About · 學校簡介

From a branch class to a school of its own.

Longsheng Elementary began in 1977 as an internal branch class of Wulun Elementary, serving the Neiliao neighborhood as "內寮分校." It became a fully independent school in August 1992 under founding principal Hu Yingjian, starting with just six classes. Between 1995 and 1999, a new 16-classroom building was constructed as the school rapidly expanded from six to eighteen classes, reflecting steady growth in the surrounding community.

隆聖國小於民國66年（1977年）以武崙國小內寮分校的形式起步，服務內寮地區的學童。民國81年（1992年）8月正式獨立設校，首任校長為胡英健，創校初期僅有6個班級。民國84至88年（1995-1999年）間，因應班級數由6班快速成長至18班，學校興建了一棟16間教室的新校舍。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 Grown From a Branch Class

從內寮分校起步

Longsheng began in 1977 as a branch class of Wulun Elementary before becoming a fully independent school in 1992 — a grassroots growth story typical of Keelung's expanding suburbs.

📈 Rapid Growth After Independence

獨立設校後快速擴班

Starting with just six classes in 1992, the school expanded to eighteen classes within a few years, prompting the construction of a new 16-classroom building.

🏘️ Rooted in the Neiliao Neighborhood

深耕內寮里社區

Longsheng continues to serve the Neiliao (內寮) neighborhood of Anle District, the same community it was founded to support back in 1977.
