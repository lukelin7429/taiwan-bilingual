About · 學校簡介

From water-bamboo fields to Jingmei's first junior high.

Jingmei Junior High School was founded in 1968 as the first public junior high school in the Jingmei area, created to meet Taiwan's new nine-year compulsory education reform. Construction began in April 1968, and a four-story building was substantially complete by late August — classes started in early September the same year.

The school was built on what had been low-lying, flood-prone land historically used to grow water bamboo (筊白筍) along the Jingmei River floodplain. Former neighbors on the site included an early apartment block and a chemical factory, since redeveloped into Jingxing Park and the Minsheng Market building.

景美國中創立於1968年（民國57年），為景美地區第一所公立國中，因應九年國民義務教育改革而設立。1968年4月動工，四層樓校舍在8月底已大致完工，同年9月初即開學上課。

校地原為景美溪畔低窪易淹水之地，昔日種植茭白筍。周邊曾有早期公寓與一間化工廠，如今已改建為景興公園與民生市場大樓。
