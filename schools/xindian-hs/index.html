Xindian Senior High School

新店高級中學

About · 學校簡介

A landmark school in the old town center.

Xindian Senior High opened in 1992 as Taiwan Provincial Hsin Tien Senior High School, on a 42,417-square-meter campus along Zhongyang Road in the historic heart of Xindian, near the river that gives the district its name. Today it enrolls around 1,824 students across 51 classes. The school established a dedicated music class in 1995 that remains one of its signature programs, and it maintains sister-school partnerships with institutions in Japan, Malaysia, and South Korea, giving students regular exposure to international exchange.

新店高中創立於民國81年（1992年），前身為臺灣省立新店高級中學，校園占地42,417平方公尺，座落於新店老城區中央路，緊鄰賦予地名由來的新店溪。目前全校約有1,824名學生、51個班級。學校自民國84年（1995年）設立音樂班，至今仍是其特色課程之一，並與日本、馬來西亞、韓國等地學校締結姊妹校，讓學生經常參與國際交流。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎻 A Music Class Since 1995

音樂班特色課程

One of the school's signature programs, the music class has run for three decades, giving students a structured path into performance and music theory alongside academics.

🌏 Sister Schools Across Asia

海外姊妹校交流

Partnerships with schools in Japan, Malaysia, and South Korea give Xindian High students regular opportunities for international exchange.

🏫 A Large Campus in the Old Town

新店老城區大型校園

With roughly 1,824 students across 51 classes on a 42,417 m² site, it is one of Xindian's largest schools, anchored in the district's historic center.

🌊 Beside the River That Named the District

新店溪畔

The campus sits along Zhongyang Road near the Xindian River, the waterway from which the entire district takes its name.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring Xindian's historic core.

🏞️

The school sits at the historic core of Xindian, a district named for the Xindian River that flows past its old town — once a river-trade landing point and now the administrative and commercial heart of the area. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
