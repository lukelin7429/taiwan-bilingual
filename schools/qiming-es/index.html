About · 學校簡介

A school born to serve a famous pilgrimage village.

Qiming Elementary began in 1940 as the Baishatun separate classroom, established near what is today the Baishatun police station. It became an independent school in 1945, relocated to its current site in 1950, and was renamed Qiming that December. Along with Xinpu Elementary, it later lent its name to Qixin Junior High, one of its feeder schools.

啟明國小於1940年（昭和15年）以白沙屯分教場形式創立，地點鄰近今日的白沙屯派出所。1945年獨立設校，1950年遷至現址，同年12月更名為啟明國小。學校後來與新埔國小共同將校名各取一字，成為啟新國中校名的由來。
