About · 學校簡介

A quiet school at the county's southern edge.

Chaoyang Elementary's exact founding date is not documented, but the earliest known record shows its first principal, Xu Zhonghua, serving from August 1960 — suggesting the school was established at or before that time. Since then, the school has had 13 principals, with the current principal in office since August 1, 2025. Chaoyang is a small rural school on the banks of the Zhuoshui River, in the southernmost part of Changhua County, and is known for a quiet, elegant, and hardworking community atmosphere paired with a lively, varied curriculum.

潮洋國小確切創校年份並無明確紀錄，但可考的最早紀錄顯示，首任校長徐鐘華自民國49年（1960年）8月起任職，推測學校建校時間應在此之前。學校歷任13位校長，現任校長自2025年8月1日起接任。潮洋國小是一所座落於濁水溪畔的小型鄉村學校，位處彰化縣最南端，以「縣境之南、濁水之北」的純樸校風聞名，課程活潑多元。

🌊 By the Banks of the Zhuoshui River

濁水溪畔的小校

Chaoyang Elementary sits at the southernmost edge of Changhua County, just north of the Zhuoshui River — a small rural school with a close-knit farming community around it.

The school has been guided by 13 principals over its documented history, with the earliest known serving from 1960 — a long, steady lineage of local leadership.
