About · 學校簡介

Taiwan's largest indigenous-area junior high.

Tongfu Junior High School opened in August 1970 as a branch campus of Xinyi Junior High School, becoming a fully independent school in 1975. In the decades since, it has grown into what is widely described as Taiwan's largest indigenous-area junior high school, with approximately 70% of its student body drawn from the local mountain-indigenous Bunun community. This scale and identity make Tongfu a central institution for Bunun secondary education in Nantou County.

同富國中於民國59年（1970年）8月以信義國中分校形式創立，1975年（民國64年）正式獨立設校。歷經數十年發展，如今已成為公認全台灣規模最大的原住民地區國中，約七成學生來自當地布農族原住民社群。這樣的規模與定位，使同富國中成為南投縣布農族中等教育的核心機構。

🏔️ Taiwan's Largest Indigenous-Area Junior High

全台最大原住民地區國中

Tongfu is widely described as the largest junior high school of its kind in an indigenous area anywhere in Taiwan, a scale unusual for a mountain township school.

約七成布農族學生

Roughly 70% of Tongfu's students are mountain-indigenous Bunun, making the school a central hub for Bunun youth education across Xinyi Township.

🎉 Gateway to the Ear-Shooting Festival

打耳祭文化圈

As part of the Tongfu community, students grow up within reach of the annual Bunun Ear-Shooting Festival (打耳祭), one of the tribe's most important cultural ceremonies.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A Bunun community and Yushan's gateway.

🏹

As Taiwan's largest indigenous-area junior high, Tongfu serves Bunun (布農族) students from across Xinyi Township, the gateway community to Yushan National Park and the annual Bunun Ear-Shooting Festival (打耳祭), one of the tribe's most significant traditional ceremonies.
