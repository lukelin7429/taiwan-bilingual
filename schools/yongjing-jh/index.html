Yongjing Junior High School

永靖國中

A public junior high school founded to give Yongjing Township its own school under a county-wide "one township, one junior high" policy.

在「一鄉鎮一國中」政策下，為永靖鄉而設立的公立國中。

About · 學校簡介

Born from a policy, shaped by a model.

Yongjing Junior High was founded in 1964 as Changhua County, under county leader Lu Shih-ming, pursued a policy of establishing one junior high school per township. The school began as a branch of Beidou Junior High, temporarily using the facilities of Yongjing Elementary School before relocating to its own campus in 1965 and gaining independent status as Changhua County Yongjing Junior High School in 1966. Over the decades, the school developed what became known as the "Yongjing Model" — an aptitude-based instruction and vocational-exploration approach that drew recognition from education experts.

永靖國中創立於民國53年（1964年），在時任彰化縣長呂世明推動「一鄉鎮一國中」政策下設立。學校初期為北斗國中分校，暫借永靖國小校舍上課，民國54年（1965年）遷入自建校舍，民國55年（1966年）正式獨立設校，定名為彰化縣立永靖國民中學。多年來，學校發展出著名的「永靖模式」——一套因材施教並結合職業試探的教學方式，獲得教育界人士的肯定。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Founded by County Policy

因縣府政策而生

The school was established under county leader Lu Shih-ming's "one township, one junior high" policy, giving Yongjing Township its own dedicated secondary school.

🎓 The "Yongjing Model"

永靖模式

The school developed an aptitude-based instruction and vocational-exploration approach known as the "Yongjing Model," recognized by education experts for its effectiveness.

🏗️ From Borrowed Rooms to Its Own Campus

從借用校舍到獨立校園

Starting as a branch of Beidou Junior High using Yongjing Elementary's classrooms, the school moved to its own campus in 1965 and became fully independent in 1966.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Yongjing's community.

🏘️

Yongjing Junior High's founding was tied directly to the local community's need for accessible secondary education, established specifically to give Yongjing Township its own junior high under the county's "one township, one junior high" policy. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
