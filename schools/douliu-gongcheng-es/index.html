Gongcheng Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

Grown through Douliu's postwar expansion.

Gongcheng Elementary was established in 1960. In 1964, it absorbed the Huxi and Sanping village school-zone areas, taking on 9 classes and 10 teachers transferred from Zhenxi Elementary School — a significant expansion that helped make it one of the larger elementary schools in Yunlin County. The school runs an art program with dedicated gallery space for student work, alongside a string orchestra that gives students a structured path into music.

公誠國小創校於民國49年（1960年）。民國53年（1964年），學校併入湖西、三平兩里學區，並自鎮西國小轉入9班、10位教師，是學校規模擴張的重要一步，也讓公誠國小成為雲林縣規模較大的國小之一。學校設有美術課程並配置專屬藝廊展示學生作品，另設弦樂團，為學生提供有系統的音樂學習路徑。
