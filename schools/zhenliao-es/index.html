About · 學校簡介

A tiny school with an outsized web presence.

Zunliao Elementary began in October 1957 as a branch class of Chenggong Elementary School, upgraded to a full branch campus that same month before becoming an independent school on August 1, 1962, under its first principal, Yan Ming-de. It took its current name, Zunliao Elementary School, on August 1, 1968. Despite its small size — a campus of just 5,410 square meters with about 80 students across six classes — the school earned a Special Commendation at the 2011 Taiwan School Web Expo and a Platinum Award for music and arts at the 2012 International Web Expo.

圳寮國小創校於民國46年（1957年）10月，最初為成功國小的分班，同月即升格為分校，並於民國51年（1962年）8月1日在首任校長顏明德的帶領下獨立設校，於民國57年（1968年）8月1日定名為圳寮國民小學。學校規模雖小——校地僅5,410平方公尺，全校約80名學生、6個班級——卻曾在2011年台灣學校網界博覽會獲得特別獎，並於2012年國際網界博覽會音樂藝術類獲得白金獎。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏆 An International Web Expo Award

國際網界博覽會白金獎

In 2012, Zunliao's student web team won a Platinum Award for music and arts at the International Web Expo, a striking achievement for such a small rural school.

🌉 Near the Xiluo Bridge

西螺大橋北端

The school sits near the northern end of the Xiluo Bridge over the Zhuoshui River, anchoring a tight-knit farming village in Xizhou Township.

🏫 A Campus of Just 80 Students

迷你校園、80位學生

With a campus of only 5,410 square meters and about 80 students across six classes, Zunliao is one of the smallest — and closest-knit — schools in the township.
