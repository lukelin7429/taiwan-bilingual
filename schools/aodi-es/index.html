About · 學校簡介

A coastal school with a historic harbor at its doorstep.

Aodi Elementary began in 1913 as a branch of Toushuangxi Public School, becoming an independent school in 1923. Located beside the coastal highway near Aodi fishing harbor, the school has been renamed several times over its history, spanning both the Japanese colonial period and the early years of the Republic of China.

澳底國小於1913年（日治時期）以頭圍公學校分教場的形式設立，1923年正式獨立設校。校址位於沿海公路旁、緊鄰澳底漁港，歷經日治時期與戰後初期，校名歷經多次更迭。

緊鄰澳底漁港

The school sits along the coastal highway right next to Aodi fishing harbor, keeping daily school life closely tied to the rhythms of a working fishing village.

日治時期建校

鄰近歷史登陸地點
