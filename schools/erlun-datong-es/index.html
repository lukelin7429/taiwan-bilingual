Erlun Datong Elementary School

大同國民小學

A rural school south of the Zhuoshui River recognized county-wide for its track and field, wooden ball, martial arts, and calligraphy programs.

About · 學校簡介

Sport and art south of the Zhuoshui River.

Erlun Datong Elementary is located south of the Zhuoshui River in a rural farming area of Erlun Township, where roughly 46% of its students come from disadvantaged backgrounds. Despite its modest size, the school has built a strong reputation across Yunlin County for its programs in track and field, wooden ball, martial arts, and calligraphy and painting. Its precise founding date is not confirmed in available records, but its athletic and artistic achievements have given the small rural school an outsized regional profile.

大同國小位於濁水溪以南的二崙鄉農村地區，學生中約有46%來自弱勢家庭。儘管規模不大，學校在田徑、木球、武術以及書畫等項目上，於雲林縣內建立了良好的聲譽。確切創校年代目前尚無確切紀錄可考，但其體育與藝文成就，讓這所鄉村小學在地區內享有相對突出的知名度。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏃 Track and Field Strength

田徑表現優異

The school's track and field program has earned county-wide recognition, a notable achievement for a small rural elementary school.

🥋 Wooden Ball and Martial Arts

木球與武術訓練

Students take part in wooden ball (木球) and martial arts programs, both recognized across Yunlin County for their quality.

🖌️ Calligraphy and Painting

書法與繪畫特色

A calligraphy and painting program rounds out the school's offerings, giving students a creative outlet alongside its athletic strengths.

🌊 South of the Zhuoshui River

濁水溪畔農村校園

Situated among rice and vegetable farms south of the Zhuoshui River, the school reflects the rural character of this stretch of Erlun Township.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rice, vegetables, and Zhang-Zhou roots.

🧄

Situated among rice and vegetable farms south of the Zhuoshui River, the school reflects Erlun's Zhang-Zhou Hoklo agricultural heritage and its reliance on garlic and rice cultivation. The river to the north has long shaped the farmland and settlement patterns of this part of the township. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
