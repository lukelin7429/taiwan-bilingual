Jinhu Elementary School

錦湖國小

A tiny riverine school between the Bazhang and Jishui rivers whose students have traveled four consecutive years to Penghu's Dongji Island for a beach-cleanup program.

座落於八掌溪與急水溪之間的迷你學校，學生連續四年前往澎湖東吉嶼淨灘。

About · 學校簡介

A small school between two rivers, reaching outward.

Jinhu Elementary's exact founding date is not confirmed in available records, but the school today is very small — about six classes and 30 students in the 2022–23 school year — serving four hamlet communities: Duzaitou, Xinduzaitou, Beima, and Baimi. It is bounded by the Bazhang River to the north and Jishui River to the south, in the district's historic salt-producing lowland, near the Nankunshen Daitian Temple and the Leofoo Wildlife Zoo.

錦湖國小確切創校年份尚待考證，但學校現今規模很小，111學年度僅約6班、30名學生，服務杜仔頭、新杜仔頭、北馬、白米四個聚落。學校北臨八掌溪、南臨急水溪，位於北門區歷史悠久的產鹽低地，鄰近南鯤鯓代天府與六福野生動物園（近學甲）。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏝️ Reaching All the Way to Penghu

連續四年前往東吉嶼淨灘

Students have traveled four consecutive years to Dongji Island in the Penghu archipelago for a beach-cleanup program — a remarkably outward-looking environmental effort for such a small school.

🥁 Lion Dance, Taiko, and Cajón

舞獅、太鼓、木箱鼓社團

The school runs lion-dance, taiko, and cajón (box drum) clubs, giving its small student body a range of performing-arts activities.

🌊 Between Two Rivers

夾於八掌溪與急水溪之間

Jinhu is bounded by the Bazhang River to the north and the Jishui River to the south, sitting in the heart of Beimen's historic salt-producing lowland.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Between two rivers, in salt country.

🌾

Jinhu is a tiny riverine school between two of Beimen's defining waterways, in the heart of the district's historic salt-producing lowland. Its outward-looking environmental program — reaching all the way to Penghu's outer islands — stands in contrast to the school's small, quiet setting.
