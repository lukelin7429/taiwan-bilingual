Taixing Elementary School

台興國民小學

A small public elementary school in Kouhu Township, serving a farming-and-fishing community amid the area's milkfish aquaculture ponds.

口湖鄉的小型公立小學，服務虱目魚養殖漁塭環繞的農漁社區。

About · 學校簡介

A small campus among the fish ponds.

Taixing Elementary began in 1953 as a branch class of Jinhu National School, becoming independent in 1960 and taking its current name in 1968. The school currently operates 6 classes with about 70 students, serving a small farming-and-fishing community whose surrounding landscape is dotted with milkfish aquaculture ponds.

台興國小於民國42年（1953年）以「金湖國小分班」設立，民國49年（1960年）獨立設校，民國57年（1968年）更為現名。學校目前設有6班、約70名學生，服務周邊漁塭遍布的農漁社區。
