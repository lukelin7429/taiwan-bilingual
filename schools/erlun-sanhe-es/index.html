About · 學校簡介

A small school with a big-hearted motto.

Sanhe Elementary is a small rural school serving the Sanhe and Nanzai villages of Erlun Township, a farming community in Yunlin County. Its founding date is not confirmed in available records, but the school is known locally for a motto rooted in the concept of "三德三慧" — three virtues and three wisdoms — a guiding phrase that reflects its character-education philosophy. As with many small township schools in the area, it serves a close-knit rural population shaped by generations of local agriculture.

三和國小是一所服務二崙鄉三和村與南仔村的小型鄉村小學，創校年代目前尚無確切紀錄可考。學校在地方上以「三德三慧」的校訓精神聞名，這句話反映了學校重視品格教育的辦學理念。如同雲林縣許多小型鄉鎮學校一樣，三和國小服務著一個世代務農的緊密社區。

🙏 Three Virtues, Three Wisdoms

三德三慧校訓

The school's guiding motto references "三德三慧" — three virtues and three wisdoms — a character-education philosophy that shapes its approach to student development.

扎根農村生活步調

Local Culture · 在地文化

Erlun's Zhang-Zhou Hoklo heartland.

🧄

Located in Erlun Township's Zhang-Zhou Hoklo heartland, the school serves families rooted in the area's traditional garlic and rice farming economy. Erlun's identity has long been shaped by settlers from Zhangzhou, Fujian, whose descendants still farm the same fields that surround the school today.
