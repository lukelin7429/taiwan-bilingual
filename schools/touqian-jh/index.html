About · 學校簡介

From farming village to fourth junior high.

Touqian Junior High School's preparatory office was established in 1985, and the school opened in 1986 as the fourth junior high built in Xinzhuang. It today runs about 42 classes, including dedicated sports classes at every grade level, and completed a multi-function hall known as the "Lehuo Guan" (樂活館) in 2014.

頭前國中籌備處成立於民國74年（1985年），民國75年（1986年）正式招生，是新莊第四所國中。現有約42班，各年級皆設有體育班，並於民國103年（2014年）完成多功能館「樂活館」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏃 Sports Classes at Every Grade

各年級皆設體育班

Touqian runs dedicated sports classes across all three grade levels, giving athletically inclined students a consistent track through junior high.

🏢 The Lehuo Guan Multi-Function Hall

樂活館

Completed in 2014, the school's "Lehuo Guan" multi-function hall added modern indoor space for sports, assemblies, and school events.

🌾 Named for a Qing-Era Village

校名源自清代村莊頭前庄

The school takes its name from Touqian Zhuang, a farming village renamed "Touqian" during Japanese-era administrative reforms in 1920 — the old settlement is now the site of the Touqian Industrial Park.

Local Culture · 在地文化

From rice paddies to factories.

🏭

The name Touqian traces back to Touqian Zhuang, a Qing-dynasty farming village that was renamed under Japanese-era administrative reform in 1920. That same ground is now home to the Touqian Industrial Park, tracing a direct line from rice paddies to factories over the past century. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
