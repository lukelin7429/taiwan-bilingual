Pengnan Junior High School

澎南國中

A junior high serving Magong's remote southern coastal villages, with a dedicated seagrass education program tied to Penghu's marine ecology.

About · 學校簡介

From a branch campus to a coastal community school.

Pengnan Junior High began in August 1970 (ROC 59) as a branch campus of Zhongzheng Junior High, opening with just three classes and 153 students. It became an independent school, "Penghu County Pengnan Junior High School" (澎湖縣立澎南國民中學), in August 1974 (ROC 63). Enrollment grew steadily, reaching 606 students across 13 classes by 1975 and peaking at 707 students in 16 classes by 1980, before gradually declining from 1994 onward as the county's population shifted. The school marked its 50th anniversary with a community parent-child sports day.

澎南國中前身為中正國中澎南分校，成立於民國59年（1970年）8月，初期僅3班153名學生。民國63年（1974年）8月獨立設校，正式定名「澎湖縣立澎南國民中學」。學生人數穩定成長，民國64年（1975年）達606人（13班），民國69年（1980年）達到高峰707人（16班），其後自民國83年（1994年）起隨人口結構變化逐漸減少。學校曾舉辦五十週年校慶親子運動會慶祝建校半世紀。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 From Branch Campus to Independent School

由分校獨立設校

Pengnan began in 1970 as a small branch campus of Zhongzheng Junior High with just three classes, becoming fully independent in 1974.

🌾 A Wide Coastal School District

涵蓋多個沿海村里

The school serves a wide, remote coastal district covering Wude, Suogang, Tiexian, Jing'an, Fenggui, Shili, and Shanshui villages — classic Penghu fishing communities.

🌿 A Dedicated Seagrass Education Program

海草生態教育專區

The school runs a dedicated seagrass education section with presentations, videos, and student-designed games — a direct tie to Penghu's coastal marine ecology.

🎉 Half a Century of Community Ties

五十週年校慶

The school celebrated its 50th anniversary with a community parent-child sports day, reflecting its long-standing role at the heart of its coastal villages.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Penghu's coastal ecology.

🌊

Pengnan Junior High's school district covers classic fishing villages like Fenggui (風櫃) and Shili (嵵裡), and the school runs a dedicated seagrass education program — with presentations, videos, and student-designed games — that directly connects classroom learning to Penghu's coastal marine ecology and fishing traditions.
