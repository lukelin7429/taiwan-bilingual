Taichung Municipal Longjin Senior High School

臺中市立龍津高級中等學校

About · 學校簡介

Built for a growing harbor-side district.

Longjin Senior High School opened in 2001 as a junior high school, established to relieve overcrowding and traffic-safety concerns in Longjing District as new residential development spread near Taichung Harbor. In 2017, the school was upgraded to a complete secondary school offering both junior and senior high education, becoming Taichung's tenth municipal complete secondary school. Its creation reflects the district's transformation from a quiet coastal town into a fast-growing bedroom community for the wider Taichung metropolitan area.

龍津高中創立於民國90年（2001年），最初為國中部，設校目的是為紓解龍井區因台中港週邊新興住宅開發而日益嚴重的學生擁擠與交通安全問題。民國106年（2017年），學校升格為兼具國中部與高中部的完全中學，成為台中市第十所市立完全中學。學校的設立過程，見證了龍井區從寧靜濱海小鎮轉變為大台中都會區快速成長之衛星城鎮的歷程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Young Complete Secondary School

年輕的完全中學

Opening as a junior high in 2001 and upgraded to a full junior-and-senior secondary school in 2017, Longjin is one of the newest complete secondary schools in the Taichung municipal system.

🚧 Built to Solve a Growing Pain

因應人口成長而設立

The school's founding was a direct response to overcrowding and traffic-safety concerns as Longjing's population grew rapidly near Taichung Harbor.

🏗️ Taichung's Tenth Complete School

全市第十所完全中學

With its 2017 upgrade, Longjin became the tenth municipal complete secondary school in Taichung, joining students from Grade 7 through Grade 12 on one campus.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Longjing, a coastal town turned harbor suburb.

⚓

Longjing District takes its name from a local legend of a "dragon-eye well" (龍目井), and the district has transformed in recent decades from a farming and fishing community on the Dadu River plain into a rapidly growing bedroom community near Taichung Harbor.
