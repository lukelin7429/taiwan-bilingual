About · 學校簡介

Born inside a temple, in 1897.

Taoyuan Elementary traces its origin to a Mandarin-language instruction branch — "國語傳習所桃仔園分教場" — established inside Taoyuan's historic Wenchang Temple (文昌宮) on October 1, 1897, meaning the school's very first classroom was a room inside a temple that still stands in Taoyuan today. It was renamed multiple times through the Japanese era and postwar period: 桃仔園公學校 (1898) → 桃園公學校 (1905) → a new campus built at its current site (1916) → 武陵國民學校 (1941) → 桃園中心國民學校 (1945). Today the school has roughly 1,000 students and 74 teachers under Principal Yang Ya-zhen, with the motto "卓越傳承" (Excellence and Heritage).

桃園國小的歷史源自1897年（明治30年）10月1日在文昌宮內設立的「國語傳習所桃仔園分教場」——換言之，學校最初的教室正是至今仍矗立於桃園的文昌宮內一室。歷經日治與戰後多次更名：1898年改稱桃仔園公學校，1905年改稱桃園公學校，1916年遷至現址新校舍，1941年改名武陵國民學校，1945年再改名桃園中心國民學校。如今全校約有1,000名學生、74位教師，現任校長楊雅真，校訓「卓越傳承」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏮 A Classroom Inside a Temple

文昌宮內的第一間教室

The school's very first classroom in 1897 was a room inside Taoyuan's historic Wenchang Temple, a Confucian/literary temple that still stands in the district today.

🎨 Nearly 40 Years of Visual Arts

1987年創立的美術班

A long-standing visual-arts program, established in 1987, offers dedicated classes for grades 3 through 6.

💃 A 30-Year Children's Dance Ensemble

30年舞蹈團與兒童管弦樂團

The school runs a children's dance ensemble active for over 30 years, alongside a children's orchestra, reflecting a deep and sustained arts tradition.

🏛️ The District's Founding School

桃園教育體系的起點

As the origin point of Taoyuan's modern school system, Taoyuan Elementary predates even the formal Japanese colonial school administration, making it the district's oldest educational institution.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The living root of Taoyuan's school system.

🏮

Taoyuan Elementary is literally the founding school of Taoyuan's entire public education system — its 1897 origin inside Wenchang Temple (a Confucian/literary temple still standing in the district) directly ties it to Taoyuan District's identity as the city's historic administrative and cultural core, predating even the Japanese colonial administration's formal school system.
