About · 學校簡介

Nearly a century of educating Keelung's students.

National Keelung Senior High traces its history back to March 31, 1927, when it was approved as "Taihoku Prefecture Keelung Middle School" under Japanese colonial rule. The school moved to a new campus in Badou in 1929. After Taiwan's retrocession, it was renamed Taiwan Provincial Keelung Senior High in 1970 following a merger with the senior division of Keelung Municipal First Middle School, and became "National Keelung Senior High School" in 2000. In 2018, the school began admitting female students, becoming fully coeducational after nine decades as a boys' school.

基隆高中創校可追溯至民國16年（1927年）3月31日，日治時期核准設立「臺北州立基隆中學校」。民國18年（1929年）遷至八斗子新校址。臺灣光復後，於民國59年（1970年）與基隆市立第一中學高中部合併，更名為臺灣省立基隆高級中學，並於2000年改制為「國立基隆高級中學」。2018年起開始招收女學生，結束近九十年的男校歷史，成為男女合校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Nearly a Century of History

近百年建校歷史

Founded in 1927 as a Japanese-era middle school, National Keelung Senior High is one of the oldest secondary schools in the city, with roots reaching back nearly a hundred years.

⚓ A Campus Built at Badou

遷校八斗子

The school moved to its Badou campus in 1929, placing it in one of Keelung's best-known fishing and coastal districts from an early stage in its history.

👩‍🎓 Coeducational Since 2018

2018年起男女合校

After nearly nine decades as a boys' school, National Keelung Senior High began admitting female students in 2018, marking a significant modern milestone.

🔁 Renamed Through Taiwan's History

歷經多次更名與改制

From its Japanese-era founding through a 1970 merger and a 2000 renaming to its current national designation, the school's name changes trace Taiwan's own educational history.
