Junkeng Elementary School

郡坑國小

A century-spanning mountain-village school, built from grassroots effort in 1932 and rebuilt by community volunteers after a 1941 typhoon.

About · 學校簡介

Built by the community, for nearly a century.

Junkeng Elementary School traces its roots to 1932, when a village elder began private Chinese-character instruction in a workshop for the children of Shang-an Village. The school became independent on April 1, 1935. After being damaged by a typhoon in 1941, it relocated and was rebuilt with volunteer community labor. Over 3,000 students have graduated since its founding — a century-long history of grassroots, community-built education that echoes the self-reliant logging-town spirit of the wider Shuili area.

郡坑國小的歷史可追溯至1932年，當時一位地方耆老在工寮中為上安村的孩童開設私塾教授漢字。學校於1935年（民國24年）4月1日正式獨立設校。1941年遭風災損毀後，遷址並由社區志工協力重建。創校至今已有超過3,000名畢業生——這段近百年的草根社區辦學歷史，正呼應著水里鄉整體自力更生的林業小鎮精神。

What makes this school worth knowing.

📜 Roots in Grassroots Instruction

草根私塾起源

The school traces its roots to 1932, when a village elder began private Chinese-character instruction in a workshop for local children — becoming a fully independent school in 1935.

🔨 Rebuilt by Community Hands

社區志工協力重建

After a 1941 typhoon damaged the original site, the school relocated and was rebuilt with volunteer community labor, embodying the self-reliant spirit of the region.

🎓 Over 3,000 Graduates

逾3,000名畢業生

Since its founding, Junkeng Elementary has produced more than 3,000 graduates, a testament to its long-standing role in the Shang-an Village community.

⛰️ Serving a Remote Mountain Village
