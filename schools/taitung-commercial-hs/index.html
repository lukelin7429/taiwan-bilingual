About · 學校簡介

Preparing students for business, since 1958.

The school began in 1958 as the "Taitung County Commercial Vocational Supplementary School," becoming a full vocational school in 1961 and gaining national status in 2000. It was upgraded to provincial status in 1968, and between 2000 and 2018 operated under the comprehensive high school model before transitioning back to a technical-type senior high school in 2018. The campus covers approximately 4.93 hectares and runs both day and evening divisions under the school motto "Sincerity, Simplicity, Diligence, Alertness" (誠樸勤警).

學校於1958年（民國47年）創立，初名「臺東縣立臺東商業職業補習學校」，1961年改制為正式職業學校，2000年改隸國立。1968年升格為省立學校，2000年至2018年間曾採綜合高中學制，2018年轉型為技術型高中。校地面積約4.93公頃，設有日間部與夜間部，校訓為「誠樸勤警」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💼 A Long Commercial-Education Lineage

六十餘年商業教育傳承

Tracing back to 1958, the school has provided commercial and business vocational training in Taitung for more than six decades, evolving through several institutional models along the way.

🌙 Day and Evening Divisions

日間部與夜間部並行

The school runs both day and evening divisions, widening access for students balancing work or other commitments alongside their studies.

🎯 A School Motto That Sticks

校訓「誠樸勤警」

"Sincerity, Simplicity, Diligence, Alertness" has guided the school's character education since its founding decades ago.

🔄 Adapting Through the Decades

歷經多次學制轉型

From supplementary school to full vocational school, to comprehensive high school, and finally technical-type senior high in 2018, the school has continually adapted its model to serve changing educational policy.
