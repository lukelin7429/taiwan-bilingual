About · 學校簡介

A different Indigenous story, in the same township.

Lishan Elementary began in 1949 as a branch of Lunshan Elementary, became independent on February 16, 1952, relocated to its present site the following year, and took its current name in 1968. The school marked its 70th anniversary in 2022. What sets Lishan apart within Zhuoxi Township is its student body: while most Zhuoxi schools serve overwhelmingly Bunun communities, Lishan's Indigenous students — who made up about 98% of enrollment as of 2018 — are roughly half Seediq and half Truku, two ethnically distinct Indigenous peoples with their own languages and traditions.

立山國小於民國38年（1949年）以崙山國小分校的形式創立，民國41年（1952年）2月16日獨立設校，隔年遷至現址，民國57年（1968年）定名為現名，並於民國111年（2022年）舉辦建校七十週年慶祝活動。立山國小在卓溪鄉內的特別之處，在於它的學生組成：當卓溪鄉多數學校以布農族社群為主時，立山國小的原住民學生（截至2018年約佔全校98%）大致各半為賽德克族與太魯閣族——這是兩個擁有各自語言與傳統的不同原住民族群。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🧬 A Seediq–Truku Community

賽德克族與太魯閣族並存

Unlike most schools in Bunun-majority Zhuoxi Township, Lishan's Indigenous students are roughly evenly split between Seediq and Truku heritage — a distinct ethnic makeup within the district.

🎉 Seventy Years and Counting

建校七十週年

Lishan celebrated its 70th anniversary in 2022, tracing its roots back to 1949 as a branch of Lunshan Elementary before becoming independent in 1952.

📊 A Nearly Fully Indigenous School

原住民學生比例逾九成

As of 2018, roughly 98% of Lishan's students were Indigenous — one of the highest concentrations in the county — making cultural identity central to daily school life.

Zhuoxi Township is best known as a Bunun Indigenous mountain district near Yushan National Park, but Lishan Elementary reflects a different thread within that same township — a community rooted in Seediq and Truku heritage instead. See the Hualien schools page for the full township list.
