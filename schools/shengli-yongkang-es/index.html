About · 學校簡介

Built to relieve a crowded, growing district.

Founded in 2002, Shengli Elementary is one of Yongkang District's newest elementary schools, built to relieve overcrowding at neighboring schools as the district's population kept climbing through the 2000s. The school offers EQ/emotional-education and math-logic curricula alongside bilingual instruction, and fields track-and-field, taiko drumming, string orchestra, and choir teams — an unusually arts-heavy activity roster for a school its size.

勝利國小創校於2002年，是永康區最新設立的國小之一，設校目的在於紓解區內學校因2000年代人口持續成長所面臨的擁擠問題。學校開設情緒教育（EQ）及數學邏輯課程，並推動雙語教學，同時設有田徑隊、太鼓隊、弦樂團與合唱團——以校規模而言，藝文性社團活動相當豐富。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Yongkang's last population boom.

📈

Its 2002 founding date situates the school among the last generation of Yongkang schools built purely to absorb the district's suburban population boom, a pattern repeated at neighboring Da Ciao Junior High and Daqiao Elementary. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
