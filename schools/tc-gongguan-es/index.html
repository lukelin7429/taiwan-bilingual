About · 學校簡介

A century of names and moves.

Gongguan Elementary's roots trace back to 1916, when a branch station was first established to serve Han Taiwanese children in Shalu under Taiwan's Japanese-era public school system. The school formally opened in 1926 and went through several relocations and renamings over the following decades before settling into its present form and campus in 1968.

公館國小的歷史可追溯至1916年設立的分教場，屬日治時期公學校制度下服務沙鹿地區台灣人子弟的教育設施。學校於1926年正式設立，其後歷經多次遷校與更名，直到1968年才確立今日的校名與規模。
