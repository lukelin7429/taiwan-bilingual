About · 學校簡介

Taiwan's most beautiful forest school.

Neihu Elementary School, founded in 1956, is famous across Taiwan as the country's "most beautiful forest elementary school" (最美森林小學), its Japanese-inspired wooden campus set among preserved cedar and pine forest along the scenic route to Xitou Forest Recreation Area. The original campus was severely damaged in the catastrophic 1999 921 earthquake — of 293 schools nationwide destroyed by the quake, Neihu was the very last to be rebuilt, reopening only in 2004 after years of careful reconstruction. Today the school opens its grounds free to visitors on weekends and holidays.

內湖國小創立於民國45年（1956年），以日式風格木造校舍聞名全台，享有「最美森林小學」美譽，校園座落於通往溪頭森林遊樂區沿途、保育完善的柳杉與松林之間。原校舍在民國88年（1999年）921大地震中嚴重受損，是全台293所受災學校中最晚完成重建的一所，直到民國93年（2004年）才重新啟用。如今學校於假日與國定假日免費開放校園供民眾參觀。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌲 Taiwan's "Most Beautiful Forest School"

最美森林小學

Neihu is famous nationwide for its Japanese-inspired wooden architecture, set among preserved cedar and pine forest — a campus that looks more like a mountain retreat than a typical school.

🏗️ The Last School Rebuilt After 921

921重建最晚完工

Of the 293 schools nationwide destroyed by the devastating 1999 921 earthquake, Neihu was the very last to be fully rebuilt, finally reopening in 2004.

🚶 Open Free to Visitors

假日免費開放參觀

The school opens its scenic grounds free to the public on weekends and holidays, drawing visitors traveling the popular route to Xitou Forest Recreation Area.

🍃 On the Road to Xitou

溪頭沿線風光

Located along the scenic route to Xitou Forest Recreation Area, one of central Taiwan's most visited mountain destinations, the school shares in the region's forested beauty.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Lugu, home of Dong Ding Oolong.

🍵

Nestled on the road to Xitou in Lugu Township — Taiwan's premier Oolong tea region famous for Dong Ding tea — the school's forest campus reflects the same misty hill terrain that produces the area's celebrated tea. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
