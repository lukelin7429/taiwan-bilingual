About · 學校簡介

Nearly a century of local history, carefully preserved.

Jianxing Elementary School was established on April 1, 1935, during the Japanese colonial era, as a branch educational site of Fangliao Public School at Shuidiliao, moving to its current site later that same year. Over the following decades it changed names several times — becoming independent Shuidiliao Public School in 1938, Shuidiliao National School in 1941, and then, after Taiwan's 1945 retrocession, Kaohsiung County Fangliao Township Shuidiliao National School. It was renamed Jianxing National School in 1950, and took its present name, Jianxing Elementary School, in 1968 under the nine-year compulsory education reform. The school maintains a dedicated online school-history museum documenting these milestones in detail.

建興國小創立於昭和10年（1935年）4月1日，最初為枋寮公學校在水底寮的分教場，同年7月1日遷至現址。其後歷經多次更名：民國27年（1938年）獨立為水底寮公學校，民國30年（1941年）改稱水底寮國民學校，台灣光復後改隸高雄縣枋寮鄉水底寮國民學校，民國39年（1950年）更名建興國民學校，最終於民國57年（1968年）九年國教改制時定名為建興國小。學校並設有線上校史館，詳細記錄這些歷史沿革。

🏛️ Nearly Ninety Years of History

近九十年校史

Founded in 1935 as a branch site in the Shuidiliao area, the school has passed through several names across the Japanese colonial era, postwar retrocession, and the 1968 compulsory-education reform.

💻 A Dedicated School-History Archive

線上校史館

The school maintains its own online history museum (校史館), preserving documentation of its many name changes and milestones for students, alumni, and researchers.

📍 Rooted in Shuidiliao

扎根水底寮

Located in the Shuidiliao area of Fangliao, the school sits within the township's agricultural and aquaculture belt, part of a coastal community built on fishing, mariculture, and rail transport.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fangliao, a coastal farming and fishing community.

🌾

Jianxing Elementary sits in the Shuidiliao area of Fangliao Township, part of a coastal belt known for aquaculture — grouper and whitebait farming — as well as agriculture. The wider township also serves as the gateway to the South-Link Railway, linking this historic school community to Taiwan's scenic southern coast.
