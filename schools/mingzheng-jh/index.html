Mingzheng Junior High School

屏東縣立明正國民中學

Founded in 1946, one of the oldest and largest junior highs in the county seat, and currently the school with the most classes in Pingtung City.

創校於1946年，是屏東市歷史最悠久、規模最大的國中之一，現為全市班級數最多的國中。

About · 學校簡介

From Japanese rule to the county's largest junior high.

Mingzheng Junior High School was founded on June 28, 1946, as "Pingtung City First Junior High School." In 1947 it merged with the city's girls' commercial vocational school, was renamed Mingzheng Junior High School in 1950, and moved to its current Dalian Road site between 1952 and 1953. It took on its "國民中學" designation in 1968. Its long, multi-stage institutional history spans the transition from the Japanese-era to the postwar Taiwanese education system, and Gongzheng Junior High School was originally founded as one of its branches. Today it is the school with the most classes in Pingtung City.

明正國中創立於民國35年（1946年）6月28日，初名「屏東市立第一初級中學」。民國36年（1947年）與市立女子商業職業學校合併，民國39年（1950年）更名為明正初級中學，並於民國41至42年間（1952-53年）遷至現今大連路校址。民國57年（1968年）改制為「國民中學」。學校歷經日治至戰後台灣教育體制轉型的多階段沿革，公正國中即為其分部之一。現為屏東市班級數最多的國中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 The City's Largest Junior High

全市班級數最多

Mingzheng currently has more classes than any other junior high in Pingtung City, a reflection of its long institutional growth.

📜 A Multi-Era History

歷經多次改制更名

From its 1946 founding through a 1947 merger and multiple renamings, the school's history traces the transition from Japanese to postwar Taiwanese education.

🌱 A Parent School to Others

公正國中原為其分部

Gongzheng Junior High School was originally established as a branch of Mingzheng before becoming independent, part of the city's postwar school expansion.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Central to the county seat's education system.

🏙️

One of the oldest and largest junior highs in the county seat, Mingzheng embodies Pingtung City's postwar urban expansion and its centrality to secondary education in the region. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
