About · 學校簡介

A name carrying Kaohsiung's coastal wetland history.

Erling Elementary School was approved for establishment in 1980 and formally founded in July 1981, under its first principal, Zhang Dengshou. Its campus was built out over three construction phases, completed in 1983, 1984, and 1987. The school's name, “二苓” (“second reed”), belongs to a distinctive place-naming pattern found across coastal Kaohsiung, where “苓” (reed/rush) marked the reed-filled wetlands that once lined the shoreline.

二苓國小於民國69年（1980年）核准設校，民國70年（1981年）7月正式創校，首任校長為張燈壽。校園歷經三期工程，分別於1983年、1984年與1987年完工。校名「二苓」屬於高雄沿海地區特有的地名傳統，「苓」字指的是早年沿海地帶叢生蘆葦、燈心草的濕地地貌。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Reeds and rushes along the old shoreline.

🌾

Place names containing “苓” (reed/rush) — including Erling's own — are scattered across coastal Kaohsiung, preserving the memory of the reed-filled wetlands that once lined the city's shoreline before land reclamation and urban development reshaped the coast.
