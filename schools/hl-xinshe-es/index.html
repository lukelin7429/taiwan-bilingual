About · 學校簡介

The only school of its kind in Taiwan.

Xinshe Elementary holds a unique place in Taiwan's education system: it is the country's only elementary school serving the Kavalan people, one of Taiwan's officially recognized Indigenous peoples. In October 2024, the school completed a transformation into an Indigenous experimental school, adopting the Kavalan name "pateRungan," meaning "a place where boats moor and dock." Its student body reflects the mixed Indigenous character of the surrounding village, drawing Amis, Bunun, Sakizaya, and Kavalan students alike. The school has also installed solar power generation equipment as part of its campus development.

新社國小在台灣教育體系中佔有獨特地位：它是全台灣唯一以噶瑪蘭族為主體的國小，噶瑪蘭族為台灣官方認定的原住民族之一。民國113年（2024年）10月，學校完成轉型為原住民族實驗小學，並採用噶瑪蘭族語校名「pateRungan」，意為「船隻靠岸停泊之地」。學生組成反映了周邊村落多元族群的樣貌，涵蓋阿美族、布農族、撒奇萊雅族與噶瑪蘭族學生。學校也已裝設太陽能發電設備，作為校園建設的一環。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛵ Taiwan's Only Kavalan School

全台唯一噶瑪蘭族國小

Xinshe Elementary is the sole elementary school in Taiwan specifically serving the Kavalan people, giving it a singular role in sustaining Kavalan language and culture for the next generation.

🚤 A New Name, pateRungan

「船隻靠岸停泊之地」

Since October 2024, the school has carried the Kavalan name "pateRungan" — a place where boats moor and dock — as part of its transformation into a full Indigenous experimental school.

🌏 A Village of Many Peoples

阿美、布農、撒奇萊雅、噶瑪蘭族共學

The student body includes Amis, Bunun, Sakizaya, and Kavalan children, mirroring the mixed Indigenous character of Xinshe village itself.

☀️ Solar Power on Campus

校園太陽能發電設備

The school has installed solar power generation equipment, part of a broader push toward sustainability on its campus.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xinshe (Kaluluan), a landmark Kavalan and Amis village.

🌾

Xinshe (also known by its Indigenous name Kaluluan) is Taiwan's best-known Kavalan and Amis Indigenous village, famous nationwide for its terraced rice paddies facing the Pacific Ocean along the scenic east coast highway. As Taiwan's only Kavalan-designated elementary school, pateRungan sits at the very center of efforts to keep the Kavalan people's language and identity alive for future generations.
