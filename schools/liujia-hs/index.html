About · 學校簡介

From junior high to a complete middle school.

Liujia's junior high division traces its roots to 1968. In December 2013, the school was approved to convert into a "complete middle school" combining junior and senior high divisions, and the senior high division opened its doors in August 2015. Today the school offers a direct-advancement track from junior high into its own senior high program, and it maintains sister-school relationships with Westmont High School (Illinois, USA), Syracuse High School (Utah, USA), and Takehara High School (Hiroshima, Japan).

六家高中國中部歷史可追溯至1968年。民國102年（2013年）12月，學校奉准改制為兼具國中部與高中部的完全中學，高中部於2015年8月正式招生。目前學校設有國中直升高中的銜接管道，並與美國伊利諾州的Westmont High School、猶他州的Syracuse High School，以及日本廣島縣的竹原高等學校締結姊妹校關係。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Complete Middle School

國中部直升高中部

Since its 2013 conversion, students can progress from junior high straight into the school's own senior high division, opened in 2015, without changing campuses.

🌏 Sister Schools Across Three Countries

跨國姊妹校締結

The school maintains sister-school ties with Westmont High School (Illinois) and Syracuse High School (Utah) in the United States, and Takehara High School in Hiroshima, Japan.

🏘️ A Name Rooted in History

六家地名的傳承

The school's name preserves "Liujia" (Six Families), one of Zhubei's oldest Hakka place names, even as the surrounding farmland has urbanized into part of the modern city.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A Hakka name in a growing city.

🏘️

Located in the historic Liujia (Six Families) settlement area, the school's name preserves one of Zhubei's oldest Hakka place names even as the surrounding area has urbanized into part of greater Zhubei — now one of Hsinchu County's fastest-growing tech-commuter districts.
