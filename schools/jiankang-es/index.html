About · 學校簡介

Taipei's first open-campus school.

Jiankang Elementary opened in September 1999 under founding principal Chen Shun-he, who led the school until 2006. It was Taipei's first "wall-less," open-campus elementary school design and the first in the city to adopt the "班群" (class-cluster/pod) team-teaching organizational model. Built within the Minsheng Community as part of Songshan's late-1990s push for progressive, open-plan public school architecture, the campus today serves roughly 1,200 students and 95 staff, with an indoor heated swimming pool and dedicated nature, music, English, computer, art, and ceramics classrooms.

健康國小於民國88年（1999年）9月創校，首任校長陳順和任職至民國95年（2006年）。學校是台北市第一所「無圍牆」開放式校園設計小學，也是全市首創「班群」協同教學組織模式的學校。校園座落於民生社區內，體現1990年代末松山區推動開放式校園建築的先驅精神，目前約有1,200名學生與95名教職員，並設有室內溫水游泳池，以及自然、音樂、英語、電腦、美術、陶藝等專科教室。
