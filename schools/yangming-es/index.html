Yangming Elementary School

楊明國民小學

A public elementary school in Yangmei's Xinnong Street education district, once operating a Xiucai branch campus, and neighboring Yangming Junior High though run as an independent system.

位於楊梅新農街文教區的公立國小，曾設有「秀才分校」，與楊明國中相鄰但為不同學制的獨立學校體系。

About · 學校簡介

An independent school beside its namesake junior high.

Yangming Elementary, known in English on its own materials as "Yang-Ming Primary School," sits in the Xinnong Street education district of Yangmei alongside Yangming Junior High — though the two are separate school systems serving different grade levels, not one combined institution. The school has, at points in its history, operated a branch campus known as Xiucai (秀才分校), reflecting a broader footprint within the local school network.

楊明國小校名為「Yang-Ming Primary School」，與楊明國中同處楊梅新農街一帶文教區，共同服務同一學區內的學齡人口，但為不同學制的獨立學校體系。學校曾設有「秀才分校」，顯示其在地方學校網絡中曾有更廣的辦學範圍。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Independent from Its Namesake Junior High

與楊明國中為不同學制

Though it shares a name and neighborhood with Yangming Junior High, Yangming Elementary is run as a completely independent school system serving its own catchment.

🎓 A Former Xiucai Branch Campus

秀才分校

The school has, at points in its history, operated a branch campus known as Xiucai, extending its reach within the local education network.

📍 Anchoring Yangmei's Xinnong Street District

新農街文教區

Located along Zhongshan North Road near Xinnong Street, the school shares this education-focused corridor of Yangmei with several other district schools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of a shared education district.

📚

Yangming Elementary sits together with Yangming Junior High in Yangmei's Xinnong Street area, a corridor that has become a small education district serving the school-age population of this part of the city. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
