Dongxi Elementary School

東溪國民小學

A small rural elementary school in Xihu Township, built around quality bilingual education and hands-on food-and-farming learning.

About · 學校簡介

A small school with a big local heart.

Dongxi Elementary began in 1969 as a branch campus of neighboring Xihu Elementary School, becoming an independent school in November 1972. Today it is a small rural school of around seven classes and roughly 130 students, serving families in Xihu Township, a Changhua County community known for its vegetable farming. The school was recently rated "excellent" in Changhua County's 113th academic-year school affairs evaluation.

東溪國小自民國58年（1969年）起作為溪湖國小的分校，於民國61年（1972年）11月獨立設校。目前是一所小型鄉村學校，約有7班、130餘位學生，服務彰化縣溪湖鎮這個以蔬菜種植聞名的地區。學校近期在彰化縣113學年度校務評鑑中獲評「優等」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 Growing From a Branch Campus

從分校到獨立設校

Dongxi began life in 1969 as a branch of Xihu Elementary School before becoming a fully independent school in 1972 — a quiet, steady growth typical of many rural Changhua schools.

🏅 Rated "Excellent" Countywide

縣府校務評鑑優等

The school was recently rated "excellent" in Changhua County's 113th academic-year school affairs evaluation, a strong result for a small campus of about 130 students.

🗣️ A Focus on Quality Bilingual Education

優質雙語教育

Dongxi's teaching direction places real emphasis on bilingual education, alongside character and etiquette training and the development of diverse student talents.

🥬 Food-and-Farming in the Classroom

食農教育

Reflecting Xihu Township's strong vegetable-farming economy, the school builds food-and-agriculture education directly into its curriculum.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xihu Township's vegetable fields.

🌿

Xihu Township is well known across Changhua County for its vegetable farming, especially chives and leek flowers, grown in the fields surrounding towns like Dongxi. That agricultural identity shows up directly in the school's food-and-farming education program. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
