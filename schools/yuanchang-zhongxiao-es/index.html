About · 學校簡介

Building a healthy school culture.

Zhongxiao Elementary is located in Longyan Village, Yuanchang Township, Yunlin County. The school places particular emphasis on gender-equality education, anti-bullying programs, and health-promotion and positive-psychology initiatives — a curriculum focus aimed at nurturing a safe, supportive environment for its students.

忠孝國小位於雲林縣元長鄉龍岩村。學校特別重視性別平等教育、反霸凌宣導，以及健康促進與正向心理相關的計畫，致力於為學生打造安全且支持性的學習環境。
