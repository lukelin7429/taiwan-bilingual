About · 學校簡介

One school, one family.

Xinsheng Elementary was established in 1995 to serve Xinsheng Village as Dongshi's hillside orchard communities continued to expand through the 1990s, making it one of the district's newest elementary schools. The school describes its guiding philosophy as "one school, one family," and it runs folk-sports programs for its students alongside its core curriculum.

新盛國民小學創立於民國84年（1995年），因應1990年代東勢山區果園社區持續擴展，服務新盛里，是東勢區最年輕的國小之一。學校秉持「一校一家」的辦學理念，並開設民俗體育課程，作為核心課程之外的特色活動。
