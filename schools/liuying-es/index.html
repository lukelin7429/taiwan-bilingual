About · 學校簡介

A 120-year-old centenary school.

Liuying Elementary began in 1902 as "Yanshui Public School's Chamuying Branch," became an independent school in 1908 as "Chamuying Public School," was renamed "Liuying Public School" in 1921, became "Liuying National School" in 1941, and took its current name in 1968. Now over 120 years old, it is one of Tainan's officially recognized "centenary schools" (百年老校). The school's motto emphasizes inheritance, innovation, happiness, and excellence, and it states a mission to cultivate 21st-century global citizens.

柳營國小創立於明治35年（1902年），原為「鹽水港公學校查畝營分校」，明治41年（1908年）獨立設校為「查畝營公學校」，大正10年（1921年）4月24日更名為「柳營公學校」，昭和16年（1941年）改稱「柳營國民學校」，民國57年（1968年）改為現名。學校創校逾120年，是臺南市官方認定的「百年老校」之一。校訓強調「傳承、創新、幸福、卓越」，並以培育21世紀世界公民為使命。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Liuying's old garrison name.

🏯

The school's original name, Chamuying (查畝營), is Liuying's old Qing-dynasty place name, referring to land measured out for garrison troops. Its founding as a branch of the Yanshui public school reflects the historic administrative link between Liuying and Yanshui before Liuying became a separate district — a connection that also runs through several other schools in this batch.
