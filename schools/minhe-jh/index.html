Minhe Junior High School

民和國民中學

A mountain-foothill junior high in Fanlu Township, home to a county baseball championship team and a national award-winning choir.

位於番路鄉山麓的國中，擁有多次奪冠的縣賽棒球隊，以及榮獲全國優等獎的合唱團。

About · 學校簡介

From branch campus to independent school.

Minhe Junior High traces back to 1966, when it broke off from Lantan Junior High's Fanlu branch to become an independent school, "Chiayi County Minhe Junior High." In 1968, under Taiwan's nine-year compulsory education reform, it was renamed "Chiayi County Minhe Junior High School," the name it carries today. As of the 2025 school year, the school serves 129 students (105 on the main campus across 9 classes, plus 24 at a branch campus across 3 classes) with a faculty of 56.

民和國中前身為嘉義縣立蘭潭初級中學番路分部，民國55年（1966年）獨立設校為「嘉義縣立民和初級中學」；民國57年（1968年）因應九年國教改制，更名為「嘉義縣立民和國民中學」，沿用至今。114學年度，全校共129位學生（本校105人、9班；分校24人、3班），教職員56人。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚾ A Championship Baseball Tradition

棒球隊多次奪冠

Founded in 1994, the school's hardball team has won the county junior-high baseball league championship multiple times, making it a signature program for the school.

🎶 A Nationally Recognized Choir

合唱團全國優等

The school choir has earned national excellence awards, giving this small mountain-township school an outsized reputation in music education.

🍽️ Locally Sourced School Lunches

午餐全採台灣豬牛

The school uses only domestic, Taiwan-raised pork and beef in its lunch program, reflecting a commitment to food safety and support for local agriculture.

⛩️ Neighbor to Ziyun Temple

緊鄰紫雲寺

Sitting at roughly 200m elevation near Bantianyan Caigongdian Ziyun Temple, the campus is embedded in one of Fanlu's most recognizable pilgrimage landmarks.

Local Culture · 在地文化

In persimmon country, on the road to Alishan.

🟠

Minhe Junior High sits in the heart of Taiwan's largest "ox-heart persimmon" (牛心柿) growing region, amid Fanlu's famous fruit orchards near Ziyun Temple. The persimmon harvest and the temple-pilgrimage traffic heading up the mountain road toward Alishan are both woven into daily life around the school, tying its identity firmly to Fanlu Township's rural, mountain-foothill character.
