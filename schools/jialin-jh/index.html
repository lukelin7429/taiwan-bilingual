About · 學校簡介

Built to keep pace with a booming new town.

Jialin Junior High opened in 2011, established specifically to absorb the wave of school-age children arriving with Linkou New Town's rapid population growth. The campus was built at a cost of roughly NT$527 million and today enrolls about 917 students across 35 classes. The school has developed a reputation for its bilingual education initiatives and a hands-on career-exploration program that connects classroom learning to real-world pathways.

佳林國中於民國100年（2011年）創校，設立目的即是為了因應林口新市鎮人口快速成長所帶來的學齡兒童就學需求。校舍斥資約新台幣5億2700萬元興建，目前約有917名學生、35個班級。學校以扎實的雙語教育與貼近實務的生涯探索課程聞名，讓課堂學習與真實世界的職涯路徑產生連結。
