Wucuo Elementary School

吳厝國小

A community-rooted public elementary school in Jiulong Village, marking its 80th anniversary with a focus on reading, oral English, and dental health.

About · 學校簡介

Eighty years of community learning.

Wucuo Elementary was founded around 1945–1946, in the years surrounding Taiwan's retrocession, and has spent the decades since building close ties with its Jiulong Village community. In March 2025 the school held an "80th Anniversary School and Community Joint Sports Day," celebrating this long relationship. Academically, the school places particular emphasis on reading education, oral English proficiency, and dental health promotion.

吳厝國小創校於約民國34至35年間（1945至1946年），台灣光復前後，多年來與九隆里社區建立深厚情誼。民國114年（2025年）3月，學校舉辦「80週年校慶暨社區聯合運動會」，慶祝這份長久的緊密關係。在教學方面，學校特別著重閱讀教育、英語口說能力，以及牙齒保健推廣。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎉 An 80th Anniversary Milestone

建校80週年慶

In March 2025, the school held an "80th Anniversary School and Community Joint Sports Day," bringing generations of alumni and neighbors together to mark the occasion.

📖 A Strong Reading Culture

閱讀教育

Reading education is a defined emphasis at Wucuo, reflecting a school-wide push to build strong literacy habits from an early age.

🦷 Dental Health and Oral English, Together

牙齒保健與英語口說

The school pairs oral English proficiency training with dental health promotion — two distinct "oral" priorities that together round out its student wellbeing focus.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Community roots on the Zhuoshui plain.

🌉

Set in Jiulong Village within Xiluo Town, the school's community roots run through the same fertile Zhuoshui River plain that made the town famous for soy-sauce brewing and its historic old street. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
