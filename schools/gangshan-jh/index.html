Gangshan Junior High School

岡山國民中學

One of the district's largest junior high schools, having spun off junior highs across four neighboring townships as Gangshan urbanized.

岡山地區規模最大的國中之一，隨著岡山快速都市化，歷史上曾分設出周邊四個鄉鎮的國中。

About · 學校簡介

A commercial hub's flagship junior high.

Gangshan Junior High was founded on October 24, 1956, initially borrowing classrooms from nearby Gangshan Elementary School before establishing its own campus. Today the school spans 41,336 square meters and offers 61 classes, including a sports class and a special-education class in every grade. Over the decades, Gangshan Junior High has historically spun off junior highs serving the neighboring townships of Alian, Hunei, Ziguan, and Yanchao.

岡山國中創立於民國45年（1956年）10月24日，初期借用鄰近岡山國小的教室上課，其後才建立自己的校園。學校現有校地41,336平方公尺，共61個班級，每個年級皆設有體育班與特教班。歷年來，岡山國中曾分設出服務阿蓮、湖內、梓官、燕巢等周邊鄉鎮的國中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏃 A Class for Every Grade in Sport

各年級皆設體育班

The school maintains a dedicated sports class and a special-education class in every grade level, spanning 61 classes across its 41,336-square-meter campus.

🌆 A Feeder for the Whole Region

分設四鄉鎮國中

Gangshan Junior High has historically spun off junior highs serving Alian, Hunei, Ziguan, and Yanchao townships, a legacy of its role as the area's original school.

🏙️ Rooted in a Commercial Crossroads

南北往來的商業樞紐

The school was founded as Gangshan rapidly urbanized into the commercial hub between Tainan and Zuoying, a role the district's historic name, Akangtiam, reflects.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Gangshan's rise as a regional hub.

🏙️

The school was founded as Gangshan (formerly known as Akangtiam, 阿公店) rapidly urbanized into the commercial hub between Tainan and Zuoying, a growth that also drove the founding of several neighboring junior highs. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
