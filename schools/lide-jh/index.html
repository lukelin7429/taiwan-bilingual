About · 學校簡介

Two renamings, one shared campus history.

Lide Junior High School was founded in 1974 on 2.4 hectares of land split off from neighboring Mingde Elementary School. It was initially named Mingde Junior High, then briefly renamed Jiucheng Junior High after a naming conflict was discovered with an existing school in Keelung, before settling on its current name, Lide, in March 1975. In its early years, more than 80% of the school's students came from military families, reflecting Zuoying's identity as a major naval district and home to extensive military housing communities.

立德國中創立於民國63年（1974年），校地為2.4公頃，係從鄰近的明德國小分出。初名「明德國中」，後因與基隆一所學校校名重複而短暫改名為「舊城國中」，民國64年（1975年）3月才定名為「立德國中」。創校初期，超過八成學生來自軍人家庭，反映左營作為主要海軍軍區與眷村聚落的地方特色。
