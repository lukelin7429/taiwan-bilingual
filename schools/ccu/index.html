About · 學校簡介

A newer national university built for southern and central Taiwan.

National Chung Cheng University (CCU) was founded on July 1, 1989, under its first president, Lin Ching-chiang, at a time when Taiwan's government sought to expand access to national higher education outside the traditional northern hubs. Its name — Chung Cheng — is the courtesy name of Chiang Kai-shek. Built on a large campus in Minsyong Township, CCU has grown into a comprehensive research university with seven colleges: Liberal Arts, Science, Social Sciences, Engineering, Management, Law, and Education. Alongside neighboring National Chiayi University's Minsyong campus, CCU has helped shape Minsyong into a genuine university town, with student housing, shops, and transit built up around the two campuses over more than three decades.

國立中正大學創立於民國78年（1989年）7月1日，首任校長為林清江。校名「中正」取自蔣中正的字號。學校座落於民雄鄉一片廣闊的校地，逐步發展為擁有文學院、理學院、社會科學院、工學院、管理學院、法學院、教育學院等七大學院的綜合型研究大學。中正大學與鄰近的國立嘉義大學民雄校區，共同形塑了民雄鄉「大學城」的地方風貌，三十餘年來帶動了學生住宅、商店與交通建設的發展。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Seven Colleges

七大學院

CCU is organized into seven colleges — Liberal Arts, Science, Social Sciences, Engineering, Management, Law, and Education — spanning 29 departments and dozens of graduate programs.

🌏 Office of International Affairs

國際事務處

CCU's Office of International Affairs maintains a genuine, actively updated English-language web portal covering admissions, exchange programs, dual degrees, and partner institutions for international and exchange students.

⚖️ Strong Law and Management Colleges

法學院與管理學院見長

CCU's College of Law and College of Management are widely regarded as among its more selective and competitive colleges within Taiwan's national university system.

🏆 Internationally Ranked

國際排名肯定

CCU appears in the QS World University Rankings, and its graduates include Academia Sinica academicians, former Ministers of Education, and numerous university presidents.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The heart of Minsyong's university town.

🏫

Together with National Chiayi University's Minsyong campus, CCU has anchored Minsyong Township's identity as a "university town" (大學城) for more than three decades — the presence of two national universities has shaped the township's economy and civic life, from student housing to local shops and transit.
