About · 學校簡介

A school raised alongside Daya's growth.

Wenya Elementary was established in 1994, part of a wave of new schools built as Daya District urbanized through the 1990s. Groundbreaking took place in 1995, with the school's buildings completed between 1996 and 1997 — a relatively fast construction timeline that reflects the urgency of the district's growth at the time. It remains one of Daya's younger public elementary schools.

文雅國小創立於民國83年（1994年），是1990年代大雅區都市化浪潮中興建的新學校之一。學校於民國84年（1995年）動土，校舍於民國85至86年（1996至1997年）間完工——相對快速的興建時程，反映出當時大雅區發展的急迫性。至今仍是大雅區歷史較年輕的公立國小之一。
