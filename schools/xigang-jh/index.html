About · 學校簡介

Xigang's only junior high, built on reclaimed flood-prone ground.

Xigang Junior High began planning in May 1968 under founding principal Shen Zhengfan, with enrollment beginning that September with 383 students across seven classes. It remains the only junior high school in Xigang District. The original campus site included low-lying mud pits and burial grounds that flooded the schoolyard during rain, requiring extensive early landscaping and greening work that made it a model for campus beautification — though the school still suffered severe damage during Typhoon Morakot (八八水災) in 2009. Today it is a small school, with roughly 224 students across three grade levels.

西港國中於民國57年（1968年）5月開始籌設，首任校長沈正凡，同年9月開學，共383名學生、7個班級。本校是西港區唯一的一所國中。創校初期校地包含低窪泥沼與墓地，下雨時常淹水，經過大規模綠美化工程才逐漸改善，一度成為校園美化的典範，然而2009年八八水災期間校園仍遭受嚴重損害。目前全校規模較小，三個年級合計約224名學生。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xigang, home of the triennial King Boat Festival.

🚢

Xigang District sits on the low-lying Zengwen River floodplain, a geography reflected in the flood-prone founding site of Xigang Junior High and its Typhoon Morakot damage in 2009. The district is renowned across Taiwan for its own triennial Xigang Wangye's Boat Festival (西港刈香/王船祭), centered on Qing'an Temple (慶安宮) — one of Taiwan's most significant Wangye folk-religion events.
