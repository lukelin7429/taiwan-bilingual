Zhaonan Junior High School

苗栗縣立照南國民中學

A public junior high sharing its athletic field with sister school Zhaonan Elementary, home to the culturally themed Dunpin Library.

About · 學校簡介

A close-knit campus shared with its elementary neighbor.

Zhaonan Junior High School was established in 1969 and today spans about 3.24 hectares, serving roughly 1,435 students. The school shares its athletic field with the adjacent Zhaonan Elementary School, creating a shared-campus feel between the two institutions. Its Dunpin Building (敦品樓) houses the Dunpin Library, which blends literary and aesthetic design with the school's traditional culture theme.

照南國中創立於民國58年（1969年），校地約3.24公頃，現有學生約1,435人。學校與緊鄰的照南國小共用運動場，形成兩校共構、緊密相連的校園氛圍。校內的敦品樓設有敦品圖書館，融合文學與美學設計，呼應學校傳統文化的辦學主題。
