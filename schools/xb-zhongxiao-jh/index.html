About · 學校簡介

One campus, three chapters of Banqiao's school history.

Zhongxiao Junior High School's current site was originally home to Zhongxiao Elementary School from 1982 to 1997. The site then served as a temporary campus for Zhongshan Junior High School from 1997 to 1999, before Zhongxiao Junior High School was formally established there in 1999 under founding principal Wu Song-jiang.

忠孝國中現址原為忠孝國小校地（民國71年至86年，即1982年至1997年）。之後於民國86年至88年（1997年至1999年）作為中山國中的臨時校區，直到民國88年（1999年）忠孝國中正式設校，首任校長為吳松江。
