Ganghe Elementary School

港和國民小學

Established in 1999 with a name blending “harbor” and “harmony,” Ganghe Elementary is a designated flagship astronomy-education school with its own on-campus observatory hall.

About · 學校簡介

A late-1990s school with its eyes on the stars.

Ganghe Elementary School began with a preparatory office established in 1997, followed by construction in 1998, and was formally founded on July 1, 1999. Its name combines “港” (harbor, from Xiaogang) and “和” (harmony). The school has been designated a flagship astronomy-education school, with a dedicated on-campus astronomy hall supporting its science curriculum.

港和國小於民國86年（1997年）成立籌備處，1998年動工興建，並於民國88年（1999年）7月1日正式創校。校名結合「港」（取自小港）與「和」（和諧）二字。學校獲指定為天文教育重點學校，並設有校內天文館，支援科學課程教學。

港與和的融合校名

“港和” combines “港” (harbor, from Xiaogang's identity) with “和” (harmony) — a deliberately composed name for a school built at the turn of the millennium.

🏗️ A Late-1990s Founding

1990年代末創校

From its 1997 preparatory office through 1998 construction to its 1999 formal founding, Ganghe was built in the final years of the 20th century.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Hands-on science learning in the neighborhood school.

🔭

Ganghe Elementary's astronomy hall reflects a broader emphasis in Taiwanese elementary education on hands-on science learning, giving Siaogang's students direct access to astronomy education rarely available at an ordinary neighborhood school. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
