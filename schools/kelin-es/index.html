Kelin Elementary School

柯林國小

A public elementary school recognized with Taiwan-US Eco-Schools and international Health-Promoting Schools honors, in a village transformed by a nearby sports park.

About · 學校簡介

From a branch classroom to an eco-award winner.

Kelin Elementary began in July 1951 as a branch class of Shun'an National Elementary School. It was transferred to become a branch of Guangxing Elementary in 1955, upgraded to a full branch school in 1960, and became fully independent in August 1964. In recent years, the school has earned a Taiwan-US Eco-Schools Silver Award, recognition as a Ministry of Education Climate-Friendly School, and Silver Certification in the international Health-Promoting Schools program.

柯林國小於民國40年（1951年）7月以順安國民學校分班形式創立。民國44年（1955年）轉為廣興國小分校，民國49年（1960年）升格為分校，並於民國53年（1964年）8月正式獨立設校。近年來，學校榮獲台美生態學校銀牌獎、教育部氣候友善學校認證，以及國際健康促進學校計畫銀質認證。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌍 A Taiwan-US Eco-Schools Award

台美生態學校銀牌

Kelin has earned a Silver Award in the Taiwan-US Eco-Schools program, reflecting its commitment to environmental education and sustainable campus practices.

🌡️ Recognized as Climate-Friendly

教育部氣候友善學校

The Ministry of Education has recognized Kelin as a Climate-Friendly School, part of a broader push toward environmental responsibility in its curriculum and operations.

💚 An International Health Honor

國際健康促進學校銀質認證

Kelin holds Silver Certification in the international Health-Promoting Schools program, recognizing its focus on student wellbeing alongside academics.

🏟️ Growing With a New Sports Park

鄰近羅東運動公園發展

The school has benefited from the growth of the nearby Luodong Sports Park area, as the surrounding countryside blends traditional farming village life with newer suburban development.
