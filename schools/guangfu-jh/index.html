Guangfu Junior High School

光復國中

A majority-Amis Indigenous junior high built on donated farmland, near the Matai'an Wetland at the heart of Guangfu Township.

建立於捐贈農地上、鄰近馬太鞍濕地的原住民（阿美族）人數居多的國中，是光復鄉的教育重鎮。

About · 學校簡介

Built by the community it serves.

Guangfu Junior High opened in 1962 as a branch of Fenglin Junior High, becoming independent in 1965 and taking its current name in 1968. Its campus was built partly on land donated from Guangfu Elementary School's agricultural practice field, with construction funded in part by a grassroots "rice bucket movement" fundraising drive among local residents. As of 2024, about 67% of its roughly 176 students are Indigenous, mostly Amis, and the school quickly restored normal operations after the September 2023 Matai'an Creek dammed-lake overflow event that affected the area.

光復國中創立於民國51年（1962年），最初為鳳林國中分校，民國54年（1965年）獨立設校，民國57年（1968年）定名為光復國中。校地部分來自光復國小農業實習地的捐贈，建校經費也曾透過地方居民發起的「捐米運動」募集而成。截至2024年，全校約176名學生中約67%為原住民，以阿美族為主。民國112年（2023年）9月馬太鞍溪堰塞湖溢流事件後，學校迅速恢復正常運作。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 Built by a "Rice Bucket" Campaign

捐米運動建校

The school's construction was funded in part by a community "rice bucket movement," with local residents donating rice to raise money — a grassroots origin story that still shapes local pride in the school.

🏃 Strong Athletic Teams

棒球、田徑、女子足球隊

Guangfu Junior High runs baseball, track and field, and women's soccer teams, reflecting Amis communities' well-known strength in Taiwanese school athletics.

🌊 Resilience After the Matai'an Overflow

馬太鞍溪堰塞湖事件後迅速復課

After the September 2023 Matai'an Creek dammed-lake overflow event disrupted the area, the school moved quickly to restore normal classes, a mark of the community's resilience.

🪶 A Majority-Amis Student Body

阿美族學生為主

About two-thirds of students are Indigenous, mostly Amis, placing the school at the center of Guangfu's Indigenous community life.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Beside the Matai'an Wetland.

🌾

Guangfu Junior High sits near the Matai'an Wetland (馬太鞍濕地), home to the Matai'an (Fata'an) Amis community — one of Hualien's largest and best-known Amis settlements, famous for traditional fishing methods and wetland ecology. With a majority-Amis student body, the school is deeply woven into that community's daily life.
