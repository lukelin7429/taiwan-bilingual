Zhonggang High School

臺中市立中港高級中學

A complete six-year school born from Taichung Harbor's 1970s development boom, pairing a competitive junior-high art-gifted class with a senior-high athletics track in taekwondo, archery, and table tennis.

About · 學校簡介

A school born from the harbor's growth.

Zhonggang High School's roots trace back to 1971, when the start of Taichung Harbor's construction touched off a rapid population surge in Wuqi and local planners began laying the groundwork for a new school to serve the growing township. The school itself opened in 1993 and was reorganized and renamed in 2000 into its present form: a complete six-year school spanning both junior and senior high divisions. Today it runs a competitive junior-high art-gifted class and a senior-high athletics track covering taekwondo, archery, and table tennis.

中港高中的緣起可追溯至民國60年（1971年）——台中港開始興建，梧棲地區人口隨之激增，地方隨即著手籌設新校因應成長中的鄉鎮需求。學校本身於民國82年（1993年）正式創校，並於民國89年（2000年）整併更名，成為現今涵蓋國中與高中部的六年一貫完全中學。現設有國中部美術資優班，以及高中部運動人才班，涵蓋跆拳道、射箭與桌球項目。
