About · 學校簡介

The mother school of Sezi Island.

Sezi Elementary School was established in 1902 as a branch of Bazhilan Public School, "八芝蘭公學校社仔分校," following a petition by local ward chief Chen Huanlan. It became an independent school, "Sezi Public School," in 1913, was renamed "Sezi National School" in 1942, and took its current name in 1974. One of the oldest schools in Shilin, with roots going back over 120 years, the school celebrated its 120th anniversary with then-Mayor Ko Wen-je in attendance. At its peak the school had over 5,000 students; two entirely new schools — Bailing Elementary (1970) and Hulu Elementary (1975) — were later split off to relieve overcrowding.

社子國小創立於明治35年（1902年），初為「八芝蘭公學校社仔分校」，源自地方保正陳煥瀾的陳情。民國2年（1913年）獨立設校為「社仔公學校」，民國31年（1942年）改稱「社子國民學校」，民國63年（1974年）定名現名。作為士林區歷史最悠久的學校之一，創校已逾120年，並曾在時任市長柯文哲出席下慶祝120週年校慶。學校鼎盛時期學生曾超過5,000人，後來陸續分出百齡國小（1970年）與葫蘆國小（1975年）以紓解過度擁擠問題。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring the Sezi Island community.

🌊

As the mother school of both Bailing and Hulu Elementary, Sezi Elementary anchors the historic Sezi Island community at Shilin's western edge, a farming and fishing settlement shaped for centuries by its position at the confluence of the Danshui and Keelung Rivers.
