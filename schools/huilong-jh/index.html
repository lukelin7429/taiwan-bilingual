Hueilong Combined Junior High and Elementary School

迴龍國民中小學

A combined elementary-and-junior-high school on Guishan's northern edge, where the district blends into New Taipei's Xinzhuang and Shulin as a bedroom-community extension of greater Taipei.

About · 學校簡介

A K-9 school on Taipei's doorstep.

Hueilong Combined Junior High and Elementary School grew out of a preparatory office set up in 1994, with the elementary school formally established in 1998 and a junior high division added in 2002. It was upgraded to municipal status in 2014, when a kindergarten was also added. As one of the newer public schools built during Taiwan's 1990s school-expansion period, it now enrolls roughly 234 elementary and 133 junior-high students. The school sits in the Hueilong (迴龍) area, directly abutting New Taipei City's Xinzhuang and Shulin districts.

迴龍國民中小學前身為1994年設立的籌備處，國小部於1998年正式設校，2002年增設國中部，2014年改制為市立學校並增設幼兒園。作為台灣1990年代學校擴建潮下新設的公立學校之一，目前國小部約234人、國中部約133人。校址位於迴龍地區，緊鄰新北市新莊區與樹林區交界。
