Dajia Junior High School

大甲國民中學

A junior high in Dajia's historic core, minutes from the Mazu temple that draws Taiwan's largest annual pilgrimage.

座落於大甲歷史核心區的國中，鄰近吸引全台最大規模進香活動的媽祖廟。

About · 學校簡介

At the heart of the pilgrimage town.

Dajia Junior High School opened in 1968 in the heart of old Dajia town, near the district's historic Mazu-temple core. Since then it has added an art-focused class in 1989, a gifted-education class in 2005, and a sports class in 2012, broadening its academic and athletic offerings. The school has also run a "long-green academy" continuing-education program for adult learners since 1986.

大甲國中創立於民國57年（1968年），校址位於大甲舊市區核心，鄰近媽祖廟歷史街區。學校陸續於1989年增設美術班、2005年增設資優班、2012年增設體育班，逐步擴大學術與體育發展。自1986年起，學校亦開辦「長青學苑」，提供成人繼續教育課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎨 Expanding Specialty Classes

特色班級陸續設立

Since opening in 1968, the school has added an art-focused class (1989), a gifted-education class (2005), and a sports class (2012).

📖 A Long-Running Adult Academy

長青學苑持續開辦

A "long-green academy" continuing-education program for adult learners has run at the school since 1986, extending its role beyond regular junior-high classes.

⛩️ In the Shadow of the Mazu Temple

鄰近媽祖廟歷史街區

Sited in Dajia's historic town center, the campus draws its student body from the same streets that host Taiwan's largest annual religious pilgrimage.

Local Culture · 在地文化

In Dajia's Mazu pilgrimage heartland.

🛕

The campus sits within walking distance of Jenn Lann Temple (鎮瀾宮), whose annual Mazu pilgrimage draws hundreds of thousands of pilgrims and is one of Taiwan's largest religious processions. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
