About · 學校簡介

A school-zone map rooted in old canal villages.

Wenlin Elementary School was established in 1970, with a founding school district drawn from the 圳安, 保安, and 潭底 neighborhoods. These village names — several referencing irrigation canals (圳) — preserve the memory of the agricultural, canal-based communities that once defined this part of Shulin before the district's later urbanization.

文林國小創立於民國59年（1970年），創校學區涵蓋圳安、保安、潭底三里。這些里名——多與灌溉圳道相關——保留了樹林這一帶在後續都市化之前，以水圳灌溉為核心的農業聚落記憶。
