About · 學校簡介

From industrial junior college to national university.

National United University (NUU) traces its roots to 1972, when it opened as Lien Ho Industrial and Technological Junior College. It was upgraded to a national institute of technology in 1999, and became a full national university in 2003. Today NUU is the only public university based in Miaoli County, offering 19 academic departments and 15 master's programs across six colleges, including a College of Hakka Studies dedicated to researching and preserving Hakka language and culture.

國立聯合大學創校於民國61年（1972年），前身為聯合工業技藝專科學校，民國88年（1999年）改制為國立聯合技術學院，民國92年（2003年）正式升格為大學。作為苗栗縣唯一的公立大學，聯合大學現設有19個學系與15個碩士班，橫跨六大學院，其中客家學院致力於客家語言與文化的研究與保存。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 Miaoli's Only Public University

苗栗縣唯一公立大學

NUU serves as the county's flagship higher-education institution, with 19 departments and 15 master's programs spread across six colleges.

🏮 A Dedicated College of Hakka Studies

客家學院

One of NUU's six colleges is devoted entirely to Hakka Studies — research and teaching aimed at preserving the language and culture of Hakka-majority Miaoli.

🏗️ Roots in Industrial and Technical Education

工業技術教育起家

Originally founded in 1972 as a private industrial and technological junior college, NUU was upgraded to a national institute of technology in 1999 before becoming a university in 2003.
