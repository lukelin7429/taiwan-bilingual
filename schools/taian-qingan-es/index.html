About · 學校簡介

A school beside Taiwan's famed hot springs.

Qing'an Elementary is located in Taian Township, Miaoli County's only mountain indigenous township, close to the area's famed Taian Hot Springs. The hot springs were originally discovered by local Atayal residents and later developed further during the Japanese colonial era, and today draw visitors from across Taiwan — placing the school at the heart of what is often called Miaoli's "hot spring township."

清安國小座落於苗栗縣唯一的山地原住民鄉——泰安鄉，鄰近著名的泰安溫泉。泰安溫泉最初由當地泰雅族人發現，日治時期進一步開發，如今吸引全台各地遊客造訪，也讓學校座落於這個常被稱為苗栗「溫泉之鄉」的核心地帶。
