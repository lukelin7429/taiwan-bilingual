Puding Elementary School

埔頂國民小學

A small rural school that has turned Xinwu's surrounding farms, temples and organic gardens into a living environmental-education campus.

這所小型農村學校將新屋周邊的農田、廟宇與有機農場化為環境教育的活教材。

About · 學校簡介

A campus without walls, built on local farmland.

Puding Elementary is a small rural school of roughly 32 staff and 151 students, serving the Puding and Jiudou communities of Xinwu District. Rather than a conventional campus, the school has deliberately built its curriculum around the agricultural resources of its surroundings — the Changxiang Wugu Shennong Temple, an educational farm, a pheasant and exotic-bird garden, a rice-wine distillery, and an organic vegetable farm — developing itself as an environmental-education and eco-tourism hub for the Xinwu area.

埔頂國小是一所約有32名教職員、151名學生的小型農村學校，服務埔頂與九斗地區的社區居民。學校並未採用一般傳統的校園規劃，而是刻意將周邊的農業資源融入課程——包括仁和五穀神農宮、教育農場、雉雞與珍禽花園、菸酒釀造坊，以及有機蔬菜農場——將自身發展為新屋地區的環境教育與生態旅遊據點。
