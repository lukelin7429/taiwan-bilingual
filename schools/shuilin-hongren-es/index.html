Hongren Elementary School

宏仁國民小學

A small rural elementary school in Wandong Village that waives tuition and fees, offering drum, martial arts, go, and diabolo clubs to its close-knit student body.

灣東村的小型農村學校，全免學雜費，並提供鼓藝、武術、圍棋、扯鈴等社團活動。

About · 學校簡介

A small school with a big menu of clubs.

Hongren Elementary is a small rural school in Wandong Village, Shuilin Township, with around 51 students and 11 teachers. Despite its size, the school offers a notably rich set of after-school clubs, including drum/guqin, martial arts, go (weiqi), and diabolo. The school also waives tuition and miscellaneous fees for its students, easing the financial burden on local farming families.

宏仁國小是位於水林鄉灣東村的一所小型農村學校，全校約51名學生、11位教師。儘管規模不大，學校提供相當豐富的課後社團，包括鼓藝／古琴、武術、圍棋、扯鈴等。學校並全額補助學生學雜費，減輕在地農家的經濟負擔。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥋 A Rich Club Program for a Small School

多元社團活動

With only around 51 students, Hongren still offers drum/guqin, martial arts, go (weiqi), and diabolo clubs — an unusually full roster for a rural school of its size.

💰 Tuition and Fees Waived

學雜費全免

The school waives tuition and miscellaneous fees for its students, a meaningful support for families in this farming community.

🧑‍🏫 A Close Teacher-Student Community

小校緊密師生關係

With roughly 51 students and 11 teachers, Hongren maintains a close-knit, low student-to-teacher ratio typical of Yunlin's smaller rural campuses.

Located in Wandong Village, this small rural Shuilin Township school reflects the depopulating farming communities of the sweet-potato-growing region, even as it works to keep local children engaged through free tuition and a full slate of clubs. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
