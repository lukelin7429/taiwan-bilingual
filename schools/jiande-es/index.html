About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school with its own kindergarten.

Jiande Elementary sits in the same Anle District neighborhood as Jiande Junior High, serving as the community's foundational school for younger children. Alongside its regular elementary classes, the school runs an affiliated kindergarten, giving local families a continuous early-education pathway before students move on to junior high just down the road.

建德國小座落於基隆市安樂區，與建德國中同在一個社區，是當地兒童的基礎教育學校。除了國小部課程外，學校也附設幼兒園，讓在地家庭能享有從學前教育延續到升上鄰近國中的完整學習路徑。
