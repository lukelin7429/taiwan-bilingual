About · 學校簡介

Orchid Island's oldest school, on an international stage.

Yeyou Elementary began in 1947 as a branch of Lanyu National School and became fully independent in August 1963, making it one of Orchid Island's oldest schools. In August 2020 it became the first school on the island to convert to Indigenous experimental education. Since 2006 its campus has also hosted the Lanyu branch of Taitung County's Indigenous Tribal College, extending its role in community education well beyond its own students. The school is best known, however, for founding the "Little Flying Fish Cultural Performance Group" in 2006 — a student troupe that has won national awards and performed internationally, including in New Zealand in 2015 and Poland in 2019, and whose story helped inspire the 2018 Taiwanese film "Only the Sea Knows."

椰油國小創立於1947年，原為蘭嶼國校分校，1963年8月正式獨立設校，是蘭嶼歷史最悠久的學校之一。2020年8月，該校成為全島第一所轉型為原住民實驗教育的學校。自2006年起，校園也設有台東縣原住民族部落大學蘭嶼分校，教育角色早已超越一般在校學生。不過，椰油國小最為人所知的，是2006年成立的「小飛魚文化展演隊」——這支學生表演隊曾榮獲全國獎項，並多次赴海外演出，包括2015年紐西蘭與2019年波蘭，其故事更成為2018年台灣電影《只有大海知道》的靈感來源。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🐟 An Internationally Touring Student Troupe

小飛魚文化展演隊揚名國際

Founded in 2006, the Little Flying Fish Cultural Performance Group has won national recognition and performed abroad in New Zealand (2015) and Poland (2019), carrying Tao songs and rituals onto international stages.

🎬 The Inspiration Behind a Feature Film

啟發電影《只有大海知道》

The performance group's story inspired the acclaimed 2018 Taiwanese film "Only the Sea Knows" (只有大海知道), bringing wider public attention to Orchid Island's Tao culture and its children.

🏫 The Island's First Indigenous Experimental School

全島首所原住民實驗教育學校

Yeyou became the first school on Orchid Island to convert to Indigenous experimental education in August 2020, paving the way for other Lanyu schools to follow.

🎓 A Hub for Adult and Community Learning

部落大學蘭嶼分校所在地

Since 2006, the campus has also hosted the Lanyu branch of Taitung County's Indigenous Tribal College, extending the school's role into lifelong and community education.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Flying-fish season, kept alive by children.

🎭

The Little Flying Fish Cultural Performance Group keeps the Tao (Yami) people's flying-fish-season rituals and songs alive for a new generation, translating the community's oral and ceremonial traditions into performance and taking them, remarkably, onto international stages far from Orchid Island.
