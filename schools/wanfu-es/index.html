Wanfu Elementary School

萬富國小

A public elementary school in Sanxing Township, founded in 1927 and rebuilt after a devastating 1942 typhoon-driven flood.

About · 學校簡介

Nearly a century on the Sanxing plain.

Wanfu Elementary was founded in 1927 during the Japanese colonial era as the "Dazhou Public School Hongchai Forest Branch." It became independent in 1943 as "Sanxing East National School," but a severe typhoon in 1942 had already caused catastrophic flooding that destroyed the original campus, forcing a relocation. After Taiwan's retrocession in 1945, the school was renamed the following year, and it took its current name, Wanfu Elementary School, in 1968.

萬富國小創立於日治時期民國16年（1927年），初名「大洲公學校橫材分校」。民國32年（1943年）獨立設校，改稱「三星東國民學校」，然而民國31年（1942年）一場強颱曾造成嚴重洪災，沖毀原校舍，迫使遷校。台灣光復後（1945年）翌年更名，至民國57年（1968年）定為現名「萬富國小」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌀 Survived a 1942 Typhoon Flood

1942年颱風洪災

A severe typhoon in 1942 caused catastrophic flooding that destroyed the school's original campus, forcing relocation just before formal independence.

🏫 Multiple Names Across Eras

歷經數次更名

From its 1927 origins as a branch school through renaming after retrocession in 1946 and again in 1968, its history tracks the shifting eras of 20th-century Taiwan.

🌾 Rooted in a Forest Branch School

源自橫材分校

The school began as a small branch of Dazhou Public School, tied to the forested and agricultural landscape of the surrounding area.

💧 A Neighbor to Xianming's Flood Story

與憲明國小同受水患考驗

Like nearby Xianming Elementary, Wanfu's flood-damage history reflects the shared vulnerability of Sanxing's lowland plain to typhoon-driven flooding.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fertile, flood-prone farmland.

🌊

Like neighboring Xianming Elementary, Wanfu's typhoon-flood history reflects the flood-vulnerable lowland geography of the Sanxing plain that also makes it fertile ground for scallion and taro farming.
