About · 學校簡介

A campus at the center of town.

Gongzheng Elementary was established in 1950 as the Taipei County Luodong Township Gongzheng National School, later adopting its current name in 1969. The campus spans about 2.3 hectares and sits literally in the middle of Luodong, bounded on four sides by roads — making it one of the most centrally located schools in the township.

公正國小創立於民國39年（1950年），初名「台北縣羅東鎮公正國民學校」，民國58年（1969年）更為現名。校地約2.3公頃，座落於羅東鎮正中心，四周皆為道路所環繞，是羅東鎮地理位置最核心的學校之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📍 A Campus Bounded by Four Roads

四面環路的校地

Gongzheng's campus is fully enclosed by streets on every side, a distinctive layout that reflects its position at the literal center of Luodong town.

🗺️ Dead Center of Luodong

羅東鎮的地理原點

Its location in the very heart of Luodong makes it a literal geographic anchor of the town's dense commercial core, close to shops, markets, and transit.

🏫 Renamed at the Height of Growth

1969年更名現名

Founded in 1950 under a different name, the school took on its current identity in 1969, part of a wave of renaming among Yilan's postwar elementary schools.

🌳 A Sizeable In-Town Campus

約2.3公頃校地

At roughly 2.3 hectares, the campus is notably spacious for a school sitting directly in the middle of a busy township center.
