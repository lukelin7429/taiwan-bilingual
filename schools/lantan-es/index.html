Lantan Elementary School

蘭潭國民小學

A school named after Chiayi's scenic Lantan reservoir, with its own observatory, virtual film studio, and Hakka-culture program.

以嘉義知名風景區蘭潭為名的學校，擁有天文觀測台、虛擬攝影棚，並推動客家文化特色課程。

About · 學校簡介

A campus built for hands-on discovery.

Lantan Elementary, named for the scenic reservoir and park it sits beside, has built a campus rich in facilities: a library, athletic field, golf practice area, basketball court, dance room, a virtual film studio, and a school observatory. Alongside its academic program, it runs an unusually varied set of extracurriculars, including a violin club, ukulele club, dance club, broadcasting club, dodgeball team, and a Hakka-culture feature-school program, and regularly holds joint community sports events with neighboring residents as part of its anniversary celebrations.

蘭潭國小以校園毗鄰的風景名勝蘭潭命名，校內設施豐富，包括圖書館、運動場、高爾夫練習場、籃球場、舞蹈教室、虛擬攝影棚及學校天文觀測台。除正規課程外，學校也推動多元社團，包括小提琴社、烏克麗麗社、舞蹈社、廣播社、躲避球隊，以及客家文化特色課程，並於校慶期間定期與鄰近居民舉辦社區聯合運動會。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🔭 A School Observatory

天文觀測台

Lantan runs astronomy observation activities from its own on-campus observatory, giving students direct, hands-on access to stargazing and science learning.

🎬 A Virtual Film Studio

虛擬攝影棚

A virtual-studio facility supports media and broadcasting courses, a distinctive resource among East District elementary schools.

🎻 A Wide Range of Clubs

多元社團

From violin and ukulele clubs to a dance club, broadcasting club, and dodgeball team, students have an unusually broad set of extracurricular options.

🏵️ A Hakka Culture Feature Program

客家文化特色課程

The school runs a dedicated Hakka-culture feature-school program, keeping this strand of Taiwanese heritage alive for its students.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for one of Chiayi's best-known landmarks.

🏞️

The school is named after the adjacent Lantan (蘭潭/"Orchid Pond") reservoir and scenic area, one of Chiayi's best-known local landmarks, and partners with the surrounding neighborhood for joint community sports events. See the Chiayi City page for more on the region.
