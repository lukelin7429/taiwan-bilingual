About · 學校簡介

A small school tied to the coastal wetlands.

Longgang Elementary School was founded in 1963 in Lishui Village, in the northwest corner of Longjing District. Classified as a small, remote school in a culturally disadvantaged area, it has recently seen its aging campus rebuilt. The school's location places it near the Taichung Thermal Power Plant and the Dadu River Estuary Wildlife Refuge, tying it directly to Longjing's identity as a coastal wetland ecology district.

龍港國小創立於民國52年（1963年），位於龍井區西北角的麗水里。學校被列為文化不利地區的小型、偏遠學校，近年進行了老舊校舍的重建工程。學校所在位置鄰近台中發電廠與大肚溪口野生動物保護區，與龍井作為海岸濕地生態區的地方認同緊密相連。
