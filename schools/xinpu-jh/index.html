Hsinpu Junior High School

About · 學校簡介

A Hakka town's school with a branching past.

Hsinpu Junior High School was founded on October 26, 1946 as Xinpu Junior Middle School, and was reorganized into a full junior high school in 1968. Over the decades it has grown to serve as an educational anchor for Xinpu, a historic Hakka market town in Hsinchu County. Today the school integrates digital-learning platforms such as Yuni, Kahoot!, and Cool English into classroom teaching, and runs a community reading center that extends learning beyond the school gates.

新埔國中創立於民國35年（1946年）10月26日，初名新埔初級中學，民國57年（1968年）改制為國民中學。多年來持續作為新埔——新竹縣歷史悠久的客家老街市鎮——的教育重鎮。現今學校將優學堂（Yuni）、Kahoot!、Cool English 等數位學習平台融入課堂教學，並設有社區共讀站，延伸學習觸角至校園之外。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌿 A School That Once Branched Out

曾設關西、竹北分班

The school once operated a branch in Guanxi (1955) and another in Zhubei (1957), along with a short-lived senior-high branch of Hsinchu No. 1 Senior High from 1957 to 1968 — reflecting its historic role as a regional education hub.

💻 Digital Tools in Daily Class

優學堂、Kahoot!、Cool English

Teachers weave platforms like Yuni, Kahoot!, and Cool English into everyday lessons, keeping the century-old school connected to current classroom technology trends.

📖 A Reading Hub for the Community

社區共讀站

The school runs a community reading center, opening its literacy resources to residents of Xinpu beyond its own student body.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xinpu, town of persimmons and ancestral halls.

🏮

Hsinpu Junior High sits in the historic Hakka town of Xinpu, long known for its autumn persimmon-drying tradition and its cluster of well-preserved ancestral halls — a living record of the area's deep Hakka heritage.
