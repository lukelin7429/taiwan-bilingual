Taipei Municipal Longmen Junior High School

臺北市立龍門國民中學

One of Da'an's newest schools, built around a protected Qing-era historic residence after a decade of land disputes delayed its 2004 opening.

大安區最年輕的學校之一，歷經十餘年土地爭議才於2004年開校，校園更完整保留清代古蹟「龍安坡黃宅濂讓居」。

About · 學校簡介

Built around a preserved historic residence.

Longmen Junior High opened in August 2004 under its first principal, Dai Xue-qing, but planning for the school actually dated back to 1988 — construction was delayed for over a decade by land-acquisition disputes involving military authorities and NTNU faculty-housing residents. The finished campus, covering about 34,613 square meters and enrolling roughly 1,500 students, was deliberately designed to preserve the “Long-an-po Huang Residence” (龍安坡黃宅濂讓居), a historic house designated a Taipei city monument in 1999, within the school grounds.

龍門國中於民國93年（2004年）8月開校，首任校長為戴學卿。學校規劃早於1988年即已提出，卻因軍方及師大教職員宿舍住戶的土地爭議延宕十餘年才得以動工。完工後的校園占地約34,613平方公尺，可容納約1,500名學生，並特別將1999年公告為臺北市市定古蹟的「龍安坡黃宅濂讓居」完整保留於校園內。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ A School Built Around a Monument

校園中的市定古蹟

Longmen's campus was deliberately designed to preserve the Qing-era “Long-an-po Huang Residence,” a Taipei city monument, within its own grounds rather than displacing it.

⏳ A Decade of Delay

延宕逾十年的建校計畫

Though first planned in 1988, land disputes with military authorities and NTNU faculty-housing residents postponed construction for more than ten years before the school finally opened in 2004.

🆕 One of Da'an's Newest Schools

大安區最年輕的學校之一

Opening in 2004, Longmen is among the newest public junior highs in a district otherwise dominated by schools founded decades or even a century earlier.

🏫 A Sizeable Modern Campus

現代化大型校園

At roughly 34,613 square meters with about 1,500 students, Longmen is one of the more spacious junior-high campuses in the district.

Local Culture · 在地文化

New campus, layered history.

🏛️

Built around a preserved Qing- and Japanese-era historic residence rather than displacing it, Longmen Junior High reflects Da'an's layered history — a brand-new campus rising directly alongside protected heritage architecture from the neighborhood's agrarian past, on Jianguo South Road near the district's academic core.
