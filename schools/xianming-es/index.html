Xianming Elementary School

憲明國小

A public elementary school in Sanxing Township, twice relocated after a devastating 1943 flood destroyed its original campus.

About · 學校簡介

Rebuilt twice after disaster.

Xianming Elementary opened in 1941 during the Japanese colonial era as a branch school known as "三星西國民學校清水分校." A flood in 1943 destroyed the original campus, forcing it to close, and the school did not reopen until 1945. It gained independence in 1948, then relocated in 1950 to a site near Fushan Temple in Tiansongpi before moving again to its present location in 1954. Its history of flood damage and repeated relocation mirrors the flood-prone river geography of the Sanxing plain.

憲明國小創立於日治時期民國30年（1941年），初為分校，稱「三星西國民學校清水分校」。民國32年（1943年）一場洪水沖毀原校舍，被迫停辦，直至民國34年（1945年）始復校。民國37年（1948年）獨立設校，民國39年（1950年）遷至天送埤福山宮附近，民國43年（1954年）再遷至現址。其洪水受損與多次遷校的歷史，正反映了三星平原易受水患侵襲的河川地理特性。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌊 Rebuilt After a 1943 Flood

1943年洪水重建

A devastating flood destroyed the school's original campus in 1943, forcing a two-year closure before it reopened in 1945.

📍 Relocated Twice in a Decade

十年內兩度遷校

The school moved first to a site near Fushan Temple in 1950, then to its present location in 1954, reflecting the challenges of rebuilding on flood-prone land.

⛩️ A Temple-Adjacent Chapter

曾遷至福山宮附近

For several years, the school operated near Fushan Temple in Tiansongpi, a detail that ties its history to local religious and community landmarks.

🌧️ A Story Written by the River

河川地理下的校史

The school's repeated flood damage and relocations trace directly to the Lanyang River tributaries that shape the fertile but flood-vulnerable Sanxing plain.
