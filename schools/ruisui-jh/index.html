Ruisui Junior High School

瑞穗國中

Serving most of Ruisui Township plus neighboring border villages, in the heart of Taiwan's hot spring and river-rafting country.

About · 學校簡介

Borrowed classrooms to a township's hub.

Ruisui Junior High began in 1956 as the "Ruisui Branch" of Fenglin Junior High, becoming independent in 1958 and taking its current name in 1968. Before settling into its own site, the school borrowed classrooms from nearby Ruimei Elementary. Today its school district covers most of Ruisui Township plus small parts of neighboring Wanrong Township and Yuli Town, and it runs general classes alongside sports, resource, and special education programs.

瑞穗國中創立於民國45年（1956年），初期為鳳林國中「瑞穗分部」，民國47年（1958年）獨立設校，民國57年（1968年）定名為現名。學校在遷入現址前，曾借用鄰近瑞美國小的教室上課。如今學區涵蓋瑞穗鄉大部分地區，並包括萬榮鄉、玉里鎮部分村里，除一般班級外，也設有體育班、資源班與特教班。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Borrowed Classrooms, Then Independence

借用瑞美國小教室起家

Before settling into its current campus, Ruisui Junior High held its first classes in borrowed rooms at Ruimei Elementary — a common origin story among rural Taiwanese schools of the era.

🗺️ A School District Crossing Boundaries

跨鄉鎮學區

The school's district reaches beyond Ruisui Township itself into small parts of neighboring Wanrong Township and Yuli Town, reflecting the practical realities of rural school zoning.

🎓 Classes for Every Kind of Learner

體育班、資源班、特教班

Alongside general education, the school runs dedicated sports, resource, and special education classes, serving a broad range of student needs.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Serving Ruisui's hot spring and river country.

♨️

Ruisui Junior High serves the broader Ruisui Township community, famous across Taiwan for its carbonated hot springs, papaya milk, and its role as a launch point for Xiuguluan River rafting — one of the island's most popular river adventure destinations.
