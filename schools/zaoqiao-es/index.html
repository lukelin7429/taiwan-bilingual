About · 學校簡介

A century-old mother school for the township.

Zaoqiao Elementary began in 1916 as a branch campus during the Japanese colonial era, becoming an independent school in 1921. The school marked its centennial in 2015, having sustained over 100 years of continuous operation. Over the decades, Zaoqiao Elementary became the mother campus to several other schools in the township, including the Jinshui branch (established 1937) and the Tanwen branch (established 1953), both of which later became independent schools in their own right. The school is also a designated center for promoting Taiwanese folk and traditional sports.

造橋國小創校於1916年（大正5年）的分校階段，1921年（大正10年）獨立設校。學校於2015年舉辦建校百年校慶，見證超過百年不曾間斷的辦學歷程。多年來，造橋國小陸續成為錦水分校（1937年設立）與談文分校（1953年設立）的母校，兩校其後皆獨立設校。學校同時也是推廣台灣民俗傳統體育的指定學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💯 Over a Century of Continuous History

跨越百年校史

Founded in 1916 as a branch campus and independent since 1921, Zaoqiao Elementary marked its centennial in 2015 with more than 100 years of unbroken operation.

🌳 Mother Campus to the Township

多校之母校

The school served as the founding mother campus for both Jinshui Elementary (est. 1937) and Tanwen Elementary (est. 1953), which later became independent schools in the township.

🎎 A Center for Folk Sports

民俗傳統體育推廣學校

Zaoqiao Elementary holds a designation for promoting Taiwanese folk and traditional sports, keeping cultural physical activities alive for a new generation of students.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring a century of Hakka education.

🏫

As the township's founding school and mother campus to several other Zaoqiao elementary schools, Zaoqiao Elementary anchors more than a century of Hakka educational history in this hilly farming township. See the Miaoli county page for more on the region.
