About · 學校簡介

A century-old school looking to the stars.

Yuemei Elementary was founded on April 1, 1915, as a branch of Xingang Public School, and became fully independent on April 1, 1920. The school recently marked its 111th anniversary, reflecting over a century of continuous history in one of the many small villages that grew up around Xingang's temple-market economy. Today it blends that long heritage with forward-looking programs: a bilingual education initiative featuring student podcast channels and a "World Classroom" platform, strong coding instruction using Code.org and CodeCombat, and astronomy-themed morning classes nicknamed "Zhuluo City Starry Sky," which helped the school place third in the 65th County Science Exhibition.

月眉國小創立於大正4年（1915年）4月1日，最初為新港公學校的分校，並於民國9年（1920年）4月1日獨立設校。學校近年慶祝建校111週年，見證了新港廟街經濟周邊眾多小型村落學校之一逾百年的校史。如今學校在深厚歷史底蘊之上，發展出多項前瞻計畫：結合學生Podcast頻道與「世界教室」平台的雙語教育計畫、以Code.org與CodeCombat為主的程式設計課程，以及暱稱「諸羅城星空」的天文主題晨間課程，並曾在第65屆縣科學展覽會中獲得第三名。
