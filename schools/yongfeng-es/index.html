About · 學校簡介

A school reborn under its traditional name.

Yongfeng Elementary began in 1948 as a branch of Fuli Elementary, became a full branch campus in 1953, gained independence in 1956, and took its present name in 1968. In 2021, the school underwent a meaningful transformation: it was formally reorganized as Hualien County's third Amis Indigenous experimental elementary school, adopting the traditional place name "Cilamitay" for its new identity — the Cilamitay Amis Experimental Elementary School. Cilamitay is a well-known Amis settlement recognized for its terraced rice paddies and organic farming practices, and the school's renaming reflects a broader effort in Taiwan to restore Indigenous place names and curricula to public education.

永豐國小於民國37年（1948年）以富里國小分班形式創立，民國42年（1953年）升格為分校，民國45年（1956年）獨立設校，民國57年（1968年）定名為現名。民國110年（2021年），學校經歷一次意義深遠的轉型：正式改制為花蓮縣第三所阿美族實驗小學，並採用傳統地名「吉哩米代」（Cilamitay）作為新身份——「吉哩米代阿美族實驗小學」。吉哩米代是知名的阿美族聚落，以梯田水稻與有機農作聞名，學校的更名也呼應了台灣近年來將原住民地名與課程重新帶回公共教育體系的努力。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fuli Township, rice country at the valley's edge.

🌾

Fuli Township is Hualien County's southernmost township, a rice-farming community near the Tropic of Cancer at the southern tip of the East Rift Valley. Yongfeng's rebirth as an Amis experimental school reflects the Indigenous heritage woven throughout Fuli's farming communities. See the Hualien schools page for the full township list.
