About · 學校簡介

Named for a temple that no longer stands.

Guanting Elementary School traces its roots to 1940, when it was established as a branch campus of Neimen National School called '內門國民學校觀音亭分校場' — its records date the founding using the Republic of China calendar (ROC year 29), even though Taiwan was under Japanese colonial rule at the time, a calendar convention rather than a claim about sovereignty. The school became independent in 1946 as 'Kaohsiung County Neimen Township Guanyinting National School,' and was renamed Guanting Elementary in 1951. In 1966, one of its own branch campuses, 內東分班, split off to become 西門國民學校 (Ximen National School). Today it is designated a remote-area (偏遠/特偏) school.

觀亭國小的歷史可追溯至1940年，當時以「內門國民學校觀音亭分校場」之名設立分校，校史記錄採用民國紀年（民國29年），雖然當時台灣仍在日治時期，此為曆法換算慣例，並非主權主張。1946年獨立設校，定名「高雄縣內門鄉觀音亭國民學校」，1951年更名為觀亭國小。1966年，校內的內東分班獨立分出，成為西門國民學校。現為偏遠（特偏）地區學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛩️ A Name from a Vanished Temple

校名源自觀音亭

The school's name comes from a Guanyin (觀音) temple pavilion (亭) that once stood in the area — a piece of local history preserved in the school's name long after the structure itself disappeared.

🌱 From Branch School to Independent Campus

從分校到獨立設校

Guanting began in 1940 as a branch of Neimen National School, becoming fully independent in 1946 and settling into its current name in 1951.

🌿 A Branch of Its Own Split Off

分出西門國民學校

In 1966, Guanting's own 內東分班 branch campus separated to become an independent school, Ximen National School — part of the area's ongoing pattern of small branch schools growing and splitting.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for a temple long gone.

⛩️

Guanting Elementary takes its name from a Guanyin (觀音) temple pavilion (亭) that once stood in this part of Neimen District — the temple itself is gone, but its name lives on through the school. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
