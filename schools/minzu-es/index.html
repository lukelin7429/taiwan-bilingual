Minzu Elementary School

民族國民小學

About · 學校簡介

A century of names, a century of neighborhood memory.

Minzu Elementary was founded on April 1, 1919, under Japanese rule, as Chiayi's "Second Public School," with a new campus building completed the following year. It was renamed Tomon (東門) Public School in 1932/33 and Tomon National School in 1941. After Taiwan's 1945 retrocession to the Republic of China, the school moved to its present site and was renamed Minzu National School in 1946. It marked its 100th anniversary in March 2019 with a street parade and the unveiling of a centennial commemorative stone.

民族國小創校於日治時期大正8年（1919年）4月1日，初名「嘉義第二公學校」，隔年完成新校舍。昭和7、8年間（1932、1933年）更名為「東門公學校」，昭和16年（1941年）改稱「東門國民學校」。1945年臺灣光復後遷至現址，並於民國35年（1946年）更名為民族國民學校。民國108年（2019年）3月，學校以踩街遊行及揭幕百週年紀念石慶祝建校百週年。
