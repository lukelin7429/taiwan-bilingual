Su'ao Junior High School

蘇澳國民中學

Su'ao Township's namesake junior high, sitting at the administrative and cultural center of this fishing and hot-spring town.

About · 學校簡介

Passionate, soaring, and at the town's center.

Su'ao Junior High traces its history back to around 1955, based on a 70th-anniversary reference, though the exact founding date remains unconfirmed. The school operates under the motto "Passionate, Soaring, Vibrant Su-Zhong," runs an evening continuing-education division for adult learners, and maintains a notable youth baseball team tradition.

蘇澳國中校史可追溯至約民國44年（1955年），此推估依據校方70週年校慶的相關記載，確切創校年份尚待確認。學校校訓為「熱情、飛揚、活力蘇中」，設有夜間進修部服務成人學習者，並擁有頗具口碑的少棒隊傳統。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚾ A Youth Baseball Tradition

少棒隊傳統

The school has built a notable reputation for youth baseball, a sport with deep roots in Taiwanese school culture.

🌙 An Evening Division for Adults

夜間進修部

Beyond its regular junior high program, the school operates a continuing-education division that gives adult learners in Su'ao a path to complete their education.

🏛️ The Township's Namesake School

蘇澳鎮之名的國中

As the junior high that shares its name with Su'ao Township itself, the school sits at the administrative and cultural heart of the community.
