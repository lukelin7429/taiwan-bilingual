About · 學校簡介

The district's oldest school, and its namesake.

Xiaogang Elementary School is the oldest school in Siaogang District, tracing its founding to April 1, 1909, during the Japanese colonial era, when it opened as “鳳山公學校港仔埑分教場,” a branch classroom of Fongshan Public School. It was renamed “港仔埑公學校” in 1920 before eventually taking the Xiaogang name. As the district's first school, its name directly preserves “小港” (“small harbor”) — the tidal inlet that gave the entire district its identity, long before land reclamation reshaped the coastline into the site of Kaohsiung's airport and seaport.

小港國小是小港區歷史最悠久的學校，創校可追溯至1909年（日治明治42年）4月1日，最初為「鳳山公學校港仔墘分教場」，隸屬鳳山公學校的分教場。1920年（大正9年）改稱「港仔墘公學校」，其後才逐漸採用「小港」之名。作為區內第一所學校，其校名直接保留了「小港」（意指「小型港灣」）這個地名——早在填海造陸將海岸線改造為高雄機場與港口用地之前，這正是整個地區得名的潮汐港澳。
