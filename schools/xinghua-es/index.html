Xinghua Elementary School

興華國民小學

A public elementary school in Erlin Township that grew from a small branch campus into an independent school, long supported by a local temple's scholarship tradition.

位於二林鎮的公立國小，從小型分校發展為獨立學校，長年獲在地廟宇獎學金傳統支持。

About · 學校簡介

A branch school that grew into its own community.

Xinghua Elementary School began in August 1959 as a branch of Erlin Elementary School, opening on 5,670 square meters of land with five classes. It became an independent school in September 1963 under its first principal, Xie Haitu. By 1965 the campus had expanded to 14,150 square meters, and the school marked its 50th anniversary in 2013.

興華國小創立於民國48年（1959年）8月，最初是二林國小的分校，校地5,670平方公尺，設有5個班級。民國52年（1963年）9月獨立設校，首任校長為謝海圖。至民國54年（1965年），校地已擴展至14,150平方公尺，並於民國102年（2013年）舉辦建校五十週年慶祝活動。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 From Branch to Independent School

從分校到獨立設校

Opened in 1959 as a branch of Erlin Elementary with just five classes, Xinghua became a fully independent school within four years, a milestone still marked in its history today.

🏞️ A Campus That Grew Quickly

校地快速擴展

The school's grounds more than doubled in size within its first six years, from 5,670 to 14,150 square meters, reflecting steady community investment in local education.

🎊 Half a Century of History

建校五十週年

Xinghua celebrated its 50th anniversary in 2013, a milestone that brought together generations of alumni and the surrounding Erlin community.

🙏 A Temple's Century-Long Support

仁和宮獎學助學

The nearby Renhe Temple has long provided scholarships to the school's outstanding students, a close bond between local faith community and education.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A temple that invests in its neighborhood's children.

⛩️

Xinghua Elementary maintains close ties to the local Renhe Temple (仁和宮), which awards scholarships to outstanding students — a tradition reflecting how deeply community and faith institutions in Erlin Township support local education. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
