National Experimental High School at Hsinchu Science Park

國立新竹科學園區實驗高級中等學校

About · 學校簡介

One school, five divisions, ages 3 to 18.

National Experimental High School at Hsinchu Science Park began its preparatory phase in June 1982 and was officially established in August 1983 to serve the educational needs of children of employees at companies, government agencies, and research institutions in the Hsinchu Science Park. Uniquely among Taiwan's schools, it operates five co-located divisions — preschool, elementary, junior high, senior high, and a dedicated bilingual department — for students aged 3 to 18 under a single institution. It took on its current English and Chinese name on January 1, 2021. The bilingual department is designed to align with international standards and bridge education systems for the children of returning overseas scholars and international families.

新竹科學園區實驗高級中等學校於1982年（民國71年）6月開始籌備，1983年（民國72年）8月正式成立，旨在服務新竹科學園區內公司、政府機關及研究機構員工子女的教育需求。學校獨特地將幼兒園、國小部、國中部、高中部與雙語部五個學制整合於同一校園內，招收3至18歲的學生。2021年1月1日起改用現行中英文校名。雙語部的設計旨在銜接國際教育體系，服務歸國學人及國際家庭子女。
