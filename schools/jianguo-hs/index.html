Chien Kuo High School

臺北市立建國高級中學

One of Taiwan's oldest and largest public boys' senior highs, founded in 1898 and known for its historic Red Building and free-spirited campus culture.

台灣歷史最悠久、規模最大的公立男子高中之一，創校於1898年，以歷史建築「紅樓」與自由開放的校園文化聞名。

About · 學校簡介

One of Taiwan's first modern secondary schools.

Chien Kuo High School traces its roots to 1898, when it began as a secondary department attached to the "National Language School" — one of the very first Western-style secondary schools established in Taiwan. It was reorganized as "Taiwan Provincial Taipei Chien Kuo Middle School" in December 1945 after Taiwan's retrocession, and took its current name in 1967. For decades the school was Taiwan's largest public high school by enrollment — in 1969 its day, night, and supplementary divisions together enrolled more than 7,500 students — and it has long sent roughly half of each graduating class, over 500 students a year, on to National Taiwan University. With over 120,000 alumni to date, it remains known for an unusually free-spirited campus culture and vibrant student clubs.

建國高中創校於明治31年（1898年），前身為附屬於「國語學校」的中學部，是台灣最早成立的西式中學之一。1945年台灣光復後，於同年12月改制為「台灣省立台北建國中學」，1967年定名為「臺北市立建國高級中學」。本校曾長期是台灣規模最大的公立高中——1969年日間部、夜間部與補校合計學生數逾7,500人，且長年約有半數畢業生（每年逾500人）錄取國立台灣大學。建校至今校友已逾12萬人，以自由開放的校風與活躍的社團文化聞名。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🧱 The Historic Red Building

紅樓古蹟

Completed in 1909 and designed by government architect Kondō Jūrō in a late-Renaissance-influenced red-brick style, the campus's "Red Building" was designated a Taipei city monument in 1998 and restored in 2005, after a 1970s demolition plan was blocked by the then-principal.

👥 Once Taiwan's Largest High School

全台規模最大高中

By 1969, the school's day, night, and supplementary divisions together enrolled over 7,500 students, making it Taiwan's largest public high school by enrollment for much of the 20th century.

🎓 A Feeder to National Taiwan University

半數畢業生錄取台大

The school has historically sent roughly half of each graduating class — 500-plus students a year — to National Taiwan University, while cultivating an unusually free-spirited campus culture with vibrant student clubs.

🎉 Over 120,000 Alumni

逾十二萬校友

More than 120,000 alumni have graduated from Chien Kuo since its 1898 founding, reflecting well over a century of continuous operation at the heart of Taipei.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Facing the South Sea Academy.

🏛️

Chien Kuo High School faces the "South Sea Academy" (南海學園) cultural precinct directly across South Sea Road — home to the Taiwan Museum's Nanmen branch, the Botanical Garden, and other Japanese-era cultural institutions — situating the school within Zhongzheng District's cluster of colonial-era education and culture buildings.
