Daping Multiple Intelligence Ecological Aesthetics Experimental Elementary School

大坪多元智能生態美學實驗小學

A public school unlike almost any other in Taiwan — its curriculum is built entirely on Howard Gardner's theory of multiple intelligences, and its remote campus draws tens of thousands of butterflies a year, reportedly the highest butterfly diversity of any school in the country.

全台罕見的公立實驗小學，課程完全建構於迦納「多元智能理論」之上，偏遠校園每年吸引數萬隻蝴蝶造訪，據稱是全台蝴蝶物種多樣性最高的校園。

About · 學校簡介

An ordinary village school, reinvented as something extraordinary.

Daping Elementary was founded in 1943, near the end of Japanese colonial rule, as a modest village school in Beipu's remote Waiping area. Facing the same shrinking enrollment that threatens many rural Taiwanese schools, it took a radically different path around 2017: reorganizing entirely as a public experimental school built on psychologist Howard Gardner's theory of multiple intelligences. Rather than a standard curriculum, students move through eight distinct intelligence-based learning tracks, take mandatory violin lessons in the lower grades that grow into string ensembles by the upper grades, and are guided by an "OWL" character-education honor system. Perhaps most remarkable of all is the campus itself: an ecological showcase said to host the highest diversity of butterfly species recorded on any school campus in Taiwan, with tens of thousands of individual butterflies visiting each year.

大坪國小創立於1943年，正值日治時期末期，原是北埔外坪地區一所樸實的村落小學。與許多台灣偏鄉學校一樣，大坪也曾面臨學生人數銳減的困境，卻在2017年前後走上了一條截然不同的道路：徹底轉型為以美國心理學家迦納「多元智能理論」為根基的公立實驗小學。學校不採用傳統單一課程架構，而是讓學生走過八大智能學習軌道，低年級必修小提琴課程，並在高年級發展成弦樂團，同時以「貓頭鷹（OWL）」品格教育榮譽制度引導學生成長。而最令人驚艷的，莫過於校園本身——這裡是一座生態展示場，據稱擁有全台校園蝴蝶物種多樣性之冠，每年吸引數萬隻蝴蝶造訪。

Highlights · 學校特色

A school built entirely around a different idea of intelligence.

🦋 10,000s

Butterflies visit the campus every year — reportedly the highest species diversity recorded on any school campus in Taiwan, turning the grounds themselves into a living classroom.

🧠 Eight Intelligences, Eight Paths

八大智能學習軌道

The entire curriculum is organized around Howard Gardner's theory of multiple intelligences, giving students eight distinct intelligence-based learning tracks instead of a single standardized path.

🎻 Violin for Everyone

全體必修小提琴

Every student in the lower grades takes mandatory violin lessons, which grow into full string ensembles by the upper grades — a rare commitment to music education for a small rural public school.

🦉 The OWL Honor System

OWL品格教育制度

Character education runs through an "OWL" honor system, a distinctive framework for shaping student behavior and values across the whole school day.

🦋 A National-Level Butterfly Campus

全台蝴蝶多樣性之冠

The school's ecological campus reportedly hosts the highest diversity of butterfly species of any school campus in Taiwan, drawing tens of thousands of visiting butterflies each year and anchoring its science and arts curriculum in direct observation of nature.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The forested hills of remote Waiping.

🌲

Located in Beipu's remote Waiping area, this experimental school channels the township's forested hill ecology into a nationally distinctive butterfly-based science and arts curriculum — a modern echo of Beipu's deep connection to its natural landscape alongside its famous Old Street and lei cha culture.
