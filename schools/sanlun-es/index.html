About · 學校簡介

From branch class to milestone anniversary.

Sanlun Elementary was founded in August 1951 as the "Feisha National School Sanlun Branch Class," a single classroom serving the inland community of Lunnan Village. It later grew into an independent school and celebrated its 60th anniversary in 2016, marking six decades of continuous service to the farming families of this part of Sihu Township.

三崙國小創立於民國40年（1951年）8月，最初名為「飛沙國校三崙分班」，服務內陸的崙南村社區。學校後來獨立設校，並於民國105年（2016年）舉辦六十週年校慶，標誌著長達一甲子持續服務四湖鄉農家子弟的歷史。
