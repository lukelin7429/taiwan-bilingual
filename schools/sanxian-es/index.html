Sanxian Elementary School

三仙國民小學

Serving the Amis fishing village of Pisirian and neighboring coastal communities, with its own natural history education hall.

About · 學校簡介

A school shaped by shells and the sea.

Sanxian Elementary opened in 1949 as a branch school before becoming independent. Its district covers four coastal Amis Indigenous communities, including Pisirian, a village known for its distinctive "Beach Cat" drum circle and cat-themed folk art. In 1985 the school set up a shell-specimen exhibition room that later grew into a Taitung County Natural History Education Hall, and it added a preschool class in 2005.

三仙國小創立於民國38年（1949年），最初以分校形式設立，後獨立設校。學區涵蓋四個阿美族沿海部落，其中包括以「比西里岸」漂流木貓咪意象與貓群鼓陣聞名的比西里岸部落。學校於民國74年（1985年）設立貝殼標本展示室，後擴展為「臺東縣自然史教育館」，並於民國94年（2005年）增設幼兒園班。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🐚 A Natural History Education Hall

自然史教育館

What began as a shell-specimen exhibition room in 1985 has grown into a full Taitung County Natural History Education Hall, giving students hands-on access to the region's marine life.

🐱 Home to the "Beach Cat" Village

比西里岸貓群鼓陣

The school district includes Pisirian, an Amis coastal village famous across Taiwan for its cat-themed folk art and its "Beach Cat" drum circle performances.

🌊 Four Amis Coastal Communities

四個阿美族部落學區

Sanxian draws students from four Amis Indigenous fishing communities along the coast, giving the school a strong sense of shared maritime and cultural identity.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Pisirian and the Amis coast.

🥁

Sanxian Elementary serves Pisirian (比西里岸) and other Amis fishing villages, embodying Chenggong's identity as home to a large Indigenous Amis population whose way of life has long been tied to the sea. Pisirian in particular is known nationwide for its driftwood cat sculptures and "Beach Cat" drum circle.
