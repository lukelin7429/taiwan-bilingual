About · 學校簡介

A century-old school wrapped in mountains and canals.

Shanjia Elementary School has over a century of history according to official school materials, though its exact founding date is not documented in available records. The school is nicknamed a 'forest elementary school within the city' for being surrounded by mountains and water: it fronts Dadong Mountain (大棟山) and is encircled by two historic irrigation canals, Houcun Canal (後村圳) and Shierbo Canal (十二股圳). The school recently completed a second-phase new campus building project.

山佳國小依校方資料記載已有逾百年校史，惟確切創校年份未見於現有公開紀錄。學校有「城市中的森林小學」美譽，因校園四周群山環繞、水圳交織：面向大棟山，並被後村圳與十二股圳兩條歷史悠久的灌溉水圳環抱。學校近年完成第二期新校舍建設工程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌲 A 'Forest School' in the City

城市中的森林小學

Shanjia Elementary is nicknamed a 'forest elementary school within the city' for its setting, surrounded by mountains and water on all sides.

⛰️ Facing Dadong Mountain

面向大棟山

The campus fronts Dadong Mountain (大棟山), giving students daily views of one of the area's most recognizable natural landmarks.

💧 Encircled by Historic Canals

百年古圳環繞

Two historic irrigation canals, Houcun Canal (後村圳) and Shierbo Canal (十二股圳), encircle the school, tying it to the region's agricultural water-management heritage.

🏗️ A Newly Expanded Campus

第二期新校舍完工

The school recently completed a second-phase new campus building project, modernizing its facilities while preserving its natural surroundings.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where century-old canals still shape the land.

💧

The school's mountain-and-canal setting reflects Zhunan's traditional Hakka irrigation-agriculture heritage, with the century-old canals still shaping the local landscape today. See the Miaoli county page for more on the region.
