Wenshan Junior High School

文山國中

About · 學校簡介

A school built on colonial-era foundations.

Wenshan Junior High was established in 1946, in the first full year after the end of Japanese rule, on a site in Xindian's Wenzhong (文中) neighborhood that had previously housed a branch of the Japanese-era Taipei Normal School's affiliated elementary school. The school's name and location are drawn directly from this neighborhood, linking it to Xindian's colonial-era education history. Its badge cleverly combines the characters "文" (wen) and "中" (zhong) with triangular shapes representing "山" (shan, mountain) — spelling out the school's own name in visual form.

文山國中創立於民國35年（1946年），是臺灣光復後首年成立的學校，校址位於新店文中里，前身為日治時期臺北師範學校附屬國小新店分教場。校名與校址皆源自文中里地名，與新店的日治教育史一脈相承。校徽巧妙結合「文」與「中」二字，並以三角形圖案象徵「山」字，將校名以圖像方式呈現。
