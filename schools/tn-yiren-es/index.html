About · 學校簡介

A centennial school shaped by an industrial park.

Yiren Elementary School was founded in 1921. It celebrated its 100th-anniversary, combined with a community sports day, in 2021. The school sits at the southernmost point of Rende District; the surrounding catchment was historically farming-dependent until the establishment of the Bao'an Industrial Park (保安工業區) reshaped local livelihoods. Today the school runs five signature extracurricular clubs and teams: a jump-drum team, a soccer team, a recorder ensemble, an English reader's-theater troupe, and Cub Scouts.

依仁國小創校於大正10年（1921年），2021年舉辦「百週年校慶」並結合社區運動會慶祝。學校位於仁德區最南端，周邊學區原以農業為主，直到保安工業區設立後，才轉變為工業聚落。學校現辦理五項特色社團與代表隊：跳鼓陣、足球隊、直笛團、英語讀者劇場，以及幼童軍。
