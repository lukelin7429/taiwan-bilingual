Meilun Junior High School

美崙國民中學

A public junior high school named for the low hill on Hualien City's north side, rebuilt in 1996 with a distinctive classroom-by-classroom design.

以花蓮市北側的美崙山為名的公立國中，1996年重建校舍，設計上採每間教室皆設廁所的特色規劃。

About · 學校簡介

Named for the hill that shapes its neighborhood.

Meilun Junior High School traces its founding to March 1966, when the Hualien County government began planning it as "Hualien City First County Junior High School" the previous year. It was renamed Meilun Junior High in 1968, taking its name from Meilun — the hill and district on the north side of Hualien City. The school's current buildings were rebuilt in 1996, incorporating a "restroom in every classroom" design that was distinctive for its time.

美崙國中的歷史可追溯至1966年3月，花蓮縣政府於前一年開始規劃籌設「花蓮市第一縣立初級中學」。1968年更名為美崙國中，校名取自花蓮市北側的美崙山與美崙地區。現有校舍於1996年重建，並採用當時頗具特色的「每間教室設置廁所」設計。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛰️ Named for Meilun Hill

以美崙山為名

The school takes its name directly from Meilun, the low hill and surrounding district on Hualien City's north side, anchoring it to a specific local landmark.

🚻 A Restroom in Every Classroom

教室內附設廁所

The 1996 campus rebuild introduced a "restroom in every classroom" layout, an unusual and forward-thinking design choice for a Taiwanese public school of that era.

🏛️ Planned Since 1965

1965年開始規劃

County planning for the school began in 1965, a year before it opened as Hualien City's first county-run junior high school — part of Taiwan's postwar expansion of universal junior high education.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchored to Meilun's identity.

🏞️

Named for and located near Meilun Mountain, the low hill on Hualien City's north side that gives the surrounding district its identity and remains a popular local park and viewpoint. See the Hualien County schools page for more on the region.
