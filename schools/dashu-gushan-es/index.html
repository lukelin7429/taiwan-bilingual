Gushan Elementary School

姑山國小

Named for a foothill known as "Aunt Mountain," this small school tripled its campus size after becoming independent in 1981.

About · 學校簡介

A branch class that grew into its own campus.

Gushan Elementary School began in 1965 as the Gushan branch class of Xipu National School (溪埔國民學校姑山分班), becoming fully independent in 1981 as Kaohsiung County's Gushan Elementary School. Since independence, its campus has grown from 0.3 to 0.9 hectares. The school takes its name from Gushan ("Aunt Mountain"), a local hill in the Dashu foothills.

姑山國小始於1965年（民國54年），最初為「溪埔國民學校姑山分班」，1981年（民國70年）正式獨立設校，改制為高雄縣姑山國民小學。獨立設校後，校地面積由0.3公頃擴增至0.9公頃。校名源自大樹丘陵地帶的姑山（「姑姑的山」）。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A hill in the Dashu foothills.

⛰️

Gushan is named for Gushan ("Aunt Mountain"), a local hill in the Dashu foothills — a reminder of the district's hillside geography, and unrelated to the differently-written Gushan District (鼓山區) elsewhere in Kaohsiung. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
