About · 學校簡介

A mountain school reimagined.

Dongbian Experimental Elementary School was founded in 1979 and converted into an experimental-education school in 2015, running mixed-age classes and innovative small-group co-learning models. The school takes its name from Dongbian, a hillside, rural community on Taiping District's mountainous eastern edge.

東汴實驗國小創立於民國68年（1979年），並於民國104年（2015年）轉型為實驗教育學校，推動混齡教學與創新的小組共學模式。校名取自東汴，是太平區東側山區的丘陵農村聚落。
