About · 學校簡介

A school growing up alongside the Science Park.

Jiangong Elementary was established on May 1, 1958 as Hsinchu County Hsinchu City Jiangong National School, with its first principal, Liu Yuhua, taking office that October. The school went through several renamings over the following decades — in 1968, 1982, and again in 2002 — as Hsinchu's administrative status changed. In 2025 the school celebrated its 67th anniversary, with school-based curricula that now emphasize global citizenship and sustainability. Its recorder ensemble has won national honors twice, in 2009 and 2015, and the school received a bronze certification from the Ministry of Education's health-promotion program in 2012.

建功國小創校於民國47年（1958年）5月1日，首任校長劉玉華同年10月20日到任。校名歷經多次更迭，分別於1968年、1982年及2002年因應新竹行政地位變動而改名。民國114年（2025年）學校舉辦建校六十七週年活動，校本課程著重全球公民素養與永續發展教育。學校直笛團曾於2009年及2015年兩度獲得全國性獎項，並於2012年獲教育部健康促進學校銀質獎肯定。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎵 A Nationally Honored Recorder Ensemble

全國獲獎直笛團

Jiangong's recorder ensemble won national-level honors in both 2009 and 2015, a signature achievement in the school's music program.

💧 Beside a Historic Irrigation Channel

緊鄰歷史水圳

The campus sits beside the historic Tingpu Canal (汀浦圳), locally known as Jiangong River, in the Chihtuchi/Jungong area — the school's curriculum sends students out to explore this local industrial-heritage landscape firsthand.

🌱 Health and Sustainability Focus

健康與永續教育

The school received Ministry of Education bronze certification for health promotion in 2012, and its 67th-anniversary curricula in 2025 emphasized global citizenship and sustainability.

🎓 Neighbor to Tsing Hua and the Science Park

鄰近清華大學與科學園區

Located near National Tsing Hua University and the Hsinchu Science Park, giving students everyday proximity to Taiwan's leading research and tech institutions.
