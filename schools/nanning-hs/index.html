About · 學校簡介

Tainan's southernmost high school, rooted in a fishing-village branch.

Tainan Municipal Nanning Senior High School began in 1960 as a branch campus known as "Tainan Municipal Middle School, Wanli Branch," named for Wanli (灣裡), the old sand-flat fishing settlement at the city's southern coastal edge. It became an independent junior high the following year, on October 24, 1961, and was converted into a full junior-and-senior high school in 2003. Today it is the southernmost senior high school in Tainan City proper, serving about 699 students across 29 classes — including a resource class — on a 4.7-hectare campus. Its school emblem features four bells representing virtue, intellect, physique, and community, and it supports an active student life with 16 clubs.

臺南市立南寧高級中學創立於1960年（民國49年），最初為「臺南市立中學灣裡分部」，以臺南南端海岸沙洲漁村灣裡命名。隔年（1961年10月24日）獨立設校為「臺南市立南寧初級中學」，2003年（民國92年）改制為完全中學。目前是臺南市區最南端的高中，全校約699名學生、29班（含資源班），校地4.7公頃。校徽以四口鐘象徵「德智體群」，並設有16個學生社團。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for a historic coastal fishing village.

🐟

Nanning's original 1960 branch campus was named for Wanli (灣裡), a historic sand-flat fishing village on Tainan's southern coastal edge. That naming ties the school directly to the Bao'an and coastal fishing communities that have long defined South District's waterfront identity, long before the area's postwar urban growth.
