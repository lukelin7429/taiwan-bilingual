Liuhe Elementary School

六合國民小學

A postwar neighborhood school on Toufen's east side, freshly renovated for its 74th anniversary after decades serving the city's dense Hakka residential core.

頭份市東側的社區學校，創校逾七十年，2019年整修校舍慶祝74週年校慶，服務頭份密集的客家住宅核心區。

About · 學校簡介

Serving five neighborhoods for over seventy years.

Liuhe Elementary School traces back roughly to 1945, making it more than seventy years old at the time of its most recent major renovation. The school draws students from five neighborhoods on Toufen's east side — Dongzhuang, Minzu, Minquan, Minsheng, and Heping li — and today runs around 32 classes, including a special-education class, with roughly 66 faculty and staff. Its front building was rebuilt and reopened in December 2019 to mark the school's 74th anniversary.

六合國小創校約在1945年（民國34年）前後，至最近一次大規模整修時已逾七十年校史。學校招收頭份市東側東庄、民族、民權、民生、和平等五個里的學童，目前約有32班（含一班特教班），教職員約66人。校舍前棟於2019年12月整修完工並重新啟用，適逢創校74週年。
