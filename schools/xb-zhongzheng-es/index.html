Zhongzheng Elementary School

中正國小

A 1970s elementary school built to serve Xindian's growing town-center population, marked by a distinctive heart-shaped emblem.

About · 學校簡介

Built for a growing town center.

Zhongzheng Elementary opened in 1976, one of several schools built in Xindian's town center during a period of rapid postwar population growth. The school is easily recognized by its heart-shaped badge, rendered in orange, red, green, and blue, with the characters "中正" at its center. Like its sibling schools from the same era, it continues to serve families in the older residential core of the district.

中正國小創立於民國65年（1976年），是新店市區為因應戰後人口快速成長而興建的數所國小之一。學校校徽為心形設計，以橘、紅、綠、藍四色組成，中央書寫「中正」二字，辨識度高。與同期興建的姊妹校一樣，中正國小至今持續服務新店老城區的居民家庭。
