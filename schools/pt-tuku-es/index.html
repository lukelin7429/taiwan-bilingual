About · 學校簡介

A farming-community school by the Gaoping River.

Tuku Elementary is located in Zhonghe Village, Ligang Township — distinct from the older, 1899-founded Tuku Elementary of the same name in Yunlin County. The school once had a branch campus, Xinguo Elementary, in Tuku Village, which operated from 1965 until it was discontinued on August 1, 2020. Most parents in the school district work as farmers growing lower-value crops, with some engaged in aquaculture or commuting to factory jobs elsewhere — a snapshot of the economic realities facing Ligang's farming families.

土庫國小位於里港鄉中和村，與雲林縣同名、創校於1899年的土庫國小並非同一所學校。學校過去曾設有信國分校，位於土庫村，自1965年運作至2020年8月1日停辦。學區內多數家長從事附加價值較低的農作，部分投入養殖業，或需通勤至外地工廠工作——反映出里港農業社區所面臨的經濟現實。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Discontinued Branch Campus

信國分校（1965–2020）

The school's former Xinguo Branch Campus in Tuku Village operated for 55 years, from 1965 until its discontinuation in August 2020, reflecting the demographic shifts in Ligang's rural villages.

🐟 A Farming and Aquaculture Community

農業與養殖並存

Families in the school district work in a mix of lower-value crop farming, aquaculture, and commuting to outside factory jobs, giving the school a direct connection to the area's working economy.

🏷️ A Distinct School Despite the Shared Name

與雲林同名學校無關

This Tuku Elementary in Ligang Township is a separate institution from the older, 1899-founded Tuku Elementary of the same name in Yunlin County.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Farmland along the Gaoping River.

🌾

Tuku Elementary serves a farming community in Ligang Township's agricultural plains near the Gaoping River, part of the broader Cishan-adjacent farmland known for betel-nut and rice cultivation. The mix of crop farming, aquaculture, and outside factory work among local families reflects the everyday economic texture of rural Pingtung life.
