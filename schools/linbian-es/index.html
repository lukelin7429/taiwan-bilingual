About · 學校簡介

An old school on a sinking street.

Linbian Elementary School was founded in 1913, in the 19th year after Japanese rule began, as "Linzibian Public School" (林仔邊公學校) under Japan's compulsory education policy. It was renamed "Linbian National School" in 1941, one of several name changes through the colonial period, making it one of the oldest institutions in Linbian Township. Old Japanese-era merchant houses still line Zhongshan Road in front of the school gate — their windows now nearly level with the road, a striking sign of the land subsidence that has reshaped this coastal community over the decades.

林邊國小創立於日本統治第19年（1913年，大正2年），初名「林仔邊公學校」，屬日本推行義務教育體制下設立的學校。民國30年（1941年）更名為「林邊國民學校」，歷經日治時期多次更名，是林邊鄉歷史最悠久的學校之一。校門前中山路上至今仍林立著日治時期的老商號，這些房舍的窗戶已因地層下陷而幾乎與路面齊平，鮮明呈現這個沿海社區數十年來的地貌變化。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Grouper country on the recovery road.

🐟

Linbian Township's coastal aquaculture economy, centered on grouper (石斑魚) farming, developed alongside a history of significant land subsidence — visible right outside the school gate in the sunken old buildings along Zhongshan Road. The township also carries the memory of Typhoon Morakot's devastating 2009 flood. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
