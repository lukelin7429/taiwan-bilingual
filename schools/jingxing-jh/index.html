About · 學校簡介

A standout school beside Xianjiyan.

Jingxing Junior High School was founded in 1985 and has grown into one of the stronger-regarded public junior high schools in Wenshan District.

The school emphasizes flexible curriculum design aimed at building transferable, "take-it-with-you" student competencies, and sits beside the entrance to the Xianjiyan (仙跡岩) hiking trail, one of Wenshan's best-known urban-adjacent green spaces.

景興國中創立於1985年（民國74年），是文山區公認辦學表現優異的公立國中之一。

學校強調彈性課程設計，致力培養學生「帶著走的能力」，校址緊鄰仙跡岩登山步道入口，是文山區著名的都市近郊登山景點。
