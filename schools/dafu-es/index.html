Dafu Elementary School

大福國民小學

A small Zhuangwei elementary school recognized for its work preserving Indigenous and mother-tongue languages alongside a strong environmental curriculum.

About · 學校簡介

Rooted in language and land.

Dafu Elementary School traces its lineage to a teaching annex known as "Dafu Fenjiaochang," which existed as early as 1937 before the school later became fully independent. Today Dafu is recognized as an outstanding school for Indigenous and mother-tongue language education, taking an active role in initiatives around Taiwan Mother Tongue Day. The school also runs a strong environmental-awareness curriculum, giving students a grounding in both cultural heritage and ecological responsibility.

大福國小的前身可追溯至「大福分教場」，早在1937年即已存在，後來獨立設校。如今大福國小因致力原住民族語及母語教育而獲獎肯定，並積極參與「本土語言日」相關活動。學校同時發展扎實的環境教育課程，讓學生在文化傳承與生態責任兩方面都能扎根。
