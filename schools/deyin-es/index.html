Deyin Elementary School

德音國小

A newer Wugu school whose students have twice placed at the National Science Fair, built for the district's late-1980s suburban growth.

五股新興學校，曾兩度於全國科展獲獎，見證1980年代末的郊區擴張。

About · 學校簡介

A newer school with a growing reputation.

Deyin Elementary opened in 1989, with enrollment beginning in 1991, as part of Wugu District's late-1980s suburban expansion when Taipei-adjacent development pushed into the area. The school established a dedicated physical-education class in 2007 and has built a strong record in academic competition, taking first and second place nationally at the National Science Fair in 2014 and 2015.

德音國小於民國78年（1989年）成立，民國80年（1991年）開始招生，是五股區1980年代末因鄰近台北都會發展外溢而興建的新興學校之一。學校於民國96年（2007年）成立體育班，並在學術競賽上表現亮眼，於民國103至104年（2014至2015年）連續兩年榮獲全國科學展覽會第一名與第二名的佳績。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏆 Back-to-Back National Science Fair Wins

全國科展連續獲獎

Deyin students took first place nationally at the 2014 National Science Fair and second place the following year — a rare two-year run for a Wugu elementary school.

🤸 A Dedicated PE Class

體育班特色

Since 2007, the school has run a dedicated physical-education class, giving athletically inclined students a structured track alongside academics.

🏘️ Built for Suburban Growth

郊區擴張新興學校

Opening in 1989, Deyin is one of the newer schools built as Taipei-adjacent development pushed into Wugu District in the late 1980s.
