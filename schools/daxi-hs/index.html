About · 學校簡介

In the shadow of the Daxi retreat.

Daxi Senior High traces back to 1964 as a branch division of Daxi Junior High, becoming independent in 1967 as Taoyuan County Dahan Junior High School. In 2009 it converted fully to senior-high status as Taoyuan County Daxi Senior High School, and it was renamed Taoyuan Municipal Daxi Senior High School in 2018 following the transfer of Taoyuan's national high schools to municipal administration. Its junior-high division stopped admitting new students in 2008 when the conversion took effect, with those students redirected to Daxi Junior High.

大溪高中的前身可追溯至1964年（民國53年）設立的「大溪初中一心分部」，1967年（民國56年）獨立設校，定名為桃園縣立大漢初級中學。2009年（民國98年）全面轉型為高中，更名為桃園縣立大溪高級中學，2018年隨桃園市屬高中改隸市府，再更名為桃園市立大溪高級中等學校。學校國中部已於2008年轉型時停止招收新生，原國中部學生轉入大溪國中就讀。

Local Culture · 在地文化

In the shadow of the Daxi retreat.

🏔️

Daxi Senior High sits in the same historic zone as 大溪行館 (Chiang Kai-shek's Daxi retreat), tying the school's neighborhood directly to the Cihu and Daxi mausoleum area's 20th-century political history — a landscape of memorial parks and mountain scenery that draws visitors from across Taiwan.
