About · 學校簡介

Built to relieve a booming district.

Xingren Junior High was established on August 1, 1980, specifically to relieve overcrowding at Cianjhen Junior High, and moved into its current campus on August 27, 1982. Its founding reflects the direct pressure that Kaohsiung Harbor's industrial and factory jobs placed on Cianjhen's population through the 1970s and 1980s.

興仁國中創立於民國69年（1980年）8月1日，設校目的即為紓解前鎮國中的就學壓力，並於民國71年（1982年）8月27日遷入現址。學校的誕生直接反映了1970至80年代高雄港埠與工廠就業機會為前鎮帶來的人口壓力。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A district shaped by the harbor.

⚓

Xingren's founding is a direct artifact of Cianjhen's 1970s-80s industrial boom, driven by jobs at Kaohsiung Harbor and the factories that grew up around it — the same forces that shaped many of the district's newer schools. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
