Dongguang Elementary School

東光國小

A century-old school in the tea-growing hills of Yuchi Township, rebuilt with Tzu Chi Foundation support after the 1999 earthquake.

About · 學校簡介

Rebuilt after disaster, rooted in the tea hills.

Dongguang Elementary traces its roots to 1922, when it was founded as the "Mujilunglang Branch Teaching Place" of Yuchi Public School, a satellite classroom serving the tea-growing hill community. It became an independent school, Dongguang National School, in 1941. The 1999 921 earthquake severely damaged the school's buildings, but with assistance from the Tzu Chi Foundation, a fully rebuilt campus was completed in November 2001, allowing the school to continue serving Dongguang Village's children.

東光國小的歷史可追溯至1922年（大正11年），前身為魚池公學校「木屐囒分教場」，服務茶山丘陵地帶的社區。1941年（昭和16年）獨立設校，正式定名為東光國民學校。1999年921大地震使校舍嚴重損毀，後在慈濟基金會的協助下於2001年11月完成重建，讓學校得以持續服務東光村的學童。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📜 A Century of History

百年校史

Founded in 1922 as a branch teaching place and made independent in 1941, Dongguang carries over a century of educational history in the Yuchi hills.

🏗️ Rebuilt with Tzu Chi's Help

慈濟援建校舍

After the devastating 1999 921 earthquake, the Tzu Chi Foundation helped rebuild the school's campus, completed in November 2001 — a landmark of post-disaster community recovery.

🍃 A Namesake Rooted in Tea History

木屐囒與茶產業歷史

The school's original name, "Mujilunglang," ties it directly to the historic tea-growing area near Sun Moon Lake that has shaped this community for generations.

Local Culture · 在地文化

In the tea-growing hills near Sun Moon Lake.

🍵

Dongguang Elementary is located in Dongguang Village, in the tea-growing hills of Yuchi Township, near the historic Mujilunglang (木屐囒) area long associated with Sun Moon Lake's celebrated tea culture. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
