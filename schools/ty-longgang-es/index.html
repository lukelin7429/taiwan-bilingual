About · 學校簡介

At the heart of a distinctive migrant community.

Longgang Elementary was founded in 1962 and sits at the center of one of Taiwan's most distinctive ethnic enclaves — a large community of Yunnan and Burma-origin KMT veterans and their descendants, famous locally for the neighborhood's "Yunnan-Burma alley" and its rice noodle (米干) cuisine. Repeated road-widening projects have shrunk the existing campus over the years, prompting the city to build an entirely new campus roughly one kilometer from the current site — a facility that will also partly serve nearby Longxing Elementary. The "build first, then relocate" project includes underground parking and carries a total cost exceeding NT$1.1 billion, with completion expected in 2027.

龍岡國小創立於民國51年（1962年），座落於台灣最具特色的族群聚落之一——雲南、緬甸籍國軍老兵及其後裔聚居的地區，當地著名的「雲南、緬甸巷」與米干文化即源於此。因道路多次拓寬而使校地不斷縮減，市府因此規劃於現址約一公里外興建全新校舍，未來也將部分供龍興國小使用。此「先建後拆」遷校計畫包含地下停車場，總經費逾新台幣11億元，預計於民國116年（2027年）完工。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Taiwan's Yunnan-Burma enclave.

🍜

Longgang is one of Taiwan's most distinctive ethnic enclaves — home to a large community of Yunnan and Burma-origin KMT veterans and their descendants, famous for the area's "Yunnan-Burma alley" and its rice noodle (米干) dishes. Longgang Elementary sits at the center of this specific migration history, distinct from Zhongli's more typical Hakka or Hoklo population, making it one of the district's most culturally distinctive school communities.
