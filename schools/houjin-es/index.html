About · 學校簡介

A school founded after tragedy, in a neighborhood of protest.

Houjin Elementary was independently established on March 1, 1952, under the name "Kaohsiung Municipal Heping National School," later renamed "Kaohsiung Nanzih Heping Elementary School" in August 1968 and given its current name in August 2006. Before the school existed, children in this area had to travel a long distance to attend Nanzih Elementary School; earlier branch schools had been established here in 1921 and 1934 but both closed due to insufficient enrollment. It was a 1950 traffic accident that killed a local student commuting to school that ultimately spurred local gentry to organize a new branch campus, leading to the school's founding two years later.

後勁國小於民國41年（1952年）3月1日獨立設校，原名「高雄市和平國民學校」，民國57年（1968年）8月改稱「高雄市楠梓區和平國民小學」，民國95年（2006年）8月1日改為現名。設校前，當地學區學生需遠赴楠梓國小就讀；1921年、1934年曾兩度在此設立楠梓國校後勁分校，但皆因人數不足而廢校。民國39年（1950年）一場造成通學學生車禍喪命的意外，促成地方仕紳重新籌設分校，兩年後正式設立。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💔 Founded After a Tragedy

因意外促成設校

A 1950 traffic accident that killed a student commuting to a distant school spurred local gentry to organize a new branch campus, leading directly to the school's founding in 1952.

🔁 Two Failed Attempts Before Success

兩度設校未成

Branch schools were attempted here twice before, in 1921 and 1934, but both closed due to insufficient enrollment — making the eventual 1952 founding the third and lasting attempt.

✊ In the Heart of Protest History

座落於社運歷史重地

The school sits in Houjin, one of Nanzih's most historically significant neighborhoods and the birthplace of the 1980s–90s anti-Fifth Naphtha Cracker (反五輕) environmental protest movement.

Local Culture · 在地文化

In the birthplace of a protest movement.

✊

"Houjin" (後勁) is a Qing-dynasty Hoklo settlement name, and the neighborhood remains one of Nanzih's most historically resonant — home to the 1980s–90s "anti-Fifth Naphtha Cracker" (反五輕) protest movement, a landmark chapter in Taiwan's grassroots environmental history.
