About · 學校簡介

Xiluo's oldest school, still teaching after 125 years.

Wenchang Elementary was founded in 1899 (Meiji 32), during the early years of Japanese colonial rule, and is officially recognized as one of Taiwan's "century-old elementary schools" — a designation reserved for campuses with more than a hundred years of continuous history. The school took its current name in 1968. Its old dormitory cluster has been registered as a historic building, and the school maintains active baseball, badminton, and table-tennis teams.

文昌國小創校於明治32年（1899年），為日治時期初期設立的學校，是台灣官方認定的「百年老校」之一——這項殊榮僅授予校史超過百年的學校。學校於民國57年（1968年）更為現名。校內舊宿舍群已登錄為歷史建築，同時也組有活躍的棒球隊、羽球隊與桌球隊。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏆 An Officially Recognized Century School

官方認定百年老校

Founded in 1899, Wenchang is one of Taiwan's officially recognized "century-old elementary schools" — a rare distinction tied to its 125-plus years of history.

🏛️ A Historic Dormitory Cluster

登錄歷史建築宿舍群

The school's old dormitory cluster has been registered as a historic building, preserving physical remnants of its long institutional past.

⚾ Three Active Sports Teams

棒球、羽球、桌球隊

Wenchang maintains baseball, badminton, and table-tennis teams, giving students a range of athletic outlets alongside its historic academic legacy.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Woven into the old street's fabric.

🌉

As Xiluo's oldest school, tracing back over a century, Wenchang Elementary is woven into the fabric of the town's historic old street and its renowned soy-sauce brewing tradition. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
