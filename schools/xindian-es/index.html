About · 學校簡介

The school that shares the district's name.

Xindian Elementary is the founding, flagship school of the district that shares its name — opening in 1898 as the "Xindian Branch of the Taipei Mandarin Training Institute," one of the earliest modern schools established in the area under Japanese rule. It went through several renamings over the following seven decades before settling on its current name in 1968. The school anchors the historic town center on Xindian Road, near the riverside settlement from which the entire district takes its name.

新店國小是與新店區同名的創始旗艦國小，創立於1898年（日治時期），前身為「臺北國語傳習所新店分教場」，是當地最早設立的新式學校之一。其後七十年間歷經多次更名，直到民國57年（1968年）才定名為現今的新店國小。學校座落於新店路，位於新店老城中心，緊鄰賦予整個新店區地名由來的溪畔聚落。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚩 The District's Namesake School

與新店同名的旗艦校

As the school that shares its name with the entire district, Xindian Elementary holds a unique flagship status among the area's schools.

📜 Roots in an 1898 Language Institute

國語傳習所分教場起源

The school began life as a branch of the Taipei Mandarin Training Institute, one of the earliest formal educational institutions introduced under Japanese rule.

🔄 Several Names Before Settling in 1968

歷經多次更名

Renamed repeatedly over seven decades, the school only adopted its current name in 1968, reflecting the shifting educational administration of 20th-century Taiwan.

🌊 At the Historic Riverside Center

新店溪畔老城中心

The campus sits on Xindian Road at the heart of the old town, near the riverside settlement that gave the entire district its name.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring the riverside settlement it's named for.

🏮

As the founding school of the district, Xindian Elementary anchors the historic riverside town center from which Xindian takes its name — a settlement that grew up along the Xindian River long before the surrounding hills filled with suburbs. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
