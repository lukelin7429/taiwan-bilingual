Guangchun Junior High School

光春國民中學

A Chaozhou Town junior high with a distinctive five-discipline art curriculum and a long-running youth baseball team, sitting near several notable local temples and Taiwan's famed Ming Hwa Yuan opera troupe.

About · 學校簡介

Where art and athletics meet local heritage.

Guangchun Junior High School has built a distinctive reputation around a five-discipline art curriculum — sketching, ink painting, printmaking, design, and watercolor — with student artwork recognized for its quality and diversity. The school also fields a long-running youth baseball team that has competed in numerous national tournaments. Its location places it near several notable local temples (Zunwang Temple, Tiandi Temple, and Juehui Temple) and close to the home base of Ming Hwa Yuan (明華園), one of Taiwan's most celebrated traditional opera troupes — a reflection of the rich cultural resources surrounding the school.

光春國中以五大類美術課程——素描、水墨、版畫、設計、水彩——建立起獨特的辦學特色，學生作品在質量與多樣性上備受肯定。學校也長年組織青少棒隊，多次參加全國性賽事。校址鄰近尊王宮、天帝廟、覺慧寺等地方廟宇，並靠近台灣知名歌仔戲團「明華園」的基地，展現學校周邊豐富的在地文化資源。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Chaozhou, southern Pingtung's rail and market hub.

🚂

Chaozhou is a major market town and transportation hub in southern Pingtung, tied historically to the sugar industry, rail transport, and the Chaozhou Forest Railway heritage. Guangchun Junior High's proximity to Ming Hwa Yuan and several historic temples places it right in the middle of this living cultural landscape.
