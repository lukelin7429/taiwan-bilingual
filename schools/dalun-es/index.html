About · 學校簡介

A century built on one leader's generosity.

Dalun Elementary School was founded on April 24, 1921, after the local village chief, Lu Dian, donated private land so children would not have to walk long distances to Shuishang Public School. It was approved as the Dalun detached classroom of Shuishang Public School, with Cai Zang serving as the first head teacher. The school now has over a hundred years of history. Today it runs a distinctive unicycle (獨輪車) team with documented performances and competitions, a "閱讀磐石" (Reading Cornerstone) reading-promotion initiative, agricultural and educational farm programs, and received a 2021 "School Characteristics Certification," competing in AI literacy competitions.

大崙國小創立於民國10年（1921年）4月24日，源於地方保正呂典捐出私有土地，讓孩童不必長途跋涉前往水上公學校上課，獲准設立為水上公學校大崙分離教室，首任教師為蔡藏。學校至今已有逾百年歷史。現今學校經營特色鮮明的獨輪車隊，累積演出與競賽紀錄，推動「閱讀磐石」閱讀推廣計畫及農場教育課程，並於民國110年（2021年）獲得「學校特色認證」，同時參與AI素養競賽。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚲 The Unicycle Team

獨輪車隊

A distinctive unicycle (獨輪車) team program has a documented history of performances and competitions, a standout activity for a rural elementary school.

📚 Reading Cornerstone

閱讀磐石計畫

The "閱讀磐石" (Reading Cornerstone) initiative promotes reading habits across the student body alongside agricultural and educational farm programs.

🤖 AI Literacy Competitions

AI素養競賽

Following its 2021 "School Characteristics Certification," the school competes in AI literacy competitions, reflecting a modern, forward-looking curriculum.

🌾 A Century of Land Donation
