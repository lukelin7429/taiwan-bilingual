Hequn Junior High School

和群國民中學

A public junior high in Hemei Township, established in 1980 to serve a growing community, with a shared sports complex bringing students and residents together.

座落於和美鎮的公立國中，成立於民國69年（1980年），設有與社區共用的運動場館，連結學校與居民生活。

About · 學校簡介

A community school built for a growing township.

Hequn Junior High received Ministry approval in 1979 and was officially established on August 1, 1980, initially overseen by a concurrent director from Hemei Junior High, with Chang Baichao appointed as the first official principal on August 23, 1980. In its first year, the school borrowed classrooms from Hemei Junior High and Hemei Elementary before moving to its current site in July 1981. Originally serving 11 villages, the school's district expanded in 2005, and it now spans 53 classrooms and offices, alongside a sports complex completed in 2021 that is shared with the local community.

和群國中於民國68年（1979年）獲教育部核准，民國69年（1980年）8月1日正式成立，初期由和美國中校長張雲霆兼任主任，同年8月23日聘任張百超為首任正式校長。創校首年借用和美國中及和美國小教室上課，民國70年（1981年）7月遷入現址。學區初期涵蓋11個村里，2005年擴大學區範圍，目前共有53間教室與辦公室，並設有一座於2021年完工、與社區共用的運動場館。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏟️ A Shared Sports Complex

社區共用運動場館

Completed in 2021, the school's sports complex is jointly used by students and area residents, reflecting Hequn's deep integration into the Ganjing community.

🏫 Built From Borrowed Classrooms

借教室起家

The school opened in 1980 by borrowing classrooms from neighboring Hemei Junior High and Hemei Elementary before finally moving into its own campus in 1981.

📐 Growing With Its District

學區持續擴大

Originally serving 11 villages, Hequn's school district expanded in 2005, and the campus has grown to 53 classrooms and offices to match demand.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in the Ganjing community.

🏘️

Hequn Junior High sits in Ganjing District (甘井里), Hemei Township. Its 2021 sports complex, shared by students and local residents alike, reflects how closely the school is woven into daily community life in this part of Changhua. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
