Zhonglun Senior High School

中崙高級中學

One of three Taipei senior highs founded together in 2002, built on the site of the old city bus repair yard and now anchoring Songshan's rail-and-tobacco heritage corridor.

About · 學校簡介

Built on Songshan's industrial past.

Zhonglun Senior High School opened in 2002 as one of three Taipei senior highs founded simultaneously — alongside Nanhu and Yucheng High Schools — after site planning that began in the 1970s and a preparatory office approved in 1997. In 2005 it became the first school in the world designated a Microsoft/Taipei Department of Education "Future School" pilot site, reflecting an early commitment to technology-integrated teaching. The campus itself sits on the former site of the Taipei City Bus Depot's repair yard, a quiet marker of Songshan's industrial past.

中崙高中創立於民國91年（2002年），與南湖高中、育成高中同時設校，是台北市當年新設的三所高中之一。校地早於民國62年（1973年）即已選定，並於民國86年（1997年）成立「中崙國中」籌備處，最終直接以高中型態開校。民國94年（2005年），中崙高中成為全球首批獲微軟與台北市教育局指定為「未來學校」的試辦學校之一，展現其推動科技融入教學的早期投入。校園基地前身為台北市公車處公車修護場，見證松山區的工業發展歷史。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 One of Three 2002 Sister Schools

同屆創校三校之一

Zhonglun opened in 2002 alongside Nanhu and Yucheng High Schools, part of a wave of new Taipei senior highs built to meet rising demand for public high-school seats.

💻 A Global "Future School" Pioneer

全球未來學校先驅

In 2005, Zhonglun became the first school in the world named a Microsoft/Taipei Department of Education "Future School" pilot site, an early bet on technology-driven classrooms.

🚌 Built on a Bus Depot's Legacy

公車修護場舊址

The campus occupies the former Taipei City Bus Depot repair yard, a physical link to Songshan's mid-20th-century transportation infrastructure.

🚂 Steps from Taiwan's Rail Heritage

鄰近鐵道文化廊帶

The school sits a short walk from the old Taipei Railway Workshop (today's National Railway Museum) and the Songshan Cultural and Creative Park, the former Songshan Tobacco Factory.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Songshan's rail-and-tobacco heritage.

🚂

The campus is a short walk from the old Taipei Railway Workshop (now the National Railway Museum) and the Songshan Cultural and Creative Park (former Songshan Tobacco Factory), placing the school at the heart of Songshan's rail-and-tobacco industrial heritage corridor — a legacy that predates the airport and postwar suburbs that later defined the district.
