Chaozhou Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A landmark school at the center of town.

Chaozhou Elementary School was founded during the Japanese colonial era, likely in the late Meiji period, and is described as a historically and culturally significant landmark of the Chaozhou area — noted for its convenient central location and long-standing role as a recognizable feature of the town. Its history is documented in Taiwan's National Cultural Memory Bank (國家文化記憶庫), reflecting deep roots in local heritage and its position as likely one of the earliest schools established in the Chaozhou area.

潮州國小創立於日治時期，推測為明治年間晚期，被形容為潮州地區具重要歷史文化意義的地標學校——交通便利，長年是潮州地區顯著地標之一。學校歷史收錄於國家文化記憶庫，深植地方文化根基，推測是潮州地區最早設立的學校之一。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Chaozhou, southern Pingtung's rail and market hub.

🚂

Chaozhou is southern Pingtung's major market town and transportation hub, its growth historically tied to the sugar industry, the 1920 rail line, and the Chaozhou Forest Railway heritage. As one of the town's earliest schools, Chaozhou Elementary has watched this history unfold from its central location for generations.
