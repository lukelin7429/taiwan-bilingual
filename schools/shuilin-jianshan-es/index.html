About · 學校簡介

A tiny school facing a shrinking village.

Jianshan Elementary, located in Dashan Village, is one of the smallest campuses in Shuilin Township. As of 2025 it has just 6 classes and 20 students in total, a stark illustration of the rural depopulation affecting many of Yunlin's farming villages. Despite its size, the school remains an important community fixture for the families who remain.

尖山國小位於水林鄉大山村，是水林鄉規模最小的校園之一。截至民國114年（2025年），全校僅6班、共20名學生，鮮明地反映出雲林許多農村面臨的人口外流問題。儘管規模微小，學校依然是留守家庭重要的社區據點。
