Lienchiang County Ren-Ai Elementary School

連江縣南竿鄉仁愛國民小學

A Nangan elementary school whose earliest classes leaned heavily on garrison soldiers volunteering to teach when faculty and facilities were scarce.

About · 學校簡介

From a temple branch school to a single modern campus.

Ren-Ai Elementary School was established in 1951 as "South Nangan District Jen'ai National Elementary School" under the Matsu Administrative Bureau. In 1953 it relocated to temple premises and became known as "Matsu National School Second Branch." The school gained independent status in 1960, then oversaw two branch schools of its own, before being renamed to its current name in 1969. Its various sites were consolidated onto a single campus in 1986, with a new teaching building completed in 2011 and a faculty dormitory added in 2018.

仁愛國民小學創校於民國40年（1951年），隸屬馬祖行政公署，初名「南竿區仁愛國民學校」。民國42年（1953年）遷至廟宇場地上課，稱為「馬祖國民學校第二分校」。民國49年（1960年）獨立設校，並轄有兩所分校，民國58年（1969年）更名為現名。民國75年（1986年）將各分散校區整併為單一校區，民國100年（2011年）完成新教學大樓，民國107年（2018年）增建教師宿舍。

🪖 Taught by Volunteer Soldiers

駐軍義務任教

In its earliest years, the school's teaching "largely relied on garrison troops providing volunteer instruction" due to inadequate facilities and a shortage of trained faculty — a direct legacy of Matsu's frontline military governance.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Garrison soldiers as the earliest teachers.

🪖

The school's own history records that early operations "largely relied on garrison troops providing volunteer teaching" because of inadequate facilities and a shortage of qualified faculty — a direct reflection of Matsu's military frontline history and the Fujian-provincial-era administrative governance under which the school first took shape.
