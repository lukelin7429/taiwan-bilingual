Yong'an Elementary School

永安國民小學

Grown from a 1970 branch campus into an independent school serving Daan's growing fishing-village population.

About · 學校簡介

Growth along the shoreline.

Yong'an Elementary School began in 1970 as a grades 1–4 branch campus of Haiqian Elementary School, known then as "Haiqian Elementary Yong'an Branch." It became fully independent in 1975, a split that reflects the growth of Daan District's fishing-village population along the district's shoreline.

永安國小最早於1970年以「海墘國小永安分校」之姿設立，招收一至四年級學生，1975年（民國64年）正式獨立設校。這次分校獨立，反映了大安區沿海漁村人口持續成長的趨勢。

因漁村人口成長而獨立

創校初僅設一至四年級

When it opened in 1970 as a branch campus, Yong'an Elementary served only grades 1 through 4 before growing into a full independent school.

🌊 Rooted in Daan's Shoreline Communities

扎根大安濱海社區

As one of several schools born from Daan's coastal population growth, Yong'an remains closely tied to its shoreline fishing communities.
