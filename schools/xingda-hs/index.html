About · 學校簡介

A national high school reshaped by a university partnership.

Originally established in 1997 and enrolling its first students in 2001, the school operated for over a decade as a national senior high before being formally affiliated with National Chung Hsing University (NCHU) in 2014, taking its present name. It is one of the "Taichung Big Five" alliance of senior highs and today enrolls roughly 1,520 students across 42 classes. In 2011 it launched a Science & Mathematics Gifted Program, reflecting a strong academic orientation reinforced by its university partnership.

學校創立於民國86年（1997年），民國90年（2001年）招收首屆學生，歷經十餘年的國立高中歷程後，於民國103年（2014年）正式與國立中興大學建立附屬關係，並更為現名。學校為台中「五校聯盟」成員之一，現有約1,520名學生、42個班級。民國100年（2011年）成立科學與數理資優班，展現與大學合作後更強的學術導向。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 A University Partnership since 2014

2014年起與大學攜手

The school's 2014 shift from a national high school into an NCHU-affiliated campus mirrors Taiwan's broader push to connect senior-high education with nearby research universities.

🔬 Science & Math Gifted Program

科學與數理資優班

Since 2011, a dedicated gifted program in science and mathematics has given standout students an accelerated academic track.

🤝 One of Taichung's "Big Five"

台中「五校聯盟」成員

The school belongs to an informal alliance of five leading Taichung senior highs, a marker of its standing among the city's academic schools.

📈 A Growing Campus

穩定成長的校園規模

From its first graduating class in the early 2000s, the school has grown to around 1,520 students across 42 classes.
