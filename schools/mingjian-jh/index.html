About · 學校簡介

A central school with strong student support.

Mingjian Junior High School serves as the central junior high for Mingjian Township, drawing students from across Taiwan's largest tea-growing township. The school offers gifted-student identification and special-education programs, and it runs a 24-hour anti-bullying hotline alongside counseling and family-education services. Mingjian Junior High has also been recognized for its work integrating information technology into everyday teaching.

名間國中是名間鄉的中心國中，招收來自這個台灣最大茶葉產區各聚落的學生。學校提供資優鑑定與特殊教育服務，並設有24小時反霸凌專線，搭配輔導與家庭教育服務。名間國中亦因將資訊科技融入日常教學而獲得肯定。
