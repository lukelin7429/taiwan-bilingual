Xiding Elementary School

西定國小

A public elementary school in Anle District, running an Ocean Curriculum and building its teaching around design-thinking, project-based learning, and reading promotion.

位於安樂區的公立國小，開設海洋課程，並以設計思考、專題學習與閱讀推廣為教學特色。

About · 學校簡介

Nine principals, one enduring mission.

Xiding Elementary has been guided by nine principals over its history, a marker of a school with decades of accumulated local experience. Today it offers a dedicated "海洋課程" (Ocean Curriculum) alongside a strong emphasis on design-thinking, project-based learning, and social responsibility through community service. Its reading-promotion efforts have even been featured in a national public library journal for successfully boosting student engagement with books.

西定國小歷經九任校長，累積了數十年的辦學經驗。學校目前開設「海洋課程」，並著重設計思考與專題式學習，同時透過社區服務培養學生的社會責任感。學校的閱讀推廣成效更曾登上全國公共圖書館期刊的報導。
