About · 學校簡介

Taiwan's first Truku experimental school.

In 2017, Wanrong Elementary made history as the first school in Taiwan to formally establish a "Truku Indigenous Experimental Education" program — a distinct educational track built specifically around Truku identity and knowledge. Rather than treating Indigenous culture as an add-on subject, the school reorganized its curriculum around "Gaya," the traditional Truku code of conduct that governs relationships, ethics, and community life. Students have even recreated traditional Truku dwellings on campus, turning the school itself into a living classroom for cultural continuity.

民國106年（2017年），萬榮國小成為全台灣第一所正式設立「太魯閣族實驗教育」課程的學校，並非僅將原住民文化作為附加科目，而是以太魯閣族傳統核心規範「Gaya」為主軸，重新建構整體課程架構。學生們甚至在校園中重建了太魯閣族傳統家屋，讓校園本身成為文化傳承的活教材。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A First for Taiwan

全台第一所太魯閣族實驗小學

Wanrong became Taiwan's first school to formally establish a Truku Indigenous Experimental Education program in 2017, a milestone in Indigenous education nationwide.

⚖️ Curriculum Built on Gaya

以Gaya規範為課程主軸

Rather than teaching culture as a single subject, the school organizes its whole curriculum around Gaya — the Truku code of conduct governing ethics, kinship, and community life.

🏠 A Campus With Traditional Dwellings

重建太魯閣族傳統家屋

Students have recreated traditional Truku dwellings on the school grounds, turning the campus into a hands-on space for learning ancestral building and living traditions.

📸 A Graduation Photo That Went Viral

賽德克巴萊風格畢業照爆紅

A graduation photo styled after the film "Seediq Bale" — depicting Indigenous resistance and identity — spread widely online, drawing national attention to the school's cultural mission.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The heart of Truku Wanrong.

🪢

Wanrong Township 萬榮鄉 is a mountainous Truku Indigenous township along Hualien County's Central Mountain Range, and Wanrong Elementary — sharing the township's name — sits at the heart of its cultural identity. As Taiwan's pioneering Truku experimental school, it embodies the township's broader effort to keep Truku language, ethics, and craft alive for the next generation.
