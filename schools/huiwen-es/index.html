About · 學校簡介

Twinned schools for a planned new district.

Huiwen Elementary was established in 1999, with its first students enrolling in 2001. It was built together with Huiwen Senior High as twinned schools within the 7th Redevelopment Zone (七期), forming part of the zone's deliberately planned “core cultural-education block.” The school's founding illustrates Nantun's broader shift from farmland to a master-planned residential and educational district.

惠文國小創立於民國88年（1999年），2001年正式招生。學校與惠文高中同步興建，作為七期重劃區內的姊妹校，共同構成該重劃區規劃中的「文教專用區」核心。學校的成立見證了南屯從農地轉型為規劃完善的住宅暨教育區的歷程。
