Zaixing Elementary School

載興國民小學

Established in 1954 with seven classrooms, Zaixing Elementary grew steadily to 13 buildings within its first thirteen years.

About · 學校簡介

Discover yourself, appreciate yourself, showcase yourself.

Zaixing Elementary was established on May 1, 1954, as an independent Pingtung County Ligang Township school, opening with seven initial classes under its first principal, Xu Jiang-long. Three single-story tile-roof classroom buildings were built at founding, and the campus expanded rapidly to 13 buildings by 1967. Located in Zaixing Village, the school's motto — "Discover yourself, appreciate yourself, showcase yourself" (發現自己、欣賞自己、展現自己) — reflects a student-centered educational philosophy that has guided the school for over 70 years.

載興國小創立於民國43年（1954年）5月1日，正式獨立設校，首任校長為許江龍先生，開校時共有7個班級。創校初期興建三棟單層瓦頂教室，至民國56年（1967年）已擴建至13棟校舍。學校位於載興村，校訓「發現自己、欣賞自己、展現自己」體現了以學生為本的教育理念，指引學校走過超過70年的歷史。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A village school in Ligang's farmland.

🌾

Situated in Zaixing Village within Ligang Township's agricultural plains, part of the farming area near Cishan known for pig's-knuckle, betel-nut, and rice cultivation, the school's steady growth from three buildings to thirteen mirrors the postwar expansion of rural communities across this corner of Pingtung County.
