Ailiao Elementary School

隘寮國民小學

A public elementary school along the Ailiao River, near the Liangshan Waterfall and Liuli Suspension Bridge, at the meeting point of Hakka and Indigenous mountain communities.

About · 學校簡介

A school on the edge of the mountains.

Ailiao Elementary sits along the Ailiao River in Neipu Township, near Provincial Highway 185, known locally as the "mountainside road" (沿山公路). The school's location places it close to well-known scenic spots including the Liangshan Waterfall and the Liuli (Glass) Suspension Bridge, at the foothills where the Hakka Liudui plain meets Indigenous Rukai and Paiwan mountain communities near Sandimen.

隘寮國小座落於內埔鄉隘寮溪畔，鄰近台三線的沿山公路（省道185號）。學校地理位置靠近涼山瀑布與琉璃吊橋等知名景點，正處於六堆客家平原與三地門一帶魯凱族、排灣族原住民山區社區交會的山腳地帶。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏞️ On the Banks of the Ailiao River

隘寮溪畔

The school sits along the Ailiao River near Provincial Highway 185, giving students easy access to the area's scenic mountain-foothill landscape.

💎 Near the Liuli Suspension Bridge

鄰近琉璃吊橋

Well-known scenic spots including Liangshan Waterfall and the Liuli (Glass) Suspension Bridge are close to the school, part of a popular local tourist route.

🤝 Where Hakka Meets Indigenous Communities

客家與原民交會地帶

Situated at the foothills where the Hakka Liudui plain meets Rukai and Paiwan Indigenous mountain communities near Sandimen, the school serves a culturally mixed border area.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where the Liudui plain meets the mountains.

⛰️

Ailiao Elementary sits at the foothills where the Hakka Liudui plain meets Indigenous (Rukai/Paiwan) mountain communities near Sandimen — a mixed Hakka-Indigenous border area of Neipu Township. This geographic and cultural crossing point, framed by the Ailiao River, the Liangshan Waterfall, and the Liuli Suspension Bridge, gives the school a setting unlike any other in the flatland heart of the Liudui region. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
