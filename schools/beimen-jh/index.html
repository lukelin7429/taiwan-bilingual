Beimen Junior High School

北門國中

Sited at Jingzijiao, steps from Beimen's iconic salt-pan tourist site, and the first junior high in the former Tainan County to run a student lunch program.

About · 學校簡介

A salt-flat school with a record of firsts.

Beimen Junior High was founded in 1968 under its first principal, Yang Jin-fu, initially borrowing classrooms from Beimen Elementary School for its first half-year. In 1974 it became the first junior high in the former Tainan County to run a student lunch program. Its band and choir programs, established in 1981–82, both went on to win county-level championships, and its student activity center was completed in 1987.

北門國中創立於民國57年（1968年），首任校長為楊進福，創校首學期曾借用北門國小教室上課。民國63年（1974年），學校成為原台南縣第一所推行學生營養午餐的國中。創立於1981至1982年間的樂隊與合唱團皆曾榮獲縣級冠軍，學生活動中心則於民國76年（1987年）完工。
