About · 學校簡介

From provincial high school to city landmark.

Xinzhuang Senior High School opened in 1992 as Taiwan Provincial Xinzhuang Senior High School, becoming Xinzhuang's first public senior high in a town whose population had boomed since the 1970s. The school was reclassified as a national high school in 2000 and became a city-run school under New Taipei City in 2013. It now enrolls about 2,200 students across roughly 57 classes, offering a general academic program, an art class, and an experimental finance-management/language track.

新莊高中創立於民國81年（1992年），初名「臺灣省立新莊高級中學」，是新莊——這個自1970年代工業擴張後人口迅速成長的市鎮——第一所公立高中。民國89年（2000年）改隸國立，民國102年（2013年）再改為新北市立。現有約57班、學生近2,200人，設有普通科、美術班及財經管理/語文實驗班。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥇 The First Public High School in Xinzhuang

新莊第一所公立高中

Opening in 1992 as the town's first public senior high, the school symbolizes Xinzhuang's shift from an old Qing-era river port to a modern education hub.

🔔 The Top Scholar Bell

狀元鐘

A bell in the school courtyard, nicknamed the "Top Scholar Bell," is traditionally rung by top academic performers and marathon champions to mark their achievement.

🎨 Three Tracks, One Campus

普通、美術、財經語文三軌並行

Alongside its general academic program, the school runs an art class and an experimental finance-management/language track, giving students distinct paths through senior high.

🏛️ From Provincial to City-Run

從省立到市立的沿革

The school shifted from provincial to national administration in 2000, then became a New Taipei City-run school in 2013, tracking Taiwan's broader changes in school governance.

Local Culture · 在地文化

From river port to education hub.

🏞️

Xinzhuang grew from a Qing-dynasty river port on the old Danshui River trade route into one of New Taipei's most populous districts after 1970s industrial expansion. As the town's first public senior high, this school stands as a marker of that transition from riverside trading town to modern education hub. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
