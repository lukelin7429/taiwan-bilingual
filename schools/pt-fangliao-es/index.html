About · 學校簡介

The school at the heart of Fangliao's town center.

Fangliao Elementary School sits in the town center of Fangliao Township, the township's namesake seat. Fangliao's name historically comes from its role as a lumber-storage and trading point — literally a “plank shed” — and the township today is a hub for fishing, aquaculture, and the South-Link Railway's Blue Train tourist route toward Kenting.

枋寮國小位於枋寮鄉街區核心，也就是枋寮這個地名的發源地。「枋寮」之名源自昔日堆置木材的貿易據點（「枋」即木板），如今的枋寮則是漁業、水產養殖與南迴鐵路藍皮解憂號觀光路線通往墾丁的重要門戶。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏘️ At the Township's Namesake Center

枋寮地名發源地

Fangliao Elementary sits at the historic town center that gave the entire township its name — a former lumber-storage and trading point known locally as a “plank shed.”

🚄 Near the South-Link Railway

鄰近南迴鐵路

The school's location in central Fangliao places it near the starting point of the South-Link Railway and its scenic Blue Train route toward Kenting.

🐟 In a Fishing and Aquaculture Hub

漁業與養殖重鎮

Like the rest of the township, the school's neighborhood is shaped by Fangliao's identity as a fishing port and aquaculture center.
