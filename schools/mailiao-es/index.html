About · 學校簡介

From a century-old port town, Yunlin's largest elementary school.

Mailiao Elementary traces its roots to around 1920 and celebrated its centennial in 2020. It has grown into the largest elementary school in Yunlin County for four consecutive years, with 52 classes serving the township's children. Beyond academics, the school runs a volleyball sports class and maintains several traditional performing-arts groups: a drum troupe, a puppet theater group, and a dragon dance team, keeping local heritage alive for new generations.

麥寮國小創校可追溯至民國9年（約1920年），並於2020年舉辦建校百週年慶祝活動。學校連續四年蟬聯雲林縣規模最大國小，全校共52班。除了學科教育，學校也開設排球體育班，並設有傳統技藝團隊——鑼鼓陣、布袋戲團與舞龍隊，讓在地文化技藝得以代代相傳。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Yunlin's Largest Elementary School

雲林縣規模最大國小

With 52 classes, Mailiao Elementary has been the largest elementary school in Yunlin County for four consecutive years — a reflection of the township's scale and growth.

🏐 A Dedicated Volleyball Program

排球體育班

The school runs a volleyball sports class, giving athletically inclined students a structured path to develop their skills alongside their regular studies.

🥁 Traditional Performing Arts, Kept Alive

鑼鼓陣、布袋戲、舞龍隊

A traditional drum troupe, a puppet theater (布袋戲) group, and a dragon dance team let students carry forward performing-arts traditions rooted in Hoklo coastal culture.

🎉 A Century of History

建校破百年

Founded around 1920 and marking its centennial in 2020, the school's century-long history mirrors Mailiao's own development from a historic port settlement into a modern township.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Mailiao's historic port and Matsu temple traditions.

⛩️

As the largest school in Mailiao, a township defined by its historic port, Matsu temple traditions, and modern petrochemical industry, Mailiao Elementary reflects the scale and diversity of coastal Yunlin's Hoklo settlement. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
