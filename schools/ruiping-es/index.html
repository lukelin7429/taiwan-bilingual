Ruiping Elementary School

新北市林口區瑞平國民小學

A small hillside school of about 71 students in the rural hamlet of Houkeng, whose waist-drum team won national honors in 2018.

位於後坑山區的小校，全校約71名學生，其腰鼓隊曾於2018年獲得全國殊榮。

About · 學校簡介

A small school with a big drumbeat.

Ruiping Elementary began in 1955 as a branch of neighboring Jiabao Elementary School, becoming independent in 1963. Rooted in Houkeng, a small hillside hamlet on Linkou's rural fringe, the school remains small today — about 6 classes and 71 students — reflecting the area's pre-new-town agricultural village pattern. Despite its size, its waist-drum team earned national honors in 2018.

瑞平國小於民國44年（1955年）以嘉寶國小分校形式設立，民國52年（1963年）獨立設校。學校位於後坑——林口郊區一處小型山坡聚落，至今規模仍小，僅約6個班級、71名學生，呈現出新市鎮開發前農業聚落的樣貌。儘管校小，該校腰鼓隊卻在2018年獲得全國性殊榮。
