Tainan Municipal Sinhua Junior High School

新化國中

Xinhua District's central junior high school, home to a city-wide Maker Education and Technology Center and an award-winning wind ensemble.

About · 學校簡介

The junior high at the center of Xinhua.

Sinhua Junior High School already existed by 1959 — under the name "Tainan County Xinhua Junior High School," it lent classrooms to the newly founded Xinhua Senior High branch that year — though no precise founding date could be confirmed despite multiple searches of the school's official site, Wikipedia, and news sources. Today the school hosts a Tainan City "Maker Education and Technology Center" (自造教育及科技中心) serving the wider district, and it serves as the central junior high feeding into both National Hsinhua Senior High and National Hsinhua Vocational High School on the same district.

新化國中至遲於民國48年（1959年）已存在——當年校名為「台南縣立新化初中」，曾借出教室予新設立的新化高中分部使用——但儘管多方查詢學校官網、維基百科及新聞資料，仍無法確認精確創校年份。學校目前設有台南市「自造教育及科技中心」，服務整個新化學區，並作為銜接國立新化高中與國立新化高工的核心國中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛠️ A City-Wide Maker Education Center

自造教育及科技中心

The school hosts a Tainan City "Maker Education and Technology Center," a hub for hands-on technology learning that serves the wider Xinhua district, not just its own students.

🎺 An Award-Winning Wind Ensemble

管樂團佳績

The school's wind ensemble (管樂團) received an "excellence" rating at the 2026 National Student Music Competition, a strong showing on the national stage.

💡 Life-Technology Design Winners

生活科技創意設計競賽

Students have placed first and second in municipal life-technology creative-design competitions, reflecting the school's strength in applied technology education.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The educational hub of Xinhua District.

🏫

As Xinhua's central junior high, this school feeds directly into both National Hsinhua Senior High and National Hsinhua Vocational High School in the same district, anchoring the local pipeline of secondary education for the historic old-street town.
