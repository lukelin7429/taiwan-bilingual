Zhuqi Senior High School

嘉義縣立竹崎高級中學

A complete secondary school at the gateway to Alishan, celebrated for its six-year sports-talent pipeline and award-winning music program.

About · 學校簡介

From junior high to a complete secondary school.

Zhuqi Senior High School was founded on December 1, 1957 as Zhuqi Junior High School, and was renamed Zhuqi Junior High under the national nine-year compulsory education reform in 1968. In the 2005 academic year the school expanded into a complete secondary school (完全中學) by adding a senior high division, and it now serves students from junior high through senior high in Zhuqi Township, the gateway community to the Alishan Forest Railway and the broader Alishan tourism corridor.

嘉義縣立竹崎高級中學創立於民國46年（1957年）12月1日，初名「嘉義縣立竹崎初級中學」；民國57年（1968年）因應九年國教改制，更名為「嘉義縣立竹崎國民中學」。自94學年度（2005年）起，學校增設高中部，轉型為兼具國中部與高中部的完全中學，服務阿里山森林鐵路起點暨阿里山觀光廊帶門戶——竹崎鄉的學子。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏃 A Six-Year Sports Pipeline

國小到高中一貫運動人才培育

The school runs an integrated six-year sports-talent development system spanning elementary through senior high, and has built a strong record in national and international athletic competitions as a recognized rural sports hub.

🎶 National Music Champions

全國音樂競賽常勝軍

Zhuqi's orchestra, marching band, and piano students have repeatedly won awards at national music competitions, alongside consistent recognition in science fairs and reading/writing contests.

🏛️ A Ministerial Visit

部長蒞校參訪

In June 2026, the Minister of Education visited the school, recognizing its athletics program and facilities — a sign of the school's standing as a rural sports and arts talent hub.

🎓 Multiple Paths to University

多元升學管道

Graduates advance to university through the Stars Program (繁星推薦), special talent-based admissions, and regular admissions, reflecting the school's balance of academics, sport, and the arts.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The gateway to Alishan.

🚂

Zhuqi Township is the gateway to Alishan — the starting point of the historic Alishan Forest Railway and the broader Alishan tourism corridor that winds up into the high mountains. The school's strong sports and cultural profile reflects this identity as the township that has long welcomed travelers on their way into the mountains.
