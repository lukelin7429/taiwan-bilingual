Wufeng Junior High School

五峰國民中學

The only junior high in Wufeng Township, serving an entirely Atayal and Saisiyat Indigenous student body with a curriculum honored nationally for its cultural depth.

About · 學校簡介

Wufeng's only secondary school.

Wufeng Junior High traces its roots to 1956, when it began as a branch class of Zhudong Junior High, becoming an independent school in 1969. As the highest level of schooling available within Wufeng Township, it serves as the educational capstone for the township's two Indigenous communities. As of 2018, 100 percent of its students were Indigenous, with a majority Atayal population alongside Saisiyat students. In 2020, the school received the Ministry of Education's Silver Award for Teaching Excellence for a curriculum that weaves together Atayal and Saisiyat culture with local economic life, technology, and the arts.

五峰國中的歷史可追溯至民國45年（1956年），最初為竹東國中的分部班，民國58年（1969年）獨立設校。作為五峰鄉境內最高等級的學校，它是鄉內兩個原住民族群的教育重心。截至2018年，全校學生100%為原住民，以泰雅族為多數，並有賽夏族學生。學校於2020年榮獲教育部教學卓越獎銀質獎，肯定其融合泰雅族與賽夏族文化、在地經濟、科技與藝術的課程設計。

Local Culture · 在地文化

An anchor for Wufeng's two Indigenous communities.

🏔️

As Wufeng's only junior high, serving an entirely Indigenous student body drawn primarily from the Atayal community with a Saisiyat minority, the school is a direct educational anchor for the township's two Indigenous peoples, and its award-winning curriculum reflects a deliberate effort to keep that heritage central to student learning.
