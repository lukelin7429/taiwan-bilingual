Kaohsiung City Schools

高雄市

Every public elementary, junior high and senior high school in Kaohsiung City — 181 schools across 38 townships/districts.

高雄市所有公立國中小及高中職——181 校，橫跨 38 個鄉鎮市區。

🚧 Kaohsiung is Taiwan's largest city by area (38 districts) — this directory currently covers 17 of 38 districts in full. The remaining 21 (mostly the former Kaohsiung County towns) are still being researched and will be added township by township.｜高雄市幅員最廣（38 區），目前完整建置 17 個區，其餘 21 區（多為原高雄縣鄉鎮）仍在陸續建置中。
