About · 學校簡介

Three institutions, one university, five campuses.

National Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology was formed on February 1, 2018, through the merger of three older institutions: National Kaohsiung First University of Science and Technology, National Kaohsiung University of Applied Sciences, and National Kaohsiung Marine University. The merger brought together their combined strengths and campuses, and NKUST now operates across five campuses spanning Yanchao, Nanzih, Sanmin (Jiangong), and Cijin. Its College of Maritime and College of Hydrosphere Science carry forward the heritage of the former marine university, reflecting Kaohsiung's identity as Taiwan's largest port.

國立高雄科技大學於民國107年（2018年）2月1日由三所學校合併而成：國立高雄第一科技大學、國立高雄應用科技大學、國立高雄海洋科技大學。合併後整合了三校原有的優勢與校區，目前橫跨燕巢、楠梓、三民（建工）、旗津共五個校區。其海事學院與水圈科學學院延續了原高雄海洋科技大學的傳統，呼應高雄作為台灣最大港口城市的產業特色。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🔗 Formed from Three Institutions

三校合併而成

NKUST was created by merging National Kaohsiung First University of Science and Technology, National Kaohsiung University of Applied Sciences, and National Kaohsiung Marine University into a single institution in 2018.

🗺️ Five Campuses Across Kaohsiung

橫跨高雄五大校區

The university operates five campuses spread across the city, including its First Campus in Yanchao/Nanzih, the Jiangong Campus in Sanmin, and a campus on Cijin.

⚓ Colleges of Maritime and Hydrosphere Science

海事與水圈科學學院

NKUST's College of Maritime and College of Hydrosphere Science reflect the merger's marine-university heritage, connecting the school directly to Kaohsiung's port and fishing industries.

🌏 Roughly 28,000 Students

學生總數約28,000人

With around 28,010 students, NKUST is one of Taiwan's largest technological universities, ranked 551–600 in Asia by QS in 2024.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Kaohsiung's port and fishing heritage.

🐟

NKUST's Cijin campus and its maritime and hydrosphere science colleges connect directly to Kaohsiung's identity as Taiwan's largest port and a historic fishing and shipping hub. See the Kaohsiung City county page for more on the region.
