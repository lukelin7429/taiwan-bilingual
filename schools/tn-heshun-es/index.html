Heshun Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A branch that grew into an award-winning school.

Heshun Elementary began on August 1, 1962, as a branch campus of Anshun Elementary School, becoming fully independent the following year, 1963. It is one of three schools — along with Haidong and Chang'an Elementary — descended from Anshun Elementary, which functions as the "mother school" of Annan District's education network. Today the school has 89 teachers and 5 staff, a campus with a library, computer lab, swimming pool, and athletics field, and is designated a Category A bilingual education school, with roughly one-third of weekly instructional periods taught bilingually. It has won the Ministry of Education's Reading Pinnacle Award for reading education.

和順國小於1962年（民國51年）8月1日以安順國小分校身分創立，隔年（1963年）正式獨立設校。與海東、長安國小同為安順國小這所安南區「母校」所分出的三所學校之一。現有教師89人、職員5人，校園設有圖書館、電腦教室、游泳池與運動場，並獲評定為A級雙語教育重點學校，每週約三分之一課程以雙語授課，並曾榮獲教育部「閱讀磐石獎」。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Annan's oldest school family tree.

🌳

As one of three schools that branched off Anshun Elementary — the historic "mother school" of Annan — Heshun Elementary is part of the direct institutional family tree tracing Annan's education system back to the district's 1916-era founding public school.
