Hukou Senior High School

新竹縣立湖口高級中學

Hukou Township's only senior high school, freshly independent after seven decades sharing a campus with its junior high division.

About · 學校簡介

From a shared campus to its own identity.

Hukou Senior High School traces its roots to 1953 as a branch of Hsinchu Middle School serving Hukou Township. It grew into an independent complete middle school in 1997, offering both junior and senior high divisions, and took its current name in 2003. For decades the senior and junior high divisions operated together as a single "complete middle school" on shared grounds, before formally splitting into two separate institutions in August 2025. Today Hukou Senior High stands as the township's only senior high school, offering both general academic and vocational tracks.

新竹縣立湖口高級中學前身可追溯至民國42年（1953年）新竹中學湖口分部，民國86年（1997年）獨立設立為完全中學，涵蓋國中部與高中部，並於民國92年（2003年）更名為現名。長期以來，國中部與高中部以「完全中學」形式共用校區，直到民國114年（2025年）8月正式分立為兩所獨立學校。如今，湖口高中是湖口鄉唯一的高級中學，同時提供普通學程與職業學程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🤖 An AI Experimental Class

人工智慧實驗班

Launched in 2025 in partnership with National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University and National Taiwan Normal University, giving students early exposure to AI coursework alongside university researchers.

🛠️ Vocational Tracks Alongside Academics

商業經營與多媒體設計科

The school offers business management and multimedia design programs alongside its general academic track, giving students multiple pathways after graduation.

🏫 A Fresh Start After 2025

2025年國高中分立

After operating for decades as one combined "complete middle school" with its junior high division, Hukou Senior High became a fully independent institution in August 2025.

🎖️ Anchoring a Garrison Town

湖口鄉教育樞紐

As Hukou's only senior high school, it anchors secondary education in a township otherwise known for its historic Old Street and large armored-corps military base.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Old Street and armored corps.

🪖

Hukou Township is best known for its historic Old Street (老街), one of Taiwan's best-preserved Hakka commercial streetscapes, and for hosting a large armored-corps military base that has shaped the local economy and community for generations. See the Hsinchu County page for more on the region.
