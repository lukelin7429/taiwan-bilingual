About · 學校簡介

Kaohsiung's fourth public high school.

Cianjhen Senior High School opened in 1969 as the Taiwan Provincial Cianjhen Senior High School, becoming the fourth public academic high school in the Kaohsiung region. It was reorganized as a municipal school in 1979 and, in 1984, established the region's first experimental fine-arts class. Today it enrolls roughly 1,700 students.

前鎮高中創立於民國58年（1969年），初名「臺灣省立前鎮高級中學」，是高雄地區第四所公立高中。民國68年（1979年）改制為市立高中，民國73年（1984年）成立高雄地區第一個美術實驗班。現有學生約1,700人。

⚔️ A District Named for a Garrison

地名源於軍屯

Local Culture · 在地文化

A district named for a vanguard garrison.

⚔️

The district name "Cianjhen" (前鎮) dates to the Ming dynasty, when a vanguard garrison of Koxinga's forces — 宣毅前鎮 — settled here to farm the land and guard the coast. The school's very name carries that centuries-old military history forward. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
