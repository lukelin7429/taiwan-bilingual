About · 學校簡介

A school built around language and culture.

Xinxing Elementary is a small Paiwan Indigenous community school in mountainous Jinfeng Township that places tribal-language and cultural transmission at the heart of its teaching. The school runs an immersive tribal-language kindergarten program and makes use of an Indigenous-language e-learning platform, giving young students consistent exposure to Paiwan language from an early age. Students also take part in the National Indigenous Children's Painting Competition and go on tribal cultural tours woven directly into the curriculum.

新興國小是金峰鄉山區一所小型排灣族部落學校，將族語與文化傳承置於教學核心。學校推動幼兒園族語沉浸式教學，並運用原住民族語數位學習平台，讓孩子從小持續接觸排灣族語。學生也參與全國原住民兒童繪畫比賽，並將部落文化參訪納入課程規劃。
