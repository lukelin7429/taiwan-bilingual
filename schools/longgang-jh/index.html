Longgang Junior High School

桃園市立龍岡國民中學

Founded in 1968, serving Taiwan's best-known Yunnanese and overseas-Chinese-military resettlement community, famous for its Rice Noodle Street.

About · 學校簡介

Serving Taiwan's Yunnanese heartland.

Longgang Junior High was approved for establishment in 1968 and formally became Taoyuan County Longgang National Junior High School by 1969. Its school activity center was completed in June 2001, and a new front gate and guard house were added in May 2005. The school became municipal on December 25, 2014. It is located in the Longgang (龍岡) area, home to Taiwan's best-known Yunnanese and overseas-Chinese-military (滇緬義胞) resettlement community, famous for its "Rice Noodle Street" (米干街).

龍岡國中於1968年獲准設立，1969年正式定名為桃園縣立龍岡國民中學。學生活動中心於2001年6月完工，2005年5月新建校門與警衛室。2014年12月25日改制為市立學校。學校位於龍岡地區，是台灣最著名的滇緬義胞（雲南、緬甸僑民）聚落，以「米干街」的雲南美食聞名全台。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🍜 In the Heart of Rice Noodle Street

鄰近米干街

The school sits in the Longgang area, right next to Taiwan's famous "Rice Noodle Street" (米干街), a cluster of Yunnanese and Burmese food stalls found nowhere else in Taiwan.

🏘️ A Unique Resettlement Community

滇緬義胞聚落

Longgang is nationally known for its concentration of Yunnanese and overseas-Chinese-military families resettled after 1949, giving the neighborhood a distinctive cultural identity.

🏫 Over Half a Century of History

逾半世紀校史

Approved for establishment in 1968, the school has since added an activity center (2001) and a rebuilt front gate (2005) as part of its steady development.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Taiwan's Yunnanese food and culture hub.

🍜

Longgang is nationally known for its concentration of Yunnanese and overseas-Chinese-military (滇緬義胞) families resettled after 1949, giving this school's neighborhood a distinctive Yunnanese food culture (mixian/米干) found nowhere else in Taiwan. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
