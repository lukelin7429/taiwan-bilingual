Fugang Junior High School

富岡國民中學

A small municipal junior high in Yangmei's historic "railway Hakka town" of Fugang, where students have placed highly in national Hakka arts competitions.

位於楊梅「鐵道客庄小鎮」富岡的小型市立國中，學生曾在全國客家藝文競賽中獲優等成績。

About · 學校簡介

A small school anchoring a railway Hakka town.

Fugang Junior High began as a branch campus of Taoyuan County Yangmei Middle School at Fugang in 1966, becoming an independent school after the 1968 nine-year compulsory education reform under the name Taoyuan County Fugang Junior High. It took its current name, Taoyuan City Fugang Junior High, in December 2014 when the county became a special municipality. As of late 2023, the school enrolled about 157 students with 25 teachers, making it one of the smaller junior highs in Yangmei District.

富岡國中創立於民國55年（1966年），原為桃園縣立楊梅中學富岡分部；民國57年（1968年）九年國教實施後獨立為桃園縣立富岡國民中學；民國103年（2014年）12月更名桃園市立富岡國民中學。2023年底統計約157名學生、25名教師，屬中小型國中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎭 Hakka Arts Achievement

客家藝文競賽表現優異

Fugang students have earned outstanding results at the northern regional preliminary round of the National Hakka Arts and Culture Competition for schools, reflecting the school's engagement with local Hakka heritage.

🚂 In the Heart of Railway Fugang

位於鐵道小鎮富岡

The school sits in Fugang, an area famous as a "railway town" (鐵道小鎮) with a well-preserved old street and deep Hakka roots along Taiwan's main north-south rail line.

🍵 Near the Hakka Tea Culture Museum

鄰近臺灣客家茶文化館

Fugang Junior High is close to the Taiwan Hakka Tea Culture Museum, one of the area's landmark cultural institutions celebrating the region's tea-growing and Hakka heritage.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fugang, Yangmei's railway Hakka town.

🚉

Fugang is renowned across Yangmei as a "railway Hakka town" (鐵道客庄小鎮), preserving an old street and the historic texture of the north-south trunk line. Fugang Junior High's strong showing in Hakka arts competitions directly echoes this deep local cultural heritage. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
