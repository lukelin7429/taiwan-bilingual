About · 學校簡介

The only junior high in the Nanhua Reservoir watershed.

Nanhua Junior High School serves as the sole junior-high catchment for the sparsely populated, reservoir-adjacent Nanhua District. As one of the smaller and more remote public schools covered in this batch, detailed founding-year records are not publicly available on the school's own site, but the school continues to serve as the only option for local families in this mountainous watershed community.

南化國中是南化區唯一一所國中，服務人口稀少、鄰近南化水庫的山區學區。作為此批介紹中規模較小、地處較偏遠的公立學校之一，該校官網並未公開詳細創校年份資訊，但學校仍持續作為當地家庭在這個山區水庫流域社區的唯一選擇。
