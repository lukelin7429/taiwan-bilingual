About · 學校簡介

Founded by a community, for its children.

Fushan Elementary grew out of grassroots organizing by residents of Dapu and Jiushe villages, who pooled funds to purchase the school site in 1976 so local children would not face a long daily commute to Fenyuan Elementary School. It opened as a branch campus in 1978, was upgraded to a branch school in 1980, and became fully independent in 1983. Today the school promotes unicycling as a signature student activity, alongside folk sports such as spinning tops, diabolo, and hoop rolling, plus food, agriculture, and environmental education.

富山國小的誕生源自大埔村與舊社村居民的自發籌措，他們於1976年集資購地，希望讓當地孩童不必長途通勤至芬園國小就讀。學校於1978年以分班形式成立，1980年升格為分校，1983年正式獨立設校。如今學校以推廣「獨輪車」作為學生特色活動聞名，並發展陀螺、扯鈴、呼拉圈等民俗體育，以及食農教育與環境教育。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚲 A Unicycling Signature Program

獨輪車特色活動

Since 2011, Fushan has promoted unicycling as a signature student activity, alongside folk sports like spinning tops, diabolo, poi, and hoop rolling.

🌾 Learning From the Land

食農與環境教育

The school offers food and agriculture education — including mochi-making and ice cream DIY — alongside environmental education and Taiwanese-language instruction.

📚 A Book Cart That Travels

雲水書車閱讀推廣

A mobile "cloud-water book cart" (雲水書車) brings reading promotion directly to students, while the school also explores VR and AI in STEM learning.
