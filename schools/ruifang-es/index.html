Ruifang Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

The founding school of an entire district.

Ruifang Elementary was founded in 1901, predating even the gold-rush boom of Jiufen and Jinguashi, making it the educational taproot of the whole district. Over the following decades, four other schools — Jiufen, Guashan, Ruibin, and Ruigan Elementary — all began as branches of Ruifang Elementary before becoming independent, making it the parent school for much of the district's public education system.

瑞芳國小創立於1901年，甚至早於九份、金瓜石淘金熱潮的興起，堪稱瑞芳全區教育的發源地。往後數十年間，九份、瓜山、瑞濱、瑞柑等四校皆是由瑞芳國小分出的分校後獨立設校，使其成為區內公立教育體系的母校。
