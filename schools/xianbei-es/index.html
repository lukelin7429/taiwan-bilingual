About · 學校簡介

A young school built around its river.

Sianbei Elementary School was founded in 2006, making it one of the newest schools in North District — it will mark its 20th anniversary around 2026. The school is located where the Yanshui River meets the sea, and it has built its school-based curriculum around this local river-and-estuary environment since founding. Sianbei has been designated an "AI Robot Education Focus Development School" for 2026, and about 70% of its teaching staff hold bilingual-teaching credentials. Its signature program, "Yanshui River Environmental Smart King," combines LEGO robotics, AI, and environmental studies of the river.

賢北國小創立於2006年（民國95年），是北區較新設立的學校之一，將於2026年前後迎接建校20週年。學校位於鹽水溪出海口，自創校以來即以此在地河流與河口環境為核心，發展學校本位課程。賢北國小獲選為2026年（民國115年）「AI機器人教育重點發展學校」，約七成教師具備雙語教學專業認證。學校特色課程「鹽水溪環保智慧王」結合樂高機器人、人工智慧與河流環境教育。
