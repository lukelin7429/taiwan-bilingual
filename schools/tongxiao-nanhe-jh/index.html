About · 學校簡介

A rural junior high in Tongxiao's farming interior.

Nanhe Junior High is one of five junior high schools serving Tongxiao Township, located in the town's rural interior. The area has long been shaped by Hakka settlement and farming traditions, particularly sweet potato and peanut cultivation, alongside the broader cultural pull of the region's famous Baishatun Mazu pilgrimage.

南和國中是通霄鎮五所國中之一，位於通霄鎮的內陸農業地帶。這個地區長期以來由客家聚落與農業傳統形塑，特別是地瓜與花生的種植，同時也深受當地著名的白沙屯媽祖進香文化影響。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 One of Tongxiao's Five Junior Highs

通霄鎮五所國中之一

Nanhe Junior High serves families in Tongxiao's rural interior as one of five junior high schools covering the township.

🍠 Rooted in a Farming Interior

農業內陸地帶

The school sits in a Hakka-settled farming area where sweet potato and peanut cultivation have long shaped daily village life.

🙏 Near the Baishatun Pilgrimage Route

白沙屯媽祖進香路線

Nanhe lies within reach of Tongxiao's renowned Baishatun Mazu pilgrimage traditions, one of Taiwan's most storied folk-religion walking processions.

🌄 A Rural Interior Identity

客家聚落特色

Set apart from Tongxiao's coastal fishing villages, Nanhe's Hakka farming heritage gives the school a distinct rural character within the township.
