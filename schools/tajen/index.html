Tajen University

大仁科技大學

A private university founded as a pharmacy institute in 1966, one of only ten universities in Taiwan with a Department of Pharmacy, with its own professional-quality English-language website.

About · 學校簡介

From a single pharmacy department to a university.

Tajen University was founded in 1966 as Tajen Pharmacy Institute by Taoyi Huang and Yuan Tsai Lee, starting out with a single five-year Department of Pharmacy. Additional departments were added in 1980, and the school was upgraded to full university status on 1 August 1999, taking its current name while retaining some affiliated five-year junior-college-track programs. Pharmacy remains its founding flagship department: Tajen is one of only ten universities in Taiwan with a Department of Pharmacy. Today it offers programs across four colleges — Pharmacy and Health Care, Leisure and Hospitality, Humanities and Informatics, plus additional programs in biotechnology, computer science and engineering, and food science and technology.

大仁科技大學創立於民國55年（1966年），由黃陶一與李遠彩共同創辦，初名大仁藥學專科學校，最初僅設五年制藥學科一科。1980年陸續增設科系，並於民國88年（1999年）8月1日升格為大學，即現今校名，同時保留部分五專學制班別。藥學系至今仍是該校的招牌科系，大仁也是全台僅十所設有藥學系的大學之一。現今設有藥學暨健康照護學院、休閒與觀光學院、人文暨資訊學院等四大學院，另設有生物科技、資訊工程與食品科學等相關科系。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

💊 One of Ten Pharmacy Programs

全台十校之一

Tajen's Department of Pharmacy, its founding department, is one of only ten pharmacy programs offered by any university in Taiwan.

🏨 Leisure and Hospitality

休閒與觀光學院

Alongside its healthcare programs, Tajen's College of Leisure and Hospitality trains students for Taiwan's tourism and hospitality industries.

🔬 Biotech and Food Science

生物科技與食品科學

Its Innovation and Incubation Center supports biotech research applied to crop protection and sustainable farming, alongside departments in Biotechnology and Food Science and Technology.

🌐 Genuine English Website

專業英文官網

Tajen maintains a professional, human-quality English site describing its colleges and educational philosophy, plus a separate English site for its Office of International Foundation Program.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A secondary link to Pingtung's farmland.

🌿

While Tajen's best-known department is Pharmacy, its Biotechnology and Food Science and Technology departments — along with its Innovation and Incubation Center's reported work on crop protection and sustainable farming — connect the university to Pingtung's agricultural economy, a secondary strand alongside its core healthcare identity.
