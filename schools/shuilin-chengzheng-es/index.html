About · 學校簡介

A border-town school marking 70 years.

Chengzheng Elementary was founded around 1954–1955 and recently celebrated its 70th anniversary. Located at the northern edge of Shuilin Township, the school sits at the crossroads of two distinct local cultures, bordering both Beigang Town and Sihu Township. Alongside its regular curriculum, the school offers English conversation, mother-tongue instruction, and disaster-prevention education for its students.

誠正國小創校於民國43、44年間（約1954、1955年），近年舉辦建校70週年慶祝活動。學校位於水林鄉北端，緊鄰北港鎮與四湖鄉交界，地理位置介於兩種在地文化之間。除正規課程外，學校另開設英語會話、母語教學及防災教育課程，充實學生的學習內容。
