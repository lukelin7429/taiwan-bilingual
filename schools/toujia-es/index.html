About · 學校簡介

Founded amid an apartment boom.

Toujia Elementary opened in 1999, built specifically because a wave of new apartment towers within the Qiaozhong Elementary zone had caused a sharp spike in local student numbers. Since then, the school has built a reputation for strong swimming and martial-arts teams, alongside its core elementary curriculum.

頭家國小創立於民國88年（1999年），設校原因是僑忠國小學區內因大量公寓大樓興建，導致學生人數急遽增加。此後，學校在游泳與武術隊方面建立起亮眼的成績，與基礎課程並重發展。

Tanzi's shift to high-density living.

🏙️

Toujia Elementary's creation directly traces Tanzi District's shift from farmland to high-density residential development through the 1990s, as new apartment towers drew families — and their children — into the area. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
