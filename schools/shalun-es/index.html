About · 學校簡介

From zero new students to a formal closure.

Shalun Elementary was founded in 1960 and served Shalun Village in Dayuan District for over six decades. The school became notorious in local press as "Taoyuan's only elementary school with zero new students" in the 2023 school year, after its single incoming pupil transferred away before the term began. It stopped accepting new students in April 2024 and was formally closed at the end of July that year — one of several Dayuan elementary schools abolished in 2024 due to land expropriation for the Taoyuan Aerotropolis project. Its remaining students transferred to Dayuan Elementary and Guolin Elementary. We keep this page as a historical record of a school whose dwindling enrollment mirrored the broader hollowing-out of Dayuan's original farming and fishing villages.

沙崙國小創立於1960年（民國49年），服務大園區沙崙里社區逾一甲子。學校曾在地方媒體上以「全桃園唯一零新生國小」聞名——112學年度唯一一名新生入學前便轉學離開。學校自2024年4月起停止招收新生，並於同年7月底正式廢校，是當年因桃園航空城土地徵收而廢校的幾所大園國小之一。原有學生轉學至大園國小與果林國小。我們保留此頁面，作為這所學生人數逐漸凋零、映照大園原始農漁村社區逐漸空洞化的學校歷史紀錄。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A village hollowed out, and a village overflowing.

📉

Shalun's dwindling-to-zero enrollment and subsequent closure is one of the most vivid data points illustrating how the airport-driven Aerotropolis project has hollowed out Dayuan's original farming and fishing villages — a trend starkly at odds with overcrowded Chaoyin Elementary just a few kilometers away.
