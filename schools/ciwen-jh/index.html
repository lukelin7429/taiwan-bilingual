Ciwen Junior High School

桃園市立慈文國民中學

A public junior high in Taoyuan's Art & Culture Special District, widely regarded as having the highest rate of students advancing to the city's top senior highs.

About · 學校簡介

A cultural-district school known for academic strength.

Ciwen Junior High's preparatory office was established on August 1, 1992, hosted at nearby Wenchang Junior High. The school formally opened in 1994, admitting its first cohort of 25 classes. Located in the 藝文特區 (Art & Culture Special District), it is widely regarded as the public junior high with the highest rate of students advancing to top senior highs in Taoyuan City. In 2016 it was approved to add an English-gifted class and an athletics class. Class numbers have declined over time as nearby Tongde Junior High (2003) and Jingguo Junior High (2007) opened and absorbed students from its original attendance zone.

慈文國中籌備處於民國81年（1992年）8月1日成立，暫設於鄰近的文昌國中，並於民國83年（1994年）正式設校，首屆招收25個班級。學校位於「藝文特區」，普遍被視為桃園市升學表現最優異的公立國中。2016年獲准增設英語資優班與體育班。隨著鄰近的同德國中（2003年）與經國國中（2007年）陸續設立並分流原學區學生，慈文國中的班級數逐漸減少。
