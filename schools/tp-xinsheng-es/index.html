Taipei Municipal Xinsheng Elementary School

臺北市立新生國民小學

One of the few schools in Taipei with a park-front address, its campus facing Da'an Forest Park directly across the street.

About · 學校簡介

A park-front address, rare in the city.

Xinsheng Elementary's campus faces Da'an Forest Park directly across the street, giving it one of the most desirable addresses of any elementary school in Taipei. Spanning about 24,528 square meters, the school enrolls roughly 1,300 students in total — about 1,100 in elementary grades, 120 in language-education classes, and 120 in kindergarten. The school recently marked a milestone anniversary under the slogan “傳承．創新．國際” (heritage, innovation, internationalization).

新生國小校園正對大安森林公園，是全台北少數坐擁「公園第一排」地址的學校之一。校地面積約24,528平方公尺，全校學生約1,300人，其中國小部約1,100人、語文教育班約120人、幼兒園約120人。學校近年以「傳承．創新．國際」為口號，慶祝重要建校里程碑。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 A Park-Front Address

公園第一排

Facing Da'an Forest Park directly across the street, Xinsheng is one of the few schools in the city with a true park-front campus.

🏫 A Large, Spacious Campus

校地寬廣

At roughly 24,528 square meters, the school has more room to breathe than many of its densely packed Da'an neighbors.

🌐 Heritage, Innovation, Internationalization

傳承．創新．國際

The school's recent anniversary slogan captures its self-described identity: rooted in tradition, but oriented toward innovation and the wider world.

👶 A Full Range of Programs

涵蓋語文班與幼兒園

Beyond its roughly 1,100 elementary pupils, the school also runs dedicated language-education classes and a kindergarten.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Taipei's most park-adjacent school.

🌳

With its campus directly across from Da'an Forest Park — the district's namesake green lung — Xinsheng Elementary is one of the few schools in the city with a park-front address, embodying Da'an's identity as Taipei's most park-adjacent, livable district.
