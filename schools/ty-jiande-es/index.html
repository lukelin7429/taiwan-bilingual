Jian-De Elementary School

建德國民小學

A large elementary school behind Taoyuan Railway Station, built on former pond land and once a national pilot school for Taiwan's nine-year integrated curriculum.

位於桃園後站的大型國小，校地前身為溜池，曾為全國九年一貫課程試辦學校。

About · 學校簡介

A rising star behind Taoyuan Station.

Jian-De Elementary was approved for establishment by the Taiwan Provincial Government and Taoyuan County Government on February 1, 1995, with formal instruction beginning in 1998. The campus covers roughly 2.5 hectares and, as of 2023, served over 2,100 students across 74 regular classes plus resource and kindergarten classes — making it one of the larger elementary schools in the district. The school served as a pilot school for Taiwan's nine-year integrated curriculum (九年一貫課程) in 1998–99, and is currently led by its fifth principal, Wu Ming-fang, who has served since 2019.

建德國小於民國84年（1995年）2月1日經台灣省政府與桃園縣政府核准設校，民國87年（1998年）正式招生上課。校地約2.5公頃，截至2023年計有74個普通班，加上資源班與幼兒園班，全校學生逾2,100人，為區內規模較大的國小之一。學校曾於1998至1999年間擔任九年一貫課程試辦學校，現任第五任校長為吳明芳（自2019年起）。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🦢 Once a Pond of Egrets

曾是白鷺翱翔的溜池

The school's own history page poetically describes its site as once "a pond where egrets soared over rippling blue waters" before the campus was built.

📘 Pilot School for a National Curriculum

九年一貫課程試辦學校

Jian-De served as a pilot school for Taiwan's nine-year integrated curriculum (九年一貫課程) in 1998–99, part of a landmark national education reform.

🏫 Over 2,100 Students

學生逾2,100人

With 74 regular classes on a 2.5-hectare campus, Jian-De is one of the larger elementary schools in Taoyuan District.

⭐ "A Rising Star Behind the Station"

桃園後站的明日之星

The school describes itself as "a rising star behind Taoyuan Station" (桃園後站的明日之星), reflecting its role in the rapidly developed station-area neighborhood.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Built on reclaimed station-area wetland.

🚉

Jian-De Elementary was built on reclaimed pond and marsh land behind (north of) Taoyuan Railway Station, reflecting the rapid land conversion of the old station-area wetlands into residential neighborhoods during Taoyuan's 1990s growth boom. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
