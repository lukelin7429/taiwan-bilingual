About · 學校簡介

A school moved by a river.

Binjiang Elementary School began in 1949 as a satellite classroom, "西松國民學校下塔悠分教室," becoming an independent school in 1953 as "下塔悠國民學校" and taking the name "濱江國民學校" in 1954. It was originally located in what was then Songshan District, at Binjiang Street.

The school was forced to relocate in 1991-92, when the Keelung River-straightening project ("基隆河截彎取直案") required demolition of its old campus, a move approved by the Executive Yuan. Its new campus — built in the Dazhi land-readjustment zone at 26,336 square meters, larger than the original 21,994-square-meter site — used green-building construction methods. Its former neighborhood is now shared with the newer Binjiang Experimental Junior High, both named for their riverside ("濱江") location.

濱江國小創立於1949年（民國38年），初為「西松國民學校下塔悠分教室」，1953年（民國42年）獨立設校，定名「下塔悠國民學校」，1954年（民國43年）更名為「濱江國民學校」。原址位於當時的松山區濱江街。

1991至92年間（民國80至81年），因基隆河截彎取直案經行政院核定，學校舊校舍遭拆除，被迫遷校。新校舍座落於大直土地重劃區，佔地26,336平方公尺，較原址21,994平方公尺為大，並採用綠建築工法興建。原址所在區域如今與較新設立的濱江實驗國中相鄰，兩校皆以「濱江」為名，反映共同的河濱地緣。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌊 Displaced by River-Straightening

因截彎取直遷校

The school's original campus was demolished under the 1991-92 Keelung River-straightening project, forcing a full relocation to the Dazhi zone.

🏗️ A Green-Building Campus

綠建築新校舍

The rebuilt campus, larger than the original site, was constructed using green-building methods on a 26,336-square-meter plot.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A defining infrastructure event, felt directly.

🌊

Like Dajia Elementary, Binjiang Elementary's entire relocation history is a direct consequence of the 1991-92 Keelung River-straightening project — one of the defining infrastructure events that reshaped the Dazhi and Binjiang riverside portion of Zhongshan District.
