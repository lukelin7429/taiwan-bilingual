About · 學校簡介

A hillside school born from a mayor's education plan.

Dongxin Elementary was founded in 1954 under Keelung Mayor Xie Guan-yi, who designated Xinyi District as an education and culture zone and personally selected a hillside site for the new school. At the time, nearby Shen'ao Elementary was the only school serving the district, so Dongxin was created to meet growing local need. The school's first principal, Shang Damei, was appointed the same year. The campus sits on a scenic hillside, bordering Zhongzheng Park on one side and undeveloped mountain terrain on the other.

東信國小創立於1954年（民國43年），由當時基隆市長謝貫一將信義區規劃為文教區，並親自選定山坡地作為新校址。當時信義區僅有深澳國小一所學校，東信國小的成立正是為了因應地方日益增長的教育需求。首任校長為同年上任的尚達美。校區座落於風景優美的山坡地，一側緊鄰中正公園，另一側則為未開發的山林地。
