About · 學校簡介

One of the city's youngest schools, beside the rails.

Wenfu Junior High School opened around 2014-2015, making it one of the youngest junior high schools in Kaohsiung City — a 2021 source described it as the city's youngest. With about 708 students, the school sits directly beside the Kaohsiung High Speed Rail station and has built a strong focus on international education, reflecting its location at Zuoying's transit gateway to the rest of Taiwan.

文府國中約於民國103至104年間（2014-2015年）創校，是高雄市最年輕的國中之一——2021年的一份資料甚至稱其為全市最年輕的國中。全校約708名學生，校址緊鄰高雄高鐵站，並致力發展國際教育，呼應其位於左營交通樞紐的地理位置。

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the gateway to Zuoying.

🚄

Wenfu's location right beside the Kaohsiung High Speed Rail station places it at the literal gateway to Zuoying and the rest of Kaohsiung City, a very different setting from the district's historic naval and walled-city neighborhoods. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
