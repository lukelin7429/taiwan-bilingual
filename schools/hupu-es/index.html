About · 學校簡介

A rural school with a wide range of programs.

Hupu Elementary serves the community of Huxia in Jinning Township, Kinmen County. Rather than a single defining milestone, the school is best known today for the breadth of its programming: it places emphasis on bilingual education, science education, and the preservation of local folk arts and traditional skills, while also maintaining an active attached kindergarten for the township's youngest learners.

湖埔國小服務金門縣金寧鄉湖下地區的學童。學校目前並無單一著名的創校事蹟，而是以多元的教學面向見長：重視雙語教育、科學教育，以及民俗藝術與傳統技藝的傳承，並設有附幼，服務鄉內學齡前幼兒。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗣️ Bilingual and Science Education

雙語與科學教育

The school places particular emphasis on bilingual education and science education as core parts of its curriculum, alongside broader international education initiatives.

🎨 Folk Arts and Traditional Skills

民俗藝術與傳統技藝

Students engage with local folk arts and traditional craft skills, helping preserve Kinmen's cultural heritage alongside the modern curriculum.

🏓 A Strong Athletics Tradition

體育傳統

The school has a strong record in table tennis, track and field, baseball, and traditional rope-skipping competitions.

🧸 An Attached Kindergarten

附設幼兒園

Hupu Elementary operates an attached kindergarten, extending its educational programs to the township's youngest children.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Jinning Township, on Kinmen's western plain.

🌾

Hupu Elementary sits in Jinning Township, part of Kinmen County — an outlying island county long shaped by its frontline military history and traditional Minnan village culture. See the Kinmen county page for more on the region.
