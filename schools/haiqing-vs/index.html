About · 學校簡介

From a Nanjing Navy school to a design powerhouse.

Haiching Vocational High School traces its history to the Nanjing Naval Dependents' School, founded in 1949 by Admiral Gui Yongqing to educate the children of Republic of China Navy personnel. When the Navy retreated to Taiwan that year, the school relocated along with naval families to Zuoying, and it became a Kaohsiung municipal school in 1981. Haiching has built a strong national reputation for its design programs, earning the nickname "Fu Hsing in the north, Haiching in the south" (北復興，南海青) alongside Taipei's Fu Hsing Trade and Arts School. Its alumni include Olympic weightlifting champion Hsu Shu-ching and former Premier Lin Chuan.

海青工商的歷史可追溯至民國38年（1949年）由桂永清將軍在南京創辦的「海軍子弟學校」，專為海軍子弟提供教育。同年海軍撤退來台，學校隨海軍眷屬遷至左營，民國70年（1981年）改制為高雄市立學校。海青工商的設計類科在全國享有盛名，與台北復興商工並稱「北復興，南海青」。知名校友包括奧運舉重金牌選手許淑淨與前行政院長林全。

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚓ Born From a Navy Dependents' School

源自海軍子弟學校

The school traces to a 1949 Nanjing institution founded by Admiral Gui Yongqing for Navy families, which relocated to Zuoying with the ROC Navy that same year.

🎨 "North Fu Hsing, South Haiching"

「北復興，南海青」美譽

Haiching's design programs are nationally recognized, earning it a nickname pairing it with Taipei's celebrated Fu Hsing Trade and Arts School as one of Taiwan's two leading design-education schools.

🏋️ Alumni Who Made History

傑出校友

Graduates include Olympic weightlifting gold medalist Hsu Shu-ching and former Premier Lin Chuan, reflecting the school's reach well beyond Zuoying.

🔗 A Shared Origin With Yongqing Elementary

與永清國小同源

Both Haiching and nearby Yongqing Elementary trace their founding to Admiral Gui Yongqing's Navy dependents' schools — living monuments to Zuoying's post-1949 naval history.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A living link to the Navy's 1949 retreat.

⚓

Haiching's 1949 origin as a Navy dependents' school, and its relocation to Taiwan alongside fleeing naval families, is a direct living link to the ROC Navy's retreat to Taiwan and the naval villages that shaped Zuoying's postwar character — a history it shares with nearby Yongqing Elementary, also founded by Admiral Gui Yongqing.
