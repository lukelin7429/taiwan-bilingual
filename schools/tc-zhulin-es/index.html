Zhulin Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

Built from disaster, grown into the coast's largest school.

Zhulin Elementary was founded in 1960, built in part with donations from overseas Chinese communities in the wake of the devastating "8/7 flood" of 1959, one of Taiwan's worst natural disasters of the 20th century. The school grew steadily in the decades that followed, and by 2010 it had the largest student enrollment of any school on Taichung's coastal strip.

竹林國小創立於民國49年（1960年），部分建校經費來自海外華僑於1959年八七水災後的捐款援助——這場水災是二十世紀台灣最嚴重的天災之一。學校其後穩定成長，至2010年已成為台中海線在學人數最多的學校。
