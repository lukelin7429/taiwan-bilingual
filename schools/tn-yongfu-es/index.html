Yongfu Elementary School

永福國民小學

Founded in 1915 on the former site of a Qing-dynasty military office, home to two of the historic "Eight Views of Taiwan" and a tree personally planted by Hu Shih.

About · 學校簡介

A campus that is a cross-section of Tainan's history.

Yongfu Elementary School was founded in 1915 as "Tainan Second Ordinary Higher Elementary School," originally serving Japanese children. Its campus site had earlier been the Qing-dynasty Taiwan Military Defense Circuit office (臺灣兵備道道署); under Japanese rule the same site was later used as Tainan Higher Commercial School and Tainan City Hall before finally becoming the school's grounds. A 2018 campus reconstruction project uncovered Qing-dynasty building remains, which were designated a Tainan municipal historic monument in 2021. The school compiled a full centennial history volume around 2015, marking over a century of continuous operation.

永福國小創校於1915年（大正4年），初名「臺南第二尋常高等小學」，原為日籍學童就讀。校地前身為清代「臺灣兵備道道署」；日治時期此地曾先後作為臺南高等商業學校及臺南市役所使用，其後才成為現今的校地。2018年校舍整建工程期間，挖掘出清代建築遺跡，2021年被指定為臺南市市定古蹟。校方於2015年前後編纂完成《永福國小百週年校史冊》，紀念創校逾百年的歷史。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏞️ Two of the "Eight Views of Taiwan"

台灣八景之二

Feiting Pavilion (斐亭聽濤) and Chengtai Terrace (澄臺觀海) — two sites from the historic "Eight Views of Taiwan" (臺灣八景) — are located within the school grounds.

🌳 The Hu Shih Tree

胡適樹

Essayist and reformer Hu Shih (胡適) spent part of his childhood on campus, where his father's residence stood; he revisited in 1952 and personally planted a tree still known today as the "Hu Shih Tree."

🏛️ Qing-Era Remains Beneath the Campus

校地下的清代遺跡

A 2018 campus reconstruction uncovered Qing-dynasty building remains, formally designated a Tainan municipal historic monument in 2021.

🕰️ Over a Century of History

校史逾百年

Founded in 1915, the school marked over a century of history with a full centennial history volume compiled around 2015.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A cross-section of Tainan's administrative history.

🏛️

Built on the former site of the Qing-dynasty Taiwan Military Defense Circuit office, with Qing-era architectural remains unearthed during a 2018 renovation, the campus is a literal cross-section of Tainan's walled-city administrative history from Qing rule through the Japanese era to today.
