Yongguang Elementary School

永光國民小學

A 120-year-old mountain-village school in Gukeng Township, known for a traditional Japanese-style Taiko drum team and strong showings in music, dance, and calligraphy.

建校120年的古坑鄉山村小學，以日式太鼓隊聞名，並在音樂、舞蹈、書法競賽中表現活躍。

About · 學校簡介

120 years of drumbeats in the mountains.

Yongguang Elementary recently celebrated its 120th anniversary, placing its founding around 1906 and making it one of the older schools in Gukeng Township's mountain villages. The school maintains a traditional Japanese-style Taiko drum team, a signature activity that has become part of its identity, and its students are active participants in county-level music, dance, and calligraphy competitions.

永光國小近期舉辦建校120週年慶祝活動，推算創校年份約為1906年，使其成為古坑鄉山村學校中歷史較悠久的一所。學校保有傳統日式太鼓隊，已成為學校特色活動之一，學生也積極參與縣級音樂、舞蹈、書法等競賽。
