Sanjiang Elementary School

三江國小

A small coastal elementary school serving families historically engaged in oyster farming and inshore fishing along the Dongshi tidal flats.

座落於東石潮間帶沿海地區的小學，服務長期從事蚵仔養殖與近海漁業的居民。

About · 學校簡介

A coastal village's own small school.

Sanjiang Elementary School opened in September 1956, originally as a branch related to the lineage that became Longgang Elementary School, before achieving full independence in September 1963. Under its motto of "diligent learning, information technology, arts," the school serves Sanjia Village on the Dongshi coastal plain, running online classrooms for all six grades and a local-language achievement network that supports Taiwanese and other native-language education.

三江國小創立於民國45年（1956年）9月，最初與後來獨立設校的龍港國小系統有分校淵源，於民國52年（1963年）9月完全獨立設校。學校秉持「勤學、資訊、藝術」的校訓，服務東石海岸平原上的三家村，開設一至六年級線上教室，並經營本土語言成果網，支持台語等母語教育。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📖 "Diligent, Digital, Artistic" Motto

勤學、資訊、藝術

The school's motto of diligent learning, information technology, and arts shapes its curriculum, balancing academic discipline with creative and digital skills.

💻 Online Classrooms for Every Grade

一至六年級線上教室

Online classroom resources are available for grades 1 through 6, giving students continued access to learning materials beyond the physical classroom.

🗣️ Local-Language Achievement Network

本土語言成果網

The school runs an indigenous/Taiwanese local-language achievement network, reinforcing mother-tongue education alongside its academic curriculum.

🏃 Active Lifestyle Station

樂活運動站

A "樂活運動站" (active lifestyle station) supports student fitness, and the school maintains an honor roll recognizing student competition results.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Life on the Dongshi tidal flats.

🦪

Located in Sanjia Village along the Dongshi coastal plain, the school serves families historically engaged in oyster farming and inshore fishing typical of the Dongshi tidal flats, one of Taiwan's traditional aquaculture regions. See the Chiayi county page for more on the region.
