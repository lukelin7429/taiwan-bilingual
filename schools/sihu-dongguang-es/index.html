About · 學校簡介

A name settled after a year of reorganization.

Dongguang Elementary was founded on September 11, 1950 as "Dongguang National School," before being renamed to its current form after an administrative reorganization that December. The school sits in Xidi Village, a location that bridges Sihu Township's inland rice-and-garlic fields with the fishing communities closer to the shoreline.

東光國小創立於民國39年（1950年）9月11日，初名「東光國民學校」，同年12月因行政區劃調整而更名為現名。學校位於溪底村，地理位置介於四湖鄉內陸稻米蒜田與沿海漁村之間。
