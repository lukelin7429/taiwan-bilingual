高雄市立瑞祥高級中學

About · 學校簡介

From Kaohsiung's first complete high school to a bilingual campus.

Ruixiang Senior High School was established on July 1, 1983, and later became the first school in Kaohsiung to be converted into a "complete" junior-and-senior high school, combining both levels on one campus. It opened a bilingual experimental class in 2021 and has run a dual-diploma partnership with an American high school since 2020, letting students earn both a Taiwanese and an American diploma.

瑞祥高中創立於民國72年（1983年）7月1日，是高雄市第一所改制為完全中學（國中部＋高中部）的學校。學校於2021年成立雙語實驗班，並自2020年起與美國高中合作雙聯學制，讓學生可同時取得台灣與美國兩地的高中文憑。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Kaohsiung's First Complete High School

高雄首創完全中學

Ruixiang was the first Kaohsiung school converted into a "complete" junior-and-senior campus, combining seven years of secondary education under one roof.

🌐 A Bilingual Experimental Class

雙語實驗班

Since 2021, the school has run a bilingual experimental class, part of a broader push toward English-medium instruction in Kaohsiung.

🎓 A Dual-Diploma Path to America

台美雙聯學制

A partnership begun in 2020 lets students pursue both a Taiwanese and an American high school diploma without leaving Kaohsiung.

🏹 Named for a Han-Dynasty General

班超路上的校園

The campus sits on Banchao Road, named for Han-dynasty general Ban Chao — one of a cluster of Cianjhen streets honoring classical Chinese historical figures.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Streets that carry classical history.

🏹

The school's location on Banchao Road (班超路), named for the Han-dynasty general and diplomat Ban Chao, is one of several Cianjhen streets honoring classical Chinese historical figures — a naming layer that sits alongside the district's own Ming-dynasty military history.
