About · 學校簡介

A mountain-view school with a strong indigenous identity.

Luye Junior High opened in 1968 as part of Taiwan's nationwide rollout of nine-year compulsory education. The school has since been designated an "Indigenous Focus School" — as of 2018, 64% of its students identified as Indigenous, mostly Amis and Bunun. Its campus sits between the Xinwuluxi River and Hongye Valley, commanding views of both the Central Mountain Range and the Coastal Mountain Range. The school is also known for a well-regarded indigenous choir.

鹿野國中創立於民國57年（1968年），是台灣推行九年國民義務教育政策下的產物。學校已被列為「原住民重點學校」，截至2018年，64%的學生具原住民身分，以阿美族與布農族為主。校園座落於鹿野溪與紅葉溪谷之間，可遠眺中央山脈與海岸山脈。學校亦以優秀的原住民合唱團聞名。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎤 A Celebrated Indigenous Choir

原住民合唱團

Luye Junior High runs a well-known indigenous choir, giving voice to the school's strong Amis and Bunun heritage through music and performance.

🪶 An Indigenous Focus School

原住民重點學校

With roughly two-thirds of its students Indigenous — mostly Amis, then Bunun — as of 2018, the school has been officially designated an Indigenous Focus School.

🏔️ Views of Two Mountain Ranges

雙山脈校園景觀

Sitting between the Xinwuluxi River and Hongye Valley, the campus offers a rare vantage point spanning both the Central Mountain Range and the Coastal Mountain Range.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Luye Township, land of balloons and tea.

🎈

Luye Township 鹿野鄉 is famous for its annual hot-air balloon festival and its high-altitude tea-growing plateau. Luye Junior High sits near the balloon festival grounds, within view of both the highland tea plateau and the mountain ranges that frame the East Rift Valley.
