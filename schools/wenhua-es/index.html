About · 學校簡介

One of Beitou's newer schools.

Wenhua Elementary was founded in 1991, making it one of the newer public elementary schools in Beitou District. Its school district spans six neighborhoods — Wenhua, Fengnian, Zhiren, Zhongyong, Xiushan, and Zhonghe — reflecting the residential growth of the area in the late twentieth century. The school has run themed community events, including a Harry Potter-style "flip the school day" celebration for Children's Day.

文化國小創校於民國80年（1991年），是北投區較新設立的公立國小之一。學區涵蓋文化、豐年、智仁、中庸、秀山、中和六個里，反映該地區在二十世紀後期的居住發展。學校曾舉辦特色主題活動，如以哈利波特風格設計的「翻轉上學日」兒童節慶祝活動。
