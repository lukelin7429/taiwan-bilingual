About · 學校簡介

Eight decades serving Anding and Tucheng.

Anding Elementary opened in 1940 as a branch school, becoming independent in 1946. The school has celebrated its 80th anniversary, with a lineage tracing directly back to Waipu Elementary as its parent institution. Its name, drawn from the Anding/Tucheng (安定/土城) area, reflects how Waipu's farming hamlets were subdivided into separate school catchments after Taiwan's retrocession.

安定國小於1940年（昭和15年）以分校形式設立，1946年獨立設校。學校已舉辦建校八十週年慶祝活動，其淵源可直接追溯至外埔國小這所母體學校。校名取自安定／土城地區，反映出台灣光復後外埔農村聚落逐步劃分獨立學區的歷程。
