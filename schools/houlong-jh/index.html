About · 學校簡介

Houlong's original junior high school.

Houlong Junior High traces its origins to 1953, when it began as the "Zhunan Junior High Houlong Branch Class." It became a fully independent school in 1956, making it the oldest of Houlong's three junior high schools. Over more than 70 years of continuous operation, the school has served generations of students from the town's coastal community.

後龍國中的歷史可追溯至民國42年（1953年），創校時為「竹南國中後龍分班」，於民國45年（1956年）正式獨立設校，是後龍鎮三所國中中歷史最悠久的一所。歷經超過70年的持續辦學，學校已服務這個沿海城鎮數個世代的學子。
