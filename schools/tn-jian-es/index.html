About · 學校簡介

The youngest school in Madou, named for an old temple.

Ji'an Elementary School was founded in 1963 as a branch of Anye Elementary School and became independent in 1969, making it the youngest of Madou District's eight elementary schools. Its name derives from the nearby Ji'an Temple (紀安宮), which enshrines a deity known as "Ling Xiang Fo Gong" (靈相佛公), reputedly brought to the area by settlers of the Xie (謝) clan who reclaimed this land around 1700 during the Kangxi era. The school completed a rebuild of its rear classroom block in January 2020. As of 2026, Ji'an is also hosting the temporarily displaced Anye Elementary School on its campus while Anye's own buildings are being rebuilt.

紀安國小創立於民國52年（1963年），原為安業國小分校，民國58年（1969年）獨立設校，是麻豆區八所國小中最年輕的一所。校名源自鄰近的紀安宮，宮內供奉「靈相佛公」，相傳由康熙年間（約1700年）在此開墾的謝姓移民所奉祀。學校於民國109年（2020年）1月完成後棟教室大樓重建。截至2026年，紀安國小也暫時接納因校舍重建而遷出的安業國小師生於校內上課。
