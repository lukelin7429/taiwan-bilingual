Zhongshan Senior High School

中山高級中學

A large modern campus known for its inflatable-dome gymnasium, elite science tracks, and a nationally competitive debate team — students proudly call themselves the "Frog Tribe."

About · 學校簡介

A large campus with a striking dome and a champion debate club.

Zhongshan Senior High opened in July 1990, following preparatory work that began in 1987. Spread across roughly 34 hectares, it is one of the larger senior high campuses in the Nanzih area, with a membrane-structure multipurpose dome and a five-story library holding more than 100,000 volumes. The school runs elite tracks in biology, chemistry, and physics, and its debate club, founded in 1998, has built a strong record in national competitions. Since 2016 the school has maintained an exchange program with a school in Jakarta, Indonesia.

中山高中創立於民國79年（1990年）7月，籌備處則於民國76年（1987年）3月成立。校地約34公頃，是楠梓地區規模較大的高中校園之一，設有膜結構多功能巨蛋體育館及藏書逾10萬冊的五層樓圖書館。學校設有生物、化學、物理菁英班，創立於1998年的演辯社在全國賽事屢獲佳績。自2016年起，學校與印尼雅加達一所中學展開交流計畫。

Local Culture · 在地文化

The valley of camphor trees.

🌳

Zhongshan Senior High sits at the historic heart of Nanzih, a district whose name derives from the Qing-dynasty place name "楠仔坑" (Nanzihkeng, or "Camphor-Tree Gully") — so called because camphor trees once grew thickly along the local stream valleys. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
