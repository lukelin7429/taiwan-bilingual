About · 學校簡介

A century-old school reborn as an eco-humanities campus.

Taiping Elementary was founded in 1922, during the Japanese colonial era, and celebrated its 100th anniversary with a commemorative publication, the "Taiping Elementary 100th Anniversary Special Edition." Sitting at roughly 1,000 meters elevation — one of the highest-altitude schools in Chiayi County — the school transformed in 2015 into an "ecological and humanities experimental school," describing itself as "a school of ecology that practices cross-disciplinary innovation and exploratory experience." Its efforts have been recognized with the Ministry of Education's Teaching Excellence gold medal in 2020 and Taiwan-US Eco-Schools International Bronze Certification.

太平國小創立於大正11年（1922年）日治時期，並曾出版「太平國小100週年校慶特刊」慶祝建校百年。學校座落於約1,000公尺海拔，是嘉義縣海拔最高的學校之一；2015年轉型為「生態人文實驗小學」，自我定位為「一所實踐跨域創生、探索體驗的生態小學」。學校於2020年榮獲教育部教學卓越獎金質獎，並取得臺美生態學校國際認證銅牌。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌍 An Ecological & Humanities Experimental School

生態人文實驗小學

Since 2015, Taiping has operated as an ecological/humanities experimental school, emphasizing cross-disciplinary, exploratory, hands-on learning.

🥇 A Ministry of Education Gold Medal

教學卓越獎金質獎

The school won the Ministry of Education's Teaching Excellence Award (gold quality medal) in 2020, a top national recognition for teaching innovation.

🌱 International Eco-School Certification

臺美生態學校銅牌認證

Taiping holds Taiwan-US Eco-Schools International Bronze Certification, validating its environmental curriculum on an international standard.

⛰️ One of Chiayi's Highest Schools

海拔近千米

Sitting at roughly 1,000m elevation, Taiping is one of the highest-altitude elementary schools in Chiayi County.

Local Culture · 在地文化

From subsistence farming to tea tourism.

⛰️

Perched high in the Meishan mountains near famous tea plantations and hiking trails, Taiping's century of history and its transformation into an ecological school mirror the area's broader shift from subsistence mountain farming toward tea tourism and eco-education.
