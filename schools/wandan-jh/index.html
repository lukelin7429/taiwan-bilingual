Wandan Junior High School

About · 學校簡介

From agricultural school to modern junior high.

Wandan Junior High traces its history back to April 26, 1921, when it was founded as “Wandan Simple Agricultural School” (萬丹簡易農業學校) during the Japanese colonial era. It was renamed Wandan Agricultural Continuing School the following year, and after Taiwan's 1945 retrocession became Pingtung City Junior Agricultural School. In October 1950, when Pingtung became a county, it was renamed Pingtung County Wandan Junior Middle School, and it took its present name, Wandan Junior High School, on August 1, 1968, under the nine-year compulsory education reform. Having passed through four official name changes over more than a century, the school stands as one of the oldest educational institutions in the Wandan area, mirroring Taiwan's own educational history from agricultural vocational roots to a modern junior high.

萬丹國中的歷史可追溯至大正10年（1921年）4月26日，日治時期創立的「萬丹簡易農業學校」。隔年更名為萬丹農業補習學校，台灣光復後於民國34年（1945年）12月改制為屏東市立初級農業學校。民國39年（1950年）屏東設縣後，改稱屏東縣立萬丹初級中學，並於民國57年（1968年）8月1日九年國教改制時定名為屏東縣立萬丹國民中學。歷經四次正式更名，逾百年校史，是萬丹地區歷史最悠久的學校之一，也見證了台灣教育從農業職業學校走向現代國中的歷程。

🌾 Roots in an Agricultural School

源自農業學校

The school began in 1921 as Wandan Simple Agricultural School, reflecting the rural, farming-based origins of education in this part of Pingtung.

四次更名一脈相承

From agricultural school to agricultural continuing school, to junior middle school, to today's junior high, the school's four official renamings trace the arc of Taiwan's modern education history.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Wandan, hometown of the red bean.

🌱

Wandan Township is Pingtung County's most populous rural township, a flat agricultural plain known especially as the “hometown of red beans” (紅豆的故鄉) for its extensive red-bean farming, alongside lotus-root and lotus-seed cultivation in its water-rich wetland fields. Growing up in Wandan means growing up alongside these harvests, a rhythm of rural life that has shaped the township for generations.
