Fong-Ming Junior High School

鳳鳴國中

A junior high at the base of Bagua Mountain, with a martial arts team that won four national medals and a nationally honored lion dance team.

About · 學校簡介

A school built on martial and folk sports tradition.

Fong-Ming Junior High School was founded in 1972 (ROC 61) on Bagua Road at the base of the mountain range for which it is named, making it one of the Nantou City campuses closest to the scenic Bagua Mountain trail system. The school has built a strong athletic and cultural reputation: its martial arts team won one gold, one silver, and two bronze medals at the 2025 National Chung Cheng Cup, its lion dance team earned top honors in a national folk sports competition, and the school received county-level environmental education recognition in 2024.

鳳鳴國中創立於民國61年（1972年），座落於八卦路、緊鄰同名山脈的山腳下，是南投市內最靠近八卦山風景步道系統的校區之一。學校在體育與文化方面表現亮眼：柔道隊在2025年全國中正盃榮獲一金一銀二銅，舞獅隊在全國民俗體育競賽中獲得最高榮譽，並於2024年獲得縣級環境教育肯定。
