About · 學校簡介

A hillside campus that climbs with its students.

Jingxing Elementary School serves several Wenshan District neighborhoods, including Xingfu, Jingdong, Jinghua, and Xing'an, and has grown from an initial 17 classes to 43 classes over its history.

The campus is built directly into the hillside beside the Xianjiyan (仙跡岩) hiking trail, accessible via a side gate on Jinghua St. Ln. 150. The school describes its terraced, hillside design as symbolizing students' "step-by-step rise" (步步高升) in learning.

景興國小服務文山區多個里別，包括興福、景東、景華與興安等里，學校規模已從創校初期的17班成長至43班。

校舍依山勢建於仙跡岩步道旁，可由景華街150巷側門進出。學校將這種依山而建、層層而上的校園設計，比喻為學生學習「步步高升」的象徵。
