About · 學校簡介

Blending 5G learning with garden and poetry traditions.

Zhongxiao Junior High School serves students in Xinfeng Township, Hsinchu County, and has been designated a "5G Smart Learning School" demonstration site, reflecting the school's investment in digital-age classroom technology. The school also holds "Reading Cornerstone" recognition for its literacy programs. Alongside its technology focus, Zhongxiao maintains distinctive cultural traditions: an annual "Cherry Poetry Recitation Festival" and a Year-End Gratitude Tea Ceremony, both centered around its dedicated library, known as "Wenqiao Garden."

忠孝國中位於新竹縣新豐鄉，獲評選為「5G智慧學習」示範校，展現學校對數位時代課堂科技的投入。學校並獲頒「閱讀磐石獎」肯定其閱讀教育成果。除了科技教育之外，忠孝國中也維持著獨特的文化傳統：每年舉辦「櫻花詩詞朗誦節」及歲末感恩茶會，兩者皆以校內特色圖書館「文喬花園」為核心場域。

📡 A 5G Smart Learning Demonstration Site

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of coastal Xinfeng Township.

Zhongxiao Junior High serves the community of Xinfeng Township, a coastal Hakka farming area in Hsinchu County known for its mangrove reserve and rail history. See the Hsinchu County page for more on the region.
