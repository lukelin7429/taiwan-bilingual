National Tainan Chia-Chi Senior High School

About · 學校簡介

A century of change, from craft school to coed comprehensive high.

National Tainan Chia-Chi Senior High School traces its roots to April 1, 1924, when it began as a one-year women's program attached to the Tainan Commercial Supplementary School. It became an independent institution in 1933 as the "Tainan Women's Craft School," and was renamed several more times through the rest of the Japanese era. After 1945 it became a municipal junior commercial vocational school for girls, then a provincial home-economics vocational school in 1962, and was elevated to "Taiwan Provincial Tainan Chia-Chi Girls' Senior High School" in 1972. It was nationalized in 2000, and in 2016 — a major institutional shift for a school with nearly a century of history as a girls' school — it began admitting male students to its general-academic track and dropped "girls" from its name, becoming today's National Tainan Chia-Chi Senior High School.

國立臺南家齊高級中等學校校史可追溯至1924年（大正13年）4月1日，最初是附設於臺南商業補習學校的一年制女子講習科。1933年獨立設校，改稱「臺南女子技藝學校」，其後在日治時期歷經多次更名。戰後（1946年）改為市立初級商業職業學校，1962年改為省立家事職業學校，1972年升格為「臺灣省立臺南家齊女子高級中學」，2000年精省後改為國立。2016年——對這所近百年歷史的女校而言是一項重大轉變——學校開始招收男學生進入普通科，並自校名中拿掉「女子」二字，成為今日的國立臺南家齊高級中等學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🕰️ Over a Century of Continuous Operation

創校超過百年

Founded in 1924, the school has operated continuously for over 100 years, evolving from a one-year women's craft program into today's comprehensive national senior high.

💃 A Rare Gifted Dance Program

舞蹈資優班

In 1990 the school added a gifted dance program alongside its home-economics and vocational tracks — one of the few schools nationally to combine the two.

🚻 Only Recently Coed

2016年才全面招收男生

The campus admitted male students for the first time in 2016, a relatively recent and significant shift for an institution with nearly a century of history as a girls' school.

🏯 In the Old City Core

舊城核心地帶

The campus sits near Health Road (健康路) in Tainan's historic walled city, within walking distance of Chihkan Tower and the old commercial streets that defined the city as Taiwan's original capital.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Steps from Tainan's original capital core.

🏯

Located in the old city core near Health Road (健康路), the campus is within walking distance of Chihkan Tower (赤崁樓) and the historic commercial streets that defined Tainan as Taiwan's original capital under Qing and Japanese rule alike. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
