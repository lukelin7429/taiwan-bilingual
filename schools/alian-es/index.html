About · 學校簡介

Alian's historic parent school.

Alian Elementary School sits at the historic center of Alian District. Its precise founding date isn't confirmed, but the school has long served as the area's parent institution, spinning off several branch campuses over the decades that later became independent schools in their own right — including Fuan Elementary School.

阿蓮國小座落於阿蓮區的歷史核心地帶。確切創校年代尚未確認，但多年來一直是本地區的母體學校，先後分出多所分校，日後陸續獨立設校——復安國小便是其中之一。

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the heart of a Siraya-rooted place name.

🪷

Alian Elementary sits at the historic center of Alian, a district whose name traces to the Siraya language and was later reinterpreted using characters meaning “lotus” for their Buddhist associations. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
