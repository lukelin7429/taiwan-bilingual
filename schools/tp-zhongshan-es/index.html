About · 學校簡介

The district's namesake school, with heritage roots.

Zhongshan Elementary School opened on April 1, 1934, as "Miyamae Public School" (宮前公學校) under Japanese rule. Part of its original Japanese-era campus buildings survive today and were designated a Taipei City historic building in 2019. After Taiwan's retrocession in 1945, it was renamed "Taipei Municipal Zhongshan National School," with the city government appointing Jiang Huolong as its first postwar principal.

The school has a long record of pioneering special education: it opened an early intellectual-disability support class in 1962 and a "gifted" (資優班) special class in 1973, among the earliest such programs in Taipei. The school and the district itself both take their name from Dr. Sun Yat-sen (中山), and the surviving Miyamae-era buildings are a direct physical link to Zhongshan District's identity as one of Taipei's earliest Japanese-era planned residential and commercial districts.

中山國小於1934年（昭和9年）4月1日創校，日治時期名為「宮前公學校」，部分原始校舍至今留存，並於2019年（民國108年）獲指定為臺北市市定古蹟。1945年臺灣光復後更名為「臺北市中山國民學校」，由市府派任江火龍為戰後首任校長。

學校在特殊教育方面具有先驅地位：1962年開辦智能障礙特殊班，1973年更開辦「資優班」，皆為臺北市最早期的相關計畫之一。學校與中山區皆以孫中山先生之名命名，而留存至今的宮前公學校舊建築，正是中山區作為臺北早期日治規劃住商區域身分的實體見證。
