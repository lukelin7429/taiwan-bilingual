Mingde Junior High School

明德國民中學

A public junior high school beside the Keelung River, rebuilt after wartime destruction and serving Qidu District since 1939.

About · 學校簡介

Rebuilt from wartime ruins into a modern junior high.

Mingde Junior High School was originally established in 1939 next to a hillside bordering the Keelung River. Its school buildings were destroyed by wartime bombing in May 1945, forcing classes to be suspended. The school reopened in July 1947 as the coeducational "Keelung Municipal Girls' Middle School," and was reorganized into its current junior-high form in 1968. Today it continues to serve the Qidu District community from its riverside campus.

明德國民中學創校於民國28年（1939年），校址位於基隆河畔的山坡地。民國34年（1945年）5月，校舍遭戰爭轟炸摧毀，被迫停課。民國36年（1947年）7月，學校以「基隆市立女子中學」（男女兼收）之名復校，民國57年（1968年）改制為現今的國民中學。至今持續於基隆河畔的校園服務七堵地區學子。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏞️ A Riverside Campus

基隆河畔校園

The school sits on a hillside bordering the Keelung River, a setting it has kept since its founding in 1939, distinguishing Qidu's rail-and-river geography from Keelung's coastal harbor districts.

🕊️ Rebuilt After Wartime Loss

戰後重建的歷史

After its original buildings were destroyed by bombing in May 1945, the school was rebuilt and reopened in 1947 — a history of resilience shared by many schools of its generation in Taiwan.

📜 Nearly a Century of History

近百年校史

Founded in 1939 and reorganized into today's junior high in 1968, the school carries over eight decades of educational history in the Qidu community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Qidu, shaped by river and rail rather than harbor.

🚆

Located beside the Keelung River in Qidu District, the school sits in a community defined more by rail and river transport history than by the coastal harbor that shapes central Keelung. See the Keelung county page for more on the region.
