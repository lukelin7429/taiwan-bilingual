About · 學校簡介

Built for a growing east side.

Changlong Elementary was founded in 1985, part of a wave of new schools built to keep pace with the rapid urbanization of Xinzhuang's eastern neighborhoods during the 1980s. Its school zone spans the Xingfu, Xinyi, Heping, Changming, Changxin, and Changlong communities, reflecting how quickly the surrounding area filled in with new housing during that period.

昌隆國小創立於民國74年（1985年），是1980年代新莊東側社區快速都市化下興建的新校之一。學區涵蓋幸福、信義、和平、昌明、昌新、昌隆等里，反映當時周邊地區住宅開發快速填滿的景況。
