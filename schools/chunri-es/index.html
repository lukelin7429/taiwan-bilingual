Chunri Elementary School

春日國民小學

About · 學校簡介

A school that crossed from one era into the next.

Chunri Elementary opened in 1940 under Japanese rule as "春日公學校," making it one of the last schools founded before the end of the colonial era in 1945. The transition from the Japanese public-school system to Republic of China administration happened directly at this campus, giving it a front-row seat to one of the most significant transitions in Taiwan's modern history. Today, the school runs a dedicated Indigenous mother-tongue teaching program, keeping local language instruction a living part of its curriculum.

春日國小創立於日治時期民國29年（1940年），原名「春日公學校」，是日治時期結束（1945年）前設立的最後幾所學校之一。從日本公學校體制轉為中華民國學制的過程，就發生在這座校園裡，見證了台灣近代史上最重要的轉折之一。如今，學校設有原住民族語專屬教學課程，讓在地語言持續活躍於課堂之中。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A farming community in the valley.

🌾

Chunri sits within Yuli Township 玉里鎮, a rice-farming and hot-springs town in Hualien's Hua-Tung Valley near the Tropic of Cancer. The school's Indigenous mother-tongue program reflects the broader mix of Indigenous and settler communities that shape daily life across this stretch of the valley.
