About · 學校簡介

Eighty years of training Madou's technical workforce.

National Tseng-Wen Agricultural and Industrial Vocational High School was founded on April 22, 1941, as "Tseng-Wen Special Agricultural School" (曾文專修農業學校). It became a county junior agricultural vocational school in 1946, was reorganized as Taiwan Provincial Tseng-Wen Senior High School in 1968, became an agricultural-industrial vocational school in 1987, and gained national (國立) status in 2000. Today it offers a broad mix of agricultural and industrial departments — horticulture, food processing, animal-health care, machining, automotive, electronics, electrical engineering, chemical engineering, and computer-aided drafting — plus a sports class and a special-education vocational track. Its self-branded slogan, "technical-vocational pioneer, Madou Tseng-Nong" (技職先鋒麻豆曾農), reflects its role as the area's flagship vocational-industrial school for over 80 years.

國立曾文高級農工職業學校創立於昭和16年（1941年）4月22日，初名「曾文專修農業學校」。民國35年（1946年）改為縣立初級農業職業學校，民國57年（1968年）改制為台灣省立曾文高級中學，民國76年（1987年）改制為農工職業學校，民國89年（2000年）改為國立。學校現設有園藝、食品加工、畜牧保健、機械、汽車、電子、電機、化工、電腦輔助製圖等多元農工科別，並設有體育班與特教職業類科。校方自我標榜的口號「技職先鋒麻豆曾農」，反映其作為區域農工職教龍頭超過八十年的地位。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Trained for the sugarcane and pomelo fields.

🍈

The school's origins as an agricultural training institute directly served the sugarcane- and later pomelo-farming economy of Madou and the wider former Tseng-Wen (曾文) administrative region, a legacy still visible in its horticulture and food-processing departments today.
