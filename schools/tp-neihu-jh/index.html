About · 學校簡介

Founded the same year Neihu joined Taipei City.

Taipei Municipal Neihu Junior High School was established in 1968, and according to the Neihu District Office's official district gazetteer, it was the first junior high school established in the district. Its founding year coincides exactly with Taiwan's nationwide extension of compulsory education from six to nine years, a policy shift that triggered a wave of new junior-high construction across the country — and, remarkably, with the same year Neihu itself was formally incorporated into Taipei City after having previously been part of Taipei County. The school's motto, "Health, Reason, Care" (健康、明理、關懷), reflects its long-standing educational values.

內湖國中創立於民國57年（1968年），根據內湖區公所編纂的《內湖區志》記載，這是內湖區設立的第一所國民中學。其創校年份恰與臺灣全面實施九年國民義務教育的年份相同，當年全臺掀起一波國中興建潮；更巧合的是，這一年也正是內湖從臺北縣改隸臺北市、正式併入臺北市的年份。學校校訓「健康、明理、關懷」，承載著學校長久以來的教育理念。
