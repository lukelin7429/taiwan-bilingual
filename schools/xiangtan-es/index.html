About · 學校簡介

An experimental school built on ecology and reading.

Xiangtan Elementary School transitioned to a publicly-operated experimental school (公辦公營實驗小學) in 2017, then to a privately-managed public experimental school (公辦民營實驗小學) in 2018. Branded as a "multi-talent, ecological wisdom school" (多元展能．生態智慧學校), its curriculum emphasizes reading and ecology education. The transformation was marked by an official signing ceremony with the Pingtung County government, covered in local press.

餉潭國小於民國106年（2017年）轉型為公辦公營實驗小學，並於民國107年（2018年）再轉型為公辦民營實驗小學。學校以「多元展能．生態智慧學校」為品牌定位，課程強調閱讀教育與生態教育。這次轉型並與屏東縣政府舉行簽約儀式，獲地方媒體報導。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Liudui Hakka farmland.

🌾

Xiangtan Elementary is located in Xinpi Township, part of the historic Liudui Hakka settlement region with a farming heritage built on sugarcane and betel-nut cultivation — a heritage the school's ecological curriculum draws on directly. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
