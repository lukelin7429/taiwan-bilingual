National Chaozhou Senior High School

國立潮州高級中學

Over 80 years old and originally founded as a girls' school, now a national senior high and designated High School Quality Improvement school at the heart of southern Pingtung.

About · 學校簡介

From a girls' school to a national senior high.

National Chaozhou Senior High School was founded in March 1942 as “Chaozhou Shude Girls' School” (潮州淑德女學校), with an opening ceremony held on May 23, 1942. It was renamed Chaozhou Agricultural Practical Girls' School in 1944. After World War II, it became Kaohsiung County Chaozhou Junior Middle School in February 1946 (Chaozhou was then under Kaohsiung County's jurisdiction), and was renamed Taiwan Provincial Chaozhou Middle School with a senior-high division added in January 1949. It became Taiwan Provincial Chaozhou Senior High School in July 1970, and came under direct national administration as National Chaozhou Senior High School on February 1, 2000, following Taiwan's provincial-government streamlining reform. With over 80 years of continuous operation, the school is now a Ministry of Education-designated “High School Quality Improvement” institution.

國立潮州高級中學創立於昭和17年（1942年）3月，前身為「潮州淑德女學校」，並於同年5月23日舉行開學典禮。民國33年（1944年）更名為潮州農業實踐女學校。二戰結束後，於民國35年（1946年）2月改制為高雄縣立潮州初級中學（潮州當時隸屬高雄縣），並於民國38年（1949年）1月更名為臺灣省立潮州中學，並增設高中部。民國59年（1970年）7月改稱臺灣省立潮州高級中學，並於民國89年（2000年）2月1日隨「精省」改制，改隸國立潮州高級中學。逾80年辦學歷程，現為教育部指定的「高中優質化」重點學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 Over 80 Years, Originally a Girls' School

逾80年校史·前身女校

Founded in 1942 as Chaozhou Shude Girls' School, the institution has evolved through multiple name changes into today's coeducational national senior high — a rare, well-documented lineage.

🏫 A Ministry of Education Quality School

高中優質化重點學校

National Chaozhou Senior High is a designated “High School Quality Improvement” (高中優質化) school under the Ministry of Education's program, reflecting recognized institutional strength.

🏛️ Directly National Since 2000

精省後改隸國立

The school came under direct national administration in February 2000 following Taiwan's provincial-government streamlining reform, a status it has held for over two decades.

🚉 Anchoring Southern Pingtung's Hub Town

屏南樞紐重鎮

As Chaozhou's senior high school, the institution sits at the heart of southern Pingtung's major market town and transportation hub.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Chaozhou, southern Pingtung's rail and market hub.

🚉

Chaozhou is a major market town and transportation hub in southern Pingtung, historically built around the sugar industry and rail transport — the town grew rapidly after a 1920 rail line was completed and a 1939 south-link highway connected Kaohsiung, Pingtung, Chaozhou, and Taitung. That same crossroads energy has shaped this senior high's identity as a regional educational anchor for over eight decades.
