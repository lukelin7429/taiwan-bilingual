Xinlu Elementary School

新路國小

Founded in 1965 to serve military dependents' children, Xinlu Elementary marked its 60th anniversary in 2025 under the motto 'diversity, innovation, mutual benefit.'

About · 學校簡介

Six decades serving military families.

Xinlu Elementary was established in 1965, originally to serve the children of military dependents ('軍眷子女') living in the surrounding community. The school celebrated its 60th anniversary in 2025, consistent with its founding year, and today describes its mission as blending 'humanistic heritage with innovative energy' under the motto 'diversity, innovation, mutual benefit.'

新路國小創校於1965年，原為服務周邊軍眷子女而設立。2025年（民國114年）舉辦建校60週年慶祝活動，與創校年份相符。學校以「人文底蘊與創新活力」為理念，並以「多元、創新、共好」為校訓。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A legacy of military dependents' villages.

🎖️

Like neighboring Shanding Elementary, Xinlu's founding to serve military dependents' children reflects Guishan's mid-20th-century role hosting military housing communities — a layer of the district's history that predates its current tech-park identity.
