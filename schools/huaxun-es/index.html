About · 學校簡介

A five-part vision on Veterans South Road.

Huaxun Elementary's establishment was approved on May 31, 1991, with a founding principal appointed that August. First-phase construction was completed and inspected in July 1993, and the school's distinctive "Starry Sky Building" (星空樓) was finished in April 1994, in time for the school's first graduating class that June. The school operates under a five-part vision — health, professionalism, dedication, warmth, and humanities (健康、專業、盡心、溫馨、人文) — and its address on Rongmin ("Veterans") South Road places it within Zhongli's postwar military-settlement geography, alongside nearby communities like Longgang and Zili.

華勛國小於民國80年（1991年）5月31日獲准設校，同年8月任命創校校長。第一期校舍工程於民國82年（1993年）7月完工並驗收，深具特色的「星空樓」於民國83年（1994年）4月完工，同年6月迎來第一屆畢業生。學校秉持「健康、專業、盡心、溫馨、人文」五大願景辦學。校址位於榮民南路，地處中壢戰後軍眷聚落地理脈絡之中，與鄰近的龍岡、自立等地區相呼應。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

✨ The Starry Sky Building

星空樓

Completed in April 1994, Huaxun's distinctively named "Starry Sky Building" (星空樓) was finished just in time to welcome the school's very first graduating class two months later.

🎯 A Five-Part School Vision

五大願景辦學

The school organizes its educational philosophy around five values — health, professionalism, dedication, warmth, and humanities — a guiding framework since its early 1990s founding.

🎖️ On Veterans South Road

榮民南路上的校園

Huaxun's address on "Veterans South Road" situates it within Zhongli's postwar military-settlement geography, in a district with a significant concentration of KMT-veteran communities.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Zhongli's veteran-settlement geography.

🎖️

Huaxun Elementary's address on Rongmin — "Veterans" — South Road is no accident: the school sits within Zhongli's broader postwar military-settlement geography, echoing nearby Longgang and Zili, both historically tied to communities of KMT veterans and their descendants who resettled in the district after 1949.
