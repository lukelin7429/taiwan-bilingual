About · 學校簡介

A campus designed for growth, and for green.

Huilai Elementary School was planned starting in 2005 and opened in 2009, built to relieve overcrowding from new population growth under a "small class, small school" policy. The campus emphasizes ecological and organic design, and sits on Wenzhong Road in one of Xitun's newest, most rapidly developed residential and commercial zones.

惠來國小自民國94年（2005年）開始規劃，民國98年（2009年）正式創校，依循「小班小校」政策興建，以紓解新興人口成長帶來的入學壓力。校園強調生態、有機設計理念，座落於文中路上，屬西屯區發展最新、最快速的住商區域之一。
