Jingxiu Elementary School

靜修國民小學

The only elementary school west of the railway line in Yuanlin City, founded in 1951 and steeped in the community's rail-side heritage.

創校於民國40年（1951年），是員林市鐵道以西唯一的國小，承載著鐵道沿線的地方記憶。

About · 學校簡介

Named for an old village, west of the tracks.

Jingxiu Elementary began in October 1951 as the Nanping branch campus (南平分校) of Yuanlin Town's First National School, named after the old local place name "Nanping Village" (南平庄). It became an independent school in August 1953, under the name "Changhua County Yuanlin Town Jingxiu National School." The school was renamed to its current name, Jingxiu, in 1968. It later added a class for hearing-impaired students in 1986, a kindergarten in 1994, and a supplementary school in 1996 — steadily expanding its role in the community.

靜修國小前身為員林鎮第一國民學校的南平分校，於民國40年（1951年）10月設立，校名取自舊地名「南平庄」。民國42年（1953年）8月獨立設校，定名為「彰化縣員林鎮靜修國民學校」。民國57年（1968年）更名為現今的「靜修國小」。此後陸續增設啟聰班（1986年）、幼稚園（1994年）與補校（1996年），持續擴展在地服務範圍。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚂 West of the Tracks

鐵道以西唯一國小

Jingxiu is the only elementary school west of the railway line in Yuanlin City, giving it a unique geographic identity within the city's school system.

🛤️ A Railway-Heritage Community

鐵道文化社區

The school's district takes in the rear side of the old Yuanlin train station, a rail-side grain silo, and the narrow-gauge sugar railway — a neighborhood shaped by the rails.

🌱 A School That Kept Growing

持續擴展的服務

From a hearing-impaired class in 1986 to a kindergarten in 1994 and a supplementary school in 1996, Jingxiu has steadily broadened who it serves over the decades.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Life along Yuanlin's old rail line.

🚂

Jingxiu's school district encompasses the old Yuanlin train station's rear side, a rail-side grain silo, and the narrow-gauge sugar railway, giving the community a distinctive railway-heritage identity unlike anywhere else in the city. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
