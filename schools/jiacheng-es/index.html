About · 學校簡介

A forest-like campus beneath Guanyin Mountain.

Jiacheng Elementary School is classified as a remote, isolated school, tucked into the northeast corner of Dashe District beneath Guanyin Mountain. Its precise founding date isn't confirmed, though its small size and location suggest origins as a satellite campus serving families too far from central Dashe to commute easily. Today it is known for its small, forest-like setting, with the mountain's wooded slopes forming a quiet backdrop to daily school life.

嘉誠國小被列為偏遠、孤立型學校，座落於大社區東北角、觀音山山腳下。確切創校年代尚未確認，但其規模小、地理位置偏僻，推測早期可能作為服務大社中心地帶交通不便家庭的分校而設立。如今學校以林木環繞、宛如森林小學的校園環境聞名。
