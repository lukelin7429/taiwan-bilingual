Dingzhou Elementary School

頂洲國小

A riverside school whose award-winning 2021 rebuilt campus is shaped like a pigeon-racing loft merged with a watermelon, honoring the community's century-old traditions.

About · 學校簡介

An award-winning campus built on flood-formed land.

Dingzhou Elementary was founded in March 1918 as the Ding-Xizhou branch of Xuejia Public School, becoming independent as Xizhou Public School in 1922. The school sits on land at the south bank of the Bazhang River that was formed by silt deposits from a major Kangxi-era flood in 1701. Its rebuilt 2021 campus, shaped like a double-tiered pigeon-racing loft merged with a watermelon motif, won the Kaohsiung Architects Association's '2020 Renhe Award' Outstanding Prize in the education category.

頂洲國小創立於大正7年（1918年）3月，前身為學甲公學校頂溪洲分教場，民國11年（1922年）獨立設校為溪洲公學校。學校座落於八掌溪南岸，該地為康熙40年（1701年）大洪水沖積而成的土地。2021年重建的校舍以雙層「鴿笭」融合西瓜造型設計，向頂洲、洪家社區百年的養鴿與種瓜傳統致敬，並榮獲高雄市建築師公會「2020仁和賞」教育類優秀獎。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🕊️ A Campus Shaped Like a Pigeon Loft

校舍以鴿笭造型重建

The school's 2021 rebuilt campus takes the form of a double-tiered 'pigeon-racing loft' (鴿笭) merged with a watermelon motif, honoring the century-old pigeon-racing and watermelon-farming traditions of the Dingzhou and Hongjia community.

🏆 An Award-Winning Design

榮獲仁和賞優秀獎

The rebuilt campus won the Kaohsiung Architects Association's '2020 Renhe Award' (仁和賞) Outstanding Prize in the education category.

🌊 Land Born from a 1701 Flood

校地為康熙年間洪水沖積而成

The school sits on land at the south bank of the Bazhang River that was formed by silt deposits from a major flood in 1701, during the Kangxi era.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A pigeon loft and a watermelon, in concrete.

Dingzhou's award-winning rebuilt campus is a direct architectural tribute to the pigeon-loft racing and watermelon-farming traditions unique to this remote riverside corner of Xuejia's milkfish district — a rare case of local culture translated straight into a school's design.
