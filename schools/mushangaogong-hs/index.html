Muzha Vocational High School

木柵高級工業職業學校

A public vocational high school by Muzha's MRT gateway to Maokong, training students in precision machinery and hosting Taipei's Drone Education Center.

About · 學校簡介

A vocational school at Muzha's mountain gateway.

Muzha Vocational High School was established in 1982, with planning led by founding director Zhan Chuzhui beginning in 1980, to train skilled workers for Taiwan's growing precision machinery industry.

The school sits adjacent to Muzha MRT Station on the Wenhu Line, the same line that climbs toward the Maokong Gondola. It began offering a comprehensive high school curriculum in 2002, earning an "excellent" rating in the Ministry of Education's 2006 review, and now houses Taipei City's designated Drone Education Center.

木柵高工創立於1982年（民國71年），由首任籌備主任詹楚圭自1980年起規劃籌設，旨在為臺灣精密機械產業培育技術人才。

學校緊鄰文湖線木柵捷運站，該線延伸至貓空纜車。2002年起開設綜合高中課程，並於2006年教育部綜合高中評鑑中獲得「特優」評等，現設有臺北市無人機教育中心。
