Zhinan Experimental Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A school built for Maokong's tea-farming families.

Zhinan Experimental Elementary School opened in 1975 as "Taipei Muzha District Zhinan Elementary School," established specifically as a community school for the children of tea farmers in the Muzha Tieguanyin (鐵觀音) tea-growing region. It opened with six classes and 306 students, initially borrowing space from Muzha Elementary School.

The school's first-phase buildings — nine ordinary classrooms — were completed in August 1976, with music, science, and art rooms added the following year. It took its current name in March 1990, following the merger of the Muzha and Jingmei districts into Wenshan District.

指南實小創立於1975年（民國64年），初名「臺北市木柵區指南國民小學」，是專為服務木柵鐵觀音茶區茶農子弟而設立的社區學校。創校時借用木柵國小校舍，設六班，學生306人。

第一期校舍（九間普通教室）於1976年8月完工，音樂教室、自然科教室與美術教室則於隔年增建。1990年3月，隨木柵與景美兩區合併為文山區，學校正式更名為現名。

Local Culture · 在地文化

The Maokong tea district's own school.

🍵

Zhinan Experimental Elementary is the Wenshan school most directly tied to the district's signature Maokong tea culture — it was founded explicitly to serve the children of Maokong-area tea farmers and sits at the base of the tea gardens served today by the Maokong Gondola.
