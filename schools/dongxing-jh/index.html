Dong-Xing Junior High School

東興國中

A public junior high built from scratch in 2015 to serve Zhubei's newly urbanized county government district, its name drawn from two local villages.

About · 學校簡介

A school named for the neighborhoods it serves.

Dong-Xing Junior High opened in August 2015, its name combining "東" from Dongping Village and "興" from Zhongxing Village — the two communities the new school was built to serve. In 2016, students temporarily borrowed classrooms at nearby An-Xing Elementary for about 10 weeks before moving into the permanent campus. The school graduated its first class in 2019, and by the 111th school year enrolled roughly 1,699 students across 62 classes, reflecting the rapid growth of the surrounding district.

東興國中於2015年8月啟用，校名結合東平里的「東」與中興里的「興」，代表學校服務的兩個在地社區。2016年，學生曾暫時借用鄰近安興國小教室約10週，之後才遷入正式校舍。學校於2019年送出首屆畢業生，至111學年度全校已有約1,699名學生、62個班級，反映出周邊地區的快速成長。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Built for a rezoned county seat.

🚄

Dong-Xing is a textbook case of Zhubei's rezoned "county government district" — a school built from scratch to serve the wave of young families who moved in as the area urbanized around the new county seat.
