About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school with a competitive spirit.

Baifu Junior High School serves the Baifu community within Qidu District, Keelung City. Alongside its regular academic programs, the school has built a reputation for student athletics: it fielded a school baseball team from 2000 to 2011, and more recently its boys' volleyball team placed second in the 2025 Taipei-Keelung district preliminary competition.

百福國民中學服務基隆市七堵區百福地區。除一般學科教育外，學校在學生體育活動方面亦頗具口碑：民國89年至100年間（2000–2011年）曾組建校內棒球隊；近期於民國114年（2025年），男子排球隊更於北北基分區預賽中榮獲亞軍。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚾ A Decade of School Baseball

十餘年棒球隊傳統

The school fielded a competitive baseball team from 2000 to 2011, giving a generation of students a hands-on introduction to Taiwan's most popular team sport.

🏐 Runner-Up in Regional Volleyball

北北基排球賽亞軍

In 2025, the school's boys' volleyball team placed second in the Taipei-Keelung district preliminary competition, a strong showing for the Baifu community's school sports program.

🏘️ Serving the Baifu Community

深耕百福社區

As the junior high serving the Baifu neighborhood of Qidu District, the school anchors education for families in this part of Keelung's inland district.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Qidu, a rail and river junction feeding Keelung's port.

🚆

Qidu District's identity is defined by its history as a rail and transport junction, with the district's rail lines and river connections long channeling goods and people toward Keelung's harbor, distinct from the coastal fishing culture found in the city's other districts.
