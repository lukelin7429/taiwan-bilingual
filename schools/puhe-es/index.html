About · 學校簡介

Seven decades of steady growth in Xinfeng.

Puhe Elementary School serves Xinfeng Township, Hsinchu County, and recently celebrated its 70th anniversary, placing its founding in the mid-1950s. The school has earned recognition for its participation in the 2022 Hsinchu County Programming Education Enhancement Plan, reflecting an early commitment to computer science education. Puhe has also received a county award for its health-promotion initiatives, and runs a video-based English mentoring program that pairs older students with younger ones to build language skills together.

埔和國小位於新竹縣新豐鄉，近年舉辦建校70週年慶祝活動，推估創校年代約在1950年代中期。學校曾參與民國111年（2022年）新竹縣程式教育推動計畫並獲得肯定，展現其對資訊科學教育的早期投入。埔和國小亦曾獲縣級健康促進計畫獎項，並推行以影片為媒介的英語陪伴計畫，讓高年級與低年級學生共同學習語言能力。
