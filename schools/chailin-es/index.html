Chailin Elementary School

柴林國民小學

One of Xikou's smallest rural schools, home to a harmonica ensemble and a world-champion robotics team.

About · 學校簡介

A small school, a world champion team.

Chailin Elementary became an independent school on August 1, 1976, having originated as a branch campus of Meilin Elementary School since 1964. The school runs an experimental education program and is well known for its harmonica ensemble, which has earned consistent county and national recognition. Its robotics program has also achieved international success — winning the 2025 Robofest world championship in the robot-parade category — alongside strong results in English and Hakka language proficiency. The campus building itself won a 2019 architectural design award.

柴林國小於民國65年（1976年）8月1日獨立設校，前身為民國53年（1964年）美林國小設立的柴林分校。學校推動實驗教育課程，並以口琴合奏團聞名，屢獲縣級與全國性肯定。機器人課程更於國際舞台大放異彩，於2025年Robofest世界錦標賽機器人遊行組奪冠，學生在英語與客語能力方面亦表現優異，校舍建築更曾於2019年獲建築設計獎。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🤖 Robofest World Champions

Robofest世界冠軍

The school's VEX IQ robotics team won the 2025 Robofest world championship in the robot-parade category, a remarkable feat for a small rural school.

🎵 A Celebrated Harmonica Ensemble

口琴合奏團

The school's harmonica program has earned consistent recognition at both the county and national level, a signature musical tradition on this small campus.

🏆 Award-Winning Architecture

建築設計獎

The school's campus building won a 2019 architectural design award, giving this small school a modern, purpose-built home.

🗣️ English and Hakka Language Skills

英語與客語能力

Students have achieved notable results in both English-language and Hakka-language proficiency, reflecting the school's experimental, multi-language curriculum.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Born from a growing farm village.

🌾

One of Xikou's smallest rural schools, Chailin Elementary was born out of the township's need to split growing farm-village enrollment into separate branch schools during the 1960s and 70s, a pattern typical of this small agricultural community.
