About · 學校簡介

110 years old, and a "school without fences."

Shun'an Elementary opened in April 1914, during the Japanese colonial era, as a branch of Luodong Public School known as "羅東鎮公學校鹿埔分校," with 40 students. It became independent in April 1920 as Shun'an Public School, and was renamed twice more, in 1941 and 1950, before settling on its current name in 1968. One of the oldest schools in Dongshan Township, it celebrated its 110th anniversary in May 2025. Its old auditorium is registered as a historic building on Taiwan's National Cultural Heritage database, having undergone renovations in 1985 and 2011 and seismic reinforcement in 2013. The school is locally known as a "school without fences" (沒有圍牆的學校).

順安國小於日治時期大正3年（1914年）4月創校，初名「羅東鎮公學校鹿埔分校」，創校時僅40名學生。民國9年（1920年）4月獨立設校，更名為順安公學校，其後又於民國30年（1941年）與民國39年（1950年）兩度更名，最終於民國57年（1968年）確立現名。作為冬山鄉歷史最悠久的學校之一，學校於民國114年（2025年）5月歡慶建校110週年。校內的舊禮堂已登錄為國家文化資產建築，歷經民國74年（1985年）、民國100年（2011年）整修，並於民國102年（2013年）完成耐震補強工程。學校在地方上以「沒有圍牆的學校」著稱。
