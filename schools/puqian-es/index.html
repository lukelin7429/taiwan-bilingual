Puqian Elementary School

新北市板橋區埔墘國民小學

About · 學校簡介

From farmland edge to independent school.

Puqian Elementary traces back to 1956, when it opened as a branch campus of Banqiao Elementary School; it became a fully independent school on 1 August 1960. The name 埔墘, meaning "edge of the plain," recalls the school's original setting on the agricultural fringe of old Banqiao, long before the area filled in with housing.

埔墘國小前身為民國45年（1956年）設立的板橋國小分校，於民國49年（1960年）8月1日獨立設校。「埔墘」意為「平原邊緣」，訴說著板橋市街尚未擴張前，此地仍是農地邊陲的歷史記憶。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💃 A Dance Program With Its Own Team

舞蹈班與運動隊

Puqian runs a dedicated dance class alongside a competitive sports team, giving students a performing-arts outlet uncommon among neighborhood elementary schools.

⚡ An "Energy Education" Center

能源教育中心

The school operates an on-campus energy-education center, folding sustainability and science literacy into everyday classes.

📱 Tablets for Digital Literacy

平板數位素養

A tablet-based digital-literacy program gives every student hands-on practice with technology as part of the core curriculum.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A name that remembers the plain's edge.

🌾

The name 埔墘, "edge of the plain," is a linguistic fossil of Banqiao's farmland past — before postwar urban sprawl swallowed the fields, this was literally the boundary land at the edge of the cultivated plain. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
