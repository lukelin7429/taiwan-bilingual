About · 學校簡介

A small school on a small island.

Jibei Junior High traces back to 1968, when it began as a branch of Baisha Junior High School serving children on the remote offshore island of Jibei (吉貝嶼). It became an independent school in August 1976. Enrollment peaked that same year at 226 students across six classes, but like many of Penghu's outlying-island schools it has shrunk sharply since — down to just 59 students in three classes by 2003. Over its history the school has had eight principals, a reflection of its small, remote character.

吉貝國中的歷史可追溯至民國57年（1968年），最初是白沙國中的分校，服務吉貝嶼上的學童，於民國65年（1976年）8月獨立設校。同年在學人數達到高峰，共226人、6個班級，但如同澎湖許多離島學校，之後人數大幅下滑，至民國92年（2003年）僅剩59名學生、3個班級。建校以來歷經8任校長，反映出這所離島小校的特殊樣貌。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚤 An Island School Reachable Only by Boat

僅能搭船抵達的離島學校

Jibei Junior High sits on Jibei Island, off the northern tip of Penghu — every student and teacher travels to and from school by boat.

📉 A Living Record of Island Demographics

離島人口變遷的縮影

From 226 students in 1976 down to under 60 by 2003, the school's enrollment history tracks the broader story of population change on Taiwan's outlying islands.

🏫 Eight Principals, One Small Community

八任校長，一個小社區

Since its founding, the school has been led by eight different principals, each serving this tight-knit island community in turn.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Jibei Island, Penghu's sandbar getaway.

🏖️

Jibei Island (吉貝嶼) is one of Penghu's most iconic outlying islets, famous for its long white sandbar and centuries-old fishing and tourism economy. Students at Jibei Junior High grow up immersed in this small, close-knit Minnan fishing community. See the Penghu county page for more on the region.
