Gucheng Elementary School

古城國小

An elementary school named for and sitting within Old Kinmen City, the Ming-dynasty walled county seat and one of the island's oldest settlements.

校名源自並座落於明代金門古城——「金門城」內，是島上最古老的聚落之一。

About · 學校簡介

A name built from two villages.

Gucheng Elementary was formed in 1963 from the 1962 merger of two schools: Gugang School, a private village academy in Gugang, and Old Kinmen City Elementary. Its name combines "Gu" (古) from Gugang village and "Cheng" (城) from Jinmen Cheng, or "Old Kinmen City" — reflecting both of its founding communities. The school today partners with three local communities: Gugang, Jinmen City, and Qianshuitou, and sits in one of Kinmen's oldest historic settlement areas.

古城國小成立於民國52年（1963年），由民國51年（1962年）合併的兩所學校組成：古崗學校（古崗地區的私立村校）與金門城國小。校名取「古崗」之「古」字與「金門城」之「城」字組成，彰顯兩所創校社區的淵源。學校現與古崗、金門城、前水頭三個在地社區合作，座落於金門歷史最悠久的聚落地帶之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏯 Named for Old Kinmen City

校名取自金門城

The school's name combines characters from Gugang village and Jinmen Cheng ("Old Kinmen City"), the two communities whose schools merged to form it in 1963.

🤝 Three Community Partners

三社區合作

Gucheng Elementary partners with three local communities — Gugang, Jinmen City, and Qianshuitou — reflecting its role as a shared institution for the area's historic villages.

🏛️ Roots in a Private Village Academy

源自私立村校

One of its predecessor schools began life as a private academy in Gugang village before the 1962 merger that eventually formed today's Gucheng Elementary.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Old Kinmen City, the island's original walled county seat.

🏯

The school sits in and is named after 金門城 (Old Kinmen City), the original walled Ming-dynasty county seat of Kinmen — a direct tie to the island's historic Minnan-era fortification and settlement heritage. See the Kinmen county page for more on the region.
