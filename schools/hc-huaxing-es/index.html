About · 學校簡介

An industrial-park school with a reading focus.

Huaxing Elementary School is located within or immediately adjacent to the Hsinchu Industrial Park (Hukou) area, reflecting the township's evolution from a farming and rail community into a manufacturing hub. The school marked its 40th anniversary in recent years, placing its founding in the early 1980s. In 2020, the Ministry of Education recognized Huaxing as an outstanding reading-promotion school, and the school also runs an art education program sponsored by Quanta Computer, a major Taiwanese technology company.

華興國小位於新竹工業區（湖口）範圍內或緊鄰其旁，反映出湖口鄉從農業與鐵路聚落轉型為製造業重鎮的歷程。學校近年舉辦建校四十週年慶祝活動，推估創校年代約為1980年代初期。民國109年（2020年），教育部評選華興國小為閱讀推動績優學校，學校並設有廣達電腦贊助的藝術教育計畫。
