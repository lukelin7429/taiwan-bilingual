Fuyang Elementary School

福陽國民小學

A small elementary school opened in 2005 to relieve overcrowding at neighboring Nanyang Elementary, Taichung's most populous school.

About · 學校簡介

Built to relieve a crowded neighbor.

Fuyang Elementary School was established in 2001 and opened its doors in 2005, built specifically to relieve overcrowding at nearby Nanyang Elementary School — Taichung's most populous single-grade school. Fuyang remains deliberately small, with roughly 300 students across 12 classes, offering a quieter alternative within the same fast-growing part of Fengyuan.

福陽國小創立於民國90年（2001年），民國94年（2005年）正式開學，設立目的是為紓解鄰近南陽國小——全台中單一年級招生人數最多的學校——的擁擠問題。福陽刻意維持小校規模，全校約300名學生、12個班級，在豐原這個快速發展的區域中提供較為安靜的就學選擇。

Fuyang Elementary was created as a direct offshoot of Fengyuan's 1981 urban-planning expansion, part of a broader pattern of new schools built across the district to keep pace with its growing population. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
