About · 學校簡介

The only junior high for a mountain township.

Haiduan Junior High sits in Haiduan Village at the foot of the Central Mountain Range, near the start of the Southern Cross-Island Highway. As the sole junior high serving all of Haiduan Township, it draws students from Bunun Indigenous communities scattered across a wide, mountainous district — some traveling considerable distances from villages along the highway corridor. The school runs health promotion, science exhibition, and anti-bullying and drug-prevention programs alongside its regular curriculum.

海端國中位於中央山脈山腳下的海端村，鄰近南橫公路起點。身為海端鄉唯一的國中，學校招收來自鄉內各布農族部落的學生，部分學生需從南橫公路沿線的部落長途通勤而來。學校除正規課程外，也推動健康促進、科展及反霸凌與反毒教育。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛰️ The Educational Hub of Haiduan

海端鄉唯一國中

As the only junior high in the entire township, the school serves as the shared destination for Bunun students from villages spread along the Southern Cross-Island Highway corridor.

🔬 A Focus on Science and Wellbeing

科展與健康促進

Alongside academics, the school runs a science exhibition program and health-promotion initiatives, including anti-bullying and drug-prevention education for its students.

🏔️ Gateway to the Southern Cross-Island Highway

南橫公路起點鄰近

The campus sits near the start of one of Taiwan's most scenic mountain highways, connecting the school to a chain of remote Bunun villages further up the valley.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Serving Bunun Haiduan.

🏔️

Haiduan Township (海端鄉) is a mountainous, predominantly Bunun Indigenous district along the Southern Cross-Island Highway, in the Central Mountain Range west of the Taitung valley. The township is home to a scattering of small Bunun farming villages, several of which send their students to this school after elementary.
