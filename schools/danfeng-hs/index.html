Danfeng Senior High School

新北市立丹鳳高級中學

A complete secondary school named for Xinzhuang's fast-growing Danfeng neighborhood, fielding varsity teams from woodball to swimming.

座落於新莊丹鳳地區、隨著捷運新蘆線發展而興起的完全中學，木球、跆拳道、游泳等代表隊表現亮眼。

About · 學校簡介

From junior high to complete secondary school.

Danfeng Senior High School opened in 1990 as Taipei County Danfeng Junior High School. Around 2009–2010 it was upgraded into a complete secondary school and took on its current name. Today it enrolls roughly 3,700 students across about 96 classes, making it one of the larger secondary campuses in Xinzhuang.

丹鳳高中創立於民國79年（1990年），初名「臺北縣立丹鳳國民中學」。約於民國98至99年（2009至2010年）間升格為完全中學並更名為現名。目前全校約96班、學生數近3,700人，是新莊地區規模較大的中等學校之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏆 Varsity Teams Across Five Sports

五項運動代表隊

Danfeng fields competitive teams in woodball, taekwondo, swimming, women's softball, and track and field, giving students many paths into competitive athletics.

🚇 Growing Up Along the MRT Line

隨捷運新蘆線發展

The school and its namesake MRT station take their name from the Danfeng area of lower Xinzhuang — once known as 十八分坑 — which grew quickly into a residential corridor after the Zhonghe–Xinlu MRT line opened.

📈 From Junior High to Complete Secondary

從國中升格完全中學

Founded in 1990 as a junior high school, Danfeng was upgraded to a complete secondary school and took its current name around 2009–2010, adding a senior-high division.

🎒 A Large Modern Campus

規模龐大的現代校園

With about 96 classes and roughly 3,700 students, Danfeng is one of the larger secondary campuses serving Xinzhuang's booming residential population.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for a neighborhood on the move.

🚇

Danfeng Senior High takes its name — like the MRT station beside it — from the historic Danfeng area of lower Xinzhuang, once called 十八分坑. The neighborhood transformed from a quiet corner of the district into a dense residential corridor after the MRT Zhonghe–Xinlu Line arrived, and the school's own growth mirrors that change.
