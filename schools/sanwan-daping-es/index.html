About · 學校簡介

A branch campus that grew out of a village temple.

Sanwan Elementary School, Daping Branch is a branch campus of Sanwan Elementary School, serving the more remote Daping village area of Sanwan Township. It was founded on August 1, 1954, originally as the "Jinshui National School, Daping Branch Class," borrowing classrooms from the Daping Citian Temple (慈天宮) before permanent classrooms were built in 1957. The branch became an independent "Sanwan Township Daping National School" in 1959, and was renamed Daping Elementary School in 1968. Today it serves a cross-township catchment spanning Sanwan's Daping village and neighboring Zaoqiao Township's Dalong village, operating as a small satellite of Sanwan Elementary School.

三灣國小大坪分校是三灣國小轄下的分校，服務三灣鄉地處偏遠的大坪村一帶。創立於1954年（民國43年）8月1日，原名「錦水國小大坪分班」，初期借用大坪慈天宮教室上課，直到1957年才興建正式教室。1959年（民國48年）獨立設校，改名「三灣鄉大坪國民學校」，1968年（民國57年）更名為大坪國民小學。如今學校服務範圍橫跨三灣鄉大坪村與鄰近造橋鄉大龍村，作為三灣國小轄下的山區小型分校持續運作。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛕 Born in a Village Temple

起於慈天宮教室

Classes originally met in borrowed rooms at the Daping Citian Temple before permanent classrooms were built in 1957, a humble start typical of remote mountain schools.

🗺️ Serving Two Townships

橫跨兩鄉的招生範圍

The branch's catchment spans both Sanwan's Daping village and neighboring Zaoqiao Township's Dalong village, uniting families across a township boundary.

🏔️ A Lifeline for a Remote Hamlet

偏遠山區教育據點

As a small rural branch campus, the school remains a vital local resource, and residents have advocated to formally reclassify it as a "Sanwan branch school."

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where Hakka temple life meets rural education.

⛩️

Housed originally in a Hakka village temple and still serving remote mountain hamlets, Daping Branch embodies the deep integration of Hakka folk religion and rural education in Sanwan's hill country. See the Miaoli county page for more on the region.
