About · 學校簡介

A school rebuilt by history.

Dayang Elementary traces its founding to 1920, during the Japanese colonial era, but its story since has been one of repeated displacement: the school has moved and been rebuilt four or five times over the past century, most recently relocating to its current site in 2000. Its original location now lies inside the grounds of Taichung International Airport. The present campus was built in a traditional Minnan red-tile style, echoing the vernacular architecture of the surrounding region.

大楊國小創校可追溯至日治時期的1920年，百年來歷經四、五次遷校與重建，最近一次是在民國89年（2000年）遷至現址。學校原址如今已劃入台中國際機場範圍內。現今校舍採用傳統閩南紅瓦風格建造，呼應周邊地區的傳統建築特色。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

✈️ Displaced by the Airport

因機場擴建遷校

The school's original site now lies inside Taichung International Airport, a direct result of the airport's expansion over the decades.

🏚️ Rebuilt Four or Five Times

歷經多次遷校重建

Few schools in Taiwan have moved and rebuilt as often as Dayang, which has relocated four or five times since its 1920 founding.

🧱 Traditional Minnan Red-Tile Architecture

閩南紅瓦建築風格

The current campus, completed in 2000, was built in a traditional Minnan red-tile style, giving it a distinctly regional architectural character.

🛩️ A Living Record of Local History
