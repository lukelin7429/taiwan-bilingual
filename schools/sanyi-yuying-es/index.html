Yuying Elementary School

育英國民小學

A school that puts woodcarving directly on the curriculum, with a "forest elementary school" branch near the famous Longteng Bridge ruins.

About · 學校簡介

Woodcarving on the curriculum.

Yuying Elementary offers a rich hands-on curriculum that includes woodcarving classes, plant-dyeing, dance, ukulele, and food and agriculture education — directly reflecting Sanyi Township's identity as Taiwan's premier woodcarving center. The school also operates a remote branch campus, Longteng (龍騰分校), known as a "forest elementary school" for its scenic setting and its distinctive Hakka drama programs.

育英國小提供豐富的實作課程，包括木雕、植物染、舞蹈、烏克麗麗以及食農教育，直接呼應三義鄉作為台灣木雕重鎮的地方特色。學校並設有偏遠的龍騰分校，因其優美的森林環境而有「森林小學」之稱，並以獨具特色的客家戲曲課程聞名。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🪵 Woodcarving in the Classroom

木雕課程

Students take dedicated woodcarving classes as part of the regular curriculum, directly connecting them to Sanyi's world-renowned craft tradition from a young age.

🌲 The Longteng "Forest Elementary School"

龍騰分校(森林小學)

Yuying's remote Longteng branch campus, near the famous Longteng Bridge ruins, is known as a "forest elementary school" for its scenic surroundings and Hakka drama programs.

🎨 A Broad Hands-On Arts Curriculum

多元實作課程

Beyond woodcarving, the school offers plant-dyeing, dance, ukulele, and food and agriculture education, giving students a well-rounded creative foundation.
