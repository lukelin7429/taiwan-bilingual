Nanmei Elementary School

南美國小

Grown from 48 to 65 classes and rated a top-tier health-promotion school in 2025, Nanmei Elementary sits amid the industrial-park corridor that has driven population growth across Guishan since the 1990s.

About · 學校簡介

A school that keeps growing.

Nanmei Elementary School has grown substantially over the years, expanding from an original 48 classes to 65 — a sign of steady population growth in its catchment area. In the 2025 academic year it was rated 'excellent' (特優), the top tier, in Taoyuan's health-promotion school evaluation. The school sits in the Nanmei/Nanshang village area of Guishan, within the same industrial-park corridor along Shanying Road that has fueled elementary-school growth across the district since the 1990s and 2000s.

南美國小班級數逐年成長，從最初的48班擴增至65班，反映學區內持續增加的人口。2025學年度（民國114年）獲評為健康促進評鑑「特優」等級。校址位於龜山區南美／南上里，鄰近山鶯路一帶的工業廊帶——正是1990年代以來帶動龜山區各國小人口成長的產業重鎮。
