Wuqi Elementary School

梧棲國民小學

The oldest school in Wuqi District, tracing back to 1897 — over a century before Taichung Harbor's modern port was built, rooted in the old fishing and trading settlement that came before it.

About · 學校簡介

The district's oldest school.

Wuqi Elementary opened in 1897 during the Japanese colonial era, and was formally reorganized the following year, in 1898, as Wuqi Public School. It moved to its present site in 1936. As the oldest school in the district, its history predates the modern Taichung Harbor entirely, embodying old Wuqi — the fishing and trading settlement and old-street community that existed long before the 1970s industrial transformation of the port.

梧棲國小創立於西元1897年（日治時期），隔年（1898年）正式改制為「梧棲公學校」，並於1936年遷至現址。作為區內歷史最悠久的學校，其校史早於現代台中港的興建，體現了1970年代港口工業化轉型之前，梧棲作為漁業與貿易聚落及老街社區的樣貌。
