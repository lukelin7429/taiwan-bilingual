About · 學校簡介

A missionary school that grew with Minsyong.

Concordia Private Senior High School was founded on June 26, 1967, by the U.S. Lutheran Church–Missouri Synod, with founder Robert Zimmer (齊慕博) establishing what began as just four junior-high classes. Over time it grew into a comprehensive school spanning junior high through senior high and vocational tracks, adding an attached kindergarten along the way. Today it is the only complete private school of its kind in Minsyong Township, with a strong string ensemble program that has won the county music competition's top "special excellence" rating four years running, and an emphasis on bilingual and international education with English-focused instructional grouping.

協同高級中學創立於民國56年（1967年）6月26日，由美國信義會宣教士齊慕博（Robert Zimmer）創辦，草創初期僅有4班國中部。歷經多年發展，逐步擴充為國中到高中、高職一貫的完全中學，並附設幼兒園。如今是民雄鄉唯一一所完全型態的私立學校，弦樂團更連續四年榮獲縣音樂比賽特優成績，並以雙語與國際教育為特色，設有以英語為重點的分組教學。
