About · 學校簡介

From a branch station to its own school.

Wannei Elementary School traces its roots to 1921, when Suantou Public School established a branch station there, upgraded to full branch-school status in 1923. It became independent in 1940 as Wannei Public School during the Japanese colonial era, and was renamed Wannei National School in 1941. Today the school maintains an environmental education website and indigenous/native-language programs, participates in county-level music competitions, and provides online classroom access for every grade level from 1 through 6. Located in Wannan Village, the school has also hosted a fire-rescue special operations team safety demonstration for students.

灣內國小校史可追溯至大正10年（1921年）蒜頭公學校於此設立的分教場，大正12年（1923年）升格為正式分校。昭和15年（1940年）日治時期獨立設校為灣內公學校，昭和16年（1941年）改稱灣內國民學校。學校現今設有環境教育網站與本土語言課程，並參與縣級音樂競賽，同時提供一至六年級的線上教室資源。學校座落於灣南村，亦曾邀請消防特搜隊為學生進行安全示範。

What makes this school worth knowing.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Suantou's farming and sugar heritage.

🌾

Like its parent school Suantou, Wannei's lineage is tied to the historic rice, sugar cane, and vegetable farming economy of Liujiao's inland plain villages. See the Chiayi county page for more on the region.
