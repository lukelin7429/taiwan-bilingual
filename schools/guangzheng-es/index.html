Guangzheng Elementary School

光正國民小學

A village elementary school by the Wu River, set among rice paddies and mushroom farms.

About · 學校簡介

Serving three villages by the Wu River.

Guangzheng Elementary began as a branch school in 1952 and became independent in 1957. It serves children from Beishi, Nanshi, and Dingtai villages, three rural communities near the Wu River on Wufeng's western edge. The surrounding landscape of rice paddies and mushroom-growing sheds has shaped daily life for generations of the school's families.

光正國小自民國41年（1952年）以分校形式設立，於民國46年（1957年）獨立設校。學校服務北柿、南柿與頂太等村落的學童，這幾處聚落座落於霧峰西側、烏溪畔的鄉間地帶。周遭的稻田與菇寮景觀，形塑了世代學生家庭的日常生活樣貌。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🍄 A Community of Mushroom Growers

菇寮環繞的社區

Many students' families grow mushrooms in the sheds that dot the countryside around the school, alongside the district's traditional rice farming.

🌊 By the Banks of the Wu River

烏溪畔的校園

The school's location near the Wu River places it on Wufeng's rural western edge, in a landscape shaped by the river's proximity.

🏘️ Serving Three Villages

服務三村學子

Guangzheng draws students from Beishi, Nanshi, and Dingtai villages, tying together several small rural communities around a single school.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Wufeng's rice-and-mushroom plain.

🌾

Guangzheng's surroundings of rice paddies and mushroom cultivation near the Wu River typify Wufeng's agricultural plain, a farming landscape that has defined this part of the district for generations. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
