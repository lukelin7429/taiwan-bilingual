About · 學校簡介

A small school steeped in tea.

Longshan Elementary was founded in November 1955 and is designated a remote-area school in Zhuqi Township. Its best-known program is "Little Tea Master" (小小泡茶師), a tea-brewing certification course in which every participating student has passed certification — a direct reflection of the tea-growing culture surrounding the school. Longshan also runs environmental and gender-equality education, mother-tongue instruction, English theater, and calligraphy competitions, and has an attached kindergarten.

龍山國小創立於民國44年（1955年）11月7日，被列為竹崎鄉「偏遠地區」學校。最具代表性的課程是「小小泡茶師」認證計畫，參與學生全數通過檢定，充分反映學校周邊的茶產業文化。此外，學校也推動環境教育、性別平等教育、母語教學，並辦理英語話劇與書法比賽，並設有附設幼兒園。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Zhuqi's tea-growing heart.

🍃

The "小小泡茶師" (Little Tea Master) program directly reflects Zhuqi Township's identity as a high-mountain tea-growing township along the Alishan tea corridor, teaching children the brewing traditions that shape their families' livelihoods. See the Chiayi county page for more on the region.
