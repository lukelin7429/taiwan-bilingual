Minquan Elementary School

民權國民小學

A public elementary school in Fangyuan Township that became Changhua County's first public Waldorf experimental school, blending practical arts with place-based local study.

位於芳苑鄉的公立國小，現為彰化縣第一所公辦公營華德福實驗學校，結合實作課程與在地文化探索。

About · 學校簡介

From an ordinary elementary school to a Waldorf pioneer.

Minquan Elementary was founded in 1968 as a standard public elementary school. In 2016 it was approved to become Changhua County's first public-run Waldorf experimental school (公辦公營), a significant shift in its educational approach. In 2019 it was restructured into a combined elementary-junior high school serving grades 1 through 9, and formally renamed Changhua County Minquan Waldorf Experimental Elementary and Junior High School (彰化縣民權華德福實驗國民中小學). This site continues to catalogue it under its elementary-school grades.

民權國小創立於民國57年（1968年），最初是一所普通公立國小。民國105年（2016年）獲核准成為彰化縣第一所公辦公營華德福實驗學校，教育理念因此有了重大轉變。民國108年（2019年），學校重整為涵蓋一至九年級的國中小合併學校，正式更名為「彰化縣民權華德福實驗國民中小學」。本網站仍以其國小學制介紹此校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌻 Changhua's First Public Waldorf School

彰化縣首所公辦公營華德福學校

In 2016, Minquan became the first public-run Waldorf experimental school in Changhua County, marking a distinctive shift toward experiential, arts-integrated education.

🔨 Hands-On Practical Arts

木工、金工與農耕課程

The curriculum includes woodworking, metalworking, and agriculture, giving students direct hands-on experience alongside their academic studies.

🚲 Learning From Local Heritage

在地文化探索課程

Students engage in place-based study of local Changhua heritage sites, including the Erlin bicycle trail, connecting classroom learning to the surrounding region.

🌱 Grown Into a K–9 Campus

擴編為國中小九年一貫學校

Restructured in 2019 into a combined elementary-junior high school serving grades 1 through 9, Minquan now offers a continuous educational pathway for local families.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Learning rooted in Changhua's own landscape.

🚲

As Changhua's first public Waldorf school, Minquan emphasizes place-based learning, sending students to actively study and engage with local regional history and heritage sites — including the Erlin bicycle trail — right in the surrounding community.
