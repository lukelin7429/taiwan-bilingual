About · 學校簡介

A school among Tongluo's celebrated tea hills.

Jiuhu Elementary is located in Jiuhu Village, Tongluo Township, home to the famous Tongluo Tea Factory and the Jiuhu Forest Leisure Farm. The area is renowned for producing Oriental Beauty tea and honey-scented black tea, and the school's community life reflects this agricultural identity, alongside active Hakka drama and cultural programming that keeps local traditions alive for younger generations.

九湖國小位於苗栗縣銅鑼鄉九湖村，鄰近知名的銅鑼茶廠與九湖休閒農場森林區。當地以生產東方美人茶與蜜香紅茶聞名，學校的社區生活也與這項農業特色緊密相連，並積極推動客家戲曲與文化活動，讓在地傳統得以在年輕世代中延續。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🍵 Beside the Tongluo Tea Factory

鄰近銅鑼茶廠

The school's village is home to the well-known Tongluo Tea Factory and the Jiuhu Forest Leisure Farm, celebrated for prized Oriental Beauty and honey-scented black teas.

🎭 Hakka Drama on Campus

客家戲曲活動

Jiuhu hosts Hakka drama and cultural programming that connects students directly to the performing-arts traditions of the local Hakka community.

🌿 A Village Known for Fine Tea

茶鄉九湖村

Set in a village long recognized for its high-quality tea gardens, students grow up surrounded by the sights and rhythms of a working tea-growing community.
