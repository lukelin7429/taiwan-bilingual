About · 學校簡介

A green school in the valley's tea country.

Yong'an Elementary serves the community of Yong'an Village in Luye Township's tea and rice highlands. For decades, the school operated the Yonglong branch campus, established in 1973 to serve four remote communities before it closed in 2013 as the surrounding population declined. Yong'an maintains an education trust fund account with the local Farmers' Association, an unusual community-support arrangement that has helped sustain the school over the years. Locally, it is sometimes called a "green elementary school of the valley."

永安國小服務鹿野鄉茶葉與稻米高地的永安村社區。學校曾設有永隆分校，成立於民國62年（1973年），服務四個偏遠部落，直到民國102年（2013年）因周邊人口減少而裁併。永安國小與在地農會設有教育基金專戶，這項不常見的社區支持機制多年來協助學校永續經營。當地人有時稱它為「縱谷綠色小學」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌿 The Valley's "Green School"

縱谷綠色小學美名

Locals affectionately call Yong'an a "green elementary school of the valley," reflecting its setting amid Luye's tea-growing and rice-farming highlands.

🏫 A Branch Campus That Served Remote Villages

永隆分校服務偏遠部落

From 1973 to 2013, Yong'an operated the Yonglong branch campus, extending education access to four remote communities before declining population led to its closure.

🌾 Backed by the Local Farmers' Association

農會教育基金專戶

An education trust fund account held jointly with the local Farmers' Association reflects the close ties between the school and its agricultural community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Luye Township, land of balloons and tea.

🎈

Luye Township 鹿野鄉 is known across Taiwan for its high-altitude tea and rice-growing highlands and its internationally famous hot-air balloon festival. Yong'an Elementary sits within this scenic agricultural landscape.
