About · 學校簡介

A riverside school beside the Green Tunnel.

Ailiao Elementary School serves the villages of Tianliao and Ailiao along the Zhuoshui River in Jiji Township, in the same riverside terrain that shaped the township's historic rail and banana-growing economy. The school has passed A-level web accessibility certification, reflecting its attention to making school information available to all families.

隘寮國小服務南投縣集集鎮田寮與隘寮兩里，座落於濁水溪畔，正是形塑集集鎮鐵道與香蕉產業歷史的同一片河岸地形。學校通過網站無障礙 A 級認證，展現對於讓所有家庭都能取得學校資訊的重視。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 Beside Jiji's Green Tunnel

集集綠色隧道旁

The school sits along the Zhuoshui River near Jiji's famed "Green Tunnel," a canopy-lined stretch of road that is one of the township's best-known landmarks.

🏘️ Serving Two Riverside Villages

服務田寮與隘寮兩里

Ailiao Elementary draws students from Tianliao and Ailiao villages, rural communities along the Zhuoshui River that have long depended on the land shaped by the river.

♿ A-Level Web Accessibility

無障礙網站A級認證

The school has passed A-level web accessibility certification for its website, ensuring school information reaches families of all abilities.
