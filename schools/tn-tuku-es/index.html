About · 學校簡介

A green campus on the rural edge of Xinying.

Tuku Elementary's exact founding year is not confirmed in available records. Located on Xinying's rural outskirts, the school has over 80 percent green cover on campus and emphasizes environmental and nature-conservation activities. It runs a public kindergarten, and its students have been recognized in folk-arts and fine-arts/writing competitions.

土庫國小確切創校年份尚待考證。學校位於新營區鄉間，校園綠覆率超過80%，重視環境教育與自然保育活動。學校附設公立幼兒園，學生也曾在民俗藝術與美術寫作競賽中獲獎。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 Over 80% Green Cover

校園綠覆率逾八成

Tuku's campus has over 80 percent green cover, an unusually high figure that underpins its emphasis on environmental and nature-conservation education.

🎨 Recognition in Folk Arts and Writing

民俗藝術與寫作競賽獲獎

Students at Tuku have been recognized in folk-arts and fine-arts/writing competitions, reflecting the school's creative programming alongside its environmental focus.

👶 A Public Kindergarten on Campus

附設公立幼兒園

The school runs a public kindergarten, extending its educational role to the youngest children in its rural catchment area.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A green fringe to a rail-hub district.

🌳

Tuku's green, low-density campus on Xinying's rural fringe stands in deliberate contrast to the dense rail-hub commercial core the district is known for, offering students a nature-focused alternative within the same administrative area. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
