Qianfeng Junior High School

前峰國民中學

A large public junior high school whose 2007 campus is a recognized Taiwan green-building case, and whose neighborhood traces back to a Ming-Zheng era military garrison.

About · 學校簡介

From a branch campus to a green-building school.

Qianfeng Junior High opened in August 1965 as the "Gangshan Branch" of Gangshan Middle School, becoming independent as Qianfeng Junior High in 1968. The school has grown to more than 1,600 students, and its 2007 campus rebuild is recognized as a Taiwan green-building case. Over its history, Qianfeng has itself spun off two other schools in the district: Yongan Junior High in 1979 and Jiaxing Junior High.

前峰國中於民國54年（1965年）8月以「岡山國中前峰分部」創校，民國57年（1968年）獨立設校，更名為前峰國中。學校現有學生逾1,600人，民國96年（2007年）重建的新校舍是台灣認證的綠建築案例。前峰國中歷史上曾分設出永安國中（1979年）與嘉興國中。

🌱 A Recognized Green-Building Campus

綠建築認證校園

The school's 2007 campus rebuild is a recognized Taiwan green-building case, reflecting an emphasis on sustainable design.

🌳 A School That Grew Other Schools

分設出兩所國中

Qianfeng has historically spun off two other junior highs in the district — Yongan Junior High in 1979 and Jiaxing Junior High — a sign of the district's rapid postwar growth.

⚔️ A Name From a Military Garrison

前鋒鎮右營的地名由來

The neighborhood name Qianfeng (前峰) derives from a Ming-Zheng era military-agricultural garrison, the "Right Battalion of the Vanguard Garrison" (前鋒鎮右營); the character 鋒 was later changed to 峰 for numerological reasons.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A neighborhood named for soldier-farmers.

⚔️

The Qianfeng (前峰) neighborhood takes its name from a Ming-Zheng era military-agricultural garrison once stationed here, originally written 前鋒 ("vanguard"); the second character was later changed to 峰 ("peak") for numerological reasons. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
