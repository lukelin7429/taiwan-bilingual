About · 學校簡介

From a village branch to an experimental school.

Yangming Elementary School began in 1959 as Lunbei Elementary School's Shuiwei branch. In 2024, it was transformed into "創思實驗小學" (Creative Thinking Experimental School), becoming Yunlin County's 5th public experimental elementary school — and notably, the first experimental school established within a Zhang-Zhou Hoklo (詔安客) community.

陽明國小創立於1959年，最初為崙背國小的水尾分校。2024年，學校轉型為「創思實驗小學」，成為雲林縣第5所公立實驗小學，也是詔安客家社區首見的實驗學校，別具意義。

As the first experimental school rooted in a Zhang-Zhou Hoklo (詔安客家) community, Yangming's transformation directly celebrates Lunbei Township's distinctive linguistic and garlic-farming heritage. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
