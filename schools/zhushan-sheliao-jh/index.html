Sheliao Junior High School

社寮國民中學

A small rural junior high serving four villages near Zhushan's historic Zinan Temple, weaving community tea-arts and craft traditions into its curriculum.

服務竹山鎮四個村里的小型鄉村國中，緊鄰歷史悠久的紫南宮，將社區茶藝與工藝傳統融入課程。

About · 學校簡介

A branch school grown independent, beside a Qing-era temple.

Sheliao Junior High began life as a branch campus of Yanhe Junior High before becoming an independent school in 1976. It is a small rural school serving four villages, home mostly to Hoklo farming families. The school partners closely with the community's historic Zinan Temple — founded in 1745 — to run tea-ceremony arts and craft clubs, connecting students directly to one of Zhushan's most storied local landmarks.

社寮國中原為延和國中的分校，民國65年（1976年）獨立設校。學校規模不大，服務四個村里，居民多為福佬農業家庭。學校與擁有近三百年歷史、建於乾隆十年（1745年）的紫南宮社區密切合作，開設茶藝與工藝社團，讓學生與竹山最具代表性的地方信仰中心產生連結。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛕 Partnership With a Qing-Era Temple

與紫南宮社區合作

Sheliao Junior High partners with the community's historic Zinan Temple, founded in 1745, to run tea-ceremony arts and craft clubs for its students.

🌾 Four Villages, One School

服務四村的小型國中

As a small rural school, Sheliao Junior High serves four villages, most of them home to Hoklo farming families in Zhushan's rural southern reaches.

🍵 Tea-Arts and Craft Clubs

茶藝與工藝社團

Community elders' tea-arts and craft knowledge is woven directly into school clubs, keeping local traditions alive for the next generation.

🌱 Grown From a Branch Campus

分校獨立設校

Originally a branch of Yanhe Junior High, the school became fully independent in 1976, following a common path for Taiwan's rural mid-century schools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Beside a nearly 300-year-old shrine.

🍵

Sheliao Junior High draws directly on its neighborhood's most famous landmark, the historic Qing-era Zinan Temple (founded 1745), weaving community elders' tea-arts and craft knowledge into its curriculum — a rare example of a school built around a centuries-old place of worship.
