About · 學校簡介

An early mover on bilingual education.

Bo-Ai Junior High was planned in 1995 and formally established in 1996, with its campus completed and occupied by September 1998. The school launched an English Experimental Program in 2004, followed by a Chinese Music Experimental Program in 2005 — putting language learning and traditional music side by side in its curriculum. Today the school's focus spans entrance-exam performance, digital learning, bilingual education, and athletics, and it hosts a Maker Education & Technology Center for hands-on STEM learning.

博愛國中於1995年開始籌設，1996年正式設校，1998年9月校舍完工啟用。學校於2004年開辦英語實驗班，2005年再設立國樂實驗班，將語言學習與傳統音樂教育並列為特色課程。目前學校在升學表現、數位學習、雙語教育與體育發展上均衡並重，並設有自造教育科技中心，推動動手實作的STEM學習。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗣️ An English Program Since 2004

英語實驗班

Bo-Ai's English Experimental Program, launched in 2004, gave the school an early head start on bilingual education well ahead of Taiwan's national Bilingual 2030 push.

🎶 Chinese Music Alongside English

國樂實驗班

A Chinese Music Experimental Program, added in 2005, pairs the school's language focus with training in traditional Chinese instruments and ensemble performance.

🛠️ A Maker Education Center

自造教育科技中心

The school's Maker Education & Technology Center gives students hands-on access to STEM tools and fabrication equipment as part of its digital-learning focus.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Zhubei's growing school network.

🏙️

Bo-Ai sits within Zhubei City's county-government district, an area transformed over the past two decades from farmland into Hsinchu County's administrative and tech-commuter hub, drawing families relocating for jobs at nearby Hsinchu Science Park.
