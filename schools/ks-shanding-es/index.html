Shanding Elementary School

山頂國小

About · 學校簡介

Built for a growing neighborhood.

Shanding Elementary opened on August 1, 1994, built to accommodate the growing number of families moving into new apartment developments in the area during the early 1990s. Named for and situated on the site of Shanding Village's old settlement, the school has developed signature programs in recorder, calligraphy, and bilingual education, and it maintains a "Daliao cultural history trail" on campus that introduces students to the district's local heritage.

山頂國小創立於民國83年（1994年）8月1日，因應1990年代初期當地新建公寓帶來的人口成長而設立。學校以山頂聚落舊址命名並座落於此，發展直笛、書法、雙語教育等特色課程，校園內並設有「大寮文化史蹟步道」，引導學生認識在地文化歷史。

Local Culture · 在地文化

The school takes its name from — and sits on the site of — Shanding Village's old settlement, and its on-campus "Daliao cultural history trail" makes local heritage part of students' everyday surroundings rather than something taught only from a textbook.
