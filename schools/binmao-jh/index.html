About · 學校簡介

The junior high of the South-Link mountains.

Binmao Junior High opened on August 1, 1968, as Taiwan extended compulsory education to nine years nationwide. Designated an Indigenous-focus school with a predominantly Paiwan student body, it serves as the central junior high for the so-called "South-Link four townships" area, drawing students not only from Jinfeng but also from neighboring Taimali and Daren Townships. Over the decades it has built a reputation well beyond its remote mountain setting, most notably through its youth baseball team and a past national teaching-excellence gold award.

賓茂國中創立於民國57年（1968年）8月1日，正值台灣全面推行九年國民義務教育之際。學校被列為原住民重點學校，學生以排灣族為主，作為「南迴四鄉」地區的核心國中，招收金峰鄉，以及鄰近太麻里鄉、達仁鄉的學生。多年來，學校憑藉青少棒隊與曾獲得的全國教學卓越金質獎，讓這所深居山區的學校聲名遠播。

⚾ A Standout Youth Baseball Team

The school has previously won a national teaching-excellence gold award, a notable achievement for a junior high serving one of Taitung's most remote townships.
