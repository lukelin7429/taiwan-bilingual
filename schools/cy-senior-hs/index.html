National Chiayi Senior High School

國立嘉義高級中學

A century-old public senior high school on Xuling Ridge, near the scenic Lantan Reservoir, long regarded as the region's top choice for academically outstanding students.

About · 學校簡介

A regional flagship with a century of history.

National Chiayi Senior High School traces its founding to 1924, when it opened under Japanese rule as Tainan Prefecture Chiayi Middle School. It took its present name in 2000, and today sits on an 11-hectare campus on Xuling Ridge near the Lantan Reservoir scenic area. Over the course of a century, the school has graduated more than 80,000 alumni and remains one of the most sought-after senior highs in the Chiayi-Yunlin region.

嘉義高中創校於1924年（大正13年），日治時期名為臺南州立嘉義中學校，於民國89年（2000年）改為現名「國立嘉義高級中學」。校園座落於仙嶺、鄰近蘭潭風景區，佔地約11公頃。建校百年來已培育超過八萬名校友，至今仍是嘉雲地區學子心目中的第一志願之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ A Century of History

百年歷史校名沿革

Founded in 1924 as Tainan Prefecture Chiayi Middle School under Japanese rule, the school has carried several names through Taiwan's changing eras, adopting its current name, National Chiayi Senior High School, in 2000.

🎓 80,000+ Alumni

逾八萬校友

A century of continuous operation has produced over 80,000 graduates, giving the school deep roots across the Chiayi-Yunlin region's professional and civic life.

⛰️ A Campus on Xuling Ridge

仙嶺校園

The school's 11-hectare campus sits on Xuling Ridge, adjacent to the Lantan Reservoir scenic area — one of Chiayi's most recognizable natural landmarks.

🥇 Regional First Choice

嘉雲地區第一志願

Long regarded as a top academic destination for junior-high graduates across the greater Chiayi-Yunlin area, drawing students seeking rigorous college-preparatory education.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Beside the waters of Lantan.

🏞️

The school's location near the Lantan (Lake) scenic area places it among the greenest campuses in Chiayi City. The reservoir is one of the city's best-known recreation spots, and its proximity has shaped the school's identity as a peaceful place for serious study, set apart from the busier downtown core.
