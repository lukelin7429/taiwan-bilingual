About · 學校簡介

Inclusive education with a hands-on spirit.

Caotun Beitou Elementary School (北投國小) offers both general and special-education classes, reflecting a commitment to inclusive schooling within the community. The school participates in the "Kuang Ta Traveling Through Arts" (廣達游於藝) cultural-appreciation program, exposing students to the arts beyond the standard curriculum, and it runs agricultural-education initiatives alongside a range of after-school clubs.

北投國小開設普通班與特教班，展現對社區融合教育的重視。學校參與「廣達游於藝」藝術文化巡迴展覽計畫，讓學生在正規課程之外接觸藝術，並推動食農教育及多元課後社團活動。

🤲 Inclusive General and Special Education

普通班特教班並設

Beitou Elementary offers both general and special-education classes, reflecting the school's commitment to serving all students in its community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Farming and craft, side by side.

🪵

Situated in Caotun — a town shaped by generations of woodworking and furniture craftsmanship near Taichung — Beitou Elementary's agricultural and hands-on enrichment programs reflect the area's blend of farming and artisan traditions. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
