Qilin Elementary School

麒麟國小

About · 學校簡介

From branch school to independent campus.

Qilin Elementary was founded in 1950 as a branch of nearby Nanguang National School, becoming an independent school in its own right in 1953. It took its current name, Qilin Elementary, in 1968. The 1999 921 earthquake caused severe damage to the campus, which was subsequently rebuilt, with construction completed in 2001. Today the school sits along Wujie Road on the rural fringe of Puli Township.

麒麟國小創校於1950年（民國39年），最初為南光國校的分校，1953年（民國42年）獨立設校，1968年（民國57年）定名為麒麟國小。1999年921大地震重創校舍，其後展開重建工程，於2001年完工。學校座落於埔里鎮鄉間邊陲的武界路沿線。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Puli's papermaking and flower-growing basin.

🌺

Located along Wujie Road on Puli's rural fringe, Qilin Elementary belongs to the basin township celebrated for its handmade papermaking and orchid and flower cultivation — traditions that shape everyday life throughout the Puli basin. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
