About · 學校簡介

A hundred years of names, one enduring school.

Tongren Elementary School was founded on April 1, 1921, during the Japanese colonial era, as the Baimangpu Annex of Zhongpu Public School. It became an independent school in 1938, was renamed multiple times through the postwar transition, came under Chiayi County jurisdiction in 1950, and adopted its current name, Tongren Elementary, as part of the 1968 nine-year compulsory education reform. The school celebrated its 100th anniversary around 2021. Today it uses digital learning platforms including 因材網 and Junyi Academy (均一教育平台), runs a "Green Tongren" environmental education program documenting campus flora, and maintains a strong emphasis on gender equality education alongside an attached kindergarten.

同仁國小創立於大正10年（1921年）4月1日，日治時期名為中埔公學校白芒埔分教場。民國27年（1938年）獨立設校，歷經多次更名，民國39年（1950年）劃歸嘉義縣管轄，民國57年（1968年）九年國教實施後定名「同仁國民小學」。學校於民國110年（2021年）前後舉辦建校百週年校慶。現今運用因材網、均一教育平台等數位學習工具，推動「綠色同仁」環境教育記錄校園植物生態，並高度重視性別平等教育，同時設有附設幼兒園。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌿 Green Tongren

綠色同仁環境教育

The "Green Tongren" (綠色同仁) program has students document campus flora as part of a broader environmental education initiative.

⚖️ Gender Equality Education

性別平等教育

A strong policy emphasis on gender equality education reflects the school's commitment to values-based instruction alongside academics.

💻 Personalized Digital Learning

因材網與均一平台

Digital learning platforms including 因材網 (personalized learning) and Junyi Academy (均一教育平台) support individualized instruction alongside an active library and digital reading platform.

🎉 A Century of History

建校百週年

Founded in 1921, the school celebrated its 100th anniversary around 2021, marking a century of continuous service to the Baimangpu community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A century in a farming hamlet.

🎋

Located in the rural hamlet of Baimangpu in Zhongpu Township, the school reflects the area's century-long agrarian community built around bamboo shoot and citrus farming. See the Chiayi County page for more on the region.
