About · 學校簡介

Grown from a branch classroom in coal country.

Jiqing Elementary began in 1951 as a branch classroom of Ruiting Elementary, serving Ji'an village in Ruifang's Sijiaoting area. It became an independent school a decade later, in 1961. The village was once a busy staging point along the rail line that hauled coal out of Ruifang's mountain mines.

吉慶國小於1951年以瑞亭國小分班教室起家，服務瑞芳四腳亭地區的吉安里。十年後的1961年獨立設校。吉安里過去曾是運煤鐵路的重要中繼站，見證了瑞芳山區礦業的興盛歲月。

Jiqing Elementary is located in Ruifang's Sijiaoting coal-mining area, once a busy staging point along the coal-hauling rail line that connected the district's mountain mines to the wider network. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
