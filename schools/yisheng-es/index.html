About · 學校簡介

A campus built to match a waterfall landscape.

Yisheng Elementary was founded on January 31, 1950 as a branch campus (“義盛分校”) of Jiaoban National School, today's Jieshou Elementary. It became independent on November 25, 1952 as “義盛國民學校,” and was renamed 義盛國民小學 in August 1968 under the nine-year compulsory education reform. The school is located inside the Xiaowulai (小烏來) Scenic Area, one of Fuxing's best-known waterfall and scenic tourist destinations, and its campus buildings use an Atayal-style architectural design set against mountain and river scenery — frequently described as one of the most scenic school campuses in all of Taoyuan City. The current principal is Chen Chien-teng (陳建灯).

義盛國小民國39年（1950年）1月31日以「義盛分校」創立，隸屬角板國民學校（即今日介壽國小前身），民國41年（1952年）11月25日獨立設校為「義盛國民學校」，民國57年（1968年）8月九年國教實施後更名「義盛國民小學」。學校位於小烏來風景區內，是復興區知名的瀑布景觀觀光勝地之一，校舍採泰雅風格建築設計，搭配山水景致，常被譽為桃園市最美校園之一。現任校長為陳建灯。
