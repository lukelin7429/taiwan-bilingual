About · 學校簡介

A century of preserving the mother tongue.

Chenggong Elementary was founded in 1924 under Japanese rule and celebrated its 100th anniversary in December 2024. Across its century of operation, the school has maintained Hakka mother-tongue instruction for its students, helping preserve the dialect spoken by the Hakka families who originally settled Dongshi's hill country.

成功國民小學創立於1924年日治時期，於2024年12月歡慶建校百週年。學校長年開設客語母語課程，延續東勢開墾先民所使用的客家話，讓這份語言傳承在百年間持續流傳。
