About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school on the city's southern edge.

Douhuan Elementary School serves Douhuan Li, a hillside neighborhood on the southern edge of Toufen City along the old Zhongzheng Road corridor. As with many small public elementary schools in Miaoli County, Douhuan provides the standard elementary curriculum to local children while anchoring day-to-day life in its traditionally Hakka farming community.

斗煥國小服務頭份市南緣、沿舊中正路廊帶的斗煥里丘陵社區。如同苗栗縣許多小型公立小學，斗煥國小為當地學童提供標準國小課程，同時也是這個傳統客家農村社區日常生活的重要據點。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛰️ A Hillside Setting

丘陵地帶校園

Douhuan sits in a traditionally Hakka farming and hillside community on Toufen's southern edge, giving the school a distinctly rural setting within the growing city.

🏫 A Neighborhood Anchor

在地社區的核心據點

As Douhuan Li's local elementary school, it plays a central role for families in this quieter corner of Toufen along the old Zhongzheng Road corridor.

🌾 Part of Toufen's Farming Fringe

頭份農業邊緣地帶

The school serves families connected to Douhuan's farming traditions, offering a contrast to the denser, more urbanized neighborhoods elsewhere in Toufen City.
