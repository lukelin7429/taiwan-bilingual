About · 學校簡介

Taitung County's youngest public elementary school.

Donghai Elementary is notable as the youngest public elementary school in Taitung County, built through a preparatory process that ran from 1994 to 1997. The first phase of classrooms was completed in October 1996, followed by an activity center and library complex in June 1997 — the same year the school formally opened. A playground renovation followed in 1998, with the administration building completed in 1999, rounding out the campus within its first few years.

東海國小是臺東縣最年輕的公立國小，籌備期自民國83年（1994年）至86年（1997年）。第一期教室工程於民國85年（1996年）10月完工，活動中心暨圖書館則於民國86年（1997年）6月完工，同年學校正式開校。隔年（1998年）完成操場整建，行政大樓則於1999年完工，短短數年間便建構出完整校園。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏗️ Taitung's Youngest Public School

臺東縣最年輕的公立國小

Donghai Elementary holds the distinction of being the youngest public elementary school in Taitung County, purpose-built rather than evolved from an older branch campus.

📐 Built in Fast Succession

短期內完成整體校園建設

From its first classrooms in 1996 to a completed administration building by 1999, the school's entire campus took shape within about five years — a compressed construction timeline.

📚 A Combined Activity and Library Hub

活動中心暨圖書館複合設施

The school's activity center and library were built as a combined complex, completed in June 1997 just as the school itself opened its doors.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Taitung City's growing east side.

🌇

Donghai Elementary serves families in Taitung City, the county seat of Taitung County on Taiwan's southeast coast, in an area that grew enough by the mid-1990s to need a brand-new public school. See the Taitung county page for more on the region.
