About · 學校簡介

A small school beside a famous wetland.

Haomei Elementary School was founded in September 1961 in Haomei Village, home to the well-known "Haomei Beach/Swallow Islet" (好美寮) wetland and its colorful 3D wall-art village — one of Budai's best-known tourist attractions. As a small remote coastal school, it places strong emphasis on outdoor and environmental education, alongside local/indigenous-language instruction and special education services. Its nutrition program emphasizes locally sourced pork and beef, reflecting a broader commitment to using domestic ingredients in school lunches.

好美國小創立於民國50年（1961年）9月，位於好美里，鄰近知名的「好美寮」濕地與色彩繽紛的3D彩繪村，是布袋鎮著名的觀光景點之一。學校規模小、地處濱海偏遠地區，特別著重戶外教育與環境教育，並開設本土語言課程及特殊教育服務。學校午餐營養計畫強調使用在地生產的豬肉與牛肉食材，展現對國產食材的重視。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎨 Neighbor to the 3D Wall-Art Village

緊鄰3D彩繪村

The school sits in Haomei Village, home to the famous Haomei Beach/Swallow Islet wetland and its colorful 3D wall-art murals, one of Budai's most photographed tourist spots.

🌿 Outdoor & Environmental Education

戶外與環境教育

As a small remote coastal school, Haomei places strong emphasis on outdoor learning and environmental education, drawing on the wetland ecology right outside its doors.

🗣️ Local Language & Special Education

本土語言與特殊教育

The curriculum includes local/indigenous language instruction and dedicated special education services, supporting the diverse needs of this small coastal community's students.

🥩 Locally Sourced School Lunches

堅持國產豬牛肉

The school's nutrition program emphasizes exclusively locally sourced pork and beef, part of a broader effort to keep student meals grounded in domestic, traceable ingredients.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Living beside Budai's wetlands.

🦩

Haomei Village sits beside Budai's coastal lagoons and the Beimen–Budai wetland system, an area shaped by oyster racks, fish ponds, and the salt-flat-turned-wetland ecology that defines this part of the coast — the same landscape that makes the village's wall-art and wetland scenery a draw for visitors.
