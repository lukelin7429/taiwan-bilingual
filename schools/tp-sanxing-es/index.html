Sanxing Elementary School

臺北市三興國民小學

Founded in 1955 in the historic Sanzhangli area, this "mother school" seeded two other Xinyi elementary schools during the district's 1960s population boom.

About · 學校簡介

The mother school of two Xinyi neighbors.

Sanxing Elementary School was founded in August 1955 under founding principal Xi Huizhen. Its name honors both its location in the historic "Sanzhangli" (三張犁) area and its parent school, Xingya Elementary — "Sanxing" (三興) combines characters meaning "to commemorate" and "Xingya." Near Taipei Medical University and the Linjiang Street ("Tonghua") Night Market, the school played an outsized role in Xinyi's mid-century growth: in 1968 it transferred its 4th- and 5th-grade students to help found the newly created Guangfu Elementary School, and in 1969 it sent 22 more classes to help populate Wuxing Elementary School — seeding two other Xinyi elementary schools in the space of two years.

三興國小創立於1955年（民國44年）8月，首任校長席慧珍。校名取自校址所在的歷史地名「三張犁」，並紀念其母校興雅國小之「興」字，合為「三興」。學校鄰近臺北醫學大學與臨江街（通化）夜市。三興在信義區1960年代人口激增期間扮演重要角色：1968年撥出四、五年級學生協助創立光復國小，1969年再撥出22班學生協助吳興國小成立，兩年內接連孕育出兩所鄰校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 The Mother School of Two Neighbors

孕育兩所鄰校的母校

In 1968 and 1969, Sanxing transferred students and classes to help found both Guangfu Elementary School and Wuxing Elementary School, seeding two schools during Xinyi's population boom.

🏀 An Elementary Basketball Powerhouse

籃球傳統強校

Sanxing has long been a traditional powerhouse in Taiwan's Elementary Basketball League (EBL) top division.

🏘️ Rooted in Historic Sanzhangli

根植三張犁

The school's name commemorates the historic "Sanzhangli" area where it sits, alongside its parent school Xingya, linking today's campus to a name that predates modern Xinyi District.

🌃 Near the Tonghua Night Market

鄰近通化夜市

The school's location near Taipei Medical University and the Linjiang Street ("Tonghua") Night Market places it in one of Xinyi's liveliest neighborhoods.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A record of Xinyi's mid-century growth.

🌱

Sanxing's role as the "mother school" that spun off Guangfu and Wuxing elementaries in the late 1960s directly mirrors Xinyi's own explosive population growth in that era, as the rural Sanzhangli/Xingya area urbanized ahead of the 1990s creation of the Xinyi Planning District. The school's history is, in effect, a record of the district's transformation from farmland to city neighborhood. See the Taipei city page for more on the region.
