About · 學校簡介

A landmark school at the foot of the mountain.

National Hsinchu Senior High School was founded in April 1922 under Japanese rule as "新竹州立新竹中學校," a five-year general middle school for boys, later shortened to four years in 1941. After Taiwan's retrocession in 1945, it was renamed Taiwan Provincial Hsinchu Middle School, and became a senior-high-only institution in 1970. The school sits at the foot of Shibajianshan (Eighteen Peaks Mountain), neighboring Hsinchu Girls' Senior High School as well as National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University and National Tsing Hua University — placing it at the heart of the city's academic and research corridor. Its motto, "誠、慧、健、毅" (Sincerity, Wisdom, Health, Perseverance), reflects over a century of educational tradition.

新竹高中創校於1922年（大正11年）日治時期，原名「新竹州立新竹中學校」，是一所五年制的男子中學，1941年學制縮短為四年。1945年台灣光復後，改名為「台灣省立新竹中學」，1970年起改制為純高中部。學校座落於十八尖山山麓，與新竹女中比鄰而居，並與國立陽明交通大學、國立清華大學相鄰，位處新竹學術與研究廊帶的核心地帶。校訓「誠、慧、健、毅」承載了超過百年的教育傳統。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛰️ At the Foot of Eighteen Peaks

十八尖山山麓校園

The school's campus sits at the foot of Shibajianshan (Eighteen Peaks Mountain), one of Hsinchu's most recognizable natural landmarks.

🏛️ Over a Century of Tradition

逾百年建校歷史

Founded in 1922 under Japanese rule, the school is one of Taiwan's oldest and most prestigious public senior highs, carrying more than a century of academic tradition.

🎓 Neighbor to Two National Universities

緊鄰兩所國立大學

Located near National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University and National Tsing Hua University, the school is embedded in Hsinchu's academic and research hub.

✒️ A Motto of Four Virtues

誠、慧、健、毅

The school's motto — Sincerity, Wisdom, Health, Perseverance — guides its educational philosophy across generations of students.
