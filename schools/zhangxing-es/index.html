About · 學校簡介

A village with deep educational roots.

Changxing Elementary School serves Changxing Village in Changzhi Township, part of the Liudui Hakka cultural region. The village carries a notable place in local education history: its civic leader, Qiu Runkuan, led the 1937 founding of what became Changzhi Junior High School, suggesting a similarly long institutional tradition for Changxing's own elementary school. Today the school maintains a dedicated "local education" (本土教育) website and stays active on Facebook and Instagram.

長興國小服務長治鄉長興村，該地區屬於六堆客家文化區。長興村在地方教育史上占有重要地位：村長邱潤寬曾於1937年領銜創辦後來的長治國中，顯示長興村自身國小教育傳統同樣悠久。學校目前經營「本土教育」網站，並活躍於臉書與Instagram。

Changxing Village sits within Changzhi Township's Liudui (六堆) Hakka cultural region — the historic "front camp" — an agricultural area known for betel nut farming. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
