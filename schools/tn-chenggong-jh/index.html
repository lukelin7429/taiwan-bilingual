About · 學校簡介

A closure order reversed by parent advocacy.

Chenggong Junior High School was founded in 1967, per the Tainan City Education Bureau's official school directory. In January 2017, it became the first school in Taiwan to which the "Regulations on Suspension and Merger of Public Elementary and Junior High Schools" was formally applied, due to declining enrollment — but sustained advocacy from the school's parents' association led the Education Bureau to reverse the closure decision, and the school continued normal enrollment for the 2017 school year. Today Chenggong runs bilingual-education programming and a digital learning center, and its cafeteria policy uses only domestically-sourced pork and beef.

成功國中依臺南市教育局官方學校名錄，創立於1967年（民國56年）。2017年（民國106年）1月，成功國中成為全臺第一所被正式適用「公立國民小學及國民中學合併停辦條例」的學校，起因為學生人數下滑；然而在家長會積極爭取下，教育局最終撤銷停辦決定，學校於2017學年度持續正常招生。目前學校推動雙語教育課程與數位學習中心，午餐政策也堅持僅使用國產豬肉與牛肉。

✊ A Closure Order, Reversed

全臺首例撤銷併校令

In 2017, Chenggong became the first school in Taiwan formally subject to a school-closure regulation — and the first where determined parent advocacy successfully reversed that decision.

🗣️ Bilingual Programming in Action

推動雙語教育課程

The school runs active bilingual-education programming alongside a digital learning center, keeping pace with Taiwan's national bilingual-education push.

🥩 A Domestic-Sourcing Cafeteria Policy

午餐堅持國產豬牛肉

Chenggong's cafeteria policy commits to using only domestically-sourced pork and beef, a small but concrete food-safety commitment to its students' families.

Chenggong Junior High sits in North District near Tainan's historic rail corridor, part of the district's older residential fabric that developed around the train station area — a neighborhood whose community strength was on full display when parents rallied in 2017 to keep the school's doors open.
