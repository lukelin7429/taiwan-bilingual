Dingpu Elementary School

頂埔國民小學

A near-century-old school with a “Contemplation Forest” of over a hundred plant species, once one of Tucheng's outlying hamlets.

建校近百年，校園內設有逾百種植物的「靜思林」，昔日為土城偏遠農村聚落。

About · 學校簡介

Nearly a century of growth from a rural hamlet.

Dingpu Elementary began in 1922 during the Japanese colonial era as a branch classroom, and was formalized as an independent school in 1946. Now nearly a century old, the campus features a flower garden, a water park, and a “Contemplation Forest” home to more than a hundred plant species, and the school has won the national Reading Cornerstone Award for its reading culture.

頂埔國小創立於日治時期大正11年（1922年），最初為分教室，民國35年（1946年）正式獨立設校。如今建校將近百年，校園設有花園、戲水設施，以及擁有逾百種植物的「靜思林」，學校並曾榮獲全國閱讀磐石獎，肯定其閱讀推廣成果。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 The “Contemplation Forest”

靜思林

Dingpu's campus features a “Contemplation Forest” home to more than a hundred plant species, giving students a green, nature-rich learning environment.

🏆 A National Reading Cornerstone Award

全國閱讀磐石獎

The school has won the national Reading Cornerstone Award, recognizing an exceptional school-wide reading culture.

💦 A Campus Water Park and Flower Garden

戲水設施與花園

Alongside its forest, the campus includes a water park and flower garden, adding to its reputation as one of Tucheng's most nature-rich schools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

From an outlying hamlet to a nature-rich campus.

🌳

Dingpu Elementary's near-century history and nature-rich campus reflect its origins as one of Tucheng's original outlying farming hamlets before it became a dense residential and MRT-linked area. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
