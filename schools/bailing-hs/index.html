Bailing Senior High School

臺北市立百齡高級中學

A complete six-year secondary school beside the historic Bailing Bridge, anchoring a working-class riverside neighborhood along the Keelung River.

位於基隆河畔百齡橋旁的完全中學，扎根於士林較樸實的河濱社區。

About · 學校簡介

Named for the bridge it stands beside.

Bailing Senior High School began in 1991 as Bailing Junior High School, growing out of a 1987 preparatory office that was first called the Jiantan Junior High preparatory office before being renamed Bailing in 1988. It enrolled its first junior-high cohort of 13 classes as construction finished in 1991. In 1999, the school enrolled its first senior-high class and was upgraded to a full six-year "complete secondary school" (完全中學), a structure shared by only a handful of other Taipei schools that run both junior and senior divisions on one campus.

百齡高中前身為百齡國民中學，源於民國76年（1987年）成立的籌備處，原名「劍潭國中籌備處」，民國77年（1988年）更名百齡。民國80年（1991年）校舍完工，首屆招收13班國中新生。民國88年（1999年）起招收高中部學生，正式升格為兼辦國中、高中部的六年一貫「完全中學」，此體制在臺北市僅少數學校採行。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌉 Named for the Bailing Bridge

因百齡橋得名

The school takes its name from the neighboring Bailing Bridge, which spans the Keelung River and connects Shilin to Datong District.

🏫 A Six-Year Complete Secondary School

六年一貫完全中學

Upgraded to full senior-high status in 1999, the school runs both junior and senior divisions on one campus — a structure shared by only a handful of Taipei schools.

🏗️ Grown from a 1987 Preparatory Office

籌備處起家

The school's roots trace to a 1987 preparatory office, first named for neighboring Jiantan before being renamed Bailing in 1988 and formally opening in 1991.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A riverside neighborhood along the Keelung.

🌊

Located along Chengde Road near the Keelung River and the Bailing Bridge crossing into Datong District, Bailing Senior High anchors a working-class riverside neighborhood in eastern Shilin — a district quite distinct from Shilin's more touristy Old Street and Tianmu areas.
