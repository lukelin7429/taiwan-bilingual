Wulong Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

From a mountain branch to a maker-education pioneer.

Wulong Elementary began in 1920 during the Japanese colonial era as a branch of Jiuqionglin Public School, located in the mountain village of Luliaokeng. It became independent as Luliaokeng Public School in 1928, went through several name changes, and took its current name in 1968. In a striking modern turn, the school was designated Hsinchu County's 9th experimental education school starting in 2022 through 2027, with a focus on maker education, "5C" skills, AI, and robotics.

五龍國小創立於大正9年（1920年），日治時期為九芎林公學校於鹿寮坑的分教場。昭和3年（1928年）獨立設校，命名鹿寮坑公學校，歷經多次更名，民國57年（1968年）改為現名。近年學校迎來轉型契機，自民國111年（2022年）起至116年（2027年），獲選為新竹縣第9所實驗教育學校，聚焦自造教育、5C能力、人工智慧與機器人課程。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🤖 A County Experimental Education School

縣立第9所實驗教育學校

Since 2022, Wulong has served as Hsinchu County's 9th experimental education school, with a five-year focus (through 2027) on maker education, 5C skills, AI, and robotics.

🏔️ Century-Old Mountain Roots

鹿寮坑山區分教場起家

The school traces back to 1920 as a mountain-village branch campus in Luliaokeng, giving today's tech-forward programs a deep rural, century-old foundation.

🔧 Hands-On, Future-Focused Learning

自造教育與機器人課程

Students engage in maker-education projects and robotics, giving this small rural school an unusually forward-looking curriculum for its size and setting.
