Mayuan Elementary School

麻園國小

A public elementary school with a winding history — its original 1916 lineage moved on to become another school, leaving the area without one for 38 years before Mayuan was rebuilt.

校史曲折的公立國小——1916年創校的原始學校後來遷校並成為另一所國小，當地一度38年沒有學校，直到麻園國小重新設立。

About · 學校簡介

A school with two beginnings.

Mayuan Elementary's history is unusually complex. A branch of Shulingtou Public School was established in the area in 1916 under Japanese colonial rule, becoming independent in 1920 — but in 1922, that original school lineage relocated, eventually becoming today's Xinshe Elementary. This left the Mayuan area without a school of its own for 38 years. A new branch was established in 1960, and it became independent as Mayuan Elementary on August 17, 1963, taking its current name in 1968. The first teachers back in 1916 were a Japanese instructor from Fukuoka and a local Taiwanese aide.

麻園國小的校史相當曲折。1916年，日治時期樹林頭公學校在當地設立分教場，1920年獨立設校，但1922年該校系脈遷校，後來成為今日的新社國小，使麻園地區有長達38年沒有自己的學校。1960年重新設立分校，1963年8月17日獨立設校，定名為麻園國小，1968年確立現今校名。1916年最初的教師為一位來自福岡的日籍教師與一位在地台籍助教。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🔀 A School That Split in Two

校史一分為二

The original 1916 school lineage relocated in 1922 and became today's Xinshe Elementary, while a separate school later rose on the Mayuan site — two schools from one origin.

⏳ Thirty-Eight Years Without a School

38年沒有在地學校

After the original school relocated in 1922, the Mayuan area went without its own school for 38 years until a new branch was established in 1960.

👨‍🏫 A Teacher From Fukuoka

來自福岡的日籍教師

The school's earliest known teachers, back in 1916, were a Japanese instructor from Fukuoka and a local Taiwanese aide — a small but telling detail of colonial-era education.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Zhubei's layered history.

🏘️

Mayuan's discontinuous founding story — split between two schools, with decades of absence in between — reflects the layered, sometimes complicated history behind many of Zhubei's older neighborhoods, now part of a fast-growing county seat.
