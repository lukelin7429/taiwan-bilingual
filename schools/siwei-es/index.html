About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school in Siwei Village.

Siwei Elementary School serves the children of Siwei Village in Wandan Township, Pingtung County. As a neighborhood elementary school in Taiwan's most populous rural township, it anchors daily community life for local families amid Wandan's flat, water-rich agricultural plain.

四維國小服務屏東縣萬丹鄉四維村的學童。作為這座屏東人口最多鄉鎮裡的社區型國小，學校在萬丹平坦而水源豐沛的農業平原上，扎根於地方居民的日常生活之中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Village Anchor School

村落核心學校

Siwei Elementary serves as the neighborhood school for Siwei Village, giving local families a nearby place for their children to learn and grow.

🌾 Part of Wandan's Farming Plain

萬丹農業平原一隅

The school sits within Wandan Township's flat, water-abundant agricultural plain, an area shaped by generations of rice, lotus, and red-bean farming.

🏘️ Rooted in Community Life

深植社區生活

As one of several elementary schools serving Wandan's villages, Siwei Elementary plays a steady, everyday role in the life of its surrounding community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Wandan, land of lotus and red beans.

🪷

Wandan Township is Pingtung's most populous rural township, known across Taiwan for its lotus-root and lotus-seed cultivation in flat, water-abundant fields, as well as its status as the island's “hometown of red beans.” Students at Siwei Elementary grow up surrounded by these wetland harvests, a defining feature of the township's rural character.
