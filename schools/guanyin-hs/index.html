Guanyin Senior High School

桃園市立觀音高級中等學校

Guanyin District's only complete public high school, offering both academic and vocational tracks to local junior-high graduates since it became a complete middle school in 2012.

觀音區唯一的完全中學，自民國101年（2012年）改制以來，同時提供學術與技職課程，讓在地國中畢業生就近升學。

About · 學校簡介

From a branch campus to Guanyin's complete high school.

Guanyin Senior High traces back to 1955, when it opened as the Guanyin branch of Yangmei Middle School. It became an independent junior high in 1968, and in 2012 it was upgraded into a full “complete middle school” (完全中學) spanning both junior-high and senior-high divisions under the name Taoyuan County Guanyin Senior High School. The school was renamed municipal in 2014 when Taoyuan became a special municipality, and adopted its current full name in 2018. As of 2017 it enrolled roughly 894 senior-high students across 27 classes and 762 junior-high students across 24 classes, making it a sizable anchor campus for secondary education in Guanyin.

觀音高中的歷史可追溯至民國44年（1955年），創校時為楊梅中學觀音分部，民國57年（1968年）獨立為觀音國中，民國101年（2012年）改制為兼具國中部與高中部的完全中學，定名「桃園縣立觀音高級中學」。民國103年（2014年）桃園升格直轄市後更名市立，民國107年（2018年）確立現今全名。截至2017年，全校高中部約894名學生（27班）、國中部約762名學生（24班），是觀音區規模可觀的中等教育重鎮。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 Academic and Vocational Tracks

學術與技職並行

Alongside general academic studies, the school runs vocational departments in Chemical Engineering (化工科) and Multimedia Animation (多媒體動畫科), plus a dedicated Physical Education program.

🏫 A Complete Middle School Since 2012

2012年改制完全中學

The 2012 upgrade combined junior-high and senior-high divisions under one roof, and by 2017 the campus enrolled nearly 1,700 students across both divisions.

🚌 Guanyin's Local High School Option

觀音在地升學選擇

As the only complete public high school serving the district, it gives Guanyin's junior-high graduates a “just around the corner” option to continue their education without commuting into Taoyuan City proper.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring education on Taoyuan's coast.

🌊

As the only complete public high school serving Guanyin's coastal farming and industrial communities, the school anchors secondary education in a district otherwise best known for its historic Guanyin Temple, offshore wind turbines, and protected algal reef coastline.
