About · 學校簡介

The historic center of Yanpu Township schooling.

Yanpu Elementary School sits at the center of Yanpu Township, an agricultural plain community also home to Tajen University. The school predates 1943, having served as the administrative parent school for a branch class that became Zhenxing Elementary School upon its independence in 1944. Today it enrolls around 216 students with 22 teachers.

鹽埔國小位於鹽埔鄉中心，該鄉為涵蓋大仁科技大學的農業平原地帶。學校歷史早於1943年，曾是振興國小前身分班的母校，直到該分班於1944年獨立設校。目前全校約有216名學生與22位教師。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Southern Taiwan's flower capital.

🌸

Yanpu Elementary sits in the heart of an agricultural plain community home to Tajen University, known for its mango, wax apple, jujube, and flower cultivation exported regionally. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
