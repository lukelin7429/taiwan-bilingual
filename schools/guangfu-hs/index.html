New Taipei Municipal Guangfu Senior High School

新北市立光復高級中學

A comprehensive senior high near Guangfu Bridge in Banqiao, home to a national-champion mallet ball team.

About · 學校簡介

From junior high to comprehensive senior high.

Guangfu Senior High School opened in 1999 as Guangfu Junior High School and was upgraded in 2013 to a comprehensive senior high school with its own junior high division. Today it enrolls around 1,368 students taught by roughly 125 teachers. Senior-division students choose among four tracks starting in their second year: social studies, natural science, multimedia design, and computer animation. The school is also known for its mallet ball (木球) team, founded in 2005 and a repeat national champion, and a shooting team established in 2016.

光復高中於民國88年（1999年）以光復國中之姿創立，民國102年（2013年）升格為附設國中部的綜合高中。目前全校約有1,368名學生、125位教師。高中部學生自二年級起可選擇社會學程、自然學程、多媒體設計學程或電腦動畫學程。學校木球隊創立於2005年，屢獲全國冠軍，另於2016年成立射擊隊。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏐 National Champions in Mallet Ball

木球全國冠軍

Guangfu's mallet ball (木球) team, founded in 2005, has repeatedly claimed national championship titles — a signature athletic strength of the school.

🎯 A Rising Shooting Program

2016年成立射擊隊

The school established a shooting team in 2016, adding to its growing roster of competitive athletics programs.

🎨 Choose-Your-Path Curriculum

四大分流學程

Starting in their second year, senior-division students choose among social studies, natural science, multimedia design, or computer animation tracks.

🌉 Named for Taiwan's Retrocession

光復意涵

The school's name — “光復” (Retrocession) — and its location near Guangfu Bridge in the historic Puqian (埔墘) area tie it to Taiwan's post-1945 return to Chinese rule.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Banqiao's Puqian neighborhood.

🌉

Located near Guangfu Bridge in the traditional Puqian (埔墘) area of Banqiao, the school's name — “光復,” meaning Retrocession — ties it to Taiwan's post-1945 return to Chinese rule. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
