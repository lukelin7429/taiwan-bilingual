Songlin Elementary School

松林國民小學

A Ministry of Education-designated remote school amid sesame fields, celebrated nationally for its reading programs, treehouse, and hidden slide — and for growing enrollment against the trend.

教育部認定之偏遠國小，位於胡麻田間，以閱讀推廣、樹屋與隱藏溜滑梯聞名全國，並在少子化中逆勢成長。

About · 學校簡介

A remote sesame-field school known for its love of reading.

Songlin Elementary's founding year is best documented as approximate — around 1973, inferred from press coverage of its 50th-anniversary celebration in 2023, since the school's own site did not surface an explicit founding date in this research pass. The school is officially designated a "remote school" (偏遠國小) by the Ministry of Education, sitting amid sesame (胡麻) fields with no public bus access. It joined CommonWealth Magazine's "Hope Reading" (希望閱讀) rural-literacy alliance in 2006 and, in 2008, became home to the sixth "Yushan Library" nationwide — the first in what was then Tainan County — funded by E.Sun Bank. It went on to win the Ministry of Education's "Reading Cornerstone School Award" (閱讀磐石學校獎) in 2011.

松林國小的創校年份屬於推估性質：約為民國62年（1973年），係根據2023年前後其50週年校慶相關報導推估，官網「沿革」頁面於本次查證中並未提供明確創校日期，故應視為約略估計。學校為教育部認定之「偏遠國小」，坐落於胡麻田間，無公車可達。2006年加入天下雜誌「希望閱讀」偏鄉閱讀推廣聯盟，2008年獲統一集團旗下玉山銀行贊助，成為全國第六座「玉山圖書館」，亦是當時臺南縣第一座。2011年更榮獲教育部「閱讀磐石學校獎」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📚 A National Reading Cornerstone School

閱讀磐石學校獎

The school joined the "Hope Reading" rural-literacy alliance in 2006, opened the 6th Yushan Library nationwide in 2008, and won the Ministry of Education's Reading Cornerstone School Award in 2011.

🌳 A Treehouse and a Hidden Sky Slide

樹屋與隱藏溜滑梯

The campus is known locally for a large treehouse and a hidden "sky slide," drawing family day-trippers from beyond its own school district.

📈 Growing Against the Demographic Tide

逆勢成長的偏遠小校

Despite rural depopulation, enrollment has grown, with nearly two-thirds of its students commuting in from outside its official catchment area.

🌱 Officially a Remote School

教育部認定偏遠國小

Sitting amid sesame fields with no public bus access, Songlin Elementary is officially designated a "remote school" by the Ministry of Education.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xigang, home of the triennial King Boat Festival.

🌾

A showcase example of a small Xigang farm-village school reversing rural enrollment decline through reading programs and destination-style campus design, Songlin Elementary sits amid the sesame-field surroundings typical of the district's farming interior. Xigang as a whole remains famed for its triennial Xigang Wangye's Boat Festival (西港刈香/王船祭), centered on Qing'an Temple (慶安宮). See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
