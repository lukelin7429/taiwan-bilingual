Zhongshan Elementary School

中山國小

A hillside elementary school founded in 1957 in a citrus-growing community on the foothill road toward Alishan.

About · 學校簡介

From a branch school to an independent hillside institution.

Zhongshan Elementary School became independent on November 4, 1957, when Zhongpu Elementary School's Shixia Branch was converted into its own branch and then spun off as Zhongshan Elementary. In 1962, the Shixia Branch briefly became independent as Shixia Elementary before further administrative reorganization. Today the school runs an attached kindergarten, teaches online via Google Meet and platforms like Learnmode, Junyi Academy, and PaGamO, and maintains an indigenous/native language education program alongside a student honor roll recognizing achievements in music, English, and academic competitions.

中山國小於民國46年（1957年）11月4日獨立設校，源於中埔國小石硦分校的改制與分出。民國51年（1962年），石硦分校曾短暫獨立為石硦國小，後續再經行政調整。學校現設有附設幼兒園，並透過Google Meet進行線上教學，運用因材網、均一教育平台、PaGamO等數位學習平台，同時推動原住民族語教育，並設有「中山風雲榜」表揚音樂、英語與學科競賽的優秀學生。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💻 Digital-First Learning

線上教學與數位平台

Online teaching via Google Meet, alongside platforms like Learnmode, Junyi Academy (均一), and PaGamO, gives students varied digital learning tools.

🏆 The Zhongshan Honor Roll

中山風雲榜

A student honor roll called "中山風雲榜" recognizes winners in music, English, and academic competitions, celebrating achievement across disciplines.

🌏 Indigenous Language Education

原住民族語教育

Indigenous and native-language education initiatives reflect the school's commitment to Taiwan's multicultural and multilingual heritage.

👶 Attached Kindergarten

附設幼兒園

An attached kindergarten allows the school to serve the youngest children of the Shixia community before they enter elementary grades.
