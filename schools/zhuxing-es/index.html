About · 學校簡介

A school built around nurturing talent.

Zhuxing Elementary School serves the Zhuxing community in Zhunan Township. The school offers a non-categorical gifted education program (不分類資優教育), which identifies and supports academically talented students across subject areas rather than limiting gifted education to a single discipline. It also maintains a dedicated music class (音樂班) program, giving students structured opportunities to develop musical skill alongside their core academic studies.

竹興國小服務竹南鎮竹興地區的學童。學校設有不分類資優教育班，跳脫單一學科限制，發掘並支持各領域表現優異的學生。另設有音樂班，提供學生系統化的音樂學習機會，與一般學科課程並行發展。
