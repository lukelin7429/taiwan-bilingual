About · 學校簡介

A village school with a famous alumna.

Shetuan Elementary was established in March 1958 as a branch school and became independent the following year, named for the nearby Shetuan New Village, a military-dependents' community that needed its own school. The school celebrated its 60th anniversary in 2019 with alumni events, and counts among its notable graduates the actress Brigitte Lin (林青霞) and TV personality Hsu Nai-lin. Designated a remote-area school, it runs an environmental education center themed around the Chu-Lo tree frog and follows the motto "Reading, Music, Information, Vitality."

社團國小於民國47年（1958年）3月設立分校，隔年（民國48年）獨立設校，校名源自鄰近的軍眷社區「社團新村」。學校於2019年舉辦六十週年校慶及校友活動，知名校友包括影星林青霞與電視名人徐乃麟。學校被列為偏遠地區學校，設有以諸羅樹蛙為主題的生態教育中心，校訓為「閱讀音樂資訊活力」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌟 A Famous Alumna

知名校友

Actress Brigitte Lin (林青霞) and TV personality Hsu Nai-lin both attended Shetuan Elementary, giving this small rural school an outsized place in Taiwanese popular culture.

🐸 Chu-Lo Tree Frog Ecology Center

諸羅樹蛙生態教育

An environmental education program built around the Chu-Lo tree frog, a species native to this part of Chiayi County, anchors the school's science curriculum.

🥇 National Folk Sports Honors

全國民俗體育競賽獲獎

At the 2026 National Folk Sports Festival, the school won two special commendations, two excellence awards, and three first-class awards.

🏘️ Roots in a Military Village

軍眷村的起源

The school grew directly out of the Shetuan New Village community in the 1950s–60s, and its motto — "Reading, Music, Information, Vitality" — still guides campus life today.
