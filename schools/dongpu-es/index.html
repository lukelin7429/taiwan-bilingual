About · 學校簡介

A school at the gateway to Taiwan's tallest peak.

Dongpu Elementary School is located in Dongpu Village, a well-known hot springs and hiking gateway that serves as the main trailhead for one of Yushan (Jade Mountain) National Park's most popular routes. The school directly serves the local Bunun tribal community at the mountain's foot, and it maintains a dedicated "Dongpu Tribe" section on its website that highlights local Bunun culture, alongside a broader indigenous-language education website supporting tribal language learning.

東埔國小位於東埔村，這是一處知名的溫泉與登山門戶聚落，同時是玉山國家公園熱門登山步道的主要起點。學校直接服務玉山山腳下的布農部落社群，並在校網設有專屬的「東埔部落」專區，介紹在地布農文化，同時也建置原住民語教育網站，支持族語學習的持續推展。
