About · 學校簡介

A school that helped its neighbors get started.

Lixing Elementary was founded in March 1941, during the Japanese colonial era, as "Yilan City North National School" (宜蘭市北國民學校). It was renamed Taipei County Yilan City Lixing National School in 1946, then Yilan County Yilan City Lixing National School after Yilan became its own county in 1950, and finally took its current name in 1968. The school has a quiet but notable role in local education history: when Yilan Junior High School was first getting organized in 1956, its earliest classes were held at Lixing's campus before that school found its own independent footing.

力行國小創立於1941年（日治時期）3月，初名「宜蘭市北國民學校」。1946年更名為台北縣宜蘭市力行國民學校，1950年宜蘭縣獨立設縣後改為宜蘭縣宜蘭市力行國民學校，1968年始定名為現名。學校在地方教育史上扮演過重要角色：1956年宜蘭國中草創初期，最早的班級便是借用力行國小的校舍上課，直到後來獨立設校。
