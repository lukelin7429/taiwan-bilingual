Tatung Junior High School

大同國民中學

Founded in 1982 as Yuanlin's third junior high to keep pace with the city's booming commerce and industry — now the largest school in Changhua County.

About · 學校簡介

Built for a booming city.

Tatung Junior High was established on August 31, 1982, as Yuanlin's third junior high school, at a time when the city's existing two junior highs could no longer accommodate its rapidly growing population, driven by local commerce and industry. It opened under first principal Tu Chun-mu with just 15 classes and 729 students. Its first campus building, nicknamed "Chunfeng Lou" (春風樓, "Spring Breeze Hall"), was completed in November 1982, and students fully moved in by July 1983. Today Tatung has grown into the largest school in Changhua County, with nearly 70 classes and about 2,000 students and staff.

大同國中創立於民國71年（1982年）8月31日，是因應員林工商業蓬勃發展、原有兩所國中已無法容納快速成長人口而設立的第三所國中。首任校長為杜春木，創校初期僅15班、729名學生。第一棟校舍「春風樓」於民國71年（1982年）11月完工，學生於民國72年（1983年）7月全數遷入。如今大同國中已成長為彰化縣規模最大的學校，近70班、約2,000名師生。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏆 The Largest School in the County

全縣規模最大

With nearly 70 classes and roughly 2,000 students and staff, Tatung has grown from a modest 15-class start into the biggest school in all of Changhua County.

🏭 Born of a Boom Town

工商蓬勃下的產物

The school exists because Yuanlin's late-1970s commercial and industrial growth outpaced the capacity of its first two junior highs — a direct product of the city's economic rise.

🏢 Spring Breeze Hall

春風樓

The school's very first campus building, poetically named "Chunfeng Lou" (Spring Breeze Hall), was completed within months of the school's founding in 1982.

📈 From 729 to Nearly 2,000

從729人到近2,000人

Tatung's growth from 729 founding students to a present-day community of roughly 2,000 traces the broader growth story of Yuanlin City itself.
