About · 學校簡介

From a borrowed classroom to a national vocational school.

National Keelung Commercial & Industrial Vocational High School traces its roots to 1949, when it opened as a junior commercial vocational school borrowing classroom space from neighboring Xinyi Elementary School. In 1968, the school relocated to its permanent campus in Qidu District and added industrial programs in electrical and electronics engineering alongside its original business curriculum. It was elevated to national status in 2000, and today it runs both comprehensive-high and vocational tracks spanning business management, design, and electrical/electronic engineering.

國立基隆高級商工職業學校創校於民國38年（1949年），最初為初級商業職業學校，借用信義國小教室上課。民國57年（1968年）遷至七堵區現址，並增設電機電子等工業類科，與原有的商業類科並行發展。民國89年（2000年）改制升格為國立學校。現今同時設有綜合高中與職業類科，涵蓋商業經營、設計與電機電子工程等領域。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚉 A Campus Beside the Rails

緊鄰鐵道的校園

The school's Qidu campus sits beside the Qidu Railway Station and the Keelung River, placing it at the heart of a district shaped by rail and river transport feeding into Keelung's port economy.

💼 Business Roots, Since 1949

創校即為商業職校

Founded as a junior commercial vocational school in 1949, the school has carried a business-education identity for over seven decades, long before industrial programs were added.

⚡ Electrical & Electronics Since 1968

電機電子類科自民國57年設立

When the school moved to its permanent Qidu campus in 1968, it added electrical and electronics engineering programs, broadening beyond its original commercial curriculum.

🎓 Comprehensive High School Tracks

綜合高中學程

Today the school offers both comprehensive-high and vocational tracks across business management, design, and electrical/electronic engineering groups, giving students flexibility in choosing a path.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Qidu, a rail and river junction feeding Keelung's port.

🚆

Qidu District's identity is defined by its history as a rail and transport junction — the Qidu Railway Station and the Keelung River have long channeled goods and people toward Keelung's harbor, distinct from the coastal fishing culture found in the city's other districts.
