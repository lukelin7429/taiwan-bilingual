About · 學校簡介

From satellite campus to independent school.

Hsinfeng Junior High School originated in 1956 as a satellite campus of Hukou Junior High School, established to serve students in Xinfeng Township without requiring a long commute. The school gained full independence the following year, in 1957, and was renamed in 1968 to reflect its permanent status. In recent years, students have achieved strong results in entrance examinations, and the school has become active in computational-thinking and digital-literacy programs, keeping pace with Taiwan's push toward technology-integrated education.

新豐國中創立於民國45年（1956年），初期為湖口國中的分校，設立目的是讓新豐鄉學生免於長途通勤。翌年（1957年）即獨立設校，並於民國57年（1968年）更名，確立其永久校名地位。近年來，學生在升學考試中表現優異，學校也積極投入運算思維與數位素養教育，跟上台灣科技融入教育的腳步。
