About · 學校簡介

Over 130 years at the civic heart of Miaoli.

Jiangong Elementary traces its roots to 1896, when it was founded as the "Miaoli Kokugo Denshūjo" (Japanese-language training institute) during the early years of Japanese colonial rule — making it the oldest modern school in the Miaoli area, with more than 130 years of continuous history. The campus sits on the former site of the Qing-dynasty Miaoli county government yamen, linking the school physically to the county's pre-modern administrative history. It was renamed 建功國民學校 in 1947 after Taiwan's retrocession, and took its current name, 建功國民小學, in 1968. Today the school maintains a dedicated history and culture museum that preserves this long institutional memory for students and visitors alike.

建功國小創校於1896年（明治29年），前身為「苗栗國語傳習所」，是苗栗地區歷史最悠久的現代學校，至今已有超過130年的校史。校園座落於清代苗栗縣衙舊址，與地方前現代行政史緊密相連。1947年臺灣光復後更名為建功國民學校，1968年改為現名建功國民小學。學校目前設有專屬的校史文化館，保存並展示這段悠久的辦學歷程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Taiwan's Oldest School in Miaoli

苗栗地區最古老的學校

Founded in 1896 as a Japanese-language training institute, Jiangong is the oldest modern school in the Miaoli area, with a continuous history spanning more than 130 years.

👑 Built on the Old County Yamen Site

清代縣衙舊址建校

The campus occupies the site of the former Qing-dynasty Miaoli county government office, physically connecting the school to the region's administrative history before modern Taiwan.

🏺 A Dedicated School History Museum

校史文化館

Jiangong maintains its own history and culture museum, preserving artifacts and records that trace the school's — and the county town's — transformation over the past century.

🏫 From Language Institute to Modern School

從國語傳習所到現代國小

Renamed 建功國民學校 after Taiwan's 1945 retrocession and again as 建功國民小學 in 1968, the school's several name changes trace Taiwan's own 20th-century political transitions.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Miaoli's civic and educational cornerstone.

🏯

As Miaoli's very first modern school, built directly on the old county-government site, Jiangong Elementary embodies the civic heart of this Hakka-majority county town — a living link between the Qing-era administrative center and the modern school system that grew up around it.
