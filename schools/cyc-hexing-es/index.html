Hexing Elementary School

和興國小

One of the newest schools in Zhongpu Township, founded in 1997, now known for its drone education program and a national runner-up basketball team.

About · 學校簡介

A young school with a modern edge.

Hexing Elementary School is one of the newest schools in this batch, established on August 1, 1997 as Chiayi County Zhongpu Township Hexing Elementary School, with Xu Ruishan as its first principal. Despite its relatively young age, the school has built a distinctive profile: an orchestra and string ensemble program that has achieved Grade A in county music competitions, a notable drone/UAV education program with students competing in drone soccer and drone maintenance competitions, and a basketball team whose U12 boys' MINI division finished as national championship runner-up. English instruction includes remote video lessons with foreign teachers.

和興國小是本批次中較為年輕的學校，創立於民國86年（1997年）8月1日，定名嘉義縣中埔鄉和興國民小學，首任校長為許瑞山。雖然創校歷史不長，學校已發展出鮮明特色：管弦樂團在縣內音樂比賽中榮獲甲等，並開設引人矚目的無人機教育課程，學生參與無人機足球與無人機維修競賽；籃球隊U12男子組MINI級更曾於全國賽獲得亞軍。英語教學則包含與外籍教師的遠距視訊課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚁 Drone Education Program

無人機教育

A notable drone/UAV education program has students competing in drone soccer and drone maintenance competitions, a distinctive STEM offering for an elementary school.

🎻 A Grade-A Orchestra

管弦樂團甲等佳績

The school's orchestra and string ensemble program achieved Grade A (甲等) in county music competitions, reflecting strong arts education.

🏀 National Basketball Runner-Up

全國籃球賽亞軍

The school's U12 boys' MINI division basketball team finished as runner-up at a national championship, a standout athletic achievement for a young school.

🗣️ Remote English with Foreign Teachers

外師遠距英語教學

English instruction includes remote video teaching with foreign teachers, giving students exposure to native-speaking instruction.
