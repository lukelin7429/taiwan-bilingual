About · 學校簡介

The neighborhood school of Taipei's skyline.

Xinyi Elementary School sits on Songqin Street, a short walk from Taipei 101/World Trade Center MRT Station's Exit 3 — placing it among the elementary schools closest to the Taipei 101 tower itself. Of all the schools in Xinyi District, its catchment area lies most directly within the modern Xinyi Planning District, the master-planned financial and shopping core that gives the whole district its contemporary identity.

信義國小座落於松勤街，鄰近台北101/世貿站3號出口，是信義區內距離台北101大樓最近的國小之一。在信義區所有學校中，其學區最直接座落於信義計畫區核心地帶——這片規劃完善的金融與購物中心，正是整個信義區當代形象的代表。

Local Culture · 在地文化

The literal heart of modern Xinyi.

🏙️

Of all 16 schools researched in this district, Xinyi Elementary sits closest to Taipei 101 and the MRT station bearing its name. Its student catchment area is the literal heart of the modern Xinyi financial and shopping district — the same skyline of skyscrapers and malls that gives the whole district its contemporary identity, in a spot that just decades ago was a much quieter, semi-rural edge of the city.
