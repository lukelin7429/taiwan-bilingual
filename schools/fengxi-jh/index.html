Fongxi Junior High School

About · 學校簡介

A directional name from Fongshan's old county seat.

Fongxi Junior High was approved for establishment on December 19, 1970, and later became a Kaohsiung municipal school following the 2010 city-county merger. Its name, "鳳西" ("west of Fongshan"), is a directional place name relative to the historic Fongshan county seat, part of a broader pattern of compass-point school names found throughout the district.

鳳西國中於民國59年（1970年）12月19日奉准設立，2010年高雄縣市合併後改為市立學校。校名「鳳西」意指「鳳山以西」，是相對於鳳山舊縣治的方位地名，也是鳳山地區以方位命名學校的常見模式之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🧭 A Directional Name

以方位命名

"鳳西" means "west of Fongshan," marking the school's position relative to the historic Fongshan county seat.

📅 A 1970 Founding

1970年創校

Approved on December 19, 1970, Fongxi Junior High is among the older junior highs in the Fongshan area, predating the district's major postwar growth spurts.

🏛️ Municipal Since the 2010 Merger

2010年改制市立

Like many former Kaohsiung County schools, Fongxi Junior High became a Kaohsiung municipal institution after the 2010 city-county merger.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fongshan's compass-point place names.

"鳳西" ("west of Fongshan") is a directional place name relative to the old Fongshan county seat — a naming convention that helps residents orient themselves within the district's many sub-communities. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
