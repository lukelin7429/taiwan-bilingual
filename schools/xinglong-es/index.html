About · 學校簡介

A young school with an international stage.

Xinglong Elementary opened in 2011 in Zhubei City and has since built one of the area's most decorated music programs. Its recorder band won first place at the Vienna International Youth Music Festival in 2023, and the school operates multiple award-winning ensembles — recorder band, string orchestra, and choir — alongside an active culture of science exhibitions, robotics, and Scratch programming competitions. Students also compete in badminton, tee-ball, soccer, table tennis, and track and field.

興隆國小於2011年在竹北市成立，此後發展出區內數一數二的音樂教育成果。學校直笛隊於2023年榮獲維也納國際青少年音樂節冠軍，並經營多個屢獲佳績的社團——直笛隊、絃樂團與合唱團，同時積極參與科展、機器人與Scratch程式設計競賽。學生也在羽球、樂樂棒球、足球、桌球與田徑等項目上參與競技。
