Taipei Municipal Fanghe Experimental Middle School

臺北市立芳和實驗中學

Taipei's first publicly run experimental complete school, built on Wolong Street near Da'an Forest Park around a curriculum of exploration, cross-disciplinary learning, and expeditions.

全台北市第一所公辦完全實驗中學，校址位於臥龍街、鄰近大安森林公園，課程以「探索．跨域．遠征」為核心。

About · 學校簡介

Taipei's first public experimental complete school.

Fanghe's campus opened in August 1976 as Taipei Municipal Fanghe Junior High School, on a site vacated when Minzu Junior High relocated to Roosevelt Road. In the 2018 school year it was converted into an experimental junior high, and in August 2021 it added a senior-high division and took its present name, becoming Taipei's first publicly run experimental “complete school” spanning both junior and senior grades. Its curriculum is built around three ideas — exploration, cross-disciplinary learning, and expeditions (探索．跨域．遠征).

芳和的校舍於民國65年（1976年）8月啟用，初名「臺北市立芳和國民中學」，校地為民族國中遷往羅斯福路後所留下。107學年度改制為實驗國中，民國110年（2021年）8月增設高中部並更名為「芳和實驗中學」，成為全台北市第一所公辦完全實驗中學，課程以「探索．跨域．遠征」為核心理念。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🧭 Taipei's First Public Experimental Complete School

全市第一所公辦完全實驗中學

Fanghe was Taipei's first publicly run experimental school to span both junior and senior high, a closely watched pilot in public alternative education.

🗺️ Exploration, Cross-Disciplinary Learning, Expeditions

探索．跨域．遠征

The school's curriculum philosophy is built around these three pillars, encouraging students to move beyond a single-subject classroom model.

🏫 A Campus with a Prior Life

校地的前世今生

Fanghe's site originally belonged to Minzu Junior High, which relocated to Roosevelt Road in 1976, opening the way for Fanghe's own campus to open that same year.

🌳 Steps from Da'an Forest Park

鄰近大安森林公園

Located on Wolong Street, the school sits close enough to Da'an Forest Park to fold the park into everyday school life.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A testing ground for progressive education.

🌳

Sitting on Wolong Street a short walk from Da'an Forest Park, Fanghe's transformation into Taipei's flagship public experimental school reflects Da'an's role as a testing ground for progressive education — driven by a concentration of university-educated, reform-minded parents unmatched almost anywhere else in the city.
