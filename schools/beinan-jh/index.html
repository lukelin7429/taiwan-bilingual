About · 學校簡介

Not to be confused with Beinan Township — this is Taitung City's own Peinan.

Peinan (Beinan) Junior High School opened in 1962 as "卑南初級中學" and took its current name in 1968. Despite the shared name, this school sits within Taitung City itself, on the south side of Nanwang Mountain near the Peinan Cultural Park — it is a distinct institution from the schools of Beinan Township, a separate rural township elsewhere in Taitung County. The school's baseball team made history in 1971 by winning the championship of Taiwan's first-ever National Junior High School Baseball League, an early landmark in Taitung's now-celebrated baseball tradition.

卑南國中創校於1962年（民國51年），初名「卑南初級中學」，1968年更為現名。雖然名稱相同，本校位於臺東市市區內，座落於南王山南側、卑南文化公園附近——與臺東縣卑南鄉（另一獨立鄉鎮）的學校並非同一所。學校棒球隊於1971年寫下歷史，奪得全國第一屆國中棒球聯賽冠軍，是臺東棒球傳統早期的重要里程碑。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚾ Taiwan's First National Baseball Champions

全國第一屆國中棒球聯賽冠軍

In 1971, the school's baseball team won the very first National Junior High School Baseball League championship — an early landmark in Taitung County's storied baseball history.

🏛️ Neighbor to an Ancient Site

緊鄰卑南文化公園

The campus sits near the Peinan Cultural Park, home to one of the largest Neolithic slate-coffin burial sites in the circum-Pacific region — placing the school within one of Taitung's most significant archaeological landscapes.

📍 A Name Worth Clarifying

位於臺東市，非卑南鄉

Though it shares its name with Taitung County's separate Beinan Township, this school is located within Taitung City proper — a distinction worth knowing when navigating the county's schools.
