Lixing Elementary School

力行國民小學

Named for the road at its original gate, this school split off from a neighboring elementary as East District's population expanded.

About · 學校簡介

Named for the road at its gate.

Lixing Elementary began in 1955 as a branch campus of Chenggong Elementary School, becoming independent in 1957 as East District's population expanded in the postwar decade. The school takes its name from Lixing Road, where its school gate was originally located — a naming pattern that ties the institution directly to its immediate neighborhood.

力行國小前身為民國44年（1955年）設立的成功國小分班，民國46年（1957年）獨立設校，正值東區人口在戰後十年間快速擴張的時期。校名取自最初校門所在的力行路，直接反映了學校與周邊社區的緊密連結。
