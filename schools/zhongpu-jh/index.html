Zhongpu Junior High School

中埔國中

A public junior high tracing back to 1957, marking six decades of service at the market center of Chiayi County's bamboo-shoot and citrus farming heartland.

About · 學校簡介

A market-town school with a long civic history.

Zhongpu Junior High School traces back to 1956–1957, when it began as the Zhongpu Branch of Chiayi County Middle School before being renamed Zhongpu Junior High School in 1957. It became a full junior high school under Taiwan's nine-year compulsory education system in 1968, and has since celebrated a 60th-anniversary sports meet marking its long institutional history. Located in Zhongpu Village, the administrative and market center of the township, the school today runs technical/vocational skills programs, active reading promotion initiatives, and bilingual education programs for the surrounding farming community.

中埔國中源起於民國45、46年間，初為嘉義縣立中學中埔分部，後於民國46年（1957年）更名為嘉義縣立中埔初級中學；民國57年（1968年）九年國教實施後，改制為完全國中。學校曾舉辦建校六十週年運動會，見證其悠久的辦學歷史。校址位於中埔鄉行政與市集中心的中埔村，現今推動技藝教育、晨讀活動與雙語教育，服務周邊農業社區。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛠️ Technical Skills Education

技藝教育與競賽

The school runs a technical/vocational skills education program with annual inter-school competitions, giving students hands-on career exploration alongside standard academics.

📖 A Culture of Reading

晨讀與閱讀推廣

An active reading promotion program includes MSSR morning silent reading and reading comprehension contests, building literacy habits across the student body.

🗣️ Bilingual Education Push

雙語教育與英語競賽

Bilingual education initiatives and English proficiency competitions reflect the school's commitment to preparing students for Taiwan's bilingual future.

🎉 Six Decades of History

建校一甲子

A 60th-anniversary sports meet celebrated the school's long institutional history, tracing back to its founding in the 1950s.

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the heart of Zhongpu's farming country.

🎋

Located in Zhongpu Village, the administrative and market center of Zhongpu Township, the school sits at the heart of an agricultural township famous for its bamboo shoots (麻竹筍) and citrus farming, just southeast of Chiayi City. See the Chiayi County page for more on the region.
