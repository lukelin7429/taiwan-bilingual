Xiaogang Senior High School

小港高級中學

A public senior high school built for a district reshaped by land reclamation — land that gave Xiaogang, “small harbor,” its name.

座落於因填海造陸而重塑地貌的小港區，公立高中，「小港」之名源於填海前的一灣潮汛港澳。

About · 學校簡介

A young school for a fast-growing district.

Xiaogang Senior High was approved for establishment in 1990 and formally founded in 1992, welcoming its first freshman class that August. The campus was built out in phases through 1998, and in 2010 students founded their own baseball team — sports culture growing alongside the school's build-out.

小港高中於民國79年（1990年）核准設立，民國81年（1992年）正式創校，同年8月迎來第一屆新生。校園歷經多階段擴建，至民國87年（1998年）完成，民國99年（2010年）學生自組棒球隊，為建校歷程增添運動色彩。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A School Built in Phases

分階段建成的校園

The campus expanded steadily from its 1992 founding through 1998, growing to meet the needs of a rapidly developing district around Kaohsiung's airport and harbor.

⚾ A Student-Founded Baseball Team

學生自組棒球隊

In 2010, Xiaogang's own students organized a baseball team — grassroots athletics growing out of the school rather than handed down from above.

⚓ A Name from the Sea

源於海洋的地名

“小港” (“small harbor”) recalls the tidal inlet that once cut into this stretch of coastline, long before land reclamation turned it into the site of Kaohsiung's airport and seaport.

🎓 A Young School for a Young District

年輕校區的年輕學校

Established only in 1992, the school is itself a product of Xiaogang's rapid growth after the former township was annexed into Kaohsiung City.

Local Culture · 在地文化

From tidal inlet to airport and seaport.

⚓

Xiaogang Senior High takes its district identity from “小港” (“small harbor”), the tidal inlet that once cut into this stretch of coast — long since reshaped by land reclamation into the site of Kaohsiung's international airport and seaport. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
