About · 學校簡介

A century-old school with roots in the colonial era.

Taiping Elementary School traces its origins to April 1, 1920, during the Japanese colonial era, when it opened as “小港公學校大坪頂分教場,” a branch classroom of Xiaogang Public School. It became an independent school in 1937 under the name “太平公學校.” The school has been renamed several times over the following decades as Taiwan's administrative eras changed, and it marked its full centennial with a commemorative volume celebrating a century of local schooling. Its name, “太平” (“great peace”), is among the most common auspicious names given to Taiwanese schools.

太平國小創校可追溯至1920年（日治大正9年）4月1日，最初為「小港公學校大坪頂分教場」，是小港公學校的分教場。1937年（昭和12年）獨立設校，改稱「太平公學校」。其後隨著台灣歷經不同時代，校名數度更迭，並曾出版建校百年紀念專書，慶祝這所百年老校的地方教育史。校名「太平」是台灣學校最常見的吉祥校名之一。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in the Japanese colonial school system.

🏯

Taiping Elementary's 1920 origins as a 公學校 (public school) branch classroom reflect the Japanese colonial-era education system that established Taiwan's first modern schools for local children, decades before the district's postwar industrialization.
