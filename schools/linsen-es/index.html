Linsen Elementary School

林森國民小學

An 85-year-old school named for ROC statesman Lin Sen, known for its tree-lined "green tunnel" walkway along Linsen East Road.

建校85年、以國民政府主席林森為名的老校，以林森東路上綠意盎然的「綠色隧道」棚架聞名。

About · 學校簡介

A school that moved, and a name that stuck.

Linsen Elementary opened on April 1, 1939, as "Chiayi Shinko Public School," with 15 classes and 1,123 students, on a site now part of National Chiayi University's Linsen campus. It was renamed Linsen National School in December 1945 in honor of former ROC Chairman Lin Sen. In 1957, its original campus land was given to the newly formed Chiayi Normal School, and the school relocated to its current Linsen East Road site, moving into new buildings in 1959. It celebrated its 85th anniversary in November 2024.

林森國小創校於民國28年（1939年）4月1日，初名「嘉義新高公學校」，開校時有15班、1,123名學生，原址即今國立嘉義大學林森校區所在。民國34年（1945年）12月更名為林森國民學校，以紀念國民政府主席林森。民國46年（1957年），原校地撥交新設之嘉義師範學校使用，學校遷至現址林森東路，於民國48年（1959年）遷入新校舍。民國113年（2024年）11月，學校歡慶創校85週年。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎋 The Green Tunnel

綠色隧道棚架

A well-known tree-lined trellis walkway along Linsen East Road has become a community landmark and walking corridor beloved by neighbors, not just students.

🎖️ Named for a Republic of China Statesman

紀念林森主席

Renamed in 1945 to honor Lin Sen, a former Chairman of the National Government of the Republic of China, the school's name carries a direct link to modern Chinese history.

🎉 85 Years and Still Growing

建校85週年

Having relocated once already in its history, the school celebrated its 85th anniversary in November 2024 on its current Linsen East Road campus.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A shaded corridor woven into daily life.

🌳

The school maintains a well-known tree-lined "green tunnel" trellis along Linsen East Road that has become a community landmark and walking corridor, softening the busy road for residents throughout the neighborhood. See the Chiayi City page for more on the region.
