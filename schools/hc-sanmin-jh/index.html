About · 學校簡介

A school built on community-donated land.

Sanmin Junior High School opened on August 1, 1989, initially as a branch campus of Guanghua Junior High School before gaining independent status in 1991. The school was founded on land donated by local residents between Zhongyang, Ziyou, and Minzhu Roads — a site that had long been earmarked as a designated "junior high school site" for the neighborhood. This grassroots origin reflects the direct civic investment local families made in the district's education infrastructure.

三民國中創校於1989年（民國78年）8月1日，初期作為光華國中的分校，1991年（民國80年）獨立設校。學校校地由當地居民捐贈，位於中央路、自由路與民族路之間，這塊土地長期被劃定為社區的「國中預定地」。這樣的創校淵源，反映了當地居民對社區教育建設的直接投入與貢獻。
