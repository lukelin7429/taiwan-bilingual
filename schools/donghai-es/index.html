About · 學校簡介

An established school investing in its future.

Donghai Elementary opened on August 1, 1956, as a branch of Liujia National School, becoming independent in 1959 and adopting its current name in 1988. Today the school is undergoing a major new campus and activity-center construction project, with a budget of roughly NT$54 million, specifically to accommodate Zhubei's growing population. The newer campus building project broke ground with expected completion around August 2026.

東海國小於1956年8月1日以六家國校分校之姿創立，1959年獨立設校，1988年定名為現名。目前學校正進行一項大規模新校舍與活動中心興建工程，經費約新台幣5,400萬元，專為因應竹北人口持續成長而規劃。新校舍工程已動工，預計於2026年8月前後完工。
