Zhongpu Elementary School

中埔國小

A century-old elementary school founded in the Japanese colonial era, serving as the administrative anchor of Chiayi County's bamboo-shoot and citrus farming heartland.

創校於日治時期、已逾百年歷史的國小，是嘉義縣麻竹筍與柑橘產業核心——中埔鄉的行政重鎮。

About · 學校簡介

A century of schooling at Zhongpu's civic core.

Zhongpu Elementary School was founded during the Japanese colonial era, likely around 1914 to 1916, though its exact founding date is not precisely confirmed. The school celebrated its 100th anniversary and held its 71st graduation ceremony in 2016, consistent with a founding in the 1910s. Today the school runs about 12 classes, two per grade, and offers afternoon enrichment classes in English, board games, ukulele, and art, alongside a badminton training program and bilingual Mandarin/Taiwanese instruction. Video-conferencing classrooms connect students with remote and rural learning collaborations.

中埔國小創校於日治時期，約在民國3至5年間（1914至1916年），確切創校日期尚待考證。學校於民國105年（2016年）舉辦建校百週年慶並辦理第71屆畢業典禮，與1910年代創校的時間相符。目前學校每年級兩班，共約12班，開設英語、桌遊、烏克麗麗、美術等課後社團課程，並設有羽球隊訓練及國語、台語雙語教學，同時運用視訊教室與偏鄉學校進行遠距協作學習。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎨 Rich Afternoon Enrichment

多元課後社團

Afternoon enrichment classes span English, board games, ukulele, and art, giving students a well-rounded set of activities beyond the standard curriculum.

🏸 Badminton Training Program

羽球隊訓練

A dedicated badminton training program gives students a competitive sports outlet within the school.

🗣️ Mandarin-Taiwanese Bilingual Instruction

國台語雙語教學

Bilingual education in Mandarin and Taiwanese (Minnan) reflects the school's commitment to preserving local language alongside standard instruction.

💻 Video-Conferencing Classrooms

視訊教室遠距共學

Video-conferencing classrooms allow this school to collaborate remotely with other schools, expanding learning opportunities across its roughly 12 classes.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring Zhongpu's agricultural heartland.

🍊

Sitting at the administrative heart of Zhongpu Township, the gateway agricultural township between Chiayi City and the mountains, the school is embedded in a community known for bamboo shoot and citrus cultivation. See the Chiayi County page for more on the region.
