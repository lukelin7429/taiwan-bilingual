About · 學校簡介

Older than the university, older than the highway.

Fengli Elementary began in 1913 under Japanese rule as the "Hualien Port Prefecture Toyota Ordinary Higher Elementary School" — a school originally built exclusively for the children of Japanese settlers in the "Toyota" immigrant farming village (today's Fengtian/Fengli area). After Taiwan's 1945 retrocession the school was renamed, and it took its current name in 1968. Over 110 years old, it predates both National Dong Hwa University and today's mixed Amis and Han population that now calls the area home.

豐裡國小創立於民國2年（1913年）日治時期，初名「花蓮港廳豐田尋常高等小學校」——當時是專為「豐田」移民村（今豐田／豐裡一帶）日本移民子弟所設立的學校。1945年台灣光復後更名，並於民國57年（1968年）確立現名。逾百一十年的校史，早於國立東華大學的成立，也早於今日阿美族與漢人混居的社區樣貌。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A Japanese-era farming village's legacy.

🏯

Fengli's roots trace back to Toyota (豐田), one of several Japanese-era immigrant farming villages established across Hualien in the early 1900s. Its preserved historic auditorium is a rare physical trace of that era, standing now within Shoufeng Township's modern mixed community.
