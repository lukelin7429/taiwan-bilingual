About · 學校簡介

A school in the heart of old Anping.

Anping Elementary sits in the historic core of Anping District, amid the same streets and old settlement blocks that grew up around the Dutch-era trading post and Qing-era fishing port. The school runs an attached kindergarten and a campus meal program using domestically sourced pork and beef, and it offers immersive Taiwanese-language (台語) instruction alongside environmental education programs. In recent years it has been recognized for its professional learning communities and academic competition results.

安平國小座落於安平歷史核心區，周邊街道與舊聚落格局，正是荷治時期商館與清代漁港發展而來的老街廓。學校附設幼兒園，午餐採用國產豬肉與牛肉；並提供沉浸式台語教學與環境教育課程。近年來，學校在教師專業學習社群與學科競賽方面屢獲肯定。
