About · 學校簡介

A newer school on the Xinglong Road corridor.

Xinghua Elementary School was founded in August 1992, one of Wenshan's newer elementary schools, established to serve growth along Xinglong Road.

The school is designated one of Taipei's information-technology "重點" (focus) schools, and sits on Xinglong Road in the same corridor as Xingde and Xinglong elementary schools — part of a cluster of schools built to serve Wenshan's mid-to-late 20th century residential expansion.

興華國小創立於1992年（民國81年）8月，是文山區較晚設立的國小之一，為服務興隆路沿線人口成長而設立。

學校為臺北市資訊教育重點學校之一，校址位於興隆路，與興德國小、興隆國小同處一條路線，是文山區為因應二十世紀中後期住宅擴張而興建的學校聚落之一。
