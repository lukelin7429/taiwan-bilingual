Zhongxin Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school for a growing district.

Zhongxin Elementary School was founded in 1990 to serve a fast-growing residential neighborhood in Xinzhuang District. Detailed public records of the school's early history are limited, but like many of its neighbors, it opened during a period when Xinzhuang's population was expanding rapidly and new campuses were needed across the district. The school includes an attached kindergarten serving local families.

中信國小創立於民國79年（1990年），為服務新莊區內快速發展的住宅社區而設立。學校早期歷史的公開資料有限，但與周邊多所學校相同，其設立正值新莊人口快速成長、各地陸續增建校舍的時期。校內附設幼兒園，服務在地家庭。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Founded in a Growth Decade

誕生於人口成長年代

Zhongxin opened in 1990, part of a wave of new schools built across Xinzhuang as its population and housing stock expanded rapidly in the late 20th century.

🧒 An Attached Kindergarten

附設幼兒園

The school includes an attached kindergarten, extending its service to the youngest children in the neighborhood before they enter elementary grades.

🏘️ A Neighborhood School

服務在地社區

As a community elementary school, Zhongxin primarily serves families in its immediate residential catchment area in Xinzhuang District.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Xinzhuang's growth story.

🏙️

Zhongxin sits within Xinzhuang District, a town that grew from a Qing-dynasty river port on the old Danshui River trade route into one of New Taipei's most densely populated districts after decades of rapid residential and industrial development. Like much of the district, the neighborhood around the school reflects that late-20th-century growth. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
