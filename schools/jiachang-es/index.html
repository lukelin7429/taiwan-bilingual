About · 學校簡介

One of Nanzih's largest elementary schools.

Jiachang Elementary is today one of the larger elementary schools in Nanzih District, with about 71 classes — including 65 regular classes — roughly 105 teachers, and around 2,200 students. Its name, "Jiachang" (加昌), comes from the local Jiachang Road, a road name that, together with the neighboring Houjin and Youchang areas, forms part of the fabric of Nanzih's early agricultural settlements.

加昌國小現為楠梓區規模較大的國小之一，現有約71班（含普通班65班）、教師105位、學生約2,200人。校名「加昌」取自當地「加昌路」地名，該路名與鄰近的後勁、右昌等聚落共同構成楠梓早期農業聚落的紋理。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A road name from the old farming era.

🌾

Jiachang's name is drawn from the local road it sits on, a naming pattern it shares with nearby Houjin and Youchang — together these place names trace the agricultural settlement fabric of early Nanzih, long before the district's industrial growth. See the Kaohsiung county page for more.
