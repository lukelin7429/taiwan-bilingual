Hougang Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A century of relocations and name changes.

Hougang Elementary traces its roots to March 20, 1928, when it opened as "七股公學校後港分教場" under the Japanese colonial education system. It became an independent school, "後港公學校," in July 1935, and was renamed "後港國民學校" in 1941. After WWII it was retitled "臺南縣七股鄉第二國民學校" in 1946, then reverted to "後港國民學校" later that same year, with a campus relocation completed in 1947. The school historically served as the parent school for nearby Duga Elementary, which began as its branch campus in 1948.

後港國小源自昭和3年（1928年）3月20日設立的「七股公學校後港分教場」，屬日治時期公學校系統。昭和10年（1935年）7月獨立設校，定名「後港公學校」，昭和16年（1941年）更名為「後港國民學校」。戰後於民國35年（1946年）改稱「臺南縣七股鄉第二國民學校」，同年又改回「後港國民學校」，並於民國36年（1947年）完成校舍遷建。學校歷史上也是鄰近篤加國小的母校——篤加國小於1948年以其分校身分創立。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Roots in the Colonial-Era Kōgakkō System

源自日治「公學校」系統

Hougang traces its lineage to a 1928 branch classroom under Japan's colonial-era "公學校" (kōgakkō) system, making it one of the oldest schools in Qigu District.

🔄 Renamed Four Times

歷經多次更名

The school's name and status shifted repeatedly across the colonial era and postwar reorganizations — from branch classroom to independent "公學校," through wartime and post-retrocession renamings, mirroring the shifting administrative geography of Qigu's lagoon-side hamlets.

🌱 Parent School to Duga Elementary

篤加國小的母校

Hougang historically served as the founding parent school for nearby Duga Elementary, which began in 1948 as one of its branch campuses before becoming independent in 1959.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A living record of Qigu's shifting administrative history.

Hougang's century of relocations and name changes — from a colonial-era branch school through post-war reorganizations — mirrors the shifting administrative geography of Qigu's lagoon-side fishing hamlets, a district long shaped by salt production and aquaculture along its tidal flats.
