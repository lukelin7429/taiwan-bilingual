About · 學校簡介

A small campus with a nation-building name.

Jianguo Elementary School was established in 1954, part of a wave of new elementary schools built across Kaohsiung in the years following the postwar reorganization of the city's education system. Its name, "Jianguo" (建國, "nation-building"), reflects the optimistic civic language common to schools founded in that era. Today it remains a small, close-knit campus, with about 271 students across 14 classes serving families in Cianjin District.

建國國小創立於民國43年（1954年），是戰後高雄市教育體系重整期間新設立的學校之一。校名「建國」反映了當時普遍的建校命名風氣，帶有振興國家的期許。如今學校仍維持小而精緻的規模，全校約271名學生、14個班級，服務前金區的學童家庭。

Jianguo Elementary sits within Cianjin (前金), one of old Takao's earliest-settled hamlets whose name traces back to a historic capital fund used for early land reclamation near the Love River estuary. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
