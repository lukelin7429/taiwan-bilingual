Beishi Elementary School

北勢國民小學

A large elementary school covering numerous Pingzhen neighborhoods, its name pairing with neighboring Nanshi Elementary as part of the district's historic geographic divide.

About · 學校簡介

The "north" to Nanshi's "south."

Beishi Elementary was founded in 1985 and today sits on a campus of about 3.29 hectares. As of 2017, enrollment stood at 1,048 students across 38 elementary classes plus 3 kindergarten classes. Its school district covers numerous Pingzhen neighborhoods, including Beixing, Beifu, Beigui, Xingui, Jinling, Beihua, Xinfu, and Longen. The school's name, "Beishi" (north terrain), pairs with neighboring Nanshi (south terrain) Elementary as part of the historic geographic division used to describe Pingzhen's early Hakka settlement pattern.

北勢國小創立於1985年，校地約3.29公頃。截至2017年，全校學生達1,048人，共38個國小班級及3個幼兒園班級。學區涵蓋平鎮多個里別，包括北興、北福、北貴、新貴、金陵、北華、新富、龍恩等里。校名「北勢」與鄰近的「南勢」國小相對，源自平鎮早期客家聚落地理劃分的傳統地名。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for the north side of a Hakka landscape.

🧭

Beishi Elementary's name reflects the historic "north terrain / south terrain" (北勢/南勢) geographic division that early Hakka settlers used to describe Pingzhen's landscape — a naming pattern still visible today in the district's two paired elementary schools.
