Dongzhu Elementary School

東竹國小

About · 學校簡介

A farmland school beneath the day-lily hills.

Dongzhu Elementary is located in Zhutian Village, the same village where Fubei Junior High later stood — in fact, Fubei's earliest classes borrowed space from Dongzhu in 1968 while its own campus was being built. Dongzhu's founding is inferred to date to around 1939, based on an 80th-anniversary observance held around 2019, placing its roots firmly in the Japanese colonial era. The school sits in some of Fuli's richest farmland, close to Liushishishan, the mountain famous for its terraced day-lily fields.

東竹國小位於竹田村，後來富北國中也設立於此——事實上，富北國中最早期的班級曾於民國57年（1968年）借用東竹國小的空間，當時富北自己的校舍仍在興建中。根據民國108年（2019年）前後舉辦的建校八十週年紀念活動推算，東竹國小的創校時間約在昭和14年（1939年），紮根於日治時期。學校座落於富里最富饒的農地之一，鄰近以金針花梯田聞名的六十石山。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Once Home to a Future Junior High

曾借予富北國中使用

In 1968, Fubei Junior High's very first classes were taught in borrowed space at Dongzhu Elementary, a small but meaningful link between the two schools' histories.

🌼 Neighboring the Day-Lily Hills

鄰近六十石山

The school sits close to Liushishishan, whose terraced day-lily fields turn golden orange each summer and draw visitors from across Taiwan.

🏛️ Roots in the Japanese Colonial Era

創校於日治時期

With an 80th-anniversary observance around 2019, Dongzhu's founding traces back to approximately 1939, giving it deep historical roots in Fuli's farmland community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fuli Township, rice country at the valley's edge.

🌾

Fuli Township is Hualien County's southernmost township, a rice-farming community near the Tropic of Cancer at the southern tip of the East Rift Valley. Dongzhu's setting near Liushishishan places it right in the middle of the region's most scenic farmland. See the Hualien schools page for the full township list.
