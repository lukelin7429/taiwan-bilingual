About · 學校簡介

From a Japanese-era girls' school to an NCU-affiliated high school.

Founded in 1940 under Japanese rule as the Zhongli Girls' Home Economics School, this school has been renamed and reorganized many times over its 86-year history — from a county junior middle school in 1945, through provincial and national governance, to its current status as a school formally affiliated with National Central University since October 2013. Its campus, covering 8.88 hectares in the heart of Zhongli, was built on the former site of the Japanese-era Zhongli Shinto Shrine. Today the school enrolls roughly 2,100 students under the motto "盡忠尚義，耐勞崇禮" (loyalty, righteousness, diligence, and propriety).

學校創立於昭和15年（1940年）日治時期，初名「中壢家政女學校」。86年來歷經多次改制與更名——自1945年的新竹縣立中壢初級中學，歷經省立、國立時期，至2013年10月起正式成為國立中央大學附屬高級中學。校地占地8.88公頃，座落於中壢市中心，前身為日治時期的中壢神社所在地。現有學生約2,100餘人，校訓為「盡忠尚義，耐勞崇禮」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 An NCU-Affiliated High School

國立中央大學附屬高中

Since October 2013 the school has been formally affiliated with National Central University, one of Taiwan's leading research universities — reflecting Zhongli's identity as a university town.

⛩️ Built on a Former Shrine Site

校地前身為神社

The campus occupies the former grounds of the Japanese colonial-era Zhongli Shinto Shrine, a physical link to the district's layered history under successive governments.

🏛️ Eight Decades of Reinvention

歷經多次改制更名

Founded in 1940, the school has changed names and administrative status at least seven times — from a girls' home-economics school through county, provincial, and national governance to its present form.

📖 "Loyalty and Diligence" Motto

校訓：盡忠尚義，耐勞崇禮

The school's four-character motto, still used today, emphasizes loyalty, righteousness, hard work, and propriety as guiding values across an 8.88-hectare campus of roughly 2,100 students.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A university town with layered history.
