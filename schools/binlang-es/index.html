Binlang Elementary School

賓朗國小

A school that began in a Puyuma community assembly hall, later pioneering both special education and deaf education programs in the county.

About · 學校簡介

From a community hall to a pioneer in special education.

Binlang Elementary began in 1946 as a branch of Nanwang Elementary School, using the borrowed "Xiabinlang" (下賓朗) Puyuma community assembly hall as its original classroom space, before becoming fully independent in February 1947. Over the following decades the school took on a role well beyond ordinary elementary education: from 1971 to 1985 it ran a special "Benevolence Class" (仁愛班) for physically disabled children, and beginning in 1981 it hosted a countywide deaf-education program. It also absorbed, and later spun off, a Meinong branch campus between 1987 and 1993.

賓朗國小創立於1946年，最初為南王國小分校，借用「下賓朗」卑南族部落集會所作為最初的上課空間，1947年2月正式獨立設校。往後數十年間，學校承擔了遠超出一般國小的角色：1971年至1985年間，開辦專為肢體障礙兒童設立的「仁愛班」；自1981年起，更成為全縣聽障教育班的所在地。此外，該校也曾在1987年至1993年間併入、後又分出美農分校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Born in a Puyuma Assembly Hall

創校借用部落集會所

The school's 1946 branch campus borrowed the "Xiabinlang" Puyuma community assembly hall as its first classroom space, a striking origin that ties the school's founding directly to local Indigenous community infrastructure.

💙 A "Benevolence Class" for Disabled Children

仁愛班（1971–1985）

From 1971 to 1985, the school ran a dedicated "Benevolence Class" for physically disabled children — an early commitment to inclusive education well ahead of many peer schools.

🤟 A Countywide Deaf-Education Program

1981年起承辦全縣聽障教育班

Beginning in 1981, Binlang hosted a deaf-education program serving students from across the county, extending the school's reach and mission well beyond its own village.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xiabinlang and the Puyuma people.

🏛️

The school's original 1946 branch campus borrowed the "Xiabinlang" Puyuma community assembly hall, reflecting its roots serving the local Puyuma population near Nanwang — one of the historic centers of Puyuma Indigenous life in the Beinan Township area.
