Neili Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

Neili's oldest school.

Neili Elementary traces its roots to April 1940, when it opened as a satellite classroom of Xinjie Elementary School during the Japanese colonial era. It became an independent school in 1945 under the name Kanzikya (崁子腳) Elementary, then took its current name, Neili, in 1950. As the oldest school in the Neili area, it holds a dedicated archival record in Taiwan's National Culture Memory Bank documenting its development from 1940 to 2001. The school grew explosively from the postwar period through the 1970s and 80s as mainland-relocated factories and the Neili/Zhongli Industrial Park drew waves of workers' families into the area — at one point in the 1980s, it was the most populous elementary school in all of Taoyuan County.

內壢國小創校於日治時期昭和15年（1940年）4月，最初為新街國小的分教場，1945年獨立設校，初名「崁子腳國民學校」，1950年（民國39年）改為現名「內壢」。作為內壢地區歷史最悠久的學校，國家文化記憶庫特別收錄其1940至2001年的發展史料。戰後隨著遷台工廠與內壢（後擴大為中壢）工業區的興起，大量工人家庭遷入，學校在1970至80年代經歷爆炸性成長，一度是桃園縣就學人數最多的國小。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A timeline of Zhongli's industrial rise.

🏭

Neili Elementary's enrollment curve is essentially a timeline of Zhongli's transformation from farmland into one of Taiwan's densest manufacturing belts. As mainland-relocated factories opened through the postwar decades, workers and their families settled around the school in growing numbers, culminating in its status as Taoyuan County's largest elementary school in the 1980s — a growth pattern repeated, in smaller form, across several of Zhongli's other industrial-era schools.
