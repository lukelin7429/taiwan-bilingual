About · 學校簡介

The parent school of two others.

Jiading Elementary School's own history dates its founding to 1906 (recorded in its records as '民前6年,' six years before the founding of the Republic of China), when it opened as a branch of Dahu Public School called 'Dinjiading Branch School,' with its earliest classes held at the Dinjiading Cifu Temple (頂茄萣賜福宮). It became independent in 1917 as 'Dinjiading Public School' and relocated in 1938 to its present site near Mazu Mountain (媽祖婆山). Over the following decades, it gave rise to two other schools now serving the district — Chenggong Elementary School split off in 1952 and Shalun Elementary School in 1957–58. Enrollment has ranged between roughly 606 and 667 students depending on the source year, and student clubs include ukulele, Chinese orchestra, Songjiang Battle Array (宋江陣) folk martial arts, judo, and tennis.

茄萣國小校史記載創校於1906年（校史紀錄為「民前6年」），最初作為大湖公學校的分校「頂茄萣分校」，最早借用頂茄萣賜福宮上課。1917年獨立設校，改稱「頂茄萣公學校」，1938年遷至現址、鄰近媽祖婆山。其後數十年間，先後分出成功國小（1952年）與砂崙國小（1957至58年）兩校。學生數依資料年份不同，約在606至667人之間，社團包括烏克麗麗、國樂團、宋江陣、柔道與網球隊。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛩️ Classes Once Held in a Temple

曾借用廟宇上課

Jiading Elementary's earliest classes, in 1906, were held at the Dinjiading Cifu Temple (頂茄萣賜福宮) before the school had its own permanent building.

🌱 The Parent School of Two Others

分出兩所學校的母校

Over the decades, Jiading Elementary gave rise to two other schools now serving Cieding District — Chenggong Elementary (1952) and Shalun Elementary (1957–58).

🥋 Its Own Songjiang Martial Arts Club

校內宋江陣社團

Jiading Elementary runs its own Songjiang Battle Array (宋江陣) club, linking Cieding's coastal community to the same folk martial-arts tradition found inland in Neimen District, alongside ukulele, Chinese orchestra, judo, and tennis clubs.

Local Culture · 在地文化

By Mazu Mountain, practicing Songjiang.

🥋

Jiading Elementary sits by Mazu Mountain (媽祖婆山), reflecting Cieding's maritime Mazu-worship traditions, and its own Songjiang Battle Array (宋江陣) club links this coastal fishing community to the same folk martial-arts tradition found inland in Neimen District.
