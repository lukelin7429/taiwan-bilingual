About · 學校簡介

A name taken straight from the mountain.

Qingshan Elementary was founded in August 1949 (民國38年). Its name, "Qingshan" (青山, "Green Mountain"), is taken directly from the mountain village where it sits — the same mist-covered peaks where Dongshan's celebrated coffee is grown. The school operates an attached kindergarten and a campus health center, and runs ongoing teacher-development and curriculum workshops, typical of the small rural schools that serve Dongshan's mountain villages.

青山國小創校於民國38年（1949年）8月。校名「青山」直接取自其所在的山村地名，也是東山知名咖啡產地雲霧繚繞的同一片山脈。學校附設幼兒園與校園保健中心，並持續辦理教師專業成長與課程研習，是典型服務東山山區聚落的小型鄉村學校。
