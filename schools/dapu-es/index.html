Dapu Elementary School

苗栗縣大埔國民小學

About · 學校簡介

A small school with a big focus on character.

Dapu Elementary School is a small community school with about 13 classes and roughly 314 students, serving Zhunan's Dapu community. The school places strong emphasis on character and moral education alongside multi-intelligence discovery for its students. Its active reading-promotion program includes a dedicated 'Yushan Library' (玉山圖書館), and campus facilities include a computer room, AV room, library, English classroom, art room, and basketball court.

大埔國小是一所小型社區學校，現有約13班、314名學生，服務竹南鎮大埔地區。學校特別重視品格教育與多元智能開發，並透過「玉山圖書館」積極推動閱讀活動。校園設施包括電腦教室、視聽教室、圖書館、英語教室、美術教室與籃球場。
