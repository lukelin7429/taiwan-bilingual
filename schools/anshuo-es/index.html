Anshuo Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

At the edge of Taiwan's last wild coastline.

Anshuo Elementary serves one of Taitung's southernmost and most remote communities, in Daren Township. Over 90% of its students are Indigenous, mainly Paiwan, and the school operates a branch campus, Xinhua Branch School, to reach families further from the main site. Anshuo Village lies near the Alangyi Ancient Trail, a stretch of coastal path that remains one of Taiwan's last undeveloped coastlines — no coastal highway has ever been built through it, leaving the area remarkably wild for a heavily built-up island.

安朔國小服務臺東最南端、最偏遠的社區之一，位於達仁鄉。全校逾九成學生為原住民，以排灣族為主，並設有新化分校，服務更偏遠地區的家庭。安朔村鄰近「阿朗壹古道」，這段海岸步道是台灣僅存的原始海岸線之一——從未興建濱海公路，在這座高度開發的島嶼上顯得格外珍貴。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌊 Neighbor to the Alangyi Ancient Trail

阿朗壹古道旁

Anshuo Village sits near the Alangyi Ancient Trail, one of the last stretches of coastline in Taiwan with no road ever built through it — a rare, protected corridor of wild coast.

🏘️ A Predominantly Paiwan School

逾九成原住民學生

More than 90% of Anshuo's students are Indigenous, mainly Paiwan, making the school a central institution for preserving and passing on Paiwan identity in this remote township.

🚌 Reaching Families Through a Branch Campus

新化分校

The school operates the Xinhua Branch School to serve families living even farther from the main campus, a practical response to the demands of Taitung's most remote administrative area.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Daren, Taitung's remote southern tip.

🏔️

Anshuo Village, home to this school, lies along the Alangyi/Shouka coastal trail corridor at Taitung's southern tip — one of Taiwan's last roadless coastlines — reinforcing Daren Township's remoteness and its strong Paiwan Indigenous character.
