About · 學校簡介

From night school to national vocational campus.

National Yilan Commercial Vocational Senior High School traces back to 1952, when it began life as a night-school supplementary program known as the Yilan Commercial Vocational Cram School. It was formally established as Yilan County's own commercial vocational school in 1963, and was later upgraded to national status in February 2000. Today the school offers a Comprehensive High School track alongside specialized programs in Data Processing, International Trade, Applied English, Applied Japanese, Multimedia Design, and Business Information — training generations of Yilan's commercial and administrative workforce.

國立宜蘭高級商業職業學校創立於民國41年（1952年），最初為「宜蘭商業職業補習學校」的夜間進修班，民國52年（1963年）正式改制為「宜蘭縣立宜蘭商業職業學校」，並於民國89年（2000年）2月改制為國立高職。現今學校設有綜合高中學程，並提供資料處理、國際貿易、應用英語、應用日語、多媒體設計、商業經營等專業科別，長年培育宜蘭地區的商業與行政人才。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💼 Six Specialized Career Tracks

六大專業科別

Students choose among Data Processing, International Trade, Applied English, Applied Japanese, Multimedia Design, and Business Information programs, preparing them for a wide range of commercial careers.

🎼 Extracurricular Life Beyond Business

多元社團活動

The school is known for extracurricular groups including a Chinese orchestra, a traditional opera club, and an honor guard/drill team, giving vocational students a rich cultural life alongside coursework.

🏛️ Seven Decades of Growth

七十餘年辦學歷程

What began as a night-school supplementary program in 1952 has grown, through formal establishment in 1963 and national status in 2000, into one of Yilan's principal vocational institutions.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Training Yilan's commercial workforce.

🏙️

As Yilan City's principal commercial vocational school, it has long supplied local businesses and offices with graduates trained in trade, data processing, and languages — a quiet but steady contributor to the city's economic life. See the Yilan County schools page for more on the region.
