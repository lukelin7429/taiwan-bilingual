About · 學校簡介

Steady upgrades for a small campus.

Youche Elementary is a small rural school on Ganghou Road in Erlun Township. Its precise founding date is not confirmed in available records, but the school has undertaken a series of campus improvements in recent years, including new playground equipment and the construction of a synthetic sports field. These upgrades reflect a steady, practical investment in the everyday experience of its students within a farming community.

油車國小是一所位於二崙鄉港後路的小型鄉村學校。確切創校年代目前尚無確切紀錄可考，學校近年進行了一系列校園改善工程，包括更新遊樂設施與興建人工草皮運動場。這些升級反映出學校對學生日常校園生活的持續、務實投入，服務著一個農業社區。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Garlic and rice, generation after generation.

🧄

Set among Erlun's farming villages, the school is part of a Zhang-Zhou Hoklo community long sustained by garlic and rice cultivation. This agricultural tradition continues to shape the households and daily life of the families the school serves. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
