About · 學校簡介

A small school with a big view.

Luocuo Elementary is a small rural school in Luocuo Village, Puxin Township, with a current enrollment of around 41 students. Its setting offers broad open vistas near local religious sites and agricultural attractions, reflecting the close-knit farming community it serves. At some point in its history, the school published a "50th Anniversary Yearbook" (羅厝國小50週年校刊), suggesting roots reaching back decades, though its exact founding year has not been confirmed.

羅厝國小是位於埔心鄉羅厝村的小型鄉村學校，目前在校學生約41人。校園四周視野開闊，鄰近宗教勝地與農業景點，反映出當地緊密的農業社區生活。學校歷史上曾出版「羅厝國小50週年校刊」，顯示創校已有數十年歷史，但確切創校年份尚待確認。
