About · 學校簡介

From branch campus to national university.

National Quemoy University traces its origins to July 1997, when it was established as a branch campus of National Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology. It became an independent institution on August 1, 2003, as National Kinmen Institute of Technology, and was upgraded and renamed National Quemoy University on June 14, 2010. The English name "Quemoy" was chosen deliberately over the Hanyu Pinyin romanization, because it is the historically internationalized name for Kinmen that has appeared on Western maps since the age of exploration. As the only university in Kinmen County, NQU anchors the county's higher-education identity.

國立金門大學的前身可追溯至民國86年（1997年）7月，最初為國立高雄科技大學的分部。民國92年（2003年）8月1日獨立設校，改制為「國立金門技術學院」，並於民國99年（2010年）6月14日升格改名為「國立金門大學」。英文校名刻意選用「Quemoy」而非漢語拼音，因為這是自大航海時代以來，西方地圖上長期使用、國際通行的金門歷史名稱。作為金門縣唯一的大學，金門大學是全縣高等教育的重要指標。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Four Colleges

四大學院

NQU is organized into four colleges — Humanities and Social Sciences, Management, Engineering, and Health and Nursing — spanning 17 departments, 12 master's programs, 2 executive master's programs, and 1 doctoral program.

🌏 39 International Partner Universities

39所國際姊妹校

The university has signed cooperation and sister-school agreements with 39 universities across 13 countries, supporting exchange and collaboration for its students.

🗺️ The Historic Name "Quemoy"

「Quemoy」的歷史淵源

The university's English name deliberately uses "Quemoy," the historically internationalized name for Kinmen used on Western maps since the age of exploration, rather than the Hanyu Pinyin romanization.

👩‍🎓 A Growing Student Body

學生規模持續成長

Enrollment stands at roughly 3,000-plus undergraduates and 200-plus graduate students, for a total of around 4,500 — significant growth since the university's founding as a small branch campus in 1997.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring Kinmen's identity as an international crossroads.

🗺️

As Kinmen's only university, NQU anchors the county's higher-education identity, and its choice of the historic Western name "Quemoy" consciously reflects Kinmen's centuries-long role as an internationally-known frontline and maritime crossroads. See the Kinmen county page for more on the region.
