About · 學校簡介

A rare home for two tribal cultures, side by side.

Jiumei Elementary School occupies a distinctive place among Taiwan's indigenous-area schools: it is one of the very few nationwide running a formal bicultural education program that teaches both the Bunun and Tsou languages together. Located in Wangmei Village at the foot of Yushan, the school serves a rare mixed Bunun-and-Tsou tribal community. Its commitment to this dual heritage extends into its architecture — new school buildings incorporating both Tsou and Bunun architectural elements won the 2021 Jianzhu Yuanzhi Award, and the school built a traditional Tsou "hʉfʉ" structure with support from the government forestry agency.

久美國小在台灣原住民地區學校中地位特殊：它是全台少數正式推行布農族與鄒族雙語雙文化教育的學校之一。學校位於玉山腳下的望美村，服務著罕見的布農、鄒族混合部落社區。這份對雙重文化傳承的重視也延伸至建築——融合鄒族與布農族建築元素的新校舍榮獲2021年建築園冶獎，學校也在林務單位協助下搭建了傳統鄒族「hʉfʉ」（男子會所）建築。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗣️ A Rare Bicultural Curriculum

布農、鄒語雙語教育

Jiumei is one of Taiwan's few schools formally teaching both Bunun and Tsou languages side by side, reflecting its uniquely mixed tribal community.

🏆 Award-Winning Architecture

2021建築園冶獎

New school buildings blending Tsou and Bunun architectural elements won the 2021 Jianzhu Yuanzhi Award, a striking physical expression of the school's dual heritage.

🏛️ A Traditional Tsou Men's House

傳統鄒族會所 hʉfʉ

With support from the government forestry agency, the school built a traditional Tsou "hʉfʉ" structure on campus, keeping tribal architectural knowledge alive for students.

🌾 Harvest Festival Traditions

小米收穫祭與傳統摔角

Students take part in harvest-festival programs including millet processing and traditional wrestling, keeping both tribes' seasonal customs part of school life.
