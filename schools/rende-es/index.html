About · 學校簡介

A century-old school that began in a temple.

Rende Elementary School was founded on March 31, 1918, originally as “Chelujiao Public School Tuku Branch,” using the Qingning Temple as its campus. It became independent as “Rende Public School” in 1921, and was renamed several times through the mid-20th century before becoming “Rende Elementary School” in 1968. The school celebrated its 100th anniversary around 2018, marked by a dedicated commemorative page. Denan Elementary split off from it in 1962, and a Shanglun branch school was established in 1947. Today the school serves about 1,064 students across the Rende, Renyi, and Xintian villages, with a campus area of about 2.1148 square kilometers.

仁德國小創校於民國7年（1918年）3月31日，初名「車路墘公學校土庫分校」，最初借用青寧宮作為校舍。民國10年（1921年）獨立設校，改稱「仁德公學校」，歷經多次更名，1946年改稱「台南縣仁德鄉第一國民學校」，1947年「台南縣仁德鄉仁德國民學校」，1968年定名「台南縣仁德鄉仁德國民小學」。學校於2018年前後舉行建校百週年慶祝活動，並設有專屬紀念頁面。德南國小於1962年自本校分出設校，1947年並曾設立「上崙分校」。現全校學生約1,064人，學區涵蓋仁德、仁義、新田等里，校地面積約2.1148平方公里。

🛕 Born in a Local Temple

The school's origin story — a branch school that once borrowed Qingning Temple as makeshift classrooms — reflects how Rende's farming villages built out their own school system piece by piece.

💯 A Century Celebrated in 2018

2018年百週年校慶

The school marked its 100th anniversary around 2018 with a dedicated commemorative page, honoring a century of continuous operation.

🌱 The Parent School of Denan Elementary

德南國小自此分出

Denan Elementary split off from Rende Elementary in 1962 as the district's population grew, making Rende Elementary the parent school for one of the area's other campuses.
