Yongchun Senior High School

臺北市立永春高級中學

Built on the site of a vanished Qing-era reservoir, this senior high school is the only one in Taiwan where French is a compulsory first-year subject.

校地座落於清代永春陂舊址，是全台唯一將法文列為高一必修課程的高中。

About · 學校簡介

Built on the memory of a vanished pond.

Yongchun Senior High School opened in 1968 as "Taipei Municipal Yongchun Junior High School," moving to its present site — the former "Yongchun Slope" (永春陂), a Qing-dynasty irrigation reservoir in what was then rural Songshan — in 1976. It converted to senior-high-only status in 1994 and took its current name in 1996.

永春高中創立於1968年（民國57年），初名「臺北市立永春國民中學」，1976年遷至現址——清代灌溉埤塘「永春陂」的舊址，當時仍屬松山鄉間地帶。1994年（民國83年）轉型為純高中部，1996年（民國85年）正式定名為現今校名。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🇫🇷 Compulsory French

全台唯一必修法文

Uniquely among Taiwan's public high schools, French is a compulsory (not elective) first-year subject worth a full credit — a rare requirement nationwide.

🔭 A Rooftop Observatory

校內天文台

The school's "Yinghua Building" houses one of the few astronomical observatories among Taiwan's senior high schools, supporting its math/science gifted program.

⛰️ A Mountain Orientation Tradition

象山迎新登頂

New students climb Elephant Mountain at orientation and receive a "summit certificate" from the principal — a rite of passage tying the school to its neighborhood landmark.

🎵 An Alma Mater by a Famous Songwriter

小蟲譜寫校歌

The school's alma mater was composed in 1997 by celebrated songwriter-producer Xiao Chong, after a chance meeting with a school staff member.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A campus named for a vanished pond.

💧

The campus occupies the former "Yongchun Slope" (永春陂), a Qing-era irrigation reservoir in what was then rural Songshan. The school's very name and location record a vanished agrarian landscape — a pond that once irrigated nearby rice fields — that predates Xinyi's transformation into Taipei's modern financial core anchored by Taipei 101.
