About · 學校簡介

The school that waited its turn.

Changliu Elementary serves Changliu Village in Guoxing Township. Unlike most schools in the area, its campus was not badly damaged in the catastrophic 1999 921 earthquake. But because neighboring schools with more urgent structural damage were rebuilt with new facilities first, Changliu's campus became relatively the oldest in the area — and the Ministry of Education did not rebuild it until roughly nine years after 921.

長流國小服務國姓鄉長流村。與該地多數學校不同，其校舍在1999年921大地震中並未遭受嚴重損毀。然而，由於鄰近受災更嚴重的學校優先獲得重建與新設施，長流國小的校舍相對成為當地最老舊的校舍，教育部直到921地震後約九年才進行重建。
