Wu-Ling Senior High School

桃園市立武陵高級中等學校

Regarded as one of Taiwan's stronger co-ed academic public high schools, with gifted math-science, language, and music programs running since the 1980s and 90s.

被視為台灣數一數二的公立男女合校明星高中，數理、語文與音樂資優班自1980、90年代起陸續開辦至今。

About · 學校簡介

From a branch campus to a gifted-education powerhouse.

Wu-Ling Senior High started life in 1955 as a branch campus of Taipei's Provincial Chenggong High School, becoming an independent school in 1959 under the name "Taiwan Provincial Wu-Ling Senior High School." It became national in 2000 and municipal in 2018. Over the decades the school has built a reputation for gifted and specialized programs: math-science gifted classes since 1988, a science program run jointly with National Central University, a music-gifted program since 1991, and a language-gifted program added in 2004. As of 2017 the school enrolled about 2,489 students in 63 classes on a campus of roughly 49,250 square meters, and it has held sister-school relationships with institutions in Japan and France since 2016.

武陵高中的前身是民國44年（1955年）設立的「臺灣省立臺北成功中學茄苳溪分部」，民國48年（1959年）獨立設校，定名「臺灣省立武陵中學」。民國89年（2000年）改為國立，2018年改為市立。多年來學校以資優與特色課程著稱：數理資優班自1988年開辦，並與國立中央大學合作辦理科學班；音樂資優班自1991年開辦；語文資優班則於2004年增設。2017年資料顯示全校約2,489名學生、63個班級，校地約49,250平方公尺，並自2016年起與日本、法國的學校締結姊妹校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🔬 Decades of Gifted Programs

歷史悠久的資優教育

Math-science gifted classes since 1988, a music-gifted program since 1991, and a language-gifted program since 2004 make Wu-Ling one of Taoyuan's most established academic high schools.

🎓 A University Partnership

大學合作科學班

The school runs a joint science program with National Central University, giving strong students an early bridge into university-level research.

🌍 Sister Schools Abroad

日本、法國姊妹校

Since 2016 Wu-Ling has maintained sister-school relationships with institutions in Japan and France, broadening students' international exposure.

Local Culture · 在地文化

In the shadow of Tiger Head Mountain.

⛰️

Wu-Ling sits on Zhongshan Road near Tiger Head Mountain (虎頭山), the forested hill on the northern edge of old Taoyuan City that has long served as the district's scenic and recreational backdrop. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
