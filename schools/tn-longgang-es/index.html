About · 學校簡介

A century in the heart of Sikunshen fishing village.

Longgang Elementary School was founded on April 1, 1919, as a satellite classroom of Yancheng Public School, using a local temple as its temporary classroom. It became independent in 1921 as "Xiakunshen Public School," was renamed "Masago National School" in 1941, became "Longgang National School" after 1945, and took its current name, Longgang Elementary, in 1968. The school sits in the old Sikunshen (四鯤鯓) fishing-village area; a footbridge behind the campus, known as the Longgang Skywalk, is also called the "Sikunshen Sea View Bridge." The neighboring Sikunshen Longshan Temple, founded in 1665, is a major community faith center — said to be the first temple in Taiwan to use the Four Great Ghost Kings as door guardians — and the school sits within this historic fishing-and-oyster-farming settlement, now marketed as the "Golden Coast" (Kunxiwan Cultural Park).

龍崗國小於1919年（大正8年）4月1日創立，初為鹽埕公學校下鯤鯓分離教室，借用當地廟宇作為臨時教室。1921年（大正10年）獨立設校，定名「下鯤鯓公學校」，1941年（昭和16年）更名「真砂國民學校」，1945年後改為「龍崗國民學校」，1968年（民國57年）定名為龍崗國民小學。學校座落於四鯤鯓漁村舊聚落，校舍後方的「龍崗天橋」又稱「四鯤鯓海景橋」。鄰近的四鯤鯓龍山寺創建於1665年，是重要的信仰中心，相傳是全臺首座以四大鬼王為門神的廟宇——學校正處於這個現已行銷為「黃金海岸」（鯤喜灣文化園區）的歷史漁村與蚵田聚落之中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛩️ Started in a Temple Classroom

廟宇臨時教室起家

Longgang's earliest classes, in 1919, were held as a satellite classroom borrowed from a local temple, before the school became independent two years later.

🌉 A Sea-View Footbridge

龍崗天橋／四鯤鯓海景橋

A footbridge just behind the campus, known as the Sikunshen Sea View Bridge, connects the school to the coastal geography of the old fishing village around it.

🦪 In the Heart of Oyster-Farming Country

四鯤鯓蚵田聚落核心

The school sits within the historic Sikunshen fishing and oyster-farming settlement, one of the clearest living links in South District to Tainan's old coastal economy.

🐉 Neighbors With a 17th-Century Temple

鄰近1665年創建龍山寺

The nearby Sikunshen Longshan Temple, founded in 1665, is said to be Taiwan's first temple to use the Four Great Ghost Kings as door guardians, anchoring the school's neighborhood in centuries of local faith tradition.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Living alongside Tainan's old coastal fishing economy.

🦪

Longgang Elementary is directly embedded in the historic Sikunshen (四鯤鯓) fishing and oyster-farming community, one of the clearest living links in South District to Tainan's old harbor and coastal-fishing economy — a heritage now celebrated in the area's rebranding as the "Golden Coast" (Kunxiwan Cultural Park).
