Xinzhuang Elementary School

高雄市左營區新莊國民小學

About · 學校簡介

The school that gave its name to a neighborhood.

Xinzhuang Elementary School's roots trace to 1951, when it began as a branch campus of Dingjin National School. It was reorganized the following year as a branch of Jiucheng National School, before becoming a fully independent school in August 1954. It was renamed in 1968. The school later gave rise to its own branch campus in 1984, which became independent as Xinmin Elementary School in 1985 — continuing a local tradition of schools splitting to serve Zuoying's growing "Xinzhuang" neighborhood, whose name is also shared by nearby Hsin Chuang Senior High School.

新莊國小的歷史可追溯至民國40年（1951年），最初為鼎金國民學校新莊分部，隔年改隸舊城國民學校新莊仔分部，民國43年（1954年）8月正式獨立設校，民國57年（1968年）更名。學校其後於民國73年（1984年）設立分校，並於民國74年（1985年）獨立為新民國小，延續左營「新莊」地區學校因人口成長而分校設立的傳統——「新莊」之名也為鄰近的新莊高中所沿用。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A neighborhood's name, worn by three schools.

🏘️

The "Xinzhuang" name — shared by this elementary school, its own offshoot Xinmin Elementary, and nearby Hsin Chuang Senior High — marks the "new village" sub-area of Zuoying, a growing residential pocket distinct from the district's older naval-town core.
