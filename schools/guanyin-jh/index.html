Guanyin Junior High School

觀音國中

One of Guanyin District's two junior highs, with a nationally recognized drama club and technical-arts electives in hospitality and culinary arts.

觀音區兩所國中之一，戲劇社屢獲全國肯定，並開設餐旅與烘焙等技藝選修課程。

About · 學校簡介

A community junior high with a competitive edge.

Guanyin Junior High opened in 1966 as a branch of a nearby junior high school, becoming fully independent as Taoyuan County Guanyin Junior High School in 1968 under the nine-year compulsory education reform. It was renamed municipal in 2014. Today the school feeds directly into Guanyin Senior High and shares the district's emphasis on vocational and technical pathways, alongside standout achievements in the arts and sciences.

觀音國中民國55年（1966年）創校，初為鄰近國中之分部，民國57年（1968年）九年國教實施後獨立為「桃園縣立觀音國民中學」，民國103年（2014年）更名市立。學校直接銜接觀音高中，並延續觀音區重視技職教育的傳統，同時在藝文與科學競賽方面表現亮眼。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎭 A Nationally Recognized Drama Club

全國創意戲劇比賽獲獎

The school's drama club won national recognition at the 114th academic year National Student Creative Drama Competition (全國學生創意戲劇比賽), a standout result for a district junior high.

🍳 Hospitality and Culinary Arts Electives

餐旅與烘焙技藝選修

Beyond standard academics, the school offers technical-arts electives in hospitality and culinary arts, giving students an early taste of vocational pathways.

🔬 Science Exhibition Champions

科學展覽第一名

Students have taken first place in the Taoyuan City Science Exhibition, reflecting a campus that balances arts, vocational skills, and STEM competition.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Feeding Guanyin's technical education pipeline.

🎭

As one of two junior highs serving Guanyin's farming and industrial communities, the school feeds directly into Guanyin Senior High and shares the district's broader emphasis on vocational and technical pathways alongside the arts and sciences. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
