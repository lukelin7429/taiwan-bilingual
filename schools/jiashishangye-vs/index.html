About · 學校簡介

From home economics to a Google Reference School.

Taichung Home Economics and Commercial Senior High School traces its origins to 1935, when it was founded as "Taichung Prefecture Taichung Women's Home Economics School" (臺中州臺中家政女學校) under Japanese colonial rule, at a time when vocational education for women centered on domestic skills. Over nine decades, the school evolved into a coeducational commercial and technical high school, and today it holds the distinction of being the first technical high school in Taiwan certified as a Google Reference School, alongside a consistent ranking among central Taiwan's top vocational senior highs.

臺中家商創立於1935年（昭和10年），原名「臺中州臺中家政女學校」，成立於日治時期，當時女子職業教育多以家政技能為主。歷經近九十年發展，學校逐步轉型為男女合校的商業與技術型高中，如今更是全台第一所獲得Google推薦學校（Google Reference School）認證的技術型高中，並長期穩居中台灣頂尖職業高中之列。
