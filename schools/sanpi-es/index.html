About · 學校簡介

A small school with a nationally known puppet troupe.

Sanpi Elementary School is a small rural school in Sanye Village, Lujhu District, enrolling roughly 61 students as of 2023. Though its own founding date is not recorded, the school holds historical standing in the area as a "mother school" — it spun off a branch campus in 1952 that later became today's independent Beiling Elementary School. Sanpi has earned national recognition for its traditional puppet theatre (傀儡戲) troupe, a distinction unusual for a school of its size.

三埤國小是位於路竹區三爺里的小型鄉間學校，截至2023年約有61名學生。雖然創校年份未有明確記載，但學校在地方上具有「母校」地位——曾於1952年分出一個分班，後來獨立成為今日的北嶺國小。三埤國小以全國知名的傀儡戲團聞名，對於這樣規模的學校而言相當難得。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for the ponds that watered the fields.

💧

"Sanpi" (三埤, "three ponds") names the historic irrigation reservoirs that once watered the surrounding farmland, a reminder of the agricultural roots of this small Lujhu community. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
