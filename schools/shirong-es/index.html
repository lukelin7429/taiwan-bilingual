About · 學校簡介

The "Happiness Campus" that bucked the trend.

Shirong Elementary School, known locally as "幸福學園" (Happiness Campus) through its Facebook branding, serves Shirong Village in Yanpu Township. Once the smallest school in the township, it has drawn media attention — including a Liberty Times report — for growing its student numbers against the broader trend of rural enrollment decline, with support from the Child Welfare League Foundation's rural-outreach programs.

仕絨國小以臉書粉專「幸福學園」為人所知，服務鹽埔鄉仕絨村。曾是全鄉規模最小的學校，卻在少子化浪潮中逆勢成長，自由時報等媒體曾報導此現象，並獲兒童福利聯盟文教基金會偏鄉扶助計畫支持。

😊 The "Happiness Campus"

Shirong is known locally by its Facebook branding, "幸福學園" (Happiness Campus), reflecting the warm, tight-knit character of school life here.

📈 Growing Against the Trend

Once Yanpu Township's smallest school, Shirong has been noted in media reports for growing its enrollment even as rural schools across Taiwan face declining student numbers.

🤝 Backed by Child Welfare League

兒福聯盟支持

The school is supported by the Child Welfare League Foundation's rural-outreach programs, part of a broader effort to strengthen small schools in remote areas.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A small school in the flower plain.

🌷

Shirong Village sits within Yanpu Township's agricultural plain, alongside Tajen University, where farms grow mango, wax apple, and flowers — part of the broader landscape that has earned the township its reputation as a flower-growing hub. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
