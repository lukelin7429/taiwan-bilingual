About · 學校簡介

A larger campus at the heart of the "Wen-" neighborhoods.

Wende Elementary School is one of Fengshan's larger elementary campuses, with 56 classes, roughly 1,456 students, and 98 teachers. The school serves the neighboring Wenying, Wenshan, Wende, and Wenheng communities — a cluster of areas that share the "文" (culture) naming convention with several nearby schools. Wende has also piloted bilingual instruction in health and physical education classes.

文德國小是鳳山區規模較大的國小之一，共56班、約1,456名學生、98位教師。學校服務文英、文山、文德、文衡等相鄰里別，這些地區皆與鄰近數所學校共享「文」字命名傳統。文德國小也在健康與體育課試辦雙語教學。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Fengshan's "Wen" school cluster.

📖

Wende is part of a cluster of Fengshan schools sharing the "文" (culture/literature) name root — alongside 文山 and 文華 — a naming pattern that took hold during the district's postwar school-building boom. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
