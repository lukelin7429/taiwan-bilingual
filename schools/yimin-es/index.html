About · 學校簡介

A large campus still investing in growth.

Yimin Elementary was established in 1990 and has grown into one of Dali's larger schools, with 55 classes and roughly 1,408 students. The campus recently added a new activity center, underground parking, and an attached kindergarten — a large-scale facility investment that reflects Dali's continued status as one of Taichung's fastest-growing residential districts even in the 2020s.

益民國小創立於民國79年（1990年），已發展為大里規模較大的學校之一，設有55班、約1,408名學生。校園近年增建活動中心、地下停車場及附設幼兒園，這樣大規模的設施投資反映出大里即使到了2020年代，仍是台中成長最快的住宅區之一。
