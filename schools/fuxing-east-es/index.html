Fuxing Elementary School

復興國民小學

Founded in 1995 in the Huweiliao redevelopment zone, sharing a name, adjacency, and founding lineage with its junior-high namesake established two decades earlier.

About · 學校簡介

Following its junior-high namesake into a growing district.

Fuxing Elementary School officially opened in August 1995, though preparations began back in November 1991 under Lin Zhengzong, then-principal of Degao Elementary, who was appointed concurrent preparatory director. In March 1995, the preparatory directorship transferred to Zheng Yanhong, principal of Shenggong Elementary, after Lin sought early retirement. The school is located in the Huweiliao (虎尾寮) land-readjustment zone, adjacent to its sister school, Fuxing Junior High, which was established two decades earlier in 1974 — the elementary school followed the junior high into the same growing district a generation later.

復興國小於1995年（民國84年）8月正式開學，惟籌備工作早在1991年（民國80年）11月即已展開，由時任德高國小校長林正宗兼任籌備處主任。1995年3月因林校長申請提前退休，籌備處主任一職轉由聖功國小校長鄭燕紅接任。學校座落於虎尾寮土地重劃區，緊鄰1974年創校的姊妹校復興國中——國小晚了整整二十年才跟隨國中的腳步，落腳於同一片快速發展的區域。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Junior-High Namesake, Two Decades Apart

與復興國中同名相鄰

The school shares a name, adjacency, and founding-era timeline with Fuxing Junior High (est. 1974) — the elementary school followed the junior high into the same growing district two decades later.

🏘️ Part of the Huweiliao Redevelopment Wave

虎尾寮重劃區浪潮

Fuxing Elementary is part of the same Huweiliao redevelopment wave that also produced Yuwen Elementary a decade later, reflecting continued growth in the district.

📋 A Four-Year Preparation Period

籌備期長達四年

Preparations for the school began in 1991, four years before it finally opened in 1995, with the preparatory directorship changing hands partway through the process.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring the newer, suburbanizing edge of East District.

🏘️

Anchoring the Huweiliao (虎尾寮) redevelopment zone alongside its junior-high namesake, the school represents the newer, rapidly suburbanizing edge of East District — a sharp contrast to Zhongxi District's centuries-old walled core. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
