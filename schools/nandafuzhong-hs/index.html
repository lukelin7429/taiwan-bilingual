About · 學校簡介

From farmland training grounds to a comprehensive high school.

Founded on April 1, 1939 under Japanese rule as "Tainan Prefecture Agricultural School," this school today occupies nearly 33 hectares — the largest campus of any senior high school in Tainan City, a legacy of its founding as an agricultural school with farmland and livestock training grounds. The campus preserves original Japanese-era dormitory buildings, constructed between 1939 and 1943, which were designated historic architecture in 2009. In 2005 the school discontinued its purely vocational tracks and added general-academic classes, becoming a "comprehensive high school" (綜合高中) that now offers seven specialized tracks — culinary arts, food processing, IT, technical drawing, construction, animal husbandry, and horticulture — alongside a college-prep track. About 1,739 students study across 45 classes, 12 general-academic and 33 comprehensive-track.

學校創立於昭和14年（1939年）4月1日，日治時期名為「臺南州立農業學校」。校地近33公頃，是台南市各高中中面積最大的一所，反映其創校時作為農業學校、擁有農田與畜牧實習用地的歷史。校內仍保留1939至1943年間興建的日治時期宿舍建築，並於2009年獲指定為歷史建築。學校於2005年停辦純職業類科、增設普通科，轉型為「綜合高中」，目前設有餐飲、食品加工、資訊、製圖、營建、畜產、園藝七個專門學程，並附設學術學程，全校約1,739名學生，共45班（普通科12班、綜合高中學程33班）。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 Tainan's Largest Senior-High Campus

全台南市校地最大

With nearly 33 hectares of grounds, the school has the largest campus area of any senior high school in Tainan City — inherited from its founding as an agricultural training school.

日治時期宿舍建築

🐄 Roots in an Agricultural School

農業學校的起源

Founded in 1939 as Tainan Prefecture Agricultural School, the school's sprawling grounds still echo its original mission of farmland and livestock training.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A memory of Yongkang's farmland past.

🌱

The school's agricultural-school origins and sprawling grounds reflect Yongkang's pre-war identity as farmland and orchard country on Tainan's northern outskirts, before postwar industrialization and suburbanization took over the district. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
