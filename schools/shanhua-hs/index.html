National Shanhua Senior High School

國立善化高級中學

A national senior high school at the geographic center of Tainan, born as a 1955 wartime-evacuation branch of three Tainan city high schools and long the academic anchor of the region's sugar-market towns.

About · 學校簡介

The academic anchor of Tainan's sugar-market heartland.

National Shanhua Senior High School traces its roots to 1955, when it began as a joint wartime-evacuation branch serving students from Tainan First, Tainan Second, and Tainan Girls' high schools. It became a fully independent school on August 1, 1956, as “Taiwan Provincial Shanhua Middle School.” Junior-high classes were phased out in 1968 as nine-year compulsory education rolled out nationwide, leaving it a pure senior high, and it took its present name, National Shanhua Senior High School, in 2000 following the streamlining of Taiwan's provincial government. Sitting at the geographic center of greater Tainan on Dacheng Road, the school has long served as a hub for families from the surrounding sugar-market towns.

國立善化高級中學創校可追溯至1955年，最初是臺南一中、臺南二中與臺南女中戰時疏散聯合分部。民國45年（1956年）8月1日正式獨立設校，定名「臺灣省立善化中學」。民國57年（1968年）九年國教實施，初中部逐年停辦，成為純高中；民國89年（2000年）精省後改為現名「國立善化高級中學」。學校位於大成路，處於大台南地理中心位置，長期是周邊糖業市鎮學子求學的重鎮。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 27 Classes and Counting

27班規模穩定發展

The school runs 27 regular classes plus 3 athletic-program classes under the school motto “Public-spirited, Sincere, Diligent, Simple” (公誠勤樸).

📍 Geographic Heart of Tainan

地理位置居大台南之中

Sitting at the geographic center of greater Tainan, the school has historically served as an educational hub for the surrounding sugar-market towns.

🧭 Early Counseling Center

1984年設輔導室

Shanhua Senior High established a student counseling center in 1984, early among Tainan-area schools to formalize student guidance services.

⚾ Part of a Baseball Tradition

善化棒球傳統一環

Its youth baseball program (青棒隊) is part of the wider Shanhua baseball tradition shared with neighboring Shanhua Elementary and Shanhua Junior High School.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchored to Shanhua's sugar-refinery heritage.

🎋

Shanhua grew up as the market town surrounding the Meiji-era Shanhua Sugar Refinery, and this national high school — sited on Dacheng Road at the center of town — has long served as the area's academic anchor for generations of families whose lives were tied to that sugar economy.
