Hsu Kuang Senior High School

南投縣立旭光高級中學

A public senior high school rebuilt with Tzu Chi Foundation funding after the 1999 921 earthquake, and Caotun's first complete high school.

About · 學校簡介

Rebuilt after disaster, rooted in the community.

Hsu Kuang Senior High School was founded in August 1968 as Hsu Kuang Junior High School. Its campus was totally destroyed in the catastrophic 1999 "921" earthquake, and the school was rebuilt with funding from the Tzu Chi Foundation, reopening in 2002. In 2001, the school was upgraded to become Caotun's first senior/complete high school, and one of Nantou County's first "complete middle schools" spanning both junior and senior high levels.

旭光高中創立於民國57年（1968年）8月，最初名為旭光國中。校舍在1999年921大地震中全毀，其後獲慈濟基金會捐助重建，於2002年復校啟用。民國90年（2001年），學校升格為草屯鎮第一所高中／完全中學，也是南投縣最早成立的完全中學之一，橫跨國中與高中部。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🕊️ Rebuilt With Tzu Chi Support

慈濟援建重生

After the 1999 921 earthquake totally destroyed the campus, the Tzu Chi Foundation funded its reconstruction, and the rebuilt school reopened in 2002 — a landmark of post-disaster recovery in Caotun.

🏫 Caotun's First Complete High School

草屯首所完全中學

Upgraded in 2001, Hsu Kuang became Caotun Township's first senior high school and one of Nantou County's earliest complete middle schools, spanning junior and senior high grades.

📜 Six Decades of History

逾半世紀校史

Founded in 1968 as Hsu Kuang Junior High, the school has grown alongside Caotun for more than half a century, weathering disaster and rebuilding to serve new generations of students.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Caotun's post-921 story.

🪵

Hsu Kuang's Tzu Chi-funded post-921 reconstruction and its role as Caotun's first senior high school reflect the town's steady growth as a populous manufacturing and woodworking hub near Taichung — a community that rebuilt its schools alongside its factories and furniture workshops.
