National Hualien Senior High School

國立花蓮高級中學

A public senior high school founded in 1936, whose campus preserves a county-designated historic music hall and a national championship football tradition.

About · 學校簡介

A hometown school built on Hualien's own philanthropy.

National Hualien Senior High School was founded in 1936 under Japanese rule as Hualien Harbor Middle School, with funding support from local philanthropists — a notably community-driven origin for a colonial-era institution. Historically a boys' school, it opened its music and athletics programs to both genders starting in 2002 and became a national school in 2000. The campus preserves the county-designated historic Kuo Tzu-chiu Music and Culture Hall, and the school's football team captured the national High School Football League championship in 2019.

花蓮高中創立於1936年日治時期，初名「花蓮港中學校」，創校經費部分來自地方仕紳捐助——這在殖民時期的學校中頗具在地色彩。學校歷史上原為男子中學，自2002年起音樂與體育班對兩性開放，並於民國89年（2000年）改隸國立。校園內保存縣定古蹟郭子究音樂文化館，足球隊更於2019年勇奪全國高中甲級足球聯賽冠軍。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎵 A Historic Music Hall

郭子究音樂文化館

The campus preserves the county-designated historic Kuo Tzu-chiu Music and Culture Hall, named for a renowned Hualien composer and honoring the school's long musical tradition.

⚽ National Football Champions

全國足球聯賽冠軍

The school's football team won the national High School Football League championship in 2019, a standout achievement for a school in eastern Taiwan.

🤝 Founded by Local Philanthropy

仕紳捐助創校

Established in 1936 as Hualien Harbor Middle School with funding help from local benefactors, the school's roots reflect an early tradition of community investment in education.

🚻 Opening Doors Since 2002

音樂體育班兩性開放

Though historically a boys' school, Hualien Senior High opened its music and athletics programs to female students starting in 2002, broadening access to its signature programs.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Woven into Hualien City's colonial-era development.

🏔️

As one of Hualien City's oldest schools, its campus preserves buildings tied to the city's development along the Central Mountain Range foothills since Japanese-era urban planning. See the Hualien County schools page for more on the region.
