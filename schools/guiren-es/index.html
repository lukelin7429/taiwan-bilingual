About · 學校簡介

Guiren's centennial anchor school.

Guiren Elementary was founded in 1920, during the Japanese colonial era, making it the oldest continuously operating school in the district — it has already passed its 100th anniversary. The campus is shaded by a roughly 20-year-old cluster of royal palm trees and 50-year-old mahogany trees, and the school published a "百年校慶特刊" (centennial commemorative volume) documenting its long history for the occasion.

歸仁國小創校於大正9年（1920年）日治時期，是歸仁區歷史最悠久且持續辦學至今的學校，已跨越百年校慶。校園內有約20年樹齡的大王椰子樹群與50年樹齡的桃花心木，學校並曾出版「百年校慶特刊」，記錄這段悠久校史。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Guiren's Oldest School

歸仁區最古老學校

Founded in 1920, Guiren Elementary is the district's oldest continuously operating school, having already celebrated its centennial anniversary.

🌴 A Century-Old Tree Canopy

大王椰子與桃花心木

The campus features a cluster of royal palm trees roughly 20 years old alongside mahogany trees around 50 years old, part of its lush, mature grounds.

🏓 A National Table-Tennis Powerhouse

女子桌球全國勁旅

The school's girls' table-tennis team has long been a national powerhouse, alongside a folk-song (鄉土歌謠) team that won the southern-region championship.

📖 A Centennial Commemorative Volume

百年校慶特刊

To mark its 100th anniversary, the school published a commemorative volume chronicling a century of educating Guiren's children.

Local Culture · 在地文化

An anchor for Guiren's changing identity.

🏛️

As Guiren's oldest school, this century-old institution anchors the district's identity as a farming town whose old-village heart is now overshadowed by the growth of the Tainan High Speed Rail and airport corridor nearby. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
