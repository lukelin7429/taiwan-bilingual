About · 學校簡介

A central school with river-hamlet roots.

Dingxi Elementary was established as a branch school in 1954 and became independent in 1955. Like several other schools in Yonghe, its lineage traces back to being a branch of Xizhou (溪洲) Elementary, reflecting the area's original identity as a river-adjacent farming hamlet. The school has been renamed multiple times to track Yonghe's changing administrative status, and it now sits centrally near the Dingxi MRT area.

頂溪國小於民國43年（1954年）以分校形式設立，翌年（1955年）獨立設校。與永和區其他數所學校相同，其校史源自溪洲國小的分校，反映當地原為溪畔農村聚落的歷史身份。學校因永和行政地位變遷歷經多次更名，如今座落於頂溪捷運站周邊核心地帶。
