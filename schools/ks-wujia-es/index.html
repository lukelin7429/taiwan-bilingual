About · 學校簡介

Over a century of schooling in Wujia.

Wujia Elementary's history stretches back to 1921 (大正10年), when it opened during the Japanese colonial era as a branch of Fongshan Public School. It was formally established as its own institution, Wujia Public School, in 1924. Over a century old, it is by far the oldest school in this batch, its history spanning the Qing, Japanese colonial, and Republic of China eras alongside Wujia's long agricultural community life.

五甲國小的歷史可追溯至大正10年（1921年），日治時期作為鳳山公學校的分校成立，並於1924年正式獨立設校，定名為五甲公學校。創校已逾百年，是本批學校中歷史最悠久者，其發展橫跨清代、日治與民國三個時代，也見證了五甲地區長久的農村社區生活。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Over a Century of History

創校逾百年

Tracing back to 1921, Wujia Elementary is over 100 years old — the oldest school covered in this batch of Fongshan profiles by a wide margin.

🏫 Born as a Branch School

前身為分教場

The school began as a branch of Fongshan Public School in 1921, gaining independent status as Wujia Public School just three years later, in 1924.

🕰️ Spanning Three Eras

歷經清代、日治、民國三時期

Its long history spans the Qing, Japanese colonial, and Republic of China eras — a rare living record of Wujia's century-plus community life.

🌾

The school's century-plus history embodies Wujia's long agricultural community life, spanning the Qing, Japanese colonial, and ROC eras — making it a living record of one of Fongshan's oldest identifiable neighborhoods. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
