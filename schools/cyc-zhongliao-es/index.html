Zhongliao Elementary School

重寮國民小學

A small rice-country school whose dedicated 'rice curriculum' teaches children the farming identity of their own township.

以「食米課程」為特色的稻米之鄉小校，讓孩子從小認識自己家鄉的農耕身分。

About · 學校簡介

A rice-country school with a curriculum to match.

Zhongliao Elementary began in 1952 as a branch of Lucao Elementary School, becoming independent on October 31, 1955. Its motto, 'Happy Learning, Lively Growth' (快樂 學習 活潑 成長), sets the tone for a school best known for its dedicated rice-farming curriculum (食米課程), which teaches students about rice cultivation as a living local tradition rather than an abstract textbook topic. The school also offers Taiwanese-language (母語) instruction and gender-equality/health education programs, and maintains active community engagement through Facebook and video channels.

重寮國小前身為民國41年（1952年）成立的鹿草國小分校，民國44年（1955年）10月31日獨立設校。校訓「快樂 學習 活潑 成長」點出辦學精神，學校最為人知的是專屬的食米課程，將稻作文化化為活生生的在地學習，而非課本上的抽象知識。此外，學校也提供母語教學與性別平等/健康教育課程，並透過臉書與影音頻道積極經營社群。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 A Dedicated Rice Curriculum

食米課程特色教學

The school's rice-farming curriculum (食米課程) teaches students hands-on about rice cultivation, directly reflecting the township's identity as a rice- and crop-farming community.

😊 Happy Learning, Lively Growth

快樂 學習 活潑 成長

臉書與影音頻道經營

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rice country on the Chianan Plain.

🌾

Zhongliao Elementary sits in Zhongliao Village, Lucao Township, part of the rice- and crop-farming belt of the Chianan Plain. Its dedicated rice curriculum is a direct, hands-on expression of the township's agricultural identity, giving children a structured way to understand the crop that has shaped their community for generations.
