About · 學校簡介

A large campus with a global outlook.

Taipei Municipal Nanhu Elementary School was founded in 1994 and has grown into one of the larger elementary schools in Neihu District, with 64 general classes, 2 special-education classes, and 4 kindergarten classes serving roughly 1,810 students and 122 teachers across a 25,122-square-meter campus. The school maintains a sister-school relationship with a primary school in Brisbane, Australia, and operates a digital planetarium and observatory for astronomy education — giving students hands-on exposure to both international exchange and hands-on science.

南湖國小創立於民國83年（1994年），是內湖區規模較大的國小之一，設有普通班64班、特教班2班、幼兒園4班，全校約1,810名學生、122位教師，校地面積達25,122平方公尺。學校與澳洲布里斯本一所小學締結姊妹校關係，並設有數位天文台與觀測室，讓學生同時接觸國際交流與天文科學的實作體驗。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A name that splices two districts.

🗺️

Like its Nanhu Senior High neighbor, Nanhu Elementary's name splices "Nangang" and "Neihu" together, reflecting its position near the boundary between the two adjoining districts. See the Taipei county page for more on the region.
