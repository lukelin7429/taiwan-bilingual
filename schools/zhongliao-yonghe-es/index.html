Yonghe Elementary School

永和國民小學

A small mountain-township school in Zhongliao where every student has an iPad, art and violin programs flourish, and an all-English summer camp brings the world a little closer.

About · 學校簡介

A small school with big ambitions.

Yonghe Elementary is a small school in mountainous Zhongliao Township that has invested heavily in enrichment despite its size. Every student is provided an iPad, and the school runs an 'Alice Painting World' art initiative alongside a violin elite class. Classrooms are fully air-conditioned and the library has been renovated. Students take part in an all-English summer camp and have won awards in classical poetry recitation and international children's art exhibitions.

永和國小是位於中寮鄉山區的一所小型學校，儘管規模不大，卻在多元學習上投入相當資源。全校學生配有iPad，並推動「愛麗絲彩繪世界」美術特色課程與小提琴精緻班。教室全面裝設冷氣，圖書館也經過整修翻新。學生參與全英語夏令營，並曾在古詩文朗讀比賽與國際兒童美術展中獲獎。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rural roots, close-knit rebuilding.

🍯

A small mountain-township school in longan- and honey-farming Zhongliao, Yonghe Elementary's classical-poetry and art programs carry on the area's tradition of close-knit, community-rebuilt rural education after the 921 earthquake. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
