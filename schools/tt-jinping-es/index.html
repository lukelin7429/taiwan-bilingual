Jinping Elementary School

錦屏國民小學

A small school with its own identity within Guangyuan Village, deep in the mountain farmland of Haiduan Township. Not to be confused with the unrelated Jinping Elementary in Jianshi Township, Hsinchu County.

行政上隸屬廣原村，但擁有獨立校名與校園認同，座落於海端鄉深山農村地帶。與新竹縣尖石鄉的錦屏國小並非同一所學校。

About · 學校簡介

A separate identity within Guangyuan.

Jinping Elementary is administratively located within Guangyuan Village but maintains its own separate campus and school identity as "Jinping." It sits deep in Haiduan Township's mountain terrain within a Bunun farming community. The school is active in athletics, fielding students in track and relay events as well as soccer competitions, and it has an attached kindergarten serving the youngest children in the area.

錦屏國小行政上隸屬廣原村，但擁有獨立的校園與校名「錦屏」。學校座落於海端鄉深山地帶的布農族農村社區。學生活躍於田徑、接力賽及足球等體育項目，校內並設有附設幼兒園，服務當地學齡前幼兒。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Its Own Identity Within Guangyuan

獨立校名與校園

Although administratively part of Guangyuan Village, Jinping Elementary keeps its own distinct campus and name, giving its students a school identity separate from its larger neighbor.

🏃 A Track for Runners and Footballers

田徑與足球活躍

Students compete in track and relay events as well as soccer, giving this small mountain school a lively athletics culture.

🧒 A Start for the Youngest Learners

附設幼兒園

An attached kindergarten means the school welcomes children from the local Bunun farming community well before they reach first grade.
