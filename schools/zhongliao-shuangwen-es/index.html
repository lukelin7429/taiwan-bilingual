Shuangwen Elementary School

爽文國民小學

A Zhongliao Township elementary school with a butterfly-garden campus, distinct from the neighboring junior high that shares its village name.

中寮鄉爽文村的國小，校園栽植蝴蝶食草蜜源植物，與同村同名的爽文國中為兩所不同機構。

About · 學校簡介

An ecological campus, distinct from its namesake neighbor.

Shuangwen Elementary is a distinct institution from the neighboring Shuangwen Junior High, despite sharing the same village name. The school promotes ecological education, with a campus planted specifically with butterfly host plants and nectar sources to attract local butterfly populations. Students take part in a recorder ensemble, diabolo, inline skating, and puppetry clubs, giving the school a lively extracurricular culture.

爽文國小與鄰近的爽文國中雖同屬爽文村、校名相似，卻是兩所各自獨立的學校。學校推動生態教育，校園特別栽植蝴蝶食草與蜜源植物，吸引在地蝴蝶棲息繁衍。學生參與直笛團、扯鈴、直排輪與布袋戲等社團活動，展現豐富多元的課外學習文化。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🦋 A Butterfly-Garden Campus

蝴蝶食草蜜源植物

Shuangwen's campus is planted with butterfly host and nectar plants as part of its ecological education program, drawing local butterfly populations onto school grounds.

🪈 A Recorder Ensemble and Diabolo Club

直笛團與扯鈴社

Students take part in a recorder ensemble and diabolo club, part of a lively roster of extracurricular activities.

🎭 Puppetry and Inline Skating Clubs

布袋戲與直排輪社

Puppetry and inline skating clubs round out Shuangwen's extracurricular offerings, giving students a range of creative and athletic outlets.

🏫 A Distinct School From Its Namesake

與爽文國中各自獨立

Despite sharing the Shuangwen village name with the neighboring junior high school, Shuangwen Elementary is an entirely separate institution.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Ecology and agriculture in Shuangwen Village.

🦋

Named for Shuangwen Village, one of the longan- and honey-farming communities of Zhongliao Township, Shuangwen Elementary's butterfly-garden campus reflects the township's agricultural, ecologically minded rural character. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
