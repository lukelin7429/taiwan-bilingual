Qingcaohu Elementary School

青草湖國民小學

A 2006 school named for the scenic Qingcaohu reservoir it sits beside, home to a Gold Award-winning kindergarten.

About · 學校簡介

One of Hsinchu's newest schools, named for a scenic lake.

Qingcaohu Elementary is one of Hsinchu's newer schools, under 20 years old, named for the nearby Qingcaohu (Green Grass Lake) scenic area. For over a decade after opening, the school had no indoor gymnasium, until a new NT$84.45 million two-story multi-function activity center opened in 2024. Its attached kindergarten has won a Gold Award for Teaching Excellence, one of the school's proudest achievements in its short history.

青草湖國小是新竹市較新設立的學校之一，校史未滿20年，校名取自鄰近的青草湖風景區。學校啟用後逾十年間都沒有室內體育館，直到2024年一座斥資新台幣8,445萬元興建的兩層樓多功能活動中心啟用。學校附設幼兒園曾榮獲教學卓越金質獎，是這所年輕學校最引以為傲的成就之一。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Beside a lake steeped in local history.

🏞️

Qingcaohu Elementary is located beside Qingcaohu (青草湖), a scenic reservoir area home to Lingyin Temple (built 1924) — one of northern Taiwan's historic "eight great scenic spots" and a center of local religious and cultural heritage. See the Hsinchu City county page for more on the region.
