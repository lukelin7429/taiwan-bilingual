About · 學校簡介

A coastal school with an athletic identity.

Qingquan Junior High was founded in 1968 near Qingshui's coastline, in a district long shaped by its identity as a fishing and coastal community facing the Taiwan Strait. Since 1993 the school has been designated a key national boxing development school, and it also fields competitive teams in kendo and cycling — giving the school a distinct athletic identity within the district.

清泉國中創立於民國57年（1968年），校址鄰近清水海岸，坐落於這個長期以漁業與濱海生活為特色、面向台灣海峽的區域。自民國82年（1993年）起，學校獲指定為全國拳擊發展重點學校，並設有劍道與自行車代表隊，形成校內鮮明的體育特色。
