About · 學校簡介

Born from a community petition.

Ciaotou Elementary opened on August 1, 1997, following a petition from local residents in 1994 — making it explicitly the most recently established elementary school in Ciaotou District. Its founding traces directly to Ciaotou's transformation from a sugar-industry town into a planned "new town" residential area during the 1990s.

橋頭國小成立於1997年（民國86年）8月1日，源自1994年當地居民的陳情，是橋頭區最晚設立的國小。學校的誕生與橋頭在1990年代從糖業小鎮轉型為規劃新市鎮的歷程直接相關。
