About · 學校簡介

A campus that feels like a small forest.

Dahu Elementary was founded in 1960 and is one of the smallest schools in Hsinchu City — its campus covers just 0.34 hectares, small enough that visitors often describe it as feeling like a small forest rather than a school. That intimacy shapes its entire approach: the school's guiding vision is to "keep a green seed in your heart, be a nature-practicing citizen," expressed through a distinctive "Travel-Reading the Land" curriculum that takes students out into the surrounding community and mountains on foot and by bicycle. With only about 54 students across 6 classes and 13 teachers, it is a genuinely small, place-based school in the hills of southern Xiangshan.

大湖國小創立於民國49年（1960年），是新竹市規模最小的學校之一——校地僅0.34公頃，讓人感覺更像一座小森林而非學校。這份親密感形塑了學校的核心理念：「心中留一顆綠色種子，做個實踐自然的公民」，並透過獨特的「走讀大地」課程，帶領學生以步行、單車走入社區與山林。目前僅約54名學生、6個班級、13位教師，是香山區南端山區裡真正的小校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌲 A Campus Like a Small Forest

如森林般的校園

At just 0.34 hectares, Dahu's campus is among the smallest in Hsinchu City — intimate, green, and shaped as much by its surrounding trees as by its buildings.

🚲 "Travel-Reading the Land"

走讀大地課程

A signature curriculum sends students out on foot and by bicycle to explore the community and nearby mountains — learning geography and ecology firsthand rather than from a textbook.

🌱 "A Green Seed in Your Heart"

心中留一顆綠色種子

The school's guiding vision — to raise "nature-practicing citizens" — runs through everything from its curriculum to its use of the surrounding forested land.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xiangshan, Hsinchu's coastal Minnan heartland.

🦪

Xiangshan District is Hsinchu City's coastal district, long shaped by Minnan fishing and oyster-farming traditions nearer the shore, while its southern hills — where Dahu Elementary sits — are given over to forest and farmland. The district's historic "mother school," Xiangshan Elementary, was founded in 1908 through the philanthropy of a local gentry family. See the Hsinchu City county page for more on the region.
