About · 學校簡介

A border-community school with a winning soccer team.

Guogou Junior High School began in 1968 as a branch campus of Budai Junior High serving the Guogou community, becoming an independent school on August 1, 1969, under founding principal Zhu Ruhai. Its campus sits just across the township line in neighboring Dongshi, reflecting how the Guogou community itself straddles the Budai–Dongshi border. Today the school is known for strong athletic programs — especially soccer, with recent county- and national-level results — alongside heavy use of digital learning platforms like Google Classroom, Pagamo, Kahoot, and Canva, and active career guidance, gender-equality, and anti-bullying programs.

過溝國中前身為布袋國中過溝分部，設立於民國57年（1968年），民國58年（1969年）8月1日獨立設校，首任校長為朱汝海。校地座落於鄰近的東石鄉，反映過溝聚落原本即橫跨布袋、東石兩鄉的地理特性。學校以足球等體育項目表現優異，近年在縣賽及全國賽事屢獲佳績，並大量運用Google Classroom、Pagamo、Kahoot、Canva等數位學習平台與雲端行政系統，同時積極推動生涯輔導、性別平等教育及反霸凌教育。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚽ A Standout Soccer Program

優異足球隊

The school has built strong athletic programs, notably soccer, with recent county- and national-level competition results that are a source of pride for this small coastal community.

💻 Digital-First Classrooms

數位化教學

Teachers make heavy use of Google Classroom, Pagamo, Kahoot, and Canva alongside cloud-based administrative systems, giving students in a remote coastal town access to modern digital-learning tools.

🌊 A Border-Straddling Campus

橫跨兩鄉的校區

Though administratively grouped with Budai-area schools, the campus itself sits in neighboring Dongshi Township — a reflection of how the historic Guogou fishing community spans both townships.

🤝 Guidance & Wellbeing Programs

輔導與品格教育

Active career guidance/counseling, gender equality education, and anti-bullying programs round out the school's support for students in this small rural community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A shared coastal, aquaculture community.

🦪

Serving the Guogou fishing and aquaculture community on the Budai–Dongshi coastal border, the school reflects the shared coastal and lagoon economy — oyster and milkfish farming — that spans this stretch of Chiayi's coastline, regardless of which township line runs through it.
