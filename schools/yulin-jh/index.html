About · 學校簡介

Founded to ease a crowded district.

Yulin Junior High was established in 1995 to relieve overcrowding after neighboring Shulin Junior High converted into a complete secondary school. Its founding created a clear north-south divide across Shulin's school-zoning map: schools north of the railway line feed into Yulin Junior High, while those to the south feed into Shulin Senior High's junior division.

育林國中創立於民國84年（1995年），因應樹林國中改制為完全中學後所產生的就學壓力而設立。創校後，樹林的學區劃分明確以鐵路線為界：北側學校學生升入育林國中，南側學校則升入樹林高中國中部。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚆 Born From a Railway Divide

因鐵路分界而生

The school's creation formalized a north-south split in Shulin's junior-high school zoning, with the railway line as the dividing line.

🏫 Relieving a Crowded District

紓解學區壓力

Yulin Junior High opened in 1995 specifically to absorb students after Shulin Junior High converted into a complete secondary school.

🤝 A Shared Address With Its Elementary Namesake

與育林國小同校址

The school shares its street address with Yulin Elementary School, suggesting a close, adjacent campus relationship between the two.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Divided by the tracks.

🚆

Shulin's rail line has long shaped daily life and school zoning in the district — Yulin Junior High's own founding was a direct response to that geography, formalizing which side of the tracks each neighborhood's students would attend. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
