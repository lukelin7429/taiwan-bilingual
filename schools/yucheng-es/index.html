About · 學校簡介

Home of a historic swimming gold.

Yucheng Elementary was founded in 1946, one of the two schools spun directly off the original Nangang Elementary as its 1945 "後山埤分教場" hillside branch, tracing back to Nangang's early farming-village school system. In the 1984–85 school year, it established a Ministry of Education-approved physical-education experimental class. Alumna Cheng Wan-jung won Taiwan's first-ever swimming gold medal at the 2009 East Asian Games (400m individual medley). The school began bilingual education programming in the 2020–21 school year, and it is WHO International Safe School certified and a designated Taipei family-education demonstration school.

玉成國小創立於民國35年（1946年），是南港國小1945年設立的「後山埤分教場」山坡分校直接獨立而成的兩校之一，源自南港早期農村學校體系。民國73學年度（1984–85年），學校設立教育部核定的體育實驗班。校友鄭莞融於2009年東亞運動會游泳項目（女子400公尺混合式）為台灣奪下史上第一面東亞運游泳金牌。學校自109學年度（2020–21年）起推動雙語教育課程，並獲世界衛生組織（WHO）國際安全學校認證，同時為台北市家庭教育示範學校。
