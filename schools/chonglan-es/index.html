Chonglan Elementary School

崇蘭國民小學

A Pingtung City public elementary school in Chonglan Village, one of the city's oldest Han-settlement neighborhoods, dating to the Qing-dynasty Qianlong era.

位於屏東市崇蘭里的公立國小，崇蘭里是市內最早開墾的漢人聚落之一，開發史可追溯至清代乾隆年間。

About · 學校簡介

A large, newer school in an old neighborhood.

Chonglan Elementary sits in Chonglan Village, on Guangdong Road in Pingtung City. Local news coverage describes it as one of Pingtung City's newer, larger consolidated schools, even though it serves a neighborhood with roughly 300 years of settlement history. Chonglan Village is home to the historic Xiao family ancestral mansion, founded by Xiao Weitian, who settled in the area during the Qing-dynasty Qianlong era — making the school a modern institution rooted in one of the city's oldest communities.

崇蘭國小位於屏東市崇蘭里廣東路。地方報導稱其為屏東市新成立的大型學校之一，然而所在的崇蘭里聚落開發史卻長達約三百年。崇蘭里以蕭氏家族古厝聞名，該古厝由蕭維天於清代乾隆年間定居此地時所建立——使這所現代學校座落於屏東市最古老的聚落之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Neighbor to a Historic Family Mansion

緊鄰蕭氏古厝

Chonglan Village is home to the Xiao family ancestral mansion, founded by Xiao Weitian during the Qing-dynasty Qianlong era — one of the city's most notable heritage sites.

三百年聚落歷史

Chonglan Village is described as having roughly 300 years of settlement history, making the school's neighborhood one of the oldest Han-Taiwanese communities in Pingtung City.

🏫 A Newer, Larger Consolidated School

屏東市新成立大型學校

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in one of Pingtung's oldest settlements.

🏘️

Chonglan Village is one of Pingtung City's oldest Han-settlement neighborhoods, tracing its founding to the Qing-dynasty Qianlong era (roughly 300 years ago) and home to the historic Xiao family ancestral mansion — a direct tie to the city's pre-Japanese-era Hakka and Hoklo settlement history.
