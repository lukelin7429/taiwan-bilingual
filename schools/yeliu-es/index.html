About · 學校簡介

Students become ambassadors for a world-famous coastline.

Yeliu Elementary School was founded in 1968 and sits right beside the world-famous Yehliu Geopark, home of Taiwan's iconic mushroom-rock coastline. The school runs a signature "Little Yehliu Docent" (小小野柳解說員) student guide program alongside a marine-education curriculum, turning its own students into ambassadors for the landmark on their doorstep. The program has earned the school a Ministry of Education Teaching Excellence Gold Award.

野柳國小創立於民國57年（1968年），校址緊鄰享譽國際的野柳地質公園——台灣最具代表性的奇岩海岸景觀所在地。學校推行特色的「小小野柳解說員」學生導覽計畫，並結合海洋教育課程，讓學生成為家門口這座地標的親善大使。此計畫更為學校贏得教育部教學卓越金質獎。
