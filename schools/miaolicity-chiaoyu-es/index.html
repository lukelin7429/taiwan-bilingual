About · 學校簡介

From flood-relief school to bilingual pioneer.

Chiaoyu Elementary School was founded in 1960, funded in part by donations from overseas Chinese communities following flood relief efforts in 1959. In 2022, the school transformed into an experimental school, becoming Taiwan's first Hakka-English bilingual "multiple intelligences" experimental elementary school. About 90% of its students are Hakka, and the school uses Hakka-English immersive teaching throughout the school day, alongside programs in table tennis, track, dance, mosaic-glass lamp making, and "mountain-town exploration."

僑育國小創立於民國49年（1960年），部分建校經費來自1959年八七水災後海外華僑的捐款。民國111年（2022年）轉型為實驗學校，成為全台首創的客語—英語雙語多元智能實驗國小。全校近九成學生為客家子弟，全天採客語—英語沉浸式教學，並開設桌球、田徑、舞蹈、馬賽克玻璃燈製作與「山城探索」等課程。
