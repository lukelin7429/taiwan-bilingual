Zhengyi Elementary School

正義國民小學

A performing-arts stronghold with choirs, orchestras, and guitar ensembles, born from one of Sanchong's earliest school divisions.

About · 學校簡介

Part of Sanchong's earliest wave of school-building.

Zhengyi Elementary was established in 1958 when 21 classes were split off from Sanchong Elementary School, part of the same early wave of divisions — alongside Sanguang, Houde, and Guangrong — that carved up the century-old institution as the district's population boomed after WWII. Today the school is known for an unusually broad performing-arts program, running a choir, a wind band, a string orchestra, and a guitar ensemble side by side, alongside competitive teams in baseball, soccer, table tennis, track and field, and girls' dodgeball.

正義國小成立於民國47年（1958年），由三重國小分出21個班級設校，是三光、厚德、光榮等校同一波分校歷史的一部分——這些學校皆源自戰後三重人口暴增，從這所百年老校陸續分出。如今學校以陣容多元的表演藝術聞名，合唱團、管樂團、絃樂團、吉他隊並存，同時也組有棒球、足球、桌球、田徑及女子躲避球等運動代表隊。
