Pinglin Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A small school reaching for big tech and global horizons.

Pinglin Elementary is a small school of about 329 students across 16 classes, one of several elementary schools scattered across Dalin Township's farming villages outside the old sugar-town core. It is known for two signature initiatives: "Tech Pinglin," which brings AI curriculum and VR-based teaching into the classroom, and "International Pinglin," which builds English proficiency through ICRT radio programs and cloud-based learning resources. The school also fields an unusually wide range of competitive teams, from taekwondo and soft tennis to taiko drumming and violin.

平林國小是一所約329名學生、16個班級的小型學校，是大林鎮舊糖業核心地帶外圍眾多鄉村小學之一。學校以兩大特色計畫聞名：結合AI課程與VR教學的「科技平林」，以及透過ICRT廣播與雲端學習資源提升英語能力的「國際平林」。此外，學校也組建了跆拳道、軟式網球、太鼓、小提琴等多樣化的校隊。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🤖 Tech Pinglin

科技平林

An AI curriculum and VR-based teaching initiative that brings emerging technology into daily classroom learning at this small rural school.

🌐 International Pinglin

國際平林

An English-development program built around ICRT radio programming and cloud learning resources, connecting students to the wider English-speaking world.

🥋 A Deep Bench of Competitive Teams

多元校隊陣容

Despite its small size, the school fields taekwondo, soft tennis, track and field, table tennis, recorder ensemble, taiko drumming, violin, and dance teams.

📖 Reading for Happiness

平林閱讀 預約幸福

The school's "Pinglin Reading, Booking Happiness" initiative promotes reading alongside recent honors in Taiwanese-language proficiency, go (weiqi), and aerobic karate.
