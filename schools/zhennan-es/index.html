Zhennan Elementary School

鎮南國民小學

A rural school serving four farming villages just south of central Douliu, historically rooted in agricultural community life.

服務斗六市中心以南四個農村里別的鄉間學校，歷史上以農業聚落為根基。

About · 學校簡介

Serving four villages south of the city center.

Zhennan Elementary's school zone covers four villages — Zhennan, Shekou, Longtan, and Jiadong — just south of central Douliu City. Historically an agricultural and rural-village school, it continues to serve as the educational anchor for these farming communities on the edge of the county seat. The school's exact founding date is not documented in available sources.

鎮南國小學區涵蓋斗六市中心以南的四個里別——鎮南、社口、龍潭與嘉東。學校歷史上以農業聚落型態為主，至今仍是這些縣治邊緣農村社區的教育重心。學校確切創校年份目前尚無資料可考。
