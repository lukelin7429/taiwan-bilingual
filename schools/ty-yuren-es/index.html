About · 學校簡介

A small coastal school investing in digital equity.

Yuren Elementary describes itself as a small, remote coastal school (臨海偏鄉小校) with over 70 years of history, located near the Guanyin Lotus Festival (觀音蓮花季) cultural attraction. Despite its size, the school has built out 1-to-1 digital infrastructure — campus-wide Wi-Fi, touchscreen displays in every classroom, and tablets for every student — specifically to narrow the urban-rural education gap. It also hosts a “Jing Si” reading corner (靜思閱讀書軒) for reading and character education, and runs Guanyin District's Senior Learning Center (樂齡學習中心) for lifelong and intergenerational learning.

育仁國小自我定位為「臨海偏鄉小校」，創校已逾70年（確切年份待考）。學校鄰近觀音蓮花季景點，儘管規模不大，卻已建置全校無線網路、每間教室觸控螢幕及一人一平板的數位設備，致力縮短城鄉教育落差。校內並設有「靜思閱讀書軒」推動閱讀與品格教育，同時承辦觀音區樂齡學習中心，服務全齡與跨世代學習。
