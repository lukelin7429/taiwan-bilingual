Jisui Junior High School

新北市立積穗國民中學

About · 學校簡介

From six borrowed classrooms to an international award.

Jisui Junior High School opened in 1971 with just six classes, initially borrowing classroom space from neighboring Jisui Elementary School. In 1973, it pioneered one of the area's earliest junior-high night and continuing-education programs. Decades later, in 2014, the school won an international Health-Promoting School award. Its name comes from the Jisui neighborhood, one of the paddy-field hamlets absorbed into Zhonghe's postwar urban sprawl.

積穗國中創立於民國60年（1971年），初期僅有六班，借用鄰近積穗國小的教室上課。民國62年（1973年），學校率先開辦地區內早期的國中夜間部／進修教育課程之一。多年後的2014年，學校榮獲國際健康促進學校獎項。校名源自積穗地區——這是中和戰後都市擴張過程中被納入市區的稻田聚落之一。
