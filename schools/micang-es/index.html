Micang Elementary School

米倉國民小學

A small, sea-facing hillside school near the Bali ferry pier, nicknamed the 'forest elementary school closest to the city.'

座落於八里渡船頭附近山坡的小型濱海學校，人稱「離城市最近的森林小學」。

About · 學校簡介

A forest school overlooking the river mouth.

Micang Elementary was founded in 1922. Perched on a hillside overlooking the Tamsui River mouth near the historic Bali ferry pier, the small school — with around 330 students — has earned the nickname "the forest elementary school closest to the city," and is recognized as a US-Taiwan Eco-Campus partner school.

米倉國小創立於1922年（日治時期）。校舍座落於淡水河口旁的山坡地，緊鄰歷史悠久的八里渡船頭，全校約330名學生，因而有「離城市最近的森林小學」美名，並獲認證為台美生態學校夥伴校。
