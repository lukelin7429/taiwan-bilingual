Dahu Junior High School

大湖國民中學

A small rural junior high in the heart of the "Strawberry Kingdom" that produced back-to-back top scorers on Taiwan's national exam in 2023 and 2024.

座落於「草莓王國」核心地帶的小型國中，連續於2023、2024年產出全國會考頂尖成績學生。

About · 學校簡介

Small school, big results.

Dahu Junior High was founded in 1970 in the heart of Taiwan's strawberry-growing region. With about 79 students and 17 teachers, it is a small rural school — yet despite being remote, it produced back-to-back top scorers in Taiwan's national Comprehensive Assessment Program in both 2023 and 2024, a striking achievement for local farming-family students that has become a source of community pride.

大湖國中創立於民國59年（1970年），位於台灣草莓主要產區的核心地帶。全校約79名學生、17位教師，是一所小型鄉村學校，卻在2023與2024年連續兩年產出全國會考頂尖成績的學生，對於在地農家子弟而言是相當亮眼的成就，也成為社區引以為傲的一頁。
