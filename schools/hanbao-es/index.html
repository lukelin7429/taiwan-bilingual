Hanbao Elementary School

漢寶國民小學

A coastal public elementary school in Fangyuan Township, rooted since the Japanese era in the fishing and farming community of Hanbao.

About · 學校簡介

A coastal school shaped by change and renewal.

Hanbao Elementary traces its roots to 1941, when it opened as "Bazhou National School," a branch school established during the Japanese colonial era. It became an independent school in 1951 and took on its current name, Hanbao National School, in 1957. The school marked its 70th anniversary in 2001 with a round of campus renovations, and today continues to serve the children of Hanbao Village, a coastal community on Changhua's western shore.

漢寶國小的歷史可追溯至民國30年（1941年），創校之初為日治時期設立的「八洲國民學校」分校。民國40年（1951年）獨立設校，民國46年（1957年）更名為漢寶國民小學。學校於民國90年（2001年）舉辦建校七十週年慶祝活動，並進行校園整建，至今持續服務彰化縣芳苑鄉漢寶村這個西部沿海聚落的學童。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🦁 Lion Dance Champions

舞獅雙料冠軍

The school's lion dance team took home dual championships in a county-wide competition in 2021, a proud tradition alongside consistently strong showings in English recitation contests.

🌊 A Marine-Based Curriculum

海洋基地課程

Reflecting its coastal setting, the school runs a dedicated marine education program, connecting classroom learning to the tides, wetlands, and working shoreline just beyond the schoolyard.

☀️ A Modernizing Campus

校園設施升級

Between 2009 and 2022, the school added solar panels and upgraded its digital and smart-classroom systems, steadily modernizing facilities for a new generation of students.

🌾 Learning From the Land

在地語言與農業教育

Local-language classes and hands-on agricultural education give students a direct connection to the land and traditions of the Fangyuan coastal community they call home.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Hanbao, a village between farmland and shore.

🌊

Hanbao Village sits within Fangyuan Township, a coastal district on Changhua's western edge where fishing, oyster farming, and wetland ecology shape daily life. The school's marine-based curriculum and agricultural education programs are designed to root students firmly in this land-and-sea community. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
