Jiazhong Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

From a branch class to an independent school.

Jiazhong Elementary began in September 1953 (民國42年) as a branch class of Hejian (賀建) Elementary called "甲中分班," expanding into a full branch campus in 1954 and becoming an independent school in August 1957 (民國46年); it was renamed 甲中國民小學 in 1968. Because it draws students citywide rather than from a fixed catchment, it is classified as a "free enrollment" (自由學區) school — one of several small Xiaying elementary schools that trace their origins back to larger parent schools in the district's farming villages, a pattern typical of how rural Tainan's one-room branch schools grew into today's independent campuses.

甲中國小前身為民國42年（1953年）9月設立的賀建國小「甲中分班」，1954年擴編為分校，民國46年（1957年）8月獨立設校，1968年改稱甲中國民小學。由於招生不限固定學區、而是全市開放入學，該校被歸類為「自由學區」學校——是下營區數所源自鄰近農村母校分班、分校，最終獨立成校的小型國小之一，這種發展軌跡在台南鄉村地區相當典型。
