Wuhan Junior High School

武漢國中

One of Longtan's newest junior highs, opened in 2004 to serve the growing Wuhan neighborhood beyond the historic town center.

龍潭最年輕的國中之一，2004年設校，服務逐漸擴張的武漢地區。

About · 學校簡介

Built to serve a growing Longtan.

Wuhan Junior High's preparatory office was established in 2002 at Longtan Junior High, and the school formally opened in 2004 with nine first-year classes, initially borrowing classrooms at Longtan Junior High before its own campus was completed in July 2005. In 2006 it was approved to add a physical-education special class and a multi-learning center, and in recent years it has opened a new library and activity center building to expand its facilities. The school was renamed from Taoyuan County to Taoyuan City designation in December 2014.

武漢國中的籌備處於民國91年（2002年）在龍潭國中成立，並於民國93年（2004年）正式開學，首屆招收九個班，初期借用龍潭國中教室上課，直到民國94年（2005年）7月自有校舍完工。民國95年（2006年）獲准增設體育班及多元學習中心，近年更新建圖書館暨活動中心大樓，擴充校園設施。民國103年（2014年）12月因桃園縣升格直轄市而改為市立學校。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Built for a growing Longtan.

🏘️

As one of Longtan's newest junior highs, Wuhan Junior High was built to serve the growing Wuhan neighborhood as the district's population expanded beyond the historic town center — reflecting Longtan's transition from a rural Hakka township to a denser Taoyuan suburb.
