Hemei Elementary School

和美國民小學

One of Gongliao's oldest schools, built into the cliffs of Longdong Bay and known today for its ocean and marine education program.

About · 學校簡介

A century-old school on the cliffs of Longdong Bay.

Hemei Elementary was founded in 1899, with a permanent campus completed in 1905, making it one of the oldest schools in Gongliao District. Sited directly on Longdong Bay, the school has built a distinctive ocean and marine education program around its coastal setting, including snorkeling and rock-climbing activities that make use of the surrounding sea cliffs.

和美國小創立於1899年（日治時期），並於1905年完成永久校舍，是貢寮區歷史最悠久的學校之一。學校座落於龍洞灣畔，發展出以海洋為核心的特色教育課程，結合浮潛、攀岩等活動，充分運用周邊海蝕地形。

🤿 Ocean and Marine Education

The school runs a signature ocean and marine education program, including snorkeling and rock climbing, that makes direct use of the sea cliffs surrounding Longdong Bay.

Founded in 1899 with a permanent campus completed in 1905, Hemei is among the longest-standing schools in Gongliao District, spanning well over a century of local history.
