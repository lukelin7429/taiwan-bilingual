About · 學校簡介

A small school with a green, bilingual outlook.

Jiujiong Elementary is a small school serving Linnei Township, with a resource classroom supporting students who need special-education services. Beyond its core academics, the school runs an oral-English proficiency program and mother-tongue instruction to strengthen students' language skills in both English and Taiwanese, and it has embraced ecological and organic campus initiatives that connect learning to the natural environment around the school.

九芎國小是服務林內鄉的小型學校，設有資源班以支援有特殊教育需求的學生。除核心學科外，學校推動英語口說能力課程及母語教學，強化學生的英語與台語能力，並積極推行生態、有機校園理念，讓學習與周邊自然環境緊密結合。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Linnei, land of sugar, bamboo, and heritage.

🎋

Jiujiong Elementary is located in Linnei Township along the Zhuoshui River area, a district historically shaped by the Lin Nei Sugar Refinery (林內糖廠) and known today for its bamboo forest and the Wu Family Mansion (林內濟公廟/吳氏宗祠). See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
