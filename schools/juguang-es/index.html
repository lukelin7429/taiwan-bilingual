Juguang Elementary School

新北市板橋區莒光國民小學

About · 學校簡介

From 45 classes to one of Banqiao's biggest schools.

Juguang Elementary opened on 1 August 1978 under its first principal, Lin Xiudi, with 45 classes. It grew into one of Banqiao's largest schools, peaking at 117 classes, and today hosts the New Taipei City Calligraphy Teaching Resource Center. Its scouting program is especially strong, having won the overall championship at the national Scout Jamboree in 2018.

莒光國小創校於民國67年（1978年）8月1日，首任校長林秀地，創校初期即有45班規模，後成長為板橋規模最大的學校之一，班級數曾高達117班。校內設有新北市書法教學資源中心，童軍活動表現亮眼，曾於2018年榮獲全國童軍大露營總冠軍。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A Cold War slogan, carried in a school name.

🎖️

The name 莒光 references the Cold War-era national slogan "勿忘在莒" ("never forget Ju"), a patriotic naming convention widely applied to Taiwanese schools built during the 1970s nation-building period. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
