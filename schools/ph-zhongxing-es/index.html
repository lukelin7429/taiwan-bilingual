About · 學校簡介

Split off from the county's mother school, now a pioneer of its own.

Zhongxing Elementary was formed on August 1, 1958, when nine classes were split off from Magong Elementary School and combined with three newly recruited classes to form an independent school, renamed Zhongxing Elementary in 1968. In 2007 it launched the "Chung Hsing English Living Village," an English-immersion program that became one of the county's early models for experiential language learning, and in 2017 it marked its 60th anniversary with a Maker-space partnership with National Kaohsiung Normal University.

中興國小成立於民國47年（1958年）8月1日，由馬公國小分出9個班級，加上新招收的3個班級共同組成獨立學校，並於民國57年（1968年）更名為中興國小。學校於2007年成立「中興英語生活村」沉浸式英語課程，成為澎湖縣早期體驗式語言學習的示範，並於2017年慶祝創校六十週年，與國立高雄師範大學合作推動自造者（Maker）教育。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗣️ The Chung Hsing English Living Village

中興英語生活村

Launched in 2007, this immersion program puts students in real-life English scenarios, making Zhongxing an early model for experiential language learning in the county.

🛠️ A Maker-Space Partnership

自造者教育合作

To mark its 60th anniversary in 2017, the school partnered with National Kaohsiung Normal University on Maker-space education, bringing hands-on STEM learning to students.

🏫 Split From the County's Mother School

源自馬公國小分校

Zhongxing was formed in 1958 from nine classes split off Magong Elementary, part of the wave of school-building that grew out of Penghu's oldest school.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Magong, Penghu's island capital.

🌊

Zhongxing Elementary is located on Guangfu Road in Magong City, the seat of Penghu County government and the archipelago's largest urban center, shaped by generations of fishing and seafaring life. See the Penghu county page for more on the region.
