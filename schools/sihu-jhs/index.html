Sihu Junior High School

About · 學校簡介

Born of a national education reform.

Sihu Junior High was founded in 1968, as Taiwan rolled out nine-year compulsory education nationwide, initially borrowing classrooms from Sihu Elementary School next door. Its Feisha division later split off to become the independent Feisha Junior High in 1972. In 1982, the school also opened a continuing-education program aimed at coastal-area adults who had missed out on schooling earlier in life.

四湖國中創立於民國57年（1968年），正值台灣全面推行九年國民義務教育之際，初期借用鄰近四湖國小的教室上課。其飛沙分部於民國61年（1972年）獨立為飛沙國中。民國71年（1982年），學校另開辦補習教育班，服務沿海地區失學的成年人。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📖 Founded in the 1968 Reform Era

九年國教改革中誕生

Sihu Junior High opened in 1968 as part of Taiwan's nationwide shift to nine-year compulsory education, starting out in borrowed classrooms.

🌱 A Division That Grew Independent

飛沙分部獨立設校

The school's Feisha division became its own independent school, Feisha Junior High, in 1972 — a sign of the township's growing student population.

🎓 Second-Chance Education for Adults

補習教育班服務失學成人

In 1982, the school opened a continuing-education program for coastal-area adults who had missed schooling, extending its mission beyond its teenage students.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where rice, garlic, and the sea meet.

🌾

As the township's junior high, the school draws students from Sihu's rice- and garlic-farming villages as well as its coastal fishing communities, embodying the area's blend of rural agriculture and seaside life. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
