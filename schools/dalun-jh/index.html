About · 學校簡介

From girls' school to basketball powerhouse.

Dalun Junior High School was founded in 1960, originally as the girls-only Miaoli Girls' Junior Middle School, before evolving into today's coeducational junior high in the heart of Miaoli City. The school has become a perennial contender in the JHBL, Taiwan's national junior-high basketball league, and has long promoted local and indigenous-culture programs. A major campus rebuild broke ground in October 2025, marking a new chapter for the school.

大倫國中創立於民國49年（1960年），初創時為苗栗女子初級中學，後來逐漸發展為今日的男女合校國中，位於苗栗市中心。學校長年是全國國中籃球聯賽（JHBL）的常勝軍，並長期推動在地與原住民文化課程。校舍重建工程已於2025年10月動土，為學校揭開新的篇章。
