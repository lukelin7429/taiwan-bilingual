Pijiao Elementary School

埤腳國民小學

A small rural elementary school serving farming communities on the southwestern edge of Tuku's old soy-sauce trading town.

About · 學校簡介

A satellite school that grew into its own community.

Pijiao Elementary began in 1921 as a satellite classroom of Tuku Public School, becoming an independent school in 1940. It is a small school today, with about 6 classes and under 40 students, serving the Pijiao, Fuchao, and Qihu communities — farming families on the southwestern edge of Tuku Town.

埤腳國小創立於1921年（民國10年），最初是土庫公學校的分教場，1940年獨立設校。目前是一所小型學校，約有6個班級、不到40名學生，服務埤腳、複朝、崎湖等以務農為主的聚落，位於土庫鎮西南邊。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 A Century of Farming Roots

世代務農的社區

Founded in 1921 as a branch of Tuku Public School, Pijiao Elementary has served the farming families of Pijiao, Fuchao, and Qihu for over a century.

🏫 A Small, Close-Knit Campus

小而緊密的校園

With about 6 classes and under 40 students, Pijiao offers the kind of close teacher-student relationships typical of Yunlin's small rural elementary schools.

🏘️ Three Villages, One School

跨村里服務範圍

The school draws students from three neighboring communities — Pijiao, Fuchao, and Qihu — knitting together the rural southwest corner of Tuku Town.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Farmland beside a soy-sauce town.

🌾

Located in the farming communities on Tuku's southwestern edge, the school reflects the rice- and produce-farming livelihoods typical of the townships surrounding Tuku's old soy-sauce trading center — a market street famed for centuries-old soy-sauce and sesame-oil brewing.
