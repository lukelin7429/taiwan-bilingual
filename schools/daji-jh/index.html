About · 學校簡介

A campus rebuilt after disaster.

Daji Junior High began life in 1969 as "民雄國中大崎分班," a branch class of Minsyong Junior High, was upgraded to a full branch campus in 1970, and finally became an independent school in 1975. Its most defining chapter came after the devastating 1999 (921) earthquake, when the campus was rebuilt with support from the Tzu Chi Foundation's "Hope Project" (慈濟希望工程), making it a documented landmark of Taiwan's post-earthquake school reconstruction effort. Today the school runs a visual-arts talent class with an active social-media presence, has earned recognition in the National Geography Knowledge Competition, English competitions, and Mother Language Day arts contests, and maintains a fully wheelchair-accessible campus layout.

大吉國中的前身是民國58年（1969年）成立的「民雄國中大崎分班」，於民國59年（1970年）升格為分部，最終於民國64年（1975年）獨立設校。學校最重要的一頁，是在1999年921大地震重創後，獲得慈濟基金會「希望工程」援建重建，成為台灣震後校園重建的重要地標之一。如今學校設有活躍於社群媒體的美術才能班，並在全國地理知識競賽、英語競賽及母語日藝文活動中屢獲肯定，校園也採全面無障礙設計。

Its 921-earthquake reconstruction via Tzu Chi's Hope Project makes Daji Junior High a physical landmark of Taiwan's post-1999 school-rebuilding effort in rural Chiayi, in the same farming district that gave rise to nearby Daqi (大崎) village. See the Chiayi County page for more on the region.
