Zhongli Junior High School

中壢國民中學

Despite its name, this nearly 90-year-old school sits in Pingzhen District — tracing back to a 1934 agricultural training school for the district's farming communities.

校名雖為「中壢」，校址卻位於平鎮區——創校近90年，前身為服務地方農村的農業訓練學校。

About · 學校簡介

A name that doesn't match its home district.

Zhongli Junior High was founded in 1934, during the Japanese colonial era, as the "Hsinchu Prefecture Zhongli Agricultural Village National School" — despite the Zhongli name, the campus has always stood in what is now Pingzhen District. In 1941 it became an agricultural vocational school, and after Japan's surrender in 1945 was reorganized as the "Hsinchu County Zhongli Junior Agricultural Vocational School," upgraded to a senior agricultural vocational school in 1951. In July 1968, as Taiwan rolled out nine-year compulsory education nationwide, the school converted into a standard junior high school, and a supplementary night-school division was added in 1973. With roughly 90 years of continuous institutional history, it is one of the oldest schools in the district.

中壢國中創立於1934年日治時期，初名「新竹州中壢農村國民學校」，校名雖冠「中壢」，校址卻始終位於今日的平鎮區。1941年改制為農業實踐學校，1945年日本戰敗後改組為「新竹縣立中壢初級農業職業學校」，1951年升格為高級農業職業學校。1968年7月，配合全國實施九年國民義務教育，改制為一般國民中學，並於1973年增設補校（夜間部）。學校擁有近90年的連續辦學歷史，是平鎮區歷史最悠久的學校之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 An Agricultural School's Legacy

農業學校的前身

Founded in 1934 as an agricultural training school, the campus reflects Pingzhen's historically farming-based economy before the postwar industrial-park boom transformed the district.

📜 Nearly 90 Years of History

近九十年校史

Through name changes spanning the Japanese colonial era, postwar reorganization, and the 1968 shift to nine-year compulsory education, the school has operated continuously for close to nine decades.

🌙 A Long-Running Night School

附設補校

A supplementary night-school division, added in 1973, has given adult and working students in the area access to continuing junior-high education for decades.

🏷️ A Name That Doesn't Match Its Address

校名與校址不符

Despite being named for neighboring Zhongli, the campus has always been located within Pingzhen District — a quirk of its founding-era administrative boundaries.

Local Culture · 在地文化

An agricultural school rooted in Pingzhen's farming past.

🌾

Zhongli Junior High's roots as an agricultural training school reflect Pingzhen's historically farming-based economy, long before the postwar industrial parks that reshaped the district's economy and population. Its near-90-year history makes it a living thread connecting Pingzhen's rural past to its urbanized present.
