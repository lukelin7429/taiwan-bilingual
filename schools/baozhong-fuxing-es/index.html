About · 學校簡介

A feeder school for Baozhong's farming villages.

Fuxing Elementary is one of four feeder elementary schools that send graduates on to Baozhong Junior High School, forming part of the township's small local education network. In 2017, the school faced a serious test when a nearby drainage canal overflowed during flash flooding, forcing an emergency school closure — a reminder of the challenges rural schools in low-lying farmland can face.

復興國小是褒忠國中四所學區小學之一，構成當地小型教育網絡的一部分。2017年，學校曾面臨嚴峻考驗：鄰近排水渠道因暴雨溢流，導致學校緊急停課——這也提醒了低窪農地鄉村學校可能面對的挑戰。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Baozhong, home of Yunlin's pickled mustard greens.

A feeder school for Baozhong's farming villages, Fuxing Elementary sits amid the rice, peanut, and garlic fields that define this stretch of the Chianan Plain. Baozhong Township is also well known across Taiwan for suan cai (pickled mustard greens) production. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
