Yongji Junior High School

臺北市永吉國民中學

Established in 1977 to serve a rapidly growing Songshan District, this junior high sits about 100 meters from Songshan Railway/MRT Station.

About · 學校簡介

Born from a booming 1970s Songshan District.

Yongji Junior High School was established on July 1, 1977, with planning having begun the previous year under founding principal Chen Bidu in response to rapid population growth in the then-Songshan District. Note: since a 2022 merger created the separate Shuangyong Elementary School from the former Yongchun and Yongji elementary schools, Yongji Junior High School itself remains a distinct, still-independent institution — unrelated in current form to that elementary-level merger. Under its second principal, Zhao Jilin, who took over in 1988, the school expanded its student-life programs and teaching facilities.

永吉國中於1977年（民國66年）7月1日正式成立，前一年（1976年）8月即由創校校長陳必讀展開籌備工作，以因應當時松山區人口快速成長的需求。（附註：2022年由原永春國小與永吉國小合併而成的雙永國小，是另一所獨立的國小層級學校，與永吉國中並無隸屬關係，永吉國中至今仍為獨立招生的國中。）1988年起，第二任校長趙吉林任內持續擴充學生活動與教學設施。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚉 Steps from Songshan Station

鄰近松山車站

The campus sits about 100 meters from Songshan Railway Station and Songshan MRT Station, roughly a 10-minute walk away, at the historic rail gateway into the Xinyi/Songshan commercial corridor.

🏀 A Basketball Venue

籃球錦標賽場地

The school's 4th-floor activity-center gymnasium has hosted city-level secondary-school basketball championship games.

📈 Born from Population Growth

因應人口成長而生

Founded in 1977 under planning that began the year before, the school was created specifically to serve Songshan District's rapidly growing population in the 1970s.

🏫 A Distinct Institution from Shuangyong

與雙永國小為不同學校

Despite the similar name, Yongji Junior High is a separate, still-independent junior high — not to be confused with the newly merged Shuangyong Elementary School nearby.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A rail gateway into modern Xinyi.

🚉

Its proximity to Songshan Station situates Yongji Junior High at the historic rail gateway into what became the Xinyi/Songshan commercial corridor. Established in the 1970s as the district's population surged, the school's founding is itself a record of the same urbanization wave that eventually produced the high-rises of the modern Xinyi Planning District.
