About · 學校簡介

Over a century old, with an art program of its own.

Xinshe Elementary opened on April 1, 1916, as a branch of Shulingtou Public School, giving it more than a century of continuous history. Since 1985, the school has been home to the only elementary art-talent class in Hsinchu County, a distinction that has shaped its identity ever since. The school regularly co-hosts joint graduation art exhibitions with the art program at nearby Chu-Pei Junior High, giving students a showcase that spans elementary and secondary education.

新社國小於1916年4月1日以樹林頭公學校分教場之姿創校，至今已有逾百年歷史。自1985年起，學校設立全新竹縣唯一的國小美術班，長期形塑學校特色。學校也經常與鄰近竹北國中美術班合辦畢業美展，讓國小與國中學生的作品有共同展出的舞台。
