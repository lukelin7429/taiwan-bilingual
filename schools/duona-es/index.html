About · 學校簡介

Nearly a century in Kungadavane.

Duona Elementary School was founded in October 1926, during the Japanese colonial era, as the "Dona Type-A Aboriginal Children's Education Institute." It became Dona Village National School in 1945, became independent in January 1952, took the name Duona Elementary School in 1968, and was renamed for Kaohsiung City in 2010. In 2004, it was designated an "Indigenous key school," and in 2017 it transformed into an official Rukai-language experimental school, also known as the Kungadavane Rukai Ethnic Experimental Elementary School — Kungadavane being the Rukai name for Duona village.

多納國小創校於大正15年（1926年）10月，日本統治時期名為「多納甲種蕃童教育所」，民國34年（1945年）改為多納村國民學校，民國41年（1952年）1月獨立設校，民國57年（1968年）改名多納國小，民國99年（2010年）改隸高雄市。民國93年（2004年）獲指定為「原住民重點學校」，民國106年（2017年）轉型為魯凱族語實驗學校，亦稱「Kungadavane多納魯凱族實驗國民小學」——Kungadavane正是魯凱族對多納部落的稱呼。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Kungadavane, a classic Rukai village.

🪨

Duona, known in Rukai as Kungadavane, is one of the classic Rukai Indigenous villages of Maolin District, known for its traditional stone-slab architecture. The school's 2017 conversion into a Rukai-language immersion school reflects the community's own effort at cultural and linguistic continuity. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
