About · 學校簡介

A small school with a big commitment to local memory.

Jinshan Elementary is a very small, remote rural school tucked into the hills of Guanxi Township. Despite its modest size, the school maintains dedicated websites for local and indigenous-culture education and environmental education, reflecting a deliberate effort to document and pass on the character of its surrounding community even as the area's population has thinned over the decades.

錦山國小是一所座落於關西鎮山區、規模極小的偏遠學校。儘管校地不大，學校仍用心經營在地與原住民文化教育網站，以及環境教育網站，展現出即使在地人口逐年減少的情況下，仍積極記錄並傳承周邊社區特色的用心。
