About · 學校簡介

A branch school in the hills above the reservoir.

Beiliao Elementary School was originally established in 1921 as the “Beiliao Branch School” (北寮分教場) of Nanhua Public School, when that school was renamed amid administrative reorganization. Located in the hills above Nanhua Reservoir, the school today operates a kindergarten branch (瑞峰分班) in cooperation with neighboring Ruifeng Elementary School. Its lunch program uses only domestically sourced pork and beef, and the school website holds AA web-accessibility certification.

北寮國小最初於民國10年（1921年）設立為「北寮分教場」，隸屬於當時改名的南化公學校。學校位於南化水庫上游山區，現與鄰近的瑞峰國小合作，共同經營幼兒園「瑞峰分班」。校園午餐全面採用國產豬肉與牛肉，網站並取得AA無障礙網站標章。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌊 Above the Nanhua Reservoir Watershed

南化水庫上游山區

Located in the hills above Nanhua Reservoir, the school's small size reflects the scattered mountain-village settlement pattern typical of this reservoir watershed district.

👶 A Shared Kindergarten Branch

與瑞峰國小合辦幼兒園

The school operates a kindergarten branch (瑞峰分班) in cooperation with neighboring Ruifeng Elementary School, a practical solution for serving thinly populated mountain communities.

🌱 Born as a Branch School

源自分教場

Originally established in 1921 as the “Beiliao Branch School” of Nanhua Public School, the campus grew from a small branch into today's independent elementary school.

🥩 Domestically Sourced School Meals

校園午餐國產肉品

School meals use only domestically sourced pork and beef, and the school website holds AA-level web-accessibility certification.
