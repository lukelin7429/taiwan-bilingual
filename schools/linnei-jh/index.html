Linnei Junior High School

林內國民中學

A public junior high in Linnei Township, known for a specialized shooting and archery program and hands-on vocational training.

座落於雲林縣林內鄉的公立國中，以射擊、射箭體育專項及技職技能訓練聞名。

About · 學校簡介

Where athletics meets hands-on skills.

Linnei Junior High serves students in Linnei Township with a distinctive combination of specialized athletics and practical vocational training. The school runs a dedicated shooting and archery program — a less common offering among Taiwan's junior highs — alongside food-preparation and woodworking skills courses that give students hands-on experience beyond the standard academic curriculum. The school also maintains ongoing Taiwanese-language and cultural preservation activities.

林內國中服務雲林縣林內鄉學子，結合體育專項訓練與實用技職課程為一大特色。學校設有射擊、射箭專項運動代表隊，在台灣國中相對少見；同時開設餐飲烹調與木工等技能訓練課程，讓學生在正規學科之外累積實作經驗。此外，學校也持續推動台語與在地文化保存相關活動。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎯 A Shooting and Archery Program

射擊射箭專項運動

The school runs a specialized shooting and archery athletics program, a distinctive offering that gives students a competitive outlet uncommon among Taiwan's junior highs.

🔨 Hands-On Vocational Training

餐飲與木工技能課程

Students can take food-preparation and woodworking courses, building practical skills that complement the academic curriculum and open pathways toward vocational interests.

🗣️ Keeping Taiwanese Language Alive

台語文化保存活動

The school maintains ongoing Taiwanese-language and cultural preservation activities, helping students stay connected to local linguistic heritage.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Linnei, land of sugar, bamboo, and heritage.

🎋

Linnei Junior High sits in Linnei Township along the Zhuoshui River area, a district historically shaped by the Lin Nei Sugar Refinery (林內糖廠) and known today for its bamboo forest and the Wu Family Mansion (林內濟公廟/吳氏宗祠). See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
