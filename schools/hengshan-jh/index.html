Hengshan Junior High School

橫山國民中學

About · 學校簡介

Learning that reaches beyond the classroom.

Hengshan Junior High serves students along the Zhongfeng Road corridor in Hengshan Township, a rural district in Hsinchu County best known for the historic Neiwan Old Street. The school pairs everyday academics with digital learning platforms and career-exploration programs that take students out into the community, including activities tied to nearby Neiwan — the township's signature tourist destination on the old branch rail line.

橫山國中座落於橫山鄉中豐路二段一帶，是新竹縣以內灣老街聞名的鄉村地區。學校將日常學業與數位學習平台（如因材網、Pagamo）結合，並推動生涯探索課程，帶領學生走入社區，包括結合鄰近內灣——橫山鄉在舊內灣支線鐵路上最具代表性的觀光景點——所規劃的活動。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚉 Career Exploration at Neiwan

結合內灣老街的生涯探索

Career-exploration field trips connect classroom learning to nearby Neiwan Old Street, giving students hands-on exposure to the township's tourism and small-business economy.

💻 Everyday Digital Learning

數位學習平台日常化

Students regularly use adaptive digital platforms such as AILEAD365 and Pagamo, alongside environmental education woven into the curriculum.

🎭 Life Skills Through Drama

戲劇與情感教育課程

Life-skills programs use drama and emotional-education activities alongside career seminars featuring visiting professionals, helping students imagine future paths.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Beside the old branch line.

🚂

Hengshan Township is home to Neiwan Old Street (內灣老街), the terminus of the historic Neiwan branch railway and one of northern Taiwan's most visited old-street destinations, with timber-era shopfronts, hakka snacks, and mountain scenery drawing visitors year-round.
