Huilai Elementary School

惠來國民小學

A public elementary school that grew out of Zhongxi Elementary's expansion during Huwei's 1960s sugar-boom years.

1960年代虎尾糖業繁榮期，自中溪國小擴校而生的公立國小。

About · 學校簡介

From branch campus to independent school.

Huilai Elementary School was established in September 1961 as a branch of Zhongxi Elementary School, becoming fully independent in September 1964. Its founding reflects Huwei's 1960s population growth, part of the broader sugar-boom era that reshaped the township. The school's namesake Huilai area has long been tied to the surrounding sugarcane farmland.

惠來國小於民國50年（1961年）9月以中溪國小分校形式成立，民國53年（1964年）9月正式獨立設校。其創校背景反映出虎尾鎮1960年代因糖業繁榮帶動的人口成長。「惠來」地名長期與周邊的甘蔗田農業地帶相連結。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 From Branch to Independent School

分校獨立設校歷程

Huilai began as a branch of Zhongxi Elementary in 1961 and became fully independent just three years later in 1964, a common pattern among Huwei's growing schools.

📈 A Child of the 1960s Sugar Boom

1960年代糖業成長期

The school's founding coincides with Huwei's 1960s population growth, a period tightly connected to the ongoing prosperity of the local sugar industry.

🌾 Rooted in Sugarcane Farmland

惠來地區甘蔗田淵源

The Huilai area surrounding the school has long been tied to sugarcane farmland, part of the agricultural base that once fed the Huwei Sugar Refinery.
