About · 學校簡介

A school rooted in veteran housing communities.

Chun'an Elementary began in 1964 as a branch of Wenshan Elementary School, becoming independent in 1966. Its founding traces directly to the growth of post-war army-veteran housing communities (陸軍眷舍) that settled in Nantun during the 1950s and ’60s.

春安國小於民國53年（1964年）以文山國民學校分校形式成立，1966年獨立設校。學校的創立直接源自1950、60年代南屯區陸軍眷舍聚落人口成長的歷史背景。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Settled by Taiwan's post-war veterans.

🎖️

Chun'an Elementary's founding traces directly to the settlement of army-veteran housing communities (陸軍眷舍) in Nantun during the 1950s and '60s, part of the broader post-war migration that reshaped many Taiwanese neighborhoods. See the Taichung page for more on the region.
