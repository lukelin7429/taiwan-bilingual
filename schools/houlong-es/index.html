About · 學校簡介

A historic anchor for the township seat.

Houlong Elementary is one of the township's oldest and largest elementary schools, serving the community from the town's administrative center. Its Japanese-style teacher dormitory is officially registered as a historical building by Taiwan's Bureau of Cultural Heritage, a physical marker of the school's long presence in the area — its early 20th-century origins are suggested by this protected structure, even though the exact founding date is not documented in available records.

後龍國小是後龍鎮歷史最悠久、規模最大的國小之一，座落於鎮治所在地服務地方社區。學校的日式教師宿舍已由文化部文化資產局登錄為歷史建築，是學校在當地悠久歷史的實體見證——雖然確切創校年份未見於現有資料，但這棟受保護的建築暗示其源自二十世紀初期。
