About · 學校簡介

The township's founding school.

Tongluo Elementary was founded in 1899 (Meiji 32) during the Japanese colonial era as "Tongluowan Public School" (銅鑼灣公學校), making it one of the oldest schools in the township. Like many schools of its era, it was renamed multiple times through the colonial and postwar periods. Today the school's campus covers approximately 3.3 hectares and enjoys a scenic setting beside Niubei Mountain (牛背山).

銅鑼國小創立於日治明治32年（1899年），創校時稱為「銅鑼灣公學校」，是銅鑼鄉歷史最悠久的學校之一。如同同時期的許多學校，銅鑼國小歷經日治與戰後時期多次更名。如今校地面積約3.3公頃，緊鄰牛背山，景致優美。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Founded in 1899

創校於1899年

As "Tongluowan Public School," this is one of the oldest schools in the township, tracing back to the earliest years of Japanese colonial-era education.

⛰️ Beside Niubei Mountain

緊鄰牛背山

The school's roughly 3.3-hectare campus sits scenically beside Niubei ("Ox Back") Mountain, one of the notable landscape features of Tongluo Township.

🟠 Anchoring Persimmon Country

柿餅之鄉的核心學校

As the township's founding school, Tongluo Elementary anchors a community renowned nationwide for its Hakka persimmon-drying craft, especially visible each autumn.
