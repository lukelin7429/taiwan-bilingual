About · 學校簡介

A near-century of history and a national basketball name.

Datong Elementary School was founded in 1930 under Japanese rule as 苗栗第二公學校 ("Miaoli Second Public School"), giving it nearly a century of continuous history in Miaoli City. The school today houses an art-talent class and fields one of the top-ranked elementary school basketball teams in Taiwan. Its facilities include a library, playground, basketball courts, and an all-weather gymnasium.

大同國小創立於1930年（昭和5年），日治時期名為「苗栗第二公學校」，在苗栗市已有近百年不間斷的校史。學校目前設有美術資優班，籃球隊更是全台頂尖的國小球隊之一。校內設施包括圖書館、操場、籃球場與全天候體育館。
