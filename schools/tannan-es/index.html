About · 學校簡介

Rebuilt from ruin into a landmark of Bunun identity.

Tannan Elementary School began in 1951 as a branch of Dili Elementary School, becoming independent in 1958 and taking its current name in 1968. The school's history took a dramatic turn when the 1999 921 earthquake completely destroyed its original campus. In the rebuilding that followed in 2000, funded by the Hao Ran Foundation, architects chose not to erect an ordinary school building — instead, they designed the new campus in the form of a traditional Bunun slate stone house, complete with Bunun totems, slate walls, and street-art murals. The reconstruction won the 3rd Far Eastern Architectural Award's "Special Award for Campus Reconstruction," transforming a moment of disaster into a lasting architectural celebration of Bunun heritage.

潭南國小創立於民國40年（1951年），最初為地利國小分校，1958年獨立設校，1968年正式定名。1999年921大地震徹底摧毀了原有校舍，成為學校歷史的重大轉折。在浩然基金會資助下，2000年展開的重建工程並未選擇興建一般校舍，而是將新校園設計為傳統布農石板屋的建築形式，並融入布農圖騰、石板牆與街頭藝術壁畫。這項重建工程榮獲第三屆遠東建築獎「校園重建特別獎」，將一場災難轉化為布農文化傳承的永久建築見證。
