Yuan Ze University

元智大學

A private university endowed by the Far Eastern Group, and the first university in Taiwan to receive ISO9001 certification and win the National Quality Award.

由遠東集團捐資興學的私立大學，是全台第一所取得ISO9001認證、也是唯一獲頒國家品質獎的大學。

About · 學校簡介

Endowed by the Far Eastern Group.

Yuan Ze University was founded through an endowment from Yu-Ziang Hsu, founder of the Far Eastern Group, a major Taiwanese conglomerate. Planning began in the mid-1980s, and the school opened as Yuan Ze Institute of Technology, enrolling its first 134 undergraduates in July 1989 in programs including Electrical Engineering and Mechanical Engineering. It was named "Yuan Ze" (元智) after the given name of Hsu Yu-Ziang's father, honoring his commitment to education. The institute was upgraded and renamed Yuan Ze University effective August 1, 1997.

元智大學由遠東集團創辦人徐有庠先生捐資興學。1980年代中期開始籌設，初名「元智工學院」，1989年7月首屆招收134名大學部學生，設有電機工程、機械工程等系。「元智」二字取自徐有庠先生尊翁的名諱，以彰其興學之志。1997年8月1日改制升格為元智大學。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Five to Six Colleges

五至六學院

YZU's core colleges include Engineering, Informatics, Management, and Humanities and Social Sciences; some sources also list a College of Medicine and Nursing, which may be a newer addition.

🏅 First in Taiwan for Quality

品質標竿

YZU was the first university in Taiwan to receive ISO9001 certification (1998) and, to date, the only university to win Taiwan's National Quality Award (2003).

🔬 "Aiming for the Top" University

頂尖大學計畫

In 2006, Taiwan's Ministry of Education designated YZU as one of 12 first-tier research universities under the "Aiming for the Top" program.

🏆 Internationally Ranked

國際排名

YZU sits in the 601–800 band of the THE World University Rankings 2026, ranks #229 in Asia (THE Asia Rankings 2025), and in the 1001–1200 band of QS World Rankings 2026.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Born of Zhongli's industrial heritage.

🏭

YZU was endowed by the Far Eastern Group, a conglomerate whose textile and telecommunications operations were long anchored in the Zhongli/Taoyuan industrial belt, embodying the region's shift from manufacturing base to higher-education hub. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
