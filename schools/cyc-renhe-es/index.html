Renhe Elementary School

仁和國民小學

An ultra-small mountain school serving Taihe Village deep in Meishan Township, running a reading-promotion program and an affiliated branch campus.

About · 學校簡介

A mountain school for Taihe Village.

Renhe Elementary took its current name in July 1965. Its Taihe Branch campus has a much older lineage, tracing to 1942 as a branch classroom of Shengmaoshu National School, becoming independent in 1945 as "Geliwei National School," and passing through several renamings — Meishan Township's Fifth National School (1946), Tainan County Meishan Township Taihe National School (1947), Chiayi County Meishan Township Taihe National School (1950), and Taihe National Elementary School (1968) — before finally becoming Renhe Elementary's Taihe Branch in August 2023. Today the school is exceptionally small: the main campus has only 8 elementary students plus 5 kindergarteners, while the Taihe branch has 5 elementary students plus 4 kindergarteners.

仁和國小現名確立於民國54年（1965年）7月。其太和分校歷史更為悠久，可追溯至民國31年（1942年）設立的「生毛樹國民學校蛤里味分校場」，民國34年（1945年）獨立為「蛤里味國民學校」，其後歷經多次更名——梅山鄉第五國民學校（1946）、台南縣梅山鄉太和國校（1947）、嘉義縣梅山鄉太和國民學校（1950）、太和國民小學（1968）——最終於民國112年（2023年）8月1日成為「仁和國小太和分校」。如今學校規模極小：本校僅8名國小學生、5名幼兒園學生；太和分校則有5名國小學生、4名幼兒園學生。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📖 A Reading-Promotion Program

喜閱綠園計畫

The school runs a reading-promotion initiative called "喜閱綠園" alongside writing competitions, nurturing literacy in a very small student body.

🏘️ Two Campuses, One School

本校與太和分校並存

Renhe operates both a main campus and the Taihe Branch campus, each with its own small student body and affiliated kindergarten.

👶 A Tiny but Complete School Community

迷你但完整的教育體系

With only a handful of children across all six grades, Renhe operates affiliated kindergartens at both its main campus and its branch, keeping full elementary education alive for the smallest of communities.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The hollowing-out of Chiayi's tea hamlets.

🏔️

Serving Taihe Village deep in Meishan's mountains, this ultra-small school reflects the demographic hollowing-out of Chiayi's tea- and bamboo-farming mountain hamlets, where entire grade levels now have only a handful of children. See the Chiayi County page for more on the region.
