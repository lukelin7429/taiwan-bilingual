About · 學校簡介

A school whose name changed with the island's history.

Zhengyi Elementary originated in 1924 as the private "Shangqing Elementary School," run out of a borrowed ancestral temple. It closed under Japanese occupation in 1938 and reopened in 1945. In 1950, when Nationalist forces arrived on Kinmen, it was renamed "Chenkeng Elementary School," before becoming "Zhengyi Elementary School" in 1960 amid administrative consolidation. The school went through periods as a satellite or branch campus, regaining independent status in 1998. Major campus works followed in 1964, 1967, 1989, and 1991–92, and the school has held a 93rd-anniversary celebration.

正義國小起源於民國13年（1924年），初名私立「尚卿國小」，借用宗祠辦學。民國27年（1938年）因日軍佔領而停辦，民國34年（1945年）復校。民國39年（1950年），隨國軍進駐金門，更名為「陳坑國小」，民國49年（1960年）行政整併後改為現名「正義國小」。學校歷經分校時期，於民國87年（1998年）恢復獨立設校。歷年重大工程包括1964年新建校舍、1967年擴建教室、1989年整修，以及1991至1992年的改善工程，並曾舉辦93週年校慶。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛩️ Founded in a Borrowed Temple

借用宗祠創校

The school began in 1924 as the private "Shangqing Elementary School," operating out of a borrowed ancestral temple before later becoming a public institution.

🏳️ Renamed as the Military Arrived

隨軍隊進駐更名

In 1950, exactly as Nationalist forces arrived on Kinmen, the school was renamed "Chenkeng Elementary School" — a direct mark of the island's frontline transition that year.

🔁 Independence Regained in 1998

1998年恢復獨立設校

After periods operating as a satellite or branch campus, the school regained its independent status in 1998, following decades of campus expansion and renovation work.
