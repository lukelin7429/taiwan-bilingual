About · 學校簡介

A century of schooling in the Baifu community.

Wudu Elementary School was founded in April 1921 during the period of Japanese rule and was upgraded to full public school status in 1923. It was renamed several times through 1941 and again after Taiwan's 1945 retrocession, before finally becoming "Wudu Elementary School" under Keelung City's administration in 1968. In recent years, the school has held centennial celebration events marking over 100 years of continuous history serving the historic Baifu community.

五堵國民小學創校於日治時期的民國10年（1921年）4月，民國12年（1923年）升格為公學校。歷經民國30年（1941年）與台灣光復後的多次更名，最終於民國57年（1968年）定名為「五堵國民小學」，隸屬基隆市政府。近年學校舉辦建校百年校慶活動，紀念這座服務百福社區逾百年的學校。
