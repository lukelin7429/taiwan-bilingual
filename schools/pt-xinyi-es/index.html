About · 學校簡介

Arts, dance, and maker education near the county seat.

Xinyi Elementary School is a mid-sized school of about 717 students, located near the Pingtung County Government offices. It is known for its arts-and-humanities focus, including a dance program established in 2006 — the first elementary dance class in Pingtung — and its "maker education" (自造教育) initiatives, which blend hands-on creativity with technology.

信義國小是一所約717名學生的中型學校，鄰近屏東縣政府。學校以人文藝術特色見長，設有民國95年（2006年）成立的舞蹈班——為屏東縣國小首創的舞蹈班——並推行結合創意實作與科技的「自造教育」課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💃 Pingtung's First Elementary Dance Class

全縣國小首創舞蹈班

Established in 2006, the school's dance program was the first elementary-level dance class in all of Pingtung County.

🛠️ Maker Education

自造教育

The school's maker education initiatives give students hands-on experience blending creativity, design, and technology.

🏛️ Neighboring the County Government

緊鄰屏東縣政府

Located near the Pingtung County Government complex, the school sits at the administrative heart of the county seat.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Arts and administration side by side.

🎭

Its proximity to the county government complex places Xinyi Elementary at the administrative heart of Pingtung City, and its dance/arts specialization contributes to the city's cultural life as county seat. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
