Fengrong Elementary School

豐榮國小

An Indigenous-focus school in Taitung City whose award-winning campus has been recognized nationally for design and for promoting student health.

About · 學校簡介

An award-winning campus with deep Indigenous ties.

Fengrong Elementary opened in September 1971, initially borrowing classrooms from neighboring Fengli Elementary while its own campus was under construction; it moved into its completed buildings in May 1972. The school was designated an Indigenous-focus school in 1998. Its more recently rebuilt campus won the 2015 National Outstanding Construction Award for Best Environmental and Cultural Quality, and in 2016 the school received a Bronze award in the international Health Promoting Schools certification.

豐榮國小創立於民國60年（1971年）9月，初期借用鄰近豐里國小教室上課，待自有校舍完工後於民國61年（1972年）5月遷入。民國87年（1998年）獲列為原住民重點學校。近年重建的新校舍榮獲2015年國家卓越建設獎「最佳環境文化類」肯定，並於2016年取得國際健康促進學校銅牌認證。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏆 A Nationally Award-Winning Campus

2015年國家卓越建設獎

Fengrong's rebuilt campus won the 2015 National Outstanding Construction Award for Best Environmental and Cultural Quality — recognition for thoughtful, community-rooted design.

🥉 International Health Promotion Honors

健康促進學校銅牌認證

In 2016, the school earned a Bronze award in the international Health Promoting Schools certification, reflecting its commitment to student wellbeing.

🪶 An Indigenous-Focus School Since 1998

1998年列為原住民重點學校

Designated an Indigenous-focus school in 1998, Fengrong Elementary has built its curriculum and campus culture around serving and honoring its Indigenous students.
