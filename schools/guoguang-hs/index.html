Guoguang Senior High School

About · 學校簡介

From a refinery workers' school to a university affiliate.

Guoguang Senior High was founded in 1959 as a private school established by the Chinese Petroleum Corporation (CPC), Taiwan's state oil company, to educate the children of its refinery employees. In February 2005, the school was converted from private to public and placed under the affiliation of National Sun Yat-sen University, becoming the university's affiliated secondary school. Today it operates as a complete secondary school combining junior high and senior high divisions on a single six-year track, with a campus of about 6.13 hectares adjacent to Youchang Refinery Elementary School, and around 1,080 students studying under the school motto "Diligence, Respect, Sincerity, and Perseverance" (勤敬誠毅).

國光高中創立於民國48年（1959年），原為台灣中油（中國石油公司）為安置員工子弟就學而設立的私立學校。民國94年（2005年）2月1日，學校由私立改制為公立，並改隸國立中山大學，成為該校的附屬中學。現為國中部與高中部六年一貫的完全中學，校地約6.13公頃，緊鄰油廠國小，現有學生約1,080人，校訓為「勤敬誠毅」。

⛽ Neighboring Youchang Refinery Elementary

緊鄰油廠國小

The campus sits directly beside Youchang Refinery Elementary School, another institution built for CPC employees' families — together the two schools mark Nanzih's postwar petrochemical history.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Built on the history of a refinery.

⛽

Guoguang's origin is inseparable from Nanzih's Chinese Petroleum Corporation refinery — the refinery itself grew out of the Japanese-era "Imperial Japanese Navy Sixth Fuel Plant," taken over by CPC after 1945. Establishing schools for employees' children, including Guoguang and Youchang Refinery Elementary, was part of stabilizing the refinery workforce, making these campuses a living record of Nanzih's industrial development.
