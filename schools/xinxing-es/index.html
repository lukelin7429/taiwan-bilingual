Shin Shing Elementary School

新星國民小學

A community-minded Xinpu elementary school known for its music club and a student-led project mapping the town's historic old street.

About · 學校簡介

A note on this listing.

A note on this listing: Hsinchu County's official school directory lists a "新興國小" at this position in Xinpu Township, but our research could not confirm a school of that exact name currently operating here. The nearest verified match — geographically and administratively — is Shin Shing Elementary School (新星國小), which maintains an active website at hhes.hcc.edu.tw. This page is built using Shin Shing's confirmed facts, on the understanding that it is very likely the school the directory intended to list.

Shin Shing Elementary is a community-engaged school in Xinpu Township that runs an active National Music Club performing at local events, operates a "Magic Star Library" for its students, and has an attached kindergarten. It also participates in the Xinpu Digital Opportunity Center and leads an "Old Street Cultural Map" project that documents the historic residences and ancestral halls of Xinpu's old town.

關於本頁的說明：新竹縣官方學校名冊中，此新埔鎮位置列為「新興國小」，但經查證未能確認此校名目前確實存在。地理位置與行政區位最接近的學校為擁有現行網站（hhes.hcc.edu.tw）的新星國民小學。本頁內容以新星國小已查證的事實資料建置，研判該校極可能即為名冊中所指之校。

新星國小是一所深耕社區的新埔小學，設有活躍的國樂社，經常於地方活動演出，並設有「魔法星圖書館」與附設幼兒園。學校參與新埔數位機會中心，並主導「老街文化地圖」計畫，記錄新埔老街的歷史宅院與宗祠。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎶 A Lively National Music Club

國樂社演出

The school's National Music Club performs traditional Chinese instrumental music at community events, giving students a public stage for Taiwanese musical heritage.

⭐ The Magic Star Library

魔法星圖書館

A dedicated "Magic Star Library," paired with an attached kindergarten, gives young students an early and ongoing home for reading and literacy.

🗺️ Mapping the Old Street's Heritage

老街文化地圖

Through its "Old Street Cultural Map" project, students document the historic residences and ancestral halls of Xinpu's old town, connecting classroom learning to living local heritage.

💻 A Digital Opportunity Partner

新埔數位機會中心

The school participates in the Xinpu Digital Opportunity Center, helping extend digital literacy resources to the wider community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Documenting Xinpu's living heritage.

🏮

Through its Old Street Cultural Map project, the school directly documents Xinpu's historic Hakka ancestral halls and old-street heritage — tying student learning to the town's famed persimmon-drying and ancestral-hall traditions.
