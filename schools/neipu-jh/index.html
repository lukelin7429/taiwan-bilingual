About · 學校簡介

A junior high in the Rear Pile.

Neipu Junior High School serves the students of Neipu Township, a large and historically significant Hakka community in Pingtung County. The township is known as the "Hou Dui" (後堆, Rear Pile) of the Liudui Hakka confederation, an area renowned for a scholarly tradition centered on the historic Changli Temple, dedicated to Tang-dynasty scholar Han Yu.

內埔國中服務屏東縣內埔鄉的學子，這是一個歷史悠久、意義重大的客家聚落。內埔鄉為六堆客家「後堆」，以供奉唐代大儒韓愈的昌黎祠為核心，孕育出深厚的文教傳統而聞名。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in the Liudui Hakka heartland.

📖

Neipu Township is the "Hou Dui" (後堆, Rear Pile) of the Liudui confederation, home to the Changli Temple (昌黎祠) — Taiwan's only temple primarily dedicated to Tang-dynasty scholar-official Han Yu, built in the early 1800s and historically the Liudui region's foremost center of Confucian learning, where hired scholars helped produce many imperial-exam degree holders. The Hakka ethos of "diligent farming, studious reading" (勤耕雨讀) still defines the township's cultural identity today. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
