Xinmin Elementary School

新民國民小學

About · 學校簡介

A well-rounded village school.

Xinmin Elementary School serves Xinmin Village within Mingjian Township, and is active in county-level student athletic competitions. The school runs disaster-prevention and anti-fraud safety education programs to keep students prepared, and it offers an "English Learning Passport" supplementary program to build students' English skills alongside their regular studies.

新民國小服務名間鄉新民村，積極參與縣級學生運動競賽。學校推動防災教育與反詐騙安全教育，協助學生做好準備，並提供「英語學習護照」補充課程，在一般課程之外強化學生的英語能力。

Local Culture · 在地文化
