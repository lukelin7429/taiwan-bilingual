About · 學校簡介

From complete middle school to independent senior high.

Nankan Senior High School was established in 2000 as "Taoyuan County Nankan Junior-Senior High School," a combined ("complete") middle school offering both junior and senior high classes. In 2011 the two divisions split into independent schools, with the senior high continuing on the original campus in the heart of Nankan. Renamed to its current name in 2014 when Taoyuan County became a special municipality, the school today enrolls roughly 1,400 students across 42 classes, offering specialized art, music, PE, and science/language-focused tracks developed progressively between 2001 and 2009, along with international exchange partnerships with schools in Texas and Japan.

南崁高中創立於民國89年（2000年），初名「桃園縣立南崁國民中學」附設高中部，為結合國中部與高中部的完全中學。民國100年（2011年）8月1日，國中部與高中部分別獨立設校，高中部續留原校區，即今日南崁市街核心一帶。民國103年（2014年）12月25日，桃園縣升格直轄市後更名為現名。學校現有42班、學生逾1,400人，設有美術、音樂、體育及理科語文特色班（民國90年至98年間陸續增設），並與美國德州達拉斯及日本多所學校締結國際交流夥伴關係。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in the historic heart of Nankan.

⛩️

Nankan Senior High sits in the heart of Nankan (南崁), the historic settlement whose name has become synonymous with Luzhu District itself. The area's old street and Wufu Temple (五福宮) cluster anchor a community that predates the modern district, and the school's very name ties it directly to that founding neighborhood — one of the three historic hamlet cores (alongside Dazhu and Kengzi) around which Luzhu grew. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
