About · 學校簡介

From Guizhou to Fongshan.

Fongshan Junior High's predecessor, the private Chengzheng Middle School (私立誠正中學), was established in 1939 in Guizhou, China, as a school for army dependents. It relocated to Fongshan in 1948, came under county administration in 1957, and was renamed Fongshan Junior High School in 1968. Today it enrolls more than 1,500 students across 61 classes, and the Kaohsiung MRT Orange Line station "Fongshan Junior High" (O14) is named directly for the school.

鳳山國中前身為私立誠正中學，於1939年在中國貴州創立，作為軍眷子弟學校。民國37年（1948年）遷校至鳳山，民國46年（1957年）改為縣立，民國57年（1968年）更名為鳳山國民中學。現有1,500餘名學生、61班，高雄捷運橘線「鳳山國中站」（O14）即以此校命名。

1949年遷台歷史的縮影

The school's predecessor was founded in 1939 in Guizhou, China as an army-dependents' school, then relocated to Fongshan in 1948 — a direct thread of the Nationalist retreat to Taiwan.
