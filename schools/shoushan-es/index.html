Shoushan Elementary School

壽山國小

Set on a hillside beside the historic Shoushan Yan Guanyin Temple, this is one of four schools that originally branched off from century-old Guishan Elementary.

About · 學校簡介

A hillside school beside an old temple.

Shoushan Elementary began as the 'Lingding branch class' (嶺頂分班) of Guishan Elementary School, established on October 7, 1960. It was renamed the 'Shoushan branch school' in 1962 before becoming a fully independent school on April 27, 1963. The campus sits on a hillside along Section 2 of Wanshou Road, near Shoushan Yan Guanyin Temple, a well-known local pilgrimage site — and the school is one of four historical branch schools that split off from the original Guishan Elementary, alongside Dagang, Longshou, and Fuyuan elementary schools.

壽山國小前身為龜山國小「嶺頂分班」，設立於1960年10月7日；1962年更名為「壽山分校」，1963年4月27日正式獨立設校。校園座落於萬壽路二段山坡地，鄰近當地知名信仰中心壽山巖觀音寺。壽山國小是自百年老校龜山國小分出的四所學校之一，另三所為大崗、龍壽與福源國小。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛩️ Beside Shoushan Yan Guanyin Temple

鄰近壽山巖觀音寺

The hillside campus sits near Shoushan Yan Guanyin Temple, one of Guishan's best-known folk-religion pilgrimage sites, giving the school its name.

🌳 One of Four Branch Schools

龜山國小四大分校之一

Shoushan is one of four historical schools that split off from the original Guishan Elementary over the 20th century, alongside Dagang, Longshou, and Fuyuan elementary schools.

🏔️ A Hillside Campus

山坡地校園

The school occupies a hillside site along Section 2 of Wanshou Road, reflecting the hilly terrain typical of Guishan's older village areas.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in temple-village history.

⛩️

Shoushan Elementary's name and hillside site are tied directly to Shoushan Yan Guanyin Temple, one of Guishan's oldest folk-religion landmarks — linking the school to the district's pre-industrial agricultural and temple-village history, long before the tech parks and hospitals arrived.
