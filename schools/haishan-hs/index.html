About · 學校簡介

From a 1968 junior high to a 3,700-student campus.

Haishan Senior High School began in 1968 as Haishan Junior High School, became a complete middle-and-high school in 1997, and was upgraded to full senior high status in 2000. Today it serves roughly 3,731 students across 120 classes spanning its senior high, junior high, and evening divisions. Its 5.08-hectare campus sits near Banqiao Station, directly across from Banqiao Sports Stadium, and the school has built a strong reputation in table tennis under its guiding motto: 公誠勤毅 (public-mindedness, sincerity, diligence, perseverance).

海山高中前身為民國57年（1968年）創立的海山國中，民國86年（1997年）改制為完全中學，民國89年（2000年）升格為高級中學。目前全校約有3,731名學生、120班，涵蓋高中部、國中部及夜間部。校地5.08公頃，鄰近板橋車站，正對板橋運動場，桌球隊實力堅強，校訓為「公誠勤毅」。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for a historic Qing-era district.

⛩️

The school is named for the historic Haishan (海山) area of western Banqiao, once a major Qing-era administrative and agricultural district name that predates modern Banqiao. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
