About · 學校簡介

An elementary school built for support and safety.

Guangming Elementary is a public elementary school in Taitung City that operates a preschool division alongside its regular elementary classes, giving families a continuous educational pathway from early childhood. The school maintains dedicated resource centers for anti-bullying prevention, family education, and gender equality, along with a nutrition and health service center — a support structure aimed at keeping students safe and well cared-for throughout their school years.

光明國小是臺東市的公立國小，除一般國小課程外，另附設幼兒園，讓家庭能享有從幼兒教育到國小教育的連貫學習路徑。學校設有防治霸凌資源中心、家庭教育資源中心、性別平等資源中心，以及營養與健康服務中心，致力於在學生求學期間提供全面的安全與照護支持。
