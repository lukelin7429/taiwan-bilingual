Shangren Elementary School

尚仁國民小學

A public elementary school near the Keelung River and Yingge Creek, independent since 1965 and known for its distinctive playground.

About · 學校簡介

From branch campus to riverside neighborhood school.

Shangren Elementary School originated as a branch campus of Qidu Elementary School before becoming independent in September 1965. Located near the confluence of the Keelung River and Yingge Creek, the school has become known locally for a distinctive playground slide feature that has drawn attention as a family attraction beyond its role as a neighborhood school.

尚仁國民小學前身為七堵國民小學的分校，於民國54年（1965年）9月獨立設校。校址鄰近基隆河與鶯歌溪匯流處，校內一座特色溜滑梯遊具頗負盛名，除了服務在地學童外，也吸引許多親子家庭前來造訪。
