About · 學校簡介

The highest school in the county, in the heart of Rukai country.

Wutai Elementary dates to 1927, founded during the Japanese colonial era in what is now the historic heartland of Rukai culture. In 2021 the school became an indigenous experimental education school (原住民族實驗教育學校), and it also operates the Ligulily Branch Campus, established in 2013 and opened in 2014 on Culture Street in Wutai Village. The school is often cited as the elementary school at the highest elevation in Pingtung County, its campus set among mountain clouds and surrounded by slate carving and Rukai ancestor-lily motifs woven through the village around it.

霧臺國小創立於1927年日治時期，位於魯凱族文化的歷史核心地帶。2021年，學校轉型為原住民族實驗教育學校，並設有「勵古百合分校」，成立於2013年、2014年正式啟用，座落於霧臺村文化街。學校常被稱為屏東縣海拔最高的國小，校園終年雲霧繚繞，周邊村落處處可見石板雕刻與魯凱族百合花圖騰。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛰️ The Highest School in Pingtung

屏東縣海拔最高的國小

Wutai Elementary is often cited as the elementary school at the highest elevation anywhere in Pingtung County, its campus perched among mountain clouds.

🏫 An Indigenous Experimental School

原住民族實驗教育學校

Since 2021, the school has operated as an indigenous experimental education school, weaving Rukai culture and language directly into its curriculum.

🌸 The Ligulily Branch Campus

勵古百合分校

The school's Ligulily Branch Campus, opened in 2014 on Culture Street in Wutai Village, extends its reach and takes its name from the Rukai lily, a symbol of nobility and honor.

🪨 Surrounded by Slate and Ancestor-Lily Imagery

石板雕刻與百合花圖騰

The campus and surrounding village are steeped in Rukai cultural motifs — slate carving and ancestor-lily imagery — that reflect the deep symbolic world of Rukai tradition.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The historic heartland of Rukai culture.

🏔️

Wutai Township is the historic heartland of Rukai culture, famed for its slate-house architecture — including the old Wutai and Rinari slate villages — and for its traditions of Rukai woodcarving and weaving. Sitting at the county's highest-elevation campus amid these mountain slate villages, Wutai Elementary is both literally and culturally at the summit of Rukai heritage in Taiwan.
