Taipei Municipal Lishan Junior High School

麗山國民中學

The founding member of Neihu's three-school "Lishan" (beautiful mountain) cluster, later joined by Lishan Senior High and Lishan Elementary in the district's hilly eastern terrain.

About · 學校簡介

The original school of the Lishan cluster.

Taipei Municipal Lishan Junior High School traces its start to a preparatory office that opened on August 11, 1978. The school was formally established on August 1, 1979, and began full operations the following year, on July 18, 1980. Lishan Junior High shares its "Lishan" (麗山, "beautiful mountain") name and general neighborhood with the later-built Lishan Senior High School, founded in 2000, and Lishan Elementary School, founded in 1988 — together forming a three-school Lishan cluster in Neihu's hilly eastern terrain.

麗山國中的籌備處成立於民國67年（1978年）8月11日，學校於民國68年（1979年）8月1日正式設校，並於隔年民國69年（1980年）7月18日全面運作。麗山國中與後來成立的麗山高中（創校於2000年）及麗山國小（創校於1988年）共用「麗山」校名與所在區域，三校共同構成內湖東側丘陵地帶的「麗山」學校群。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏔️ Founder of the Lishan Cluster

麗山學校群創始校

Lishan Junior High is the original school of the three-school "Lishan" cluster, later joined by Lishan Senior High (2000) and Lishan Elementary (1988) in the same neighborhood.

🗓️ A Two-Year Path to Full Operation

從籌備到全面運作

The school moved from a preparatory office in 1978, to formal establishment in 1979, to full operations in 1980 — a careful, staged founding process.

⛰️ Rooted in Neihu's Eastern Hills

內湖東側丘陵

Like its cluster-mates, Lishan Junior High sits in Neihu's hilly eastern terrain, the landscape that gave the "Lishan" (beautiful mountain) name to all three schools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of a shared mountain landscape.

⛰️

Lishan Junior High sits in Neihu's hilly eastern terrain, for which it is named, as part of the district's broader basin-and-ridge landscape — the same hills that later gave rise to the neighboring Lishan Senior High and Lishan Elementary schools. See the Taipei county page for more on the region.
