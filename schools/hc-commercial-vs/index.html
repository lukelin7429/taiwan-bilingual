About · 學校簡介

Eight decades of business education.

National Hsinchu Commercial Vocational Senior High School was founded on April 1, 1940, under Japanese rule as "新竹州立新竹商業學校," a five-year commercial school. It was briefly renamed an agricultural school in 1944 before reverting to commercial education after Taiwan's retrocession in 1945. The school began admitting female students in 1950, and was renamed Taiwan Provincial Hsinchu Commercial Vocational Senior High School in 1970. It converted to national administration in 2000. Located on Xuefu Road, the school sits within Hsinchu's education corridor near National Hsinchu Senior High School, National Tsing Hua University, and the Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI).

新竹高商創校於1940年（昭和15年）4月1日日治時期，原名「新竹州立新竹商業學校」，為五年制商業學校。1944年一度改為農業學校，1945年台灣光復後恢復商業教育。1950年起開始招收女學生，1970年改名為「台灣省立新竹高級商業職業學校」，並於2000年改制為國立。學校座落於學府路，位處新竹學府路教育廊帶，鄰近新竹高中、國立清華大學及工業技術研究院（工研院）。

🏫 A School of Many Names

歷經多次更名

Founded in 1940 as a commercial school, briefly converted to agricultural education in 1944, then restored to its commercial focus after 1945 — reflecting the turbulence of the era.

The school converted to national administration in 2000, following six decades as a provincial-run commercial vocational institution.
