Sanxia Junior High School

三峽國民中學

The first junior high in Sanxia, founded right after Taiwan's 1945 retrocession in the historic river-port town once called Sankyō.

About · 學校簡介

Sanxia's first junior high, born after retrocession.

Sanxia Junior High opened in 1946 as "Taipei County Sanxia Junior Middle School," the first junior high in the district, embodying the rapid postwar expansion of public education after Taiwan's 1945 retrocession. It originally used the Japanese-era "常小學" school site on Wenhua Road before the nine-year compulsory education reform brought its renaming to the current form in 1968. The school moved to its present Fuxing Road campus in 1992, where it continues to serve students in the historic river town.

三峽國中創立於民國35年（1946年），初名「臺北縣立三峽初級中學」，是三峽第一所國民中學，體現了1945年台灣光復後公共教育迅速擴張的時代背景。創校初期借用文化路上日治時期「常小學」校舍，民國57年（1968年）因九年國教改制而更名為現名，並於民國81年（1992年）遷至現址復興路校區，持續服務這座歷史悠久的河港古鎮。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Sanxia's First Junior High

三峽第一所國中

Established in 1946, Sanxia Junior High was the district's very first junior high school, founded amid the postwar expansion of public education following Taiwan's 1945 retrocession.

🏯 Rooted in a Japanese-Era Campus

日治時期校舍起家

The school's earliest classes were held on the Japanese-era "常小學" site on Wenhua Road, before it later moved to its present Fuxing Road campus in 1992.

🔤 Renamed Through Education Reform

因九年國教改制更名

The school took its current name in 1968 as part of Taiwan's nine-year compulsory education reform, a milestone shared by many junior highs founded in the same era.

Local Culture · 在地文化

In the historic river-port town of Sankyō.

🚤

Sanxia was once known by its old Qing-era name "三角湧" (Sankyō), tied to the town's location at a river confluence that made it a bustling inland port for camphor and tea trade. Founded immediately after retrocession, the school embodies the postwar rebuilding of public education in this historic town. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
