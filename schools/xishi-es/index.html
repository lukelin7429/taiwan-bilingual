About · 學校簡介

A century of schooling in Xishi Village.

Xishi Elementary was established in 1924, during the Japanese colonial era, as the "Zhutian Public School Xishi Branch Site." Over the following century, the school passed through the Japanese colonial era, the post-war Nationalist government period, and the post-martial-law expansion of Taiwanese education, producing generations of local graduates from Xishi Village and the surrounding farming community.

西勢國小創立於1924年（大正13年）日治時期，初名「竹田公學校西勢分教場」。百年來，學校歷經日治時期、戰後國民政府時期，以及解嚴後的教育發展階段，培育出西勢村及周邊農村社區一代又一代的畢業生。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in the Liudui Hakka heartland.

🏮

Xishi Village sits within Zhutian Township, the historic "Zhong Dui" (中堆, Middle Pile) of the Liudui 六堆 Hakka militia confederation, formed by Qing-era Hakka settlers to defend their farming communities. The area's identity has long been tied to rice and tobacco farming, and Xishi Elementary's century of history mirrors the township's own path from Qing-era Hakka settlement through Japanese colonial rule to the present day.
