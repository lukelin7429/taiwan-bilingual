Shuimei Elementary School

水美國民小學

A public elementary school in Yangmei's Shuimei area, part of the same historic railway-side education network as neighboring Ruiyuan Junior High.

About · 學校簡介

Part of Shuimei's railway-side school network.

Shuimei Elementary sits in Yangmei's Shuimei area, sharing the same educational community as neighboring Ruiyuan Junior High, which itself began as a branch of Yangmei Junior High in this same neighborhood. Detailed founding records were not accessible through the school's official website during this research, but the school continues to serve the Shuimei community today.

水美國小位於楊梅水美地區，與瑞原國中同屬水美聚落教育體系。官網歷史頁面文字編碼異常無法讀取，多次查證未能取得可靠的創校民國年份，但學校持續服務水美地區的學童。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🚆 Part of the Shuimei Railway Community

水美聚落教育體系

Shuimei Elementary shares its neighborhood and educational community with Ruiyuan Junior High, both serving families in this railway-adjacent part of Yangmei.

🏘️ A Community School for Shuimei

服務水美地區學童

The school continues to serve as a neighborhood anchor for the families of Shuimei, one of Yangmei's distinctive railway-side settlements.

🛤️ Near the Main North-South Line

緊鄰縱貫鐵路與瑞原地帶

Situated close to Taiwan's main north-south railway, Shuimei Elementary sits within the same "railway Hakka village" landscape that defines much of eastern Yangmei.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Along the railway Hakka settlements.

🚂

The Shuimei area, close to the main north-south railway and the neighboring Ruiyuan area, is one of Yangmei's well-known "railway Hakka village" communities, and Shuimei Elementary is one of the schools that anchors daily life there. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
