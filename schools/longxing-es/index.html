Longxing Elementary School

龍星國民小學

Opened in 1999 to relieve overcrowding at Longtan's two oldest elementary schools, this "Dragon Star" school welcomed nearly 1,300 students in its very first year.

1999年設校，為紓解龍潭兩所老校的就學壓力而生，首年即有近1,300名學生入學。

About · 學校簡介

Born from a 1990s growth boom.

Longxing Elementary was approved by the county government in 1996, with first principal Hu Jin-song — transferred from Shuimei Elementary School — taking office that August. The school opened for instruction on August 1, 1999, absorbing second-through-sixth-grade students transferred from both Longtan Elementary and Wuhan Elementary — about 1,297 students across 36 regular classes plus one special-education class, an unusually large founding enrollment for a brand-new school. Its new campus construction was completed with a formal dedication ceremony in January 2000.

龍星國小於民國85年（1996年）獲縣府核准設校，首任校長胡進松（由水尾國小轉任）於同年8月到任。學校於民國88年（1999年）8月1日正式招生，並自龍潭國小與武漢國小轉入二至六年級學生，開學即有約1,297名學生、36個普通班加1個特教班，創校規模在新設校中相當罕見。新校舍工程於民國89年（2000年）1月舉行落成典禮。
