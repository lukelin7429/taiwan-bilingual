Qing-Xi Elementary School

青溪國民小學

About · 學校簡介

A late-century school with an early lead in gifted education.

Qing-Xi Elementary broke ground around 1989 and completed construction in 1990, formally admitting its first full class in 1992. Its 1990 founding reflects Taoyuan District's late-20th-century population boom. In 2016 it launched Taoyuan City's first "creative-ability gifted class" (創造能力資優班) in partnership with National Taiwan Normal University and National Taipei University of Education, open to citywide 3rd- and 4th-graders. The school is also designated a Bilingual Spotlight School, houses a "TEAM Model" smart classroom, and operates a seismic monitoring station connected to the Central Weather Bureau's earthquake network.

青溪國小約於1989年動工，1990年完成校舍興建，1992年正式招收第一屆完整班級。1990年的創校時間點反映了桃園區在20世紀末期的人口成長熱潮。2016年，學校與國立臺灣師範大學及國立臺北教育大學合作，開設桃園市首個「創造能力資優班」，招收全市三、四年級學生。學校亦獲認證為雙語亮點學校，設有「因材網」智慧教室，並與中央氣象局地震觀測網合作設置地震觀測站。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 The City's First Creative-Ability Gifted Class

桃園市首創資優班

Launched in 2016 with National Taiwan Normal University and National Taipei University of Education, this program was Taoyuan City's first creative-ability gifted class, open to citywide 3rd/4th graders.

🌐 A Bilingual Spotlight School

雙語亮點學校

Designated a Bilingual Spotlight School, Qing-Xi runs mandatory increased weekly English-instruction hours with co-teaching by local and foreign teachers.

🌍 A Seismic Monitoring Station

地震觀測站

The campus hosts a seismic monitoring station operated in connection with the Central Weather Bureau's earthquake network, alongside an industry-academic "TEAM Model" smart classroom.

🌱 Parent School of Da-Ye Elementary

大業國小的母校

Qing-Xi is the parent institution of Da-Ye Elementary, which began as its branch campus in 1997 before becoming independent.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring the eastward expansion.

🌊

As the parent institution of 大業國小 (which sits near Huweishan and the Nankan River), Qing-Xi is part of the same eastward expansion of schools away from the old walled-city core into Taoyuan's newer residential districts. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
