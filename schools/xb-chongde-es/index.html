About · 學校簡介

Where fireflies still light up the school year.

Chongde Elementary School was founded in 1972 and has grown into one of Xizhi's larger elementary schools, enrolling roughly 1,253 students as of 2022. It is best known for its signature firefly (螢火蟲) ecology program and a bilingual/English-enriched curriculum, both of which have helped it earn a place among CommonWealth Magazine's Top 100 Featured Elementary Schools. Its firefly program reflects Xizhi's semi-rural fringe environment, where urban development still meets natural streams and greenery.

崇德國小創立於民國61年（1972年），如今已成為汐止區規模較大的國小之一，截至民國111年（2022年）學生人數約1,253人。學校以螢火蟲生態教育及雙語／英語特色課程聞名，並曾入選天下雜誌「特色100小學」。其螢火蟲教育計畫正反映了汐止都市邊緣半鄉村化的環境特色——都市開發與自然溪流綠地在此並存。
