About · 學校簡介

A century of bamboo, badlands, and branch schools.

Longqi Elementary School was founded on April 1, 1922, as "Longqi Public School Zhongkeng Branch." Its site changed in 1929, and it became a fully independent school as "Longqi Public School" in 1932. The school runs a curriculum built around Longqi's well-preserved bamboo groves and bamboo-weaving craft tradition, offering programs in silk-and-bamboo music (絲竹樂), bamboo weaving, and picture-book illustration. Its branch campus, Longchuan, focuses on wilderness and outdoor education, drawing on the district's dramatic badlands terrain. The school sits beside Tiger-Shape Mountain (虎形山), where Longqi hosts its annual "Kong Shan Ji" (空山祭) light-art festival each winter.

龍崎國小創立於大正11年（1922年）4月1日，初名「龍崎公學校中坑分校」。1929年遷校，1932年獨立設校，定名「龍崎公學校」。學校以龍崎保存完好的竹林與竹編工藝傳統為核心，開設絲竹樂、竹編與繪本創作等課程。其分校龍船分校則著重野外與戶外教育，善用龍崎的惡地地形。校址緊鄰虎形山，龍崎區每年冬季在此舉辦「空山祭」光影藝術節。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A curriculum grown from bamboo and badlands.

🎋

Longqi's school curriculum is built directly around the district's two defining natural resources — its bamboo forests, the basis of the school's signature bamboo-weaving and silk-and-bamboo music programs, and its eroded badlands hill terrain, the basis of the sister campus's wilderness-education focus.
