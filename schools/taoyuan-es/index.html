About · 學校簡介

Rebuilding cultural confidence, one classroom at a time.

Taoyuan Elementary traces its roots to a branch education institute established in 1938 under Japanese colonial rule, becoming an independent school in 1947. In August 2017, the school converted to the KIST (誠致教育基金會, Chengzhi Education Foundation) public-private-partnership experimental school model, a governance structure that lets a nonprofit foundation run curriculum and school culture within the public system. Since then, Taoyuan has become known as the "cradle of Bunun culture" — a nickname reflecting its focused mission of rebuilding Indigenous students' cultural confidence and identity alongside academic achievement.

桃源國小的歷史可追溯至日治時期1938年設立的分教場，1947年正式獨立設校。民國106年（2017年）8月，學校轉型為「誠致教育基金會」公辦民營實驗學校，這種治理模式讓非營利基金會得以在公立體制內主導課程與校園文化。此後，桃源國小以「布農文化搖籃」之名廣為人知，這個稱號反映出學校致力於在學業表現之外，重建原住民學生文化自信與認同的核心使命。
