About · 學校簡介

A steady presence in farming country.

Daxing Elementary opened in 1957 and took its current name in 1968. Located in a rural farming area of Guangfu Township, the school has long served as more than a place of learning: when Typhoon Toraji struck in 2001, Daxing Elementary served as a community shelter and anchor for the surrounding area's recovery, a role that speaks to how central small rural schools remain to Taiwanese township life.

大興國小創立於民國46年（1957年），民國57年（1968年）定名為現名，位於光復鄉的農業地帶。學校在地方社區中扮演的角色不僅止於教育——民國90年（2001年）桃芝颱風來襲時，大興國小曾作為社區避難與災後重建的據點，體現了台灣鄉村小學在地方生活中的核心地位。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Guangfu's Amis-centered heartland.

🌱

Daxing Elementary sits within Guangfu Township, a rural district with a large Amis Indigenous population and a farming economy tied to the legacy of the historic Hualien Sugar Factory. See the sister schools of the township — including Guangfu Elementary and Tafalong Elementary — for more on the surrounding Amis community.
