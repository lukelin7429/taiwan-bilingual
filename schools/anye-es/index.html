About · 學校簡介

A century-old small school, currently rebuilding.

Anye Elementary School traces its founding to around 1918, based on its 2024 celebration of its 106th/107th anniversary, making it the second-oldest school in Madou after Madou Elementary itself. As a listed remote/rural small school, its enrollment had dropped to just 38 students across 6 classes around 2011, but has since grown to about 119 students in 8 classes through new clubs, after-school care, innovative English instruction, and tech-integrated teaching. The school's aging campus buildings were fully demolished starting in September 2024 as part of a NT$176 million reconstruction project. As of 2026, while its new campus is being built, Anye is temporarily operating out of the campus of neighboring Ji'an Elementary School (紀安國小); the rebuild is expected to continue through roughly mid-2026, so visitors and families should confirm the school's current location directly with the school before visiting.

安業國小創校年份可追溯至約1918年（依據2024年慶祝建校106/107週年推算），是繼麻豆國小之後麻豆區歷史第二悠久的學校。作為列冊的偏遠小校，約2011年時全校僅剩38名學生、6個班級，此後透過新增社團、課後照顧、創新英語教學與科技融入教學，成長至約119名學生、8個班級。校舍因老舊，自2024年9月起全面拆除，展開一項總經費約新台幣1.76億元的重建工程。截至2026年，在新校舍興建期間，安業國小暫時遷至鄰近的紀安國小校區上課；重建工程預計持續至2026年年中左右，實際安排請逕洽學校確認最新狀況。

Anye's turnaround story — from a shrinking rural school to a growing one through creative programming — reflects the demographic pressures facing small farming-village schools across Madou's countryside as the district's pomelo-growing population ages.
