New Taipei Municipal Ruifang Industrial Vocational Senior High School

新北市立瑞芳高級工業職業學校

Founded in 1934 by the engineer behind the Chianan Canal, this vocational school has trained generations of tradesmen for Ruifang's mining and industrial economy.

創立於1934年，由嘉南大圳工程師八田與一參與創辦，長年培育瑞芳礦業與工業人才的高職。

About · 學校簡介

From a surveying institute to a district's vocational anchor.

The school began in 1934 in Taipei as the "Civil Engineering and Surveying Technician Training Institute" (土木測量技術員養成所), co-founded by Hatta Yoichi, the engineer famed for designing the Chianan Canal. It relocated to Ruifang in 1948, becoming a national institution in 2000 and a New Taipei municipal school in 2013. Throughout its history, the school has trained generations of local youth for the mechanical, electrical, and mining trades that once powered Ruifang's coal and gold industries.

學校於1934年創立於臺北，初名「土木測量技術員養成所」，由以嘉南大圳聞名的工程師八田與一共同創辦。1948年遷至瑞芳，2000年改制為國立，2013年改為新北市立。長期以來，學校培育瑞芳地方青年投入機械、電機與礦業等技術行業，支撐了當地的煤金產業發展。

🎓 Roots in Hatta Yoichi's Engineering Vision

八田與一的工程教育理念

Co-founded in 1934 by Hatta Yoichi, the engineer renowned for the Chianan Canal, the school began life in Taipei as a civil-engineering and surveying training institute.

⚙️ Trained Generations of Mining-Era Tradesmen

培育礦業時代技術人才

After relocating to Ruifang in 1948, the school became the district's dedicated industrial-vocational school, training local youth in the mechanical, electrical, and mining trades that powered its coal and gold industries.

🏫 From National to Municipal Status

從國立到市立

The school became a national institution in 2000 and was reclassified as a New Taipei municipal school in 2013, tracking decades of change in Taiwan's vocational education system.
