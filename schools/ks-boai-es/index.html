Boai Elementary School

博愛國小

A neighborhood elementary school with roughly 40 classes and 885 students, sharing a city block with Kaohsiung Medical University, the Sanmin Public Library, and Sanmin Park.

About · 學校簡介

A campus in the middle of a civic cluster.

Boai Elementary School takes its name from "博愛" (universal fraternity), one of a wave of virtue-themed school names adopted in Taiwan after 1945, often associated with Sun Yat-sen's ideals. The school today serves roughly 885 students across about 40 classes (as of 2022), and sits within easy walking distance of Kaohsiung Medical University, the Sanmin Public Library, Sanmin Junior High School, and Sanmin Park — a small civic cluster at the heart of the district.

博愛國小校名取自「博愛」一詞，是台灣1945年後常見與孫中山思想相關的德目校名之一。學校目前約有885名學生、40個班級（2022年資料），校址步行即可抵達高雄醫學大學、三民區圖書館、三民國中及三民公園，形成三民區核心地帶的一處小型公共設施聚落。

鄰近圖書館與三民公園

Boai Elementary sits within walking distance of the Sanmin Public Library and Sanmin Park, giving students easy access to public green and reading spaces.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A shared name with a national ideal.

🤲

The school's name, "博愛" (universal fraternity), is part of a wave of virtue-themed school names — alongside 愛國 (patriotism) and 民族 (nationalism) — adopted across Taiwan after 1945. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
