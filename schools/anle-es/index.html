About · 學校簡介

One of Keelung's oldest schools, still teaching from its own backyard.

Anle Elementary was founded in 1923 as "第二公學校" (Second Public School), later renamed Keelung Municipal Bao Public School in 1933 and given its current name, Anle National Elementary School, in 1968. Over a century old, it sits beneath Fozuling near a small fishing inlet called "蚵殼港" (Oyster-Shell Harbor), and has built its place-based curriculum directly around that local landmark. In 2022, it was designated one of Keelung City's bilingual "lighthouse" schools, and its dance and choir clubs regularly bring home national competition awards.

安樂國小創立於民國12年（1923年），初名「第二公學校」，民國22年（1933年）改稱基隆市寶公學校，民國57年（1968年）定名為安樂國民小學。學校座落於佛祖嶺下，鄰近舊稱「蚵殼港」的小漁村港灣，並以此在地地景發展出獨具特色的鄉土課程。2022年獲選為基隆市雙語「燈塔學校」之一，舞蹈與合唱社團也屢獲全國賽事佳績。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Over a Century of History

建校逾百年

Founded in 1923, Anle Elementary is one of Keelung's oldest schools, having been renamed twice through the city's changing eras before settling on its current name in 1968.

🐚 Curriculum Rooted in Oyster-Shell Harbor

扎根蚵殼港的鄉土課程

The school has developed a distinctive place-based curriculum centered on "蚵殼港" (Oyster-Shell Harbor), the small fishing inlet in its own neighborhood beneath Fozuling.

🌐 A Designated Bilingual Lighthouse School

2022年獲選雙語燈塔學校

In 2022, the school became one of Keelung City's designated bilingual "lighthouse" schools, tasked with modeling bilingual education for other schools in the city.

🎶 Strong Dance and Choir Traditions

舞蹈與合唱社團屢獲佳績

Anle's dance and choir clubs are frequent award-winners at national competitions, a long-standing point of pride for the century-old school.
