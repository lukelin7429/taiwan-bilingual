Chang Gung University of Science and Technology

長庚科技大學

A health-and-human-services-focused university of science and technology in Guishan — a separate institution from neighboring Chang Gung University, though sharing the same Chang Gung Medical Foundation lineage.

About · 學校簡介

A distinct institution, same medical-founder lineage.

Chang Gung University of Science and Technology (CGUST) is a legally and organizationally separate university from Chang Gung University (CGU) — the two are distinct institutions under Taiwan's Ministry of Education records, though both descend from the same Chang Gung Medical Foundation lineage founded by Wang Yung-ching, and both are named after his father, Wang Chang-gung. CGUST's own roots trace to a two-year nursing internship program run under Ming Chi College of Technology starting in 1983. In 1988, Wang Yung-ching established Chang Gung Institute of Nursing as an independent two-year junior college. It became a five-year nursing program and was renamed Chang Gung Institute of Technology in 1991, began offering four-year bachelor's degrees and opened a second campus in Chiayi in 2004, and was renamed Chang Gung University of Science and Technology in August 2011.

長庚科技大學（CGUST）與長庚大學（CGU）是教育部登記上各自獨立的兩所學校，並非同一所學校，惟兩校皆源自王永慶先生創辦的長庚醫療財團法人體系，校名亦皆取自其尊翁王長庚先生。長庚科大的前身可追溯至1983年附設於明志工專的二年制護理專科班；1988年，王永慶先生創立獨立的「長庚護理專科學校」；1991年改制為五年制並更名「長庚技術學院」；2004年增設四年制學士班並於嘉義設立第二校區；2011年8月改制更名為長庚科技大學。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🏥 Two Colleges

兩大學院

CGUST is organized around a College of Nursing and a College of Human Ecology, plus a Center for General Education — the university's own materials describe two colleges, three graduate programs, six departments, and four centers.

🩺 Vocational Healthcare Focus

實務健康照護

CGUST's programs concentrate on practical, vocational healthcare and human-services fields — nursing, gerontology and long-term care, respiratory care, and human ecology areas such as fashion, textiles, and child development.

🚉 Near Airport MRT

機場捷運鄰近

CGUST's Guishan (Linkou) campus is within walking distance of National Taiwan Sport University Station on the Taoyuan Airport MRT line.

📶 A Thinner English Website

英文網站內容較精簡

CGUST's English website (english.cgust.edu.tw) is real but noticeably thinner than most peer universities — its homepage functions largely as a navigation shell, though linked subpages such as its Brief History page contain genuine, substantive content. No major global ranking (QS, THE, or ARWU) was found for CGUST in available searches.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Guishan's medical-education cluster.

🏥

CGUST's Guishan campus sits immediately alongside Chang Gung University and Chang Gung Memorial Hospital, together forming a single Wang Yung-ching-founded medical-education campus that made Guishan one of Taiwan's most concentrated healthcare-industry clusters.
