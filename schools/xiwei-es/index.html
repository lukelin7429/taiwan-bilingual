About · 學校簡介

The only school on the far bank.

Xiwei Elementary School traces its origins to 1945, when it operated as a branch classroom of Kali Elementary School. It became fully independent in 1954. Today it holds a distinctive geographic status as the only elementary school in Wuri District situated on the south bank of the Wu River, setting it apart from the district's other schools clustered north of the waterway.

溪尾國小的歷史可追溯至民國34年（1945年），最初為喀哩國小的分班，民國43年（1954年）正式獨立設校。如今是烏日區唯一座落於烏溪南岸的國小，地理位置有別於本區其他多聚集於溪流以北的學校。
