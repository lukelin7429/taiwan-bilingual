About · 學校簡介

A quiet village school along Road 193.

Songpu Elementary opened on September 1, 1946 as a branch of Guanshan Elementary, became independent on February 1, 1953, and took its current name in 1968. The school sits in a remote rural setting along County Road 193, within an Amis Indigenous village — the same Songpu community that is also home to Yudong Junior High, nationally known for its student woodworking program.

松浦國小於民國35年（1946年）9月1日以關山國小分校形式設立，民國42年（1953年）2月1日獨立設校，民國57年（1968年）確立現名。學校位於193縣道旁的偏遠鄉間，屬阿美族部落聚落——正是以學生木工班聞名全台的玉東國中所在的同一個松浦社區。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛣️ Along a Quiet County Road

193縣道旁的偏遠校園

Songpu Elementary sits along County Road 193 in a remote rural setting, giving it a quiet, close-knit village atmosphere distinct from Yuli's town center.

🤝 Neighbor to a Famous Woodworking School

與玉東國中木工班同鄉

Songpu shares its village with Yudong Junior High, whose student woodworking program has been exhibited in Taipei — a point of local pride for the wider community.

🏘️ An Amis Village Identity

阿美族部落聚落

The school serves an Amis Indigenous village, part of the broader mosaic of Indigenous communities that shape rural life across Yuli Township.

Local Culture · 在地文化

An Amis village along the valley road.

🛤️

Songpu sits within Yuli Township 玉里鎮, a rice-farming and hot-springs town in Hualien's Hua-Tung Valley near the Tropic of Cancer. As an Amis Indigenous village along County Road 193, Songpu reflects the rice-farming, Indigenous character found throughout rural Yuli.
