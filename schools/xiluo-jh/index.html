About · 學校簡介

A town school with a national badminton pipeline.

Xiluo Junior High was founded in 1946, shortly after Taiwan's retrocession, and took its current name in 1968 as part of the island-wide reorganization of junior high education. The school is best known today for its badminton program, established in 1986, which has developed into a noted feeder program for national-level players. Its athletic facilities grew alongside that success: a comprehensive sports gymnasium was completed in 1994, followed by a dedicated NT$20 million badminton facility that opened in 2012.

西螺國中創立於民國35年（1946年），並於民國57年（1968年）配合全台國中改制更為現名。學校目前最為人稱道的是自民國75年（1986年）創立的羽球隊，多年來已成為培育國手的重要搖籃。伴隨佳績，體育設施也逐步擴建：民國83年（1994年）落成綜合體育館，並於民國101年（2012年）啟用耗資新台幣2000萬元的羽球館。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏸 A National Badminton Pipeline

羽球國手搖籃

Founded in 1986, the school's badminton team has become a noted feeder program producing players who go on to represent Taiwan nationally.

🏟️ A NT$20 Million Badminton Facility

2000萬元羽球館

Opened in 2012 at a cost of NT$20 million, the dedicated badminton facility reflects the school's sustained investment in its signature sport.

🏋️ A Comprehensive Sports Gymnasium

綜合體育館

Completed in 1994, the school's comprehensive gymnasium laid the groundwork for its later rise as an athletics-focused institution.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A hub near Xiluo's heritage landmarks.

🌉

Located in Xiluo Town — celebrated for its century-old soy-sauce breweries, historic old street, and the landmark Xiluo Bridge across the Zhuoshui River — the school is a hub for the town's next generation near this heritage. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
