About · 學校簡介

A tiny school at the mountain's edge.

Ruifeng Elementary is an extremely small, remote school in Shangping Village, with just 16 elementary students and 8 kindergartners in recent counts. Its oldest surviving teaching building dates to 1967, though the school itself is locally described as roughly 80 years old. Despite its size, Ruifeng has built a distinctive identity: students perform Atayal folklore stories in theatrical form, winning recognition at the county level, and the curriculum weaves together food and agricultural education, bilingual instruction, calligraphy, and environmental conservation.

瑞峰國小是位於上坪里的極小型偏鄉學校，近年統計僅有16名國小學生及8名幼兒園生。現存最早的教學大樓建於民國56年（1967年），但學校本身在地方上被稱為已有約80年歷史。儘管規模迷你，瑞峰國小發展出鮮明特色：學生以戲劇形式演出泰雅族傳說故事，並在縣級比賽中獲得肯定，課程也融合食農教育、雙語教學、書法及環境保育。

Standing at the gateway to Wufeng.

Located in Shangping Village at the gateway to Wufeng Township via County Road 122, this tiny school sits at the literal transition point between Zhudong's commercial lowlands and the Atayal mountain communities beyond — a location echoed in its students' performances of Atayal folklore.
