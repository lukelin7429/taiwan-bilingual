Beiyuan Junior High School

北園國中

A West District junior high named after “Northern Fragrance, Autumn Lotus,” one of the classical Eight Scenic Sights of old Chiayi.

About · 學校簡介

Named for one of Chiayi's classical scenic sights.

Beiyuan Junior High School was established with Yang Qingming, then principal of Xikou Junior High, serving as preparatory director. Its first official principal, Chen Zong-ern, took office in August 1973. The school takes its name from “Northern Fragrance, Autumn Lotus” (北園秋蓮), one of the traditional “Eight Sights of Zhuoluo” (old Chiayi), tying it to the city's classical heritage. As of December 2023, the school served about 343 students with 40 teachers.

北園國中創校時由溪口國中校長楊清明兼任籌備主任，首任正式校長陳宗恩於民國62年（1973年）8月到任。校名取自諸羅（嘉義舊稱）八景之一的「北園秋蓮」，與嘉義的古典人文地景相連結。截至2023年12月，全校約有343名學生、40位教師。
