About · 學校簡介

Over a century of names and history on Hsinchu's coast.

Neihu Elementary traces its roots to 1918, when it opened as the Yanshui Branch of Xiangshan National School. Over the following decades it was renamed several times as Taiwan's administration changed — Xiangshan Yanshui Public School in 1928, Hsinchu City Yanshui National School in 1941, Hsinchu City Eighth National School in 1946 — before finally becoming Neihu National School in 1947 and, in 1982, Hsinchu City Neihu Elementary School. Today it serves nearly 500 students across 19 classes, with a school district that extends into part of neighboring Zhunan Township in Miaoli County.

內湖國小創立於民國7年（1918年），最初為香山公學校的鹽水分教場。歷經數次更名——1928年香山鹽水公學校、1941年新竹市鹽水國民學校、1946年新竹市第八國民學校——於1947年定名為內湖國民學校，並於1982年改為現名新竹市內湖國民小學。學校已有逾百年歷史，目前約有19班、近500名學生，學區更延伸至苗栗縣竹南鎮部分地區。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📜 A Century of Names

百年校名沿革

Few schools in Hsinchu can trace as long or winding a naming history — from an 1918 branch school through five name changes to its present identity, mirroring a century of change in Taiwan itself.

🌊 A Coastal Fishing Heritage

濱海漁村文化

The school sits within a traditional Minnan coastal settlement long sustained by oyster farming and fishing, giving students a strong sense of place rooted in the sea.

🏫 A Cross-County Community

跨縣市學區

Its school district reaches beyond Hsinchu City into part of Zhunan Township in neighboring Miaoli County, making it a shared anchor for families on both sides of the boundary.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Xiangshan, Hsinchu's coastal Minnan heartland.

🦪

Xiangshan District is Hsinchu City's coastal district, long shaped by Minnan fishing and oyster-farming traditions and fringed by tidal wetlands. Its historic "mother school," Xiangshan Elementary, was founded in 1908 through the philanthropy of a local gentry family and later gave rise to several neighborhood schools, including this one's neighbors across the district.
