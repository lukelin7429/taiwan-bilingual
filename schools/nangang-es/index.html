Nangang Elementary School

南港國民小學

A small rural elementary school in Puyan Township, celebrating 50 years of independence with a strong taiko-drumming and food-and-farming tradition.

About · 學校簡介

Half a century of independence, marked in 2025.

Nangang Elementary began in August 1962 as a branch campus of Puyan Elementary School, becoming a fully independent school in August 1975 under founding principal Xu Lantian. The school is celebrating its 50th anniversary of independence in 2025. A small, close-knit campus in Nangang Village, it has built a distinctive identity around taiko drumming, the arts, and a dedicated food-and-agriculture curriculum, with students also competing nationally in Cool English, martial arts, and Go.

南港國小於民國51年（1962年）8月，最初作為埔鹽國小的分校，並於民國64年（1975年）8月在創校校長許蘭田的帶領下正式獨立設校。學校於2025年慶祝獨立設校50週年。這所座落於南港村的小型學校，發展出以太鼓、藝術與食農教育為特色的辦學方向，學生也在Cool English、武術與圍棋等項目上屢獲全國性佳績。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥁 The Sound of Taiko

太鼓隊特色

Nangang Elementary is known for its taiko drumming program, giving students a distinctive performing-arts outlet alongside its academic curriculum.

🎉 A Golden Anniversary

獨立設校50週年

The school is celebrating 50 years of independence in 2025, having become its own school in 1975 after 13 years as a branch of Puyan Elementary.

🌾 Learning From the Land

食農特色課程

A dedicated food-and-agriculture curriculum connects students directly to the farming life of Puyan Township, alongside a strong visual-arts program.

🏆 Competing Beyond the Classroom

Cool English、武術、圍棋競賽

Students from this small school have gone on to compete nationally in Cool English, martial arts, and Go — a notable range of achievement for its size.
