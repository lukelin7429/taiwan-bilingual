Dongrong Junior High School

東榮國中

A small junior high in Minsyong's farming villages, offering a taekwondo resource class alongside food, agricultural, and environmental education.

About · 學校簡介

A small school with a big heart.

Dongrong Junior High was established in August 1968 with an initial cohort of eight classes. Today it remains a small school — as of August 2025, it enrolled just 88 students across three grades (36 in Grade 1, 23 in Grade 2, and 29 in Grade 3), served by one principal, 17 teachers, five staff, and one nurse. The school offers a taekwondo resource class, runs dedicated food/agricultural and environmental education programs with their own web resources, and holds "教學正常學校" (normal teaching / non-cram-school) certification status, reflecting its focus on well-rounded, standards-based instruction.

東榮國中創立於民國57年（1968年）8月，草創初期共8班。如今學校規模依然精小──截至2025年8月，全校僅88名學生（一年級36人、二年級23人、三年級29人），由1位校長、17位教師、5位職員及1位護理師共同服務。學校設有跆拳道資源班，並推動設有專屬網站的食農教育與環境教育課程，且獲頒「教學正常學校」認證，展現對均衡、正規教學的堅持。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥋 A Taekwondo Resource Class

跆拳道資源班

A dedicated taekwondo resource class gives students at this small school a distinctive athletic track uncommon among rural junior highs of its size.

🌱 Food, Agricultural, and Environmental Education

食農與環境教育

The school runs dedicated food/agricultural and environmental education programs, each supported by its own web resources, connecting students to Minsyong's farming landscape.

✅ Normal-Teaching Certified

教學正常學校認證

The school holds "教學正常學校" status, a certification recognizing its commitment to standards-based, non-cram-school instruction.

👥 A Close-Knit, Small Community

小而精的校園社群

With just 88 students across all three grades as of 2025, Dongrong offers a tight-knit environment where students receive close attention from teachers and staff.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A window into rural Minsyong.

🌾

As a small, low-enrollment junior high in Minsyong's farming villages, Dongrong Junior High illustrates the rural depopulation pattern common across the township outside its university-town core, home to National Chung Cheng University and National Chiayi University's Minsyong campus.
