About · 學校簡介

A school defined by its affection for one village.

Jiangong Elementary was founded in 1964 to serve Shifen, a small oyster-farming and fishing village in Qigu District. The school celebrated its 60th anniversary in 2024 under the theme "建功薪傳一甲子 菁莪永固十份情" — roughly, "sixty years of passing on Jiangong's legacy, forever bound to Shifen." That closing phrase, "十份情" ("affection for Shifen"), captures how tightly the school's identity is woven into its single fishing hamlet. The school and its oyster-farming curriculum ties were also featured in a public-television segment on the customs of the Shifen fishing village.

建功國小創校於民國53年（1964年），服務七股區的養蚵漁村十份。學校於2024年舉辦建校六十週年慶祝活動，主題為「建功薪傳一甲子 菁莪永固十份情」，其中「十份情」一詞道出學校與這個小漁村緊密相繫的情感連結。學校也曾在公視「十份漁村采風」專題中被報導，反映其結合在地養蚵產業的課程特色。

🎉 Sixty Years, One Guiding Phrase

六十週年「十份情」

The school's 2024 60th-anniversary slogan, "建功薪傳一甲子 菁莪永固十份情," explicitly names its affection for Shifen village, underscoring a tight school-community bond built over six decades.

🦪 Curriculum Tied to Oyster Farming

🏘️ A Single-Village School
