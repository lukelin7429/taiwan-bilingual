Fengdong Junior High School

豐東國民中學

A junior high school on Fengyuan's east side, recently awarded over NT$200 million to rebuild its aging school buildings.

About · 學校簡介

A school investing in its future.

Fengdong Junior High School was established around 1968, taking its name — meaning "Fengyuan East" — from its position on the eastern side of the district. Its catchment area grew alongside Fengyuan's postwar suburban expansion, and the school has more recently received over NT$200 million in government funding to rebuild its aging campus buildings.

豐東國中創立於約民國57年（1968年），校名「豐東」意指「豐原之東」，取自其座落於豐原區東側的地理位置。其學區隨著豐原戰後的郊區發展而擴大，近年更獲政府補助逾兩億元新台幣，用於重建老舊校舍。
