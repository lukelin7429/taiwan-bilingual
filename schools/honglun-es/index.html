About · 學校簡介

A quiet school among the rice paddies.

Honglun Elementary is a small rural school with 6 classes and around 48 students, located among the rice paddies outside Tuku's old market street. Alongside core academics, the school offers wood-ball, dance, diabolo, and recorder clubs, giving its small student body a well-rounded set of extracurricular options.

宏崙國小是一所小型鄉村學校，共有6個班級、約48名學生，座落於土庫老街外圍的稻田間。除了一般課程外，學校也開設木球、舞蹈、扯鈴、直笛等社團，讓為數不多的學生也能有多元的課外學習選擇。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏑 A Wood-Ball Tradition

木球社團特色

Despite its small size, Honglun offers a wood-ball club — a Taiwan-invented sport — alongside dance, diabolo, and recorder clubs for its roughly 48 students.

🌾 Set Among the Rice Paddies

環繞稻田的校園

The campus sits in the farmland outside Tuku's old market street, giving students an everyday view of the rice cultivation that shapes the surrounding villages.

🎶 Music and Movement for All

音樂與體育並重

With just 6 classes total, Honglun keeps class sizes small while still offering recorder and dance clubs alongside its physical education program.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Farmland beside a soy-sauce town.

🌾

Tucked among the rice paddies outside Tuku's old market street, Honglun Elementary reflects the quiet agricultural rhythms of the villages that once supplied the trading town's soy-sauce and grain commerce — Tuku remains known today as a traditional soy-sauce brewing town.
