Cheng-Gong Junior High School

成功國中

A public junior high built to meet Zhubei's surging enrollment, with campus buildings arranged around a running track in a Chinese-character layout.

About · 學校簡介

Born from Zhubei's population boom.

Cheng-Gong Junior High was approved in August 2007 under the working name "Shixing Junior High," renamed Cheng-Gong in February 2008, and enrolled its first class in 2009 before its new campus opened in September 2010. That first cohort of 10 classes was temporarily housed at the county sports stadium while construction finished. The permanent campus buildings are arranged around a 200-meter track to form a Chinese-character layout, and the school has run athletics classes since 2010 and a math/science gifted resource class since 2011. A 2024 news report noted that roughly 35% of students earn straight-A grades.

成功國中於2007年8月奉准籌設，原定名稱為「十興國中」，2008年2月更名為成功國中，2009年首屆招生，2010年9月新校舍啟用。首屆10個班級曾暫時借用縣立體育場上課，直到新校舍完工。校園建築環繞200公尺跑道排列成漢字造型，自2010年起開設體育班，2011年起設立數理資優資源班。根據2024年新聞報導，該校約35%學生成績維持全A。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏟️ A Campus Shaped Like a Character

漢字造型校舍

The school's buildings are arranged around a 200-meter track to form a Chinese-character layout — a distinctive design choice among Zhubei's newer campuses.

Cheng-Gong has run athletics classes since 2010 and a math/science gifted resource class since 2011, giving students structured tracks in both sport and academics.

快速成立又迅速額滿

Local Culture · 在地文化

A textbook case of Zhubei's growth.

🚄

Cheng-Gong's rapid founding-to-overcrowding cycle mirrors Zhubei's population boom, as families relocating for Hsinchu Science Park jobs and the High Speed Rail station have driven persistent demand for new school seats across the city.
