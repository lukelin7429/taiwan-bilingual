National Tsing Hua University

國立清華大學

A leading national research university re-established in Hsinchu in 1956, sitting at the heart of Taiwan's semiconductor and high-tech research corridor.

About · 學校簡介

From Boxer Indemnity scholarships to Taiwan's tech corridor.

National Tsing Hua University's origins reach back to April 11, 1911, when it was founded in Beijing as Tsinghua College, funded through the return of Boxer Indemnity scholarship money. The university was re-established at its present Hsinchu location in 1956. Its main campus, along with the additional Nanda Campus, sits adjacent to the Hsinchu Science Park and near major research institutes including ITRI, the National Synchrotron Radiation Research Center, and the National Center for High-Performance Computing. Today NTHU is organized into 12 colleges with 26 departments and 31 graduate institutes, offering more than 140 degree programs to roughly 18,000 students — about 9,000 undergraduates and 9,000 graduate students — supported by around 3,000 faculty.

國立清華大學的歷史可追溯至1911年4月11日，當時以「清華學堂」之名在北京創立，經費來自庚子賠款的退還。學校於1956年在新竹現址復校。主校區與南大校區皆緊鄰新竹科學園區，並鄰近工研院、國家同步輻射研究中心、國家高速網路與計算中心等重要研究機構。目前清華大學設有12個學院、26個系及31個研究所，提供逾140個學位學程，學生總數約18,000人——其中大學部與研究所各約9,000人，並有約3,000位教職員。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Twelve Colleges

十二學院

NTHU spans 12 colleges, 26 departments, and 31 graduate institutes, offering more than 140 degree programs across the sciences, engineering, humanities, and beyond.

🏛️ A Century-Old Lineage

百年學術傳承

Founded in Beijing in 1911 through Boxer Indemnity scholarship funds and re-established in Hsinchu in 1956, NTHU carries one of the longest continuous academic lineages of any Taiwanese university.

💻 At the Heart of Hsinchu Science Park

緊鄰新竹科學園區

The campus sits directly adjacent to the Hsinchu Science Park and near research heavyweights like ITRI and the National Synchrotron Radiation Research Center, tightly linking the university to Taiwan's tech industry.

🌏 Roughly 18,000 Students

學生總數約18,000人

With about 9,000 undergraduates and 9,000 graduate students supported by around 3,000 faculty, NTHU operates at a scale that combines research intensity with a strong postgraduate community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A campus inside Taiwan's tech corridor.

💡

NTHU is deeply embedded in the Hsinchu Science Park tech corridor, functioning as a key research and talent pipeline for Taiwan's semiconductor and high-tech industry cluster — one of the most important such clusters in the world. See the Hsinchu City county page for more on the region.
