Daxi Elementary School

大溪國民小學

A coastal elementary school facing Turtle Island directly across the Pacific, next to Toucheng's famous Honeymoon Bay surfing coast.

About · 學校簡介

A schoolyard view of Turtle Island.

Daxi Elementary traces its history to 1918, when it opened as a branch of Toucheng Public School under the name 頭城公學校大谷分校. It became independent in 1922 as 大谷公學校, was renamed 大溪國民學校 in 1946, and moved into its current campus building, completed in 1998. The school sits directly opposite Turtle Island (龜山島) on the Pacific coast, next to the scenic Waiao area known as "Honeymoon Bay" (蜜月灣), a popular surfing destination.

大溪國小前身為民國7年（1918年）成立的頭城公學校大谷分校，民國11年（1922年）獨立設校，改名大谷公學校，民國35年（1946年）更名為大溪國民學校，現今校舍於民國87年（1998年）落成啟用。學校正對太平洋上的龜山島，緊鄰外澳「蜜月灣」風景區，是熱門的衝浪勝地。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🐢 A View of Turtle Island

正對龜山島

The school directly overlooks Turtle Island (龜山島) on the Pacific horizon, one of Yilan's most iconic natural landmarks.

🏄 Next Door to Honeymoon Bay

緊鄰蜜月灣

The school sits beside Waiao's "Honeymoon Bay" (蜜月灣), a well-known surfing area that draws visitors from across Taiwan.

🏫 A Modern Campus, a Century-Old School

1998年新校舍

Though its roots trace back to 1918, the school's current campus building was completed in 1998, blending a long history with modern facilities.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Toucheng's scenic gateway coastline.

🌊

The school's direct view of Guishan Island (龜山島) and its proximity to Honeymoon Bay embody Toucheng's identity as a scenic gateway coastline — the same shores early Han settlers first encountered when arriving in Yilan.
