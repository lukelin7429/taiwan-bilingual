About · 學校簡介

Taiwan's only public school built entirely around martial arts.

Founded in 2011, Fuxing Wushu Junior High and Elementary School is Taiwan's first and only public school dedicated to martial arts education, covering all nine grades from elementary through junior high on a single campus. The school trains students in wushu, taekwondo, judo, wrestling, sanda, and lion dance, producing national-team athletes and champions. Boarding students come from all over Taiwan to train in its dedicated combat sports hall, judo and wrestling area, taekwondo hall, wushu hall, and weight room.

福興武術國中小創立於2011年（民國100年），是全台第一所、也是唯一一所以武術教育為特色的公立學校，涵蓋國小到國中共九個年級，一貫制設校。學校訓練學生武術、跆拳道、柔道、角力、散打與舞獅，培育出多位國手與冠軍選手。來自全台各地的住宿生在專屬的搏擊館、柔道角力場、跆拳道館、武術館與重量訓練室中接受訓練。

A distinctive modern addition to Tongxiao's cultural landscape, Fuxing Wushu blends nationwide martial-arts talent development with the township's traditional identity as a coastal farming and fishing community along the Baishatun Mazu pilgrimage route.
