About · 學校簡介

An old school with an unexpected icon.

Dulan Elementary was founded in 1907 during the Japanese colonial era under the name "Turan Public School," going through several renamings before settling on its current name in 1968. It was officially designated an Indigenous-focus school in 2006. In 1947, the school spawned a branch in Xingchang, which became independent the following year. Located in Dulan — Taitung's celebrated Amis Indigenous village turned artist-and-surfer haven — the school is one of the oldest in the area, with more than a century of history serving the community.

都蘭國小創立於1907年（日治時期），初名「都鑾公學校」，歷經多次更名，1968年定名為現名，並於2006年正式列為原住民重點學校。1947年，學校分出興昌分校，隔年獨立設校。學校位於都蘭——台東知名的阿美族部落，如今已發展為藝術家與衝浪客聚集的天地——是當地歷史最悠久的學校之一，逾百年來持續服務這個社區。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎒 A Backpack That Became a Souvenir

全台聞名的書包

Dulan Elementary's school backpack has become famous throughout Taiwan, turning into a popular fashion item and souvenir sold in shops around the village — a rare case of a school uniform accessory becoming a cultural icon.

🏛️ More Than a Century of History

創校超過百年

Founded in 1907, Dulan Elementary is one of the oldest schools on this stretch of coast, having grown and renamed itself several times across more than a hundred years.

🌺 A Designated Indigenous-Focus School

原住民重點學校

Officially designated an Indigenous-focus school in 2006, Dulan Elementary formally recognizes and supports the Amis heritage of the village it serves.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Dulan, an Amis village turned cultural landmark.

🎒

Dulan Village is Taitung's best-known Amis Indigenous community, having grown over recent decades into a magnet for artists and surfers along the East Coast. Amid that transformation, this century-old school's backpack has become an unlikely and beloved cultural icon, sold as a keepsake by shops throughout the village.
