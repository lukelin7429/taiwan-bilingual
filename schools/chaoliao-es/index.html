About · 學校簡介

From branch campus to sugar-land school.

Chaoliao Elementary began in September 1948 as a branch campus of Zhaoming Elementary, becoming a formally established independent school on March 13, 1953, with five classes. The land-purchase agreement for its current campus was finalized in 1972 with the Taiwan Sugar Corporation, reflecting the historic role of the sugar industry in shaping land use across this part of Kaohsiung.

潮寮國小於民國37年（1948年）9月以昭明國小分校形式成立，民國42年（1953年）3月13日正式獨立設校，初期設有五個班級。現址校地的購地協議於1972年與台灣糖業公司完成，反映了糖業在高雄這一帶土地利用歷史上的重要角色。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Grown From Zhaoming Elementary

源自昭明國小

The school began in 1948 as a branch campus of Zhaoming Elementary before becoming fully independent in 1953.

🌾 A Land Deal With Taiwan Sugar

與台糖的購地協議

The current campus site was secured through a 1972 land-purchase agreement with the Taiwan Sugar Corporation, tying the school's history to the region's sugar-industry past.

🌊 Sharing a Riverside Place-Name Story

共享河濱地名故事

Like its junior-high namesake, Chaoliao Elementary carries the "潮寮" (tide hut) place name, reflecting the area's historical proximity to tidal influence near the Gaoping River.
