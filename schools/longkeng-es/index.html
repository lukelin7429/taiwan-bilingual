About · 學校簡介

A coastal-belt school with six decades of local schooling.

Longkeng Elementary started in 1953 as a branch campus of Tongguang Elementary School, becoming fully independent on September 1, 1956. The school celebrated its 60th anniversary in November 2016, and its students have been recognized in county-wide health exercise (健康操) competitions, reflecting a steady commitment to physical education alongside academics.

龍坑國小於1953年（民國42年）以通校（通霄國小系統）分校形式設立，1956年（民國45年）9月1日正式獨立設校。學校於2016年（民國105年）11月舉辦六十週年校慶，學生曾在全縣健康操競賽中獲得肯定，展現學校對體育與學業並重的用心。

Highlights · 學校特色

🏫 Six Decades of Independent Schooling

Longkeng began in 1953 as a branch of Tongguang Elementary School and became fully independent in 1956, celebrating its 60th anniversary in November 2016.

Students have been recognized in county-wide health exercise (健康操) competitions, a signature of the school's focus on physical fitness education.

Longkeng village lies in Houlong's coastal farming belt, part of the township renowned across Taiwan as the watermelon and peanut growing region, built on sandy alluvial soil that has shaped local livelihoods for generations. See the Miaoli county page for more on the region.
