Wenxian Elementary School

文賢國小

A century-old school that began in a local temple, and went on to become the "mother school" of two neighboring elementary schools.

About · 學校簡介

From temple classrooms to a school family tree.

Wenxian Elementary began in 1920, holding its first classes in the local Cihji Temple, before becoming independent on April 1, 1922, as Hunei Village's Third Public School. The school marked its centennial with alumni reunions, and over the decades served as the "mother school" for two neighboring elementary schools — Sanhou Elementary, which split off in 1965, and Haipu Elementary, in 1955.

文賢國小創立於1920年（日治時期），最初借用當地慈濟宮作為教室上課，於民國11年（1922年）4月1日獨立設校，定名「湖內庄第三公學校」。學校曾舉辦建校百週年校友返校活動，並在其後數十年間先後分校出三侯國小（1965年）與海埔國小（1955年），成為兩校的「母校」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛩️ Classes That Began in a Temple

借用慈濟宮上課

Wenxian's earliest classes were held inside the local Cihji Temple, reflecting how early Taiwanese public education under Japanese rule often relied on existing community religious buildings.

🌳 Mother School to Two Neighbors

分校出兩所國小

Both Sanhou Elementary (1965) and Haipu Elementary (1955) trace their roots to Wenxian, making it the root of a small family of Hunei District schools.

🎓 A Century of Alumni

百年校友歡聚

The school's centennial celebration brought generations of alumni back together, honoring a century of continuous education in the community.
