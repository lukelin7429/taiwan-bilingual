About · 學校簡介

A motto for whole-child growth.

Houzhuang Elementary serves the "後庄" (Houzhuang) community — a village name meaning roughly "village behind" or "village beyond," a common settlement-pattern place name found across Taiwan, here describing its position relative to central Daliao. The school operates under the motto "多元、人本、適性、自然" ("diverse, humanistic, individualized, natural") and includes an attached kindergarten serving the youngest members of its community.

後庄國小服務「後庄」聚落，這個地名意指位於某個中心之外或之後的聚落型態，是台灣常見的命名方式，在此則描述其相對於大寮中心區的地理位置。學校秉持「多元、人本、適性、自然」的校訓，並附設幼兒園，服務社區內最年幼的孩子。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A place name that marks position.

🗺️

"後庄" is one of many Taiwanese place names built around a village's position relative to a more central settlement — in this case, central Daliao. Similarly named "後庄" or "后庄" communities exist elsewhere in Taiwan, including Pingtung and Miaoli, though they are unrelated to this one. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
