About · 學校簡介

A small, open campus in Kaohsiung's old harbor district.

Guangrong Elementary School is a small, old-fashioned campus in Yancheng District, with just two classes per grade level. Unlike many modern Taiwanese schools, it has no concrete perimeter wall, giving the campus an open, low-density feel that stands out in the dense, historic streets of downtown Kaohsiung. Its precise founding date is not confirmed, but the school has long served families in this reclaimed harbor-side district.

光榮國小是鹽埕區一所小巧、帶有古早味的校園，每個年級僅設兩個班。與許多現代化的台灣校園不同，光榮國小沒有水泥圍牆，讓校園在高雄市區密集的老街巷弄中，顯得格外低密度而開放。確切創校年代尚未確認，但學校長期服務這個由港灣填海而成的地區的學童家庭。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Guangrong Elementary sits on land reclaimed from Yancheng's former salt flats and harbor fill, part of the transformation that turned the district into Kaohsiung's earliest commercial center. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
