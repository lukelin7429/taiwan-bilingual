Chaosheng Elementary School

潮昇國民小學

A purpose-built elementary school serving a newly developed urban-renewal district in central Chaozhou Town, among the larger elementary schools in this part of Pingtung.

為服務潮州鎮新興都市更新住宅區而設立的國小，是本批屏東學校中規模較大者之一。

About · 學校簡介

Built to serve a growing town.

Chaosheng Elementary School was established relatively recently, in the post-war era, specifically to relieve overcrowding at Chaozhou Elementary and Guanghua Elementary as Chaozhou's urban-renewal district grew and its population increased — in line with Taiwan's “small class, small school” education policy. The school now has a relatively large enrollment for the area, with about 539 students and 36 staff, making it among the larger elementary schools in this part of Pingtung, purpose-built to balance regional education access as the town urbanized.

潮昇國小為戰後相對晚近設立的學校，目的是紓解潮州國小與光華國小因潮州鎮都市更新地區發展、人口成長所帶來的招生壓力，呼應台灣「小班小校」的教育政策。學校目前規模相對較大，約有539名學生、36位教職員，是這批屏東學校中規模較大者之一，其設立初衷正是為了平衡都市化過程中的區域教育資源分配。

Chaosheng Elementary was purpose-built to serve a newly developed urban-renewal residential district in Chaozhou Town, a direct response to the town's growth.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Chaozhou, southern Pingtung's rail and market hub.

🚂

Chaosheng Elementary sits in central Chaozhou Town — southern Pingtung's major market town and transportation hub, historically built on the sugar industry and rail transport, including the nearby Chaozhou Forest Railway heritage park. The school's own recent founding mirrors the town's ongoing growth and renewal.
