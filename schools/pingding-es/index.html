About · 學校簡介

A hill-country school carrying forward folk performing arts.

Pingding Elementary sits on hilly terrain at the border of Tongxiao and Sanyi Township, celebrated for its 60th anniversary. About half the school district's families farm, and roughly a quarter of students come from disadvantaged households. The school is known for its traditional dragon dance and war-drum (戰鼓) troupes, a soccer team, and a Shennong farming-education curriculum.

坪頂國小座落於通霄鎮與三義鄉交界的丘陵地帶，曾舉辦六十週年校慶。學區內約半數家庭務農，約26%學生來自弱勢家庭。學校以傳統舞龍與戰鼓表演隊、足球隊，以及神農食農教育課程聞名。
