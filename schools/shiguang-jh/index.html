About · 學校簡介

A farming-district school built for the digital age.

Shiguang Junior High serves a quiet farming community in the hills around Guanxi's historic old town, and recently marked around seventy years since its founding in the mid-1950s. Alongside its academic program, the school runs "Shiguang Cloud Academy," its own online learning platform, and offers career and technical education plus environmental and gender-equality education programs for its students.

石光國中座落於關西老街周邊的寧靜農村地帶，近年慶祝創校約七十週年，推估創立於1950年代中期。除一般學科教育外，學校自建「石光雲端學院」線上學習平台，並開設生涯技職教育、環境教育與性別平等教育等課程。
