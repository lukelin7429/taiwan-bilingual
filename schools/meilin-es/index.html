About · 學校簡介

A quiet school in Douliu's farmland.

Meilin Elementary sits on Meilin Road in a rural section of Douliu City, serving a farming community typical of the pomelo- and rice-growing periphery that surrounds the county seat. Detailed institutional facts beyond its basic contact and location information are not documented in available sources, but the school continues to serve as a neighborhood anchor for the agricultural villages in this part of Douliu.

梅林國小座落於斗六市梅林路的鄉間地帶，服務典型以文旦與稻米種植為主的縣治周邊農村社區。除了基本聯絡與地理位置資訊外，目前並無更多可考的詳細校史資料，但學校持續作為斗六這一帶農業聚落的社區據點。
