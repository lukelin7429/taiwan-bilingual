About · 學校簡介

Sharing in a district's own legend.

Niaosong Junior High School had about 157 students and 23 teachers as of 2023. Like other schools in the district, it shares in the local place-name legend behind 'Niaosongjiao' (鳥松腳), the origin story of the Niaosong District name itself.

鳥松國中截至2023年約有157名學生、23位教師。與區內其他學校一樣，鳥松國中也承載著「鳥松腳」地名由來的在地傳說。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Mid-Size District Junior High

中型社區國中

As of 2023, Niaosong Junior High served about 157 students with 23 teachers, serving families across Niaosong District.

🐦 Sharing the 'Niaosongjiao' Legend

「鳥松腳」地名傳說

Like other schools in the district, Niaosong Junior High shares in the local legend behind the district's own name, 鳥松腳 (Niaosongjiao) — a story about a banyan tree and the birds that fed on it.

🏘️ Serving the Heart of Niaosong District

服務鳥松區

The school serves families in Niaosong District, a suburban Kaohsiung community best known for Chengqing Lake and the Kaohsiung Golf Course.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Sharing in the Niaosongjiao legend.

🐦

Niaosong Junior High shares in the local legend behind the district's own name — 鳥松腳 (Niaosongjiao) — said to come from a banyan tree near a village hall where birds gathered to feed. See Niaosong Elementary School's page for the full story, and the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
