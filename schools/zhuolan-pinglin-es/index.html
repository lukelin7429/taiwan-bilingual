About · 學校簡介

The mother school of Zhuolan's mountain hamlets.

Pinglin Elementary traces its origins to the Japanese colonial era, when it began as a branch teaching site. Over the decades it grew into the "mother school" of several communities in Zhuolan Township, spinning off multiple branch schools that later became fully independent: the Caoliao Branch, now Jingshan Elementary School, and the Shuanglian Branch, now Shuanglian Elementary School. It also once oversaw the Baifan Branch, which was later transferred to Neiwan Elementary. The school is also known for its notable achievements in folk dragon-dance activities.

坪林國小的歷史可追溯至日治時期，最初以分教場的形式設立。歷經數十年發展，坪林國小成為卓蘭鎮多個社區的「母校」，先後分出多所分校，日後獨立設校：草寮分校（今景山國小）與雙連分校（今雙連國小）。學校也曾管轄白布帆分校，後來改隸內灣國小。此外，坪林國小在民俗舞龍活動方面表現亮眼。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 A True Mother School

卓蘭鎮的母校

Pinglin Elementary historically served as the parent school for several branch campuses across Zhuolan Township, a legacy still felt in the area's school network today.

🏫 Gave Rise to Two Independent Schools

催生兩所獨立學校

Its former Caoliao Branch and Shuanglian Branch both grew into fully independent schools — now known as Jingshan Elementary and Shuanglian Elementary.

🐉 A Tradition of Dragon Dance

民俗舞龍活動

The school has earned recognition for its folk dragon-dance activities, keeping a vibrant piece of traditional performance culture alive among its students.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Zhuolan's fruit-farming frontier.

🍐

Pinglin's settlement history as a fruit-farming frontier community, and its role as the "mother school" that spun off Jingshan and Shuanglian elementary schools, epitomizes Zhuolan Township's identity built around high-mountain orchard agriculture.
