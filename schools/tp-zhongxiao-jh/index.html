Zhongxiao Junior High School

忠孝國中

A public junior high built up from scratch in the early 1970s, whose campus took on its present arts-oriented look after a 2011 beautification project.

About · 學校簡介

Built up from scratch, block by block.

Zhongxiao Junior High began preparatory work in July 1969 under founding principal Zhang Liangbin and was formally established on August 1, 1970. Its main campus buildings — three teaching blocks, a reviewing stand, a sports field, and basketball and volleyball courts — were completed by June 1975, and a student activity center followed in 1979. In 2011, a "campus beautification" project gave the school its current arts-oriented campus look, which visitors still see today.

忠孝國中於民國58年（1969年）7月開始籌備，首任校長為張良弼，並於民國59年（1970年）8月1日正式成立。主要校舍——三棟教學大樓、司令台、運動場及籃排球場——於民國64年（1975年）6月前陸續完工，民國68年（1979年）增建學生活動中心。民國100年（2011年）的「校園美化」工程，賦予校園今日充滿藝術氣息的風貌。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Taipei's oldest district.

🏮

Zhongxiao Junior High sits within Datong District, Taipei's oldest settled area, though no distinct historical tie between the campus itself and specific nearby landmarks has been documented. The district around it remains closely identified with the historic Dadaocheng old street, its Qing-era tea-export trade, and landmarks such as the Confucius Temple and Bao'an Temple in nearby Dalongdong.
