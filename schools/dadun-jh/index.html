About · 學校簡介

A young school with an old name and a famous song.

Dadun Junior High opened in 2010, one of the newer schools built as Nantun District continued its rapid growth. Its school song, “Forest of Hope” (希望森林), was written by celebrity Mandopop lyricist Fangwenshan, with music composed by singer Yuan Yong-lin — an unusual pairing of pop-culture talent with a public junior high school. The school's own name, “Dadun” (大墩, Big Mound), recalls the old Qing-dynasty name for the area that grew into modern central Taichung, linking a brand-new campus to the city's historic toponym.

大墩國中創立於民國99年（2010年），是南屯區持續快速發展下誕生的新興學校之一。校歌「希望森林」由知名作詞人方文山填詞、歌手袁詠琳譜曲，讓一所公立國中擁有流行音樂人加持的校歌。校名「大墩」則取自清代對今日台中市中心一帶的舊稱，讓嶄新校園與城市歷史地名產生連結。
