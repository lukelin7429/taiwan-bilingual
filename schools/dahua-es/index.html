About · 學校簡介

Founded for Air Force families.

Dahua Elementary began on September 1, 1950 as the "Dahua Branch Class" of the Air Force Dependents' School, established to serve children of Air Force military-dependent families living near a nearby air base, alongside surrounding farming households. It became an independent school — Dahua Elementary of Luzhu Township, Taoyuan County — on August 1, 1970, after the township office purchased the school site in 1969. Ten principals have served since founding principal Chen Xigun (1970–1978), and the school continues to serve a farming-community and suburban-fringe catchment area.

大華國小於民國39年（1950年）9月1日創校，初名「空軍子弟小學大華分班」，專為鄰近空軍基地的空軍眷屬子弟及周邊農家子弟而設。民國58年（1969年）鄉公所購置校地，民國59年（1970年）8月1日獨立設校，定名「桃園縣蘆竹鄉大華國民小學」。自創校校長陳錫崑（1970–1978年）起，歷任校長已達十位，學校至今持續服務農村與郊區學童。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A marker of Luzhu's aviation history.

✈️

Dahua Elementary's founding purpose — educating the children of Air Force dependents — is a direct historical marker of Luzhu District's decades-long relationship with military and civil aviation, a geography that today makes the district home to the northern approach path of Taoyuan International Airport. The school's origin near an air base, serving both military families and local farming households, captures a chapter of Luzhu's history that predates the modern airport itself.
