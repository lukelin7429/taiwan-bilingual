About · 學校簡介

A forest school with a century of history.

Shanjia Elementary School opened in 1920 during the Japanese colonial era and has grown into what its community calls a "forest school in the city" — a century-old campus backed by Dadong Mountain and encircled by two historic irrigation channels. In a modern twist on its long history, the school has repurposed recycled shipping containers as classrooms, blending its old-town setting with contemporary sustainable design.

山佳國小創立於1920年日治時期，如今被在地社區稱為「都市中的森林小學」——這所百年老校背倚大棟山，並被兩條歷史悠久的圳道環繞。學校近年更以回收貨櫃改建教室，將老聚落的歷史氛圍與現代永續設計巧妙結合。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌲 A "Forest School" Backed by a Mountain

「都市中的森林小學」

Community members describe the century-old campus as a forest school in the city, set against the backdrop of Dadong Mountain.

💧 Encircled by Historic Irrigation Channels

兩條歷史圳道環繞

The school sits between two old irrigation channels that once watered Shanjia's farmland, tying its campus directly to the area's agricultural past.

📦 Recycled Shipping-Container Classrooms

貨櫃教室

In a striking modern touch, the school has repurposed recycled shipping containers as classrooms, pairing sustainable design with its century-old campus.

🚉 Named for the Historic Shanjia Station

因山佳車站得名

The school shares its name with Shanjia, an old Hakka hill-and-water settlement and its namesake historic railway station on the Shulin rail line.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Home to a historic railway settlement.

🚉

Shanjia (山佳) is an old Hakka hill-and-water settlement whose name and identity are inseparable from its historic railway station on the Shulin line — one of the best-preserved Japanese-era train stations in the New Taipei area. The elementary school shares that namesake and setting. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
