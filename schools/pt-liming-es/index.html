About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school in the Rear Pile.

Liming Elementary serves families in Neipu Township, part of the historic Liudui Hakka "Rear Pile" region of Pingtung County. As a local public elementary school, it provides steady, community-based education for the township's farming villages.

黎明國小服務屏東縣內埔鄉的學童，這裡是六堆客家「後堆」的歷史區域。學校作為在地公立國小，長期為鄉內農村聚落提供穩定的社區教育。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏘️ A Community Anchor

社區教育重心

Liming Elementary serves as a steady community fixture for families across Neipu Township's farming villages.

🏮 Part of the "Rear Pile"

六堆「後堆」一員

Neipu Township, home to Liming Elementary, is the historic "Hou Dui" (Rear Pile) of the Liudui Hakka confederation, giving students a strong sense of local heritage.

🌾 A Farming Village Setting

農村聚落環境

The school reflects Neipu's long farming economy, rooted in rice and tobacco cultivation across generations of local families.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in the Liudui Hakka heartland.

🏮

Liming Elementary sits within Neipu Township, the "Hou Dui" (後堆, Rear Pile) of the Liudui Hakka confederation — a historically Hakka farming region also home to the scholarly tradition of the Changli Temple and the enduring ethos of "diligent farming, studious reading" (勤耕雨讀). See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
