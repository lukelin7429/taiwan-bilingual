Tianwei Junior High School

田尾國民中學

A public junior high school in Taiwan's "Flower Village," home to a national champion marching band and a Presidential Education Award winner.

About · 學校簡介

From village branch to award-winning campus.

Tianwei Junior High traces its roots to 1961, when it began as the planning committee for a branch of Beidou Middle School. It became a fully independent junior high school in 1964, under the name Changhua County Tianwei Junior Middle School, and took its current name in 1968. Located in Raoping Village in Tianwei Township — central Taiwan's flower-growing heartland — the school has grown alongside its farming community for over six decades, building strengths in music, sport, and technology education along the way.

田尾國中的歷史可追溯至民國50年（1961年），最初以「北斗中學田尾分部籌備處」的形式成立，於民國53年（1964年）獨立設校，定名為「彰化縣立田尾初級中學」，並於民國57年（1968年）改為現名。學校位於田尾鄉饒平村——中台灣花卉種植的核心地帶，六十餘年來與在地農村社區一同成長，並在音樂、體育與科技教育方面累積深厚實力。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎺 A National Champion Marching Band

全國冠軍管樂隊

The school's wind and marching band took first place in the central regional finals of the National Student Music Competition in 2016 — a source of lasting school pride.

🏅 A Presidential Education Award

總統教育獎得主

The school's sports program — spanning track and field, korfball, and table tennis — produced a student who won Taiwan's Presidential Education Award in 2020.

🛠️ A Maker Education Center

田尾自造教育中心

In 2018, the school established the Changhua County Tianwei Maker Education and Technology Center, bringing hands-on STEM learning to students across the township.

🌾 Grown From a Village Branch

從分部籌備處起步

Founded in 1961 as a planning committee for a branch campus, the school became fully independent within a few years — a steady rise shared with its farming community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Tianwei, Taiwan's Flower Village.

🌼

Tianwei Junior High sits in Raoping Village, part of Tianwei Township — known across Taiwan as the "Flower Village" (花卉之鄉), the island's largest flower-growing and landscaping district. The school has grown alongside this agricultural community since its founding as a village branch school in the 1960s. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
