Daxi Junior High School

大西國民中學

Founded to serve students in Zaoqiao's remote eastern hills, where the commute to town was once impractical.

About · 學校簡介

Bridging the distance across Zaoqiao Township.

Daxi Junior High was established in 1972, approved initially as the "Zaoqiao Junior High Daxi Branch." The school was founded because Zaoqiao Township's terrain stretches wide from east to west, making the daily commute from the Daping area to Zaoqiao's town center impractical for students. Construction of the first campus buildings began in August 1973, and the branch later became an independent junior high school in its own right, giving Zaoqiao's eastern hill communities a school of their own.

大西國中創校於1972年（民國61年），最初核准設立為「造橋國中大西分部」。設校原因在於造橋鄉地形東西狹長，大坪地區學生若要通勤至造橋市區上學相當不便。首批校舍建築於1973年8月開工興建，其後分部升格獨立設校，讓造橋鄉東側丘陵地區的學子擁有屬於自己的國中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛰️ Built for a Township's Wide Terrain

解決東西狹長地形通勤問題

Daxi Junior High was founded specifically because Zaoqiao Township's east-west terrain made commuting from Daping to the town center impractical for local students.

🏗️ From Branch to Independent School

由分部升格獨立設校

Approved in 1972 as a branch of Zaoqiao Junior High, the school's first buildings went up in August 1973 before it became a fully independent junior high.

🏔️ Serving the Daxi and Daping Hills

服務大西、大坪山區

The school anchors education for Zaoqiao's more remote eastern hill communities, giving families in Daxi and Daping villages a local option for secondary schooling.

Born from the need to serve students in Zaoqiao's remote Daxi and Daping hills, the school reflects the practical, community-driven spirit of this Hakka farming township — solving a real commuting problem with a school built close to home. See the Miaoli county page for more on the region.
