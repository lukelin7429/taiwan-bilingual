About · 學校簡介

A school that has watched Jiufen reinvent itself.

Jiufen Elementary began in 1913 as a branch school, becoming independent in 1919. It has been renamed several times over the decades — including "Jishan National School" (基山國校) after 1945 — before settling on its current name, Jiufen Elementary, in 1968. Historically tied to the Yan family, the mining magnates who shaped Jiufen's development, the school sits just above the town's famous Old Street.

九份國小於1913年以分校形式創立，1919年獨立設校。歷經多次更名，1945年後曾稱「基山國校」，直到1968年才定名為「九份國小」。學校與塑造九份發展的顏氏礦業家族淵源深厚，校址就位於知名的九份老街上方。
