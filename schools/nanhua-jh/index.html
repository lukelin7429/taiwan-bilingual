Nanhua Junior High School

南華國中

A public junior high school by the sea, grown from a branch campus into an independent school serving Hsinchu's coastal Nanliao area.

About · 學校簡介

From a coastal branch campus to its own school.

Nanhua Junior High began in 1979 as "南寮澳分部," a branch campus of Guanghua Junior High serving the coastal Nanliao area. It became a fully independent school, "新竹市立南華國民中學," in August 1983. The school sits near Hsinchu's coastline, giving it a distinct identity within North District's cluster of schools.

南華國中的前身為民國68年（1979年）設立的光華國中「南寮澳分部」，服務新竹沿海的南寮地區。民國72年（1983年）8月正式獨立設校，定名為「新竹市立南華國民中學」。學校鄰近新竹海岸，在北區學校群中具有獨特的地理定位。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌊 A School Born by the Coast

誕生於海濱地區

Nanhua started life in 1979 as Guanghua Junior High's Nanliao'ao branch campus, serving families along Hsinchu's coastal Nanliao area before becoming independent.

🏫 Independent Since 1983

1983年獨立設校

The school gained full independence in August 1983 as "新竹市立南華國民中學," marking its transition from branch campus to a standalone institution serving North District.

📍 Serving Hsinchu's Coastal Community

服務沿海社區

Located on Haibin Road near the coast, the school serves a community shaped by its proximity to the sea and to Nanliao's fishing and harbor life.
