Fucheng Senior High School

高雄市立福誠高級中學

A municipal senior high built to relieve overcrowding as Wujia's population boomed after the war.

市立高中，因戰後五甲人口暴增、原有國中不敷使用而創立。

Founded1991 (民國80年)
Grades7–12 · Junior & Senior High

About · 學校簡介

Founded to relieve a growing community's schools.

Fucheng's junior high section was established in 1991, initially borrowing classrooms from nearby Wujia Junior High because the single existing junior high could no longer serve the fast-growing Wujia population. The school moved onto its own campus in 1995, added a senior high section in 2000, and became a Kaohsiung municipal school after the 2010 city-county merger.

福誠國中部創立於民國80年（1991年），創校初期借用鄰近五甲國中的教室，因當時唯一的五甲國中已無法容納快速成長的五甲地區人口。學校於民國84年（1995年）遷入自有校地，民國89年（2000年）增設高中部，並於2010年高雄縣市合併後改制為市立學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Born From Overcrowding

因人口暴增而創校

The school was created in 1991 because Wujia's rapidly growing population had outgrown the area's single existing junior high, Wujia Junior High.

🏗️ From Borrowed Classrooms to Its Own Campus

從借教室到自有校園

Fucheng opened by borrowing classrooms from Wujia Junior High, then moved into its own campus in 1995 — a common growing-pain story for postwar Taiwanese schools.

🎓 Junior High to Senior High in a Decade

十年內增設高中部

A senior high section was added in 2000, less than ten years after the junior high section first opened, tracking Wujia's continued population growth.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A direct record of Wujia's growth.

📈

Fucheng's founding story is a direct record of Wujia's explosive postwar population growth — the school exists specifically because the district's original junior high could no longer keep up with demand. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
