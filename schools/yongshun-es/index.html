Yong-Shun Elementary School

永順國民小學

One of the newest schools in Taoyuan District, established in 2004 with its very name chosen by a vote of local residents during the planning process.

桃園區最年輕的學校之一，2004年設校，校名由地方居民票選產生。

About · 學校簡介

A school named by its own community.

Yong-Shun Elementary's planning was approved by the Taoyuan County Government in September 2001, and its name was chosen by a vote of local residents in March 2002. An architectural design contract was awarded to You Ming-guo Architects in October 2002, and the campus master plan was completed that December. The school was formally established on May 5, 2004, with enrollment beginning August 1, 2004. Tong Zheng-xian, then-principal of Bei-Men Elementary, served concurrently as preparatory principal during the planning phase. The school opened a non-profit affiliated preschool in October 2018, and is currently on its fourth principal, Lin Pei-juan, who has served since August 2024.

永順國小的籌設計畫於民國90年（2001年）9月經桃園縣政府核准，校名於2002年3月由地方居民票選產生。建築設計案於2002年10月委由游明國建築師事務所承接，校園規劃於同年12月完成。學校於民國93年（2004年）5月5日正式成立，同年8月1日開始招生。籌備期間由時任北門國小校長童振賢兼任籌備處主任。學校於2018年10月開辦非營利幼兒園，現任第四任校長林佩娟自2024年8月起接任。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗳️ A Name Chosen by the Community

居民票選校名

Yong-Shun's name was chosen through a vote of local residents in March 2002, before the campus was even built — a direct expression of community involvement in the school's founding.

🏫 A Purpose-Built 21st-Century Campus

21世紀新設校園

Its architectural plans were commissioned in 2002 and the school opened for enrollment in 2004, making it one of the newest campuses on this list.

👶 A Non-Profit Affiliated Preschool

非營利附設幼兒園

The school opened a non-profit affiliated preschool (非營利幼兒園) in October 2018, extending its services to younger children in the community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Growth in the 21st century.

Yong-Shun is another of Taoyuan District's newest schools — just over two decades old — founded through direct community naming input, reflecting how the district's historic core has kept expanding its public-school network to keep pace with continued in-migration into the 21st century.
