Zhongyuan Elementary School

中園國民小學

Built in 2002 to ease overcrowding at Sanxia Elementary, and a national award-winning leader in green-energy education.

About · 學校簡介

A school built for growth, powered by the sun.

Zhongyuan Elementary opened in 2002 to relieve overcrowding at nearby Sanxia Elementary School amid rapid population growth in the area. The school has since built a flagship "green energy education" program, featuring solar photovoltaic panels, a dedicated energy classroom, rainwater recycling systems, and a smart grid — earning it a national outstanding green-energy school award in 2010.

中園國小創立於民國91年（2002年），是為紓解鄰近三峽國小因當地人口快速成長而產生的就學壓力所設立。學校發展出「綠能教育」特色課程，設有太陽能光電板、能源教室、雨水回收系統及智慧電網，並於民國99年（2010年）榮獲全國績優綠色能源學校獎項。

☀️ A National Green-Energy Award Winner

全國績優綠能學校

The school's solar PV panels, energy classroom, and smart grid earned it a national outstanding green-energy school award in 2010.

💧 Rainwater Recycling in Action

雨水回收系統

Beyond solar power, the campus runs a rainwater recycling system as part of its hands-on approach to environmental and energy education.

🏗️ Built to Meet a Growing Town's Needs

因應人口成長而設立

Founded in 2002 specifically to relieve overcrowding at Sanxia Elementary, the school's very existence reflects Sanxia's rapid urban growth in the early 2000s.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Sanxia's newer residential growth.

🏘️

Named for its location beside Zhongyuan Street, the school reflects the newer residential infill that grew as Sanxia urbanized in the 2000s, expanding beyond its historic old-town core. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
