About · 學校簡介

A name that means victory.

Shengli Elementary School — its name meaning "Victory" — was founded in 1964 under its first principal, Lin Wei-hsing, and was renamed four years later, in 1968. Like many of Zuoying's public schools, it has served generations of local families in the decades since, growing alongside the district's development from a naval town into a broader residential community.

勝利國小（校名意為「勝利」）創立於民國53年（1964年），首任校長為林維興，四年後的民國57年（1968年）更名。如同左營許多公立學校，勝利國小數十年來服務當地世代家庭，並隨著左營從海軍重鎮發展為更廣泛的住宅社區而持續成長。
