About · 學校簡介

A century of teaching, from an ancestral hall to a modern campus.

Shagang Elementary's origins trace to April 1920, when it began as a branch of Huxi Public School at Dingwan Village. It became independent as "Huxi No. 2 Public School" in 1927, was renamed "Penghu Prefecture Shagang National School" in 1941, and took its current name in 1968. Classes were originally held in the Hong-clan ancestral hall before a dedicated schoolhouse was built. The school celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2020, drawing over 500 alumni back to campus, and has educated more than 3,000 students over its century of history — including a former Speaker of the Penghu County Council.

沙港國小校史可追溯至民國9年（1920年）4月，最初為湖西公學校設於鼎灣村的分校，民國16年（1927年）獨立設校，定名「湖西第二公學校」，民國30年（1941年）改名「澎湖廳沙港國民學校」，民國57年（1968年）定名為現名。創校之初曾借用洪氏宗祠上課，其後才興建專屬校舍。學校於民國109年（2020年）舉辦百年校慶，吸引逾500位校友返校共襄盛舉，百年來培育逾3,000名學生，校友中包括一位曾任澎湖縣議會議長者。

Highlights · 學校特色

Shagang Elementary's 2020 centennial celebration brought together more than 500 alumni, a testament to the school's century-long role in the community.

🏛️ Classes Once Held in an Ancestral Hall

Before a dedicated schoolhouse existed, the school's earliest classes were held in the Hong-clan ancestral hall, reflecting the close ties between Penghu's clan villages and their local schools.
