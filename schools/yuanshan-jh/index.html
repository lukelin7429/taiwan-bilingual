Yuanshan Junior High School

員山國民中學

A junior high school on the edge of Yuanshan's famous hot spring district, with competitive teams in badminton, women's basketball, and cycling.

About · 學校簡介

A school built on sport and international exchange.

Yuanshan Junior High School was founded around 1957 and has spent more than six decades serving students in Yuanshan Township. The school has built a strong athletics tradition, fielding competitive teams in badminton, women's basketball, and cycling — its women's basketball team has even trained abroad in Susaka City, Japan. Beyond sport, the school offers mother-tongue and Indigenous language electives alongside sustainability-themed coursework, rounding out a curriculum that looks both locally and globally.

員山國中創立於約民國46年（1957年），逾一甲子以來持續服務員山鄉學子。學校建立起深厚的體育傳統，羽球隊、女子籃球隊與自由車隊皆具競爭實力——女籃隊甚至曾遠赴日本須坂市受訓。除體育外，學校亦開設母語與原住民族語選修課程，並搭配永續發展主題課程，讓教學內容兼顧在地與國際視野。
