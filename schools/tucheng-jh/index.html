About · 學校簡介

From three classes to Tucheng's founding junior high.

Tucheng Junior High opened in 1968 as Taipei County Tucheng Junior High, with its first class made up of just three sections — a modest beginning for what would become one of Tucheng's oldest junior high schools. The school now offers a math-gifted class and language-gifted resource services, extending specialized academic support well beyond its founding scale.

土城國中創立於民國57年（1968年），初名「台北縣土城國中」，首屆僅招收三個班級，規模十分有限，日後發展為土城地區歷史最悠久的國中之一。學校現設有數理資優班與語文資優資源班服務，提供學生更多元的學術支持。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 From Three Classes to a Full School

從三個班到完整規模

Tucheng Junior High opened in 1968 with just three sections, growing over decades into one of Tucheng's oldest and largest junior highs.

🧮 A Math-Gifted Class

數理資優班

The school offers a dedicated math-gifted class, providing advanced coursework for students with strong quantitative abilities.

🗣️ Language-Gifted Resource Services

語文資優資源班

Alongside its math program, Tucheng Junior High runs language-gifted resource services, supporting students with strong verbal and linguistic aptitude.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Tracking Tucheng's shift from farmland to suburb.

🌾

As Tucheng's founding junior high, the school's growth from 3 classes to a full-scale school mirrors the district's shift from rural farmland to dense Taipei-metro suburb. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
