Cuiping Combined Junior High & Elementary School

翠屏國民中小學

A K-9 combined elementary-and-junior-high campus, one of Nanzih's newer schools built for a growing population.

About · 學校簡介

One campus, spanning elementary through junior high.

Cuiping Combined Junior High and Elementary School began preparations in March 1997 and formally began enrolling students on August 1, 1999, as a nine-year integrated experimental school combining elementary and junior high divisions on one campus. Spread across roughly 28,614 square meters, it today has about 54 classes and 1,399 students, guided by an educational vision of being "diverse, open, optimistic, and enterprising." The school's name, "Cuiping" (翠屏, meaning "emerald screen"), draws on the nearby Cuiping Road and the area's mountain scenery.

翠屏國民中小學籌備始於民國86年（1997年）3月，民國88年（1999年）8月1日正式招生，為國中部與國小部合一的九年一貫實驗學校。校地約28,614平方公尺，現有約54班、學生約1,399人，教育願景強調「多元、開放、樂觀、進取」。校名「翠屏」取自鄰近的翠屏路及當地山勢景觀意象。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 One Campus, Grades K–9

K-9一貫學制

Cuiping is a nine-year integrated experimental school where elementary and junior high students share a single campus and administration — a structure still uncommon among Nanzih's schools.

🌄 Named for the Local Landscape

校名取自山勢景觀

The name "Cuiping" (翠屏, "emerald screen") reflects the area's mountain scenery and the nearby Cuiping Road.

📈 Built for a Growing Community

因應人口移入設校

With around 1,399 students across 54 classes, the school was established in the late 1990s as Nanzih's population grew, part of a wave of new school construction in the district.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A newer school for a newer Nanzih.

🏔️

Cuiping is one of several schools established in Nanzih during the district's 1990s population growth, and its nine-year integrated model — combining elementary and junior high on one campus — reflects experimentation in Taiwanese school design during that era.
