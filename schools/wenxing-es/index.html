About · 學校簡介

Zhubei's newest school, still under construction.

Wenxing Elementary opened for first-grade enrollment in the 2025 academic year, making it the newest of Zhubei's elementary schools. It is part of a roughly NT$1.48 billion campus project in the city's High Speed Rail district, built to relieve severe shortages of school seats in the area. The school opened with 8 first-grade classes, and its full campus of 48 classrooms is targeted for completion around mid-2026. It is designed as part of a future integrated K-12 "Wenxing Smart Learning Park."

文興國小於2025學年度開始招收一年級新生，是竹北最新成立的國小。學校是竹北高鐵特區一項耗資約新台幣14.8億元校園計畫的一部分，目的是紓解當地學校學位嚴重不足的問題。學校開學時先設有8個一年級班級，全校48間教室的完整校舍預計於2026年年中前完工，未來將發展為涵蓋國小到高中的「文興智慧學習園區」。

🏗️ Opened Mid-Construction

邊興建邊開學

Wenxing welcomed its first 8 first-grade classes in 2025 even as construction continued, with the full 48-classroom campus expected around mid-2026.

文興智慧學習園區

The school is designed as the first piece of a future "Wenxing Smart Learning Park," an integrated campus cluster meant to span from elementary through senior high.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The newest chapter in a boomtown's growth.

🚄

As the newest of Zhubei's elementary schools, Wenxing was built as part of a planned "cradle-to-college" campus cluster to absorb the ongoing surge of families moving to this tech-commuter boomtown near Hsinchu Science Park and the High Speed Rail station.
