About · 學校簡介

Unity builds a fortress.

Chengcheng Elementary School was founded in 1963. Its campus is built on a hillside with sweeping views overlooking the Zhuoshui River and the Yushan mountain range. The school's name and motto center on the phrase "眾志成城" — that unity of purpose builds a fortress — a fitting emblem for a small mountain-country school.

成城國小創校於1963年（民國52年）。校園建於山丘之上，可俯瞰濁水溪與玉山山脈的壯闊景色。學校校名與校訓皆圍繞「眾志成城」的精神，象徵著這所山區小學團結一心的信念。
