Ludong Elementary School

鹿東國民小學

A young public elementary school in the historic heritage town of Lugang, built to cultivate a new generation of readers rooted in old-town culture.

座落於百年歷史古鎮鹿港的年輕公立小學，致力於培養扎根古鎮文化的新一代讀書人。

About · 學校簡介

A new school in an old town.

Ludong Elementary began preparations in 1995 and officially opened its doors on August 1, 1998, under founding principal Liang Jinli. The school drew its first students from three existing schools in the area, starting with grades 1 through 5 across 21 classes. An attached kindergarten was added in 2002, and a second campus was completed in 2022 — today the school runs 58 classes across its two campuses, serving the historic town of Lugang.

鹿東國小自民國84年（1995年）開始籌備，於民國87年（1998年）8月1日正式開學，首任校長為梁進立。創校之初，學生來自鄰近三所學校，初期設有一至五年級共21班。民國91年（2002年）附設幼兒園成立，第二校區則於2022年完工啟用，目前兩校區合計共58班，服務歷史古鎮鹿港的學子。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Two Campuses, One School

雙校區規劃

What began in 1998 as a single campus drawing students from three neighboring schools has grown into a two-campus school of 58 classes, with the second campus completed in 2022.

🧒 Growing With Its Youngest Learners

附設幼兒園

An attached kindergarten, established in 2002, means Ludong has welcomed local children even before their first day of elementary school for over two decades.

📖 Building a Reading Culture

閱讀教育扎根

The school places a strong emphasis on reading education, aiming to cultivate what it calls a "new Lugang cultural generation" — young readers grounded in their hometown's heritage.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Lugang, a living heritage town.

🏮

Ludong sits in Lugang Township (鹿港鎮), a town with more than a century of documented cultural heritage and long known as one of Taiwan's most historic ports. Though Ludong itself is a young school — not yet 30 years old — it draws directly on this deep local heritage, framing reading education as a way to raise a "new Lugang cultural generation."
