About · 學校簡介

Over 120 years of educating Qionglin.

Qionglin Elementary was founded in 1898 during the Japanese colonial era, originally teaching classes in borrowed private houses before the school purchased land and built permanent buildings at its current site in 1909. Over the decades it was renamed multiple times, and once operated an affiliated Agricultural Supplementary School starting in 1926, reflecting the township's farming-based economy. The school celebrated its 120th anniversary in recognition of this long history.

芎林國小創立於明治31年（1898年），創校初期借用民宅上課，明治42年（1909年）購地興建永久校舍於現址。歷經數次更名，昭和元年（1926年）曾附設農業補習學校，反映當地以農業為主的經濟型態。學校曾舉辦創校120週年慶祝活動，紀念這段悠久的歷史。
