About · 學校簡介

A small school with a green-education reputation.

Toushe Elementary is a small mountain school with six classes, serving the children of Toushe Village in Yuchi Township. Despite its modest size, the school has built a strong identity around environmental and outdoor education, becoming an active participant in the Ministry of Education's Green School Partners Network and earning a 15-leaf environmental education badge — a notable achievement recognizing sustained work in green curriculum. The school also runs farm-experience and tree-appreciation programs that connect students directly to the natural landscape around them.

頭社國小是一所僅有6個班級的山區小學，服務魚池鄉頭社村的學童。儘管規模不大，學校在環境與戶外教育方面建立了鮮明特色，積極參與教育部「夥伴學校網路」，並獲頒十五葉環境教育認證——這是對學校長期投入綠色課程的肯定。學校也開設農場體驗與賞樹活動，讓學生與周遭的自然環境緊密連結。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Beside a rare floating wetland.

🪷

Toushe Village is home to the famous Toushe Wetland (頭社活盆地), a floating peat bog near Sun Moon Lake tied to the historical Thao and plains-indigenous settlement of the basin — one of Taiwan's most distinctive natural landmarks. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
