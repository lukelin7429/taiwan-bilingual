About · 學校簡介

One of North District's newest schools.

Wenyuan Elementary School was founded on August 1, 1999, making it one of the newest elementary schools in North District. Detailed programmatic history beyond its founding date and location has not been confirmed from available sources. The school sits on Hai'an Road, in an area historically tied to Tainan's old canal- and harbor-side road network leading toward the coast.

文元國小於1999年（民國88年）8月1日創立，是北區較新設立的國小之一。除創校日期與校址外，目前尚無法從現有資料確認更詳細的校務發展歷程。學校位於海安路，這一帶與臺南通往海濱的舊運河、港區道路網絡有著歷史淵源。
