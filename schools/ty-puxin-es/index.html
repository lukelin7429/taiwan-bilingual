About · 學校簡介

From an agricultural branch school to the airport district.

Puxin Elementary was founded in 1941, during the Japanese colonial era, as a branch of Dayuan National School (大園國民學校埔心分教場), becoming independent two years later in 1943 under the name "大原國民學校." It was renamed Puxin National School in 1946, then took its current name of Puxin Elementary School in 1968. Like many Dayuan schools, its Japanese-era founding as an agricultural-village branch school reflects the district's long history as farmland later reshaped by the arrival of Taoyuan International Airport. The school's current branding slogan is "創新永續" (Innovation, Sustainability).

埔心國小創立於1941年（昭和16年）日治時期，最初為大園國民學校埔心分教場，兩年後（1943年，昭和18年）獨立設校，初名「大原國民學校」，1946年更名為埔心國民學校，1968年定名為今日的埔心國小。與大園區許多學校相似，其日治時期以農村分校起家的歷史，反映了這片後來因桃園國際機場興建而改變樣貌的農地。學校現今的品牌口號為「創新永續」。
