About · 學校簡介

A junior high on the shore of Sun Moon Lake.

Mingtan Junior High School was founded in 1971 and is located in Shuishe Village, the main tourist hub on Sun Moon Lake's shore. Students here grow up immersed in the lake's tourism economy and in close proximity to the indigenous Thao community at nearby Ita Thau. Despite declining enrollment pressures common to small rural schools, local county officials have publicly resisted proposals to merge or close the school, underscoring its importance to the community.

明潭國中創校於1971年（民國60年），校址位於日月潭畔的水社村，是湖區主要的觀光聚落。學生在湖泊觀光經濟與鄰近伊達邵邵族部落的文化氛圍中成長。儘管面臨鄉間小校常見的招生壓力，地方縣府官員仍公開表態反對合併或裁併學校，凸顯這所學校對社區的重要性。
