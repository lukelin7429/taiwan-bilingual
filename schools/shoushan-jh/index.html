About · 學校簡介

A school on Kaohsiung's landmark mountain.

Shoushan Junior High School is located on Shoushan Mountain, near the Ai (Love) River, in Kaohsiung's Gushan District. Shoushan — meaning "Longevity Mountain" — is the limestone hill and national nature park that dominates the district's geography, and the school takes its name directly from this landmark setting.

壽山國中座落於高雄市鼓山區的壽山之上，鄰近愛河。「壽山」意為「長壽之山」，是主導整個鼓山區地貌的石灰岩山丘與國家自然公園，學校也因此地標而得名。
