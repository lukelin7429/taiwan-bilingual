Xingde Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A small school with a big history.

Xingde Elementary School was established in 1954 as a branch of Jingmei Elementary School, known as "Xingde Branch" (興德分校), and became independent as "Xingde National School" in 1962. It is the oldest school in Wenshan's historic "Shiwufen Village" (十五分庄) area.

Xingde is one of Taipei's smallest elementary schools by both campus size — about 3,500 square meters, with no full field, only a roughly 1,000-square-meter court — and enrollment, at about 270 students as of 2019, down from a peak of about 36 classes. A nearby sister school, Xinglong Elementary, was created in 1975 specifically to relieve its overcrowding.

興德國小於1954年（民國43年）創立為景美國小分校，稱「興德分校」，1962年（民國51年）獨立設校。是文山區歷史悠久的「十五分庄」地區最早設立的學校。

興德國小是全臺北市規模最小的國小之一，校地僅約3,500平方公尺，沒有完整操場，僅有約1,000平方公尺的球場；學生人數截至2019年約270人，較高峰期36班已大幅減少。鄰近的興隆國小即於1975年為紓解興德國小過度擁擠而創立。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Compact Historic Campus

小而歷史悠久

With a campus of only about 3,500 square meters and no full field, Xingde is among Taipei's smallest public elementary schools.

🌱 Rooted in Shiwufen Village

十五分庄最早設校

Xingde is the oldest school in Wenshan's historic Shiwufen Village area, tracing back to its 1954 founding as a Jingmei Elementary branch.

👯 A Sister School Born of Overcrowding

分校紓解過度擁擠

Neighboring Xinglong Elementary was created in 1975 specifically to relieve Xingde's overcrowding, once its enrollment had grown to around 36 classes.

Local Culture · 在地文化

In the historic Shiwufen area.

🏘️

Xingde's small, tightly hemmed-in campus is a direct product of Wenshan's dense, older residential fabric in the historic Shiwufen area, one of the district's earliest-settled neighborhoods.
