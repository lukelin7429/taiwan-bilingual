About · 學校簡介

Founded by factories, named for a folk tradition.

Shijia Elementary School was founded on September 23, 1948, under its first principal, Xu Ziqiang, established through a cooperative association formed by a local aluminum plant, steel plant, and fertilizer plant together with the city government — an unusual industrial-civic partnership for a public elementary school. Enrollment surged after a military ordnance factory relocated to the area in 1950, and the school spun off Aiqun Elementary School in 1957 to relieve the resulting overcrowding. Its name honors "戲獅甲" (Xi-shi-jia), the historic hamlet where Qing-dynasty villagers performed lion-dance and martial-arts drills to defend against pirates — a heritage it shares with neighboring Shijia Junior High.

獅甲國小創立於民國37年（1948年）9月23日，首任校長為許自強，由當地鋁廠、鋼鐵廠、肥料廠與市政府共同合作組成的協會設立——對公立國小而言相當罕見的產業與公部門合作模式。民國39年（1950年）一座軍工兵工廠遷入附近後，學生人數大增，學校於民國46年（1957年）分出愛群國小以紓解壓力。校名承襲「戲獅甲」——清代居民藉舞獅武術操演抵禦海盜的歷史聚落名稱，與鄰近的獅甲國中共享同一份文化淵源。

Local Culture · 在地文化

"戲獅甲" — a shared cultural heritage.

Shijia Elementary shares its name and its pirate-defense heritage — "戲獅甲" (Xi-shi-jia), the Qing-dynasty lion-dance and martial-arts tradition — with neighboring Shijia Junior High, two schools bound together by the same historic hamlet. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
