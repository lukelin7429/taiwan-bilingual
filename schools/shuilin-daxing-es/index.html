About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school for a farming village.

Daxing Elementary is a small public elementary school serving Dagou Village in Shuilin Township. Like many of the township's rural campuses, it plays a central role as a neighborhood gathering point for a farming community whose livelihood centers on sweet potato cultivation. Detailed institutional records beyond basic contact information were not available for this profile — if you are affiliated with the school, we welcome corrections and additions.

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏘️ A Village Anchor

大溝村的教育據點

As Dagou Village's local elementary school, Daxing serves as an educational anchor for one of Shuilin Township's smaller farming communities.

🍠 Sweet Potato Roots

地瓜產業家庭

Most of the school's families are engaged in Shuilin's signature sweet potato cultivation, tying daily school life closely to the local agricultural calendar.

🌾 A Small Rural Campus

典型農村小校

Like several of its neighboring schools in Shuilin Township, Daxing operates as a small rural campus closely woven into the fabric of village life.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Rooted in Dagou Village's fields.

🍠

Serving Dagou Village in Shuilin Township, the school's student body comes largely from families engaged in the township's signature sweet potato (地瓜) cultivation — the same crop that has built the "Shuilin sweet potato" brand recognized across Taiwan.
