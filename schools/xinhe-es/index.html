Xinhe Elementary School

新和國民小學

Founded in 1959 for military-dependents'-village families, this Wanhua elementary school once held Taiwan's highest single-school enrollment before a full 2017–2023 rebuild.

創校於1959年，初期主要招收眷村子弟，全盛時期學生人數居全台之冠，並於2017至2023年間完成校舍全面重建。

About · 學校簡介

From a military-village school to a rebuilt campus.

Xinhe Elementary School opened in September 1959 with its first cohort of 443 first-graders across seven classes, most of them children from military-dependents'-village (眷村) families in the area. With its own buildings still under construction, those early classes were borrowed from nearby Yingqiao and Dongyuan elementary schools. Enrollment grew rapidly, peaking at 3,865 students across 68 classes in 1971 — the highest in the school's history. The campus was rebuilt from 2017 to 2023, receiving its occupancy permit in September 2023, and today serves a much smaller but modern student body on Xizang Road.

新和國小創立於民國48年（1959年）9月，首屆招收443名一年級新生，共7班，多為附近眷村子弟。由於自有校舍尚未完工，初期課程借用鄰近迎橋國小與東園國小教室上課。此後學生人數快速成長，於1971年達到全校68班、3,865名學生的歷史高峰。校舍於2017年至2023年間進行全面重建，2023年9月取得使用執照，如今座落於西藏路上，以現代化校園服務規模較小但穩定的學生群體。

The school's first cohort in 1959 was made up mostly of children from nearby military-dependents' villages, reflecting a distinct chapter of postwar Taipei's social history.

Local Culture · 在地文化

On the old drainage line into old Bangka.

🌊

Xinhe Elementary sits on Xizang Road near where the old "No. 3 Special Drainage Ditch" (特三號排水溝) once ran, part of Wanhua's flood-prone riverside geography. That same low-lying terrain shaped the growth of Bangka (艋舺), also known as Monga, whose historic core — anchored by Longshan Temple (龍山寺) and the traditional shops of Herb Lane (青草巷) — sits just to the north, tying this newer campus back to the district's much older riverside story.
