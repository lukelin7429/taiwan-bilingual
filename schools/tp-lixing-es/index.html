Lixing Elementary School

力行國民小學

A busy Muzha-area elementary school known for an unusually wide range of clubs, from taiko drumming to string orchestra.

About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school with a rich club life.

Lixing Elementary School serves the Jingmei/Muzha transition zone of Wenshan District, a densely residential stretch of the district along Muxin Road. The school currently has about 44 classes and roughly 1,162 students.

Lixing offers an unusually wide range of extracurricular clubs, including table tennis, diabolo, STEAM science, taiko drumming, ukulele, and a full string orchestra — giving students access to both athletic and artistic programs well beyond the typical elementary school offering.

力行國小位於文山區景美與木柵交界的密集住宅區，鄰近木新路。學校目前約有44班，學生人數約1,162人。

力行國小提供種類豐富的社團活動，包括桌球、扯鈴、STEAM科學、太鼓、烏克麗麗以及完整的弦樂團，讓學生在體育與藝術領域都有多元發展的機會。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥁 An Unusually Rich Club Program

豐富多元的社團

From table tennis and diabolo to STEAM science, taiko drumming, ukulele, and a full string orchestra — Lixing's club offerings go well beyond the elementary-school norm.

🎻 A Full String Orchestra

弦樂團

The school maintains a complete student string orchestra, a relatively rare offering among Wenshan's public elementary schools.

🏫 A Sizeable Neighborhood School

中大型社區學校

With about 44 classes and over 1,100 students, Lixing is one of the larger elementary schools serving Wenshan's Jingmei/Muzha transition zone.

Located on Muxin Road in the Jingmei/Muzha transition zone, Lixing Elementary sits within one of Wenshan's most densely residential stretches, reflecting the district's growth from farmland into a compact urban neighborhood.
