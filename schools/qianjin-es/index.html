Qianjin Elementary School

前進國民小學

A Pingtung City public elementary school founded in 1949 on former sugar-company farmland at the eastern gateway to the Pingtung Plain.

創校於民國38年（1949年）的屏東市公立國小，座落於昔日台糖農地上，位處屏東平原東側門戶地帶。

About · 學校簡介

From sugar-company farmland to a neighborhood school.

Qianjin Elementary traces its roots to around 1947, when it began as a branch of neighboring Datong Elementary School, before becoming an independent school in 1949. Forty years later, in 1989, the school relocated from its original site — by then a busy intersection on Jianguo Road that was being widened into an eight-lane thoroughfare — to a new campus built on former Taiwan Sugar Corporation farmland, where it stands today near the Gaoping River at the eastern edge of the Pingtung Plain.

前進國小的歷史可追溯至民國36年前後，最初為大同國小的分教場，於民國38年（1949年）正式獨立設校。四十年後的民國78年（1989年），因原址位於建國路口，該路段拓寬為八線道，學校遂遷至現址——一塊昔日台糖公司的農地，緊鄰高屏溪，位於屏東平原東側的門戶地帶。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

☯️ "Ceaseless Self-Strengthening"

天行健，君子以自強不息

The school's emblem draws on the classical I Ching phrase "as the heavens move with vigor, the noble person ceaselessly strengthens himself" — a motif notable enough to be featured in a national newspaper's series on school emblems.

🚜 Built on Former Sugar-Company Land

昔日台糖農地重建校園

The current campus was constructed in 1989 on farmland once owned by the Taiwan Sugar Corporation, part of the broader redevelopment of company land on Pingtung City's eastern edge.

🌊 By the Gaoping River

緊鄰高屏溪

The school sits near the Gaoping River, the wide waterway that marks the eastern approach into the Pingtung Plain from the highlands beyond.

🏫 A Branch That Grew Independent

分教場獨立設校

Like several other Pingtung City schools, Qianjin began life as a branch campus of a larger neighborhood school before growing into its own independent institution.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Sugar fields into schoolyards.

🌾

Qianjin Elementary's move onto former Taiwan Sugar Corporation (台糖) farmland in 1989 reflects Pingtung City's broader transition from a sugar-agriculture hinterland into an urbanized school district, as land once used for cane cultivation was repurposed for the city's expanding neighborhoods near the Gaoping River.
