About · 學校簡介

A name built from two districts.

Taipei Municipal Nanhu Senior High School was established on August 1, 2002, with its campus completed that June and the first students enrolling that September. Its name is literally constructed from "Nangang" (南港) and "Neihu" (內湖) — the city built the school specifically to balance high-school access between the two adjoining districts, which had previously shared a more limited set of nearby options. In 2006, the school established an athletics special class, adding to its academic programming.

南湖高中創立於民國91年（2002年）8月1日，校舍於同年6月完工，9月首屆學生入學。校名取自「南港」與「內湖」二字，是臺北市為平衡南港、內湖兩區高中就學機會而興建。民國95年（2006年），學校成立體育班，擴充學校的多元學程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🔤 A Name Built From Two Districts

校名合併南港內湖

Nanhu High's name literally splices "Nangang" and "Neihu" together, reflecting the city's intent to serve both adjoining districts with one new high school.

🏗️ A Rapidly Delivered Campus

建校即開學

The campus was completed in June 2002 and welcomed its first enrolling class just three months later, in September of the same founding year.

🏃 An Established Athletics Program

體育班

The school added an athletics special class in 2006, broadening its offerings beyond its founding academic programs.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A product of Neihu's administrative geography.

🗺️

Nanhu High sits near the boundary between Nangang and Neihu Districts, and its very name is a direct product of that administrative geography — a school deliberately positioned and named to serve residents on both sides of the district line. See the Taipei county page for more on the region.
