Xilin Elementary School

西林國民小學

A tiny Truku village school where archery tradition and language competitions share the same small campus.

About · 學校簡介

A small campus with a strong bow arm.

Xilin Elementary is one of the smallest schools in Wanrong Township, with just six classes and around 65 students. Despite its size, the school has built a distinctive identity around traditional archery, maintaining an active archery team alongside its academic programs. It also takes part regularly in Indigenous-language word competitions and English-language contests, giving students in this small Truku village exposure well beyond the classroom.

西林國小是萬榮鄉規模最小的學校之一，全校僅6班、約65名學生。儘管規模不大，學校仍發展出以「傳統射箭」為特色的活動，長期維持一支活躍的射箭隊，並積極參與原住民族語單字競賽與英語競賽，讓這個太魯閣族小部落的孩子有機會接觸更寬廣的舞台。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏹 A Traditional Archery Team

傳統射箭隊

Xilin maintains a traditional archery team, connecting students to a skill long practiced by Truku hunters and passed down through generations.

🗣️ Truku Language in Daily Life

族語單字競賽

Students take part in Indigenous-language word competitions, reinforcing everyday use of Truku alongside Mandarin and English.

🌍 Small School, Big World

積極參與英語競賽

Despite its small enrollment of roughly 65 students, Xilin actively enters English-language competitions, balancing cultural roots with outward-looking ambition.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A Truku village in the foothills.

🎯

Xilin Village lies within mountainous Wanrong Township 萬榮鄉, a Truku Indigenous community along Hualien's Central Mountain Range. The school's archery team reflects a hunting tradition still valued across Wanrong's Truku villages, even as daily life has changed with the times.
