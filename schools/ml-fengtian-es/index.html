About · 學校簡介

A "mini but refined" school in suburban Zhuolan.

Fengtian Elementary was founded in 1956 and serves the suburban Fengtian Village community on the edge of Zhuolan Township. True to its small size, the school is often described as "mini but refined" — offering six general classes plus one kindergarten class, with about 89 students in total. This small scale allows for a close, personal learning environment for its students.

豐田國小創立於民國45年（1956年），服務位於卓蘭鎮郊的豐田里社區。學校規模雖小，卻常被形容為「小而美」——設有六個普通班加上一個幼兒園班，全校學生約89人。這樣的小校規模，讓學生得以享有更緊密、個人化的學習環境。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Zhuolan's mountain-farming villages.

🍐

Fengtian's small-village setting in Zhuolan reflects the township's characteristic blend of Hakka and Hoklo mountain-farming communities that have long grown high-elevation fruit on the Miaoli-Taichung border. See the Miaoli county page for more on the region.
