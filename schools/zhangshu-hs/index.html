Zhangshu International Experimental Technical Senior High School

樟樹國際實創高中

New Taipei's first experimental technical high school, blending vocational training with hands-on innovation beside the Keelung River.

About · 學校簡介

An experimental technical school on the riverbank.

Zhangshu International Experimental Technical Senior High School opened in 1998 on land set aside during Xizhi's 1990s urban expansion, on the banks of the Keelung River. In 2017 it became New Taipei's first school designated an "experimental technical senior high school," combining vocational tracks — fashion and apparel design, multimedia animation, and information technology — with an experimental-education framework that gives students more say in shaping their own learning path. Its circular, red-brick "Colosseum-style" plaza has become a recognizable campus landmark.

樟樹國際實創高中創立於民國87年（1998年），校地源自汐止區1990年代都市擴張時期所保留的用地，坐落於基隆河畔。民國106年（2017年）該校成為新北市第一所獲認證的「實驗性技術型高中」，結合服飾流行設計、多媒體動畫、資訊科技等職業類科，並融入實驗教育精神，讓學生擁有更多元的學習路徑。校內圓形紅磚「競技場式」廣場已成為校園代表性地標。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ A Colosseum-Style Campus

競技場式紅磚廣場

The school's circular red-brick plaza, reminiscent of a Roman colosseum, has become one of Xizhi's most distinctive pieces of campus architecture.

🧵 Fashion, Media, and Tech Tracks

服飾、多媒體與資訊類科

Students choose among vocational tracks in fashion and apparel design, multimedia animation, and information technology, each paired with industry-style studio space.

🧪 New Taipei's First Experimental Technical High

新北市首創實創技術型高中

Since 2017, the school has held the distinction of being New Taipei's first officially designated experimental technical senior high school.

🌊 A Riverside Campus

基隆河畔的校園

Located along the banks of the Keelung River, the campus reflects Xizhi's 1990s expansion into new urban land along the waterway.
