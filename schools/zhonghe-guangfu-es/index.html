About · 學校簡介

A name that marks 1945, a school built for scale.

Guangfu Elementary School is one of Zhonghe's larger elementary schools, with 63 regular classes and roughly 1,652 students, plus additional special-education classes. Like many schools carrying the name "Guangfu" (光復, meaning "Retrocession"), it commemorates Taiwan's return from Japanese rule after 1945 — though the school's own precise founding date is not confirmed in available records. The school maintains an active program of international exchange.

光復國小是中和地區規模較大的國小之一，設有63個普通班、約1,652名學生，另設特殊教育班。如同許多以「光復」為名的學校，校名紀念台灣自1945年脫離日本統治、重返中華民國治理的歷史——不過目前可查證的資料中並未確認學校確切創校年份。學校積極推動國際交流活動。
