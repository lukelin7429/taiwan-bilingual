About · 學校簡介

A first of its kind, still standing.

Lunshan Elementary holds a distinctive place in Hualien County's educational history: founded in 1917 under Japanese rule as the "Lunbushan Indigenous Children's Education Institute" (崙布山蕃童教育所), it was the very first elementary school established within a Bunun settlement anywhere in the county. More than a century later, the school still serves Lunshan Village, and today occupies the largest school campus and land area of any school in Zhuoxi Township — grounds rich with natural ecological resources that surround the classrooms with forest and mountain landscape.

崙山國小在花蓮縣的教育史上佔有特殊地位：創立於大正6年（1917年）日治時期，最初名為「崙布山蕃童教育所」，是全花蓮縣第一所設立於布農族部落內的小學。逾百年後的今天，學校仍持續服務崙山村，並擁有卓溪鄉全鄉最大的校地面積——豐富的自然生態資源環繞著校園，讓教室被森林與山巒景色包圍。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥇 The First of Its Kind

花蓮縣布農部落首校

Lunshan was the first elementary school ever established within a Bunun settlement in Hualien County, founded in 1917 — a genuine milestone in the region's educational history.

🗺️ The Largest Campus in Zhuoxi

全鄉最大校地

The school holds the largest school land area of any campus in Zhuoxi Township, giving students unusually spacious grounds for a remote mountain school.

🌳 Rich Natural Surroundings

豐富生態資源

🏛️ Over a Century of Continuous History

逾百年校史

Tracing its roots back to a 1917 Japanese-era education institute, Lunshan carries more than a hundred years of continuous educational presence in its village.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Zhuoxi Township, a Bunun mountain home.

⛰️

Zhuoxi Township is a remote, mountainous district of Hualien County near Yushan National Park, home to a majority Bunun Indigenous population. Its eight elementary schools — and no junior high — reflect villages small enough that most students continue their education elsewhere after sixth grade.
