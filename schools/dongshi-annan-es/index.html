About · 學校簡介

A village elementary school in garlic country.

Annan Elementary is located in Annan Village, Dongshi Township, serving the local farming community. Detailed institutional records for this small rural school are limited beyond its address and contact details, but like its neighboring schools in Dongshi, it forms part of the township's network of village elementary schools supporting one of Taiwan's most productive agricultural areas.

安南國小座落於東勢鄉安南村，服務當地的農業社區。這所小型鄉村學校目前公開的機構資料相對有限，僅有地址與聯絡方式，但如同東勢鄉其他鄰近學校一般，它是這個台灣重要農業產區村里小學網絡的一部分。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Dongshi, Taiwan's garlic and carrot heartland.

🧄

Located in Annan Village amid Dongshi's farmland, the school sits in a township renowned as one of Taiwan's top garlic- and carrot-producing areas — with nearly nine in ten of Taiwan's garlic bulbs grown in Yunlin. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
