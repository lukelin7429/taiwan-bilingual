About · 學校簡介

Over a century under many names.

Fuyuan Elementary was established in 1914 under Japanese rule as "Bazai Public School," and has carried several different names through both the colonial period and the postwar decades since. Located in Ruisui Township, it shares its "Fuyuan" name with the separate Fuyuan Junior High School in neighboring Guangfu Township — the two are distinct institutions in different townships, though both draw on the broader Fuyuan community that straddles the border between them.

富源國小創立於日治時期大正3年（1914年），原名「馬佐公學校」，此後歷經日治時期與戰後多次更名，位於瑞穗鄉。學校名稱「富源」與鄰近光復鄉的富源國中相同，但兩者是位於不同鄉鎮的獨立學校——兩校皆服務橫跨兩鄉邊界的「富源」地區社區。

Fuyuan Elementary shares its name with Fuyuan Junior High in neighboring Guangfu Township — a naming overlap that reflects how the Fuyuan community itself spans the two townships' shared border.

🌾 A Quiet Anchor in Ruisui

瑞穗鄉的教育據點

As one of Ruisui Township's elementary schools, Fuyuan has served generations of local families in this hot-spring and river-rafting corner of Hualien County.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Ruisui's rural heartland.

🌿

Fuyuan Elementary sits within Ruisui Township, the area famous throughout Taiwan for its hot springs, papaya milk, and Xiuguluan River rafting — one of several elementary schools serving this scenic east-Hualien community. See the Fuyuan Junior High page for the related but distinct school in neighboring Guangfu Township.
