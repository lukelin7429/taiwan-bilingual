About · 學校簡介

A century since Douliu Girls' Public School.

Zhendong Elementary was established on April 1, 1923, during the Japanese colonial era, as "Douliu Girls' Public School" — splitting off from Douliu Public School as a dedicated institution for girls. The school began coeducation in 1944, opening its doors to boys as well. It celebrated its 100th anniversary in April 2023, marking a full century as one of Douliu's founding educational institutions.

鎮東國小創立於1923年4月1日，日治時期名為「斗六女子公學校」，自斗六公學校分出、專收女學生。民國33年（1944年）起開始男女合校。學校於2023年4月歡慶建校百週年，見證了作為斗六最早創立學校之一的完整百年歷程。
