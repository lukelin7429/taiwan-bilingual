About · 學校簡介

A centennial school on the Dadu Plateau.

Longfeng Elementary School began as a branch school in 1920 under Japanese colonial rule, becoming independent in 1942 as "Longgang National School." Sitting atop Dadu Mountain, the school celebrated its centennial in 2021 and carries one of several "Long-" (dragon) names shared among schools across the Dadu Plateau, each reflecting the district's collective "Longjing" toponymic identity.

龍峰國小創校於1920年（日治時期），最初為分校，於民國31年（1942年）以「龍崗國民學校」之名獨立設校。學校座落於大肚山頂，於2021年慶祝建校百年，校名承襲大肚台地上多所「龍」字輩學校之一，共同體現龍井區集體的地名認同。

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎉 A Centennial Milestone

建校百年紀念

Founded in 1920, Longfeng marked its 100th anniversary in 2021 — one of the longer-running schools among Longjing's upland communities.

⛰️ Atop Dadu Mountain

座落大肚山頂

The school's hilltop campus places it among Longjing District's cluster of upland schools, distinct from the coastal and river-mouth communities below.

🐉 One of the "Dragon" Schools

「龍」字輩學校之一

Its name is one of several schools across the district sharing a "Long" (dragon) character, echoing Longjing's own name and shared local identity.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Shared "dragon" names across Longjing.

Longfeng is one of several schools across Longjing District carrying a "Long" (dragon) character in its name, part of a shared toponymic identity rooted in the district's own "Longjing" name. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
