About · 學校簡介

From branch classroom to independent public school.

Jiuzhuang Elementary School traces its roots to 1922, when it began as a branch classroom of Dapi Public School. It became an independent public school in 1940 as "Jiuzhuang Public School," was renamed to its current title in 1968, and in 1976 a branch of the school separated to become Lianmei Elementary School. As one of Dapi Township's oldest schools, it is rooted in the farming communities whose mustard-green harvests sustain the township's famous suan cai pickling trade.

舊庄國小的歷史可追溯至民國11年（1922年），最初是大埤公學校的分教場。民國29年（1940年）獨立設校，定名為「舊庄公學校」，民國57年（1968年）更改為現名，民國65年（1976年）分校獨立成為聯美國小。作為大埤鄉歷史最悠久的學校之一，其根基深植於支撐大埤知名酸菜醃製業的芥菜種植農家社區。
