About · 學校簡介

The founding school of Meishan Township.

Meishan Elementary opened in 1906, during the Japanese colonial era, as "Meizaikeng Public School" — Meishan Township's very first school, established through the advocacy of local leader Wu Yan and others. It was later renamed "Xiaomei Public School" and then "Xiaomei National School." After Taiwan's 1945 retrocession, it became "Xiaomei Township's First National School" in 1946, then "Meishan Township Meishan National School" in 1947 when the township itself was renamed from Xiaomei to Meishan, and finally "Meishan National Elementary School" in 1968 under the nine-year compulsory education reform. Now over 100 years old, it is the oldest school in Meishan Township, and as of the most recent school year enrolls 202 students (106 male, 96 female) across 12 classes.

梅山國小創立於明治39年（1906年）日治時期，原名「梅仔坑公學校」，是地方仕紳吳炎等人倡議下設立的梅山鄉第一所學校。其後更名「小梅公學校」，再改為「小梅國民學校」；1945年台灣光復後，1946年改稱「小梅鄉第一國民學校」，1947年因鄉名由小梅改為梅山而更名「梅山鄉梅山國民學校」，1968年因應九年國教改制為「梅山國民小學」，沿用至今。學校已逾百年歷史，是梅山鄉歷史最悠久的學校，現有12班、202名學生（男106、女96）。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🧭 Self-Leadership Education

自我領導力教育

The school promotes "Self-Leadership Education" (自我領導力教育), based on the widely used 7 Habits framework popular in Taiwanese schools.

🎬 An Award-Winning Dental-Health Film

牙齒保健微電影獲獎

Students won an award for producing a dental-health micro-film, one example of the school's project-based approach to health education.

🏫 An Affiliated Kindergarten & Central Kitchen

附設幼兒園與中央廚房

The school runs an affiliated kindergarten and central kitchen, supporting both early education and healthy meals for the wider student body.

🏔️ The Mother School of the Township

梅山鄉的母校

As the founding school of the entire township, Meishan Elementary later gave rise to schools like Meibei and Meizhen, which split off as the township grew.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The name behind the tea, bamboo, and plum industries.

🌸

As the founding school of the entire township — from which Meibei, Meizhen, and other schools later split off — Meishan Elementary's century-plus history parallels the development of the township's tea, bamboo, and plum (梅) industries that gave the area its name.
