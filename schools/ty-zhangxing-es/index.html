Changxing Elementary School

About · 學校簡介

A century-old school reborn for a shrinking mountain community.

Changxing Elementary was founded in 1920 by the Japanese colonial government as a “番童教育所” (aboriginal children's education station). It was renamed 長興國民學校 after Taiwan's 1945 retrocession, became 桃園縣立長興國民小學 in 1968 under the nine-year compulsory education reform, and took its current municipal name in 2014. The school began formally converting into a Taoyuan “school-type Indigenous experimental education” institution starting in the second semester of the 2023-24 academic year, completing the transition in 2024-25 — one of Taoyuan's first three such conversions, alongside Luofu and Guanghua. It is now extremely small — about 6 classes, 33 students, and 28 staff — and every student at the school is Atayal. It sits along the Roma Highway in a spur of the Xueshan mountain range, overlooking the upper Dahan River basin.

長興國小創立於大正9年（1920年），為日本殖民政府設立之「番童教育所」。1945年台灣光復後更名「長興國民學校」，民國57年（1968年）九年國教實施後改為「桃園縣立長興國民小學」，民國103年（2014年）桃園升格後定名現今校名。學校自112學年度下學期（2023至2024學年）起正式轉型為桃園市「原住民族學校型態實驗教育」機構，於113學年度（2024至2025年）完成轉型，是桃園首批三所轉型學校之一（與羅浮、光華同批）。目前規模極小——約6班、33名學生、28名教職員，全校學生皆為泰雅族。學校座落羅馬公路沿線、雪山山脈支稜，俯瞰大漢溪上游流域。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A direct response to mountain depopulation.

🏔️

Like Guanghua and Luofu, Changxing's recent shift to Indigenous-experimental status is a direct response to Fuxing's declining birthrate and rural depopulation — with a 100% Atayal, roughly 33-student student body, it shows just how small these mountain campuses have become.
