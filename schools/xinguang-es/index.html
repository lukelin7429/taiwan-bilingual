About · 學校簡介

Two hours into the mountains, and still counting.

Xinguang Elementary began as a branch classroom built in 1952, operating under Xiuluan National School from 1953 before becoming fully independent in July 1961 and taking its present name in 1968. It is the most remote elementary school in all of Hsinchu County — some 50 kilometers from the Jianshi Township office, sitting at 1,650 meters elevation, roughly a two-hour drive from Zhudong. Given that remoteness, the school built dormitories for teachers and students in 1996. Its most widely known feature is the Smangus Experimental Branch Class, which the Smangus tribal community itself established in 2003; the branch gained particular significance after Typhoon Aere in 2004 cut off the mountain road to the outside world.

新光國小前身為1952年興建的分校教室，1953年起隸屬秀巒國小分校，1961年7月正式獨立設校，1968年更為現名。它是新竹縣最偏遠的國小——距尖石鄉公所約50公里，海拔1,650公尺，從竹東開車約需兩小時。正因如此偏遠，學校於1996年興建教師與學生宿舍。學校最為人熟知的特色，是由司馬庫斯部落自主於2003年設立的「司馬庫斯實驗分班」；2004年艾利颱風重創聯外山路後，這座分班的重要性更加凸顯。

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛰️ Hsinchu County's Most Remote Elementary School

全新竹縣最偏遠國小

At 1,650 meters elevation and roughly a two-hour drive from Zhudong, Xinguang holds the distinction of being the county's single most remote elementary school.

🌲 The Smangus Experimental Branch

司馬庫斯實驗分班

Established by the Smangus tribal community itself in 2003, this branch class became a lifeline for local children after a 2004 typhoon severed the mountain road.

🏠 Dormitories for an Isolated Community

1996年興建師生宿舍

The school built on-site dormitories for teachers and students in 1996, a direct response to the sheer distance and difficulty of the daily commute.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Gateway to Smangus.

🌲

Xinguang Elementary directly serves the Atayal Indigenous community of Smangus, one of Jianshi Township's most famous and remote mountain villages, known across Taiwan for its ancient cypress groves and for the tribe's self-governed approach to tourism. The school sits near Lala Mountain (Xiuluan), deep in Jianshi's "back mountain" region.
