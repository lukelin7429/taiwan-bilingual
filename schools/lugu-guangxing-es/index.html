Guangxing Elementary School

廣興國民小學

A rural elementary school serving Guangxing Village, set among the tea terraces of Taiwan's most famous Oolong tea-growing township.

座落於廣興村的鄉間國小，四周環繞著台灣最著名烏龍茶鄉的茶園梯田。

About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school for Guangxing families.

Guangxing Elementary School serves Guangxing Village in Lugu Township, Nantou County. To support local preschool families, the school has operated an attached kindergarten since 2002, extending its role as a community hub for the youngest children in the village through to elementary age.

廣興國小服務對象為南投縣鹿谷鄉廣興村。為支持在地學前幼兒家庭，學校自民國91年（2002年）起附設幼兒園，讓學校從幼兒階段便成為村內孩童與家庭的重要據點。

🧸 An Attached Kindergarten Since 2002

附設幼兒園

Guangxing has operated an attached kindergarten since 2002, giving local preschool families a trusted early-learning option right within the village.

🏘️ Anchoring Guangxing Village

村落核心據點

As the school serving Guangxing Village, it functions as a community anchor spanning early childhood through elementary education for local families.

🍵 Growing Up in Tea Country

茶鄉子弟

Students here grow up surrounded by the Dong Ding Oolong tea terraces that shape Guangxing Village's rural economy and daily rhythms.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Lugu, home of Dong Ding Oolong.

🍵

Guangxing Village lies in Lugu Township's tea country, and the school's rural setting is shaped by the surrounding Dong Ding Oolong tea terraces that define the local economy. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
