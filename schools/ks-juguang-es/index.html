About · 學校簡介

A name from an old idiom of resilience.

Juguang Elementary's exact founding date is not precisely confirmed, but the school celebrated its 49th anniversary during the 2025 academic year, placing its founding at around 1977. Its name, "Juguang" (莒光), derives from the idiom "毋忘在莒" ("Never forget Ju"), a story of resilience often invoked during Taiwan's martial-law era — one of many politically inflected school names common to that period. As of the 2022–23 academic year, the school had 42 classes and 1,086 students, with specialized rooms for energy education, social studies, comprehensive activities, English, and an all-weather sports court.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A name born of the martial-law era.

📜

The name "Juguang" (莒光) comes from the ancient idiom "毋忘在莒" ("Never forget [the exile in] Ju"), a story of enduring hardship and eventual restoration that was widely invoked in Taiwanese school and place names during the martial-law era of the 1970s — a naming pattern this school shares with many others founded in the same period.
