Xinglong Experimental Elementary School

An experimental school blending innovative bilingual arts education with a national football title and deep Hakka farming roots.

About · 學校簡介

An experimental school with award-winning range.

Xinglong Elementary was renamed under Miaoli County administration in October 1950 and today operates as an "experimental school" (實驗學校) with an alternative, innovative curriculum model. The school has built a reputation well beyond its size — its team won the 25th Miaoli County Mayor's Cup national 5-a-side football championship, and it runs an active bilingual arts program that has drawn international recognition.

興隆國小於民國39年（1950年）10月苗栗縣政府成立後更名，如今以「實驗學校」模式運作，採用另類創新的課程規劃。學校雖規模不大，卻聲名遠播——校隊曾榮獲第25屆苗栗縣縣長盃全國五人制足球錦標賽冠軍，並推動積極的雙語藝術教育課程，屢獲國際肯定。
