Yangguang Combined Junior High & Elementary School

楊光國民中小學

One of the few combined nine-year "junior high plus elementary" schools in Taoyuan City, and a designated Hakka-language school with a lively club program.

全市少見的「國中小合一」九年一貫學制學校，同時是客語特色學校，社團活動豐富多元。

About · 學校簡介

A newer school built for a growing community.

Yangguang's preparatory office was established in 2002, and the combined nine-year school formally opened in 2005 as Taoyuan County Yangguang Junior High and Elementary School. It took its current name, Taoyuan City Yangguang Combined Junior High and Elementary School, in December 2015 when the county became a special municipality. As one of the city's rare combined "junior high plus elementary" institutions under a single nine-year curriculum, it was built to serve the growing school-age population of Yangmei's expanding residential neighborhoods.

楊光國民中小學於民國91年（2002年）成立桃園縣立楊光國民中小學籌備處；民國94年（2005年）正式成立；民國104年（2015年）12月因桃園縣升格直轄市更名為桃園市立楊光國民中小學。作為全市少見的「國中小合一」九年一貫學制學校，設校較晚，是楊梅較新興的社區型學校，服務因周邊住宅開發而增加的學齡人口。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🔗 A Rare Combined Nine-Year School

全市少見國中小合一學制

Yangguang is one of the few schools in Taoyuan City that runs elementary and junior-high grades together under a single unified nine-year curriculum, rather than as separate institutions.

🗣️ A Designated Hakka-Language School

客語特色學校

The school carries a designated Hakka-language focus, reflecting Yangmei District's identity as one of Taoyuan City's most significant Hakka communities.

🤖 Robotics and Martial Arts Clubs

機器人與武術等多元社團

Students can join LEGO EV3 and fourth-generation SPIKE robotics camps, Shaolin boxing, table tennis, Go, and taekwondo clubs — an unusually broad extracurricular lineup.

🏀 A New Multi-Purpose Activity Center

綜合活動中心

The campus includes a comprehensive activity center with a basketball court, a performance stage, and an ecology teaching garden, supporting both sport and outdoor learning.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Carrying forward Yangmei's Hakka language.

🗣️

As a designated Hakka-language school, Yangguang carries forward the linguistic and cultural transmission mission that has long defined Yangmei District, one of Taoyuan City's most important Hakka settlements. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
