Dapi Junior High School

大埤國民中學

Dapi Township's public junior high, established in 1970 under Taiwan's "one township, one junior high" compulsory education policy.

About · 學校簡介

Born from a national education policy.

Dapi Junior High School was established in 1970 under the 1968 nine-year compulsory education "one township, one junior high" policy, which required every township in Taiwan to have its own junior high school. Before its permanent campus was resolved — a land dispute was settled only in 1988 — the school used a private school building as an interim campus. Today it serves as the sole public junior high for Dapi Township.

大埤國中創立於民國59年（1970年），依循1968年九年國民教育「一鄉鎮一國中」政策設立，規定台灣每個鄉鎮都須設有自己的國中。在永久校地問題解決之前（土地糾紛直到1988年才落幕），學校曾借用私立學校校舍作為臨時校址。如今是大埤鄉唯一的公立國中。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Dapi, home of suan cai.

🥬

Dapi Township is famous nationwide for suan cai (酸菜, pickled mustard greens) production, and the school's student body is drawn largely from families connected to this local pickling industry and surrounding farmland. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
