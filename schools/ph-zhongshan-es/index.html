About · 學校簡介

From a naval shipyard's children's school to a county elementary.

Zhongshan Elementary began life on March 1, 1948, as the "Naval Second Shipyard Employees' Children's School," educating the children of naval shipyard workers stationed in Magong. As Penghu's county government took over local education administration, the school passed into county hands and was renamed Zhongshan Elementary around 1965–1968. Today it is a small neighborhood school in Anshan Village, on the edge of Magong City, with roughly 160 students and 17 teachers.

中山國小的前身，是民國37年（1948年）3月1日成立的「海軍第二造船廠員工子弟學校」，最初專為駐守馬公的海軍造船廠員工子女提供教育。隨著澎湖縣政府接管地方教育行政，學校於民國五十年代後期改隸縣府並更名為中山國小。如今，這所座落於馬公市案山里的學校，是一所小型的社區學校，約有160名學生與17位教師。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏀 National Basketball Champions

全國籃球冠軍

Zhongshan's boys' basketball team won the small-school division at the 2024 National Elementary School Basketball Championship — a remarkable result for a school of its size.

⚓ Born From a Naval Shipyard

海軍造船廠子弟學校起源

The school's roots as the "Naval Second Shipyard Employees' Children's School" reflect Penghu's long military history as a strategic outpost in the Taiwan Strait.

🏫 A Close-Knit Small School

小而精緻的校園

With around 160 students and 17 teachers, Zhongshan keeps the intimate, closely-supervised learning environment typical of Penghu's smaller island-city schools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Magong, Penghu's island capital.

Zhongshan Elementary sits in Anshan Village within Magong City, the administrative and population center of Penghu County — an archipelago shaped by fishing, seafaring, and a long military presence in the Taiwan Strait. See the Penghu county page for more on the region.
