About · 學校簡介

A bilingual classroom on Muzha Road.

Mingdao Elementary School opened on August 1, 1976, with Tian Qingcheng as its first principal, on Muzha Road in the same corridor as several of the district's older Muzha-area schools.

The school is known for its bilingual education program, which combines large touch-screen displays with one-tablet-per-student technology, and has hosted roughly 150 open classroom observation and discussion sessions for visiting educators. It currently has about 20 classes and 347 students, and added a mobile book cart and reading corners around campus starting in 2023.

明道國小於1976年（民國65年）8月1日創校，首任校長田慶成，校址位於木柵路，與文山區多所歷史悠久的木柵地區學校同在一條路上。

學校以雙語教育著稱，結合大型觸控螢幕與一人一平板科技教學，累計舉辦約150場公開觀課與議課活動，供教育界人士參訪交流。目前全校約20班、347名學生，並自2023年起於校園各處增設行動書車與閱讀角。
