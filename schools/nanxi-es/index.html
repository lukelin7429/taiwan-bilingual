About · 學校簡介

A century-old school named for its town, relocated three times.

Nanxi Elementary School was established on April 20, 1920, with Governor-General approval, as “Tapani Public School Qieba Branch” (噍吧哖公學校茄拔分校) — “Qieba” being Nanxi's old name. It became an independent school as “Nanxi Public School” on April 24, 1921, was renamed “Nanxi National School” in 1941, and became “Nanxi Elementary School” in 1968. The school celebrated its 100th anniversary on March 5, 2022, with Tainan Mayor Huang Wei-che in attendance. Over its history the campus has relocated three times: from its original site near today's Nanxi market, to what is now Nanxi Park, and finally to its current location. Today the school runs an outdoor-education program and a traditional drum-and-lion-dance team, which earned an “excellent” rating in the 2024 Traditional Arts Competition.

楠西國小創校於大正9年（1920年）4月20日，經總督府核准設立「噍吧哖公學校茄拔分校」，「茄拔」即楠西舊稱。民國10年（1921年）4月24日獨立設校，改稱「楠西公學校」，1941年改名「楠西國民學校」，1968年定名「楠西國民小學」。2022年3月5日舉行建校百週年校慶，台南市長黃偉哲親自出席。校史上曾三度遷校：最初校址鄰近今日楠西市場，後遷至今楠西公園處，最終遷至現址。學校現辦理戶外教育課程，並擁有一支傳統鼓獅隊，於2024年傳統藝術比賽中榮獲優等成績。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring the gateway to Yellow Butterfly Valley.

🦋

Named after the district itself, this century-old school anchors the small town center that serves as the gateway to Yellow Butterfly Valley and Nanxi's mangosteen and mango orchards. Its traditional drum-and-lion-dance team and outdoor-education program keep students connected to the mountain landscape around them. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
