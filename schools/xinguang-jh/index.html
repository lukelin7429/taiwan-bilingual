About · 學校簡介

Growing with a booming suburb.

Xinguang Junior High School was founded in 1996 to serve the fast-growing Xinguang residential cluster of Taiping District, which expanded rapidly as the district urbanized through the 1990s. Today the school enrolls roughly 1,596 students across 57 classes, drawing students from seven villages across Taiping.

新光國中創立於民國85年（1996年），因應太平區新光地區在1990年代快速都市化而興建，服務周邊迅速成長的新興社區。現今全校約有1,596名學生、57個班級，學生來自太平區七個里。
