About · 學校簡介

A school whose growth shaped the town's education map.

Guanghua Elementary School is one of the established elementary schools serving central Chaozhou Town. As the town's population grew during Taiwan's urbanization, enrollment pressure at Guanghua and neighboring Chaozhou Elementary helped prompt the later founding of Chaosheng Elementary in a newly developed residential district — a small but telling piece of the town's educational history.

光華國小是潮州鎮中心地帶的老牌國小之一。隨著台灣都市化進程中潮州鎮人口增長，光華國小與鄰近的潮州國小招生壓力漸增，促成日後在新興住宅區設立潮昇國小——這也是潮州鎮教育發展史上耐人尋味的一頁。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Chaozhou, southern Pingtung's rail and market hub.

🚂

Chaozhou Town is southern Pingtung's key market town and transportation hub, historically tied to the sugar industry and rail transport heritage. Guanghua Elementary's role in the town's educational growth reflects Chaozhou's own steady expansion over the decades.
