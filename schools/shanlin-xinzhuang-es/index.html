About · 學校簡介

A small village school in rural Shanlin.

Xinzhuang Elementary's founding date is not recorded in available sources. It is a small rural school, with enrollment as low as 66 students in one dataset, serving the Xinzhuang neighborhood of Shanlin District. Its name, "Xinzhuang" (新庄, "new settlement"), is a common Taiwanese place name marking communities founded during the post-Qing wave of agricultural expansion.

新庄國小確切創校年代未見於現有資料。學校規模小，某資料顯示學生數僅66人，服務杉林區新庄一帶的學童。校名「新庄」是台灣清代以降拓墾擴張聚落常見的地名之一。
