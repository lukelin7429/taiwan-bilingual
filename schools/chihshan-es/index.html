About · 學校簡介

Nurturing talent, keeping campus safe.

Chihshan Elementary School, in Qixian Village, Yuanshan Township, takes part in the "Three Flowers Elite Seeds Academy" (三花菁英幼苗學院) program, a talent-development initiative for young students. The school has also invested in student wellbeing through clear mobile-device management policies and a campus safety mapping system, giving families confidence that daily campus life is well looked after.

七賢國小位於員山鄉七賢村，參與「三花菁英幼苗學院」計畫，致力於培育學生潛能。學校同時投入學生福祉，訂定明確的行動載具管理規範，並建置校園安全地圖系統，讓家長對日常校園生活更加放心。
