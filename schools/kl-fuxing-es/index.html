Fuxing Elementary School

復興國民小學

A century-old public elementary school in the remote mountain village of Youna, founded to serve the children of local coal miners.

About · 學校簡介

A mountain-village school that outlasted the mines.

Fuxing Elementary School was founded in April 1922 as the "Youna Improvement Study Hall," serving the children of miners in the remote mountain village of Youna. It was renamed Fuxing Elementary School in January 1969 and celebrated its 100th anniversary in April 2023. During the coal-mining boom of the 1960s, its student population once exceeded 700, but as the mining industry declined, enrollment has since shrunk to roughly 150 students today.

復興國民小學創校於民國11年（1922年）4月，初名「友蚋改進講習所」，服務偏遠山村友蚋地區礦工子弟。民國58年（1969年）1月更名為「復興國民小學」，並於民國112年（2023年）4月舉辦建校百週年慶祝活動。民國50年代（1960年代）礦業興盛時期，學生人數曾超過700人；隨著礦業式微，現今學生人數已縮減至約150人。
