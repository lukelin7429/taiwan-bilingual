Jidong Elementary School

吉東國民小學

Taiwan's first Hakka-humanities themed experimental school, built around local food, agriculture, and Hakka culture.

台灣第一所客家人文主題實驗學校，以在地食農教育與客家文化為核心課程。

About · 學校簡介

Founded for a safer commute, reborn as a Hakka-humanities pioneer.

Jidong Elementary School opened in September 1953, splitting off from Jiyang Elementary's school district because children faced a long and unsafe commute to the original school. Today it is one of Kaohsiung's designated "campus maker bases," and in 2018 it was approved as Taiwan's first Hakka-humanities themed experimental school, with its entire curriculum now built around Hakka humanities alongside local food and agriculture education.

吉東國小創立於民國42年（1953年）9月，因學童通勤路途遙遠且不安全，從吉洋國小學區分出獨立設校。如今學校為高雄市指定的「校園自造基地」之一，並於2018年獲核定為台灣第一所客家人文主題實驗學校，全校課程皆圍繞客家人文精神與在地食農教育設計。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 Taiwan's First Hakka-Humanities Experimental School

全台首創客家人文實驗學校

Approved in 2018, Jidong became Taiwan's first Hakka-humanities themed experimental school, building its entire curriculum around Hakka culture and identity.

🌱 Food and Agriculture at the Core

食農教育為核心

Alongside its Hakka-humanities focus, the school's curriculum centers on local food and agriculture education, connecting students to Meinong's farming traditions.

🔧 One of Kaohsiung's Campus Maker Bases

高雄市校園自造基地

Jidong is designated as Kaohsiung's sixth "campus maker base," giving students hands-on maker-education facilities and programs.

🚸 Founded for a Safer Route to School

為安全通勤而設校

The school was established in 1953 after splitting from Jiyang Elementary's district specifically because local children faced a long, unsafe commute.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A curriculum built entirely around Hakka humanities.

📖

As Taiwan's first Hakka-humanities themed experimental school, Jidong Elementary has redesigned its entire curriculum around Hakka culture, local food, and agriculture education — a deep expression of Meinong's Hakka farming identity. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
