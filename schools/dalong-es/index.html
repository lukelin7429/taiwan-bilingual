Dalong Elementary School

大龍國小

One of Taipei's oldest schools, founded in 1896, with a 1933 heritage building registered as a Taipei City historic site and a century-plus tradition of Go and nanguan music education.

About · 學校簡介

A historic building at one of Taipei's oldest schools.

Dalong Elementary was founded in 1896, during the Japanese colonial era, as the 3rd Affiliated School of the Government-General's Language School (臺灣總督府國語學校第三附屬學校) — making it one of the oldest elementary schools in Taipei. Its rear school building dates to 1933, originally two stories and expanded to three in 1968; it still retains original arcades and terrazzo (磨石子) stairs, and was registered as a Taipei City historic building in 2006. The school built a swimming pool and opened a kindergarten in 1985, and celebrated its 100th anniversary on November 12, 1996, with then-President Lee Teng-hui attending and planting a commemorative tree. It also runs Go (圍棋) education and a nanguan (南管, traditional Southern Fujian music) club established in 1994.

大龍國小創校於1896年（明治29年）日治時期，初名「臺灣總督府國語學校第三附屬學校」，是台北歷史最悠久的國小之一。學校後棟校舍建於1933年，原為兩層樓，1968年增建為三層樓，至今仍保留原有拱廊與磨石子樓梯，2006年登錄為台北市市定古蹟。學校於1985年（民國74年）興建游泳池並開辦幼兒園，1996年11月12日舉辦建校百年校慶，時任總統李登輝親臨並種下紀念樹。學校並推展圍棋教育，以及成立於1994年的南管（傳統閩南音樂）社團。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Beside the Confucius Temple and Bao'an Temple.

⛩️

As one of Taipei's oldest schools, dating to 1896, Dalong Elementary sits in Dalongdong beside the Confucius Temple and Bao'an Temple — its century-plus history is inseparable from Datong District's identity as Taipei's earliest-settled area. The school's own heritage building now stands as a physical landmark alongside the neighborhood's other registered historic sites.
