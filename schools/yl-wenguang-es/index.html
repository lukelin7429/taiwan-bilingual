About · 學校簡介

Over a century serving Kouhu's coast.

Wenguang Elementary was founded on April 10, 1922, making it one of the oldest schools in Kouhu Township with over a century of history. Beyond its main campus, Wenguang operates two branch campuses — the Hukou Branch and the Guogang Branch — extending its reach into outlying coastal communities that would otherwise be a long walk from any school.

文光國小創校於民國11年（1922年）4月10日，是口湖鄉歷史最悠久的學校之一，已有百年以上校史。除本校外，文光國小另設有湖口分校與過港分校兩所分校，將教育資源延伸至偏遠的沿海聚落。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Over a Century of History

百年校史

Founded April 10, 1922, Wenguang is one of the oldest schools in Kouhu Township, with more than a hundred years of continuous operation.

🏫 Two Branch Campuses

湖口與過港分校

Wenguang operates the Hukou Branch and the Guogang Branch, extending schooling into outlying coastal villages within the township.

🐟 A Fishing-Village Heritage

漁村世代傳承

As one of Kouhu's oldest schools, Wenguang has educated generations of children from local fishing and fish-farming families.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Kouhu's milkfish coast.

🐟

As one of the oldest schools in Kouhu Township, a coastal aquaculture township famed for milkfish (虱目魚) farming, Wenguang has educated generations of children from local fishing and fish-farming families. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
