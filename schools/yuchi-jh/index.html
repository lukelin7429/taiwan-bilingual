魚池國中

About · 學校簡介

A school tied to the land and its tea.

Yuchih Junior High School is located in the center of Yuchi Township. Its curriculum ties directly into the region's agricultural and tea industry, linking classroom learning to the highland tea gardens that surround Sun Moon Lake. The school also holds a point of pride in its academic record: student Zhan Xiang-an became the first in school history to score a perfect 5A on the high-school entrance exam.

魚池國中位於魚池鄉市街中心。課程與當地農業及茶產業緊密結合，將課堂學習與環繞日月潭的高山茶園連結起來。學校也以其學業成就為榮：學生詹翔安是校史上首位在會考取得5A滿級分的學生。
