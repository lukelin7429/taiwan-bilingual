Xinglong Elementary School

興隆國民小學

Founded 50 meters from its overcrowded neighbor Xingde Elementary, created specifically as an overflow relief school.

About · 學校簡介

Built to relieve a crowded neighbor.

Xinglong Elementary School was established in 1975, roughly 50 meters from Xingde Elementary School, specifically to absorb overflow enrollment as the local population grew.

The school's founding is a direct record of Wenshan's rapid mid-1970s residential population growth in the Xinglong/Fuxing corridor, a pattern echoed by several other schools founded in the district around the same period.

興隆國小創立於1975年（民國64年），校址距興德國小僅約50公尺，專為紓解該校因人口成長而過度擁擠的招生壓力而設立。

學校的創立直接反映了1970年代中期文山區興隆路、福興路一帶人口快速成長的歷史，與同期在文山區設立的其他學校情況相呼應。
