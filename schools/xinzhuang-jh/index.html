Xinzhuang Junior High School

新北市立新莊國民中學

Xinzhuang's oldest junior high, founded in 1946 through local donations on a site once used as a Japanese-era school.

創立於1946年、新莊歷史最悠久的國中，由地方居民捐地捐款興學，校地更與日治時期學校淵源深厚。

About · 學校簡介

A postwar school built by its own community.

Xinzhuang Junior High School traces its founding to 1946, when it opened as Xinzhuang Junior Middle School on a site that had previously housed a Japanese-era elementary school for Japanese settlers. Local residents donated land and funds between 1949 and 1952 to help establish the school on firmer footing. It briefly became a complete secondary school in 1953 before losing its senior-high division in 1968, and today stands as the oldest junior high in Xinzhuang.

新莊國中創立於民國35年（1946年），初名「新莊初級中學」，校地原為日治時期供日籍移民子弟就讀的小學用地。民國38至41年間（1949至1952年），地方居民捐地捐款協助建校。民國42年（1953年）曾一度升格為完全中學，民國57年（1968年）高中部裁撤，是新莊歷史最悠久的國中。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A campus layered with history.

🏘️

Xinzhuang Junior High's founding is woven into the town's postwar civic revival, when residents pooled land and money to rebuild local education after Japanese rule ended. Its campus's earlier use as a school for Japanese settlers is a reminder of Xinzhuang's long, layered history as an old river port turned modern district.
