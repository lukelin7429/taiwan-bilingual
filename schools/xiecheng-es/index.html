About · 學校簡介

A small school for a small mountain village.

Xiecheng Elementary was established in 1983, making it one of the newest and smallest schools in Xinshe District. It serves Xiecheng village, one of several scattered highland farming communities across the district's hilly terrain, reflecting Xinshe's identity as a place of small, close-knit rural settlements.

協成國小創立於民國72年（1983年），是新社地區規模最小、設校最晚的學校之一，服務協成里這個座落於山區、地處分散的農村聚落，體現新社地區小型緊密農村社區的特色。
