Shipai Elementary School

臺北市立石牌國民小學

Founded in 1922, once one of Taiwan's largest elementary schools with over 6,000 students, named for a Qing-dynasty boundary stone.

創校於大正11年（1922年），全盛時期學生人數曾逾6,000人，是台灣歷史最大的國小之一，校名源自清代界石「石牌」。

About · 學校簡介

From over 6,000 students to a steadier 2,400.

Shipai Elementary opened on April 1, 1922, as a branch classroom of Beitou Public School, becoming independent on April 1, 1926, as Shipai Public School. Enrollment once exceeded 6,000 students, making it historically one of Taiwan's largest elementary schools; today around 2,400 students attend across 108 classes. The school took its current Taipei City form in 1974, following the dissolution of the Yangmingshan Administration.

石牌國小創校於大正11年（1922年）4月1日，最初為北投公學校分教場，於大正15年（1926年）4月1日獨立設校，稱為石牌公學校。全盛時期學生人數曾逾6,000人，是台灣歷史上規模最大的國小之一；如今學生人數約2,400人，設有108班。學校於民國63年（1974年）陽明山管理局裁撤後，改制為現今的臺北市立學校名稱。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named for a Qing-era boundary stone.

🪨

Shipai Elementary takes its name from the Qing-era boundary marker stone ("石牌") that once separated Han settler land from Indigenous Ketagalan territory — set within the same geothermal belt that produces Beitou's famous hot springs. See the Taipei county page for more on the region.
