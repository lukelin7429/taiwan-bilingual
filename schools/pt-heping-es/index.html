About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood school in the county seat.

Heping Elementary is a public elementary school located on Huazheng Road, right in the heart of Pingtung City. As the administrative and commercial center of Pingtung County, the city is home to a dense network of neighborhood elementary schools, and Heping serves the families of its immediate school zone — organized, like its peer schools, around a principal's office and the standard academic and student-affairs administrative divisions.

和平國小是一所位於屏東市華正路的公立國小。屏東市作為屏東縣的行政與商業中心，市區內設有密集的國小學區網絡，和平國小服務其所屬學區的學童，行政組織與其他鄰近學校相同，設有校長室及教務處、學務處等一般行政單位。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏙️ At the City's Administrative Heart

位於市中心行政區

Located on Huazheng Road in central Pingtung City, placing the school within the county seat's dense administrative and commercial core.

☮️ A Name Rooted in Peace

校名寓意和平

The school's name, Heping (和平), means "peace" — a value reflected in its role as a steady neighborhood institution serving the families of its local school zone.

🏫 Part of a Dense Urban School Network

屏東市密集學區網絡的一員

As one of many elementary schools serving Pingtung City's urban core, Heping is part of the county seat's tightly knit network of neighborhood schools.
