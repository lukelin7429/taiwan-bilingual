About · 學校簡介

From a girls' school to the archipelago's hub.

National Magong Senior High School was founded on April 1, 1943, during the Japanese colonial era, as the "Penghu Prefecture Magong Girls' Higher School" (澎湖廳立馬公高等女子學校). After Taiwan's retrocession, it was reorganized as a coeducational school, "Taiwan Provincial Magong Middle School" (臺灣省立馬公中學). In 1949, three of the county's junior high schools — from Baisha, Huxi, and Xiyu townships — merged into the school, consolidating secondary education for students across Penghu's outlying islands. It became a senior high school in 1970, added business, tourism, art, and music programs between 1972 and 1997, and was elevated to national ("國立") status on February 1, 2000.

國立馬公高級中學創校於昭和18年（民國32年，1943年）4月1日，前身為日治時期的「澎湖廳立馬公高等女子學校」。台灣光復後改制為男女兼收的「臺灣省立馬公中學」。民國38年（1949年），縣內白沙、湖西、西嶼三所國中併入本校，整合了全澎湖群島各離島地區的中等教育資源。民國59年（1970年）改制為「臺灣省立馬公高級中學」，並於民國61年至86年間陸續增設商業、觀光、美術、音樂等科別，民國89年（2000年）2月1日改制為國立。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Rooted in a 1943 Girls' School

前身為高等女子學校

The school traces its origins to 1943 as the Penghu Prefecture Magong Girls' Higher School, later reorganized into a coeducational middle school after Taiwan's retrocession.

🏝️ Uniting the Outlying Islands' Education

整併離島三校

In 1949, three junior high schools from Baisha, Huxi, and Xiyu townships merged into this school, consolidating secondary education for students across Penghu's scattered islands.

🎨 Business, Tourism, Art and Music

商業、觀光、美術、音樂科

Between 1972 and 1997, the school steadily expanded beyond the general academic track, adding business, tourism, art, and music programs to serve a broader range of students.

🏛️ National Status Since 2000

民國89年改制國立

The school became a national ("國立") institution in February 2000 when Taiwan's provincial school system was reorganized, a status it continues to hold today.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The archipelago's education hub.

🏝️

As the main comprehensive senior high serving all of Penghu's outlying-island students — having absorbed island-wide junior high schools from Baisha, Huxi, and Xiyu townships in 1949 — National Magong Senior High School has long functioned as the archipelago's central hub for secondary education, drawing students from across the scattered islands of the county.
