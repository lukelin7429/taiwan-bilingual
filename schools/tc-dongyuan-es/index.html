Dongyuan Elementary School

東園國民小學

A centrally located Wuri District elementary school, set on the alluvial farmland between the Dali and Wu rivers.

座落於烏日區中心地帶，位處大里溪與烏溪之間的沖積平原農地。

About · 學校簡介

Between two rivers, at the heart of the district.

Dongyuan Elementary School is located centrally within Wuri District, south of the Dali River and east of the Wu River, placing it squarely within the district's core alluvial-plain farmland. This central position has long made it an anchor school for the surrounding agricultural communities.

東園國小座落於烏日區中心地帶，位處大里溪以南、烏溪以東，正處於本區核心的沖積平原農地範圍內。這樣的中心位置，使其長久以來成為周邊農業社區的重要據點學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌊 Between the Dali and Wu Rivers

大里溪與烏溪之間

The school sits on the alluvial farmland between the Dali River to the north and the Wu River to the south, a defining geographic feature of central Wuri.

🌾 Anchor of the Farmland Core

農地核心的據點學校

Its central position within Wuri's core agricultural plain has long made Dongyuan a neighborhood anchor for surrounding farming communities.

📍 Right at the District's Center

位處烏日中心

Its location, centrally placed within Wuri District rather than at its periphery, gives students easy access to community life across the district.
