Beibin Elementary School

北濱國小

A seaside school at the foot of Huagang Mountain, designated a Hualien County Ocean Education Center with its own marine life and archaeology museums.

About · 學校簡介

A school shaped by the Pacific coast.

Beibin Elementary sits at the foot of Hualien's Huagang Mountain, right along the North Shore (Beibin) coastal strip — a location that has long shaped the school's identity and its student body, which historically included many children from railway workers' and fishermen's families. Reflecting its seaside setting, Beibin has been designated one of Hualien County's Ocean Education Centers, and the campus houses its own on-site marine life museum as well as an archaeology museum.

北濱國小座落於花蓮花崗山腳下，緊鄰北濱濱海地帶——這個地理位置長期形塑著學校的特色，早期學生多來自鐵路員工與漁民家庭。呼應濱海環境，北濱國小獲指定為花蓮縣海洋教育中心之一，校內設有海洋生物博物館與考古博物館。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌊 A Designated Ocean Education Center

花蓮縣海洋教育中心

Beibin is one of Hualien County's designated Ocean Education Centers, using its coastal location to bring marine science directly into student life.

🐠 An On-Campus Marine Life Museum

校內海洋生物博物館

The school hosts its own marine life museum, giving students hands-on exposure to the Pacific ecosystem right outside their classroom doors.

🏺 An Archaeology Museum, Too

校內考古博物館

Alongside its marine collection, Beibin maintains an on-site archaeology museum, reflecting the layered history of the coastal land the school occupies.

🚂 Roots in Rail and Fishing Families

鐵路與漁民家庭子弟

The school historically served the children of railway workers and fishermen who lived along the North Shore, a working-class heritage still felt in the community today.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A seaside county seat.

🌊

Directly abutting Beibin (North Shore) Park and the Pacific coastline, the school embodies Hualien City's identity as a seaside county seat shaped by its fishing and rail heritage.
