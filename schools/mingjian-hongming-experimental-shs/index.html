Hongming Experimental Senior High School

南投縣私立弘明實驗高級中學

A private K-12 school built around Confucian-classics education, character formation, and the arts, in the heart of Taiwan's tea country.

以讀經教育、品格教育與藝術陶冶為核心的私立完全中小學，座落於台灣茶鄉的中心地帶。

About · 學校簡介

An education rooted in the classics.

Hongming Experimental Senior High School was founded in 2007, with its educational lineage tracing back to a nursery founded in Taichung in 2002. The school was established in the spirit of the teachings of Buddhist scholar Li Bingnan by founder Lien Shu-mei, and it built its curriculum around Confucian-classics recitation, character education, and the arts across a full K-12 continuum. The school held a "Hongming Expo" marking its fifth anniversary in 2011.

弘明實驗高中創立於民國96年（2007年），其教育淵源可追溯至2002年於台中設立的幼兒園。學校由創辦人連淑美依循佛教學者李炳南教授的教育理念創辦，以讀經教育、品格教育與藝術陶冶為核心，發展出從幼兒園到高中的完整教育體系。2011年學校舉辦「弘明博覽會」，慶祝創校五週年。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📖 Classics-Based Curriculum

讀經教育

The school builds its curriculum around Confucian-classics recitation and character education, following the educational spirit of Buddhist scholar Li Bingnan's teachings.

🎨 A Focus on the Arts

藝術陶冶

Alongside classics study, Hongming emphasizes the arts as part of a well-rounded character education for students from preschool through senior high.

🏫 A Full K-12 Continuum

完整教育體系

As a private institution spanning preschool through senior high, the school offers families a continuous educational philosophy across every stage of childhood and adolescence.

🎉 A Legacy of Community Events

弘明博覽會傳統

The school marked its fifth anniversary in 2011 with a "Hongming Expo," reflecting its practice of sharing its educational approach with the wider community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Classical values in tea country.

🍵

Though not indigenous-themed, Hongming's classics- and character-based curriculum reflects Mingjian's reputation as a township that pairs traditional values with its tea-growing agricultural heritage — home to the famous Songbolang evergreen tea. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
