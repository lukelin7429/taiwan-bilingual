Dongmen Elementary School

Named for one of the historic gates of old Meinong, this school traces its roots to a 1962 branch campus of Fu'an Elementary.

About · 學校簡介

Named for Meinong's old East Gate.

Dongmen Elementary School was established on September 1, 1962, as a branch campus of Fu'an Elementary School, known as the Dongmen (East Gate) Branch. The exact year it became fully independent has not been confirmed. The school takes its name from Meinong's old East Gate area, one of the historic entry points to the town's early walled settlement.

東門國小於民國51年（1962年）9月1日設立，最初為福安國小的分校，稱為「東門分校」。獨立設校的確切年份尚未確認。學校因位於美濃舊聚落「東門」地區而得名，這裡是早期美濃圍庄聚落的歷史入口之一。

🚪 Named for Meinong's Historic East Gate

以「東門」舊地名命名

🏫 Grew Out of a 1962 Branch Campus

1962年設立分校

Dongmen Elementary began life on September 1, 1962, as a branch campus of Fu'an Elementary School before eventually becoming an independent school.

🏘️ Serving Meinong's Old East Gate Area

服務美濃舊東門地區

The school continues to serve the neighborhood built around Meinong's historic East Gate, one of several gated entry points into the town's original walled core.
