Fengnan Junior High School

豐南國民中學

A junior high school established in 1968 as part of Taiwan's rollout of nine-year compulsory education, named for its position in southern Fengyuan.

創校於1968年，配合台灣九年國民義務教育政策設立，因位處豐原南側而得名。

About · 學校簡介

A name that marks its place.

Fengnan Junior High School opened in 1968 as part of the nationwide rollout of nine-year compulsory education, one of several new junior highs built across Taiwan that year to accommodate the policy shift. Its name, meaning "Fengyuan South," directly reflects its location in the southern part of the district, and it continues to serve families in that part of Fengyuan today.

豐南國中創校於民國57年（1968年），正值台灣九年國民義務教育推行之際，是當年全台各地為因應此政策而新設的國中之一。校名「豐南」意指「豐原之南」，直接呼應其座落於豐原區南側的地理位置，至今持續服務該區域的學子。
