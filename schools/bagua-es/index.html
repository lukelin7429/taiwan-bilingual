About · 學校簡介

From baseball fields to a changing landscape.

Bagua Elementary opened in 1959 as a branch of Renwu Elementary School and became independent in 1968. Historically known for its baseball program, it has since shifted its athletic focus toward dance, dodgeball, and its wind band, while still serving a sizeable student body of around 1,021 students across 42 classes. The campus sits near the Dazhong Freeway interchange, between the Renwu Industrial Park and Chengcing Lake.

八卦國小創立於1959年（民國48年），最初為仁武國小分校，1968年獨立設校。學校過去以棒球隊聞名，如今則將體育重心轉向舞蹈、躲避球與管樂團，全校約1,021名學生、42個班級。校址鄰近大眾交流道，介於仁武工業區與澄清湖之間。
