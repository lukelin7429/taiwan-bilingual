About · 學校簡介

A newer school for a coastal village.

Jinmei Elementary School was founded in 1989, making it one of Jinshan District's newer elementary schools. Located in Jinmei Village, it was established relatively recently to serve the local coastal-village community, part of a wave of school expansion driven by North Coast development in the late 1980s.

金美國小創立於民國78年（1989年），是金山區較新設立的國小之一，位於金美里。學校設立時間相對較晚，旨在服務當地海濱聚落社區，屬於1980年代末期北海岸地區發展所帶動的學校擴建浪潮之一。
