About · 學校簡介

From junior high to "AI Technology High School."

Jinhe Senior High School was founded in 1990 as Jinhe Junior High School. It expanded into a complete secondary school in 2000, and today runs both a regular day division and a continuing-education division. In 2024 the school rebranded itself as an "AI + Technology High School," reflecting a push to bring artificial intelligence and technology education into its curriculum. Its name comes from the surrounding Jinhe area, near Yuantong Temple (圓通寺) and Jinhe Sports Park — a corner of Zhonghe where old temple-anchored settlements meet newer civic infrastructure.
