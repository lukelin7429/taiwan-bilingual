Shalu Junior High School

臺中市立沙鹿國民中學

About · 學校簡介

The founding school of Shalu's secondary education.

Shalu Junior High opened in 1961 as the district's first junior high school, initially taking over six wooden classrooms from Shalu Elementary School. As Shalu's population grew rapidly through the 1960s to 1980s, the school became so overcrowded that it eventually spun off two new junior highs — Beishi and Luliao — to absorb the overflow. Today it remains the founding institution of secondary education in the district.

沙鹿國中創立於民國50年（1961年），是沙鹿地區第一所國中，草創初期借用沙鹿國小六間木造教室上課。隨著沙鹿人口在1960至1980年代快速成長，學校一度人滿為患，因而先後分設出北勢國中與鹿寮國中，分擔就學壓力。至今仍是沙鹿地區中等教育的創始學校。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A record of postwar growth.

📈

Shalu Junior High's crowded decades are a direct record of the district's rapid postwar population growth — a boom so fast that one school's overflow became the seed of two more, a pattern repeated across Shalu's education history. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
