桃園市立平鎮高級中等學校

About · 學校簡介

A senior high reshaped by two splits and a merger.

Pingzhen Senior High traces its roots to 1998, when the Taoyuan County government assigned the principal of Pingzhen Junior High to plan a new complete-secondary school on land originally set aside for a proposed Beishi Junior High. The senior-high division's campus was completed in June 2000, with the first class enrolling that September as "Taoyuan County Pingzhen Middle School," combining junior and senior high on one campus. When the complete-secondary pilot program ended in 2001, the school was renamed Pingzhen Senior High School, and in 2002 its junior-high division split off to become an independent Pingzhen Junior High on a separate campus. The school became a municipal institution in 2014 amid Taoyuan's upgrade to special-municipality status, and took its current "Senior High and Vocational" designation in 2018.

平鎮高中的歷史可追溯至1998年，桃園縣政府指派平鎮國中校長規劃一所完全中學，校地原本是預定興建北勢國中的用地。高中部校舍於2000年6月完工，同年9月首批學生入學，當時校名為「桃園縣立平鎮中學」，國、高中部合併於同一校園。2001年完全中學試辦計畫結束後，學校更名為「平鎮高級中學」；2002年國中部因兩校區位置不同而獨立設校，成為現今的平鎮國中。2014年桃園縣升格直轄市，本校改制為市立學校，並於2018年隨桃園市高中職移撥市府主管，正式定名為現制。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥋 A Home for Taekwondo and Wushu

武術與跆拳道專班

The school runs specialized athletics tracks in taekwondo, wushu, badminton, and table tennis, echoing Pingzhen's long-standing local martial-arts tradition tied to its historic old street.

🎨 Dedicated Art Classes

美術班

Alongside its athletics programs, the school offers dedicated art classes, giving students a specialized track beyond the standard academic curriculum.

🏫 A School Twice Reshaped

兩度合併又分立

Originally combined with Pingzhen Junior High as a complete secondary school in 2000, the two divisions split into separate independent schools in 2002 after operating on different campuses.

🏛️ From County to City School

縣立升格市立

The school became a municipal institution in 2014 when Taoyuan County was upgraded to special-municipality status, and took its current senior-high-and-vocational designation in 2018.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A martial-arts tradition on the old street.

🥋

Pingzhen District's historic old street (平鎮老街) has long carried a local reputation for martial-arts tradition, and Pingzhen Senior High keeps that heritage alive through its athletics program, which centers on taekwondo (跆拳道) and wushu (武術) alongside badminton and table tennis.
