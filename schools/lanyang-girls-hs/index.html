About · 學校簡介

An early academy for girls, now a home for dance.

National Lanyang Girls' Senior High School was founded on April 20, 1938, as "Taihoku Prefectural Lanyang Girls' High School" — making it the second school established in the Yilan area, after the predecessor of National Ilan University. It became a national school in 2000. The school is best known today for its dance program: in 1989 it launched Taiwan's first dedicated dance-gifted class, and it now houses a dedicated dance building with multiple studios and an auditorium seating over 1,000. Since 2011, its "Flying Dance" program has sent students on international cultural exchanges to perform and study abroad.

國立蘭陽女子高級中學創立於1938年4月20日，日治時期名為「臺北州立蘭陽高等女學校」，是宜蘭地區繼國立宜蘭大學前身（宜蘭農林學校）之後成立的第二所學校，並於2000年改制為國立高中。學校如今以舞蹈教育聞名，民國78年（1989年）創立全台第一個舞蹈資優班，現設有專屬舞蹈大樓，內含多間舞蹈教室及可容納千人以上的大禮堂。自2011年起推動「飛舞計畫」，讓學生赴海外進行文化交流演出與學習。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

💃 Taiwan's First Dance-Gifted Program

全台首創舞蹈資優班

Established in 1989, the school's dance-gifted program was the first of its kind in Taiwan, and remains a defining feature of the school today.

🎭 A Dedicated Dance Building

專屬舞蹈大樓

Multiple studios and an auditorium seating more than 1,000 give students purpose-built space to train, rehearse, and perform.

✈️ The Flying Dance Program

飛舞計畫

Since 2011, this overseas cultural exchange initiative has sent students abroad to perform and study dance, connecting Yilan's young dancers to the wider world.

🏫 Nearly Nine Decades of History

近九十年校史

Founded in 1938 as a girls' high school under Japanese rule, it is one of the oldest schools in the Yilan area, tracing its roots to the colonial era.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A cultural landmark in Yilan City.

As one of Yilan's oldest schools and the birthplace of Taiwan's first dance-gifted program, Lanyang Girls' has become closely associated with the performing arts in the region, with its "Flying Dance" exchange bringing Yilan's dance tradition to international stages.
