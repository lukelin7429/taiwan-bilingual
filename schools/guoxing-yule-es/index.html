About · 學校簡介

A small school, rebuilt and repurposed after disaster.

Yule Elementary, founded in 1959, is a small remote-area school in Guoxing Township with roughly 31 students across 6 classes. Its buildings were destroyed in the catastrophic 1999 921 earthquake, and the campus was rebuilt with help from the Buddhist organization Ling Jiou Mountain. In the aftermath, the rebuilt campus also served as a base for the "Nantou County Northern Disaster Relief Center," playing a role in the township's broader recovery effort.

育樂國小創立於民國48年（1959年），是國姓鄉一所偏遠小校，全校約31名學生、6個班級。校舍於1999年921大地震中全毀，其後在佛教靈鷲山協助下重建。重建後的校園一度做為「南投縣北區災區重建服務中心」的基地，在該鄉整體災後重建工作中扮演一定角色。
