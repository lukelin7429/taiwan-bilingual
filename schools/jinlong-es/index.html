About · 學校簡介

A school named for a lake, shaped like a dragon.

Jinlong Elementary School takes its name from nearby Jinlong Lake (金龍湖), one of Xizhi's most scenic natural landmarks, and its campus architecture intentionally incorporates dragon (龍) motifs in tribute to that connection. The school brands itself around creativity, digital technology, and bilingual exploration, and its library — established in 1997 — has anchored decades of that mission even though the school's precise founding year is not confirmable from available sources.

金龍國小校名源自鄰近的金龍湖，這是汐止區最富詩意的自然地景之一，校園建築也刻意融入龍形意象以呼應此淵源。學校以「創意、數位科技、雙語探索」作為特色定位，雖然確切創校年份難以從現有資料中考證，但該校圖書館自民國86年（1997年）啟用以來，已見證了數十年的辦學使命。
