About · 學校簡介

A riverside school with a hard-won history.

Chaoliao Junior High began in 1978 as a branch campus of Daliao Junior High, becoming an independent school in 1983. Located near the Gaoping River and the Dafa Industrial Park, the school and its surrounding community drew public and legislative attention in 2009 when industrial odor pollution forced classes to be relocated — an episode that highlighted the environmental pressures facing riverside communities in this part of Kaohsiung.

潮寮國中前身為大寮國中分校，成立於民國67年（1978年），民國72年（1983年）獨立設校。校區鄰近高屏溪與大發工業區，2009年曾因工業惡臭污染問題導致班級被迫遷移上課，引發社會與立法機關高度關注，凸顯了河濱社區所面臨的環境壓力。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A river that shapes daily life.

🌊

Chaoliao ("tide hut") takes its name from the area's history of tidal and flood influence near the Gaoping River — a reminder of how closely daily life in this part of Daliao has long been tied to the river system that also shapes the district's industrial development today.
