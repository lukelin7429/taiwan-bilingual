About · 學校簡介

The mother school of Puxin.

Ruipu Elementary was established in March 1928 as a branch campus of Yangmei Elementary, becoming an independent school in March 1933 under the name "Caonanpo Public School." It was later renamed "Dahe National School," and took its present name, Ruipu Elementary School, in August 1952 — a name it has kept ever since. Nearly a century old, the school also spun off two branch campuses of its own: Gaorong (established 1946, independent 1953) and Ruimei (established 1966, independent 1970), making Ruipu the founding "mother school" for several elementary schools across Yangmei's Puxin area.

瑞埔國小創校於西元1928年3月，原為楊梅國小分校；1933年3月獨立設校，定名「草湳坡公學校」；後改稱「大和國校」；民國41年（1952年）8月更名為現名「瑞埔國民小學」，沿用至今，已有近百年校史。曾設高榮分校（1946年設立，1953年獨立為高榮國小）與瑞梅分校（1966年設立，1970年獨立為瑞梅國小），是楊梅埔心地區多所小學的「母校」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 The Mother School of Puxin

埔心地區的母校

Ruipu spun off both Gaorong Elementary (1953) and Ruimei Elementary (1970) as branch campuses that later became independent, making it the historic anchor of several schools in the area.

🏸 Strong in Hakka Culture and Badminton

客家文化、語文及羽球項目表現良好

Located in the heart of Puxin, Ruipu performs well in Hakka culture, language, and badminton programs, and runs a dedicated physical-education class track with regular recruitment tryouts.

🏘️ Nearly a Century in Puxin's Heart

埔心地區精華地段

Sitting in a prime location within the Puxin area, Ruipu has anchored this Yangmei neighborhood for nearly one hundred years since its 1928 founding.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The founding school of a Hakka farming community.

🌱

Built during the Japanese colonial era and still standing today, Ruipu is the founding school of Puxin's Hakka educational network, having given rise to both Gaorong and Ruimei elementary schools as the local population grew. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
