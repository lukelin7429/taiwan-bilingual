Caopu Elementary School

草埔國小

A very small, remote Paiwan mountain elementary school in Shizi Township, with only around 28 students, offering indigenous mother-tongue and information-technology education.

獅子鄉偏遠排灣族小校，全校僅約28名學生，提供本土語言與資訊科技教育。

About · 學校簡介

A tiny school in a remote mountain village.

Caopu Elementary School is a very small school: as of the 111th school year (2022–23), it had just 13 teachers, 2 staff, 6 classes, and only 28 students in total. Despite its size, the school offers indigenous mother-tongue (本土語言) education, online learning, and information-technology programs alongside its standard elementary curriculum, and operates an attached kindergarten.

草埔國小是一所規模很小的學校：截至111學年度，全校僅有13名教師、2名職員、6個班級，學生總數僅28人。儘管規模小，學校仍提供本土語言教育、線上學習與資訊科技課程，並附設幼兒園。

🏫 A Tiny School, 28 Students Strong

全校僅28名學生

As of the 2022–23 school year, Caopu Elementary had just 6 classes and 28 students total — one of the smallest schools in the region, yet still offering a full curriculum.

🗣️ Indigenous Mother-Tongue Education

本土語言教育

Alongside standard subjects, the school offers indigenous mother-tongue instruction and information-technology programs tailored to its small student body.

🏔️ A Remote Mountain Community

偏遠山區聚落

Shizi Township's Caopu (Grass Field) community is a small, remote Paiwan mountain settlement, reflecting the township's very low population density along the corridor toward the South Cross-Island Highway.
