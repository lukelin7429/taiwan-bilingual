About · 學校簡介

From a rice-paddy hamlet's branch class.

Chengzhou Elementary began in 1921 as a branch of Wugu Common School serving the Chengziliao (成子寮) settlement, becoming an independent school in 1941. The campus was historically surrounded by rice paddies on both sides of its entrance road, reflecting the farming character of the old Chengziliao hamlet — a low-lying river-plain community within Wugu near the Tamsui River tributaries.

成州國小於1921年（日治時期）設立為五股公學校成子寮分班，1941年獨立設校。校園歷史上校門兩側曾盡是稻田，反映出成子寮這個五股低窪河岸平原聚落的農業本色，該地鄰近淡水河支流水系。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A farming hamlet's landscape.

🌾

Chengzhou Elementary's name and setting come from Chengziliao, an old farming hamlet on Wugu's low-lying river plain near the Tamsui River's tributaries — a landscape once dominated by rice paddies surrounding the school itself. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
