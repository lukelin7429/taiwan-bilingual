About · 學校簡介

The root of Wugu's school system.

Wugu Elementary opened in 1921 as Taipei Prefecture Wugu Common School, making it the oldest and founding school of the entire district. Its branch classrooms at Chengziliao and Gengliao later grew into today's independent Chengzhou and Gengliao Elementary Schools — meaning three of Wugu's current schools all trace back to this single root institution. Set near Guanyin Mountain, the school embodies the district's development from a single Japanese-era rural school into today's multi-school educational network.

五股國小創立於1921年（日治時期），前身為臺北州五股公學校，是五股區歷史最悠久的創始學校。其位於成子寮與更寮的分班教室，後來分別獨立設校，成為今日的成州國小與更寮國小——換言之，五股現今三所學校皆源自這一所根源學校。學校座落於觀音山麓附近，見證了五股從單一日治鄉村學校發展為今日多校教育網絡的歷程。
