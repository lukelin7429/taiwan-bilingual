Danei Elementary School

大內國小

About · 學校簡介

A remote-area school with a Siraya heritage branch.

Danei Elementary School's history dates to 1912, when it began as the “Neizhuang” branch of Liujia Public School; it became independent as “Neizhuang Public School” on April 1, 1917, and was renamed “Danei Public School” on October 1, 1920. Classified today as a remote-area (偏遠地區) school, it operates a branch campus at Toushe (頭社分校) — the site of the famous annual Siraya “Night Sacrifice” (頭社太祖夜祭), held every year on the 14th-15th of the 10th lunar month. The Toushe area is promoted in local tourism as “Mariyang,” Siraya for “beautiful,” centered on the branch school and the adjacent Siraya communal worship hall (公廨).

大內國小創校可追溯至明治45年／大正元年（1912年），前身為六甲公學校內莊分校，大正6年（1917年）4月1日獨立設校為「內莊公學校」，大正9年（1920年）10月1日改名「大內公學校」。學校目前列為偏遠地區學校，並設有頭社分校——正是每年農曆10月14、15日舉行的西拉雅族「頭社太祖夜祭」所在地。頭社地區在地方觀光宣傳中被稱為「Mariyang」（西拉雅語意為「美麗」），以分校及鄰近的西拉雅公廨為核心。

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the heart of Danei's Siraya heritage.

🪶

Danei Elementary's Toushe branch campus sits at the heart of Danei's Siraya indigenous heritage — the Toushe Night Sacrifice is one of the most intact surviving Siraya ceremonial traditions in Taiwan, held at the public worship hall (公廨) just steps from the branch school.
