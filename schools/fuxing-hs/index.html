Fuxing Senior High School

臺北市立復興高級中學

A public arts-focused senior high founded in 1953 as the first secondary school the ROC government established in Taiwan, set at the foot of Datun Mountain.

About · 學校簡介

The first secondary school of postwar Taiwan.

Fuxing Senior High opened in 1953 as Taiwan Provincial Fuxing Middle School — the first secondary school the ROC government established in Taiwan after retreating there. It ran as a complete middle school, with both junior and senior divisions, until 1970, when the junior division was dropped and it became senior-high only. Since 1997, as part of Taipei's arts-high-school policy, the school has run Arts Talent Classes in fine art, music, dance, and drama, alongside an athletics class. It has also built sister-school ties with institutions in Japan since 1999 and South Korea since 2017, and its alumni association was founded in 2002 at the school's 50th anniversary.

復興高中創校於民國42年（1953年），初名「臺灣省立復興中學」，是中華民國政府遷台後在台灣設立的第一所中等學校。學校原為完全中學，兼辦國中部與高中部，直到民國59年（1970年）裁撤國中部，改制為純高中。自民國86年（1997年）起，配合台北市高中藝術才能班政策，開設美術、音樂、舞蹈、戲劇等藝術才能班，並設有體育班。學校自民國88年（1999年）起與日本、民國106年（2017年）起與韓國的姊妹校締結交流，校友會則於民國91年（2002年）建校50週年時成立。

⛰️ The Mountain City

Set at the foot of Datun Mountain, the school earned the old nickname "the mountain city" (山城) for its dramatic hillside setting within the Yangmingshan volcanic massif.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Set against Datun Mountain.

⛰️

Fuxing Senior High sits at the foot of Datun Mountain (大屯山), the peak that gives the school its old nickname "the mountain city" (山城). Datun Mountain is part of the Yangmingshan volcanic massif whose geothermal activity also produces Beitou's famous hot springs on the other side of the district.
