Pingding Elementary School

坪頂國民小學

A public elementary school known for its outdoor tree-climbing program and recognition under the Magnetic Stone Reading Plan.

以攀樹活動聞名的公立小學，並在「磁石閱讀計畫」中獲肯定。

About · 學校簡介

An outdoor spirit in the hills above Caotun.

Pingding Elementary School offers a 360-degree virtual campus tour for prospective families and visitors, and it has built a distinctive identity around outdoor recreation, including a tree-climbing activity program alongside after-school care. The school has also been recognized for its reading-promotion efforts under the "Magnetic Stone Reading Plan" (磁石閱讀計畫), a county-level initiative encouraging literacy.

坪頂國小提供360度線上校園導覽，方便家長與訪客認識校園環境。學校發展出獨特的戶外教育特色，設有攀樹活動課程，並提供課後照顧服務。此外，學校也因推動「磁石閱讀計畫」而在閱讀推廣方面獲得肯定。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 A Signature Tree-Climbing Program

攀樹活動特色課程

Pingding runs a distinctive tree-climbing activity program, giving students an outdoor, physically engaging complement to the standard curriculum.

📚 Recognized for Reading Promotion

磁石閱讀計畫肯定

The school has earned recognition under the county's "Magnetic Stone Reading Plan," reflecting its sustained effort to build strong reading habits among students.

🖥️ A Virtual Campus Tour

360度線上校園導覽

Pingding offers a 360-degree virtual tour of its campus, making it easy for prospective families and visitors to explore the school online before visiting in person.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Timber traditions, reaching upward.

🌲

Pingding Elementary sits in Caotun, long known as a woodworking and furniture-crafting town near Taichung, and its outdoor tree-climbing program echoes the area's deep ties to timber and forestry. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
