Baodong Elementary School

A century-old rural school in Guanmiao District that marked its 100th anniversary with a citywide alumni photo drive, deep in Tainan's bamboo-shoot country.

About · 學校簡介

A century-old school in the countryside.

Baodong Elementary School was founded in 1922, making it one of the older schools in Guanmiao District, predating the public-school reorganizations of the 1940s. The school marked its 100th anniversary with a dedicated centennial-celebration website and an alumni photo collection drive that drew coverage from the Liberty Times. Alongside its standard curriculum, Baodong runs a Hakka-language and mother-tongue promotion program.

保東國小創立於民國11年（1922年），是關廟區歷史較悠久的學校之一，早於1940年代的日治公學校改制。學校曾建立專屬的百年校慶網站，並發起校友老照片徵集活動，獲《自由時報》報導。除一般課程外，保東國小亦推動客語與母語推廣計畫。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A century in the bamboo-shoot fields.

🎋

Baodong's 2022 centennial fell squarely within the long agricultural history of Guanmiao District, where bamboo-shoot (綠竹筍) farming and Guanmiao noodle production have shaped the local economy for generations — a heritage the school's alumni photo drive helped document.
