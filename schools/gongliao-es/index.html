About · 學校簡介

The founding school of Gongliao District.

Gongliao Elementary traces back to 1906 as a branch of Toushuangxi Public School, taking its current name in 1968. It is the oldest and, historically, the largest school in the district — once enrolling around 900 students in the 1960s, though it is much smaller today. With over 120 years of history, the school has weathered significant challenges, including severe flood damage reported in 2025.

貢寮國小校史可追溯至1906年（日治時期）設立的頭圍公學校分教場，民國57年（1968年）定名為貢寮國小。該校為貢寮區歷史最悠久、規模歷史上最大的學校——1960年代學生人數一度達約900人，如今規模已大幅縮小。學校擁有逾120年歷史，2025年曾傳出嚴重淹水災情。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Over 120 Years of History

逾120年校史

Tracing back to a 1906 branch school, Gongliao Elementary is the founding school of the entire district, carrying more than a century of local educational history.

📉 From Nearly 900 Students to a Smaller School

學生人數今非昔比

The school once enrolled roughly 900 students at its peak in the 1960s; enrollment is far smaller today, tracking Gongliao's broader population decline.

🌧️ Weathering Real Challenges

面對災害挑戰

The school reported severe flood damage in 2025, a reminder of the practical challenges facing this century-old coastal-township school today.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The district's founding school.

🏘️

As the district's founding school, Gongliao Elementary has tracked the township's transformation from a bustling mining and fishing community to today's tourism-and-depopulation coastal town. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
