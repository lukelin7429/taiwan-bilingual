About · 學校簡介

A hillside school on the district's northern edge.

Zhongtai Elementary School was founded in 1956 as a branch of Tunshan Elementary School, serving families living at the foot of Datun Mountain near the boundary with Sanzhi District. Its small size and rural setting have kept it closely tied to the surrounding farming community in Tamsui's northeastern hill country.

中泰國小創立於民國45年（1956年），原為屯山國小分校，服務大屯山山麓、鄰近三芝區的居民。校園規模小巧，座落於淡水東北山區，與周邊農村社區關係緊密。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛰️ At the Foot of Datun Mountain

座落大屯山麓

The school sits in Tamsui's volcanic foothill country, part of the Datun Mountain range that defines the district's rural north.

🌾 A Small School, A Close Community

小校緊密社區

As a small mountainside school bordering Sanzhi District, Zhongtai maintains close ties to the farming families who make up its student body.

🌱 Roots in a Larger School

源自屯山分校

Zhongtai began as a branch campus of Tunshan Elementary School, part of a broader pattern of small Tamsui hill schools growing out of larger "mother schools."

Local Culture · 在地文化

In the shadow of a volcano.

🌋

Zhongtai's hillside setting places it within the Datun Volcano Group foothills that ring northern Tamsui, a landscape of dormant volcanic peaks shared with neighboring Sanzhi and Beitou. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
