About · 學校簡介

Rebuilt after disaster, rooted in tradition.

Zhongyuan Elementary School was established around 1955 and has marked a 70th-anniversary celebration in recent years. Like many schools across Nantou County, its campus was rebuilt with the assistance of the Tzu Chi Foundation following the devastating 1999 921 earthquake. Today the school maintains traditional dragon-dance and drum-troupe performance programs, keeping folk performing arts alive for a new generation of students.

中原國小創校於民國44年（1955年）左右，近年舉辦了創校七十週年慶祝活動。與南投縣許多學校一樣，校舍在1999年921大地震後於慈濟基金會的協助下重建。如今學校持續維持舞龍與鼓陣等傳統表演藝術課程，讓民俗技藝在新一代學生中傳承下去。
