About · 學校簡介

From four classes to a school that "sets sail."

Dingnei Elementary was established by government order in 1957 under founding principal Fan Kunyuan, starting with just four classes. The school grew steadily in its early decades — eight classes by 1959, twelve by 1964, and eighteen by 1968 — and celebrated its 68th anniversary in 2025. Its enduring motto, "揚帆啟碇、名聞宇內" ("set sail and become renowned"), plays on the school's own name (碇, meaning "anchor") to build a seafaring metaphor directly into its identity.

碇內國小於民國46年（1957年）奉令設校，首任校長范坤元帶領學校從僅有4個班級起步。學校在草創初期穩定成長——1959年增至8班，1964年12班，1968年達18班——並於2025年慶祝建校68週年。校訓「揚帆啟碇、名聞宇內」巧妙運用校名中的「碇」字（意指船錨），將航海意象融入學校精神。
