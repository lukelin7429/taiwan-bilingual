About · 學校簡介

Fuli's oldest school, still going strong.

Fuli Elementary was founded in 1901 during the Japanese colonial era, making it the oldest school in the Fuli area by a wide margin. The school celebrated its 120th anniversary in 2021 with a joyful, unconventional touch: the principal dressed as a character from the anime "Demon Slayer" and led an anniversary parade of students through the township. Over more than 120 years, the school has graduated over 10,000 alumni, cementing its role as the civic and educational anchor of Fuli's main village.

富里國小創立於明治34年（1901年）日治時期，是富里地區歷史最悠久的學校，遠早於其他學校。學校於民國110年（2021年）舉辦建校120週年慶祝活動，並以一個活潑有趣的方式呈現——校長裝扮成動畫《鬼滅之刃》的角色，帶領學生遊行慶祝，走遍全鄉。逾120年來，學校已培育出萬名以上校友，穩固了它作為富里主村行政與教育核心的地位。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fuli Township, rice country at the valley's edge.

🌾

Fuli Township is Hualien County's southernmost township, a rice-farming community near the Tropic of Cancer at the southern tip of the East Rift Valley. As the township's oldest school, Fuli Elementary embodies the deep-rooted civic identity of this agricultural town. See the Hualien schools page for the full township list.
