About · 學校簡介

A small school, big on results.

Huashan Junior High sits along the Provincial Highway 3 corridor in Hengshan Township, one of two small junior high schools serving the township's Neiwan-area farming and old-street communities. With only about four classes and roughly 70 students, it is a genuine "mini school" — yet it has earned certification as a Green Flag Eco-School and produced national skills-competition winners in fields including culinary services, business management, and cosmetology, alongside a curriculum that stretches to drone piloting and STEM.

華山國中座落於省道台3線沿線的橫山鄉，是服務內灣周邊農村與老街社區的兩所小型國中之一。全校僅約4班、70餘名學生，是名副其實的「迷你學校」，卻已取得「綠旗環保學校」認證，並在餐飲服務、企業管理、美容美髮等領域的全國技能競賽中屢獲佳績，課程更延伸至空拍機操作與STEM教育。

Local Culture · 在地文化

One of two small schools near Neiwan.

🛣️

Huashan Junior High sits beside the Provincial Highway 3 corridor in Hengshan Township, a rural district also home to the historic Neiwan Old Street and its old branch railway, giving the school's small farming community close ties to one of northern Taiwan's best-known tourist destinations.
