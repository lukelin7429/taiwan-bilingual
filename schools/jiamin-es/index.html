About · 學校簡介

A school whose family tree runs deep.

Jiamin Elementary traces its roots to 1934, when it opened under Japanese colonial rule as a "蕃童教育所" (banjo kyōikusho) — an education post specifically for Indigenous children. It was reorganized as Jiamin National School in 1946 after Taiwan's retrocession. As the population of Xiulin's mountain valleys grew through the mid-20th century, Jiamin spawned two branch schools that later became fully independent: Shuiyuan Elementary in 1955 and Jingmei Elementary in 1958. In its early decades, the school also took part in American-aid and World Food Programme child nutrition supplement efforts, reflecting how deeply postwar international aid touched even Taiwan's most remote schools.

佳民國小的歷史可追溯至1934年，日治時期以「蕃童教育所」之名創立，專門招收原住民學童。台灣光復後，於1946年改制為佳民國民學校。隨著秀林鄉山谷人口在二十世紀中葉持續增長，佳民國小陸續分出兩所日後獨立設校的分校：1955年的水源國小與1958年的景美國小。建校初期，學校也曾參與美援與聯合國世界糧食計畫的兒童營養補給工作，可見二戰後的國際援助也深入台灣最偏遠的校園。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A colonial-era origin, a Truku homeland.

🏔️

Jiamin's origin as a Japanese-era indigenous education post reflects Xiulin's long history as a Truku settlement area under colonial administration. Today the school continues to serve the Truku community of Jiamin Village in the township's mountain interior. See the Hualien county page for more on the region.
