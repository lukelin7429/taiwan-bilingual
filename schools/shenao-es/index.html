About · 學校簡介

From a fishing village's private tutoring school to a modern campus.

Shen'ao Elementary traces its earliest roots to a private Han-Chinese tutoring school established in the Shen'ao area in 1926, which was followed by a Japanese-language institute in 1941. The school became a branch of Xinyi National School in 1947, before being independently established on August 1, 1951. It took its current name in 1968. Due to space constraints at its original site, the school relocated to a new campus in 2009, at No. 55-1, Shen'aokeng Rd., where it remains today.

深澳國小的校史最早可追溯至1926年（大正15年）在深澳地區設立的漢文私塾，1941年（昭和16年）改為日語講習所。1947年（民國36年）成為信義國民學校分校，1951年（民國40年）8月1日正式獨立設校，1968年（民國57年）定名為深澳國小。由於原校址空間不足，學校於2009年（民國98年）遷至深澳坑路55之1號的新校區，即今日校址。

📜 Roots in a 1926 Private School

校史源自1926年私塾

Shen'ao's educational lineage stretches back to a private Han-Chinese tutoring school in 1926, nearly a quarter-century before its formal independent founding.

2009年遷至新校區

Facing space constraints at its original location, the school relocated to a new campus on Shen'aokeng Road in 2009, where it operates today.

⚓ Named for a Fishing Port

以漁港命名

The school takes its name directly from Shen'ao, one of Keelung's well-known fishing harbors, reflecting its deep roots in the local fishing community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A school born of a fishing port.

⚓

Shen'ao (深澳) is a well-known Keelung fishing port and harbor area, and Shen'ao Elementary's name and original location are directly rooted in this fishing community — a strong tie to Keelung's port and fishing culture. See the Keelung county page for more on the region.
