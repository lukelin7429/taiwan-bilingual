Lanya Elementary School

臺北市士林區蘭雅國民小學

Located in the Lanya neighborhood between old Shilin and Tianmu, home to a public artist-in-residence program and the feeder school for Lanya Junior High.

位於士林老街與天母之間的蘭雅地區，設有駐校藝術家計畫，是蘭雅國中的學區來源。

About · 學校簡介

Named for a historic neighborhood.

Lanya Elementary School is located on Huangxi Street in the Lanya neighborhood between old Shilin and Tianmu, named for the same historic "Nanya" (湳雅) place-name that gives neighboring Lanya Junior High its name — though the elementary school's own founding date could not be confirmed from available sources. The school has hosted an artist-in-residence program, with public "resident artist showcase" events reported by the Taipei city government, and feeds into the geographically adjacent Lanya Junior High School.

蘭雅國小位於磺溪街，坐落於士林老街與天母之間的蘭雅地區，校名源自與鄰近蘭雅國中相同的歷史地名「湳雅」——惟該校確切創校年份目前尚無可查證資料。學校曾舉辦駐校藝術家計畫，並多次獲臺北市政府報導其「駐校藝術家成果展」等公開活動，學區銜接地理位置相鄰的蘭雅國中。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Between Old Street and Tianmu.

🏘️

Sitting in the Lanya/Nanya area, a mid-density residential zone that grew as Shilin urbanized between its historic Old Street core and the more international Tianmu neighborhood. See the Taipei county page for more on the region.
