Minggang Elementary School

名崗國民小學

A 120-year-old village school among Mingjian's tea terraces, home to a champion recorder ensemble and a doctoral fellowship named for a notable alumnus.

座落於名間鄉茶園間的百廿年老校，設有直笛隊佳績與以知名校友命名的博士獎學金。

About · 學校簡介

Over a century in Dakeng Village.

Minggang Elementary School traces its roots to the Japanese colonial era, circa 1904-1905, and celebrated its 120th anniversary in recent years. The school awards a Chen Zailai Doctoral Fellowship, named after a notable alumnus, recognizing academic achievement in the school's tradition. Its recorder ensemble and table-tennis team are regular fixtures in county-level competitions, reflecting a long-standing balance of arts and athletics.

名崗國小的歷史可追溯至日治時期明治37、38年（約1904-1905年）左右，近年舉辦建校120週年校慶。學校設有以知名校友命名的「陳再來博士獎學金」，表彰學業成就的傳統。該校直笛隊與桌球隊經常在縣級比賽中獲獎，展現藝術與體育並重的長久傳統。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎉 120 Years in Dakeng Village

大坑村百廿年校史

Minggang Elementary traces its roots to the Japanese colonial era around 1904-1905 and celebrated its 120th anniversary, one of the longest histories among Mingjian's village schools.

🎓 A Fellowship Honoring an Alumnus

陳再來博士獎學金

The school awards a Chen Zailai Doctoral Fellowship, named after a notable alumnus, carrying forward a tradition of recognizing academic achievement.

🎵 A Champion Recorder Ensemble

直笛隊常勝軍

Minggang's recorder ensemble is a regular contender in county-level competitions, part of a strong tradition in music education.

🏓 Table Tennis Tradition

桌球隊佳績

Alongside music, the school's table-tennis team is a consistent presence in county competitions, reflecting a balanced arts-and-athletics culture.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Among the tea terraces of Dakeng.

🍵

With over 120 years of history in Dakeng Village, Minggang Elementary sits amid Mingjian's famed tea terraces, the heart of Taiwan's Songbolang tea-growing tradition. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
