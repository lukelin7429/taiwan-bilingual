Lixing Elementary School

力行國小

One of Ren'ai Township's oldest schools, founded in 1922 deep in the mountains and serving a community that is about 90% Atayal.

About · 學校簡介

Over a century deep in the mountains.

Lixing Elementary was founded in April 1922 as a "蕃童教育所" — an education institute for Aboriginal children — under Japanese rule, making it one of the oldest schools in Ren'ai Township. The school relocated from its original site at Wangyang in 1978 and today operates a branch campus, the Cuilan Branch School. As of 2019, about 90% of its students were Atayal and 10% Seediq.

力行國小創校於1922年（大正11年）4月，日治時期設立為「蕃童教育所」，是仁愛鄉歷史最悠久的學校之一。1978年（民國67年）自原望洋校址遷移至現址，並設有翠巒分校。截至2019年，全校學生約九成為泰雅族，一成為賽德克族。

What makes this school worth knowing.

📜 A Century of History

創校逾百年

Founded in April 1922 as an Aboriginal children's education institute, Lixing is one of the oldest schools anywhere in Ren'ai Township.

🏘️ Relocated in 1978

1978年自望洋遷校

The school moved from its original site at Wangyang in 1978, a relocation that shaped its present-day campus and community footprint.

🏫 A Branch School at Cuilan

Lixing operates a branch campus, the Cuilan Branch School, extending its reach to serve families living even deeper in the surrounding mountains.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Deep in Ren'ai's remote mountains.

⛰️

Deep in Ren'ai Township's remote mountains, the school has served an Atayal and Seediq community for over a century, reflecting the township's indigenous heritage near the historic Wushe area, where the 1930 Wushe Incident continues to shape regional memory.
