About · 學校簡介

Secondary education carved into the mountains.

Datong Junior High traces its origins to 1962, when it began as a borrowed-classroom branch class of Sanxing Junior High in the remote settlement of Tuchang — a reflection of how difficult it was to reach secondary education from Datong's scattered mountain villages. The school became independent in 1969, alongside Taiwan's move to nine-year compulsory education, and its campus was relocated twice more, eventually settling on its present site on "Duli Mountain" (獨立山) after a flood destroyed an earlier building site. Today it remains the only junior high school serving Datong Township, and offers Atayal Indigenous language courses as part of its curriculum.

大同國中的歷史可追溯至民國51年（1962年），最初是三星國中在偏遠的土場借用教室設立的分班，反映出大同鄉山區聚落要接受中等教育的不易。民國58年（1969年），隨著台灣推行九年國民義務教育，學校正式獨立設校，其後校址歷經兩次遷移，一次還因為洪水沖毀原訂建校地基，最終落腳於現今「獨立山」校區。如今，它仍是大同鄉唯一的一所國中，並開設泰雅族語課程與文化教學。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛰️ Refounded After a Flood

洪水後遷校重建

The school relocated twice in its early years, including after a flood destroyed the foundation of a planned campus — a story that mirrors the challenges of building schools in Datong's steep, river-cut terrain.

📖 A Branch Class in the Mountains

土場分班起家

Before it had its own campus, Datong Junior High began life as a borrowed-classroom branch of Sanxing Junior High in the remote settlement of Tuchang, showing how far secondary education once had to travel to reach these villages.

🗣️ Atayal Language in the Classroom

泰雅族語課程

The school offers Atayal Indigenous language courses and cultural curriculum, supporting students in maintaining their mother tongue alongside their regular studies.

🏔️ The Only Junior High in Datong

大同鄉唯一國中

As the township's sole junior high, the school draws students from villages scattered across Datong's mountainous terrain, making it a genuine community anchor point.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Serving an Atayal mountain community.

🏔️

Datong Township is a mountainous, entirely Atayal Indigenous community, and Datong Junior High serves as the anchor for secondary education across its dispersed villages — a role shaped by the area's historic remoteness and difficult terrain. See the Yilan county page for more on the region.
