About · 學校簡介

A school built to feel like a park.

Hongwacuo Elementary officially opened on August 1, 2004. Planning began in 2002 under then Tainan County Education Bureau director Yeh Tse-shan; the first construction phase, designed by architect Hsu Yen-chi and built by Honghua Construction, broke ground in 2003 and finished in July 2004, with a second phase completed in December 2006. The school was deliberately built as a "wall-less" (無圍牆) campus, landscaped with lawns, trees, and campus art installations so it reads more like a public park than a conventional school. It now enrolls roughly 819 students across 31 classes with about 67 teachers.

紅瓦厝國小於民國93年（2004年）8月1日正式啟用。籌設工作始於民國91年（2002年），由時任台南縣教育局長葉澤山主導；第一期工程由建築師許燕記設計、宏華營造施工，民國92年（2003年）動工，民國93年（2004年）7月完工，第二期工程則於民國95年（2006年）12月完成。學校刻意打造為「無圍牆」校園，以草坪、樹木及校園藝術裝置佈置，讓校園更像公園而非傳統學校。目前約有819名學生、31個班級及67位教師。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 A "Wall-Less" Campus

無圍牆校園

Deliberately built without a boundary wall, the campus is landscaped with lawns, trees, and art installations so it feels like an extension of the public park nearby.

🏗️ A Two-Phase Modern Build

分兩期完工

Construction ran in two phases — the first completed in July 2004 in time for opening, the second finishing in December 2006 — designed by architect Hsu Yen-chi.

🚄 A Product of the Early-2000s Boom

高鐵建設同期產物

Planned starting in 2002 and opened in 2004, the school was purpose-built during the same infrastructure wave that brought the Tainan HSR line through Guiren.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Part of Guiren's early-2000s growth wave.

🚄

One of Guiren's newest schools, Hongwacuo Elementary was purpose-built during the same wave of infrastructure investment in the early-to-mid 2000s that brought the Tainan High Speed Rail line through the district. See the Tainan county page for more on the region.
