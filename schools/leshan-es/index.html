About · 學校簡介

From a temple's classrooms to an independent school.

Leshan Elementary began as the 'Leshan branch school' (樂善分校) of Dagang Elementary — then known as 'Taoyuan County Dagang National School' — with its earliest classes held inside Leshan Temple (樂善寺). It became an independent school in 1969, taking its name from the temple where it began. The school celebrated its 50th anniversary with new campus buildings around 2018–2019, following a major campus-rebuilding groundbreaking in February 2018. It sits near the boundary between Guishan District and New Taipei's Linkou District, close to the industrial zone, Chang Gung Memorial Hospital, and National Taiwan University of Sport.

樂善國小前身為大崗國小（時稱「桃園縣大崗國民學校」）之「樂善分校」，最早的班級借用樂善寺上課，1969年正式獨立設校，並以起源地樂善寺命名。學校於2018年2月動土進行大規模校舍重建，並於2018至2019年間慶祝建校50週年暨新校舍落成。校址鄰近龜山區與新北市林口區交界，鄰近工業區、長庚紀念醫院及國立體育大學。
