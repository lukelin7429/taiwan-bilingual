About · 學校簡介

Pingtung's flagship boys'-heritage school.

National Pingtung Senior High School, affectionately known as "Pingzhong" (屏中), was founded in 1938 during the Japanese colonial era as Takao Prefectural Pingtung Middle School. It became a national (國立) school in 2000 after Taiwan's provincial government reorganization. Today it enrolls around 1,791 students in 57 classes with 126 teachers, and is well known for its athletics program, including a baseball team with multiple runner-up finishes and a basketball team that has won the high school league championship four times.

國立屏東高級中學，在地人暱稱「屏中」，創校於昭和13年（1938年）日治時期，前身為「高雄州立屏東中學校」。民國89年（2000年）精省後改制為國立高中。現有學生約1,791人，共57班，教師126人，以體育表現聞名，棒球隊屢獲亞軍，籃球隊更四度奪得高中聯賽冠軍。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚾ A Storied Baseball Program

棒球隊屢獲佳績

The school's baseball team has finished runner-up multiple times in national competition, part of a long athletic tradition at this Japanese-era institution.

🏀 A Four-Time Basketball Champion

籃球聯賽四度封王

The basketball team has won the national high school league championship four times, cementing the school's reputation for athletic excellence.

🥥 The Coconut Tree Mascot

校園吉祥物：椰子樹

The school's mascot is the coconut tree, and the rain tree is its designated school tree — familiar symbols on the historic Zhongxiao Road campus.

🏛️ From Provincial to National

精省後改制國立

Originally a Japanese-era middle school, it transitioned to national administration in 2000 following Taiwan's provincial government reorganization.
