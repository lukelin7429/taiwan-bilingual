Ruiping Junior High School

瑞坪國民中學

One of Yangmei's newer municipal junior highs, serving the growing Puxin area with an athletics program and a non-profit preschool for local families.

楊梅較晚設立的國中之一，服務都市化中的埔心地區，設有體育班與非營利幼兒園，照顧在地雙薪家庭需求。

About · 學校簡介

A newer school for a growing Puxin.

Ruiping Junior High's preparatory office was established at Renmei Junior High in 2002 as the "Ruitang Branch," gained approval to become independent in 2006 as Taoyuan County Ruiping Junior High, and enrolled its first cohort of seventh-graders in 2007. It took its current name, Taoyuan City Ruiping Junior High, in December 2015. Located in Yangmei's Puxin area, it is one of the district's more recently established junior highs, founded to serve population growth in the area.

瑞坪國中於民國91年（2002年）在仁美國中成立桃園縣立仁美國民中學瑞塘分校籌備處；民國95年（2006年）奉准獨立，定名桃園縣立瑞坪國民中學；民國96年（2007年）招收第一屆國一新生；民國104年（2015年）12月更名桃園市立瑞坪國民中學。位於楊梅埔心地區，是楊梅較晚設立的國中之一，服務埔心一帶人口成長需求。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

👶 A Non-Profit Preschool for Working Families

瑞坪非營利幼兒園

Ruiping opened a non-profit preschool in 2020 to serve childcare needs among Puxin's dual-income and Hakka families, extending the campus's role beyond secondary education.

📚 A Multi-Learning Center Since 2012

2012年設立多元學習中心

Approved to open a multi-learning center in 2012, the school offers alternative and supplementary learning pathways alongside its core academic program.

🏃 An Active Athletics Program

體育班積極招生

Ruiping runs a dedicated physical-education class track, actively recruiting and training student athletes as part of its academic offerings.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Born of Puxin's urbanization.

🏘️

Ruiping Junior High was founded amid the urbanization of the Puxin area, representing the wave of new schools established over the past two decades as Yangmei's population has grown and its old and new communities have blended together. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
