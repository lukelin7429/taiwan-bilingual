About · 學校簡介

A neighborhood anchor in central Miaoli City.

Fuxing Elementary is located in the Fuxing neighborhood at the center of Miaoli City, Miaoli County's seat of government. As a community elementary school in the urban core, it serves families living the everyday rhythms of Hakka town life in the county's administrative and commercial center. Detailed historical records for the school are limited, but it remains an active neighborhood institution within walking distance of Miaoli City's civic center.

福星國小位於苗栗縣治所在地苗栗市中心的福星里。作為市區社區型國小，服務範圍涵蓋苗栗市這個行政與商業中心裡日常生活的客家家庭。學校詳細歷史紀錄目前資料有限，但仍是苗栗市中心一所活躍的社區學校，鄰近市區行政核心地帶。
