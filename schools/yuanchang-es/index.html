About · 學校簡介

The central school of a farming township.

Yuanchang Elementary is the township's largest and most central elementary school, with a "百年校慶" (centennial celebration) referenced in school records suggesting a founding sometime around 1921–1925. Today it runs an art-talent class, fields baseball and bocce sports teams, and maintains an online teaching platform spanning grades 1 through 6 — a blend of tradition and modern classroom tools.

元長國小是元長鄉規模最大、位居核心的國小，校史紀錄中曾提及「百年校慶」，推估創校時間約在民國10至14年間（1921–1925年）。學校目前設有美術班，並組有棒球隊與滾球隊，同時建置線上教學平台，涵蓋一到六年級的教學內容，融合傳統與現代教學工具。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎨 An Art-Talent Class

美術班

Yuanchang Elementary offers a dedicated art-talent class, giving students across the township's central school a structured path to develop creative skills.

⚾ Baseball and Bocce Teams

棒球隊與滾球隊

The school fields both baseball and bocce sports teams, giving students an active, competitive outlet alongside their academics.

💻 An Online Teaching Platform

線上教學平台

A maintained online teaching platform covers all six elementary grades, supporting learning both in and outside the classroom.

🏫 The Township's Central School

元長鄉核心校

As the largest school in Yuanchang, it serves as a historic anchor point for the township's community, with roots reaching back roughly a century.
