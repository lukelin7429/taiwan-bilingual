About · 學校簡介

Over a century in a Baisha fishing village.

Houliao Elementary traces its origins to 1901, when it was founded as "Tongliang Public School" (通樑公學校) during the Japanese colonial era. It was renamed Houliao National School after WWII and took its current name in 1968. In 1946 the school had seven classes and 318 students in total, plus a branch class at Dacang Island, and it added a kindergarten the following year. With over 120 years of continuous history, it stands among the oldest schools in Penghu County.

後寮國小校史可追溯至民前11年、明治34年（1901年），日治時期創校時名為「通樑公學校」。二戰後改名為後寮國民學校，民國57年（1968年）定名為現名。民國35年（1946年）時全校共7個班級、318名學生，另設有大倉分班，隔年（1947年）增設幼兒園。校史逾120年，是澎湖縣歷史最悠久的學校之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Over 120 Years of History

逾120年校史

Founded in 1901 as Tongliang Public School, Houliao Elementary is one of the oldest continuously operating schools in Penghu County.

🏫 A School With Its Own Branch

曾設分班

In 1946 the school ran a branch class on Dacang Island in addition to its main seven classes, reflecting how Penghu's rural schools once supported scattered outlying communities.

👶 Kindergarten Since 1947

1947年增設幼兒園

A kindergarten class was added the year after WWII's end, extending the school's role to the youngest children in the village.
