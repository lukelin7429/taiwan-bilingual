About · 學校簡介

From branch division to its own campus.

Feisha Junior High originated in 1968 as a branch of Sihu Junior High, before gaining independent status in 1972. The school is located in Feisha Village, one of Sihu Township's coastal communities, giving students on that side of the township a junior high closer to home rather than a long trip inland.

飛沙國中最初於民國57年（1968年）作為四湖國中的分部設立，於民國61年（1972年）獨立設校。學校位於四湖鄉沿海的飛沙村，讓沿海一帶的學生不必長途跋涉至內陸就讀國中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌱 Independence After Four Years

四年後獨立設校

Feisha began in 1968 as a branch of Sihu Junior High and became a fully independent school in 1972, reflecting the coastal area's growing student population.

🏖️ A Coastal Village Campus

飛沙村沿海校園

Located in Feisha Village, the school gives students from Sihu's coastal communities a junior high closer to home.

🎣 Serving a Fishing Community

服務沿海漁村

As a school on Sihu's coastal edge, Feisha Junior High draws students whose families are tied to the township's fishing traditions.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Flying Sand Village on the coast.

🏖️

Feisha ("Flying Sand") Village sits on Sihu's coastal edge, and the school's community reflects the fishing side of the township's rice-garlic-and-coast economy. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
