National Huanan Commercial Vocational Senior High School

國立華南高級商業職業學校

Founded in 1918 and later renamed in honor of alumnus and Hua Nan Bank chairman Liu Chi-guang's donation to education, one of Chiayi's oldest commercial schools.

創校於1918年，為紀念校友暨華南銀行董事長劉啟光先生捐資興學而更名，是嘉義歷史最悠久的商業學校之一。

About · 學校簡介

A school named for a benefactor's gift.

National Huanan Commercial Vocational Senior High School was founded in April 1918, splitting off from Chiayi Public School as Chiayi Elementary Commercial School. In 1950, it was renamed "Huanan" in honor of alumnus Liu Chi-guang, chairman of Hua Nan Bank, following his donation to the school. It became a national school in 2000, and in 2017 reintroduced a general-studies curriculum, one of only a few schools in Chiayi besides the two major public high schools to offer this option.

華南高商創立於1918年（民國7年）4月，自嘉義公學校分出，初名嘉義公商業學校。民國39年（1950年），為感念校友、華南銀行董事長劉啟光先生捐資興學，更名為「華南」。民國89年（2000年）改隸國立。民國106年（2017年），該校恢復設置普通科，是嘉義除兩所主要公立高中外少數設有普通科的學校之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Over a Century of History

創校逾百年

Founded in 1918 as Chiayi Elementary Commercial School, the school has served the Chiayi community for well over a hundred years across several names and eras.

🙏 Named for a Benefactor

紀念劉啟光校友捐資

Renamed "Huanan" in 1950 to honor alumnus Liu Chi-guang, chairman of Hua Nan Bank, whose donation to education shaped the school's modern identity.

📖 A Return to General Studies

106年恢復普通科

In 2017, the school reintroduced a comprehensive general-studies curriculum, an option offered by only a few schools in Chiayi besides its two major public high schools.

🤝 International and University Ties

與國立嘉義大學及泰國姊妹校合作

The school maintains partnerships with National Chiayi University as well as sister-school ties with institutions in Thailand.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A legacy of local philanthropy.

🏦

The school's name honors Liu Chi-guang (劉啟光), an alumnus who became chairman of Hua Nan Bank and gave back to his alma mater — a story of local philanthropy that still shapes the school's identity today. See the Chiayi City page for more on the region.
