About · 學校簡介

The centennial school at the township's heart.

Zhuqi Elementary is the township's namesake central school, with a centennial history — the exact founding date is not confirmed, but the school has celebrated its "百年校慶" (centennial celebration), placing its origins around 1924-1925 during the Japanese colonial era. As the anchor school of Zhuqi, the historic gateway to Alishan, it runs an English Village program and an art-talent special class alongside a children's orchestra that earned "special excellence" honors at the 114th academic year National Student Music Competition.

竹崎國小是竹崎鄉的核心代表學校，具有百年歷史——確切創校年份尚待考證，但學校已舉辦「百年校慶」，推估創立於日治時期大正13、14年（民國13、14年，約1924、1925年）左右。作為阿里山門戶竹崎鄉的核心學校，學校經營英語村課程與美術藝才班，兒童管弦樂團更於114學年度全國學生音樂比賽榮獲特優。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎻 A National-Champion Orchestra

全國學生音樂比賽特優

The school's children's orchestra achieved "special excellence" (特優) at the 114th academic year National Student Music Competition — a top honor among elementary schools nationwide.

🌍 Its Own English Village

英語村

Zhuqi Elementary runs an "English Village" program, giving students immersive English practice alongside an art-talent special class.

🏅 A Leadership Team Award

領航團隊獎

The school received a "Leadership/Pilot Team Award" recognizing its learning-support initiatives for students who need extra help.

🥁 Dance and Percussion

舞蹈社與打擊樂團

Beyond its orchestra, the school supports dance and percussion ensemble programs, giving students multiple performing-arts outlets.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring the gateway to Alishan.

🚂

As the township's namesake central school, Zhuqi Elementary anchors the community that historically supported the starting terminus of the Alishan Forest Railway — the mountain gateway that gives the whole township its identity. See the Chiayi county page for more on the region.
