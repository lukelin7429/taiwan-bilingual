About · 學校簡介

A village school with a historic dormitory.

Raoping Elementary traces its founding to around 1945, the year of Taiwan's retrocession, and has grown into a school recognized for its holistic approach to student wellbeing. It carries a "Health Promotion School" designation and runs an "Ecological Organic Campus" program, while one of its most distinctive features is an old dormitory building on campus that has been registered as a historic building.

饒平國小創校可追溯至約民國34年（1945年）台灣光復之際，多年來發展成為重視學生全人健康的學校。學校獲頒「健康促進學校」認證，並推動「有機生態校園」計畫，而校內最具特色之處，是一棟已登錄為歷史建築的老舊宿舍。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ A Registered Historic Dormitory

縣定歷史建築宿舍

The school's old dormitory building has been officially registered as a historic building, preserving a physical piece of its decades-long history on campus.

💚 A Certified Health Promotion School

健康促進學校

Raoping holds a "Health Promotion School" designation, reflecting a sustained institutional focus on student physical and mental wellbeing.

🌱 An Ecological Organic Campus

有機生態校園

The school runs an "Ecological Organic Campus" program, giving students hands-on exposure to sustainable, organic approaches to the land.

History and harvest in Raoping Village.
