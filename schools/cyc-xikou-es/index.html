About · 學校簡介

Carrying on a century of memories.

Xikou Elementary was founded on August 6, 1902, during the late Qing-to-Japanese colonial transition, making it more than 120 years old — the oldest school in the township. Its site tagline, "carrying on a century of memories," reflects that long history. The school's signature curriculum, "the Magic Book Program," is built around rice cultivation and organic farming, and links its content to the local Beiji Temple and its traditional architecture. Students take part actively in reading promotion, calligraphy, folk sports, and Mother Language Day broadcasting competitions, under current principal Fang Cheng-yi, who took office in August 2024.

溪口國小創立於民國前10年（1902年）8月6日，橫跨清末與日治轉換時期，至今已逾120年，是溪口鄉歷史最悠久的學校，網站標語為「傳承百年的時光回憶」。學校特色課程「i玩魔法書」以稻作與有機農業為主軸，並將課程內容與在地北極殿及其傳統建築相連結。學生積極參與閱讀推廣、書法、民俗體育及母語日創意廣播等競賽，現任校長方正一於民國113年（2024年）8月上任。
