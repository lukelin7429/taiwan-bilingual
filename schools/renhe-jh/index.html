Renhe Junior High School

仁和國中

Settled on its name only after two earlier proposals were rejected, on the west bank of the Dahan River near Daxi's historic riverside trades.

About · 學校簡介

Named after two rejected alternatives.

Renhe Junior High opened for the 1971 school year under founding principal Zhu Guozhang, with five classes and 239 students. The name went through two rejected proposals first — “Yuanshulin Junior High” and then “Ren'ai Junior High” (the latter turned down for duplicating other schools' names nationwide) — before “Renhe Junior High” was finally settled on. While its own campus was under construction, the school initially borrowed five classrooms from Daxi Junior High.

仁和國中於1971年（民國60學年度）開學，首任校長朱國璋，創校時僅5班、239名學生。校名原先提議「員樹林國中」，後改提「仁愛國中」，因與全國其他學校重名而遭駁回，最終才定名為「仁和國中」。創校初期校舍尚在興建，曾借用大溪國中五間教室上課。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Named after two rejected alternatives.

🌊

Located on the west bank of the Dahan River (大漢溪), Renhe Junior High sits along the same waterway whose old river port once fed Daxi Old Street's historic tofu, soy-sauce, and wood-furniture trades — industries that grew up around the river's shipping routes generations ago.
