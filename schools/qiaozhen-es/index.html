Qiaozhen Elementary School

僑真國小

A public elementary school in Dounan with competitive badminton, track and field, and shooting teams, plus an award-winning children's orchestra.

位於斗南鎮的公立國小，設有羽球、田徑、射擊等競技隊伍，兒童管弦樂團屢獲全國賽事佳績。

About · 學校簡介

A school built on sport and music.

Qiaozhen Elementary School was founded in 1961 in Dounan Town, the county's key rice and vegetable transshipment market town, with community roots tracing to local farming and produce-trading families. The school maintains competitive badminton, track and field, and air rifle/pistol shooting teams, and its children's orchestra has won multiple national competition awards.

僑真國小創校於民國50年（1961年），位於雲林縣重要的稻米與蔬菜轉運集散市鎮斗南鎮，社區根源與當地務農及果菜交易家庭密切相連。學校設有羽球、田徑、氣步槍/氣手槍射擊等競技隊伍，兒童管弦樂團更曾多次獲得全國賽事獎項。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏸 Competitive Badminton and Track

羽球與田徑隊

Qiaozhen fields competitive badminton and track and field teams, giving students a strong pathway into sport beyond the regular PE curriculum.

🎯 Air Rifle and Pistol Shooting

氣步槍/氣手槍射擊隊

The school also maintains an air rifle and pistol shooting team, a less common but well-established competitive program among Yunlin's elementary schools.

🎻 An Award-Winning Children's Orchestra

獲獎兒童管弦樂團

Qiaozhen's children's orchestra has won multiple national competition awards, reflecting a strong music program alongside its athletics.
