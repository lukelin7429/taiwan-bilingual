About · 學校簡介

A school created by Yingge's tourism revival.

Changfu Elementary was founded in 1999 in direct response to a wave of change transforming Yingge: the opening of the North Second Expressway, the redevelopment of the historic Old Pottery Street, and the establishment of the New Taipei City Yingge Ceramics Museum. The school has built a strong record in art competitions and coding literacy, reflecting both Yingge's creative-craft identity and a modern emphasis on digital skills.

昌福國小創立於民國88年（1999年），是為了因應北二高通車、老街改造，以及新北市立鶯歌陶瓷博物館成立等一連串變化而設校。學校在美術競賽與程式設計素養方面表現亮眼，同時呼應鶯歌的創意工藝傳統與現代數位能力的培養。
