About · 學校簡介

A century of names, an unbroken lineage.

Dongshi Elementary began in April 1938 as a satellite classroom of Nanshi Public School — itself the district's oldest school — before becoming independent on April 1, 1943, as "Dongshi National School." Over the following decades the school's name changed repeatedly to track Taiwan's political transitions: it became "Hsinchu County Pingzhen Township Dongshi National School" in 1945 after Japan's surrender, was folded into Taoyuan County in 1950, renamed "Dongshi Elementary School" in 1968, "Pingzhen City Dongshi Elementary School" in 1992, and took its current municipal name in 2014. The school's history is tracked in detail on a dedicated Facebook page marking its 70th-anniversary milestones.

東勢國小創立於1938年4月，最初是南勢公學校（平鎮區歷史最悠久的學校）的分教場，1943年4月1日獨立設校，定名「東勢國民學校」。此後數十年間，校名隨台灣政治變遷數度更改：1945年日本戰敗後改為「新竹縣平鎮鄉東勢國民學校」，1950年劃入桃園縣，1968年更名「東勢國民小學」，1992年改稱「平鎮市東勢國民小學」，2014年桃園升格直轄市後定名為現今校名。學校的校史沿革詳細記載於慶祝建校70週年的專屬臉書粉絲頁。
