About · 學校簡介

Nearly half a century of vocational strength.

Jinghua Junior High School serves Xinfeng Township, Hsinchu County, and recently celebrated its 49th anniversary, placing its founding in the mid-to-late 1970s. The school has built a strong reputation for vocational and technical education, offering programs in food production, hospitality, and design. In 2025, its students placed first through fourth in county-level skills competitions across categories including industrial electronics, machinery, design, and culinary arts — a standout showing for a township junior high.

精華國中位於新竹縣新豐鄉，近年舉辦建校49週年慶祝活動，推估創校年代約在1970年代中後期。學校在職業與技術教育方面表現突出，開設餐飲、觀光及設計等課程。民國114年（2025年），學生在縣級技藝競賽中，於工業電子、機械、設計、餐飲等項目囊括前四名——對一所鄉鎮國中而言相當亮眼的成績。
