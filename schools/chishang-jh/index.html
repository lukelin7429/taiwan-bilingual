Chishang Junior High School

About · 學校簡介

A small school with a technical edge.

Chishang Junior High serves the students of Chishang Township, a rural community in Taitung's East Rift Valley best known for its world-famous rice and its scenic "Mr. Brown Avenue" — a rice-paddy road made famous by television advertising. Records suggest the school marked a 60th-anniversary reference around 2025–2026, placing its founding in the mid-1960s, though the exact year remains unconfirmed. As of 2023, the school had approximately 140 students and 26 teachers, and it operates a "Smart Control Education Training Base," a technical education facility that stands out for a school of its size.

池上國中服務臺東縱谷池上鄉的學生，這個鄉間社區以享譽全球的池上米與知名的「伯朗大道」——因電視廣告而聲名大噪的稻田景觀道路——聞名。根據記載，學校曾於2025至2026年間提及60週年紀念，推算創校時間約在民國50年代中期，確切年份尚待考證。截至2023年，學校約有140名學生與26位教師，並設有「智慧控制教育訓練基地」，這項技術教育設施對於一所規模不大的國中而言相當突出。
