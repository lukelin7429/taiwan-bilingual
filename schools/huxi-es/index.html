About · 學校簡介

The mother school of Huxi Township.

Huxi Elementary was founded in 1903 as "Huxi Public School," renamed "Huxi No. 1 Public School" in 1921, "Huxi National School" in 1941, "Huxi National Elementary School" in 1946, and its current name in 1968. As the oldest and central school of the township, nearly all other schools in Huxi were originally founded as its branches. Its original schoolhouse was destroyed by a typhoon in 1912, but the community came together to rebuild it, completing the work by 1913. The school published a special 100th-anniversary commemorative volume, "湖西百歲" ("A Century of Huxi"), and its school district covers seven villages.

湖西國小創立於明治36年（1903年），初名「湖西公學校」，民國10年（1921年）改名「湖西第一公學校」，民國30年（1941年）改為「湖西國民學校」，民國35年（1946年）改為「湖西國民學校」，民國57年（1968年）定名為現名。作為湖西鄉歷史最悠久的核心學校，鄉內幾乎所有其他學校最初皆為其分校。原校舍於民國元年（1912年）遭颱風摧毀，社區同心協力於隔年（1913年）完成重建。學校曾出版百年校慶紀念專書《湖西百歲》，學區涵蓋七個村里。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌳 The Mother School of the Township

湖西鄉的核心母校

Nearly every other elementary school in Huxi Township was originally founded as a branch of this school, making it the historical center of local education.

🌪️ Rebuilt After a 1912 Typhoon

1912年颱風後社區重建

When a typhoon destroyed the original schoolhouse in 1912, the community came together to rebuild it within a year — a testament to local resilience.

📖 A Commemorative Centennial Volume

出版百年校慶紀念專書

The school published "湖西百歲" ("A Century of Huxi"), a special volume marking its 100th anniversary and preserving its long local history.
