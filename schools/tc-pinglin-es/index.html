About · 學校簡介

From a temple hall to a school of its own.

Pinglin Elementary School traces its beginnings to 1952, when classes were first held by borrowing space in the Pinglin Village assembly hall — a site now occupied by Yulin Temple. It operated as a branch of Taiping Elementary School before becoming independent in 1965. The school takes its name from Pinglin Village, one of Taiping District's outlying farming communities that was gradually absorbed into the area's expanding school district system.

坪林國小的歷史始於民國41年（1952年），當時借用坪林里活動中心（今已為育林宮所在地）上課，最初是太平國小的分校，於民國54年（1965年）正式獨立設校。校名取自坪林里，是太平區逐漸納入學區體系的外圍農村社區之一。
