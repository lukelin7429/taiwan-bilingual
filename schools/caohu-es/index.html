Caohu Elementary School

草湖國民小學

A public elementary school in Fangyuan Township whose roots reach back nearly a century to a private academy founded by local families in 1931.

位於芳苑鄉的公立國小，其歷史可追溯至1931年由地方家族創立的私塾，近百年辦學傳統。

About · 學校簡介

From a family-founded academy to a public elementary school.

Caohu Elementary traces its origin to April 1, 1931, when local residents Chen Jianzong, Chen Jianshang, Chen Yi, and Chen Ya founded "Caohu Private Academy" (草湖私古軒), teaching both Chinese and Japanese. The site became a Japanese-era government language institute in 1941 and a branch school in 1943, before becoming an independent public school in 1941 under principal Chen Lieh. In 1946 the school relocated to take over a former Japanese immigrant-village school site, opening with 233 students across five classes, and was formally established as Changhua County Fangyuan Township Caohu Elementary School in 1945.

草湖國小的歷史可追溯至民國20年（1931年）4月1日，由地方居民陳建宗、陳建賞、陳義、陳雅共同創辦「草湖私古軒」，教授漢文與日文。該址於1941年成為日治時期的公學校，1943年設為分校，並於1941年在校長陳烈的帶領下獨立設校。民國35年（1946年），學校遷至一處原日本移民村學校舊址，開校時有233名學生、五個班級。學校於民國34年（1945年）正式定名為「彰化縣芳苑鄉草湖國民小學」。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📜 Nearly a Century of History

近百年辦學歷史

The school's roots stretch back to 1931, when four local Chen-family residents founded a private academy — making Caohu one of the oldest educational institutions in Fangyuan Township.

🏘️ A Home on Historic Ground

日本移民村舊址重生

After relocating in 1946, the school took over the site of a former Japanese immigrant-village school, opening with 233 students — a layered history connecting colonial-era and postwar Taiwan.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Founded by Local Families

陳氏家族創校精神

The academy that became today's school was founded directly by community members — Chen Jianzong, Chen Jianshang, Chen Yi, and Chen Ya — reflecting grassroots local investment in education.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A layered history, from private academy to public school.

🏯

Caohu Elementary's founding by four local Chen-family community leaders in 1931, and its postwar relocation onto a former Japanese immigrant-village school site, tie it deeply to the layered colonial-to-postwar history of the Caohu area. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
