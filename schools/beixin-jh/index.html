Beixin Junior High School

北新國中

A Beitun junior high school built on a former private-school campus, and the only public junior high in Taichung City with its own swimming pool.

前身為私立中學校地的北屯國中，也是全台中市唯一擁有游泳池的公立國中。

About · 學校簡介

From a private campus to a public landmark.

Beixin Junior High School opened in 1965 on the former campus of the private Zhicheng Middle School (志成中學). It became an independent public school the following year under the name "Sixth Junior High School" (第六國中), before taking its current name, Beixin, in 1971. With around 1,488 students today, the school is the only public junior high in Taichung City to have its own swimming pool, built in 1981, and has run special-education classes since 1986 alongside a skills-training track and a gifted-education resource program.

北新國中於民國54年（1965年）在原私立志成中學校地上創校。隔年獨立設校，初名「臺中市立第六國民中學」，民國60年（1971年）改為現名「北新國中」。目前全校約有1,488名學生，是全台中市唯一擁有專屬游泳池的公立國中（泳池建於民國70年／1981年），並自民國75年（1986年）起開設特殊教育班，另設有技藝訓練班與資優資源班。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏊 Taichung's Only Public JH Pool

全市唯一泳池的公立國中

Built in 1981, the school's swimming pool remains unique among Taichung City's public junior high schools, giving students a facility most peer schools lack.

🌟 Four Decades of Inclusive Education

特殊教育與資優教育並行

Since 1986 the school has run special-education classes, and it now also operates a gifted-education resource program alongside a skills-training track for students.

🏛️ A Campus With a Layered History

校地歷經多次轉變

The campus began as the private Zhicheng Middle School before becoming a public school in 1965 — renamed twice before settling on Beixin in 1971.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Growing alongside Beitun.

🏊

The school's swimming pool and vocational skills-training track reflect Beitun District's mid-20th-century growth into a denser residential area that needed fuller public-school infrastructure. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
