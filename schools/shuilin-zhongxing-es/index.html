Zhongxing Elementary School

中興國民小學

A Qiongpu Village elementary school known for its kapok-lined entrance and its dedication to keeping the traditional spinning-top folk game alive.

座落於瓊埔村的小學，以校門口的木棉樹與陀螺民俗技藝的傳承聞名。

About · 學校簡介

A village school known for its trees and its top-spinning.

Zhongxing Elementary began on January 2, 1963, as the "Niaosong Elementary School Xiangpu Branch," becoming independent in 1966 and taking its current name in 1977. The school is locally known for the row of kapok and tallow trees flanking its entrance, and for its long-standing effort to keep the traditional spinning-top (陀螺) folk game alive among its students.

中興國小前身為民國52年（1963年）1月2日成立的「蔦松國小瓊埔分班」，民國55年（1966年）獨立設校，民國66年（1977年）更為現名。學校以校門兩側的木棉與烏桕樹聞名地方，並長期推廣傳統陀螺民俗技藝，讓這項技藝在學生間傳承下去。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌀 Keeping the Spinning-Top Tradition Alive

陀螺民俗技藝傳承

The school actively promotes the traditional spinning-top (陀螺) folk toy among students, keeping a piece of local folk culture alive for a new generation.

🌳 A Tree-Lined Entrance

木棉與烏桕的校門

Zhongxing is known locally for the kapok and tallow trees that flank its school entrance, a distinctive and long-standing landmark for the campus.

🌱 From Branch Class to Independent School

從分班到獨立設校

What started as a 1963 branch of Niaosong Elementary became an independent school in 1966, later taking its current name in 1977.

🍠 A Home in Sweet Potato Fields

地瓜田中的校園

Set in Qiongpu Village amid Shuilin's sweet potato farmland, the school's students grow up surrounded by the crop that has made the township nationally famous.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Folk games amid the sweet potato fields.

🍠

Set in Qiongpu Village amid Shuilin Township's sweet potato fields, the school preserves local folk culture through its spinning-top (陀螺) tradition, passed down in the surrounding farming community — a living link between the classroom and the customs of the region's agricultural households.
