Guoguang Elementary School

新北市板橋區國光國民小學

About · 學校簡介

Born from Banqiao's population boom.

Guoguang Elementary opened on 25 August 1978, splitting off from the century-old Banqiao Elementary School as the district's population swelled and a new neighborhood school became necessary. Today it runs an attached kindergarten alongside its primary classes and serves the families of central Banqiao.

國光國小創校於民國67年（1978年）8月25日，由歷史悠久的板橋國小分設而出，因應板橋人口快速成長、學區亟需新設學校而誕生。現設有附設幼兒園，服務板橋市中心一帶的學童與家庭。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥋 A Martial-Arts Tradition

國術跆拳傳統

The school fields a 國術 (martial arts) and taekwondo team that has picked up recent competition wins, giving Guoguang a distinct athletic identity among Banqiao's elementary schools.

🗣️ Bilingual by Design

雙語教學設計

Guoguang pairs Taiwanese mother-tongue instruction with English bilingual classes, reflecting the layered language education found across New Taipei's public schools.

🧒 A School With Its Own Kindergarten

附設幼兒園

An attached kindergarten lets Guoguang serve neighborhood families from early childhood straight through sixth grade.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Banqiao's growth, one new school at a time.

🏙️

Guoguang's 1978 founding traces directly to Banqiao's rapid mid-20th-century shift from a satellite town on Taipei's edge into a dense residential district — a period when the original 枋橋 (Banqiao) core split off school after school to keep pace with new neighborhoods.
