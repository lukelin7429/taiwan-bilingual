Longyuan Elementary School

龍源國民小學

One of Longtan's smallest schools, a hillside campus of citrus orchards, mosaic-decorated gates, and fewer than 100 students.

龍潭規模最小的學校之一，座落於柑橘果園環繞的山坡校地，學生人數不到百人，校門飾有馬賽克拼貼藝術。

About · 學校簡介

A small hillside school amid citrus orchards.

Longyuan Elementary was founded in 1955 on a small hillside campus of about 1.74 hectares. Its current enrollment is under 100 students across 6 classes, making it one of the smallest schools in the district. The school has a distinctive vertically-oriented playground layout — unusual compared to the typical horizontal school-yard design — enclosed by surrounding buildings. Its gate features red-brick pillars with white walls and two decorative columns topped with planetary sculptures of Earth and Venus, and the entrance is decorated with student-made ceramic and mosaic artwork. The campus is surrounded by citrus and pomelo orchards, and features a bronze statue of Sun Yat-sen.

龍源國小創立於民國44年（1955年），校地約1.74公頃，座落於山坡上。目前全校學生不到百人，共6班，是區內規模最小的學校之一。校內操場採取罕見的縱向配置，與一般橫向操場設計不同，四周由校舍環繞。校門為紅磚柱搭配白牆，兩側裝飾柱頂端分別為地球與金星造型雕塑，入口處則以師生自製的陶瓷與馬賽克拼貼藝術裝飾。校園周邊環繞著柑橘與柚子果園，並設有一座國父孫中山銅像。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A dragon-named school amid citrus orchards.

🍊

Longyuan Elementary is one of the three original "dragon-named" Longtan elementary schools, alongside Longtan Elementary and Longxing Elementary; its small hillside site amid citrus orchards reflects the rural highland-farming character of Longtan outside the town center.
