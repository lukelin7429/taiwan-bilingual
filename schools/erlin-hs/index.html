Erlin Senior High School

二林高級中學

A public secondary school that grew from a small junior high into a full senior-high campus, serving as an educational anchor for the Erlin community for over six decades.

從一所小型初中成長為完整高中校區的公立學校，六十餘年來一直是二林地區的教育重鎮。

About · 學校簡介

From a small junior high to a full senior-high campus.

Erlin Senior High School was established in 1961 as Changhua County Erlin Junior High School. In 1969 it was reorganized under the national nine-year compulsory education system as Erlin Junior High School, and then in 2002–2003 it was upgraded to add senior-high divisions, becoming the comprehensive secondary school it is today. Over the decades the campus grew from about two hectares to roughly 4.5 hectares, and the school has marked milestones including its 65th anniversary.

二林高中創校於民國50年（1961年），最初名為「彰化縣立二林初級中學」。民國58年（1969年）隨九年國教改制為「彰化縣立二林國民中學」，並於民國91至92年間（2002–2003年）增設高中部，成為今日兼具國中、高中的完全中學。校地也從最初約兩公頃逐步擴展至約4.5公頃，並曾舉辦建校65週年等重要慶祝活動。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A School That Grew Up

從初中到高中的成長歷程

Founded in 1961 as a junior high, the school was upgraded to senior-high status in the early 2000s — a decades-long evolution that reflects the growing educational needs of the Erlin area.

🌳 A Campus More Than Doubled

校地擴展逾兩倍

The school's grounds expanded from an original two hectares to about 4.5 hectares as it grew, giving today's students a much larger campus than the school's founders first built on.

🎉 65 Years and Counting

六十五週年校慶

The school has celebrated major anniversaries, including its 65th, with community fair-style events ("園遊會") that draw strong local participation and pride from Erlin residents.

Local Culture · 在地文化

An anniversary the whole town turns out for.

🎪

The school's anniversary celebrations take the form of community-style fairs (園遊會) rather than purely internal events — a tradition that draws strong participation and pride from families across Erlin Township. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
