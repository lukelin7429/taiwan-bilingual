About · 學校簡介

Nearly a century of continuous schooling.

Puyan Elementary's roots trace back to a Japanese-language promotion society formed in 1921 and a 1922 branch campus of Haoxiu Public School. The school became fully independent in 1928 under its first principal, Shinohasui Kouji, and took its current name, Puyan Elementary School, in 1957 with the introduction of Taiwan's nine-year compulsory education system. Located along the provincial Zhangshui Road on the eastern side of Puyan Township, the school is now on its 13th principal since August 2024, reflecting nearly a century of continuous operation.

埔鹽國小的歷史可追溯至民國10年（1921年）成立的日語推廣會，以及民國11年（1922年）設立的好修公學校分校。學校於民國17年（1928年）在首任校長篠橋鑛治的帶領下正式獨立設校，並於民國46年（1957年）九年國教實施之際定名為埔鹽國民小學。校址位於埔鹽鄉東側的省道彰水路上，現任校長為第13任校長（自民國113年8月起），見證了學校近百年來的持續辦學歷程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗣️ Born From a Language Society

源自語言推廣會

The school's earliest roots go back to a 1921 Japanese-language promotion society, an unusual origin story that predates its formal branch-campus status by a year.

🏫 Independent Since 1928

1928年獨立設校

Under founding principal Shinohasui Kouji, Puyan Elementary became a fully independent school in 1928, after several years as a branch of neighboring Haoxiu Public School.

👥 Thirteen Principals, One School

現任第13任校長

The school is currently led by its 13th principal, appointed in August 2024 — a marker of nearly a century of steady, continuous leadership and operation.

🛣️ On the Provincial Road

彰水路上的校園

Located on the eastern side of Puyan Township along provincial Zhangshui Road, the school sits at a historic crossroads once tied closely to neighboring Haoxiu village.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Puyan Township's rural heart.

🏘️

Puyan Elementary has long been tied to the village of Haoxiu (好修), from which it originally branched, and remains one of the anchor schools of Puyan Township — a quiet farming community in central Changhua County. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
