About · 學校簡介

Rooted in one of Pingzhen's oldest Hakka villages.

Pingnan Junior High was established in 1979 as Taoyuan County Pingnan Junior High School, serving the Nanshi (南勢) area — one of Pingzhen's earliest-settled Hakka communities and the namesake of the district's oldest elementary school. An attached supplementary night-school division was added in 1988, and the school was renamed a municipal institution in 2014 following Taoyuan County's upgrade to special-municipality status. By 2016 enrollment had grown to roughly 1,383 students across 48 classes. The school operates under the motto "恭勤實健" — Respectful, Diligent, Honest, Healthy.

平南國中創立於1979年，初名「桃園縣立平南國民中學」，校址位於南勢地區——平鎮區最早開發的客家聚落之一，也是區內歷史最悠久的南勢國小的所在地。1988年增設附設補校，2014年桃園縣升格直轄市後，本校改制為市立學校。截至2016年，全校學生約1,383人，共48班（七年級15班、八年級15班、九年級17班）。校訓為「恭勤實健」。
