Keelung Municipal Badou Senior High School

市立八斗高級中學

A complete junior-and-senior-high school rooted in the Badouzi fishing harbor community, known for its gifted physical education program.

About · 學校簡介

A fishing-village school that grew into a complete high school.

Badou Senior High School began in 1977 as a junior high preparatory office set up to serve students in the Badouzi fishing harbor area. It was renamed several times in its early years — first Badei Junior High in 1982, then Badou Junior High in 1983 — before a senior high division was added in 2011, transforming it into a "complete school" spanning both junior and senior high. The school has run a gifted physical education program since 1996 and a science experimental class since 2000.

八斗高中創立於民國66年（1977年），最初是為服務八斗子漁港地區學生而設立的國中籌備處。校名幾經更迭，民國71年（1982年）改稱「八德國中」，民國72年（1983年）再改為「八斗國中」，直到民國100年（2011年）增設高中部，轉型為橫跨國中與高中的完全中學。學校自民國85年（1996年）起開辦體育資優班，並自民國89年（2000年）起設有科學實驗班。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏅 A Gifted PE Program Since 1996

體育資優班

The school has run a dedicated gifted physical education program since 1996, one of its longest-standing academic tracks.

🔬 A Science Experimental Class

科學實驗班

Since 2000, the school has also offered a science experimental class, broadening its academic offerings beyond athletics.

🏫 From Junior High to Complete School

2011年增設高中部

Originally a junior high founded in 1977, the school added a senior high division in 2011, becoming a complete school spanning grades 7 through 12.
