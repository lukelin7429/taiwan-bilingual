Chang Gung Elementary School

長庚國小

Named for and situated within the Chang Gung Medical Village, this school is a direct product of the Chang Gung Memorial Hospital and Chang Gung University complex anchoring Guishan's north side.

以長庚醫護新村為名並座落其中，是長庚紀念醫院與長庚大學醫療園區的直接產物。

About · 學校簡介

Built inside a hospital's staff village.

Chang Gung Elementary was approved as a Leshan Elementary branch school on August 1, 2002, and formally named 'Taoyuan County Guishan Chang Gung Elementary School' the following month. It became fully independent and began enrollment on August 1, 2004, with Chung Yueh-ching as its first principal. Its students were initially housed temporarily at Wenxin Elementary School before moving into their own completed campus in January 2005; a phase-2 building followed in 2007, and a phase-3 building — dedicated in 2016 and praised by the Taoyuan mayor as a green-building design model — rounded out the campus.

長庚國小於2002年8月1日獲核准設立為樂善國小分校，同年9月正式定名為「桃園縣龜山鄉長庚國民小學」。2004年8月1日正式獨立設校並開始招生，首任校長為鍾月靜。師生原暫居文欣國小，2005年1月遷入自有校舍；二期校舍於2007年完工，三期新校舍於2016年落成啟用，並獲桃園市長譽為綠建築示範學校。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏥 Named for the Chang Gung Medical Village

以長庚醫護新村為名

Chang Gung Elementary is named directly after — and sits within — the Chang Gung Medical Village staff-housing community, tying it to the hospital complex next door.

🏗️ A Campus Built in Three Phases

分三期興建校舍

The campus grew in stages: phase-1 buildings were completed in 2004–2005, phase-2 in 2007, and a phase-3 building in 2016, praised by the Taoyuan mayor as a green-building design model.

🤝 A Temporary Start at Wenxin Elementary

曾借址文欣國小

Before its own campus was ready, Chang Gung's first students were temporarily housed at nearby Wenxin Elementary School, moving in January 2005.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A school born from Chang Gung's medical hub.

🏥

Named directly after and situated within the Chang Gung Medical Village (長庚醫護新村) staff-housing community, Chang Gung Elementary is a direct product of the Chang Gung Memorial Hospital and Chang Gung University medical-education complex that anchors Guishan's northern side — one of the clearest examples in the district of a school built around a single institution.
