About · 學校簡介

Over 125 years, starting inside a temple.

Beipu Elementary's history stretches back to 1898, when it opened under Japanese rule as a branch of the Xinzhu Japanese-Language Training Institute at Beipu. Classes were first held inside the Cihtian Temple, the central Hakka temple at the heart of Beipu, before the school relocated to a dedicated campus in 1902. It was renamed Beipu National School in 1941 and reorganized following Taiwan's 1945 retrocession, giving it well over 125 years of continuous history — making it one of the oldest schools in Hsinchu County.

北埔國小的歷史可追溯至1898年，日治時期設立時為「新竹國語傳習所北埔分教場」。最初借用北埔信仰核心「慈天宮」內部空間上課，1902年遷至獨立校舍。1941年更名為「北埔國民學校」，1945年台灣光復後再度重整學制，至今已有超過125年的連續辦學歷史，是新竹縣歷史最悠久的學校之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🛕 Born Inside a Landmark Temple

起源於慈天宮

The school's very first classes were held inside Cihtian Temple, Beipu's central Hakka temple, before it moved to its own campus in 1902 — a founding story tightly woven into the town's civic and religious life.

🕰️ Over 125 Years of History

125年校史

Few schools anywhere in Taiwan can trace a continuous history back to 1898, making Beipu Elementary a living thread connecting today's students to the earliest years of formal schooling in the region.

🏘️ Renamed Through Taiwan's Changing Eras

歷經時代更名

From its Japanese-era origins through its 1941 renaming and 1945 postwar reorganization, the school's name changes trace the sweep of Taiwan's twentieth-century history.
