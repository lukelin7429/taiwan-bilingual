About · 學校簡介

120 years serving a tea-and-river town.

Pinglin Elementary opened in 1906, during the Japanese colonial era, and in May 2026 marked its 120th anniversary — a milestone the school tied explicitly to Pinglin's identity as a century-old tea-and-river town. Over the decades its catchment grew to span the entire mountainous township, after absorbing two remote satellite campuses: Kuoshi Elementary in 1985 and Yuguang Elementary in 2006. Today the school also runs the "Kuoshi English Magic Academy," an English-immersion program that carries the name of one of its absorbed branch schools forward.

坪林國小創立於1906年（明治39年），日治時期設校，已於2026年5月歡慶建校120週年——這項里程碑也緊扣著坪林做為百年茶鄉與河流小鎮的身分。學校學區歷經數十年逐步擴展至涵蓋整個山區坪林轄區，先後併入兩所偏遠分校：1985年的Kuoshi分校，以及2006年的Yuguang分校。學校目前另設有「Kuoshi English Magic Academy」英語沉浸式課程，沿用其中一所併校分校的名稱。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Inseparable from Pinglin's tea-and-river story.

🍵

Pinglin is the historic heart of Taiwan's Pouchong tea (包種茶) trade, its river-valley hillsides given over to tea gardens for generations, with the Beishi River (北勢溪) threading through the township on its way to the Feitsui Reservoir. Pinglin Elementary's 120th-anniversary branding leaned directly into this identity, presenting the school as inseparable from the township's tea-and-river story.
