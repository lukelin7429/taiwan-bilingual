About · 學校簡介

A tiny school above the clouds.

Cing Jing Elementary sits high in the mountains of Ren'ai Township, within the well-known Qingjing (Cingjing Farm) tourist area, above the historic Wushe area. It is a small, geographically isolated school that builds mountain and water-safety education directly into its curriculum, alongside indigenous-language certification and tribal dance instruction that keep Seediq and Atayal traditions part of daily student life.

清境國小座落於仁愛鄉高山地帶，位處知名的清境農場觀光區，海拔高於歷史悠久的霧社地區。這所地處偏遠、規模迷你的學校，將登山與水域安全教育納入日常課程，並開設原住民族語認證與部落舞蹈課程，讓賽德克族與泰雅族文化融入學生的日常生活。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗣️ Indigenous Language Certification

原住民族語認證課程

Students can pursue formal indigenous-language certification at Cing Jing, reinforcing Seediq and Atayal fluency alongside standard schoolwork.

💃 Tribal Dance Programs

部落舞蹈課程

A dedicated tribal dance program gives students hands-on practice in traditional Seediq and Atayal performance, connecting movement to cultural memory.

⛰️ High-Mountain Safety Education

高山與水域安全教育

Given its remote location high above the Wushe area, the school places special emphasis on mountain and water-safety education for its small student body.
