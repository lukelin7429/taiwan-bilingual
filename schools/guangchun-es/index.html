About · 學校簡介

A steady, mid-sized school with baseball pride.

Guangchun Elementary School is a mid-sized school with roughly 349 to 366 students and about 34 teachers across 17 classes, according to recent data. The school has maintained stable enrollment — two to three classes per grade — even as Taiwan's broader trend of declining birth rates has affected many rural schools. Guangchun is also home to a notable little-league baseball team, documented in the Taiwan Baseball Wiki, a source of local pride within Chaozhou Town.

光春國小為潮州鎮中型學校，近年資料顯示全校約349至366名學生，教師約34人，共17個班級。儘管台灣整體出生率下降趨勢影響許多鄉村學校，光春國小仍維持穩定招生規模——每個年級約2至3個班。學校的少棒隊亦頗具知名度，事蹟收錄於台灣棒球維基館，是潮州鎮地方的驕傲。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Chaozhou, southern Pingtung's rail and market hub.

🚂

Chaozhou is southern Pingtung's major market town and transportation hub, historically shaped by the sugar industry and rail transport, including the heritage Chaozhou Forest Railway. Guangchun Elementary's steady community presence mirrors the town's own long-standing role as a regional anchor.
