About · 學校簡介

A school born from the sugar refinery.

Xingtang Elementary was established on September 1, 1947, by the Taiwan Sugar Corporation to educate the children of workers at the Ciaotou Sugar Refinery, initially covering grades 1 through 4. It was transferred to government administration and renamed in 1968, when the Taiwan Sugar Corporation divested its company schools nationwide. Nicknamed the "magical forest school of nine-and-three-quarters," it uses the refinery's historic colonial-era architecture, along with old trains and sugar carts, as hands-on teaching resources.

興糖國小於1947年（民國36年）9月1日由台灣糖業公司創立，最初設有1至4年級，供橋頭糖廠員工子弟就讀。1968年台糖公司釋出全國各地的員工子弟學校，學校移交政府接管並更名。學校暱稱為「九又四分之三的魔法森林小學」，善用糖廠日治時期的歷史建築，以及舊時的火車與糖業台車作為體驗式教材。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A school tied to Taiwan's first modern sugar factory.

🏭

Xingtang Elementary is directly tied to the Ciaotou Sugar Refinery (est. 1902), Taiwan's first modern mechanized sugar factory — the school was originally built as a company school for the refinery's workers' children. See the Kaohsiung County/City page for more on the region.
