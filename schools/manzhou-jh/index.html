About · 學校簡介

The junior high that nine-year education built.

Manzhou Junior High School was established in 1968, the year Taiwan implemented nine-year compulsory education nationwide. Before then, Manzhou's remoteness and poor transportation meant only elementary-level schooling existed in the township — first just Manzhou Elementary School, with feeder elementary schools including Jiuben, Yonggang, and Changle added over time to build up the district that the junior high would eventually serve. The township's name, "滿州" (Manzhou), comes from a local Paiwan-language pronunciation and has no connection to the historical Manchuria. The school is currently led by principal Chen Ying-bang.

滿州國中創立於民國57年（1968年），正值台灣全面實施九年國民義務教育之際。在此之前，滿州因地處偏遠、交通不便，僅有國小程度的教育機構——最初只有滿州國小，後續陸續增設九棚、永港、長樂等小學，逐步建立起日後滿州國中所服務的學區。「滿州」之名源自排灣族語發音，與歷史上的「滿洲」並無關聯。學校現任校長為陳英邦。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Where the hawks fly and the old songs echo.

🦅

Manzhou Township is nationally known for the autumn gray-faced buzzard hawk (灰面鵟鷹) migration, when thousands of raptors pass over the township's ridgelines on their way south each year, drawing birdwatchers from across Taiwan. The township is also cited as the birthplace of "思想起" (Thinking Of), the classic Hengchun-Manzhou folk ballad sung to moon-guitar (月琴) accompaniment. Manzhou Junior High's students grow up immersed in both traditions — the seasonal rhythm of migrating birds overhead and the region's centuries-old singing culture.
