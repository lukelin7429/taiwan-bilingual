Zhihang Elementary School

志航國小

About · 學校簡介

A name honoring a Republic of China air force ace.

Zhihang Elementary School was founded in 1949, originally serving as an air force military-affiliated school. It was transferred to Chiayi County government administration in 1967, and became “Chiayi City Zhihang Elementary School” in 1982 when the city was upgraded to provincial-city status. Its name, “志航,” honors Gao Zhihang, a celebrated Republic of China Air Force ace, reflecting the school's historical ties to the nearby air force base community.

志航國小創立於民國38年（1949年），原為空軍子弟學校。民國56年（1967年）移交嘉義縣政府接管，71年（1982年）嘉義市升格省轄市後，定名為「嘉義市志航國民小學」。校名「志航」取自空軍抗日名將高志航，彰顯學校與鄰近空軍基地社區的歷史淵源。

Founded in 1949 as an air force military-affiliated school, Zhihang Elementary began serving the children of air force families before transitioning to civilian administration in 1967.
