About · 學校簡介

A hillside school looking out to sea.

Luliao Junior High was established in 1989 on a hillside in Shalu's Luliao area, a site local residents describe as "mountain behind, sea in front" — looking out toward the Taiwan Strait from the Dadu Tableland. Alongside its academic programs, the school runs an industrial-electronics certification program, giving students a pathway into technical careers.

鹿寮國中創立於民國78年（1989年），校址位於沙鹿鹿寮山丘，當地人形容為「傍山面海」，可自大肚台地眺望台灣海峽。學校除一般學科課程外，亦開設工業電子檢定專班，為學生提供技職教育的另一種選擇。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌄 A "Mountain Behind, Sea Ahead" Campus

傍山面海的校園

Luliao Junior High sits on a hillside overlooking the Taiwan Strait, a site locals describe as "mountain behind, sea in front."

🔌 An Industrial-Electronics Track

工業電子檢定專班

The school offers a certification program in industrial electronics, giving students an early path toward technical and vocational careers.

🏞️ Part of Shalu's Upland "Lu-" Cluster

沙鹿「鹿」字地名群

Luliao's hillside setting is part of a cluster of Shalu place-names — Luliao, Lufeng, Luyang — that trace the district's upland terrain on the Dadu Tableland.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Facing the Taiwan Strait.

Looking out from its hillside campus toward the Taiwan Strait, Luliao Junior High embodies the Dadu Tableland terrain that shapes so much of Shalu's geography — upland, agricultural, and facing the sea. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
