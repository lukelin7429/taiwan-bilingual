Hui-Ji Elementary School

會稽國民小學

One of the oldest schools in Taoyuan District, founded in 1945, known for varsity soccer, badminton and wrestling — and as the parent school of Happy Elementary.

About · 學校簡介

An 80-year anchor of Taoyuan's school system.

Hui-Ji Elementary was founded in 1945, the same year Taiwan was returned to Republic of China administration, making it one of the oldest schools in this part of Taoyuan District. The school is well known for athletics, fielding varsity teams in soccer, badminton, and wrestling. In 2003 it spun off its own "快樂分校" (Happy Branch), which became the independent Happy Elementary in 2004 — meaning Hui-Ji is literally the parent school of another campus in the district. Its name references the ancient Chinese place name "會稽" (Kuaiji), a common naming convention among schools of that founding era.

會稽國小創立於民國34年（1945年），與台灣光復同年，是桃園區歷史最悠久的學校之一。學校以體育聞名，設有足球、羽球、角力等代表隊。2003年分出「快樂分校」，於2004年獨立設校為快樂國小——會稽國小可謂該校的母校。校名取自中國古地名「會稽」，是當時許多台灣學校常見的命名方式，援引古典地理文化而非在地桃園地名。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🥋 Varsity Soccer, Badminton, and Wrestling

足球、羽球、角力代表隊

Hui-Ji fields varsity teams in soccer, badminton, and wrestling (角力), giving it a strong athletics reputation within Taoyuan District.

🌱 The Parent School of Happy Elementary

快樂國小的母校

Hui-Ji spun off its own "快樂分校" (Happy Branch) in 2003, which became the independent Happy Elementary in 2004 — one of only a few schools in the district to have spawned another.

🏛️ Anchoring the Postwar Core

戰後初期建校核心

As one of only two 1945-founded schools in this area (alongside East Gate Elementary, founded a decade earlier in 1934), Hui-Ji anchors the historic core of Taoyuan District's public-school system from the immediate post-retrocession period.
