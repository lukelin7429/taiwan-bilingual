About · 學校簡介

At the confluence of two rivers.

Zhiqing Elementary School was founded in 1976, on the plain formed at the confluence of the Jingmei River (景美溪) and the Xindian River (新店溪) — land that was historically the farming settlement of "Wansheng Village" (萬盛庄).

The school has won multiple Taipei municipal honors, including recognition for curriculum development, resource integration, and reading-promotion programs, and has been recognized as an "Education 111" benchmark school.

志清國小創立於1976年（民國65年），坐落於景美溪與新店溪匯流沖積而成的平原上，此地在歷史上曾是農耕聚落「萬盛庄」。

學校曾榮獲多項臺北市級獎項，包括課程發展、資源整合與閱讀推廣等領域的肯定，並獲選為「教育111」標竿學校。
