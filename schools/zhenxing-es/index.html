Zhenxing Elementary School

振興國小

Founded in 1943 as a temple classroom during the Japanese colonial era, now a modernized school along the Ailiao River at the foot of Dawu Mountain.

About · 學校簡介

From a temple classroom to a modern campus.

Zhenxing Elementary traces its origins to April 1943, when it began as a branch class of Yanpu National School, holding lessons in a local temple under teacher Zheng Dengke. It became an independently established school the following year, 1944, under the name "Kaohsiung Prefecture Pingtung District Zhenxing National School," with the Yanpu Elementary principal initially serving concurrently as its head. The school has since rebuilt its old buildings with modern electronic and digital classroom equipment, and maintains a detailed published chronology of its history.

振興國小前身為昭和18年（1943年）4月成立的「鹽埔國民學校振興分班」，最初借用廟宇上課，由鄭登科老師任教。翌年（昭和19年，1944年）獨立設校，定名「高雄州屏東郡振興國民學校」，初期由鹽埔國小校長兼任校長。學校歷經舊校舍拆除重建，導入現代電子與數位教學設備，並出版詳盡的「振興大事記」記錄校史。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏮 Born in a Temple Classroom

廟宇起家

The school's first classes in 1943 were held in a local temple, taught by Zheng Dengke, before it became an independent school the following year.

📖 A Documented School Chronology

振興大事記

Zhenxing publishes a detailed "振興大事記" chronology tracing its history from its 1943 founding through today, a rare level of documented institutional memory.

💻 Rebuilt for the Digital Age

數位教學設備

Old school buildings have been demolished and reconstructed, now incorporating modern electronic and digital classroom equipment.

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the foot of Dawu Mountain.

⛰️

Zhenxing sits at the foot of Dawu Mountain (大武山) along the Ailiao River (隘寮溪), in a Hoklo (Minnan) settlement within Yanpu Township's agricultural plain — a landscape that has shaped the school's identity since its 1943 founding. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
