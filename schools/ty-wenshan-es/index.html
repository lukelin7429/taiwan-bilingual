Wen-Shan Elementary School

文山國民小學

A designated Music Education specialty school established in 1981 on former irrigation-pond land, near the freeway gateway into southern Taoyuan District.

創立於1981年、校地前身為灌溉溜池的音樂教育特色學校，鄰近桃園南區交流道門戶地帶。

About · 學校簡介

Built on reclaimed pond land for a growing community.

Wen-Shan Elementary was established July 1, 1981 as a branch of Zhongshan Elementary, becoming independent the following month. The campus was built on land that had been a water-conservancy retention pond ("溜池") — the government funded both this school and the adjacent Zhongxing Junior High simultaneously to serve the growing community. Designated a Music Education specialty school, it sits on Wenzhong Road next to Zhongxing Junior High, near the Taiwan No. 1 provincial highway and the freeway interchange connecting South Taoyuan.

文山國小於民國70年（1981年）7月1日設立為中山國小分校，次月獨立設校。校地前身為水利溜池，政府當時同時興建本校與鄰近的中興國中，以因應社區成長需求。學校獲指定為音樂教育特色學校，校址位於文中路，緊鄰中興國中，鄰近台1線省道及銜接南桃園的交流道。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎵 A Music Education Specialty School

音樂教育特色學校

Wen-Shan is designated a Music Education specialty school (音樂教育特色學校), reflecting a sustained institutional focus on musical training.

💧 Built on a Reclaimed Retention Pond

校地前身為溜池

The campus sits on land that had been a water-conservancy retention pond, funded and built at the same time as the adjacent Zhongxing Junior High.

🛣️ A Gateway Into South Taoyuan

南桃園門戶

Sitting near the Taiwan No. 1 provincial highway and the freeway interchange connecting South Taoyuan, the school's location has been described as a "gateway" into the district.

Local Culture · 在地文化

From irrigation pond to school campus.

💧

Wen-Shan Elementary's origin on reclaimed irrigation-pond land is a direct trace of Taoyuan District's agricultural past being converted into urban educational infrastructure as the city grew outward from its historic core in the 1980s. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
