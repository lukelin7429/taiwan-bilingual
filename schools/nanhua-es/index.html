About · 學校簡介

Founded for safety, celebrated for rhythm.

Nanhua Elementary began as a branch of Ji'an Elementary in 1954 and became independent on August 21, 1956. The school was created because children in Nanhua and Ganchang villages had to cross the busy Hua-Dong Highway to reach Ji'an Elementary — a genuine safety concern that justified a school of its own. Decades later, Nanhua has built an unexpected specialty: its African drum ensemble won first place in a 2025 percussion competition, and its students also won first place in Truku-language recitation, a combination that speaks to the school's broad cultural reach.

南華國小於1954年以吉安國小分校形式創立，1956年8月21日正式獨立設校。學校的設立原因是南華村與干城村的孩童必須橫越車流繁忙的花東公路才能抵達吉安國小就讀，這項真實的交通安全考量促成了獨立設校。數十年後，南華國小發展出一項意想不到的特色：非洲鼓隊在2025年打擊樂比賽中榮獲第一名，學生也在太魯閣族語朗讀比賽中奪冠，展現學校在文化教育上的多元廣度。
