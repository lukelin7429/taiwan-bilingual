Ercheng Elementary School

二城國民小學

A century-old elementary school whose name preserves the memory of the pioneer-era stockade settlements of Yilan's Lanyang Plain.

About · 學校簡介

A name rooted in the settlers' stockades.

Ercheng Elementary traces its origin to 1913, when it opened as a branch of Touwei Public School under the name 頭圍公學校二圍分校. It became fully independent in 1920 as Taipei Prefecture Erwei Public School, relocated in 1942, and took its present name, 二城國民小學, in 1968. Today the school runs energy education, arts enrichment, mother-tongue and indigenous-language instruction days, and environmental education, alongside a non-categorical special-education resource classroom.

二城國小前身為民國2年（1913年）成立的頭圍公學校二圍分校，民國9年（1920年）獨立設校，改名台北州二圍公學校，民國31年（1942年）遷校，民國57年（1968年）更名為二城國民小學。學校現今推動能源教育、藝術深耕、母語及原住民族語日，以及環境教育，並設有不分類身心障礙資源班。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏰 A Name From the Second Stockade

「二圍」隘寮之名

The school's original name, "二圍" (Second Stockade), reflects the defensive settlement stockades built by early Han pioneers as they pushed into Yilan through Toucheng.

⚡ Energy and Arts Education

能源與藝術深耕教育

The school runs dedicated energy education and arts-enrichment programs, giving students hands-on exposure well beyond the standard curriculum.

🗣️ A Day for Mother Tongues

母語及原住民族語日

Regular mother-tongue and indigenous-language days help preserve linguistic diversity among the school's students.

🤝 Support for Every Learner

不分類身心障礙資源班

A non-categorical special-education resource classroom ensures students with diverse learning needs are supported within the school community.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Toucheng, Yilan's gateway town.

⛩️

Its original name, "二圍" (Second Stockade), directly preserves the memory of defensive barriers early Han settlers erected as they pushed into indigenous territory along Yilan's coast — a living link to Toucheng's identity as the first stop of Han settlement.
