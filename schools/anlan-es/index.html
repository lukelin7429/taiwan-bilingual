Anlan Elementary School

安瀾國民小學

One of Kinmen's "General Schools," built in 1947 by the officer whose name it bears, set among the island's best-preserved Minnan heritage villages.

金門「將軍小學」之一，1947年由馬安瀾將軍所建並以其名命名，坐落於閩南文化保存最完整的聚落群中。

About · 學校簡介

Built by a general, named for its builder.

Anlan Elementary traces its roots to two village schools established in 1936, but the school as it stands today was built and named in the summer of 1947 by General Ma An-lan, under orders from Kinmen Defense Commander General Hu Lien. The school was officially consolidated and moved to its present site in the summer of 1951. Its original 1947 dormitory was damaged that same year in the August 23 Artillery Bombardment and had to be repaired before the school could open — a reminder of how closely the school's early history was tied to the island's wartime conditions.

安瀾國小的歷史可追溯至1936年設立的兩所村莊學校，而今日的校名與校舍則於1947年夏由馬安瀾將軍奉金門防衛司令官胡璉將軍之命興建並命名。學校於1951年夏正式整併並遷至現址。1947年興建的宿舍當年即在八二三砲戰中受損，修復後才得以開學，見證了建校初期與戰地環境的緊密關聯。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎖️ A "General School" Named for Its Builder

以將軍命名的「將軍小學」

Anlan is one of Kinmen's "General Schools" (將軍小學) — literally named after General Ma An-lan, who built it in 1947 under orders from Kinmen's Defense Commander.

🏚️ A Dormitory Damaged Before It Opened

尚未啟用便遭砲擊受損的宿舍

The school's original 1947 dormitory was damaged in that year's August 23 Artillery Bombardment and had to be repaired before classes could begin.

🏘️ Neighboring Kinmen's Best-Preserved Villages

毗鄰保存完整的閩南聚落

The school district covers well-preserved Minnan settlements including Shanhou Folk Culture Village, Bishan, Caicuo, and Yangzhai, plus natural sites like Mount Taiwu and Mount Wuhu.

🌿 A Small, Boutique-Scale Campus

小而精緻的校園規模

With about 97 students across 6 elementary classes and 2 kindergarten classes, Anlan offers a close, boutique-scale learning environment.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Amid Kinmen's best-preserved Minnan villages.

🏘️

Anlan's school district contains some of Kinmen's best-preserved Minnan (閩南) heritage villages — including Shanhou Folk Culture Village — while the school itself is a direct artifact of the island's military frontline era, founded by a general on a commanding general's order.
