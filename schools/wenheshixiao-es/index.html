About · 學校簡介

A tiny branch school reborn as a mixed-age pioneer.

Wenhe Experimental Elementary School began in 1965 as a branch of Chonghe Elementary School in Budai Village, becoming independent in 1976. In 2017, it was converted into an official "experimental school" (實驗小學) — the first school in Tainan to adopt mixed-age (混齡) instruction as its defining model. English is taught four periods a week starting in Grade 2, more than double the usual load elsewhere, with students grouped by proficiency across mixed-age classes, while core subjects such as Chinese, math, science, and social studies remain grade-based. Students, including kindergartners, are sorted into four "houses" — Lion (fire), Orca (ocean), Grizzly (earth), and Eagle (air) — each led by a teacher acting as a surrogate parent figure, a distinctive whole-school culture unique among Tainan's public schools. New buildings, funded by the Tainan City government, have since modernized the campus's safety and facilities.

文和實小前身為崇和國小布袋分校，1965年設立，1976年獨立設校。2017年（民國106年）改制為正式「實驗小學」，是台南市第一所以混齡教學為核心模式的學校。英語課程自二年級起每週上課四節，遠高於一般學校的授課時數，依程度混齡分組上課，國語、數學、自然、社會等核心科目則維持依年級分班。學生（含幼兒園）依「獅子（火）、虎鯨（海洋）、灰熊（大地）、老鷹（天空）」四個「家族」分組，各由一位老師擔任如同代理家長的角色——這是台南公立學校中獨樹一格的全校文化制度。新校舍在台南市政府經費挹注下重建，提升校園安全與設施水準。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🔀 Tainan's First Mixed-Age School

台南首創混齡教學

Converted into an official experimental school in 2017, Wenhe was the first in Tainan to build its entire teaching model around mixed-age (混齡) instruction.

🗣️ English Class Time, Doubled

英語課時大幅提升

Starting in Grade 2, students take four periods of English a week — grouped by proficiency across ages, rather than strictly by grade.

🦅 Four Elemental Houses

四大家族制度

Every student, from kindergarten up, belongs to one of four houses — Lion, Orca, Grizzly, or Eagle — each led by a teacher who acts as a surrogate parent figure.

🏗️ Rebuilt for a New Era

校舍全面重建

New school buildings, funded by the Tainan City government, have modernized the campus's safety and facilities to match its innovative teaching model.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Innovation born from a shrinking village.

🎋

As a small, formerly branch-status rural school reinvented as an experimental model, Wenhe embodies how the depopulating farm villages around Guanmiao have had to innovate pedagogically to keep their schools viable — turning a small enrollment into an advantage through mixed-age classrooms and a distinctive house system.
