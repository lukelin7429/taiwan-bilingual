Zhongxing Junior High School

桃園市立中興國民中學

A public junior high built beside a historic irrigation retention pond, established in 1980 with long-running dance and wind-band programs.

About · 學校簡介

Founded beside a retention pond, growing into a music and dance campus.

Zhongxing Junior High was established on August 1, 1980, on a 3.43-hectare site beside the Taoyuan Farmers' Irrigation Association's No. 8 retention pond. While its permanent campus was under construction, its first classes were temporarily held at nearby Taoyuan Junior High and Ximen Elementary, before the school moved into its own buildings in February 1982. In 1989 it was approved to establish dance and wind-band classes to cultivate music and dance talent for the county, a program that continues to shape the school's identity today. It was renamed from a county to a city school on December 25, 2014, when Taoyuan County was upgraded to a special municipality.

中興國中創立於民國69年（1980年）8月1日，校地3.43公頃，緊鄰桃園農田水利會第八號滯洪池。新校舍興建期間，首屆學生暫借桃園國中與西門國小上課，至民國71年（1982年）2月才遷入現址。民國78年（1989年）獲准成立舞蹈班與管樂班，為縣內培育音樂舞蹈人才，這項傳統延續至今，塑造了學校的特色。2014年12月25日隨桃園縣升格直轄市，正式更名為桃園市立中興國民中學。
