About · 學校簡介

The historic mother school of Dazhu.

Dazhu Elementary is historically the largest and most central school of the traditional three-community Luzhu school system (Nankan, Dazhu, and Kengzi). Over the 20th century it spun off at least three branch classrooms that later became independent schools — Luzhu Elementary (1946), Xinxing Elementary (1951), and Xinzhuang Elementary (1974) — indicating that Dazhu Elementary itself predates all of them, likely by decades, though a confirmed founding date was not available in the sources checked for this page. The school sits in Dazhu Village, one of the historic core hamlets of Luzhu.

大竹國小是蘆竹傳統三大聚落（南崁、大竹、坑子）校系中，歷史上規模最大、地位最核心的學校。二十世紀期間，學校陸續分出至少三所分校，後來各自獨立設校——蘆竹國小（1946年）、新興國小（1951年）、新莊國小（1974年）——顯示大竹國小本身的創校年代應早於這幾所學校甚多，惟本頁撰寫時查證所得資料中未能確認確切創校年份。學校座落於大竹村，是蘆竹歷史核心聚落之一。

Local Culture · 在地文化

One of Luzhu's three historic settlement cores.

🌳

As the "mother school" of Dazhu — one of the three historic settlement cores of Luzhu District alongside Nankan and Kengzi — Dazhu Elementary anchors a farming community whose fields have gradually given way to the industrial parks now ringing the district. Its role as the source of at least three other Luzhu elementary schools makes it one of the deepest educational roots in the area. See the Taoyuan county page for more on the region.
