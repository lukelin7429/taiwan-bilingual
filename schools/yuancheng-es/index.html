About · 學校簡介

A small school with a green mission.

Yuancheng Elementary is one of the smallest schools in Yuli Township, with roughly 40 students. Despite its size, the school runs a dedicated environmental-education website and program, giving students a structured way to engage with the natural landscape of the surrounding Hua-Tung Valley. The school is further supported by its own dedicated education foundation, an unusual level of institutional backing for a campus this small.

源城國小是玉里鎮規模最小的學校之一，全校約40名學生。儘管規模不大，學校設有專屬的環境教育網站與課程，讓學生能有系統地認識花東縱谷周邊的自然環境。學校並獲得專屬教育基金會的支持，對於如此小型的校園而言，這是相當難得的制度性支援。

Local Culture · 在地文化

A small school in the valley's landscape.

🌱

Yuancheng sits within Yuli Township 玉里鎮, a rice-farming and hot-springs town in Hualien's Hua-Tung Valley near the Tropic of Cancer. Its environmental education focus reflects the surrounding valley's natural setting, giving students hands-on ties to the landscape they call home.
