About · 學校簡介

Pingtung's first public-built, privately-run school.

On August 1, 2017, Pingtung County's Education Department commissioned the Fuzhi Culture and Education Foundation to operate the former Gaoxing Elementary School and its Guanfu branch as a combined K-9 school under a new name: Daluguan Combined Junior/Elementary School. It was the first "public-built, privately-operated" (公辦民營) school of its kind in the county, bringing Fuzhi's distinctive life-education and character-based curriculum to the Dalguan area of Gaoshu Township.

2017年8月1日，屏東縣政府教育處委託財團法人福智文教基金會，將原高興國小及其管福分校整併經營，改制為「大路關國民中小學」，成為屏東縣第一所公辦民營學校。學校位於高樹鄉大路關地區，推動福智特有的生命教育與品格導向課程。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Pingtung's First Public-Private School

屏東縣首創公辦民營

Daluguan was the first school in Pingtung County to be run under the "public-built, privately-operated" model, with the Fuzhi Culture and Education Foundation taking over administration in 2017.

🌱 Life Education Curriculum

生命教育課程

Under Fuzhi's educational philosophy, the school emphasizes life education (生命教育) and character/values-based learning alongside the standard academic curriculum.

🌊 Ocean Education Field Trips

海洋教育戶外教學

Students have taken part in ocean-education excursions, including trips to Xiaoliuqiu, as part of the school's experiential, outside-the-classroom learning approach.

🌾 Rooted in Dalguan's Orchards

扎根大路關果園

The campus sits at the foothills of the central mountain range in Gaoshu's Dalguan area, surrounded by the betel nut and dragon fruit orchards typical of the township's agricultural economy.

Local Culture · 在地文化

At the foothills of Gaoshu's orchard country.

🌱

Daluguan sits in the Dalguan (大路關) area of Gaoshu Township, at the foothills of the central mountain range — a region long shaped by betel nut, dragon fruit, and orchard farming. As the county's pioneering public-private school, it brings a values-driven, life-education model to this rural farming community. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
