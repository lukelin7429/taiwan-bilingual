About · 學校簡介

From a girls' agricultural school to a complete secondary school.

Shulin Senior High traces its roots to 1944, when it opened under Japanese rule as Yingge Girls' Agricultural Practice School (鶯歌女子農業實踐學校). After Taiwan's 1945 retrocession, it was renamed first Yingge Junior High and then Shulin Junior High, reflecting the shifting administrative geography of the area. A senior-high division was added in 1948, and in 1995 the school became one of the first three complete secondary schools in the old Taipei County, a distinction it still holds today as it continues to operate both junior-high and senior-high divisions side by side.

樹林高中的歷史可追溯至民國33年（1944年）日治時期創立的「鶯歌女子農業實踐學校」。台灣光復後，先後更名為鶯歌初中、樹林初中，反映當地行政區劃的變遷。民國37年（1948年）增設高中部，民國84年（1995年）更成為當時台北縣最早的三所完全中學之一，至今仍同時設有國中部與高中部。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎓 From Girls' Agricultural School to Complete Secondary

從女子農校到完全中學

Opened in 1944 as a Japanese-era women's agricultural vocational school, the campus was renamed twice after 1945 before adding a senior-high division in 1948.

🏫 One of Old Taipei County's First Complete Schools

首批完全中學之一

In 1995, the school became one of the first three complete secondary schools in the former Taipei County, combining junior-high and senior-high education under one roof.

🚉 Shulin's Educational Anchor

樹林的教育樞紐

Still running junior-high and senior-high divisions side by side today, the school anchors secondary education for a large share of Shulin's students.

🌾 A Legacy Rooted in Agriculture

源自農業實踐的傳統

Its earliest incarnation as an agricultural practice school echoes Shulin's roots as a riverside farming settlement before its 20th-century industrial and residential growth.

Local Culture · 在地文化

From riverside farmland to railway town.

🚂

Shulin District grew from a riverside farming settlement along the Dahan River into a busy railway town and now a dense residential suburb of New Taipei City. The school's own evolution — from a Japanese-era girls' agricultural school into the district's flagship complete secondary school — tracks this same shift from agrarian life to a modern urban district.
