About · 學校簡介

Rebuilt with the farming community's support.

Houli Junior High was founded in 1963 and relocated to a new campus in Meishan Village in 2005, following a major rebuilding project. Notably, part of the funding for that rebuild came from the local farmers' irrigation association, a detail that reflects Houli's continuing agricultural economy and the close ties between the school and its farming community.

后里國中創立於民國52年（1963年），並於民國94年（2005年）遷至梅山里的新校區，這是一項大規模重建工程的成果。值得一提的是，這次重建經費有部分來自在地農田水利會的挹注，反映了后里持續發展的農業經濟以及學校與農村社區之間的緊密連結。
