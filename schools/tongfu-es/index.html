About · 學校簡介

A community hub in Tongfu Village.

Tongfu Elementary School serves the children of Tongfu Village, one of Xinyi Township's central Bunun community hubs, located near the gateway to Yushan National Park. The school operates an attached kindergarten and runs active programs in reading promotion, anti-bullying education, and gender-equality education, reflecting a well-rounded approach to student development in this mountain community.

同富國小服務同富村的學童，該村是信義鄉布農族社群的核心聚落之一，鄰近玉山國家公園門戶。學校附設幼兒園，並積極推動閱讀教育、反霸凌教育與性別平等教育，展現這座山區社區對學生全人發展的重視。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🧸 An Attached Kindergarten

附設幼兒園

Tongfu Elementary operates its own attached kindergarten, giving local families continuity from early childhood through elementary school.

📖 Building a Reading Habit

閱讀推廣

The school runs an active reading promotion program, encouraging students to build strong literacy habits from an early age.

🛡️ A Safe, Respectful Campus

反霸凌與性別平等教育

Anti-bullying and gender-equality education programs help the school foster a safe and respectful learning environment for all students.
