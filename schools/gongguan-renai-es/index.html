About · 學校簡介

A small school with an international reach.

Renai Elementary sits in central Gongguan along Ren'ai Road, serving a small rural community rooted in Hakka farming traditions. Despite its size, the school looks outward: students take part in joint painting and calligraphy competitions with Taiwan-Japan Rotary Club partners, connecting the school to an international arts exchange. Its attached kindergarten has also earned recognition as a "Parenting" magazine recommended preschool.

仁愛國小位於公館鄉中心地帶的仁愛路，服務一個根植於客家農業傳統的鄉間小社區。儘管規模不大，學校仍積極對外交流：學生參與台日扶輪社聯合舉辦的書畫比賽，與國際藝文交流接軌。附設幼兒園也獲得《親子天下》雜誌推薦幼兒園的肯定。
