Hesheng Elementary School

鶴聲國民小學

Descended from the 1948 Pingtung Air Force Children's School and named for a fallen Air Force officer, now one of the largest elementary schools in Pingtung City.

About · 學校簡介

Named for a fallen Air Force officer.

Hesheng Elementary traces its origin to 1948, when it opened as the "Pingtung Air Force Children's School," established under the Air Force Headquarters as it relocated from Xuzhou with the national government — initially borrowing classroom space from neighboring Ren'ai Elementary before moving to its current site in 1953. In August 1968, the school was renamed "Pingtung County Hesheng Elementary School" to commemorate Air Force martyr Lin Hesheng, whose bronze statue still stands on campus today. As of the 2022–23 school year, Hesheng was one of the largest elementary schools among Pingtung City's neighborhood schools, with 45 classes, 1,228 students, 80 teachers, 6 staff, and 2 janitors. The school's longest-serving principal, Zhang Hongzhong, led the school for 27 years, from 1953 to 1980 — the longest tenure in its history.

鶴聲國小的歷史可追溯至民國37年（1948年），創校時名為「屏東空軍子弟小學」，隨國民政府自徐州遷台的空軍總司令部所設立，初期借用仁愛國小教室上課，於民國42年（1953年）遷至現址。民國57年（1968年）8月，為紀念空軍烈士林鶴聲，學校改名為「屏東縣鶴聲國民小學」，校園內至今仍立有林鶴聲的銅像。111學年度，鶴聲國小是屏東市規模最大的國小之一，全校共45班、1,228名學生、80位教師、6位職員、2位工友。該校任期最長的校長張洪忠，自民國42年至69年（1953年至1980年）在任長達27年，為校史上最長紀錄。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🎖️ Named for an Air Force Martyr

紀念空軍烈士林鶴聲

The school's name honors Lin Hesheng, an Air Force officer who died in service, and his bronze statue stands on campus, documented in Taiwan's National Cultural Memory Bank.

✈️ Descended from an Air Force Dependents' School

源自空軍子弟小學

Founded in 1948 as the Pingtung Air Force Children's School, the school traces directly to the Air Force Headquarters' relocation from Xuzhou with the national government.

👨‍🏫 A 27-Year Principal's Tenure

校長任期長達27年

Principal Zhang Hongzhong led the school from 1953 to 1980, a 27-year tenure that remains the longest in the school's history.

🏫 One of the City's Largest Schools

屏東市規模最大學校之一

With 45 classes and 1,228 students as of the 2022–23 school year, Hesheng offers after-school programs, language competitions, club showcases, and English listening competitions.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A garrison town's living memorial.

✈️

Directly descended from the Pingtung Air Force Dependents' School, Hesheng Elementary embodies Pingtung City's identity as a major ROC Air Force garrison town — home to Pingtung Air Base and multiple air force dependents' villages — with its very name honoring fallen Air Force officer Lin Hesheng.
