Tianmu Elementary School

臺北市士林區天母國民小學

Founded in 1981 in the heart of Tianmu, one of Taipei's largest elementary schools with an unusually extensive facility set and a new Art Talent program.

About · 學校簡介

A large school for a growing community.

Tianmu Elementary School was founded on August 1, 1981, on Tianyu Street in the heart of the Tianmu neighborhood. It has grown into a large elementary school by Taipei standards — as of the 2021-22 academic year it had 65 classes and roughly 1,794 students. The campus offers an unusually extensive facility set for the district, including a library, athletic field, computer classrooms, basketball courts, an activity center, a soccer field, tennis courts, a music/dance room, an orchestra room, and a taekwondo room, reflecting Tianmu's affluent, internationally-oriented community. In the 2024-25 academic year, the school began offering a dedicated "Art Talent" (美術班) program.

天母國小創立於民國70年（1981年）8月1日，位於天母核心地帶的天玉街。學校規模龐大，是臺北市規模數一數二的小學之一——111學年度共有65班、學生約1,794人。校園設施相當完善，包括圖書館、田徑場、電腦教室、籃球場、活動中心、足球場、網球場、音樂舞蹈教室、管弦樂教室及跆拳道教室等，反映出天母社區的富庶與國際化特色。113學年度起，學校更開設美術班。
