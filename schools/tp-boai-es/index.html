Bo'ai Elementary School

臺北市博愛國民小學

One of Xinyi's newest and largest elementary schools, built to serve the community that grew up alongside the modern Xinyi Planning District.

信義區最新、規模最大的國小之一，因應信義計畫區興起而設立，服務周邊快速成長的住宅人口。

About · 學校簡介

A large modern school built for a growing district.

Bo'ai Elementary School began preparation in 1988 and was formally established in September 1992 under founding principal Lin Tusheng, who served until 1997. It is a large campus by Taipei standards — as of the 2021 school year it had about 74 classes and roughly 2,051 students, with 146 teachers. Its multi-function sports center, swimming pool, auditorium, and multimedia center all opened together on January 9, 2004.

博愛國小自1988年（民國77年）開始籌備，1992年（民國81年）9月正式設校，首任校長林土生任職至1997年。以台北市標準而言屬大型學校，110學年度約有74班、學生約2,051人、教師146人。學校的多功能運動中心、游泳池、大禮堂及多媒體中心於2004年1月9日同步啟用。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 A Large Modern Campus

大型現代化校園

As of the 2021 school year, Bo'ai had about 74 classes and roughly 2,051 students, making it one of the largest elementary schools in the district.

🏊 A 2004 Facilities Upgrade

2004年設施全新啟用

The school's sports center, swimming pool, auditorium, and multimedia center all opened together on January 9, 2004, giving it an unusually complete set of facilities.

🎭 A Wide Range of Clubs

多元社團選擇

Students can join clubs ranging from scouting, diabolo, string orchestra, and choir to taiko drumming, street dance, and golf — an unusually broad extracurricular offering.

🏙️ Built for the Modern Xinyi District

因應信義計畫區而生

Established in 1992, Bo'ai is one of the newest elementary schools in the district, built specifically to serve the residential population that grew up alongside the Xinyi Planning District.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Growing up with the Xinyi Planning District.

🏙️

One of the newest elementary schools in Xinyi District, Bo'ai was built specifically to serve the residential population that grew up alongside the master-planned Xinyi Planning District as it developed through the 1990s and 2000s into Taipei's modern financial and civic core — a direct product of the same redevelopment that produced Taipei 101 and its surrounding neighborhoods.
