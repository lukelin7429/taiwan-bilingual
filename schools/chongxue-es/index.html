Chongxue Elementary School

崇學國民小學

Founded in 1982 by combining overflow students from three older East District schools, once one of the largest single-campus elementary enrollments in Tainan history.

創校於1982年，由東區三所鄰近學校的過剩學生合併組成，全盛時期曾是臺南單一校區學生人數最多的國小之一。

About · 學校簡介

Born from three overcrowded schools.

Chongxue Elementary School was approved for establishment in January 1981 as the "Degao Elementary School Chongxue Branch" (德高國民小學崇學分校). It opened that August with six first-grade classes, borrowing classrooms from Dongguang, Shengli, and Degao elementary schools, with groundbreaking presided over by then-mayor Su Nan-cheng. The branch became an independent school effective the 1982 academic year, receiving transferred 3rd–6th grade classes — 15 classes in total — from the same three schools, for a founding total of 29 classes. By the 1995 academic year, enrollment had peaked at an extraordinary 119 classes and 5,586 students, one of the largest single-campus elementary enrollments in Tainan's history.

崇學國小於1981年（民國70年）1月獲准設立，初名「德高國民小學崇學分校」。同年8月開學，首設六個一年級班級，借用東光、勝利、德高三校教室上課，動土典禮由時任市長蘇南成主持。1982學年度起獨立設校，並自上述三校轉入三至六年級共15班，創校總班級數達29班。至1995學年度，全校規模達到高峰，共119班、5,586名學生，是臺南單一校區歷史上罕見的超大型國小之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

📈 A Record-Setting Enrollment Peak

1995年學生數5,586人

At its 1995 peak, the school had 119 classes and 5,586 students — one of the largest single-campus elementary enrollments in Tainan history.

🤝 Founded by Combining Three Schools

三校合併創校

The school was formed by transferring overflow students from three separate existing schools — Dongguang, Shengli, and Degao — an unusually collaborative founding.

📊 A Marker of 1980s Growth

1980年代人口成長縮影

Its founding by consolidating overflow enrollment from three older schools illustrates East District's rapid mid-1980s population growth, a generation after its parent schools were founded.
