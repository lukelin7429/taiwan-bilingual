Zhongli Home Economics & Commercial Senior High School

桃園市立中壢家事商業高級中等學校

Founded in 1954 as a girls' home-economics vocational school, now training culinary and commercial talent for Zhongli's dense restaurant and hospitality economy.

About · 學校簡介

From girls' home-economics school to coed culinary training ground.

Founded in 1954 as the Taoyuan County Junior Home Economics Vocational School, this school was originally an all-girls institution — it began admitting male students for the first time in its history only in 2017. It became a municipal school on January 1, 2018, following Taoyuan's upgrade to a special municipality, and today enrolls roughly 890 students across 27 classes (2022 figures). Alongside culinary arts and commercial programs, the school fields competitive teams in shooting, cycling, and taekwondo, under the motto "誠、敬、勤、樸" (sincerity, respect, diligence, and simplicity).

學校創立於民國43年（1954年），前身為桃園縣立初級家事職業學校，原為女子學校，直到2017年才首度招收男學生。2018年1月1日隨桃園市升格為直轄市而改制為市立學校，現有學生約890人、27班（2022年統計）。除餐飲與商業科系外，學校也設有射擊、自行車、跆拳道等競技隊伍，校訓為「誠、敬、勤、樸」。
