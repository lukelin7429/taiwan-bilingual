About · 學校簡介

The newest school in New Taipei.

Nanshi Junior High is, as of this writing, New Taipei's newest public junior high school, established in 2025 to relieve overcrowding at Linkou's other three junior high schools. It takes its name from Nanshi, the fastest-growing neighborhood within Linkou New Town, reflecting how quickly this once-quiet corner of Linkou has filled with new residents and their school-age children.

南勢國中是（截至目前）新北市最新成立的公立國中，於民國114年（2025年）創校，目的是紓解林口區其他三所國中的就學壓力。校名取自「南勢」——林口新市鎮成長最快速的區域，反映出這個原本寧靜的林口角落，如今已迅速湧入大量新居民與學齡兒童。
