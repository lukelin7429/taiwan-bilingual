About · 學校簡介

From a merchant family's private school to a public one.

Banqiao Elementary opened in 1899 during the Japanese colonial era as 枋橋公學校 (Banqiao Public School), converting a private "national-language institute" that had been run by the wealthy Lin Ben Yuan (林本源) family. It is the oldest school in the district, and the campus marked a schoolwide centennial celebration in 2025 (ROC 114). A stone monument, 枋橋建學碑, commemorates the Lin family's founding donation, and the school today runs wind and Chinese orchestras alongside handball, badminton, table tennis, and track teams.

板橋國小創校於1899年（日治時期），初名「枋橋公學校」，由板橋首富林本源家族創辦的私塾「國語傳習所」改制而成，是板橋歷史最悠久的學校。校內立有「枋橋建學碑」，紀念林家的創校捐獻，並於民國114年（2025年）舉行百週年校慶活動。學校現設有管樂團與國樂團，並有手球、羽球、桌球、田徑等隊伍。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Founded by Taiwan's Wealthiest Merchant Clan

林本源家族創校

The school began as a Lin Ben Yuan family-run "national-language institute," converted into 枋橋公學校 in 1899 — a direct link to Taiwan's most prominent 19th-century merchant family.

🗿 A Stone Monument to Its Founding

枋橋建學碑

The 枋橋建學碑 monument on campus commemorates the Lin family's founding donation, a physical marker of the school's Qing-dynasty-era roots.

🎻 Wind and Chinese Orchestras

管樂團與國樂團

Banqiao Elementary runs both a wind orchestra and a Chinese orchestra, alongside handball, badminton, table tennis, and track teams.

🎉 A Centennial Celebration

百週年校慶

The school marked a centennial celebration in 2025 (ROC 114), a milestone for the district's oldest campus.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The market town built by a merchant family.

🌉

Directly descended from the Lin Ben Yuan family's 大觀義學 charitable school, this campus embodies Banqiao's Qing-dynasty identity as 枋橋 ("raft bridge"), the market town built around Taiwan's wealthiest 19th-century merchant clan. See the New Taipei county page for more on the region.
