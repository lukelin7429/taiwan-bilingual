About · 學校簡介

A small school for a remote mountain community.

Yuanfu Junior High opened in 1970 as a branch campus of Qishan Junior High, becoming an independent school in 1972 and adopting its current municipal name in December 2010. Today it is a small school of roughly 103 students, around three-quarters of whom come from disadvantaged backgrounds, serving the Yuantan community and mountain villages spanning Cishan and neighboring Neimen.

圓富國中於民國59年（1970年）以旗山國中分校形式成立，民國61年（1972年）獨立設校，並於2010年12月改制為市立學校，定名至今。目前全校約103名學生，其中約四分之三來自弱勢家庭，服務範圍涵蓋旗山北部的圓潭社區及橫跨旗山、內門兩區的山區聚落。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⛰️ Serving Cishan's Mountain Communities

服務山區聚落

The school's catchment stretches across the mountain villages of northern Cishan and neighboring Neimen District, an area of small, dispersed rural communities.

🌱 A Branch That Quickly Grew Independent

分校升格獨立設校

Opened in 1970 as a branch of Qishan Junior High so children wouldn't face long journeys to town, it became fully independent just two years later, in 1972.

🤝 Supporting a High-Need Student Body

弱勢家庭學生比例高

With around three-quarters of its roughly 100 students from disadvantaged backgrounds, the school plays an outsized role in supporting its rural community.
