About · 學校簡介

Four decades in Minsheng Community.

Minzu Elementary was founded in 1977 and celebrated its 40th anniversary in 2017. It shares its site and administrative complex with the adjacent Minzu Experimental Junior High School (民族實驗國中). The school runs 28 general classes, 4 art classes, and 1 resource class, plus an attached kindergarten, serving roughly 720 students — part of Songshan's continued residential build-out in the Minsheng Community area through the 1970s.

民族國小創立於民國66年（1977年），民國106年（2017年）歡慶四十週年校慶。學校與鄰近的民族實驗國中共用校地及行政大樓。全校設有28個普通班、4個美術班、1個資源班，並附設幼兒園，學生人數約720人，是1970年代民生社區持續開發過程中興建的學校之一。
