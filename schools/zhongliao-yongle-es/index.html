About · 學校簡介

A small village school with an eye on the wider world.

Yongle Elementary serves Fusheng Village in rural Zhongliao Township, operating an attached kindergarten alongside its elementary program. Despite its small size, students have won awards in English speech competitions and national children's art contests, and the school places strong emphasis on traffic safety and disaster-prevention training as part of everyday student life.

永樂國小服務中寮鄉福盛村的鄉村地區，附設幼兒園，並提供國小教育課程。儘管規模不大，學生仍曾在英語演說比賽與全國兒童美術競賽中獲獎，學校也十分重視交通安全與防災訓練，將其融入日常學生生活之中。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🗣️ English Speech Competition Wins

英語演說比賽獲獎

Students at Yongle have won awards in English speech competitions, a notable achievement for a small rural elementary school in Zhongliao Township.

🎨 National Children's Art Contest Recognition

全國兒童美術獲獎

The school's students have also earned recognition in national children's art contests, reflecting a broader emphasis on creative expression.

🧒 An Attached Kindergarten

附設幼兒園

Yongle operates its own attached kindergarten, giving Fusheng Village families a continuous educational path from early childhood onward.

交通安全與防災訓練

The school builds traffic safety and disaster-prevention training into everyday student life, a common and important emphasis across Nantou County's rural schools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A small community in the heart of Zhongliao.

🍯

Located in Fusheng Village in the heart of longan- and honey-farming Zhongliao Township, Yongle Elementary serves one of the small rural communities that rebuilt around agriculture after the 1999 earthquake. See the Nantou county page for more on the region.
