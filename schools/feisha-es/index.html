About · 學校簡介

A century tracing the township's growth.

Feisha Elementary was founded in April 1921 as "Taiwan Public Feisha Public School" under Japanese rule, giving it over a century of history. The school has historically served as the parent institution for several branch schools across the township that later became independent, making its story closely intertwined with the growth of education throughout Sihu.

飛沙國小創立於1921年（日治時期）4月，初名「台灣公立飛沙公學校」，至今已有百年以上校史。學校在歷史上曾是鄉內多所後來獨立設校之分校的母校，其發展歷程與四湖鄉整體教育版圖的擴展緊密相連。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Flying Sand's century of change.

🏖️

Feisha ("Flying Sand") Village sits along Sihu's coastal fringe, and the school's century-plus history traces the township's shift from a purely coastal fishing hamlet into today's rice-garlic-and-fishing community. See the Yunlin county page for more on the region.
