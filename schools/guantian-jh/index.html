Guantian Junior High School

官田國中

A public junior high founded in 1968, just minutes from the wetlands that made Guantian nationally famous for water-chestnut farming and water-rail conservation.

About · 學校簡介

Founded for nine-year education, rebuilt through local advocacy.

Guantian Junior High School was founded in 1968 to accommodate Taiwan's extension of compulsory education to nine years, with Li Zhang-tian serving as its first principal. In 1996, under principal Gu Chuan-long, the school relocated to a newly built campus after years of local campaigning for a better site. It sits close to the Guantian Water-rail (水雉) Ecological Education Park, one of Taiwan's key habitats for the water rail, a bird that nests specifically in the district's water-chestnut paddies.

官田國中創立於民國57年（1968年），因應九年國教延長而設立，首任校長為李樟田。民國85年（1996年），在校長古川隆帶領下，經過多年爭取後遷入新建校舍。學校鄰近官田水雉生態教育園區——這是台灣重要的水雉（菱角鳥）棲地之一，這種鳥類專門在官田的菱角田築巢。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 Founded for Nine-Year Education

因應九年國教設立

Guantian Junior High opened in 1968 as part of Taiwan's extension of compulsory education to nine years, under its first principal, Li Zhang-tian.

🏗️ A Campaigned-For New Campus

爭取多年遷入新校舍

The school relocated to a newly built campus in 1996 under principal Gu Chuan-long, the result of years of local campaigning for better facilities.

🐦 Neighbor to the Water-Rail Wetlands

鄰近水雉生態園區

The campus sits just minutes from the Guantian Water-rail Ecological Education Park, one of Taiwan's key habitats for the water rail.

🌰 In Water-Chestnut Country

菱角之鄉

Students at the school grow up in a district nationally known for water-chestnut farming — the very crop whose paddies sustain the rare water rail nearby.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Water chestnuts, water rails, and the school beside them.
