About · 學校簡介

Taiwan's first school of industrial education.

National Taipei University of Technology, widely known as Taipei Tech, was founded on July 5, 1912, under Japanese colonial rule as the Auxiliary Industrial Institute, opening in Taipei Prefecture's Ta-an Precinct with three departments — Woodcraft, Metalwork, and Electrical Engineering. It marked the beginning of formal industrial and vocational education in Taiwan. The school was renamed Provincial Taipei Institute of Technology in 1948, offering five-year associate-degree programs, and was finally promoted to full university status in 1997 as National Taipei University of Technology. Today it is Taiwan's oldest and one of its most prominent technological universities, concentrated in technology, design, and management.

國立臺北科技大學（通稱北科大）創立於1912年7月5日，日治時期名為「工業講習所」，設於台北廳大安支廳，初設木工、金工、電工三科，開啟台灣正式工業與職業教育的先河。1948年改制為「省立台北工業專科學校」，開設五年制專科課程，1997年正式升格為大學，定名國立臺北科技大學。如今北科大是台灣歷史最悠久、最具代表性的科技大學之一，專長領域集中於科技、設計與管理。

Academics · 學術特色

What makes this university worth knowing.

🎓 Six Colleges

六個學院

Taipei Tech offers 19 undergraduate, 26 master's, and 17 doctoral programs across six colleges, concentrated in technology, design, and management.

🏆 Taiwan's Top Technological University

技職院校第一

Ranked #1 among Taiwan's technological/vocational universities in Ministry of Education teaching-excellence evaluations, and previously ranked QS World #420, QS Asia #73.

🌱 Leading Green Campus

綠色大學典範

Recognized as a leading "green university," ranked 6th worldwide among city-center institutions in the UI GreenMetric rankings.

🌏 International Community

國際學生社群

Taipei Tech enrolls roughly 1,090 international students from over 50 countries, supported by its Office of International Affairs.

Local Culture · 在地文化

A century alongside Taipei's growth.

🛠️

Founded in 1912 as Taiwan's first institute of industrial education, Taipei Tech has grown alongside the city's own industrial and urban development for over a century, and its Zhongxiao East Road campus remains a landmark of applied technical training in the heart of Da'an District.
