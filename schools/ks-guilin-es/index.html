About · 學校簡介

Roughly four decades of neighborhood schooling.

Guilin Elementary School serves families in Siaogang District. Precise founding records are limited, but the school celebrated a documented 40th-anniversary milestone in 2023–24, placing its founding at approximately 1983 or 1984.

桂林國小服務小港區的學童與家庭。由於現存資料有限，難以確認精確創校年份，但學校於2023至2024年間舉辦了有紀錄可查的建校四十週年慶祝活動，據此推估創校年約在1983或1984年。

🎉 A Documented 40th Anniversary

建校四十週年

Guilin Elementary marked a 40th-anniversary milestone in 2023–24, giving the clearest documented anchor point for its founding decades earlier.

🏘️ Serving Siaogang's Families for Decades

服務小港數十年

For roughly four decades, Guilin Elementary has served the children of Siaogang District, part of the network of schools built as the area urbanized.

📍 A Neighborhood School on Guiyang Road

桂陽路上的社區學校

Located on Guiyang Road, Guilin Elementary remains a neighborhood elementary school within Siaogang's residential fabric.

Local Culture · 在地文化

One of many schools built as Siaogang grew.

🏘️

Like many of Siaogang's elementary schools, Guilin serves a neighborhood shaped by the district's rapid growth and urbanization since Xiaogang Township's 1979 annexation into Kaohsiung City. See the Kaohsiung county page for more on the region.
