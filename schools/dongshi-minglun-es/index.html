Minglun Elementary School

明倫國民小學

A small elementary school in Yuemei Village, serving one of Yunlin's most productive garlic- and carrot-growing communities.

座落於東勢鄉月眉村的小型國小，服務雲林重要的蒜頭與紅蘿蔔農業社區。

About · 學校簡介

A small school with well-rounded facilities.

Minglun Elementary is a small school in Yuemei Village, Dongshi Township, with about 62 students across 6 classes and 11 teachers. Despite its modest size, the campus is well equipped, with a library, computer classroom, athletic field, and basketball court supporting a full range of student activities.

明倫國小是一所座落於東勢鄉月眉村的小型學校，全校約62名學生、6個班級、11位教師。儘管規模不大，校園設施相當完善，設有圖書館、電腦教室、運動場與籃球場，支持學生多元的學習與活動需求。

📚 Well-Rounded Facilities

設施完善的小校

🧑‍🏫 A High Teacher-to-Student Ratio

師生比高

With 11 teachers for roughly 62 students across 6 classes, students benefit from close attention typical of Yunlin's small rural schools.

月眉村在地學校
