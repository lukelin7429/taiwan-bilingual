Zhongming Senior High School

臺中市立忠明高級中學

A West District complete secondary school whose growth from a 1950s junior middle school traces Taichung's postwar expansion of city-center education.

About · 學校簡介

From a 1950s junior middle school to a complete high school.

Zhongming Senior High School was founded in 1957 as "Taichung Municipal Third Junior Middle School," one of the junior middle schools established during the city's postwar expansion of secondary education. It became a complete junior-senior high school in 2000 and adopted its current name the following year. The campus sits near the National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts, in the heart of West District.

忠明高中創立於民國46年（1957年），初名「臺中市立第三初級中學」，是台中市戰後普及中等教育擴張時期設立的學校之一。民國89年（2000年）改制為完全中學，翌年正式更名為現名。校址鄰近國立臺灣美術館，座落於西區市中心地帶。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏫 From Junior Middle to Complete School

從初中到完全中學

Founded in 1957 as the city's third junior middle school, Zhongming expanded into a full junior-senior high in 2000 and took its current name in 2001, a path shared by several of Taichung's mid-century secondary schools.

🎨 In the Museum District

美術館旁的校園

The campus sits close to the National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts, placing it within West District's arts-and-culture corridor alongside several other neighborhood schools.

🏙️ A City-Center Education

市中心的中學教育

As one of the schools founded during Taichung's postwar push to expand secondary education, Zhongming continues to serve families living in the historic core of the city.

Local Culture · 在地文化

West District, Taichung's historic core.

🖼️

West District is home to the National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts and the Green Way corridor, forming one of Taichung's most established arts-and-culture neighborhoods — a setting Zhongming shares with several other West District schools. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
