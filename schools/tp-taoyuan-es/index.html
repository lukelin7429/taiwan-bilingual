About · 學校簡介

Half a century of "薪傳" — passing the flame.

Taoyuan Elementary was founded around 1972 and celebrated its 50th anniversary in December 2022 under the theme "桃源薪傳半世紀" ("Taoyuan: Passing the Flame for Half a Century"). The school fields a notable Little League baseball team (少棒隊), documented on Taiwan's baseball history wiki, and sits along the Zhongyang North Road corridor on the approach toward Guandu and the Yangmingshan foothills.

桃源國小創校於民國61年（1972年）左右，於民國111年（2022年）12月以「桃源薪傳半世紀」為主題歡慶建校50週年。學校少棒隊小有名氣，曾被收錄於台灣棒球史相關資料庫。校址位於中央北路沿線，通往關渡與陽明山麓方向。
