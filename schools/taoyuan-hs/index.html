Taoyuan Senior High School

桃園市立桃園高級中等學校

One of the oldest secondary schools in Taoyuan District, founded in 1941 as a Japanese-era girls' home economics school and now a co-ed public senior high on the same historic street.

桃園區歷史最悠久的中等學校之一，創校於1941年日治時期的女子家政學校，如今是位於同一條歷史街道上的公立男女合校高中。

About · 學校簡介

Eight decades of names, one enduring campus.

Taoyuan Senior High traces back to 1941, when it opened under Japanese rule as the "Shinchiku Prefectural Taoyuan Home Economics Girls' School." It went through several name and status changes over the decades — becoming Taoyuan Junior Middle School in 1946, Provincial Taoyuan Middle School in 1954, and, after losing its junior-high division, Provincial Taoyuan Senior High School in 1970. It became a national school in 2000 and took its current municipal name after Taoyuan's 2018 reorganization into a special municipality. As of 2018 the school enrolled about 2,584 students in 66 classes with 157 full-time teachers, and it maintains a sister-school partnership with Indong High School in Gumi, South Korea. Its motto — 誠、愛、勤、樸 (Integrity, Love, Diligence, Simplicity) — has guided generations of students.

桃園高中前身可追溯至1941年日治時期創立的「新竹州立桃園家政女學校」。歷經多次更名與體制轉變：1946年改為桃園初級中學、1954年改為省立桃園中學，1970年因裁撤初中部改制為省立桃園高級中學。民國89年（2000年）改為國立，2018年桃園升格為直轄市後改為現名。2018年資料顯示全校約2,584名學生、66個班級（每年級22班）、157位專任教師，並與韓國龜尾市仁東高等學校締結姊妹校。校訓「誠、愛、勤、樸」引領著一代代學子。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏛️ Eighty Years of History

八十餘年校史

From a 1941 Japanese-era girls' home economics school through provincial, national, and now municipal status, the school has carried Taoyuan's oldest secondary-education lineage through every era of local government.

🤝 A Korean Sister School

韓國姊妹校

The school maintains an international sister-school partnership with Indong High School in Gumi, South Korea, giving students a window into cross-border educational exchange.

📜 A Four-Word Motto

誠、愛、勤、樸

The school's motto — Integrity, Love, Diligence, Simplicity — reflects the values instilled through decades of teaching roughly 2,500 students across 66 classes.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Anchoring Taoyuan's historic old town.

🏘️

As one of the oldest secondary schools in the district, Taoyuan Senior High sits on Chenggong Road near the historic commercial core that grew up around the area's original Qing- and Japanese-era administrative center — placing it right at the heart of old Taoyuan.
