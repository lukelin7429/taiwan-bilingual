About · 學校簡介

A name built from its villages.

Guangde Junior High School was founded in 1998, making it one of the newer junior high schools in Wuri District. Its name, "光德," is a composite drawn from the names of its multiple feeder villages, directly encoding the rural village geography of the area it was created to serve.

光德國中創立於民國87年（1998年），是烏日區較新設立的國中之一。校名「光德」由其多個學區村落名稱組合而成，直接反映了學校服務地區的鄉村地理樣貌。
