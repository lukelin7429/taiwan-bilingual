About · 學校簡介

A coastal branch that grew into its own school.

Feng-Gang Junior High began in 1975 as a branch campus ("鳳岡分部") of Chu-Pei Junior High, later becoming an independent school serving the western, coastal side of Zhubei. The campus sits near Nanliao Fishing Port, with Fengbi Mountain to its north and the Fengshan River running alongside it. The school operates a Maker Education & Technology Center and has been recognized among the nation's "Top 100 Quality Junior High Schools."

鳳岡國中於1975年以「鳳岡分部」之姿自竹北國中分出，後獨立設校，服務竹北西側沿海地區。校址鄰近南寮漁港，北倚鳳鼻山，鳳山溪流經校旁。學校設有自造教育科技中心，並曾獲選為全國「優質國中百大」之一。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🏆 Top 100 Quality Junior High

優質國中百大

Feng-Gang has been recognized among the nation's "Top 100 Quality Junior High Schools," a mark of distinction for a school that began as a branch campus.

🌊 A Setting Between Mountain and River

鳳鼻山與鳳山溪環抱

The campus sits with Fengbi Mountain to its north and the Fengshan River running alongside it, near Nanliao Fishing Port on Zhubei's coastal edge.

自造教育科技中心

Like several of Zhubei's junior highs, Feng-Gang runs a Maker Education & Technology Center, giving students hands-on access to STEM and fabrication tools.

Local Culture · 在地文化

The quieter, coastal side of Zhubei.

⛵

Feng-Gang's coastal, semi-rural location near Nanliao Fishing Port represents the quieter, still-agricultural side of Zhubei, contrasting with the dense tech-commuter development around the county government and High Speed Rail station areas.
