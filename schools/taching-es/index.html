Daxing Elementary School

大興國民小學

A historic public elementary school in Fuxing Township, reborn as a small forest-themed, soccer-focused campus with Changhua County's first artificial turf field.

About · 學校簡介

An old school reborn as a small forest.

Daxing Elementary was established during Taiwan's Japanese colonial period as a public school near a former airfield and artillery fortification in Waibu, Fuxing Township. Its exact founding year is not documented in available records, but the school has marked a long string of milestones with published anniversary yearbooks for its 50th, 70th, 75th, 80th, 85th, and 90th anniversaries — suggesting roots stretching back to the early-to-mid 1930s. As of 2022, the school reinvented itself as a small forest-themed, ecology- and soccer-focused campus with 12 classes spread across more than 4 hectares of grounds, including Changhua County's first artificial turf soccer field.

大興國小創設於日治時期，校址鄰近福興鄉外埔一處舊機場及砲台遺跡。確切創校年份未見於現有紀錄，但學校陸續發行了50週年、70週年、75週年、80週年、85週年及90週年校慶紀念特刊，推測創校年代約在1930年代初中期。截至2022年，學校轉型為小森林生態、足球特色學校，佔地逾4公頃，設有12班，並擁有彰化縣第一座人工草皮足球場。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

⚽ Changhua's First Turf Field

全縣首座人工草皮球場

Daxing is home to Changhua County's first artificial turf soccer field, anchoring the school's identity as a soccer-focused campus for its students.

🌲 A Small Forest Campus

小森林生態學校

Spanning more than 4 hectares, the redeveloped campus has embraced an ecology-focused, forest-themed design for its 12 classes of students.

🎉 Nearly a Century of Anniversaries

悠久校史紀念特刊

The school has published yearbooks celebrating its 50th, 70th, 75th, 80th, 85th, and 90th anniversaries, reflecting deep roots dating to the Japanese colonial era.

🏯 Neighboring Historic Sites

鄰近歷史古蹟

The campus sits near a former airfield and artillery fortification from the colonial era, as well as the historic Meijing Hall and a Qingshui Zushi temple.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Waibu's layered history.

⛩️

The area surrounding Daxing Elementary, in Waibu, Fuxing Township, holds notable heritage sites including the Meijing Hall (梅鏡舉人梁濟時公館), a historic rice mill, and a Qingshui Zushi temple — reflecting the school's rootedness in a historic agricultural community layered with colonial-era military remnants.
