Fengtian Elementary School

豐田國民小學

A spacious, rural-edge elementary school on Fengyuan's outskirts, spread across a campus of more than 34,000 square meters.

座落於豐原邊陲的寬闊校園，佔地逾34,292平方公尺。

About · 學校簡介

Room to grow on Fengyuan's rural edge.

Fengtian Elementary School became independent from Nanyang Elementary School in 1961. Located on the rural outskirts of Fengyuan, its campus spans roughly 34,292 square meters — an unusually spacious, quiet setting that reflects the still-agricultural character of the area it was built to serve.

豐田國小於民國50年（1961年）自南陽國小獨立設校。校址位於豐原邊陲的鄉村地帶，佔地約34,292平方公尺，寬闊靜謐的校園環境，反映出其所服務地區至今仍保有的農業地貌。

Highlights · 學校特色

What makes this school worth knowing.

🌾 A Rural Campus on Fengyuan's Edge

座落豐原邊陲鄉村

Fengtian sits on the rural outskirts of Fengyuan, in an area that has retained much of its agricultural character.

🏞️ An Unusually Spacious Campus

校地寬闊

The school's roughly 34,292-square-meter campus is notably spacious and quiet compared to Fengyuan's more built-up central schools.

🌱 Independent Since 1961

1961年獨立設校

Fengtian became a fully independent school in 1961, having previously been part of nearby Nanyang Elementary School.

Local Culture · 在地文化

Fengyuan's agricultural fringe.

🌾

Fengtian Elementary's spacious rural campus reflects the still-agricultural fringe of Fengyuan District, a contrast to the dense commercial core of the old Huludun town closer to the city center. See the Taichung county page for more on the region.
