About · 學校簡介

The village school on Mazu Road.

Xinyuan Elementary is located on Mazu Road (媽祖路) in Xinyuan Village — a name that suggests the school's proximity to a local Mazu temple, reflecting the township's folk-religion and agricultural village character. Detailed enrollment and program records are limited, but as the central village school in a township of rice paddies and banana orchards near where the Gaoping River meets the sea, it continues to serve as an anchor institution for the local farming community.

新園國小位於新園村媽祖路，路名暗示著學校鄰近當地一座媽祖廟，也呼應這座鄉鎮民間信仰與農業聚落的性格。目前公開可查的招生與課程資料有限，但作為坐落於高屏溪出海口稻田與香蕉園之鄉鎮中心村落的學校，新園國小持續作為在地農業社區的重要據點。

Local Culture · 在地文化

The alluvial plain of the Gaoping River.

🌾

Xinyuan Township (新園鄉) sits on an agricultural plain formed by alluvial deposits of the Gaoping River and Donggang River, known for rice, banana, and fruit farming, plus fish, shrimp, and eel aquaculture near the river mouth. As the school in the township's namesake central village, Xinyuan Elementary sits at the heart of this rice-and-banana farming landscape. See the Pingtung county page for more on the region.
