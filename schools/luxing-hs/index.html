About · 學校簡介

Six decades of secondary education in Pingtung City.

Lu Shing Senior High School was founded in 1959 as "Taiwan Provincial Pingtung County Private Dongda Senior High School," renamed "Qingnian Senior High School" in 1972, and took its current name in 1991. A junior high division was added in 1993, making it a complete secondary school offering both vocational and general academic tracks. The school gained university-affiliated foundation status in 2011 and is currently led by principal Ye Chunli, appointed in June 2025.

陸興高中創立於民國48年（1959年），初名「臺灣省立屏東縣私立東大高級中學」，民國61年（1972年）更名為「青年高級中學」，民國80年（1991年）始用現名。民國82年（1993年）增設國中部，成為兼具職業與普通學程的完全中學。民國100年（2011年）取得大學附屬學校基金會身份，現任校長葉春利於2025年6月上任。
