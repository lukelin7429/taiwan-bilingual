About · 學校簡介

A young school with a baseball legacy.

Xingfu Junior High School was founded in November 1975, with Pan Weijian as its first principal — a comparatively young school reflecting Wenshan's mid-1970s residential growth as farmland gave way to new neighborhoods. The school takes its name from its local cadastral district designation, "Xingfu" (興福).

The school emblem features a bat (蝙蝠), a homophone-based symbol of good fortune referencing "福" in the school's name. In 2009, Xingfu established Wenshan District's first youth baseball team, and baseball has since become the school's signature program.

興福國中創立於1975年（民國64年）11月，首任校長潘維健。作為一所相對年輕的學校，其創校反映了1970年代中期文山區農地逐漸轉為新興社區的都市化過程。校名取自當地地籍分區「興福」。

校徽以蝙蝠圖案為主視覺，取其與「福」諧音，象徵吉祥如意。2009年（民國98年），興福國中成立文山區第一支少棒隊，棒球自此成為學校的招牌特色。

A 1970s neighborhood school.

As a comparatively young school founded in 1975, Xingfu Junior High reflects Wenshan District's mid-1970s residential growth, a period when farmland across the district steadily gave way to the dense neighborhoods that define much of Wenshan today.
