Sanchun Elementary School

三春國民小學

A century-old elementary school drawing students from five villages, in a township known as the hometown of jasmine flowers.

About · 學校簡介

A hundred years of schooling for five villages.

Sanchun Elementary was founded in 1923, making it one of the older schools in the Huatan area, and celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2023. The school draws students from five neighboring villages — Sanchun, Yongchun, Changchun, Wandong, and Wanya — and sits conveniently near Provincial Highway 74 and County Route 139. Its campus is known locally for lush, mature trees and a well-equipped, elegant learning environment.

三春國小創立於民國12年（1923年），是花壇地區歷史較悠久的學校之一，並於民國112年（2023年）歡慶建校百週年。學校學區涵蓋三春、永春、長春、灣東、灣雅等五個村落，鄰近台74線快速道路與縣道139線，交通便利。校園以林蔭茂密、環境優美且設備完善聞名。

Local Culture · 在地文化

Huatan, home of the jasmine flower.

🌸

The school district sits within Huatan Township, known across Taiwan as "the hometown of jasmine flowers" (茉莉花的故鄉) — tying Sanchun Elementary to the area's signature agricultural and cultural identity. See the Changhua county page for more on the region.
