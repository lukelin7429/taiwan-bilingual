About · 學校簡介

A century-long lineage known locally as 'Nanzhong.'

Zhunan Junior High School's roots trace back to 1914, when it opened during the Japanese colonial era as Zhunan Higher Elementary School. It took its current form as Zhunan Junior High School in 1968. The school once had an attached senior high division from 1952 to 1962, before that division separated to become what is today National Zhunan Senior High School. In 1996, the school established a music class program, and it remains known locally by the nickname 'Nanzhong' (南中).

竹南國中的校史可追溯至日治時期大正3年（1914年）成立的竹南高等小學校，於民國57年（1968年）改制為現今的竹南國民中學。學校曾於民國41年至51年（1952年至1962年）附設高中部，其後獨立為今日的國立竹南高級中學。民國85年（1996年）設立音樂班，至今地方仍習慣以「南中」稱之。
