Anxing Elementary School

安興國小

A public elementary school in Zhubei's High Speed Rail commercial district, its architecture referencing local geography and the county's own pitta bird.

About · 學校簡介

A name — and a design — rooted in place.

Anxing Elementary broke ground in December 2014 and opened for enrollment in September 2016. Its name combines "安" from the old place name "Anxi Liao" and "興" from the Xingli neighborhood, tying the school directly to the local geography it now sits within — Zhubei's High Speed Rail commercial district. The campus design references its surroundings, backed by Litou Mountain and flanked by two irrigation canals, and incorporates a five-color pitta bird motif tied to Hsinchu County's official county bird.

安興國小於2014年12月動工，2016年9月正式招生。校名結合舊地名「安溪寮」的「安」與「興利」地區的「興」，直接連結學校所在的竹北高鐵商業區地理脈絡。校園設計呼應周邊環境——背倚犁頭山、兩側夾帶灌溉水圳，並融入象徵新竹縣縣鳥八色鳥的五彩意象。

🏔️ Architecture Rooted in Geography

背倚犁頭山、水圳環繞

The campus design directly references its physical setting — backed by Litou Mountain and flanked by two irrigation canals that run through the surrounding land.

八色鳥意象融入校園

A five-color pitta bird motif woven into the campus design ties the school to Hsinchu County's official county bird, a species prized for its vivid plumage.

📛 A Name From Two Old Places

安溪寮與興利里的傳承

The school's name preserves fragments of two historic local place names, "Anxi Liao" and "Xingli," even as the district transforms into a modern commercial zone.
