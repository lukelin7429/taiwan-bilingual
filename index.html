Why this hub exists ↓
Our purpose · 成立宗旨

Built for the 2030 Bilingual Nation — and for the teachers making it real.

Taiwan's 2030 Bilingual Nation policy is being promoted intensively across the education sector. But bilingual teaching is still new to many teachers, and confidence takes time to build. We created the Taiwan Bilingual Resource Hub to close that gap — serving schools at every level with practical, ready-to-use bilingual teaching resources.

「2030 雙語國家」政策正在教育界如火如荼地推廣。但因為許多老師還不太習慣使用雙語教學，信心需要時間累積。我們特別建立「臺灣雙語資源網」，就是為了補上這道落差——以實用、可直接上手的雙語教學資源，服務臺灣教育界的各級學校。

Serving schools across Taiwan

What began with schools in Chiayi and Taichung is growing into a national hub. Each campus carries its own town and its own English — and every new school plugs into the same shared resources.

從嘉義、臺中的學校起步，逐步成長為服務全臺的資源網。每一所學校都承載自己的城鎮與英語，而每一所新加入的學校，都能直接共用同一套資源。
