Top of the County at the National Exam会考で県トップ

When the results of the 2026 national junior-high exam were announced, all five of Pingtung County's top scorers came from Nan Jung — a remarkable result that reflects the school's steady, whole-person approach to learning.

Across the graduating class, the school recorded the county's highest number of top-band results, with more than half of students reaching the upper levels in writing. Students credited their success to disciplined revision, steady sleep, less time on their phones, and a daily habit of reading.

Principal Wu Shu-hui pointed to the school's long emphasis on language and literacy — monthly reading certification, an annual literary award and handwriting competitions — and to an education that values character and the arts alongside academics, aiming to raise well-rounded citizens with both an international outlook and a sense of social responsibility.

2026年の国中教育会考の結果が発表されると、屏東県の最高得点者5名はすべて南榮の生徒でした。学校の着実で全人的な学びの姿勢を映す、見事な成果です。

卒業学年全体でも、県内最多の上位層の成績を記録し、作文では半数以上の生徒が上位等級に到達しました。生徒たちは、計画的な復習、十分な睡眠、スマホ時間を減らすこと、そして日々の読書の習慣を成功の鍵に挙げました。

呉淑惠校長は、毎月の読書認証や年度文学賞、書写コンクールなど、語文・読解を長く重視してきたこと、そして学力とともに品格・芸術を大切にする教育を挙げました。国際的視野と社会的責任を併せもつ、バランスのとれた市民を育てたいと語ります。
