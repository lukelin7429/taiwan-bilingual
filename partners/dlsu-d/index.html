🇵🇭 Lasallian Partner · 拉薩爾夥伴 · Dasmariñas, Cavite

De La Salle University–Dasmariñas

拉薩爾大學達斯馬里納斯分校 · 高中部（DLSU-D）
"Empower your child's global journey."

The High School of De La Salle University–Dasmariñas — a Lasallian Catholic institution in Cavite, Philippines — educates Junior and Senior High students in the centuries-old De La Salle tradition of faith, service, and academic excellence. It is My Culture Connect's partner institution in the Philippines.

拉薩爾大學達斯馬里納斯分校（DLSU-D）高中部位於菲律賓甲米地省，是一所拉薩爾天主教學校，以延續數百年的「信仰、服務與學術卓越」傳統培育國中與高中學生，也是人師（MCC）在菲律賓的夥伴機構。

Why we are partners · 為何結為夥伴

Across the Pacific, one purpose

A shared mission · 共同的使命

DLSU-D and My Culture Connect are partners in the same work: language learning, cultural exchange, and educational excellence. Both believe an education widens when students meet the world beyond their own — and both build the bridges that make that meeting real.

DLSU-D 與人師（MCC）是同一件事上的夥伴：語言學習、文化交流與教育卓越。雙方都相信，當學生遇見自身之外的世界，教育的視野才會真正開闊——也都在搭建讓這份相遇成真的橋樑。

A Philippine bridge · 菲律賓的橋樑

The Philippines has long been one of the wellsprings of the warm, dedicated English educators who give Taiwan's students living English. As MCC's Lasallian partner institution, DLSU-D anchors that connection — a meeting of two values-driven schools committed to global, service-minded learning.

菲律賓長久以來是那些溫暖、敬業、為臺灣學生帶來「活的英文」的英語教育者的搖籃之一。作為人師的拉薩爾夥伴機構，DLSU-D 正是這份連結的支點——兩所同樣重視價值、致力於全球與服務導向學習的學校的相遇。

Lasallian Identity · 拉薩爾精神

Faith, service, and well-rounded formation

"Dedicated to shaping well-rounded and future-ready individuals" — through holistic Lasallian formation that joins academic excellence with faith, character, and a heart for service.

「致力於培養全面發展、面向未來的人」——透過拉薩爾的全人教育，將學術卓越與信仰、品格及服務之心結合在一起。

De La Salle University–Dasmariñas is part of De La Salle Philippines, the network of Lasallian schools founded in the tradition of St. John Baptist de La Salle, patron of teachers. DLSU-D 屬於菲律賓拉薩爾教育網絡（De La Salle Philippines），承續「教師主保聖若翰·喇沙」的辦學傳統。
