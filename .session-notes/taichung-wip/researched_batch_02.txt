### 四張犁國小
- URL: https://jres.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1933 (Japanese colonial era — established as 北屯公學校四張犁分教場; became independent school 1940)
- Facts: Name means "four plows" — early settlers needed one plow per five jia of reclaimed land, and this area required four; renamed 建仁國小 in 1970 then restored to its historic name 四張犁 in 1999 by local petition; serves the 仁和/四民/同榮/后庄 neighborhoods.
- Culture connection: The school's very name preserves a Qing-dynasty land-reclamation unit of measure from when Han settlers first plowed this stretch of the Taichung Basin.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區后庄路910號
- Level: ES

### 四維國小
- URL: https://swes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 81 / 1992
- Facts: Approved 1990, opened for classes August 1992; became Taichung City's Hakka Language Teaching Resource Center in 2006; sits near the old locality of 二分埔.
- Culture connection: Its role as the city's Hakka-teaching resource center links the school to Beitun's historic Hakka settlement pockets amid the wider Hoklo-majority district.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區文心路四段956號
- Level: ES

### 大坑國小
- URL: https://dkes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 37 / 1948 (branch of 軍功國小), independent ROC 42 / 1953
- Facts: Small mountain school; located inside the 大坑風景區 scenic area, often called Taichung's "little Yangmingshan."
- Culture connection: The school sits within Beitun's famous 大坑 hiking-trail hills, tying its identity directly to Taichung's best-known nature-recreation area.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區東山路2段112號
- Level: ES

### 廍子國小
- URL: https://buzies.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 107 / 2018 (first classes ROC 109 / 2020)
- Facts: Newest school on this list, built to relieve overcrowding as 廍子里 became one of Taichung's fastest-growing neighborhoods; roughly 26 classes/781 students shortly after opening.
- Culture connection: Its founding traces the rapid suburbanization of 廍子里 — an old sugar-mill ("廍") place-name now surrounded by new high-rise housing developments.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區景賢路290號
- Level: ES

### 建功國小
- URL: https://jkes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 47 / 1958 (branch), independent ROC 48 / 1959
- Facts: About 49 classes and 1,300+ students; motto emphasizes diligence, simplicity, filial piety, respect, loyalty; sits at the foot of the 大坑 hills.
- Culture connection: Located at the base of Beitun's 大坑 foothills, the school's long history reflects the area's rural farming-village roots before urbanization.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區軍功里軍和街43號
- Level: ES

### 文心國小
- URL: https://wxes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 79 / 1990 (approved/groundbreaking), opened ROC 81 / 1992
- Facts: Built inside Taichung's 4th land-readjustment zone along the busy 文心路 corridor.
- Culture connection: The school's founding is directly tied to Taichung's 1990s urban land-readjustment boom that transformed 文心路 into one of the city's major arteries.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區文心路四段575號
- Level: ES

### 文昌國小
- URL: https://wces.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 77 / 1988 (August 1)
- Facts: Founded in response to a population surge in a land-readjustment zone starting around 1981; known for its handball team (has represented Chinese Taipei internationally), ocean/marine education program.
- Culture connection: Its student handball program and marine-education outreach have made this originally rural-turned-suburban school a modest cultural export from Beitun.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區昌平路一段91號
- Level: ES

### 新興國小
- URL: https://xxes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 55 / 1966 (August 1)
- Facts: Switched to school-district enrollment system in 1972; new teaching building completed 2000; Culture Building rebuilt with a NT$230 million renovation.
- Culture connection: One of Beitun's older mid-century schools, its multiple building renewals mirror the district's steady residential redevelopment since the 1960s.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區新興路20號
- Level: ES

### 松強國小
- URL: https://sqes.tc.edu.tw/

- Facts: Newest school in this batch; built in the 14th land-readjustment zone specifically to relieve chronic overcrowding at 仁美國小; ~3.5 hectare campus, groundbreaking September 2022.
- Culture connection: Its creation is a direct response to Beitun's most explosive recent housing boom — the 14th land-readjustment district.

- Level: ES

### 松竹國小
- URL: https://szes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1904 (Japanese colonial era, as a 大雅公學校 branch class held at the 四張犁文昌廟); renamed 三份埔公學校 1913, 北屯公學校 1921, current name ROC 57/1968
- Facts: One of the oldest schools in the batch; roughly 55 classes / ~1,469 students; offers unplugged coding, Scratch programming, AI morning clubs, badminton/table-tennis/abacus/diabolo/lion-dance/track teams.
- Culture connection: Its roots as a 1904 branch classroom held inside the 四張犁文昌廟 (a local temple) tie the school directly to the district's oldest place-name and folk-religious heritage.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區昌平路二段12號
- Level: ES

### 軍功國小
- URL: https://jges.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1920 (Japanese colonial era, as 軍功寮公學校)
- Facts: Renamed 軍功國民小學 in ROC 58/1969; campus totally destroyed in the 921 earthquake (1999) — students relocated temporarily, rebuilt campus reopened 2001; a historic Japanese-era teacher's dormitory on campus is registered as a Taichung historic building.
- Culture connection: The surviving Japanese-era dormitory and the school's dramatic 921-earthquake rebuilding make it a physical record of both Beitun's colonial past and Taiwan's most significant modern natural disaster.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區和平里軍福十三路300號
- Level: ES

### 逢甲國小
- URL: https://fjes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 45 / 1956
- Facts: Named after Qing-era reformist scholar 丘逢甲, whose family developed the 大坑 area; nicknamed "逢甲森林小學" for its deep-mountain setting; enrollment nearly collapsed in the 1980s (down to ~27 students around 1984) when a golf course development emptied the surrounding hamlet, later reinvented around nature-based, small-school programming.
- Culture connection: Both its name (honoring Qing scholar-official 丘逢甲) and its "forest school" character root it in the history and topography of the 大坑 mountains, distinct from the unrelated Feng Chia University in downtown Taichung.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區北坑巷60號
- Level: ES

### 陳平國小
- URL: https://cpes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 86 / 1997
- Facts: Serves the 忠平里/陳平里 neighborhoods near the 水湳/后庄 area; a joint school-gym/community-activity-center building project broke ground.

- Address: 臺中市北屯區陳平路58號
- Level: ES
