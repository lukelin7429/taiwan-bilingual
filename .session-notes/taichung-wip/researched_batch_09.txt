## Dongshi District 東勢區 (Hakka hill town, historic timber trade)

### 臺中市立東勢工業高級中等學校
- URL: https://tsvs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 35 / Western 1946 (as "東勢工業技術講習班," founded by local physician Dr. Liu Jiang-xing)
- Facts: ~1,557 students; 11 daytime + 6 evening/continuing-ed technical programs; campus rebuilt 2004 after 921 earthquake destroyed original site.
- Culture connection: Grew out of a grassroots vocational class founded by a local doctor to train the timber-and-craft trades that built Dongshi's hillside economy.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區東關路6段1328號
- Level: SH

### 東勢國中
- URL: https://tsjs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 35 / Western 1946
- Facts: Long-running arts-talent dance program (since 1981); campus destroyed in 1999 921 earthquake, rebuilt with Tzu Chi Foundation aid.
- Culture connection: Rebuilt with Buddhist charity (Tzu Chi) support after 921, embodying the hill town's post-quake community resilience.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區東關路5段
- Level: JH

### 東新國中
- URL: https://tsihs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 56 / Western 1967 (opened 1968)
- Facts: STEM/robotics focus; hosts Foxconn AI robot workshop for 8th graders.
- Culture connection: One of three junior highs built in Dongshi to serve the town's Hakka farming and orchard families as population grew in the 1960s.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區東環街123號
- Level: JH

### 東華國中
- URL: https://thjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 60 / Western 1971
- Facts: Small remote school (~160 students); serves Damaobu Hakka village plus indigenous students from Guguan/Heping/Xinshe-Fuxing.
- Culture connection: A gateway school on the Dajia River linking Dongshi's Hakka lowland to the indigenous mountain communities of Guguan and Heping District.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區東關路五段500號
- Level: JH

### 中山國小
- URL: https://csps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1916 (originally 東勢角尋常小學校)
- Facts: Over a century old; four-story building totally destroyed in 921 earthquake; AI/robotics/3D-printing curriculum.
- Culture connection: One of the original Japanese-era schools serving "Dongshi Corner" (東勢角), the historic core of the timber-trading hill town.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區泰昌里中泰街88號
- Level: ES

### 中科國小
- URL: https://ckees.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 48/1959 (branch); independent 1961, renamed 1968
- Facts: Recognized "Outstanding Hakka Language School"; Erhu/traditional instrument club.
- Culture connection: Founded by local Hakka gentry pooling funds to buy land, reflecting the community self-reliance typical of Dongshi's hill villages.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區中嵙里東崎路4段92巷16號
- Level: ES

### 成功國小
- URL: https://ckaps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1924 (celebrated 100th anniversary Dec 2024)
- Facts: Offers Hakka mother-tongue instruction.
- Culture connection: A century-old school whose Hakka-language program preserves the dialect spoken by Dongshi's founding hakka settler families.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區東關路五段600號
- Level: ES

### 新成國小
- URL: https://tsces.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 71 / Western 1982
- Facts: Remote/rural designated school; AI-education pilot with Taichung Digital Bureau & Tunghai University; Hakka curriculum.
- Culture connection: A newer school built to serve Dongshi's outlying Shangcheng hillside farming community as it grew in the 1980s.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區上城里上城街260號
- Level: ES

### 新盛國小
- URL: https://ssps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 84 / Western 1995
- Facts: One of Dongshi's newest elementary schools; "one school, one family" ethos; folk-sports programs.
- Culture connection: Established to serve Xinsheng Village as Dongshi's hillside orchard communities expanded in the 1990s.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區新盛里新盛街342號
- Level: ES

### 明正國小
- URL: https://mjes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 41/1952 (branch of Shicheng ES); independent 1961
- Facts: Started as a single wooden classroom in a village meeting hall (1954); rebuilt in reinforced concrete 1971.
- Culture connection: Traces its roots to a village assembly hall, reflecting how Dongshi's smaller hamlets built schooling from the ground up in the postwar decades.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區東蘭路永盛巷57-1號
- Level: ES

### 東勢國小
- URL: https://tses.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1898 (oldest school in Dongshi, originally 臺中國語傳習所東勢角分教場)
- Facts: Over 125 years old; arts-talent (strings/piano) program with national competition wins.
- Culture connection: The founding school of the entire Dongshi area, established at Dongshi's Taian Temple.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區廣興里第五橫街1號
- Level: ES

### 東新國小
- URL: https://tshes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 89 / Western 2000 (as 中山幼兒實驗學校 on the old Zhongshan ES site; renamed Dongxin ES 2003)
- Facts: Built on land freed when 921 earthquake destroyed Zhongshan ES; pioneered Taiwan's early "K-1 unified" school model.
- Culture connection: A literal phoenix-from-the-rubble school born of Dongshi's 921 earthquake recovery effort.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區東蘭路1-1號
- Level: ES

### 石城國小
- URL: https://sches.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1918 (originally 石圍牆公學校)
- Facts: Over a century old; located in the historic "Shiweizhang" stone-walled fort settlement.
- Culture connection: Named for the old stone perimeter wall (石圍牆) built by early Hakka settlers to defend against indigenous raids.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區茂興里石城街182巷26號
- Level: ES

### 石角國小
- URL: https://sjps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 40/1951 (branch); independent 1953
- Facts: Sits at the foot of the Xueshan mountain range; runs its own fossil museum (~20,000+ specimens).
- Culture connection: The school's fossil collection directly showcases the Dajia River gorge geology that shaped Dongshi's mountainous landscape.
- Address: 臺中市東勢區東坑路西盛巷22號
- Level: ES

## Nan District 南區

### 臺中市立臺中工業高級中等學校
- URL: https://tcivs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1938 (originally 臺中州立臺中工業學校)
- Facts: One of Taiwan's historic "eight major provincial industrial schools"; 15+ technical departments; sister-school ties with Germany's Max-Eyth-Schule.
- Culture connection: One of central Taiwan's oldest technical schools, its graduates helped build Taichung's postwar machinery and manufacturing industry.
- Address: 臺中市南區高工路191號
- Level: SH

### 四育國中
- URL: https://syjhs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 58/1969 (originally "Taichung Municipal 12th Junior High"; renamed 四育 1971)
- Facts: Built on requisitioned South District farmland; strong showing in digital-reading and IT competitions.
- Culture connection: Founded to serve the working-class Xiaqiaozitou neighborhood as South District urbanized in the late 1960s.
- Address: 臺中市南區復興路二段152號
- Level: JH

### 崇倫國中
- URL: https://cljh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 50/1961 (originally "Taichung Municipal 4th Junior High"; renamed 崇倫 1971)
- Facts: Over 60 years old; sports-focused academic track; robotics and tech competition programs.
- Culture connection: One of Taichung's original postwar "numbered" junior highs, renamed to honor Confucian ethical values as South District grew.
- Address: 臺中市南區忠明南路653號
- Level: JH

### 信義國小
- URL: https://xyes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 68/1979 (Heping ES branch); independent 1982
- Facts: Named to complete Taichung's "Eight Virtues" naming set for elementary schools, filling the missing "Xinyi" name.
- Culture connection: Created because South District's rapid postwar growth left children in Yongxing/Heping villages walking long distances to school.
- Address: 臺中市南區五權南路325號
- Level: ES

### 和平國小
- URL: https://hpes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1920 (earliest school south of the rail line in Taichung)
- Facts: Celebrated 100th anniversary; rebuilt 1997, damaged again in 921 earthquake, reconstruction completed 2000.
- Culture connection: Reputedly the oldest school "south of the tracks," reflecting South District's position as Taichung's earlier-developed southern flank.
- Address: 臺中市南區復興路二段57號
- Level: ES

### 國光國小
- URL: https://kkes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 40/1951 (Datong ES branch); independent 1953
- Facts: 70+ years old; Taiwan-Malaysia cultural exchange program; soft-tennis team with international competition record.
- Culture connection: One of only two elementary schools (with Heping) originally serving South District before its 1980s–2000s population boom.
- Address: 臺中市南區國光路261號
- Level: ES

### 樹德國小
- URL: https://sdps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 111/2022
- Facts: Taichung's newest South District elementary school, built to relieve overcrowding from rail-elevation and MRT Green Line development.
- Culture connection: A direct product of South District's 21st-century transit-driven redevelopment (rail undergrounding + MRT), unlike the district's century-old schools.
- Address: 臺中市南區復興北路566號
- Level: ES

### 樹義國小
- URL: https://syes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 91/2002
- Facts: Built to relieve overcrowding at Heping/Xinyi/Guoguang schools amid South District's rapid growth; has a dedicated bilingual program and traditional Chinese orchestra.
- Culture connection: Named after Shuyi Village, with the name reinterpreted as "cultivating people, upholding righteousness."
- Address: 臺中市南區復大路11號
- Level: ES
