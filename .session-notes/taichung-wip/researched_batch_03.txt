### 臺中市立文華高級中等學校
- URL: https://whsh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 78 / Western 1989 (provincial school; national 2000, municipal 2017)
- Facts: ~2,000 students; part of the "Taichung Big Five" elite public high school alliance; runs humanities-gifted and math/science-gifted classes plus a dance talent track; originally a girls' school, went co-ed from its second cohort.
- Culture connection: Sits beside the Taichung MRT Green Line's Wenhua High School Station in the university-dense western part of Xitun, reflecting the district's postwar identity alongside Tunghai and Feng Chia universities.
- Address: 臺中市西屯區寧夏路240號
- Level: SH

### 臺中市立西苑高級中學
- URL: https://sysh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 59 / Western 1970 (opened as "Taichung Municipal 13th Junior High School"; complete school 1999, current name 2001)
- Facts: Large complete (JH+SH) school, ~2,700 students across 83 classes; strong athletics tradition (baseball, badminton, taekwondo, swimming); 4-hectare campus.
- Culture connection: One of the older post-war schools in Xitun, founded as the area transitioned from farmland to an urbanizing Taichung suburb ahead of the Tunghai/Feng Chia university boom.
- Address: 臺中市西屯區西苑路268號
- Level: SH (complete JH+SH)

### 中山國中
- URL: https://csjhs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 72 / Western 1983 (began 1980/81 as a branch of Zhongming JH)
- Facts: Nationally competitive baseball program (Little League World Series 40th International Division champions); alumni include pro baseball players.
- Culture connection: Located beside the Taichung MRT Green Line's Chungshan Station near Wenhua Senior High, illustrating how Xitun's baseball culture and new MRT-linked development have grown together.
- Address: 臺中市西屯區寧夏路179號
- Level: JH

### 安和國中
- URL: https://ahjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 83 / Western 1994 (approved 1993)
- Facts: Large 5.2-hectare campus; ~1,300 students; name combines the two founding catchment neighborhoods 福安里 and 協和里 into "安和."
- Culture connection: Built to serve population growth around the Taichung Industrial Park and Tunghai University area, embodying Xitun's shift from farming villages to an industrial/academic suburb.
- Address: 臺中市西屯區天祝街1號
- Level: JH

### 西屯國小
- URL: https://stes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1903 (Japanese colonial era, opened 1904, independently named Dadun Public School)
- Facts: The oldest school on this entire list, giving it the district's own name; renamed several times through the 1947 municipal reorganization and 1968 nine-year-education reform.
- Culture connection: As the namesake school of Xitun District itself, it is the direct institutional descendant of the old Xidaduan settlement that gave the district its historic identity before Tunghai/Feng Chia universities arrived.
- Address: 臺中市西屯區西屯路二段300號
- Level: ES
