### 臺中市立長億高級中學
- URL: https://cyhs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 79 / Western 1990 (senior-high division added 1999)
- Facts: Complete middle+high school (~1,494 students, 49 classes); art-talent program (1996) and bilingual experimental class (2019); fencing/karate/track teams.
- Culture connection: Created when Taiping's population neared 500,000 with no public high school of its own, it embodies Taiping's transformation from a rural township into a populous Taichung suburb.
- Address: 臺中市太平區長億六街1號
- Level: SH (complete JH+SH)

### 中平國中
- URL: https://cpjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 70 / Western 1981
- Facts: Built on former Taichung County government tree nursery land, so campus retains old cypress/pine trees; recorder ensemble won national competition four times.
- Culture connection: Named "中平" for sitting amid Zhongping Village, blending "中" and "平" (from Taiping) as its founding ideal.
- Address: 臺中市太平區中平路300號
- Level: JH

### 太平國中
- URL: https://tpjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 55 / Western 1966 (branch of Shalu Junior High; independent 1967)
- Facts: Original campus badly damaged in the 921 earthquake (1999); rebuilt with Tzu Chi Foundation assistance and relocated in 2002.
- Culture connection: Its post-921 rebuilding, aided by Tzu Chi, is a shared memory point for many Taiping schools that suffered quake damage.
- Address: 臺中市太平區中山路二段116號
- Level: JH

### 新光國中
- URL: https://skjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 85 / Western 1996
- Facts: Large school, ~1,596 students / 57 classes; draws students from seven Taiping villages.
- Culture connection: Serves the fast-growing "Xinguang" residential cluster of Taiping that expanded as the district urbanized in the 1990s.
- Address: 臺中市太平區樹德九街139號
- Level: JH

### 中華國小
- URL: https://chps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 81 / Western 1992 (built on a former public cemetery site)
- Facts: Central kitchen since 1997; early "informatics seed school" (2003); dedicated soccer class (2012), competed in Australia's Kangaroo Cup.
- Culture connection: Located in the "豐年社區" of Zhongshan Village, reflecting Taiping's postwar residential expansion.
- Address: 臺中市太平區大源路1之20號
- Level: ES

### 光隆國小
- URL: https://klps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1930 (Japanese era, as "大平公學校籠埔分校"; independent 1942 as 車籠埔國民學校)
- Facts: Renamed 光隆 in 1968; relocated after severe 921 earthquake damage.
- Culture connection: Its original name "車籠埔" ties it to the Chelungpu Fault, the geological feature that caused the 1999 earthquake centered in this area.
- Address: 臺中市太平區光興路487巷5號
- Level: ES

### 坪林國小
- URL: https://ples.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 54 / Western 1965 (branch of Taiping Elementary from 1952)
- Facts: Began in 1952 borrowing space at the Pinglin Village assembly hall (now the site of Yulin Temple).
- Culture connection: Named for Pinglin Village, one of Taiping's outlying farming communities absorbed into the school district system.
- Address: 臺中市太平區坪林路45號
- Level: ES

### 太平國小
- URL: https://tpps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1918 (Japanese era, as "太平公學校")
- Facts: The oldest elementary school in Taiping District; rebuilt after severe 921 earthquake damage, new campus completed 2001.
- Culture connection: As Taiping's first school, it is the historical root from which several other Taiping schools later split off (坪林、宜欣、建平、東平).
- Address: 臺中市太平區中興路35號
- Level: ES

### 宜欣國小
- URL: https://ysps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 76 / Western 1987 (branch from 1984)
- Facts: 34 regular classes plus 1 resource class.
- Culture connection: Grew directly out of Taiping Elementary as the district's population expanded eastward in the 1980s.
- Address: 臺中市太平區新平路二段100號
- Level: ES

### 建平國小
- URL: https://jpps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 80 / Western 1991 (branch from 1988)
- Facts: Renamed with Taiping's 1996 upgrade to county-administered city, then again after the 2010 city-county merger.
- Culture connection: Its school district covers Jianguo/Jianxing/Ping'an villages, newer subdivisions that emerged as Taiping urbanized in the late 1980s.
- Address: 臺中市太平區鵬儀路 (exact street number unconfirmed)
- Level: ES

### 新光國小
- URL: https://sges.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 56 / Western 1967 (branch from 1959)
- Facts: Moved to a new, colorfully themed campus in 2016 designed around fairy-tale/storybook motifs; distinct from the newer 新光國中.
- Culture connection: Its whimsical, community-integrated campus design reflects Taiping's push to brand its schools as neighborhood landmarks.
- Address: 臺中市太平區新興路200號
- Level: ES

### 新平國小
- URL: https://spes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 86 / Western 1997
- Facts: Built on land the county government took over in Sanbian Village in 1996.
- Culture connection: One of several schools created in the 1990s to absorb Taiping's rapid suburban population growth spilling out from Taichung City proper.
- Address: 臺中市太平區樹孝路336巷9號
- Level: ES

### 新高國小
- URL: https://xges.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 107 / Western 2018 (opened 2020)
- Facts: Newest school on this list; built (~NT$211 million) because neighboring 新光 and 新平 elementary schools reached enrollment capacity.
- Culture connection: A direct product of Taiping's most recent housing boom in the Xinguang/Xinping area northeast of central Taichung.
- Address: 臺中市太平區立功路68號
- Level: ES

### 東平國小
- URL: https://dpps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 83 / Western 1994 (approved 1993 as a Taiping Elementary branch)
- Facts: Sits between Zhongxing East Road, Dongping Road, and Jianxing Road.
- Culture connection: Formed when Taiping Elementary's Dongping-area students "graduated" en masse into their own school building in 1994.
- Address: 臺中市太平區中興東路一帶 (exact street number unconfirmed)
- Level: ES

### 東汴實驗國小
- URL: https://dbes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 68 / Western 1979
- Facts: Converted to an experimental-education school in 2015, running mixed-age classes and innovative small-group co-learning models.
- Culture connection: Named for Dongbian (東汴), a hillside/rural community on Taiping's mountainous eastern edge.
- Address: 臺中市太平區東汴里山田路100號
- Level: ES

### 車籠埔國小
- URL: https://clpes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 89-92 / Western 2000-2003 (first students 2001)
- Facts: Created in part to relocate students from 光隆國小 after 921 earthquake damage; originally conceived as an experimental early-childhood/elementary demonstration school.
- Culture connection: Like 光隆國小, its name references the Chelungpu Fault (車籠埔斷層) responsible for the 921 earthquake.
- Address: 臺中市太平區光明路10-2號
- Level: ES

### 長億國小
- URL: https://cyes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 92 / Western 2003 (opened 2005)
- Facts: ~3-hectare campus, 64 classrooms; shares its "長億" name/lineage with the neighboring 長億高中.
- Culture connection: Named for the same "長億" development area of Taiping as the senior high school above.
- Address: 臺中市太平區長億三街 (exact house number unconfirmed)
- Level: ES

### 頭汴國小
- URL: https://tbps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1942 (Japanese era, as "大平公學校頭汴坑分教場"; independent 1946)
- Facts: Small mountain-area school, self-branded "green elf of the mountains."
- Culture connection: Sits in Taiping's remote eastern mountains (頭汴坑), representing the district's rural/highland character.
- Address: 臺中市太平區北田路20號
- Level: ES

### 黃竹國小
- URL: https://hjes.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: ROC 55 / Western 1966
- Facts: Extremely small school — only about 18 students across five grade levels; self-described "legendary forest school in the mountains."
- Culture connection: Though administratively part of Taiping, the school can only be reached by road through neighboring Dali District.
- Address: 臺中市太平區黃竹里竹村路41號
- Level: ES
