### Tunghai University (東海大學)
- URL: https://eng.thu.edu.tw/ (real English site)
- Founded: ROC 44 / Western 1955
- Type: Private — Students: ~16,000
- Facts: Taiwan's first private university; campus is a designated cultural heritage site and the largest private university campus by land area in Taiwan; famous for the Luce Chapel designed by I.M. Pei and Chen Chi-kwan; 9 colleges / 34 departments.
- Address: 臺中市西屯區臺灣大道四段1727號

### Feng Chia University (逢甲大學)
- URL: https://www.fcu.edu.tw/en/ (real English site)
- Founded: ROC 50 / Western 1961 (university status 1980)
- Type: Private — Students: 20,000+
- Facts: One of Taiwan's largest private universities; strong in engineering, IT, and business/management; runs Fengjia Night Market adjacent to campus, a Taichung landmark.
- Address: 臺中市西屯區文華路100號

### National Chung Hsing University (國立中興大學)
- URL: https://www.nchu.edu.tw/en-index (real English site)
- Founded: ROC 8 / Western 1919 (as agronomy/forestry academy; national university 1971)
- Type: Public — Students: ~16,000
- Facts: One of Taiwan's oldest and top public research universities, renowned for agriculture, forestry, life sciences; spans 11 colleges including Veterinary Medicine, Medicine; 100+ English-taught courses.
- Address: 臺中市南區興大路145號

### Providence University (靜宜大學)
- URL: https://enpu.pu.edu.tw/ (real English site)
- Founded: ROC 45 / Western 1956
- Type: Private — Students: 10,000-15,000
- Facts: A Catholic university founded by the Sisters of Providence of Saint Mary-of-the-Woods; strong international exchange culture (1,000+ international students); International College with English-taught degree programs.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區臺灣大道七段200號

### Chaoyang University of Technology (朝陽科技大學)

- Founded: ROC 83 / Western 1994 (university status 1997)
- Type: Private — Students: ~18,000
- Facts: Taiwan's first private University of Technology; QS World Ranking 1201-1400 band; 5 colleges / 23 departments.

### Asia University (亞洲大學)
- URL: https://www.asia.edu.tw/?Lang=en (real English site)
- Founded: ROC 90 / Western 2001 (renamed Asia University 2005)
- Type: Private
- Facts: Home to the Asia University Museum of Modern Art, designed by Pritzker Prize laureate Tadao Ando (first university-campus museum in the world by him); three academic clusters — Health, Management, Information Technology.
- Address: 臺中市霧峰區柳豐路500號

### National Taichung University of Science and Technology (國立臺中科技大學)
