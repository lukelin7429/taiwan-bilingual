## Longjing District 龍井區 (coastal plain, "dragon-eye well" legend)

### 臺中市立龍津高級中等學校
- URL: https://ljjhs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 90/2001 as junior high; upgraded to complete secondary school ROC 106/2017
- Facts: Established to relieve overcrowding/traffic-safety concerns; became Taichung's 10th municipal complete secondary school in 2017.
- Culture connection: Its creation reflects Longjing's rapid population growth as a bedroom community near Taichung Harbor.
- Address: 臺中市龍井區三港路130號
- Level: SH (complete JH+SH)

### 四箴國中
- URL: https://scjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 59/1970
- Facts: Named "四箴" (Four Admonitions, from Confucius) after the classical maxim on propriety; runs a continuing-education night school.
- Culture connection: Sits atop Dadu Mountain, the tableland separating Longjing from the coast, reflecting a hillside farming community.
- Address: 臺中市龍井區新庄里中沙路2號
- Level: JH

### 龍井國中
- URL: https://lcjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 53/1964
- Facts: Renamed with the nine-year compulsory education reform in 1968; the district's original, oldest junior high.
- Culture connection: Serves the historic town core of Longjing, the administrative and commercial center since Qing/Japanese times.
- Address: 臺中市龍井區觀光路9號
- Level: JH

### 龍井國小
- URL: https://ljes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1905 (as Lishui Togakutsu Public School)
- Facts: Over 100 years old; renamed multiple times through the colonial era.
- Culture connection: Located near the mouth of the Dadu River, embodying Longjing's history as a farming/fishing village shaped by river flooding.
- Address: 臺中市龍井區龍西里龍門路51號
- Level: ES

### 龍山國小
- URL: https://lsps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 57/1968 (branch from 1960)
- Facts: Grew from a single-classroom mountain branch to full independence in 1968.
- Culture connection: Sits in Shanjiao ("Foot of the Mountain") Village on the Dadu Plateau's edge, reflecting suburbanization of Longjing's upland areas.
- Address: 臺中市龍井區山腳里中山一路一段六號
- Level: ES

### 龍峰國小
- URL: https://lfps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1920 (branch; independent 1942 as "龍崗國民學校")
- Facts: Over 100 years old (centennial 2021); sits atop Dadu Mountain.
- Culture connection: One of several "Long-" (dragon) named schools on the Dadu Plateau, reflecting the shared "Longjing" toponymic identity.
- Address: 臺中市龍井區新庄里中沙路新庄仔巷2號
- Level: ES

### 龍泉國小
- URL: https://lces.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: 1920 (as "龍目井公學校" — literally "Dragon-Eye Well Public School")
- Facts: Direct namesake of the district's founding legend (the "dragon eye well").
- Culture connection: Its original name preserves the "dragon-eye well" legend from which "Longjing" itself is said to derive, the most direct link to the district's own name origin.
- Address: 臺中市龍井區龍泉里龍新路162號
- Level: ES

### 龍津國小
- URL: https://lges.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: ROC 11/1922 (branch of Longjing Public School in Sankuaicuo)
- Facts: Moved to its current site in 1923; renamed "Sankuaicuo National School" 1941, then "Longjin" 1946.
- Culture connection: Preserves in memory the old hamlet name Sankuaicuo ("Three House-Clusters"), a Han-Chinese settlement pattern typical of Longjing's inland farming villages.
- Address: 臺中市龍井區龍津里中央路一段165巷50號
- Level: ES

### 龍海國小
- URL: https://lhes.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: ROC 65/1976 (branch from 1959)
- Facts: Located in the district's northwest; became independent in stages.
- Culture connection: Its name ("Dragon-Sea") reflects its position closest to the coastline among the district's schools.
- Address: 臺中市龍井區忠和里中央路三段206號
- Level: ES

### 龍港國小
- URL: https://lgps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 52/1963
- Facts: Classified as a small, remote/"culturally disadvantaged area" school; recently had its aging campus rebuilt.
- Culture connection: Located in Lishui Village near the Taichung Thermal Power Plant and the Dadu River Estuary Wildlife Refuge, tying the school directly to Longjing's coastal wetland ecology.
- Address: 臺中市龍井區麗水里三港路一號
- Level: ES

## Heping District 和平區 (Taichung's largest, mountainous Atayal Indigenous district)

### 和平國中
- URL: https://hpjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 57/1968 (branch from 1967)
- Facts: Now operates as "Taichung Heping Junior High Atayal Culture Experimental Education" — the district's Indigenous-culture experimental curriculum.
- Culture connection: As the only junior high in a district that is roughly 40% of Taichung's land area and home to most of the city's Atayal population, its shift to Atayal-culture experimental education reflects the district's Indigenous identity.
- Address: 臺中市和平區南勢里東關路三段崑崙巷62號
- Level: JH

### 中坑國小
- URL: https://zkes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 44/1955 (branch from 1952)
- Facts: Converted in 2021 to Montessori-method experimental education, becoming Taiwan's first public Montessori elementary school.
- Culture connection: Sits within Atayal traditional territory; its Montessori pivot reflects the district's turn toward small-school experimental education against rural depopulation.
- Address: 臺中市和平區中坑里中坑巷41號
- Level: ES

### 博屋瑪國小 (Atayal Indigenous-focus experimental school)
- URL: https://tges.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: 1949 (branch); independent 1965 as "達觀國民小學"; renamed "博屋瑪" (P'uma) in 2016
- Facts: The first public school in Taiwan to convert to Indigenous-peoples experimental education; sits in the Dakuan (L'olu) Atayal community beside the Da'an River.
- Culture connection: "P'uma" is Atayal for carefully tending a hearth fire so it never goes out — the school frames its mission as "transmission never extinguished" (傳承不滅), teaching Atayal language, weaving, hunting culture, and ancestral songs. This naming legend should be presented as oral tradition, not asserted as literal fact.
- Address: 臺中市和平區達觀里東崎路一段育英巷6號
- Level: ES

### 和平國小
- URL: https://hpps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 35/1946 (branch of Bo'ai National School; independent 1948)
- Facts: Damaged by the 921 earthquake; reconstruction completed by 2000.
- Culture connection: Located in Nanshi Village, the administrative seat of Heping District, serving a mixed Atayal and Han farming community.
- Address: 臺中市和平區南勢里東關路三段54號
- Level: ES

### 平等國小
- URL: https://pdes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1914 (as "臺中州東勢郡鹿洋社番童教育所," an Indigenous-children's education post)
- Facts: Has moved sites three times; celebrated its 110th anniversary in 2024/2025.
- Culture connection: Its original name and mission — a dedicated education post for Atayal children in the Guyang community — makes it a direct historical thread of Japanese-era Indigenous education policy.
- Address: 臺中市和平區中興路三段環山3巷35號
- Level: ES

### 德芙蘭國小 (Atayal-area school)
- URL: https://baes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1916 (as "久良栖蕃童教育所"); long known as Bo'ai (博愛) Elementary; renamed "德芙蘭" (Devilan) in September 2022 after a community vote
- Facts: Has a branch campus (谷關分校) near Guguan hot springs.
- Culture connection: "Devilan" is Atayal for "a place with good mountains and good water, fit for people to live" — the renaming was explicitly framed as restoring Atayal place-identity to a school whose Japanese-era and Mandarin names had erased the original Indigenous toponym. Present this as the school's own stated rationale.
- Address: 臺中市和平區博愛里東關路一段松鶴三巷10號
- Level: ES

### 白冷國小
- URL: https://plps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 42/1953 (branch from 1949)
- Facts: Growth spurred by construction of the Tianlun hydroelectric plant in 1953.
- Culture connection: Named for Bethlehem ("白冷," a transliteration used by the Swiss Bethlehem Mission active in the area), its founding is tied to Taipower's mid-century hydroelectric development.
- Address: 臺中市和平區天輪里東關路二段天輪巷42號
- Level: ES

### 自由國小
- URL: https://yyps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1922 (as "埋伏平蕃童教育所," an Indigenous-children's education post)
- Facts: The oldest school lineage in Heping District (over a century old); redesignated a "primary Indigenous-focus school" in 1998.
- Culture connection: Its Japanese-era name and site reflect the earliest formal education effort aimed at Atayal children in the Dajia River highlands.
- Address: 臺中市和平區自由里東崎路二段49號
- Level: ES

## Shigang District 石岡區 (small Hakka/Han farming district, 921 earthquake epicenter-adjacent)

### 石岡國中
- URL: https://sgjhs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 55/1966
- Facts: The district's only junior high; devastated by the 921 earthquake; sister-school ties with schools in Japan and South Korea.
- Culture connection: As Shigang's sole JH, it anchors a small Hakka-influenced farming town whose identity is marked by both its 921 earthquake recovery and its Dajia River setting.
- Address: 臺中市石岡區梅子里豐勢路國中巷1號
- Level: JH

### 土牛國小
- URL: https://tnps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 10/1921 (branch; independent naming 1945)
- Facts: Campus reconstruction after the 921 earthquake completed in phases (1991–2003).
- Culture connection: "Tuniu" (土牛, "earthen ox") refers to the Qing-dynasty earthen mounds built to mark the boundary between Han settlement and Indigenous territory — one of Taiwan's most concrete surviving traces of the 18th-century frontier line.
- Address: 臺中市石岡區土牛里豐勢路308號
- Level: ES

### 石岡國小
- URL: https://skaps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1899 (first held in the Lin family ancestral hall)
- Facts: Campus totally destroyed in the 921 earthquake; rebuilt with Tzu Chi Foundation sponsorship, reopening in 2001.
- Culture connection: One of the oldest schools in the Dajia River valley, its post-921 rebuilding is central to Shigang's modern identity as an earthquake-recovery and eco-tourism community.
- Address: 臺中市石岡區石岡里石岡街123號
- Level: ES
