## Wufeng District 霧峰區

### 臺中市立霧峰農業工業高級中等學校
- URL: https://wufai.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 44 / Western 1955
- Facts: Vocational senior high offering agriculture, machinery, electrical, electronics, automotive, and food-processing/culinary tracks.
- Culture connection: Founded as an agricultural vocational school, it reflects Wufeng's historic identity as a farming basin before the district became known for Chaoyang and Asia universities.
- Address: 臺中市霧峰區中正路1222號
- Level: SH (vocational)

### 霧峰國中
- URL: https://wfjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 55/56 / Western 1966/1967
- Facts: Campus sits amid rice paddies at the confluence of Qian Creek and Caohu Creek.
- Culture connection: Its site on the plain between two converging creeks reflects Wufeng's traditional rural, water-dependent farming landscape.
- Address: 臺中市霧峰區國中路110號
- Level: JH

### 五福國小
- URL: https://wfups.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 46 / Western 1957 (branch; independent 1960)
- Facts: School rebuilt after the 921 earthquake with Tzu Chi Foundation funding, reopened 2001.
- Culture connection: One of the many Wufeng schools whose post-921 reconstruction is a direct legacy of the earthquake now memorialized at the nearby 921 Earthquake Museum.
- Address: 臺中市霧峰區五福村79號
- Level: ES

### 僑榮國小
- URL: https://cres.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: ROC 49 / Western 1960 (built with overseas-Chinese donations after the 1959 flood)
- Facts: Totally destroyed in the 921 earthquake, rebuilt by Tzu Chi Foundation, reopened Dec 2002.
- Culture connection: Founded through overseas Chinese charity and later rebuilt after 921, it embodies Wufeng's history of disaster and communal rebuilding.
- Address: 臺中市霧峰區 (exact street number unconfirmed)
- Level: ES

### 光正國小
- URL: https://kces.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: ROC 46 / Western 1957 (branch from 1952)
- Facts: Serves Beishi, Nanshi, and Dingtai villages; near the Wu River, surrounded by rice paddies and mushroom cultivation.
- Culture connection: Its rice-and-mushroom farming surroundings typify Wufeng's agricultural plain.
- Address: 臺中市霧峰區豐正路566號
- Level: ES

### 吉峰國小
- URL: https://jfes.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: ROC 85/87 / Western 1996 (approved) / 1998 (opened)
- Facts: Established when 僑榮國小 became overcrowded; often cited as one of Taichung's most scenic campuses.
- Culture connection: Its creation to relieve overcrowding at older Wufeng schools reflects the district's rapid suburban growth toward Dali.
- Address: 臺中市霧峰區民生路349號
- Level: ES

### 四德國小
- URL: https://sdes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1916 (founded by local gentry Lin Jinshun as a private study hall)
- Facts: Straddles the Wufeng–Wuri district boundary; over a century of history.
- Culture connection: Its origins as a private "study hall" founded by local gentry reflect Wufeng's tradition of homegrown, community-driven education.
- Address: 臺中市霧峰區四德路504號
- Level: ES

### 峰谷國小
- URL: https://fgps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 45 / Western 1956
- Facts: A small, remote mountain school (6 classes) beneath Jiujiu Peak.
- Culture connection: Sits directly below Jiujiu Peak, the distinctive bare-ridged mountain range that is one of Wufeng's most iconic natural landmarks.
- Address: 臺中市霧峰區峰谷路496號
- Level: ES

### 復興國小
- URL: https://fses.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 45 / Western 1956
- Facts: Built alongside Guangfu New Village, the former Taiwan Provincial Government dormitory community.
- Culture connection: Its founding is inseparable from Guangfu New Village, the mid-century provincial-government housing estate that is now a well-known heritage/arts tourist site in Wufeng.
- Address: 臺中市霧峰區信義路50號
- Level: ES

### 桐林國小
- URL: https://tles.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 39/49 / Western 1950 (branch) / 1960 (independent)
- Facts: A small mountain "forest school" that reinvented itself as a nationally recognized innovative rural school (featured in "2019 Education Innovation 100").
- Culture connection: Nearly closed due to rural depopulation, its turnaround into a celebrated "forest school" mirrors Wufeng's broader shift from agriculture toward eco-tourism.
- Address: 臺中市霧峰區桐林里民生路675號
- Level: ES

### 萬豐國小
- URL: https://wfes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1915 (independent 1933 as "萬斗六公學校")
- Facts: Renamed several times through the postwar period.
- Culture connection: Its original name "萬斗六" preserves the old Hoklo/Pingpu place-name for this part of Wufeng, predating the Japanese-era renaming to "萬豐."
- Address: 臺中市霧峰區萬豐村中正路224號
- Level: ES

### 霧峰國小
- URL: https://wfps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1898
- Facts: One of Wufeng's oldest schools (120+ years); large campus with ~1,456 students; rebuilt by Tzu Chi Foundation after the 921 earthquake, reopened 2001.
- Culture connection: As Wufeng's original public school, it stands near the historic town center associated with the prominent Wufeng Lin family, the district's most famous historical clan.
- Address: 臺中市霧峰區中正路736號
- Level: ES

## Daya District 大雅區

### 大華國中
- URL: https://dhjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 81 / Western 1992 (first students 1994)
- Facts: Established an art-talent program starting around 2009–2010.
- Culture connection: Created to relieve overcrowding at the original Daya Junior High, its founding reflects Daya's rapid suburban growth.
- Address: 臺中市大雅區雅環路二段301號
- Level: JH

### 大雅國中
- URL: https://tyjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 51 / Western 1962
- Facts: Daya's original, longer-established junior high (predating 大華國中 by three decades).
- Culture connection: As Daya's founding junior high, it grew alongside Daya's transformation from a rural scallion-farming township into a satellite urban district.
- Address: 臺中市大雅區學府路280號
- Level: JH

### 三和國小
- URL: https://shes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 46 / Western 1957
- Facts: 36 classes, ~775 students.
- Culture connection: Located in the Magangcuo area, an old Hakka/Hoklo settlement name reflecting Daya's long farming-village history.
- Address: 臺中市大雅區三和里馬岡厝 (exact street number unconfirmed)
- Level: ES

### 上楓國小
- URL: https://sfes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 37/54 / Western 1948 (branch) / 1965 (independent)
- Facts: Originated as a branch of Daya Elementary School before independence in 1965.
- Culture connection: Its origin as a branch split off from the historic Daya Elementary School reflects the postwar population growth across Daya's rural villages.
- Address: 臺中市大雅區上楓里楓林街232號
- Level: ES

### 六寶國小
- URL: https://lbes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 92 / Western 2003
- Facts: One of the newest schools in Daya, created under a "small class, small school" policy.
- Culture connection: Its 2003 founding reflects Daya's more recent residential growth as Taichung's western plain continued to suburbanize.
- Address: 臺中市大雅區光復路15之2號
- Level: ES

### 大明國小
- URL: https://tmes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: circa ROC 50 / Western 1961
- Facts: Located on Zhongqing Rd., a major arterial connecting toward Taichung's Central Taiwan Science Park.
- Culture connection: Its location places it along Daya's modern corridor toward the Central Taiwan Science Park, illustrating the district's shift from farmland to tech-adjacent development.
- Address: 臺中市大雅區中清路四段158號
- Level: ES

### 大雅國小
- URL: https://tyaes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1898 (as "Bazi Public School" at Yongxing Temple; renamed "Daya Public School" 1921)
- Facts: One of the oldest and largest schools in the district (120+ years).
- Culture connection: Founded at Yongxing Temple, a historic local shrine, the school is directly tied to Daya's oldest religious and community gathering site.
- Address: 臺中市大雅區二和村學府路230號
- Level: ES

### 文雅國小
- URL: https://wyes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 83 / Western 1994
- Facts: A relatively young school; groundbreaking 1995, building completed 1996–1997.
- Culture connection: One of the newer schools built as Daya urbanized in the 1990s.
- Address: 臺中市大雅區文雅村中山北路235號
- Level: ES

### 汝鎏國小
- URL: https://rles.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1950 (as Air Force branch); independent ROC 58/1969
- Facts: Originally "Air Force Taichung Children's School, Gongguan Branch," set up near Ching Chuan Kang Air Base; relocated in 1996 due to aircraft noise/safety.
- Culture connection: Its founding by the Air Force and proximity to Ching Chuan Kang Air Base reflect Daya's decades-long identity as home to one of Taiwan's most important military airfields.
- Address: 臺中市大雅區秀山里平和路238號
- Level: ES

### 陽明國小
- URL: https://ymies.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 48 / Western 1959 (independent from Daya Elementary's Xiushan branch)
- Facts: A small rural school (about 10 classes) near the Central Taiwan Science Park.
- Culture connection: Located in Xiushan village near Ching Chuan Kang airbase and the science park, it reflects Daya's dual character as both an old farming settlement and a modern tech/military-adjacent area.
- Address: 臺中市大雅區秀山村秀山路365號
- Level: ES
