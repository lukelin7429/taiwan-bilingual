## Bei District 北區

### 臺中市立臺中第一高級中等學校
- URL: https://tcfsh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1915
- Facts: Founded by five Taiwanese Han gentry as the first middle school in Taiwan built specifically for Taiwanese students under colonial rule; widely regarded as Taiwan's single most prestigious public boys' high school.
- Culture connection: Its founding by public subscription among Taiwanese notables symbolizes the early-20th-century Taiwanese elite's push for self-determined education, and gave its name to the "一中商圈" shopping district around campus.
- Address: 臺中市北區育才街2號
- Level: SH

### 臺中市立臺中第二高級中等學校
- URL: https://tcssh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1922
- Facts: Established as "臺中州立臺中第二中學校" to educate Japanese settler children, as a counterpart to 一中; merged after WWII with the parallel girls' school.
- Culture connection: Its founding embodies the ethnically segregated schooling policy of Japanese-era Taichung, the historical mirror-image of 台中一中.
- Address: 臺中市北區英士路109號
- Level: SH

### 五權國中
- URL: https://wcjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 59 / 1970 (renamed 1971)
- Facts: Has a dedicated art class track; built on the former 建國國小 school site.
- Culture connection: Sits in North District's dense historic urban core near the 一中商圈.
- Address: 臺中市北區英才路1號
- Level: JH

### 立人國中
- URL: https://ljjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 80 / 1991
- Facts: Grew to a 40-classroom-scale campus after a multi-year construction project.
- Culture connection: Named "立人" ("to establish the person"), following a common naming convention among North District's postwar school cluster.
- Address: 臺中市北區北平路一段62號
- Level: JH

### 雙十國中
- URL: https://ssjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1954 (branch); independent 1968; renamed 1971
- Facts: Home to the first-ever music-gifted class in a Taiwanese junior high (1973); won the "triple crown" at the national student music competition (2016).
- Culture connection: Its name "雙十" (Double Ten) commemorates the ROC National Day and ties to its founding site at the Taichung Martyrs' Shrine.
- Address: 臺中市北區力行路258號
- Level: JH

### 中華國小 (北區)
- URL: https://zhes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 47 / 1958 (relocated 1992)
- Facts: Established after the "8-7 Flood," funded in part by overseas Chinese donations.
- Culture connection: Its overseas-Chinese-funded founding reflects mid-century Taichung's ties to the global Chinese diaspora.
- Address: 臺中市北區漢口路三段2號
- Level: ES

### 健行國小
- URL: https://jxes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 48 / 1959 (branch of 篤行國小 from 1957)
- Facts: Campus site was originally bamboo groves and lychee orchards before construction.
- Culture connection: Split off from the century-old 篤行國小, illustrating North District's rapid postwar population growth.
- Address: 臺中市北區健行路666號
- Level: ES

### 太平國小 (北區)
- URL: https://tpes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1918
- Facts: One of the oldest schools in North District; extended to a full 6-year public school in 1922.
- Culture connection: One of the earliest Japanese-era public schools in the Taichung basin.
- Address: 臺中市北區太平路74號
- Level: ES

### 省三國小
- URL: https://sses.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 36 / 1947 (Air Force children's school)
- Facts: Founded by ROC Air Force Headquarters on the former site of the Taichung Shinto Shrine; renamed 1967 to honor Air Force Major General Zhang Sheng-san, killed in action.
- Culture connection: Embodies the postwar arrival of KMT air force families and the adaptive reuse of a Japanese-era shrine site.
- Address: 臺中市北區崇德路一段107號
- Level: ES

### 立人國小 (北區)
- URL: https://lres.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 77 / 1988
- Facts: Classes borrowed 健行國小 classrooms before own campus opened 1989.
- Culture connection: Reflects North District's continuing postwar subdivision of school catchments as neighborhoods densified further.
- Address: 臺中市北區北平路一段60號
- Level: ES

### 篤行國小
- URL: http://www.dxes.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: Western 1918 (as 臺中女子公學校)
- Facts: Over a century old; originally an all-girls' school, one of Taichung's earliest.
- Culture connection: An early landmark in local girls' education, founded specifically to educate Taiwanese girls during the colonial era.
- Address: 臺中市北區篤行路321號
- Level: ES

### 賴厝國小
- URL: https://ltes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 88 / 1999
- Facts: Split off from 立人國小's catchment area as North District grew.
- Culture connection: Named for "賴厝廍" (the Lai family's historic sugar-mill hamlet), anchoring the modern school to Taichung's pre-urban agricultural settlement history.
- Address: 臺中市北區漢口路四段168號
- Level: ES

## Dong District 東區

### 國立中興大學附屬臺中高級農業職業學校
- URL: https://www.tcavs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1937 (as 臺中州立農業學校)
- Facts: Became formally affiliated with NCHU in 2014; Forestry Department manages nearly 100 hectares of practice forest.
- Culture connection: Its agricultural-vocational mission reflects central Taiwan's historic role as a major agricultural production region.
- Address: 臺中市東區台中路283號
- Level: SH

### 臺中市立臺中家事商業高級中等學校
- URL: https://tchcvs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1935 (as 臺中州臺中家政女學校)
- Facts: The first technical high school in Taiwan certified as a Google Reference School; consistently top-ranked among central Taiwan's vocational high schools.
- Culture connection: Began as a women's home-economics school under Japanese rule, tracing the century-long evolution of women's vocational education in Taichung.
- Address: 臺中市東區長福路245號
- Level: SH

### 東峰國中
- URL: https://tfjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 54 / 1965 (renamed 1968)
- Facts: Relocated to a rebuilt campus in 2004.
- Culture connection: Reflects East District's mid-1960s population growth that drove creation of new junior highs.
- Address: 臺中市東區仁和路330號
- Level: JH

### 育英國中
- URL: https://yejh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 57 / 1968
- Facts: Located in the 旱溪 reclamation area, immediately adjacent to 成功國小.
- Culture connection: Founded amid the 1968 nine-year compulsory-education reform.
- Address: 臺中市東區育英路30號
- Level: JH

### 力行國小
- URL: https://lxes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 46 / 1957 (branch of 成功國小 from 1955)
- Facts: Named for 力行路, where its school gate was originally located.
- Culture connection: Split off from 成功國小 as East District's population expanded in the 1950s.
- Address: 臺中市東區進化路223號
- Level: ES

### 大智國小
- URL: https://dzes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 48 / 1959 (split off from 建中國民學校)
- Facts: Catchment covers several East District neighborhoods.
- Culture connection: Reflects the postwar subdivision of East District schools as neighborhoods around 旱溪 grew.
- Address: 臺中市東區大智路359號
- Level: ES

### 成功國小
- URL: https://ckes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1944 (as 干城國民學校)
- Facts: Opening ceremony was actually held at 新高國民學校 (today's 太平國小); renamed 1946, moved to present site 1948.
- Culture connection: Its wartime founding and immediate postwar renaming mirror the turbulence of the 1944–48 handover period.
- Address: 臺中市東區旱溪畔 (exact street address unconfirmed)
- Level: ES

### 樂業國小
- URL: https://lyes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 36 / 1947 (as "臺中市私立糖廠國校")
- Facts: Originally founded by the Taichung Sugar Factory to educate employees' children; taken over by city government in 1968.
- Culture connection: A direct legacy of the Japanese-era sugar industry that once anchored East District's economy.
- Address: 臺中市東區樂業路60號
- Level: ES

### 臺中國小
- URL: https://tces.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1923 (branch); independent 1926
- Facts: Originally "曙公學校"; today neighbors 台中家商, 興大附農, the old Taichung Railway Station, Third Market, Zhongxiao Night Market.
- Culture connection: Its location amid the historic train-station/market district makes it a living witness to Taichung's original commercial core.
- Address: 臺中市東區台中路153號
- Level: ES

### 進德國小
- URL: https://jdes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 52 / 1963 (branch of 成功國小 from 1961)
- Facts: Suffered serious building damage in the 1999 921 earthquake and used temporary container classrooms afterward.
- Culture connection: Named for 進化路, tracing East District's postwar growth and its shared origin with the neighboring 成功國小 and 力行國小.
- Address: 臺中市東區進化路135號
- Level: ES
