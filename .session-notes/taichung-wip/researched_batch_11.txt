## Shalu District 沙鹿區

### 臺中市立沙鹿工業高級中等學校
- URL: https://slvs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 41 / Western 1952 (as a private school; transferred to county government 1953)
- Facts: Founded when local textile entrepreneur Li Qing-yun donated land to create Taiwan's only vocational school then dedicated to textile/dyeing-finishing technicians.
- Culture connection: Embodies Shalu's mid-20th-century identity as a textile and dyeing manufacturing hub.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區臺灣大道七段823號
- Level: SH (vocational)

### 公明國中
- URL: https://gmjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 90 / 2001
- Facts: One of Shalu's newest junior highs; recorder ensemble has won national music competition honors.
- Culture connection: Serves the newer, fast-growing Gongming residential area along Zhongqing Rd.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區中清路6段567號
- Level: JH

### 北勢國中
- URL: https://bsjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 78 / 1989
- Facts: Carved out of overcrowded Shalu Jr. High as the town's population boomed.
- Culture connection: Named for the Beishi ("north terrace") rural area on the Dadu Tableland.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區英才路150號
- Level: JH

### 沙鹿國中
- URL: https://sljh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 50 / 1961
- Facts: Oldest junior high in Shalu, originally took over 6 wooden classrooms from Shalu Elementary; later spun off Beishi and Luliao Jr. Highs.
- Culture connection: Its 1960s–80s overcrowding directly produced Shalu's two other junior highs, mirroring the district's rapid postwar growth.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區中正街1號
- Level: JH

### 鹿寮國中
- URL: https://lljh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 78 / 1989
- Facts: Sits on Luliao hill "傍山面海" (mountain behind, sea in front); runs an industrial-electronics certification program.
- Culture connection: Its hillside site typifies Shalu's Dadu-Tableland terrain, looking out toward the Taiwan Strait.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區光華路385號
- Level: JH

### 公明國小
- URL: https://kmes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 49/1960 (branch); independent ROC 57/1968
- Facts: Began as a branch of Gongguan Elementary.
- Culture connection: Product of Shalu's mid-century subdivision of school districts amid rural population growth.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區公明里忠貞路213號
- Level: ES

### 公館國小
- URL: https://kkps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1926 (roots trace to a 1916 branch station)
- Facts: Renamed multiple times before settling into today's form in 1968.
- Culture connection: Its chain of Japanese-era relocations/renamings traces the colonial-era public-school system serving Han Taiwanese children in Shalu.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區西勢里中航路二段1號
- Level: ES

### 北勢國小
- URL: https://bses.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1922 (as Beishikeng branch station)
- Facts: Independent as "Beishikeng Public School" 1941; grew to 26 classes by 1962.
- Culture connection: Named for the Beishi terrace landform, one of Shalu's older rural settlements.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區六路里南陽路376號
- Level: ES

### 文光國小
- URL: https://wkes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 52/1963 (branch; independent 1963)
- Facts: Created so children in the outlying Doudi/Doutan area wouldn't have to walk long distances to school.
- Culture connection: Founded explicitly to shorten rural children's commute in Shalu's Doutan Road area.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區斗潭路文光巷12號
- Level: ES

### 沙鹿國小
- URL: https://sles.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1907 (as Shalu Public School)
- Facts: Oldest school in the district (110th anniversary 2017); first classes were held in a temple; designated Shalu's central school in 1968.
- Culture connection: As Shalu's founding school, it anchors the identity of one of Taichung's earliest-settled coastal townships.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區中正街3號
- Level: ES

### 竹林國小
- URL: http://www.cles.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 49/1960
- Facts: Built partly with overseas-Chinese donations following the 1959 "8/7 flood"; had the largest enrollment on Taichung's coastal strip by 2010.
- Culture connection: Its post-flood, donation-funded founding reflects mid-century community rebuilding on Taichung's coastal plain.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區竹林里臺灣大道七段821號
- Level: ES

### 鹿峰國小
- URL: https://lfes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 41/1952
- Facts: Began as a single-class branch of Shalu Elementary meeting in a village hall; moved to a new campus in 1997.
- Culture connection: Sits on the Lufeng hillside, part of Shalu's upland "Lu-" place-name cluster shared with Luliao and Luyang.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區星河路209號
- Level: ES

### 鹿陽國小
- URL: https://lyps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 114/2025 (opened Aug 2025) — Taichung's newest elementary school
- Facts: Built to relieve overcrowding from Shalu's population boom; named for and located near the historic home of traditional swordsmith Chen Tian-yang.
- Culture connection: Directly named after and sited beside Shalu's famed traditional swordsmith's residence, tying the school to local craft heritage.
- Address: 臺中市沙鹿區保定路107號
- Level: ES

## Xinshe District 新社區

### 臺中市立新社高級中學
- URL: https://sshs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1925 (as an agricultural supplementary school; reorganized into senior high ROC 99/2010)
- Facts: ~15-hectare campus at ~500m elevation; offers academic and vocational tracks (data processing, business management, horticulture, farm management); preserves a registered historic Japanese-era faculty dormitory.
- Culture connection: Its horticulture and farm-management vocational tracks train students directly for Xinshe's signature flower- and mushroom-growing economy.
- Address: 臺中市新社區福興里中和街三段國中巷10號
- Level: SH

### 中和國小
- URL: https://ches.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 45/1956 (split from Dalin Elementary's "Zhonghe" branch)
- Facts: Rebuilt after severe damage in the 921 earthquake.
- Culture connection: Born from Xinshe's postwar subdivision of rural school districts as its farming hamlets grew.
- Address: 臺中市新社區中興街129號
- Level: ES

### 協成國小
- URL: https://scps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 72/1983
- Facts: One of the newest and smallest schools in the district.
- Culture connection: Serves Xiecheng village, part of Xinshe's scattered highland farming community.
- Address: 臺中市新社區協成里興義街219號
- Level: ES

### 大南國小
- URL: https://dnps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 8/1919
- Facts: Celebrated its 100th anniversary with a restored school-history museum.
- Culture connection: Its century of history parallels Xinshe's transformation from subsistence farming to today's flower- and fruit-growing "back garden of Taichung."
- Address: 臺中市新社區興中街47號
- Level: ES

### 大林國小
- URL: https://dles.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 34/1945 (independent as Dalin Elementary ROC 39/1950)
- Facts: Its own "Zhonghe" branch later split off in 1956 to form Zhonghe Elementary.
- Culture connection: Located amid Xinshe's mountainside orchards in Fuxing village.
- Address: 臺中市新社區福興里美林38號
- Level: ES

### 崑山國小
- URL: https://ksps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 79/1990
- Facts: Distinctive arts-focused curriculum — violin, recorder, taiko drumming, dance, roller-skating, woodball, ceramics, painting.
- Culture connection: Brings enrichment programs unusual for its size to Xinshe's remote Kunshan mountain hamlet.
- Address: 臺中市新社區崑山里崑南街崑崙巷5-2號
- Level: ES

### 新社國小
- URL: https://hsps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1899 (as "Xinshe Public School")
- Facts: Over 125 years old; once hosted the district's first agricultural supplementary program (1925) that eventually grew into today's Xinshe Senior High.
- Culture connection: As Xinshe's founding school, it is the literal educational root of the district.
- Address: 臺中市新社區新社村興社街四段一巷2號
- Level: ES

### 東興國小 (Xinshe District) — NOTE: a DIFFERENT, unrelated 東興國小 also exists in Taichung's Nantun District (founded 1990) — township-qualify both slugs
- URL: https://dxps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1926
- Facts: Marked its 100th anniversary; its decades-old "East Building" classrooms were damaged in the 1999 earthquake and rebuilt with sponsorship from Formosa TV.
- Culture connection: Sits on the south bank of the Dajia River, reflecting Xinshe's location on the river terrace between the Taichung basin and Dongshi.
- Address: 臺中市新社區 (exact street address unconfirmed)
- Level: ES

### 福民國小
- URL: https://fmes.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: ROC 64/1975 (split off from Heping Elementary's remote "Baimao" branch)
- Facts: Nicknamed "first school of the Baimao plateau"; campus destroyed in the 1999 earthquake and rebuilt with Red Cross funding; won a national Teaching Excellence gold award for its mountain-field outdoor-education program.
- Culture connection: Its remote plateau location and outdoor-education focus embody Xinshe's identity as a highland farming community once jointly administered with neighboring Heping.
- Address: 臺中市新社區福興里福民16之7號
- Level: ES
