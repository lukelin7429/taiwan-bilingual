## Qingshui District 清水區 (coastal, home to the Gaomei Wetlands)

### 臺中市立清水高級中等學校
- URL: https://cshs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 35 / Western 1946
- Facts: The 1962 auditorium and circular Roman-style science building are notable campus landmarks.
- Culture connection: Sits at the base of Aofeng Mountain in the old Qingshui town center, the historic hub of this former Japanese-era port district.
- Address: 臺中市清水區中山路90號
- Level: SH

### 清水國中
- URL: https://csjs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 57 / Western 1968
- Facts: A comprehensive school offering gifted classes, a music-talent class, an athletics class, vocational/skills courses.
- Culture connection: Built at the foot of Aofeng Mountain, the scenic hill overlooking Qingshui's old town.
- Address: 臺中市清水區西社里鰲峰路250號
- Level: JH

### 清泉國中
- URL: https://ccjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 57 / Western 1968
- Facts: Designated a key boxing development school since 1993, with additional competitive teams in kendo and cycling.
- Culture connection: Located near Qingshui's coastline, reflecting the district's identity as a fishing/coastal community facing the Taiwan Strait.
- Address: 臺中市清水區臨海路27-2號
- Level: JH

### 清海國中
- URL: https://chjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 81 / Western 1992
- Facts: One of the newer junior highs in the district, built specifically to relieve enrollment pressure from Qingshui Junior High.
- Culture connection: Its name ("Qing-Hai") directly echoes Qingshui's coastal geography and the sea that defines the district's western edge.
- Address: 臺中市清水區中央路51-60號
- Level: JH

### 三田國小
- URL: https://stps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1918 (Qingshui's second public school)
- Facts: Over a century old; name combines the merged place-names 三塊厝 and 田寮.
- Culture connection: Named for the farming hamlets of inland Qingshui, reflecting the district's agricultural interior away from the coast.
- Address: 臺中市清水區田寮里三田路4號
- Level: ES

### 吳厝國小
- URL: https://wtes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 89 / Western 2000
- Facts: Self-described "mini school" on Aofeng Hill near Taichung International Airport; bilingual/dual-language education.
- Culture connection: Its hilltop location beside the airport ties it to Qingshui's role as home to the airbase-turned-Taichung Airport.
- Address: 臺中市清水區吳厝路35號
- Level: ES

### 大楊國小
- URL: https://dayes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1920 (relocated to current site 2000)
- Facts: Has moved and been rebuilt four/five times; former site is now inside Taichung International Airport; buildings use traditional Minnan red-tile style.
- Culture connection: Embodies Qingshui's Dadu Plateau terrain and its layered military/aviation history, having literally been displaced by the airport's expansion.
- Address: 臺中市清水區楊厝里鱉海路325號
- Level: ES

### 大秀國小
- URL: http://www.dases.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1919/1920 (independent as Dàxiù Public School 1925)
- Facts: Celebrated its 100th anniversary around 2018-2020.
- Culture connection: Named for Xiushui, one of Qingshui's old agricultural hamlets.
- Address: 臺中市清水區五權路336號
- Level: ES

### 建國國小
- URL: https://ggps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 48 / Western 1959
- Facts: Relatively small neighborhood elementary school in central Qingshui.
- Culture connection: Its name ("nation-building") reflects the post-war naming wave common to schools founded in Taiwan's early ROC period.
- Address: 臺中市清水區建國路10號
- Level: ES

### 東山國小
- URL: https://dsps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 59 / Western 1970
- Facts: Branding itself "the fun-and-beautiful school atop Aofeng Mountain."
- Culture connection: Like several Qingshui schools, it sits on Aofeng Mountain, the wooded hill that has long served as the district's green heart.
- Address: 臺中市清水區神清路6-1號
- Level: ES

### 清水國小
- URL: https://cses.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1897 (relocated to current site 1935)
- Facts: A designated Taichung City historic monument — Taiwan's first-ever campus to be recognized as a heritage site while still an operating school.
- Culture connection: Occupies the site of Qingshui's original Japanese-era school buildings, making it a living monument to the town's transformation from the Qing-era settlement of Niumatou into modern Qingshui.
- Address: 臺中市清水區南寧里光華路125號
- Level: ES

### 甲南國小
- URL: https://jnes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 45 / Western 1956
- Facts: Runs an environmental-education program; small coastal-adjacent community school.
- Culture connection: Its "Jia-Nan" name reflects the cluster of "Jia-" place-names along Qingshui's coastal plain, an old Qing-dynasty land-survey naming convention.
- Address: 臺中市清水區臨海路26號
- Level: ES

### 西寧國小
- URL: https://hnes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 49 / Western 1960 (split from Qingshui Elementary)
- Facts: Newer of the town-center elementary schools.
- Culture connection: One of several Qingshui schools born from the postwar subdivision of the original Qingshui Elementary as the town's population grew.
- Address: 臺中市清水區五權東路50號
- Level: ES

### 高美國小
- URL: https://kmps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1919 (independent 1941)
- Facts: Noted for a rainbow-painted running track that has become a local Instagram spot.
- Culture connection: Sits beside the mouth of the Dajia River next to the famous Gaomei Wetlands (高美濕地), a 1,500-hectare tidal flat and bird sanctuary.
- Address: 臺中市清水區高美里護岸路37號
- Level: ES

## Houli District 后里區

### 臺中市立后綜高級中學
- URL: https://hzsh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1935 (as Houli Agricultural Citizens' School; complete senior high ROC 88/1999)
- Facts: Combines junior-high and senior-high divisions; agricultural-vocational roots dating back nearly 90 years.
- Culture connection: Its agricultural-school origins reflect Houli's historical identity as a farming and orchard district.
- Address: 臺中市后里區廣福里三豐路80號
- Level: SH (complete JH+SH)

### 后里國中
- URL: https://hljh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 52 / Western 1963
- Facts: Relocated to a new campus in Meishan Village in 2005 after a major rebuilding project partly funded by the local farmers' irrigation association.
- Culture connection: The irrigation-association funding for its 2000s relocation reflects Houli's continuing agricultural economy and community ties.
- Address: 臺中市后里區墩西里公安路84號
- Level: JH

### 七星國小
- URL: http://www.cshes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 48 / Western 1959
- Facts: Over 60 years old; recent construction of a new activity center and kindergarten building.
- Culture connection: Named for the Qixing (Seven Stars) area of rural Houli, part of the district's patchwork of small farming hamlets north of the Dajia River.
- Address: 臺中市后里區南村路389號
- Level: ES

### 內埔國小
- URL: https://npps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1908 (established by local resident Zhang Qingyun)
- Facts: Relocated to its present site in 1936; over a century of continuous operation.
- Culture connection: One of Houli's oldest schools, its founding by local gentry reflects the district's early Qing/Japanese-era settlement pattern.
- Address: 臺中市后里區甲後路二段588號
- Level: ES

### 后里國小
- URL: https://houlies.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 34 / Western 1945 (took over former Japanese-era school facilities at retrocession)
- Facts: First principal was appointed directly by the postwar county government.
- Culture connection: Its founding at the moment of retrocession makes it a direct marker of Houli's transition from Japanese colonial rule into the postwar system.
- Address: 臺中市后里區義里里甲后路一段168號
- Level: ES

### 月眉國小
- URL: https://ymps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: circa 1920s (exact year unconfirmed, ~95+ years old)
- Facts: Runs a long-standing food/agriculture education program using its own school farmland.
- Culture connection: Named for Yuemei ("crescent moon"), also known for the Yuemei Sugar Factory and Houli's Flora Expo/theme-park development.
- Address: 臺中市后里區甲后路二段588號
- Level: ES

### 泰安國小
- URL: https://taps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 35 / Western 1946
- Facts: Small rural school (~6 classes, ~114 students) serving Tai'an and Guanguan villages; sits beside the Da'an River and Bao'an Hills.
- Culture connection: Directly adjacent to the old Tai'an Station (泰安鐵道文化園區), tying the school to Houli's railway heritage.
- Address: 臺中市后里區泰安里 (exact street number unconfirmed)
- Level: ES

### 育英國小
- URL: https://yies.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 48 / Western 1959
- Facts: Runs a book-lending/reading program supported by the Taichung city government.
- Culture connection: Part of Houli's network of mid-century branch-to-independent schools reflecting the district's steady postwar population growth.
- Address: 臺中市后里區太平里四月路23號
- Level: ES
