## Dajia District 大甲區 (Dajia Matsu Temple pilgrimage route, rush-weaving craft heritage)

### 臺中市立大甲工業高級中等學校
- URL: https://tcvs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1937 (as "Dajia Agricultural Citizens' School")
- Facts: One of Taichung's two largest high-school campuses; ~1,300 day students plus ~500 evening; offers machinery/manufacturing, electrical-electronics, civil construction/architecture, and special-ed culinary tracks.
- Culture connection: Originating as an agricultural school in 1937, it reflects Dajia's historic identity as a farming and rush-weaving hub.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區開元路71號
- Level: SH

### 臺中市立大甲高級中等學校
- URL: https://djsh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1940 (as "大甲家政女學校")
- Facts: Began as a girls' home-economics school; became a complete middle-high school in 1948; now a comprehensive high school with arts/design and sports-class tracks.
- Culture connection: Its 1946 renaming date is still kept as the school's founding-day anniversary, tying the campus's institutional memory to Taiwan's retrocession era.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區中山路一段720號
- Level: SH

### 大甲國中
- URL: https://tjjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 57 / Western 1968
- Facts: Added an art-focused class (1989), a gifted class (2005), a sports class (2012); runs a "long-green academy" for adult learners since 1986.
- Culture connection: Sited in Dajia's historic core near the Mazu Temple district, drawing students from the town center that hosts Taiwan's largest annual religious pilgrimage.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區育英路186號
- Level: JH

### 日南國中
- URL: http://www.jnjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 57 / Western 1968
- Facts: Serves a nine-village catchment; cycling, basketball, and track teams; sister-school ties with Gyeongseong Middle School, South Korea.
- Culture connection: Named for the Rinan area north of the Dajia River, a coastal farming community historically separate from the main Dajia town center.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區中山路二段69-11號
- Level: JH

### 順天國中
- URL: https://stjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 81 / Western 1992
- Facts: One of the newest junior highs in the district; now runs band, dance, sports, and "excellence" honors classes.
- Culture connection: Built to relieve overcrowding at Dajia Junior High as the town's population grew along the coastal Da'angang Road corridor.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區大安港路67號
- Level: JH

### 大甲國小
- URL: https://tcps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1898 (as "大甲公學校")
- Facts: The oldest school in the Dajia area (124+ years); went through several renamings before becoming "大甲國小" in 1968.
- Culture connection: As Dajia's founding public school, it predates and anchors the town's identity around the Mazu Temple pilgrimage route.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區育德路233號
- Level: ES

### 德化國小
- URL: https://dwps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 51 / Western 1962
- Facts: Small rural school serving Dehua/Fenghua/Jiangnan villages, surrounded by taro fields; a unicycle team that has performed alongside the Dajia Mazu pilgrimage procession.
- Culture connection: Its unicycle team's participation in the Dajia Matsu pilgrimage directly links student life to the region's most famous religious event.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區和平路290號
- Level: ES

### 文昌國小
- URL: https://wcps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1927 (as "大甲女子公學校")
- Facts: Split off from Dajia Elementary as an all-girls school; celebrated its 90th anniversary in 2018.
- Culture connection: Named for and near the Dajia Wenchang Shrine, linking the school to the town's Qing-dynasty Confucian scholarly tradition.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區育德路113號
- Level: ES

### 文武國小
- URL: https://wwps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 51 / Western 1962
- Facts: Celebrated its 50th anniversary in 2012; serves the farming communities of Wenqu and Wuling villages.
- Culture connection: Its Wenqu/Wuling catchment lies in Dajia's rural interior, reflecting the district's agricultural rice/taro-farming heartland.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區文曲路61號
- Level: ES

### 日南國小
- URL: https://rnes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1912 (as "日南公學校")
- Facts: Started with 39 students, now ~555 students in 22 classes.
- Culture connection: Serves the Rinan coastal-plain community north of the Dajia River, one of the district's older Han-settlement areas.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區中山路二段568號
- Level: ES

### 東明國小
- URL: https://dmes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 68 / Western 1979 (branch of Rinan ES from 1958)
- Facts: Small neighborhood school in the Dongming area.
- Culture connection: Grew out of Rinan Elementary, reflecting the subdivision of Dajia's coastal-plain villages.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區東明路93號
- Level: ES

### 東陽國小
- URL: https://dyaes.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: ROC 73 / Western 1984 (split from Wenchang Elementary)
- Facts: Small school — 12 general classes, ~250 students; sits on the hillside east of Dajia town, once poorly connected by road.
- Culture connection: Its hillside, semi-isolated setting east of the rail line illustrates the geographic divide within Dajia between the coastal-plain core and its inland fringe.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區 (exact street number unconfirmed)
- Level: ES

### 華龍國小
- URL: https://hlps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 49 / Western 1960
- Facts: Built with leftover funds donated by overseas Chinese for disaster relief after the catastrophic "8/7 Flood" of 1959.
- Culture connection: Its founding story ties the school directly to one of Taiwan's worst 20th-century natural disasters and community rebuilding.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區公兒路1號
- Level: ES

### 西岐國小
- URL: https://hcps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1930 (branch of Rinan ES; independent 1941)
- Facts: Began as a satellite classroom of Rinan Elementary before becoming independent.
- Culture connection: Its origin as a branch of Rinan Elementary reflects how Dajia's outlying coastal hamlets were gradually given their own schools.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區順帆路96號
- Level: ES

### 順天國小
- URL: https://stgs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 35 / Western 1946
- Facts: Founded right after Taiwan's retrocession, using leftover buildings from Dajia Elementary, originally named "大甲第三國民學校."
- Culture connection: Established in the immediate post-1945 handover period, it embodies the rapid expansion of Chinese-medium public schooling in Dajia.
- Address: 臺中市大甲區經國路168號
- Level: ES

## Daan District 大安區

### 大安國中
- URL: https://tajh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 57 / Western 1968
- Facts: Campus bordered by rice paddies on three sides, with a view of the Taiwan Strait; sister-school relationship with Taipei Municipal Daan Junior High School since 2007.
- Culture connection: Its paddy-field surroundings and sea view directly reflect Daan's identity as a small coastal farming/fishing district facing the Taiwan Strait.
- Address: 臺中市大安區大安港路691號
- Level: JH

### 三光國小
- URL: https://skps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 55 / Western 1966 (branch of Daan ES from 1960)
- Facts: Small rural school in Guike Village.
- Culture connection: Its Guike ("turtle shell") village name reflects local coastal topography typical of Daan's low-lying farmland near the sea.
- Address: 臺中市大安區中松路297號
- Level: ES

### 大安國小
- URL: https://daes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1918 (as "中庄公學校")
- Facts: Only century-old educational institution in Daan District; several renamings tracking Taiwan's political transitions.
- Culture connection: Its original "中庄" (Zhongzhuang) name reflects the historic hamlet at Daan's geographic center before the district was consolidated under the "大安" name.
- Address: 臺中市大安區中山南路296號
- Level: ES

### 永安國小
- URL: https://yaps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 64 / Western 1975 (branch of Haiqian ES from 1970)
- Facts: Began in 1970 as "海墘國小永安分校" with grades 1–4, became fully independent in 1975.
- Culture connection: Split off from Haiqian (coastal) Elementary, reflecting the growth of Daan's fishing-village population along the district's shoreline.
- Address: 臺中市大安區東西四路252號
- Level: ES

### 海墘國小
- URL: https://hices.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1919 (as "大安港分校")
- Facts: Started as a branch of Dajia Elementary (1919), transferred to Daan (Zhongzhuang) Elementary in 1922, became independent as "海墘公學校" in 1929.
- Culture connection: Its name ("海墘," literally "seaside") and origin as the "Da'angang branch" directly reflect its location at Daan's historic fishing port.
- Address: 臺中市大安區海墘里1100號
- Level: ES
