## Fengyuan District 豐原區

### 臺中市立豐原商業高級中等學校
- URL: https://fyvs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 28 / Western 1939 (as private 豐原商業專修學校)
- Facts: One of central Taiwan's oldest commercial vocational high schools; offers Business Management, Accounting, International Trade, Data Processing, Office Practice departments.
- Culture connection: Its founding by the Fengyuan Town credit union and town office in 1939 reflects Fengyuan's identity as Taichung's early commercial and market hub.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區圓環南路50號
- Level: SH

### 臺中市立豐原高級中等學校
- URL: https://fysh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 43/57 / Western 1954 (origin as air-raid-dispersal branch) / 1968 (independent founding)
- Facts: ~45 classes, ~1,500 students; fine-arts talent program founded in 1984 — the first public high-school art program in Taiwan; teaches a required "Fengyuan Studies" course.
- Culture connection: Its custom "Fengyuan Studies" curriculum was built specifically to teach students about their home district.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區水源路150號
- Level: SH

### 豐南國中
- URL: https://fnjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 57 / Western 1968
- Facts: Established with the rollout of nine-year compulsory education; named for sitting in the southern part of Fengyuan.
- Culture connection: Its name directly encodes its position in southern Fengyuan.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區豐南街151號
- Level: JH

### 豐原國中
- URL: https://fyjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1938 (origin as 豐原家政女學校; coed junior high 1946)
- Facts: The oldest and largest-lineage junior high in Fengyuan, with nearly 50,000 graduates.
- Culture connection: Its origin as a girls' vocational school under Japanese rule mirrors Fengyuan's history as the administrative seat of Toyohara/Fengyuan under Taichū Prefecture.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區三豐路一段321號
- Level: JH

### 豐東國中
- URL: https://ftjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 57 / Western ~1968
- Facts: Named for lying east of central Fengyuan; recently received over NT$200 million for rebuilding aged school buildings.
- Culture connection: Positioned on Fengyuan's east side, its catchment reflects the postwar suburban expansion of Fengyuan.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區豐東路75號
- Level: JH

### 豐陽國中
- URL: https://fyjhs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 85 / Western 1996 (first students 1998)
- Facts: The newest and easternmost junior high in Fengyuan — said to be "the first place in Fengyuan to see the sunrise"; runs a competitive sports class.
- Culture connection: Its name and site celebrate the district's geography as the first to catch morning light.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區朝陽路33號
- Level: JH

### 南陽國小
- URL: https://nyes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1943 (renamed 南陽 1947)
- Facts: Now Taichung's elementary school with the largest single-year enrollment.
- Culture connection: Grew explosively alongside Fengyuan's postwar residential expansion.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區南陽路440號
- Level: ES

### 合作國小
- URL: https://htes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 48 / Western 1959 (branch of Ruisui ES from 1957)
- Facts: Celebrated its 60th anniversary in 2019; small, community-rooted campus.
- Culture connection: Its founding by pooling land purchased by the Fengyuan Town Office reflects the postwar cooperative spirit of rebuilding local schools.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區三村里西勢路410號
- Level: ES

### 富春國小
- URL: https://fcps.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: ROC 34/35 / Western 1945/1946 (took over a former Japanese settlers' elementary school)
- Facts: Enrollment fell from a peak of 50 classes to as few as 12 by 2015; in 2017 won a grant to host Taichung's first "Maker Education and Technology Center."
- Culture connection: Its turnaround illustrates how a historic old-town neighborhood school can be revitalized through tech education.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區中山路455號
- Level: ES

### 瑞穗國小
- URL: https://rses.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: Western 1907 (origin as a girls' branch class; renamed Ruisui/瑞穗 1935, coed)
- Facts: One of the oldest school lineages in Fengyuan, tracing back to a girls'-education branch founded east of the Fengyuan Mazu Temple.
- Culture connection: Its origin beside Fengyuan's Cixi Temple ties it to the old Huludun town's religious and commercial center.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區西安街72號
- Level: ES

### 福陽國小
- URL: https://fuyaes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 90 / Western 2001 (opened 2005)
- Facts: Small school (~300 students, 12 classes); built to relieve overcrowding at Nanyang Elementary.
- Culture connection: Created as an offshoot of Fengyuan's 1981 urban-planning expansion.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區南陽路綠山巷83號
- Level: ES

### 翁子國小
- URL: https://wtps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1921 (branch; independent from 1928)
- Facts: Its original 1928 school buildings and Japanese-style dormitory were designated a municipal historic building in 2015.
- Culture connection: The preserved Japanese-era campus makes it a living link to Fengyuan's colonial-era rural school system.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區豐勢路二段290巷3號
- Level: ES

### 葫蘆墩國小
- URL: https://hldes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 86 / Western 1997 (first class admitted 2000)
- Facts: A newly built modern school; named after Fengyuan's old Pingpu-derived town name "Huludun" as a deliberate nod to local heritage.
- Culture connection: Deliberately revived the historic Pingpu-era place name "Huludun" for a brand-new campus.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區葫蘆墩一街250號
- Level: ES

### 豐原國小
- URL: https://fyes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1898 (origin as a branch class; became Huludun Public School later that year)
- Facts: The founding root school of the Fengyuan school system, having spun off Ruisui, Nanyang, and Fengcun as branch/independent schools.
- Culture connection: As the district's original public school, it is effectively the mother school of Fengyuan's entire elementary-education network.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區新生北路155號
- Level: ES

### 豐村國小
- URL: https://fces.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: ROC 55 / Western 1966
- Facts: Founded as a branch split from Fengyuan Elementary.
- Culture connection: One of several mid-century splits from the crowded original Fengyuan Elementary, reflecting the district's baby-boom-era school expansion.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區水源路341號
- Level: ES

### 豐田國小
- URL: https://ftes.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: ROC 50 / Western 1961 (independent from Nanyang Elementary)
- Facts: Located on the outskirts of Fengyuan, spacious quiet campus (~34,292 m²).
- Culture connection: Its name and rural, spacious campus reflect the still-agricultural fringe of Fengyuan it was built to serve.
- Address: 臺中市豐原區田心路二段290號
- Level: ES

## Shengang District 神岡區

### 臺中市立神岡高級工業職業學校
- URL: https://sgihs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 45 / Western 1956 (converted 2020 to complete secondary school)
- Facts: Added a senior-high Mechanical Engineering track in August 2020; still runs its original junior-high division.
- Culture connection: Its 2020 upgrade made it Shengang's flagship for technical/vocational education, tying local youth training directly to central Taiwan's machinery-manufacturing industry.
- Address: 臺中市神岡區中山路627號
- Level: SH (with JH division; vocational)

### 神圳國中
- URL: https://szjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 94 / Western 2005
- Facts: Built as Shengang's second junior high specifically to shorten the dangerous commute along Zhongshan Road.
- Culture connection: Its creation was a direct response to Shengang's busy Zhongshan Road corridor.
- Address: 臺中市神岡區神清路61號
- Level: JH

### 圳堵國小
- URL: https://zdes.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: ROC 50 / Western 1961
- Facts: A farming-community school of roughly 400 students near the Freeway 4 Shengang interchange; strong tug-of-war and track-and-field teams.
- Culture connection: Sitting beside the Dajia River and the Shengang interchange, it serves one of Shengang's more rural, agricultural villages.
- Address: 臺中市神岡區三民路639號
- Level: ES

### 岸裡國小
- URL: https://olps.tc.edu.tw
- Founded: Western 1916 (branch; independent from 1918)
- Facts: Its original wood-frame brick school buildings and flag platform were designated a municipal historic building in 2013.
- Culture connection: Its name "岸裡" directly references the historic Ansali Pingpu Aboriginal community of the same name, one of the most prominent Indigenous settlements in the Taichung basin's history.
- Address: 臺中市神岡區中山路52號
- Level: ES

### 社口國小
- URL: https://skes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 50 / Western 1961
- Facts: Sits at the geographic center of Shengang, right by the Fengyuan-Shengang freeway interchange.
- Culture connection: Its central, highly accessible location makes it emblematic of the district's role as a crossroads community.
- Address: 臺中市神岡區社南里昌平路五段450號
- Level: ES

### 神岡國小
- URL: https://skaes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1902 (opened for classes at Shunji Temple)
- Facts: The district's oldest and central elementary school (over 120 years); survived the devastating 1935 Central Taiwan earthquake, which collapsed 8 classrooms and caused 189 student casualties.
- Culture connection: First held classes inside Shunji Temple, a local folk-religion temple, linking the school's origin to Shengang's traditional village religious life.
- Address: 臺中市神岡區神圳路1號
- Level: ES
