### 國立中興大學附屬高級中學
- URL: https://www.dali.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 86 / 1997 (first students 2001; NCHU-affiliated and renamed 2014)
- Facts: Part of the "Taichung Big 5" high school alliance; ~1,520 students across 42 classes; Science & Math Gifted Program (2011).
- Culture connection: Its 2014 shift from a provincial/national high school to a university-affiliated school reflects Taiwan's broader post-2000 push to link senior high education with nearby research universities.
- Address: 臺中市大里區國光里東榮路369號
- Level: SH

### 臺中市立大里高級中學
- URL: https://dlsh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 56 / 1967 (as "Wuqi Junior High School Dali Branch"); senior-high division ROC 90/2001
- Facts: A complete JH+SH school; severely damaged in the 921 earthquake and rebuilt with Tzu Chi Foundation support; has a Ministry-certified 400m competition track.
- Culture connection: Growing directly out of a 1960s branch campus that had to borrow classrooms from Dali Elementary School, its history traces Dali's transformation from a rural farming township into a dense satellite city.
- Address: 臺中市大里區國中路365號
- Level: SH (complete JH+SH)

### 光榮國中
- URL: https://krjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 77 / 1988 (prep); opened ROC 80/1991
- Facts: Known for its Gifted/Talented program with a strong record of advancement to top senior highs.
- Culture connection: Founded to relieve overcrowding as Dali's population surged in the late 1980s economic-boom era.
- Address: 臺中市大里區東榮路280號
- Level: JH

### 光正國中
- URL: https://gjjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 83 / 1994 (approved); opened ROC 85/1996
- Facts: Renamed "Taichung Municipal" after the 2010 county-city merger; features a rainbow-themed school emblem.
- Culture connection: Built in the mid-1990s to serve Dali's fast-growing residential districts.
- Address: 臺中市大里區鳳凰路68號
- Level: JH

### 成功國中
- URL: https://ckjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 70 / 1981
- Facts: Serves a densely populated area spanning Tucheng, Renhua, Jincheng and Ruicheng villages; notable alumni include national-record shot-put athlete Zhang Ming-huang.
- Culture connection: Initially held classes on borrowed space at Tucheng Elementary School, tying it to the historic Tucheng settlement, one of Dali's oldest Han-Chinese farming communities.
- Address: 臺中市大里區至善路157號
- Level: JH

### 爽文國中
- URL: https://swjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 88 / 1999 (first students 2002)
- Facts: Named in honor of Lin Shuang-wen (林爽文), the Qing-era Taiwanese rebel leader associated with the historic Dali area.
- Culture connection: The school's very name is a direct tribute to 18th-century local history — Lin Shuang-wen led the largest Han-Chinese uprising against Qing rule from a base in what is now Dali.
- Address: 臺中市大里區永隆三街1號
- Level: JH

### 立新國中
- URL: https://lsjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 89-90 / 2000-2001
- Facts: Offers both a regular academic track and an athletics track; weightlifting team won back-to-back national youth championships.
- Culture connection: One of the youngest junior highs in Dali, built to serve residential growth in the area, reflecting continued suburban expansion into the 2000s.
- Address: 臺中市大里區甲堤路236號
- Level: JH

### 內新國小
- URL: https://nhps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 36 / 1947 (branch of Dali Elementary); independent ROC 45/1956
- Facts: Rebuilt after being completely destroyed in the 1959 "8/7 Flood."
- Culture connection: One of the older schools in Dali, its post-war founding and flood-recovery history mirror the area's mid-20th-century transition into a formally organized administrative district.
- Address: 臺中市大里區 (exact house number unconfirmed)
- Level: ES

### 塗城國小
- URL: https://tches.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1919 (as "Tucheng Japanese-language training institute"; independent 1929)
- Facts: Sits on the bank of the Dali River in the historic "Da-Tucheng" cultural area; completely destroyed in the 1999 921 earthquake and rebuilt with Tzu Chi Foundation support.
- Culture connection: Its name and site preserve the memory of "Tucheng" (earthen walled city), one of Dali's earliest Han-Chinese fortified settlements.
- Address: 臺中市大里區文化街120號
- Level: ES

### 大元國小
- URL: https://dyps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 85 / 1996 (opened 1998)
- Facts: Noted as a "top-100 featured school" combining an art-gifted education program with green/ecological campus design.
- Culture connection: Founded during Dali's late-1990s residential boom in the Xiandai Road area.
- Address: 臺中市大里區現岱路 (exact house number unconfirmed)
- Level: ES

### 大里國小
- URL: https://dlps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1914 (as "Taichung Public School Dali Branch"; independent as "大里杙公學校" 1918)
- Facts: The first public school established within Dali itself.
- Culture connection: Its original name "大里杙" preserves the old Qing-dynasty place-name for Dali (a river port town), directly tying the school to Dali's origins as a Dali River trading port.
- Address: 臺中市大里區新興路50號
- Level: ES

### 崇光國小
- URL: https://cgps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 71/1982 (proposed); established ROC 72/1983
- Facts: Split off from the Neixin Elementary school zone; located just south of the NCHU-affiliated high school.
- Culture connection: Its location straddling the boundary between old Taichung City and Dali captures the district's role as a transitional zone.
- Address: 臺中市大里區大明路181號
- Level: ES

### 永隆國小
- URL: https://yles.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 86 / 1997
- Facts: Grew from an initial 12 classes to 59 classes at present, reflecting rapid local population growth.
- Culture connection: Its explosive growth from 12 to 59 classes in under three decades is a direct marker of northern Dali's transformation into one of Taichung's densest residential expansion zones.
- Address: 臺中市大里區永隆五街2號
- Level: ES

### 瑞城國小
- URL: https://rces.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 72/1983 (Tucheng branch); independent ROC 76/1987
- Facts: Damaged in the 921 earthquake and rebuilt with Tzu Chi Foundation aid; serves ~1,239 students.
- Culture connection: Emerging from the older Tucheng school system, it reflects the subdivision of Dali's original village-based school zones.
- Address: 臺中市大里區美村街196號
- Level: ES

### 益民國小
- URL: https://ymes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 79 / 1990
- Facts: Large modern school with 55 classes and roughly 1,408 students; recently added a new activity center, underground parking, and kindergarten.
- Culture connection: Its recent large-scale facility investment reflects Dali's continued status as one of Taichung's fastest-growing residential districts even in the 2020s.
- Address: 臺中市大里區益民路二段48號
- Level: ES

### 立新國小
- URL: https://lishin.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 90 / 2001
- Facts: One of the newest elementary schools in Dali; established a kindergarten in 2012.
- Culture connection: Built alongside the neighboring Lixin Junior High in the early 2000s to serve a newly developed residential zone.
- Address: 臺中市大里區立仁里立新街338號
- Level: ES

### 竹仔坑國小 (formerly 健民國小)
- URL: https://jmps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 45/1956 (Tucheng branch); independent as 健民國民小學 after 1968
- Facts: Severely damaged in the 921 earthquake; rebuilt in part with aid from Hyogo Prefecture, Japan; renamed from "健民" to "竹仔坑" in August 2023 after community petitions.
- Culture connection: The 2023 rename to "Zhuzikeng" restored the school's link to its actual geographic setting — a bamboo-grove hillside area of Dali.
- Address: 臺中市大里區健民里練武路67號
- Level: ES

### 美群國小
- URL: https://mcps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 76/1987 (approved); enrollment began ROC 89/2000
- Facts: Site confirmed 1986, construction began same year, but school did not open until 2000 — a long gap.
- Culture connection: Its unusually long 13-year gap between approval and first enrollment reflects the drawn-out land-acquisition and construction challenges typical of Dali's rapid but uneven urban development.
- Address: 臺中市大里區美群路99號
- Level: ES

### 草湖國小
- URL: https://thes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 41/1952 (Tucheng branch); independent ROC 44/1955
- Facts: In its earliest days classes were held in a borrowed tobacco-curing shed because the school building wasn't finished.
- Culture connection: Its founding-era use of a tobacco shed as a makeshift classroom is a direct trace of Dali's mid-20th-century tobacco-farming economy before the area urbanized.
- Address: 臺中市大里區西湖路32號
- Level: ES
