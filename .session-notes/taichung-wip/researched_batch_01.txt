### 臺中市立東山高級中學
- URL: https://tsjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 59 / Western 1970 (as a junior high, originally "臺中市立第六國民中學軍功分部"); upgraded to a complete junior-senior high around ROC 98/2009
- Facts: ~3,274 students in 97 classes (66 JH + 30 SH); fields ten varsity athletic teams (basketball, volleyball, softball, competitive cheerleading, swimming, golf, rhythmic gymnastics, table tennis, tennis, kendo); original campus was destroyed in the 1999 921 earthquake (sited on a fault line) and had to be rebuilt at a new location.
- Culture connection: the school's roots in the 軍功 (Jungong) area near Dakeng tie it to Beitun's former rural/hillside fringe before it urbanized into today's residential district.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區景賢六路200號
- Level: SH (complete JH+SH)

### 三光國中
- URL: https://sgjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 84 / Western 1995
- Facts: serves eight neighborhoods across Beitun; currently hosts the preparatory office for the brand-new 南興國小 (2026 opening) on its campus.
- Culture connection: as one of the newer Beitun junior highs (1995), it reflects the district's rapid suburban growth outward from the old Beitun core.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區三光一街77號
- Level: JH

### 北新國中
- URL: https://phjh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 54 / Western 1965 (opened on the former private 志成中學 campus; independent 1966 as "第六國中"; renamed 北新國中 1971)
- Facts: ~1,488 students; the only public junior high in Taichung City with a swimming pool (built 1981); runs special-education classes (since 1986), a skills-training track, and a gifted resource program.
- Culture connection: its swimming pool and vocational/skills track reflect Beitun's mid-20th-century growth into a denser residential area needing fuller public-school infrastructure.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區六順東街180號
- Level: JH

### 北屯國小
- URL: https://ptes.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1904 (branch teaching site of 四張犁公學校, sited at the 文昌廟; independent 1913; renamed 北屯公學校 1921, current name ROC 57/1968)
- Facts: one of Taiwan's century-old elementary schools (centennial 2003); during the Japanese era was the only school in the Dadun district to offer a "higher course" (junior-high level); spun off satellite schools including 僑孝國小.
- Culture connection: as Beitun's oldest school, tracing back to a Confucian temple (文昌廟) in the historic 四張犁 settlement, it embodies the district's earliest formal education roots under Japanese rule.
- Address: 臺中市北屯區進化北路與北屯路口
- Level: ES
