Context: Wuqi (梧棲) is Taichung's historic port township — home to Taichung Harbor, whose 1971-era construction and modern expansion drove nearly all of the district's 20th-century school-founding waves.

### 臺中市立中港高級中學
- URL: https://cgsh.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 82 / Western 1993 (upgraded to complete JH+SH, renamed ROC 89/2000)
- Facts: Comprehensive 6-year school; runs a competitive junior-high art gifted class; senior-high athletics track (taekwondo, archery, table tennis).
- Culture connection: The school was literally born of Taichung Harbor's development — planning began in 1971 when harbor construction triggered a population surge in Wuqi.
- Address: 臺中市梧棲區文昌路400號
- Level: SH (complete JH+SH)

### 梧棲國中
- URL: https://wcjs.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 55 / Western 1966 (renamed 1968)
- Facts: Offers a regular division plus an adult evening division and special education classes.
- Culture connection: As Wuqi's original public junior high, it has educated generations of children from the old port town's fishing and shipping families.
- Address: 臺中市梧棲區民生街12號
- Level: JH

### 中正國小
- URL: https://ccps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 45 / Western 1956
- Facts: Founded at the initiative of Wuqi's township head to spare children from four rural villages a muddy walk to Wuqi Elementary; runs a bilingual-arts curriculum.
- Culture connection: Its founding story is a direct artifact of pre-harbor Wuqi's agrarian geography — unpaved village roads that isolated farming hamlets from the town center.
- Address: 臺中市梧棲區中央路二段15號
- Level: ES

### 中港國小
- URL: https://ckps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 81 / Western 1992 (enrollment began 1993)
- Facts: Runs STEAM/robotics clubs, bilingual instruction resources, "Green Reading" literacy programs.
- Culture connection: Its name (中港, "Central Harbor") places it inside the modern residential zone that grew up directly around Taichung Harbor's port facilities.
- Address: 臺中市梧棲區文明街100號
- Level: ES

### 大德國小
- URL: https://ddps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 89 / Western 2000 (split off from 中正國小)
- Facts: The district's youngest elementary school; opened a new activity center in 2024 with an "ocean" design motif.
- Culture connection: Its 2024 activity center was deliberately designed around ocean/harbor imagery, an explicit architectural nod to Wuqi's identity as Taichung's port town.
- Address: 臺中市梧棲區文昌路343號
- Level: ES

### 梧南國小
- URL: https://wnps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: ROC 56/1967 (branch from 1961)
- Facts: Just completed a major reconstruction (2022–2025) including a new building and attached kindergarten.
- Culture connection: "梧南" (South Wuqi) marks it as the school for the town's southern expansion, tracking Wuqi's growth outward from its old harbor-front core.
- Address: 臺中市梧棲區文化路一段6號
- Level: ES

### 梧棲國小
- URL: https://wcies.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1897 (formally reorganized as 梧棲公學校 in 1898; moved to present site 1936)
- Facts: The oldest school in the district, with roots predating Taichung Harbor's modern port entirely.
- Culture connection: Founded a century before the modern harbor was built, it embodies old Wuqi — the fishing/trading settlement and old-street community that existed long before Taichung Harbor's 1970s industrial transformation.
- Address: 臺中市梧棲區民生街45號
- Level: ES

### 永寧國小
- URL: https://ynps.tc.edu.tw/
- Founded: Western 1922 (branch school; independent as 永寧公學校 1929)
- Facts: Serves as the educational/cultural center for three southern Wuqi villages; its original Japanese-era dormitory building is a registered municipal historic building.
- Culture connection: The school and its preserved colonial-era teacher's dormitory sit at Wuqi's southern edge, physically anchoring the district's Japanese-era port-town development history.
- Address: 臺中市梧棲區中央路一段160號
- Level: ES
