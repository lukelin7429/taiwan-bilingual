### 燕巢國民中學 (Yanchao Junior High)

- Culture connection: Yanchao (燕巢, "swallow's nest") sits atop Kaohsiung's famous mudstone badlands ("惡地"/moonscape terrain); also home to NKUST's Yanchao campus.

### 燕巢國小 (Yanchao Elementary)
- URL: https://www.yqp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1914 (ROC 3, Japanese colonial era) as "楠梓坑公學校援巢中分教場"; independent 1918; renamed 1921, 1941, 1968

- Address: 高雄市燕巢區東燕村中華路177號

### 高雄市立新興高級中學 (Xinxing Senior High)
- URL: https://www.hhhs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: April 1 1937 (Showa 12, Japanese colonial era) as "高雄女子技藝學校"
- Facts: Renamed multiple times (高雄淑德女子學校 1938, 實踐商業學校 1942, 高雄淑德女子中學 1945, 高雄市立女子中學 1948, 高雄市立新興國民中學 1968); reorganized into complete JH+SH, renamed 高雄市立新興高級中學 in 2001. Music class affiliated with Kaohsiung Youth Symphony Orchestra.
- Culture connection: Its 90-year lineage as a women's vocational/technical school traces Kaohsiung's shift from a Japanese-era port city investing in female technical education to a modern comprehensive high school.
- Address: 高雄市新興區五福二路218號
- Level: senior-high

### 高雄市立高雄高級商業職業學校 (kaoshang-vs)
- URL: https://www.ksvcs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: April 1 1937 (Showa 12, Japanese colonial era) as "高雄州立高雄商業學校" — Taiwan's third commercial vocational school after Taipei and Taichung
- Common name CONFIRMED: widely known as "雄商" (Xiong Shang) or "高商" (Kao Shang); official abbreviation KSVCS.
- Facts: Renamed 台灣省立高雄商業職業學校 (1945+), 台灣省立高雄高級商業職業學校 (1970), current name 1979; offers 商業經營科/國際貿易科/會計事務科/資料處理科/廣告設計科; ~1,676-1,751 students, 54 classes.
- Culture connection: Founded as part of Japan's 1930s "southward advancement" policy that made Kaohsiung a strategic commercial/industrial hub.
- Address: 高雄市新興區浩然里五福二路3號
- Level: senior-high (vocational)

### 信義國小 (Xinyi Elementary, slug ks-xinyi-es)
- URL: https://www.syps.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1952 (ROC 41)
- Facts: Music class since 1988 with dedicated music-gifted program; large activity center with 400-seat concert hall — one of Kaohsiung's "star" elementary schools for music; ~35 classes, ~1,300 students.
- Culture connection: Sits beside Kaohsiung's Cultural Center and Chengqing Lake-adjacent green space, tying it to the city's central cultural/civic core.
- Address: 高雄市新興區中正三路32號
- Level: elementary
- NOTE: unrelated to a different combined K-9 "信義國中小" school in Changhua County — confirmed distinct, different county and school type.

### 大同國小 (Datong Elementary, slug ks-datong-es)
- URL: https://www.ttps.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1935 (Japanese colonial era) as "高雄市第四公學校," split off from the Third Public School
- Facts: Renamed multiple times; designated in 1975 for a gifted-children experimental class; 2016 partnered with the city government and a hospital to convert unused classrooms into "大同福樂學堂" — reportedly Taiwan's first repurposing of school space for elder social-welfare care; ~276 students (2025).
- Culture connection: One of Kaohsiung's oldest school lineages, reflecting the city's early 20th-century urban core.
- Address: 高雄市新興區大同一路231號
- Level: elementary
- NOTE: confirmed distinct from the "伸港大同" (Shengang District Datong) school tracked elsewhere in this project — different schools, do not confuse.

### 大湖國小 (Dahu Elementary, slug ks-dahu-es)

### 文賢國小 (Wenxian Elementary, slug ks-wenxian-es)
- URL: https://www.wxp.kh.edu.tw/

- Facts: Centennial celebrated with alumni reunions; the "mother school" that spun off both 三侯國小 (1965) and 海埔國小 (1955) as independent branches.

- Level: elementary
