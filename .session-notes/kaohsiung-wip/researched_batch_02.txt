### 大東國小 (Dadong Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.ddp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1938 (Japanese colonial era) as "大東公學校"; renamed 大東國民學校 in 1941
- Facts: Land donated by the Peng family of Fengshan; designated a "national spirit education model school" in 1957; historically one of the three largest Fengshan schools, once over 3,000 students.
- Culture connection: Named for its location just outside the old "大東門" (East Gate) of the historic walled city of Fengshan.
- Address: 高雄市鳳山區大東一路1號
- Level: elementary

### 誠正國小 (Chengzheng Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.tzp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1939 (founded in mainland China, Guizhou) — unusual history, see flag below. Reorganized under Kaohsiung County 1958; renamed 2010.
- Facts: Originally founded by Nationalist general Sun Li-jen and his wife in Duyun, Guizhou (China) in 1939 as the "Army 38th Division Officers' and Soldiers' Children's School," serving war orphans/military dependents; relocated to Taiwan 1948 opposite the Fengshan Army Academy; a branch (力行分校) split off 1996 to become 過埤國小.
- Culture connection: Rare case of a Taiwanese public school whose lineage traces to a wartime refugee/military-dependents' school founded in mainland China before the 1949 retreat.
- Address: 高雄市鳳山區黃埔新村國校巷78號
- Level: elementary
- FLAG: unusual history — founding year genuinely ambiguous (1939 mainland origin vs. 1948 Taiwan relocation vs. 1958 civilian handover). Write honestly with this nuance, don't force a single "founded" year without context.

### 過埤國小 (Guopi Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.guo.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: approved May 8 1996 (民國85年); groundbreaking Oct 1997, phase 1 completed May 1998

- Level: elementary

### 鳳山國小 (Fengshan Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.fsp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1896 (Meiji 29, Japanese colonial era) as "鳳山國語傳習所" — a genuine "century-old school"
- Facts: Converted to "鳳山公學校" 1900; renamed "鳳山西國民學校" in 1941 (to avoid a duplicate name); renamed "鳳山國民學校" 1945; "鳳山國民小學" 1968; campus 27,826 m²; "mother school" that spawned numerous branch schools 1897-1921.
- Culture connection: Sits on the former west-gate area of the historic Qing-dynasty walled city of Fengshan — one of the oldest continuously operating schools in the Kaohsiung area.
- Address: 高雄市鳳山區中山路231號
- Level: elementary
- FLAG: name-collision risk — this school's 1941-1945 temporary name "鳳山西國民學校" is easily confused with the entirely separate, modern 鳳西國小 (founded 1989, see below). Do NOT conflate the two schools' histories.

### 鳳西國小 (Fengxi Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.fxp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: August 1989 (民國78年); classes initially held at 鳳山國小; phase-1 buildings completed May 1990
- Facts: Currently 53 classes (40 regular + 4 art-talent + 2 resource + 4 kindergarten + 3 special-ed); art-talent class since 1992; 30th anniversary 2018.
- Culture connection: Continues the phoenix ("鳳") naming motif; "鳳西" indicates its position on the western side of historic Fengshan township.
- Address: 高雄市鳳山區光華東路100號
- Level: elementary
- CONFIRMED DISTINCT from 鳳山國小's 1941-1945 temporary name "鳳山西國民學校" — genuinely different, unrelated institutions despite the similar-sounding historical name (見上方 鳳山國小 flag).
