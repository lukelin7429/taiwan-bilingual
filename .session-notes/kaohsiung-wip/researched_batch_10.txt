### 龍肚國小 (Longdu Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.ldp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1920 (Japanese colonial era, Taisho 9), originally "美濃公學校龍肚分校"; independent as 龍肚公學校 ~1923

- Level: elementary
