### 小港高中 (Kaohsiung Municipal Xiaogang Senior High School)
- URL: https://www.hkhs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: approved 1990 (民國79年); formally established 1992 (民國81年), first freshman class Aug 1992
- Facts: Campus built in phases through 1998; student-run baseball team founded 2010.
- Culture connection: District name "小港" ("small harbor") recalls the tidal inlet before land reclamation turned the area into Kaohsiung's airport/seaport site.
- Address: 高雄市小港區學府路117號
- Level: senior-high

### 小港國中 (Xiaogang Junior High School)
- URL: https://www.hkjh.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: began 1956 as branch of Linyuan JH; independent 1958 (民國47年)
- Facts: First classes held in borrowed Xiaogang Elementary classrooms; permanent campus built 1957 on land leased from the Taiwan Sugar Corporation's Xiaogang refinery.
- Culture connection: Founding on former Taiwan Sugar Corp land reflects Xiaogang's agricultural/sugar-refining past before industrialization.
- Address: 高雄市小港區平和南路185號
- Level: junior-high

### 鳳林國小 (Fenglin Elementary School, slug ks-fenglin-es)
- URL: https://www.flps.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: April 20 1918 (大正7年, Japanese colonial era) as 台南廳大林蒲公學校
- Facts: Renamed 鳳林國民學校 1951 (spinning off 鳳鳴分校 same year); Kaohsiung's first school with a nutritious school-lunch program (1962); sister-schooled with a Japanese school (1983) and with Hualien County's own 鳳林國小 (2008); final 100th-anniversary celebration Nov 2017 before village relocation.
- Culture connection: Central to the Dalinpu (大林蒲) village relocation story — the community is being relocated for Kaohsiung port/steel-industry expansion (南星計畫); the original campus is expected to become industrial land afterward.
- Address: 高雄市小港區鳳林路207號
- Level: elementary
- FLAG: strongly tied to the ongoing/recent Dalinpu village relocation — confirm whether the school has since relocated or still operates at the historic site before publishing.
