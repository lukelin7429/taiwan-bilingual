### 國立旗山高級農工職業學校 (Qishan Vocational High School)
- URL: https://www.csvs.khc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1928 (Showa 3, Japanese colonial era) as "台灣公立龍肚農業補習學校"; national school since 2000
- Facts: Nearly a century of agricultural/industrial vocational education; multiple renames tracking Taiwan's education policy shifts.
- Culture connection: Qishan was historically Taiwan's banana-export hub; this vocational school trained generations of local farmers.
- Address: 高雄市旗山區旗甲路一段195號
- Level: senior-high (vocational)

### 國立旗美高級中學 (Cishan-Meinong Senior High School, slug qimei-hs)
- URL: https://www.cmsh.khc.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1968 (民國57年), absorbing the senior-high students of the former Kaohsiung County Qishan Middle School
- Facts: ~655-671 students, 27 classes; multimedia design, home economics, child care vocational tracks plus tech-forward/international-education program; college-admission rate >80%.
- Culture connection: Name "旗美" combines Qishan and neighboring Meinong, reflecting its location at the border of these two districts, though its campus is within Qishan.
- Address: 高雄市旗山區樹人路21號
- Level: senior-high
- FLAG: a DIFFERENT, unrelated private school with an almost identical name exists — 高雄市私立旗美高級商工職業學校 (Meinong District, 中興路二段275號). Do not conflate — this genlist entry (qimei-hs) is the public/national one in Cishan.

### 旗山國小 (Qishan Elementary School)

### 溪洲國小 (Xizhou Elementary School)

### 鼓山國小 (Qishan-Gushan Elementary School, slug qishan-gushan-es)
- URL: https://www.gsa.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1909 (Meiji 42, Japanese colonial era) as "蕃薯藔公學校鼓山分教場"
- Facts: Relocated to a new 2.97-hectare campus 2001; recognized as a "life-education model school" by the Ministry of Education.
- Culture connection: Named for Gushan ("drum mountain"), a local hill/village name in Qishan District.
- Address: 高雄市旗山區永和里延平一路111號
- Level: elementary
- FLAG: NOT the same as the entirely separate "高雄市鼓山區鼓山國小" in Gushan District (harbor-side Kaohsiung) — this is specifically the Qishan District school; slug qishan-gushan-es correctly disambiguates.

### 中正高級中學 (Zhongzheng Senior High School, slug zhongzhenggaozhong-hs)
- URL: https://www.cchs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: July 1 1989 (民國78年) as Chungcheng Junior High; became full senior high July 1 1996
- Facts: ~1,109 students; campus 32,400 m²; rooftop solar since 2017; near Weiwuying Metro Station.
- Culture connection: Named "中正" after Chiang Kai-shek, a common martial-law-era school-naming convention; sits in dense post-war urban Lingya.
- Address: 高雄市苓雅區中正一路8號
- Level: senior-high
- FLAG: name collision risk — a completely different "高雄市立中正高級工業職業學校" exists in Qianzhen District, and a separate "高雄市苓雅區中正國民小學" (elementary, 輔仁路100號, see below) — all three "中正" schools are genuinely separate, do not merge.

### 苓雅國民中學 (Lingya Junior High School)
- URL: https://www.lyjh.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1957 (民國46年) as an independent school, originally a branch of Kaohsiung Municipal 2nd Middle School; renamed 苓雅國民中學 1968
- Facts: Runs a well-known Dance-Talent Class since 1981, 40+ years of dance-education history.
- Culture connection: Named after 苓雅寮, a historic fishing-and-farming hamlet near the old Takao Harbor — the district's name traces Kaohsiung's pre-modern fishing-village origins.
- Address: 高雄市苓雅區四維三路176號
- Level: junior-high

### 苓洲國小 (Lingzhou Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.ljps.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: April 1 1904 (Meiji 37, Japanese colonial era) as "打狗公學校苓雅寮分校" — same founding root/date as 成功國小 above, before splitting
- Facts: Moved to Lingyaliao proper 1905 (今成功國小現址); became "高雄第二公學校" 1921; second campus at Guotianzi (today's Lingzhou site) 1937.
- Culture connection: Lingzhou and Chenggong Elementary share the exact same 1904 founding root before splitting into separate campuses in 1937 — historically "sibling" schools, not unrelated ones.
- Address: 高雄市苓雅區四維四路61號
- Level: elementary
- FLAG: shares a documented common ancestor with 成功國小 above (both trace to the same 1904 Takao Public School Lingyaliao branch) — cross-reference so the two schools' history sections don't contradict each other.

Summary flags: (1) qimei-hs is the PUBLIC Cishan school, not the similarly-named private Meinong school; (2) qishan-gushan-es is the Qishan District school, distinct from Gushan District's own 鼓山國小; (3) three separate "中正"-named Kaohsiung schools confirmed distinct (zhongzhenggaozhong-hs / lingya-zhongzheng-es / a separate 中正高工 in Qianzhen); (4) 成功國小 and 苓洲國小 share a genuine documented common ancestor (1904) before splitting in 1937 — write their histories consistently; (5) 旗尾國小 founding year unconfirmed; (6) 旗山國小 street number on 中正路 unconfirmed.
