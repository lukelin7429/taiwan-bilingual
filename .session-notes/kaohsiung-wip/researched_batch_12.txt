### 高雄市立路竹高級中學
- URL: https://www.lchs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1952 (民國41年), originally "臺灣省立岡山中學路竹分部"
- Facts: A complete school (JH+SH); part of the "High School Quality Enhancement" program; varsity teams in soccer, korfball, boxing, weightlifting.
- Culture connection: District name Luzhu ("road bamboo") recalls the bamboo groves that once lined the old trade route between Tainan and the Kaohsiung plain.
- Address: 高雄市路竹區中華路292號
- Level: senior-high (complete school)

### 一甲國民中學
- URL: https://www.yjm.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1996 (民國85年)
- Facts: Relatively young school; bilingual experimental class and sports experimental class; school-zone covers 甲南/甲北/社東/社西/社南/社中/竹園/下坑 villages.
- Culture connection: "一甲" ("first jia") reflects the Qing-era land-survey unit used to divide reclaimed farmland.
- Address: 高雄市路竹區一甲路111號
- Level: junior-high

### 一甲國小
- URL: https://www.yjp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1964 (民國53年)
- Facts: ~318 students; Chinese orchestra club (founded 2002) repeatedly represents Kaohsiung at national competitions.
- Culture connection: Shares the same "一甲" place-name origin as the junior high above.
- Address: 高雄市路竹區甲南里大智路43號
- Level: elementary

### 三埤國小
- URL: https://www.spi.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: not found (historical "mother school" that spun off 北嶺國小 as a branch in 1952)
- Facts: very small rural school (~61 students, 2023); nationally recognized 傀儡戲 (traditional puppet theatre) troupe.
- Culture connection: "三埤" ("three ponds") names the historic irrigation reservoirs that watered the surrounding farmland.
- Address: 高雄市路竹區三爺里民權路7號
- Level: elementary

### 下坑國小
- URL: https://www.xkp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1922 (民國11年), founded as "大社公學校新園分教場," initially borrowing space in the local 福善宮 temple
- Facts: independent since 1946; added kindergarten 1977, special-ed class 1992, hot lunch 1981.
- Culture connection: Its origin story — literally starting in a temple hall — reflects how rural Luzhu communities relied on local temples as civic/educational anchors before formal school buildings existed.
- Address: 高雄市路竹區下坑里太平路384號
- Level: elementary

### 北嶺國小
- URL: https://www.blp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1952 (民國41年) as a branch of 路竹國民學校; became a branch of 三埤國小 1971; independent 1977 (民國66年)
- Facts: UNUSUAL — in Sept 2025 (民國114年) officially relaunched as Taiwan's first public Taiwanese-language (台語) experimental elementary school, nationally covered by BBC News Chinese and local media. Very recent (~10 months old) — should be verified as still accurate before publishing.
- Culture connection: Its adoption as the nation's pilot Hoklo/Taiwanese-medium school ties directly to Luzhu's historically strong Hoklo-speaking farming community.
- Address: not found (only district known: 路竹區北嶺里)
- Level: elementary

### 大社國小 (Lujhu District, slug lujhu-dashe-es)
- URL: https://www.lds.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1900 (民國前12年, Japanese colonial era)
- Facts: over 124 school anniversaries celebrated; solar-powered ecological greenhouse (unique in Kaohsiung); taiko drum team placed 2nd nationally; 路竹國小 split off from this school 1943.
- Culture connection: "大社" ("great settlement") marks one of the oldest Han settlements in the Luzhu plain.
- Address: 高雄市路竹區大社路243號
- Level: elementary
- CONFIRMED DISTINCT from the separate "大社國小" in Dashe District (dso.kh.edu.tw) — same Chinese name, different district, genuinely different institution, same pattern as the 姑山/鼓山 disambiguation.

### 竹滬國小
- URL: https://www.zfp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1922 (民國11年) as "湖內鄉第一公學校竹滬分教場"; independent 1944 as "湖內鄉竹滬國民學校"; transferred from Hunei to Luzhu township 1950
- Facts: featured in a 2019 CNA story about the century-old school reinventing itself around local history.
- Culture connection: Located in 竹滬 (near Zhuhu Fishing Port), a historic Han-Taiwanese fishing/salt-farming settlement on the Taiwan Strait coast.
- Address: 高雄市路竹區竹滬里成功路2號
- Level: elementary

### 蔡文國小
- URL: https://www.tsw.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 2001 (民國90年) — split off from 路竹國小 as "竹西國小籌備處" March 2001, renamed 蔡文國小 December 2001
- Facts: name chosen after local historical research revealed the area was originally "蔡文庄," commemorating Cai Wen, a general under Koxinga who led military-agricultural colonization here.
- Culture connection: directly named for Ming-loyalist general Cai Wen's 17th-century military-farming settlement.
- Address: 高雄市路竹區國昌路548號
- Level: elementary

### 路竹國小
- URL: https://www.lzp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1943 (民國32年) — split off from 大社國小, merged with a 三爺 branch campus
- Facts: parent school to both 竹滬國小 (via lineage) and later branches.
- Culture connection: The district's flagship elementary school, named for and anchoring the Luzhu town center.
- Address: 高雄市路竹區中山路712巷16號
- Level: elementary

### 大樹國民中學

### 溪埔國民中學

### 姑山國小 (slug dashu-gushan-es)
- URL: https://www.gsp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1965 (民國54年) as "溪埔國民學校姑山分班"; independent 1981 (民國70年) as 高雄縣姑山國民小學
- Facts: campus grew from 0.3 to 0.9 hectares after independence.
- Culture connection: named for a local hill (姑山, "Aunt Mountain") in the Dashu foothills.
- Address: 高雄市大樹區姑山里1號
- Level: elementary
- CONFIRMED DISTINCT from 鼓山國小 (Gushan District) — different Chinese characters (姑=aunt vs 鼓=drum), different district, no ambiguity.
