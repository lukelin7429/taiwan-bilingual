### 高雄市立三民高級中學 (Sanmin Senior High)
- URL: https://www.smhs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: July 1996 (民國85年)
- Facts: First intake 10 classes; elite sports programs (baseball, tennis, fencing, golf); baseball team founded 1997 has produced ~30 professional players including MLB player 曾仁和.
- Culture connection: Sits in the 覆鼎金 (Fudingjin) area, sharing its "鼎金" place-name root with several other schools on this list.
- Address: 高雄市三民區金鼎路81號
- Level: senior-high
- NOTE: unrelated to 新北市立三民高級中學 (New Taipei City) — different institution.

### 高雄市立高雄高級中學 (雄中/Xiongzhong)
- URL: https://www.kshs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: April 1 1922 (Japanese colonial era)
- Facts: Oldest secondary school in Kaohsiung-Pingtung region; preserved 1921 "紅樓" (Red Building), a listed historic building; alumni include Judicial Yuan president 戴炎輝, 彭明敏, former Kaohsiung mayor 許水德, current mayor 陳其邁.
- Culture connection: Site chosen after a fire diverted the originally-planned Pingtung campus to Kaohsiung's 三塊厝 hamlet — the same hamlet that gave 三民區 its pre-1945 name.
- Address: 高雄市三民區建國三路50號
- Level: senior-high
- Common name CONFIRMED: universally known as "雄中" (Xiongzhong).

### 高雄市立高雄高級工業職業學校 (高雄高工/雄工, slug kaogong-vs)
- URL: https://www.ksvs.kh.edu.tw/

- Facts: One of Taiwan's "eight major provincial industrial schools"; ~2,400-2,550 students across 10 departments; adjacent to NKUST's Jiangong campus.

DISTRICT-WIDE NOTE: 三民區 was originally called 三塊厝 — three house-clusters founded by Wang/Cai/Zheng clan settlers in the Ming-Zheng era. Renamed 三民區 after 1945 to embody Sun Yat-sen's "Three Principles of the People," explaining why so many schools here (三民, 民族, 博愛, 愛國) carry Three-Principles-era virtue names.
