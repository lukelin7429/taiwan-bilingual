### National Sun Yat-sen University (國立中山大學)
- URL: https://www.nsysu.edu.tw/?Lang=en (real English site — full nav with Admissions, Research, Regulations, About NSYSU, Faculty & Staff, Student Resources, Alumni; English pages for all 10 colleges)
- Founded: 1924 (as National Kwangtung University in Guangzhou, China, founded by Dr. Sun Yat-sen; renamed 1926); re-established in Kaohsiung on November 12, 1980 (民國69年)
- Type: Public — Students: approx. 10,300 (5,040 undergraduate, 5,306 postgraduate)
- Facts: Ten colleges including Marine Sciences (Taiwan's first marine sciences college), Semiconductor and Advanced Technology Research, and International Finance; consistently ranks among Taiwan's top 7-8 universities (QS/THE/ARWU), with particular strength in oceanography and mathematics; campus modeled partly on Caltech (graduate focus) and UC San Diego (construction); located at Sizihwan (西子灣) on the waterfront, with mandatory swimming proficiency as a graduation requirement.
- Culture connection: The Sizihwan campus sits directly on Kaohsiung Harbor beside the historic Cihou Lighthouse and former British Consulate, tying the university physically to the port city's colonial-era maritime trade history.
- Address: 804201 高雄市鼓山區蓮海路70號

### National Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology (國立高雄科技大學)
- URL: https://eng.nkust.edu.tw/ (real English site — dedicated pages for About Us, Admissions, Academics, Colleges & Departments, Scholarships, Academic Calendar, Visit NKUST, Site Map)
- Founded: 2018 (merger date February 1, 2018) — formed from three older institutions
- Type: Public — Students: approx. 28,010
- Facts: Formed by merging National Kaohsiung First University of Science and Technology, National Kaohsiung University of Applied Sciences, and National Kaohsiung Marine University; operates five campuses across Kaohsiung (First Campus in Yanchao/Nanzih, Jiangong in Sanmin, Nanzih, Cijin, Yanchao); includes a College of Maritime and College of Hydrosphere Science reflecting its marine-university heritage; QS Asia ranking 551–600 (2024).
- Culture connection: Its Cijin campus and maritime/hydrosphere colleges connect directly to Kaohsiung's identity as Taiwan's largest port and historic fishing/shipping hub.
- Address: 高雄市燕巢區深中路58號 (Yanchao Campus, main administrative campus); the specific Yancheng District building address was not independently confirmed this session — double-check before publishing if that campus location matters for the page.

### National Kaohsiung Normal University (國立高雄師範大學)
- URL: https://w3.nknu.edu.tw/en/ (Chinese-only / thin English stub — English nav has just Home, About NKNU, Administration, Academics, Virtual Campus Tour; minimal depth compared to the other three)
- Founded: 1954 (as Taiwan Provincial Kaohsiung Female Teachers' College); became National Kaohsiung Teachers' College in 1980; re-established as a normal university August 1, 1989
- Type: Public — Students: approx. 3,370 undergraduate, 3,393 postgraduate, 845 doctoral (total roughly 7,600)
- Facts: Five colleges — Education, Humanities, Science, Technology, Arts; two campuses (Ho-Ping, 13 hectares, urban Lingya District; Yanchao, 50 hectares, inaugurated 1990); QS Asia ranking 901+ (2025); roughly 70% of teachers and education administrators in southern Taiwan have ties to the university.
- Culture connection: As the region's principal teacher-training institution since 1954, NKNU has shaped generations of educators across southern Taiwan.
- Address (Heping Campus): 802561 高雄市苓雅區和平一路116號 (second campus: 824004 高雄市燕巢區深中路62號)

### Kaohsiung Medical University (高雄醫學大學)
- URL: https://www.kmu.edu.tw/index.php/en-gb/ (real English site — nav includes About KMU, Academic, Administration, Research, Admissions, Regulations, plus Students/Parents/Faculty/Alumni/Visitors sections)
- Founded: October 16, 1954 (民國43年)
- Type: Private (oldest private medical school in Taiwan) — Students: approx. 6,621 (2023)
- Facts: Eight academic colleges including Medicine (with Post-Baccalaureate program), Dentistry, Pharmacy, Nursing, Health Science, Life Sciences; founded by physician Tu Tsung-ming to address a shortage of doctors in southern Taiwan; operates seven affiliated teaching hospitals including Chung-Ho Memorial Hospital, the largest medical center in southern Taiwan; only medical academic institution in Taiwan offering graduate-entry (post-baccalaureate) medical programs.
- Culture connection: Founded specifically to remedy the historical lack of medical infrastructure in southern Taiwan, KMU and its hospital network remain the backbone of healthcare access for Kaohsiung and the surrounding region.
- Address: 807378 高雄市三民區十全一路100號 (main entrance via 同盟一路)
