### 高雄市立三民高級家事商業職業學校 (Sanmin Vocational High School)
- URL: https://www.smvhs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1987 (民國76年); planning began 1986
- Facts: Opened with 8 home-economics/commerce departments; PE class added 1994; comprehensive-high-school track 1999-2017; basketball team 2 HBL championships, 14 final-eight appearances.
- Culture connection: Sits amid Zuoying's modern commercial core near the Kaohsiung Arena and Ruifeng Night Market.
- Address: 高雄市左營區裕誠路1102號
- Level: senior-high (vocational)

### 高雄市立左營高級中學 (Zuoying Senior High School)
- URL: https://www.tyhs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1946 (民國35年) as 高雄市立第四初級中學; renamed 省立左營中學 (1960), 台灣省立左營高級中學 (1970), 高雄市立左營高級中學 (1979, when Kaohsiung became a special municipality)
- Facts: Address is on 海功路 ("Navy Merit Road"); coed with dance and PE special classes.
- Culture connection: Its street name and founding era reflect Zuoying's identity as Taiwan's largest naval base town.
- Address: 高雄市左營區海功路55號
- Level: senior-high

### 高雄市立新莊高級中學 (Hsin Chuang Senior High School)
- URL: https://www.hchs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: prep office 1992, established July 1 1995 (民國84年)
- Facts: ~1,492 students (2023); archery/tennis/roller-hockey since 2006; bilingual science class since 2021.
- Culture connection: Located in Zuoying's "Xinzhuang" (新莊, "new village") sub-area.
- Address: 高雄市左營區文慈路99號
- Level: senior-high
- FLAG: unrelated to the different 新莊高中 in New Taipei City — do not confuse.

### 高雄市立海青高級工商職業學校 (Haiching Vocational High School)
- URL: https://www.hcvs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1949 (民國38年); traces to "Nanjing Naval Dependents' School" founded by Admiral 桂永清 in Nanjing, relocated to Taiwan with Navy families 1949; became Kaohsiung municipal school 1981
- Facts: Nicknamed "北復興，南海青" for design programs; alumni include Olympic weightlifter Hsu Shu-ching and former Premier Lin Chuan.
- Culture connection: A living link to the ROC Navy's 1949 retreat to Taiwan and the naval dependents' villages that shaped Zuoying.
- Address: 高雄市左營區左營大路1號
- Level: senior-high (vocational)
- NOTE: shares institutional lineage (same founder, Admiral Gui Yongqing) with 永清國小 below — worth cross-referencing.

### 大義國中 (Dayi Junior High School)
- URL: https://www.dyjh.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1970 (民國59年); moved into permanent site 1972
- Facts: Wind/marching band since 1996, a fixture at local celebrations near the Lotus Pond scenic area.
- Culture connection: Near Lotus Pond (蓮池潭) and the East Gate of the old Fongshan County walled city.
- Address: 高雄市左營區翠華路687號
- Level: junior-high

### 左營國中 (Zuoying Junior High School)
- URL: https://www.tyjh.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1963 (民國52年) as 高雄市立左營第八初級中學
- Facts: Briefly girls-only from 1971 (paired with all-boys 大義國中); relocated 2007 from Lotus Pond-area campus to "New Zuoying."
- Culture connection: The 2007 relocation reflects Zuoying's shift from its historic core to newly developed urban land.
- Address: 高雄市左營區曾子路281號
- Level: junior-high

### 文府國中 (Wenfu Junior High School)
- URL: https://www.zywfjh.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: approx. 2014-2015 (民國103-104年), exact date not found — described as Kaohsiung's youngest junior high in a 2021 source
- Facts: 708 students; beside the Kaohsiung HSR station; strong international-education focus.
- Culture connection: Its location beside the high-speed rail station embodies Zuoying's role as Kaohsiung's transit gateway.
- Address: 高雄市左營區文府路401號
- Level: junior-high

### 福山國中 (Fushan Junior High School)
- URL: https://www.fsjh.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 2001 (民國90年); planning began 1999
- Facts: Triangular building design echoing nearby Banping Mountain; construction delayed by contractor bankruptcy, completed 2002.
- Culture connection: Named for and shaped after 半屏山 (Banping Mountain), the flat-topped limestone landmark dominating Zuoying's skyline.
- Address: 高雄市左營區重信路215號
- Level: junior-high

### 立德國中 (Lide Junior High School)
- URL: https://www.ltjh.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1974 (民國63年), initially named 明德國中, renamed 舊城國中 (naming conflict with a Keelung school), then 立德國中 in March 1975
- Facts: Built on 2.4 hectares split off from 明德國小; over 80% of early students from military families.
- Culture connection: Its rapid string of name changes traces to Zuoying's identity as home to a major naval district and military housing communities.
- Address: 高雄市左營區實踐路101號
- Level: junior-high
- NOTE: built on land carved from 明德國小 below — the two schools share a common origin story.

### 龍華國中 (Longhua Junior High School)
- URL: https://www.lhjh.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: approved Aug 30 1982; began enrollment Sept 1983
- Facts: Grew from 4 to 78 classes; established to relieve overcrowding from Sanmin-district population growth.
- Address: 高雄市左營區自由二路2號
- Level: junior-high
- FLAG: NOT the same as the unrelated 高雄市鼓山區龍華國民小學 (elementary school, different district and level).

### 勝利國小 (Shengli/Victory Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.slps.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1964 (民國53年); renamed 1968
- Facts: First principal 林維興.
- Address: 高雄市左營區南屏路1號
- Level: elementary

### 左營國小 (Zuoying Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.typs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1940 (Japanese colonial era) as 左營尋常小學校, established for children of Japanese naval families after the Imperial Japanese Navy built Zuoying harbor in 1937; taken over by ROC city government 1945.
- Facts: Featured in Taiwan Panorama as an "innovative future school"; has an English village and a judo room.
- Culture connection: Its founding is a direct legacy of the 1937 Japanese naval harbor construction that gave Zuoying its enduring military character.
- Address: 高雄市左營區實踐路42號
- Level: elementary

### 文府國小 (Wenfu Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.wfps.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: not found — established to relieve overcrowding at 福山國小 (opened 1999), likely early-to-mid 2000s
- Facts: 64 classes, 1,942 students, 98 staff.
- Address: 高雄市左營區文府路399號
- Level: elementary

### 新光國小 (Xinguang Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.skps.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: planning began 2000; opened August 2002 (民國91年)
- Facts: Located at Huaxia/Zengzi Rd intersection in the "凹仔底" (Aozihdi) new town; borrowed 勝利國小's facilities until 2003.
- Culture connection: Sits in Aozihdi, Zuoying's fast-growing new business/high-rise district near the Kaohsiung Arena.
- Address: 高雄市左營區華夏路800號
- Level: elementary

### 新民國小 (Xinmin Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.shmps.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: split from 新莊國小 as a branch 1984, independent July 1985 (民國74年) with 5 classes, 566 students
- Facts: Known for its "新民童畫" (Xinmin Children's Art) club; large green campus.
- Address: 高雄市左營區自由三路478號

### 新莊國小 (Xinzhuang Elementary School, Zuoying)
- URL: https://www.sjps.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1954 (民國43年8月), independent from a predecessor branch (鼎金國民學校新莊分部 1951 → 舊城國民學校新莊仔分部 1952); renamed 1968.

### 明德國小 (Mingde Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.mdps.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1957 (民國46年); Navy donated the land, city government funded construction
- Address: 高雄市左營區自立新村362號
- Level: elementary
- NOTE: 立德國中 above was carved out of this school's land in 1974 and was briefly even named "明德國中" before renaming — documented common origin.

### 永清國小 (Yongqing Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.ycps.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1948 (民國37年) in Nanjing as 海軍子弟學校 (Navy Dependents' School), founded by Admiral 桂永清; relocated to Taiwan 1949; taken over by Kaohsiung city government Aug 1 1966, renamed in honor of its founder.
- Facts: Sits within the boundary of the 鳳山縣舊城 (Fongshan County Old City) historic site, near Shoushan National Nature Park.
- Culture connection: Directly named after Admiral Gui Yongqing, whose naval dependents' schools also gave rise to 海青工商 above — both living monuments to Zuoying's post-1949 Navy identity.
- Address: 高雄市左營區左營路2-2號
- Level: elementary

### 福山國小 (Fushan Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.fsps.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: August 1 1999 (民國88年)
- Facts: Overcrowding here directly led to founding of 福山國中 (2001) and 文府國小 nearby.
- Culture connection: Named after 半屏山 (Banping Mountain), also honored in 福山國中's triangular architecture.
- Address: 高雄市左營區重愛路99號
- Level: elementary
