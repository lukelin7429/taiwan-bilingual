### 高雄女中 (Kaohsiung Girls' Senior High School)
- URL: https://www.kghs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1924 (Taisho 13, Japanese colonial era) as 高雄州立高雄高等女學校; renamed 台灣省立高雄第一女子中學 1945; current name after Kaohsiung became a special municipality. Centennial celebrated 2024.
- Facts: ~2,250 students in 63 classes; alumni include TV anchor 沈春華, Mayor 陳其邁, numerous legislators/judges; joint "over a century" reunion events with sister school 雄中 in 2024.
- Culture connection: Its 1924 founding tracks the same wave of Japanese-era girls' schools built as Takao was renamed "Kaohsiung" in 1920.
- Address: 高雄市前金區五福三路122號
- Level: senior-high
- Common name: 高雄女中/女中 confirmed.

### 前金國中 (Qianjin Junior High)
- URL: https://www.ccjh.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1946 (民國35年) as 高雄市立第二初級中學; renamed 前金國中 1968 with coeducation
- Facts: Absorbed the short-lived 市立第三初級中學 1947; added senior-high division 1954 (briefly combined); central Kaohsiung's first PU running track 1990; gifted/music/arts-talent classes.
- Culture connection: Sits in Cianjin (前金), one of old Takao's earliest-settled hamlets, named after a historic capital fund ("前金" literally "advance capital") tied to early land reclamation near the Love River estuary.
- Address: 高雄市前金區六合二路278號
- Level: junior-high

### 阿蓮國民中學 (Alian Junior High)
- URL: https://www.alm.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1957 (民國46年), organized as 縣立岡山初中阿蓮分部; independent as 高雄縣立阿蓮初級中學 by 1958-59; renamed 阿蓮國民中學 1968
- Facts: Added a supplementary/remedial school 1986.
- Culture connection: Alian's name derives from Siraya language — "Ali" referencing the ancestral spirit Alizu (阿立祖) and "-en" meaning "place of" — later reinterpreted in 1946 as "阿蓮" (lotus) for its Buddhist Pure Land associations, linking Alian and neighboring Mituo District's names.
- Address: 高雄市阿蓮區民生路178號
- Level: junior-high

### 彌陀國民中學 (Mituo Junior High)
- URL: https://www.mtm.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1975 (民國64年), independent from 梓官國中's Mituo branch (established 1973)
- Facts: promotional tagline "「彌」戀學習「陀」展漁鄉."
- Culture connection: Mituo's name evolved from the old port name "彌羅港"/"微羅港," later reinterpreted as "彌陀" after Amitabha Buddha; the district is also Taiwan's historic center of shadow-puppet theater, reputedly introduced ~300 years ago with Koxinga's forces.
- Address: 高雄市彌陀區海尾村進學路136號
- Level: junior-high

### 南安國小 (Nan'an Elementary)
- URL: https://www.nap.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: Sept 1 1953 (民國42年) as "彌陀國校南安分校," serving the remote Nanliao and Lutai villages where seasonal flooding made travel to Mituo dangerous; independent Sept 1 1954 (民國43年)
- Facts: first incoming class had 82 students in a single classroom; classes initially held partly in a local temple.
- Culture connection: Founded specifically to solve a local transportation/flooding hardship for coastal Nanliao/Lutai children — a direct product of Mituo's low-lying coastal geography.
- Address: 高雄市彌陀區漯底里樂安路1號
- Level: elementary

### 壽齡國小 (Shouling Elementary)
- URL: https://www.sli.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: Aug 1 1952 (民國41年) as a branch of 彌陀國民學校 called 文吉分校; independent Sept 1 1954 (民國43年) as 文吉國民學校; renamed "壽齡" Aug 1 1958 (民國47年) to honor local benefactor 張壽齡, who donated the school land

### 彌陀國小 (Mituo Elementary)
- URL: https://www.mtp.kh.edu.tw/

- Culture connection: Mituo is nationally known as Taiwan's shadow-puppet-theater heartland ("皮影戲窟"), and this elementary school actively teaches the art form as living heritage.
- Address: not found (village-level location only: 彌陀區海尾里)
