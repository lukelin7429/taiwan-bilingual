### 興糖國小 (Xingtang Elementary School)

### 月美國小 (Yuemei Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.ymp.kh.edu.tw/

- Culture connection: Named for Yuemei village ("crescent-moon eyebrow" — a river-bend toponym), reflecting the area's hardscrabble settlement history.

- Level: elementary

### 杉林國小 (Shanlin Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.sal.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: April 1 1922 (民國11年) as "杉林公學校杉林分教場," originally 10 boys and 8 girls in one class
- Facts: Located in a "非山非市" (neither mountain-remote nor urban-adjacent) designated community; integrates aesthetic education with local food-culture and textile-craft courses.
- Culture connection: Shanlin District's own name comes from its Japanese-era (1901) renaming — previously called "楠梓山" (Nanzi Mountain), renamed "杉林" ("cedar forest") for its dense original cedar woodland.
- Address: 高雄市杉林區杉林里合森巷41號
- Level: elementary
