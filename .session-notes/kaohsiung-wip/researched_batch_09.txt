### 中庄國民中學 (Zhongzhuang Junior High School)
- URL: https://www.zzm.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: began 1989 as branch of Daliao JH; independent 1991 (民國80年)
- Facts: First principal Qu Yan-wen.
- Culture connection: 中庄 ("middle village") sits in the historically agricultural northern part of Daliao.
- Address: 高雄市大寮區光明路三段1437號
- Level: junior-high

### 大寮國民中學 (Daliao Junior High School)
- URL: https://www.dlm.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1968-08-01 (民國57年)
- Facts: First public secondary school in Daliao; spun off two branch schools that later became independent (Chaoliao JH 1983, Zhongzhuang JH 1991); ~706 students (2022); campus reconstruction from 2011 won a 2013 Yuan-ye Architecture Award.
- Culture connection: Anchors "Daliao" (大寮, "big shed/hut"), reflecting the district's origin as farm huts/storage sheds used by early Han settlers.
- Address: 高雄市大寮區進學路150號
- Level: junior-high

### 潮寮國民中學 (Chaoliao Junior High School)
- URL: https://www.tlm.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: began 1978 as branch of Daliao JH; independent 1983 (民國72年)
- Facts: In 2009 drew public/legislative attention when odor pollution forced classes to relocate.
- Culture connection: 潮寮 ("tide hut") sits near the Gaoping River and Dafa Industrial Park, historically prone to tidal/flood influence.
- Address: 高雄市大寮區潮寮里潮寮路51-9號
- Level: junior-high

### 中庄國小 (Zhongzhuang Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.zzn.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1920 (Japanese colonial era) as branch of 大寮公學校; independent 1937 (民國26年)
- Facts: Started borrowing space in a local temple with one class; 111學年度 had 34 classes, 884 students.
- Culture connection: Traces back to Daliao's original Japanese-era public school (大寮公學校).
- Address: 高雄市大寮區中庄路59號
- Level: elementary

### 大寮國小 (Daliao Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.dlp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1965 (民國54年) as independent school (originally a branch of Yongfang Elementary; precursor founding year not found)
- Facts: First school in the township to serve school lunches (1967); ~643 students.
- Culture connection: Despite bearing the district's name, this is a relatively modern (1965) offshoot — the older lineage is actually carried by 永芳國小 (see below).
- Address: 高雄市大寮區大寮路740號
- Level: elementary
- FLAG: shares an identical "independent 1965, formerly a Yongfang ES branch" origin claim with 忠義國小 below — corroborated across multiple sources, likely a genuine coincidence (Yongfang spun off two branches the same year), not a research error.

### 忠義國小 (Zhongyi Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.zyi.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1965-08 (民國54年) as independent school (originally branch of Yongfang Elementary)
- Facts: Sits at the foot of Fenghuang (Phoenix) Mountain, adjacent to the Army Academy; bilingual/ecology/mountain-wilderness/technology-themed pilot school; first principal Luo Ren-zhen (1968).
- Culture connection: Proximity to the Army Academy at Fenghuang Mountain ties it to Kaohsiung's post-1949 military-education history.
- Address: 高雄市大寮區忠義里忠義路1號
- Level: elementary
- FLAG: extremely common school name nationwide (Taipei, New Taipei, Tainan all have unrelated 忠義國小s) — all confirmed separate institutions from this Daliao one.

### 昭明國小 (Zhaoming Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.zmp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1918 (Japanese colonial era) as "赤崁公學校"; renamed 昭明國校 after 1945, 昭明國民小學 1968
- Facts: Over 100 years of history; branch campus near Kaohsiung's Pingding Special District with Japanese-era remnants overlooking the old Xiashui River.
- Culture connection: Branch campus's hillside view of the "下淡水溪" (old name for today's Gaoping River) links it to the river system that shaped southern Kaohsiung's settlement.
- Address: 高雄市大寮區鳳林一路300巷80號
- Level: elementary

### 永芳國小 (Yongfang Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.yfp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1898 (Meiji 31, Japanese colonial era) as a "國語傳習所"; formal branch school 1908
- Facts: Long renaming lineage: 1908 山仔頂分校 → 1915 翁公園分校 → 1918 independent 翁公園公學校 → 1921 大寮公學校 → 1941 大寮國民學校 → 1947 renamed 永芳國民學校 (matching Yongfang Village location).
- Culture connection: Actually carries the historical lineage of the original "大寮公學校" (Daliao's very first school), even though now named after Yongfang village rather than Daliao — write this nuance honestly rather than presenting 大寮國小 as the oldest Daliao school.
- Address: 高雄市大寮區永芳里鳳林三路499號
- Level: elementary
- NOTE: 1915 branch name "翁公園分校" phonetically resembles the separate "翁園國小" elsewhere in the district — NOT the same institution, confirmed as a coincidental shared toponymic root, not a shared lineage.
