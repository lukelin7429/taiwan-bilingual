### 內門國小
- URL: https://www.nmp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1903 (Japanese colonial era) as "蕃薯藔公學校," renamed "內門公學校" 1904
- Facts: relocated to current campus 1910; kindergarten added 1975; centennial 2002; over 120 years of continuous operation.
- Culture connection: Neimen was historically called "羅漢門" (Luohanmen) and is the acknowledged birthplace of the region's 宋江陣 martial-arts tradition.
- Address: 高雄市內門區內豐里內埔24號
- Level: elementary

### 桃源國中 (高雄市立)

### 茄萣國小
- URL: https://www.qdp.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1906 (Japanese colonial era; school's own history records "民前6年") as a branch, "大湖公學校頂茄萣分校"; independent 1917 as "頂茄萣公學校" (classes initially held at the 頂茄萣賜福宮 temple); relocated 1938 to present site near 媽祖婆山
- Facts: branched off both 成功國小 (1952) and 砂崙國小 (1957/58); ~606-667 students depending on source year; clubs include ukulele, Chinese orchestra, 宋江陣 folk martial arts, judo, tennis.
- Culture connection: the school's location by 媽祖婆山 and its own 宋江陣 club reflect Cieding's maritime Mazu-worship culture and its historical links to the same folk martial-arts tradition found inland in Neimen District.
- Address: 高雄市茄萣區茄萣路二段307號
- Level: elementary

NOTE: 茄萣 is a polyphone read "jiā" here (as in "jiading"), not "qié" as in the common word for eggplant — worth noting for transliteration. 茄萣國小/成功國小/砂崙國小 are historically one lineage (branch splits), not duplicates.
