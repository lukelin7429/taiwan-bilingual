### 中正高工 (高雄市立中正高級工業職業學校, slug zhongzhenggaogong-hs)
- URL: https://www.ccvs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1980 (民國69年), opened July 1 1980
- Facts: Opened with machine/electrical/construction/chemical engineering departments; campus served as a relief center during the August 2014 Kaohsiung gas explosion disaster.
- Culture connection: Sits in Cianjhen, an industrial district that boomed alongside Kaohsiung's postwar heavy industry — founded to train workers for that base.
- Address: 高雄市前鎮區光華二路80號
- Level: senior-high (vocational)
- NOTE: distinct from 中正高中 (Lingya) and 中正國小 (Lingya) — three separate "中正"-named schools, do not merge.

### 前鎮高中 (高雄市立前鎮高級中學, slug qianzhengaozhong-hs)
- URL: https://www.cjhs.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1969 (民國58年), originally "Taiwan Provincial Cianjhen Senior High School"; renamed municipal 1979
- Facts: Kaohsiung's fourth public academic high school; Kaohsiung region's first fine-arts experimental class (1984); ~1,700 students.
- Culture connection: District name "Cianjhen" dates to the Ming dynasty, when Koxinga's vanguard garrison (宣毅前鎮) settled and farmed here.
- Address: 高雄市前鎮區鎮中路132號
- Level: senior-high

### 瑞祥高中 (高雄市立瑞祥高級中學, slug ruixianggaozhong-hs)
- URL: https://www.rssh.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1983 (民國72年), established July 1 1983
- Facts: Kaohsiung's first school converted into a complete (JH+SH) high school; bilingual experimental class since 2021; dual-diploma with an American high school since 2020.
- Culture connection: Located on Banchao Road (班超路), named after Han-dynasty general Ban Chao.
- Address: 高雄市前鎮區班超路63號
- Level: senior-high
- NOTE: related-by-name but a separate institution from 瑞祥國小 below (founded 1980, different school).

### 光華國中 (高雄市立光華國民中學)

### 紅毛港國小
- URL: https://www.hmgps.kh.edu.tw/
- Founded: 1924 (Japanese colonial era) as "大林蒲公學校紅毛港分教場"; multiple renames (紅毛港公學校 → 紅毛港國民學校/國民小學 1946 → 海汕國民學校 1951 → 海汕國民小學 1968)
- Facts: Development banned ~30 years due to the Hongmaogang village relocation tied to Kaohsiung Harbor's intercontinental container terminal; stopped enrolling for a year in 2007, rebuilt and reopened April 2008 under its restored historic name, incorporating Hongmaogang cultural motifs (coral-rock walls, mosaic art, memory corridor).
- Culture connection: Hongmaogang was a centuries-old fishing village at the mouth of Kaohsiung Harbor, entirely relocated/demolished in the 2000s for port expansion — this school's renames and 2008 rebuild directly track that community's displacement.
- Address: 高雄市前鎮區明鳳七街1號
- Level: elementary
- FLAG: unusually complex identity history (four name changes, village-demolition relocation, one-year enrollment suspension) — verify secondary source dates before publishing.
