### 小林國小 (Xiaolin Elementary School)
- URL: https://www.xln.kh.edu.tw
- Founded: 1937 (Japanese colonial era) at the original Xiaolin village site
- Facts: The original school and the entire Xiaolin community (neighborhoods 9-18) were destroyed Aug 9 2009 during Typhoon Morakot, when a landslide-dammed lake breach buried the village — 491 people missing/deceased per official accounts. ### 民權國小 (Namasia, slug ks-minquan-es)
- URL: https://www.mcp.kh.edu.tw
- Founded: predecessor "Magatun Aboriginal Children's Education Institute" 1904; renamed "Minquan National School" 1951; became current name 1968
- Facts: ~66 students (2019), predominantly Bunun with Kanakanavu and Hla'alua students. Original campus buried by a Typhoon Morakot landslide (2009); students temporarily relocated to Guanting Elementary and Sanmin JH. Rebuilt 2011 with Delta Electronics Foundation support on the elevated "Minquan platform," achieving Taiwan's highest "Diamond-level" green building certification; now doubles as a typhoon-season community shelter.
- Culture connection: Per the community's own oral history, the district and river carry the name of a Kanakanavu youth, Maasia, who sacrificed himself for his people — the rebuilt school stands as both an educational and cultural resilience symbol after Morakot.
- Address: 高雄市那瑪夏區瑪雅里平和巷220號
- Level: elementary
- CONFIRMED DISTINCT from 民權國小 in Cianjhen District (Kaohsiung's own urban area) — this is genuinely the older, mountain Indigenous-community school.
